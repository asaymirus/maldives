DESCRIPTION
Located on the water on Thilamaafushi Island in the Lhaviyani Atoll, Le Méridien Maldives Resort & Spa is a 360-degree canvas inspired by the formation of the Atolls, fringing reefs and marine life.
Spanning nine hectares, the island is a true castaway experience abundant with indigenous flora and fauna enveloped by a shimmering lagoon and coral reefs teeming with marine life, including pods of manta rays and turtles. Every aspect of our hotel’s design takes its cues from a mid-century design aesthetic and the rich European heritage of Maldives. Savor the sights, sounds, aromas and tastes of the Lhaviyani Atoll with six restaurants and bars and Explore Spa by Le Méridien. Inspired by the essence of the Maldives, combined with a passion for culture, cuisine and design, our overwater resort is a playful sanctuary for the curious and creative traveler to discover the European spirit of savoring the good life.
The resort’s design is a canvas inspired by the formation of the Maldives archipelago, its unique topography, fringing reefs and vibrant marine life.
This blueprint is fused with Le Méridien’s mid-century and Bauhaus philosophies to present a concept that unites art, renewable materials, and industrial design.
These ideas are coupled with splashes of muted hues and playful design touches to present a resort that sits in harmony with the natural environment

Dining
Turquoise
Eclectic in design, Turquoise is a fresh, interactive restaurant where guests can graze from breakfast to dinner with the ocean breezes and endless vistas of Maldives. Guests enjoy local dishes alongside Asian favorites and modern European tastes.
Tabemasu
Defined as “to eat” in Japanese, Tabemasu is a distinct dining experience where art, design and cuisine combine to create a chic dining journey featuring regional tastes that meld modern and traditional techniques with an haute-French mise en place.
Waves Cafe
Enjoy this space for mindfully conscious dining and drinking. Feed and nourish the soul with wholesome ingredients presented in a simply light uncomplicated fashion. Celebrate the power of produce with organic and sustainably sourced ingredients.
Velaa Bar + Grill
Velaa Bar & Grill is the essence of Maldives beachside dining, morning til night. Bask in the summer sun with light-bodied wines, hand-crafted cocktails and light dishes and once the stars twinkle relish grilled seafood, meats and wood-fired delicacies.
Riviera
Lazy lunches at this dining spot often culminate in views of the best sunsets in Maldives. Here, guests linger over petite plates, freshly shucked oysters and chilled seafood complemented by sparkling wines, rosé and crisp spritzes. Adults 18+ only.
Latitude
The social hub of our Maldives resort, this restaurant transitions from a relaxed café serving refreshing eye openers, Illy coffee and deli bites to a sparkling evening spot featuring a modern apéritif hour serving artistic liqueurs and petite plates.

Highlights From The Resort
Explore Spa by Le Meridien
Explore Spa pulses with life full of magnetic energy fused with artistic expression.
A mindful space that takes cues from the iconic European bathhouse experience – fused with simplicity, nature, and relaxation and designed to energise and inspire a sense of place.
Le Meridien Family & Kids’ Hub
To celebrate the Maldives, the Kids’ Hub is a thoughtfully designed space that educates guests on the atoll’s abundant flora and fauna thriving on land and sea.
Through an edu-taining program that nurtures discovery and conservation, the Kids’ Hub aims to engage through a curated program that invites make-believers, aspiring inventors, junior culinarians, and little culture-seekers into an immersive world where toying, tinkering, and free-play are celebrated.
Families will enjoy our curated menu of purposely designed family expeditions that include nature trails, coral restoration, mangrove conservation, traditional fishing, dolphin sightings, private island explorations, and more.
Waves Fitness centre
Revitalise your body and mind offering yoga with views of the Indian Ocean, weekly classes and more.
Weddings and Occasions
Host your wedding party in the Maldives and give them memories to take home for life.
Marine Escapades
Known for its beauty, abundant marine life, and some of the best dive sites, Lhaviyani Atoll features more than 50 dive sites, most within reach from the resort.
In collaboration with Sub-Oceanic, the resort’s dive partner, thrill-seekers can discover coral caves, sprawling reefs, and “Thilas” full of marine life.
Go scuba diving or snorkelling, drift alongside Manta Rays, turtles, and shoals of vivid marine life.
Immerse in the resort’s verdant flora and fauna above and below the waves, dive into eco-conscious philosophies, explore local customs, or glide across the Indian Ocean in search of dolphins.
