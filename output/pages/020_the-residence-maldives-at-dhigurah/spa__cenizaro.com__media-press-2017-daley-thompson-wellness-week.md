April 2017
- Olympic athlete, gold medallist and sporting legend Daley Thompson will be the star personality at a week-long wellness holiday at The Residence Mauritius from 11 - 19 September 2017. The hotel has partnered exclusively with Trailfinders to create this once-in-a-lifetime wellness experience for just 30 guests. The holiday includes group fitness sessions, yoga, Pilates, tennis, swimming, running and more…
Offering a wonderfully luxurious setting, The Residence Mauritius - a Cenizaro hotel - is set along a kilometre stretch of white powder sand on the east coast of the island. Inspired by turn-of-the-century plantation houses, it has a warm colonial charm that is combined with an air of sophisticated elegance. The Residence Mauritius offers luxuriously appointed rooms with large marble bathrooms, spacious balconies or terraces and each room has its own butler. There is also the elegant Sanctuary Spa and plenty of healthy, culinary dishes available at the hotel’s three restaurants including the oceanfront seafood and Creole restaurant, The Plantation. It really is the perfect place to ensure bodies are revitalised, weary minds are restored and ruffled spirits are soothed.
Double gold Olympic medallist Daley Thompson – who has also held four world records and won three Commonwealth titles – will be working personally with the group. Daley has created a training programme that is both engaging and fun and will suit all fitness levels; from fitness fanatics to guests looking to combine an active break with relaxation. Set over five days – to allow ample time for some R&R too - the programme will include daily hour-long group sessions led by Daley, yoga or Pilates classes led by a highly-qualified instructor. Daley’s priority is to ensure each guest finishes the programme having achieved their personal goals and objectives. Two further nights at The Residence Mauritius will give guests space and time to make use of the hotel’s tennis courts, cycling routes and beach – or simply switch off, before returning home feeling motivated, relaxed and refreshed.
