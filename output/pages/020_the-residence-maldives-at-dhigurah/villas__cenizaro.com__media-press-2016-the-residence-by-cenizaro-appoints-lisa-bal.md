October 2016 -
The Residence by Cenizaro has announced the appointment of Lisa Balsom as Director of Marketing – Europe & Middle East.
Lisa Balsom joins The Residence by Cenizaro from LUX* Resorts & Hotels where she was Head of Sales & Marketing. Bringing more than 25 years of expertise in the airline and hotel industry – across the UK, Europe and Middle East – Lisa is well placed to drive The Residence forward.
Gary Xie, Executive Director, said
“We are incredibly pleased to welcome Lisa to The Residence by Cenizaro family. Lisa is a seasoned specialist in the travel industry with a brilliant track record. We are certain that her appointment will bring strong success for the brand in the European and Middle Eastern markets and we look forward to her being key to the next phase for the company as we evolve and expand.”
Previous to her role at LUX* Resorts & Hotels, Lisa was responsible for creating the Luxury Hotels division for Dnata Travel, based in Dubai, following roles as Director of Leisure Sales with Jumeirah Hotels & Resorts and a role with British Airways.
With a deep understanding of luxury hospitality and strong reputation in the industry, Lisa will lead marketing initiatives across The Residence by Cenizaro portfolio and take responsibility for developing and enhancing the performance of key properties from her base in London.
The Residence by Cenizaro portfolio currently comprises The Residence Mauritius, The Residence Maldvies, The Residence Zanzibar and The Residence Tunis. The company is set to open The Residence Bintan, Indonesia and The Residence Dhigurah, the company’s second Maldives property, in 2017.
