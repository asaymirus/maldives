An Authentic Taste Of Tunisia
El Dar
The essence of typical Tunisian home and hospitality comes alive at El Dar. Relax amid the warm and welcoming ambience and feast on a menu that offers the distinctive spiced flavours and scents of Tunisian dishes and fresh mint tea amidst the fruity aromas of ‘shishas’ or waterpipes.
Once a week, El Dar adds a touch of mystique and magic to the night with a belly dance performance. From July to September, this magical night takes place in the gardens beneath a starlit sky.
