1001 NIGHTS SHOW
You will head towards the rocky desert of Agafay, 40 min from Marrakech, either in a WWII sidecar or in our Mercedes Vito. Upon arrival, enjoy a sunset camel ride and upon return to your camp, meet a Gnaoua band who will entertain you as you sip fresh mint tea. Interact with a Berber lady baking bread in a traditional clay oven that you can dip in virgin oil and degustate. Meet an artist who will suggest multiple designs for a memorable henna tattoo, From there, indulge yourself in a set menu dinner in a private tent while entertained by a belly dancer and fire eater.
