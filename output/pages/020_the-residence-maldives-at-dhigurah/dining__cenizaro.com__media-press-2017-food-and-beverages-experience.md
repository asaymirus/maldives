May 2017
- To celebrate summer, The Residence Maldives and The Residence Mauritius have just announced a variety of exciting gastronomic experiences for guests to enjoy.
The Residence Maldives
The Residence Maldives, is on the southern island of Falhumaafushi in the Gaafu Alifu atoll, part of one of the largest and deepest atolls in The Maldives. The beautiful island provides the perfect location for private dining experiences and romantic beach dinners with only the sound of waves softly serenading guests.
VIBES Destination Dining Experiences
Stars (USD 500++ per person)
Relax on a private castaway island and create unforgettable memories with a romantic dinner. Watch the sky fill with millions of stars as your personal chef takes you on a gastronomical experience with your loved one.
Garden (USD 300++ per person)
Set within the lush greenery and flourishing gardens on the northern tip of the island, this culinary experience includes a four-course dinner complemented with spectacular views across the Indian Ocean.
The Falhumaa Cooking Class (USD 60++ per person)
Embrace the local Maldivian culture and learn how to cook traditional dishes with Executive Chef Amour and his talented team at the Falhumaa restaurant. Guests will learn to cook Asian delicacies and European cuisine. Following the cooking class, guests will be able to enjoy their culinary creations over lunch with a glass of wine.
Island Summer Savings Package (Now half price from USD 450 ++)
Celebrate the summer holidays in the Maldives with the Island Summer Savings package and receive 50% off the best available rates, complimentary breakfast and a daily credit of USD 100 per villa.
Guests can visit this tropical paradise, swim in the turquoise    clear waters and sample local and European cuisine.

The Residence Mauritius
The Residence Mauritius is situated on the east coast of Mauritius along a mile-long beach of immaculate white sand, fringed by the azure Indian Ocean and tropical gardens.
Mauritian ‘Dine with a Local’ Cooking Class (USD 40+ per person)
The Residence Mauritius, offers guests an exotic cooking journey with Charlène the local chef who spent her childhood living in the coastal village of Trou D’Eau Douce and Belle Mare. With her passion and flair for cooking, she opened her own restaurant on the Bella Mare Public Beach and became known for her popular and tasty local dishes. Before the cooking class, Charlène will take guests to the local market of Central Flacq to purchase fresh local ingredients. During the class, guests will learn to make dishes such as local chicken or fish curry, local vegetables and pickles. In the evening, guests will be invited to Charlène’s house at Trou D’eau Douce to sample the local delicacies.
Cinema under the Stars with Pop-up Bar (Entry cost: USD 30+ per couple)
Experience an alfresco cinema at 9pm every Friday at The Residence Mauritiaus. The Cinema Under the Stars showcases a range of movies each week with comfy bean bag seating. Complete with freshly made popcorn and a pop-up bar, the evening provides a fun night on the beach.
