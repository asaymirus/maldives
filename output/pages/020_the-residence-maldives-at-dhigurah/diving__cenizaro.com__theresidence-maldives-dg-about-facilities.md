PADI 5-Star Dive Centre
Operated by a qualified international team of PADI instructors and divemasters, our comprehensive dive centre offers guided diving and snorkelling trips, a complete PADI Instruction Programme in different languages and a PADI enriched nitrox diver course. Diving equipment and accessories are available to rent or buy.
Watersports Centre
Enjoy a variety of exciting watersports ranging from snorkelling and kayaking to windsurfing and parasailing. Snorkelling equipment is available for loan.
