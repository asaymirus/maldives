In the Heart of the Medina
Location
La Maison Arabe is hidden away in the heart of the ancient medina of Marrakech, close to the Bab Doukkala Mosque and a 10-minute walk from Jemma El Fna Square, at the edge of the souks. It is centrally located for exploring most of the city’s major attractions.
