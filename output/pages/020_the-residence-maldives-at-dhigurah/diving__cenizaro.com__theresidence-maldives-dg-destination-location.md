Gaafu Alifu Atoll
Location
The Residence Maldives at Dhigurah is located in southern Maldives in the Gaafu Alifu Atoll, also known as the Northern Huvadhoo Atoll, one of the largest and deepest atolls in the world. It is connected by a private one-kilometre bridge to its sister resort on the island of Falhumaafushi. Together they make up one of the largest island offerings in the Maldives.
Getting to the resort
:
Your journey to The Residence Maldives at Dhigurah begins upon arrival at Malé International Airport, where you will be welcomed by our airport host and escorted to the domestic airport. Your journey continues with a scenic 55-minute flight (subject to flight schedules) to Kooddoo, followed by a 5-minute speedboat transfer direct to the resort.
