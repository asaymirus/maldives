OUT OF AFRICA EXPEDITION
In recreating the spirit of expeditions across Morocco, we invite you to travel back in time for an unforgettable moment. An hour away from Marrakech, set in the immense and barren vistas of Agafay desert, or in the unspoiled nature of the Kik plateau, our venues match the remarkable landscapes hosting them. You will be driven in a customised Land Rover Defender and be served by a personal cook from La Maison Arabe, and butlers well versed in the art of bespoke service.
