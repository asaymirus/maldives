Island hideaways fringed by white-sand beaches. Smiles of welcome as warm as the sun. Brilliant blue waters as far as the eye can see. Unspoilt coral reefs and spectacular marine life teeming below the ocean’s surface. Also home to secret underwater gardens; lush seagrass meadows form a vital part of the marine ecosystem; providing food, habitat, and nursery areas for numerous species. In a collaborative commitment to protect the delicate ecosystem of the marine life , The Residence Maldives forms part of
‘
Protect Maldives Seagrass
’ initiative launched by the Blue Marine Foundation and the Maldives Underwater Initiative and endorsed by the Maldivian Ministry of Tourism. The Maldives offers the ultimate retreat for beach lovers, marine enthusiasts, families and romantics.
