THE ROOFTOP
A stunning rooftop space inspired by the blue and yellow colors of the Majorelle Garden and offering a fusion cuisine that includes Moroccan flavors.
The fusion cuisine offered at the rooftop draws on the rich culinary heritage of Morocco, incorporating traditional Moroccan ingredients and cooking techniques with other international flavors. Guests can expect to savor an array of creative dishes that offer a unique and exciting dining experience.
