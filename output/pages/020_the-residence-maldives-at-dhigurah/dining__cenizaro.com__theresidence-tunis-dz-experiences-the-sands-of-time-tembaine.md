The Sands of Time – Tembaine
Travel through the Sahara’s desertscapes, graced with golden sands to Tembaine. Under the open sky, amidst the backdrop of majestic dunes and white tents, embrace the spirit of the desert on a nomadic journey. Sit and savour a sumptuous picnic in this striking desert wonder.
Depart on a whirlwind adventure on wheels through the surreal terrain of sands, stopping at the Great Dunes Circus for a breathtaking panoramic view of the tranquil oceans of sand. Uncover the magic of Sahara as the sun sets over the horizon, an enchanting delight to behold.
