The start of a new year is a great time to reset your mind, body and soul. Located in some of the most tranquil and picturesque locations of the world, The Residence by Cenizaro properties provide the ultimate destination for wellness and rejuvenation. Along with their bespoke hospitality and stunning properties, there is something for everyone with their vast array of wellness offerings and packages.
The Residence Maldives Falhumaafushi and Dhigurah
Surrounded by the vivid blues of the Indian ocean, The Residence Maldives is the perfect location to refresh and rewind. Start each day with a complimentary yoga class for all guests on the overwater deck at Falhumaafushi or on the yoga pavilion at Dhigurah. After improving your physical strength and flexibility, unwind with renowned Japanese practitioner Mariko Nakaki. Visiting until January 31st 2022, Nakaki will be sharing her holistic Chi Neng Tsai treatments and energising meditations. If it’s relaxation you’re after, indulge your senses in the first and only Spa by Clarins in The Maldives. Offering blissful spa treatments, the luxurious experience will leave you feeling completely renewed. Signature facial treatments including the ‘Moisture quencher’ restore comfort, softness and radiance. Packages such as The ‘Beauty Sleep’ treatment is a full body and face experience that is designed to have you looking like you’ve had 8 hours of restful sleep…What could be better than that!
The Residence Mauritius
A beachfront paradise surrounded by the lush nature of the island, this property is all about positive vibes and relaxation. To strengthen the body and clear the mind, why not try morning paddle boarding or water-skiing to start the day. For a luxurious spa retreat, the on-site spa is offering a new spa treatment, ‘The power to relax body and mind.’ Included for the body is a 90-minute signature massage of choice and a 30-minute body scrub in a private hammam. On top of that, there is a 60-minute foot reflexology and leg massage, a 45-minute Indian head massage, relaxing back massage and a 60-minute after sun face and body care treatment. Their decadent spa menu is available for those seeking different treatments with a plethora of options for both the face and body, designed for complete restoration.
The Residence Zanzibar
With the festive season having come to an end, take part in a wellness journey at The Residence Zanzibar for detoxing and total renewal. Customisable depending on each individual guest’s preference for length, participants will be treated to activities for the mind, body and soul, creating the ultimate rejuvenation experience. Each day begins with a fresh detox juice or smoothie as well as a foot ritual and includes a personalised chef’s meal during the day. For the four-day program: On day one, guests will be treated to a Body Kahawa Tanzanian coffee scrub, followed by a 75-minute signature massage. Guests will have a consultation with a professional yoga teacher to design a private class plan and to ensure a restful night, the day will end with traditional herbal spiced tea. The second day includes a 60-minute customised body massage as well as a private yoga class on the beach and a sand bank trip. On the third day, experience Rain Forest Face Therapy by Healing earth followed by a meditation and breathing technique session and immersive experiences including a Zanzibar village tour and a trip to the local spice farm. The fourth and final day will include a manicure and pedicure with a fruit plate and canapés as well as a scalp massage. The day will end with a chef’s menu dinner served at the jetty and a yoga nidra session on the beach for sunset. The retreat provides the opportunity to unwind in solitude and rejuvenate both physically and spiritually.
