Water Activities
Scuba Diving - PADI-5 Dive Centre
Gear up to explore the amazing aquatic world of the Indian Ocean. Learn how to scuba dive in the safe hands of our professional instructors. The atoll offers dive sites suitable for all levels.
Snorkelling
Step off your villa deck and into a living, breathing ocean paradise. Our spectacular 3-kilometre house reef begins just meters from shore, offering effortless access to some of the Maldives’ most vibrant marine life. The reef unfolds gradually beneath you, starting in calm, shallow waters perfect for beginners, then extending outward into deeper sections for more adventurous snorkellers. 
Snorkel at your own pace, any time of day, and discover ever-changing underwater scenes—from colourful coral gardens and shimmering schools of fish to graceful turtles and reef sharks gliding by. 
No boats - just slip into the water whenever you feel like it into a world-class reef - pure, immersive beauty right at your doorstep; just steps from your private deck.
Bubblemaker Dive Programme
Children can enjoy their first taste of diving in our fun Bubblemaker Dive programme conducted in the safe environment of our pool. For ages 8 years and up.
