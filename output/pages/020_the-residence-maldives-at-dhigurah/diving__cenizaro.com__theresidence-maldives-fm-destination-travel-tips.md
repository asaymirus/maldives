Travel Tips
Climate:
The Maldives enjoys a near constant temperature of around 30°C all year round. Its tropical climate has two distinct seasons: dry or northeast monsoon season from December to April, and the southwest monsoon season from May to November, which brings some rain.
Airport:
Malé International Airport, located on Hulhulé Island next to the capital Male.
Immigration:
Visitors from all countries will be issued a complimentary 30-day visa on arrival. Visitors must have a passport (with at least six months validity) and a return air ticket. Import of alcohol or alcoholic goods is illegal, including duty free.
Time:
GMT +05:00
Currency:
The local currency is the Maldivian Rufiyaa (MVR), which is divided into 100 larees. However, all prices at the resort are in US Dollars.
Credit Cards:
Credit cards accepted include VISA, MasterCard and AMEX.
Language:
The official language of the Maldives is Dhivehi. English is also widely spoken and understood.
Electricity:
220/240volt AC at 50 cycles. International adapters are available upon request at the resort.
