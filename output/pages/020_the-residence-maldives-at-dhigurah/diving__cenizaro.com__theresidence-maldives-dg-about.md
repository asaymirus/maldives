Feel the demands of the modern world melt away as this 173-villa resort transports you to a haven of unparalleled peace and relaxation, surrounded by nature’s unspoilt beauty. Here you can indulge in the purest of pleasures, lounging on pristine sun-kissed beaches, snorkelling among colourful coral reefs in clear lagoons, and diving with marine creatures in the ocean depths.
Luxuriate in the comfort of the island’s spacious villas beautifully designed in harmony with their surroundings. Savour a wondrous world of international flavours, from Indian Ocean specialties to Mediterranean tapas and contemporary Cantonese cuisine at Li Bai. For the ultimate in pampering, the resort’s exclusive Spa by Clarins wellness retreat, nestled in lush tropical gardens, promises a journey to blissful wellbeing.
Children are also well taken care of with a host of Kids Club activities designed to keep young minds entertained and enthralled. Uncover nature’s intricate eco-systems on a marine discovery or garden stroll; harvesting freshly grown produce at the resort’s own Earth Basket. Indulge further with picnics on our private Castaway Island or at Dhigurah’s sister resort on neighbouring Falhumaafushi island which you can walk to across a one-kilometre bridge. From romantic getaways to memorable family holidays, The Residence Maldives at Dhigurah is the idyllic escape you’ve been dreaming of.
