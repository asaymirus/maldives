STORYTELLING IN A PRIVATE HOME
The Moroccan tradition of storytelling is a distinctive characteristic of Moroccan culture. In an effort to preserve this dying art, folktales, myths and legends are shared with younger generations to keep them alive. Dive into the mystical world of Morocco through the narration of tales and legends of the past, accompanied by a gourmet dinner and conversation with your Host on aspects of modern Moroccan society.
