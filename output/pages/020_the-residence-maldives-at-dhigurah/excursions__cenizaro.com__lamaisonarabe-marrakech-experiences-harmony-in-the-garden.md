HARMONY IN THE GARDEN
​
Live an exceptional experience...
Give meaning to your trip. In an idyllic landscape a few kilometers from Marrakech, discover pleasure for all the senses in an ecological visit to an organic vegetable garden. Find relaxation for the soul, and immerse in the creation of tasty Moroccan cuisine – generous, authentic, and prepared in harmony with nature.

Full day

Spice workshop
Outdoor cooking workshop followed by a bread workshop
