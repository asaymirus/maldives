ON THE ROAD TO ESSAUOIRA
​
Loaded with an eventful history, the port city of Essaouira is a coastal gem, an authentic slice of Morocco facing the ocean. Behind its proud appearance as a prominent historical site, Essaouira is a city of secrets not limited to the tourist trail. Experience the process of making argan oil.

Full day
