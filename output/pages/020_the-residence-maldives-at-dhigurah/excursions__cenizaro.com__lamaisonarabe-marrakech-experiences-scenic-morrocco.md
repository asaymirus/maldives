SCENIC MOROCCO
Heading to the High Atlas Mountains for a sunrise excursion is a fantastic way to experience the natural beauty and culture of Morocco.
Toubkal Mountain, the highest peak in North Africa, will take your breath away. The views from the surrounding trails are truly majestic.
Then, exploring Imlil, a charming mountain village, will give you a taste of local life. The village is a gateway to Toubkal National Park and is famous for its traditional mud-brick houses, terraced fields, and welcoming artisans. even encounter Berber craftspeople, showcasing their beautiful handmade goods, such as rugs, jewelry, and pottery.
After the cool escape in the mountains, shifting to the Ourika Valley is a perfect change of scenery. Nestled along the Ourika River, this lush valley is known for its vibrant green landscapes, beautiful waterfalls, and traditional Berber villages.
