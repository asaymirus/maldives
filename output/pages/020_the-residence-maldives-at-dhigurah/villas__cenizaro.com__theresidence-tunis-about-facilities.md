The Spa & Thalasso
Indulge in rejuvenating thalassotherapy and pampering spa treatments inspired by global spa leader ESPA by Clarins.
The Dolphin Club for Kids
Dedicated to keeping our young guests engaged and entertained with a wide range of indoor and outdoor activities, cared for by our multilingual staff. (For children 3 to 12 years of age.)
Outdoor swimming pool *
Relax in and around our 1,500 sqm free-form swimming pool – one of the largest hotel swimming pools in Tunisia – flanked by umbrella-shaded sun loungers.
Heated to a pleasant 25°C in the cooler months from April, October and November.
Indoor seawater swimming pool *
Float away on a sensation of lightness in our beautiful seawater pool, heated to 30°C all year round to relax and revitalise your body.
The Residence Golf Course
Enjoy one of the finest golf facilities in the region comprising a spectacular 18-hole course perched at the edge of the Mediterranean Sea, a coaching academy, and a clubhouse featuring a restaurant, bar and well-stocked pro shop.
