September 2019
: The Wellness Way Forward with The Residence Maldives
This winter, enhance your wellbeing and unwind in luxury at either The Residence Maldives at Dhigurah or The Residence Maldives at Falhumaafushi, both located in the southern Maldives in the Gaafu Alifu Atoll and joined by the longest bridge in the country. Whether it’s yoga overlooking the sea, waking to the sound of the waves hitting the shores or watching the sunrise whilst you run across the sand, each property promises the ultimate retreats to re-boot tired bodies and minds.
The Residence Maldives at Dhigurah
A palm-fringed paradise cocooned by a lush oasis of nature, turquoise lagoons and calming ocean blues, the newly opened The Residence Maldives at Dhigurah offers 173 spacious beach, lagoon and water villas overlooking the dappled turquoise waters of the ocean, each accompanied by their own private pool. Set in 10 thatched roofed spa pavilions, each of which is privately situated in lush tropical gardens overlooking the ocean, Dhigurah’s Spa by Clarins offers a full-service Spa, including a hair and beauty salon and yoga garden deck. With a range of signature treatments and experiences, try the Essence of Dhigurah offering a coconut scrub to soften and exfoliate followed by herbal body and scalp massage using locally grown herbs and plants. Meanwhile, the Clear Skin Refiner Facial will detoxify and restore skin whilst the Body Wraps will leave skin feeling silky smooth. The Spa by Clarins also offers wellness treatments, offering tailor-made holistic sensorial experiences for men and women. Try the Rise & Shine to boost your mood and lift your spirits whilst the Beauty Sleep ensures you are well rested during your trip. There are also a series of treatments for a complete physical, mental and spiritual renewal including the Sun Lovers (a 120-minute polish, massage and foot ritual) and the Digital Detox.
The Residence Maldives at Falhumaafushi
Across the bridge, relax amidst sun, sea and sand with The Residence Maldives at Falhumaafushi and experience the rejuvenating Three Day Deep-Detox Retreat, a treatment that nourishes and detoxifies the body from the inside to the out. Offering three days of 90-minute treatments, the package includes sculpting, contouring and polishing. The Residence Maldives at Falhumaafushi also offers a range of signature and classic massages, facials, scrubs and spa journeys including tailor-made treatments like the Honeymoon Delight, perfect for couples and the Luminous Eyes, great for those looking to decongest, smooth and freshen the entire eye zone.
