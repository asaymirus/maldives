Pack for Hope by Cenizaro – Kindle HOPE, Ignite CHANGE
Pack for Hope
forms part of our Cenizaro Cares commitment to regenerative tourism. Hotel guests are invited to travel purposefully and come together in a shared mission to uplift, support and empower local communities.
By dedicating a small portion of their suitcase to essential items from non-perishable food items, educational supplies, books, sports equipment, personal hygiene products, clothing and more; guests can directly impact the lives of local communities in need while on vacation. These contributions
*
will empower the less fortunate in our communities; bringing hope, comfort, sustenance towards betterment of life; making a significant meaningful difference in their lives.
*Kindly present your gift/donation packs to our Front Desk team on arrival.
