# Winter Wonderland Awaits at Bandos Maldives - Bandos Maldives

Source: https://bandosmaldives.com/news/winter-wonderland-awaits-at-bandos-maldives/
Scraped: 2026-10-04
Resort: Bandos Maldives
Page type: diving

---

Bandos Maldives invites you to celebrate the magic of the holiday season with a spectacular “Winter Wonderland” themed festive program from December 20th, 2024, through January 5th, 2025. A vibrant blend of Maldivian warmth and festive cheer, the resort promises unforgettable experiences for guests of all ages.
The celebrations kick off with a sunrise yoga session on December 20th, followed by a day packed with family-friendly activities such as “Snowflake Stretch,” “Little Christmas Tree” decorating, and the much-anticipated “Jingle Bells in Wonderland” Christmas tree lighting. The fun continues through the weeks with festive games, themed workshops, live entertainment, and gourmet dining experiences.
Guests can indulge in activities like “Santa’s Magical Cruise,” an exciting “Winter Wonderland Scavenger Hunt,” seafood galas, and frosty foam fiestas. The celebration culminates with the spectacular “Wonderland Gala” dinner and a thrilling New Year’s Eve countdown party featuring live music and dazzling fireworks.
For those seeking relaxation, wellness activities like yoga, aqua-robics, and rejuvenating massages provide a tranquil retreat amidst the festive excitement. From tennis tune-ups and beach volleyball to the electrifying “DJ Wonderland” nights, Bandos Maldives offers something for everyone this holiday season.
With its warm blend of seasonal charm and cultural festivities, Bandos Maldives invites you to experience the magic of the season. Discover an island holiday filled with joy, laughter, and the natural beauty of the Maldives—where every moment feels like a wonderland!
