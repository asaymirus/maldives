# Bandos Maldives Triumphs as Resort League 2024 North Male Zone Champions - Bandos Maldives

Source: https://bandosmaldives.com/news/bandos-maldives-triumphs-as-resort-league-2024-north-male-zone-champions/
Scraped: 2026-10-04
Resort: Bandos Maldives
Page type: diving

---

Bandos Maldives proudly announces its victory as the Resort League 2024 North Male Zone Champions. In an exhilarating final held 2
nd
June 2024, Bandos Maldives triumphed over Baros with a score of 3 to 1. This marks the fifth Resort League trophy won by Bandos Maldives, setting a record as the resort with the most titles.
This outstanding achievement highlights the dedication and teamwork of both the players and the management. The commitment and hard work of the Bandos Maldives team were evident throughout the tournament, culminating in this well-deserved victory.
Bandos Maldives, sets a record as the resort with the most titles in Resort League
“We are incredibly proud of our team’s performance and their dedication to excellence,” said Ismail Rasheed, the General Manager of Bandos Maldives. “This victory is a testament to their hard work and the support provided by the entire Bandos Maldives community. We encourage all our associates to engage in such activities and continue to foster a spirit of camaraderie and sportsmanship.”
Ismail Rasheed, the General Manager of Bandos Maldives.
Bandos Maldives extends its gratitude to the fans and supporters who have stood by the team throughout the tournament. As champions of the Resort League 2024 North Male Zone, Bandos Maldives looks forward to celebrating this victory and continuing to support and nurture the talents of its associates.
