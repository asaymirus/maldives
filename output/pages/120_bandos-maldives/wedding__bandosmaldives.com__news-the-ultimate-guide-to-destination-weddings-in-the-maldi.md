With sunny skies and breathtaking beaches dominating its landscapes, the Maldives is an island nation that appears to have been tailor-made for romantic wedding ceremonies. Although there are a great many beach capitals in the world, the dream of hosting a destination wedding in the Maldives is one that most couples entertain, if only due to the archipelago’s otherworldly beauty and unique geography.
Secluded and private, Maldivian beaches are not the only venue choice couples have at their disposal, as the luxury overwater villas and tropical gardens occupying resort islands offer equally stunning backdrops for hosting the most important day of a couple’s life. Catering to those who wish to tie the knot with a simple, sacred ceremony as well as those who have aspirations of hosting a grand celebration, the Maldives offers couples an array of romantic, rustic and exclusive wedding settings and packages to suit different tastes and budgets.

Planning Your Big Day
The dry season from December to March has long been considered the perfect time for destination weddings in the Maldives. With near-perfect wedding conditions, this is also the time of the year when wedding venues in the Maldives such as at Bandos Maldives are highly sought after. Advanced bookings via email or a wedding planner in the Maldives are, therefore, not optional.
Couples who have selected an all-inclusive wedding package will find that every detail of their big day is taken care of by Bandos Maldives, which not only includes stylists, bouquets, hair and make-up but catering, photography, videography and even Bodu Beru drummers!
Those who are planning bespoke ceremonies are welcome to hire local wedding coordinators or planners to help the resort make your wedding dreams a reality, while all couples tying the knot in the islands should also note that Maldivian wedding ceremonies are not legally binding unless one or both members of the wedding party are of Maldivian origin or possess Maldivian citizenship. The symbolic certificate issued by the resort is, therefore, not a legal document but a keepsake of your island wedding celebration.
