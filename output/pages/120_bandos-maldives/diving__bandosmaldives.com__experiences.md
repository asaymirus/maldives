Clubhouse
Discover our Clubhouse Sports Complex—a haven for both play and relaxation. Immerse yourself in tennis, badminton, and squash, or hit the state-of-the-art gym for a workout. Afterwards, unwind in our steam baths and catch up on global news in the lounge, where a variety of games await
Experiences
Water Sports
At our Water Sports Centre, you’ll find top-notch equipment from well-known brands and skilled instructors ready to guide you through unforgettable aquatic adventures. Imagine sailing off into the sunset on a catamaran, soaring high above the island with parasailing for a bird’s-eye view, and feeling the warmth of the crystal-clear Maldivian waters as you jet ski, water ski, and windsurf. There is no shortage of exciting activities!
