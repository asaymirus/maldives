Dive Bandos
The Indian Ocean is a paradise for marine enthusiasts, brimming with treasures waiting to be discovered. Dive Bandos, the Maldives’ longest-established dive centre is the Indian Ocean’s Leading Dive Resort in the World Travels Awards 2024, invites you to explore 40 of the region’s best dive sites. Our dedicated team is excited to share their passion and expertise with you.
Our award-winning Dive Centre is a proud member of the internationally acclaimed PADI International Resort Association. We offer a wide range of PADI courses, led by a highly competent and multi-lingual team ready to cater to all your diving needs. Dive into adventure with us and uncover the wonders of the underwater world!
PADI Dive Courses
Bubblemaker for Kids
Experience the thrill of scuba diving with a PADI Pro! Kids aged 8 and older can take their first breaths underwater in depths of less than 2 meters, making it a fun and safe introduction to the wonders of diving
Open Water Dive Course
The PADI Open Water Diver course is your first step into the world of scuba certification. Learn to dive with a highly-trained PADI Instructor in a relaxed and supportive environment
Dive Master
Master the art of leading underwater tours, assisting with scuba classes, and inspiring ocean conservation in others
Adaptive Support Diver
Enhance your awareness of divers' varying abilities and learn adaptive techniques for diving or freediving with a physically challenged buddy
Enriched Air Diver
Enriched air, also known as nitrox or EANx, contains less nitrogen than regular air. Breathing less nitrogen means you can enjoy longer dives and shorter surface intervals
Advanced Open Water Diver
Expand your skills with underwater navigation, deep diving, and three specialty dives of your choice. After practicing with your instructor, you'll complete five open water dives
Medical Centre
Though we are just minutes away from the capital city of Male’, our on-site clinic and pharmacy prioritize guest safety with 24/7 medical assistance. With a General Practitioner, Nurse, and Decompression Chamber on standby, rest assured your well-being is our top priority
