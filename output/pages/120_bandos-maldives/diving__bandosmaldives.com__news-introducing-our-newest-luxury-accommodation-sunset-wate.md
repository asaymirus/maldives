We are thrilled to announce the unveiling of our latest and most luxurious offering, the “Sunset Water Villas with Pool.” This new room type features unique décor and breathtaking views of the ocean and beach, giving our guests a sense of floating on the ocean. Designed for luxury and tranquility, these villas offer full privacy and exclusive services, ensuring an unparalleled experience.
The living quarters come fully furnished with modern amenities to provide the utmost comfort and convenience. Each villa boasts an ultra-luxurious and spacious pool, offering uninterrupted views of the ocean and an endless horizon. The stunning location of the Sunset Water Villas allows guests to witness the uniquely colorful Maldivian sunsets from their own private pool and sundeck, creating lasting memories with their loved ones.
A Journey of Shared Memories
Our commitment to providing outstanding service and unforgettable experiences is reflected in every aspect of the Sunset Water Villas with Pool. From the exquisite décor to the stunning natural surroundings, every element has been carefully curated to ensure our guests enjoy the ultimate in relaxation and luxury.
