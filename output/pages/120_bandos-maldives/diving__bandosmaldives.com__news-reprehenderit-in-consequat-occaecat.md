Bandos Maldives is excited to unveil an exclusive special package tailored for locals and work permit holders, offering an unforgettable tropical escape starting from just 235 USD for double occupancy. This limited-time offer is available from May 20, 2024, to October 31, 2024, promising a delightful and value-packed getaway.
Package Inclusions:
Full Board Meal Plan:
Enjoy a variety of delectable dishes with soft drinks, canned juices, and water included during mealtimes.
Complimentary Combined Return Speedboat Transfers:
Hassle-free transportation from Hulhumale’, Malé, and the airport to Bandos Maldives.
Family-Friendly Perks:
Up to two children below 12 years old can stay and eat for free.
Free Recreational Activities:
Each room includes a 1-hour free kayak session per stay, complimentary snorkeling gear, and free use of Club House facilities.
Exclusive Discounts:
Guests receive a 20% discount on selected treatments at the Orchid Spa and a 25% discount on a la carte food and beverage.
Afternoon Delight:
Enjoy afternoon tea or coffee accompanied by snacks.
