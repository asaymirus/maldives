Blessed with white, sandy beaches and some of the world’s finest underwater landscapes, the Maldives is a dream destination for those travelling with children. Lined with pristine lagoons and composed of more than 1100 islands, Maldivian family vacation experiences in this sun-kissed archipelago can be as varied as the country’s unique geographical features. A world away from the hustle and bustle of busy beach capitals in Asia, a family holiday in the Maldives offers travellers the chance to get away from it all and enjoy quality time together with their loved ones.
Ideal for both young families with toddlers and adolescents and for larger broods that include teenagers and grandparents, here’s why a getaway in the island collective will prove to be a vacation to remember.
Safety, Serenity and Unlimited Opportunities to Splash Around
As a destination where resort islands serve as a private safe haven for families, the Maldives is equally renowned for its traditional and family-oriented culture. In addition to offering holidaymakers a wide variety of calm, shallow beaches, family-friendly resorts in the Maldives such as Bandos Maldives also provide a host of support services for parents travelling with their offspring. The on-site Kids Club is perhaps the most popular among these attributes as it offers young visitors the chance to play and learn while their parents unwind at the resort spa.
Conveniently located a mere 7km away from Velana International Airport, Bandos Maldives is also one of the most accessible resorts for families in the Maldives.
Family Fun in Paradise: Why Bandos is a Blissful Base for Your Brood
Those who sign up for an all-inclusive Maldivian family holiday at Bandos can not only embark on underwater expeditions but also rely on the expertise of one of the country’s longest-running dive centres. Named the Indian Ocean’s Leading Dive Resort at the 2024 World Travel Awards, Bandos not only organises
diving tours
to more than 40 biospheres or dive sites but also offers families the opportunity to learn the basics of scuba diving with an array of PADI courses.
Families with a passion for adrenaline-pumping water-based adventures can have their fill of thrills and spills at the
resort’s Water Sports Centre
. From water skiing and parasailing to windsurfing and jet skiing, the activity options accessible at Bandos also include meaningful excursions that are not limited to sunset cruises and catamaran sailing tours.
The Junior Spa at Bandos is also one of the few wellness centres in the Maldives that offers age-appropriate treatments for young visitors, while child-friendly menus and dining options are also accessible atresort restaurants and the pool bar.
The resort’s proximity to the nation’s dynamic capital, Male, also means that culturally inclined families can enjoy a day tour of the seaside city and uncover its key heritage sites and landmarks. Families with a penchant for South Asian cuisine can also set out on a food tour in Male, as it is ranked among the top things to do in the Maldives with your family.

FAQs
When is the best time to enjoy a Maldives family vacation?
The peak travel season or dry season from December to April is considered the best time to visit the Maldives as the clear weather conditions means all water sports activities are available.
When are the best family accommodation options in the Maldives?
Although water villas are among the most popular suite options for families, those who prefer an inland base, closer to on-site restaurants and fitness and spa facilities can opt for a spacious beach villa with a pool or a garden villa.
