Welcome to Bandos Maldives – The Island of Hospitality, where every moment promises unforgettable experiences and heartfelt hospitality. Uncover paradise at Bandos Maldives, a captivating coral island spanning 18.66 hectares in the North Male’ Atoll, just 7km from Velana International Airport.
Discover your sanctuary among our 8 villa categories, from cozy Beach Villas along the shoreline to Premium Beach Villas, perfect for families or friends seeking ultimate relaxation. Indulge in the luxurious Sunset Water Villas with Pool, boasting a private pool overlooking the azure waters.
Savor culinary delights at our 4 restaurants and 3 bars
Savor culinary delights at our 4 restaurants and 3 bars, each offering a unique setting to enjoy delectable dishes from around the world. Thrill-seekers will delight in water sports activities, from windsurfing to exhilarating fly boarding adventures. Dive into the depths with scuba diving or unwind at the Orchid Spa, where rejuvenating treatments await.
At Bandos Maldives, every guest, including our youngest visitors, receives VIP treatment. Our Kokko Kids Club offers fun and creativity for children, ensuring families enjoy every moment together.
Escape to Bandos Maldives - The Island of Hospitality, where every moment celebrates life, love, and the joy of island living.
