# Bandos Maldives Shines with 16 Awards at Hotel Asia Culinary Challenge 2024 - Bandos Maldives

Source: https://bandosmaldives.com/news/bandos-maldives-shines-with-16-awards-at-hotel-asia-culinary-challenge-2024/
Scraped: 2026-10-04
Resort: Bandos Maldives
Page type: dining

---

Bandos Maldives proudly announces the outstanding success of its culinary and service team members at the esteemed Hotel Asia Culinary Challenge 2024. Held from May 13th to 15th, this renowned event brought together top talents from across the region, where Bandos Maldives demonstrated unparalleled skill and creativity, securing an impressive array of awards.
The Hotel Asia Culinary Challenge is a platform renowned for celebrating culinary excellence, and this year’s event proved to be no exception. Bandos Maldives showcased its commitment to culinary innovation and service excellence, earning accolades across various categories.
In the category of Food Production, the following achievements were celebrated:
Gold Medal in Hot Cooking (Beef) by Shirantha
Gold Medal in Artistic Showpiece by Jagath
Silver Medal in Maldivian Dish by Ali Rasheed
Silver Medal in Hot Cooking (Beef) by Deka
Silver Medal in Hot Cooking (Lamb) by Shihan
Bronze Medal in Vegetable Dish by Shuv Singh
Bronze Medal in Hot Cooking (Poultry) by Deka
Bronze Medal in Fruits & Vegetable Carving by Jagath
Bronze Medal in Vegetable Dish by Nakib
Merit Certificate in Decorated Cake by Venuji
Merit Certificate in Dessert Display by Venuji
Merit Certificate in Hot Cooking (Fish Fillet) by Ali Rasheed
Merit Certificate in Hot Cooking (Poultry) by Ali Rasheed
In the Food and Beverage Service category, Bandos Maldives continued its winning streak with the following awards:
Gold Medal in Creative Beverage by Iresh Tharanga
Gold Medal in Iced Mocktail by Falih Junio
Bronze Medal in Tea Challenge by Pieter Joshua
Gold Medal in Creative Table Set-up by Uttar
Gold Medal in Creative Table Set-up by Dhamsha
The exceptional performance of Bandos Maldives' team members, with a total of 5 Gold, 2 Silver, 5 Bronze, and 4 Merit awards, underscores the resort's unwavering commitment to culinary excellence and unparalleled guest experiences.
