FAQs
Where is Bandos located?
Bandos is located in the North Male Atoll, 7KM from the Velana International Airport, we provide the best without the hassle of going any further.
What is the usual climate?
The Maldives has a tropical climate marked by warm temperatures and abundant sunshine throughout the year. The average temperature reaches 28 – 35 degrees.
How are transfers organized?
Bandos schedules speedboat transfers to/from Velana International Airport according to international flight’s arrival or departure throughout the day and evening. This is also combined with other guests arriving and departing at the same time. Our airport team members will be present at the arrival terminal area to greet and escort the guests to our speedboat. Furthermore, if there is any difficulty locating our representative, please visit Counter Number 2 in Zone A

What are the visa requirements for Maldives?
Maldives welcomes arriving tourists from all countries with 30 days on arrival visa. Please visit Maldives Immigration website to get additional and updated information regarding visa
What is the best currency to bring to the Maldives?
US Dollar is preferred all over the Maldives but we do accept Euros and British Pounds as well.
Please advise on the method of payment?
It is not necessary to change any money to the local currency. Within the resort, you are asked to sign all your extras to your villa. You may check your bills at any time and pay it daily if you wish. The resort accepts US Dollars, Euros, Sterling Pound, Swiss Francs, and major credit cards. Personal cheques are not accepted. The credit limit for each villa is setup to a maximum of US $ 1,500.
Do you offer free Wi-Fi connection?
Yes, you can enjoy free Wi-Fi access in the comfort of your villa and in all public areas.
Do I need to bring beach towels?
No, we provide beach towels in all rooms.
Are children allowed?
Yes, children of all ages are welcome. Our kids club offers daily programs for children from 3 to 11 years.
Do you provide baby-sitting service?
Yes, a babysitting service is available on request for a fee.
Does the hotel cater for special dietary requirements?
If you have any special dietary requirements, we recommend to notify us in advance. We always try our best to cater for your requirements.
Can you please let me know what kind of electrical sockets do you have in the rooms?
We have British plugs and pins in all our rooms. Universal adapters are available on request.
Could you please explain the meal plan?
Bandos Maldives offers all standard meal plans to suit our guests needs. Buffet breakfast, Lunch and Dinner included in the meal plan are served in the Gallery.
Breakfast is continental with a wide selection of fresh fruits, cereal, fresh bakeries and pastries, chilled juices and live stations offering omelettes, waffles and pancakes prepared to your own desire.
At lunch and dinner, we offer an international buffet and theme dinners spread of rich flavoured soups, fresh salads, appetizers, main dishes, carvery and pasta live stations, fresh fruits and desserts.
In addition to breakfast, lunch and dinner at Gallery, the All-Inclusive meal plan includes, selected alcoholic and non-alcoholic beverages from restaurants and bars, and minibar refilled once daily.
Is it possible to get married on Bandos?
While we offer Renewal of Vows services, it’s important to note that this ceremony is not legally recognized.
Do you have a dress code?
While visiting our restaurants, we kindly request gentlemen to wear at least a T-shirt and shorts, while ladies are encouraged to cover their bodies with a long t-shirt, sarong, or dress. Additionally, we appreciate it if guests refrain from wearing swimwear when visiting the reception and guest lounges.
Do you have evening entertainment on the island?
Regular evening entertainment is available including discos, DJs, live band, unplugged duo, movie nights and karaoke.
Do you permit drones on the island for photography/videography?
In order to protect the privacy of our guests and team members, as well as for their safety, we do not allow the operation of drones within the resort premises, unless explicitly authorized and supervised by the resort.
