# Bandos Maldives Wins Indian Ocean’s Leading Dive Resort 2025 - Bandos Maldives

We are delighted to share some wonderful news – Bandos Maldives has officially been named the Indian Ocean’s Leading Dive Resort 2025 at the prestigious World Travel Awards.
This recognition is more than just a title. It is a celebration of the passion, dedication, and hard work of our very own Dive Bandos team, who have tirelessly worked to make every diving experience unforgettable.
A Legacy of Diving Excellence
For decades, Bandos Maldives has been known as a pioneer in diving in the Maldives. With Dive Bandos established back in 1972, we’ve been fortunate to welcome divers from all over the world, from curious beginners taking their first breath underwater to seasoned professionals seeking new adventures beneath the surface.
Our team of experienced instructors and dive masters has not only maintained the highest safety and training standards but also shared their passion for the marine world with every guest. This award is a reflection of their relentless commitment to showcasing the incredible underwater beauty of the Maldives
This award would not have been possible without our guests, partners, and supporters who continue to place their trust in us. Each dive, each story, and each shared experience adds to the Bandos legacy. To everyone who has ever explored the waters with us – this recognition belongs to you as much as it does to us.
Holding this trophy reminds us of why we do what we do – to inspire wonder, create memories, and share the breathtaking beauty of the Maldivian seas with the world. The journey doesn’t end here; it motivates us to dive deeper, explore further, and raise the bar even higher.
