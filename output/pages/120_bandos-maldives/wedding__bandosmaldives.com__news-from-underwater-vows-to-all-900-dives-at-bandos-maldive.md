# From Underwater Vows to All 900 Dives at Bandos Maldives; Andy & Kate's Journey - Bandos Maldives

Source: https://bandosmaldives.com/news/from-underwater-vows-to-all-900-dives-at-bandos-maldives-andy-kates-journey/
Scraped: 2026-10-04
Resort: Bandos Maldives
Page type: wedding

---

Bandos Maldives recently
celebrated a remarkable achievement with two of its most loyal guests, Kate and
Andy, who share a decades-long connection with the island.
Andy reached an incredible
milestone of completing his 900th dive, every single one of them with Dive
Bandos. His spouse, Kate, celebrated an even more extraordinary achievement
which is her 1000th dive, with more than 900 of those dives with Bandos Team.
The Dive Bandos team joined them
in marking this special occasion, honoring not only their dedication to diving
but also the bond they’ve built with the resort.
Their story goes far beyond
numbers. Kate and Andy first arrived at Bandos in 2006 and have returned every
year since, making the island a true part of their lives. In 2014, they even
chose to exchange their vows underwater at Bandos — a unique and unforgettable
moment that cemented their love story with the resort.
“Celebrating guests like Kate and
Andy reminds us of what Bandos truly represents,” said Ismail Rasheed, the
General Manager of Bandos.
For Bandos Maldives, these moments
reaffirm why guests continue to choose the island for both adventure and
belonging. With stories like Kate and Andy’s, Bandos is not just a dive
destination, it’s a home under and above the waves.
