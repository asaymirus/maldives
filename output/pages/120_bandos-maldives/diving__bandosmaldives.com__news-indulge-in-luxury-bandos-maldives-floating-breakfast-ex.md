# Indulge in Luxury: Bandos Maldives floating breakfast experience - Bandos Maldives

Scraped: 2026-10-04

---

Indulge in luxury with the distinctive Floating Breakfast at Bandos Maldives, elevating your morning meal to unparalleled heights. This immersive dining experience combines breathtaking views with a sumptuous spread, creating unforgettable memories for guests.
For USD 129 per couple, guests are treated to an opulent breakfast spread that includes not only a delectable selection of dishes but also a choice between a regular option and an elevated experience with Champagne.
Elevating your morning meal to unparalleled heights
The menu boasts a diverse range of options to tantalize your taste buds. Start your day with a refreshing glass of Champagne, adding a touch of elegance to your breakfast affair. If you prefer a non-alcoholic option, the package is available without Champagne at USD 99 per couple. The four main breakfast options cater to various tastes and preferences. Choose from the Traditional Hearty, Egg Stream, Egg Breeze, or Egg Ocean, each offering a unique culinary experience that showcases the finest ingredients and culinary expertise.
For those with a penchant for Indian flavors, Bandos Maldives presents an exquisite Indian Vegetarian Menu. Indulge in the rich and flavorful options such as Poha with tomato chutney, Upma with pickle and peanut chutney, and Aloo Paratha with yogurt and pickle. As an additional treat, the menu includes Eggless pastries, ensuring that there’s something for everyone, priced at USD 129 ++ per person.
The Floating Breakfast at Bandos Maldives is not just a meal; it’s a celebration of indulgence, offering an unforgettable experience that combines culinary excellence with the stunning backdrop of the Maldivian paradise. Whether you’re a couple seeking a romantic escape or a group of friends looking to elevate your vacation, the Floating Breakfast at Bandos Maldives is a must-try for those who appreciate the finer things in life.
