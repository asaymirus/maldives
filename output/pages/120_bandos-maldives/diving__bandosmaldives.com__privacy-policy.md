Automatically collected data
Cookies:
Cookies are messages that web servers pass to your web browser when you visit internet sites. Your browser stores each message in a small file, called cookie.txt. When you request another page from the server your browser sends the cookie back to the server. These files contain information about your visit to the web page, as well as any information you have volunteered to share.
We may use cookies to help us and third parties: to obtain information about your visits to the Website; deliver online and mobile advertisements and content that are tailored to your apparent interests; process your reservations/bookings or requests; analyze your visiting patters; personalize your experience on the Website; facilitate social media functions and engage in website and advertising analytics.
