# Bandos Maldives Clinches Victory at VAM Resort Volleyball Champions League 2024 - Bandos Maldives

Source: https://bandosmaldives.com/news/bandos-maldives-clinches-victory-at-vam-resort-volleyball-champions-league-2024/
Scraped: 2026-10-04
Resort: Bandos Maldives
Page type: diving

---

Bandos Maldives is thrilled to announce its triumph at the VAM Resort Volleyball Champions League 2024. In a fiercely contested final, Bandos Maldives emerged victorious against One & Only Reethi Rah with a score of 3-2.
The championship match showcased exceptional talent and sportsmanship, with both teams demonstrating remarkable skill and determination. Bandos Maldives ultimately secured the win, marking a significant achievement for our resort and team.
This victory marks the fifth time Bandos Maldives has claimed the prestigious title, showcasing the team's consistent excellence and dedication over the years.
“We are incredibly proud of our team for their dedication and hard work,” said Ismail Rasheed, the General Manager of Bandos Maldives “This victory is a testament to the spirit and unity of our team, and we are excited to celebrate this success with our guests and staff.”
Bandos Maldives extends its heartfelt congratulations to One & Only Reethi Rah for their outstanding performance and sportsmanship throughout the tournament.
