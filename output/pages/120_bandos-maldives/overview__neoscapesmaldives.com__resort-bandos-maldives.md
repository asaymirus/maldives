The Island of Hospitality, Just Minutes Away
Only 10 minutes from Velana International Airport, Bandos Maldives brings you the beauty of a faraway paradise without the long journey. Surrounded by turquoise lagoons and powder-soft beaches, this coral island is home to 220 villas across eight categories, designed to suit every style of traveller.
Famed as one of the best family-friendly resorts in the Maldives, Bandos offers a safe and vibrant space where children thrive while parents unwind. Beneath the waves, its house reef is among the most beautiful in the country, perfect for first-time snorkelers or seasoned divers.
At the heart of the island lies Dive Bandos, the Maldives’ longest-running and award-winning dive centre, led by a professional multilingual team. With five decades of heritage, Bandos remains true to its name as the Island of Hospitality, creating timeless experiences for all who visit.

Dining
Gallery Restaurant
The main dining venue at Bandos, Gallery Restaurant features three spacious halls with a welcoming ambience for breakfast, lunch, and dinner. Guests can enjoy an extensive buffet offering a wide variety of international dishes, ensuring every palate is catered to. With its relaxed atmosphere and diverse menu, Gallery provides a delightful dining experience for all.
Sea Breeze Café
Perched at the water’s edge, Sea Breeze Café offers a relaxing dining experience with stunning lagoon views and the gentle sea breeze. Open 24 hours, it serves a wide variety of dishes, from a la carte favourites like spaghetti and authentic Indian curries to casual options such as pizza and hamburgers. Guests can savour diverse flavours while enjoying the serene over-water setting.
Huvan
Meaning “blue skies and calm seas,” Huvan is the island’s premier spot for sunset cocktails. Set on a spacious sundeck, it offers breathtaking views of the horizon as the sun dips below the ocean. Guests can enjoy their favourite drinks and delectable dishes while soft music enhances the serene atmosphere, making it the perfect place to unwind and end the day in paradise.
Sand Bar
The island’s lively hub, Sand Bar is perfect for enjoying tropical cocktails, refreshing beverages, and great company. Designed for fun and entertainment, it hosts karaoke sessions, live bands, DJ nights, and themed parties, creating a vibrant atmosphere that keeps guests entertained from day into night.
Pool Bar
Set amid swaying palms and fragrant blossoms, the Pool Bar offers a relaxing swim-up experience in a tropical paradise. Guests can enjoy chilled drinks from the comfort of jetted stools, unwind in the shade, and soak up the serene surroundings while sharing leisurely moments with family and friends.
Umi Yaki
Meaning “Grill by the Sea,” Umi Yaki is a charming open-air cabana restaurant offering stunning ocean views. Guests can gather around the teppan grill to watch skilled chefs craft their meals with flair, flipping, juggling, and adding a touch of fire. This theatrical dining experience combines spectacular flavours with engaging presentation, making every meal a memorable occasion.
Grill Bar
Located on the north-eastern side of the island, Grill Bar combines contemporary interiors with rustic charm. Guests can dine indoors or on the overwater deck, enjoying breathtaking ocean views. The menu features freshly grilled seafood and meats served with homemade sauces, perfectly complemented by wines or your favourite drinks. With the gentle sea breeze and stunning surroundings, Grill Bar offers a memorable and relaxing dining experience.
All-Inclusive Package
Enhance your stay at Bandos Maldives with our all-inclusive package, which covers breakfast, lunch, and dinner at the Gallery Restaurant, along with a curated selection of premium alcoholic and non-alcoholic beverages served at Gallery, Sand Bar, and Huvan. Guests can also enjoy a variety of non-motorized water sports and entertainment options, combining relaxation and adventure for an unforgettable island experience.
Private Dining
Bandos Maldives offers bespoke dining experiences designed for romance and intimacy. Guests can enjoy a secluded beach dinner, surrounded by candlelight and the gentle sound of waves, with a private chef preparing a specially curated menu. Choose from options such as a candle-lit dinner at Huvan, a luxurious lobster dinner paired with fine wine, or a four-course meal under a star-filled sky. Each experience combines exquisite cuisine, stunning settings, and soft music to create unforgettable moments for couples.

Highlights From The Resort
Night Fishing
Drop your line from a traditional dhoni as the sun sets and enjoy the calming ocean sounds while waiting for a catch.
Island Hopping
Explore nearby islands, snorkel, relax on the beach, and experience local Maldivian life. Half-day and full-day tours include a picnic and a visit to a souvenir shop.
Snorkeling & House Reef
Discover vibrant coral reefs and tropical marine life just steps from the beach or on guided reef excursions.
Sunset Dolphin Cruise
Watch playful wild dolphins against breathtaking Maldivian sunsets.
Whale Shark Expedition
Snorkel or dive with the gentle giants of the ocean on a two-hour adventure to spot whale sharks in their natural habitat.
Clubhouse & Kids

Orchid Spa & Salon
Rejuvenate with massages, body treatments, and salon services. Private villas offer intimate wellness experiences, with age-appropriate treatments for children.
Dive Bandos
Explore over 40 dive sites with professional multilingual instructors. Courses cater to all skill levels, from beginner to advanced, in the Maldives’ renowned underwater paradise.
