DESCRIPTION
‘Discover Amilla Maldives, a naturally lush private island resort where space, nature, and personalised luxury define the experience.’
Discover Amilla Maldives, a naturally lush private island resort nestled in the Baa Atoll UNESCO World Biosphere Reserve. Proudly Maldivian owned, this contemporary luxury boutique resort blends an eco-conscious soul with exceptional privacy and scale. One of the most spacious five-star islands in the Maldives, Amilla features just 67 villas set over the water, on the beach, or in the treetops, allowing nearly 70% of the island to remain untouched jungle edged by white sand. Multi-bedroom beach residences offer expansive outdoor living and private plunge pools, while the award-winning Wellness Your Way programme delivers tailored nutrition and movement experiences designed to fit seamlessly into your stay.

Dining
Feeling Koi
Amilla’s signature fine-dining restaurant, Feeling Koi presents elevated Japanese cuisine over the lagoon. Dine above the water as fish glide below, enjoying beautifully crafted sushi, sashimi, and contemporary Japanese dishes prepared in an open show kitchen. Choose between air-conditioned interiors or a romantic veranda cooled by the sea breeze.
The Emperor Beach Club
A lively beachside venue where dining, sports, and entertainment come together. Enjoy relaxed meals on the sand, crafted cocktails, and comfort favourites, alongside regular theme nights. Home to The Cellar Door for wine and cheese tastings.
Barolo
A refined Italian dining experience where homegrown ingredients take centre stage. Savour handmade pasta, tender grills, and fresh produce from Amilla’s organic gardens, paired with water views and a relaxed open-air atmosphere enhanced by live entertainment on select evenings.
Tessera
Inspired by Greek traditions of communal dining, Tessera celebrates shared meals and timeless flavours. Multiple dishes are served to the table, encouraging relaxed, social dining with family and friends in a warm Mediterranean setting.
Joe's Pizza
A casual favourite serving wood-fired pizzas made to order with fresh herbs and deli-style toppings. From indulgent combinations to vegetarian and vegan options, Joe’s Pizza delivers comfort food with a gourmet twist.
Baa Bar
A trendy beach bar offering crafted cocktails, fine wines, and island-infused spirits. Set on the sand with golden-hour DJ sets, Baa Bar is perfect for relaxed evenings by the sea
Fresh
Start your day at Fresh with a nourishing breakfast of tropical fruits, wellness bowls, homemade yoghurt, and à la carte favourites. Catering to every dietary lifestyle, Fresh also hosts weekly theme nights, including traditional Maldivian cuisine.
Sunset Bar
An elevated spot to watch the sky transform at dusk. Sip chilled champagne, explore an extensive gin selection with Homemade@Amilla tonic, and enjoy live entertainment as laid-back beats drift into the evening.
THE CELLAR DOOR
A wine lover’s haven featuring thousands of Old and New World labels. Book a private wine-tasting experience, guided cellar tour, or select the perfect bottle to complement your dining experience.
East
A vibrant journey through South Indian cuisine, East showcases delicate spices, fresh vegetables, and succulent meats. Tandoor specialities, fragrant curries, and traditional desserts offer bold, authentic flavours in an inviting setting.

Highlights From The Resort
Hanifaru Bay Manta Encounters
Located within the UNESCO Biosphere Reserve, Amilla offers access to the world-famous Hanifaru Bay, where manta rays and occasional whale sharks gather in extraordinary numbers during the season.
A Snorkeller’s Paradise
Explore one of the Maldives’ best house reefs, including Amilla’s own Blue Hole, with complimentary guided snorkelling, night snorkels, and reef explorations.
Dive Butler Scuba Experiences
Discover world-class dive sites just minutes from the island with personalised diving led by Dive Butler International, suitable for beginners and advanced divers alike.
Naturally Integrated Wellness
Wellbeing flows effortlessly through the island with yoga, fitness, steam and sauna rituals, visiting wellness practitioners, and personalised movement programmes.
One of the Maldives’ Most Spacious Islands
With nearly 70% of the island preserved as untouched jungle, Amilla delivers exceptional privacy, freedom, and the true luxury of space.
Exceptional Family Experiences
From one of the Maldives’ most engaging kids clubs to family-friendly villas and shared island adventures, Amilla creates meaningful experiences for all ages.
Bespoke Island Adventures
Enjoy private sandbank picnics, dolphin cruises, luxury sunset sails, local island visits, fishing experiences, and personalised yacht charters.
