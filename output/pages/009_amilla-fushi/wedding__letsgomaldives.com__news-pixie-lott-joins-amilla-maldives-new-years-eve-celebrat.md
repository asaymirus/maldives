British singer-songwriter,
Pixie Lott
, has been announced as another headline act for New Year’s Eve at
Amilla Maldives Resort and Residences
. Pixie, who burst onto the scene in 2009 with a UK number one, ‘Mama Do’, then went on to sell 1.5 million copies of her debut album, will join a star-studded lineup this year at Amilla.
The line-up also includes the British DJ, record producer, singer and songwriter
Shift K3y
, as well as the homegrown Maldivian talent,
Limestone Band
.
Amilla Maldives Resort and Residences is renowned for its spectacular events. From Christmas Day right through to New Year’s and Orthodox Christmas, the private island resort will be bursting with live entertainment and activities for all ages.
Area 51 Performers
, a troupe of highly-skilled entertainers, will entertain guests with performances and workshops from
December 22nd to January 7th
. ‘What the Dickens! An Island Christmas Carol Cabaret’ will thrill the crowds on Christmas Eve.
Amilla’s youngest guests can participate in a show based upon the theme of
‘the Past, Present and Future of Sustainable Fashion’
. This will be followed by a delicious
Christmas Eve Gala Dinner
with traditional fare.
Christmas Day will be celebrated with a delicious
Christmas brunch
followed by
Santa arriving
, island-style, to hand out gifts.
A
talent show
on December 30th will give kids the chance to show off their skills, with help from the Area 51 Performers.
Then Amilla’s Russian guests will be treated to a visit from
Ded Moroz
(‘Grandfather Frost’) who will arrive on New Year’s Eve with his granddaughter, the Snow Maiden, to distribute gifts to the children. It will be a wonderful opportunity for children of all nationalities to learn about another culture.
