Diving
Amilla Maldives is located in the heart of the Baa Atoll, which is home to some of the best diving sites in the Maldives. The resort’s dive center provides a variety of diving options for both beginners and experienced divers, with access to some of the world’s most beautiful and diverse underwater environments.
Here are some of the most popular dive sites near Amilla, Maldives:
Dhigu Thila:
This site is known for its colorful coral formations and abundant marine life, which includes schools of fish, reef sharks, and eagle rays. Dhigu Thila is suitable for divers of all levels.
Dharavandhoo Thila:
This site is located in the northern part of the atoll and is known for its stunning coral formations and abundant marine life, which includes schools of fish, eagle rays, and reef sharks. Dharavandhoo Thila is suitable for intermediate to advanced divers.
Dhonfanu Thila:
Well-known for its steep walls and diverse marine life, which includes colorful reef fish, turtles, and reef sharks. Dhonfanu Thila is suitable for divers of all levels and is a popular spot for night diving.
Nelivaru Thila:
This site has a series of submerged caves and swim-throughs that are home to a variety of marine life, including lobsters, groupers, and moray eels. Nelivaru Thila is suitable for advanced divers.
Dhigali Thila:
This site is located on the atoll’s eastern side and is known for its large schools of pelagic fish such as tuna, barracuda, and trevally. Dhigali Thila is only for experienced divers.
Aidhoo Corner:
This site is located in the southern part of the atoll and is known for its strong currents, which attract large schools of fish such as jacks, snappers, and barracudas. Aidhoo Corner is only suitable for experienced divers.
Blue Hole:
This is a natural underwater sinkhole surrounded by a reef wall that is home to a variety of marine life, including sharks, rays, and large schools of fish. Blue Hole is only for advanced divers.
Milaidhoo Caves
: Located in the southern part of the atoll, this site features a series of underwater caves and tunnels that are home to a variety of marine life, including lobsters, groupers, and snappers. Milaidhoo Caves is only suitable for experienced divers.
Dhega Thila:
Located on the western side of the atoll, this site is known for its colorful coral formations and diverse marine life, which includes reef sharks, turtles, and schools of fish. Dhega Thila is suitable for intermediate to advanced divers.
These are just a few of the many dive sites near Amilla Maldives, each offering a unique and unforgettable diving experience. The resort’s experienced dive guides can assist guests in selecting the best dive sites based on their skill level and interests.
