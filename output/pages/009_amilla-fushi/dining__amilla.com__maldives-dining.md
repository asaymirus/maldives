SUSTAINABILITY INSPIRED BY CULTURE
Homegrown to homemade, the Maldivian culture is deeply rooted in a rich connection with the earth, sea and trade. Across our Maldives dining venues and experiences, you will encounter the bond with the sea and culture sharing through food. Honour the connection with the earth by gathering from our organic garden or claiming your own catch of the day, prepared by our chefs. Relish in a rich cultural exchange of varied cuisines by invited world-class chefs. Cultivate sustainability with skills you can take away for a lifetime, crafting infusions or fermenting a custom kombucha to your taste.

DIETARY LIFESTYLE CHOICES
Customisable Dining
Dawn-to-dusk dining at Amilla Maldives is always a pleasure. All meal plans, from half-board to full-board, include the option to dine around at our different dining venues. Our dine around inclusive adds a selected variety of alcoholic and non alcoholic beverage options at our three bars, as well as your in-villa bar.
Amilla offers carefully crafted menus in each of our restaurants for guests to embrace their eating lifestyle while on vacation. As part of all our meal plans, request our distinctive dietary lifestyle choice menus – available at all seven of our restaurants for no extra charge, spanning vegan/vegetarian, gluten-free, dairy-free, low-carb/keto or paleo offerings. Even wellness cocktails and ice cream are prepared to suit all dietary lifestyles, or available to your whims.
