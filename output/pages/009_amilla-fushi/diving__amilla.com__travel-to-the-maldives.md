The Seaplane Arrival
After travelling to Velana International Airport, a shared 35-minute seaplane flight awaits. Maldives seaplane flights are operated by
Manta Air
and arranged by our reservations team (call
+960 6606444

All international arrivals will receive a welcome at the Amilla lounge at the seaplane terminal.
Shared seaplane transfers are US$850 round trip per adult and US$450 round trip per child (excludes taxes & service fee). Private seaplane charters are also available on request..
Seaplane transfers operate only during daylight hours and can be arranged for guests arriving on international flights landing before 3:30 PM. For arrivals after this time, domestic flight transfer options are available.

From the nearest airport, Velana International Airport, you can get to Amilla Maldives by either a 35-minute seaplane flight (arranged by our reservations team according to your scheduled arrival or departure) or if arriving at Velana International Airport after 15.00 hours alternatively by a 20-minute domestic flight plus a 10-minute Maldives boat transfer (also arranged by an Amilla reservationist).
Maafaru is the closest private international airport which is 2 hours by boat or charter a private seaplane.
CIP services can be arranged by our reservations team for both ways of your journey which includes:
Upon arrival, a black car service will meet you at the stairs of the plane, and all entrance formalities will be arranged for you in the CIP Lounge. Once your seaplane is ready, the Amilla team will greet you for your seaplane journey.
Upon departure from the Maldives, receive check-in formalities, immigration clearance and an escort to the aircraft from the CIP Lounge, which allows for direct boarding.
Transfers for infants below the age of two are free of charge. Due to legal safety requirements, there is no separate seat allocation for infants under two on the seaplane.
Luggage restrictions for your seaplane flight are 20kg per person for checked luggage and 5kg for hand luggage are free of charge. Additional weight is charged at USD 5.00 + 17% taxes per kg, payable at the seaplane check-in counter.
The exact timing for your departure will be communicated to you by your Katheeb on the evening of your departure when your final bill will be presented and signed. Your luggage will be collected one hour before you depart. Our transfer team will coordinate all logistics with our third-party flight carriers en route to Malé’s Velana International Airport.
The weather in the Maldives is normally pleasant year-round, but November to April is when we see the clearest skies. Scuba diving visibility is good enough all year round.
