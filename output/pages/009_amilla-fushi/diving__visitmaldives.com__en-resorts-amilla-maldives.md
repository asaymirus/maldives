In Harmony with Purpose
Amilla Maldives, located in the heart of the UNESCO World Biosphere Reserve of Baa Atoll, offers an idyllic retreat for couples, families, and friends alike. Surrounded by tranquil white-sand beaches and one of the Maldives’ most celebrated house reefs, the resort features 67 Pool Villas and Residences, each with private pools and spacious indoor and outdoor living areas designed for ultimate relaxation and connection with nature.
With a wealth of activities on offer, including the Sultan’s Village kids club, kombucha-making, and art classes, Amilla ensures that there is something for everyone. The resort is also home to world-renowned snorkelling and diving experiences at Hanifaru Bay and the resort’s own Blue Hole.
Amilla Maldives is proud to be an EarthCheck silver-certified member of Small Luxury Hotels' Considerate Collection, reflecting the resort’s dedication to blending luxury with sustainability.
Embracing the beauty of its natural surroundings, the resort also cherishes the rich Maldivian culture, encouraging guests to take part in sustainability and inclusivity initiatives that foster a sense of community.
The resort caters to diverse dietary preferences, with menus offering options for vegan, vegetarian, gluten-free, dairy-free, low-carb/keto, and paleo diets.
Recognised as Responsible Hospitality Partner of the Year 2024 by Forbes Travel Guide, Amilla is committed to diversity, equity, inclusion, and environmental stewardship. As an IncluCare-certified resort, it continually enhances its facilities, staff training, and services to ensure accessibility for guests with mobility or sensory needs. Additionally, Amilla is a proud member of the Conscious Travel Foundation, a global network committed to positive change in the travel industry.
