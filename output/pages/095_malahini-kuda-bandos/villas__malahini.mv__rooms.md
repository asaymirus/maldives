Sleep!
Relax #atMalahini without distractions. Sink into the most comfortable beds and bask in the tropical ambience of our rooms. Choose between our Beach Pool Suites, Sunrise Beach Pool Villas, Garden Villas, or Superior and Deluxe Rooms all equipped with a mini bar, tea and coffee facilities and all the amenities you need to feel right at home.
Beach Pool Suites
Inspired by the local Maldivian island homesteads, our Beach Pool Suites are a 200 sq m fusion of the traditional Maldivian households and lavish modern interiors. The elements in our Beach Pool Suites have been meticulously chosen to perfect the ultimate blend of culture and tradition, washed with soothing greys and gentle celadon complimented by the surrounding flora.
Sunrise Beach Pool Villa
Located along the shoreline, our sunrise beach pool villas are 100 sq m luxurious modern villas with a blend of vintage Maldivian interiors, a private pool, jacuzzi and a sunbathing deck whilst offering an impeccable view of the dazzling sunrise - Complimentary usage of non-motorized water sports - Daily Breakfast at Maaga - Amenities from the white company
Deluxe Room
Located along the shore, our Deluxe rooms affords a partial view of the Ocean. Each room is 27sq m and have the below to ensure that you enjoy a comfortable stay: - Ocean Inspired Refreshing Bathroom Amenities - Daily Breakfast at Maaga - Free SUP, Canoe, Windsurf & Paddleboat included in your room rate
Superior Room
Located above the Deluxe Rooms our Superior Rooms affords sweeping partial views of the Ocean. Each room is 22 sq m and have the below to ensure that you enjoy a comfortable stay:
- Ocean Inspired Refreshing Bathroom Amenities
- Daily Breakfast at Maaga
- Free SUP, Canoe, Windsurf & Paddleboat included in your room rate
Garden Room
Enjoy looking at exotic Banana trees and ferns from our Garden Rooms. Each room is 24 sq m and have the below to ensure that you enjoy a comfortable stay: - Ocean Inspired Refreshing Bathroom Amenities - Daily Breakfast at Maaga - Free SUP, Canoe, Windsurf & Paddleboat included in your room rate
