Before your arrival at Malahini
How do I get to Malahini?
+
Upon landing at Velana International Airport, our friendly airport representative will be there to warmly welcome you and guide you to your speedboat transfer to Malahini Kuda Bandos, just a scenic 12-minute speed boat ride away from the airport. If you are unable to locate our airport representative or have trouble locating our counter, please contact our airport representative directly at Number or find us at Malahini Kuda Bandos counter (#A2).
When can I Check-in & Check-out?
+
Check-in:
02:00 pm
Check-out:
12:00 pm
For a late check-out, please contact Reception who will be pleased to arrange a late check-out (if available). There will be a fee charged for this service.
What should I do in case I arrive ahead of schedule?
+
Early check-in between 8am and 2pm will be subject to room availability at the time of arrival and cannot be guaranteed. In case of early arrival before 8am, we suggest pre-booking an additional night to secure room availability. Please contact us for more information on early check-in options for specific arrival dates.
Is there an ID requirement?
+
For security purposes, valid photo identification is required at check-in. Photo identification must also match the name that is on the reservation.
What should I do in case I have an early departure?
+
Early departures will be treated as cancellations and will result in a 100% cancellation fee for all remaining accommodation dates.
What is the late checkout policy?
+
The standard checkout time is noon. We are pleased to offer late checkouts for an additional fee. For checkouts between 12pm and 6pm, a fee equal to 50% of the daily room rate will apply. For checkouts after 6pm, a fee equal to the full daily room rate will apply. Please contact the resort for more information on late checkouts on specific departure dates.
Is there any mandatory transportation information I should be aware of?
+
The resort is a 15 - 20-minute speedboat ride from Velana International Airport (MLE). The resort requires flight details at least 24 hours in advance to arrange a speedboat transfer. For more information or to confirm transportation, please contact the resort directly.
The resort reserves the right to refuse access to any person, items or produce who it considers objectionable (including any person engaged by the guest to provide or perform any other duties). In circumstances where the guests arrange an outside boat to be used for their own services while staying at the resort, prior written permission must be obtained from the Management and must pay a docking charge of USD$300 per pax per docking. The information required by the hotel will include: Boat Name, License number and the captain name and the contact number.
How do I get visa?
+
As part of your journey to the Maldives, a 30-day tourist visa will be granted upon arrival. To ensure your arrival experience is seamless, we recommend completing the digital Imuga form at
https://imuga.immigration.gov.mv/traveller/declaration
(imuga.immigration.gov.mv in Bing)
at least 96 hours prior to your arrival, providing clear and accurate travel and personal information. This will allow you to breeze through immigration and begin your Maldivian escape hassle-free.
For your host experience
What is the alcohol policy?
+
Alcoholic beverage service is restricted to those 18 years or older (with valid photo identification). Alcohol must be consumed within the resort area. The following items are prohibited in the Maldives and will be retained at customs upon arrival: religious materials offensive to Islam, idols for worship, pornographic materials, narcotics and psychotropic substances, arms, alcohol and spirits, pork and its byproducts, dogs, and dangerous animals.
What’s the best attire for Maldives?
+
With our warm climate and beautiful beaches, many travellers find that beachwear is the ideal attire for most of their stay. However, we do ask that swimwear be covered and shirts worn at all times when visiting the restaurants and bars. Out of respect for local customs, ladies are requested to cover their shoulders and thighs when visiting local islands and the capital city.
Can I have a babysitter?
+
Babysitting service is available upon request with a minimum of 24 hours’ advance notice. Fees apply.
How can I celebrate my special day?
+
We are delighted to help you make special occasions even more memorable. We can arrange private dinners, cakes, champagne or a candlelight dinner at your room, on the beach or anywhere in the resort. Our Executive Chef will provide a romantic menu just for you. Please contact Reception to discuss your requirements.
How do I control the climate in my room?
+
For your personal comfort, all guest rooms have individual thermostat control switches for the air-condition units and fans, enabling you to regulate your room temperature. During your stay, our Housekeeping Crew will be pleased to offer further assistance where necessary.
Should I get a connecting room?
+
The resort features the following connecting option: Garden Room & Sunrise Beach Pool Villas. Please note that Sunrise Beach Pool Villas are connected by the deck only. Connecting rooms are subject to availability at time of check-in. Please request connecting accommodations at the time of the reservation.
Do you have a doctor at the resort?
+
Should you require medical assistance during your stay, a Medical Clinic is located near the arrival jetty. The resort nurse is available at the clinic. You do not need to make an appointment. The consultation is free of charge, but if applicable, medicines will be charged to your account. In the case of an emergency please call 555, or inform our team at the reception.
Do you accommodate special dietary needs?
+
At Malahini we try to accommodate our Travelers Dietary requirements. Whether you are Vegetarian, Diabetic, have allergies, gluten free diet or should you have any other special dietary requirements to be followed, please meet our Executive Chef on your arrival and we will be happy to accommodate your request.
When is the gift shop open?
+
The boutique shop offers a small selection of quality souvenirs and gifts. Located next to the lobby, it is open daily from 10:00 am – 10:00 pm.
When do you conduct maintenance work?
+
At Malahini we strive to improve and better ourselves everyday. However, as a guest at our property you will not be disturbed as we try to undertake any maintenance works with minimal disturbances and take continual measures to mitigate any noise or disruption that our guests may face.

Can I cook in my room?
+
The safety of our travelers, crew, and this facility is extremely important to us. Hence, preparation of food in guest rooms by any type of cooking appliances is prohibited. A minimum fee of $500.00 will be charged for cooking in a room, including, but not limited to Microwave, coffee makers, hot plates, toaster ovens, water heaters, rice cookers, combustible, open flame, barbecue grill, burners, heating appliance, or any other item intended for cooking. Open fires, flames or cooking grills, either charcoal or gas are not allowed anywhere on hotel property.
Do I need power adaptors?
+
For your convenience, multi-plug adaptors are available and can be provided on request, please contact Reception. The voltage in the Maldives is 240 volts.
What are the drone policies?
+
Due to safety and privacy concerns for our guests, the resort prohibits the operation of unmanned aerial systems or drones on or above the island and resort by the general public — including model aircraft by recreational users and hobbyists. This prohibition includes drones used for filming or videotaping, as well as any drone use by media or journalists operating above or within the resort area boundaries. This prohibition extends to any devices launched or operated from Malahini Kuda Bandos, as well as any launched from private property outside of the resort area boundaries. Please contact the resort if you have any questions regarding the operation of such devices within the area boundary.
What is the smoking policy?
+
Please note that smoking is not permitted indoors in any of the accommodations. Smoking is permitted on the outdoor terraces or balconies of the villas. Smoking is also permitted in designated public areas within the resort.
Payments at Malahini
How can I cash a cheque?
+
The resort does not offer cheque cashing services.
What is the credit card policy?
+
A valid credit card must be presented and authorized at check-in. We accept VISA, MasterCard, American Express and Union Pay. Please contact the resort for further information.
What credit cards do you accept?
+
We accept the following credit cards: American Express, MasterCard, Visa and Union Pay.
What currencies do you accept?
+
We accept United States Dollars, Great British Pounds and Euros at Malahini. We also accept Maldivian Rufiyaa from Individuals with Maldivian Nationality.
What are the diving policies?
+
We have a 5 star rated PADI certified Dive Centre. The dive center is located at the Marine & Recreation Hub next to the Main Lobby. For more information about Diving at Malahini please select the Diving tab under What’s up.
What is the payment policy?
+
The resort accepts cash payments in U.S. dollars, Pounds and Euros only. Due to rules established Maldivian banks, the resort is not able to accept USD notes from the 1990s or any note that is damaged e.g., torn, dirty, pen marks. The resort does not accept traveler’s checks.
Is there anything I need to know about the prices?
+
All prices stated in all our products that is sold at the Property are excluding 10% Service Charge and 16% Government Tax. Therefore, all prices found on this website and at Malahini will be excluding these amounts.
