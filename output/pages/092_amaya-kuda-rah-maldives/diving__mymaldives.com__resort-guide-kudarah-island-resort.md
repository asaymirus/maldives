Kudarah island resort has the best of everything – from Ari Atoll’s renowned, picturesque natural beauty to the finest in luxury service and accommodation.
This is where natural beauty, exquisite design and consummate service meet to give you an unparalleled luxury experience.
Beach Villas
Located in seclusion with your own private beach, offering you a breathtaking, uninterrupted view of the lagoon this is your exclusive paradise.
With teak interiors complete with plush yet minimalist, warm and cosy furnishing, the two story Beach Villas also features an outdoor rear garden with private plunge pool, outdoor rain shower and beach front sun deck.
Beach Suites
Secluded from other travelers by lush tropical foliage, the two story suites offer the ultimate luxury experience to the travel connoisseur.
With private water front pool and sundeck, lounge and daybed areas along with garden courtyard bathroom with rain shower, this suite offers you a liberating experience; not just in terms of space but in terms of luxury like no other.
Offshore Suites
Extending from the shore line to atop the lagoon, your suite brings new meaning to exclusive lagoon accommodation.
With spacious private bathroom and interiors, large private sun deck and an infinity plunge pool, this is luxury living at its best.
Presidential Suite
With a spectacular view of the sunset, this luxurious, private suite offers you the ultimate holiday experience.
The over water, two bedroom suite with glass floor lounge, private sundecks with daybeds, Jacuzzi, in suite dining and entertainment and bar comes with plush, comfortable interiors and offers privacy second to none
