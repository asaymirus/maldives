Meeru Island Resort is one of the most popular resorts in the Maldives, the award winning choice of many honeymooners, SCUBA divers and families too! Located in North Male’ Atoll, a 50-minute speedboat ride from the airport, Meeru is a 4 star resort and offers a wonderful holiday for good value, including an All Inclusive plan option. Relax on the magnificent beach, snorkel in the crystal clear lagoon, Scuba Dive with Ocean Pro, take a massage at the over water Duniye Spa, a swim in the pool & kid’s pool and much more. Something for everyone including families.
