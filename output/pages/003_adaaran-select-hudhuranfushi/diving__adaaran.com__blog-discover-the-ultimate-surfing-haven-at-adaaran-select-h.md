Discover the Ultimate Surfing Haven at Adaaran Select Hudhuran Fushi
10 July 2024
Nestled amidst the breathtaking beauty of the Maldives lies Adaaran Select Hudhuran Fushi, the ultimate destination for surf enthusiasts. With the perfect left breaking down on one side of the island at Lohis and six other world-class waves just a short boat ride away, the resort offers the perfect blend of adventure, excitement, and comfort for your summer getaway.
Just a 30-minute speedboat ride from Velana International Airport, Adaaran Select Hudhuran Fushi is easily accessible for travellers seeking a tropical escape. With its prime location and dedicated surf school at the Lohis break, Adaaran Select Hudhuran Fushi promises an exhilarating experience for surfers of all levels. Whether you are a beginner looking to catch your first wave or a seasoned pro seeking the perfect barrel, the expert instructors will guide you through the Indian Ocean’s waters and help you master the art of surfing.
In between surf sessions, you can relax by the pool, engage in friendly competition on the courts, maintain your fitness routine at the in-resort gym or lay back and rejuvenate at our serene Chavana Spa. For those seeking tranquillity, yoga sessions and other relaxation activities await.
At Adaaran Select Hudhuran Fushi, adventure does not end with the tides. The resort offers a plethora of thrilling water sports, including snorkelling, windsurfing, catamaran sailing and diving. Moreover, marine life enthusiasts can explore our vibrant coral reefs, glide across the ocean’s surface or dive deep into its depths, the choice is yours.
For a change of scenery, join one of our twice daily surf excursions to other waves in the area. Led by experienced surf instructors, these trips allow you to discover new breaks and ride the waves to your heart’s content.
After a day of adrenaline-pumping activities, retreat to the cozy and comfortable accommodations at the resort. Choose from popular Beach Villas nestled along the shore, newly refurbished Lohis Villas just steps away from the surf break, or breathtaking Ocean Villas where tranquillity meets serenity.
Whether you are seeking adventure, relaxation, or a bit of both, Adaaran Select Hudhuran Fushi offers the perfect blend of comfort and excitement for your surf summer getaway. Immerse yourself in the beauty of the Maldives and let the waves guide you to an unforgettable experience this summer.
