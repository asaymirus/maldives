Swap Snow for Sunshine: Festive Celebrations at Adaaran Select Hudhuran Fushi
22 October 2024
As the holiday season approaches, imagine swapping winter wonderlands for sun-soaked beaches and cozy fireplaces for gentle ocean breezes. Adaaran Select Hudhuran Fushi, a Premium All-Inclusive resort nestled in the North Malé Atoll, offers a unique escape for those seeking a festive celebration away from the cold.
The seasonal celebrations kick off on 04 December with a refined four-course wine dinner at the Tiki Bar, where each dish is perfectly paired to enhance its flavours. Following this, the resort has curated a range of exciting activities to delight guests. Dine surrounded by the wonders of the ocean at the Shark View Dinner, savour a Seafood Beach BBQ featuring fresh catches grilled to perfection at the Beach Grill, or enjoy a magical Full Moon BBQ under the stars.
Guests can also immerse themselves in local culinary traditions with a Maldivian Cooking Class at the Banyan Restaurant or partake in the festive atmosphere during the Christmas Tree Lighting celebration, complete with cocktails at the Tiki Bar. An Asian-themed beach BBQ, blending vibrant flavours and seaside ambiance, will also be hosted at the Beach Grill.
On December 24, the excitement continues with Christmas Eve pre-dinner cocktails at the Sunset Reataurant, setting a cheerful tone for the evening. Guests can indulge in a special dinner buffet at the Banyan Restaurant, featuring a sumptuous array of holiday favourites, before dancing the night away at the Christmas Eve party with a DJ and live performances at the Tiki Bar.
Christmas Day brings a festive lunch buffet complemented by the joy of photos with Santa at the Banyan and Sunset Restaurants, creating memorable moments for families. Boxing Day will be filled with fun as guests enjoy a lively pool party at the Hiyala Bar, complete with refreshing drinks and upbeat music. A delightful Boxing Day snack buffet will also be available, offering comforting favourites and festive treats. Activities throughout the day include pizza making with kids, an Arabian-themed beach BBQ, sushi and maki roll-making classes, a neon party, and a black box cooking competition.
As the year comes to a close, the celebrations ramp up on 31 December. Guests can toast to the new year with pre-dinner cocktails at Sunset Restaurant before indulging in a special dinner buffet at the Banyan. The night culminates in an exhilarating New Year’s Eve show featuring spectacular fireworks and live performances at the Tiki Bar.
The festive spirit continues into January with a variety of celebrations and activities, including a Street Food BBQ with a White Bash, Shark View Dinner, Barefoot Island Dinner, Rhythm Night with Street Food BBQ, and Full Moon BBQ.
Adaaran Select Hudhuran Fushi promises a holiday season filled with warmth, joy, and unforgettable memories in paradise.
