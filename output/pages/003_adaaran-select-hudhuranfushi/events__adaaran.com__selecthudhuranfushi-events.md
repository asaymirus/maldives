Events at Adaaran Select Hudhuran Fushi
Beyond the Boardroom
At Adaaran Select Hudhuran Fushi, events move beyond formal spaces and into the energy of the island. Set in the North Malé Atoll with seamless speedboat access from Malé, the resort offers a dynamic setting for meetings, incentive groups, conferences, gala dinners and social celebrations. Flexible venues include the Nika Maalam Boardroom for focused indoor sessions and the Island Marquee for larger gatherings of up to 350 guests. With Premium All-Inclusive ease, themed dining, team-building activities, island excursions, live entertainment and dedicated event support, every occasion flows from business to celebration with warmth, clarity and confidence.
