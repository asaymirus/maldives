DESCRIPTION
Laid-back luxury where surf, sand, and stories come together.
Adaaran Select Hudhuranfushi, known as the Island of White Gold, blends laid-back luxury with authentic Maldivian charm across an expansive 83-acre island in North Malé Atoll. Fringed by shimmering white sands and crystal-clear lagoons, the resort is renowned for its iconic surf breaks, spacious natural setting, and diverse collection of beach and overwater villas. From exhilarating water sports and vibrant reef life to sunset cocktails and calming spa rituals, Hudhuranfushi delivers a relaxed island escape where every moment flows at your own pace.

Dining
Sunset Restaurant
Overlooking the harbour and open ocean, Sunset Restaurant offers an elegant fine dining experience paired with breathtaking sunset views. Featuring an international à la carte menu, it is ideal for romantic evenings and special occasions. Reserved primarily for Ocean Villa guests, other residents are invited once per stay to enjoy this refined culinary experience in a stunning oceanfront setting.
Banyan Restaurant
Banyan Restaurant presents a vibrant buffet dining experience with flavors from Maldivian, Asian, and Western cuisines. Live action stations and fresh seafood highlights add excitement to every meal. Open for breakfast, lunch, and dinner, Banyan offers variety, generous spreads, and a relaxed atmosphere that caters to guests from around the world.
Hiyala Bar
Located beside the pool, Hiyala Bar is a lively spot for refreshing cocktails, light snacks, and relaxed daytime lounging. As the day unfolds, it becomes a social hub where guests gather to unwind, share stories, and enjoy the tropical breeze. Ideal for casual drinks and laid-back island evenings.
Lohis Bar
Set along powder-soft sands with uninterrupted sunset views, Lohis Wave is a relaxed beachfront bar perfect for quiet moments and romantic drinks. Sip from a curated beverage selection as coconut palms sway overhead and the horizon glows at dusk. This is one of the island’s most atmospheric spots to slow down and savor the moment.
Tiki Bar
Tiki Bar offers a vibrant atmosphere with signature cocktails, fine wines, and premium beverages. Lively yet relaxed, it becomes the center of entertainment on Sunday nights with DJ sessions that energize the island. Ideal for social evenings, music, and memorable nights under the stars.
Beach Grill Bar
Beach Grill Bar serves casual daytime bites including wraps, burgers, and salads in a relaxed beachside setting. As evening approaches, it transforms into a cocktail spot ideal for winding down after a sun-filled day. Simple, flavorful, and informal, it is perfect for guests seeking easygoing island dining.
Indian Pavilion Restaurant
Indian Pavilion celebrates the rich culinary traditions of North and South India. Featuring a carefully curated menu, the restaurant highlights bold spices, authentic techniques, and regional favorites. Open for breakfast, lunch, and dinner, it is a must-visit for guests seeking comforting and flavorful Indian cuisine during their stay.

Highlights From The Resort
Surf at Hudhuranfushi
Adaaran Select Hudhuranfushi is one of the Maldives’ most iconic surf resorts, located beside the famous Lohis left-hand reef break. With six additional world-class surf spots accessible by boat, it is a dream destination for surfers of all levels. Comfortable accommodation, generous island space, and satisfying dining options make it an ideal base for an unforgettable surf holiday.
Mandara Spa
Mandara Spa offers a tranquil sanctuary designed for deep relaxation and renewal. Featuring double treatment rooms with outdoor showers and baths, open-air bale, manicure and pedicure services, and a retail boutique, the spa combines traditional Balinese therapies with modern wellness practices. Skilled therapists guide each guest through soothing rituals that restore balance to both body and mind.
Sunset Fishing Experience
Sunset fishing offers guests a chance to experience one of the Maldives’ oldest traditions. As the sun sets over the Indian Ocean, guests venture out to sea to try traditional line fishing with guidance from local experts. The calm evening atmosphere, pastel skies, and gentle ocean create a deeply relaxing experience. Guests who make a successful catch may even enjoy it prepared by the resort’s chefs.
Excursions and Island Adventures
Hudhuranfushi is the perfect playground for sun, sand, and sea lovers. A wide range of curated excursions allows guests to explore nearby islands, reefs, and sandbanks. From cultural encounters to marine adventures, each experience offers a deeper connection to the natural beauty and rhythm of the Maldives.
Diving and House Reef
The house reef at Hudhuranfushi is ideal for beginners and experienced divers alike, with gentle slopes reaching depths of up to 35 meters. Guided dives and introductory courses reveal vibrant coral formations and marine life. The resort’s diving center provides safe, professional access to some of the most rewarding underwater experiences in the region.
Watersports and Ocean Activities
Guests can enjoy a wide selection of water-based activities including snorkeling, canoeing, windsurfing, and more. With warm waters and excellent visibility, Hudhuranfushi invites guests to stay active and adventurous throughout their island escape.
