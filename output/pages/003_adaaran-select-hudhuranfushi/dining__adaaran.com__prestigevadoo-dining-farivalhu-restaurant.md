Farivalhu Fine Dining Restaurant
Enjoy the multitude of enticing flavours of International cuisines from Asian specialties to ingenious fusion creations in a plush, warm setting with scenic views over the Indian Ocean at one of the enticing Maldives fine dining; Farivalhu at Adaaran Prestige Vadoo.
Farivalhu is a blend of traditional and modern Maldivian aesthetics; in the evenings, soft music plays over a glimmering ocean, creating a magical backdrop for a one-of-a-kind fine dining experience.
Only the freshest seafood is delivered daily from the waters around the atoll; highlights of the menu include sumptuous lobster and other succulent seafood delicacies prepared to perfection under the guidance of our experienced culinary team.
