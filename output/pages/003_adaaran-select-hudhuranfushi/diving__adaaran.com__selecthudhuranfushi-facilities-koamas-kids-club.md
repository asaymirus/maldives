Facilities
Koamas Kids Club Fun
At Adaaran Select Hudhuran Fushi, young guests have their own island of discovery. Koamas Kids Club offers a vibrant space where children can play, explore, and enjoy activities designed to spark creativity and curiosity. From fun games and beach adventures to arts, crafts, and island-inspired activities, every day brings something new for our junior guests. It’s a place where little explorers can make friends, create memories, and experience the Maldives in their own exciting way.
Open:
Daily From 09:00hrs – 19:00hrs
Kindly Note That Kids Club
Age Group is 4 to 12 Years Old.

Rules & Regulations
Kid’s Club staff will not apply any sun block on any child, so we ask parent/guardians to do so.All children must be registered by parent/legal guardian who should also complete a disclaimer form.Kid’s Club is not responsible for any loss of personal items while under care or throughout the activities.Kid’s Club is not a personalized baby-sitting service, so we ask parent/legal guardians to always remain within the resort premises while their child is registered within Kid’s Club.No glasses containers, smoking, food or Alcoholic Beverages are permitted.
