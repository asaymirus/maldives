Planning your Maldives vacation at Adaaran Select Hudhuranfushi and wondering how to get to the resort?
Shared Speedboat Transfers
From Velana International Airport (MLE), convenient Maldives speedboat transfers to Adaaran Select Hudhuranfushi await. Upon arrival at Velana International Airport, guests are requested to proceed to the resort counter B3, where our airport representative will be available to assist with resort transportation. The journey from Velana International Airport to the resort takes approximately 30 minutes under normal sea conditions.
Our airport representative will guide you to the designated speedboat where your luggage will be loaded. Enjoy the tranquil ride as your speedboat cruises through the vivid blue waters towards your paradise.
