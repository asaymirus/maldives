Location
Your journey to the South Male Atoll begins with a swift and scenic 15-minute boat ride on a Wi-Fi-enabled speedboat to our Maldives Beach Resort. Our mature island boasts the best house reef and Vadoo caves in the South Male Atoll, teeming with vibrant underwater fauna and flora. The aquamarine waters reveal a stunning diving location suitable for both advanced and novice divers. Encounter a mesmerizing array of marine life, from colorful fish to vibrant coral and other captivating creatures of the deep.
You can also discover the beautifully landscaped English gardens, that add to the charm and tranquility of our surroundings.
Weather
Weather in Maldives
The weather in Maldives is sunny and warm throughout the year. During the drier season, visitors will enjoy no less than eight or nine hours of sunshine each day. For the greater part of the year, the climate is delightful, providing every sun lover a chance to bask in its glorious rays.
The best Time to visit Maldives is between November and April, outside of the Monsoon season. Extreme weather conditions are rare in the Maldives, with very infrequent cyclonic conditions.
