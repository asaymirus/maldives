Lohis Villa
Step straight from the sand into your Lohis Villa, a true surfer’s retreat just moments from the legendary Lohis left-hand break. Inside, cool tiled floors, a king-sized bed, and fresh espresso set the tone for easy island living. Outside, palms sway in the breeze while the sound of the surf rolls in from just steps away. Perfectly positioned beside the famous Lohis Wave, this villa places you closer to the action than anywhere else on the island.
Stay five nights and enjoy a complimentary excursion, or extend your escape for two and receive daily snorkel safaris departing directly from the jetty.
