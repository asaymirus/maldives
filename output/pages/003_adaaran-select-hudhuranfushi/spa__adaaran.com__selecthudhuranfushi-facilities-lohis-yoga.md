Facilities
Lohis Yoga
Restore balance during your stay with Lohis Yoga at Adaaran Select Hudhuran Fushi. Enjoy sessions twice a day, welcome for all levels from beginners to experienced practitioners. Start the morning with an energising Hatha–Vinyasa flow at 7:30 a.m and unwind at sunset with a calming Hatha–Yin practice at 5:00 p.m, each designed to encourage mindful movement and relaxation.
Guests can also enjoy a complimentary weekly beach taster session. Flexible yoga packages and private classes are available for those wishing to deepen their practice during their island stay.
