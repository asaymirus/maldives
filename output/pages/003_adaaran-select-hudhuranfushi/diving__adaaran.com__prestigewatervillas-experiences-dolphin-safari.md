Experiences
Dolphin Safari
Experience a relaxing Dhoni ride in search of wild dolphins over the Indian Ocean. The Maldives is home to several species of dolphins, which can be seen gracing the waters of the surrounding area.
Watch them jumping and doing their friendly tricks as they frolic and play in the wake of our Dhoni. While nothing with wildlife can be absolutely guaranteed, you will never have a better chance to watch wild dolphins in Maldives playing in their natural environment than right here.
This is one of the most fun-filled and moving water activities in Maldives you can ever experience while in the Maldives, and will create lasting memories for years to come.
