Celebrate the Season Under a Tropical Sun at Adaaran Prestige Vadoo
23 October 2024
Escape the chill of winter and step into a sun-kissed paradise at Adaaran Prestige Vadoo, an adults-only resort just a 15-minute, Wi Fi enabled speedboat ride from Male city. This holiday season, guests can immerse themselves in tropical seasonal vibes where the warmth of the ocean breeze and the beauty of white sandy beaches create the perfect backdrop for an unforgettable holiday for those seeking lively entertainment or some tranquil downtime in an intimate escape.
Seasonal celebrations will kick off on December 10 with Christmas Melodies, a festive celebration of holiday music that brings joy and warmth through seasonal tunes. Throughout the month, a variety of exciting events and activities await. Guests can savour coconut-infused cocktails during the exotic Narikela Cocktail Tasting at the Dhoni Bar or enjoy a Tropical Romantic Dinner under the stars, where exquisite seafood pairs beautifully with the gentle ocean breeze. As the day transitions into night, the Sundown Cocktail Evening at the Kandholi Sky Bar provides a relaxing atmosphere amidst sea breeze and sparkling blue waters.
The festivities continue with Sun-Kissed Dinners, offering a delightful dining experience as the sun sets, and Festive Cocktails & Balinese Dances, where crafted cocktails are complemented by entertaining Balinese performances. For a truly romantic experience, the Love in Paradise Dinner sets the scene in a breathtaking tropical backdrop. Other exciting events include lively Disco Nights, Full Moon BBQ Dinners, a Wine & Cheese Evening, a White Cocktail Evening, and Apretif Cocktail Tasting. Guests can also participate in the lighting of the Christmas tree, enjoy Christmas carols, attend a cookery demonstration, and join a festive pool party.
On December 24, the celebrations ramp up with a Christmas Eve Pre-Dinner Cocktail, allowing guests to enjoy festive drinks and seasonal cheer before the holiday dinner at The Dhoni Bar. The Christmas Eve Gala Dinner features exquisite cuisine and festive elegance at the beach, where joyful Christmas carols fill the air and Santa makes a grand arrival, bringing festive magic to the holiday season. The evening culminates in a lively Christmas party filled with decorations and holiday treats.
On December 25, guests can indulge in a delicious Christmas Brunch, offering a seasonal spread perfect for celebrating the holiday. For a more intimate dining experience, the Festive Fine Dining Sit-Down Dinner at Farivalhu Restaurant features romantic blues that enhance the ambiance.
The celebrations continue on December 26 with a Christmas Cocktail Evening at the Kandholhi Bar, featuring a selection of seasonal cocktails. The Festive Sundown Party with DJ Kristina promises an unforgettable night of fun, amazing entertainment, and positive vibes against the stunning backdrop of Vadoo’s Main Beach.
New Year’s Eve festivities begin with a Pre-Dinner Cocktail at the Kandholhi Bar, celebrating in style and flavour. The New Year’s Gala Dinner at the beach offers gourmet dishes in a festive atmosphere, culminating in a breathtaking Grand Fireworks Show set to light up the night sky with dazzling colours.
On January 1, a New Year Bubbly Brunch at Farivalhu Restaurant marks the start of the new year. Guests can enjoy crafted cocktails and captivating Balinese dance performances at The Dhoni Bar, while the Love in Paradise Dinner on the beach sets the tone for a year filled with romance and adventure.
Adaaran Prestige Vadoo promises a tropical celebration this holiday season, filled with warmth, joy, and unforgettable memories in paradise.
