Surf Package - Adaaran Hudhuran Fushi
Adaaran Select Hudhuran Fushi is one of the best surf holiday resorts in the Maldives with a perfect left breaking down one side of the island and six other world class waves just a short boat ride away making it the ideal location for Surfing! The 4star resort offers comfortable accommodation and excellent buffet dining that will satisfy even the hungriest of surfers after a long day in the waves!
Surfers can choose to stay in our popular Beach Villas nestled under the trees along the edge of a white sand beach, or in one of the Lohis Rooms that are literally a twenty second walk to the surf! There is a third option – our breathtaking Ocean Villas where your ocean experience can reach another level of enjoyment!
In between surf sessions, you can choose various Maldives water sport activites, relax in the pool or stay active on the tennis, badminton, squash or volley-ball courts, or work out in the Indoor gym & outdoor gym, Skate on our halfpipe skate ramp or enjoy a Yoga class! Alternatively, just relax and enjoy the many other attractions that make Hudhuran Fushi the ideal destination for the discerning surf traveler who is looking for a relaxing, stress-free holiday, while scoring some great waves.
In order to enhance the surfing experience and improve service levels to the surfers, the resort will restrict the maximum surfers per day to 45. You can only be registered as a surfer by booking your stay with a Surf Travel Agent, surf passes cannot be bought separate to accommodation.
If you want a change of scenery from the perfect lefts at Lohis, we run twice daily surf excursions to the other waves in the area in our large, safe, shaded Surf Dhonis. These trips are included in the surfer package when you book with a surf travel agent. We have a team of experienced surf guides on the island to help you find the perfect wave!
Please note that due to the nature of the waves in the Maldives all surfers should be of at least intermediate level of surfing ability, be capable swimmers, and of suitable fitness for an active holiday.
Surf passes are mandatory for surfers & can only be purchased together with surf packages from surf agents ONLY.
