DESCRIPTION
‘Welcome to a place where your comfort and pleasure is understood – experience the ultimate in luxury at Constance Halaveli, part of the Leading Hotels of the World group.’
Constance Halaveli is a jewel-coloured island shaped like a traditional Maldivian boat, set against the glittering waters of North Ari Atoll. The turquoise lagoon and fine white sand contrasts with the exuberant green foliage. Constance Halaveli is a place to relax and regenerate in a pristine environment of overwhelming peacefulness. All of the exclusive villas are air-conditioned and feature their own private plunge pool located on the beach or over the water. They are furnished using luxurious materials including hardwood and marble.  The three restaurants and the spa ensure that both body and mind are well cared for. The area is of the best in the Maldives for scuba diving. Vibrant coral reefs, whale sharks and sunken shipwrecks are amongst the top attractions.

Dining
Jahaz Restaurant
Elegant, laid back feel, Jahaz Restaurant, and Bar offers one refined dining room as well as an extensive outdoor dining area so that you enjoy the sand between your toes, ocean and pool-side view.
Open for breakfast, lunch, and dinner; Jahaz restaurant consists of an extensive buffet – celebrating the world’s cuisine. Enjoy the vibrant theme evenings including live music and barbecues.
Jahaz Bar & Lounge
Main Beach Bar with 75 seatings, delicious drinks can be savored while listening to live music. Experience our collection of refreshing cocktails made with the best liquors and fresh fruits or enjoy an ice-tea while making the most of the stunning view of the Indian ocean.
Jing Restaurant
Set in a serene area halfway along the Halaveli jetty, Jing restaurant and bar offers the perfect fusion of Asian and European cuisine. It is ideal for candlelit dinners where the lapping of the ocean against the shore creates a pleasant ambiance for a sumptuous dinner.
Jing Bar
Located halfway along the Halaveli jetty; Jing Bar enjoys a beautiful location and view.
Contemplate the beauty of the Indian Ocean while enjoying one of our signature cocktails.
Meeru Beach Grill
Embrace laid-back, barefoot chic at Meeru restaurant and enjoy a la carte menu of fresh seafood and meat.
Simple, smart and seriously tasty, the classic grill kitchen is open for dinner. (subject to weather conditions)
Kaika Sushi Bar
Kaika Sushi – overlooks the magnificent turquoise lagoon at Constance Halaveli Maldives offering exceptional views. Sand floored sushi bar, with an open cooking counter so that guest sees the creation of their chosen dish.
Be ready to feast on a variety of traditional Japanese sushi rolls, sashimi & Maki’s.
