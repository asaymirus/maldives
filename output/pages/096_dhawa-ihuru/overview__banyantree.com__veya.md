Banyan Tree Veya
Veya means "to weave"
Welcome to Veya, a place of connection with self, others, and nature. A safe space of both wonderment and reflection where you can discover new tools, knowledge and wisdom to integrate your own path to wellbeing. As a pioneer in the evolution of wellness and resorts, the Veya experience is built on Banyan Tree’s proprietary 8 pillars of wellbeing with an emphasis on embodiment therapies and somatic practices. The journey is led by certified multidisciplinary wellbeing hosts around a 3-step protocol of Awareness, Discovery, and Sustenance.
At Banyan Tree Veya, wellbeing is approached as a mindful journey shaped by presence, intention, and connection. Rooted in immersive and restorative practices, Veya invites guests to reconnect with themselves in a way that feels grounding, personal, and lasting long after the experience draws to a close.
Own Your Presence
Wellbeing is a lifelong journey. A path of small steps and big leaps guided by intuition, awareness, and practice. It’s an exploration of your inner self and your connection to the outside world. Often careful. Sometimes courageous. Always considered. Veya is here to support you along that path.
While everyone’s wellbeing journey is different, there are learnings and practices that universally help us find balance within our body, our mind, and our environment. Veya’s approach to wellbeing composes of a range of multidisciplinary modalities that have been distilled and clarified into 8 universal pathways toward being well – our 8 Pillars of Wellbeing. These pillars create a holistic framework that fosters emotional resilience, physical vitality, and mindful living through experiences that are both accessible and deeply personal.
The Veya Journey
Awareness
Your journey begins with an individual wellbeing assessment and mapping with a wellbeing host who will work with you to create a bespoke programme that aligns with your needs and priorities.
Discovery
Your personal programme is curated from the group wellbeing activity calendar with emphasis on mindfulness practices, somatic practices, and lifestyle learning to centre the mind and body.
Sustenance
A private end-of-stay consultation will guide you to continue on your path of wellbeing when you return home. You will have access to a range of online content to continue your wellbeing practice. By focusing on continuity rather than temporary transformation, Veya supports a more realistic and meaningful integration of wellbeing into everyday life.
We’re Here for You
We personalise your Veya journey with an individual wellbeing assessment, mapping, and consultation. A bespoke wellbeing program curated from the group wellbeing activity calendar is built around your needs and wellbeing goals. Daily rituals and mindful practices centre the mind and body. A team of resident experts and your dedicated wellbeing host are on hand to guide, support, and celebrate with you on this journey.
Dining at Veya
Veya Cuisine is a place that brings together all who desire to eat well, and enjoy conscious and nutritious cuisine that delights the senses and elevates the wellbeing. Menus are guided by seasonal ingredients and mindful preparation, offering vibrant dishes that nourish while reflecting the richness of local culinary traditions.
