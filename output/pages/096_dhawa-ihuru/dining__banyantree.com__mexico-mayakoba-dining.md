What dining options does Banyan Tree Mayakoba offer to guests?
Banyan Tree Mayakoba offers various dining options and Destination Dining experiences suitable for families and couples.
Is room service available at Banyan Tree Mayakoba?
Yes, room service is available at Banyan Tree Mayakoba. The resort offers 24-hour, in-villa dining, allowing guests to enjoy a variety of culinary options in the comfort of their own villa.
Are there any specialty dining experiences at Banyan Tree Mayakoba?
Haab: Immerse yourself in the mysticism of Mayan culture and embark on a meaningful journey through each dish prepared with regional ingredients and traditional recipes. Mexican wines and spirits harmonize the multi-course dinner, while traditional song and dance performances will transport you across time.
In- Villa Signature BBQ: Enjoy a barbecue at Banyan Tree Mayakoba in the comfort of your own villa or by the ocean! The resort offers in-villa and beach BBQ experiences for private and special dining.
Ixchel: Enjoy breakfast or an extraordinary dinner under a blanket of stars. Experience a unique culinary journey on a traditional Mexican Trajinera, floating through waterways while savoring breakfast or a remarkable dinner in the moonlight.
Sunset Cruise: Sail through the resort's canals while you admire beautiful scenery accompanied by exquisite drinks and meals in the company of your family or best friends. Navigate through waterways surrounded by greenery and extraordinary views that will steal your heart.
Latin Beach BBQ: Celebrate our Latin roots and awaken your senses as you feast on fresh seafood by the shores of the Mexican Caribbean. The experience is rounded out with live music and the cool sea breeze.
Mexican Night: Enjoy authentic Mexican cuisine and culture with live mariachi music, delicious antojitos, and artisanal cocktails made with local ingredients.
Are restaurants and bars open to the public?
Restaurants and bars at Banyan Tree Mayakoba are open to resort guests and visitors who have made a reservation or have a confirmed dining appointment.
Does Banyan Tree Mayakoba cater to specific dietary requirements?
The Banyan Tree Mayakoba resort can accommodate various dietary needs and preferences, including vegetarian, vegan, gluten-free, halal, and kosher meals with advance notice, as well as special dietary requirements for guests with allergies or intolerances.
The resort's culinary team is happy to work with guests to tailor meals to their needs. It's recommended that the resort be informed of any dietary requirements or preferences at least 24 hours in advance to ensure they can prepare accordingly.

Do you have a dress code in your restaurants at Banyan Tree Mayakoba?
Banyan Tree Mayakoba has a dress code policy for its restaurants:
Oriente: Casual attire is recommended.
At night: Saffron, Cello, Tomahawk Open Fire Latin Grill, Sands Beach Club: Elegant-casual attire is recommended. Beach shorts and flip-flops are not permitted.
Private Dining: The dress code varies based on the location and occasion, with beach dinners being more relaxed.
Please be advised that our restaurants operate according to seasonality and may be subject to changes in operating hours without prior notice.
