Frequently Asked Questions
Expand All
Is Banyan Tree Mayakoba an oceanfront resort?
Yes. Banyan Tree Mayakoba is an oceanfront resort set within the private Mayakoba community on Mexico’s Caribbean coast. The resort brings together beachfront, ocean-view, lagoon, and tropical settings within one destination.
How far is the resort from Cancún International Airport?
Banyan Tree Mayakoba is approximately 30 miles (49 km) from Cancún International Airport (CUN), with an estimated driving time of 40 minutes, depending on traffic.
Is Banyan Tree Mayakoba an all-inclusive resort?
Banyan Tree Mayakoba operates on a European Plan. For groups, an exclusive Gourmet Experience Meal Plan is available for a minimum of 30 guests or for 15 villas staying for 3 nights or more. The plan includes three selected meals daily and selected alcoholic beverages from 11:00 AM to 11:00 PM at participating restaurants and bars.
Can group programs be customized?
Absolutely. From executive retreats and incentive trips to conferences, celebrations, and wellness-focused gatherings, every program can be tailored to your objectives. Our team can assist with venue selection, dining experiences, wellbeing activities, team-building programs, and curated destination experiences to create a memorable event for your guests.
How many villas are required to qualify as a group?
Bookings of 10 or more villas for three nights or more may qualify as a group. Our Group Ambassadors will work with you to understand your program requirements and create an experience tailored to your objectives.
Is the resort available for exclusive buyouts?
Yes, subject to seasonal availability. Banyan Tree Mayakoba can accommodate resort buyouts, offering groups an exceptional level of privacy and exclusive access to accommodations and selected resort facilities.
For buyout programs, booking at least one year in advance is recommended to help secure availability.
How can I contact a Group Ambassador?
Our Group Ambassadors would be delighted to learn more about your program and RFP requirements.
