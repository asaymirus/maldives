15 Years Of Banyan Tree Mayakoba in Banyan Group's Global Story
FROM DREAM TO LEGACY
Imagine standing on the coast of Phuket in the early 1990s. Where many saw an abandoned tin mine with barren land and polluted waters, the founders of Banyan Tree saw an opportunity. Their vision transformed a damaged landscape into a thriving sanctuary, giving rise to the first Banyan Tree hotel in Phuket in 1994, a lasting testament to the power of sustainability and transformation.

THAI FEST
Every year, for a single day, Banyan Tree Mayakoba not only organizes a festival: it creates another country on its own. The aromas, the colors, the sounds... everything changes. The resort's Thai architecture comes alive with live music, iconic tuk-tuks cruise the trails as in Chiang Mai, and a spice market makes you feel like you've crossed the world without leaving the Riviera Maya.
