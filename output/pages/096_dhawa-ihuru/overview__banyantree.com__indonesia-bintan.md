Banyan Tree Bintan
Bintan, Indonesia
Discover Rustic Luxury in the Tropics
Immerse yourself in a natural sojourn on Bintan Island, where lush rainforests come alive across towering trees, hillside terrain, secluded beaches and the sounds of nature. A gem among Bintan Island resorts, Banyan Tree Bintan invites adventure seekers and holidaymakers to embrace sweeping views of the South China Sea, where the glittering ocean meets the tropical coastline.
Be mesmerised by villas elegantly decorated in traditional Balinese style, each raised on stilts and nestled into the hillside with private access to the beach. Spend your days unwinding on the resort’s exclusive shoreline, savouring unforgettable dining experiences, teeing off on the Laguna Golf Bintan championship course or rejuvenating body and soul at the award-winning Banyan Tree Spa.

The hotel is just a 1-hour ferry ride away from the Singapore city centre.
What is the best way to reach Banyan Tree Bintan from Singapore?
The best way to reach Banyan Tree Bintan from Singapore is to take the Bintan Resort Ferry from Tanah Merah Ferry Terminal to Bintan Ferry Terminal. The journey typically takes around 1 hour.
What is Bintan known for?
Bintan Island, Indonesia, is best known for its white-sand beaches, coastal rainforests, luxury resorts, championship golf courses, and convenient 45-minute ferry access from Singapore. At Banyan Tree Bintan, guests experience the island through an exclusive private beach, lush rainforest surroundings, holistic wellness programs, water sports, and nearby attractions.
What are the top attractions in Bintan?
Popular attractions include the beaches of Bintan Resorts, Gurun Pasir Busung, the Mangrove Discovery Tour along Sebung River, Tanjung Pinang, and the 500 Lohan Temple. Guests staying at Banyan Tree Bintan can also enjoy Laguna Golf Bintan, the resort's private beach, Marine Centre and nature experiences.
When is the best time to visit Bintan?
The best time to visit Bintan Island for outdoor and beach activities is during the dry season from April to October, when the weather is sunniest with low rainfall and calm seas. The wetter monsoon season runs from November to March, though Bintan remains a warm, tropical destination throughout the year.
What are the best things to do in Bintan?
The best things to do in Bintan include water sports and beach activities, championship golf, spa and wellness treatments, cycling, nature walks, guided mangrove tours, cultural excursions, and local dining. At Banyan Tree Bintan, top highlights include an exclusive private beach, an interactive Marine Centre, the award-winning Banyan Tree Spa, Laguna Golf Bintan, and signature destination dining.
Are there cultural experiences to discover in Bintan?
Yes, visitors can explore Bintan’s rich Malay, Chinese, and Riau Islands heritage through cultural landmarks such as historic Tanjung Pinang, the heritage water village of Senggarang, traditional local markets, and ancient temples. Guests staying at Banyan Tree Bintan can easily arrange customized cultural excursions through the resort.
Is Bintan a good destination for a golf holiday?
Yes, Bintan is an established golf destination featuring championship courses set within lush tropical landscapes. Banyan Tree Bintan is especially convenient for golf holidays, as Laguna Golf Bintan—an 18-hole, par-72 course—is located directly within the resort area.
What attractions are near Banyan Tree Bintan?
Banyan Tree Bintan is located in the Laguna Bintan Resort area, conveniently close to Laguna Golf Bintan and key Bintan Resorts attractions. Guests can easily explore popular nearby sites—including the Sebung River mangroves, Gurun Pasir Busung, and Plaza Lagoi—via resort-arranged transport.
