Banyan Tree Escape
An Invitation to a New Way of Being
Enveloped by landscapes rich with dense forests, peaceful coastlines, and untouched wilderness, each stay invites a deeper sense of place. Beyond a jungle retreat resort, Banyan Tree Escape is a sanctuary where nature-led living and thoughtful hospitality come together. Every day brings a slower pace of life, inviting you to enjoy meaningful experiences guided by local rhythms, authentic encounters, and reflective moments.
This is a place where time passes at a different pace. A place to disconnect and reconnect in unfamiliar ways. Welcome to Banyan Tree Escape.
Disconnect to reconnect
Sense of Arrival
A deep connection to the community. From the moment guests arrive, they leave their routines behind and are embraced by the destination, woven into its rhythm and culture.
Sense of Provenance
Every touchpoint celebrates the soul of the destination—from architecture to dining. We honour local heritage, flavours, and artisan crafts in ways that reflect the essence of each unique place.
Sense of Discovery
Embark on spontaneous journeys that reveal the destination’s secrets, where each moment unfolds into a new experience. Wander through hidden trails beneath lush canopies, encounter local traditions formed by generations, or pause to take in quiet moments amidst stunning nature. Every experience feels intuitive and unhurried, creating space for genuine discovery.
Sense of Wellbeing
We embrace the connection between self, others, and nature. Through accessible, evidence-based practices, we support your path to a balanced, meaningful life. Restorative rituals, mindful movement, and time spent outdoors lead you towards slow-paced living, allowing wellbeing to emerge naturally through the surroundings.
Sense of Taste
Regionally sourced, locally curated. The food we serve captures the spirit of each place with creative presentations and flavours rooted in local traditions. Seasonal ingredients and distinctive regional influences shape each dining experience, weaving the character of the destination in each bite.
