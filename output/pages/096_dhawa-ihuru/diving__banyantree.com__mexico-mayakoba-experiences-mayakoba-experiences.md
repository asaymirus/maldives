Mayakoba experiences
Endless Adventure Awaits at Mayakoba
Adventures at Mayakoba take you from the water’s edge to the leafy mangroves, with outdoor experiences for all ages and abilities. Whether exploring the coral reef offshore, biking miles of nature trails, or immersing yourself in Mayan culture, Mayakoba offers an endless array of unique experiences inspired by the pristine surroundings.
Private Yacht Charters
THE MAYAKOBA ESCAPE
Depart from the Mayakoba beach by private tender and travel toward Punta Maroma, an amazing snorkeling destination.
PERFECT TIMING
Board directly from our private tender at Mayakoba and cruise to Cozumel to enjoy magical views from the island's northern side. If sea life is your passion, trade Cozumel for cruising the crystal-clear waters to discover special snorkeling spots.
A DAY AT SEA
The Mayakoba Yacht will await your private tender as you set off for a day cruising to Cozumel or along the shoreline to Cancun and Isla Mujeres, enjoying incredible views of the Caribbean Sea and Riviera Maya’s famous waters along the way.
SUNSET
Enjoy a dream experience leaving the beach on the Mayakoba VanDutch 55’ Yacht to watch the sunset from the turquoise waters of Riviera Maya. The trip concludes in Puerto Aventuras, and the return is made by land.
PERSONALIZED EXCURSIONS
Design your perfect day on the water with a customizable excursion with upgrade options such as private chef, beverages, snacks, lunch, snorkel gear, and other water activities
Scuba Diving & Snorkeling
THE MAYAKOBA SEEKER
Cruise the Caribbean Sea aboard our unique dive boat as you expand your diving experience. Enjoy two dives at two different sites. Certification is required—3-hour excursion.
SNORKEL THE BARRIER REEF
Board our custom-made boat from the Mayakoba beach and snorkel along the world’s second-largest barrier reef. Enjoy expert guides, top-notch equipment, and unforgettable memories of our coral restoration nursery during this 90-minute excursion.
OPEN WATER COURSE
The most popular certificate PADI offers starts with online theory courses and exams before your arrival. Then complete your certification with four immersions across two days.
THE MAYAKOBA EXPLORER
Depart straight from our beach on our Mayakoba Dive Boat and become part of the sea for the first time.

El Camaleón Golf Course
Tee off on Latin America's most prestigious golf course, a championship experience masterfully designed by Greg Norman. Our resort offers an unparalleled golfing experience, and as our guest, you'll also enjoy exclusive access to El Camaleón Golf Course at Mayakoba.
Nestled within the pristine landscape of Mayakoba, our 18-hole golf course is a masterpiece of design, seamlessly integrating with the region's natural beauty. Its challenging fairways gracefully traverse lush greenery, wind across pristine freshwater canals embraced by mangrove and limestone walls, and run alongside the captivating Caribbean Sea, offering both strategic play and breathtaking views.
This course proudly boasts a distinguished history of hosting premier golf events, including 16 PGA TOUR events, an LPGA TOUR event in 2025, two LIV Golf dates, LACC, AJGA, and numerous other renowned international competitions. This impressive legacy firmly establishes it as a cornerstone of competitive golf.
