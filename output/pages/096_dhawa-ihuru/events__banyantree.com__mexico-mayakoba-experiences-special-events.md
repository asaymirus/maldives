Día de Muertos at Mayakoba
From October 30 to November 1, Mayakoba invites you to experience the beauty and symbolism of Día de Muertos, one of Mexico’s most treasured traditions. Set amid tranquil lagoons and lush natural surroundings, this meaningful celebration honors the enduring connection between memory, life, and those who remain forever in our hearts.
Discover thoughtfully crafted altars, evocative rituals, and moments inspired by generations of tradition, as Mayakoba comes alive with color, symbolism, and stories that celebrate the lives of those who came before us. A timeless tribute to remembrance, heritage, and the beauty of keeping memories alive.
