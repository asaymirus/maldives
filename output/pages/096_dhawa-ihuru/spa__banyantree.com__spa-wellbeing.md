About Banyan Spa & Wellbeing Academy
Celebrate the art of touch at Banyan Tree Spa & Wellbeing Academy. Located in Phuket, Thailand, and nestled in lush parkland overlooking the Andaman Sea, the academy is the first of its kind in Asia, and the first to be accredited by Thailand’s education and health ministries.
Here, ancient wisdom is inherited, not learned. From anatomy and reflexology to aromatherapy and massage techniques, over 2,000 spa therapists have graduated with certified expertise and a deep-rooted understanding of holistic wellbeing.
