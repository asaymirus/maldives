What is the Riviera Maya known for?
The Riviera Maya is best known for its pristine Caribbean beaches, lush tropical landscapes, natural cenotes, and living Maya heritage. Located on Mexico’s Yucatán Peninsula, the region offers a rich blend of historic culture, regional gastronomy, and coastal nature.
Why should you visit the Riviera Maya?
The Riviera Maya is an ideal destination for relaxing in nature, exploring Maya cultural heritage, and enjoying a tropical climate year-round. Set along the Caribbean Sea, it provides a scenic backdrop for exploring the historic wonders and biodiversity of the Yucatán Peninsula at an unhurried pace.
What makes Mayakoba a unique destination in the Riviera Maya?
Mayakoba is defined by its distinct eco-resort ecosystem, where lush mangroves, freshwater lagoons, tropical flora, and native wildlife connect with the Caribbean coast. Its tranquil waterways and natural surroundings offer a serene, private setting within the Riviera Maya.
When is the best time to visit the Riviera Maya?
The best time to visit the Riviera Maya is from November through April, when the region experiences cooler temperatures, lower humidity, and minimal rainfall. These months provide ideal weather for beaches, cenotes, and outdoor excursions.
How many days should you spend in the Riviera Maya?
A stay of 5 to 7 days is recommended to fully experience the Riviera Maya. This allows sufficient time to visit archaeological sites, explore natural cenotes, and dedicate several days to poolside or oceanfront relaxation.
Is the Riviera Maya a good destination for families or couples?
Yes, the Riviera Maya is well suited for both couples and families. The destination offers private, romantic settings for couples alongside family-friendly accommodations, watersports, and cultural experiences designed for all ages.
What should first-time visitors know before travelling to the Riviera Maya?
Visitors to the Riviera Maya can expect a warm tropical climate, Spanish as the primary language, and the Mexican peso (MXN) as local currency. The region is served by Cancún International Airport (CUN) and offers easy access to Caribbean beaches, cenotes, local communities, and Maya ruins.
What are the best things to do in the Riviera Maya?
Top things to do in the Riviera Maya include swimming in freshwater cenotes, exploring ancient Maya ruins, relaxing on Caribbean beaches, and sampling authentic regional cuisine. Visitors can also explore coastal mangroves, eco-parks, and local wildlife sanctuaries.

What cultural experiences can you enjoy in the Riviera Maya?
You can experience Maya culture by visiting renowned archaeological sites such as Tulum, Cobá, and Chichén Itzá. Exploring local artisan markets, traditional Mayan villages, and regional Yucatecan dining also offers authentic cultural insight.
What makes Banyan Tree Mayakoba unique?
Banyan Tree Mayakoba is an all-private-pool villa resort set within the protected mangroves and freshwater lagoons of Mayakoba, offering direct access to the Caribbean coast. Key resort features include signature Thai dining at Saffron and holistic treatments at the Banyan Tree Spa.
What wellness experiences can guests enjoy at Banyan Tree Mayakoba?
Banyan Tree Mayakoba offers a variety of rotating wellness practices, including Sunrise Yoga, Mindful Pilates, UP Core & Balance, SUP (Stand-Up Paddleboard) Stretching, Shinrin Yoku (forest bathing), and Duo Yoga, all hosted in serene natural settings.
