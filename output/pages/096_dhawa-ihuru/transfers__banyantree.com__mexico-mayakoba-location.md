Points of interest
Tulum Archaeological Zone
73 km | 1h 10 min, taxi and tour operator
Considered one of Mexico's most scenic archaeological sites, the emblematic Maya ruins of Tulum rise abruptly atop a 12-metre cliff from which the turquoise tones of the Caribbean Sea can be contemplated in all their splendour. With more than 60 restored temples and castles, El Castillo, with its peculiar location on the cliff, remains the most photographed site in the Riviera Maya. A place that sets the scene for mind-bending discoveries.
Chichen Itza
186 km | 2.5 hours, taxi and tour operator
Experience unequaled sensations of the ancient Maya world where history and culture come together in one of the New Seven Wonders of the World.
5th Avenue
11 km | 18 min
Stretching over a five-kilometer long pedestrian-only walkway, 5th Avenue (Quinta Avenida) is considered the beating heart of Playa del Carmen, with so much to do and see - from bustling bars and restaurants to clothing shops and live comic book characters.
