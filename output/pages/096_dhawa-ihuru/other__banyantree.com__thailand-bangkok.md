Banyan Tree Bangkok
Bangkok, Thailand
A Living Urban Sanctuary in Sathorn
Discover Bangkok through its flavours, neighbourhoods and river life at Banyan Tree Bangkok, a living urban sanctuary in Sathorn. Our five-star hotel is close to Silom and Lumphini Park, with convenient access to Lumphini MRT and Sala Daeng BTS stations. Spacious rooms and suites, together with an outdoor pool overlooking the city, offer a comfortable base for exploring at your own pace.
Experience Bangkok’s cultural heritage at the Grand Palace and Wat Arun, or wander through Chinatown. For shopping in Bangkok, explore nearby Central Park Bangkok and One Bangkok, or visit ICONSIAM for shopping and dining beside the Chao Phraya River.
Make time for yourself at Banyan Tree Spa, where massages and body treatments draw on Asian wellness traditions. Attentive care brings a personal dimension to your stay, with space to reconnect with yourself.
Thai cuisine is part of the journey, with Saffron Signature Thai Restaurant and Saffron Grill in our dining collection. Share an evening on the Chao Phraya River with a Bangkok dinner cruise aboard Apsara Cruise or Saffron Cruise, discovering another perspective on the city.
Above the city, Vertigo, our open-air rooftop restaurant, sits alongside Moon Bar, our rooftop bar on the 61st floor. Explore our dining pages to find the experience that speaks to you.

To ensure a seamless arrival experience, we recommend submitting the TDAC within

What privileges are included with Club Lounge access?
The Club Lounge on the 19th floor of Banyan Tree Bangkok is accessible to guests staying in eligible Club rooms and suites. Privileges include express check-in and check-out, welcome herbal drinks, daily buffet breakfast, afternoon tea, light snacks, free-flow local beer, evening happy hour with canapés, two pieces of complimentary laundry/pressing daily, and up to three hours of boardroom usage per stay (for up to eight guests, subject to availability).
Are two-bedroom suites available for families traveling together?
The Two-Bedroom Serenity Suite at Banyan Tree Bangkok spans 119–143 sq m and comfortably accommodates up to four guests. It features one king bedroom, one twin bedroom, two en-suite bathrooms, a shared living space, and a kitchenette. The suite also includes full Club Lounge privileges, making it ideal for families or group travelers.
What signature holistic treatments are offered at Banyan Tree Spa Bangkok?
Signature holistic journeys at Banyan Tree Spa Bangkok include Royal Banyan, which blends Eastern and Western massage with a warm herbal pouch; Tropical Rainmist, combining therapeutic stretching, a Thai herbal wrap and Ginger Bath; Thai Rattanakosin, with gentle exfoliation and traditional Thai oil massage; and the time-honoured Thai Classic massage. Treatments draw on ancient Asian healing and fresh, natural ingredients.
Does the outdoor pool deck offer skyline views of the city?
Yes, Banyan Tree Bangkok features a 4 x 25-meter outdoor swimming pool on the 21st floor, surrounded by tropical greenery and city views. Open daily from 6:00 AM to 8:00 PM, the pool area offers a juice bar alongside complimentary poolside water and fresh fruit.
Which BTS SkyTrain and MRT subway stations are closest to the hotel?
The nearest MRT subway station to Banyan Tree Bangkok is Lumphini Station, while the nearest BTS SkyTrain station is Sala Daeng Station. The hotel also operates a complimentary scheduled tuk-tuk shuttle service connecting guests to nearby BTS and MRT stations (timetables available with the concierge).
How long is the drive from Suvarnabhumi Airport to the property?
Suvarnabhumi International Airport (BKK) is located approximately 35 km from Banyan Tree Bangkok. The drive by car, taxi, or private hotel limousine typically takes 45 to 60 minutes, depending on traffic conditions.
Where does the Saffron Cruise depart from on the Chao Phraya River?
The Saffron Cruise dinner cruise operates on the Chao Phraya River departing from ICONSIAM. Check-in opens at ICONSIAM Pier 1 at 6:30 PM, followed by boarding at Pier 2 at 7:00 PM for the 7:00 PM – 10:00 PM dinner cruise. Guests are advised to allow extra travel time to reach ICONSIAM Pier during peak hours.
