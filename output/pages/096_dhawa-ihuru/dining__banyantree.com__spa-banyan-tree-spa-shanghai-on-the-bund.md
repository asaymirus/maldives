Receive a detailed consultation conducted by our skillful master therapist, and experience a carefully curated treatment featuring a repertoire of signature massage techniques as well as hot and cold treatments.

A medium to strong massage, the therapist applies thumb and palm pressure with slow, firm, rhythmic strokes. Their techniques uplift the senses and ease muscle tension, leaving you fully invigorated.

Royal Banyan delivers the best of Eastern and Western massage techniques. Featuring a warm herbal pouch dipped in sesame oil, this well-loved treatment improves circulation and eases muscle aches.
