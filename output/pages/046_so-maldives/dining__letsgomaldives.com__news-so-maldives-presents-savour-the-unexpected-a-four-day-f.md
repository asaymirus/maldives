SO/ Maldives, the island’s fashion-forward luxury resort celebrated for its bold design and avant-garde spirit, is preparing to deliver one of the most dynamic culinary events of the year. In an exclusive collaboration, the resort welcomes Chef Kareem Atef – award-winning chef, global TV personality, Africa Cup culinary champion, and master of modern fusion cuisine – for a four-day gastronomic takeover titled “Savour the Unexpected: Fusion, Fire & Flair.”
Known for blending art, science, and storytelling on the plate, Chef Kareem brings an impressive background:
Two-time Africa Cup Champion (2022 & 2024)
Harvard-trained in Science & Cooking
Recognised by S.Pellegrino Young Chef Academy
Top Chef Middle East Season 3 & All Stars alum
His boundary-pushing style, refined techniques, and sensory-driven approach make him a natural creative match for SO/ Maldives – a resort that thrives on disruptive luxury, expressive dining, and experiences that challenge convention.
A Four-Day Journey of Fusion, Theatre, and Tropical Energy
For four days, SO/ Maldives transforms into a vibrant culinary stage where flavour meets fashion, and dining becomes both performance and discovery. Each event invites guests to engage, explore, and taste in new and unexpected ways.
29 November 2025 | 18:30–22:00
Five-Course Signature Dinner at Hadaba
The programme begins with an elevated dining experience at Hadaba. Chef Kareem merges Middle Eastern roots with global influences, presenting bold contrasts, expressive techniques, and storytelling woven through every course.
30 November 2025 | 15:00–16:00
Interactive Cooking Class at The Citronelle Club
Guests are invited behind the scenes of fusion gastronomy. Chef Kareem leads a creative, hands-on class where participants learn techniques, experiment with flavours, and gain rare insight into his artistic process.
01 December 2025 | 18:30–22:00
Four-Course Beach Dinner at Lazuli Beach Club
A barefoot luxury experience with a dramatic beachside ambience. Fire, movement, and flavour take centre stage as Chef Kareem crafts a theatrical sunset dinner infused with island energy.
02 December 2025 | 19:00–22:00
Grand Finale Dinner Buffet at The Citronelle Club
The residency concludes with a vibrant buffet created by Chef Kareem and the resort’s culinary team – a colourful fusion of global traditions, tropical flair, and signature SO/ attitude.
