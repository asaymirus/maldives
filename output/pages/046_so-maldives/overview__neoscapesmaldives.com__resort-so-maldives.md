SO/ Maldives – A Bold Expression of Island Luxury
A vibrant escape set in the turquoise Emboodhoo Lagoon, SO/ Maldives reimagines the Maldivian getaway with contemporary style, artistic design, and effortless sophistication. Just 15 minutes by speedboat from Malé International Airport, the resort features 80 designer beachfront and over-water villas, each with panoramic ocean views, open-plan living spaces, and private pools that open directly to the lagoon. Guests can unwind at the serene Wellness Camp with a hammam, spa, and fitness sphere or dive into adventure with thrilling water sports. Dining unfolds in three vibrant acts: Asian-inspired creations at The Citronelle Club, Mediterranean flavours at Lazuli Beach Club, and Levantine cuisine with endless ocean views at Hadaba. From sun-drenched beaches and a mosaic-tiled pool to secret lawns, jungle speakeasies, and floating pontoons, every corner invites discovery. With personalised butler service, seamless transfers, and a fashion-forward spirit woven into every experience, SO/ Maldives is more than a resort, it is a celebration of creativity, energy, and modern island living.

Dining
Lazuli Beach Club
From sunrise to sunset, Lazuli Beach Club is the resort’s vibrant social hub, blending Mediterranean flavours, creative cocktails, and chilled-out tunes in a chic, art deco-inspired setting. Set by the beach and pool, it offers a relaxed yet sophisticated atmosphere perfect for sharing drinks with friends or enjoying à la carte favourites like grilled seabass, vegetable skewers, and themed barbecues. Open from 10am to midnight, it’s where culture, cuisine, and coastal charm come together.
The Citronelle Club
The Citronelle Club is a beachfront all -day dining venue that blends Pan Asian flavours with European flair in a bold, modern, and Zen-inspired setting. Guests can enjoy a sensory dining experience filled with the sounds of sizzling woks and freshly prepared dishes. The menu celebrates Asia’s diverse culinary traditions, featuring signature items like blackened cod, sweet and sour lamb ribs, and delicate dim sum, alongside lighter options such as rocket summer rolls. Herb-infused cocktails and crisp wines complement the vibrant flavours, creating a relaxed yet refined dining atmosphere from breakfast through dinner.
Hadaba
Perched at the edge of the resort, Hadaba is a two-story Middle Eastern restaurant that celebrates the rich flavours of the Levant. Drawing inspiration from regions across the Gulf of Arabia to the Mediterranean, it offers an authentic and elevated dining experience. A sculptural central staircase connects the two levels, framing breathtaking views of the Maldivian sea. On the menu, guests can savour freshly baked Laffa bread from the showpiece custom Saj, daily ceviche specials, and the signature slow-cooked Lamb Shawarma, all crafted to capture the essence of Middle Eastern warmth and hospitality.
Pavilion Bar
The Pavilion Bar is the ideal spot to unwind and watch the sunset, offering drinks inspired by Levant flavours with a Mediterranean twist. The menu features a curated list of champagnes by the glass and bottle, perfect for toasting to the Maldivian sunset. Cocktails are crafted using tropical fruits, aromatic herbs, and Middle Eastern spices, with signature creations served from a table-side cocktail cart for a personalised touch. Guests can also enjoy classic whites and rosés from France alongside timeless old-world favourites in a relaxed island setting.
In-Villa Dining & Meal Experiences
Savour the finest flavours in the comfort of your private villa with our bespoke in-villa dining experiences. From indulgent floating breakfasts drifting across your pool to intimate candlelit dinners under the stars, every meal is a celebration of taste and tranquillity. Choose from curated meal plans designed for every kind of traveller, whether you prefer gourmet dining at every meal or a more relaxed, flexible experience. With exquisite ingredients, impeccable service, and a touch of Maldivian magic, every bite becomes an unforgettable moment.
Private Dining
Experience the art of romance with a bespoke private dining experience by the ocean. Surrounded by candlelight and the sound of gentle waves, indulge in a personalised feast crafted exclusively for you in an intimate and unforgettable setting.

Highlights From The Resort
Lazuli Beach Club
A vibrant beach and poolside venue where Mediterranean-inspired cuisine, creative cocktails, and chilled-out tunes create a lively yet sophisticated atmosphere from sunrise to sunset.
The Link
A stylish over-water walkway offering scenic ocean views, perfect for leisurely strolls or pausing to enjoy the serene surroundings.
Wellness Camp
A tranquil retreat featuring a Vichy rainforest shower, steam and sauna, organic treatment rooms, and a state-of-the-art fitness sphere overlooking the ocean. Guests can enjoy spa treatments, holistic wellness rituals, yoga, and mindful workouts.
The Nest – Kids Club
A magical space where children explore, play, and learn in a safe, supervised environment. The Nest offers themed weekly activities, interactive learning zones, arts and crafts workshops, cooking classes, and outdoor adventures. Kids can experiment with tie-dye, pottery, and creative projects or enjoy mini-spa and salon treatments designed just for them. The expert team ensures every day is filled with fun, creativity, and discovery while parents relax nearby.
Water Sports & The Zone
Thrill-seekers can dive into a range of ocean adventures. Snorkel or dive among vibrant coral reefs, swim with stingrays and nurse sharks, try high-speed jet skiing, or paddleboard across turquoise waters. Guests can also experience hydro-flying with the X-Jetblade or embark on PADI-certified diving journeys. Teens and young adults can enjoy The Zone, featuring a game room, outdoor deck, and ocean-inspired activities.
Island Discoveries
Explore local islands, join nature walks, or participate in creative workshops such as pottery and tie-dye. Guests can enjoy barefoot beach cinema, curated retail, and professional photography to capture memorable moments.
Sustainability
SO/ Maldives blends eco-luxury with conscious practices, including solar-powered villas, zero-waste dining, vegan amenities, and partnerships that upcycle waste.
Serenity & Adventure
From rejuvenating spa treatments to thrilling water sports and immersive island experiences, SO/ Maldives offers a perfect balance of relaxation, play, and exploration for every guest.
