DESCRIPTION
Where island magic meets unmatched luxury.
Just 25 minutes by luxury catamaran from Malé, OZEN Reserve Bolifushi is a private island paradise where barefoot charm blends with refined indulgence. Stay in elegant beach villas and overwater villas, with playful slides offering a joyful plunge into the turquoise lagoon. The exclusive RESERVE™ Plan elevates every stay with fine dining, premium beverages, and curated experiences seamlessly included.
Spend your days exploring vibrant marine life, skating on the Maldives’ first ice rink, or simply unwinding at the award-winning spa. Families can delight in one of the largest kids’ clubs in the Maldives, while couples discover moments of pure serenity. With a personal Hiyani butler attending to every detail, OZEN Reserve Bolifushi ensures your escape is effortless, luxurious, and unforgettable.

Dining
Ozar
Set beneath swaying palms by the main pool, Ozar is the island’s vibrant social hub. Sip premium cocktails, enjoy barista-crafted coffee, or savour high tea as you relax on a sun lounger. As the sun sets, the atmosphere transforms with an international DJ, signature sundowners, and chilled-out beach vibes. From molecular mixology to fresh juices, there’s something for every taste throughout the day.
Origine Restaurant
Experience a multi-sensory culinary journey where each dish is thoughtfully crafted using the freshest gourmet ingredients. From refined flavours to elegant presentation, every element is designed to delight. Explore the Wine Reserve and Cigar Lounge, featuring over 2,000 bottles and 350 labels from prestigious vineyards across the globe. Let the resident sommelier guide you through perfect pairings for a truly memorable dining experience.
Vista Del Mar
Enjoy vibrant, all-day dining with world cuisine subtly infused with Spanish flair. From live cook-off stations and sushi to fragrant curries, fresh pasta, and dim sum, the variety caters to every palate. Discover gourmet cheeses, cold cuts, and decadent pastries in the cold section, or indulge in the popular steam section. With chic décor and panoramic lagoon views, every meal here is a sensory delight.
Saffron
Embark on a regal culinary journey inspired by the rich heritage of Indian cuisine and the golden essence of saffron. Set in an elegant beachside garden with open-air cabanas, this immersive dining experience features a three-course, pre-plated Thali dinner served with Moët & Chandon. Savor historical saffron-infused dishes while live music adds to the charm of this refined tribute to India’s timeless culinary traditions.
Sangu Beach Restaurant
Discover the rich flavours of the Middle East at Sangu Beach, where Modern Levant and Moroccan cuisine are served in a chic beachfront setting. Enjoy mezze platters, slow-cooked tagines, and grilled meats prepared with bold spices and regional flair. With indoor and outdoor seating, show kitchens, and views of the ocean, dining at Sangu Beach is a sensory journey through the heart of Arabic culinary tradition.
Sangu Bar
As the sun sets, Sangu Bar comes alive with vibrant energy and exotic charm. After 9 PM, this adults-only space transforms into a lively lounge with DJs spinning Arabic and Latino beats and captivating belly dance performances. Sip on handcrafted cocktails infused with Middle Eastern flavours, enjoy expertly prepared shisha by Nu-Air, and relax with a cup of authentic Moroccan coffee in this stylish beachside escape.
Soyi
Savour the bold and diverse flavours of Pan-Asian cuisine at Soyi. The menu brings together signature dishes from Chinese, Thai, Japanese, Indonesian, and Malaysian kitchens, elevated with unique spice blends and traditional techniques. Each plate is thoughtfully crafted to offer a rich culinary journey through Asia in the heart of the Maldives.
Dine by Design
Create unforgettable moments with personalised dining experiences set in the island’s most enchanting locations. Whether it’s a sunrise breakfast on the beach, a private sandbank picnic, or a starlit dinner at your villa, enjoy cuisine from Bolifushi’s finest restaurants, all served by your dedicated Hiyani butler. Every detail is tailored just for you.

Highlights From The Resort
Endless Leisure and Adventure
At Ozen Reserve Bolifushi, every moment is designed for relaxation, exploration, and well-being. Dive into a world of excitement with water sports, private yacht charters, and semi-submarine adventures. Enjoy access to a state-of-the-art overwater gym, eco-friendly open-air ice rink, cooking classes, and daily excursions. Guests can choose from motorised or non-motorised water sports, including kayaking, jet skiing, wakeboarding, and big game fishing. For underwater enthusiasts, the PADI-certified dive centre offers courses, expeditions, and access to famous reef sites.
Serenity at the Spa
Reconnect with yourself at our tranquil spa inspired by the five elements of nature. Treatments feature 100% organic products and include rituals such as Marma massages, Ayurvedic therapies, body scrubs, and facials. Choose to relax in garden sanctuaries or overwater spa villas, with options for individuals and couples. Signature rituals are designed to nourish, balance, and energise.
Family Fun and Discovery
The Kuda Koli Kids Klub and Teen Lounge offer a variety of creative and educational activities, from pizza making to island treasure hunts. Babysitting is available on request. Families can enjoy island-wide biking, pool games, and wildlife spotting together.
Romantic Moments and Fresh Flavours
Celebrate love with beachside ceremonies or weddings in our overwater pavilion. Experience farm-to-table dining with fresh produce from the resort’s Chef’s Garden. Meet chefs as they handpick herbs and prepare zero-mile gourmet dishes.
Marine Conservation and Diving
With a vibrant house reef and guidance from our resident marine biologist, guests can engage in coral planting, snorkelling tours, and marine education. Best Dives Bolifushi offers unforgettable journeys through underwater caves, wrecks, and thriving coral ecosystems.
