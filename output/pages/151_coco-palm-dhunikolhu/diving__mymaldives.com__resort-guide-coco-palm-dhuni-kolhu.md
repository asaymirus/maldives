Overlooking a breathtaking lagoon on the exquisite private island of Coco Palm Dhuni Kolhu, this unique island has been developed in complete harmony with nature to provide unrivalled luxury for the discerning traveller. The perfect honeymoon hideaway, individual villas and suites open directly on to the garden and beach front or built on stilts over the lagoon. Wood, rattan and cotton furnishing creates an atmosphere of sophisticated elegance.
