DESCRIPTION
A sanctuary where two cultures meet in harmony and luxury unfolds naturally.
Where Thai grace meets Maldivian paradise. Nestled in the UNESCO Biosphere Reserve of Baa Atoll, Dusit Thani Maldives blends elegant Thai artistry with contemporary island luxury. The resort’s 94 villas and residences offer serene comfort surrounded by turquoise lagoons and a vibrant house reef. Guests can unwind in treetop treatment pods at Devarana Wellness, swim in one of the Maldives’ largest infinity pools, or indulge in world-class dining experiences inspired by global flavours. Encircled by coconut palms and coral blues, Dusit Thani Maldives is a sanctuary of culture, tranquillity, and timeless sophistication.

Highlights From The Resort
Island & Nature Experiences
Set within the UNESCO Biosphere Reserve of Baa Atoll, Dusit Thani Maldives offers an immersive natural escape. Guests can explore the island on guided walks, discover hidden gems, and participate in conservation programmes such as tree planting and reef cleaning. Local island tours and hands-on workshops, including traditional crafts and papermaking, provide insight into Maldivian culture.
Water & Marine Activities
Experience the Maldives’ vibrant underwater world with expert-led scuba diving and snorkelling. Seasonal visits to Hanifaru Bay reveal majestic manta rays and whale sharks. Thrill-seekers can enjoy parasailing, jet skiing, or wakeboarding, while kayaking, paddleboarding, and sailing offer a gentler connection with the crystal-clear waters. Sunset dolphin cruises and private sandbank trips complete the ocean adventures.
Wellness & Spa
Devarana Spa provides a serene sanctuary with elevated treatment villas among the palm trees. Guests can indulge in massages, facials, and body treatments, or join workshops such as floating sound baths and personalized wellness product sessions. Yoga, meditation, and mindful therapies create balance for both body and mind.
Family & Kids
The Baan Sanook Kids Club offers outdoor play areas and a variety of creative activities, from arts and crafts to pool games and culinary workshops. Special events like bubble parties and family festivals ensure fun for all ages while parents enjoy ultimate relaxation.
Dining & In-Villa Comforts
Savor global cuisines at The Market, Thai specialties at Benjarong, seafood and steaks at Sea Grill, or cocktails at Sala and Sand Bar. Villas and residences offer modern luxury with private pools, outdoor rain showers, elegant Thai-inspired interiors, minibars, 55″ LED TVs with Bose sound systems, and dedicated butler service.
