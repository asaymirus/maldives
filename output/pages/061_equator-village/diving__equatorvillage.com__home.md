All you need to know
Once you land at Gan International Airport, it's a mere 5 minutes van ride to Equator Village.
If you're arriving at Velana International Airport, you’ll need to take a 90-minute domestic flight to Gan International Airport, followed by a short 5-minute van ride to reach Equator Village.
However, if you arrive directly at Gan International Airport, it’s just a quick 5-minute ride by van to the property.
Equator Village is situated on Gan Island, in Addu, the Southernmost atoll of the Maldives.
Yes, upon exiting the arrival hall at Velana International Airport, please proceed to the resort booths visible upon your exit. 
Our airport representative will welcome you at the Kaimoo Resorts and Hotels Booth A10 and assist you in reaching the domestic terminal for your flight to Gan. 

At Gan International Airport, a representative will be waiting for your arrival at the exit.
