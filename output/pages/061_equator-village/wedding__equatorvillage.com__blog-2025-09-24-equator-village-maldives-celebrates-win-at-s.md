Equator Village Maldives Celebrates Win at South Asian Travel Awards 2005
We are delighted to share that Equator Village Maldives has been recognised at the South Asian Travel Awards (SATA) 2025, winning the silver award for Leading Heritage Hotel/Resort.
The South Asian Travel Awards is one of the region’s most prestigious platforms, honouring outstanding achievements in travel, tourism, and hospitality. Being celebrated at this stage not only puts Equator Village on the regional map but also motivates us to continue raising the bar for authentic Maldivian hospitality.
This achievement is a proud moment for our team and a testament to the dedication and heart that go into making every guest experience at Equator Village truly memorable.
A Recognition of Heritage and Hospitality
Located on the southernmost tip of the Maldives in Addu Atoll, Equator Village is not just a resort but a place with a story. Once part of a British Royal Air Force base, the property has retained its colonial charm while embracing lush tropical gardens, tranquil seaside views, and the warmth of Maldivian hospitality.
A Thank You to Our Guests and Team
This recognition would not have been possible without our incredible team members, whose hard work and passion shine through every day, and our valued guests, who continue to inspire us with their love and support.
As we celebrate this milestone, we remain committed to offering guests an experience that blends history, culture, and the serenity of island life. From first-time visitors to long-time friends, every guest is welcomed with the same dedication and warmth that define the spirit of Equator Village.
