November 27
th
of 2017 marked a special day for Equator Village Maldives as they won the “ Leading Airport & City Hotel” Award at the annual Maldives Travel Awards.
“I’m delighted to receive this award on behalf of Equator Village Maldives. After recent improvements, the resort is looking better than ever and I’m delighted our work has been recognized with this honor.” Manager of Equator Resort, Mohamed Waheed expressed his excitement at the award ceremony held at Adaaran Select Hudhuranfushi.
Located in the Gan Island, Addu, the resort has recently been refurbished and includes of 78 spacious garden bungalows, a restaurant, poolside bar and a large swimming pool overlooking the ocean. Located in what used to the British Royal Air Forces Sergeant’s Mess, the hotel’s grounds still retain a unique colonial charms with large trees, manicured lawns and English-style flower gardens.
