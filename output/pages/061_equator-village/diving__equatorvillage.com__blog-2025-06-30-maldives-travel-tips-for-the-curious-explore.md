Maldives Travel Tips for the Curious Explorer
Tucked away in the far south of the Maldives lies a different kind of island escape. At Equator Village, the days are filled with adventure and the land echoes with history. If you’re planning your first visit, or simply craving a more grounded
experience of the Maldives
, these travel tips will help you settle in and soak up the soul of Addu Atoll.
1. Getting Here
Equator Village sits on Gan Island in Addu Atoll, once home to a British RAF base and now a vibrant corner of the Maldives full of character and charm. Getting here means hopping on a domestic flight from Malé to Gan, a scenic 90-minute ride that sweeps you over glittering atolls. It’s the perfect introduction to the magic of the south.
2. Pack for Island Life (and a Little Adventure)
Leave the formalwear behind. Pack light, breathable clothing, reef-safe sunscreen, your favourite swimwear, and comfy footwear for cycling and exploring. Whether you’re hopping on a dolphin cruise or discovering hidden relics, you’ll want to be ready for whatever the day brings.
3. Dress Respectfully, Explore Openly
One of the best parts of visiting Addu is getting to
meet the locals
. Whether you’re island hopping or exploring old ruins, modest clothing is appreciated. And here’s a tip: smile, ask questions, and be curious. The people of Addu love sharing stories and traditions.
4. Don’t Just Relax — Roam
In Addu, the real beauty lies in the mix of nature and nostalgia. Cycle across historic causeways, snorkel thriving coral reefs, or explore lush wetlands. Equator Village is all about discovery, whether it’s underwater, on land, or in conversation.
5. Embrace the Rhythm
Island time is a real thing, and it’s wonderful. Slow down, take in your surroundings, and let each moment unfold naturally. Watch dolphins at sunset, sip tea by the sea, and leave space in your itinerary for the unexpected, because the most memorable moments here often happen by surprise.
