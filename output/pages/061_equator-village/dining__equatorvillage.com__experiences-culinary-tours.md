Culinary Tours
Study the culinary traditions of Addu by sampling exclusive flavours of traditional home-cooked dishes. Experience the warmth of dining with a local family, sharing recipes and stories that reveal the heart and soul of the region. This unique dining encounter offers a delightful fusion of food and culture and a chance to create cherished relationships.
Taste the distinctness of the flavourful spice mix Addu Havaadhu, as it infuses its magic into the heart of your culinary journey. Bite into the transformation of fresh fish and delectable dishes, as the unique taste of this local specialty enhances your every bite.
