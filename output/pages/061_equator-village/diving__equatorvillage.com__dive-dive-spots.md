Dive Spots
Manta Point, also known as Muda Kan, is an exciting drift dive that begins outside the Maa Kandu channel and ends within the Atoll. The eastern side boasts a large plateau at around 22 metres deep, adorned with an array of table corals.
This area is frequently visited by massive manta rays, especially the Manta Alfredi species, some reaching up to 4 metres in size.
