Facilities
Enhance your holiday experience with our array of facilities, thoughtfully designed to complement and complete your stay, ensuring a truly memorable and fulfilling getaway
Swimming Pool
A place to socialise and unwind - our swimming pool at the heart of Equator village is the perfect vantage point to take in the resort.
Enjoy poolside cocktails while basking in the tropical sunshine, taking refreshing dips in between moments of pure bliss.
Entertainment
Our music offerings cater to a perfect holiday soundtrack, ensuring you find the backing to harmonise your stay.
Be captivated by the live performances of talented local musicians at our bar, gracing the stage once a week. Delight in the original island beat of the
boduberu
drums, a unique musical experience that shouldn't be missed during your stay.
Recreation
Put your skills to the test on our antique billiard table, a beautifully refurbished relic from wartime. Engage in a holiday competition with style, showcasing your prowess in this timeless game while enjoying a touch of nostalgia.
Visit our games area, offering ping pong, darts, and a wide selection of board games to add excitement to your nightly bar visits. Engage in friendly competitions, challenge your skills, and create memorable moments of fun and laughter during your stay.
GYM
Our gym is carefully crafted to ensure that you can effortlessly adhere to your fitness regime while relishing your time with us.
Whether you're a fitness enthusiast or simply looking to stay active during your vacation, we have  everything you need to achieve your wellness goals.
