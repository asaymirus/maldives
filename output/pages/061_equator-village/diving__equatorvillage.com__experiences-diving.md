Diving
A diver’s paradise nestled in the heart of the Maldives, offering some of the most captivating dive sites in the world. Addu is renowned for its pristine coral reefs that teem with life. Explore the kaleidoscope of colours as you drift past corals of all shapes and sizes.
One of the main draws of Addu Atoll is the resident manta ray population. Imagine being surrounded by these majestic creatures as they glide gracefully through the water. It’s an awe-inspiring experience you will cherish forever.
