An Exotic Eid Getaway Awaits at Equator Village
This Eid Al-Fitr, take a well-deserved break from the city and celebrate the holiday season surrounded by the beauty of the southern Maldives. At Equator Village, our special Eid offer invites locals and work permit holders to enjoy an exotic island retreat filled with comfort, nature, and warm Maldivian hospitality.
A Festive Escape in the Southern Maldives
Located on the lush island of Gan in Addu City, Equator Village offers a unique island experience quite different from the typical resort stay. With its tropical greenery, long cycling paths, and tranquil atmosphere, it’s the perfect setting to slow down and reconnect with nature.
Whether you are planning a quick getaway with friends, a couple’s retreat, or a small family break, Equator Village’s laid-back charm makes it easy to unwind and enjoy the simple pleasures of island life.
Special Eid Offer
For the travel period 10th – 31st March 2026, locals and work permit holders can enjoy a stay in a Standard Double Room for two adults at just USD 95 per night, including daily breakfast. This special package is designed to make your Eid holiday both relaxing and affordable, allowing you to focus on spending quality time with your loved ones.
Experience the Charm of Addu
Staying at Equator Village also gives guests the opportunity to explore the unique culture and landscapes of
Addu Atoll
