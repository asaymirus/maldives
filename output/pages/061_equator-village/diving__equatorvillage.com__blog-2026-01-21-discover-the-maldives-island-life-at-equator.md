Discover the Maldives’ Island Life at Equator Village
The beginning of a new year invites fresh perspectives, new journeys, and experiences that stay with you long after the calendar pages turn. If your resolution is to travel more meaningfully, an affordable Maldives holiday at Equator Village offers a refreshing alternative to the usual postcard version of the Maldives.
Located on Gan Island in Addu Atoll, Equator Village blends adventure, history, and local culture into a holiday that is both enriching and accessible.
Rich in History and Heritage
Equator Village
sits in one of the most historically significant regions of the Maldives. Addu Atoll was once a strategic British Royal Air Force base, and traces of this past remain woven into the landscape and architecture of Gan Island. Former military buildings have been thoughtfully repurposed, giving the resort its unique character and story.
Staying here means being part of living history. You can explore remnants of the island’s past, cycle along quiet roads that once served airstrips, and learn about how global events shaped this southern atoll.
Adventure Beyond the Beach
For travellers starting the year with a sense of adventure, Equator Village delivers in abundance. The calm, uncrowded surroundings make it ideal for exploration both on land and underwater.
Diving
reveals vibrant reefs, WWII shipwrecks, and diverse marine life, while cycling across Gan and neighbouring islands offers an intimate glimpse into everyday island life.
Culture, Community, and Connection
One of the most rewarding aspects of staying at Equator Village is the close connection to
local culture.
Visits to the islands of Addu Atoll offer insight into traditional Maldivian life, such as fishing, local crafts, and the rhythm of daily island living. It’s a reminder that travel can be both affordable and deeply enriching.
