An Exotic Festive Season Awaits at Equator Village
As the year draws to a close, Equator Village invites you to celebrate the festive season surrounded by tropical greenery, warm island hospitality and the exotic charm of Addu Atoll. Far from the crowds and clichés, the festive period at Equator Village is all about meaningful moments, simple pleasures and an easygoing island pace.
Christmas Eve & New Year’s Eve Celebrations
The highlights of the festive season are our special buffet dinners on Christmas Eve and New Year’s Eve, thoughtfully prepared to bring guests together in celebration. Expect a generous spread featuring traditional favourites alongside international flavours. Whether you’re toasting Christmas under the stars or counting down to the New Year, these evenings are designed to be warm, social and memorable.
An Exotic Festive Escape
Beyond the celebrations, the festive season at Equator Village is the perfect time to unwind and reconnect. Spend your days unwinding by the pool, cycling around the islands, or simply soaking in the spirit of Addu Atoll. Join a village tour, savour local cuisine, and experience a refreshing contrast to the usual holiday rush.
Adventures to Remember
For adventure seekers, the waters of Addu Atoll remain as inviting as ever. Known for year-round diving, the area offers vibrant reefs, fascinating marine life and historic wreck sites, making it an ideal festive-season escape for divers and snorkellers alike.
Celebrate Differently This Season
At Equator Village, the festive season is all about connection and authentic experiences. Whether you’re travelling as a couple, with family and friends or on a solo escape, indulge in a holiday that feels both special and effortless.
