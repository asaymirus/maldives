A Strategic Culinary Vision in the Baa Atoll
The Westin Maldives Miriandhoo Resort
has announced the appointment of
Alberto Burgio
as
Executive Chef

UNESCO Biosphere Reserve of the Baa Atoll
, the resort continues to strengthen its positioning by merging high-performance luxury with mindful, nutritional excellence.
For travel trade partners, Chef Alberto’s arrival reinforces the resort’s “Eat Well” pillar, offering a compelling narrative for
wellness-focused travelers
and
culinary enthusiasts

The Portfolio: Global Technique Meets Maldivian Soul
Chef Alberto, originally from Sicily, Italy, brings over a decade of experience from luxury hubs in the UAE, UK, and Italy. He now oversees the resort’s three signature dining venues, ensuring a diverse and high-fidelity guest experience.
