Experience Overview
Experience authentic Maldivian culture with a guided island-hopping excursion through Mulak (Meemu) Atoll. Visit local islands including Muli, Mulah, and Kolhufushi, explore traditional fishing communities, coral stone mosques, local workshops, and discover the rich heritage and everyday life of the Maldives beyond the resort.
