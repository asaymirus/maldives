Scuba Diving
Explore the spectacular underwater world of Meemu Atoll with the PADI 5-Star Dive Centre at Madifushi Private Island. Whether you're a beginner enjoying Discover Scuba Diving or earning Open Water, Advanced, Rescue, or Divemaster certification, experienced instructors guide every dive. Discover vibrant coral reefs and encounter white-tip reef sharks, hammerhead sharks, manta rays, eagle rays, sea turtles, hawksbill turtles, and seasonal whale sharks. From your Maldives overwater villa, the dive centre is just steps away, making it easy to dive straight into the crystal-clear waters of the Indian Ocean.
