Explore Somewhere Untainted
Madifushi Private Island is a five-star luxury resort in the Maldives, nestled in the pristine Meemu Atoll. The resort features 50 Maldives overwater villas, each with a private infinity pool and direct lagoon access. From a romantic overwater villa in the Maldives to a luxury water villa Maldives retreat, Madifushi offers an unforgettable private island escape.
Madifushi Private Island is a natured centric island, an unblemished serene coastal getaway for everyone ready to be relived. Its beauty lies in the untainted crystal blue waters blending as one with rich natural seagrasses and corals accompanied by wildlife, radiating soothing waves and air of purity.
