Excursions
Breathtaking Thulhagiri Island Journeys
A number of exciting excursions are scheduled weekly and whenever guests have had their fill of catching rays on the beach, they are welcome to sign up for an adventure at the reservations board at the reception.
Our Guest Experiences Team is here to arrange the ideal island adventure for you, whether you join a group excursion or opt for a private experience.
Male’ City Tour
Tuesday @ 13:30 Hrs – 17:00 Hrs. USD 71.00 per adult | Minimum 08 Pax| By Speed Boat
Explore capital city Male’ with a tour guide, a small standalone city with its compact population.The Islamic Mosque and Friday Mosque, fish market, Museum and Explore the souvenir shops, etc. =Thulhagiri to Male’ city by speedboat 25 minutes=
Snorkeling Tour
Monday, Wednesday & Friday  @ 09:30 Hrs – 11:30 Hrs. USD 36.00 per adult | Minimum 08 Pax | By Slow Boat
By Motorized local Dhoni, Explore the vast Indian ocean with its bountiful marine life, if you want to experience something more besides the island house reef it rides worth trying. (Thulhagiri to Snorkeling point 30-40 minutes).
Night Fishing Tour
Monday & Thursday @ 17:00 Hrs to 19:30 Hrs. USD 36.00 per adult | Minimum 08 Pax | By Slow Boat
By Motorized local Dhoni, just a few minutes before sunset the fish feast is on, bottom line fishing is the interest of Maldivians & tourists to catch a fish for the next day during dinner.
Sunset Dolphin
Wednesday & Saturday @ 16:00 Hrs to 18:00 Hrs. USD 46.00 per adult | Minimum 08 Pax | By Slow Boat
By Motorized local Dhoni, an hour before the sunset, we take you to see dolphins & click some open ocean sunset memories, who are abundant creatures of the Maldives ocean, seen in school & close encounters. In a calm, the excitement is much more enduring.
Sunrise Dolphin
Thursday @ 05:30 Hrs to 08:00 Hrs. USD 46.00 per adult | Minimum 08 Pax | By Slow Boat
By Motorized local Dhoni, 30 minutes before sunrise, we take you to see dolphins & click some open ocean sunrise memories, whom are abundant creatures of Maldives ocean, its seen in school & close encounters. In a calm, the excitement in much more enduring.
Fun Tour
Sunday @ 09:30 Hrs to 12:00 Hrs. USD 66.00 per adult | Minimum 08 Pax | By Speed Boat
By Speed boat explores some of the (1/2) special House reefs with bountiful marine life & High chances for turtles, and ray family. if you want to experience something more besides the island house reef rides is worth trying. (Thulhagiri to Snorkeling point 30-40 minutes).
Local Island Tour
Sunday @ 15:00 Hrs – 17:00 Hrs. USD 61.00 per adult | Minimum 08 Pax | By Slow Boat
Guests will take a 25-30 minute speedboat ride to a local island (K. Huraa or K. Himmafushi) with a population of 1000-1500, reliant on fishing and tourism. They can explore historical sites (school, lake, mosque, cemeteries) and local shops.
Island Glow Cruise
Tuesday @ 17:30 Hrs – 18:15 Hrs. USD 20.00 per adult | Minimum 08 Pax | By Slow Boat
Step aboard a traditional Maldivian dhoni for a 360° sunset cruise around Thulhagiri Island. Enjoy stunning sunset views, island scenery and the calming sway of the ocean—all to the rhythm of sunset vibe music Sip on a fresh Maldivian coconut and let the beauty of the evening unwind you!
– Meeting Point Reception (15 Minutes before Departure).
– All trips are subjected to weather conditions.
– Prices are subject to change or Excursions may cancel without prior notice due to bad weather or insufficient no.of pax reserved for the trip.
– Don’t forget to bring your sun protection lotion, towels, snorkeling equipments, money for shopping, and for sure the camera to keep those memories alive long after you leaves us. A little tip for the Boat crew would be nice as well.
– While we go to a local island please cover your shoulders and wear shorts or a skirt no shorter than knees.
– Children from the age 2 – 11.99 will get 50% Discount from the above mentioned rate.
– 50% charges apply when cancelled 24 hours prior to excursion.
