Thulhagiri Island Resort is a popular vacation spot located in the North Male’ Atoll of the Maldives. This small island is bordered by stunning turquoise waters and has a gorgeous white sandy beach, making it a picturesque destination for travelers searching for a calm and secluded getaway.
The resort is well-known for its dense and lush palm trees, which provide plenty of shade for guests wishing to avoid the sun. Visitors can take walks along the island’s sandy beaches or relax in a beach chair while taking in the breathtaking views of the Maldivian Ocean.
Thulhagiri Island is an excellent vacation destination for individuals seeking a tiny and private island. The resort’s 174 guest beds provide a more intimate and personalized experience for its visitors. The resort’s position, only 13 kilometers from the airport, makes it easily accessible for visitors to the Maldives.
Standard rooms, superior rooms, and water bungalows are among the options available to guests. All rooms are designed for comfort and luxury, with modern amenities such as air conditioning, private bathrooms, hot and cold water, mini bar and free Wi-Fi.
The resort also provides guests with a variety of activities such as snorkeling, diving, fishing, and water sports. Guests can explore the magnificent coral reefs surrounding the island by using the resort’s on-site PADI dive center.
The Island Resort also has a restaurant that provides international and regional cuisine. The “Sand Bar” and “Beach Bar” at the resort serve a variety of refreshing beverages, including unique cocktails that are ideal for sipping while watching the sunset.
Thulhagiri Maldives Resort is an idyllic resort for visitors wishing to enjoy the Maldives’ beauty and tranquillity. With its beautiful beaches, luscious palm trees, and variety of activities, it’s an ideal vacation destination for anyone wishing to get away from the hurry and bustle of daily life.
