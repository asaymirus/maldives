Thulhagiri Island Resort and Spa Maldives
Thulhagiri is situated in North Male’ atoll approximately 13 km from the airport and takes only 20 minutes by speed boat from Male' international airport.
Thulhagiri offers the perfect balance of an unspoiled tropical island with all the comforts.
Special Features
Barefoot concept Island.
Traditional Maldivian style design with Bamboo Furnitures.
Outdoor showers in all the Beach Villas.
Around the Island House reef for snorkelling.
Located just 20 Minutes by Speed Boat from the Male’ International Airport.
Home for thousands of birds (Budgies), wake up to the sound of their chimes.
Getting Here
Thulhagiri Island Resort and Spa is located in the Heart of North Male' Atoll, Republic of Maldives. 20Minutes Speedboat from Male' International Airport. Our boats are available to pick you up from the Airport at any time of the day. Our Transfers are combined with other guests.
International flight details must be provided 3 days prior to arrival for arranging the transfers to the resort. Failed to provide the flight information may result in paying for a private speed boat transfer.
Every effort will be made to arrange your resort transfer within 2 hours of arriving. Departure guests will be transferred to the airport at least 3 hours in advance of the scheduled international flight departure time.
Seasons
There are two distinctive seasons in the Maldives:
High Season
November to April is considered as the high season and often required to do advance bookings during this season to visit.
Low Season
May to November is considered as low season, you may find the best available rates with great offers during this season to visit.
