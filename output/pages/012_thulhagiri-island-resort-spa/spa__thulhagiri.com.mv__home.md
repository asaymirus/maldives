Thulhagiri Island Resort
Lovers Paradise
The island is situated in North Male’ atoll approximately 13 km from the airport and takes only 20 minutes by Speed Boat from Male' International Airport.
Thulhagiri offers the perfect balance of an unspoiled tropical island with all the natural beauty and comforts.
The Lover Paradise
This small island 31,544 sq meters(7.8  ac) is surrounded by a vast white sandy beach and is covered densely with many beautiful tall shady palm trees and vegetation filled with chiming birds.
This resort is ideal for those who are looking for a small private island at close proximity to Airport with just 50 Beach rooms and 17 Water bungalows & 20 Superior Water Bungalows.
