Hotel Policy
Thulhagiri Island is located a 20-minute Speed Boat journey from Male International Airport, International flight details must be provided 72 hrs prior to arrival for arranging the transfers to the hotel.
Every effort will be made to arrange your resort transfer within 2 hours. Departing guests will be transferred to the airport at least 3 hours in advance of the scheduled international flight departure time.
– Check-in time is at 02:00 pm and Check-out time is 12:00 Noon
– Rates are per room per night for double occupancy
– Rates are inclusive to 10% service charge, 17% GST and Government
– Green Tax of USD12.00 per person per night will be charged additional
– Additional beds: Maximum 01 (90*190cm)
Child Policy
0.0 to 1.99 years – Full complimentary transfer, baby cot and meals.
2.00 to 11.99 Years – 50% off adult seaplane transfers.
Transfers
Thulhagiri Island is located a 20-minute Speed Boat journey from Male International Airport. Return transfers are USD 150 net per adult and per Child USD 75.
Every effort will be made to arrange your resort transfer within 2 hours. Departing guests will be transferred to the airport at least 3 hours in advance of the scheduled international flight departure time.
