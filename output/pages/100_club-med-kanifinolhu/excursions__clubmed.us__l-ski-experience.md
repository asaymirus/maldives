Our Ski Resorts
La Rosière, France
Located in the Espace San Bernardo, a unique Franco-Italian ski resort offering 99 miles of interconnected slopes between France (La Rosière) and Italy (La Thuile), perfect for all skill levels.
Perched at 6,070 feet, it offers breathtaking views of the Tarentaise slopes and Mont Blanc, along with a blend of Savoyard and Aosta Valley culture. Guests can
unwind in a 7,000 sq ft spa
, enjoy
exceptional snow conditions
, and indulge in
local culinary traditions
. The family-friendly resort also features
43 exclusive suites
, making it an ideal year-round destination.
Val Thorens Sensations, France
Val Thorens, Europe’s highest ski resort, is part of the 3 Vallées, the world’s largest ski area with
372 miles of slopes
. Snow is guaranteed with 99% of slopes above 6,561 feet, and the
Cime de Caron offers stunning 360° views
.
The resort features a lively
après-ski scene with live music
,
new teen clubs
, and unique activities like an
indoor climbing wall
. It’s a vibrant destination for skiers and non-skiers alike, offering breathtaking alpine experiences.
Tignes, France
Tignes-Val d'Isère offers
186 miles of trails
and France’s longest ski season, with skiing available for up to 7 months. Known for its sporty atmosphere, it's perfect for adrenaline seekers with activities like
freestyle skiing, paragliding, ice diving, and speedriding
.
The resort has a dynamic calendar of events, major sports competitions, and wellness facilities, including the
largest indoor pool in the Alps
,
fitness programs, yoga, and a spa
. Ideal for families, Tignes offers cozy spaces, kids’ clubs, and a high-altitude Exclusive Collection Space with stunning views and concierge service for added comfort.
Val d'Isère, France
Located at
6,070 feet in the Tarentaise Valley
, this luxury resort blends authenticity with chic, sporty vibes. The Tignes-Val d'Isère ski area offers
186 miles of trails
, with
La Grande Motte reaching 11,368 feet
.
Guests can enjoy a large indoor pool, yoga, spa, and a market-style buffet, while personalized services like all-day room service and exclusive experiences elevate their stay. Off-ski activities include
snowshoeing and yurt dinners
, combining adventure with relaxation in a luxurious setting.

Frequently Asked Questions

1. What makes Club Med’s ski resorts ideal for a ski getaway?
Club Med’s ski resorts provide a blend of world-class slopes, family-friendly amenities, and luxuries that make your ski holiday unforgettable. Each resort offers all-inclusive packages that include lift passes, ski lessons, gourmet dining, wellness facilities, and tailored activities. Whether you prefer lively après-ski scenes, serene spa treatments, or thrilling off-ski adventures, Club Med’s destinations cater to every traveler’s needs.
Expand
:
2. Which Club Med ski resorts in France are best for all skill levels?
Club Med offers excellent ski resorts in France for beginners and advanced skiers alike, including:
La Rosière: A Franco-Italian resort with 99 miles of interconnected slopes and scenic views.
Val Thorens Sensations: Europe’s highest ski resort with guaranteed snow and diverse trails in Les 3 Vallées.
Tignes: Ideal for adrenaline seekers, offering freestyle skiing and other extreme activities.
Val d’Isère: A luxury retreat with chic vibes, high-altitude slopes, and off-ski adventures like snowshoeing and yurt dinners.
Each resort includes exclusive amenities like spas, kids' clubs, and gourmet dining spaces.
Expand
:
3. What does Club Med’s Snow Serenity guarantee offer?
Club Med’s Snow Serenity guarantee ensures your ski holiday won’t be disrupted by poor snow conditions. If 50% or more of your designated ski domain is closed for two consecutive days, Club Med will offer you a complimentary getaway to one of 13 Alpine resorts (subject to availability). This guarantee emphasizes Club Med’s commitment to delivering a seamless and extraordinary ski experience.
Expand
:
4. What unique experiences can guests expect from Club Med’s updated Serre Chevalier resort?
The redesigned Serre Chevalier resort offers upgraded amenities such as newly designed rooms, a Gourmet Club, a revamped restaurant, a rejuvenated bar, a new spa situated at the top of the resort, and transformed childcare areas. These enhancements make Serre Chevalier perfect for families and guests seeking modern comforts while enjoying the stunning Alpine setting.

5. What is the Chalet Experience offered at select Club Med ski resorts?
Club Med’s Chalet Experience provides exclusive services and amenities for a luxurious ski retreat. Guests enjoy a dedicated personal host for hassle-free service, WhatsApp communication, and tailored activities. Food and drink highlights include chalet dinners, cocktail hours, cozy breakfasts delivered to your chalet, and daily teatime. Ideal for families or groups, this experience ensures ultimate comfort and privacy during your mountain escape.
