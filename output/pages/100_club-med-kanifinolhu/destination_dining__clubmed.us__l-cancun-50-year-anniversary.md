The Pioneers of Paradise
1976​
Resort opened and became one of the first two resorts in Cancún, chosen by founder Gérard Blitz on a 22-acre Yucatán parcel.​
1990s ​
A high-energy, adult-focused destination with a social atmosphere, featuring communal dining, nightly shows, and beachside activities.​
2005-2006​
Renovated after Hurricane Wilma, and repositioned as a premier family-friendly all-inclusive resort.​
2017​
Exclusive Collection Space and all rooms renovated; new village center with Las Velas Bar, main pool renovation, and La Creperie added.​
2018​
Opened Taco Arte Beach Lounge and La Bodega wine cellar; main buffet renovated, Aguamarina extended, new boutique concept implemented.​

Discover a Destination of Wonders
Nature, Culture, and Heritage
Cancún is the gateway to the Yucatán destination, where nature, culture, and history meet in perfect harmony. Swim with sea turtles in the crystal-clear Caribbean waters, explore sacred cenotes hidden deep within the jungle, and discover the ancient city of Chichén Itzá, one of the world’s greatest archaeological wonders. Valladolid, a colorful colonial town, reveals local traditions, vibrant streets, and the authentic spirit of the region beyond the shoreline — a destination with far more to discover at every turn.​ ​ Explore the destination further and experience a region where every path leads to discovery.​
