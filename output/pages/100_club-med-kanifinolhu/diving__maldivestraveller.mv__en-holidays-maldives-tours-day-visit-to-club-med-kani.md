Tour full description
Spend a relaxing day at Club Med Kani, a family-friendly resort in North Malé Atoll. This all-inclusive Club Med Kani day pass includes return speedboat transfers, buffet lunch, unlimited selected alcoholic and non-alcoholic drinks, beach and swimming pool access, towels, shower facilities and selected resort activities.
Your tour begins with pickup from Malé, Hulhumalé, Velana International Airport or the Malé cruise ship pier. Upon arrival at the resort, you will be briefed about the available facilities, mealtimes and daily activity schedule.
Relax on the white-sand beach, swim in the clear lagoon or enjoy the freshwater pool overlooking the ocean. You may also join selected activities such as kayaking, sailing, stand-up paddleboarding, trapeze, badminton, beach volleyball, table tennis and scheduled resort entertainment. Activities are subject to weather conditions, the resort’s daily programme and availability.
Buffet lunch is served from 12:15 to 14:00. Unlimited selected alcoholic and non-alcoholic drinks are available from the bar during your visit. Poolside snacks and refreshments are also available until 11:00.
This Club Med Kani day tour is suitable for families, couples, solo travellers and cruise ship passengers who want to experience an all-inclusive Maldives resort without booking overnight accommodation. At the end of the tour, return to the resort jetty for the speedboat transfer back to your confirmed drop-off location.
Please note that Club Med Kani does not have an easily accessible house reef directly from the beach and is not recommended for guests primarily interested in snorkelling.

FAQ
What is included in the Club Med Kani day pass?
The day pass includes pickup and drop-off, return speedboat transfers, the resort entrance fee, buffet lunch, unlimited selected alcoholic and non-alcoholic drinks, beach and swimming pool access, towels, shower facilities and selected resort activities.
Where is pickup available?
Pickup and drop-off are available free of charge from Malé, Hulhumalé, Velana International Airport and the Malé cruise ship pier. Your confirmed pickup time will be provided after booking.
Is the Club Med Kani day pass suitable for cruise ship passengers?
Yes. Free pickup and drop-off can be arranged directly from the Malé cruise ship pier. Selected schedules are planned around cruise ship arrival, departure and last-tender times.
Are lunch and drinks included?
Yes. Buffet lunch is served from 12:15 to 14:00. Unlimited selected alcoholic and non-alcoholic drinks are available from the bar during the visit. Poolside snacks and refreshments are available until 11:00.
Which activities are included?
Selected activities may include sailing, kayaking, stand-up paddleboarding, trapeze, badminton, beach volleyball, table tennis, bocce and scheduled resort entertainment. Activities depend on weather conditions, the resort’s daily programme and availability.
Is snorkelling included in the day pass?
A dedicated snorkelling activity is not included as a standard part of this day pass. Club Med Kani does not have an easily accessible house reef directly from the beach and is not recommended for guests mainly interested in snorkelling.
Is the Club Med Kani day tour suitable for children?
Yes. Club Med Kani is a family-friendly resort, and the day tour is suitable for families with children.
Do I need to pay in advance?
No advance payment is required. You can book without providing credit card information and pay at the tour meeting point by cash or card. Online payment is also available after your booking is confirmed.
What happens if it rains on the day of my tour?
The tour operates in all weather conditions. Tropical showers in the Maldives are frequently brief, and the resort features plenty of covered lounge spaces, bars, and restaurants where you can relax. Because the speedboats are covered and safe, tours are not canceled due to rain unless the coast guard issues an official severe weather warning.
What should I bring for the Club Med Kani day pass?
Bring swimwear, sunscreen, sunglasses, a hat, suitable footwear and any personal medication you may need. Towels and shower facilities are included.
Who it is for
This tour is ideal for families, couples, solo travellers, cruise passengers and visitors staying in Malé, Hulhumalé or Velana International Airport. It is suitable for guests who want an all-inclusive resort experience without booking an overnight stay.
