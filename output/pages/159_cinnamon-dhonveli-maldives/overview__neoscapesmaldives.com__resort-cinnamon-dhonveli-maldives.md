Where Adventure Meets the Ocean
Just 20 minutes from Malé, Cinnamon Dhonveli invites you to a tropical escape of sun, surf, and serenity. Relax on its iconic white sand beach, ride the world-class waves at Pasta Point, or dive into vibrant coral reefs. Indulge at Chavana Spa, savour global and local flavours, and dance into the night. Stay in charming villas or overwater suites with endless ocean views. From dolphin cruises to island adventures, every moment blends excitement with relaxation in this unforgettable Maldivian paradise.

Dining
Tex Mex
Savor the vibrant flavours of Mexico and Spain at this à la carte restaurant. From zesty Surf & Turf Nachos to rich, spice-filled Enchiladas, every dish bursts with authentic taste. Pair your meal with a signature cocktail or specialty beer for the perfect evening after a sun-soaked day.
Beach Bar
Relax on sunbeds right on the sand while sipping expertly crafted cocktails. Whether you’re lost in a book or capturing the view for Instagram, the Beach Bar is the perfect spot to unwind and soak in the serene island vibes.
Koimala
Named after a revered Maldivian monarch, Koimala offers a grand buffet of international and local flavours, from Mediterranean and Asian delights to Maldivian favourites. With action stations, salad bars, desserts, and a special kids’ section, every meal is a feast for the senses in a vibrant, nature-inspired setting.
Raalhu Bar
Perched on a terrace overlooking the rolling waves at Pasta Point, Raalhu Bar is the ultimate surfers’ retreat. Sip cocktails, fresh juices, or international spirits while soaking in the laid-back vibe and ocean views. It is the perfect spot to unwind after a day on the waves.
Raiyvilla Bar
Located at the heart of the island, Raiyvilla Bar is the perfect spot to enjoy a drink while watching a breathtaking sunset. Relax with evening snacks, cocktails, or coffee and browse a hidden collection of books for your next holiday read.
Rehendhi Restaurant
Exclusively for guests of Overwater Suites and Beach Suites with Pool, Rehendhi Restaurant offers a chic setting with apple green and plum chairs. Enjoy a delicious buffet of international and local cuisine indoors or on the deck under the stars, complemented by stunning sea views from the nearby bar.
Dalchini
Named after the Hindi word for cinnamon, Dalchini celebrates India’s rich culinary heritage with an à la carte menu of tandoori favourites, coastal dishes, and bold spices. Overlooking the waves of Pasta Point, it offers an intimate and atmospheric dining experience full of flavour and tradition.

Highlights From The Resort
Activities & Excursions
Explore local islands, tour Male, or enjoy half-day island hopping. Watch dolphins, snorkel with turtles, try big game fishing, or take a sunset cruise. Unique experiences include photo flights and submarine tours.
Chavana Spa – Balinese Bliss
Rejuvenate with traditional Balinese massages, warm stone treatments, and Elemis products. Relax in double villas with baths and steam rooms or enjoy manicures and pedicures in the serene welcome lounge.
Surf & Marine Adventures
Ride Pasta Point waves, snorkel, dive, or try parasailing, flyboarding, and windsurfing. Lessons are available for all skill levels.
Entertainment & Fitness
Enjoy DJ nights, beach parties, and live music. Stay active with tennis, yoga, kayaking, or jet skiing, while kids have dedicated play areas.
