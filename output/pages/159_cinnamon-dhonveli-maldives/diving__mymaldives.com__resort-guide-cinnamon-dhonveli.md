Cinnamon Dhonveli Maldives is the perfect destination for the discerning pleasure seeker. The warm waters of the azure sea are an ideal balm that lifts the spirits and calms the soul. Whilst perfect waves make surfing an unforgettable experience, the clear waters allows you to sample the beauty of incredible underwater treasures.
At Dhonveli, the in-house experience is unparalleled. We have an array of fabulous options for you to select from. The choice of accommodation varies from garden bungalows to beach and water bungalows. But nevertheless, each option is unique and memorable.
The gastronomical experience of freshly caught sea food awaits you. Apart from that, we have a choice of culinary delights guaranteed to tickle your taste buds and leave you feeling sated.
Take time off to visit our Chavana spa. Let your mind experience the bliss of undisturbed peace as nimble hands work away the stress of another world. This is what relaxation is all about.
But that is not all. Our sundown entertainment includes disco nights, live music and fun themes. A fascinating Maldivian Cultural show will enthrall you.
This is a holiday experience that will remain with you forever.
This resort is formally known as Chaaya Island Dhonveli.
