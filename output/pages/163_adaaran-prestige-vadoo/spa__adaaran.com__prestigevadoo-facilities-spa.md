Mandara Luxury Spa
The Mandara luxury Maldives spa at Adaraan Prestige Vadoo offers a calm space for complete relaxation. Our spa has an experienced team of Balinese therapists and an extensive menu ranging from traditional aromatherapy massages to result-driven ELEMIS face and body treatments.
Taking advantage of its peaceful, soothing surroundings, the Mandara Spa is set over water, featuring a dedicated reception and retail area, two double rooms with shower, one double room with bath and a relaxation deck overlooking the ocean.

Spa Basics
Should I reserve my treatments?
Yes, either phone or visit the Spa to book a reservation at your earliest convenience so we may accommodate your schedule.
When should I arrive?
Please arrive at the Spa 15 minutes before the scheduled time to check-in and change.
What if I have special health considerations?
Please notify our Spa Manager or Receptionist before booking your treatment if you have high blood pressure, allergies, other physical ailments or disabilities, or if you are pregnant. If you have any concerns at all, let us know.
What do I wear during my treatment?
You may wish to wear your own bikini or briefs, or we can provide you with hygienic disposable briefs. Alternatively, treatments may be enjoyed without clothes. Choose what is most comfortable for you. Our therapists will always use draping techniques to respect your privacy.
What about my valuables?
Please leave valuables in the safe in your hotel room, as we do not assume any liability for personal items.
What if I’m late for my appointment?
Arriving late will simply limit the time for your treatment, thus lessening its effectiveness and your pleasure. Your treatment will end on time so that the next guest is not delayed.
What if I need to cancel a spa reservation?
The treatments you select are reserved especially for you. Guests will be charged 50% for treatments not canceled six hours in advance.
What about payment for spa services?
You may charge Spa services to your hotel bill.
We accept all major credit cards (Visa, Amex, MC, JCB)
