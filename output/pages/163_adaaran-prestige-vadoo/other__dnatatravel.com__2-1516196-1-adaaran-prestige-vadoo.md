Overview
Adaaran Prestige Vadoo is an adults-only sanctuary in the Maldives. Custom-made for romance and tranquillity, this Premium All-inclusive resort, located on the South Male Atoll, a 15-minute speedboat ride, offers bespoke amenities and personalized butler service for a secluded escape. Dive into your intimate getaway with direct sea access from each Water Villato swim alongside vibrant marine life.
The resort serves up a flavour explosion with three standout restaurants. Farivalhu Fine Dining Restaurant is where international dishes take center stage. Kithajima Japanese Restaurant delivers authentic Japanese delights, and Kakuni Barefoot Beach Restaurant specializes in mouthwatering Maldivian seafood. For a more laid-back vibe, Dhoni Bar and Kandolhi Bar offer light bites and refreshing drinks. With over 40 special beverages, 100+ local and international concoctions, and 20+ different teas, you will be sipping and savouring as you watch the sun say its goodbyes.
Indulge in a stay in one of our 50 uniquely designed villas, each offering a blend of elegance and comfort. Relax on your memory foam bed, draped in soft down comforters and luxurious Egyptian cotton sheets, and enjoy your favorite shows on the LED television. Each villa boasts a private, furnished patio where you can unwind and take in the serene surroundings. Pamper yourself in the private bathroom, which features both a jetted bathtub and a rainfall showerhead. Immerse yourself in the vibrant hues of your cozy living space, where the simplicity of barefoot living meets the joy of sun-soaked relaxation. The villas are adorned with rich teak wooden floors, plush beds, spacious sun decks, and inviting plunge pools that offer breathtaking views of the Indian Ocean.
Discover a world of relaxation and rejuvenation at the Mandara Spa, where you can indulge in soothing massages, revitalizing body treatments, and refreshing facials. After basking in the sun at our private beach, continue your day of leisure with a visit to our state-of-the-art health club or take a dip in the sparkling outdoor pool. The resort offers an array of amenities designed to enhance your stay, including, personalized butler services.
Adaaran Prestige Vadoo transcends the traditional resort experience, offering a sanctuary where love and connection thrive. Every detail, from the luxurious accommodations and exquisite dining to the invigorating spa treatments, is thoughtfully designed to elevate your shared journey and create unforgettable moments together.
