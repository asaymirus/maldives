Dhoni Resort Bar Maldives

Return later in the evening to watch the sky turn to shades of pink, orange and even red, as the sun sinks low over the Indian Ocean at our Maldives resort bar. Relax with a drink and enjoy a gentle Maldives breeze, meet new people, or take a sunset dip.
