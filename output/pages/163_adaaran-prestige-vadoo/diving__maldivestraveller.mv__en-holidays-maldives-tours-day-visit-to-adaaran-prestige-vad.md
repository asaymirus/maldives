Tour full description
Spend a relaxing day at Adaaran Prestige Vadoo, an adults-only private island resort just 15 minutes by speedboat from Male, Velana International Airport and the cruise ship pier. This all-inclusive day pass includes roundtrip transfers, buffet lunch, unlimited alcoholic and non-alcoholic drinks, beach access, pool access, Wi-Fi and shower facilities.
Enjoy the white sandy beach, turquoise lagoon and peaceful resort setting without booking an overnight stay. You can swim, relax by the pool, snorkel around the house reef, take photos near the water villas and enjoy a scenic escape from Male for the day.
The resort's exotic house reef boasts a treasure trove of colourful coral reefs, tropical fish, and diverse marine life. Snorkelling offers the perfect opportunity to discover this underwater paradise.
The resort is known for its clear lagoon, coral reef, tropical fish and calm island atmosphere. It is a good choice for couples, cruise ship passengers, airport layovers, short-stay visitors and adults looking for a private-island Maldives experience in one day.
Adaaran Prestige Vadoo is also a Green Globe Certified resort, recognised for its commitment to sustainable tourism and eco-friendly practices.
Children below 18 years old are not allowed on this island.

FAQ
Can children join this tour?
No. Adaaran Prestige Vadoo is a strictly adults-only private island resort. Guests must be 18 years or older to book or join this day visit.
How far is Adaaran Prestige Vadoo from Malé airport?
The resort is highly accessible, situated just a 15-minute speedboat ride from Malé City, Velana International Airport, and the main Cruise Ship Pier.
Are alcoholic drinks included in the day pass?
Yes. This all-inclusive package includes unlimited alcoholic and non-alcoholic cocktails, beer, wine, and spirits at the resort bar.
Is this day pass good for cruise ship passengers?
Yes. Cruise ship pier pickup and drop-off are available, and selected cruise ship schedules are listed on the page. If your cruise ship is not listed yet, you can contact us with your ship name, arrival time and departure time.
Do I need to bring snorkeling equipment?
Snorkeling is allowed around the island. Mask and snorkel rental may be available as a paid add-on. You can bring your own equipment if preferred.
Can I book now and pay later?
Yes. You can reserve the spot online without a credit card and pay at the meeting point before the tour begins.
What is included in the Adaaran Prestige Vadoo day pass?
The day pass includes pickup and drop off, roundtrip speedboat transfers, buffet lunch, unlimited alcoholic and non-alcoholic drinks, beach access, pool access, Wi-Fi, shower facilities and time to swim or snorkel around the island.
What should I bring for the Adaaran Prestige Vadoo day tour?
You can bring swimwear, sunscreen, sunglasses, a hat, suitable footwear and any personal medication you may need. Towels and shower facilities are included.
What happens if it rains on the day of my tour?
The tour operates in all weather conditions. Tropical showers in the Maldives are frequently brief, and the resort features plenty of covered lounge spaces, bars, and restaurants where you can relax. Because the speedboats are covered and safe, tours are not canceled due to rain unless the coast guard issues an official severe weather warning.
