Adults Only Premium All Inclusive Escape Overwater Exclusivity Awaits
Adaaran Prestige Vadoo is an intimate, adults only luxury retreat set over turquoise waters at the gateway to the South Malé Atoll. Renowned for its vibrant house reef and award winning overwater villas, the resort blends barefoot island living with refined indulgence. Private plunge pools, personal butler service and effortless access to the ocean create a sanctuary where romance, marine discovery and quiet luxury come together in perfect harmony.

Dining
Farivalhu Fine Dining Restaurant
Farivalhu presents an elegant fine dining experience overlooking the Indian Ocean. The menu features international cuisine with Asian influences and refined fusion dishes, complemented by the freshest seafood sourced daily from surrounding waters. Soft lighting, warm interiors and ocean views create a refined yet welcoming atmosphere, especially enchanting in the evening as music drifts across the moonlit sea.
Kithajima Japanese Restaurant
Unique in the Maldives, Kithajima is a dedicated Japanese specialty restaurant offering an authentic culinary journey. Enjoy expertly prepared sushi, sashimi, tempura and classic Japanese favourites made with premium seasonal ingredients. The intimate setting blends modern and traditional design, while sake and Japanese beers complete a dining experience that feels both refined and distinctive.
Dhoni Resort Bar
Open throughout the day, the Dhoni Bar is the perfect place to unwind after sun soaked hours or ocean adventures. Enjoy freshly brewed tea, tropical juices, signature cocktails or light refreshments in a relaxed setting. As evening approaches, the bar becomes a front row seat to vibrant sunsets, gentle sea breezes and easy conversations beneath changing skies.
Kandholi Overwater Sky Bar
Set just off the island, Kandholi Overwater Sky Bar offers a striking three sided deck above the ocean. Close to the water villas, it is ideal for sunset cocktails, stargazing and relaxed evenings over the sea. A curated selection of beverages and uninterrupted views make this a memorable spot day or night.

Highlights From The Resort
Parasailing
Soar high above the Indian Ocean and take in breathtaking aerial views of the Maldives. Guided by trained professionals, this exhilarating experience combines adventure with unforgettable scenery.
Whale Submarine Adventure
Explore the underwater world aboard the Whale Submarine, descending up to 120 feet below the surface. Witness coral reefs and marine life in comfort without getting wet. Marine Life Experience Discover the rich marine ecosystem of Vadoo with the guidance of experienced marine experts. Learn about reef conditions, ocean safety, and the diverse life that thrives beneath the surface.
Sunset Fishing
Experience a traditional Maldivian pastime as you fish from a Dhoni while watching the sky transform into vibrant sunset hues. Your catch can be prepared for a special dinner the following day.
House Reef & Nearby Dive Sites
Vadoo is home to one of the area’s most established house reefs, offering excellent diving and snorkelling for both beginners and experienced divers amidst colourful coral and marine species.
Sand Bank Lunch
Escape to a secluded sandbank surrounded by crystal-clear lagoons. Enjoy sunbathing, snorkelling, and a private beach lunch in a setting that feels like your own island.
Mandara Luxury Spa
Relax at the overwater Mandara Spa, featuring Balinese therapists and a curated menu of massages and ELEMIS treatments. The tranquil ocean views enhance a deeply restorative wellness experience.
