Our
Premium All-Inclusive
package at Adaaran Prestige Vadoo is designed to make your stay effortless and indulgent. It includes complimentary transfers, butler service, gourmet dining, premium beverages, in-villa mini bar, daily high tea, spa credits, and exclusive experiences—designed for a romantic, luxurious and carefree stay in the Maldives.
Adaaran Prestige Vadoo is just a short 15-minute speedboat ride from Velana International Airport. We provide complimentary
transfers
to and from the airport, ensuring a smooth and hassle-free start to your holiday.
Our cancellation policy varies depending on the season. You’ll find the general guidelines in our
Terms & Conditions.
If you’ve booked a non-refundable offer, please note that a 100% cancellation fee will apply to the total room charges.
At Adaaran Prestige Vadoo, you can enjoy a
dine-around
experience as part of our Premium All-Inclusive package. Savor gourmet cuisine, fresh seafood specialties, and romantic oceanfront dining across our three restaurants and two bars—each offering a unique setting to make your meals truly memorable.
Yes, we do! We regularly share our latest deals and seasonal packages on our
Special Offers
page. Feel free to explore them to find the offer that best suits your stay.
Adaaran Prestige Vadoo is designed as an adults-only retreat, creating an intimate and private setting perfect for couples and honeymooners looking for a romantic Maldivian escape.
When you book directly through the official Adaaran website, you can enjoy exclusive benefits including Best Rate Guaranteed, USD 50 Spa Credits, Snorkeling equipment, Non-motorized water sports, Butler Service, and Complimentary speedboat Transfers.
