Discover luxury restaurants in Maldives as you indulge in exquisite dining at Adaaran Prestige Vadoo, where culinary experiences transcend the ordinary. With fresh seafood sourced straight from the surrounding waters and seasonal ingredients procured from Malé and beyond, each guest is treated to a tantalizing array of exotic and delightful dishes. Delight your palate with fusion fine dining options and specialty Japanese cuisine at our distinguished restaurants. At Farivalhu Restaurant, savour gourmet international cuisine, while Kithajima Restaurant invites you to relish specialty Japanese fare. Perched atop the endless turquoise waters of the atoll, our main bar offers a unique setting for enjoying drinks, with a distinct three-sided wooden deck providing the perfect backdrop for gazing at the clear blue sky or the mesmerizing starry night over a floodlit ocean.
