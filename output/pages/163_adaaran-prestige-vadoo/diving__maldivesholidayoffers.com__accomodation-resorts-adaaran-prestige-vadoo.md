Adaaran Prestige Vadoo is a Maldives luxury resort with all-over-water accommodation located in South Male' Atoll. The resort has its own house reef, which is teeming with colorful marine life. Furthermore, the resort provides its guests with a special all-inclusive plan.
The All-Inclusive plan offers clients with a specially crafted dine-around menu. It also contains a variety of alcoholic and non-alcoholic beverages provided at the main bar, beach bar, and main restaurant. Furthermore, the all-inclusive plan provides guests with additional special privileges such as in-room amenities, a beach dining experience, and much more.
Adaaran Prestige Vadoo uniquely provides its visitors over water villas perched atop the lagoon's turquoise waters. Offering exclusive Maldives honeymoon villas for you and your loved one. Overwater Villas span the island's lagoon, and sunset-facing Villas allow access to the house reef.
