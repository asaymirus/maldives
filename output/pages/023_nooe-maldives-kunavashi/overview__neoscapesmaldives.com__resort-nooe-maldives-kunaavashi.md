DESCRIPTION
Rediscover Balance in Paradise at NOOE Maldives Kunaavashi
NOOE Maldives Kunaavashi is a serene island escape set in the Vaavu Atoll, famously known as the “Wow” Atoll for its stunning marine life and natural beauty.
Stay in elegant Beach Villas, Sunset Villas, or Overwater Villas with private pools and Jacuzzis, each offering privacy and breathtaking ocean views.
Embark on a culinary journey across three signature dining venues, showcasing Maldivian, Japanese, Mediterranean, Italian, and open-fire grill cuisine.
Find your own rhythm with sunrise yoga, diving with nurse sharks, water sports, cooking classes, spa treatments, and starlit evenings
NOOE: Never Odd Or Even — a perfect balance of wonder, wellness, and the wow of the Maldives.

Dining
mō·odhǔ (Sea)
Step into mō·odhǔ, where cool air, ocean views, and handcrafted delights create the perfect afternoon escape. Choose from two tempting cabinets—one filled with indulgent pastries, the other with wholesome, health-conscious treats. Whether you’re savouring a treat, sipping tea, or lost in a book, let the sea be your muse.
Vēlì (Sand)
Perched beside the crystal-clear waters of our infinity pool, Vēlì is a chic day club and poolside bar where laid-back luxury meets island flavour. Lounge on a sunbed as you enjoy wood-fired pizzas, gourmet burgers, and fresh salads, all perfectly paired with infused sundowners.
Cool off with a treat from our hand-rolled ice cream cart and take in sweeping views of the white sandy beach and the deep blue Atoll. From sun-soaked afternoons to golden-hour cocktails, Vēlì is your go-to for effortless indulgence by the pool.
Ìrǔ (Sun)
Ìrǔ, meaning “Sun” in Maldivian, is your all-day dining destination where global flavours shine. Begin your day with a vibrant breakfast buffet and return for lunch or dinner to explore a rotating menu of seven themed nights each week.
Live cooking stations and immersive entertainment add to the experience, as our chefs’ craft dishes from around the world right before your eyes. From Maldivian delicacies to international favourites, Ìrǔ offers a sensory journey you won’t forget.
w·āsho’ku
A Journey of Japanese-Inspired Dining
Rooted in the Japanese words wa (harmony) and shoku (meal), w·āsho’ku is a serene culinary experience where peace and flavour meet. From the artful precision of our sushi bar to the sizzling theatrics of the teppanyaki grill, every dish is a celebration of balance and craft.
Enjoy the freshest fish caught daily in our Atoll, alongside traditional and modern Japanese dishes made with organic, globally sourced ingredients. Pair your meal with a fine selection of sake or indulge in a signature Japanese-inspired cocktail for a contemporary twist.
Flame
Signature Chargrill Dining Over the Vaavu Atoll
Flame is the signature chargrill restaurant of NOOE Kunaavashi Maldives, perched above the dazzling waters of the Vaavu Atoll. Here, every detail is designed to ignite the senses, from the aroma of sizzling seafood and dry-aged meats to the glow of the open grill.
The menu features the finest local catch, succulent poultry, and premium cuts, each perfectly prepared to showcase bold, smoky flavours. Enjoy a curated wine selection by our expert sommeliers and end your evening with a nightcap on the deck, surrounded by the tranquil beauty of the ocean.
At Flame, every meal is an experience to remember.

Th-ari Spa by Mandara
A tranquil sanctuary offering holistic treatments rooted in ancient traditions, designed to restore mind, body, and spirit.
Kulhun Kids Club
A safe, fun space for younger guests with creative arts, games, and supervised adventures. Kids stay entertained while parents enjoy some me-time.
Ocean Adventures & Water sports
Dive into the extraordinary with world-class sites like Fotteyo Kandu and the Golden Wall, PADI courses available for all levels. Discover the magic of the sea with snorkelling trips, island-hopping, private cruises, and unforgettable encounters with turtles, nurse sharks, and shipwrecks. For thrill-seekers or those craving calm, choose from jet skiing, water skiing, paddleboarding, kayaking, or a serene lagoon cruise.
When Golf Meets Champagne
Hit eco-friendly balls into the ocean as you sip champagne on the beach. Golf, glamour, and golden sunsets in one unforgettable experience.
Private Dining
Create your dream dinner on the beach, on the deck, or under the stars. Romantic, personalised, and one-of-a-kind.
Masterclasses: Cooking, Cocktails & Coffee
Dive into hands-on sessions that awaken your senses. Learn to craft Maldivian, Japanese, Italian, and Thai dishes in our Cooking Classes using herbs from the Chef’s Garden. Shake and stir signature drinks in our Cocktail Classes guided by expert mixologists. Or explore the art of brewing, roasting, and latte design in our immersive Coffee Classes led by our in-house barista.
Fitness & Wellness
Stay active at our beachfront gym, fully equipped with cardio machines, free weights, and modern fitness gear. Join group or private sessions, whether poolside or by the sea, designed for all levels to help you rebalance, recentre, and recharge in paradise.
Weddings
Exchange vows with your toes in the sand and the Indian Ocean as your witness. From intimate elopements to grand celebrations, we will bring your vision to life.
