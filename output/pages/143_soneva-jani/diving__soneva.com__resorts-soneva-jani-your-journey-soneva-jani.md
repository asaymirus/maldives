What taxes and service charges do I pay?
Tax on villas at Soneva Jani stands at 29.92%, based on your room rate. This includes a 10% Service Charge, 2% Carbon Offset Tax, and 17% Goods and Services Tax. Additionally, a Green Tax of USD 12 per person per night applies to the villa rate.
Where is Soneva Jani located?
Soneva Jani is located in the Medhufaru lagoon in the Maldives’ Noonu Atoll. This small private archipelago includes the 150-acre Medhufaru Island and five islets, four of which remain untouched.
What is Soneva Jani time?
At Soneva Jani, we set our clocks forward by one hour ahead of Malé time so that our guests can enjoy more daylight hours. Malé time is UTC +5. During Ramadan, the clocks at Soneva Jani shift back to Malé time.
What is the weather like?
The Maldives’ tropical climate is influenced by monsoons – the average temperature is a balmy 30°C (86°F), and the coolest days are a pleasant 25°C (77°F). Sea temperatures are around 22 to 28°C (72 to 82°F).
In the dry season (November-April), expect clear blue skies, light sea breezes and tropical sunshine. In monsoon season (May-October), the weather can be more mixed, with sunshine, showers and occasional storms.
When is the best time to visit?
The Maldives is an all-season destination, with a tropical climate, sunshine-filled days and temperatures averaging 30°C all year round. Diving is good at any time of the year, however the monsoon season (May-October) is the best season for manta rays and other marine life due to the higher quantities of plankton.
Will I need any vaccinations?
Vaccinations are only required for visitors travelling to the Maldives from a region where yellow fever and cholera are endemic. For all other guests, no vaccinations are needed.
Is there a doctor on site?
We have a resort nurse available 24/7 at Soneva Jani. Our clinic is well equipped, with emergency equipment including a cardiac machine and defibrillators. The Manadhoo Atoll Hospital is a 10-minute speedboat ride away, and more serious situations might require an airlift to Malé.
