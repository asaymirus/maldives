Meal Plan Overview
Starting December 27th, 2025, You & Me Maldives will be introducing a new meal plan, the Premium All-Inclusive, designed to elevate your stay to new heights. This will replace the current All-Inclusive and All-Inclusive Extreme packages. With our Premium All-Inclusive plan, guests will enjoy an even more refined experience, including breakfast, lunch, and dinner at the Main Restaurant, offering a diverse range of fresh, vibrant flavors. You’ll also have access to a wider selection of beverages, from refreshing cocktails and premium spirits to an expanded array of snacks and daily afternoon tea or coffee.
​
In addition, the Premium All-Inclusive package will continue to include non-motorized water sports such as kayaking, snorkeling, and stand-up paddleboarding, allowing you to explore the Maldives in style. We’re excited to bring this new experience to you and can’t wait to welcome you to enjoy the many enhancements this change will bring to your stay.
