Services & Experiences
At You & Me Maldives, everything you wish for is right here on a single island, thoughtfully crafted and served with love. From wellness and fitness sessions with expert instructors in our fully equipped gym, to stunning excursions and thrilling adventures, every moment is designed to delight. Enjoy sunrise or sunset yoga, Pilates, or Zumba, explore nearby islands, snorkel among vibrant marine life, or capture your memories with a private photography session. Weddings, celebrations, and entertainment—from live music to cultural shows—are arranged with meticulous attention, ensuring every experience feels memorable. You’ll discover that everything is possible on the island of love.

We offer a variety of 30 and 60 minute Fitness and Wellness sessions, all hosted by our experienced and qualified Instructors. Whether you are a seasoned pro, a beginner or somewhere in-between; whatever your goal is, we have a class for you!
Beach Volley | Beach Tennis | Gym | Sunrise Yoga | Sunset Yoga | Pilates | Zumba | Aqua Fitness
Excursions
Excursions at You & Me Maldives are designed for those eager to explore, from adventure lovers to those seeking relaxing moments. Discover Maldivian culture on local island tours, try sunrise or sunset fishing, or glide across the lagoon on a Sunset Dolphin Cruise. Escape to a private romantic Picnic Island snorkelling among vibrant reefs, or test your skills with Big Game Fishing for sailfish, marlin, and dorado. Each day brings new adventures, blending thrill, relaxation, and romance in a pristine atoll in the Maldives.
Sunset Dolphin Cruise | Picnic Island | Sunset Fishing | Sunrise Fishing-Trolling | Local Island Tour | Big Game Fishing | Hanifaru Bay Excursion
Wedding
Destination weddings in the  Maldives are unrivalled in beauty. Say your vows under a floral arch as the sun sets and waves lap the shore. Celebrate with family and friends in a candlelit tent facing the tranquil lagoon. And if we call ourselves You & Me Maldives, it’s because on our island, love is truly in the air and every celebration of love is made unique and unforgettable, unmatched across the Indian Ocean. Emerge from paradise ready to begin the next chapter of your lives.
