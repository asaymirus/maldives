Premium All-Inclusive – A Gourmet Journey
​
At You & Me, guests on the Premium All-Inclusive plan are invited to indulge in our refined Dine Around concept, enjoying lunch and dinner in any of our à la carte restaurants (with the exception of the H2O underwater restaurant), and breakfast served daily at the main restaurant.
With a strong focus on culinary excellence, You & Me is a true gourmet island, where each dining venue offers a unique and memorable experience, from creative flavors to elegant presentation, every meal is a celebration of taste.
H2O by Andrea Berton
​
First of it’s kind, our underwater restaurant's stand out feature is a fully submerged underwater restaurant that gives diners a view of the Maldives' breathtaking marine environment, bringing a unique Michelin star dining experience to Maldives. Serving for only 20 pax at a time, a set menu is provided to indulge this luxury experience.

La Pasta
A true ‘restaurant by the beach shores’, where Italian flair meets coastal charm. La Pasta is a vibrant, communal beachside eatery that brings the heart of Italy to your plate. From freshly made regional pastas to artisanal pizzas, enjoy the best of Italian cuisine served daily for both lunch and dinner — all with your feet in the sand and the sea breeze in the air. Because, let’s be honest… Italians really do it better.

Cheers Bar
Just steps away from the main pool, you’ll find the lively and welcoming Cheers Bar — the perfect place to unwind.
Furnished with cozy sofas and stylish seating, it’s the ideal setting to sip on freshly crafted cocktails, enjoy a refined selection of wines and champagnes, and soak in the vibrant atmosphere. Every evening comes alive with live music performances and entertainment, making Cheers Bar a must-visit spot to relax, socialize, and enjoy the night.
