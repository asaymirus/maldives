Diving
​
You & Me is an ideal spot to encounter the Maldives’ charismatic marine life, including manta rays, turtles, napoleon fish, and grey reef sharks. The resort features a fully-equipped diving centre run by certified professionals, offering SSI courses from beginner to professional levels. Novices can learn to dive and complete their certification, while experienced divers can enjoy bespoke trips to the Atoll’s most vibrant dive sites. Dive Mission, located on the arrival jetty with direct lagoon access, is the departure point for our dive dhonis. Travel times to dive sites range from 10 to 60 minutes, offering options for wall and current dives where larger species such as eagle rays, grey reef sharks, tunas, and mantas are frequently spotted. The centre provides brand-new Scuba Pro equipment, aluminium cylinders, rinse facilities, and a dedicated teaching area. Full day and 2-tank trips are also available for every level of diver.
