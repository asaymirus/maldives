Frequently asked questions
What is the nearest International Airport to You & Me Maldives?
Velana International Airport, Male’ is the nearest international airport to You & Me Maldives.
How far is You & Me Maldives from the Airport?
You & Me Maldives is reachable 45 minutes by seaplane.
What is the Time Zone at You & Me Maldives?
You & Me Maldives is one hour ahead of local Maldives time, GMT+6.
Can I swim anywhere around the resort?
We would advise not to swim in front of your lagoon when the sea is very rough as there may be a strong current.  However, we do have a main pool.
How far is the house reef from You & Me?
Since we are surrounded by a rich lagoon, our house-reef can be accessible by a short boat ride. Additionally, exploring the marine life around the island is possible through snorkeling from your villas.
Do you have internet facilities available to the guest?
Yes, we have complimentary Wi-Fi access throughout the resort including guest accommodations. Log in details will be briefed at the time of check in by our Island Hosts.
What languages are spoken in You & Me?
The official languages spoken at You & Me Maldives are Dhivehi and English. However, we have multilingual staff working here as well.
What is the age restriction in You & Me?
You & Me Maldives is an adult’s only resort allowing guests above 16 years and above. Hence, we do not have any kid’s facilities in the resort.
Photography and Drone footage?
Due to privacy concerns, You & Me prohibits the operation of unmanned aerial systems or drones. We kindly ask guests to refrain from flying drones anywhere on the island. For drone shootings please contact Guest Relations. Photography is allowed for personal use.
Do you have a medical facility on the island?
We do have a clinic on the island along with an inhouse doctor and a nurse.
