Excursions
Activities at You & Me Maldives for everyone, from sports to entertainment and diving enthusiasts. Excursion activities can be reserved with Guest Relation Team. We organize Local Island Tours, Fishing Trips, Snorkeling Trips, Dolphin Cruises and many more, Daily entertainments with Live Music, DJ and Maldivian Cultural Show. Sporting activities with Big Game Fishing Tours, Beach Volley Ball and many more activities to choose from every day.
Sunset Dolphin Cruise
Join the dolphin cruise in search of fun-loving dolphins. Encouraging these beautiful creatures is a fun and an unforgettable experience. the waters around Raa Atoll are home to hundreds of dolphins, often spotted on our daily trips.
Picnic Island
Experience the thrill of your own tiny desert island surrounded by the crystal-clear waters and coral reefs. Escape to our picnic island and get ready to discover the true meaning of paradise.
Sunset Fishing
A short fishing trip in which we use
hand lines and raw fish as bait. Sunset
fishing  is a talent practiced by
Maldivians for generations.
Local Island Tour
Discover the peaceful island life in the Maldives. Visit our neighbor islands, where the population is only a few hundred, this will show you a picture of the local culture and lifestyle.
Sunrise Fishing-Trolling
Fishing, it’s anything and everything here in the Maldives. A heathy food, an escape. a competition a whim. And it’s like they say “Even a bad day fishing is better than a good day at work. Enjoy a mesmerizing sunrise while trying your luck for a wahoo, a jack fish or a tuna.
Big Game Fishing
Maldivian oceans are blue and deep with a great variety of fishes thus fishing in the Maldivian water can be an adventure of a lifetime. Not only are there a great variety of fishes but they are also huge in size ranging from 1 to 2 meters long. trolling is a method used for biggame fishing. you will be trolling for Sail fish, Marlin and Dorado. At the same time you will be able to enjoy the cool morning breeze of the Maldivian ocean with views of the reef which are surrounded by coral island filled with white sandy beaches and tall palms.
