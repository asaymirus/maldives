Water Sports
You & Me’s state of the art Water Sports Centre managed by WATERSPORTS MISSION, offers a range of activities for divers and non-divers alike. Not too far from the Dive Centre, right on the beautiful white-sand beach, the Water Sports Centre has all you need for an adrenaline kick and can give you the ultimate thrill. From snorkeling to jet skis, to sailing to nearby uninhabited islands, we have the complete range of water sports equipment and activities to satisfy even hard-core adventurers.
