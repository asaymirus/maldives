You & Me Maldives, your adults-only paradise island
​
You & Me Maldives is a private, romantic island in an untouched part of the Maldives. Far from the rush of daily life, it’s made for everyone who needs to relax, reconnect, and enjoy pure “us time.” A 5-star resort with a glam touch and tropical vibes, You & Me is set in Raa Atoll, just 45-minute scenic seaplane flight from Velana International Airport. This adults-only escape is designed for honeymooners, couples, friends, and solo travellers seeking peace, privacy, and complete relaxation. With attentive service and exceptional comfort, every moment is made effortless—zero worries, 100% emotions
You & Me Maldives is your adults-only sanctuary, where privacy, style, and comfort reign supreme
From exhilarating waterslides that launch you straight into the turquoise lagoon, to dreamy private pools and sun-soaked decks designed for two, every corner invites connection and relaxation. The rustic-chic design sets it apart: handcrafted wooden furnishings, open spaces, and natural textures create an atmosphere of warm elegance, an aesthetic you won’t find anywhere else in the Maldives. A place made for those who dream big.

From underwater marvels to barefoot elegance: your ultimate luxury dining journey in the Maldives
At You & Me Maldives, every meal is a carefully crafted experience, thoughtfully designed and served à la carte to delight your senses. From the iconic H2O by Andrea Berton, the underwater restaurant where you dine surrounded by vibrant marine life, to The Sand, offering live-cooked delicacies in a breathtaking beachfront setting, every dining venue is unique. Enjoy Teppanyaki theatre at Rising Sun, light and healthy dishes by the pool at Green Carpet, and timeless Italian comfort at La Pasta. For sunsets and music, Cheers Bar sets the perfect mood. Every bite, every setting, every moment, designed for indulgence, romance, and surprise.
