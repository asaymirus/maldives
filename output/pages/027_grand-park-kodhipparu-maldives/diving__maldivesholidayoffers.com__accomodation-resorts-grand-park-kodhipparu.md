Resort Overview
Experience paradise on earth at Grand Park Kodhipparu, Maldives. A luxurious one-islandone-resort destination featuring a collection of 120 idyllic beach-front pool villas, breathtaking overwater villas and spacious two-bedroom villas. Embark on a 360° barefoot island adventure and immerse in the tranquility that nature has to offer. Soak up the sun by the infinity pool, delight in delectable cuisine at any of our 3 restaurants & bars or simply take in the splendor of the vast azure sea, home to a variety of colourful and exotic marine life. Just a mere 20-minute speedboat ride from Male’, barefoot luxury and the stunning beauty of Maldives awaits you.
