DESCRIPTION
A rare private island retreat set within one of the Maldives’ deepest and most pristine atolls.
Alila Kothaifaru Maldives offers exceptional seclusion with a limited collection of beachfront and overwater villas, each featuring a private pool and thoughtfully positioned for complete privacy. Located in the untouched Raa Atoll, the resort provides outstanding access to vibrant marine life, with snorkelling and diving at the house reef and nearby sites, including the Baa Atoll UNESCO World Biosphere Reserve and Hanifaru Bay’s seasonal manta gatherings. Contemporary design, natural textures, and serene surroundings define this sophisticated island sanctuary.

Dining
Seasalt
A relaxed beachside restaurant celebrating fresh, sustainably sourced local seafood. Coastal cuisine blends Middle Eastern and Mediterranean influences, with signature saltbaked fish at dinner and a rotating spread of local and international favourites for breakfast and lunch.
Umami
A contemporary Japanese dining experience centred on the fifth taste of deliciousness. Enjoy sushi, sashimi, robata-grilled seafood, and teppanyaki featuring Wagyu beef, organic vegetables, and sustainably sourced seafood, served indoors or alfresco by the sea.
Mirus Bar
A vibrant social hub inspired by the Maldives’ historic spice trade routes. Sip handcrafted cocktails infused with herbs from the resort garden, explore Old and New World wines, and enjoy Spanish tapas alongside South Asian bites.
Pibati Sul Mare
A laid-back beach café serving Italian comfort classics. Expect artisanal pizzas, handcrafted pastas, fresh pastries, gelato, barista coffee, wellness drinks, and light bites, perfect for casual seaside dining any time of day.
Yakitori Bar
A sunset favourite overlooking the water. Specialises in Asian-inspired craft cocktails, premium Japanese sakes and spirits, with irresistible aromas from the robata grill setting the mood as day turns to dusk.
The Shack
A private sandbank dining escape reached by speedboat. Choose from picnic-style day experiences or an evening sunset BBQ prepared by a private chef, with bespoke options for romantic dinners, exclusive island hire, and unforgettable celebrations.

Highlights From The Resort
A Holistic Wellbeing Escape
Alila Kothaifaru Maldives is designed around mindful living, where nature and wellness move in harmony. From barefoot beach walks and yoga to nourishing cuisine and restorative spa rituals, every element supports balance, renewal, and calm.
Spa Alila – Wellness in the Treetops
Set within lush foliage, Spa Alila offers a tranquil escape with four private double treatment suites overlooking the island’s greenery. Treatments blend ancient healing traditions with modern techniques, using natural ingredients to restore body and mind.
Extraordinary Marine Experiences
Located in the deep waters of Raa Atoll, the resort offers access to vibrant house reefs, world-class snorkelling, and exceptional diving. Seasonal excursions to Hanifaru Bay (UNESCO World Biosphere Reserve) bring encounters with manta rays and, at times, whale sharks. Nearby Vaadhoo Island offers the rare chance to witness the magical Sea of Stars phenomenon.
Curated Alila Moments
Discover meaningful, story-led experiences that connect you to Maldivian culture and nature. From coconut craft rituals and island walks with a Cultural Ambassador to traditional storytelling by firelight, each moment is designed to inspire connection and reflection.
Romance, Weddings & Celebrations
From barefoot beach ceremonies to intimate sandbank vows, Alila Kothaifaru Maldives creates unforgettable moments of love. Celebrate with bespoke dining, traditional music, and stunning island settings, whether for a wedding, vow renewal, or private celebration under the stars.
