Your Private Island Sanctuary
Nestled in the pristine Raa Atoll at the northern edge of the Maldives, Alila Kothaifaru Maldives is a luxurious all-pool-villa resort accessible via a scenic 45-minute seaplane journey from Velana International Airport in Male. This 11.2-hectare private island retreat offers a serene escape surrounded by lush vegetation, turquoise waters, and abundant marine life, blending natural beauty with sophisticated comfort. Designed for peaceful seclusion, the resort provides personalized experiences, immersing guests in a sanctuary of minimalist elegance and nature’s wonders.
