Nestled in the pristine Raa Atoll,
Alila Kothaifaru Maldives
offers a perfect blend of minimalist design, luxurious comfort, and eco-conscious living. Accessible via a 45-minute seaplane from Malé, this exclusive resort is ideal for travelers seeking tranquility, adventure, and a connection with nature.
Accommodation
The resort features 44 Beach Pool Villas and 36 Overwater Pool Villas, each with a private pool. Villas are designed to harmonize with the island’s natural beauty, providing privacy, unobstructed ocean views, and serene interiors with natural textures and soothing color palettes.
Dining
Guests can enjoy a variety of culinary experiences across five venues:
Seasalt
: Coastal cuisine with Middle Eastern and Mediterranean influences.
Mirus Bar
: Refreshing cocktails crafted with ingredients from the resort’s herb garden.
Umami
: Japanese cuisine with a Maldivian twist.
Yakitori
: Grilled skewers in a casual setting.
The Shack
: Light bites and beverages in a relaxed atmosphere.
Activities and Wellness
The resort caters to both adventure seekers and those looking for relaxation:
Snorkeling, diving, paddleboarding, and kayaking on the vibrant house reef.
Spa Alila offers treatments inspired by traditional healing practices.
Fitness center, yoga sessions, beach volleyball, and table tennis.
Marine life experiences, including the “Sea of Stars” phenomenon nearby.
Sustainability
Alila Kothaifaru Maldives implements eco-friendly practices such as solar energy, grey water recycling, and eco-conscious cleaning products. Guests can participate in coral planting and reef cleaning initiatives, supporting marine conservation.
Why Choose Alila Kothaifaru Maldives
The resort is ideal for romantic getaways, family vacations, or solo retreats, offering:
Private, all-villa accommodations for an exclusive experience.
World-class amenities and services.
Stunning natural surroundings of lush greenery and crystal-clear waters.
A commitment to sustainable luxury.
