Easter in the Maldives: A Candy-Coloured Island Escape at Kuda Villingili Resort
Easter in the Maldives is a time of colour, joy, and togetherness — and at Kuda Villingili Resort Maldives, the celebration takes on a uniquely playful yet effortlessly luxurious character. Inspired by the whimsical charm of Candy Land, Easter 2026 invites guests to experience a vibrant island escape where memorable moments unfold naturally against the backdrop of the Indian Ocean.
Located in the North Malé Atoll, just 30 minutes by speedboat from Velana International Airport, Kuda Villingili Resort Maldives provides the perfect setting for travellers seeking an Easter holiday that blends relaxation, family experiences, exceptional dining, and wellbeing.
A Destination Designed for Easter Getaways
With its spacious villas and residences, expansive beaches, and relaxed island atmosphere, our
luxury resort
is is ideally suited for Easter travellers — whether visiting as a couple, a family, or a group of friends. The resort’s signature sense of space and privacy allows guests to enjoy festive experiences while still embracing the tranquillity of a Maldivian escape.
Easter Dining Experiences in the Maldives
Culinary experiences remain at the heart of Easter at Kuda Villingili. Throughout the celebration period, guests can explore a variety of themed dining journeys crafted to bring loved ones together.
The Grand Easter Gala Buffet, the highlight of Easter Sunday, presents a vibrant feast of festive favourites, live cooking stations, and live entertainment. Set beneath the stars, the evening captures the spirit of celebration while reflecting the resort’s relaxed elegance.
At Raalhu Bar, the Surfside Sunset BBQ offers a distinctly Maldivian experience. Flame-grilled specialities, fresh seafood, and panoramic sunset views combine with the dramatic scenery of the nearby Chickens surf break — creating one of the most memorable Easter dining settings in the Maldives.
Easter Activities for Families & Children
For families travelling to the Maldives during Easter, Kuda Villingili offers an engaging programme designed especially for younger guests.
At Kuda Fiyo Kids Club, Easter becomes a world of creativity and adventure, with activities ranging from Easter egg hunts and themed crafts to games and movie nights. Each experience is designed to spark imagination while allowing parents to unwind and enjoy the island.
The Little Chefs of Kuda: Easter Baking Workshop provides another guest favourite, inviting children into a playful culinary experience that perfectly complements the Candy Land theme.
Wellness & Relaxation During Easter
Beyond the festivities, Easter at Kuda Villingili also embraces moments of calm and renewal. Guests can reconnect through restorative wellness experiences including Sound Healing Sessions, Ocean Breeze Yoga, and guided practices designed to nurture balance and relaxation.
These gentle interludes create a harmonious balance between vibrant celebration and serene island living — a defining characteristic of the Kuda Villingili experience.
Easter Adventures on the Indian Ocean
For guests seeking activity and exploration, MSTS Dive & Water Sports offers a variety of ocean experiences throughout the Easter period. From lagoon adventures to family-friendly activities, the surrounding turquoise waters provide endless opportunities for discovery.
Easter at Kuda Villingili Resort Maldives is more than a seasonal celebration — it is an invitation to embrace colour, connection, and the simple joy of time shared together in one of the world’s most extraordinary destinations.
