Celebrate “Through the Decades” at Kuda Villingili Resort Maldives
This festive season, Kuda Villingili Resort Maldives invites guests to journey “Through the Decades” — a vibrant celebration of music, nostalgia, and island magic from December 23, 2025, to January 7, 2026. Inspired by the resort’s ethos of redefining time and space, the island transforms into a kaleidoscope of shimmering gold, neon hues, and joyful memories.
A Christmas of Elegance and Wonder
The celebrations begin on December 23 with a heart-warming Tree Lighting Ceremony and carols under the stars. On Christmas Eve, guests can indulge in a luxurious Gala Dinner featuring festive cocktails, gourmet creations, and live entertainment.
Christmas morning brings the arrival of Santa Claus, along with gingerbread-making and family craft sessions, ensuring that every generation experiences the joy of the holidays together.
Moments of Wellness and Reconnection
Amid the festivities, guests can retreat to The Spa at Kuda Villingili — an overwater sanctuary of calm. Special holiday spa rituals, Ayurvedic therapies, and Balinese massages await those seeking renewal between celebrations. Surrounded by lagoon views and ocean breeze, it’s a moment of serenity woven into the season’s excitement.
Countdown to a Timeless New Year
The island comes alive on New Year’s Eve with the Décade Countdown Celebration, beginning with sunset cocktails at Hawkers and a themed Gala Dinner inspired by the fashion and sounds of the ’70s to the 2000s. As the clock strikes midnight, fireworks light the sky, followed by a Neon After-Party with live DJ sets and glowing visuals — the ultimate Maldives New Year experience.
Fun for Families and Young Travelers
At the Kuda Fiyo Kids Club, little ones embark on their own Time-Travel Adventure, exploring disco playgrounds of the ’70s, cartoon fun of the ’90s, and karaoke corners of the 2000s. Creative workshops, costume parties, and talent shows keep young minds inspired and entertained throughout the holidays.
Entertainment Under the Stars
Each night, the island’s rhythm is set by fire dancers, acrobatic duos, live saxophonists, and illusionists. Performances by Show Ballet “Arriva” and singer-entertainer Madam Boyko add a glamorous crescendo to this unforgettable celebration.
A New Year to Remember
The festivities continue with a New Year’s Day Brunch, followed by Russian Orthodox Christmas celebrations on January 6–7, featuring elegant gala dinners and gourmet feasts. Whether in search of reflection, connection, or pure celebration, Kuda Villingili’s 2025–2026 Festive Program promises every guest a moment that truly redefines island living.
