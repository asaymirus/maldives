DESCRIPTION
Where Time Slows and Paradise Unfolds
Kuda Villingili Maldives is a private island retreat where turquoise waters meet the horizon and life’s pace is yours to set. Just 30 minutes by speedboat from North Malé Atoll, the resort offers the largest pool in the Maldives and the perfect balance of privacy and togetherness.
Savour gourmet dining under the stars, relax in luxurious villas, and rejuvenate at the serene spa. Guests can enjoy water sports, diving, and cultural experiences that celebrate Maldivian heritage. Whether travelling solo, as a couple, family, or group, the island encourages connection, reflection, and lasting memories.
Inspired by Asian eclectic style and enriched with traditional Maldivian arts, the resort blends timeless elegance with the natural beauty of the Maldives. Kuda Villingili Maldives is more than a destination, it is an experience where time is redefined, and every moment becomes unforgettable.

Dining
The Restaurant
The Restaurant features three elegant dining spaces. Start your day with a wholesome breakfast of smoked local fish, homemade jams, herb-infused honey, and organic produce from small-holder farmers, with plant-based, dairy-free, and gluten-free choices.
Fire –
Flames and Flavour
An American steakhouse-inspired venue perfecting the art of grilling premium meats.
Earth –
A Journey Through Asia
Fresh, organic ingredients elevate timeless Asian favourites like noodles and dim sum.
Ocean –
Timeless Seafood
Exquisite seafood prepared with European flair, showcasing the freshest daily catch.
Spice – Aromatic Treasures from India to Arabia
Savour the rich flavours of Indian-Arabic cuisine with flame-grilled kebabs, smoky tandoor specialties, vibrant mezze platters, and tender shawarma. Traditional recipes meet modern flair, creating a dining experience that is both authentic and unforgettable.
East – All Your Favourites Made Just for You
Delight in Thai, Chinese, and Korean classics, from tender steamed fish to succulent prawns. Each dish blends authentic flavours with expert preparation, offering a memorable taste of Asia in the Maldives.
Med – A Classic Pairing
Savour Italian-Mediterranean favourites with flavourful pizzas, curated charcuterie, and authentic regional dishes. Each plate captures the essence of both cuisines, creating a dining experience that is rich, vibrant, and unforgettable in the heart of the Maldives.
The Bar – A Place to Catch Up
Relax by the pool with handcrafted cocktails, mocktails, or ice-cold beers at one of the Maldives’ top bars. Choose between the main bar or poolside bar, where a live band or DJ sets a vibrant yet chill atmosphere, making every sip and moment a perfect part of your island escape.
Raalhu Bar – Your Ideal Sunset Spot
Enjoy Italian-Mediterranean favourites, from savoury pizzas to curated charcuterie, while watching the sunset over the ocean. Relax on the deck with expertly crafted cocktails and soak in the chill vibes from the resident DJ as surfers ride the waves at Chickens Break, one of the Maldives’ most iconic surf spots.
The Cigar Lounge – An Exclusive Haven
Overlooking the lagoon, the Cigar Lounge offers a curated selection of international whiskies and cognacs paired with premium cigars. Relax in a warm, sophisticated setting with sumptuous leather décor, where every detail is crafted for indulgence, making it a one-of-a-kind experience for whiskey and cigar aficionados in the Maldives.
Mar-Umi – Marvellous Culinary Delights
Set by the beach with stunning lagoon views, Mar-Umi offers Japanese Peruvian cuisine for dinner and vibrant South American dishes for lunch. Enjoy interactive Teppanyaki dining with sizzling showmanship, from classic ceviche to delectable poke bowls. The bar stays open for sunset drinks, serving handcrafted cocktails and curated beverages, making every meal a celebration of flavours and a memorable Maldivian dining experience.
Destination & In-Villa Dining
For special occasions, skilled chefs craft bespoke menus while the resort arranges tailored entertainment. Guests preferring privacy can enjoy In-Villa Dining from 06:00 to 23:00, featuring specially curated dishes, ensuring a personalized and memorable culinary experience in the comfort of their own villa.
