Celebrating Eid al-Fitr 1447 AH at Kuda Villingili Resort Maldives
A celebration of renewal, kindness, forgiveness, and unity
Eid al-Fitr marks a meaningful moment of renewal — a time to reconnect with loved ones, reflect with gratitude, and welcome joy after the holy month of Ramadan. At Kuda Villingili Resort Maldives, this special occasion is celebrated with a serene three-day island programme from 19 to 21 March 2026, thoughtfully curated to honour the true spirit of Eid in a setting where time and space are gently redefined.
Set against the calm beauty of the Indian Ocean, Eid at Kuda Villingili unfolds as a graceful transition from reflection into celebration. Families, couples, and friends are invited to come together through shared dining experiences, relaxed island gatherings, and quiet moments by the sea — all designed to be enjoyed at an unhurried pace.
A Festive Journey Through Flavours
Culinary experiences
lie at the heart of the Eid celebration. Evenings begin with the Kuda Eid Feast Buffet Dinner at The Restaurant, where Middle Eastern and Maldivian favourites are served beneath the stars, accompanied by soft island rhythms and the gentle ocean breeze.
Throughout the Eid period, guests may also gather at Spice for the Kuda Eid Lunch Feast — a refined five-course Middle Eastern fusion menu by the Qureshi brothers. Inspired by tradition and thoughtfully crafted for sharing, the experience reflects Eid’s spirit of generosity and togetherness. As the sun sets, Arabic Shisha Night at The Bar offers a relaxed atmosphere for conversation and connection, complemented by live DJ sounds under the Maldivian sky.
Quiet Moments to Begin the Day
For those who prefer a tranquil start to Eid mornings, the resort offers a Signature Floating Breakfast, served in the privacy of each villa pool — a peaceful way to begin the day with loved ones. Guests can also enjoy Eid Signature Mocktails, delicately infused with floral and spice notes, alongside the interactive Art of Shisha Making, an experience designed to encourage storytelling, discovery, and shared moments.
Eid Celebrations for the Whole Family
Eid at Kuda Villingili is thoughtfully curated for guests of all ages. Young visitors are welcomed into the colourful world of Kuda Fiyo, where Eid-themed creative workshops, treasure trails, storytelling sessions, games, and gentle movement activities bring joy while honouring the cultural significance of the occasion. These moments create lasting memories for families celebrating together on the island.
Ocean Adventures to Mark the Occasion
For families and adventure-seekers alike, Eid al-Fitr Ocean Adventures offer a meaningful way to celebrate on the water. A personalised Big Game Fishing journey invites guests to spend time together across open seas — combining quiet reflection with the gentle thrill of the Indian Ocean.
An Eid Celebration, Redefined
With its expansive island layout, eight distinctive dining destinations, and the Maldives’ largest swimming pool, Kuda Villingili Resort Maldives offers an Eid experience that balances tradition with contemporary island luxury. Generous space, privacy, and effortless hospitality allow each guest to celebrate Eid in their own rhythm — whether through festive gatherings or quiet moments of connection.
Eid Mubarak from Kuda Villingili Resort Maldives.
