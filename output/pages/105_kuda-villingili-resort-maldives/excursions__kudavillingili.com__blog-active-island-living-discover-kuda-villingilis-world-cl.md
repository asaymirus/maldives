Active Island Living: Discover Kuda Villingili’s World-Class Sports and Fitness Offerings
At Kuda Villingili Resort Maldives, luxury goes beyond comfort — it’s about movement, connection, and the joy of play. With an ever-expanding collection of sports and wellness facilities, the resort invites guests to stay active and inspired amid breathtaking island beauty.
A New Era of Island Sports
Adding to its impressive lineup, the resort has recently unveiled a brand-new pickleball court — one of the first of its kind in the Maldives. Blending the best of tennis, badminton, and table tennis, pickleball offers a lively, social, and inclusive way to stay fit. Whether you’re a beginner or a seasoned player, it’s the perfect sport to enjoy under the island sun or beneath the evening lights.
Courts That Invite Connection
Guests can now choose from an extraordinary range of facilities:
Two flood-lit tennis courts for day or night matches
A dedicated padel tennis court — fast-paced, social, and ideal for doubles play
A new pickleball court, perfect for families and groups
A futsal ground for energetic 5-a-side games
A beachside volleyball court that brings the community together at sunset
Whether it’s a friendly rally, a competitive match, or simply an excuse to move, each space has been designed to blend recreation with relaxation — all framed by the island’s lush palms and turquoise lagoon
Wellness Beyond the Courts
For a holistic balance, Kuda Villingili’s Technogym-equipped fitness centre, oceanfront yoga pavilion, and island spa offer opportunities to stretch, strengthen, and rejuvenate. Guests can alternate between invigorating workouts and calming wellness sessions — redefining what it means to live well on holiday.
An Active Escape in Paradise
From sunrise tennis matches and padel games with loved ones and friends to evening yoga by the ocean, every moment celebrates vitality, joy, and connection.
