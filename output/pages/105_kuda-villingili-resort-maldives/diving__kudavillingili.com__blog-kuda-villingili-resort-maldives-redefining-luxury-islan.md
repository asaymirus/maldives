Kuda Villingili Resort Maldives: Redefining Luxury Island Living
Just a 30-minute speedboat ride from Malé, Kuda Villingili Resort Maldives is a luxury island retreat where time and space are beautifully redefined. Surrounded by turquoise waters and lush tropical gardens, the resort offers 75 private pool villas (36 overwater villas and 39 beachfront residences), each blending Maldivian craftsmanship with modern Asian design. Every detail — from upcycled marble bathtubs to eco-friendly furnishings made of recycled materials — reflects a mindful balance of style and sustainability.
The Island’s Unique Story
Once a humble chicken farm, Kuda Villingili has transformed into one of the best luxury resorts in the Maldives. Its playful legacy lives on through the island’s world-class surf spot, “Chickens Break,” a favourite among surfers seeking the perfect wave near North Malé Atoll.
A Pool Like No Other
At the island’s heart lies the 150-metre central pool, the largest in the Maldives — a serene oasis by day and a glowing social hub by night. Guests can unwind in open-air jacuzzis, sip handcrafted cocktails, or enjoy romantic dinners under the stars in private cabanas, surrounded by tropical calm.
A Culinary Journey Across the Globe
Food lovers are spoiled for choice with eight restaurants and three bars celebrating global flavours. Dine on Mediterranean, Asian, and Arabian-Indian creations at MED, EAST, and SPICE, or experience Japanese-Peruvian Nikkei cuisine or Teppanyaki at the signature Mar-Umi Restaurant, complete with an interactive Teppanyaki counter and curated Sake collection. Each meal at Kuda Villingili is a sensory voyage through taste and culture.
Wellness and Recreation in Harmony
Cross the wooden bridge to The Spa, a tranquil overwater sanctuary offering Ayurvedic rituals, lava-shell massages, and coconut therapies. Guests can also move mindfully in the Yoga Pavilion, stay active at the Technogym fitness centre, or enjoy friendly matches on flood-lit tennis courts, padel court and the newly added pickleball court.
Families will love the colourful Kuda Fiyo Kids Club, while adventure seekers can discover endless Maldives activities — from snorkelling and surfing lessons to sunset dolphin cruises and e-foil rides.
Celebrate the coming festive season in paradise at Kuda Villingili Resort Maldives — where luxury, wellness, and connection meet the rhythm of the ocean.
