Surf & Relax Offer
Surf, relax, and indulge at Kuda Villingili! Book four or more nights and enjoy daily breakfast and dinner at five restaurants. Take advantage of surfboard rental discounts, thrilling excursions, a soothing massage, and a cocktail class. Complimentary speedboat transfers and resort credits make this the ultimate surf escape. Ride world-class waves at the Maldives’ longest left-hand barrel, Chicken’s Break and enjoy surf trips to five nearby surf spots with an exclusive 15% discount.
Offer code:
KVSURF

Applicable for Beach Villas with Private Pool (Sunrise/Sunset) and Water Villas with Private Pool (Sunrise/Sunset).

USD 100 resort credit per Beach Villa with Private Pool/Water Villa with Private Pool (Sunrise and Sunset).

Sunset Fishing Excursion once during the stay.

15% discount on surfboard rentals and surf trips (Nearby surf points: Cokes, Lhohis, Sultan, Jails, Ninjas).
25% discount on surf beach lessons and water lessons (for beginners).
15% discount on surf photography pricelist

Total USD 300 resort credit when staying 7 nights or longer with Surf Offer.
