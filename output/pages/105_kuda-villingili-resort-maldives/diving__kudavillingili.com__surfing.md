Surfing
Catch your perfect wave at Chicken's Break
Catch thrilling waves at the renowned Chicken’s Break, the longest left-hand barrel in the Maldives, or explore five other exciting
surf spots around the island
. For adventure enthusiasts, the resort offers exhilarating kite surfing and jetboarding experiences, allowing you to ride the wind and waves like never before. For those eager to learn new skills on holiday, the resort provides surfing lessons at Baby Chickens led by certified instructors – an unforgettable experience at one of the top Maldives surf resorts.
A little bit about Chicken's Break
Chickens, the northernmost break in North Male Atoll, named after the island's history as a poultry farm hence the unusual name. It is now one of the country's premier surf spots after Australian surfers shipwrecked nearby in the '70s. Renowned for its fast, lively lefts, it features two main sections, delivering exhilarating rides when conditions align perfectly.
Surf
Lessons
In addition to surfboards and other surfing equipment available for rent at the Kuda Villingili Watersports & Dive Centre, the resort also offers surf lessons for guests looking to hone their skills. Beginners can enjoy a beach lesson covering the basics of wave formation, or a starter lesson with a private surf instructor at Baby Chickens.
