Celebrate Lunar New Year 2026 in the Maldives: Year of the Horse at Kuda Villingili Resort Maldives
Welcome the Lunar New Year 2026 in a setting where tradition meets tropical luxury. From 15 to 18 February 2026, Kuda Villingili Resort Maldives invites guests from China and Southeast Asia to celebrate the Year of the Horse with a four-day festive programme inspired by togetherness, renewal, and meaningful connection.
Just 30 minutes by speedboat from Malé, the private island resort offers an effortless escape for families, couples, and multi-generational travellers seeking a refined yet festive Maldives holiday.
A Luxury Island Escape for the Festive Season
Surrounded by turquoise waters, Kuda Villingili is home to expansive Beach and Water Villas with private pools, offering privacy, space, and uninterrupted ocean views. Elevated over the lagoon, the Water Villas provide a serene sanctuary to welcome a year symbolising strength, vitality, and freedom.
Lunar New Year Dining Experiences
Food takes centre stage during the celebrations, with curated dining across the island. Guests can savour bold Asian flavours at East, enjoy communal dining at Wansuk Hot Pot, and experience live culinary performances at Teppanyaki by Mar-Umi.
The highlight of the festivities is the Lunar New Year Gala Dinner at The Hawkers, paired with sunset cocktails and vibrant cultural performances inspired by Chinese heritage.
Culture, Family & Festive Moments
Traditional lion and dragon dances, live shows, and cultural performances bring festive energy to the island, while Kuda Fiyo Kids Club hosts themed activities, crafts, and movie nights—ensuring younger guests enjoy a joyful and meaningful Lunar New Year experience.
Wellness, Adventure & Island Experiences
Beyond the celebrations, guests can elevate their stay with floating breakfasts, private destination dining under the stars, holistic spa rituals, yoga and fitness sessions, or private sound healing. Adventure seekers can explore the surrounding beauty of the North Malé Atoll through sunset dhoni cruises, watersports, and diving experiences.
Welcome the Year of the Horse in Style
Blending Lunar traditions with contemporary island luxury, Lunar New Year at Kuda Villingili is more than a celebration—it is a journey of reconnection, indulgence, and renewal, thoughtfully curated for the modern traveller.
Gong Xi Fa Cai!
