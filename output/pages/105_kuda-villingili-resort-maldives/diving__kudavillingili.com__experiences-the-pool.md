The Pool
The Largest Swimming Pool in the Maldives
Stunningly in harmony with nature, the Kuda Villingili’s central pool is the largest pool in the Maldives.
Among the best things to do in Maldives, discover the allure of a refreshing 150-meter pool, effortlessly reachable from all areas of the island. The pool, carefully crafted, offers a serene escape from the warm, humid dry monsoon and the varying moods of the wet monsoon season, enveloped by vibrant tropical foliage that amplifies its peaceful atmosphere.
Sunbeds, Cabanas,
Seaside Relaxation
Immerse yourself in this stunning pool set amidst a lush tropical garden, featuring open-air jacuzzis, sunbathing spots that are partially submerged, and shallower sections perfect for little ones to enjoy a playful splash. The pool provides a tranquil respite from the sunny, humid dry monsoon and the shifting moods of the wet monsoon season.
