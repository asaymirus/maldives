Your Private Luxury Resort in Maldives
Kuda Villingili Maldives invites you to a place where time and space are redefined.
Escape to a
private island in the Maldives
, where turquoise waters of the Indian Ocean meet the horizon.
Welcome to Kuda Villingili Resort in North Malé Atoll, home to the largest pool in the Maldives and an exceptional island getaway just 30 minutes by speedboat from Velana International Airport.
Enjoy diverse dining experiences, relax in private pool villas, rejuvenate at our private island spa, or explore a wide range of water sports, surfing, and island activities.
Our Location
Getting here
Kuda Villingili Resort, located in the breathtaking North Malé Atoll, is a 30-minute speedboat ride from the Velana International Airport (MLE). Our hospitality begins on the speedboat with thoughtful amenities, high-speed Wi-Fi, and opulent touches, offering a seamless start to your stay in paradise.

Kuda Villingili is just 30 minutes from Velana International Airport by speedboat and the around-the-clock transfers are available.
The resort offers 75 luxurious villas and residences, including overwater villas and one-four bedroom beach retreats, all featuring spacious private pools.
The resort features eight diverse restaurants featuring twelve cuisines and three bars including an exclusive whiskey and cigar lounge.
Japanese-Peruvian, South American and Teppanyaki at Mar-Umi, Thai-Chinese at East, Indian-Arabic at Spice, Italian-Mediterranean at Med, Maldivian and Vegan cuisine at Earth, seafood at Ocean, and steaks & grilled meats at Fire.
