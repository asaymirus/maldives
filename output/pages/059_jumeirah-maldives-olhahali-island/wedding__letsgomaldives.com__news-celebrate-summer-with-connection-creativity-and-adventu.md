This summer, Jumeirah Olhahali Island is inviting guests to experience something truly special-a season filled with meaningful moments, fun family activities, and delicious dining on the beautiful shores of North Malé Atoll. From May through August, this all-villa resort has crafted a program that brings people together, whether that’s through shared adventures, creative experiences, or simply relaxing and reconnecting.
A Taste of India Comes to the Maldives
One of the most exciting highlights this season is a special culinary residency with Atrangi by Ritu Dalmia, a beloved Indian restaurant from Dubai. For six nights in June, a small group of guests will get to enjoy a thoughtfully prepared tasting menu featuring both well-known and lesser-known Indian dishes-all with a modern twist that fits perfectly with the island vibe.
But the food journey doesn’t stop there. The resort will also host lively events like the Eid International Market Night on June 6 and a unique Omakase Coffee Series from June 6 to 10. Beyond these events, there are plenty of dining options to explore-from wood-fired pizzas at Shimmers served right on the beach, to inventive Japanese-Peruvian dishes at Kayto, to private picnics on sandbanks and beach barbecues that make every meal feel like a celebration.
Fun and Play for Everyone
Families are at the heart of Jumeirah Olhahali’s summer, with lots of activities designed to bring everyone together. The colorful Olhahali Kite Festival will light up the skies on several dates this summer, while the Kids’ Summer Olympics in the pool add a splash of friendly competition.
There are new games too-mini badminton tournaments and weekly penalty shoot-outs-perfect for some lighthearted fun. Little ones can build their confidence with swimming lessons throughout August, and older kids and adults can learn water sports like wakeboarding and water skiing with expert instructors.
Evenings bring everyone together for beach cinema nights, where families can relax on the sand with snacks and movies under the stars-a perfect way to end a day in paradise.
Wellness That Feels Like Home
If you’re looking to unwind and recharge, the resort’s Talise Spa offers a wonderful range of wellness experiences. From mid-May through June, you can try treatments with visiting expert Dr. Aradhana, who blends ancient healing traditions with modern therapies like sound healing and yogic detox.
For those who like to start their day active, the high-energy Technogym Bootcamps take place twice a week, set against stunning ocean views.
Creativity for the Whole Family
At MURACA Art Studio, families can enjoy creative workshops all summer long. In August, a resident ceramicist will be leading pottery classes, offering a hands-on way to connect, create, and relax together.
Your Perfect Summer Getaway
Jumeirah Olhahali Island has made it easy to plan your summer escape with special offers on private villa stays. Whether you’re traveling as a couple or with the whole family, these deals include extra perks designed to make your stay even more memorable.
If you’re ready to experience a summer full of discovery, connection, and joy in the Maldives, check out the seasonal program and book your stay with Lets Go Maldives.
