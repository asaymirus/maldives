Embark on an immersive artistic experience of 482 works by 51 artists adorning the spaces throughout our hotel. Our contemporary collection includes paintings, sculptures, video montages as well as mixed media.
Artist in Residency Programme
Launched in April 2021, the Artist in Residency Programme is an ongoing initiative at Jumeirah Creekside Hotel to support international and regional artists in advancing their skills through dedicated spaces, enhanced visibility and endorsement, while providing an inspirational setting from which to create unique pieces of art and enjoy living in one of the most diverse cities in the world. At the end of their residency, the artist will be given the opportunity to exhibit their work alongside regionally inspired artworks.
