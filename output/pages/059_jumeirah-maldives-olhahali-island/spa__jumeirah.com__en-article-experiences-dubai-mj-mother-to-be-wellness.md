Discover the new Mother-to-be wellness programme at Talise Spa.
Preparing your body and mind to be a new mother, you can ensure health and well-being are carefully nurtured at Talise Wellness. The program support you and your baby with holistic approaches with certified practitioners.
All programmes include:
Unlimited access to daily group yoga classes
Daily sunrise morning walk
Welcome Wellness Amenity with bath ritual, tea ritual, pillow mist and wellness face mask with guided meditation app
Recommended activities to choose from for this programme:
Mental Wellness | Breathwork Coaching, Art Therapy, Access Bar Therapy
Mindful Movement | Gentle Flow Yoga, Maternity Yoga, Pranayama & Meditation, Sound Healing
Internal wellbeing | Mother & Baby Nutrition Coaching
Restore | Bach Flower, Facial, Mother-to-Be Treatment
Additional activities outside the recommended ones above are available. Please feel free to reach out to explore more activities to choose from.
