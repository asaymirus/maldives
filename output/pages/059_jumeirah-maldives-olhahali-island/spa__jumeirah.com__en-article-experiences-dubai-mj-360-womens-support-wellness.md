Discover the new 360 Women's Support wellness programme at Talise Spa.
Whether your goal is to soothe your mind, invigorate your body or simply learn to love yourself, this holistic programme will feature a therapy, treatment or workshop to target your goal. Appease a busy or tense mind with sound healing meditation, breathwork coaching and qi gong and chant and sing with like-minded women in a kundalini yoga class. You can also reconnect with your playful side in a cacao ceremony, learn from a food coach and feel the warmth of a practitioner’s hands above your body in a reiki session. These activities, along with a massage, will leave you feeling revitalized and back in control.
All programmes include:
Unlimited access to daily group yoga classes
Daily sunrise morning walk
Welcome Wellness Amenity with bath ritual, tea ritual, pillow mist and wellness face mask with guided meditation app
Recommended activities to choose from for this programme:
Mental Wellness | Sound Healing Meditation & Cacao Ceremony, Breathwork Coaching, Art Therapy
Mindful Movement | Restorative Yoga, Kundalini Yoga, Ashtanga Yoga, Hatha yoga, Mindfulness Coaching
Internal wellbeing | Self-Love Food Coaching
Restore | Serenity Ritual, Facials, Reiki, Qi Gong, Bach Flower, Revitalizing Body Massage
