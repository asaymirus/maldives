DESCRIPTION
Where penthouse living rises above paradise, Jumeirah Maldives Olhahali Island reimagines island luxury with contemporary elegance and endless ocean views.
Set in the crystalline waters of the North Malé Atoll, just 55 minutes by speedboat or 15 minutes by seaplane from Malé International Airport, this all-villa resort blends Mediterranean sophistication with the soul of the Maldives. Each of the 67 double-storey residences features a private pool and an exclusive rooftop sky lounge, offering breathtaking perspectives of the turquoise lagoon. Guests can indulge in world-class dining, from Mediterranean flavours at Shimmers to Nikkei fusion at Kayto, unwind at the overwater Talise Spa or explore the island’s vibrant marine life through thrilling water sports and diving adventures. Perfect for couples, families and friends, Jumeirah Maldives Olhahali Island is an inspired escape where modern design, barefoot luxury and panoramic beauty meet in perfect harmony.

Dining
Glow
The heart of the island’s culinary scene, Glow offers all-day dining with a menu that celebrates global flavours and fresh, healthy ingredients. Set beside the ocean with an expansive terrace, it is the perfect spot for relaxed al fresco meals and unforgettable Maldivian sunsets.
Kayto
A captivating fusion of Peruvian and Japanese cuisine, Kayto takes you on a sensory journey through the art of Nikkei dining. Enjoy masterfully crafted sushi and ceviche at the live bar or dine on the outdoor terrace overlooking the shimmering Indian Ocean.
Shimmers
Vibrant and full of energy, Shimmers brings Mediterranean charm to the shores of Olhahali Island. With its laid-back beach club ambience, resident DJ and live music, it is ideal for long, lazy lunches or romantic dinners under the stars, set against stunning ocean views.
Café Lounge
At the centre of the island’s social buzz, Café Lounge is the perfect place to enjoy expertly brewed coffee by master baristas. A casual yet stylish spot for a morning pick-me-up or an afternoon pause with a view.
Private Dining
Celebrate life’s special moments with a bespoke dining experience on the Sunset Pier. Whether it is a romantic dinner or a private gathering, every detail is crafted for intimacy and unforgettable oceanfront memories.
Gelateria
A sweet retreat for all ages, Gelateria serves handcrafted artisan ice cream and frozen delights in homemade waffle cones, the perfect island indulgence.
