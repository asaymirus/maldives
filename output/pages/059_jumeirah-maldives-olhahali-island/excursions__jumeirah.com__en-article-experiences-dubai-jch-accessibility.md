Paving the way for a truly inclusive stay
At Jumeirah Creekside Hotel, accessibility is not merely a standard but an ethos of utmost refinement where every detail is meticulously orchestrated to envelop our cherished guests in an experience of unparalleled refinement and inclusivity. Our dedicated team undergoes specialized training to ensure they are sensitive to the unique needs of guests on the autism spectrum epitomizing the pinnacle of personalized service.
Moreover, Jumeirah Creekside Hotel proudly secures the prestigious Certified Autism Center™ (CAC) status, bestowed by the revered International Board of Credentialing and Continuing Education Standards (IBCCES). The CAC designation, a symbol of excellence, reaffirms the hotel's commitment to providing a haven of warmth and inclusivity for autistic and sensory-sensitive guests, and their families.
