Discover the new Family wellness programme at Talise Spa.
Take time out from work, school and social engagements to bond as a family through group activities with your loved ones. Designed for up to four participants, this programme fuses fun pastimes such as creative sketching and chanting in kundalini yoga with active ones such as beach fitness so your kids can burn off some energy, as well as plenty of calmer activities including tai chi, an aroma massage and sound healing meditation. With a focus on togetherness, the pursuits are designed like team building exercises to bring you and your brood closer together.
All programmes include:
Unlimited access to daily group yoga classes
Daily sunrise morning walk
Visit to the Turtle Rehabilitation Sanctuary
Welcome Wellness Amenity with bath ritual, tea ritual, pillow mist and wellness face mask with guided meditation app
Recommended activities to choose from for this programme:
Mental Wellness | Art Therapy, Family Constellations Workshop
Mindful Movement | Restorative/Kundalini Yoga, Yoga Nidra & Pranayama, Sound Healing Therapy
Internal wellbeing | Family Nutrition Workshop
Restore | Tai Chi, Bespoke Aroma Massage
Additional activities outside the recommended ones above are available. Please feel free to reach out to explore more activities to choose from.
Duration:
3, 5 or 7 days
Price:
AED 14,500 per family | 3-day programme inclusive of 6 activities of your choice
AED 22,200 per family | 5-day programme inclusive of 10 activities of your choice
AED 28,000 per family | 7-day programme inclusive of 14 activities of your choice
Please note that the package is for up to 4 family members.
