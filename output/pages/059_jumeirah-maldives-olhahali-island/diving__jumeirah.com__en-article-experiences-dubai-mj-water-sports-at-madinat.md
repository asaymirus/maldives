Water Sports Centre
Explore the clear and inviting waters of the Arabian Gulf while trying out the exciting activities on offer at our Water Sports Centre. From flyboarding to standup paddleboarding, waterskiing, banana rides, kayaking and wakeboarding, there’s a wide range of watersports to enjoy.
We can also arrange for you to charter a boat, enjoy the thrill of deep-sea fishing, lounge on a luxury motorboat or cruise across the sea in a chartered sailing yacht.
