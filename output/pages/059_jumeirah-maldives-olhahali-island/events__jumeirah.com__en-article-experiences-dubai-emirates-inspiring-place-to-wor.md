An inspiring place to work
With everything you need for comfortable business travel and effective working when you’re away from the office, Jumeirah Emirates Towers is a perfect base for corporate travellers.
Enjoy a fully equipped room
All rooms at Jumeirah Emirates Towers come with a comfortable business workplace, complimentary broadband internet, interactive television and hi-fi system.
Club Executive Rooms are also equipped with 32-inch Bang and Olufsen TV screens with integrated CD, DVD and iPod interface.
All suites feature an all-in-one fax, printer and scanner to make remote working easier; guests in the Presidential and Royal Suites have the use of a laptop during their stay. And to help you stay in touch with events back home, enjoy a complimentary local English language newspaper delivered to your room or suite each morning.
Meeting rooms
Jumeirah Emirates Towers is ideally situated for business, overlooking the financial district and within a short drive of the Dubai World Trade Center and Dubai International Financial Center.
The hotel is also a perfect venue for business meetings, with state-of-the-art meeting rooms equipped with advanced technological equipment - and supported by our professional team.
