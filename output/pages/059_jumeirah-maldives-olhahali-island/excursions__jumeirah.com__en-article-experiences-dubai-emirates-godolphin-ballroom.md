Our two impressive, fully serviced venue spaces have room for up to 1,000 guests, making them the perfect place to hold a memorable conference, event or wedding celebration.
Godolphin Ballroom
The elegant Godolphin Ballroom is a sophisticated venue for any occasion, with beautifully crafted fittings, walnut panelling, hand-woven carpets, high ceilings and crystal chandeliers. The ballroom can be divided into three separate function areas, all with access to state-of-the-art audio-visual technology, while refreshment breaks or smaller exhibitions can be held in the pre-function area.
Location
: Conference Centre, Jumeirah Emirates Towers
Seating capacity
: Up to 1,000 guests
Terrace at The Towers
Another versatile venue with stunning views of the Dubai skyline, Terrace at The Towers is ideal for both intimate occasions and large events – from corporate dinners and product launches, to weddings and cocktail receptions. This stylish event space also has an exclusive entrance from Jumeirah Emirates Towers offices.
Location
: Level 2, between the office tower and the hotel tower
Seating capacity
: Up to 850 guests
