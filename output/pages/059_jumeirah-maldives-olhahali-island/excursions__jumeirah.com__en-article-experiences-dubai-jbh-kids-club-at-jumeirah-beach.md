Terms & Conditions
We accommodate children between four and twelve years of age.
Any child showing signs of illness will not be permitted entry.
One parent is permitted to enter the Jumeirah Circle for children aged four years and below.
Children must be promptly collected after each session to allow the venue to be properly sanitised.
Please note that, in line with Dubai Municipality guidelines, the Jumeirah Circle operates at a reduced capacity.
Sessions run throughout the day, however, to avoid disappointment, we recommend pre-booking.
For the safety and comfort of all, parents or guardians are required to remain on the premises during the session.
In-house guests are entitled to one complimentary session per child, per day.
Advance bookings are required as availability is limited.
