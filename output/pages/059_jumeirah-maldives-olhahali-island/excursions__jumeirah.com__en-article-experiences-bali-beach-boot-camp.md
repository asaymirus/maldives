Stay fit throughout your holiday surrounded by the beauty of Bali's nature with our Beach Boot Camp. Improve both physical and mental endurance as well as muscle mass through sessions of cardio, and strength training.
Boot camp is an intensive and highly-effective fitness program, blending instinctive, natural and functional movement. Combine positive energy and motivation with our supportive trainers and a solid group bond. Every session will be a fun challenge to achieve your personal best. Make the most of your time; make every second count. The goal is to push through your limits with effective routines, and achieve stabilised core muscles and coordination, leaving you feeling inspired and motivated. This is the start of a good day.
