Adventures on the Arabian Gulf
From spices to locally made handcrafts and jewelry, Souk Madinat Jumeirah is an authentic Arabian bazaar, full of incredible finds. Wander through the stands and stalls under ornate lanterns and carved wood, immersing yourself in the shopping culture of this traditional Middle Eastern marketplace. Arrive by car, or by
abra
– a traditional Emirati boat - and stay for an hour, a day or an evening.
Souk Madinat Jumeirah is currently home to over 20 restaurants and cafés serving cuisines from across the globe. Most eateries provide picture-perfect views of the Arabian architecture and meandering waterways.
Opening Times
Shops: 10:00 – 23:00
Restaurants: 08:00 – 02:00
Parking: Self-parking and valet parking available.
Additional information: Souk Madinat Jumeirah is wheelchair friendly, with access via the main entrance elevator leading to the Shopping Mall Level, and Shopping Alleys elevator leading to the Promenade Level.
