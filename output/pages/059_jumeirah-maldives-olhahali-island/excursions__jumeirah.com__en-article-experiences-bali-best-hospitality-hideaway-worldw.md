Jumeirah Bali has been awarded the prestigious title of
Best Hospitality Hideaway Worldwide
at the 12th annual
Connoisseur Circle Hospitality Awards
,
a momentous recognition of the resort’s unwavering commitment to excellence, exceptional guest experiences, and its refined vision of modern luxury.
Selected by a distinguished panel of hospitality experts and the well-travelled Connoisseur Circle readership, this coveted accolade celebrates Jumeirah Bali’s status as a sanctuary of serenity and sophistication, tucked away on the dramatic cliffs of Uluwatu. Known for its harmonious blend of Balinese tradition and contemporary elegance, Jumeirah Bali continues to set the standard for secluded, soulful escapes in Asia.
Inspired by the grandeur architecture of the Majapahit Kingdom, the resort offers guests a rare glimpse into Indonesia’s rich royal heritage, translated into a modern-day haven of peace and privacy. Every archway, courtyard, and ornamental detail echoes the timeless legacy of this golden era, creating a setting that is both majestic and deeply grounding. At its soul, Jumeirah Bali embodies a harmonious blend of Javanese and Balinese royal culture—an elegant synthesis of ritual, refinement, and spiritual symbolism that enriches every guest experience.
Equally integral to Jumeirah Bali’s identity is its strong commitment to sustainability. From eco-conscious architectural practices to waste reduction initiatives and support for local communities, the resort integrates environmental responsibility into every aspect of its operations. This mindful approach not only preserves the island’s natural beauty but also ensures a meaningful and lasting impact for generations to come.
The Connoisseur Circle Hospitality Awards have long served as a benchmark for excellence in the travel industry. Now in their twelfth year, the awards recognise the finest hotels, airlines, and cruise lines across ten curated categories. From intimate boutique retreats to pioneering wellness resorts, this year’s 50 nominees represented the pinnacle of global travel.
"The Connoisseur Hospitality Awards are among the most important accolades in luxury tourism," said Simone Dressler, Editor-in-Chief of Connoisseur Circle. "Our readers particularly appreciate the awards’ independence and expertise. Every nominated hotel has been personally visited and anonymously evaluated by our team of professionals throughout the year, giving these awards a high level of credibility and trust."
Jumeirah Bali’s recognition in the 'Best Hospitality Hideaway Worldwide' category reaffirms its dedication to creating immersive experiences rooted in well-being, cultural richness, and meaningful connection. This award reflects the heart of Jumeirah’s philosophy: delivering luxury that inspires and endures.
With this honour, Jumeirah Bali joins a distinguished list of global hospitality leaders who continue to captivate and elevate the travel experience for discerning guests around the world.
