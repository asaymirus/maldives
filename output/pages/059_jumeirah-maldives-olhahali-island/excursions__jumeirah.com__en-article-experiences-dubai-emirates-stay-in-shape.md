Conveniently located in the Boulevard of Jumeirah Emirates Towers, and complimentary for hotel guests, Talise Fitness is the perfect place to work out at any time. With the latest equipment, impactful time-focused training programmes, and on-hand expert advice, Talise Fitness will help you achieve your fitness goals with ease.
Group exercise classes
Join one of our group exercise classes which aim to strengthen, energise and improve flexibility and balance. The weekly schedule includes yoga, HIIT (high intensity interval training) and BodyPump classes, with male- and female-only classes also available.
