Dubai has evolved into one of the world’s most popular shopping destinations. From the traditional gold souk to the opulent Dubai Mall, there are hundreds of places to enjoy browsing and buying.
Inside the Jumeirah Zabeel Saray resort, we’ve created the Avenue of Indulgence, a shopping promenade filled with some of the best luxury brands from the region, offering fine gifts, accessories and jewellery. As well as shopping, The Avenue is dotted with places to relax, from restful lounges to high-end bars and restaurants.
Stroll along grand halls with marble-paved floors, where each boutique is decorated with Ottoman-style archways, high ceilings and glittering chandeliers.
This extravagant shopping destination is a combination of elegant craft and nostalgic design. Guests can take advantage of boutiques such as:
