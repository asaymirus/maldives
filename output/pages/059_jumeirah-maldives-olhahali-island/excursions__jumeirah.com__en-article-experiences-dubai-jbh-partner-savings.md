In our journey to ensuring your family holiday in Dubai is extra special, we have partnered with some of the city’s most exciting spaces. Whether you’re travelling with little ones in tow and hoping for a day full of splashes or encouraging junior environmentalists to learn more about the natural world, we have something for all travellers to enjoy. Here is a selection of our favourite partners to help you plan your next getaway:
Inside Burj Al Arab Tour
Discover untold stories about our iconic Burj Al Arab on an exclusive tour of this architectural marvel. From design wonders and photogenic moments to exceptional dining experiences and custom-made souvenirs, embark on a journey through one of the UAE’s favourite landmarks.
Laguna Waterpark
Enjoy 40% off your visit to Dubai’s favourite marine paradise, overlooking the Arabian Gulf. Experience an adventure to remember with attractions such as the Manta family raft ride, The Tipping Bucket freefall and surfing with WaveOz 180.
The Green Planet
Calling all budding ecologists and those with a green thumb. Step into a tropical forest at The Green Planet bio-dome where you can set up a meeting with the city’s most-loved sloth duo, get up-close with many red-bellied piranhas or find yourself transported into the depths of the Amazon rainforest during the evening thunderstorm show. As our guest, you’ll save upto 40% on your green expedition.
