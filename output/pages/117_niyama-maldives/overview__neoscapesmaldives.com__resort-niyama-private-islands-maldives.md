DESCRIPTION
The Maldives’ Only Twin-Island Playground
Ranked among the Best Resorts in the World by Condé Nast Traveller, Niyama offers a rare escape across two lush islands, Play and Chill, where you set the rhythm of paradise. Discover 134 beachfront and overwater villas and pavilions, from intimate one-bedroom retreats to the ultra-exclusive Crescent of five luxury pool villas. Spend your days surfing at the resort’s private wave, cycling through jungle trails alive with birdsong, or setting off on dolphin safaris while little explorers enjoy the Indian Ocean’s largest kids’ club.
When it is time to slow down, retreat to the Drift Spa, dine beneath the treetops at award-winning Nest, or experience the Maldives’ first underwater restaurant, Subsix. With 10 distinctive dining venues, recently refurbished villas, sensory new culinary concepts and a private seaplane lounge for seamless arrivals, Niyama invites you to play hard, chill deep and live life your way in nature’s most luxurious playground.

Dining
Blu
Blu offers Mediterranean-inspired, family-friendly dining by the infinity pool on Play. Begin your day with smoothies, fresh juices, and gourmet crostinis, or create your own egg breakfast. At lunch and dinner, enjoy salads, sandwiches, seafood, pizzas, pastas, and Asian-inspired dishes, with gluten-free options available. Sunshine Thakurus circulate with iced water, coconuts, fruit skewers, and towels, ensuring a relaxed and effortless dining experience in a bright, casual-chic setting for guests of all ages.
Epicure
Epicure offers chic, poolside dining with a breakfast buffet and all-day a la carte menu of international cuisine. Begin your morning with flaky pastries, fresh fruit salads, or omelettes made with fine cheeses and herbs. At lunch and dinner, enjoy light bites, seafood, and global dishes while taking in infinity pool and ocean views. The venue also features a glass wine cellar with private tastings and 260 wine labels, along with a selection of 18 teas for a relaxed, indulgent experience.
Edge
Edge is an exclusive overwater dining destination accessible only by boat. Enjoy panoramic views from a private jetty or floor-to-ceiling windows indoors while savoring modern cuisine inspired by local flavors and the day’s freshest catch. Signature cocktails complement the experience in this chic floating restaurant and lounge. With gentle ocean breezes, stunning turquoise waters, and sophisticated dishes, Edge offers a refined, intimate setting for fine dining in the Maldives.
Nest
Perched six metres above the ground among banyan trees, Nest offers an unforgettable treetop dining experience. The menu features Asian specialties from Thai to Teppanyaki, crafted with fresh seasonal produce from the chef’s island garden. Guests can enjoy intimate family meals or romantic dinners in a stunning jungle setting. Navigate wooden walkways to your private perch and savor avant-garde presentations, chilled sashimi, and the most spectacular views, making Nest one of the Maldives’ most dazzling Asian restaurants.
Subsix
Located 500 metres offshore and accessible by boat, Subsix is a surreal underwater dining experience in the Maldives. Descend a three-tier staircase into a glowing marine world with clam-inspired seating and chandeliers resembling radiant coral. Enjoy Nikkei cuisine through champagne breakfasts, subaquatic lunches, gourmet dinners, or adults-only glow parties, all accompanied by the enchanting reef below. This extraordinary restaurant combines fine dining with an unforgettable underwater atmosphere, offering a truly immersive and one-of-a-kind culinary journey.
Tribal
Tribal brings the flavors of Africa, Central, and South America to the Maldives with a fine dining touch. Hidden behind a boma, guests dine beneath a jungle canopy by torchlight, enjoying open-flame dishes such as smoky ribs, ostrich bobotie, boerewors, and lobster. Complete the meal with a Jebena coffee pot featuring freshly ground African beans. Tribal combines adventurous, bold flavors with immersive ambiance, creating a unique culinary journey that celebrates tribal cuisine in an elegant, fireside setting.
Dune
Dune offers chic barefoot dining on white sands with ocean views and refreshing breezes. Lounge poolside on beanbags while enjoying deli-style beach snacks, fresh pizzas, gourmet burgers, sandwiches, and desserts. Sip fruity daiquiris, mocktails, or sundowner cocktails as the sun sets over the water. As night falls, the beach club comes alive with a DJ. Dip in the pool, enjoy shisha, and savor a relaxed, stylish beachside experience at one of the Maldives’ most vibrant al fresco lounges.
Fahrenheit Bar
Perched on the rooftop, Fahrenheit Bar offers spectacular ocean and sunset views from a sleek indoor lounge and outdoor deck with transparent walls. Sip specialty cocktails or sparkling wine while the sky shifts from gold to pink. As evening falls, chilled beats and live music create a lively yet relaxed atmosphere. Cozy seating and the panoramic surroundings make it the perfect spot to unwind, watch the sunset, and enjoy a vibrant Maldivian nightlife experience.
The Deli
The Deli is a gourmet grab-and-go destination in the Maldives, offering snacks and treats for every appetite. Choose from antipasti tapas, paninis, ciabattas, and sweet pastries, or indulge in homemade ice cream, gelati, and frozen yogurt. Complement your selection with a smoothie, milkshake, or freshly brewed coffee. Perfect for quick bites on the move or leisurely snacking, The Deli combines convenience with flavorful, high-quality offerings in a relaxed, delicatessen-style setting.
Dine-in
Niyama’s Dine-In service brings gourmet experiences to the comfort of your villa, offering sunrise spa cuisine, lavish breakfasts, and indulgent dinners including caviar blinis, Wagyu sirloin, and truffle-topped eggs. Children’s favorites are available for family poolside meals. Guests can also order from the nine on-island dining venues, enjoying floating breakfasts over water or beachside meals at any time. Dine-In combines flexibility, privacy, and world-class flavors for a truly bespoke Maldivian culinary experience.
Destination Dining
Destination Dining offers personalized culinary experiences for those seeking adventure or intimate moments. Enjoy gourmet picnics on a deserted island, a bonfire with barbequed mahi by the water’s edge, or a sunset degustation inspired by the ocean’s bounty. Your private chef and Thakuru bring each experience to life, paired with specialty cocktails or espresso martinis. With toes in the sand and spectacular ocean views, Destination Dining transforms every meal into a memorable Maldivian celebration.
Took Took – Asian Street Food Dinner on the Beach
Took Took brings Asian street food to the Maldives with a weekly beachside dining experience. Chefs grill dishes over coals while guests relax under bamboo teepees draped in fairy lights and hurricane lamps. Sink into beach armchairs with toes in the sand, enjoy coral-hued sunsets, and sip bubbly while tasting creatively presented street food. This unique, casual yet memorable setting combines flavors, ambiance, and ocean views for an unforgettable evening.
Food Play
Food Play offers a private cooking class in the Maldives, where guests can chop, sizzle, and sauté in a stunning open-air kitchen. Learn to prepare flavorful dishes, enjoy your creations for lunch, and take home a spice box and recipes to recreate the experience. Set against breathtaking ocean views, this interactive culinary adventure is perfect for food lovers looking to combine learning, fun, and gourmet indulgence. Activities are subject to weather conditions.
