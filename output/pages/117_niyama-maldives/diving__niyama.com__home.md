Where is Niyama Private Islands  Maldives located?
Niyama Private Islands Maldives resort
, the best private island in Maldives, is nestled southwest of Malé in the pristine Dhaalu Atoll, renowned for its crystal-clear waters and world-class dive sites.
How do I get to Niyama Private Islands Maldives?
There are two convenient ways to reach Niyama Private Islands Maldives from Malé: a scenic 45-minute seaplane ride or a 35-minute domestic flight to Dhaalu Airport followed by a 10-minute speedboat transfer to the resort. Please note that seaplane transfers operate only during daylight hours. Our reservations team will handle all travel arrangements for a smooth arrival experience.
What is the difference between Play and Chill at Niyama Private Islands Maldives?
Niyama Private Islands Maldives, one of the best twin-island resorts in the Maldives, features two unique islands—Play and Chill—connected by a picturesque boardwalk, with guests free to enjoy both at any time. Play is popular among families, surfers, and divers for its lively atmosphere, while Chill offers a more peaceful setting with the spa, gym, and all overwater villas. Both islands are home to a variety of dining experiences.
Is Niyama Private Islands Maldives family friendly?
Niyama Private Islands Maldives, the best family-friendly Maldives resort, offers something for everyone – from solo adventurers and couples to families. Children can enjoy one of the largest kids’ clubs in the Maldives, complete with a splash park, climbing wall, fun activities, and exciting outings.
How many restaurants are there at Niyama Private Islands Maldives?
Niyama Private Islands Maldives offers a diverse culinary experience with six restaurants, a café, a deli, a bar, food trucks, and exciting pop-up venues. Guests can also enjoy in-villa dining or unforgettable destination dining experiences set in stunning island locations.
What leisure facilities are there at Niyama Private Islands Maldives?
With two islands, Niyama Private Islands Maldives offers some of the most extensive resort facilities in the Maldives. Guests can enjoy surf, dive, marine, and water sports centres, various sports courts, a yoga pavilion, indoor and jungle gyms, a spa, an outdoor cinema, and much more.
When is surf season at Niyama Private Islands Maldives?
You can surf year-round at Niyama Private Islands Maldives – the Maldives’ most exclusive surf destination – with the biggest swells typically rolling in from April to October.
Does Niyama Private Islands Maldives host weddings?
Niyama Private Islands Maldives is picture-perfect for
a Maldives destination wedding
, as well as proposals, vow renewals, honeymoons, and anniversaries. With a dedicated events team on site and the option to fly in entertainment, every celebration is tailored to perfection.
