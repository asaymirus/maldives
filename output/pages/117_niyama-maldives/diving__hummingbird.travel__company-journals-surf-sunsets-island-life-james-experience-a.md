Our Business Development Manager, James, recently experienced Niyama Private Islands Maldives first-hand following its recent refurbishment. As a passionate surfer, James was excited to explore one of the Maldives’ most renowned surf spots, while discovering the dining, marine life and unique experiences that make Niyama stand out.

The Maldives is famous for its picture-perfect beaches, turquoise lagoons and barefoot luxury, but after experiencing Niyama first-hand, James discovered that the resort offers something beyond the traditional island escape.
“What stood out to me about Niyama was the balance it strikes. You have everything you’d expect from a luxury Maldives resort: incredible beaches, beautiful villas and plenty of places to unwind, but there’s also so much to discover. Whether that’s surfing at Vodi, exploring the reef or enjoying the dining experiences, there’s always something new to experience.”
For James, this blend of relaxation and adventure is what makes Niyama such a strong option for travellers looking for a Maldives experience with a little more personality.
For James, one of the highlights of the trip was experiencing Vodi, Niyama’s famous surf break.
"Vodi is an incredible wave. During the May to September season, it’s a powerful, fast wave which is definitely suited to intermediate and advanced surfers. From November to April, it becomes much more accessible and is an amazing experience for beginner to intermediate surfers too."
For guests completely new to surfing, Niyama also offers a smaller channel wave, making it a great introduction before progressing onto bigger breaks.
Agent insight:
Niyama is a fantastic option for clients who want a Maldives escape with a more active element, particularly surfers, adventure seekers and travellers who want more than just relaxing by the pool.
Beyond surfing, James found that Niyama’s marine life is another major highlight.
"The snorkelling around the island is incredible. In front of Villa 134 on Play Island, there’s a great reef area with lots of fish and it’s probably one of the best snorkelling spots on the property."
For guests wanting to explore further, James recommends the waters around Villa 61, where the deeper coral area is home to a resident turtle and the chance to spot barracuda.
Niyama’s dining scene was another highlight for James, with a variety of experiences that allow guests to enjoy something different throughout their stay.
Epicure stood out as a fantastic central hub, offering an impressive buffet selection and a lively atmosphere, while Blu provides a quieter alternative for guests looking to mix up their dining experiences.
“What I loved about Niyama was the variety. Guests can enjoy a relaxed meal one evening, then experience something completely different the next, it really adds to the feeling that every day can be different.”
However, James’ standout dining experience was Nest, a restaurant unlike any other.
"Nest was a real highlight. It feels like you’re genuinely in the middle of a jungle paradise. Being surrounded by the sounds of birds and sitting amongst the treetops makes it such an immersive and unique experience."
For honeymooners, food lovers or clients celebrating a special occasion, Nest is an experience James believes should not be missed.
For the perfect Maldives evening, James recommends two must-visit locations.
"Surf Shack is a really cool place to check out, the cocktails are incredible and it’s a great spot to catch the sunset."
For something more unique, Edge offers sunset cocktails and dining in the middle of the ocean, reached by boat and located close to Subsix, Niyama’s famous underwater dining venue.
"Edge is such a cool concept. Being out in the middle of the sea makes it feel like a real experience rather than just another restaurant."
Recommend Vodi for surf-loving clients, with different seasons offering different levels of experience.
Highlight the house reef for guests interested in snorkelling and marine life.
Encourage guests to book Nest early; it’s one of Niyama’s most memorable experiences.
Suggest Surf Shack or Edge for unforgettable sunset moments.
From James’ first-hand experience, Niyama is a strong choice for:
Couples wanting a Maldives escape with more experiences and personality
Active travellers looking to combine luxury with adventure
Surf enthusiasts searching for a world-class wave
Food-focused travellers looking for unique dining experiences
Repeat Maldives visitors wanting something beyond the traditional island stay
