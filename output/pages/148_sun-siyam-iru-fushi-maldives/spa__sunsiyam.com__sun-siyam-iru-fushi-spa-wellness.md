Rejuvenating Retreat
Ayurveda
Begin your Ayurvedic journey with a personalized consultation from our expert doctor. Rooted in ancient Indian traditions, Ayurveda focuses on holistic healing. Our range of transformative rituals includes Shirodhara and Ayur Shakti, a rejuvenating four-hands massage. Enhance your wellness with our tailored 3 to 14-day packages.
The Spa by Thalgo
Thalgo, the world leader in marine cosmetology, was created by pharmacist André Bouclet out of a fascination with the ocean and its numerous skin benefits, harnessing the therapeutic properties and mineral content of various different types of algae.
Massages
Indulge in a variety of massage experiences tailored to your needs, from relaxing Swedish and jet-lag combatting techniques to the invigorating pressure of Thai, Balinese, and Japanese Shiatsu. Enhance your connection with a personalized couples' massage lesson with our expert therapists.
Traditional Chinese Medicine
Explore the healing arts of Traditional Chinese Medicine, a practice dating back over 2,000 years. Experience therapies including acupuncture, moxibustion, cupping, and Gua Sha, all designed to promote energy flow and restore balance.
Maldivian Rituals
Discover the ancient wisdom of Dhivehi beys verikan, traditional Maldivian medicine, at The Spa. Experience local herbalism and bodywork techniques passed down through generations, while supporting island community initiatives with proceeds from each treatment.
How to Spa
Our spa is open daily from 9:00 AM to 9:00 PM. It is located between Iru Restaurant and Islander's Grill.
Please consult our
Spa Menu
for a full list of treatments.
We recommend booking in advance. Our team will gladly show you around and help you choose the treatment that’s right for you. You can also book by phone: Contact the Spa at extension 189, or dial 0 to connect through WISH.
To avoid a 50% charge, please notify us at least 12 hours in advance of any cancellations. Cancellations made without prior notice will incur a 100% charge. If you are late, we may need to shorten your treatment to accommodate the next guest.
Please arrive 15 minutes before your scheduled treatment time. This allows you time to fill out our health assessment form and unwind. Late arrivals may result in a shortened treatment time if we are unable to reschedule the next guest.
For your comfort and safety:
We recommend not bringing jewelry or valuables to the spa. The spa is not responsible for lost or damaged personal items.
To maintain a peaceful environment, please leave electronic devices in your suite or set them to airplane mode.
Kindly speak quietly while in the spa to preserve its serene atmosphere.
The spa is not suitable for infants or children under the age of 3. Please be mindful when bringing young children.
Our spa grounds are smoke-free.
Yes. Spa guests may use our hydrotherapy area, steam room, sauna, and plunge pools. We provide bathrobes and disposable underwear for your comfort.
Please inform us of any health issues, allergies, or conditions that may affect your treatment. If you are pregnant or have given birth within the last 3 months, please notify our Spa reception. We offer pregnancy treatments for the second and third trimesters, as well as for women up to three months postpartum.
Avoid sunbathing immediately after your treatment.
Gentlemen: For optimal results, we recommend shaving before your treatment, but not immediately prior.
Please remove contact lenses before your facial treatment.
All treatments will be charged to your suite and appear on your account at check-out. Prices are listed in USD and include a 10% service charge, along with applicable government taxes.
