Get ready for an extraordinary Easter celebration at Sun Siyam Iru Fushi from April 17 to 21, 2025. This magical event is designed to transport families and friends into a world of adventure, where pirates, mermaids, and enchanting surprises await. From thrilling activities to unforgettable dining experiences, every moment promises excitement for guests of all ages.
A Spellbinding Performance by Nicolas Subra
The highlight of the festivities is none other than the renowned French magician and mentalist, Nicolas Subra. With over 15 years of experience and performances in more than 20 countries, Nicolas has mesmerized audiences worldwide with his mind-bending illusions. His ability to blur the lines between reality and fantasy has earned him numerous televised appearances, making his show a must-see spectacle that will leave guests in awe.
Exciting Adventures for the Little Ones
Children can dive into the magic of Easter with a series of fun-filled activities, from the classic Easter Egg Hunt and Bunny Arrival to the enchanting Mermaid Fairy Tale experience. Budding artists can unleash their creativity with Easter Egg Painting Fun, while thrill-seekers can join the action-packed Pirates & Mermaids Battle, Splashy Pool Party, and Egg-citing Karaoke. For a more relaxed experience, families can gather for movie nights and immersive storytelling sessions under the stars.
Indulge in Exceptional Culinary Delights
The Easter celebration extends to an exquisite array of dining experiences. Wine lovers can savor fine selections at the Easter Wine Tasting while enjoying the smooth sounds of live saxophone music. The Pirates Wine Cellar Dinner offers an elegant dining experience, while the Easter Cocktail Reception presents the perfect opportunity to toast the occasion alongside the resort’s management team. Guests can also delight in beachfront dining with the Sunday Lobster Beach BBQ or soak up the lively atmosphere of the Pool Party Vibes, complete with a tempting lobster grilling station.
