Iru Fushi is located in Noonu Atoll, 91 Nautical miles from Malé International Airport. With a land area of approximately 210,000sqm, the island is naturally beautiful with stretches of white sandy beaches and an abundance of tropical vegetation. Iru Fushi is reached by a 45 minute scenic sea plane flight direct from Malé international airport crossing over to the northern atolls where the geographical formation of the islands and the variety of fauna and flora are unique. The virgin landscapes of this territory lend uniqueness to the product and a feeling of serenity and contentment settles within you the moment you arrive.
Villas and Suites
Designed to complement nature, yet incorporating luxury and modernity, all the villas and suites, in Iru Fushi reflect understated luxury and a rustic ambiance.
Dining
Drawing heavily on the local fresh fish and seafood, the Iru Fushi Chefs are ready to conjure up succulent and tempting cuisine from around the world, be it a romantic 6 course dinner under the stars or a leisurely alfresco lunch on the beach. Wherever your whims may take you, we make sure that an enticing meal awaits you.
Sun Spa
Set within the lush vegetation of the island is the Sun Spa sanctuary. In keeping with the Sun Spa philosophy, the spa at Iru Fushi will take a holistic approach to health and wellness derived from time tested procedures using the Five Elements and traditional ayurveda.
Each of the fifteen Spa Pavilions is customized according to the treatment taken; be it aromatherapy or hydrotherapy. The wall-to-wall sliding doors lead out to a spacious relaxation deck overlooking the beach.
The main complex houses the reception area, hydrotherapy pools, steam, sauna and changing rooms.
