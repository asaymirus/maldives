The
Sun Siyam Iru Fushi, located in the Noonu Atoll Maldives, introduces a special festive programme with a host of family activities included. Water sports, dive trips and soothing spa treatments all add to a celebratory atmosphere during this special time of the year from 24
th
December 2014 to 8
th
January 2015 for guests of all ages to enjoy.
The
Sun Siyam Iru Fushi presents a series of all-day programmes including tempting culinary occasions with dedicated festive menus, nightly entertainment featuring DJs and live bands, a New Year’s countdown on the beach with an exciting flyboard show as well as children’s activities and many more surprises for guests to share a memorable holiday with loved ones.
During this holiday season,
The
Sun Siyam Iru Fushi extends a special rate of USD475.00 per person for a 3-day Wellness Retreat including Hydrotherapy, massage therapy, Shirodhara and facial therapy at The Spa by Thalgo France. Younger guests are also pampered by a spa menu ‘Sweet Pea Spa’ with chocolate treats and fruity delights. The Water Sports centre will offer Feel the Sky and Enjoy the Ride – Jet Ski and Flyboard 30-minute session at a special rate of USD500.00 for up to two guests and can also enjoy a Sailing Adventure of fishing, snorkelling or sailing to an uninhabited island at USD90.00 per person.
A 52 acre island paradise,
The
Sun Siyam Iru Fushi is home to 221 luxury ocean-front villas, a 20-room tropical spa and a vast range of activities and local explorations with a focus on the surrounding community. The resort caters exceptionally well to both couples and families offering boutique service on a grand scale.
