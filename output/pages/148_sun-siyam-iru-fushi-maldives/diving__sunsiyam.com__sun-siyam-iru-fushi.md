Sun Siyam Iru Fushi
Must-Do
Thanburudhoo Venture
Climb aboard and set sail to a nearby uninhabited island, a true haven for adventure seekers. Explore your own private paradise as you snorkel among vibrant reefs, spot curious marine life, and soak up the sun on pristine sands. Cap off the day with a delicious beachfront lunch, surrounded only by the sparkling ocean and the gentle island breeze.
Intimate Maldivian Moonlight Dinner
Savor a private family gathering or a romantic dinner for two beneath the Maldivian moonlight. Set up exclusively on the soft white sands, with impeccable service and a carefully curated gourmet menu, this magical beachfront experience promises an evening of unforgettable flavors, ambiance, and memories under the stars.
Craft Classes
Unleash your creativity using materials sourced directly from the island. Turn palm leaves, shells, or driftwood into your own unique masterpiece, or explore the versatility of the coconut by crafting a handmade basket, sun hat, or fan. It’s a hands-on experience that combines island culture, artistry, and a tangible souvenir to take home.
Floating Breakfast
Enjoy a floating breakfast in the luxury of your own private pool. Start your day in style with a choice of continental, full English, or a decadent champagne breakfast, all presented on a beautifully arranged floating tray. It’s a serene, sun-soaked way to begin the day.
Nature walks inland
Discover the island’s vibrant inland on a serene nature walk. As we explore the trails together, discover hidden gems tucked within lush vegetation from native plants to fascinating wildlife. Learn about the delicate balance of this ecosystem and connect with the incredible flora and fauna that make the island so uniquely alive.
Kids are Kings
At our resort, kids truly are kings. Up to two children under 15 can sleep and dine for free when sharing a villa with two adults. Thoughtfully designed for families, this ensures everyone can relax, unwind, and enjoy quality time together completely fuss-free and full of memorable moments.
Specialty cooking classes
Our hands-on cooking classes invite you to expand your culinary skills, experiment with fresh ingredients, and create dishes that delight the senses. It’s a flavorful journey that promises both learning and unforgettable memories.
Conservation Snorkeling
Join our marine biologist for a snorkelling experience focused on the vibrant life of the lagoon and house reef. Using simple guides and charts, help collect data on coral health and fish populations, then discuss your observations to gain a deeper understanding of the rich marine ecosystem surrounding the island. It’s a unique opportunity to connect with the ocean and contribute to its preservation.
