Overview
Property Location
Set on a private island in the Noonu Atoll, this five-star Maldives retreat showcases pristine white sands, crystal-clear waters, and a secluded sanctuary designed for both couples and families seeking an immersive island escape.
Room
Luxury villas and retreats rise above turquoise lagoons or sit directly along the beach, featuring options such as Deluxe Beach Villas, Water Villas, Horizon Water Villas, Infinity Water Villas, and expansive signature retreats, many enhanced with private pools and panoramic ocean views.
Amenities
Resort highlights include an award-winning spa with over 165 treatments set within lush island surroundings, a fully equipped diving centre exploring the vibrant Nemo Reef, curated leisure experiences, and a wide variety of water and land-based activities that blend adventure with relaxation.
Dining
A diverse culinary scene spans 14 restaurants and bars, featuring experiences from beachside Asian cuisine at Bamboo and French-inspired dishes at Flavours to global buffet selections at Iru Restaurant, Italian classics at The Trio, and refined wine tastings at The Cellar.
Business Other Amenities
Tailored celebrations and events unfold in spectacular island settings, complemented by bespoke wedding experiences, curated gatherings, and personalized services designed to create memorable occasions in a tropical paradise.
