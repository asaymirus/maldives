Facilities at Sun Siyam Iru Fushi
Head out and discover the beauty of the Maldives island we call home
From first light to midnight, out at sea or on dry land
Jetcar ride
Drive a jetcar across glassy Maldivian waters—part car, part boat, all fun. A unique way to explore the lagoon with speed, splash, and style.
Stand-up Paddle
Paddle at your own pace across calm, clear waters. A peaceful way to explore lagoons, spot marine life, and take in island views.
Jetski Safari
Embark on an exhilarating jetski adventure through the Maldives, gliding past white-sand beaches, coral reefs, and stunning atolls in a guided tropical escape.
