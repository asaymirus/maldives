Filitheyo is the only island resort in the un-spoilt Faafu Atoll. A natural tropical Island, which is lush in vegetation and surrounded by a stunning house reef. The resort offers guest what can be only be described as a barefoot luxury retreat.

Filitheyo Spa is the perfect place to come and relax and un-wind. Recapture a sense of oneself to rebalance, relax and rejuvenate where you could choose from a selection of Asian inspired and traditional Maldivian treatments.
Diving
The Internationally acclaimed Werner Lau Diving Center offers the complete package, from basic course for beginners to advanced diving courses.
Advanced courses up to CMAS and PADI Dive Master are available for more experienced divers, as well as Barracuda (CMAS), SSI and PADI diving courses continually throughout the year.
Werner Lau Diving Center with its international diving instructors will help you master the art of diving in the beautiful turquoise waters of the Indian Ocean.
Activities
Take pleasure in exploring some of these beautiful islands why not try your hand at snorkeling or diving and see the beauty of the reefs under the ocean or join in and experience some traditional Maldivian night fishing. These activities are just some of the ways which the Islanders have preserved and still embrace today.
