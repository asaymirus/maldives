Celebrating 46 Years of Timeless Memories at Embudu Village
07 Feb 2025
On the 31st of January 2025, under a starlit Maldivian sky, Embudu Village marked a truly special milestone — 46 wonderful years of welcoming guests to our timeless island escape. A night of celebration, reflection, and gratitude, it was an occasion to honour our journey and the people who make Embudu feel like home.
The celebration included a gala dinner, where staff indulged in a carefully curated menu featuring fresh seafood and local favourites. The evening’s lively atmosphere was enhanced by live music, setting the perfect tone for the occasion. As the night progressed, a DJ took over, keeping spirits high with a mix of upbeat tunes and classic favorites.
One of the highlights of the night was honouring our long-serving employees. Certificates and plaques were presented as a token of appreciation for their dedication and hard work over the years. Their commitment has been a cornerstone of the warm and welcoming experience that Embudu is known for. Many of these employees have been with us for decades, watching the resort grow while continuing to provide heartfelt service that keeps guests returning year after year.
Looking back on 46 years, we take pride in the countless memories created on our shores. From honeymooners experiencing their first sunset together to divers exploring the vibrant marine life just off our reef, Embudu has been a place where stories unfold and connections deepen. Our guests, some of whom have been visiting for generations, are at the heart of our success, and we are deeply grateful for their continued support.
As we celebrate this milestone, we reflect on the memories made and look forward to creating many more. A heartfelt thank you to all our guests and team members for being part of this journey. Here’s to the future of Embudu Village, to many more years of hospitality, and to the magic that makes our island so special.
