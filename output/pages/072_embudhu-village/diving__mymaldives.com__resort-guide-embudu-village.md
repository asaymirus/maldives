Embudu has a beautiful house reef surrounding it completely. The reef plays host to a great variety of marine wildlife, ensuring that snorkeling and even shore diving around the edges are always rewarding. The water is stunningly clear, especially around the arrival jetty where large shoals of fish gather. Watching the hundreds of fish that swim beneath the jetty’s lights is a nightly ritual for many of the guests. As such a friendly environment for both couples and families with children, why not make it your next holiday destination?
