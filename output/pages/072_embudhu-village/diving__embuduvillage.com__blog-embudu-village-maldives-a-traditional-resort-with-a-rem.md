Embudu Village Maldives: A Traditional Resort with a Remarkable House Reef
28 Aug 2026

Travel Tips
Looking for a Maldives resort that combines a traditional island setting with easy access to some of the country’s most exciting underwater experiences? Embudu Village Maldives brings together more than four decades of history, a house reef accessible directly from the shore, and a prime location in South Malé Atoll.
Established in 1979 as Kaimoo’s first resort, Embudu Village is one of the long-standing resorts in the Maldives. While the country’s tourism landscape has evolved considerably, the island has retained its natural character, relaxed atmosphere and close connection to the ocean.
Just a 20-minute speedboat ride from Velana International Airport, Embudu also makes it easy to settle into island life soon after arriving in the Maldives.
A Traditional Maldives Island Experience
Embudu’s appeal lies in its uncomplicated island setting. Sandy paths wind through tropical greenery, the beach is always close by, and the compact island can easily be explored on foot. Unlike newer Maldives resorts where the architecture often becomes a major part of the experience, Embudu allows the natural surroundings to take centre stage. Guests can spend their days swimming in the lagoon, relaxing on the beach, exploring the island or simply enjoying the slower pace of island life.
Its history adds another layer to the experience. Having welcomed guests since 1979, Embudu has been part of the development of Maldivian tourism from its early years. That heritage remains visible in the resort’s traditional island atmosphere and straightforward approach to a Maldives holiday.
Embudu’s Remarkable House Reef
For snorkellers and divers, the house reef is one of the highlights of staying at Embudu Village.
The reef can be accessed directly from the beach, allowing guests to explore the underwater world without having to join a boat excursion every time they want to get into the water. Depending on conditions and the area being explored, snorkellers may encounter colourful reef fish, turtles, reef sharks, rays and other tropical marine life. Coral formations provide shelter for smaller species, while deeper reef areas and passes offer opportunities to see larger marine life.
One of the best things about having a house reef directly from the island is the freedom it offers. Snorkelling does not need to be planned as a full-day activity. Guests can simply head into the water for a morning swim, spend some time exploring the reef in the afternoon or return whenever conditions are favourable.
For those new to snorkelling, the accessible reef provides an easy way to begin discovering the underwater side of the Maldives. More experienced snorkellers can venture further along the reef and explore different areas around the island.
Diving in South Malé Atoll
The underwater experience at Embudu extends well beyond its house reef.

Each site offers a different experience, from reef formations and channels to areas where currents can bring divers closer to larger marine life. Embudu Express is particularly suited to experienced divers looking for more dynamic conditions.
Diverland also offers diving and snorkelling experiences and courses, making the resort suitable for guests with different levels of experience. Beginners can learn the basics through guided experiences and courses, while certified divers can explore further afield.
The combination of a house reef directly from the island and boat-accessible dive sites around South Malé Atoll gives guests plenty of opportunities to discover the underwater environment at their own pace.
Life on the Island
While the reef and surrounding waters are central to the Embudu experience, there is plenty to enjoy on land too.
Superior Beach Bungalows
are located close to the shoreline, keeping guests near the beach and natural surroundings.
Water Villas
offer a different perspective, positioned above the lagoon with ocean views and spacious balconies.
Away from the accommodation, guests can relax at
Serena Spa
or spend time on the water with kayaking, canoeing and stand-up paddleboarding. Excursions to local islands and Malé, traditional night fishing and sunset cruises offer opportunities to experience more of the destination.
The result is a holiday that can be as active or unhurried as you want it to be, from exploring the reef in the morning to watching the sunset from the island in the evening.
Just 20 Minutes from Velana International Airport
Reaching Embudu Village is simple. The resort is approximately 20 minutes from Velana International Airport by speedboat, with no domestic flight or seaplane transfer required.
For travellers who want to maximise their time at the resort, the short transfer means less time travelling and more time enjoying the island. It also makes Embudu a convenient choice for shorter Maldives stays or travellers who prefer a straightforward journey from the airport.
Why Choose Embudu Village Maldives?
Embudu brings together several of the experiences travellers look for when choosing a Maldives resort: a natural island setting, a traditional atmosphere, easy access to the ocean and plenty of opportunities to explore beneath the surface.
Its reef is accessible directly from the shore, while Diverland provides access to 40 dive sites across South Malé Atoll. The accommodation offers a choice between staying close to the beach or above the lagoon, and the resort’s 20-minute speedboat transfer makes reaching the island remarkably easy.
Most importantly, Embudu offers an experience that remains centred on the island itself. There is time to swim, snorkel, dive, explore, relax and simply enjoy being surrounded by the ocean.
For travellers searching for a traditional Maldives resort, a Maldives resort with a house reef, or a convenient base for diving in South Malé Atoll, Embudu Village offers a well-rounded island escape with a history that stretches back to 1979.
