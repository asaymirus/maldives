DESCRIPTION
Where modern architecture meets refined island serenity.
Located in the pristine North Malé Atoll, The Ritz-Carlton Maldives, Fari Islands is a sophisticated sanctuary designed by renowned Kerry Hill Architects. The resort blends minimalist design with Maldivian inspiration, creating a striking island escape defined by circular forms and open spaces. Guests arrive by a scenic 45-minute luxury speedboat or 10-minute seaplane journey from Velana International Airport, entering a world of refined hospitality and personalized service.
The resort features 100 elegant beachfront and overwater villas, each with a private pool and dedicated Aris Meeha island butler, ensuring seamless, intuitive service. Dining unfolds across seven exceptional venues spanning a dedicated culinary island and the vibrant Fari Marina Village. With its iconic ring-shaped spa over the lagoon, immersive marine experiences, and cosmopolitan island atmosphere, The Ritz-Carlton Maldives delivers a contemporary interpretation of Maldivian luxury.

The Ritz-Carlton Estate
The Ritz-Carlton Estate is the resort’s most exclusive residence, offering an expansive private sanctuary set along a secluded stretch of beach. Spanning approximately 1,500 sqm, the estate features four spacious bedrooms, four ensuite bathrooms, generous indoor living and dining areas, and expansive outdoor terraces designed for seamless indoor–outdoor living. Guests enjoy the privacy of a large private infinity pool, with direct access to the beach and uninterrupted ocean views.
Ideal for families or groups seeking the highest level of luxury, the estate combines refined architectural design with personalised Aris Meeha island butler service, ensuring a bespoke and effortlessly curated island experience.
Dining
La Locanda
An elegant overwater Italian restaurant featuring a lively open kitchen and authentic flavours from homemade pastas to signature dishes. La Locanda also hosts the resort’s vibrant breakfast experience with live stations and global selections, complemented by fine wines and classic Italian espresso.

Summer Pavilion
Inspired by traditional Chinese lantern architecture, Summer Pavilion showcases refined Cantonese cuisine. Signature dishes such as Peking duck and wok-fried lobster are designed for sharing, accompanied by fine Chinese teas and an extensive wine cellar.
Iwau
Meaning “celebration” in Japanese, IWAU offers an intimate Chef’s Table experience under the stars. Guests enjoy theatrical cooking featuring premium meats and fresh seafood paired with Japanese sake and champagne.
Beach Shack
A vibrant Mediterranean-inspired beachside venue offering relaxed dining steps from the ocean. Expect fresh coastal flavours, creative cocktails and themed evenings such as Maldivian nights and lobster dinners.
Eau Bar
The resort’s stylish social hub, EAU Bar transitions effortlessly from relaxed daytime champagne to lively evening cocktails crafted with fresh, locally inspired ingredients.
Arabesque
Inspired by the rich colours and textures of a Bedouin tent, Arabesque serves authentic Middle Eastern and North Indian cuisine with bold spices and modern presentation.
Tum Tum
Located at Fari Marina Village, Tum Tum celebrates Asian street food with an elevated twist. Expect vibrant flavours, bao buns, noodles and signature fried rice served in a lively setting.
