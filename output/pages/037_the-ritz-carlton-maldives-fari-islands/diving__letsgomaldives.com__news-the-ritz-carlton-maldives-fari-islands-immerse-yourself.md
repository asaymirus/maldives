This festive season, The Ritz-Carlton Maldives, Fari Islands offers guests a chance to celebrate the elemental beauty of water with a unique theme called “EAU.” From December 22, 2024, to January 9, 2025, the resort transforms into a haven of water-inspired festivities, blending the natural splendor of the Maldives with extraordinary experiences.
Guests can look forward to an array of enchanting events, including nightly performances that celebrate the transformative power of water through music, dance, and light. The EAU Bar becomes the focal point of these celebrations, where guests can sip signature Sangu cocktails while enjoying breathtaking views of the Indian Ocean. For those seeking culinary delights, exclusive dining experiences at IWAU and Summer Pavilion will take you on a gastronomic journey, culminating in a grand New Year’s Eve Gala Dinner.
In addition to these magical experiences, the resort’s ‘Festive Escape’ package offers daily breakfast at La Locanda, $300 in resort credits, and a thrilling shark snorkeling adventure. Located in the North Malé Atoll, The Ritz-Carlton Maldives, Fari Islands provides a serene and luxurious escape, accessible via a 50-minute yacht ride or a 10-minute seaplane flight from Malé International Airport.
Book your stay by September 30, 2024, to take advantage of early bird offers, including additional savings and member benefits, and experience the magic of the Maldives this festive season.
