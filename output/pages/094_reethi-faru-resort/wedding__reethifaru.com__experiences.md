Q. What water sports are available at Reethi Faru Resort in the Maldives?
Water Sports include canoeing, stand-up paddleboarding, sailing, jet ski, flyboarding, parasailing, fun-tube riding, and more. The resort's water sports centre is one of the best-equipped in the Maldives, with a wide range of activities available daily for all skill levels.Â
Q. Is there diving at Reethi Faru Resort?
Yes. Reethi Faru has the PADI 5-star accredited SeaXplorer Dive Centre on site. Guided dives explore reefs, submerged islands, dramatic overhangs, walls, and caves in Raa Atoll - one of the most biodiverse and least-visited diving regions in the Maldives. The resort also has its own house reef for snorkelling. Courses are available for beginners through to advanced divers.
Q. Does Reethi Faru have a house reef for snorkelling?Â
Yes. Reethi Faru has its own house reef directly accessible from the island - ideal for snorkelling without needing to take a boat trip. The reef is home to corals and colourful marine life in the clear waters of Raa Atoll.
Q. Is there a spa at Reethi Faru Resort?
Yes. Coconut Spa at Reethi Faru is set within a coconut grove surrounded by tropical vegetation, with private treatment rooms offering a wide range of massages and holistic treatments. The spa facilities also include a steam room, sauna, spa tub, and relaxation area. Packages are available for individuals and couples.

Q. Does Reethi Faru have an infinity pool?
Yes. The infinity pool at Reethi Faru offers panoramic views over the Indian Ocean, with warm filtered fresh water, a chequered pool floor, and daybeds. The pool merges seamlessly with the turquoise lagoon on the horizon - one of the most scenic swimming pools in the Maldives.

Q. What excursions are available from Reethi Faru Resort?
Reethi Faru offers a range of island excursions including trips to deserted sandbanks, local island visits, dolphin watching, sunset fishing, and snorkelling excursions to spot manta rays, turtles, and sharks in the waters of Raa Atoll. Excursions are available by speedboat, jet ski, or catamaran. New experiences are available daily.
