Reethi Faru Resort, located on the private island of Filaidhoo in Raa Atoll, offers a romantic island experience designed for couples seeking privacy, relaxation, and authentic Maldivian beauty. Surrounded by turquoise lagoon waters, white sandy beaches, and a vibrant house reef, the resort combines natural island charm with contemporary luxury hospitality.
The resort features 145 luxury villas across multiple accommodation categories - including Garden Villas, Deluxe Beach Villas, Water Villa Sunset View, Water Villas, Deluxe Beach Pool Villas, Deluxe Two Bedroom Beach Suites, Two Bedroom Water Villa Suites With Spa Tub- giving honeymooners the flexibility to choose the setting that best matches their travel style.
THE BEST VILLAS FOR COUPLES AT REETHI FARU
Water Villas: The Signature Honeymoon Choice
For many honeymooners, the Water Villa and Water Villa Sunset View represent the quintessential Maldives experience. Positioned directly above the lagoon in Raa Atoll, these overwater villas feature private decks with direct ocean access, panoramic Indian Ocean views, and spacious interiors designed for couples.
The Water Villa Sunset View is especially popular with honeymooners due to its west-facing orientation, allowing couples to watch the Maldives sunset directly from their private deck each evening.
Each villa includes a king-size bed, rain shower, air conditioning, ceiling fan, mini bar, tea and coffee facilities, satellite TV, in-room safe, high-speed WiFi, and sun loungers on the overwater terrace.
