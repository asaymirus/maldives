Q. Is there diving at Reethi Faru Resort?
Yes. Reethi Faru has the PADI 5-star accredited SeaXplorer Dive Centre on site. Guided dives explore reefs, submerged islands, dramatic overhangs, walls, and caves in Raa Atoll - one of the most biodiverse and least-visited diving regions in the Maldives. The resort also has its own house reef for snorkelling. Courses are available for beginners through to advanced divers.

Yes. Reethi Faru has its own house reef directly accessible from the island - ideal for snorkelling without needing to take a boat trip. The reef is home to corals and colourful marine life in the clear waters of Raa Atoll.
Q. Is there a spa at Reethi Faru Resort?
Yes. Coconut Spa at Reethi Faru is set within a coconut grove surrounded by tropical vegetation, with private treatment rooms offering a wide range of massages and holistic treatments. The spa facilities also include a steam room, sauna, spa tub, and relaxation area. Packages are available for individuals and couples.
