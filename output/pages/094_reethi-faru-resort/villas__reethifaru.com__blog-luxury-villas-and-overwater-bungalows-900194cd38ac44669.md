Experiences
Reethi Faru is a beautiful getaway that epitomizes luxury in every way, located in the center of the Maldives, where the azure sky meets the turquoise ocean. The resort's accommodations, which offer a superb fusion of contemporary comfort and classic Maldivian charm, best showcase its opulence.
Beachfront Villas:
Welcome to the Beachfront Villas of Reethi Faru, where the smooth, powdery sands await you. These villas create a sense of privacy and tranquility because they are surrounded by thick nature and are only a short distance from the breaking waves. With roomy interiors decorated with elegant furnishings, a private outdoor shower, and a terrace that offers breathtaking views of the Indian Ocean, each villa is a haven of comfort.
Deluxe Beach Villas:
A higher degree of luxury is available in the Deluxe Beach Villas for those looking for that extra bit of elegance. These homes embody tropical decadence with their terrace complete with sun loungers and direct beach access. Take a dip in the outdoor rain shower or relax in the tastefully designed rooms, where each element has been carefully chosen to provide a luxurious experience.
Water Villas:
The Water Villas at Reethi Faru call for an effortless fusion of luxury and environment. These overwater sanctuaries, perched over the peaceful lagoon, offer a sense of floating solitude. With direct access to the water and an expansive terrace that provides unobstructed views of the ocean, guests may jump into the pristine lagoon from the comfort of their villa.
Deluxe Beach Pool Villas:
The Deluxe Beach Pool Villas at Reethi Faru Resort offer an exquisite blend of luxury, privacy, and natural beauty, creating an idyllic retreat for those seeking to immerse themselves in the tranquility of island life. Situated on the pristine white sands, just steps away from the azure waters of the Indian Ocean, these villas are designed to provide guests with an unparalleled beachfront experience, complemented by their own private pools.
Garden Villa:
Reethi Faru Resort offers an enchanting escape with its Garden Villas that are nothing short of a tropical paradise. These villas provide an intimate connection with nature, positioned amidst lush, vibrant gardens that encapsulate the essence of tranquillity and beauty. Ideal for those seeking both privacy and proximity to the island's natural splendor, the Garden Villas at Reethi Faru are designed to harmonize with the surrounding landscape, offering a serene retreat from the everyday.
