Celebrate Halloween in Paradise
This Halloween, paradise gets a mysterious twist as Reethi Faru Resort invites guests to celebrate as the sun sets over the lagoon. Costumes come out, cocktails take on a spooky twist, and the tropical night sets the stage for an evening of Halloween fun.
From playful activities for little ones to a lively costume party and a specially curated Halloween feast, guests can expect plenty of reasons to dress up, join the fun, and see Reethi Faru in a whole new light.
Halloween Fun for Little Ones
Halloween isn't just for grown-ups. Our younger guests can join in the fun with face painting, themed games and specially curated activities, designed to spark their imagination. From spooky makeovers to playful adventures, little ones can enjoy an afternoon filled with laughter, creativity and plenty of Halloween spirit.
It's the perfect opportunity for families to dress up, have fun together, and create some memorable island moments.
Costumes, Music & Island Nights
When night falls, the celebrations continue with the Halloween Costume Party. Guests are invited to bring their most creative costumes and join an evening of DJ music, dancing and Halloween fun.
From mysterious characters to wonderfully imaginative creations, the island becomes a colourful stage where everyone can get into the spirit of the occasion.
A Halloween Feast
What's a celebration without a feast? Our culinary team will bring Halloween to the table with a specially themed Halloween buffet, featuring an enticing selection of international and local flavours, presented with a creative seasonal twist.
To complement the evening, those ready to raise a glass can enjoy Halloween cocktails, adding an extra touch of fun to the festivities.
Halloween Beneath the Maldivian Sky
There is something special about celebrating Halloween on a tropical island.
As the moon rises above the Indian Ocean and the island grows quieter, the familiar beauty of Reethi Faru takes on a more mysterious character. The sound of waves along the shore, the tropical night air, and the shimmering lagoon create an atmospheric backdrop unlike any other.
Whether travelling as a family, enjoying a romantic island escape, or simply seeking a memorable holiday experience, guests will find something to enjoy throughout the evening.
Celebrate Halloween in Paradise
This October, leave the ordinary behind and step into a world where Halloween meets the magic of the Maldives.
At Reethi Faru, costumes meet creativity, cuisine meets celebration, and the tropical night becomes a setting for something truly unforgettable.
