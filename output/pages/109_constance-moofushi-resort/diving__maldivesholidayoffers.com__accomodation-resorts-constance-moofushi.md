Resort Overview
All Inclusive Resort Constance Moofushi
This luxurious property is located in the middle of the Indian Ocean, surrounded by pure turquoise water and white sandy beaches. Constance Moofushi is a diving destination in South Ari Atoll, Maldives, and is regarded as one of the best in the world. Constance Moofushi has a swimming pool, a gym, diving, Dhoni excursions, snorkeling, big game fishing, and non-motorized water sports.
This hotel provides guests with an experience of chic barefoot elegance combined with 5-star comfort standards. Guests can choose between a beach villa and a water villa, both of which offer views of the Indian Ocean, and all rooms include basic amenities. Choose Constance Moofushi and you will have a fantastic time.
