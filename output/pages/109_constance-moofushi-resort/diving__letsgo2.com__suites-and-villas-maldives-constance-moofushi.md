About the resort
Located on the South Ari Atol, Constance Moofushi Resort is the perfect combination of luxury and simplicity, surrounded by crystal clear waters, the island creates a calm and relaxing atmosphere from the moment you arrive.
Whether your stay is in a beach villa or water villa, you will find them fully equipped with modern amenities including ipod docks, mini bar and a wooden terrace. The spacious beach villas offer an idyllic setting along the golden sands, where you can sit on the terrace and enjoy the turquoise waters lapping the shores. The water villas set over the lagoon offer mesmerising views across the ocean and have a large furnished terrace which is the perfect place to soak up the sun.

OUR
restaurant and bars
The all-inclusive package at Constance Moofushi provides guests with an excellent range of dining options, together with a large variety of premium branded drinks and wines.
Alizee Restaurant
Located on the white sandy beaches, the beach grill offers a casual and relaxing atmosphere. Choose from all time favourites pizza, hamburgers and fries or choose the fish and meat of the day prepared to order on the grill.
Manta Restaurant
Take a 'trip around the world' at Manta, with themes changing daily from wood fired crispy pizza to Maldivian specialities, there is something for all tastes. The over water extension provides the perfect view of the crystal clear waters of the Indian Ocean.
Manta Bar
Sample invigorating tropical mixed drinks with white sand underfoot in our enthusiastic, amicable bar. Positioned beside the Manta restaurant, visitors can have day-time beverages with delightful waffles or night-time beverages after supper whilst listening to our DJ's tunes. With open zones to appreciate the staggering views, you can delight in a wide mixed bag of beverages incorporating our outstanding cocktails or mocktails for those who want to go liquor-free.
Totem Bar
Situated on the beach by the pool, Totem Bar offers clients the perfect place to cool down with a refreshing drink and snacks.
HOTEL
experiences
Constance Moofushi's charming spa is set on a wooden jetty over the tranquil waters of the ocean and has a quiet, shaded relaxation area. Relax, rejuvenate, detox, re-hydrate or simply enjoy - the choice is yours. Their highly skilled team is at your disposal to help you decide which treatments are best suited to your needs.
There's a fully equipped gymnasium, regular beach volley activity, pool and entertainment lounge. There is also non-motorised water sports such as top cat (catamaran - compulsory orientation at a cost), pedal boats, windsurfing, kayak, snorkeling excursions with equipment included, pool table, darts, giant chess, yoga, table football, marine biology talks, flair show, Boduberu dance & music show, DJ entertainment, back of house tour, wine tasting and cocktail class. Therefore you will never be stuck for anything to do!
