THE FALHUMAA
Enjoy a memorable dining experience flavoured with the ocean breeze at this over-water venue, spectacularly located at the end of a 230-metre-long jetty above the reef. The menu features an exquisite selection of western cuisine with the emphasis on fresh seafood and grilled meats, complemented by fine wines from around the world. Relax in the intimate setting of the bar where you can begin the evening with an aperitif or retire for after-dinner liqueurs.
In the afternoons, indulgent afternoon hi-tea is available. Unwind in an intimate setting with a magnificent view along with a selection of fine teas and a medley of sweet and savoury delights.
