GOURMET JOURNEYS
Savour a range of flavours inspired by traditional family-style Moroccan cuisine with a hint of Mediterranean influences. Enjoy simple, delicious recipes all homemade by our passionate resident chef, Mi Fatema, using fresh produce from the local market.
Dine al fresco on the roof terrace or in the convivial atmosphere of our patio restaurant, Zelij, named after the handcrafted Moroccan tiles that adorn the walls of the hotel, where the sound of the fountain provides a soothing soundtrack to your culinary experience.
