GONE WITH THE WIND
Enjoy a private hot air balloon flight over Jbilet landscape, accompanied by a violinist or saxophonist on board. You will leave at dawn and travel by 4x4 to the site of take-off for a magical one-hour flight, discovering the Palmeraie, Marrakech and its villages from above, followed by a traditional Berber breakfast.
