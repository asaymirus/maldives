Ode to The Olive
Tunisia's hidden treasure... Step into a picturesque landscape of a 50 hectares verdant olive grove within an archaeological site from the Roman era and be mesmerised at the Ben Ismail Family Reserve in Toukaber, Medjez El Bab (northwest of Tunisia).
Indulge your senses as you discover the intricacies of harvesting and extracting this liguid gold on a farm and oil mill visit. A session of initiation of tasting this award-winning organic extra virgin olive oil completes the experience with typical culinary delights of the region. Savour a magical cultural journey.
