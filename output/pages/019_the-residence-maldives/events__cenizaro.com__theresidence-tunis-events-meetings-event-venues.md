Event venues
​
The Residence Tunis offers a range of venues that can host events for 15 to 500 guests. Business feels like pleasure in our three comfortable wood-panelled boardrooms, while an additional office provides an ideal space for small committee work sessions.
For events on a grander scale, impress your wedding guests or business delegates in our two ballrooms: the magnificent Chich Khan ballroom, where you can host gala dinners beneath a lofty, lace-like, fibreglass ceiling intricately crafted by Persian artisans; and the contemporary Club Ballroom, which features its own reception foyer and outdoor terrace overlooking spectacular views. Or embrace the magic of the Mediterranean with a beautiful setup on the beach.
