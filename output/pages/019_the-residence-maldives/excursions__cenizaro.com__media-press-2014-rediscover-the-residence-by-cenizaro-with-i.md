September 2014:
The Residence by Cenizaro is offering guests a new online experience to explore the group’s four luxury hotels, with a highly visual, freshly redesigned website –
www.cenizaro.com/theresidence
. The site has an updated, chic look and feel and comes as Cenizaro Hotels & Resorts, a leading hospitality company with headquarters in Singapore, continues the evolution of its multi-brand portfolio.
The Residence by Cenizaro pages are dominated by stunning imagery, drawing both tablet and mobile users to engage with the richer, in-depth content that highlights every aspect of the group’s four hotels – The Residence Tunis, The Residence Mauritius, The Residence Zanzibar and The Residence Maldives. The group’s social media channels are also fully integrated, as is an easy-to-use booking engine that allows guests to view real-time room availability, convert pricing into their local currency and save the reservation history and guest profile to save time with future bookings.
Liza Quddoos, Director of Corporate Communications for Cenizaro Hotels & Resorts comments, "A fresh website that showcases our vision for Cenizaro Hotels & Resorts was a necessity, as was an appropriate platform to accurately capture The Residence by Cenizaro, an award-winning and timelessly elegant brand. Our goal for the website -
www.cenizaro.com/theresidence
- was to present content in a way that reflects the hotels and ensures users have a consistently enjoyable and engaging on-line experience.”
The website’s key features are:
1 – A home for Cenizaro Hotels & Resorts.
The website illuminates Cenizaro Hotels & Resorts corporate position as an international hospitality company, detailing its portfolio of hotels and creating an environment for future business growth.
2 – Ease-of-use.
Every page on the site is simple and intuitive; from the new reservations system, which allows for multi-room, multi-person and activities bookings; and social media integration, to the media and trade communication areas.
3 – Out and about?
The responsive design allows viewing on both mobiles and tablets
4 – Snap Happy.
Strong visuals draw users into finding out more....and more... about the hotels
5 – Get under the skin
– there are improved spa, wedding and destination pages allowing guests to get under the skin of each hotel.
In 2015 and beyond,
www.cenizaro.com
will continue to mature with multilingual pages, video content, corporate social responsibility pages and a Reviews page being introduced. For more information about The Residence by Cenizaro and to view the website, visit
www.cenizaro.com/theresidence
.
** SPECIAL ONLINE BOOKING OFFER **
To celebrate the launch of the new website, guests booking online before 30 November 2014, for stays at any of The Residence by Cenizaro hotels before 30 March 2015, will enjoy a complimentary upgrade to full board. Every booking made online before 30 November 2014 will also be entered into a prize draw to win a five night stay at The Residence by Cenizaro hotel of the winner’s choice.
