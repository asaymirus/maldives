SPA Programmes
Retreat and rediscover your outer radiance and inner balance as you enjoy a symphony of spa treatments designed to elicit deeper levels of relaxation and renewal.
JUST THE TWO OF US — 120 mins
A luxurious and tranquil couple indulgence for the senses. The treatment begins with Foot Bath Ritual, followed by an exfoliating Clarins Body Polisher for renewed and refreshed skin. A Clarins Deep Touch Body Massage relieves stress and muscle tension. This romantic journey concludes with a Customised Facial for luminous skin. Enjoy a glass of sparkling wine or herbal tea at the end of the journey.
THE RESIDENCE BLISS — 120 mins
Surround yourself in complete bliss! A Clarins Body Scrub will gently exfoliate your skin before continuing with the Soul Body Massage that uses Ayurvedic technics to promote deep relaxation, relieving your mind of stress and tension. A pampering foot massage completes the journey as you leave with lighter steps. A selection of herbal teas or juice served with fruits completes this blissful session.
ROMANTIC HIDEAWAY — 120 mins
The romantic treatment starts with Clarins Relaxing Body Wrap to promote a state of relaxation. The ritual continues with a Peace Body Massage to stimulate your body’s natural well-being and soothes away the stress. Relax and sip on a glass of bubbly whilst dipping in an aroma bath over the infinite view of the Indian ocean.
SUN LOVERS — 120 mins
Remove impurities and dead skin cells with a thorough body exfoliation and get the skin ready for optimum absorption of the coconut oil’s hydrating properties. Using Swedish massage techniques, the Tranquility Body Massage relieves and renews tired muscles to prepare for another day out in the sun!
