The Charms of Sidi Bou Saïd
Discover the prettiest town that sits perched at the top of a steep cliff overlooking the Mediterranean Sea. Immerse in those whitewashed houses and ornate blue doors as you enjoy a stroll around the labyrinth of winding cobbled streets lined with flower-filled courtyards, art shops and cafes.
A place of inspiration for many artist over the years, including Paul Klee and Henri Matisse.
