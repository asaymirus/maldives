A world away from the everyday, The Residence Maldives creates the time and space for you to rediscover the meaning of relaxation, well taken care of by our attentive yet discreet hospitality.
Designed in harmony with the natural environment, the resort seamlessly blends traditional Maldivian architecture with contemporary elegance and modern comforts. Luxuriate in the privacy of the resort’s beachfront and over-water villas that offer an oasis just steps from the sand, or the pleasure of slipping straight from your deck into the cool, clear waters to reach your own private lagoon.
Embrace the destination through our unique dining experiences and a myriad of activities – from lazing on sun-kissed beaches to snorkelling, island hopping or exploring the remarkable marine life in many nearby dive sites. Whether seeking the perfect couple’s retreat or a memorable family holiday, surrender to the enchantment of The Residence Maldives.
