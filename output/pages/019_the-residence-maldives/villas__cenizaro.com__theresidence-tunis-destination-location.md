Nestled Between The Sea & The City
Location
Located in Northern Tunisia, The Residence Tunis is situated on the seafront in Gammarth yet close to the city centre of Tunis, a 20-minute drive from Tunis-Carthage International Airport or a 80-minute drive from Enfidha-Hammamet International Airport. While enjoying direct access to an undisturbed stretch of beach, the resort also provides the perfect base from which to explore the UNESCO World Heritage Sites of The Medina of Tunis and the ancient ruins of Carthage as well as the famous artists’ retreat Sidi Bou Said and many other historic sites.
