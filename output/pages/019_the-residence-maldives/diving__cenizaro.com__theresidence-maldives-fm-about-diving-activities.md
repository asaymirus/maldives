Dive Into The Blue
DIVING Activities
​
The sparkling lagoon beckons at The Residence Maldives, where we have our very own PADI 5-Star Dive Centre, and are surrounded by rare, unspoilt coral reefs and exceptional dive sites. From a mere five minutes to an hour away from the resort, reached by dive boat, seasoned snorkellers and divers can find themselves immersed in a spectacular underwater world, teeming with marine life including white tip sharks, barracudas, reef fishes and turtles.
For beginners, there’s no better place to take your first dive into the blue. You can take PADI courses in a relaxed and secure environment from multilingual diving instructors.
Children aged 8 years and above can also get their first taste of diving on the Bubblemaker Dive programme.
All Dive Centre and Water Sports activities are temporarily relocated to facilities on the sister resort on Dhigurah island until further notice.
Connected by a stunning private bridge that spans across both islands; our guests may alternatively choose to also delight in additional dining discoveries and facilities on the sister resort on Dhigurah island.
