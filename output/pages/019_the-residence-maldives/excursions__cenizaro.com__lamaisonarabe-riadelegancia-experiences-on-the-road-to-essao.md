ON THE ROAD TO ESSAUOIRA
​
Loaded with an eventful history, the port city of Essaouira is a coastal gem, an authentic slice of Morocco facing the ocean. Behind its proud appearance as a prominent historical site, Essaouira is a city of secrets not limited to the tourist trail. Experience the process of making argan oil, and visit a local restaurant famous for its cheese-tasting menu, where you can sample locally produced cheese and Moroccan wine.
Duration:
Full day
Inclusions:
Round trip transfers with private driver
A private guide during the visit to Essaouira
Lunch at l’Heure Bleue
