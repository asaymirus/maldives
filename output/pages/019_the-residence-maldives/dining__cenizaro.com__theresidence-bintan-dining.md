From gourmet creations to soul-pleasing comfort food, from dishes that celebrate tradition to modern interpretations of classics, dining at The Residence Bintan takes you on a gratifying journey of taste sensations. Our chefs tantalise taste buds with a celebration on the plate that showcases fresh local produce, including some of our very own vegetables grown in the resort’s gardens. Panoramic ocean views and gentle breezes as you dine provide further indulgence for your senses.
Immerse in the authentic tastes of the earth on a delicious journey into sustainability whilst indulging in sustainable nature-inspired cuisine, lovingly prepared by our talented chefs.  Our Earth Basket farm-to-table dining experience celebrates/incorporates handpicked daily harvests of farm fresh local produce grown on-site at the resort’s kitchen farms.
