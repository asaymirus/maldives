Zanzibar, February 2013:
With the days of all-inclusive holidays conjuring images of sub-standard resorts and rubbery buffet food behind us,
The Residence Zanzibar
has launched a new all-inclusive package for 2013.  On a mile-long stretch of sandy beach, the 66 all-villa hotel combines the sophistication of a 5* resort with great all-inclusive value for both families and couples so the best of both worlds can be enjoyed.
The Residence Zanzibar has created a seven-night package including not only food and accommodation but also the state-of-the-art facilities and expert practitioners to allow guests to explore a whole new world of treatments, exercises and activities; all at no extra cost.
The special package includes:
All meals (breakfast, lunch and dinner) on an a la carte basis, across all restaurants and in-villa
A wide selection of premium branded spirits, selected beers, cocktails and local spirits
The minibar – including wine, cocktail, beers, soft drinks, mineral water and snacks
Daily snorkelling excursions from the jetty (tide permitting)
Yoga sessions
Use of the fitness centre, sauna and hammam as well as non-motorised watersports, tennis, bicycles, the DVD library and Wi-Fi
The only member of The Leading Hotels of the World in Tanzania, The Residence Zanzibar is on a private, 80 acre estate.  Each villa has its own private terrace, swimming pool and personal butler service.  The hotel’s star attraction is The Spa – six pavilions set in five acres of tropical gardens and dedicated to relaxation, beauty and well-being.
