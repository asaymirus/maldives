Travel Tips
Climate:
Mauritius enjoys warm tropical weather all year round, with an average temperature of 25°C in the summer months from October to May, and a comfortable 20°C between June and September.
Airport:
Sir Seewoosagur Ramgoolam International Airport
Immigration:
Visitors must have a passport with at least six months validity. Visa requirements are determined by nationality, although no visa is required for EU nationals.
Time:
GMT +04:00
Currency:
The local currency is the Mauritian Rupee (MUR), which is comprised of 100 cents.
Credit Cards:
Credit cards accepted include VISA, MasterCard and AMEX.
Language:
The official language of Mauritius is English, while French and Mauritian Creole are also widely spoken.
Taxes:
All rates at the resort are inclusive of 15% government tax.
Electricity:
220/240volt AC at 50 cycles. International adapters are available upon request at the resort.
