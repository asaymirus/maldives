Savour good food and soothing views in the resort’s main restaurant, a modern beachfront bistro offering both indoor and outdoor seating overlooking the lagoon. Choose from an extensive à la carte breakfast menu, as well as healthy salads and light meals for lunch. In the evenings, dine on a delectable variety of World, Asian and Indian Ocean cuisine fusing classic flavours with a fresh, contemporary twist. A children’s menu is also available.
