FANTASTIC QUAD GETAWAY
​
We will take you to the beautiful Lake Lalla Takerkoust a few kilometers from Marrakech for a quad ride around the lake. Explore the area, following tracks through canyons and fords, surrounded by a sumptuous view of the Atlas Mountains. You will be supervised in complete safety by professionals who have participated in the greatest rallies, such as Paris-Dakar and Atlas Rally. A fun experience to share with family or friends!
Duration:
Half day
Inclusions:
Round trip transfers by 4x4 to Lake Lalla Takerkoust
2 hours of quad biking
Lunch at the Auberge du Flouka
Possibility of jet skiing on the lake on request
