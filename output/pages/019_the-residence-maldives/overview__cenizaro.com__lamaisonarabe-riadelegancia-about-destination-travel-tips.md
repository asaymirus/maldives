TRAVEL TIPS
Climate:
Marrakech has a subtropical, semi-desert climate. The city is at its hottest and driest in summer from June to August when temperatures can reach mid-30s Celsius. Winters are mild, averaging around 20 degrees Celsius, making it a pleasant destination to visit all year round.
Airport:
Marrakech Menara Airport, located a 20-minute drive from the hotel.
Immigration:
Visitors must have a passport with at least six months validity. Visa requirements are determined by nationality.
Time:
GMT +01:00 (GMT during the month of Ramadan)
Currency:
The local currency is the Dirham (Dhs or MAD), which is divided into 100 santimat.
Credit Cards:
Credit cards accepted include VISA, MasterCard and AMEX.
Language:
The official language of Morocco is Arabic. French and English are also widely spoken and understood.
Electricity:
220 volt AC at 50 cycles. International adapters are available upon request at the hotel.
