Water World
Get into the Water World, where you’ll feel the urge to get out onto the Indian Ocean before your feet even hit the ground. As you fly in, the allure of our sparkling sea is utterly irresistible. You can swim in it to your heart’s content, of course, but we’ll help you go further. Grab a board, snorkel, or kayak, hit the water, and feel the freedom of a world without boundaries.
