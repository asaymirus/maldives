DESCRIPTION
Amongst the turquoise waters of Raa Atoll, Cora Cora Maldives has an idyllic location.
Cora Cora Maldives is a contemporary 5-star island escape set within a vibrant turquoise lagoon in Raa Atoll. Designed around freedom, colour, and connection, the resort blends laid-back luxury with immersive culture, exceptional dining, and inclusive experiences. With 100 stylish beach and overwater villas, a gourmet all-inclusive concept, and a relaxed, social atmosphere, Cora Cora welcomes couples, families, and friends alike. From heritage discoveries and wellness rituals to playful island adventures, every stay is effortlessly indulgent and refreshingly unpretentious.
In Raa Atoll:
45 minutes by seaplane or 30 minutes by domestic flight, followed by a 20-minute speedboat from Ifuru Domestic Airport.

Highlights From The Resort
History Makers: Museum & Cultural Heritage
Cora Cora Maldives is home to the most extensive private collection of Maldivian cultural heritage outside the National Museum. Displayed across the lobby gallery and the island’s outdoor museum, artefacts span over 2,000 years of history—from the Pre-Islamic era to the Colonial period—offering guests a rare and immersive journey into the Maldives’ rich past.
Outdoor Heritage Museum
At the heart of the island lies an extraordinary open-air museum featuring an intact eightmetre-wide pre-Islamic temple tank, a second tank under excavation, and a beautifully restored 120-year-old traditional Maldivian house. This living heritage space brings ancient island life vividly to the present.
Dive & Water Sports Adventures
Explore the vibrant underwater world through guided house-reef snorkelling or dives to nearby sites teeming with marine life. The Dive Centre also offers full PADI certification programmes, catering to both beginners and experienced divers seeking new depths.
Island Excursions
Discover the Maldives beyond the resort with sunset cruises, snorkelling safaris, local island visits, and a range of non-motorised watersports. Each excursion is designed to blend adventure, nature, and authentic island encounters.
MOKSHA® Spa & Wellbeing
Rejuvenation takes centre stage at the MOKSHA® Spa & Wellbeing Centre. Featuring serene overwater and island treatment rooms, the spa offers Asian-inspired therapies, relaxation rituals, and wellness-focused experiences designed to restore balance to body and mind.
Yoga & Mindful Movement
Find calm and clarity in the overwater yoga pavilion, where guided yoga and meditation sessions are held against the soothing backdrop of the lagoon. These wellness experiences invite guests to slow down, reconnect, and embrace mindful island living.
Fitness & Feel-Good Energy
Stay active in the resort’s fully equipped fitness centre, designed to energise both body and spirit. Whether maintaining a routine or starting fresh, the space supports a balanced and uplifting wellness journey.
The Playground
An expansive outdoor leisure area offering casual games such as beach volleyball, badminton, bocce ball, and giant chess. Perfect for friendly competition, family fun, and relaxed afternoons under the Maldivian sun.
Outdoor Cinema
As night falls, the beach transforms into an intimate open-air cinema. Sink into comfortable seating under the stars and enjoy classic favourites or the latest films, creating memorable island evenings by the sea.
CoRa kids® Club
Designed for children aged 3 to 12, the CoRa kids® Club offers creative activities, games, and supervised fun in age-specific groups. While young guests enjoy their own adventures, parents are free to relax and unwind.
Special Treats & Private Experiences
Enhance your stay with curated extras including private snorkelling, fishing trips, and bespoke excursions. Thoughtfully designed to add a personal touch, these experiences create unforgettable moments tailored to your island escape.
