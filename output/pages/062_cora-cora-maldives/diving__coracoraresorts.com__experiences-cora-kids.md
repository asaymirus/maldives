CoRa kids® Club
While your little ones have fun in our activity-filled CoRa kids
® Club, you can relax by the pool and enjoy your drink to the fullest.
Open to children aged 3 to 12 and split into three age groups, you can drop in for a session or let them hang out with their new friends all day.
View CoRa kids® Activity Calendar
Feel
Gentle dancing, yoga, nail painting, t-shirt designing, and relaxation sessions are perfect for kids who want to chill out.
Our island’s natural beauty will come alive for kids in these nature-focused sessions that look at the plants, animals, and landscape of  Cora Cora Maldives.
They’ll have so much fun in the sun with treasure hunts, water-bucket races, and sports on the beach.
Let their minds explore new ideas with activities that bring out their artistic side.
Pizza, ice cream, fruit skewers and pancakes – your kids will learn to make some of their favourite meals.
Our certified Worldwide Kids’ attendants and manager are dedicated to fostering an environment where children can thrive and grow. With Worldwide Kids leading the way, we ensure that every child has the opportunity to engage in enriching activities that encourage exploration, innovation, and personal development.
