DESCRIPTION
A private retreat for the mind, body, and spirit, where global luxury meets untouched paradise.
Set on Vagaru Island in the secluded Shaviyani Atoll, JW Marriott Maldives Resort & Spa blends world-class hospitality with the unspoiled beauty of the Maldives. A scenic 55-minute seaplane journey from Malé brings you to this half-moon-shaped sanctuary, where overwater and beach villas with private pools and sun decks open to endless ocean views.
Dining is an experience in itself, from treetop venues to Japanese and Italian restaurants, vibrant buffets, and beachfront bars. Families are thoughtfully catered to at the award-winning Little Griffins Kids Club, featuring a pirate ship, creative play areas, and nature-inspired learning spaces.
Adults can find their sanctuary at Spa by JW, offering bespoke treatments, a deluxe suite, yoga pavilion, and overwater gym. For those seeking adventure, the island offers snorkeling, diving, sailing, and jet-skiing, while moments of relaxation await by the infinity pool or along pristine beaches.
At JW Marriott Maldives, every detail is designed to help you Stay in the Moment and embrace island serenity at its finest.

Dining
Aailaa – All-Day Dining
Enjoy stunning views of the Indian Ocean at Aailaa, a relaxed all-day dining restaurant perfect for couples, families, and friends. Start your day with a sumptuous breakfast buffet featuring live cooking stations, or savor an à la carte lunch and dinner with authentic Maldivian and international flavors, thoughtfully crafted by our chefs. Combining vibrant flavors with a casual, welcoming ambiance, Aailaa offers a truly memorable dining experience in a breathtaking island setting.
Wine Room
Nestled within Aailaa, the Wine Room is a haven for wine enthusiasts. Explore an impressive collection of 1,620 bottles spanning 320 labels from both Old and New World regions. Whether you’re pairing a fine wine with your meal or discovering new favorites, the Wine Room offers a refined and intimate experience in the heart of the all-day dining restaurant.
Fiamma – Italian Dining
Relax poolside and savor the flavors of Italy at Fiamma, where freshly prepared risottos, pastas, and signature brick-oven pizzas delight the senses. Complement your meal with Italian-inspired cocktails while enjoying sweeping views of the Indian Ocean. With a laid-back, al fresco setting, Fiamma combines comforting Italian cuisine with a vibrant, relaxed ambiance, perfect for enjoying a memorable dining experience in the Maldives.
Kaashi – Thai Dining
Perched high in the treetops on the island’s southern tip, Kaashi offers a unique dining experience with panoramic views of the Indian Ocean. Enjoy the vibrant, fresh flavors of Thailand, from aromatic curries to delicate stir-fries, complemented by refreshing rum cocktails. The serene treetop setting and cool sea breeze make Kaashi the perfect spot to relax and savor authentic Thai cuisine in a breathtaking island environment.
Hashi – Japanese Teppanyaki
Experience the drama and flavor of live teppanyaki at Hashi, where skilled chefs prepare fresh Japanese delicacies right before your eyes. Enjoy a selection of dishes crafted from the finest land and sea ingredients, complemented by Japanese-inspired cocktails, sake, whisky, tea, or fine wines. Set in an elegant overwater location, Hashi combines classic Japanese décor with an interactive dining experience, perfect for savoring authentic flavors in a stunning island setting.
Shio – Fine Dining Grill
Set over the water in a romantic island setting, Shio offers an elegant fine-dining experience in the Maldives. Savor the freshest seafood and prime cuts, expertly prepared on the grill, while enjoying signature smoked cocktails and an extensive selection of fine wines. With its refined ambiance and breathtaking overwater views, Shio is the perfect destination for an unforgettable culinary journey.
Wabi Sabi – Overwater Japanese Lounge
Perched over the Laccadive Sea, Wabi Sabi offers a sophisticated overwater setting to enjoy Japanese-inspired cuisine and signature smoked cocktails. Complement your experience with an extensive selection of fine wines, Japanese sakes, whiskies, and teas. With its serene ambiance and breathtaking views, Wabi Sabi is the perfect spot for a refined and memorable evening in the Maldives.
Rum Baan – Treetop Rum Lounge
Nestled beside Kaashi in a unique treetop setting, Rum Baan offers a curated selection of 98 signature rums from around the world. Enjoy Thai-inspired rum cocktails while taking in panoramic views of the island and ocean. With its elevated location and vibrant yet relaxed ambiance, Rum Baan is the perfect spot to unwind and savor a refined rum experience in the Maldives.
In-Villa Dining
Enjoy the comfort and privacy of your villa with our 24-hour In-Villa Dining service. Choose from a wide selection of local and international dishes, thoughtfully crafted to satisfy every palate. Our attentive team is on hand to accommodate dietary preferences and special requests, ensuring a personalized and seamless dining experience without leaving your villa.
Destination Dining
Experience unforgettable moments with JW Marriott Maldives’ bespoke Destination Dining. Choose from unique settings such as romantic candlelit dinners on the beach, floating breakfasts, or private meals in your villa, and savor specially curated dishes paired with wine recommended by our sommelier. Every experience is tailored to your preferences, combining exquisite cuisine with breathtaking island surroundings for a truly memorable dining journey under the stars.
Horizon – Beach Bar
Relax at Horizon, the Maldives beach bar, where swing seats and poolside seating invite you to enjoy refreshing tropical cocktails and mocktails. Take in panoramic views of the Laccadive Sea while watching a majestic sunset or enjoy live local entertainment in the evening. Horizon offers a vibrant yet laid-back atmosphere, perfect for unwinding and soaking up the beauty of the island.
