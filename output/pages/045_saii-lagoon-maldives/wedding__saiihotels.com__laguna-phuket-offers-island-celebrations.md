Island Celebrations
Celebrate Christmas and New Year beside Bangtao Beach
Bring everyone together for a festive season filled with flavour, laughter and beachfront moments at SAii Laguna Phuket. Set between a tropical lagoon and the golden sands of Bangtao Beach, our resort is made for Christmas and New Year celebrations with family and friends.
Enjoy a traditional Christmas Eve family buffet at Terra&Mar, then choose how to welcome the New Year—from a beachside dinner at SunRay and a Mediterranean family feast at Miss Olive Oyl to our sparkling New Year’s Eve Gala Celebration.
Explore the complete festive programme and find the celebration that feels right for you. Festive dining experiences may be reserved separately and do not require an accommodation booking.
Come for dinner, stay for the celebrations and make this festive season one to remember by the Andaman Sea.
