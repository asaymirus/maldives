First up, your burning questions:
Who can get married under the Marriage Equality Bill in Thailand?
Couples over the age of 18, of any gender, are now eligible to get married in Thailand, regardless of nationality.
Can non-Thai same-sex couples get married in Thailand?
Yes, foreigners of any gender can get married in Thailand as long as they are 18 years old and can prove they are not already married. After their marriage is registered in Thailand, it’s legally recognised by the Thai government.
What rights do same-sex couples have in Thailand?
Same-sex couples have the same legal rights as heterosexual couples, including a legally recognised marriage, tax benefits, joint assets, social security and health care, adoption and guardianship of a child and inheritance.
Is Thailand LGBT-Friendly?
Even before the bill passed, Thailand was considered one of Asia’s most queer-friendly countries. Now with legal same-sex marriage and laws protecting transgender rights, it’s never been safer and more inclusive.
