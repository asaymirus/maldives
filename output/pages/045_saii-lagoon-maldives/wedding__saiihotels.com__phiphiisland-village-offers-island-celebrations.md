Island Celebrations
Celebrate Christmas and New Year on the quieter side of Phi Phi Island
This festive season, leave the everyday behind and come together amid the tropical beauty of Loh Ba Gao Bay. Framed by coconut palms, turquoise waters and an 800-metre white-sand beach, SAii Phi Phi Island Village sets the scene for a Christmas and New Year filled with island flavour.
Enjoy festive dining by the sea, relaxed family moments and joyful celebrations beneath the stars. From a gourmet Christmas Eve at Miss Olive Oyl and festive feasts at Api Restaurant to our New Year’s Eve Gala on the Lazy Pool Lawn, every occasion brings its own taste of tropical magic.
Explore the complete festive programme and choose how you would like to celebrate. Festive dining experiences may be reserved separately and do not require an accommodation booking.
Come for the flavours, stay for the atmosphere and celebrate the most wonderful time of the year—Phi Phi style.
