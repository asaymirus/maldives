Maldivian Escape – Cast Away, Your Way
The moment your toes touch our sun-kissed sands, you’ll know you’ve arrived somewhere truly special. Make your dream Maldives escape as exhilarating or serene as you please at SAii Lagoon Maldives, Curio Collection by Hilton – a multi-island haven of comfort just 15 minutes by speedboat from Velana International Airport. Bask in overwater bliss as gentle waves lap beneath your villa. Sip cooling cocktails by your very own
private pool
. Or embark on adventures above and below the surface of the Indian Ocean.
With plush Maldives
overwater villas
, an array of
dining options,
diving and water sports, and lots of
activities
for younger guests, our paradise island is perfect for both couples and families. Discover curated escapes with our SAii Gurus, enjoy screen-free serenity with Digital Detox or unwind with ocean-inspired wellness at our
spa
.
The island is also home to The Marina at CROSSROADS Maldives – the Maldives’ largest lifestyle hub – so you’ll never be short of incredible things to see and do during your stay at this elegant Maldives resort.

FAQs
Are pets allowed at Saii Lagoon Maldives?
No, unfortunately pets are not allowed.
How do I reach Saii Lagoon Maldives?
The resort is accessible by speedboat from Velana International Airport. After you make your booking, our reservation team will reach out to arrange your transfers. Return shared speedboat transfer prices: Adults (12 years old and above): USD 178, Children (6–11 years old): USD 89, Children (5 years and below): Complimentary. Private return speed oat transfer: USD 1,500 for maximum of 15 pax.
Does SAii Lagoon Maldives provide free bicycles?
Bicycles can be rented at USD 10++, per bicycle per day.
Which currency is accepted at SAii Lagoon Maldives?
Our base transactional currency is USD. Payments made using any other curerncy will be subject to transaction fees and exchange rates.
Does SAii Lagoon Maldives accept Union Pay?
Yes.
What is the Wi-Fi speed at SAii Lagoon Maldives?
The Wi-Fi speed at the resort is 5 Mbps per second.
Which type of power sockets are available at SAii Lagoon Maldives
All rooms are fitted with universal sockets and USB ports.
Does SAii Lagoon Maldives provide a baby-sitting service?
Yes, baby-sitting services are provided for children aged from 1 year and above.
Does SAii Lagoon Maldives provide a baby crib for infants?
Yes, cribs are available free of charge.
Does SAii Lagoon Maldives have rooms for differently abled guests?
No, unfortunately we don’t at this time.
What are the basic entry requirements to enter the Maldives?
The Maldives Traveller Declaration has to be completed 96 hours prior to arrival through IMUGA Maldives (https://imuga.immigration.gov.mv/traveller).
Does Saii Lagoon Maldives offer a kids' club and kid-friendly activities?
Yes, we have a designated kids’ club and lots of
kid-friendly activities
.
What are the restaurants at SAii Lagoon Maldives?
There are a range of dining options available at the resort as well as the Hard Rock Hotel and Crossroads nearby.
What is covered by the all-inclusive meal plan?
All meals plus selected drinks (alcoholic and non-alcoholic) from selected outlets during operational hours (https://www.saiihotels.com/lagoon-maldives/dining/saii-meal-plans/).
