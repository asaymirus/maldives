DESCRIPTION
Where Tropical Tranquillity Meets Vibrant Island Life
SAii Lagoon Maldives, Curio Collection by Hilton, is a private island escape just 15 minutes by boat from Malé International Airport. Designed for couples, families, and friends, this bold and breezy retreat blends barefoot luxury with endless adventure.
Connected to The Marina @ CROSSROADS Maldives by a scenic footbridge, guests enjoy direct access to fine dining, high-end shopping, and indulgent spa experiences. Spend days snorkelling vibrant reefs, diving into the Indian Ocean, or simply basking in the serenity of your overwater villa or private pool.
This is not just a resort, but a multi-island lifestyle destination where relaxation and discovery flow together in perfect harmony.

Dining
Terra & Mar – All Day Dining
Savour Mediterranean-inspired cuisine where land and sea meet. Start your day with a SAiiSational breakfast of global favourites and local delicacies. Enjoy a flavourful lunch and dinner journey across world cuisines. Don’t miss our indulgent weekend brunches and themed buffet dinners.
Mr. Tomyam – Poolside Trattoria
Dive into a bold fusion of Thai flavours and global poolside bites with a playful Asian twist. From zesty Thai classics to Italian-inspired dishes and local delights, Mr. Tomyam serves up a vibrant culinary journey by the pool for every inSAiider.
Miss Olive Oyl – Beachside Grill & Wine Haven
Set on SAii Lagoon’s pristine white sands, Miss Olive Oyl is a chic beachside escape inspired by the Earth’s energy and ocean breeze. Enjoy expertly grilled dishes, Maldivian favourites, and prime-aged cuts infused with global flair. From fine wines to stylish indoor-outdoor dining, savour laid-back elegance by the Indian Ocean, day to night.
Pool Bar – Oceanfront Sips and Bites
From midday to midnight, unwind with tropical cocktails, cozy drinks, and classic bar bites. Whether lounging on a sunbed, relaxing by the bar, or sinking into a poolside couch, enjoy uninterrupted ocean views and stunning sunsets with the soothing sounds of the sea all around you.
Gelato Bar – Cool Treats and Coastal Coffee
Start your day with rich coastal coffee and ease into island life with homemade gelato, milkshakes, teas, and fresh fruits. As the day winds down, Gelato Bar transforms into a lively social lounge. Whether it’s a sweet snack or your perfect brew, this is your go-to spot to relax and refresh, any time of day.
Swim-Up Bar – Sip, Splash, relax
Chill out in the pool and sip on refreshing cocktails without leaving the water. Located at the SAii Beach Club pool, this laid-back swim-up bar offers cool drinks and tasty bites in collaboration with Mr. Tomyam. Perfect for sunny days, poolside lounging, and family fun with your favourite drink always within reach.
Badhige at SAii – Garden to Table Experience
Enjoy a hands-on culinary journey focused on healthy, sustainable dining. Badhige offers interactive cooking and private dining experiences using fresh, organic ingredients from our garden and the Maldivian seas. Learn the art of mindful eating while savouring delicious, locally inspired dishes in a fun and educational setting.
SAii Beach Club – Chill, Dine, celebrate
Soak up chic island vibes at SAii Beach Club, where infinity pool views meet beachfront bliss. Enjoy handcrafted gelato, poolside dining, premium casual restaurants, specialty bars, and a stylish boutique all in one vibrant space. With upbeat tunes, laid-back luxury, and a curated multi-cuisine menu, it’s the perfect place to unwind barefoot by the sea. Ideal for exclusive events, weddings, and corporate gatherings in a serene, scenic setting away from the crowds.
Jiao Wu – Hot & Pot by the Ocean
Dive into bold Cantonese flavours with a fresh Hot & Pot twist at Jiao Wu. This stylish alfresco deck serves up a vibrant dining experience with stunning Indian Ocean views, blending classic favourites with modern flair in a relaxed waterfront setting.
Kebab & Kurry – Tandoori with a Twist
Savour the rich flavours of authentic tandoori cooking in a vibrant, modern setting. Kebab & Kurry blends centuries-old recipes with bold mixology and industrial-chic décor. Aromas, colours, and spices come together for a lively dining experience that reimagines classic Indian cuisine with a contemporary flair.
Kin Kao – Thai Flavours by the Marina
Set near the arrival jetty, Kin Kao offers a laid-back Thai dining experience with panoramic views of the Yacht Marina. Enjoy authentic dishes in a breezy alfresco setting that turns truly magical after sunset, making it a must-visit spot from day to night.
Dine by Design – Curated Culinary Moments
Discover unforgettable dining in the Maldives with our Dine by Design experiences. From torchlit dinners on a secluded beach to private meals in your villa or floating breakfasts by the pool, each setting is thoughtfully chosen to surprise and delight. Enjoy personalised service in the resort’s most intimate locations for a truly memorable culinary journey.
