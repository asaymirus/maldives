SAii Phi Phi Island Village Launches Astro Moments: A Mystical Stargazing & Tarot Experience Under the Andaman Sky
Published on: Dec 19, 2024
SAii Phi Phi Island Village
PHI PHI ISLAND, THAILAND (December 2024)
: SAii Phi Phi Island Village, the idyllic beachside retreat from S Hotels and Resorts PCL, invites guests to discover new realms of wonder with “Astro Moments”—a unique program combining celestial stargazing and insightful tarot readings against a backdrop of lush, palm-fringed landscapes and pristine Andaman Sea vistas. Designed for those seeking both personal discovery and cosmic exploration, these experiences offer a one-of-a-kind connection to nature, self, and the universe.
An Evening Under the Stars in Loh Ba Gao Bay
Every Tuesday from 7:30 to 9:30 p.m., the resort’s experienced SAiiLanders lead an interactive stargazing session on the sands of Loh Ba Gao Bay. Guests are introduced to the wonders of the cosmos through an astronomy talk, complete with guidance on identifying stars, constellations, and planets. Using a tablet-based app, binoculars, and star maps, participants uncover the night sky’s secrets with ease and fascination. For a touch of romance, guests can elevate the evening with a Stargazing Hamper featuring shareable bites and a bottle of sparkling wine, available for THB 3,000 per couple (subject to service charge and government tax).
The Stargazing Hamper includes
:
Starry Night Chicken Skewers – Marinated with capsicum and pineapple
Tortilla Wraps under the Stars – Wok-minced chicken with chili basil sauce
Herb-Infused Pork Neck under the Stars – Grilled with Thai herbs and tamarind
Starlit Smoked Salmon Delight – Whole wheat toast, smoked salmon, and cream cheese
Starry Spiced Tom Yum Peanuts – Peanuts with chili, kaffir, and lemongrass
Starlight SAii Cookies – Sweet treats in a cookie jar
Personalised Tarot Readings: A Journey Within
Guests can explore their inner landscapes through personalised tarot readings led by English-speaking tarot readers.
These one-on-one sessions offer a unique perspective on future paths, personal insights, and self-reflection.
Sessions are available every Wednesday from 4:00 to 9:00 p.m. and Thursday from 9:00 to 11:00 a.m., priced at THB 1,500 for 30 minutes or THB 2,900 for 60 minutes.
Each reading is tailored to offer an introspective journey, making each session as enlightening as it is inspiring.
Blending the Mystical and Celestial
For those looking to connect the worlds of tarot and stargazing, SAii Phi Phi Island Village seamlessly integrates both experiences. Every Tuesday, guests can enjoy a stargazing activity, followed by a tarot reading session on Wednesday. Guided by their zodiac signs, guests will link insights from the tarot reading with the constellations above. This fusion of tarot and stargazing invites participants to draw connections between their celestial readings and the constellations above—a truly holistic experience that combines the mystical and celestial for deeper personal insights.
