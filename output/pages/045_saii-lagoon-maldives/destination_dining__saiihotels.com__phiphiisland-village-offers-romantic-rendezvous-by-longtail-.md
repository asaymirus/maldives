Romantic Rendezvous by Longtail Boat
Embark on an unforgettable four-hour luxury longtail boat cruise for two. Sail around the breathtaking Phi Phi archipelago, make magical memories swimming and snorkelling in crystal waters, explore paradise and sip Champagne with your special someone. Hop aboard your luxury private longtail boat and raise a glass to tropical romance.
THB 8,900
Cruise Options:
Morning Cruise
8.30 am – 12.30 pm (4 hours)
Snorkelling and sightseeing around Phi Phi Lay Island (Maya Bay), Pi-Leh Lagoon, Viking Cave and Monkey Bay.
Sunset Cruise
3.00 pm – 7.00 pm (4 hours)
Sail to Bamboo Island to spend the afternoon snorkelling, swimming and relaxing on the white sands as the sun sets over the sea.
Inclusions
Luxury longtail boat
Water and soft drinks
“Pinto” lunch box
Bottle of Champagne
Snorkelling equipment
Boat Captain
Marine national park entry fee
Terms & Conditions
