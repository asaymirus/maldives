The word Alimathà originally meant “woman’s face”. The island, seen from above, today has a shape that reminds of a heart, and has a beach of fine white sand that extends into the waters of the Maldives. It is surrounded by a splendid and colorful coral reef, inhabited by hundreds of species of fish and corals. The Resort offers its guests 96 Beach Bungalows, 34 Water Villas and 26 Garden Rooms. Alimathà is the perfect synthesis of two souls: one of relaxation, to which you can abandon yourself on its white beaches; and one of energy and movement, which will overwhelm those who love activities and sports.
