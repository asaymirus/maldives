Boaku
Embrace in sectusion & tranquility
On the eastern face of the island, is the Boaku, a sheltered beach, shaped like a cove, allowing the formation of a natural pool. The seclusion and stillness of the water inside Boaku, make it the perfect setting to catch the sunrise as well as a secure place to let your children play.
Veli
Enchanting stretch of pure white sand
On the very western face is Veli, a very wide and expansive flat beach of pure white sand, with crystal clear turquoise water that goes on and on, making it the most magnificent & the stunning backdrop to view the sunset.
Aanu
Captivating sound of breaking waves
The beach rock goes along the northern edge & length of the island, giving the island its unique shape, character, and natural protection.
