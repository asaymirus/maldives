Dhandifulhu Restaurant
Designed with charm and simplicity, the space itself exemplifies the dining experience as well as the style of cuisines offered at our main restaurant. With panoramic views of the ocean, the main restaurant offers a variety of flavors from every corner of the world and a dedicated Maldivian night, combining the rich flavors of our indigenous food with traditional entertainment. The restaurant also has its own bar with access to a large selection of beverages for your convenience.
