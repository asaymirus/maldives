Fishing Excursions
We offer three distinct types of fishing excursions.
Sunset Fishing
Perfect for the whole family and amateur fishermen and women. This excursion is more leisurely and relaxed, enabling you to try out more traditional fishing methods.
Game Fishing
A short boat ride to the open sea, this particular excursion is suited to the more professionally inclined and thrill seekers. We have a specially built boat, fully equipped with the necessary fishing gear and technology, as well as professionally trained experts to guide you through.
Local Fishing
For culture seekers, this excursion allows you to understand and experience the unique art of Maldivian fishing techniques using live bait. This experience is educational as well as adventurous, making it perfect for large family groups or couples.
