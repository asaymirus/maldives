Sangu Suite
The Sangu Suite is the epitome of luxury and space, situated in a semi-water location where land and lagoon merge into one. The suite features a private pool for cozying up or taking a few steps down into the vast lagoon. The villa is equipped with all the modern amenities you’ll need, and it leads to your own veranda enclosed within a private deck with views of the lagoon and the vast Indian Ocean.
