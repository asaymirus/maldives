Dolphin Cruise
Being located on a Marine Bio Reserve gives you the advantage of observing the marine ecosystem in its purest and untouched form. Baa Atoll is known for its varied marine life, especially manta rays, dolphins, and whales, providing you with the opportunity to watch them in their natural environment. We offer programmed and scheduled excursions to different spots near the island with professional guides, ensuring you have a memorable and educational experience.​
