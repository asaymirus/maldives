Water Villa
The exceptional 20 Sunrise and Sunset Water Villas epitomize comfort and leisure. These magnificent villas are bright, spacious, and luxuriously appointed. Each villa features its own staircase leading into the crystal-clear lagoon below, ideal for swimming or a snorkeling adventure. Spend your days relaxing just a few feet above the blue waters of the Indian Ocean, soaking up the sun from your private chaise lounge while being pampered by our attentive staff.
