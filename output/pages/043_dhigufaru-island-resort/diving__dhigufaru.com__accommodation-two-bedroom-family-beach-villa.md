Two Bedroom Family Beach Villa
The Two Bedroom Family Beach Villa is your very own family hideaway in the Maldives. These double luxury villas are spaces in which to relax and unwind together, offering a luxurious setting to create family memories that will last a lifetime. Perched on the beach facing toward the turquoise lagoon, each villa features two elegantly furnished bedrooms, two bathrooms, and an extensive veranda designed to feel like a private home.
