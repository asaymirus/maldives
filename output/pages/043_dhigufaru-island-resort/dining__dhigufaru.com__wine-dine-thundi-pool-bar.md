Thundi Pool Bar
Relax by the poolside or enjoy the view from inside one of the three detached lounge huts surrounding the bar. The Bageecha pool area is the perfect spot to share views of a romantic sunset, with easy access to the beach and a whole lot of entertainment. Thundi Pool Bar has a dedicated kitchen where you can order bistro-style cuisines and finger foods, including afternoon tea or coffee, as well as an extensive selection of exotic cocktails. It’s the perfect place to unwind after a swim in the sea or lounging by the poolside.
