Baraveli Kids Club
Our Baraveli Kids Club is more than just a crèche; it’s a place where kids can immerse themselves in the island’s diverse natural features and learn about the unique flora and fauna of the UNESCO Biosphere Reserve at Baa Atoll. Our dedicated team of childcare personnel ensures that your children stay engaged and entertained with diverse programs tailored to different age groups. The club boasts its own library space stocked with a variety of toys and books, along with a safe indoor playground area that includes a specially designed swimming pool exclusively for children.
