In-Villa Dining
The villas at Dhigufaru are so spacious, secluded, and enchanting that you may find it hard to leave. But with our In-Villa Dining service, you don’t have to. Enjoy complete privacy and the beautiful location while savoring exotic flavors from our 24-hour room service menu. Whether your villa is on the beach or over water, dine inside, on your over-water terrace, or on your private terrace facing the beach. From champagne breakfasts by the pool to lunches on the terrace and dinners under the stars on the beach, the choice is yours to make the most of your stay.
