Beach Villa
With stunning views and beach access from each of the 20 Sunrise and Sunset Beach Villas, you can choose between the serene and tranquil eastern facing Boaku or the stunning magnificence of Veli Beach. Each villa features a semi-outdoor shower space, an indoor bathroom, and his and her vanity space. The rooms are equipped with all the modern amenities you will need. Each room opens onto a private veranda enclosed within a garden, offering views of the beach and lagoon.
