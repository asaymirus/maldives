Athiri Grill
Athiri Grill is a popular dining option known for its casual yet elegant atmosphere. It is situated on the beach, allowing guests to enjoy their meals with stunning views of the ocean and the resort’s surroundings. Athiri Grill offers a menu focused on grilled seafood and meats, along with a variety of salads and side dishes. It’s a perfect spot for those seeking a relaxed dining experience with delightful food and a scenic setting in the Maldives.
