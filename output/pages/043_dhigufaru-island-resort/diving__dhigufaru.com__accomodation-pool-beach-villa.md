Pool Beach Villa
You couldn’t get any closer to the lagoon if you tried. With crystal-clear, shallow water beneath your private deck, it’s no wonder these are some of our most popular iconic Maldivian villa accommodations. Cozy up in the private plunge pool or take a few steps down into the vast lagoon. The room is equipped with all the modern amenities you’ll need. Each room opens onto your own veranda enclosed within a private deck with views of the lagoon and the vast Indian Ocean.
