Local Island Visits
A visit to a small inhabited island, where the population is only a few hundred at most, will give you a glimpse of the local culture and lifestyle. You’ll see kids strolling near the shore, playing with friends on the beach, and men and women busy with daily chores, illustrating how simple life can be on these islands.
