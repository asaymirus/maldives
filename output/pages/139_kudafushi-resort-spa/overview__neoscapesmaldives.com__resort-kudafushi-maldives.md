DESCRIPTION
‘The name Kudafushi means ‘little island’ in the local language – discover a small but perfectly-formed tropical nirvana’
The pretty island of Kudafushi is surrounded by a glittering turquoise lagoon. Beach villas are scattered amongst the coconut trees, whilst the water villas expand in one sweep across the surface of the lagoon. Its intimate size allows for guests to experience the personal touch; from the service of the staff right through to the emphasis on local traditions and culture, and caring for the precious marine environment. The restaurants showcase the best of Maldivian, Mediterranean and international cuisine, and the serene spa features no less than 10 treatment rooms where you can feel your worries melt away. A kid’s club, Little Bubbles, ensures your little ones will be kept entertained, so you can enjoy some romantic moments with your partner.
