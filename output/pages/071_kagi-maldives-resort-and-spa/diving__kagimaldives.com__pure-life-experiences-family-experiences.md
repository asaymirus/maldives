FAMILY EXPERIENCES |
HAPPY MEMORIES
MOMENTS TO CHERISH
Our island welcomes guests of all ages with an array of fun and special island activities to cherish for a lifetime.
ISLAND ESCAPADES
Embrace our selection of fun filled outdoor activities and creative play for the special young one’s. From sand castle building to coconut painting, in Kagi Maldives, we welcome all ages to be part of our Pure Life Experiences.
CREATIVE MINDS
Creativity and a little bit of healthy competition, in Kagi Maldives entertainment room. Here, our families can join in some fun games including billiards, foosball, table tennis and a range of fun child friendly games. We love socialization and there is no better place than this for kids and families to connect and build some joyous memories.
KIDS SCUBA DIVING
The Maldives offers one of the best places in the world for kids to dive – from those taking their first breaths underwater, to water babies who already have a few dives in their logbooks.

The calm, clear, shallow lagoons are the perfect place for learning to dive.
