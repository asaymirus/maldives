Reshma's career trajectory underscores a robust foundation in the hospitality industry, highlighting her expertise and unwavering commitment to Human Resources (HR). Her transition to Kagi Maldives in August 2023 marked a pivotal juncture in her professional journey, where she drew upon her prior experiences at esteemed hospitality brands like Four Seasons and Hilton.
Reshma holds a dual degree in Bachelor of Arts in International Hospitality and Tourism Management from Queen Margaret University (QMU), which enriches her understanding of the industry's intricacies.
By leveraging her technical knowledge and extensive experience, Reshma assumes a leadership role in driving HR departmental processes at Kagi Maldives. She adeptly manages a spectrum of HR policies and procedures while actively fostering engagement among Kagi team members. Her career trajectory serves as an inspiring exemplar of how the amalgamation of past experiences with technical proficiency can catalyze substantial career advancement and organizational impact.
Nasheed, hailing from the vibrant locale of Addu City Hithadhoo, joins our management team at Kagi Maldives as the Executive Housekeeper in July 2023, driven by a profound passion for hospitality. He embarked on his journey in the industry with a Diploma in Hospitality Management from Maldives National University in 2016.
Throughout his illustrious career, Nasheed has contributed his expertise to various CCR properties in the Maldives.
With nearly 23 years of diverse experience in the hospitality industry, Nasheed brings a wealth of knowledge and expertise to his role. He embodies a philosophy of knowledge-sharing, aligning passion with purpose, and consistently inspiring greatness among his team. Nasheed firmly believes that cultivating a culture of happiness among team members leads to exceptional results.
Lammath joined the Kagi team as Assistant Reservations Manager in 2022. Prior to joining Kagi, she spent 9+ years at Universal Enterprises. Her career began as a Reservations Agent and worked her way up to becoming a Reservation Supervisor in February 2018. With over a decade of experience in the tourism industry, she is a noteworthy leader in her workplace. Known best among her colleagues as a self-motivated and detail-oriented leader, who brings a positive energy to the workplace.
Sushil is a globe trotter with 14 years of experience in hotel and resort engineering. He has done his bachelors from University of Rajasthan in Electrical Engineering. Ahmed Areef joined the Kagi team as Assistant Purchasing Manager in 2022, bringing with him approximately 8 years of extensive experience in the industry. Throughout his career, he has demonstrated his expertise in various roles within the procurement field, having served in several renowned 5-star, 4-star, and ultra-luxury resorts. Ahmed embarked on his professional journey in 2011 and has since meticulously progressed through different positions, steadily advancing his skills and expertise in the field. Raised in Lh.Kurendhoo, his background roots him deeply within the Maldivian culture and landscape.
Dr. Hassan Shafi is the Resident Doctor at Kagi Maldives, bringing over a decade of experience in clinical operations, patient care, and emergency management. With a diverse background in general medicine and wellness, Dr. Shafi is adept at managing the health and well-being of both resort guests and staff. His expertise includes handling medical emergencies, conducting routine health checks, and implementing preventive care measures. He played a pivotal role in managing COVID-19 protocols at previous resorts. Dr. Shafi’s commitment to high-quality care ensures a safe and healthy environment at Kagi Maldives Resort and Spa.
Simad’s professional journey reflects a strong foundation in luxury hospitality, shaped by leadership roles at world-renowned resorts such as The Westin Maldives and The St. Regis Maldives. His career is defined by a guest-centric philosophy, operational excellence, and a consistent commitment to delivering seamless and memorable guest experiences.
Recognized for his calm and compassionate leadership style, Simad excels at cultivating team environments built on trust, respect, and collaboration. His ability to inspire teams through empathy and clarity aligns seamlessly with Kagi’s values and its dedication to heartfelt, authentic service.
Beyond the workplace, Simad finds balance through reading and swimming, activities that nurture both focus and creativity. Guided by the words of Seneca, “Wherever there is a human being, there is an opportunity for kindness,” he brings a philosophy of genuine care, gratitude, and human connection to every aspect of his personal and professional life.
