DIVING AT KAGI MALDIVES
Immerse yourself in the beauty of our pristine house reel
and nearby dive sites, guided by our experienced PADI instructors.
We offer diving experiences tailored to your preferences, ensuring you enioy
the luxury of diving at your own pace and comfort. Our PADI courses cater
to all levels, providing an ultimate underwater adventure
DISCOVER SCUBA DIVING
This introductory dive is tailored for beginners with no prior experience,
offering a seamless blend ol ease and excitement. Under the guidance
of our expert PADI instructors, you’ll explore the stunning house reef, getting up close to vibrant marine life and beautiful coral gardens.
SNORKELING AT KAGI MALDIVES
Snorkel Your Way to Paradise. Explore the stunning coral reefs of the
Maldives at Kagi. Marvel at diverse reef formations and embark on
exciting adventures in search of turtles, sharks, rays, and more. At Kagi,
we offer a range of snorkeling options, from exploring our
lively house reef to embarking on boat snorkeling trips.
HOUSE REEF DIVING
Dive in the comfort of our exclusive Kagi house reef, guided or unguided.
Access is easy from the arrival jetty, water sports beach, or sunset jetty.
After your welcome dive session, you can explore the house reef with
a buddy at your own pace.
HOUSE REEF NIGHT EXPLORATION
Experience the magic of the underwater world after dark with both snorkeling
and diving. The reef at night is teeming with action - from giant trevallies
hunting to sightings of nurse sharks, reef sharks, lobsters, octopuses, and
critters rarely seen during the day. For a unique experience, try the
Fluoresencent night dive, which reveals the reef in a whole new light.
DIVING FOR KIDS
Childhood memories of scuba diving among the fish and turtles of
the Maldives will last a lifetime. Children as young as eight can start diving,
and catching the diving bug at a young age opens up endless possibilities
for travel and careeres they might never have dreamed of.
It’s easy, it’s safe, and it’s fun.
