TRAVEL JOURNEY |
Your secluded destination
TRANSFERS TO KAGI MALDIVES
Peacefully situated on a charming private island, within the picturesque North Male Atoll is where you will find Kagi Maldives. The natural tropical island offers an unrivaled lifestyle amongst the natural beauty and breath-taking scenery of the pristine Maldives Indian Ocean. Here one can find the presence of serenity where your mind can become still and recognize the Art of Life.
Kagi Maldives is only a short distance from Velana International Airport, within a proximity of 57 kilometres.
Being part of this idyllic destination in the Indian Ocean, Maldives offers you the convenience of visiting the tropical island paradise anytime throughout the year.
KAGI MALDIVES ARRIVALS
First impressions last forever
The admirable beauty of this unique destination starts upon your journey from Velana International Airport to the resort. Traveling to Kagi Maldives offers you a choice between an iconic sea plane or a scenic open air sea transfer, either by a luxury yacht or a thrilling speedboat.
Upon arrival, after clearing Maldives Immigration and Customs formalities, you will be meeting our airport representatives who will take it from there.
sea plane journey
Perfect for day time traveling to or from the resort. You will be escorted to the Trans Maldivian Airways Terminal by a short transfer. Complimentary access and use of the Kagi Maldives Sea Plane Lounge is available, a perfect space to refresh after a long travel journey.
Duration - 15 minutes
Price USD 420.00 per person, combined, round trip

journey
Perfectly convenient for traveling between day and night hours to and from the resort. This adventurous travel journey introduces you to the majestically Maldivian ocean and you might just catch the most beautiful sunset or spot cheerful dolphins on your travel route.

Price USD 410.00 per person, combined, round trip
LUXURY YACHT JOURNEY
From the moment you step aboard, you enter a luxurious space, allowing you to sit back, relax and cruise your way to the resort. This luxury vessel with it’s distinctive design is entwined with the luxury of the resort which awaits you upon your arrival.

EXPLORE OUR ISLAND
The resort offers unsurpassed panoramic views of the turquoise blue ocean and is located on top of a natural coral house reef offering an abundance of undersea wild life. The petite island is 42,421 square metres (10.48 Acres/4.24Hectares) in size with 691metres of beach line.
