Baani (“The Ocean Swell” in Maldives Local Language- Dhivehi) reminds us of the rhythm of life.
The symbolical meaning of Baani is a personal journey to healthy and joyful living.
Our service menu has been crafted to deliver an authentic, personalised, outcome-focused wellness program which promises to take you on the Baani Journey to Release, Restore and Regain.
