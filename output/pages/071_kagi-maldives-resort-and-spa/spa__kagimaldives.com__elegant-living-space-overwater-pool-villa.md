PURE ELEGANCE OVER THE WATER
Awaken to endless horizons where the turquoise lagoon meets the sky. Kagi Maldives offers 5 Over Water Pool Villas, thoughtfully designed to immerse you in the beauty of the Indian Ocean. Each villa features a spacious, light-filled bedroom, an elegant indoor-outdoor living space, and a private sun deck with a plunge pool overlooking the crystal-clear waters. Relax in complete privacy, soak up the Maldivian sunshine, and step directly into the vibrant lagoon for an unforgettable overwater escape.
UNWIND IN UNPARALLELED ISLAND LUXURY
Experience the timeless allure of life above the Indian Ocean in our Over Water Pool Villas. Thoughtfully designed to blend contemporary comfort with the serenity of nature, each villa offers uninterrupted panoramic ocean views, a private infinity plunge pool, and direct access to the crystal-clear lagoon. Spend your days exploring the vibrant house reef just steps from your deck, basking in the warm Maldivian sunshine, or simply unwinding to the soothing rhythm of the waves. Every moment is an invitation to embrace complete privacy, effortless luxury, and a profound connection with the ocean.
