HOUSE REEF
Kagi boasts with a spectacular house reef. The house reef can be classified as one of the best house reefs in Maldives. Expect to see healthy corals, bright colorful tropical fishes and cute sea creatures; sea turtles, rays, reef sharks, moray eels and a chance to spot a few nurse sharks sleeping before the night show start. Kagi house reef is a hidden treasure to feast the eyes for any scuba diver and snorkeler. Access the reef from your villa stairs or straight from the beach.

MSTS at KAGI MALDIVES
Maldives Super Transfer Service (MSTS), founded in 2007, brings long-standing ocean expertise and a passion for Maldivian waters. Evolving from inter-atoll transport to safari cruising and now diving and watersports, our team prioritizes safety, guest care, sustainability, and innovation. As a PADI 5 Star dive center, we offer courses and underwater adventures. Our multilingual team and in-house engineers ensure smooth, reliable operations.
