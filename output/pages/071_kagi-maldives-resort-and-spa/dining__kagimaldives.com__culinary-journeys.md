KE - UN
Ke-Un brings to life a ‘Pacific Rim’ fusion cuisine experience which takes its inspiration from the geographical ‘ring of fire’ surrounding the Maldives. From the highly spiced and fragranced cuisine of East Asia to the bold, fiery flavors of South America and Oceania, here you can revel into good food prepared with the utmost attention whilst appreciating quality time with your companions.
