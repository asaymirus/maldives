PURE ELEGANCE OVER THE WATER
Awaken your soul with the sound of the waves lapping gently beneath your villa. Kagi Maldives offers 22 Ocean Pool Villas, each featuring a spacious, light aired room leading to a private verandah and plunge pool with panoramic views of the Indian ocean. A space to indulge in the Maldivian sunshine and delight in the live aquarium before your very eyes.
EXPERIENCE THE EPITOME OF MALDIVIAN LUXURY
An understated elegance and secluded space brings to life a bucket list experience of living in an over water villa. Access the spectacular house reef directly from the stairs into the ocean or mesmerize yourself in the comfort of your own private pool over the ocean with panoramic views all around. An alluring sense of harmony between peace and satisfaction is what you are bound to experience in these villas.
