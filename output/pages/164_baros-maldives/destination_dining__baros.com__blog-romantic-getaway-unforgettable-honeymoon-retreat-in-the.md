Romantic Honeymoon Getaway in Maldives
18 September 2023 | All Blogs, Destination, Dining, Experience
Baros
Maldives is everyone’s idea of romance. Pristine white beaches, brilliant blue sea and tropical green are the perfect island backdrop for the honeymoon of your dreams. Create unforgettable moments together in paradise.
Experience soulful moments, from a curated floating breakfast in the privacy of your villa pool to a sunset cruise followed by a candlelight dinner on a sandbank, surrounded by flickering candles and softly lapping waves.
Recognised for romance
Baros has been named the “World’s Most Romantic Resort” in 2022 and “Indian Ocean’s Most Romantic Resort” for ten consecutive years by the World Travel Awards. For five decades we have been perfecting our unique offering of extraordinary, personalised service, beautiful private luxury villas and delectable culinary offerings. We also have many memorable romantic and intimate experiences for couples to enjoy, making Baros the perfect destination for creating memories you will treasure forever.
Celebrate love
After the
vows
have been said, the cake has been cut and the presents opened, it is time to celebrate each other. Nowhere is more perfect for this than an idyllic, private island hideaway. Here you will have the space to explore, indulge, delight and celebrate paradise and your love.
Make intimate connections as you enjoy romantic moments and create memories that will last a lifetime as you embark on one-of-a-kind adventures together.
Spoilt for choice
Baros is a quaint, intimate private island but one that has a wealth of options available for you. Whether it be different choices of villa, restaurants, activities or even what pillow to sleep on, Baros offers you choices that make every moment perfect for you.
Destination dining
Our restaurants are all excellent. All day dining at our
Lime Restaurant
, al fresco over the open flame at
Cayenne
or fine dining at our signature restaurant,
The Lighthouse
– you are guaranteed to be delighted. In addition, Baros offers many other
destination dining
options for couples in unique and breathtaking settings with a customised menu for just the two of you.
Guests at Baros are spoiled for choice with a range of destination dining experiences including dining on a secluded sandbank, on island by the shore or the comfort of your own villa deck, on the Piano Deck amidst the blues of the Baros lagoon, in the middle of the Indian Ocean aboard our luxury yacht Serenity or a romantic sunset cruise followed by dinner on the traditional sailing dhoni – Nooma.
Indulgent spa treatments
Back on the island,
Serenity Spa
is a haven of relaxation. Set like a gem in the middle of lush foliage, it is a sanctuary of indulgent beauty rituals. Enjoy our Elemis Romantic Spa Retreat for Couples. This ceremony, especially designed for couples, will entice you into a transcendent relaxation experience. This immersive pampering will ease any post-wedding tension, enhance your intimacy and leave you refreshed and rejuvenated.
Romantic moments
As a favourite romantic hideaway for couples, we have created a bespoke offer that will ensure you that blissful rendezvous of your dreams. Celebrate your honeymoon, anniversary or a special time together with our
Romantic Moments
package.
We have assisted many couples add a special touch to make their romantic moments more memorable and consider it a privilege to be able to do so. Contact our team at
reservations@baros.com
to book your romantic island honeymoon.
