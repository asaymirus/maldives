The Maldives Is One Of The Most Romantic Places In The World To Propose
5 January 2022 | All Blogs, Destination, Dining, Experience
St Valentine’s Day is fast approaching, and it is one of the most popular days to propose. The Maldives is something of a fantasy island, and the ultimate destination for a truly memorable proposal. A destination proposal is a great way to combine a Maldives vacation with a life-changing engagement. Baros, voted ‘Most Romantic Resort’ for nine consecutive years by World Travel Awards, adds a bit of extra magic to your love story.
Consider Baros’ six proposal-worthy island settings for making your marriage proposal extra special and enchanting.
1. During A Picnic On A Deserted Sandbank
Indulge in a champagne picnic prepared by your personal chef and served on an idyllic private sandbank in the middle of the Indian Ocean – it’s the perfect place to get down on one knee and pop the question.
2. In A Swoon-Worthy Overwater Villa
There is something especially romantic about staying in a villa suspended right over the ocean – especially a villa with its own pool and direct access to the house reef. Propose to the one who matters the most in a private cocoon of love.
3. At Twilight Floating On A Crystal Clear Lagoon
In that enigmatic moment between sunset and dusk when the sky appears violet-blue, get down on bended knee on a timber deck floating in the lagoon. Toast your future together and savour a special gourmet meal prepared by your private chef. It’s a promising way to celebrate your coming marriage and create a lasting memory.
4. On A Luxury Yacht
Set sail for an unknown yet dreamy atoll created especially just for two. Any place on this idyllic island is proposal-worthy and our Baros team will find the perfect spot to ensure your betrothal is a treasured memory for the rest of your lives.
5. Aboard A Nooma Love Boat
A romantic cruise aboard a traditional Dhoni is the perfect craft for sunset sailing and a romantic engagement dinner just for two. As the sun dips below the horizon, say those four magic words and create a memory that neither of you will ever forget.
6. At A Toes-In-The-Sand Beach Dinner
Propose beneath a starry night on a secluded stretch of beach. Afterwards indulge in a gourmet dinner at a table set for romance just metres from gently lapping waves. The shoreside setting will add a special element of wonder to your proposal.
