Wellness Practitioner
Jang Kanlayanee
We are delighted to welcome renowned wellness practitioner, Jang Kanlayanee to Baros’s Serenity Spa for an exclusive collaboration from 1st to 15th September 2024.
Hailing from Thailand, Jang brings over 25 years of experience in the spa, wellness and hospitality sectors. With a background in Health Sciences, she has held prestigious roles at five-star wellness resorts such as Kamalaya Thailand and Soneva Kiri Six Senses. Jang’s expertise in wellness and yoga therapies is highlighted by her contributions to luxury resorts like One & Only Resorts in Australia and Museflower Retreat & Spa in Chiang Rai.
Signature treatments and wellness experience by Jang
During her exclusive residency, Baros guests will have the unique opportunity to experience Jang’s exceptional treatments, including her signature Quantum Touch Sound Healing.
