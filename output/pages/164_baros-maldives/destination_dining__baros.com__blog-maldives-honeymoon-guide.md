Ultimate Maldives Honeymoon Guide
23 January 2023 | All Blogs, Destination
Coconut palms whisper above as a soft breeze billows white linen. Side by side, you gaze into the endless blue of sea and sky and know that love doesn’t get better than this. Baros Maldives is the ultimate in romantic, luxurious island destinations, the perfect place for honeymooning couples.
Named the ‘World’s Most Romantic Resort’ in 2022 for the seventh time in ten years by the World Travel Awards, Baros also received the title of ‘Indian Ocean’s Most Romantic Resort’ for the tenth consecutive year in 2022 and was nominated as the ‘Best Honeymoon Resort in 2021’ by Elle Bridal Awards.
The spirit of Baros slowly seduces the senses.
Recognised for creating and nurturing beautifully romantic settings, Baros provides a blissful rendezvous to celebrate your honeymoon. By choosing our island resort for your honeymoon destination, you choose the perfect balance of luxury, world-class service and spell-binding romantic experiences within your own private oasis.
Baros is perfectly poised to help you make your honeymoon magical. We have everything you need from
beautifully curated villas
with private pools, to world-class dining experiences and mesmerising underwater adventures. Your honeymoon at Baros will be filled with memories that last a lifetime and our team will ensure that your special time together is truly breathtaking.
Designed with refined sophistication and timeless elegance in mind.
Our
‘Romantic Moments’ package
is the perfect collection of activities and experiences to inspire ultimate moments of togetherness.
Complimentary champagne and fruit platter on arrival
Begin your romantic escape with a little luxury: a bottle of champagne and a fresh tropical fruit platter in your villa to welcome you to heavenly Baros.
60-minute couples’ massage
Relax and replenish with a 60-minute couple’s massage at our Serenity Spa. Our skilled therapists will help you choose the best oils to relax, invigorate and awaken your senses in a fully immersive treatment.
Sunset cruise with champagne and canapés
Discover a Baros ocean dreamscape sailing on one of our private luxury boats for a sunset cruise with champagne and gourmet canapés.
Private dinner on the beach
Delight in a private candlelit dinner on the beach arranged by your personal Villa Host.
Romantic bath with flowers, wine and chocolates
Continue the pampering within your secluded private villa with the intimacy of a romantic bath prepared with fresh tropical flower petals, complemented with a bottle of wine and chocolates.
Private in-villa breakfast with champagne
A private champagne breakfast for you and your partner on your day of choice during your holiday, with a beautiful tropical fruit platter and freshly baked pastries. The perfect start to begin another day of Baros bliss.
For those planning their honeymoon to coincide with the ‘month of love’ in February, we also have a Valentine’s offering.
Celebrate infinite love at Baros Maldives this Valentine’s Day
We have curated a magical Valentine’s Day package at Baros for you with a selection of carefully crafted romantic experiences.
Taking the love underwater
, the Baros resident marine biologist will guide you in placing a token symbolising your never-ending love into our beautiful house reef. You can glue small coral pieces to an infinity-shaped coral frame via a special marine glue, or simply attach a small, engraved tag with your initials, or preferred message, onto a coral frame already placed in the Baros lagoon, leaving the marine polyps to grow with your love.
For couples looking to revitalise with a
romantic spa ritual
, Serenity Spa has prepared a special treatment for you comprising of a rose full body scrub, a romantic bath with rose petals and an intuitive aroma full-body massage.
Food lovers can sip cocktails and enjoy the unprecedented beauty of the vast Indian Ocean as the sun goes down at Lighthouse Lounge. Following this, you can enjoy a star and candlelit  special Valentine’s degustation menu at The Lighthouse Restaurant.
And for the true romantics, a private butler can serve a special 5-course set menu, on a private, secluded sandbank with a heart-shaped arrangement just for you. Starting at sunset with champagne and roses, and ending under the stars, this is as romantic as it gets.
And you can also laze in bed experiencing the cocooning romance of your luxury villa with mesmerising views of the sapphire ocean beyond. A languid floating breakfast served in your private pool will follow.
From exhilarating celebrations to intimate romantic moments, there are infinite ways for love to unfold at Baros – read more on our
Romance
section.
