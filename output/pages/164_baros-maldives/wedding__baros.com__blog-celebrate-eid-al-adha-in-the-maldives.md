Eid al-Adha Celebrations in the Maldives
30 June 2022 | All Blogs, Destination, Events, Experience
Baros Maldives is the Maldives Icon and an ideal destination for an unforgettable Eid escape in a tropical oasis unlike anywhere else.
Baros has launched a special EID getaway offer for guests hailing from the GCC to escape the summer heat and indulge in a refreshing getaway.
Throughout July, guests from the region will be offered an incredible 48% off standard room rates plus complimentary breakfast and dinner served daily in the Lime Restaurant. Guests can also enjoy more decadent dinners with 30% off at the Baros’s signature Lighthouse Restaurant famed for its gastronomic excellence or at the open-thatched Cayenne Grill perched over the Baros lagoon. Other special inclusions to look forward to are a bottle of bubbly on arrival, complimentary daily sunrise yoga sessions, 15% off on treatments at Serenity Spa, and round-trip airport transfers.
Whether you stay over the water, in a beach villa, suite or The Residence, Baros is an iconic island hideaway designed for couples to enjoy the freedom of space and understated elegance. Take this Eid holiday to savour time spent together. Indulge in couples’ spa treatments, sunset cruises and candlelight dinners on the beach at one of the world’s most romantic resorts.
Baros is easily accessible, and only a 25-minute luxury speedboat ride away from the International Airport. Dive into a world of lush tropical gardens cocooned by a canopy of palm trees, fringed by a white powdery beach and a world class house reef.
