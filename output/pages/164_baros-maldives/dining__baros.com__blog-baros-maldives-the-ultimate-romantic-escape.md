Baros Maldives: The Ultimate Romantic Escape
16 March 2025 | All Blogs, Destination, Dining, Experience
Picture this: a pristine island where ivory sands kiss the turquoise ocean, where the air is filled with the scent of tropical blooms and where every moment is crafted for two. This is Baros Maldives, the world’s most romantic resort, where love flourishes in its purest form.
Whether you’re celebrating a honeymoon, anniversary or a much-needed romantic escape in the Maldives, Baros is your secluded sanctuary – a place where unforgettable memories are made.
Baros: recognised as the most romantic Maldives holiday
Baros was crowned
World’s Most Romantic Resort seven times between 2015 and 2024
. We’ve also been lauded as the
Indian Ocean’s
Most Romantic Resort
. These aren’t just titles – they’re promises. Every experience at Baros is designed with romance in mind, from intimate dining on a secluded sandbank to indulgent spa rituals and dreamy ocean adventures. This is the ultimate romantic break at Baros, where your love takes centre stage.
Curators of unforgettable romantic experiences
Private dining in paradise
Love is best celebrated over an exquisite meal and, at Baros, dining becomes an experience of its own. Choose from an array of destination dining experiences, each more magical than the last. Every meal is a moment to cherish, created exclusively for you by our talented chefs:
A
secluded sandbank dinner
, where the only sound is the gentle lapping of waves.
A
candlelit dinner on the Piano Deck
, floating in the lagoon beneath a sky full of stars.
An
intimate meal on board Nooma
, our traditional Maldivian sailing dhoni, as the sun dips below the horizon.
A
floating breakfast
served in your own pool – a decadent way to start the day!
Gourmet dishes served in the privacy of your villa
, complete with champagne and ocean views.
Indulgent spa rituals for two
Reconnect and rejuvenate at
Serenity Spa
, an oasis of tranquility nestled within lush greenery. Let stress melt away with our
Romantic Spa Retreat for Couples
, a soothing experience designed to deepen your connection. Choose from bespoke couples’ treatments, including massages, aromatic baths and full-body rituals using luxurious Elemis products. It’s the perfect way to relax and unwind together in paradise.
Romantic ocean adventures
At Baros, romance extends beyond the shore. Set sail on an
unforgettable sunset cruise
, where the sky transforms into shades of pink and gold, or explore the vibrant marine life with a private snorkelling safari in the crystal-clear lagoon.
For a truly unforgettable journey,
step aboard Nooma
, our traditional Maldivian dhoni. This handcrafted vessel is your gateway to the beauty of the Indian Ocean, offering a voyage tailored entirely to your desires. Drift across the shimmering waters on a bespoke adventure inspired by the rich heritage of the Maldives. Whether you seek serene moments of relaxation or a journey filled with discovery, Nooma promises an experience unlike any other.
Romance, perfected – the details make all the difference
At Baros, romance isn’t just about the grand gestures – it’s in the details, the quiet moments and the thoughtful touches that turn a beautiful escape into an unforgettable love story. We take pride in crafting bespoke romantic surprises, ensuring that every experience is as unique as your love.
Indulge in a
Romantic Bed and Bath experience
, where your villa transforms into a sanctuary of intimacy. Sink into a bubble-filled bath under the stars, surrounded by flickering candlelight, or retreat to a bed adorned with delicate rose petals, setting the perfect scene for an evening of connection and relaxation.
Capture these precious moments forever with a
professional romantic photography session
. Whether it’s a golden sunset embrace or a stroll along the shoreline, our expert photographers will ensure you take home stunning images that bring your Baros memories to life for years to come.
For something truly magical, allow us to create a surprise that speaks to your love story. Perhaps a private movie night on the beach, a champagne toast at sunset or a personalised dining experience on our secluded lagoon
Piano Deck
under the stars – whatever your vision, we’re here to bring it to life.
Let time slow down. Let love take centre stage. And let Baros set the scene for your most cherished memories.
Baros: the most romantic place to celebrate your love

honeymoon, an anniversary, a renewals of vows
or a spontaneous
Romantic Moments getaway
for two, Baros offers the perfect blend of seclusion, luxury and unforgettable experiences.
