WELCOME TO BAROS
The Maldives Icon
Turquoise waters lap at white-sand shores. Towering palms rustle overhead. Welcome to Baros, a natural paradise with a lush island canopy, just 25 minutes by speedboat from the Maldives’ international airport. We’ve been welcoming guests since 1973, and have spent decades honing our services and cultivating our environment, crafting what we believe is a legendary resort. Today, we’re one of the most loved luxury Maldives resorts properties, and we’re excited to show you what makes us so special.

Baros Maldives is designed for couples. Whether you plan to visit for your honeymoon, vow renewal ceremony or simply wish to embrace the island’s romantic surroundings, this is the perfect place to spend quality time with your significant other.
Faraway but near enough. Tranquillity and relaxation are a quick 25-minute speedboat trip from touchdown at Velana International Airport near the capital city island of Male’.
Personalised, à la carte diving. There are 30 dive sites and one ancient shipwreck within 50 minutes speedboat trip of Baros, some of which are ranked among the top ten dive sites in the Maldives. Also available at our Eco Dive Centre (one of the first of its kind in the Maldives), is the revolutionary Fluo night diving experience.
The experiences offered at Baros are those you remember for a lifetime. From sunset and sunrise yogas sessions and private dining experiences to memorable dhoni cruises along the turquoise waters of the lagoon and starlit cinema offerings — a trip to Baros is bound to live on in your mind long after the flight home.
Surrounding the island and a short swim from the shore, the vibrant house reef is bright with colours, fish, turtles, rays and living coral. It is believed to be one of the healthiest and best reefs of the Maldives.
A rich heritage – one of the first resorts in the Maldives, setting the benchmark for luxury - with genuine, heartfelt Maldivian hospitality.
Each villa at Baros has a dedicated Villa Host, there to ensure the personal satisfaction of every guest.
Our three restaurants offer exquisite cuisine to suit every palate. Choose from a culinary adventure at our fine dining hallmark, Lighthouse Restaurant, a themed evening at one of our beachside eateries or an exclusive dining experience on a remote sandbank, an unusual Piano Deck in the lagoon or the beach.
