Valentine's Day at Baros Maldives
31 January 2024 | All Blogs, Destination, Events, Experience
Ancient palms whisper island love songs, as your feet sink deep into soft, white sand. Hand-in-hand you stroll beside vistas of endless blue, as sea and sky merge. Baros Maldives is synonymous with romance and is the ultimate in luxurious island destinations, the perfect place for love.
Join us this Valentine’s Day for a week-long celebration of love and enchantment on the world’s most romantic island.
A long history of romance.
For seven years in the last decade, Baros has been recognised consistently as the World’s Most Romantic Resort and for the past decade as the Indian Ocean’s Most Romantic Resort. Our island stands as an unrivalled haven for celebrating love. We have had the honour of helping many couples craft magical moments that have become the memories of a lifetime.
This Valentine’s Day…
From the 10th to the 17th of February, we invite you to bask in the beauty of our blissful island as you celebrate your love for one another.
Nestled in the heart of the Indian Ocean, Baros offers an exquisite array of exclusive experiences and bespoke romantic offerings, set within a tropical private island paradise – the ultimate Valentine’s getaway to make everlasting memories together.
Then, now and forever.
Our team has drawn inspiration from the essence of timeless love stories and crafted a Valentine’s Day offering that invites you to immerse yourself in these romantic narratives as you craft your very own tale of love, creating moments that will weave magical memories for years to come.
Choose from our
menu
of Valentine’s Day offerings, and curate your very own unforgettably romantic escape.
Savour love.
At Baros’s three gourmet restaurants, every meal is yours to savour, every bite designed to inspire. For five decades, Baros has been creating culinary magic, perfecting classical favourites, and innovating with new techniques and flavours from around the world.
For Valentine’s Day, our award-winning chefs have crafted unique opportunities for your senses to savour. Indulge in an exquisite Valentine’s Day buffet, or delight in one of our specially curated destination dining experiences in magical settings across the island. From the pristine sands of our sandbank to the romantic allure of the piano deck or our traditional Maldivian sailing dhoni, Nooma, couples can embrace their love in beautiful settings crafted exclusively for them.
Over this special time, Baros offers enhanced menus inspired by timeless love songs, meticulously designed to infuse romance into every moment. For an unparalleled dining experience, the exclusive ‘Restaurant Under the Stars’ beckons couples to indulge in an intimate dining affair surrounded by an endless star-studded sky.
Crafting lifelong memories together.
At Baros, there are many opportunities to create memories that will surprise and delight you, now and for years to come. Revel in a private cinema experience surrounded by tropical serenity or embark on a romantic cruise aboard either the traditional Maldivian sailing dhoni, Nooma, or our opulent yacht, Serenity.
Immersive spa experiences await at Serenity Spa, where you can indulge in specially crafted couple’s massages amidst intoxicating scents and exotic aromas. Enjoy transcendent relaxation with the exclusive Piano Deck Couple’s Massage. This immersive pampering experience will ease tension, enhance intimacy and leave you refreshed and rejuvenated.
Delve into the depths of Baros’s lagoon and etch your love story into the world’s most romantic island by leaving a personalised coral cube behind. Or embark on a private night snorkelling experience with glow in the dark, as the Baros world-class house reef comes alive in a fluorescence of colours after dark.
A gesture of love never to be forgotten.
This Valentine’s Day, surprise your loved one and book a stay on the world’s most romantic and intimate island. Celebrate and cherish your bond in a setting that will take your breath away. Book your unforgettable romantic journey at Baros today, and create memories of love, now and forever.
