BOUNDLESS BAROS BLUE EXCURSIONS
22 May 2022 | All Blogs, Destination, Experience
With its mesmerising ocean views and impossibly lovely natural beauty, Baros Maldives’ charming appeal is in its secluded verdant interior, fringed by a seemingly endless powder-soft white beach and a turquoise lagoon surrounded by a vibrant coral house reef. An island spa draws energy from the natural environment and responds to the serene spirit of the island. Indulge in classic fine dining experiences or a night of candlelight romance on the beach. An attentive team of private butlers enhance the indulgent experience at Baros.
At blissful Baros, a host of sea excursions await you. These memories will last for a lifetime.
Nooma
Sail the turquoise archipelago islands aboard Nooma, our beautiful handcrafted 62ft wooden Dhoni, for a sunset cruise to secret, sandy islands. Nooma offers a secluded sun deck for two in the bow and a private lounge.
As you come aboard, the friendly crew, clad in stunning black and white Maldivian lungis (sarongs), greetings of: “Assalamu alaikum”. Used predominantly by fishermen in earlier times, this traditional sailing vessel’s distinctive lateen sail and long raked bow are easily distinguishable in the archipelago as they cut through the sapphire waters of the Indian Ocean.
Cocooned on deck, sipping champagne – watch the twilight hues streak across the sky in stripes of pink and violet as the sun slips beneath the horizon. Glide along calm waters bathed in reflected amber light. The stillness of the water evokes a feeling of calm and wellbeing. The quiet journey is accompanied by the gentle swoosh of the Dhoni’s billowing sail. There is an option to enjoy a
romantic dinner aboard Nooma
, with gourmet dishes served by your personal steward. It’s a beautiful way to unwind in the evening, and the whole experience is indescribable.
2. Serenity
The North Malé atoll is a haven for island hopping. Set out on a day filled with adventure and snorkelling aboard our luxury yacht.
Serenity
is a dreamy base for exploring Baros’s surrounding waters.  Measuring 53 feet from bow to stern, Serenity moves with infinite grace and pace.
Relax in the private saloon – a  cabin made for two and stop off for lunch on a private castaway sandbank for an unforgettable afternoon of
al fresco dining
. This serene experience embodies tranquillity.
Embark on an unforgettable cruise in search of dolphins. Dolphins like to spin and corkscrew at the front of the boat and dance in the stern’s waves caused by the boat’s movement. Spinner, spotted, and striped dolphins are familiar to North Malé atoll residents.
Baros caters for all kinds of angling, from big game fishing excursions to night fishing under the stars, to Maldivian hand line trolling. Beginners and experts can enjoy exciting fishing cruises accompanied by a professional guide and friendly, experienced crew. Reel in barracuda and snappers and try your hand at traditional Maldivian fishing using a hand line with raw fish as bait.
Baros. It’s paradise personified.
