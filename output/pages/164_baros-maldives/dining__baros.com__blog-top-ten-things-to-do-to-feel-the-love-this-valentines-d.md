Top Ten Things To Do To ‘Feel The Love’ This Valentine’s Day
12 February 2022 | All Blogs, Destination, Experience
If you’re looking for a romantic escape, look no further. Baros Maldives has been named by the World Travel Awards as the Indian Oceans Most Romantic Resort for nine consecutive years and Elle Bridal Awards nominated Baros as the Best Honeymoon Resort in 2021.
Baros places a real emphasis on bespoke guest experiences – personalised destination dining options, customised diving by design programmes, and tailored spa treatments. By making every stay completely unique and incredibly romantic it’s not difficult to understand why the accolades keep pouring in.
1. Stay In Love
The exclusive Baros Residence is the ultimate romantic retreat with a stylish bedroom and bathroom, spacious lounge and dedicated butler service. Outdoors a 21-metre-long private infinity pool is set in a verdant garden shaded by trees. You can lounge on the four-poster daybed, treat yourselves to a massage, take an outdoor shower in the tropical garden and enjoy sundowner Daiquiris and complimentary canapés on your wooden sundeck just footsteps from the beach.
2. Time Spent Together
Sail away on a culinary journey on Baros’ traditional dhoni, Nooma. Savour a chef’s selection of delicious, unique seafood dishes prepared just for the two of you and experience intuitive, personal butler service.
3. Heaven Sent
The Lighthouse Lounge dazzles with sweeping ocean views. Sip on a cocktail and enjoy the Indian Ocean vastness as the sun goes down on another unbelievable day in paradise. Later, quench your thirst for romance with stars, candlelight and an incredible degustation menu at The Lighthouse Restaurant.
4. Rekindle Your Love
Revitalise with a deep tissue couple massage and booster facial treatment by ELEMIS at Serenity Spa. The massage includes a coconut sand scrub and steam bath with tropical fruit and Champagne.
5. Celestial Paradise For True Romantics
Cosy up together under the stars on your overwater pool villa deck. The Maldives is located 7 degrees north of the Equator which means you can see constellations from both hemispheres.
6. Savour Breakfast In Bed
Laze in bed together in your overwater villa taking in the mesmerising views of the sapphire ocean beyond. Indulge in a languid breakfast of French Crêpes filled with spiced chocolate, Normandes apple compote and orange blossom honey.
7. Take a soak together
Enjoy a late-night soak in an overwater bathtub surrounded by candles and sprinkles of rose petals. The air is rich with the scent of jasmine, eucalyptus and lavender. Share a frozen chocolate and nougat parfait and toast your love affair with flutes of Champagne.
8. Practice Yoga Together
Enjoy some zen time together while stretching and breathing on a private sandbank. As the sun rises over the Indian Ocean, feel the power of Mother Nature as you strike an asana together.
9. Share Your Love Of The Sea
Canoeing is recognised as the best couple’s sport and where better to spend active quality time together than kayaking in the crystal-clear waters of the Maldives. Glide along the surface of the Baros house reef in a Molokini clear-bottom canoe. Watch fascinating marine life through the transparent hull.
10. Gift-Giving
Give your partner the ultimate gift of a Baros Gift Card. Gifts and experiences range from private Nooma dinner cruises and sandbank candlelight feasts to bottles of Champagne and spa detox programmes.
