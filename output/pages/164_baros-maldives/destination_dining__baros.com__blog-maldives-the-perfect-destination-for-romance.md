Maldives: The perfect destination for romance
11 July 2023 | All Blogs, Destination, Dining, Events, Experience
Romance is not always spontaneous, sometimes it takes some thought. At Baros Maldives, we do the planning for you so that you can relax and enjoy blissful times of connection with your loved one surrounded by soft white sand, crystal clear water and the rustling of palm leaves in the sea breeze.
For decades, we have been perfecting our personalised service, private and luxurious villa accommodation and delectable culinary offerings. It is why we have held the prestigious World Travel Award for “Indian Ocean’s Most Romantic Resort” for ten consecutive years. And it is why couples keep returning to our blissful island.
Romance is the essence of Baros
Whether you are celebrating your honeymoon, vow renewal, wedding anniversary or are embarking on your life together with a marriage ceremony on our piano deck, Baros is the definition of romance. Our unobtrusive, intuitive and genuine service and attention to detail mean that every moment is a delight to the senses and a joy to the heart.
Or join us after the ceremony for a truly unforgettable honeymoon, rich with a plethora of memorable romantic and intimate experiences curated especially for you.
Enchanting culinary experiences
Destination dining at Baros makes every meal a memory. Our three exquisite restaurants offer a variety of delicious meal options in breathtaking settings. With sand underfoot and a gentle breeze teasing palm fronds above, our Sails Bar is perfect for light meals and cocktails. For dinner you can enjoy an unforgettable dining experience at our signature dining restaurant The Lighthouse, suspended over the water with stars above and the horizon stretching before you. Or if you are wanting a more relaxed end to your day, there is nothing like fresh seafood cooked over an open flame at our Cayenne Grill.
Baros also offers magical destination dining. Dining onboard Nooma, our traditional Maldvian dhoni, is the experience of a lifetime. Feel the sea breeze on your face as the wooden prow glides through the water, the sun sinking into the horizon before you.
Or indulge in a romantic dinner for two on our purpose-built Piano Deck. Perched over the lagoon, it is just the two of you and endless vistas of blue.
Our sandbank dining offers couples the chance to enjoy a private barbeque dinner or picnic on an islet of soft white sand surrounded by gently lapping waves.
Dining at Baros is always magical.
Embark on an adventure together
Head out on the water together and enjoy the islands the traditional way on Nooma, our traditional Maldivian dhoni, with only the sound of the wind in the sail and the soft splash as you glide along. Diving with manta rays, reef snorkelling adventures, traditional hand line fishing, sunset sailing or romantic dinner cruises for two, Nooma, with a sundeck, a private saloon lounge and an air-conditioned double bedroom, is the perfect vessel to take you on an adventure together.
Romance and relaxation at Serenity Spa
Our Romantic Spa Retreat for Couples is 150 minutes of transcendent relaxation. This holistic pampering journey eases muscle tension, enhances intimacy and will leave you refreshed, rejuvenated and ready to make the most of every minute of your time in paradise together.
Romantic Moments Offer
Our enchanting island beckons you to create memories together. When you book 4 nights or more you can enjoy our bespoke ‘Romantic Moments’ offer for couples. This includes among other benefits, intimate interludes that will add further magic to your time together:
Complimentary return speed boat transfer from Male’ International Airport
Daily breakfast in Lime, our all-day dining restaurant
A bottle of Champagne and fruit basket on arrival
A romantic private candlelit dinner by the beach
A Champagne breakfast in the privacy of your Villa
One 60-minute couples massage during the stay
A romantic in-villa bath experience with a bottle of wine and chocolates
A sunset cruise with champagne and canapés (sharing)
Endless options for unforgettably romantic moments
