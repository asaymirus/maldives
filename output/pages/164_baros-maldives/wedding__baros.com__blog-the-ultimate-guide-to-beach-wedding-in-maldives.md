THE ULTIMATE GUIDE TO A BEACH WEDDING IN MALDIVES
11 September 2022 | All Blogs, Destination, Events, Experience
Dreaming of a beach wedding? A beautiful sun-drenched day, waves lapping gently in the background, toes in the sand. There is something undeniably dreamy about the ocean, especially at Baros Maldives. Crystal clear turquoise water encircled by a dazzling soft white sandy beach and a coral-pink sunset painted across the sky – the perfect setting for a destination wedding.
There is nothing more romantic than tying the knot oby the shore, and we have put together a special beach
wedding package
for romantic bridal couples dreaming of the perfect day on a faraway island. The price per couple is USD 1650 plus taxes and service charges.
1. Most Romantic Resort in the World
The fabled romantic allure of our blissful island is one of the best reasons to choose this dreamlike paradise to celebrate your nuptials. Having been named the “Most Romantic Resort in the World” by the World Travel Awards for nine consecutive years, Baros delivers you magical moments to be cherished for a lifetime.
2. Beach Ceremony
Picture a stylish, laidback ceremony on the beach with palms swaying in the breeze, exotic blooms and a traditional Maldivian Bodu Beru dance procession. While the bride carries a beautiful bouquet, the groom wears a matching boutonniere, and you will also receive a crafted wedding certificate.
3. Celebratory Gifts
There is no shortage of celebratory gifts at Baros to honour your union, including a traditional sarong for the bride and a Baros polo shirt for the groom. Toast to your future with a complimentary bottle of champagne by the waves, or in your suite on the wedding night. Enjoy the unique turn-down symbolisms and floral decorations in your villa. And when it is time to leave, you will be given a farewell gift to remind you of this momentous occasion.
4. Wedding Cake
The Baros wedding cake symbolises prosperity, good luck and fertility, and is created lovingly using the best ingredients so that your union will be happy, enduring and filled with love.
5. Tree Planting Ceremony
Another charming way to commemorate your wedding day, while leaving your mark in nature, is to plant an indigenous palm tree in honour of the ceremony. It is the symbol of a true bond, as your tree is tagged not only with your names, but also the wedding date and a love message.
6. Bridal Dinner
After your beach wedding ceremony, carry on the celebration at our signature Lighthouse Restaurant, famed for its gastronomic experiences. Enjoy a candlelight ‘Ocean Degustation Menu’ with perfect wine pairing.
