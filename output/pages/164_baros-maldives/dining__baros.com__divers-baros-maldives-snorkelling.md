In a destination famous for its snorkelling, nowhere in the Maldives matches the convenience and beauty of the Baros house reef – located so close to shore that it can easily be explored by beginners.
features
Our Marine team organises a number of snorkelling adventures you can join:
DISCOVER THE BAROS HOUSE REEF TOUR
Let our Marine team guide you around the best snorkelling spots of Baros.
FISH EXPEDITION WITH MARINE BIOLOGIST
Refine your fish identification skills with a personalised lesson from our Marine Biologist.
SNORKELLING SAFARI BY BOAT
Follow up your explorations of the wonders of the Baros house reef with one of our boat trips to nearby reefs.
NIGHT SNORKELLING
The Baros house reef comes alive with a different kind of marine life after the sun sets.
PRIVATE SNORKELLING LESSONS FOR BEGINNERS
With just an hour in the water, a Private Snorkelling Lesson will show you everything you need to know about the basics of snorkelling.
CORAL SPONSORSHIP
If you would like to play your part in protecting the Baros marine environment, you can support our Coral Reef Regeneration Programme by sponsoring a coral frame. Read more about our Coral Rehabilitation Programme
here.
PRIVATE SNORKELLING EXPERIENCES AND BAROS MARINE SPECIALS
Drop by Divers Baros Maldives anytime to arrange curated snorkelling experiences on the house reef or boat trips to explore other reefs and different marine life.
