# Baros Island Resort - Maldives Tourism

Source: https://www.maldivestourism.net/resorts/baros-island-resort/
Scraped: 2026-10-04
Resort: Baros Maldives
Page type: dining

---

The Baros Island Resort is a 4 star resort with 75 luxury rooms ready to show you a holiday of a lifetime.  You can certify that you and your family will find a number of activities to take part in – bringing you closer than ever.
[adlarge] Situated in the North Male’ Atoll it makes it a very popular choice as you can always visit the Maldives capital, Male’ and experience it in all its glory.  The Baros Island Resort has 3 different kinds of rooms available, choose from a standard room, deluxe bungalow or the newly added water bungalows giving a totally different aspect to luxury.
You have a number of different activities to do during the day, take part in feeding the stingrays, play football, volleyball, badminton, golf, aerobics and many many more.  As you may know the Maldives has become infamous for its snorkelling and diving destinations so if that’s what you are into – you’re in luck!
While you are visiting the Baros Island Resort you can expect to experience some of the finest cuisines as there are numerous places to choose from.  The Turtle Restaurant is the most popular in and around this resort, it sets the menu in the morning so you can even book your table and your meals to have them ready for whenever you wish.
The night life around this resort is fantastic, they regularly have live bands playing and discos on the beach so you can party until the early morning ready for the next day.
