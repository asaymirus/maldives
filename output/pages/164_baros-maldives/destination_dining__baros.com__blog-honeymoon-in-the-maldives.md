Honeymoon in the Maldives
25 May 2021 | All Blogs, Events
The question is popped and answer is yes, and the wedding arrangements have been made: now comes the biggest decision. Where should you travel for your honeymoon?
The Maldives, known for its small, pristine, private islands, clear blue water and magical sunsets awaits your most romantic getaway. Here are 5 reasons why you should choose to honeymoon in the Maldives:
1. THE MOST ROMANTIC DESTINATION
The Maldives is truly the most romantic destination with intimate, small islands with private bungalows. There are many resorts in the Maldives to select from, and it may not be an easy choice. For your honeymoon, you would want to select a resort that is the most well-known for romance – Baros Maldives has been the ‘Most Romantic Resort in the World’ for the 7th time in 2019 by the prestigious World Travel Awards and lives up to that promise with great, personalised yet discreet service, cosy and comfortable, private villas and exquisite culinary offerings throughout the day and night.
2. HONEYMOON BENEFITS
The Maldives is very generously welcoming for honeymoon couples and the benefits that come along with this special once in a lifetime trip are truly worth it. Baros for examples offers a variety of services and inclusions such as a bottle of sparkling wine and chocolates upon arrival, your Honeymoon Picture frames before departure, a candlelit beach dinner and a romantic bed decoration during your stay. If you choose to book a higher villa category, you will also receive a USD 100 spa credit for the resort’s Serenity Spa.
3. COUPLE’S HIDEAWAY
You already know that the Maldives is known for its romance and seclusion, which makes it the perfect couple hideaway, but there is more than just romantic sunsets and the picture-perfect tropical island backdrop. For couples, resorts like Baros has put together some extra romantic experiences and moments. You can celebrate your special occasion on a sunset dinner cruise on a traditional Maldivian Dhoni during which you may expect Dolphins to accompany you for a while or choose to spend your evening on your private Sandbank with a personal chef and waiter. These are only a few of many special dining occasions you may find on Baros Maldives.
4. WEDDING CEREMONIES
You might have had your wedding back at home with your family and friends but that day may have just passed too soon and you were not able to soak in the special moment and to enjoy this day with your partner. The Maldives deliver the perfect wedding ceremony backdrop, for a small, intimate ceremony. Just you and your partner, the master of ceremony in an absolute romantic beach setting.
5. HEAR THE NEEDLE DROP
Lush vegetation, white sandy beaches, palm trees, warm sea breeze – these are a few of the features of the resorts in the Maldives that makes it such a romantic destination. Baros is also a natural and secluded island, but, the unique feature of Baros is that it also does not allow small children under the age of 8 on the island, making it the most ideal for romance and intimacy. You can walk around the small island within 10 minutes, and you will not hear motorized water sports or buggies disrupting the ambience and serenity of the blissful island.
