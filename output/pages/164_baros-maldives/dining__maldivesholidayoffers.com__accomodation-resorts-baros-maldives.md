Resort Overview
Six-time winner of the World’s Most Romantic Resort and just 25-minutes away by speedboat from VelanaInternational Airport, Baros Maldives immediately transports you to blissful tranquility. With a canopy of lush vegetation and pristine white-sand beaches, the property lies within a sparkling turquoise lagoon. The traditional style of the island’s Villas and Suites reflects its Maldivian design, ownership and management. There are 7 villa categories blessed with either sunrise or sunset, beach or ocean views.
