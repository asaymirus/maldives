ENQUIRE ABOUT THIS PROPERTY
A short speedboat ride from Malé, this long-established island in the North Malé Atoll is known for its refined, quietly confident approach to the Maldives. Villas are elegant and well proportioned, with beach and overwater options that focus on privacy, natural materials and seamless access to calm lagoon waters. The design is classic rather than trend-led, creating a sense of understated sophistication throughout the island. Dining is considered and well executed, with a small selection of venues offering international cuisine in intimate, polished settings. The surrounding house reef is a major highlight, easily accessible from shore and consistently regarded as one of the stronger reefs close to Malé. Service is discreet, attentive and highly polished, delivering a calm, adults-friendly atmosphere suited to clients seeking a traditional luxury Maldives experience with strong consistency and a strong reputation for quality.

Food &  Beverage
The Lighthouse Restaurant
Fine Dining French Cuisine. A legendary name in the Maldives, The Lighthouse shines like a beacon as you approach Baros by speedboat. Superb cuisine prepared by Baros’ master chefs is served in an atmosphere of relaxed refinement. The resident Baros sommelier is ready to provide the perfect wine-pairing suggestions.
Cayenne Grill
Al Fresco, Tepanyaki.Cayenne Grill offers an intimate al fresco dining experience with a hint of island flavour in a thatched gazebo above the lagoon. Sample perfectly marbled wagyu beef and young New Zealand lamb accompanied by a succulent selection of fresh lobster, prawns, crab and fish.
Lime Restaurant
À la carte, Breakfast. Dining in an atmosphere of informal sophistication, Lime specialises in local dishes and fresh seafood. International specialties, delicious curries or wood-fired pizza for lunch, at sunset or under the stars.
The Lighthouse Lounge
A soothing setting for sipping Champagne as the sun melts below the horizon. The upstairs lounge at The Lighthouse offers views of bewitching beauty, accompanied by delectable wines, cocktails and canapés.
Sails Bar
An intimate and peaceful wooden cabana nestled in a garden of coconut palms. Snack and sway the day away in a hammock as the lagoon sparkles in the sun. Guests can relax with a drink in the evening as soft music adds romance to the balmy night air.
