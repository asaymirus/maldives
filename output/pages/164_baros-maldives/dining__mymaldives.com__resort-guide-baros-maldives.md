THE SPIRIT OF BAROS – A Warm Welcome
Guests destined for Baros are greeted warmly at the airport by staff from the island who take care of luggage and act as escort for the brief cruise by private speedboat.
The Baros Experience
The first sight of Baros is a memory to treasure. From the bijou island’s reef-ringed sandy shores, coconut palms lean towards the inviting blue of its lagoon; palm-thatched roofs of hidden Villas peep from lush foliage.
A short boardwalk landing-pier leads beside grand timber and golden thatched restaurant pavilions, to a picturesque palm grove and the Guest Service Pavilion.
Shady paths meander through arbours bright with hibiscus and bougainvillea yielding enchanting glimpses of white sand dappled by dancing sunbeams, lazily caressed by the lagoon’s gentle surf.
Whether by the beach or overwater, gracious sandstone and timber Villas welcome guests with tranquil refinement – stress melts, bliss results, as the spirit of Baros gradually seduces the senses.
AQUUM SPA
Enter the intricately carved doors to Aquum at Baros, Maldives and immerse your being in the soothing energy that emanates from within. The lush foliage within this verdant garden spa cocoons the stylish design that blends so seamlessly with every element that has been created to ensure an unforgettable experience is met. The care and dedication of our highly skilled and intuitive therapists is beyond.
DIVING
Baros Maldives has its own oval-shaped house reef rich with marine life and being so close to the shore (10 to 15 metres) that even non-divers can swim to it and spend hours effortlessly snorkeling. The reef has gentle slopes and is easy of access. Then around the island there are 30 superlative dive sites (North Male’ Atoll) that never fail to delight even the most experienced diver. Baros International Diving Centre offers year-round Scuba Diving excursions and PADI courses.
