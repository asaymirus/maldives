Say ‘I do’ to an unforgettable honeymoon at Baros Maldives
6 April 2025 | All Blogs, Destination, Dining, Events, Experience, Villas
Imagine a honeymoon where every detail is effortlessly romantic – where the sand is as soft as silk, the ocean shimmers in endless shades of blue and every sunset feels like it was painted just for you.
At Baros Maldives, love unfolds in the most enchanting ways. Stroll hand in hand along the powder-white beach, slip into the turquoise waters of your private infinity pool and toast to your new life together as the sun melts into the horizon.
A sanctuary of barefoot luxury, Baros is designed for romance. Dine by candlelight on our floating lagoon Piano Deck, embark on a dreamy sunset cruise aboard a traditional dhoni or indulge in a couples’ spa ritual infused with tropical essences. With its intimate setting, world-class hospitality and legendary house reef, Baros is the perfect place to begin your forever.
Let us handle every detail, so you can focus on what truly matters – each other. Here’s how to create the perfect honeymoon at Baros Maldives.
Why choose Baros? The pinnacle of romance
With coconut palms swaying in the breeze and the soft murmur of the ocean all around, Baros is the very definition of romance. Here, time slows, and every moment is steeped in beauty — white linen billowing in the warm island air, endless horizons of turquoise and gold, and the gentle rhythm of waves lapping at the shore.

For couples seeking the ultimate honeymoon escape, there is simply nowhere more enchanting.
Baros Maldives honeymoon: what to expect
Just a 25-minute speedboat ride from Malé International Airport, Baros Maldives is an intimate island sanctuary celebrated for its natural beauty, timeless elegance and world-class hospitality. Home to one of the best house reefs in the Maldives, it offers an unrivalled underwater paradise teeming with vibrant marine life.
Since opening in 1973, Baros has perfected the art of Maldivian luxury, blending traditional charm with contemporary comfort. Thatched-roof villas, polished teak floors and lush tropical gardens create a serene retreat and many villas feature private pools for ultimate seclusion.

A feast for the senses: dining at Baros Maldives
Baros is famous for its beachside dining, where soft candlelight flickers against white linen-draped tables and the gentle ocean breeze carries the scent of salt and jasmine. There are few things more intimate than dining with your toes in the sand, savouring exquisite dishes crafted by world-class chefs as the waves whisper along the shore.
Renowned for its exceptional personalised dining experiences, Baros elevates every moment, especially for honeymooners seeking something extraordinary. At Baros, dining is more than a meal – it’s a culinary journey woven with romance, elegance and breathtaking surroundings.
For a setting as captivating as the cuisine, Baros offers overwater dining with sweeping views of the endless blue horizon.
The Lighthouse Restaurant
, one of the most iconic dining venues in the Maldives, provides a refined setting where newlyweds can indulge in fine cuisine while watching the sky melt into sunset hues.
For those seeking something truly unique, Baros offers private dining experiences that redefine romance. On the
Piano Deck
, a secluded wooden platform floating in the lagoon, a personal chef prepares an unforgettable meal beneath a canopy of stars. Aboard
Nooma, a traditional Maldivian dhoni, couples can set sail on a sunset voyage
, where a private butler serves a bespoke feast accompanied by flowing champagne.
Whether you’re toasting to love on a
candlelit sandbank
, indulging in a gourmet picnic on a deserted island, or enjoying a floating breakfast in your private infinity pool, every
bespoke dining experience
at Baros is designed to be as memorable as the love you celebrate.

At Baros, romance isn’t just an experience – it’s a way of life. Designed to create unforgettable moments, our island sanctuary is the perfect setting for newlyweds seeking intimacy, luxury and breathtaking natural beauty.
