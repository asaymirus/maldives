Top 4 Dining Experiences In The Maldives
9 June 2021 | All Blogs, Dining
Dining with your feet in the sand under a starlit sky with nothing but a light breeze whistling through the palm trees and the gentle lapping waves to set the scene. The dining experience you have dreamed of is not as farfetched if you chose to spend your next holidays in the Maldives – but there is more. Here are the top 4 dining experiences in the Maldives.
1. SANDBANK
Possibly the most serene, romantic and private dining experience you ever may encounter. There are many sandbanks around in the Maldives, depending on the season they are long white strips of white sandy beach or a smaller spot surrounded by the leaping waves, your dining set up in the centre of nowhere.
Luxury resorts like Baros Maldives have refined their sandbank dining experiences on offer to suit your imagination and dreams. You can opt in to cruise to the nearby sandbank just before sunrise and enjoy the magical moment when the sun rises from the ocean while enjoying a champagne breakfast. If you rather want to take in all the colours and take a swim around the sandbank, guests at Baros can book their private sandbank picnic during the day or of course a romantic dinner with no one around but the two of you and your private chef and waiter.
Baros Sandbank Dining
2. UNIQUE DESTINATION DINING
Even though dinner at the beach or on the sandbank sounds like nothing can ever top that experience, some resorts created truly unique dining experiences that shall not be forgotten easily.
Baros Maldives for example literally elevates your dining experience with the Piano Deck. The wooden piano-shaped deck is located in the island’s lagoon, built on stilts, surrounded only by water. Due to the proximity to Baros’ vivid house reef, it is a pleasure to observe the colourful fish and occasional turtle passing bye while your culinary taste buds are tempted by the outstanding creations of your private chef.
Of course, you can book the Baros Piano Deck for an exclusive dinner event, a yoga session or just to spend a casual picnic here. For all the love birds out there – if you are looking for a special destination to propose to your one and only, look no further.
Baros Destination Dining
3. DINNER CRUISES
The scene is set for your romantic dinner cruise – the two of you, somewhere out in the Indian Ocean, cruising the atoll with the leaping Dolphins accompanying you while the sun sets at the horizon. To add to the tranquil and ambience, Baros sends couples out on the traditional. Wooden Maldivian Dhoni ‘Nooma’. A one of a kind sunset dinner cruise that will create memories to cherish for a lifetime.
Discover Baros Cruises
4. OUTSTANDING RESTAURANTS IN THE MALDIVES
Even though the Maldives is one of the most remote destinations in the world, resorts work hard to bring outstanding cuisine and restaurant concepts to the islands. One of the icons in the Maldives is The Lighthouse at Baros Maldives, which rises over the lagoon of the luxury resort with this iconic white pinnacle roof. The dining experience delivers reinterpreted classical fine dining with delicacies inspired by the guests of Baros and the international flair of the resort. Since merely 15 years guests are served in The Lighthouse and the mood and ambience is nowhere else to be found.
