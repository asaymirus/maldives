Festive Season Highlights at Baros Maldives
24 December 2023 | All Blogs, Destination, Dining, Events, Experience
Leave behind those winter woollens and spend this Festive Season in paradise.
As 2023 dips below the horizon, Baros invites you to join us in creating truly memorable moments over this festive time. Enjoy authentic Maldivian hospitality, as you bask in the warmth of the Baros sun, surrounded by the crystal brilliance of our lagoon.
Sensational setting
Nestled in the heart of paradise, Baros celebrates five decades of innovative and authentic Maldivian hospitality this year. You are invited to join us and revel in the timeless charm of our iconic luxury private island resort, where sophisticated villas are set like jewels in green jungle gardens, overlooking the turquoise clarity of our lagoon. Beneath the surface lies our world-class house reef, acclaimed for its thriving marine ecosystem, and home to a myriad of colourful fish and other enchanting marine creatures.
Something for everyone.
To celebrate both this special time of year and our Golden Jubilee, we have curated a
festive programme
of iconic moments for our guests, allowing you in turn to craft your dream getaway. Select from a multitude of activities, as abundant as the crystal-clear waters surrounding our idyllic island.
Embark on a journey of discovery.
We have included many sea-bound adventures in our programme. Embrace a day of exploration and play on our captivating dolphin cruise, as you watch these joyful creatures in their natural habitat. Enjoy the gentle caress of champagne-infused sea breezes aboard our traditional dhoni,
Nooma
, as you nibble canapés on our sunset cruise. For the adventurous, head out for an exhilarating morning of big game fishing or underwater explorations of surrounding reefs.
Let the island entrance you.
Indulge in a restorative pampering session at our exquisite Serenity Spa. Fragrant aromas of lavender, coconut and lime lead you down a meandering path through beautiful vegetation, to the very heart of our island. Here you will find our spa, an oasis of rest and rejuvenation. Bid farewell to the stresses of the year with spa ceremonies conducted by our expert therapists. From aqua yoga to detoxification sessions, the all-natural spa therapies are designed to prepare you for the year ahead.
Enjoy a night amongst the stars.
Book an exclusive cinematic soirée on our enchanting Piano Deck, stars etched above and reflected in the deep blue of waters all around. This is the memory of a lifetime.
Let your senses be delighted.
Tantalise your taste buds with a plethora of delectable culinary delights. Dine in white under the elegance of the full moon or savour curated delights at The Chef’s Table in The Lighthouse Restaurant. With three gourmet-class restaurants, your culinary journey will traverse international cuisines, culminating in an authentic Maldivian gastronomic experience with a special banquet that showcases the exquisite flavours of local cuisine.
Glamourous Galas.
The zenith of festive celebrations at Baros unfolds with a special Christmas Eve gala and an opulent New Year’s Eve gala dinner. Revel in a sumptuous spread paired with an abundance of fine wines. Live music and a breathtaking fireworks display will bid adieu to an iconic year, leaving indelible memories as we usher in 2024 with unparalleled style.
Give something back.
This season Baros is poised to delight guests with a kaleidoscope of festive gifts, in the shape of experiences that will colour your life with joy. You have the opportunity to give something to the island in return. Dive into the depths of sustainability by sponsoring your own coral cube and forging an eternal bond with Baros.
The festive jubilations at Baros extend until the 7th of January 2024, ensuring that the spirit of celebration lingers. The General Manager, Mr. Ibrahim Shijah invites you to join us on an “entrancing journey where the festive season and iconic moments converge, etching tales that will linger in your heart”.
Make this festive season a celebration of authentic Maldivian luxury, hospitality and cherished memories. Contact our team at
reservations@baros.com
