5 of the Top Reasons to Honeymoon at Baros
9 June 2021 | All Blogs, Destination, Experience
To say that the last year was challenging is an understatement of the highest order. People across the globe felt the effects of the pandemic — and betrothed couples suddenly found themselves at the mercy of lockdowns and strict regulations. Weddings were put on hold or kept extremely intimate and honeymoons were postponed. Finally, however, there seems to be some light at the end of the tunnel.
As vaccines roll out and the world starts to reimagine returning to some semblance of normal, loved-up couples can finally start considering the possibility of having that long-awaited fairytale honeymoon.
Looking for the perfect way to celebrate your nuptials and make up for lost time and postponed plans? Then here are five reasons to plan your post-lockdown honeymoon at Baros:
1.THE STATUS OF “PARADISE ON EARTH”
After a difficult year spent largely within the confines of your home, can you think of anything better than travelling straight into the heart of paradise to relax and unwind with your person? Synonymous with luxury and romance, Baros is a quietly sophisticated paradise, made up of 75 overwater and garden villas and surrounded by powder-soft sands and azure waters — it’s literally the quintessential honeymoon destination.
While the natural beauty that envelopes the resort is undeniably breathtaking, the iconic resort itself oozes charm and unpretentious refinement. The open-air restaurants offer the ultimate places to indulge in gourmet food, watch the sunset and bask in the incredible views. The spa provides a sensational place to unwind, and any of the tastefully furnished villas, created to meld into the tropical surrounds, make the for the perfect tropical home away from home. Along Baros’s gorgeous shores you are sure to find the perfect place to spend some post-pandemic quality time with your partner.
2. THE ROMANTIC ALLURE
One of the very first private luxury resorts in the Maldives, this tropical haven is renowned for its romantic allure. So much so, that the iconic island resort has earned the title, among many others, of the “World’s Most Romantic Resort” at the World Travel Awards for the past eight years.
Apart from its unparalleled luxury appeal, the resort is also renowned for its ease of access (a short 25-minute boat transfer from the international airport), understated elegance and extraordinary guest experience, where no detail is ever spared. In short, it’s a perfect place for a honeymoon hideaway.
3. THE BUCKET LIST-WORTHY ACCOMMODATION
The accommodation at Baros intentionally strikes the subtle balance between natural elements and luxury comforts. This culminates in a comfortable, laidback island oasis — a veritable sanctuary where you can connect with your loved one in the most magical ways. Here, along silk-like white shores, you can choose from an array of accommodation options; from dreamy suites and pool options to water and water pool villas — no matter the choice, your trip to paradise is sure to be utterly exceptional.
4. THE COMPLIMENTARY HONEYMOON TREATS
Here, under swaying palm trees and along silver shores, Baros’s honeymoon guests are treated to complimentary spoils; sparkling wine and chocolates on arrival, an unforgettable private candlelit dinner on the beach and romantic in-villa decorations to mark the occasion.
While all guests looking to celebrate their nuptials this year will also receive these treats, those who book their honeymoon between May and September in 2021, will be given something a little extra! Over and above the standard benefits, honeymoon couples visiting in these months can tailor-make their ultimate honeymoon by choosing three complementary experiences from a wide array of romantic offerings.
Nature lovers can embrace a symbolic tree-planting ritual, make the most of the sublime tropical surroundings on a guided snorkelling trip or kayaking adventure of the house reef, or participate in a coral gardening workshop. Hopeless romantics can indulge in a special bath experience or relish a dreamy sunset cruise on a traditional Maldivian dhoni. Gourmands can sip on tantalizing sunset cocktails at the iconic Lighthouse Restaurant or savour a floating breakfast served in their very own villa pool. At Baros, there are ample ways to curate unforgettable moments with your person.
5. THE ENDLESS POSSIBILITIES
While newly-wed couples will have complimentary access to three of the added benefits (mentioned above), there’s really no limit on the number of exceptional experiences you can enjoy at this veritable paradise. From outdoor adventures and eco-conscious offerings to fine-dining castaway experiences and more, there’s so much to experience at Baros where the word “no” is quite simply not part of the vocabulary.
