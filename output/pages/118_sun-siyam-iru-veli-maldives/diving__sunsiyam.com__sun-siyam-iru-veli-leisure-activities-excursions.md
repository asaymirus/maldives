EXCURSIONS & EXPEDITIONS
Some days call for open water. Others, for wherever the day happens to take you. Swim alongside reef sharks and turtles, chase manta rays across a lucky current, or watch the reef glow electric blue after dark. Then step ashore: wander a local island, cast a line as the sun goes down, or claim a stretch of deserted sand that belongs to no one but you for the afternoon. However you like your adventure, by boat, on foot, guided, private, or gloriously spontaneous, there's always somewhere new calling.
