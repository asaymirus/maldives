An Intimate Island Hideaway
Sun Siyam Iru Veli is where couples, honeymooners, and small families come to experience the Maldives distilled to its most essential pleasures: Lagoon swims, spa rituals, starlit dinners. Intimacy, elevated. The only agenda is yours.
Resort
Highlights
There are islands that dazzle, and those that restore. Set in South Nilandhe Atoll, Sun Siyam Iru Veli is firmly the latter. A boutique haven of 125 beach and overwater villas, each with a private pool, it is designed for couples, honeymooners, and small families seeking space to slow down. Days unfold naturally, from lagoon swims to spa rituals and starlit evenings, all supported by a seamless 24-hour all-inclusive experience, where every moment feels effortless and deeply personal.

Sun Siyam Iru Veli
Must-Do
Floating Breakfast or Lunch
Indulge in a truly unique dining experience with a floating breakfast or lunch. Whether you choose to start your morning chilling in your pool with fresh fruits, pastries, and gourmet delights, or enjoy a sunlit midday spread, each floating tray is perfectly presented and paired with sparkling beverages or tropical refreshments.
Pool Parties
Make a splash at our weekly pool parties, where the turquoise waters set the stage for endless fun. Feel the island breeze as laid-back beats from the DJs fill the air, keeping the energy high and the vibe carefree. Whether you’re dancing poolside, floating with friends, or simply soaking up the sun, each party promises pure island magic.
Private Sunset Isle
Escape to a private sandbank, just for two, where the sky glows with the colors of sunset and the turquoise ocean stretches to the horizon. Toast with champagne, enjoy a selection of canapés, and savor an intimate, unforgettable moment. With the world fading away around you, this is a memory made entirely for the two of you.
Cast Away
Spend the day on a private sandbank, where it’s just you, the sun, and the turquoise waters stretching as far as the eye can see. Inspired by the spirit of Cast Away, snorkel among vibrant reefs, play games on the soft sand, and enjoy a beachside lunch in total seclusion—knowing that a safe return is just a short ride away. It’s a carefree, sun-soaked escape into your own island paradise.
Local Island Experience
Step beyond the postcard-perfect beaches and explore nearby local islands where life moves at a gentle rhythm—watch fishermen at work, browse lively village markets, and meet the warm, welcoming locals. Knowledgeable guides reveal the hidden stories, customs, and secret corners of island life, offering an authentic glimpse into the culture, colors, and daily rhythms of Maldivian living.
Bodu Beru Night
Kick off your sandals and feel the sand beneath your feet as the rhythm takes over. Let loose on the sandy dance floor to the pulsating beats of a traditional Maldivian Boduberu drum show, where hypnotic drumming, energetic dancing, and vibrant songs create an unforgettable celebration of island culture.
