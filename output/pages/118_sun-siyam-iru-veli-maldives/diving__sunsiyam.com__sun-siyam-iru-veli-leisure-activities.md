Recreation Facilities at Sun Siyam Iru Veli
Discover local islands, or feel the rush with exhilarating water sports
An abundance of adventure awaits, right on your doorstep
Watersports
The water here doesn't just look inviting, it's practically begging you to jump in. Kayak across still lagoons, catch the wind on a kitesurf board, or simply paddle out and see where the current takes you. Whatever your speed, our instructors are on hand to help you make the most of it.
Excursions
Some days are made for wandering further. Set sail for a sunset cruise, swim alongside gentle giants, or discover a nearby island all your own. However you like to explore, there's always somewhere new waiting just beyond the lagoon.
Diving
All around Sun Siyam Iru Veli is a whole world to be explored. From vibrant coral gardens to curious reef life, our dive team takes guests of every level deep into the Indian Ocean, because no trip to the Maldives is quite complete until you've seen it from below.
