Step into a world of wonder this Easter as
Sun Siyam Iru Veli
transforms into a vibrant springtime paradise for its
“Blooming Bunny Bash”
from
April 16 to 22, 2025
. This week-long celebration promises a delightful mix of feasts, games, and immersive experiences, creating cherished memories for guests of all ages.
Enchanting Activities for Young Guests
The excitement begins with a series of creative and interactive adventures at
Turtle Kids Club
. Little ones can enjoy hands-on Easter-themed activities, from
Bunny Ear Crafting
and
Easter Card Making
to
Egg Painting
and
Tree Decorating
. A highlight of the festivities is the
Bunny Petting Activity
, where children can interact with adorable rabbits while learning about their care.
Unique Experiences for Families & Adults
Beyond the kids’ festivities, Sun Siyam Iru Veli offers a carefully curated selection of
elevated experiences for families and adults
. Indulge in the
Lagoon Wine & Cheese Tasting
, set against the backdrop of stunning ocean views, or unwind with
live music and DJ Nisho’s special sets at Chemistry Bar
. For a truly memorable dining experience, guests can savor a
Private Easter Picnic on a secluded sandbank
or come together for a
Family BBQ under the stars
, blending island flavors with a festive spirit.
Mesmerizing Magic with Aaron Pang
Prepare to be amazed as internationally renowned magician
Aaron Pang
takes the stage at Sun Siyam Iru Veli. Known for his spellbinding illusions and captivating performances, Aaron will weave magic throughout the week, culminating in his breathtaking
Magic of Easter
showcase on
April 20
.
Easter Sunday: A Celebration to Remember
The festivities reach their peak on
Easter Sunday
, beginning with the
Bloom Brunch at Aqua Orange
, a feast of flavors that sets the tone for a day of excitement. As the sun shines over the island, the
Easter Pool Party
kicks off with
canapés, desserts, and electrifying DJ beats
. As the evening unfolds, guests can dance the night away at
Chemistry Bar
, where a
live band
creates the perfect rhythm for an unforgettable night.
The celebrations don’t end there—
Sun Siyam Iru Veli keeps the Easter spirit alive for two more days
, ensuring every guest enjoys a magical and immersive experience.
