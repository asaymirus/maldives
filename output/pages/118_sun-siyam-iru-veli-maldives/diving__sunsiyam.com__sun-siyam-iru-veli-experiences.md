Sun Siyam Iru Veli Must-Do
Pleasure, leisure, and memories to treasure — you can have it all.
From relaxing to heart-pounding, here is a rundown of our favourite activities Sun Siyam has to offer for every guest mindset.
Floating Breakfast
Indulge in a truly unique dining experience with a floating breakfast or lunch. Whether you choose to start your morning chilling in your pool with fresh fruits, pastries, and gourmet delights, or enjoy a sunlit midday spread, each floating tray is perfectly presented and paired with sparkling beverages or tropical refreshments.
Weekly Pool Parties
Make a splash at our weekly pool parties, where the turquoise waters set the stage for endless fun. Feel the island breeze as laid-back beats from the DJs fill the air, keeping the energy high and the vibe carefree. Whether you’re dancing poolside, floating with friends, or simply soaking up the sun, each party promises pure island magic.
Private Sunset Isle
Escape to a private sandbank, just for two, where the sky glows with the colors of sunset and the turquoise ocean stretches to the horizon. Toast with champagne, enjoy a selection of canapés, and savor an intimate, unforgettable moment. With the world fading away around you, this is a memory made entirely for the two of you.
Cast Away
Spend the day on a private sandbank, where it’s just you, the sun, and the turquoise waters stretching as far as the eye can see. Inspired by the spirit of Cast Away, snorkel among vibrant reefs, play games on the soft sand, and enjoy a beachside lunch in total seclusion—knowing that a safe return is just a short ride away. It’s a carefree, sun-soaked escape into your own island paradise.
Local Island Experience
Step beyond the postcard-perfect beaches and explore nearby local islands where life moves at a gentle rhythm—watch fishermen at work, browse lively village markets, and meet the warm, welcoming locals. Knowledgeable guides reveal the hidden stories, customs, and secret corners of island life, offering an authentic glimpse into the culture, colors, and daily rhythms of Maldivian living.
Bodu Beru Night
Kick off your sandals and feel the sand beneath your feet as the rhythm takes over. Let loose on the sandy dance floor to the pulsating beats of a traditional Maldivian Boduberu drum show, where hypnotic drumming, energetic dancing, and vibrant songs create an unforgettable celebration of island culture.
Sunk In Sand Dining
Step beyond traditional dining and settle into a setting shaped by the island itself. Sit within the shoreline, where soft white sand meets endless ocean views and enjoy a beachfront breakfast, a leisurely lunch, or ease into a sunset moment. Framed by the horizon and complemented by curated menus, this dining concept transforms the beach into your table, creating a moment that feels both intimate and unmistakably Maldivian.
Hotpot Lunch & Dinner
Gather around a dining ritual designed for warmth, flavour, and shared enjoyment. With aromatic broths, premium ingredients, fresh seafood, meats, and vibrant vegetables, create flavours at your own pace. From light, comforting selections to rich, indulgent combinations, every hotpot offers a balance of taste and interaction. Perfect for relaxed lunches or unhurried dinners, savour a meal that celebrates both culinary exploration and togetherness.

LEISURE ACTIVITIES
From night dives to freediving, from jetskiing to sunset cruises, we have it all. Truly.
Watersports
The water here doesn't just look inviting, it's practically begging you to jump in. Kayak across still lagoons, catch the wind on a kitesurf board, or simply paddle out and see where the current takes you. Whatever your speed, our instructors are on hand to help you make the most of it.
Excursions
Some days are made for wandering further. Set sail for a sunset cruise, swim alongside gentle giants, or discover a nearby island all your own. However you like to explore, there's always somewhere new waiting just beyond the lagoon.
Diving
All around Sun Siyam Iru Veli is a whole world to be explored. From vibrant coral gardens to curious reef life, our dive team takes guests of every level deep into the Indian Ocean, because no trip to the Maldives is quite complete until you've seen it from below.
