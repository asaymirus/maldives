Bandhi Pool Bar in Maldives
Located by the poolside with plenty of overhead tropical foliage for a shady afternoon, the Bandhi Bar in Maldives is a buzz of activity and a great spot to mingle with other guests over creative cocktails, shisha and snacks. Enjoy innovative cocktails and delicious shisha flavours all day by the pool. As evening sets in, the space transforms into an atmospheric venue, complete with live music and flair bartending.
Pro tip: Try out the signature jellyfish cocktail and catch the signature flair bartending act, Lamborghini.
