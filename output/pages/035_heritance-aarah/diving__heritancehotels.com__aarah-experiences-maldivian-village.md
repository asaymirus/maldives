The Maldivian Village
The Maldivian Village at Heritance Aarah offers an immersive journey into the island’s living heritage, where culture, nature, and storytelling come together. Guided by Aarah’s storytellers, guests walk barefoot along
Dhandahelu Magu
, a symbolic path honouring Maldivian history, before stepping into everyday island life as it once was.
Along the way, traditional resting spaces, coral-stone homes, handicraft workshops, and sustainable fishing stories reveal how island communities lived in balance with the sea. Hands-on experiences, from coconut husking to rope making, bring these traditions to life. The journey ends with authentic Dhivehi flavours, featuring roshi, masroshi, bajiya, kulhimas, and sweet bondi bai, offering a true taste of Maldivian culture.
