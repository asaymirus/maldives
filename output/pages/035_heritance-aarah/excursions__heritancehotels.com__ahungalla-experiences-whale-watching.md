Whale Watching
It will be an early start, as you leave Heritance Ahungalla at around 4 am to catch a boat from Mirissa at 6.30am. The whales usually start to appear after about an hour out to sea. Mostly Blue Whales, but sometimes Killer Whales also appear. Sightings of dolphins are also common on the way out and back. We advise you to bring a hat and plenty of sun cream, as well as a camera and binoculars. You will arrive back to the hotel in time for lunch.
Whale watching season: November to April
