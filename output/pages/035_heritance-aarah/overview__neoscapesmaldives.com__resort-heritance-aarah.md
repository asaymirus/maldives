DESCRIPTION
Paradise personified in the untouched waters of Raa Atoll.
Located in the pristine Raa Atoll, Heritance Aarah is an all-villa luxury resort surrounded by turquoise lagoons, untouched reefs, and abundant marine life. Combining contemporary elegance with authentic Maldivian aesthetics, the resort delivers a refined island escape where personalised service and immersive experiences take centre stage.
Guests can choose from elegant beach villas, spacious family villas, and luxurious overwater suites, many featuring private plunge pools and dedicated butler service. With a Premium All-Inclusive offering, guests enjoy exceptional dining across multiple restaurants and bars, curated wellness experiences, exciting water sports, and unforgettable excursions. Whether relaxing beside the lagoon, discovering marine life beneath the waves, or indulging in gourmet cuisine, Heritance Aarah offers a seamless blend of luxury, comfort, and island tranquillity.

Highlights From The Resort
Premium All-Inclusive Experience
Heritance Aarah redefines all-inclusive luxury with gourmet dining, premium beverages, personalised experiences, and tailored island journeys included throughout the stay.
Overwater Medi Spa
The resort’s Medi Spa is one of the first of its kind in the Maldives, featuring overwater treatment rooms, wellness consultations, steam facilities, and holistic therapies designed to restore balance and relaxation.
Exceptional Marine Experiences
Located near magnificent thilas and vibrant marine ecosystems, the resort offers incredible opportunities for snorkeling, diving, dolphin cruises, and fishing adventures.
Family-Friendly Luxury
With spacious family villas, Koka Kids Club, educational activities, sports facilities, and entertainment options, the resort is ideal for families travelling together.
Romantic Escapes & Destination Weddings
