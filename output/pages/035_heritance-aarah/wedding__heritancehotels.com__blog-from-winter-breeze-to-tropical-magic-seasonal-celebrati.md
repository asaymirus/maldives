From Winter Breeze to Tropical Magic: Seasonal Celebrations at Heritance Aarah Maldives
Nestled in the serene Raa Atoll, Heritance Aarah, a premium all-inclusive resort, offers a refreshing twist on seasonal celebrations. This festive season, trade snow for stunning white sands and bask in the warmth of the tropical ocean breeze. Here, the spirit of celebration comes alive in a unique blend of exclusivity and relaxation, creating truly memorable experiences.
Celebrations begin on December 10 with A Night of Mexican Delights. Experience the zest of Mexico with a night filled with delicious dishes, live music, and refreshing drinks at Ranba Beach. The festivities continue with Holiday Mixology Lessons, where guests can shake up their holidays by mastering festive cocktails with expert tips. Little explorers will also find joy in special activities designed just for them.
The Holiday Street Fare Fest invites guests to explore an array of mouthwatering street food delights from around the world, while Holiday Cookery Lessons spark creativity and passion in the kitchen. Join the celebrations at the Bandhi Pool Bar for Enchanted Illuminations, a celebration of the season with sparkling lights, festive vibes, and holiday cheer.
Starting December 13, kids can immerse themselves in Santa’s Workshop at Kokaa Kids Club, enjoying various daily activities such as Festive Cupcake Decorations, a Festive Children’s Movie with Popcorn, Toasty Tales – A Festive Kids’ Bonfire, Santa’s Scavenger Hunt, Fashion Atelier, Gingerbread House Decorating, New Year Banner Making, Campfire Storytelling, and more.
The seasonal celebrations at Heritance Aarah promise enchanting experiences for all ages. Guests can sip on festive cocktails during the Holiday Cocktail Soirée, and savor lavish dinners at the Ranba and Ralu Restaurants, offering an international buffet filled with holiday favorites. For a more intimate dining experience, the Baani Restaurant features a curated selection of seasonal delights.
Guests can also immerse in captivating performances that begin with the rich heritage of Sri Lankan traditional dancing and drumming. Next, be mesmerized by a stunning LED and fire dancing display by Zatsepina Alina. As the evening unfolds, groove to the beats of DJ Kir Royal, ranked among the Top 100 Female DJs in the World, as she delivers a seamless mix of vibrant music and high-energy rhythms.
As the year comes to a close, the celebrations continue with a Boxing Day Bonfire Cocktail, enjoy cocktails by a bonfire with live entertainment at Ranba Beach. Indulge in a decadent multi-course New Year’s Eve Gala Dinner crafted by award-winning chefs or enjoy a special set dinner at Baani Restaurant.
As midnight approaches, dance the night away to live music and raise a glass to new beginnings, all while reveling in the chance to win exciting prizes. Start the New Year on a delightful note with a leisurely brunch at the Ranba Main Restaurant, setting the stage for a year filled with joy and adventure.
As a celebrated culinary destination in the Maldives, Heritance Aarah features a dine-around concept across seven exquisite culinary outlets and five vibrant bars. Each outlet transforms beautifully for the season, inviting guests to explore a delightful array of festive flavours. This year, every meal becomes a celebration, with special menus that reflect the spirit of the season.
Join us at Heritance Aarah this holiday season, where warmth, joy, and unforgettable memories await in paradise!
