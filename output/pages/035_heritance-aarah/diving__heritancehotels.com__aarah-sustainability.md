Sustainability
Discover an unparalleled experience at Heritance Aarah, where exceptional hospitality meets commitment to sustainability. As the first LEED (Leadership in Energy and Environmental Design) certified property in the Maldives and a GSTC (Global Sustainable Tourism Council) recognized Green Globe certified resort, we are proud to uphold the highest standards in environmental stewardship, social responsibility, economic resilience, and cultural preservation.
Your stay at Heritance Aarah is more than just a getaway – it’s a meaningful choice for a better tomorrow. Immerse yourself in our environmental initiatives, thoughtfully designed to inspire and engage. From participating in hands on activities to exploring our dedicated sustainability programs, you’ll find countless ways to connect with our mission.
Don’t miss the Maldivian village experience, an enchanting journey into the heart of the Maldives’ rich cultural heritage. It’s a unique opportunity to celebrate the traditions that make our island home so extraordinary.
For a deeper understanding of our efforts, we invite you to explore our sustainability reports or watch our insightful videos. Together, let’s create a greener, more responsible future.
