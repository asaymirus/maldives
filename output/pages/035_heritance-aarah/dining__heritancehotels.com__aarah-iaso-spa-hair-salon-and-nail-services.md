Hair Salon and Nail Services
Experience refined beauty and relaxation at IASO Medi spa, where our oceanfront Hair Salon & Nail Services deliver luxurious treatments designed to leave you feeling polished, pampered, and radiant throughout your stay
.
Classic Manicure |
60 minutes
Our Classic Manicure begins with gentle nail shaping and cuticle care, followed by a relaxing hand soak and exfoliation to leave your skin silky smooth. Enjoy a soothing hand massage with hydrating lotion, then nail polish.
Classic Pedicure |
75 minutes
Smooth, soothe, and polish your feet to perfection. Begin with expert nail shaping and cuticle care to create a clean, elegant look. Next, enjoy a warm foot soak to relax and soften the skin, followed by a thorough exfoliation and foot filing on the soles to buff away rough patches and calluses. A relaxing massage with hydrating lotion melts away tension, and your choice of nail polish adds the final touch of beauty.
Express Manicure |
45 minutes
Includes nail shaping, cuticle work, buffing, nail cleaning, exfoliating scrub, and moisturizer application for clean, refreshed hands without polish.
Express Pedicure |
45 minutes
Includes a foot soak, nail shaping and buffing, nail cleaning, and callus removal for smooth, refreshed feet without polish.
Traditional Hair Cream Bath |
60 Minutes
A nourishing scalp and hair treatment, featuring a rich herbal cream massage to deeply condition the hair and scalp, stimulate circulation, and relieve tension—followed by a relaxing shoulder massage for total rejuvenation.
Nourishing Ayurvedic Hair Oil Treatment |
60 Minutes
A luxurious scalp ritual using warm, herbal-infused Ayurvedic oil to stimulate hair growth, strengthen roots, and reduce hair fall. This ancient blend of botanicals deeply nourishes the scalp while calming the senses through rhythmic massage techniques that balance the doshas and restore vitality.
