Celebrate ‘100 Days To Christmas’ with Heritance Aarah
As the countdown to Christmas begins, Heritance Aarah invites UK travellers to mark the season with an exclusive offer in the heart of the Maldives. Nestled in the Raa Atoll and celebrated for its Premium All-Inclusive experience, the resort unveils festive packages designed for families and couples alike, blending five-star comfort with island enchantment.
Families booking the Family Getaway Package this festive period can revel in having one child under 12 years of age staying and dining for free and also enjoy three complimentary hours of babysitting per day for children aged 2 to 4 years at the resort’s fun-filled Koka Kids Club
Couples seeking an intimate escape can select the
Romantic Getaway Package
and celebrate Christmas in exclusivity or bring in the New Year in utmost privacy. An in-villa breakfast or a floating breakfast awaits couples staying for three nights or more in the overwater Ocean Suites, while stays of seven nights or more are crowned with a romantic candlelit dinner on the beach.
This Premium All-Inclusive resort will transform into an island where magic, adventure, and meaningful experiences come together with a beachfront Christmas tree covered in a thousand twinkling lights setting the stage for the resort’s seasonal activities. With a Christmas Eve party with a DJ and a visit from Santa himself, candlelit dinners on traditional Maldivian dhonis, and a plethora of thrilling activities for children, including gingerbread house decorating and treasure hunts, Heritance Aarah is a warm Winter Wonderland!
