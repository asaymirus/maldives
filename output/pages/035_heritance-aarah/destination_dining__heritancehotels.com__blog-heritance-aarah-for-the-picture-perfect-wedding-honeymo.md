Heritance Aarah: For The Picture-Perfect Wedding & Honeymoon
In the heart of the Maldivian Raa Atoll, surrounded by turquoise waters and soft ivory sands, where couples can make their vows to the sound of waves,
Heritance Aarah
emerges as a sanctuary for boundless romance. Couples seeking an unforgettable
island wedding
experience can make dreams come true at the award-winning premium all-inclusive resort, part of the renowned
Aitken Spence Hotels
portfolio, weaving the tapestry of their love story in an atmosphere of natural beauty and handcrafted celebration.
Whether through the simplicity of the Classic Aarah Ceremony or the grandeur of the Premium Aarah Experience, each ceremony offers an unmatched setting, with world-class hospitality and attention to every detail. Every
wedding
at the resort is a personal masterpiece, individually tailored to reflect the couple’s vision while capturing the spirit of the Maldives.
The Classic Aarah Ceremony
Perfect for couples who dream of a naturally elegant celebration, The Classic Aarah Ceremony, $3,200 (£2,440), distils the essence of Maldivian romance into every moment. It invites couples to immerse themselves in a luxurious yet heartfelt experience that celebrates both the union and the island’s vibrant spirit.
Before the big day, an expert event co-ordinator handles planning and event details, while a single’s night out at the Sky Bar sets a relaxed, celebratory tone with unlimited Heritance Liquid Craft cocktails. The couple’s attire is pressed to perfection in preparation for the ceremony, allowing them to focus solely on their special day.
As the bride walks along the beach, escorted by the rhythmic beats of traditional bodu-beru drums, her fresh flower bouquet and headpiece complement the groom’s buttonhole, both sourced from local tropical blooms. A decorated buggy ride carries the couple to their ceremonial venue, where a two-tiered wedding cake, champagne toast, and live music create an atmosphere of joy and intimacy.
A private sunset cruise and access to premium dining outlets, Baani and Hathaa, further add to the enchantment, marking the start of married life with the blissful tranquillity of island life.
The Premium Aarah Experience
For couples who seek the epitome of Maldivian grandeur, The Premium Aarah Experience, $4,750 (£3,630), offers a deeply immersive celebration, wrapped in sophistication and style. Every aspect of this package is shaped to create lasting memories – from the breathtaking ceremony to the intimate post-wedding indulgences.
Before the big day, a dedicated planner ensures every detail goes according to plan, while the couple enjoys a night out at the Sky Bar, savouring the resort’s signature cocktails in the company of ocean views.
On the day of the wedding, the white sands become a stage of dreams, styled with elegant podiums, floral aisles, and bespoke decorations. A master of ceremonies guides the event, while the bride and groom arrive accompanied by traditional drummers. The celebration unfolds with champagne toasts, and live music, culminating in a symbolic certificate of vow exchange.
Beyond the ceremony, the Premium Aarah Experience extends the celebration into a multi-sensory honeymoon, with a seven-course dinner and wine pairing at Baani restaurant, a champagne or floating breakfast served in the villa, and a complimentary treatment for two at IASO Medi Spa, the resort’s serene island wellness retreat. A local island experience with a sunset dhoni cruise completes the journey, with mocktails and canapés at twilight.
Heritance Aarah
offers couples a seamless transition to their honeymoon. The resort’s villa collections, ranging from Ocean Suites suspended over clear lagoons to Beach Villas cocooned in tropical gardens, offer privacy, comfort, and awesome views. The adults-only Ocean Suites are the pinnacle of elegant hospitality, the perfect atmosphere for a honeymoon with a complimentary mini bar consisting of 16 sommelier-selected wines, and exclusive access to an adults-only restaurant and private infinity pool. The result is a honeymoon hideaway for a once-in-a-lifetime experience.
Itineraries are complete with coral reef exploration in the Raa Atoll rich with marine life, indulging in gourmet experiences across the resort’s premium restaurants, or simply surrendering to stillness at the
IASO Medi Spa
, where holistic therapies combine ancient healing rituals with modern wellness techniques. As night falls, stargazing by the sea or enjoying a private dinner on the sand allows the magic of the Maldives to complete the ultimate
destination wedding experience
.
The resort’s culinary venues include Baani, Ambula, Ranba, Ginifati, and Hathaa, each with a distinctive
dining culture
. Couples can embark on a global culinary itinerary without ever leaving the island, enjoying everything from ocean-fresh seafood to contemporary fine dining with world-class wines. For those eager to explore the Maldives beyond the lagoon, Heritance Aarah devises bespoke excursions, from sandbank picnics and snorkelling trips to deep-sea fishing adventures and sunset sailing.
For couples dreaming of an
island wedding
where the atmosphere feels spontaneous,
Heritance Aarah
is the destination where it all comes together. Whether through the simplicity of The Classic Aarah Ceremony or the grandeur of The Premium Aarah Experience, each celebration is handcrafted to tell a love story as enduring and infinite as the ocean itself.
