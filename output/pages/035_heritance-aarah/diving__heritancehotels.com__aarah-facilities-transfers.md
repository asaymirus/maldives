Transfers
Planning your Maldives vacation at Heritance Aarah and wondering how to get to the resort? Check out the different ways.
Shared Sea Plane Transfers (Round Trips)
Seaplanes offer a thrilling, safe and efficient means of reaching Heritance Aarah and witnessing the breathtaking beauty of the Maldives from the sky is an unforgettable experience. Notably, Male airport boasts the world’s largest seaplane operations. At Heritance Aarah, our exclusive seaplane lounge awaits providing a haven where you can enjoy light snacks, tea, coffee and refreshing juices. Additionally, indulge in a rejuvenating spa treatment to ensure your seaplane journey is as exceptional as your stay at our resort.

Shared Domestic Flight + Speedboat Transfers (Round Trips)
Heritance Aarah can be reached with convenient domestic flights to the nearest local airport. From there, a seamless transition awaits as you embark on a speedboat journey to the island.
Upon arrival at the local airport, our alternative airport representative will guide you to the Domestic Flight Check-in counters and the service provider will arrange the lounge facility along with selection of light snacks, tea, coffee and refreshing juices for our domestic clients.
