Overview
Heritance Aarah, Maldives - Premium All Inclusive features accommodation in Raa Atoll. This 5-star resort offers a 24-hour front desk and a shared lounge. The property has a garden, as well as a restaurant that serves Middle Eastern cuisine.
Guest rooms in the resort are fitted with a flat-screen TV. Featuring a private bathroom, rooms at Heritance Aarah also boast free WiFi.
A continental, buffet or American breakfast is available each morning at the property.
Spa and wellness facilities including a hot tub and a fitness centre are at guests disposal during their stay at the accommodation. Guests at Heritance Aarah - Premium All Inclusive will be able to enjoy activities in and around Raa Atoll, like skiing.
Baa Atoll is 24.2 miles from the resort.
