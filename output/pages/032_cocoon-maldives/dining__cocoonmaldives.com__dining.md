Restaurants & Bars
Indulge in a culinary journey with our diverse dining experiences, featuring three exquisite restaurants and two lively bars. The main restaurant, Octopus, treats you to global cuisines through themed buffets and interactive live stations. For a romantic ambiance, Manta Restaurant offers enchanting à la carte dining by candlelight in the lagoon, perfect for moonlit evenings. Immerse yourself in authentic Japanese flavors at Japanese Garden, where stunning views of the Indian Ocean enhance your meal. Unwind at Kurum-Bar with refreshing sunset drinks, or enjoy lively evenings at Loabi Loabi pool bar. Experience unforgettable destination dining options, from sunset dinners on a sunken sand table to picnics on a serene sandbank.
Octopus
Restaurant
Showcasing culinary highlights from around the world the Resort Buffet Restaurant, the Octopus serves Breakfast, Lunch and Dinner. Enjoy weekly themed nights such as Asian and Sushi Night, Maldivian Night, Italian Night, Mediterranean Night, Indian Night, Moroccan Night which transform the atmosphere of the Octopus restaurant into a great dining experience.

Manta Restaurant
Manta Restaurant located over-water is opened for lunch and dinner for à la carte dining experience. Enjoy romantic candlelit dining in the Maldivian lagoon for all rising lovers’ moon. It’s absolutely a culinary journey.

Japanese Garden
The unique experience of Teppanyaki is awaiting you. Experience authentic Japanese flavors in a Maldives rustic Jungle setting with a breathtaking view of the Indian Ocean. Japanese Garden is open for lunch and dinner with curated à la carte menus to soothe your taste buds!
Japanese Garden
Kurum - Bar
Kurum-Bar offers daily refreshments and beverages within idyllic settings and close to the best sunset view on the island. Come and enjoy a delicious lunch every day with our BBQ Chef's Experience. We cook fresh fish and offer a variety of tasty brunch options that will satisfy your taste buds.
Kurum Bar
Loabi Loabi Bar
The Resort main bar, the Loabi Loabi Bar is available throughout the day for all guests and offers refreshing beverages along with Maldivian cocktails. Cocoon’s pool-side bar and a convivial meeting spot, Loabi Loabi Bar hosts weekly entertainments such as DJ, Live Music, Latino Night, White Party, Maldivian Night and Karaoke to keep you engaged and grooving all night!
