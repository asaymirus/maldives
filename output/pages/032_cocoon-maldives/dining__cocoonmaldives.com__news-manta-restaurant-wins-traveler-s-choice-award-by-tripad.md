Manta Restaurant Wins Traveler's Choice Award by TripAdvisor
​
Oct 10, 2022
​
Manta Restaurant at
Cocoon Maldives
has been awarded Trip Advisor's 2022 Travelers’ Choice Awards for Restaurants. Travelers’ Choice recognizes businesses that earn consistently great reviews. Award-winners are among the top 10% of listings on Tripadvisor, determined not by a panel of judges, but by those who matter most: your guests who have actually visited.
Manta restaurant is our finest a' la carte dining restaurant located overwater with a spectacular view of the ocean and the beach designed by
LAGO
. It is a perfect choice for a romantic and intimate dinner with your loved ones.
