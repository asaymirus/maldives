DESCRIPTION
In the heart of the Maldives, discover an adults-only haven where rustic luxury, Michelin-starred dining beneath the sea, and endless ocean horizons create the ultimate private escape.
You & Me Maldives is an adults-only sanctuary in the pristine Raa Atoll, designed for couples, honeymooners, and friends seeking pure tranquility. The resort features 99 overwater villas and 10 beach suites, all with personalized island host service, blending rustic charm with modern elegance.
Guests can indulge in curated dining experiences through four à la carte restaurants, a vibrant bar, and the iconic H2O Underwater Restaurant, the ﬁrst of its kind in the Maldives, helmed by Michelin-starred Chef Andrea Berton. Rejuvenation awaits at the Elizabeth Arden Spa, complete with overwater treatment rooms, yoga pavilion, and ﬁtness centre. For adventure, the island offers diving, snorkeling, water sports, and romantic excursions such as dolphin and sunset cruises.
From world-class cuisine to serene wellness and ocean adventures, You & Me is a private escape where romance, indulgence, and unforgettable memories come alive.

Dining
Dine Around the Island
You & Me Maldives offers a unique dine around concept, allowing guests on Premium All-Inclusive, Full Board Plus, or Half Board Plus packages to enjoy lunch and dinner at any of the à la carte restaurants (excluding H2O) and breakfast at The Sand, the resort’s main restaurant. With a wide variety of gourmet options and select beverages included at mealtimes, guests can savor an array of flavors while experiencing the best of the island’s gastronomy.
The Sand
The resort’s main restaurant, The Sand, is open for breakfast and dinner, offering freshly prepared dishes from bespoke live cooking stations with international flavors. Guests can enjoy a wide selection of cuisine served to the table in a rustic, elegant setting inspired by nature. The restaurant also features a well-stocked wine cellar with an exceptional collection of vintages and champagnes.
Rising Sun
Delight in an authentic Teppanyaki experience at Rising Sun, where skilled chefs prepare sushi, sashimi, tender cuts of beef, and other Japanese specialties right before your eyes. The intimate setting and live cooking shows create a vibrant dining experience rich in flavor and flair, bringing the essence of Japan to the Maldives.
Green Carpet
Set by the pool in a vibrant, urban-chic setting, Green Carpet is the perfect spot for a healthy yet indulgent lunch. Fresh flavors and a lively atmosphere make it a refreshing break under the Maldivian sun.
La Pasta
True to the Italian spirit, La Pasta brings authentic flavors to the beachside with a communal, relaxed vibe. Serving freshly made pastas, wood-fired pizzas, and classic Italian specialties for lunch and dinner, it’s the perfect place to savor Italy’s finest cuisine by the ocean.
Cheers Bar
Located by the main pool with stunning views, Cheers Bar is the resort’s lively social hub. Relax in hammocks or lounge chairs while enjoying handcrafted cocktails, fine wines, and champagne. Evenings come alive with live music and entertainment, making it the perfect spot to unwind under the stars.
H2O by Andrea Berton
The jewel of You & Me Maldives, H2O is the country’s first fully submerged underwater restaurant, offering an extraordinary Michelin-starred dining experience by Chef Andrea Berton. With seating for just 20 guests, this intimate venue pairs exquisite set menus with panoramic views of the Maldives’ vibrant marine world, creating a once-in-a-lifetime culinary journey beneath the waves.

Highlights From The Resort
Adults-Only Island
Designed exclusively for couples, honeymooners, and friends, You & Me offers a serene, child-free escape. Nestled on an eco-friendly island built with natural wood, it provides a rustic-luxury vibe and an intimate atmosphere far removed from other resorts.
Orchid Spa by Elizabeth Arden PRO
Set overwater with four private bungalows, a sauna, steam room, and outdoor jacuzzi, Orchid Spa is a sanctuary of wellness. Skilled therapists offer treatments using Elizabeth Arden PRO and HESITO® products, combining global traditions to rejuvenate the mind, body, and spirit.
H2O Underwater Restaurant
A signature experience, H2O by Michelin-starred Chef Andrea Berton is the Maldives’ first fully submerged underwater restaurant. With seating for 20 guests, it pairs a curated set menu with panoramic views of vibrant marine life, offering an intimate and unforgettable dining experience.
The Bubble Island by Veuve Clicquot
The first of its kind in the Maldives, The Bubble Island is a collaboration with the world-renowned Champagne House Veuve Clicquot. Guests can indulge in exclusive champagne tastings, awakening all five senses in a unique natural setting.
Dine Around the Island
Guests on Half Board Plus, Full Board Plus, or All-Inclusive packages can enjoy flexible dining, choosing lunch and dinner at any à la carte restaurant or the main restaurant. Select beverages are included, allowing guests to explore the full range of culinary offerings across the island.
Water Sports and Diving
Managed by Emperor Maldives, the Water Sports Centre provides activities for both beginners and experienced adventurers. Guests can enjoy snorkeling, sailing, jet-skiing, and trips to nearby uninhabited islands. The PADI-certified dive centre introduces guests to manta rays, reef sharks, turtles, and other marine life with the guidance of certified professionals.
Excursions and Experiences
The resort organizes dolphin cruises, private sandbank dinners, fishing trips, local island tours, picnic islands, whale shark snorkeling at Hanifaru Bay, and romantic sunset or candlelit cruises. Each experience highlights the natural beauty and culture of the Maldives.
Weddings and Photography
You & Me offers destination weddings with beachside floral arches, candlelit receptions, and professional photography, ensuring every moment is beautifully captured.
Entertainment
Evenings feature live music, DJs, Maldivian cultural shows, sandbank parties, and glow events, providing the perfect balance of relaxation and celebration.
