FAQs
What is the nearest International Airport to Cocoon Maldives?
Velana International Airport, Male’ is the nearest international airport to Cocoon Maldives.
What is the Time Zone?
The resort time at Cocoon Maldives is six hours ahead of Greenwich Mean Time (GMT+6).  One hour ahead of Male time. Providing an extra one hour of daylight each day so guests can get the most out of every day.
How far is Cocoon Maldives from the Airport?
Cocoon Maldives is located 120km from Male', reachable within a 30-minutes scenic seaplane transfer journey.
Do you cater for special dietary requirements?
Yes, we do. Upon reservation or on arrival, please discuss any dietary requirements or allergies you may have with our villa hosts. Our chefs can then prepare a menu based on your requirements.
Can I swim anywhere around the resort?
Cocoon Maldives is blessed with a beautiful lagoon. While swimming in the lagoon is generally safe, we recommend avoiding it in adverse weather conditions like strong winds and rough seas. In an unlikely case, our main pool is the best place to swim.
Can I smoke anywhere in the resort?
You may smoke in designated areas but please note that all our villas are non-smoking.
Do you have e-mail and internet facilities available to the guest?
Yes, We have complimentary Wi-Fi access throughout the resort including guest accommodations. Log in details will be briefed at the time of check in by our Island Hosts.
Photography and Drone footage?
Due to privacy concerns, Cocoon prohibits the operation of unmanned aerial systems or drones. We kindly ask guests to refrain from flying drones anywhere on the island. For drone shootings please contact Guest Relations. Photography is allowed for personal use.
