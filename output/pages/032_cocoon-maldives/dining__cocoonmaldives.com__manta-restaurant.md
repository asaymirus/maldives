​
Manta Restaurant, perched gracefully over the water, offers a sublime à la carte dining experience for lunch and dinner. This enchanting venue sets the perfect stage for romantic candlelight dinners under the Maldivian moon, making it an ideal choice for couples and lovers. Guests can indulge in a diverse menu that showcases fresh, locally sourced ingredients while soaking in breathtaking views of the lagoon. Each meal is a culinary journey, crafted to delight the senses and create unforgettable memories. Whether it’s a casual lunch or an intimate dinner, Manta Restaurant promises a magical dining experience in paradise.
