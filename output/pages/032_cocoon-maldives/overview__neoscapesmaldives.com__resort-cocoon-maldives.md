DESCRIPTION
Cocoon Maldives is the Maldives’ ﬁrst design hotel, seamlessly blending Italian artistry with island serenity for an experience found nowhere else.
Cocoon Maldives, the Maldives’ ﬁrst design hotel, is a sanctuary where Italian sophistication meets the raw beauty of Lhaviyani Atoll. Just a 30-minute seaplane ride from Malé, this award-winning resort was designed by Italian ﬁrm Lago, blending understated elegance with panoramic ocean views. Interiors feature ﬂoating beds, wildwood furniture and ﬁne linens that create an atmosphere of reﬁned comfort in harmony with nature.
Guests can indulge in three restaurants, two bars, a dive centre close to 50 spectacular sites, The Cube Spa, yoga pavilion, ﬁtness centre, Kids Village, and a wide range of excursions from dolphin cruises to private sandbank picnics. With island hosts curating personalised experiences, lively entertainment, and complimentary non-motorised water sports, Cocoon Maldives offers a seamless fusion of style, luxury, and natural splendour.

Dining
Octopus Restaurant
Octopus Restaurant is Cocoon’s main buffet dining venue, serving freshly prepared meals for breakfast, lunch, and dinner. Guests can enjoy live cooking stations featuring Asian, Italian, and grilled specialties, complemented by a glass-walled wine cellar with fine wines from around the world. Weekly themed nights such as Maldivian Night, Mediterranean Night, Indian Night, Moroccan Night, and Sushi Night offer vibrant culinary experiences. The restaurant also features a community table where guests from different countries can connect over food and shared stories.
Manta Restaurant
Perched entirely over the turquoise lagoon, Manta Restaurant offers an unforgettable fine dining experience. Designed by LAGO, the contemporary interiors complement the stunning natural backdrop. Guests can enjoy à la carte lunch and dinner featuring an international menu, while candlelight evenings create a romantic setting under the Maldivian sky. The overwater location, combined with exquisite cuisine and panoramic lagoon views, makes Manta a perfect choice for intimate dinners and special celebrations.
Japanese Garden
Japanese Garden offers an authentic Teppanyaki experience in a rustic Maldivian jungle setting with breathtaking views of the Indian Ocean. Guests can enjoy carefully curated à la carte lunch and dinner menus, featuring traditional Japanese flavours prepared with precision. The combination of skilled culinary craftsmanship, serene surroundings, and panoramic ocean vistas makes dining here a unique and memorable experience for every guest.
Kurum Bar
Kurum Bar is Cocoon’s beachside retreat, perfect for daily refreshments, snacks, and tropical beverages. Located on the west coast near the Pupa Kids Club, it offers lounge seating and stunning views of the Maldivian sunset. Guests can enjoy freshly prepared BBQ dishes, including local lagoon fish, alongside a variety of tasty brunch options. With its idyllic setting and vibrant atmosphere, Kurum Bar is the ideal spot to relax, unwind, and soak in the island’s natural beauty.
Loabi Loabi Bar
Loabi Loabi Bar is Cocoon’s vibrant poolside hub, serving refreshing beverages and signature Maldivian cocktails throughout the day. As a lively meeting spot, it hosts nightly entertainment including live DJ sessions, Boduberu performances, karaoke, and themed parties such as Latino Night, White Party, and Maldivian Night. With its energetic atmosphere, poolside location, and engaging events, Loabi Loabi Bar is the perfect place to socialize, unwind, and enjoy the island’s spirited nightlife.

Highlights From The Resort
Design & Location
The Maldives’ first design hotel, created by Italian firm Lago, where floating beds and wildwood interiors blend seamlessly with the turquoise lagoon of Lhaviyani Atoll, just a 30-minute seaplane ride from Malé.
The Cube Spa
Expert therapists use global wellness traditions and natural products to create a serene escape of relaxation and renewal.
Diving Centre
Close to 50 dive sites, including two shipwrecks at “Shipyard” and shark encounters at “Kuredhoo Express,” ideal for both beginners and seasoned divers.
Water Sports Centre
From paddleboarding and kayaking to jet skiing and adrenaline-fuelled adventures, guests can enjoy endless thrills on the water.
Excursions
Explore the Maldives with dolphin cruises, sunset fishing, snorkelling trips, local island visits, big game fishing, picnic islands, and private sandbank escapes.
Kids Village
A safe, fully supervised space featuring playgrounds, a cinema, and a kids’ pool, designed to keep younger guests entertained and inspired.
Entertainment
Lively evenings with live bands, DJs, disco nights, cultural Boduberu shows, beach BBQs, and open-air movie nights.
Dining
Five unique venues, from Octopus Restaurant’s live cooking stations to fine dining at overwater Manta, Teppanyaki at Japanese Garden, cocktails at Loabi Loabi pool bar, and stunning sunsets at Kurum-Bar.
