What's New at Cocoon Maldives?
Step into a refreshed paradise as Cocoon Maldives unveils its latest upgrades after reopening!
From enhanced villas featuring modern comforts and stylish designs to new dining experiences that elevate your culinary journey, every detail has been thoughtfully curated to make your stay even more memorable. Dive into upgraded leisure facilities, indulge in rejuvenating spa treatments, and explore revamped activities that bring you closer to nature’s wonders. The magic of Cocoon Maldives is back—now better than ever.
Come and experience the transformation for yourself!
New Lagoon Suites With Slide!
Dive into a world of excitement with our ten brand-new Lagoon Suites, each equipped with a private waterslide that whisks you directly into the turquoise lagoon. Perfect for thrill-seekers and families, these suites offer not just luxurious comfort but also a playful, adventurous touch. With stunning views, modern interiors, and direct water access, they are the ultimate overwater retreat.
New Lagoon Suites With Pool!
Immerse yourself in unparalleled luxury with our new five Lagoon Suites featuring private infinity pools. Elevated above the serene waters, these suites combine contemporary design with the tranquility of nature. Lounge by your personal pool, watch the sun set over the horizon, and enjoy a haven of exclusivity and relaxation.
New Beach Suites with Pool!
Experience the perfect blend of seclusion and sophistication in our newly launched five Beach Suites with private pools. Nestled amidst lush tropical greenery and just steps from the white sandy beach, these spacious suites offer modern design, expansive outdoor spaces, and your own pool to cool off in ultimate privacy.
Refurbishing Of All Villas & Suites!
All our villas and suites have been reimagined to enhance your stay. With elegant new interiors, upgraded furnishings, and state-of-the-art amenities, every space now reflects a modern, sophisticated vibe while maintaining the cozy charm that defines Cocoon Maldives.
New Fitness Centre!
Our brand-new fitness center offers a cutting-edge workout experience in an inspiring tropical setting. Equipped with top-of-the-line cardio machines, weights, and fitness accessories, it’s designed to cater to all your wellness needs while letting you enjoy stunning lagoon views as you exercise.
New Yoga Pavilion!
Find your zen in our serene new yoga pavilion, a sanctuary surrounded by lush greenery and ocean views. Whether it’s a guided morning meditation or a sunset yoga class, this space is designed to rejuvenate your body, mind, and spirit, creating moments of calm amid the island’s natural beauty.
Extension of Loabi Loabi Bar!
The Loabi Loabi Bar has been thoughtfully extended to offer more space for relaxation and entertainment. With additional seating, enhanced décor, and an updated layout, it’s the perfect spot to mingle, enjoy handcrafted cocktails, and soak in panoramic ocean views with friends and loved ones.
Extension of Beach!
The expanded beach near Kurum-Bar now offers more room to bask in the sun, build sandcastles, or enjoy leisurely strolls along the shoreline. This newly enhanced area provides a picturesque setting for relaxation, with soft sands and gentle waves creating an even more inviting escape.
Lagoon Enhancement!
Our lagoon has been carefully enhanced to enrich the marine ecosystem, offering clearer waters and vibrant marine life right at your doorstep. Whether you’re snorkeling, paddleboarding, or simply admiring the scenery, the lagoon’s revitalized beauty invites you to explore and connect with nature like never before.
