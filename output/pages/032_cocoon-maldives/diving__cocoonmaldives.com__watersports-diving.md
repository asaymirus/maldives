Water Sports & Diving
Cocoon Maldives Water sports & Dive Centre is managed by Oceanico Maldives.
Cocoon is a great location to see some of the Maldives’ most charismatic megafauna, such as manta rays, turtles, napoleon fish, and grey reef sharks.
Cocoon houses a fully-equipped diving centre, run by certified professionals. Novices can learn to dive, and complete their PADI certificate here. Experienced divers can enjoy bespoke dive trips to some of the Atoll’s most vibrant dive sites.
​
Are you an adrenaline junkie? Cocoon’s Water Sports Centre offers everything you need for the ultimate water sports thrill. From snorkelling to jet skis, we have the complete range of water sports equipment and activities to satisfy even hard-core adventurers.
