Meal Plan Overview
At Cocoon Maldives, our meal plans are crafted to elevate your island experience with a blend of variety, taste, and convenience.
​
Indulge in a world of dining options and refreshing beverages, thoughtfully selected to cater to every preference. From sumptuous buffet spreads to carefully curated à la carte dishes, each meal plan is designed to provide a seamless balance of flavors and indulgence.
​
Whether enjoying a signature cocktail by the pool or savoring a gourmet dining experience, our plans ensure every moment is memorable. Explore the brochure below for full details on each exclusive meal plan.

All Inclusive (AI)
Enjoy a full culinary experience at Cocoon Maldives, with breakfast, lunch, and dinner served daily at the Main Restaurant. Afternoon tea or coffee with snacks is available. Quench your thirst with bottled water and a range of beverages by the glass, including soft drinks, beer, espresso, house wine, and a variety of spirits such as gin, vodka, whiskey, brandy, tequila, rum, arrack, and martini, served from 10:00 to midnight (post-midnight drinks are charged at standard rates). Selected cocktails and mocktails are also included. In-room mini-bar, replenished daily, offers soft drinks and water, complemented by tea and coffee-making facilities.
All Inclusive Extreme (AIE)
At Cocoon Maldives, indulge in a curated selection of premium wines from around the world, sparkling wines, unlimited cocktails, signature drinks, fresh juices, soft drinks, and a variety of tea and coffee blends, all available at our restaurants and bars. Enjoy a premium minibar, replenished daily with beer, wine, soft drinks, water, and snacks. Guests can look forward to two included excursions, such as a Sunset Fishing trip and a Local Island excursion (weather permitting). Enhance your relaxation with a 20% discount on all spa services, plus one romantic dinner at the acclaimed Manta Restaurant and a BBQ beach dinner during your stay.
