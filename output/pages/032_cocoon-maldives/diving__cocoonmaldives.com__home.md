The first design hotel in the Maldives
Cocoon Maldives blends the best of Italian design with the Maldives’ stunning natural beauty, all set against the backdrop of the azure Indian Ocean. The resort is located in the Lhaviyani Atoll, a scenic 30-minute seaplane journey from Malé.
Cocoon Maldives is your designer haven, where Italian elegance, nature, and ocean views create timeless serenity.
Your Villa is perfectly designed for your comfort. Contemporary interior of your villa surrounded by infinite turquoise blue ocean is a blend of luxury and tropical tranquillity. The villas are fully designed for you to relax, unwind and experience a timeless vacation with us. The beachfront terrace overlooks the stunning crystal clear ocean while your private garden is the ideal way to experience sunshine filled days with sun loungers, day bed, swimming pool and outdoor shower. The Maldives’ natural beauty with the best of Italian design is a blend of style and nature. With beds that float in the air, rustic wildwood tables, Cocoon has 150 guest villas, 3 restaurants and 2 bars exclusively designed by LAGO, the award-winning Italian designer. It is chic luxury with an affordable price tag.

From lagoon sunsets to island flavours: your timeless dining experience in the Maldives
With a selection of three restaurants and two bars, you will be spoilt for choice. The main Restaurant, Octopus Restaurant, indulges you with cuisines from around the world in themed buffet and interactive live stations. For those seeking a romantic dinner by candlelight to the rising lovers’ moon, Manta Restaurant offers à la carte dining in the lagoon. Japanese Garden is dedicated to authentic Japanese flavors with a breathtaking view of the Indian Ocean. Kurum-Bar is a laid-back space for sunset drinks, while the Loabi Loabi pool Bar is a convivial meeting place hosting parties every evening. Experience our destination dining options, from a magical sunset dinner on a sunken sand table, floating in your own private pool, or a picnic on the sandbank.

Reborn in barefoot luxury,
where the world begins again
Cocoon Maldives, the first design hotel in the Maldives, is set in the untouched beauty of Lhaviyani Atoll, where nature defines every moment of your stay. An island wrapped in lush tropical vegetation and surrounded by an endless turquoise lagoon, it is a sanctuary where barefoot luxury blends seamlessly with raw natural wonder. Walk along pristine white sands, drift into crystal waters, and experience an ecosystem alive with marine life, from graceful manta rays and reef sharks to dolphins dancing offshore. Above the surface, vibrant birdlife and tropical species move freely across the island, while the surrounding atoll reveals one of the richest marine environments in the Maldives. Here, design, comfort, and nature exist in perfect harmony, creating a refined yet deeply immersive escape into pure island life.
