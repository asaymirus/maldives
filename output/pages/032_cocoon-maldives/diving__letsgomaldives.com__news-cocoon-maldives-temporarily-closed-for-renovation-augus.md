Cocoon Maldives
, renowned for its luxury and hospitality, is embarking on an extensive renovation project to redefine the guest experience. From
August 15 to September 30, 2024
, the resort will undergo a temporary closure to undergo
transformative changes
.
Key Enhancements:
Accommodation:
Introducing five new Beach Suite Pool rooms and ten Sunset Lagoon Suites featuring Water Slides, offering exclusive and luxurious options for guests.
Wellness Facilities:
A cutting-edge gym facility and Sunset Yoga Pavilion will cater to guests’ wellness needs, ensuring a holistic retreat experience.
Dining and Leisure:
Bars will be extended and upgraded, while guest villas will undergo rejuvenation to create a refreshed ambiance.
Commitment to Excellence:
Cocoon Maldives is unwavering in its commitment to maintaining the highest standards of quality and guest satisfaction. Through meticulous planning, the resort aims to set new benchmarks for luxury hospitality in the Maldives.
Anticipation Builds:
With the grand reopening on the horizon, travelers can look forward to an extraordinary blend of luxury, adventure, and relaxation at Cocoon Maldives. The resort’s visionary makeover promises an unforgettable escape in the heart of paradise.
