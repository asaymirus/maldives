Fishing
Fishing is more than an activity in the Maldives. It is a tradition, an adventure, a friendly competition, and the perfect escape. As the saying goes, “Even a bad day fishing is better than a good day at work.” Set out at sunrise and try your luck catching wahoo, jackfish, or tuna as the first light stretches across the Indian Ocean. Or choose a sunset fishing experience, surrounded by spectacular views as the day fades into the horizon. With deep blue waters and an incredible variety of fish, fishing in the Maldives can truly be an adventure of a lifetime. For an authentic taste of this island tradition, join our Maldivian Sports Fishing excursion and experience the thrill of fishing in paradise.
Kids Village
Kids Club is located on the west coast of the island, the kids club facility offers a brilliant concept for children spending holidays at the island resort. ​ The new Kids Club of Cocoon now features an indoor playground with interactive learning area, and a cinema room. It also has a large outdoor playground with a ping-pong table and a kid’s pool for a fun splash. Open from 10:00 am to 06:00 pm, it is a complimentary facility for children aged 3 to 12 years old. Babysitting service is also available for babies and toddlers. At the Kids Club, we will take the children for an exciting day of learning and discovering new things, from Scavenger Hunt, Treasure Hunt, Mini Olympic, to Kids Pool Party. Snack times are double the fun with cooking classes. Our kids' club team will keep the young guests active and creative all day.
