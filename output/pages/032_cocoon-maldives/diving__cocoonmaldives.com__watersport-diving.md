Water Sport & Diving
​
​
Cocoon Maldives Water sports & Dive Centre is managed by Oceanico Maldives.
Cocoon is a great location to see some of the Maldives’ most charismatic megafauna, such as manta rays, turtles, napoleon fish, and grey reef sharks. Cocoon houses a fully-equipped diving centre, run by certified professionals. Novices can learn to dive, and complete their PADI certificate here. Experienced divers can enjoy bespoke dive trips to some of the Atoll’s most vibrant dive sites.​ Are you an adrenaline junkie? Cocoon’s Water Sports Centre offers everything you need for the ultimate water sports thrill. From snorkelling to jet skis, we have the complete range of water sports equipment and activities to satisfy even hard-core adventurers.
Water Sports
​
For those seeking excitement on the water, Cocoon Maldives Water Sports Centre offers a wide range of thrilling activities for every level of adventure. From snorkelling in crystal-clear lagoons to high-energy experiences on jet skis, guests can enjoy the ultimate mix of fun, adrenaline, and ocean discovery. With a full selection of water sports equipment and experiences, Cocoon is the perfect destination for anyone looking to make the most of the Maldives in an active and unforgettable way.
Diving
​
At Cocoon Maldives, the Dive Centre managed by Oceanico Maldives offers unforgettable underwater experiences in one of the most spectacular areas of the Maldives. Surrounded by vibrant marine life, guests can discover manta rays, turtles, napoleon fish, and grey reef sharks while exploring some of the Atoll’s most stunning dive sites. With a fully equipped centre and certified instructors, beginners can start their diving journey and earn their PADI certification, while experienced divers can enjoy tailor-made excursions designed to reveal the very best of the ocean.
Between surface thrill and underwater wonder,
the ocean becomes your playground
At Cocoon Maldives, watersports and diving reveal the ocean in all its forms, from effortless surface adventures to the silent beauty beneath. Explore vibrant coral reefs through guided snorkeling excursions and daily trips across the lagoon, or dive deeper with immersive scuba diving experiences discovering the rich marine world of Lhaviyani Atoll. Above the water, enjoy the thrill of jet ski rides, flyboarding, and shared moments on banana boats, all set against an endless expanse of turquoise. Whether gliding, diving, or simply drifting, every activity is shaped by crystal-clear waters and extraordinary natural surroundings, turning the ocean into a space of discovery, energy, and pure freedom.
