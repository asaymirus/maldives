OBLU SELECT
Sangeli
Vibrant Chic Paradise
Tucked away on the northwestern tip of the Malé Atoll, and just a 50-minute speedboat ride from Malé International Airport, OBLU SELECT Sangeli is a sun-drenched love letter to the Maldives. Wake up to endless romantic ocean views, spend your days between white sand beaches and crystal-clear lagoons, and drift into evenings filled with fine dining, handcrafted cocktails and sunsets that melt into the sea. The Serenity Plan offers an all-inclusive island experience with dining, beverages, minibar, spa treatments, excursions and snorkelling equipment included, making every moment effortless and unforgettable.
