The Best Vegetarian Dishes to Try at Oblu Select at Sangeli Maldives
At Oblu Select at Sangeli Maldives, you can enjoy a variety of delicious vegetarian dishes that are sure to tantalize your taste buds. From fresh salads and hearty main courses to sweet desserts and snacks, there's something for everyone on the resort's menu. Here are a few of the best vegetarian dishes to try during your stay at Oblu Select at Sangeli Maldives.
1. Mixed Vegetable Curry
: This dish features a variety of fresh vegetables simmered in a rich and flavorful curry sauce. The spices used in the dish are carefully selected to bring out the natural flavors of the vegetables, and the result is a delicious and satisfying main course.
2. Avocado and Chickpea Salad
: This light and refreshing salad features ripe avocados, creamy chickpeas, and a variety of fresh greens. The dressing is made from a blend of lemon juice, olive oil, and spices and provides the perfect balance of flavor and texture.
3. Stuffed Bell Peppers
: These colorful bell peppers are filled with a mixture of rice, vegetables, and spices and are then baked to perfection. The result is a delicious and filling dish that is sure to satisfy.
4. Eggplant Parmesan
: This classic Italian dish features thin slices of eggplant that are breaded and fried until crispy, then topped with marinara sauce and melted mozzarella cheese. This dish will be your perfect comfort food for a cozy night in.
5. Vegetable Samosas
: These crispy and flaky pastry pockets are filled with a mixture of vegetables and spices and are a popular snack throughout the Maldives. Serve with a side of mint chutney for a delicious and satisfying treat.
Whether you're a vegetarian or simply looking to try something new, the dishes at Oblu Select at Sangeli Maldives are sure to impress.
