About the resort
Oblu Select at Sangeli is a premium all-inclusive resort on the edge of the Maldives' North Male Atoll. It's lucky enough to feature an outer reef as well as a lagoon reef, so there's an abundance of marine life and plenty of fantastic diving and snorkelling opportunities nearby. It's a modern, design-led hotel, with a really fresh feel (it opened late 2018). It's a classy place with an element of fun - one of the beaches even has a spiral slide into the sea.
Oblu Select at Sangeli is made up of the main Sangeli island, as well as a smaller adults-only island, One Banyan. The two islands are linked by a string of overwater villas. The adults-only island is where you'll find the Elena spa, as well as the adults-only infinity pool and fitness centre. Designed for couples and families alike, you can choose to stay in either overwater villas or beach villa at Oblu Select at Sangeli. The deluxe versions come with private pools. The overwater villas are encircled by a coral reef; slip into the waters from your private deck and you're likely to be greeted by turtles, rays and all sorts of colourful fish.
The creme-de-la-creme of accommodation comes in the form of the 6 honeymoon suites on Banyan Island. These feature luxurious round beds and fancy mirrored skylights over stylish bright-red bathtubs.
To get here, it's a 50-minute speedboat ride from Male.

HOTEL
experiences
There are two pools at this resort, one dedicated for adults-only. The ELENA Spa offers specially crafted therapies and massages, a yoga pavilion and steam room facilities. A fully furnished gym is available for use and offers regular classes through out the week. The kid's club will keep everyone happy with a daily menu of fun and engaging activities.
Make a splash during your stay with a range of complimentary water sports activities. Paddle board across the beautiful crystal-clear waters or snorkel underneath them to see the array of brightly coloured fish.  SSI and PADI courses are available at the island's watersports and dive centre. End your day with a complimentary sunset fishing trip and sit back to some live music when you return.
