DESCRIPTION
Two Islands, One Chic Escape
Discover a unique two-island resort where vibrant luxury meets barefoot bliss. Linked by a water villa jetty or a boat shuttle, OBLU SELECT Sangeli offers the best of both worlds.
Set in the north-western tip of Malé Atoll, this five-star deluxe resort by Atmosphere Hotels & Resorts blends affordable luxury with laid-back island charm. Stay in tropical villas and suites, dine at eclectic restaurants, and explore some of the best snorkelling and diving spots in the Maldives. Whether it’s a romantic getaway or a fun family holiday, Sangeli promises a carefree, unforgettable escape.

Dining
The Courtyard
The vibrant heart of Sangeli Island, The Courtyard offers all-day dining with freshly prepared local snacks, breakfast, lunch, and dinner. Enjoy a daily changing international buffet, traditional Maldivian dishes, and à-la-carte options. Live cooking stations add excitement, while the Indian Ocean Gala Dinner every Friday features local delicacies and live performances.
Just Grill
An elegant lagoon-facing restaurant specializing in fresh seafood and meat grills. Enjoy tender steaks, juicy reef fish, and a variety of gourmet dishes. Complete your meal with indulgent desserts in a refined setting.
The Sangs
Located by the main pool with stunning ocean views, The Sangs blends Maldivian charm with modern flair. Unwind with expertly crafted cocktails, fine wines, chilled beers, and a variety of light bites in a vibrant, laid-back setting.
The Rock
A sunset bar built on stilts over the shallow lagoon at the western tip of Sangeli Island. Relax on comfy bean bags or a traditional Maldivian swing as you enjoy ocean breezes and panoramic sunset views. The perfect spot for casual drinks and stunning scenery.
JustWok
A contemporary Pan-Asian restaurant offering vibrant, family-style platters packed with exotic flavours. Enjoy wok-tossed delicacies, aromatic soups, handmade dim sums, and daily chef’s specials. Dine al fresco by the beach for a relaxed and flavourful experience.
One Banyan Island Pool Bar
A serene adults-only escape from sunrise to sunset. Sip on refreshing wines, cocktails, and mocktails by the pool, surrounded by swaying palms and ocean views. Enjoy light lunches like salads, pasta, sandwiches, and burgers, finished with ice cream or sorbet for the perfect poolside indulgence.
Dine by Design
Enjoy a private, romantic dining experience with a curated menu and candlelight setup at the island’s most scenic spots. A perfect way to celebrate special moments in style.

Highlights From The Resort
Underwater Adventures
Explore coral reefs with SSI and PADI-certified diving, boat dives, and magical night dives led by expert instructors.
Water Sports
Enjoy complimentary snorkelling gear and non-motorised water sports like kayaking or paddleboarding. Jet skis and private water adventures are also available.
Wellness & Spa
De-stress with unique Maldivian treatments like sand bundle massages, sunburn therapies, and coconut-infused rituals at ELENA Spa.
Fitness & Recreation
Stay active with beach yoga, Pilates, and group classes. The ocean-facing gym and recreation centre are open to all guests.
Family-Friendly Fun
A colourful Kids’ Club with a pool, playground, trampoline, and fun daily activities for children aged 4 and above.
Dining & Drinks
Savour global cuisine at The Courtyard buffet, indulge in grilled seafood at Just Grill, explore Pan-Asian flavours at JustWok, and sip cocktails with sunset views at The Rock.
Evening Entertainment
Live music five nights a week, Bodu Beru on Fridays, seasonal events, and themed dinners keep evenings lively.
Ocean Excursions
Experience sunset fishing, dolphin watching, or a moonlight cruise. Private charters also available for a personalised sea journey.
Romantic Experiences
Celebrate love with beach vow renewals, candlelit dinners, floating breakfasts, and dreamy photoshoots framed by Maldivian sunsets.
