DESCRIPTION
Island of Love
Just 15 minutes from Malé International Airport, OBLU SELECT Lobigili is a contemporary 5-star retreat exclusively for adults. In Dhivehi, ‘Loabi’ means love and ‘Gili’ means island. Lobigili truly is the island of love.
Romance permeates the air here. Idyllic tropical vistas and nature-inspired design create a private, pristine getaway. Set along a white sandy beach and turquoise lagoon, the resort features 68 stylish villas and a vibrant mix of experiences. Enjoy snorkelling at the marine-rich house reef, dining beneath the ocean at Only BLU, and unwinding in the serene garden spa. Let the island rhythm soothe your soul while our warm island hostesses help you create lasting memories together.

SunNest Water Pool Villa
Surrounded by the serene Indian Ocean, this elegant one-bedroom overwater villa is a dream escape for couples. Sun-drenched interiors feature custom-made furnishings, warm wooden floors, and a luxurious bathroom with a deep-soak tub and private overwater hammock. Dining
Ylang Ylang – All-Day Dining
Ylang Ylang blends romantic charm with global flavours, offering vibrant world cuisine, live cooking stations, and stunning ocean views. Enjoy breakfast buffets with made-to-order eggs, waffles, fresh bakes, tropical fruits, and local specialties.
For lunch and dinner, savour artisanal pizzas, tandoor delights, and international dishes from chalkboard menus and themed set menus. Dine indoors, in intimate pods, or outdoors with your feet in the sand. A cosy library zone adds a unique touch to this relaxed yet chic dining experience.
The Swing Bar
Overlooking a brilliant turquoise lagoon, The Swing Bar is the perfect spot for beachside relaxation. Lounge on stylish swings, hammocks, or in cozy cabanas, drink in hand and sea breeze on your skin.
Cool off in the infinity pool with its sunken bar serving icy slushes, frappes, herbal iced teas, and handcrafted cocktails. In the evening, enjoy crispy Maldivian hedhika snacks and live Duo band performances that bring the night to life.
Only BLU – Under Ocean Restaurant
Dine 6.8 meters below sea level at the Maldives’ largest underwater restaurant, where vibrant marine life glides past your table. Surrounded by hypnotic blue hues, savour modern gourmet cuisine with curated menus featuring seafood, meats, or vegetarian creations, perfectly paired with fine wines.
Begin with a glass of sparkling wine and canapés, then enjoy artfully presented courses served in elegant rantangs. With discreet service and panoramic views of the ocean, every moment at Only BLU is unforgettable.
Now included with the Lobi Plan.
Gaadiya 17
Tucked beneath swaying palms at the northern tip of the island, Gaadiya 17 brings a fun, flavour-packed twist to traditional food truck dining.
Enjoy a laid-back, open-air dinner with the chef’s selection of appetisers followed by grilled favourites like fish, octopus, tiger prawns, Angus striploin, Indonesian chicken, and exotic vegetables. A creamy coconut dessert rounds out the experience, perfectly paired with fine wines and refreshing drinks.
Dine barefoot on the beach, under starry skies, with mellow tunes and the ocean breeze setting the mood.
