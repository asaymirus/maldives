DESCRIPTION
A private island sanctuary where nature and elegance live in harmony.
Emerald Maldives Resort & Spa, a proud member of The Leading Hotels of the World, is a 5-star Deluxe All-Inclusive retreat set in the stunning Raa Atoll. Spread across a 20-hectare private island surrounded by 1.5 km of white sandy beach, the resort seamlessly blends natural beauty with contemporary sophistication. Its 120 villas, equally divided between beach and overwater, are designed with privacy, style, and comfort in mind. Highlights include modern bathrooms with walk-in and outdoor “under the stars” showers, as well as spacious bathtubs for two, creating spaces that invite both relaxation and indulgence. Thoughtful details such as private terraces, daily refreshed minibars, and twice-daily service add to the sense of effortless luxury. With its unique balance of tropical charm and refined elegance, Emerald Maldives offers an atmosphere of natural serenity, where every moment becomes a lasting memory.

Dining
Beach Club Grill Restaurant
Set on the main beach overlooking the turquoise lagoon, the Beach Club Grill offers a premium dining experience for lunch and dinner. Guests can enjoy a wide selection of fresh fish, seafood, meat, and vegetables, all expertly prepared on a stone grill to enhance the natural flavors. The restaurant also features a pizza and pasta station, while the Beach Pool Bar provides refreshing beverages, completing a luxurious all-inclusive offering in one of the resort’s most scenic locations.
Amazònico Restaurant
Experience the vibrant flavors of Central and South America at Amazònico, where all dishes are prepared in an open kitchen for guests to witness the culinary artistry. Drawing inspiration from Brazil, Mexico, Argentina, Peru, and Chile, the menu features highlights such as quesadillas, beef ribs, enchiladas, and the signature Brazilian beef served from skewers. Using only the freshest ingredients, Amazònico offers an unforgettable dining journey and a true oasis of bold and authentic flavors in a lively and inviting setting.
Le Asiatique
Nestled in the heart of the resort within an authentic tropical forest, Le Asiatique invites guests to explore the rich and diverse flavors of Asia. The restaurant showcases the culinary creations of skilled Asian chefs, prepared with respect for traditional recipes and techniques. Guests can enjoy a variety of dishes from across the East, with the Teppanyaki grill offering live cooking shows that add both entertainment and freshness to the dining experience. Le Asiatique provides an immersive journey into Oriental cuisine in a serene and enchanting setting.
Aqua Restaurant
Set directly on the beach with views of the clear turquoise ocean, Aqua Restaurant offers both air-conditioned indoor and outdoor dining, accompanied by a fully equipped main bar. Guests can enjoy a lavish buffet for breakfast, lunch, and dinner, featuring multiple live cooking stations that showcase international cuisines. From Mediterranean and Italian to Japanese, Chinese, and vegetarian options, every station is designed to delight the palate and satisfy even the most discerning gourmet. Aqua provides a vibrant and immersive dining experience in a stunning beachfront setting.
Sunset Pool Cafè
Perched in one of the most stunning areas of the island, the Sunset Pool Café is the perfect spot to enjoy the romance of the sunset. Guests can sip refreshing drinks, listen to music with friends, or play a game of billiards right on the white sand beach, all while soaking in the serene and picturesque surroundings.
Sunrise Café
Perfect for early risers, the Sunrise Café offers a serene setting to begin the day while watching the Maldivian sunrise over the crystal-clear turquoise lagoon. Guests can enjoy a peaceful morning with coffee or light refreshments as the gentle light illuminates the tranquil surroundings.

Highlights From The Resort
The Emerald Spa
Excellence, luxury, and relaxation are at the heart of The Emerald Spa. Enjoy a full range of tailored treatments from Thai, Balinese, and Indonesian therapists in nine air-conditioned treatment rooms. The spa also features an Indonesian-style pool, jacuzzi, sauna, and Turkish baths for total rejuvenation.
Fitness Centre
Stay active at the air-conditioned fitness centre equipped with Technogym machines and personal trainers available for tailored sessions.
Diving Centre
Explore the Maldives’ stunning coral reefs and over 1,000 fish species. The diving centre offers PADI-certified courses and guided snorkeling experiences for all skill levels.
Sports Centre
Keep active with 2 tennis courts (one convertible to a soccer field), 1 paddle court, and a 5-a-side football field, with professional guidance available for personalized training.
Water Sports Centre
Located on the main beach, guests can enjoy Hobie-cats, kayaks, windsurfing, and motorized water sports such as jet skis under the supervision of the watersports team.
Dolphin Kids Club
Designed for children aged 3 to 14, the 1,500 sqm kids’ club offers indoor and outdoor activities, a kids’ pool, and a dedicated restaurant, ensuring children are entertained while parents relax.
