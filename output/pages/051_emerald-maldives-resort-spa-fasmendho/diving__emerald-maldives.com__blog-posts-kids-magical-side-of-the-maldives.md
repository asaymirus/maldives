Kids magical side of the Maldives
If you are planning your next holidays and you would like to escape to a tropical and remote destination without leaving your beloved ones, you need to discover the resort facilities!
Emerald Maldives Resort & Spa is the perfect destination for couples in search of romantic moments, but it is also a
family-friend place
for those who want to spend some good time together in complete privacy and relaxing atmosphere.
The new
“Disneyland” of the Maldives - The Dolphin Kids Club
a 1500 sqm area dedicated to our little guests, aging from 3 to 12 years, with large outdoor playground, and a wide selection of indoor and outdoor activities! Complementary and part of the Deluxe All Inclusive formula, the Dolphin Kids Club has its own
pool
and its
restaurant
in order to create a playful atmosphere, exclusively for your beloved ones!
The Dolphin Kids Club, with highly qualified staff, is open from 9 am to 8 pm allowing parents to fully enjoy their time at the Resort.
This playful corner is the perfect place for every kid, thanks to the many activities as well as a dedicated
PlayStation corner
, movies and crafts!
Face painting, beach fun games, cupcake decoration, Dolphin cinema, crab hunting, singing competition, coconut painting, pool splash, pizza making, croissants baking and many other activities are waiting to welcome your little ones and let them live one of their best memorable experience ever!
The Dolphin Kids Club, nestled in the heart of the island’s tropical ambience, is the perfect place to get ready for some playful barefoot games!
