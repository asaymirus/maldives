How to Get Here
A scenic seaplane journey from the Malé airport will cover the distance in 40 minutes highlighting the beauty of the islands and the turquoise sea of the Maldives. Alternatively, a 30-minute domestic flight to the Ifuru Domestic Airport (particularly convenient to easily connect with the Malé airport in case of evening’s international flights) is also available and a 30 minutes speedboat ride will complete the journey to reach the Emerald Maldives Resort.
For arrival, same-day seaplane transfers can be arranged for international flights landing at the airport on or before 03:30 PM (Malé Time). For departure, same-day seaplane transfers can be arranged for international flights departing from the airport on or after 09:00 AM (Malé Time).
A scenic seaplane journey from Malé will cover the distance in 40 minutes or alternatively you can choose a 30-minute domestic flight to Ifuru Domestic Airport where guests will enjoy a 15 minutes speedboat ride to the Emerald Maldives Resort.
