Love Paradise Destination
There’s nothing more special than celebrating your honeymoon and wedding with the person you love.
EmeraldMaldives Resort & Spa is an extraordinary location to treasure memories and to strengthen the love between two hearts. Whether it’s a wedding or a honeymoon, the Maldives is the ideal idyllic destination for some one to onetime with your better half. Guests can live their dream and make their desires into reality by organizing a wedding to remember on the beach, during the day or at night where the sun will set to create that perfect fairytale moment, and where the vows will be said as well as the “I do’s.” If its not a wedding, it might be a honeymoon, where guests can enjoy that same sunset while sipping some wine or by participating at the resorts wine tasting on the beach.
With Emerald Maldives Resort & Spa’s All-inclusive formula, the best way to benefit from a carefree holiday is to not think about extra costs, as everything will be included. There will be other included surprises awaiting, for guests that will take advantage of the wedding and/or honeymoon package.
Moreover, the Deluxe All-inclusive formula covers exquisite courses over the resort’s four restaurants, where guests will be able to dine a few steps from the Indian Ocean. In addition, couples can choose some of the services that are not included such as, a romantic dinner on the beach, a lunch on a desert island, a movie under the stars or, the Emerald Spa which offers 10 private treatment rooms for couples that will favor guests maximum relaxation.
On request, a photographer can also be hired to take photos to remember forever. The framing of love, of affection, the screenshot of a moment in eternity, one can look at infinite times. The go to occasion to have a special photo shoot on the sand and flower assemblages.
Lastly, the PADI Diving Center will also have excursions available to adda top-notch moment to the stay. Guests should not miss the incredible opportunity of booking the turtle island snorkeling or, the lucky dolphins cruise where they will be able to travel alongside dolphins and crossing their paths while on the Dhoni. The sunset cruise is another extraordinary excursion guests can take part in; enjoying a very suggestive landscape with your better half is truly unforgettable.
Celebrate your next honeymoon or wedding in the Maldives, in the midst of nature and a tropical paradise, with blissful sunshine, magical sunsets and romantic moments!
