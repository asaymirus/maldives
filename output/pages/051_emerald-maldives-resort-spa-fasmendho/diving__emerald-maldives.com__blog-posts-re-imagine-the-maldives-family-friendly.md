Re-imagine The Maldives Family Friendly
Travelling now comes with a twist; now more than ever it is important to travel to a secure place in which safety measures have been implemented to assure the best and safest stay.
At Emerald Maldives Resort & Spa, guests will not only be safe but, it will seem as if nothing ever changed! Situated on one of the biggest islands in the Maldives, the Resort is characterized by 1,5 km of soft white sand and, 100-hectares of blue lagoon. Given the size of the Resort and the wide spaces, guests will feel like they live in their own private island and social distancing will come naturally.
Furthermore, at Emerald Maldives Resort &Spa, clients can benefit from many activities that will enhance guests experience with a full immersion in the Maldivian culture.
‍
From the four restaurants that serve a range of diverse cuisines, offering international gustatory experiences, to in-room dining service tailored for all your Family or, by directly enjoying a family dinner on the beach, with some of Chef Aldo’s specialties that will astound even the youngest of guests and captivate all food lovers. Moreover, family open-air cooking classes with Chef Aldo are also available.
When the sun starts to set, the Beach Club is the perfect place to enjoy an aperitif a few meters from the sea with the whole family. While parents can savour alcoholic cocktails, kids can relish delicious tropical fruit juices or smoothies that are all included in the Deluxe All-inclusive formula. The perfect way to relax and for boosting up kids’ immune system and energy levels, all while listening to live music and the sound of the waves.
Also, when travelling with the family is important to choose a place that cares of kids happiness. In addition, the Resort has one of the biggest outdoor playground areas in the Maldives at the ‘Dolphin’Kids Club, in which creativity is the key of every activities. Everything is disinfected before and after use allowing parents to feel safe about leaving their kids for a while and move on to the Spa. With the finest cleaning procedures and maximum privacy and relaxation, at the Emerald Spa guests can enjoy a massage in one of the 10 air-conditioned couple rooms. Water Sports activities are also available as well as playing tennis, paddle and soccer on the courts present at the Sports Center.
For the more curious, the Marine Biologist is also available for talks about marine species and biodiversity of the Maldivian ecosystem as well as the PADI certified diving center, that offers snorkelling as one of the main activities allowing even the youngest ones to discover what the Indian Ocean has to offer.
Families can therefore fill their days with joy and laughter, sun and swims in the safest way possible, feeling what natural freedom is all about.
