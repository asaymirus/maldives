Deluxe All-Inclusive Sanctuary
Inspired by a unique contemporary Deluxe All-Inclusive concept, the Resort is ideal for both couples in search of intimacy and romance and families seeking a more playful experience, offering a full range of activities and emotions for both the more and less active travelers worldwide.
The Resort is also a real laboratory and a point of reference for flavors and taste, featuring the international cuisine of the Aqua Restaurant, the Beach Club Grill and its Mediterranean cuisine and two other outlets offering respectively Latin American and Asian cuisine. Natural ingredients are treated with a superior level of creativity in all restaurants, to offer guests a deluxe all-inclusive formula in all outlets.
