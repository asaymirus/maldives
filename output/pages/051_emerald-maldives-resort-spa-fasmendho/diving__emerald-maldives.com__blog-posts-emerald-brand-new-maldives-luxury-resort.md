Emerald: The brand new Maldives Luxury Resort
Emerald Maldives Resort & Spa is the brand new 5 star luxury resort in the Maldives, member of the prestigious chain Leading Hotels of the World which includes amongst other countries, the best Maldives resorts in 2019.
Amongst the 1,200 islands of the Maldivian Archipelago, the Resort is situated north, precisely on the Raa atoll.
The Resort extends over a luxurious private island of 20 hectares, surrounded by 100 hectares of a splendid crystal blue lagoon, where it is possible to admire the extraordinary landscapes of the Maldivian lagoon. Emerald Maldives Resort & Spa boasts 120 villas, half of which are built directly on the water. The structure of the Resort was built with natural materials such as stones, "Langhi Langhi" leaves, bamboo and wood, in order to maintain the connection with the surrounding environment, with a constant focus on safeguarding the surrounding nature and territory.

Fun at Emerald Maldives Resort & Spa
If you are planning in which resort to stay in the Maldives, the Indian Ocean’s jewels where you can have the possibility of finding different offers, ranging from guest houses, resorts, residences, and hotels; but if you are looking for a chic stay, where you can take advantage of a wide range of services you can take advantage of the Special Winter Offer to book your stay in one of the brand new 5-star luxury resorts in the Maldives: Emerald Maldives Resort & Spa.
Amongst one of the best hotels in the Maldives, this Resort offers various services designed to satisfy any request. For example, the Water Sports Center, which ensures multiple opportunities for fun, not only for water sports enthusiasts but also for those who would like to engage in sports experiences with motorized equipment.
There is also the Diving Center, where you can find PADI certified professionals, who will accompany you to discover the wonders of Maldivian underwater life. In particular, you can take advantage of the period between December and March, to enjoy the excellent visibility of the water, admiring the seabed inhabited by colorful corals, tropical fish, manta rays, sea turtles and sharks.
This is not all!
Those who care about their physical well-being and being in shape, can take advantage of the dedicated sports center, taking advantage of the gym, fully equipped with Technogym equipment! All outdoor sports lovers will also be able to take advantage of the outdoor courts, taking advantage of the opportunity to be in one of the Maldivian resorts that provide tennis courts.
To complete this paradisiacal 5-star luxury Maldivian picture, Emerald Maldives Resort & Spa offers a large wellness center, where excellence and luxury meet, offering customers a professional service in total respect of guests' privacy. The Resort also boasts an area dedicated to children, the largest in the Maldives, where professional instructors will dedicate themselves to young guests with a wide variety of activities.
