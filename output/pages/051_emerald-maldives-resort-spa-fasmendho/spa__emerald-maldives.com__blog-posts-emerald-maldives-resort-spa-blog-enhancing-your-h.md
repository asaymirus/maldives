Enhancing Your Holiday At The Emerald Spa
Holidays at Emerald Maldives Resort & Spa are quality ones. Guests will have the possibility to experience many activities from the PADI Diving Center, the Gym & Sports Center and Water Sports Center. However, after a day performing more concentrated activities, relaxing moments at The Emerald Spa are a must.
At the Emerald Spa, excellence, luxury and satisfaction are our main priority, from the numerous treatments inspired by Thai and Indonesian techniques, the relaxation area, the 10 air-conditioned treatments rooms and a featured beauty salon. An oasis of bliss created in the midst of Fasmendhoo Island, filled with nature and conferring a calm and Zen look ambience.
It is important to keep in mind the scope of your visit to the Spa, the type of treatment you want to experience and for what reason. Is it just for an overall sense of relaxation or is it to ease off muscle tension after an intense gym session? Is it to rejuvenate your beauty and tone up or to alleviate stress?
Some massages or treatments you might want to look at vary from Aromatherapy, Shiatsu (a Thai traditional massage),Abhyanga, Shirodhara, different scrubs, to various Hair Wraps or facial treatments for the maximisation of personal care.
While in Aromatherapy, the massage therapist will use aromatherapy oils in juxtaposition with gentle movements to remove stress, Shiatsu does not involve the use of oils but in contrast, is based on a medium to strong pressure on specific body points. In Abhyanga, the process consists of medium and gentle strokes in the downwards direction, giving longterm beneficial effects such as, good appetite, more alertness during the day and a better quality of sleep. In Shirodhara, medication is dripped slowly and steadily on the forehead to induce a released stress awareness that results in a dynamic Psychosomatic balance in combination with Abhyanga massage movements.
In addition, the scrub will encourage the regeneration of new cells, and the wraps will sooth, nourish both hair and body. Lastly, depending on which facial treatment, fine lines and wrinkles can be treated with the age-defying benefits of marine charged Padina Pavonica and coral reef, which confer the skin a radiant and sooth look.
The outstanding standards will ensure that the massage therapists and practitioners regularly train on the most effective and latest techniques to perform a top-notch service.
Awaiting of welcoming you at Emerald Maldives and get a glowing skin at our Spa, here below a homemade natural Avocado Hair Wrap for your needs:
Avocado is very famous for its antioxidant properties. Apart from using it in salads and juices, it can also be utilised as natural hair wrap to nourish and prevent dry scalp hair.
Preparation:
1      pcs of fresh avocado
2      15 ml coconut oil/olives oil
Smash the avocado on an empty bowl, pour the coconut oil over and stir all the ingredients until they are well combined. Apply the mixed ingredients into the scalp (cover all the hair) and do a little massage. Wrap your hair with a wet and warm towel and leave it for 10 minutes. Then, wash the hair and let it dry naturally.
