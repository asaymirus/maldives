The best time to visit the Maldives
The Maldives islands are hot and sunny all year long, with average temperatures of 23ºC/31ºC, making it a perfect year-round destination, for some fun in the sun. There are, however, better times of the year to travel to the Maldives.
Let’s check them out!
The characteristic of the Maldivian seasons is the subdivision of the dry and wet weather period; the latter, determined by intense rain and considerable wind, runs from August to November, while the dry period, runs from December to April.
From the end of November until April, the days are mostly sunny, and the water will start to be clearer due to less plankton, this will improve the conditions for good scuba-diving and snorkeling. Furthermore, if you are passionate about surfing, you can discover the Maldives in March 2020, taking advantage of the beginning of the surfing season! However, if you like diving, snorkeling or just relaxing on the beach you have to opportunity to choose the month which suits you best, to live your experience in the paradise corner of the Indian Ocean!
For the next winter holidays, the good weather, being it equatorial, allows you to fully enjoy a wealth of activities for all tastes, ranging from snorkeling and scuba-diving to swimming and sun-worshipping, allowing you to enjoy your holiday in the pure splendor of its breathtaking views. This winter season will also give you the chance to enjoy the blue lagoon, without winds or rainfall.
