5 good reasons to spend your holidays in the Maldives
If you have dreamt of an exotic escape for a long time, your next holidays are the ideal period to catch a wonderful opportunity!
Imagine being in a tropical wonderland with endless soft sand, peachy sunsets and thousand palms… We are talking about Maldives! One of the must visit destination that will not disappoint you.
However, if you need more motivation, below 5 good reasons of why Emerald Maldives Resort & Spa should be your next getaway!
‍
1.	Barefoot luxurious experience
At Emerald Maldives Resort & Spa you might
walk barefoot around the lagoon
while admiring the beauties of the natural Maldivian ecosystem, and watching
outstanding sunsets
! It is a worthwhile experience to share with someone special or with the whole family, especially, if you want a break from the chaotic daily life routine. At Emerald Maldives Resort & Spa, we offer the ideal formula for your stay, where the only thing to do is to simply unwind and relax! In a hidden jewel of the Indian Ocean!
‍
2.	Relish in savoring delightful recipes
At Emerald Maldives Resort & Spa you can expect to eat
delicious food
: from the Oriental flavors to the Mediterranean ones, but also passing through the
South-American traditional dishes
, the Resort is considered a
unique dining experiences
thanks to the Deluxe All Inclusive formula over
4 concept restaurants
, that make you the protagonist of a dream like vacation! If you are travelling with your beloved, you may experience a
romantic beach dinner
for two on the beach!
‍

‍
Manta rays
,
baby sharks
,
giant turtles
,
dolphins

kayaks
,
jet skiing

4.	Long lasting memories
If you want to try grand and lush accommodations, at Emerald Maldives Resort & Spa you can enjoy your relaxing holidays
waking up

Sustainability plays a central role
at Emerald Maldives Resort & Spa, which has been realized in harmony with the island's nature. The
