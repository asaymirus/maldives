A Family Friendly Destination
Considered the kids magical side of the Maldives, Emerald Maldives Resort & Spa, offers an enchanting setting for families to experience pleasant moments on vacation. The Resort boasts 120 villas, all built to blend harmoniously with the tropical surroundings and local environment, with carefully studied landscaping and crafted materials such as bamboo, natural stones and Langhi Langhi leaves.
Despite all of the Villas can accommodate at least two adults and two kids, the Resort has had a high demand in Family Beach Villas with Pool since its opening in 2019 and is now considered the families’ favourite Maldivian resort with over 50% of families booking. Moreover, in the upcoming year, Emerald Maldives decided to duplicate and expand 10 of its Beach Villas into Family BeachVillas with Pool, that will always be designed with a contemporary-style and tropical architecture, offering the perfect go to oasis of bliss for kids and parents.
This is just the beginning of many initiatives that Emerald Maldives Resort & Spa is launching for its little guests. In fact, starting fromJuly, The Emerald Spa is expanding its treatments menu with a range of dedicated massages for the youngest ones, as a way to incentivize them to take care of themselves and to learn to put aside their restlessness. Last but not least, they will also have the possibility of learning how to cook Italian dishes such as Spaghetti al Pomodoro and Gelato, with Chef Aldo Cadau, the Resort’s Italian Head Chef. Also, the Dolphin Kids Club will offer the occasion of attending yoga classes where the little ones will master and increase their flexibility and coordination under the expert guide of professionals. The perfect way to encourage mindfulness by letting them reconnect with nature and build strength.
