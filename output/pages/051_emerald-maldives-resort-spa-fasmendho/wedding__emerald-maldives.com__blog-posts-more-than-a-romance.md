More Than a Romance
The top romantic things you can do at Emerald Maldives
Emerald Maldives Resort & Spa is the go-to place for spending time with the people you love, from honeymoons with many features included, such as a complimentary villa upgrade upon arrival and a 60 min couple massage at the Emerald Spa, to the ideal venue for a tropical and unique wedding on the beach with a private photoshoot, live musicians for the event and a private sunset cruise after the ceremony, crafting every detail to perfection in order for it to be a magical celebration that will be remembered for a lifetime.
Moreover, apart from the Honeymoon experience and Wedding Celebration, Emerald Maldives Resort & Spa is the ideal destination for all couples that want to have some privacy and live into a romance.
5 top romantic things you can do at Emerald Maldives:
1.    TheBeach Club is the ultimate location to have a romantic sunset dinner directly on the beach, a multi sensory experience one can hardly forget. Watching the rose gold sky at golden hour while indulging on Chef Aldo’s signature dishes and hearing the sound of the waves on the seashore is something truly magical.
2.    By involving your senses, sipping a cocktail or wine tasting while having an aperitif and listening to live music will definitely be a flawless experience.
3.    The PADI Diving Center is also full of resources as it has many activities that are perfect for unforgettable moments.  The resort can organise dolphin excursions at sunset time, in which, the sea, the view, the boat journey, everything combines perfectly to create long-lasting memories.
4.    Couples can also apply to butlers at the resort and ask for in-room service, a perfect way to relish a breakfast in the privacy of their own villas. From the sweet to the savoury, the union of flavours will definitely make this an event to remember. Also, the floating breakfast will be available from the comfort of guests’ private pools. The perfect way to start the day on a positive note with your beloved one.
5.    For those that wants to relax, couple massages are available in one of the 10 private treatment rooms designed for couples at the Emerald Spa. The ideal choice to feel immersed into a true oasis of bliss while abandoning your senses to a Thai and Indonesian inspired massage.
Emerald Maldives Resort & Spa is an unmissable destination for a love rush, from the stunning Island wide spaces to breathtaking views, privacy and wonderful, colourful sunsets, everything will become more romantic.
