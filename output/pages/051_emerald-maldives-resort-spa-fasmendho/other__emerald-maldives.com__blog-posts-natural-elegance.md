Natural Elegance
“We want to stimulate the creation of long-lasting memories for all of our guests by offering a surprising and original elegance, meeting and exceeding expectations at all times to make our guests experience what we call the “Natural Elegance” of the Maldives”.
And what about you? Are you ready to feel the “Natural Elegance” of Maldives?
At Emerald Maldives Resort & Spa, located in the Raa Atoll, you could have the holiday you have always dreamt of. Our special concept of “Natural Elegance” is tailored for people like us who love wandering around the amazing shades of natural surroundings of the Maldives.
This is the new 5-star
Deluxe All-Inclusive Resort
, member of
Leading Hotels of the World
, which extends over 1.5 km of white sandy beach on a private 20-hectares luxurious island.
The blue sky. Footsteps in the sand. The sound of the waves in your ears. The sun on your skin. The salt in your hair. We believe these four elements are the recipe for peace and relaxation at Emerald Maldives Resort & Spa.
The Resort was built to be in harmony with the surrounding environment, using materials such as bamboo and natural stones in order to focus on the environmental preservation and sustainability. The Resort combines tropical scenery with modern design, creating an atmosphere of natural and informal elegance, offering the ideal setting for guests to build new and memorable experiences.
Natural Elegance
for #foodlovers
The Emerald Maldives Resort & Spa offers a new take on All-Inclusive dining with 4 concept restaurants, ensuring an extraordinary culinary experience able to satisfy every guest’s desire.
Our guests are free to choose among Aqua Restaurant, which offers a lavish buffet with a variety of international cooking stations; the Beach Club Grill, with a unique combination of taste and elegance on the beach; Le Asiatique Restaurant, which is the perfect choice for those who wish to taste a delicious selection of Asian cuisine created by our Asian Chefs; and the Amazònico Restaurant that offers Central and South America traditional cuisine.
Natural Elegance
for #sportlovers
Sport lovers will be excited by the sport facilities available on the island!
The 5 star Diving Center offers the support of high-skilled qualified PADI instructors ready to assist you in the discovery of the stunning Maldivian coral reef.
The fully equipped Water Sports Center is available for all guests that want to feel the adrenaline around the lagoon. Whereas, those who want to keep in shape every time they wish, cannot miss a Technogym fully equipped Gym and Sport Center, in the midst of the island.
Let yourself be pampered by the #NaturalElegance of Emerald Maldives Resort & Spa!
