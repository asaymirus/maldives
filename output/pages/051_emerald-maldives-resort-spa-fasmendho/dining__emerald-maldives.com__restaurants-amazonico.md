Prepared in the open kitchen to allow guests to witness the cooking process, the dishes offered at the Amazònico are based on original recipes and ingredients from Central and South America.  With typical dishes from Brazilian to Mexican, Argentinian, Peruvian, and Chilean cuisine, guests can have a taste of quesadillas, beef ribs, enchiladas and Brazilian beef served from the skewers just to mention a few highlights of the menu.
