At the Maldives With Your Family
Have you ever been to Emerald Maldives Resort & Spa?
Emerald Maldives Resort & Spa is the perfect destination for your holidays especially when travelling with your family and loved ones. There are many facilities and opportunities for you to experience and explore at our brand new 5 star resort:
Emerald Dolphin Kids Club available kids from age 3-12 in which our high qualified staff will interact our little guests with a series of activities starting from the playground, the pool, the indoor game area as well as, sunny and salty outdoor activities!
The Deluxe All-inclusive formula will make your stay more pleasant through the resort’s 2 bars and the possibility to choose among 4 different restaurants:
‍
From an international lavish buffet with different cooking stations at the main Aqua Restaurant; a sea grill and Mediterranean cusine at the Beach Club Grill Restaurant, to something more particular such as the flavors of Asia at The Asiatique and, the Central and South American traditional recipes at the Amazònico.
Lastly, the fully equipped PADI Diving Center with certified trainers and instructors, are ready to show you the most beautiful places in Raa Atoll. The Maldives are surrounded by a paradisiac underwater life ranging from different fishes, to different corals, turtles, manta rays to many other different species. Snorkeling and diving would be two of the experiences that are best recommended but, the boat excursions, in which you can meet the dolphins, would be ideal for the whole family.
Come have a blast with your family and enjoy a 360° Emerald Maldives Resort & Spa experience!!
