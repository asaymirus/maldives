DESCRIPTION
A Tropical Paradise Within Easy Reach
A quick 15-minute complimentary speedboat ride from Velana International Airport transports you to crystal-clear waters, white-sand beaches, and lush tropical gardens. Spend your days diving, snorkelling, or enjoying water sports, then unwind by the pool or at the award-winning Shine Spa for Sheraton™, set on its own tranquil island.
Savor diverse cuisine at seven restaurants and bars, stay active at the gym and tennis court, or join family activities with the Side-by-Side Family Program. From romantic escapes to group celebrations at the stunning Jalsaa venue, every moment here is unforgettable.

Dining
Feast – Global Flavors in a Tropical Setting
Start your day with a vibrant breakfast of tropical fruits, fresh pastries, and live-cooking stations. In the evening, enjoy themed dinner buffets with international dishes, beachside ambience, and live entertainment. With modern tropical décor and a thatched roof, Feast offers a warm island atmosphere perfect for memorable dining.
Sea Salt – Romantic Seafood by the Lagoon
Dine al fresco by the beach with fresh seafood prepared to order at live cooking stations. Choose your catch from live tanks and savour signature dishes like Seafood Furanafushi, all in a serene, candlelit setting perfect for a romantic evening.
Baan Thai – Authentic Flavors from Thailand
Experience authentic Thai cuisine crafted by an all-Thai culinary team, blending fresh Maldivian seafood, island-grown produce, and premium ingredients flown in from Thailand. Enjoy a sleek, modern setting while savouring vibrant flavours and creative dishes that capture the essence of Thailand.
ChopstiX – Asian Comfort by the Pool
Enjoy handmade dumplings, noodles, and flavourful stir-fries in a relaxed poolside setting. Choose from breezy al fresco seating or a cool indoor space, perfect for a casual lunch or dinner.
Kakuni Hut – Beachside Mexican Flavors
Savor fresh Mexican dishes like fish tacos and homemade jalapeño chocolate ice cream, paired with chilled drinks and craft cocktails. Located on the white sands of the lagoon beach, it’s the perfect spot for a laid-back oceanfront lunch.
Anchorage – Sunset Views & Mediterranean Flavors
Set along Anchorage Beach, this inviting spot is perfect for a light lunch, romantic dinner, or refreshing sundowners. Enjoy live music, signature cocktails, and Mediterranean favourites from Greek to Italian, all with breathtaking North Malé Atoll sunset views.
Destination Dining – Unforgettable Moments
Savor unique experiences from a Sunrise Champagne Breakfast and tranquil Lagoon Lunch to private sandbank meals and romantic beach dinners. Enjoy themed evenings like Oriental Night or Bubbles Under the Stars or join a fun Cocktail Masterclass.
Masala Hut – Spices of India in Paradise
Enjoy North and South Indian specialties in a charming thatched-roof pavilion. From sizzling chicken tikka to fragrant curries, this al fresco venue offers a vibrant, family-style dining experience in a relaxed island setting.

Highlights From The Resort
Sheraton Adventure Club
A lively kids’ program for ages 4-12 featuring beach games, scavenger hunts, coconut bowling, and cultural activities including traditional Maldivian music, dance, and language lessons. Supervised snorkelling and crab races add to the excitement.
Spa and Wellness
Shine Spa for Sheraton™ offers signature treatments in private pavilions with sea views, lush gardens, and a yoga pavilion. It is the ideal place to relax and rejuvenate.
Water Sports and Recreation
Guests can enjoy snorkelling, diving, big game fishing, jet skiing, parasailing and many other activities. The resort also offers a fitness center, tennis courts, and pools.
Meetings and Events
Jalsaa is a versatile venue with over 5,000 square feet of space. It is perfect for intimate meetings, incentive groups, and weddings. Private island takeovers are available.
Coral Conservation and Family Programs
Guests can participate in coral planting and guided snorkelling tours led by marine biologists. The “Side by Side” program creates special family experiences for all ages to connect and enjoy.
