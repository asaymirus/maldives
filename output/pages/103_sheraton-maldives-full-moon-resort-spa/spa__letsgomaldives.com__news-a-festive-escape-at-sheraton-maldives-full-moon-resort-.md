Set among tropical gardens and soft white sands, Sheraton Maldives Full Moon Resort & Spa has crafted a holiday season where celebration meets serenity. With its Festive Early Bird package, the resort turns the year’s most magical time into an island escape filled with flavors, family, and moments that linger long after the journey ends.
A holiday made effortless
Running from December 20, 2025, to January 10, 2026, the Festive Early Bird package is designed to take the stress out of planning, leaving more space for joy. Families can enjoy the warmth of togetherness with children up to 11 staying and dining free when sharing with parents-a thoughtful touch that makes the season even more rewarding.
Each day begins with a vibrant breakfast at Feast, where freshly baked pastries, juices, and aromatic coffees set the tone. Lunch invites exploration across four restaurants, from Mediterranean favorites to Indian, Mexican, and Asian-inspired dishes. Evenings bring a choice of a lively buffet or a curated three-course dinner at selected venues. Guests seeking something extra special can use dining credits to savor Thai flavors at Baan Thai or indulge in ocean-to-table dining at Sea Salt.
More than just dining
Arriving is simple, with complimentary speedboat transfers from Velana International Airport. Once on the island, the days unfold at your own pace-paddle boarding, kayaking, snorkeling, or a friendly match on the tennis court. For quiet moments, Shine Spa for Sheraton offers soothing treatments, with festive savings adding to the appeal.
The spirit of the season
What makes the experience memorable are the little details—the thoughtful amenities, the balance of play and relaxation, and the way each day feels like its own celebration. Between turquoise waters, golden sunsets, and the laughter of loved ones, the festive season here is more than a holiday-it’s a memory in the making.
Reserve your festive escape
Bookings for the Festive Early Bird package are open until October 15, 2025. If your holiday dream is a blend of Maldivian beauty and festive cheer, Sheraton Maldives Full Moon Resort & Spa is ready to welcome you.
