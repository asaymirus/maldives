DESCRIPTION
‘Seclusion and five-star luxury go hand-in-hand at the pristine Maldives island of Amari Havodda Maldives’
For travellers who want to get away from it all but not compromise on luxury, Amari Havodda Maldives resort is a perfect choice. This remote tropical island is far from any metropolis, yet here you can discover a world of style and comfort. The sumptuous Maldives villas are complimented by excellent service and international dining options including the Japanese restaurant, Ember Robatayaki grill. Soothe your body, mind and soul at the stunning Breeze Spa, cocooned by exotic plants and flowers. Explore the tropical marine life of the vibrant reefs on a scuba diving excursion, or even hire snorkel gear and browse the coral gardens just metres from your villa.

55 minutes by domestic flight, then 10-minute speedboat transfer

Dining
Indulge in unique destination dining experiences during your Maldives holiday with the finest selection of Asian and international cuisine at Amari Havodda Maldives. Tantalise your palate at any of the five distinctive restaurants and bars, each with enchanting vistas of the turquoise lagoon.
Amaya Food Gallery
From Thai delights to tapas and other international favourites, the signature all-day dining pavilion presents food from around the world in a market-style venue, guaranteed to tantalise your tastebuds.
Ember Grill
Set on the northern shore of the island, this Maldives restaurant celebrates the simple elegance of grilled cuisine. From tender meats to succulent seafood, the chef and team take pride in providing a relaxing and intimate dining experience for all. The al-fresco tables are the ideal setting for a romantic dinner under the stars.
Thari Bar
Relax poolside during your Maldives vacation with a chilled beverage while enjoying the gentle breeze and views over the ocean. Cool off at the sunken wet lounge area or lie back on one of the welcoming outdoor cabanas.
Iru Bar
A superb spot to sit and chill, Iru Bar offers a laidback beach bar vibe where you can spend many a pleasant evening as you watch as the stars come out to play.
Pizza Kaage
Relaxed yet lively, explore the Mediterranean-inspired pizzeria set upon this beautiful Maldives island resort. Enjoy views of the pristine beach as you feast upon a selection of freshly baked pizzas.
