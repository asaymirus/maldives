What would you name a star?
Jun 10, 2017
Milaidhoo Moments
If you could name a star, what would you call it? Something romantic, perhaps? Something that means something special to you and your partner? Maybe you’d like to name your star after someone close to you?
At Milaidhoo Maldives, our guests on honyemoon can name a star as part of their honeymoon package. Out of curiosity we’ve taken a look at some of the names that they’ve given their stars so far and the results are quite . . . unexpected:
Fat Ming L’Amour
Moraman
Smoo
Satellite
Lemone
ihyk
yorneer
buttercream
Want to see your star on this list? Then all you need do is book our honeymoon package and we’ll let you name a star. You’ll get a certificate of registration and the exact coordinates of your star.
