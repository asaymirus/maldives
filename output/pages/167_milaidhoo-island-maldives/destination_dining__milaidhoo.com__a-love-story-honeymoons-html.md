Honeymoons
It’s our wish to make your honeymoon a dream come true because at Milaidhoo, love is always in the air. As a Maldives honeymoon resort created for romance, we specialise in crafting moments as unique as your love story.
Honeymooners feel right at home here, especially when booking our ‘Perfect Honeymoon’ package. As part of this, we’ll name a star in the night sky after you, a timeless symbol of your love. You’ll receive a certificate and celestial coordinates, allowing you (and future generations) to find your star, uniquely named and forever yours.
Your honeymoon includes a complimentary 60-minute spa treatment for two, a romantic three-course candlelit dinner served on the deck of your villa, and a dreamy bath infused with local flower petals, champagne, and handcrafted chocolates.
And to welcome you in true Milaidhoo style, our chef will bake you a special signature cake, just for you.
Please note: From January 2021, these inclusions are available only for honeymooners who book our Honeymoon Package and stay a minimum of four nights. The ‘Perfect Honeymoon’ package is available exclusively to couples travelling within 12 months of their wedding date. A copy of the wedding certificate must be presented at check-in. Milaidhoo reserves the right to withhold benefits if eligibility criteria are not met.
If you’d like us to arrange something personal and meaningful for the two of you during your stay, simply contact us at
welcome@milaidhoo.com
and we’ll make it happen.
