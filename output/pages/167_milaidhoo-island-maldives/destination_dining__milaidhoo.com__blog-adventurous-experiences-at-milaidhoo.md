Adventurous experiences at Milaidhoo
Jul 24, 2021
Milaidhoo Moments
Maldives is not just a destination for romance and serenity but also for adventure, for learning something new and venturing out of your comfort zone.
Here are five ways to experience awesome adventure at Milaidhoo:
1. Water Sports
Start simple, and embark on overwater exploration on a Stand Up Paddleboard, in a Kayak or even Wind Surfing. At Milaidhoo, most non-motorised water sports are complimentary for the first hour.
2. Diving
The underwater world surrounding Milaidhoo truly is a never-ending story, with a new chapter beginning every day. It’s for first-time divers as well as for experienced ones who have seen dive spots all over the world. Diving at the Milaidhoo reef wall is famous and there are many nearby dive spots to discover.
3. Meet and greet the Gentle Giants
An awesome adventure is snorkelling with Manta Rays in Hanifaru Bay. Meet these gentle giants close up and witness a feeding frenzy below you while you float on the surface with your life vest and snorkel. The most rewarding sight you can imagine.
4. Deserted Island excursions
Ever wondered how it feels to be stranded on a deserted island? At Milaidhoo, we bring you as close to that experience as can be. Take a cruise by boat and have the crew drop you off at a deserted island in the lagoon close to the resort. Take a picnic and enjoy a few hours or full day of total seclusion, exploring the island or the pristine underwater world around you.
5. Fishing with local fishermen
The sea around the Maldives is not just beautiful but also a paradise for fishermen. At Milaidhoo, you can join the crew of a local fishing boat to learn and experience how the fishermen of the islands get each day’s catch.
