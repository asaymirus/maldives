The perfect ten: unmissable experiences as Milaidhoo enters its 10th year
Dec 11, 2025
A Love Story
From coral planting to a sunset dolphin cruise, here are the authentic Milaidhoo moments you need to discover as we celebrate our anniversary.
Imagine a place where time slows down, and every moment is crafted just for you. As our boutique island resort celebrates its ninth anniversary, we’re feeling reflective and full of excitement as we look ahead to our 10th year.
At Milaidhoo Maldives, we’ve perfected the art of slow living. This is our take on reinvented luxury – less about the grand displays and more about enjoying a seamless tapestry of highly personalised, authentic, out-of-this-world experiences that linger in your mind long after you’ve left our pristine shores. We are also proud to be a quiet, adults-focussed haven.
This is our home, and we want you to feel like you belong here, too. So, to celebrate our journey, we’re sharing the 10 unforgettable experiences our Milaidhoo Family just can’t stop talking about.
Kick off your shoes and dive in!
Connect to the ocean
At Milaidhoo, we embrace the ocean, protecting its natural wonders by actively helping to grow our house reef. Here are three incredible experiences that connect us to the waters surrounding us, allowing us to learn about the ecosystem that has shaped Maldivian culture and is a fundamental part of who we are.
1.     Coral planting with a marine biologist
Join our
resident marine biologist and dive deeper into the story of conservation
and species identification, gaining rare insights into this vibrant underwater world.
Milaidhoo’s coral planting initiative is a part of the resort’s commitment to promoting sustainability and protecting the local marine environment, part of the Baa Atoll UNESCO Biosphere Reserve. You can get involved in this important project by
helping to plant coral frames, sponsoring a frame or taking part in educational workshops on coral reef conservation.
By involving you, we hope to raise awareness about the importance of protecting coral reefs.
2.     Sunset dolphin cruise
After exploring the Indian Ocean’s coral reefs during the day, it’s time to swap marine conservation under the waves for a
sunset dolphin cruise above the water
. Hop aboard
Saima
, our traditional Maldivian
dhoni
, just as the sun starts its spectacular descent, keeping your eyes peeled for our playful neighbours: the spinner dolphins.
Imagine watching these magical creatures putting on a synchronised performance in the wake of the boat, as the sky turns to fire. You’ll never want to leave after witnessing this awe-inspiring sight.
3.     Dive in the Milaidhoo caves
The dolphins aren’t the only ocean-dwelling creatures that you’ll have the pleasure of meeting at Milaidhoo: our house reef is teeming with life and is a protected marine area as we’re part of the UNESCO biosphere reserve, with the world-renowned Hanifaru Bay just minutes away. Grab your
complimentary mask and flippers to snorkel the reef or dive with our experts to discover the untouched beauty of the Milaidhoo caves
. As you dip under the waves, a private world of natural wonder awaits that will take your breath away.
Indulge your senses
This journey of the senses will capture the genuine taste of the Maldives, prepared with heart and flair. Time is the greatest luxury here; island life beats to its own drum.
4.     Cinema under the stars
A Milaidhoo moment that offers pure, unadulterated time together is a private cinema with the entire night sky as your roof. Snuggle up on a plush daybed as you watch your favourite film projected onto a screen, with champagne, popcorn and nothing but the soft breeze and gentle lapping of waves as company. Time will feel infinite as you indulge your senses on a secluded beach.
5.     Spa treatment in the villa
Another sense that you can pamper while on the island is touch. Enjoy a
tailored massage on your private sun deck
, because why move when you can have the spa come to you? With only the sound of the ocean as your soundtrack, this effortless service cultivates utter contentment that will lead to a deep repose.
6.
Malafaay
dinner at Ba’theli
After touch comes taste, and what better way to experience true Maldivian culture than a traditional culinary delight, a
Malafaay
dinner at Ba’theli: the
first Maldivian fine-dining restaurant, with feasts inspired by traditional island fare, set in a gorgeous, reimagined dhoni shape.
7.     Floating breakfast
This is a favourite among our honeymoon guests. It’s pretty simple: wake up and step into your villa’s private pool to be greeted by a floating tray of breakfast treats. A moment of pure, secluded indulgence, perfectly tailored to you. This bespoke comfort and effortless ease, the Milaidhoo way. Because, why not?
Live the authentic Maldivian life
Here are our favourite cultural experiences, unique to our gem of an island, making your trip a true luxury travel experience.
8.     Local island tour
We exist to offer you a genuine experience of the Maldives. Our
local island tour provides exactly that: meet the community and get a sense of the vibrant local culture.
This isn’t just a hotel stay; it’s a chance to build a connection with the broader Maldivian culture and the people who call this ocean home.
9.     Maldivian cooking class at the Chef’s Garden
Another important aspect of Maldivian life is food, particularly the use of spices and fresh, local ingredients. A lovely way to spend a morning is with our chef learning about traditional Maldivian cooking. Wander through the Chef’s Garden picking produce to take back to the kitchen for a fun, relaxed cooking class. Learn to weave Maldivian flavours into dishes; the culinary knowledge learnt will come in handy once you’re back home, keeping the memory of Milaidhoo alive.
10.Traditional Maldivian fishing
After a morning learning about typical Maldivian cuisine with our chef, trade your apron for a line and join our team for a classic Maldivian hand-line fishing trip. Learn the ancient, respectful techniques that have sustained our island community for generations. It’s a beautiful, simple, humbling experience and a great way to enjoy the tranquil blue that Maldivians treasure so deeply.
Will you join us during our 10th year for your bespoke vacation?
As we step into our 10th year, our heart remains committed to offering you a secluded island sanctuary where you feel you truly belong. This is our promise: to continue to surprise and delight you with exceptional, heartfelt hospitality and authentic Maldivian experiences.
At Milaidhoo, we have perfected the art of slow living, creating a seamless tapestry of bespoke adventures in an authentic, secluded private island setting. These 10 moments are just a glimpse of the unique exploration that awaits. Come and join our family, craft your own story and rediscover the luxury of time.
