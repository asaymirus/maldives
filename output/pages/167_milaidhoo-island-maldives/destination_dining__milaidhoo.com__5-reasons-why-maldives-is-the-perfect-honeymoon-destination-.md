5 Reasons why Maldives is the Perfect Honeymoon Destination
Mar 10, 2020
Stories of a Small Island
Every list of the best tropical islands in the world, always includes The Maldives. That’s because the tropical resort islands of the Maldives feature a combination of natural attractions, superb accommodation, good food, amazing underwater experiences and fun.
Here are 5 reasons why Maldives is the Perfect Honeymoon Destination
1.
Picture Perfect and Romantic
Every island is different but most of them are breathtakingly beautiful, fringed with soft white sand beaches. Some resorts like Milaidhoo Island Maldives are embraced by a living and vibrant coral reef and a beautiful turquoise lagoon which makes the perfect backdrop for those honeymoon pictures during the day. Once the sun starts to sink into the Indian Ocean, the time is right for sunset champagne cruises accompanied by leaping dolphins or for romantic candle-lit dinners at the beach.
2. Honeymoon Benefits and Renewal of Vows
The Maldives is always named as a honeymoon hot spot and as one of the most romantic destinations in the world. At Milaidhoo, for example, honeymooners are treated to a romantic beach dinner, a couple’s massage, and honeymooners can name a star. There a few other perks included as well. As a special extra, couples can choose to say their vows again on the beach on a secluded sandbank in the lagoon during a unique wedding vow ceremony.
3. Privacy and spacious island living
The Maldives are known for the one island, one resort concept, which provides plenty of space and privacy for couples. At Milaidhoo island-inspired spacious residences set beachside in lush palm groves or private overwater retreats present an exclusive niche of gracious seclusion. The villas offer privacy yet open up 180 degrees for couples to enjoy a private pool, the view and all-around outdoor space. To maintain the peaceful island style, the resort welcomes adults and children above the age of nine. Only registered guests are allowed, so exclusivity and tranquillity is preserved.
4. Epicurean delights
One might not think it possible, but the culinary side of most resort islands is diverse and exquisite. Exotic island or international gourmet cuisine in
three restaurants
and two bars at Milaidhoo rival any city establishment. And you will not have to wander far to enjoy exquisite culinary experiences from all around the world or locally inspired creations.
5. Wellness, Yoga & Spa
Yoga on a sandbank, in an overwater pavilion or on you private villa deck. Luxury spa treatments, inspired by techniques and cultures from around the world or scented wellness baths. Wellness and relaxation is a must in the Maldives and often the setting is magical. Treatment in
luxury private suites
poised overwater induces relaxation in Milaidhoo’s unique setting. The couple’s treatment rooms are built on columns overwater with fantastic views of the Indian Ocean.  Treatments can also be arranged anywhere on the island or even on a secluded sandbank in the island’s lagoon.
