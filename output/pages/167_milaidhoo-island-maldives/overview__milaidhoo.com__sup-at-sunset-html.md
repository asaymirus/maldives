Stand-up Paddle Boarding at Sunset
May 18, 2018
Ocean Stories
Our Ocean Stories team took our Stand-up Paddle Board (SUP) out on the Milaidhoo Island Maldives lagoon for a beautiful sunset ride – and besides the magical scenery they also encountered a couple of our friendly sting rays.
The Maldives are famous for the spectacular sunsets and how the colours change with every blink of an eye – sunset being unique and mesmerising over and over again. Milaidhoo guests can borrow Stand-up Paddle boards and Kayaks for free to venture out and find their own spot in the middle of our lagoon to enjoy the sunset.
