An enchanting Easter escape at Milaidhoo Maldives
Mar 20, 2025
Milaidhoo Moments
This Easter, trade chocolate bunnies for sun-kissed beaches and egg hunts for barefoot luxury on a private island in the Maldives. At Milaidhoo Maldives, Easter is not just a holiday – it’s a celebration of serenity, indulgence and renewal, designed exclusively for discerning travellers who seek refined elegance in a setting of absolute tranquility.
Here, amid the crystal-clear waters of the Baa Atoll, you’ll find an intimate haven, free from crowds and distractions – just you, your loved one and the soft whisper of the ocean breeze. This is Easter, reinvented.
Why visit Milaidoo at Easter?
Easter is a season of renewal – a time to shed the weight of winter and embrace the promise of brighter days ahead. It’s the perfect moment to pause, refresh and awaken your senses in a place of calm and beauty.
At Milaidhoo, Easter isn’t just for children. It’s an invitation for grown-ups to indulge in a little playful escapism. Whether it’s the joy of an underwater egg hunt, the artistry of a coconut painting workshop or the delight of a sunset cocktail in hand, this is a time to rediscover the simple pleasures of life [link to slow living blog].
And because
every renewal begins with rest
, Milaidhoo offers the ultimate sanctuary to recharge, reset and ready yourself for the summer ahead. So why not gift yourself the luxury of time – time to unwind, to explore, to celebrate? Easter at Milaidhoo is more than a getaway –
it’s a reawakening.
A feast for the senses
Easter at Milaidhoo
is a celebration of taste, where every meal is an experience to savour. From indulgent champagne breakfasts to barefoot beachfront feasts, dining here is an exquisite journey of flavour and elegance. Whether you prefer a lavish buffet by the water’s edge, a starlit fine-dining experience or a private barbecue on a secluded sandbank, each moment is designed to delight.
Champagne breakfast
: begin your day with a decadent breakfast served with bubbles, setting the tone for a day of indulgence.
Beachfront seafood buffet
: feast on the freshest ocean delicacies while dining with your toes in the sand.
Full moon dinner at Ba’theli by the reef
: savour a refined Maldivian-inspired menu under the glow of the full moon, surrounded by the gentle sounds of the ocean.
Sandbank barbecue
: for the ultimate private dining experience, a personal chef will craft a bespoke barbecue feast on a secluded sandbank.
At Milaidhoo, Easter dining is more than a meal – it’s an unforgettable celebration of flavour, setting and island charm.
Easter with a playful twist
Who says Easter is just for children? At Milaidhoo, the holiday spirit is alive in sophisticated, grown-up fun. Get creative in the underwater egg hunt among a kaleidoscope of color fish, or master the art of mixology in a cocktail masterclass inspired by Easter flavours.
Indulge your sweet tooth with chocolate art workshops, where Milaidhoo’s expert pastry chefs unveil the secrets behind their decadent creations.
Indulge in Easter wellness
This Easter, rejuvenate your body and soul at
Serenity Spa
, where signature treatments and bespoke wellness rituals are designed to inspire inner calm and outer radiance. Enjoy sunrise meditation by the beach, luxurious spa therapies using natural Maldivian ingredients and personalised wellness programs that help you embrace the spirit of renewal.
Easter luxury, your way
Beyond Easter festivities, Milaidhoo offers unparalleled experiences to suit your every whim. Discover vibrant marine life on a
guided snorkeling adventure
, embark on a
sunset dolphin cruise
, or simply unwind in your overwater villa, where your private infinity pool meets the horizon.
Whether you’re exploring the wonders beneath the waves or savouring a lazy day on the beach, every moment at Milaidhoo is one of pure indulgence.
Easter 2025 at Milaidhoo: a celebration of sun, sea and serenity
Easter 2025 at Milaidhoo is a time of exquisite indulgence, where each day unfolds like a beautifully curated experience, blending adventure, relaxation and refined island elegance. From sunrise cruises to seafood feasts, ocean explorations to barefoot celebrations, every moment is designed to embrace the season of renewal.
Whether you’re seeking playful escapades, tranquil retreats or gourmet delights, Good Friday through to Easter Monday at Milaidhoo invites you to celebrate in the most extraordinary way – surrounded by turquoise waters, soft sandy shores, and the effortless luxury of island living.
Good Friday 18 April 2025: sunrise to seafood feasts
Sunrise Champagne Cruise
Welcome Easter weekend with a serene champagne cruise, watching the sunrise paint the horizon in hues of gold.
Coconut Shell Painting Workshop
Create a one-of-a-kind Maldivian keepsake, guided by the talented local artist, Nassey.
Happy fishing hours
Experience the thrill of traditional Maldivian handline fishing, and reel in your own catch of the day.
Seafood galore
Indulge in an exquisite beachfront seafood feast, featuring the finest ocean delicacies with your toes in the sand.
Easter Saturday 19 April 2025: from ocean adventures to gourmet journeys
Sandbank yoga
Find inner peace in a secluded sandbank yoga session, surrounded by the sounds of the waves.
Underwater egg hunting
Dive into the vibrant waters of the Baa Atoll to uncover hidden Easter treasures beneath the sea
Sunset dolphin cruise
Set sail in search of playful dolphins “spy-hopping” in the shimmering Maldivian waters.
A five-course culinary journey
Indulge in a spectacular dining experience crafted by Executive Chef Ken Gundu, paired with the finest wines
Easter Sunday 20 April 2025: a celebration of indulgence
Eggs-ploring Easter brunch
Start Easter morning with a sumptuous island-inspired brunch, celebrating fresh flavors and tropical delights.
Easter beach games
Enjoy a playful afternoon of Easter-themed games, hosted by the Milaidhoo Family on our pristine sandy shores.
Easter afternoon tea
Savour a delightful selection of sweet and savory Easter treats, perfectly paired with refreshing teas.
Sandbank cinema under the stars
End your Easter Sunday with an intimate movie night on a private sandbank, beneath a starlit Maldivian sky.
Easter Monday 21 April 2025: exploration and relaxation
Maldivian handline trolling
Try your hand at traditional Maldivian fishing, a cultural experience like no other.
Marine talk and house reef snorkelling
Join our marine biologist for an insightful ocean talk before exploring the wonders of our vibrant house reef.
Explore a local island
Immerse yourself in authentic Maldivian culture with a guided visit to a nearby island.
Easter cocktail stories
Toast to the season with signature Easter-inspired cocktails, crafted with Milaidhoo’s signature flair.
Your Easter, reinvented
This Easter, escape to Milaidhoo Maldives, where intimacy, luxury and natural beauty create the perfect setting for renewal and relaxation. Savour exquisite cuisine, embrace wellness, and celebrate new beginnings in a sanctuary designed just for you.
