The Milaidhoo Star
Oct 07, 2018
A Love Story
Did you know that Milaidhoo is the first resort in the Maldives to have a star named after itself? It’s in the constellation of Gemini at RA 6.462 and declination +18.50.
We liked the idea so much that we name stars after our honeymooning guests too. It’s part of our ‘Perfect Honeymoon’ package: the happy couple will have a star in the night skies named after them and take home a naming certificate of registration and star chart so they can always find it. Guests can choose the name of their star and which constellation it’s in.
We’re wondering: would you choose the constellation of the month you got married or another date?
That’s not all you get as a honyemooner at Milaidhoo Maldives. Our package also includes:
Naming a star after the couple, with certificate and star chart
One utterly relaxing 60-minute spa treatment for two in a spa pavilion set on stilts over the ocean
A romantic three-course candlelit dinner on the deck of the guests’ villa
The resort’s signature honeymoon cake which awaits the guests in their villa on arrival along with a bottle of champagne and tropical fruit basket.
Why do we do this? Because we’re not just another five star resort in the Maldives: at Milaidhoo, we like to do things differently and naming stars after our honeymooning guests is just one of our many singular guest experiences as part of our ‘custom made and made for comfort’ service philosophy of only offering the very best in a way that’s individually tailored to each guest.
