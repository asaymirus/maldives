Valentine’s in the Maldives: 4 Romantic Valentine’s Treats
While the Northern Hemisphere is still coated in snow and icy temperatures, the Maldives is still a sunny spot on the map that welcomes guests all around the year. Valentine’s Day might be a more than welcome excuse to pack light dresses and swim wear from a long lost summer and make ones way to the Maldives. Many resorts like Milaidhoo Island Maldives celebrate Valentine’s Day with special arrangements and experiences for the occasion. Here are a few ideas and treats on how to perfect your Valentine’s Day in the Maldives.
1. Book a Romantic Escape Offer
Of course most resorts in the Maldives offer packages which are created for couples and are all about romance. You can book ‘A Love Story’ at Milaidhoo, a special package that entitles you to enjoy daily Champagne breakfast in your villa, a private sunset cruise on a traditional Maldivian Dhoni and a dinner at the beach under the stars for two. Of course there will be that special bed and bath decoration as well, which will make your Valentine’s Day just that extra special.
2. Let the food do the talking
Of course Valentine’s Day is just the day for a romantic beach dinner with fine food and even finer Champagne. Couples who are visiting Milaidhoo during Valentine’s Day can expect nothing less than a love inspired menu that will be served at the beach for all love birds that wish to join. This would also be the perfect evening to enjoy a rose petal bath upon return to the private villa, which can be thoughtfully prepared by the villa’s Island Host.
3. That romantic extra mile
Overall, love and romance is about spending meaningful time together. The team at Milaidhoo is specialised in creating those moments and experiences for guests. Cruises and excursions can be customized per the guest’s preferences and extraordinary once in a lifetime events like spending a night on a secluded sandbank in a full bed set up or naming you own star in the Maldivian night sky.
4. Romance has not expiry date
Understanding that many countries and traveller’s are still under lock down and are not able to travel as they would like to, resorts like Milaidhoo have extended their romance boasting packages and offers until the end of October 2021. Couples can choose to stay romantic but travel later, as the Maldives will always be that perfect escape – never mind in winter or summer.
