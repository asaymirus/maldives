Diving
While the beauty of the Maldives is breathtaking, what you’ll find below the surface is even more captivating. Our experienced diving team holds the keys to the best dive sites in the atoll, welcoming everyone from novices making their first dive to experts logging their 1000th.
At Milaidhoo, a Maldives luxury resort, our five-star PADI dive centre offers a range of Maldives diving packages, including house reef diving, boat diving, private dives, night dives, fluo night dives, Manta Ray encounters and nitrox options.
Recognised among the top resorts in Baa Atoll, Milaidhoo is a must on your Maldives bucket list for scuba diving in the Maldives. Our PADI Five Star Dive Resort status reflects our commitment to quality diver training, continuing education and environmental awareness.
