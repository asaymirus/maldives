Babymoon at Milaidhoo Island Maldives
Oct 17, 2019
A Love Story
Enjoy a romantic adults-only break with the ultimate Indian Ocean babymoon, complete with pregnancy spa treatments, pre-natal yoga, private movie screenings and gender reveal surprises. No one deserves peace of mind more than expecting parents, and Milaidhoo Island Maldives is the ultimate haven to relax and prepare for a new arrival. With secluded private villas situated overwater or on the beach and the recently launched spectacular Ocean and Beach Residences, parents-to-be are spoilt for choice at this luxurious Maldivian hideaway.
Couples can visit the island’s Serenity Spa for bespoke treatments, including special pre-natal pampering for the mother-to-be, which can be enjoyed in a spacious over-water spa suite looking out to the calming blue hues of the Indian Ocean. Pre-natal yoga classes are also on offer, guided by Milaidhoo’s resident expert yogi.
For the ultimate movie night, parents-to-be will be treated to a private cinema screening under the stars complete with delicious homemade popcorn. Couples can choose to enjoy their outdoor cinema in the privacy of their villa or opt to watch their movie on a private stretch of white-sand beach.
Milaidhoo is perfect for those who love the underwater world and is particularly safe for those who are pregnant, thanks to their house reef. Couples can discover the spectacular Maldivian sea life and even opt to plant a coral frame for their new baby. The coral frames are cleverly designed to help coral re-grow and multiply, helping to rehabilitate the reef.  Couples will receive regular updates on the growth once home.
Babymoon benefits:
60-minute Spa treatment for the couple with a customized massage for the mother-to-be.
A Cinema under the Stars experience on your villa deck or at the beach
Planting a coral frame for your offspring with regular updates on the growth of the coral sent to you as your baby grows.
A classic signature cake or a customized gender-reveal cake to send a surprise message home.
Note: The Babymoon benefits at Milaidhoo Island Maldives are applicable for any expecting parents staying for a minimum of 4-nights. This offer cannot be combined with any other offers/entitlements.
