Things to Do
From diving among colourful coral reefs to sunset sailing on a traditional dhoni, Milaidhoo offers a curated blend of relaxation and discovery. Enjoy private beach dinners, explore nearby sandbanks or unwind with a bespoke wellness ritual at the spa. Whether you’re snorkelling in a UNESCO biosphere reserve or simply stargazing from your villa deck, Milaidhoo is the perfect base for unforgettable moments. For couples or solo travellers, there’s no shortage of inspiring things to do in the Maldives.
