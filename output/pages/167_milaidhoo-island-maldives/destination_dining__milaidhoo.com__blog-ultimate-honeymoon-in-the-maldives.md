The ultimate Honeymoon in the Maldives
Jan 16, 2023
A Love Story
The tropical paradise, the Maldives, presents new lovers with the perfect destination to write their love story at. With crystal clear waters expanding beyond the horizon, powdery white sand beaches enveloping the islands, and luxurious overwater pool villas scattered over the turquoise lagoon, it’s no wonder the island country is a top honeymoon spot. Setting a scene for a romantic tale that’s never been told before, Miladhoo Maldives presents the perfect
honeymoon destination
for lovers.
Here are Milaidhoo’s most cherished experiences to uncover with your loved one, all scripted to satisfy your penchant for romance:
1. Barefoot at the beach
Renowned for its stunning beaches that encircle the islands, Maldives welcome newlyweds to reminisce their love on the expansive shores. There’s no better way to spend your honeymoon than lounging on the sand, soaking up the sun in your corner of paradise at Miladhoo. The aquamarine waters surrounding the tropical isle call for a dip, where the lovers can swim alongside the rich marine life and witness a beauty unlike any other.
2. An unspoiled haven over waters
Milaidhoo is home to truly one-of-a-kind
overwater villas
. The accommodations on stilts over the ocean offer the ultimate in romance, with private balconies overlooking the ocean and direct access to the water. Adorned in lavish comforts, these spacious homes embrace everything that is truly Maldivian — from the ambience to the cultural elements sequined in the villas. Lovers can take in the breathtaking scenes displayed in front of them and truly unwind atop the waters.
3. Tour the tropical isles of Maldives
With over 1000 islands spanning over 26 atolls, Maldives presents an abundance of destinations to visit in your explorations. For couples with an adventurous streak, island hopping presents the perfect opportunity to discover all the unique and untold stories of the Maldives. While each atoll has its unique charm, Baa Atoll is exquisitely unique as it displays one of the
richest biodiversity in the Maldives
. Home to 75 islands —13 of which are inhabited, Milaidhoo remains just one chapter of the Baa atoll story. Lovers can enjoy their romantic getaway at our small island resort, and continue their adventure by experiencing the rich culture and all the local anecdotes by hopping over to the local islands of the atoll.
4. Cruise the waters at sunset
There’s nothing quite like a sunset in the Maldives. Take in the scenery painted ahead of you as the colours awash the waters of Baa atoll. Milaidhoo invites you to a
romantic cruise
along the coast. A truly exquisite experience awaits you as the sun dips below the horizon, and you sip on a glass of champagne. The sweeping panoramas paired with the heavenly ambience promise a memory that will not fade.
5. Unwind and relax with your loved one
After all the planning and stress of your wedding, it’s time to relax and pamper yourselves. At Milaidhoo, you can unwind from the stress and breathe — letting the blissful relaxation take over your body. Treat yourselves to a couples massage at Serenity Spa in Milaidhoo and embrace the holistic journey. With organic and natural spa therapies, you can choose the perfect treatment that embodies your love story.
With endless sights, and adventures to embrace, the Maldives presents the perfect destination for a romantic and relaxing honeymoon. From the stunning beaches, to luxurious accommodations, and endless activities, every moment is to be cherished — promising memories that will last a lifetime. Read more about
