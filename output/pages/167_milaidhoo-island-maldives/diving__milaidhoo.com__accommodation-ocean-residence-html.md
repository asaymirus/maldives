Two-bedroom Ocean Residence with Private Pool
Welcome to the ultimate escape at our Maldives luxury resort, the Ocean Residence, a stunning Two Bedroom Villa Maldives experience designed for those seeking space, privacy and uninterrupted ocean views.
Set at the far end of the lagoon jetty, this 564 sqm thatched-roof residence is suspended above crystal-clear waters, offering total seclusion. With two spacious bedrooms — each with its own en-suite bathroom — and a separate living room, it’s perfect for families or friends travelling together.
Your dedicated personal butler is always on hand to arrange everything you need, from in-villa dining to personalised excursions or private wellness sessions.
Outside, the broad sundeck is made for lounging and daydreaming. Dip into your private freshwater infinity pool, relax on the swing chair or step directly into the lagoon for a swim or snorkel. There’s even a secret rooftop gym, ideal for workouts, yoga or meditation with inspiring views of the ocean.
Inside, the design feels airy and calm. The master bedroom’s king-size bed faces the sea, while high ceilings and natural textures create a sense of barefoot elegance. You’ll also find thoughtful touches like a curated wine chiller, gourmet coffee, premium teas and three discreetly placed flat-screen TVs – just enough, never too much.
The master bathroom is purely indulgence. One side opens completely to let in sunlight and sea breeze, with a deep bathtub overlooking the ocean and a tropical rain shower that feels like it belongs in nature. Acqua Di Parma amenities add a final touch of comfort.
This isn’t just a villa. It’s your own private world in one of the most exclusive resorts in Baa Atoll. Whether you’re sharing the space or enjoying it all to yourself, the Milaidhoo Ocean Residence is made for slow living, salty air and stories you’ll want to tell again.
