Your Bespoke Festive Story at Milaidhoo Island Maldives
Dec 07, 2018
Stories of a Small Island
The small, private island of Milaidhoo in the Maldives is commemorating this year’s Festive Season in intimate style with celebrations in harmony with nature to create meaningful experiences, custom made for your comfort.
Every day of your holiday indulging in reinvented luxury paired with barefoot informality can begin with breakfast in the spacious privacy of your Villa, on our secluded sandbank or on the deck of our traditional Dhoni sailing vessel. Participate in excursions like Maldivian hand-line trolling at dawn, live-bait fishing, or head out to meet turtles, clown fish and other marine life during day and night snorkelling expeditions, join reef-hopping and dolphin watching trips or local island exploration. Focus on your health and wellbeing in our Serenity Spa and participate in invigorating yoga or gentle meditation sessions.
With nature at our doorstep, you can participate in protecting our fragile underwater world by joining our Marine Biologist in grafting a coral frame to commemorate the Festive Season. Embrace local culture on our Maldivian Cultural Day at our island square with a demonstration of Bodu Beru dancing and an insight into the Maldivian lifestyle.
As well as seasonal cocktail receptions and special occasion gala dinners – and a New Year’s Day champagne brunch – there will be celebratory barbecues on our sandbank and themed dinners crafted with passion for flavours in our iconic Ba’theli restaurant. To add to your story, learn how to bring those flavours back home at special cookery classes, held in the inspiring and memorable island setting that is unique to Milaidhoo.
Festive Story Programme 2018/2019
