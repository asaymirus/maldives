Crafting the Perfect Maldives Honeymoon: A Romantic Escape Like No Other
May 10, 2024
Uncategorized
After the whirlwind of planning your wedding and the momentous occasion of your Big Day, its heavenly to experience the cut-off-from-the-world tranquillity of the Maldives on honeymoon. With its 500-mile collection of coral islands stretching across azure blue waters, the Maldives offers an ideal start to nuptial bliss.
Milaidhoo Maldives with its soft powdery white sand beach, crystal clear waters and bone deep pampering, is the top resort in Maldives for couples. Here are a few of Milaidhoo’s most coveted experiences to uncover with your beloved, all designed to fulfil your dream of a small island romantic escape.
Retreat to an Overwater Villa
Milaidhoo offers both luxurious beach and water villas but it’s the resorts’ wooden stilt overwater villas that attract honeymooners. Your secluded water pool villa offers the seclusion you’ve been dreaming of. Seamlessly integrated with the surrounding nautical nature, each thatch roof villa is decorated in soothing hues of blue and white with Maldivian art, a chaise lounge and super-comfy king-size beds. A sun-kissed deck features loungers, an outdoor shower and private half-moon infinity pool. A swing daybed is the perfect spot to while away the afternoon together, and a ladder-style staircase leads directly to the coral reef below. The bathrooms boast a double vanity, an oval bath tub overlooking the ocean with luxurious Acqua di Parma amenities, and a shower with a glass floor so can watch tropical fish dart about the coral reef whilst showering. You will feel yourself surrendering to the ambiance and serenity from the moment you enter your private sanctuary. Your dedicated Island Host will surprise you with unique touches, ranging from botanical bed decorations, bubble baths with floating hibiscus flowers to a champagne breakfast served on a floating tray in your villa pool.
Destination Dining
At Milaidhoo you will find a myriad of destination dining experiences ranging from a sandbank barbecue lunch to a romantic moonlit beach dining experience or an intimate feast set on your villa deck. Each culinary journey is prepared by a personal chef and includes a choice of starters, mains, and dessert. You can choose from a selection of curated menus, or your chef will endeavour to create the perfect feast filled with your favourite dishes.

Romantic Culinary Voyage at Ba’theli
Savour a specially curated degustation menu at Ba’theli, Milaidhoo’s signature floating restaurant. Fashioned from three interlinked traditional ba’theli cargo boats, the restaurant is  moored in the turquoise lagoon with stirring views over the shimmering sea. Each course, prepared by the resort’s talented local chefs, will delight and awaken your senses. Immerse yourself in a gastronomic adventure that celebrates the rich heritage of the Maldives. From must-try delicacies to innovative creations, every bite tells a story of the Maldives’ ancient Spice Route.
Sail Away Together
Board one of our private yachts or traditional Dhoni for your maiden voyage as husband and wife at sunset. Bask in the last rays of sunlight, sip champagne and enjoy a quiet moment together. Set sail on an all-day adventure – explore the magnificent underwater world with your dive master, picnic on a sandbank and go island hopping in search of dolphins and mythical mermaids. You can also enjoy the timeless romance of a full moon stargazing cruise, revelling in the wonder of other galaxies.
