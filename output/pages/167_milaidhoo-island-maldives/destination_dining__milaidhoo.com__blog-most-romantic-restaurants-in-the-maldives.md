Most Romantic Restaurants in the Maldives
Aug 01, 2021
Milaidhoo Moments
Which are the most romantic restaurants in the Maldives, the world’s most romantic holiday destination? Here is a selection of the top romantic restaurants you’ll find in the Maldives:
1. Ba’theli by the Reef
At the most romantic restaurants in the Maldives, ambiance and location matter just as much as great food. In the middle of a turquoise lagoon at Milaidhoo in the Maldives is Ba’theli, a traditional Maldivian sailing dhoni, built on pillars, seeming to float in the water. Only for a limited number of guests to dine each evening, the restaurant assures romantic privacy for dining on delicious island-inspired, gourmet dishes.
2. Sandbank dining
A private pop-up restaurant on a sandbank is the perfect romantic restaurant for two.  Only you and your partner, a waiter and a chef on a deserted sandbank, the starry sky above, white powdery sand beneath your feet, and light ocean breeze and gentle waves as your soundtrack. A personal chef will prepare your selected dishes in front of you, while your waiter serves you with all the styles of an established romantic restaurant.
3. Private Dining in your villa
A romantic restaurant of pure intimacy can be created in the privacy of your spacious, luxury villa. With a table, decorated with candles and flowers set up on your villa deck, the Villa Dining team of Milaidhoo will serve dinner in your villa, making sure your every whim is taken care of. Unforgettable dining in total privacy against a tropical backdrop.
4. The Shoreline Grill
At Milaidhoo, we know the recipe of romance and love to share it with you. The Shoreline Grill, fringed with lush greenery, creates an utterly private setting right by the beach. You can forget that you are dining in a restaurant as romance is in the air. The menu promises fine meats and freshly caught local fish, prepared in a show kitchen.
