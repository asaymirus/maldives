Sleep Under the Stars on Your Private Sandbank
Dec 07, 2018
Milaidhoo Moments
We are excited to offer our new Sleeping Under the Stars experience at Milaidhoo. A couple can spend a night under the stars alone like castaways on a private sandbank.
Set in the tranquil waters of a lagoon, Milaidhoo’s mini-island of golden sand with no vegetation or wild life can be a couple’s exclusive private paradise for a night of romance under the stars.
The sandbank is transformed with the same comfortable facilities of your Milaidhoo villa, complete with four-poster bed draped in muslin. A yacht for your exclusive use with dressing room, shower, toilet and equipped with all amenities, is anchored by the sandbank during your stay. Candles and battery-powered lanterns enhance the moonlight and the only music is the wash of the waves on the sandbank’s shore.
Your memorable night as a castaway couple at Milaidhoo’s sandbank begins with a short dolphin cruise at sunset on a luxury yacht or a traditional Maldivian sailing boat with a butler offering champagne and canapés as the sun sets and dolphins leap. When you land on the sandbank, sun-downer cocktails are served as a welcome drink. Then follows a four-course dinner especially created to your request by the Chef and served at your table by your personal butler.
You are invited to sit by a cosy fire as you enjoy after-dinner drinks while the crew withdraw, leaving you and your partner in romantic, star-lit privacy. A luxurious, canopied king-size bed awaits as you are embraced by the magic of the moon and stars on this isolated desert sandbank far from everywhere.
A mobile phone is provided in case you want to call your butler. Otherwise you will be left alone until you wake in the morning. By request, the Milaidhoo Yogi can visit to offer a sunrise yoga session before your butler arrives to present a freshly prepared breakfast served on the sandbank.
This is a rare opportunity for a couple on honeymoon or celebrating precious time together to enjoy romantic tropical bliss in the tranquil seclusion of the Milaidhoo sandbank.
