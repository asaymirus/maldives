Destination Dining
You can enjoy dining together at another level with our Destination Dining Maldives experience. Excellent food prepared for your wishes and preferences can be served anywhere on the island: at the beach, on the villas or residence’s deck, on our traditional boat or exclusively on our nearby sandbank. Especially arranged meals like sunrise breakfast, picnic lunch or dinner under the stars are available on request.
Private dining Maldives also takes centre stage with in-villa dining, creating a magical experience for you in the privacy of your villa’s deck. On the menu, you’ll find a carefully curated selection of dishes designed to please. If you’re craving something different, simply let our chefs know what you fancyand it will be our pleasure to prepare it for you.
Open 24 hours, every day. Booking is required.
We update and change our menus regularly, so items and pricing on this menu may differ when you stay with us.
