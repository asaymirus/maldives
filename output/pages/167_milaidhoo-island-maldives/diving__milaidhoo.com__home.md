Welcome to
Milaidhoo Maldives
Kick off your shoes and let us tell you a story…
Once upon a time, in the heart of a UNESCO Biosphere Reserve, there was a small tropical island, an emerald drop of paradise wrapped in soft white sands, surrounded by thriving coral reefs and gently kissed by warm turquoise waters.
That island is Milaidhoo. That time is now. And this is where your story begins.
More than just a five-star resort, Milaidhoo redefines island luxury. As a leading Maldives luxury resort, we believe every guest is a storyteller and every stay is a chapter in a deeply personal journey. This is where your unforgettable escape unfolds.
Opened in November 2016, Milaidhoo is an intimate boutique resort in Baa Atoll, just 126 km northwest of Malé and moments from Hanifaru Bay, famed for its seasonal gatherings of manta rays. Among the top resorts in Baa Atoll, Milaidhoo offers an experience that’s as natural as it is extraordinary.
Fringed by powdery white sands and lush tropical greenery, our island measures just 300 by 180 metres, small, secluded and utterly serene. Surrounded by a protected house reef, it’s a haven for snorkelling, diving and connecting with the beauty of the ocean.
Designed especially for couples and those seeking peace and privacy, we welcome guests aged eight and above and observe a strict no-drone policy to protect the tranquillity of the island.
Everything here is tailor-made for comfort and crafted around you, from our thoughtful design to the intuitive, heartfelt service that reflects our barefoot informality. Above all, Milaidhoo invites you to discover the true Maldives, rich in culture, nature and moments that feel genuinely yours.
