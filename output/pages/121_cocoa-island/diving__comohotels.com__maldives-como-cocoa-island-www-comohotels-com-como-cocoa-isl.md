FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS
1
Where is COMO Cocoa Island located in the Maldives?
COMO Cocoa Island, known locally as Makunufushi, is located in the South Malé Atoll of the Maldives — about 30 kilometres south of Malé, surrounded by coral reefs and clear turquoise waters
2
How far is COMO Cocoa Island from Malé International Airport?
COMO Cocoa Island is located just 30 kilometres (19 miles) from Malé International Airport, making it one of the most conveniently accessible Maldives resorts. Guests reach the island via a scenic 40-minute speedboat transfer across the turquoise waters of the South Malé Atoll — no seaplane required.
3
How long is the speedboat transfer from Malé International Airport to COMO Cocoa Island?
The speedboat transfer from Malé International Airport to COMO Cocoa Island takes approximately 40 minutes, offering a seamless journey across the South Malé Atoll. As the resort is near Malé Airport and accessible by speedboat, guests can begin their Maldives island escape shortly after landing — perfect for those seeking a Maldives resort without a seaplane transfer.
4
Does COMO Cocoa Island offer direct access to a house reef for snorkeling and diving?
Yes. COMO Cocoa Island is a luxury Maldives resort with direct access to a vibrant house reef, just steps from each overwater villa. Guests can snorkel or dive among coral gardens home to reef fish, turtles, and rays in the calm, clear waters of the South Malé Atoll.
5
Is COMO Cocoa Island a good choice for a honeymoon in the Maldives?
Yes. COMO Cocoa Island is a luxury Maldives water-villa resort ideal for couples and honeymooners seeking privacy and ocean views. Its 33 overwater villas, inspired by traditional Maldivian dhonis, feature private terraces and direct lagoon access. The resort offers romantic dining, serene sunsets, and holistic wellness at COMO Shambhala Retreat.
6
What is included when booking a stay at COMO Cocoa Island?
A stay at COMO Cocoa Island includes daily replenishment of selected minibar items and bottled water, Wi-Fi throughout the resort, COMO Shambhala bathroom amenities, a beach bag, and coffee and tea-making facilities. Guests also enjoy complimentary use of snorkelling and non-motorised water sports equipment, access to the COMO Shambhala Retreat with steam room, hydrotherapy pool, and gym, and scheduled wellness classes six times a week. Butler service is provided for selected villas.
7
Is COMO Cocoa Island committed to sustainability and eco-friendly practices?
Yes. COMO Cocoa Island is a sustainable luxury resort in the Maldives, holding EarthCheck Silver certification for its environmental performance. The resort integrates eco-friendly initiatives such as on-site water bottling, coral restoration, waste reduction, and refillable amenities — offering a mindful, low-impact island experience without compromising on comfort.
