Join us for one of the most vibrant Eid traditions at CROSSROADS Maldives! The Bodu Eid Maali Parade, led by the Chaaley Boduberu Group, winds its way from Hard Rock Hotel Maldives all the way to SAii Beach Club, filling the island with the thunderous beat of Boduberu drums, colorful costumes, and the energy of traditional Maldivian culture.
Whether you are watching from the waterfront or joining the procession, this is a celebration you will not want to miss.
