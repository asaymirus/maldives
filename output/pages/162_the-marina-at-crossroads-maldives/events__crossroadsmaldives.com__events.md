MEET & GREET IN TROPICAL STYLE
INSPIRING & STYLISH EVENTS
Infuse your meeting, event or conference with the Maldivian tradition of celebration in your choice of unique and distinctive settings. As one of the premier event venues in Maldives, The Event Hall @ CROSSROADS is housed in a magnificent building overlooking the heart of the complex, ideal for hosting all manner of truly memorable events. Covering 326 square metres and framed by beautiful high ceilings, the hall and its range of versatile break-out rooms can be arranged in a variety of configurations to suit any occasion, from sumptuous gala dinners and special celebrations to themed events, performances and corporate incentive meetings. The Event Hall @ CROSSROADS has the capacity to cater for trade shows with space to accommodate over 20 booths, and we can also assist with seamless Maldives local transfers for your attendees.
