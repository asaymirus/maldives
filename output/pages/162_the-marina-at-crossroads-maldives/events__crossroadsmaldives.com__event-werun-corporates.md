A Relay of Teamwork, Fitness, and Corporate Pride
Join teams from across the Maldives at CROSSROADS Maldives for WeRUN Corporates, an annual 5 KM relay race that brings organisations together in the spirit of competition and wellness. Each company fields a team of five runners, each covering 1 KM, as they race the resort’s scenic marina route with baton exchanges, matching uniforms, and team spirit on full display.
More than a race, WeRUN Corporates is a celebration of workplace culture, health, and unity, capped off with prizes, medals, and a festive atmosphere for all participants.
Location: The Marina at CROSSROADS Maldives
Time: Female Category 16:30 – 17:10 hrs / Male Category 17:15 – 17:55 hrs
Participation Fee: MVR 7,000 per team
