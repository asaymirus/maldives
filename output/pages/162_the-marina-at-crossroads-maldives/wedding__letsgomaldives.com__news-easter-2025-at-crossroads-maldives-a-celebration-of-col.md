This Easter, forget the usual egg hunts and pastel parties-CROSSROADS Maldives is flipping the script and inviting you to celebrate under the sun with style, rhythm, and a whole lot of tropical flair. From SAii Lagoon Maldives, Curio Collection by Hilton to Hard Rock Hotel Maldives, the entire destination becomes a playground of festive experiences that stretch far beyond the shoreline.
Island Adventures for Every Kind of Explorer
At SAii Lagoon Maldives, Easter isn’t just a day-it’s a full week of wonder running from April 14 to 20. Families can dive into a carefully curated mix of play, creativity, and nature-led learning at Koimala and Maalimi’s Junior Beach Club and Camp. Kids wake up to paddle boat rides and friendly beach competitions, get their hands messy crafting bunny masks or painting Easter eggs, and follow adventure trails that spark their imagination.
But there’s a deeper layer to the fun here. Coral planting and upcycled art sessions introduce young minds to environmental care, all while they’re fully immersed in play. Afternoon gelato tastings and bubbly poolside cool-downs keep the vibe light, and evenings come alive with DJ beats, kids’ mini discos, and open-air cinema under the stars.
Where the Beat Drops and the Colour Pops
Across the Marina, Hard Rock Hotel Maldives cranks up the volume with a signature mix of music, energy, and full-tilt celebration. It all starts with Bunny Beats at the Pool, a Friday bash where DJs and splash zones blend into a sunlit party. As the day unfolds, Sessions becomes a creative corner for the Easter Cookie Jam, where decorating sweet treats gets an extra edge with curated playlists.
Saturday goes big with the Roxity Fun Fair and a Tie Dye Party, turning the resort into a rainbow-hued haven of laughter and colour. That night, the stage is set for a grand Easter Feast and Live Show, blending unforgettable entertainment with mouthwatering island flavours.
Easter Sunday strikes a laid-back note with a tropical brunch at The Elephant and The Butterfly, featuring live rhythms, surprise bunny sightings, and a family show to remember. While little Rock Stars race through egg hunts and groove at the Roxity Easter Party, aerial artists take the sky, transforming the moment into magic. As the weekend eases out, Hera’s Acoustic Vibes offers a dreamy wind-down under the stars, before the Limbo Showdown turns the Pool Deck into one last joyful uproar.
A Celebration That Stays With You
Beyond the activities, the spirit of celebration carries over into style. At the Hard Rock Rock Shop, guests who hit a minimum spend are treated to complimentary exclusive pieces—thanks to the BE BOLD BE HAPPY promotion. It’s a little extra something to take home along with the memories.
Why Easter at CROSSROADS Maldives Feels Different
This isn’t just a holiday-it’s a full sensory experience. With two incredible resorts, a marina buzzing with energy, and a schedule that blends chill vibes with high-energy moments, CROSSROADS Maldives offers something far beyond the ordinary. It’s the kind of Easter where every day feels like a new story, and the soundtrack of the island never fades.
