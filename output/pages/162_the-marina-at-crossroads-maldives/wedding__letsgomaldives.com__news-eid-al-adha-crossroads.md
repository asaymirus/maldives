The Marina at Crossroads is home to Maldives Discovery Center; the only one of its kind in the Maldives. This Eid, as part of celebrations we displayed ‘Bodu Mas’ a cultural Maldivian dance symbol of Eid, sacrifice of fisherman and the joy of achievement.
The celebrations began with the Eid parade began from our hotel crossing the bridge and ending at The Marina with Bodu Beru performances and the Catching of the Bodu Mas (big fish) a Maldivian folklore story.
The celebrations continued in the evening with Eid Variety Show of cultural performances by the team until late night.
The energy, the vibe and the Band Members waiting to host your guests throughout their stay set this resort apart and brings a new dynamic to the Maldivian vacation experience.
