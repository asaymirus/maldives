1 Bedroom Beach Hideaway
A private retreat ideal for small families or couples, our Beach Hideaway is surrounded by pristine mangroves and is just steps from the azure waters of the Indian Ocean. A wooden terrace hugs the private swimming pool to offer ample space for relaxing under sunny skies. The luxurious master bedroom, situated at the back of the villa, comes with a retractable roof, an outdoor bathroom with a bath, and two showers encircled by a water feature.
