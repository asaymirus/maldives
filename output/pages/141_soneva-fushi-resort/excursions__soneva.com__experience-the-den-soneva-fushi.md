A space for children and teens to explore, create and roam freely, where play, curiosity and new discoveries unfold each day.
A Place to be Young, Freely
The Den unfolds as a space for imagination to roam freely. A technology-free space, where children and teens are free to play, create and discover at their own pace. Guided with care and attention, each day is shaped by curiosity, quiet adventure and the freedom to find their own way.
The Space
Spanning more than 1,365 sqm, The Den opens out like its own small world. A space larger than six tennis courts, shaped for movement, play and imagination. An eight-metre pirate ship invites adventure. Giant piano keys echo with sound and laughter. Two pools offer time to splash and slow down, while a dedicated cinema brings quiet moments in between. Each corner reveals something new, with space to explore and space to simply be.
Creative Play
A space without screens, where curiosity sets the rhythm. The Den invites children to move freely between moments of making and quiet discovery.
Instruments wait to be played. Costumes to be tried on and stories to be imagined. A room of more than 8,000 Lego and Duplo pieces opens up endless possibilities, while a small library offers space to slow down, read and learn. Each corner is there to be found in its own time.
Daily Experiences
Each day unfolds a little differently. A weekly rhythm of thoughtful, complimentary activities brings families together, with space to join in or simply follow what feels right. From cooking and nature walks to island adventures and hands-on workshops, the programme is shaped with care. Always changing, never hurried and open to discovery.
