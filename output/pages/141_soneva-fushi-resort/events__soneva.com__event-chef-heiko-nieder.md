Award winning chef
Heiko Nieder completed his culinary training at the Fairmont Hotel Vier Jahreszeiten in Hamburg before going on to establish himself as one of Europe’s most celebrated chefs. During his time at L’Orquivit in Bonn, he was recognised as Gault Millau’s “Discovery of the Year” and earned his first Michelin star, alongside widespread acclaim for his imaginative and highly refined approach to cuisine.
Since joining The Dolder Grand in Zurich as Chef Fine Dining, Heiko has led The Restaurant to international recognition, earning multiple Michelin stars and consistently high Gault Millau scores. Over the years, he has received numerous distinctions, including “Chef of the Year”, “Hotel Chef of the Year” and “Best German Chef Abroad”, cementing his place among the world’s leading culinary figures
