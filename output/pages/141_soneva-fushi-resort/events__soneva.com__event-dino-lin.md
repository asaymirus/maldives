Physical Therapist, Acupuncturist and Personal Trainer.
With a refined understanding of movement, recovery and performance, Dino Lin brings over 15 years of international experience to his work as a Physical Therapist, Acupuncturist and Personal Trainer. His practice is grounded in a results-driven philosophy that seamlessly bridges Eastern healing traditions with Western clinical expertise, offering a deeply personalised approach to modern wellbeing. Having worked with Olympians and elite athletes across multiple disciplines, Dino applies a precise and intuitive methodology, using detailed assessment and movement analysis to identify the root causes of physical discomfort and restore balance, mobility and strength.
During his visit to Soneva Fushi, Dino will offer bespoke one-on-one sessions designed to support the body through every stage of recovery and performance, from injury rehabilitation and pain management to strength development and longevity. Integrating advanced therapeutic techniques, functional training and targeted recovery modalities such as myofascial release and corrective exercise, his work is focused on achieving lasting, measurable results while empowering each guest to move with greater ease, resilience and vitality.
