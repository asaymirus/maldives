Soneva has revealed the launch of the new, ultra-bespoke Soneva Secret. Located in the Maldives’ remote Makunudhoo Atoll, Soneva Secret will welcome its first guests in early 2024. Thirty years in the making, Soneva Secret builds upon Soneva’s experience at the forefront of exceptional hospitality and will set a new, unmatched standard in intuitive, personalised service, exquisite private villas and unspoiled settings, surrounded by nature.
