Award winning chef
Konstantin Filippou developed his distinctive culinary style through a deep respect for simplicity, seasonality and the finest ingredients from both land and sea. After refining his craft in some of Europe’s leading kitchens, he opened his eponymous restaurant in Vienna, where his precise yet expressive approach quickly earned international acclaim, including two Michelin stars.
Today, Konstantin is celebrated for creating deeply personal dining experiences that balance technical precision with emotion, memory and a strong connection to nature.
