Two Bedroom Beach Pool Villa
A spacious hideaway embedded in the carefully preserved natural environment of Maamutaa Island. A large deck and a covered cabana overlooking the pool and jacuzzi. A secluded garden opening onto sand and sea. The Pullman Two-bedroom Beach Pool Villa is a place for family and friends to relax together and renew connections.
The best of outdoor and indoor Maldivian living. Intelligent design inside and out merges Maldivian tradition with contemporary style.
Maintaining privacy at all times. Two bedrooms separated by a living room. The master bedroom with a king sized bed, the second bedroom with twin beds.
Each villa with it’s own private bathrooms, the Two Bedroom Beach Pool Villa is the perfect choice for small groups and families alike.
Never let the day end without organizing a moonlit barbeque dinner for all just right in front of your doorstep.
Relive the moments for the day and plan ahead for the next day’s adventure.
