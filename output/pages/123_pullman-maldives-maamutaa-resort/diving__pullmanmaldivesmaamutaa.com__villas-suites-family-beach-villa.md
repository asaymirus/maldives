Family Beach Villa
Lush vegetation envelops a stylish contemporary hideaway. The only sounds are the gentle waves and the rustle of palms. A few steps lead to white sands and aquamarine Indian Ocean panoramas. The Pullman Family Beach Villa is a place to slow down and re-energize.
A family that stays together enjoys together. A king bed for the parents is connected to a bunk bed for the little ones. Because Pullman understands the need for family connectivity.
A separate room with a bunk bed gives the kids their own private playground – and some rest for peace-seeking parents.
The indulgent island bath tub with an airy interior matches a truly Maldivian family getaway.
At Pullman, there is always enough space for everyone. The Family Beach Villa outdoor living area provides ample choices of things to do.
