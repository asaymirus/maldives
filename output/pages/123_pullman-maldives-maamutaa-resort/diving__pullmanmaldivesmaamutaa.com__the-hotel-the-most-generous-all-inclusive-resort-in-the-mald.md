The Most Generous All-Inclusive Resort in The Maldives
Embark on a tropical getaway where nature sparks inspiration and adventure beckons. Experience the Maldives at a whole new level where the possibilities are endless and where the natural beauty takes your breath away.
Imagine a vacation where every whim is catered to, every desire fulfilled, and every moment is filled with pure bliss. Indulge in gourmet dining experiences, sip on a selection of over 80 alcoholic labels at stylish bars and unwind in spacious accommodations with stunning views.
Pullman Maldives Maamutaa is the most generous five-star all-inclusive resort in the Maldives. To get to the island, guests take a 55-minute flight from Male Airport to Kooddoo Airport in the Gaafu Alifu Atoll, followed by a 15-minute speed boat ride. This island playground spans 18 hectares of lush tropical vegetation and features 122 spacious villas including beachside, overwater, and unique underwater options. Guests can indulge in diverse culinary experiences across three exquisite restaurants and three bars. Throughout the year, exciting experiences and events are designed to instill inspiration and enjoyment among the guests staying on the island. Whether looking for an exhilarating adrenaline rush with a fully equipped water sports, dive center, workout programs, and fitness activities or wanting to wind down for an afternoon of rejuvenation at The Pullman Spa, guests are not confined to one type of holiday in the Maldives.

ACTIVITIES & RECREATION
SKILLZ (TEENS ONLY AREA)
A destination for teenagers and the young at heart. Rent a bike to leisurely explore the island or partake in exciting indoor activities while you’re here. Additionally, you can reserve the tennis court, karaoke room, and golf simulator room for your personal entertainment.
KID’S PLAYGROUND ( 4 to 11.99 YEARS)
Kids on this island are always part of the fun with a variety of daily activities tailored just for them. They can delight in a mix of indoor and outdoor activities designed to keep them engaged in their own special area. Daily nanny services are also available.
PULLMAN FIT
Stay active during your vacation by visiting our fully equipped 24-hour gym or join guided fitness experiences with the Skillz team. The resort offers a range of experiences, from beginner to intermediate sessions at various locations across the island.
