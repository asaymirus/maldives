Four Bedroom Ocean Pool Retreat
If life is all work and no play, it’s time to get away! the Four Bedroom Ocean Pool Retreat is a sprawling overwater villa outfitted with modern high-tech amenities and luxe accommodations for the perfect fun-filled, sundrenched gathering with family and friends.

THE CONCEPT
Within 1,474 square meters (4,835 square feet), The Four Bedroom Ocean Pool Retreat’s living and dining areas open up to the spacious sundeck with direct access to the ocean for snorkeling right off the villa. A Jacuzzi and outdoor shower flank one of the largest private overwater infinity pools in the Maldives.
At Pullman, fun means peak performance and wellness too: the villa comes with its own private gym, a Spa Room for in-villa treatments, and a steam room. Entertainment is abundant, care of large screen Smart TVs and a game room with a billiard table.
While staying at the Four Bedroom Ocean Retreat, guests are also treat to Pullman Maldives’ generous all-inclusive benefits which include unlimited dining and drinks at the resort’s three restaurants and three bars, a twice-daily replenished mini-bar with premium alcohol, and a slew of activities and excursions, from adventurous watersports to centering yoga classes.
Designed with the world’s New Entrepreneurs in mind, the Four Bedroom Ocean Pool Retreat is the ultimate island playground with everything needed for living your best life to the fullest in one of the most sought-after locations in the Maldives.
