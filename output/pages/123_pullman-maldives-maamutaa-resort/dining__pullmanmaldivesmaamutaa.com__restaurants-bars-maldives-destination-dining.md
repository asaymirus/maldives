Destination Dining
Embark on a culinary journey at Pullman Maldives Maamutaa, where destination dining is an artful experience tailored to your desires. Indulge in the ultimate privacy with a delectable floating breakfast served right in your villa, or elevate your evening with champagne and canapés overwater or underwater with our exclusive Aqua Villa Canapé Experience.
Ignite romance with bubbles and bliss as you unwind in a bath adorned with rose petals, accompanied by a refreshing glass of champagne. For a more casual and charming affair, enjoy a beachside picnic, toes in the sand, and flavors that linger in the sea breeze.
As the sun dips below the horizon, choose to celebrate the day’s end with a Sunset Soirée or opt for an intimate private dinner at Panorama Beach or Romeo and Juliet. Each dining experience is a symphony of flavors and ambiance, curated to create unforgettable moments against the breathtaking backdrop of the Maldivian sunset.
