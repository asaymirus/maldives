Fitness bootcamp 2021
Are you ready to set yourself for a successful 2021?
At Pullman Maldives we take fitness and wellness seriously, that’s why we are having the second edition of one of the best fitness & wellness bootcamps in Maldives to help you unlock your potential over the New Year period.
CHECK THE PROGRAM
WHAT IS THIS EVENT?
This is a
19 days holistic wellness & fitness bootcamp
that combines beach workouts, fitness classes, fun family workouts, nutrition classes, yoga and meditation. This is the perfect way to refresh and start 2021 the right way.
Get ready to push your boundaries with Sarah Hoey – Pullman Wellness ambassador- and Allaoua Gaham, Yoga instructor & Lululemon Ambassador in Middle East.
The event is starting on 31st December 2020 with a sunset yoga class, where Sarah will be leading an intention setting, continuing with a detox brunch on 1st January 2021 and a combination of fitness activities to set up for a successful and productive year ahead.
Your wellbeing is our priority and we are ready to help you prepare for a successful year ahead. There is no better place to be than Pullman Maldives Maamutaa to reconnect with nature, family and yourself this New Year.
CHECK THE PROGRAM
WHO IS THIS FOR?
This event is for all our guests that are looking to balance their performance and reconnect with themselves.
Perfect for all fitness levels combining gentle, medium to strong outdoors fitness workouts. Nutrition, relaxation, animal flow and Systema are part of the various activities you’ll be able to experience to condition your mind and body.
But that’s not all…
We also have some fun workouts for the little ones too, so get ready to have fun with all your family! And the best part: there’s
no extra costs
involved as this is part of our
generous all inclusive benefits.
Last year, Sarah spent 9 days on the island leading a series of unique
fitness activities
with a traditional Maldivian twist, including: beach workout, Stand up paddle boarding racing, meditation class in the middle of the Indian Ocean as well as some high intensity activities to push the boundaries like Aqua Fitness, Body Combat and much more.
TELL ME MORE
MALDIVIAN WORKOUT
Experience a complete outdoor workout inspired by the traditional Maldivian
“boduberu”
dancing followed by a beach bootcamp. To finish this one-of-a-kind workout, a coconut toddy collector will explain this tradition and provide you with fresh coconut water. A truly unique immersion to the Maldivian culture.
WATER WORKOUT
At Pullman Maldives we are taking the fitness concept to a whole new level. Get ready to unplug and recharge your mind and body with the calming breeze from the Indian Ocean. This workout covers aqua zumba and a stand up paddleboard challenge around the island.
