Dhivehi Heritage À la Carte Experience
At Phat Chameleon, our menu celebrates the rich culinary heritage of the Maldives through a modern, mindful lens. Rooted in wellbeing, balance, and responsible sourcing, our cuisine embraces a plant-forward philosophy, offering thoughtfully crafted vegetarian and vegan dishes alongside responsibly sourced seafood that honours local fishing traditions and respects the surrounding marine ecosystem.
We highlight key Maldivian ingredients such as coconut, curry leaf, lime, banana blossom, and breadfruit, weaving them throughout the menu to reduce food miles, minimise waste, and support sustainable, locally influenced sourcing. Each dish is designed to reflect a sense of place while delivering fresh, vibrant flavours that nourish both body and soul.
This inspiring and innovative gourmet cuisine, with a touch of fusion Maldivian flair. A truly atmospheric dining experience with a focus on long-term sustainability, ensuring that all ingredients are grown and sourced organically on the island and its surroundings, and then turned into something magical in the kitchen.
