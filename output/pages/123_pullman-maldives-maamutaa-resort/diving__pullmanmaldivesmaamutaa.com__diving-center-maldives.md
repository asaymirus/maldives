Diving & water sports
At Pullman Maldives we aim to create unique experiences, and our 5-star PADI certified Suvadiva Dive Centre is the perfect place to do so.
Our unique location make us one of the best places for diving in South Maldives, a truly unique experience for marine wildlife enthusiasts. This is the only place where divers could spot up to 13 species of sharks, and the only known location in the Maldives where you could dive alongside Spinner sharks – a rare breed famous for their acrobatic water skills.
Here, we are proud to be a 5 star
PADI certified
diving center, which means that we offer all the
PADI courses
from beginners to advanced up to Dive Master. But that’s not all… We also offer some specialty courses like Nitrox, DPV dive propulsion vehicle, side mouth and many more…
With 26 dive sites located along the Gaafu Alifu Atoll, Pullman Maldives Maamutaa Resort’s 5 Star PADI certified dive centre gives guests the unique opportunity to dive deep into the Indian Ocean and experience exotic marine life up close.
No trip to the Maldives is complete without a guided snorkeling tour of its enchanting underwater sights. Our experienced dive instructors are here to make you live an unforgettable experience and they also speak your language! From English, French, German, Italian, Spanish and Mandarin our international team will be happy to make you discover all the beauty that our lagoon has to offer.
