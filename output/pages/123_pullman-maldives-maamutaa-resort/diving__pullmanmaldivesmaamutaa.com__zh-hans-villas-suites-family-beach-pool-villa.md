Family Beach Pool Villa
A thatch-roofed getaway hides among palm trees and island plants. A few short steps lead to white coral sands and the aquamarine sea. The Pullman Family Beach Pool Villa is an idyllic island playground – for the whole tribe.
Intuitive contemporary design offers plenty of room for the family to stretch out. A separate kids’ room with a bunk bed offers exactly the right blend of privacy and company. High-speed Wi-Fi gives everyone the connectivity they need. And the Pullman Kids’ Club means adventure for them – and relaxation for you.
What better way to spend time with family than a cool dip, some invigorating laps or simply splash around? A large pool beckons for it all.
