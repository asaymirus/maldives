TRAVELIFE GOLD CERTIFICATION
Travelife Gold Certified Accommodations
We are proud to hold
Travelife Gold Certification
across all our properties—an achievement that reflects our ongoing commitment to responsible tourism, environmental care, and community impact.
Travelife is a globally respected sustainability certification aligned with the
UN Sustainable Development Goals
, awarded only after an independent on-site audit confirms full compliance with
over 160 criteria
. These cover everything from energy efficiency and waste reduction to ethical labor practices and local engagement. Reaching this milestone took coordinated effort across all our resorts and offices. We implemented measurable environmental systems, trained our teams, partnered with local suppliers, supported cultural and social inclusion, and worked to reduce water, energy, and single-use plastics.
Certification is not a one-time goal—it’s a long-term commitment to improvement, renewed every two years. For us, Travelife is more than a standard; it’s a meaningful part of our
ESG strategy
that builds guest trust, empowers our teams, and drives positive impact wherever we operate.
