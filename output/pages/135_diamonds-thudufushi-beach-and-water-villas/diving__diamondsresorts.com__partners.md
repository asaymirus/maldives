UNIVERSITÀ DEGLI STUDI DI MILANO BICOCCA
The marine environment is a precious gift to pass on to future generations, for this reason Planhotel has always been involved in its promotion and protection, also thanks to the support of marine biologists who, on the islands of Athuruga and Thudufushi, daily aware guests through special snorkeling activities and specific information moments.
In addition to this, on the island are carried out research activities together with the support of the University of Milano Bicocca that owns a research Centre in the Maldives, the MaRHE Center, recognized by the Maldivian government and the international scientific community.
Planhotel is proud to promote the scientific research by funding scholarships in Marine Biology to be held in Athuruga and Thudufushi under the supervision of the University of Milano Bicocca.
