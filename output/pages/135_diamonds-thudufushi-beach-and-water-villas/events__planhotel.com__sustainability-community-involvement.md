COMMUNITY INVOLVEMENT
Empowering Local Youth for Coral Conservation
Community involvement is key to our
corporate sustainability strategy
. Through the “Playing with Corals” project, a collaboration between MaRHE and Inter Campus of Inter Milan, local children and coaches learn about coral conservation through a mix of
marine education and sport
. Over two years, the program has evolved from land-based training with donated reef stars to hands-on coral planting in the ocean, giving participants a deeper connection to reef restoration.
At Sandies Bathala, the Marine Life Sustainability Challenge 2025 welcomed students from Ukulhas School for a full
day of learning and fun
. From interactive sustainability workshops and a “green walk” around the resort to deploying reef stars and
snorkeling the restoration site
, students experienced conservation in action. The day ended with beach games and meaningful conversations about marine careers and internships.
These initiatives are
planting seeds of awareness and ambition
, inspiring a new generation of ocean advocates across the Maldives. When local communities are empowered, the future of our reefs becomes brighter: one coral, one child, and one island at a time.
