SEAPLANE – CHILDREN
From Malé International Airport with just a 25-30 minute flight.
We kindly inform you that the standard operating hours of seaplane transfer is from sunrise to sunset, approximately from 07 am to 4 PM.
Price is per child (children under 2 years old fly for free). Only people below 12 years old are considered as children. From 12 years persons are to be considered as adults. Taxes included.
Please note the seaplane to reach resort from Malé Airport are weather dependent. Diamonds Resorts can not guarantee same day or short notice (below 48 hours) seaplane transfers from Malé to the resort.
