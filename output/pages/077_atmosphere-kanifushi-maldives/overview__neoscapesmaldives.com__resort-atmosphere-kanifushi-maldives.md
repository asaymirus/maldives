DESCRIPTION
Escape to Where Turquoise Waters Meet Untouched Tranquillity
Discover a five-star, all-inclusive retreat in the secluded Lhaviyani Atoll. Just a 35-minute seaplane ride from Malé, Atmosphere Kanifushi stretches across 2km of palm-fringed coastline, surrounded by a vibrant lagoon and coral reef.
Stay in spacious beachfront or overwater villas crafted from natural materials and designed with a blend of traditional Maldivian charm and modern style. Cycle through lush pathways, soak in the ocean breeze, and explore the turquoise waters teeming with marine life.
Ideal for families and couples alike, the Platinum Plus™ Plan offers exceptional value, covering premium dining, drinks, excursions, snorkelling, and more.
Whether you seek adventure or total relaxation, Atmosphere Kanifushi delivers an unforgettable Maldives experience in one of the most peaceful and untouched atolls.

Dining
The Lantern Bar – Specialty Bar
Perched above a tranquil lagoon, The Lantern Bar invites you to unwind in a lantern-lit setting as the sun sets over the horizon. This elegant overwater extension of Just Veg serves artistic Asian vegetarian platters and Ayurvedic “Elixirs,” which are flavourful, healing mocktails crafted by a specialist. With panoramic views and a calming atmosphere, it is the perfect place to relax and reconnect with nature.
The Spice – Main Restaurant
The Spice is the resort’s all-day dining venue offering a diverse selection of Asian, European, and Maldivian cuisine. Enjoy generous buffet spreads, five live cooking stations, and fresh grills served in a bright, spacious setting with views of the lagoon. Daily theme nights add variety,
including Asian Street Food, Indian Ocean Night, and Around the World. With friendly service and a relaxed atmosphere, The Spice delivers a satisfying culinary journey from breakfast through dinner.
The Liquid
The Liquid serves an array of exotic cocktails and premium alcohol brands and has a large international wine list. It’s one of the most popular spots at Kanifushi to unwind, soak up the atmosphere and embrace the island lifestyle. Every evening, there is a Fish ‘n’ Chips night, while theme nights, disco nights and live performances are regular occurrences throughout the year.
The Sunset
Admire stunning lagoon views as you indulge in light à la carte dishes by the pool during the day. Come evening, this restaurant offers fine dining experiences showcasing the best of Asian and Mediterranean cuisine, including juicy grilled meats. The cuisine is complemented by a broad selection of drinks, from cocktails to Russian vodka and Mexican beer.
Just Veg – Pure Vegetarian Restaurant
The Maldives’ first à la carte vegetarian restaurant, Just Veg offers innovative plant-based cuisine inspired by Mediterranean, Arabic, and Indian traditions. Helmed by Chef Fabrizio Marino, it features a dedicated Jain menu and beautifully plated dishes that impress vegetarians and meat lovers alike. Set over the ocean, it promises a serene and refined dining experience.
Teppanyaki Grill
Gather around a sizzling hotplate at our overwater Teppanyaki Grill, where skilled chefs entertain with flames, flips, and juggling while preparing fresh seafood, Wagyu beef, and classic Japanese dishes. Enjoy an interactive dining experience paired perfectly with chilled or hot sake. Located within the Pier Six complex, this vibrant spot transforms every meal into a captivating culinary show.
Pier Six – Overwater Seafood Restaurant
Enjoy fresh seafood and signature dishes like Tuna Tataki and Reef Fish wrapped in banana leaf, all in a stunning overwater setting. With ocean-inspired interiors and options for all dietary needs, Pier Six delivers a stylish, flavour-packed dining experience.
Ceylon Bliss – Traditional Sri Lankan Street Food
Nestled under a shady tree by the ocean, Ceylon Bliss offers authentic Sri Lankan Street food made from time-honoured recipes. Enjoy fiery spices, creamy curries, and light meals in a rustic setting with swings and natural wooden seating. Perfect for a relaxed meal while soaking in sea breezes and ocean views, especially during happy hour.
The Sunset - Pool Bar
Located by the adults-only pool with panoramic lagoon views, The Sunset offers light à la carte dishes by day and transforms into a fine dining venue at night. Enjoy Asian and Mediterranean cuisine with expertly grilled meats, paired with an extensive drinks menu. Highlights include Moroccan tea, flavoured shisha, and the weekly White BBQ Night under the stars.

Highlights From The Resort
Kanifushi Experiences
Atmosphere Kanifushi offers a classic Maldivian escape with white sand beaches, turquoise lagoons, and vibrant coral reefs. It is the perfect place for relaxation, adventure, and cultural immersion.
Marine Adventures and Water Sports
Swim with turtles, snorkel through colourful reefs, or enjoy adrenaline-filled water sports. The PADI-licensed Dive & Sail center offers dive courses, certifications, and expert-guided excursions.
Akiri Spa by Mandara
Set in a tropical garden with ocean views, the spa offers healing therapies from India, Bali, Hawaii, and Thailand. Enjoy signature treatments, Ayurvedic rituals, and daily scrubs made from fresh island ingredients.
Cultural Discovery
Join local island excursions across the Lhaviyani Atoll. Visit Naifaru, the most populated small island in the Maldives, or explore the legends of Kurendhoo Island.
Sunset Cruise and Romantic Moments
Sail across calm waters and watch the sky shift from turquoise to warm pink and orange. Celebrate your love with a beachside renewal of vows, framed by the beauty of the island.
Wellness and Daily Activities
Begin your day with sunrise yoga. Join fitness sessions like aqua aerobics and body sculpt, or take part in beach games, diving prep, and tournaments in tennis, football, and chess.
Evening Entertainment
Enjoy live guitar and vocal acts, karaoke, DJ sets, and open-air movie nights under the stars.
Kids Club
Children can enjoy creative workshops, adventure games, birthday events, and guided play hosted by caring and energetic staff.
