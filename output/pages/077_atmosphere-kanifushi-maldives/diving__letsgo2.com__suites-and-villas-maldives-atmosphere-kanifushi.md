About the resort
Atmosphere Kanifushi is set amidst an expansive turquoise lagoon, surrounded by long uninterrupted stretches of pristine white sand. It's known for its generous premium all-inclusive package, which includes a choice of over 80 wines.
The resort is made up of 150 villas and suites, which all come with sunset views. Every one of the villas and suites has direct beach access. They're all surrounded by lush tropical vegetation for maximum privacy, and come with stunning open-air bathrooms.
The Sunset Junior Suites are perfect for couples, with your own open-air veranda leading out onto the beach. The Sunset Family Villas are made up of two interconnecting Beach Villas, giving you plenty of space for a comfortable family holiday in the Maldives.
You'll find a range of activities on offer daily here. Whether it's a morning gym class or a cocktail making class - there's something to suit every mood.
Discover the colourful underwater life that Maldives holidays are famous for with unlimited complimentary snorkelling excursions throughout your entire stay. The professionals at the resorts' PADI Dive Centre will gladly take you to a number of well-regarded dive sites nearby, easily reached by boat. There are also lots of motorised and non-motorised watersports to enjoy - including sailing, paddle boarding and water-skiing.
Feel well and truly pampered at The Akiri Spa by Mandara, which offers a choice of luxurious health and beauty treatments. The Spa also has a sauna, steam room, jacuzzi and hair salon.
Children are kept entertained at the Atmosphere Kanifushi Kids Club, with lots of fun, adventurous, learning experiences on offer - like nature walks, tennis lessons and treasure hunts. For under 4s, babysitting services are available. Teens and active adults can also enjoy the facilities at The Clubhouse - the resorts' sports and recreation centre - which includes a fully-equipped gym and tennis courts, as well as daily organised activities such as dodgeball, volleyball and table tennis tournaments.
With a choice of six restaurants, it's safe to say you'll be well and truly spoilt when it comes to dining at Atmosphere Kanifushi. The variety of dishes laid on for the daily buffet at The Spice is nothing short of astonishing, with live cooking stations and theme nights, too. For each week you stay, you'll also be treated to a complimentary fine-dining experience at The Sunset restaurant. Other dining venues include Just Veg, the Maldives' first vegetarian restaurant; the menu here is good enough to convert even the most die-hard carnivores.
This beautiful island is reached by a 30-minute seaplane transfer from Male Airport. The transfer is a holiday highlight in itself, giving you the chance to see the incredible colours of the Maldives islands from above.

restaurant and bars
Experience a special a la carte meal at The Sunset restaurant or enjoy a flavoursome meal at Just Veg, the Maldives first speciality vegetarian restaurant. Two further restaurants serve meals throughout the day with live cooking stations and theme nights to whet-your appetite. Enjoy a cocktail overlooking the ocean at The Liquid bar.
Ceylon Bliss
A new pop up restaurant along the island - Ceylon Bliss offers authentic Sri Lankan cuisine.
Just Veg
The first pure vegetarian restaurant in The Maldives, enjoy a delightful selection of Mediterranean, Arabic and Indian dishes.
Teppanyaki Grill
Enjoy an authentic Teppanyaki experience, share a table surrounded by a large hot plate where a chef will prepare your meal with a theatrical twist.

Meals at Teppanyaki are not included on the all inclusive plan.
The Liquid
The Liquid offers an array of exotic cocktails and premium alcohol including a wide selection of wines from around the world. You will also find snacks and delicious smoothies served throughout the day.  The Liquid is one of the most popular locations on the island to unwind and enjoy a drink.
The Pier Six
The stunning restaurant has been designed in the shape of a Manta and offers speciality seafood dishes along with stunning views across the ocean.

experiences
The island's Clubhouse Sports & Recreation Centre provides a variety of leisure activities including tennis, beach volleyball, sunrise yoga and movie nights. Table tennis tournaments and scavenger hunts are held here for the more competitive guest. A fully equipped gym is staffed by experienced trainers to assist with your work out. For something more relaxing try the Akiri Spa where you will find peace and tranquillity beside the calming lagoon waters.
The resort's PADI Dive Centre will be able to assist you with your underwater exploration around several excellent dive sites in close proximity to the hotel.  There is a full range of motorized and non-motorized watersports to choose from for some fun in the warm tropical waters. With six excursions to opt from and a fantastic Kids Club, there is plenty of adventure for everyone at Kanifushi.
