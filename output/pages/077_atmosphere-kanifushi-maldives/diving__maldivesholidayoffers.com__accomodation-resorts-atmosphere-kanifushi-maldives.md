Resort Overview
Think of an idyllic tropical island with age-old palm trees, vivid green cover and beach villas spread along an endless turquoise lagoon. Atmosphere Kanifushi Maldives brings that dream to life, with vast living spaces accentuated with Maldivian interiors.
Located in the sparsely populated Lhaviyani Atoll, the resort offers a hassle-free five-star experience with Platinum Plus™, a premium all-encompassing holiday plan. Choose from a selection of spacious villas and suites with private outdoor areas and the option of pools. Relax at the spread-out garden spa complex or lounge by the two swimming pools, one of which is adults only, situated at the sunrise and sunset side of the island.
The plan comprises a fine collection of premium wines and spirits, a combination of buffet, fine dining and theme night dining options, clubbed with an array of activities ranging from excursions and sunset fishing to snorkelling and nonmotorised water sports.
