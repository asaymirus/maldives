DESCRIPTION
Discover an island of wonders.
Anantara Kihavah Maldives Villas is an award-winning luxury resort located in the pristine Baa Atoll, surrounded by turquoise lagoons, powdery white beaches, and vibrant coral reefs. Set within a UNESCO World Biosphere Reserve, the resort offers extraordinary marine experiences, including manta ray encounters at nearby Hanifaru Bay and snorkeling directly from the island’s spectacular house reef.
Guests can choose from spacious beach villas, overwater pool villas, and expansive private residences designed for couples, families, and groups seeking exceptional privacy and comfort. From underwater fine dining and stargazing experiences to wellness journeys at the overwater spa and thrilling ocean adventures, Anantara Kihavah delivers one of the Maldives’ most unique and luxurious island escapes.

Highlights From The Resort
UNESCO Biosphere Reserve Location
Situated within Baa Atoll, the resort offers extraordinary opportunities to encounter manta rays, whale sharks, and vibrant marine ecosystems at nearby Hanifaru Bay.
House Reef & Golden Wall
The island’s spectacular house reef is home to over 300 fish species and 130 coral varieties, including Hawksbill turtles, manta rays, reef sharks, and the famous Golden Wall dive site.
SEA Underwater Restaurant
Enjoy one of the Maldives’ most iconic dining experiences inside an underwater restaurant and wine cellar surrounded by marine life.
SKY Overwater Observatory
The Maldives’ first overwater observatory offers guided stargazing experiences through the country’s most powerful telescope.
Anantara Spa
The overwater spa offers holistic wellness journeys, Ayurvedic therapies, Thai massages, saunas, plunge pools, and rejuvenating rituals inspired by nature and traditional healing.
