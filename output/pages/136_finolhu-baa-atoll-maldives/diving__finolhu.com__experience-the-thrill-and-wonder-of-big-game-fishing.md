Experience the thrill and wonder of big game fishing
16. March 2022
A Maldivian adventure at Finolhu Baa Atoll
Over the centuries, the people of the Maldives have learned to live in harmony with the ocean, turning to it as their primary source of sustenance, while respecting the natural balance that allows the flora and fauna within it to flourish.  Drawing on the local wisdom and traditional values that have been passed down from generation to generation of Maldivian fishermen, Finolhu is now offering guests the opportunity to join its Big Game Fishing excursions for the chance to experience the thrills and wonder of a genuine Maldivian adventure.
Kept to a limit of four guests per trip to ensure a more
exclusive
experience while adhering to local fishing practices, Finolhu’s Big Game Fishing
excursions
take you on an exhilarating pursuit of some of the fastest and largest predatory fish in the tropical waters of the
Baa Atoll
.  From the
stealthy
mahi-mahi (dorado) to giant trevally and lightning-quick tuna, the local game fish each pose different challenges
to
test your skills and provide opportunities to learn about
centuries-old
fishing methods from the locals.
Whether you choose to try the hand-line fishing method perfected by the Maldivians or prefer to stick with your own tried-and-tested techniques, a variety of fishing methods and newly-fitted boats are available for this excursion.  A local Fishing Master accompanies each trip, to share his knowledge and insights and ensure every excursion is a fun, safe and successful adventure.
Finolhu’s Big Game Fishing Excursions last from 3 to 4 hours – ensuring you net a good catch while exerting enough energy to build-up a hearty appetite in anticipation of savouring the fresh-from-the-ocean catch.
Post-adventure, select from a specially prepared menu to have your catch cooked and served up for lunch or dinner the following day at Beach Kitchen – the perfect way to end an exhilarating Big Game Fishing experience – by feasting on your freshly caught fish, from the Indian Ocean to your plate.
