Ten Reasons to Stay at Seaside Finolhu Baa Atoll Maldives
13. September 2024
Looking for the perfect tropical escape? Seaside Finolhu in the Baa Atoll, Maldives, is a paradise of endless possibilities, combining adventure, relaxation, and luxury. Here are ten reasons why Seaside Finolhu should be your next dream getaway.
Crab Shack: Toes-in-the-Sand Dining Delight
For a truly Maldivian dining experience, the
Crab Shack
offers a secluded retreat on the sandbank, just a short stroll or boat ride from the main island. Enjoy fresh seafood with your toes in the sand, surrounded by the stunning lagoon views, making every bite an indulgent experience.
Romantic Starry Escape in the Beach Bubble
Spend a magical night under the stars in
Finolhu’s beach bubble
, the first of its kind in the Maldives. Imagine a
private beach dinner
, a cosy night under the stars, and waking up to breakfast by the ocean — it’s the ultimate romantic getaway.
Explore UNESCO’s Hidden Gem
Seaside Finolhu is located near the
UNESCO
-protected Hanifaru Bay, one of the world’s most vibrant marine life destinations. Snorkel or dive to witness an underwater paradise of colourful coral reefs and diverse marine species just waiting to be explored.
Swim with Marine Majesties
Get up close with
gentle giants
like Manta Rays and Whale Sharks in Hanifaru Bay. This unforgettable encounter with marine life will leave you in awe, creating memories you’ll treasure forever.
Playground for Everyone
Whether you’re traveling with family, friends, or your significant other, Finolhu offers fun for all ages. From beach volleyball and
Indoor Golf
to
tennis
and private beach dinners, every guest can find something to love.
A Place Where Design Meets Luxury
As the first design hotel in the Maldives, Seaside Finolhu blends sophisticated aesthetics with island luxury. Every corner is Instagram-worthy, offering you the perfect mix of modern elegance and tropical charm.
A Place to Rejuvenate
Fehi Spa
at Seaside Finolhu is your personal sanctuary for relaxation and transformation. With treatments ranging from soothing massages to wellness therapies like yoga, the spa offers a holistic escape to rejuvenate your body, mind, and soul.
Culinary Delights
The resort’s four restaurants offer diverse
dining
experiences, from fresh seafood to exotic spices. For a unique dining adventure, indulge in the “5 Degrees North” experience, where you dine under the stars, surrounded by the tranquil lagoon waters.
Maldives’ Longest Sandbank
Take a romantic stroll along the Maldives’ longest sandbank, stretching nearly two kilometres. This scenic spot offers breath-taking views and secluded moments, perfect for couples looking to exchange vows or share a sunset kiss.
The White Party
A highlight of Seaside Finolhu’s social scene, the White Party is held at the Crab Shack, featuring live entertainment, fire dancers, and complimentary sundowners. Celebrate in style as the sky turns golden, offering the perfect blend of luxury and fun.
Seaside Finolhu Baa Atoll is where luxury meets adventure, offering guests endless opportunities to relax, explore, and create cherished memories. Whether you’re here for a romantic escape, a family getaway, or just to soak in the island’s natural beauty, Finolhu promises an unforgettable experience at every turn.
