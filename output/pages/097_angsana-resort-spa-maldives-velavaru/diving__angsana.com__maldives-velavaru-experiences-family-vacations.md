Family Adventures
Spark endless curiosity and create lasting memories
Our family-friendly haven offers a captivating array of activities designed to entertain, educate, and connect.
Terra Kid's Club
Unleash your child's inner explorer at Terra Kids Club, our vibrant hub for nature enthusiasts. From thrilling scavenger hunts to eco-conscious craft sessions, our dedicated team guarantees endless learning and creativity throughout your stay.
View Activities
Junior Divers
Introduce your little adventurers (ages 8-9) to the wonders of the underwater world with a playful bubble-making lesson in a shallow lagoon. For kids aged 10 and over, we offer the PADI Discover Scuba Diving course, allowing them to explore the depths under professional guidance.
Price
USD 150 per child
Banana Boat Ride
Strap yourselves in for a side-splitting ride with the whole family aboard a bright yellow Banana Boat. Shriek with laughter as you bounce over the waves, feeling the spray of the ocean on your faces.
Price
USD 55 per person for 15 minutes
Bird Feeding
Gather around our bird sanctuary and watch in delight as lovebirds with vibrant red, yellow, and green wings flit around, seeking a tasty treat. It's a charming experience for the whole family to enjoy.
Daily at 10:30 am and 4:30 pm
