Location
Angsana Velavaru
Transport
Velana International Airport (MLE)
Velana International Airport is the main international airport in Maldives. It is approximately 45 minutes from the resort by seaplane.

Transfers from Velana International Airport:

The resort will arrange the mandatory round-trip transfers by seaplane according to the guest's international flight.

Shared Seaplane Transfer Charge
• Children (2–11 years): USD 365 nett per person
• Adults (12 years and above): USD 580 nett per person
• Infants (below 2 years): Complimentary
Points of interest
Whale Shark Point
55 km | 1 hour, speedboat 
Located at the southern tip of Ari Atoll, Whale Shark Point is one of the best places in the world to swim with whale sharks. It is about 1 hour from the resort by speedboat. Please contact our Dive Centre for inquiries.
Meedhoo Island
1 km | 5 minutes, speedboat 
Meedhoo Island in Dhaalu Atoll is an island south of the Maldives capital, Malé. Please contact our reception for inquiries about local island tours.
