Spa
Head to the award-winning Angsana Spa and enjoy a soothing treatment in one of its 8 outdoor spa pavilions.
Opening Hours: 11:00 am - 8:00 pm
Dive Centre
Angsana Velavaru is a secluded Maldives dive resort, offering 30 diving spots to explore around the South Nilandhe Atoll. The resort’s PADI 5-star Gold Dive Centre is professionally managed and offers classes for divers of every experience level.
Opening Hours: 8:00 am - 5:00 pm
Terra Kid's Club
Bring your children to our Terra Kid's Club for a day filled with fun and games. Our Maldives family resort offers a wide range of indoor and outdoor activities, from sandcastle building and T-shirt painting to treasure hunts and beach games.
Opening Hours: 9:00 am - 6:00 pm

Angsana Gallery
Take home beautiful souvenirs to remember your trip to the Maldives, from handicrafts to resort apparel. Choose from a range of signature spa amenities and recreate the Angsana Spa experience at home.

Marine Lab
The Angsana Maldives Marine Lab at Velavaru is dedicated to protecting coral reef ecosystems through research and conservation efforts. Local marine life, including sea turtles and corals, are closely tracked and monitored. Diverse reef species are also protected through research and careful follow-up. You can play your part by attending a marine biology class or exploring the coral reefs on a guided snorkelling safari. Take part in the resort's coral replanting programme and plant corals in the lagoon.
Opening Hours: 8:00 am - 05:00 pm
Ferry Service
Enjoy easy access to the island's offerings with our hourly ferry service. This convenient service whisks you between your InOcean Villa and the main island, allowing you to explore the vibrant island scene at your own pace. Discover delicious restaurants, pamper yourself at the renowned Angsana Spa, or try thrilling water sports – all just a short ferry ride away.
