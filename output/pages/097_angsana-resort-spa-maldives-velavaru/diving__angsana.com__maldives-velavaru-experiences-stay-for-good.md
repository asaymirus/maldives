Stay for Good
Angsana Velavaru
At Angsana Velavaru in the Maldive, guests can immerse in a coral planting experience with the resort’s marine lab team, contributing to reef restoration and ocean conservation.
Citizen Science Snorkelling
Join a guided snorkelling adventure with our resident marine biologists at Angsana Velavaru. Explore nearby reefs while recording sightings of iconic marine species such as turtles, rays, and reef sharks. This hands-on citizen science activity blends exploration, allowing travellers to play a role in protecting marine biodiversity while discovering the Maldives’ underwater treasures.
Coral Planting
Take part in coral planting with our marine experts and contribute to reef restoration in the Maldives. Attach coral fragments onto frames placed in the lagoon, helping reefs regenerate and supporting diverse marine life. This immersive activity not only nurtures the ocean but also create a personal connection, leaving behind a meaningful conservation legacy.
Marine Conservation Talk
Gain valuable insights in the Maldives’ delicate marine ecosystems during an engaging session with our passionate Marine Lab team. Discover ongoing coral restoration, sustainability initiatives, and the importance of reef health. This interactive experience deepens awareness of ocean conservation while inspiring travellers to take part in protecting one of the world’s most vibrant underwater habitats.
Reef Clean-Up
Support marine conservation firsthand by joining a reef clean-up lead by our dedicated marine team at Angsana Velavaru. Actively help remove debris from the lagoon and nearby reefs, safeguarding coral ecosystems and promoting marine health. This rewarding activity empowers travellers to protect the ocean’s beauty while contributing to the long-term wellbeing of the Maldives’ environment.
