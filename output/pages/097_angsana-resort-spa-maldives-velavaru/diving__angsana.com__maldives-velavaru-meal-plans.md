Meal Plans
Indulge in Exceptional Culinary Journeys
Discover flexible meal plans at Angsana Velavaru, Maldives. Choose from Bed & Breakfast, Half Board, Full Board, or All-Inclusive options, tailored to elevate your dining experience in paradise.
View Meal Plan Details
Bed and Breakfast
Begin your day with a refreshing breakfast at Kaani or Azzurro Restaurant, where vibrant flavours and serene surroundings set the tone for a perfect Maldivian morning.
Half Board
Embrace the ease of effortless dining with our Half Board meal plan. Start your day with breakfast at Kaani or Azzurro Restaurant, then enjoy a leisurely evening meal at Kaani Restaurant, Azzurro Restaurant, or Kuredhi Pool Bar, each offering a unique ambiance and menu to suit your mood.
Full Board
Indulge in a full sensory dining adventure with our Full Board meal plan. Begin with a satisfying breakfast at Kaani or Azzurro, and allow yourself to be swept away by the diverse, mouthwatering flavours of lunch and dinner at Kaani, Azzurro, or Kuredhi Pool Bar, where each bite tells a story.
All-Inclusive Dine
Savour the ultimate convenience with our All-Inclusive Dine plan, designed to enhance every moment of your stay. From bountiful breakfasts to buffet or à la carte lunches and dinners, indulge in a variety of dishes at Kaani, Azzurro, or Kuredhi Pool Bar. Enjoy a wide selection of beverages at the bars and the added indulgence of a daily replenished in-villa minibar stocked with soft drinks and snacks.
All-Inclusive Premium
Elevate your dining experience with the All-Inclusive Premium plan, offering unparalleled variety and value. Delight in breakfast, lunch, and dinner paired with premium beverages, including an extensive selection of wines and cocktails. Explore the Dine Around experience, unlocking exclusive access to all restaurants, including Funa and Azzurro. Enjoy enhanced perks like spa and recreation credit, discounted seaplane transfers, and curated destination dining savings for a truly exceptional stay.
Azzurro Restaurant & Bar is exclusively available to guests staying in InOcean Villas, with access also included as part of the Dine Around option for All-Inclusive Premium guests.
