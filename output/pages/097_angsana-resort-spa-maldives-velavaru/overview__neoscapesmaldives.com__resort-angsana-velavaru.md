Discover Velavaru: 101 Things to Do.
Located in the pristine South Nilandhe Atoll, Angsana Velavaru is a tropical island retreat surrounded by crystal-clear lagoons, vibrant coral reefs, and breathtaking marine life. Combining contemporary Maldivian luxury with immersive ocean experiences, the resort offers the perfect balance of relaxation, adventure, and family-friendly hospitality.
Guests can choose from elegant beachfront villas tucked among lush tropical gardens or the resort’s iconic standalone InOcean Villas suspended above the lagoon. Whether snorkeling vibrant reefs, indulging in rejuvenating spa rituals, exploring marine conservation activities, or enjoying romantic dining experiences, Angsana Velavaru creates unforgettable island escapes for couples, families, and groups alike. With its welcoming atmosphere and exceptional marine experiences, the resort truly captures the spirit of the Maldives.

Highlights From The Resort
Iconic InOcean Villas
The resort’s standalone overwater villas are uniquely located away from the main island, creating an exclusive floating sanctuary above the lagoon.
Marine Conservation & House Reef
Guests can explore vibrant marine life through coral planting, reef clean-up activities, marine talks, snorkeling safaris, and encounters with whale sharks and manta rays.
Angsana Spa
The award-winning Angsana Spa offers rejuvenating treatments inspired by Asian wellness traditions, creating a peaceful sanctuary for relaxation and renewal.
