Kuredhi Pool Bar
From Poolside Bliss to Nightlife Beats
Kuredhi Pool Bar is the heart of Angsana Velavaru’s social scene. By day, it’s the perfect spot to unwind with a refreshing cocktail or enjoy a casual meal from the à la carte menu, all while soaking up the poolside vibes.
As the sun sets, the bar transforms into the resort’s entertainment hub, hosting lively evenings with live music, DJ parties, and movie nights. Whether you're looking for laid-back relaxation or upbeat energy, Kuredhi Pool Bar offers the best of both worlds.
