InOcean Pool Villa
Serenity by the Sea
Perched above the Indian Ocean, this overwater villa offers a new definition of the Maldives escape, with a spacious king-sized bedroom, an indulgent bathroom, and a stylish living area with exclusive rooftop access.
The expansive terrace, featuring private dining and sunbeds, offers a secluded retreat to bask in uninterrupted ocean views and timeless tranquillity. Relax on the wooden poolside deck, where a hammock gently sways above the water.
The infinity pool stretches over crystal-clear turquoise waters, providing direct access to the coral reef and the perfect setting for a romantic floating breakfast.
