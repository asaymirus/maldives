Indulge In Diverse Dining Experiences
Savour the flavours of the Mediterranean at Azzurro, or explore the vibrant tapestry of Pan-Asian cuisine at Funa. For a casual atmosphere, the Kuredhi Pool Bar offers all-day dining and drinks.
Craving something extraordinary? Indulge in a romantic beach side lobster dinner, a magical floating breakfast, or an unforgettable rooftop barbecue under the stars.

Embrace True Serenity
Step into Angsana Spa, a sanctuary of tranquility where time seems to melt away. Our expert therapists will guide you on a personalized journey of rejuvenation with a range of pampering massages and holistic treatments. Let the soothing sounds of nature and the gentle touch of our therapists melt away your stress.

Uncover Maldivian Heritage
Immerse yourself in the rich tapestry of Maldivian culture.  Experience the infectious energy of a traditional Boduberu drumming performance and delve deeper with a guided tour to nearby islands, connecting with the local way of life in South Nilandhe Atoll.
Learn More
Join Conservation Activities
Visit our on-site Marine Lab and discover its dedicated program to protect endangered marine life and restore coral reefs.  Learn from our passionate experts about their vital work in ensuring the future of the Maldives' breathtaking underwater world.
Learn More
Excursions & Water Sports
Craving a a special kind of thrill?  Zoom across the azure waters on a jet ski or explore our lagoon by kayak. As the sun dips below the horizon, set sail on a luxurious boat, sip on chilled champagne, and witness a breathtaking Maldivian sunset.
Learn More
Explore The Underwater World
Explore the exceptional visibility of our house reef, encountering a dazzling array of marine wonders and majestic creatures in their natural habitat.  Seeking an even more extraordinary adventure?  Embark on a thrilling whale shark trip and witness these gentle giants glide through the ocean.
