Seize the moment and plunge into the uplifting world of our Maldives resort and spa, the ideal destination playground for a sensational year-round tropical escape.

Where is Angsana Velavaru Maldives located and how can we reach the resort?
Angsana Velavaru Maldives is located in the pristine South Nilandhe (Dhaalu) Atoll.
We offer seamless transfers from Velana International Airport in Malé to our resort via a scenic seaplane journey, followed by a short 5-minute speedboat ride. We are situated approximately 144 km away from Velana International Airport (a 40-minute journey by seaplane, subject to weather conditions).
Seaplanes are operated by a third-party company and are designed for water take-off and landing.  Therefore, their flying times are restricted to daylight hours (between 6:00 am and 3:30 pm only) by the Maldives Civil Aviation Authority.
Please note that villa rates do not include transfers.
Charges for shared or private seaplane transfers are mandatory.

What is the weather like in the Maldives throughout the year?
The Maldives enjoys a tropical climate with warm temperatures year-round. While the dry season typically lasts from November to April and the wet season runs from May to October, weather forecasts can be hard to predict accurately due to the region's tropical nature. Occasional rain showers can occur throughout the year, but they are usually brief, and the seas remain calm for most of the time.
Which languages are spoken by the island hosts at Angsana Velavaru?

Can I fly a drone at the property?
Guests are welcome to fly drones within the designated area. To ensure a safe and enjoyable experience for all, flights are limited to a maximum height of 30 feet, in consideration of regular seaplane activity. Drones may be registered upon arrival, and we kindly ask guests to inform reception before each flight to confirm ideal flying conditions.
