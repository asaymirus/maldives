Why Ayada Maldives Is One of the Best Resorts for Group Getaways
The Maldives may be best known as a honeymoon destination, but travellers in the know are discovering that it’s also a remarkable place for group holidays. Whether you're celebrating a milestone birthday, hosting a family reunion, or gathering friends for a luxury escape, Ayada Maldives stands out as one of the best resorts in the Maldives for group travel, offering not only five-star accommodations but also the space and flexibility to make shared memories seamless and special.
One of Ayada’s greatest strengths is its size. Located in the Gaafu Dhaalu Atoll, the island is significantly larger than many Maldivian resorts. This allows for a more natural layout, with villas spread generously across the island, ensuring privacy for individuals while keeping the group connected. With over 120 villas in various configurations—including beachfront family villas, two-bedroom suites, and overwater options—it’s easy to customise stays for groups of any size or age mix.
Groups often find comfort in knowing they won’t have to compromise. Everyone gets their space and their preferences catered to—whether that means daily yoga at sunrise, afternoon snorkelling excursions, or private cocktail hours on the beach. The island’s diverse activities range from diving and paddleboarding to cultural performances and group spa experiences, making it easy to create a balanced itinerary that includes both action and relaxation.
Dining at Ayada is another highlight for groups. Guests can dine together at the elegant buffet restaurant Magu, enjoy an informal afternoon at Zero Degree by the pool, or reserve private dining experiences just for their group. The resort’s team excels at organising tailored meals—from intimate in-villa dinners to beachfront barbecues or even extravagant setups on a private sandbank. These moments, beautifully set and flawlessly executed, are often what guests remember most.
The service is another reason why Ayada is among the top resorts in the Maldives for groups. Each villa has a dedicated butler, and the resort staff are well-versed in accommodating large parties. Whether coordinating multiple spa appointments or managing arrival times across different flights, the team handles logistics gracefully and professionally.
Ayada Maldives blends the exclusivity of a private island with the energy of a vibrant community, making it the perfect choice for those who want to experience paradise not alone, but with the people who matter most.
