The Ultimate Honeymoon Destination
The islands of the Maldives are some of the most beautiful in the world and have topped the list of ‘the best honeymoon destinations’ year after year. So if there is one place in the world to take the most epic honeymoon ever it’s the Maldives, and a honeymoon at
Ayada Maldives
cannot be topped. Think luxury villas, vibrant
marine life
, endless ocean views with the most stunning shades of blue you’ve ever seen, some of the best service in the world and much much more!
Our beautifully remote tropical island greets honeymooners with white-sandy beaches, crystal-clear waters and tropical jungle greenery. Whether you're looking for some R & R with your new spouse or something a little more adventurous, we've got you covered at Ayada Maldives.
Top 10 things to do on honeymoon at Ayada Maldives:
Relax on the most pristine white sandy beach you’ve ever seen
Get certified in
scuba diving
at the nearby dive center and take your new skills for a test-drive
Swim with exotic sea creatures – sea turtles, baby reef sharks, a variety of rays & many colourful tropical fish
Explore the crystal clear lagoon in a glass bottom kayak
Enjoy a romantic
floating tray breakfast
in your own private infinity pool
Pamper yourself with a luxurious couples treatment at
AySpa
(one of the most sought after spas in the Maldives)
Sip on a cocktail or two at
Zero Degree
whilst watching the sun go down
Indulge in an unparalleled
culinary experience
in one of our eight outlets
Experience a romantic picnic at a nearby uninhabited island
Soak up the sun on a floating sundeck set in the middle of the beautiful lagoon
Now that you have chosen to spend your honeymoon at Ayada Maldives, the only decision left to make is if you want to go for one of those picture-perfect
ocean villas
, which allow you to jump off your private deck straight into an azure ocean. Or if you prefer to stay on dry land, just steps away from the sugary soft sands, then a
beach villa
may be the best option for you.
