Join our brand ambassador program
Are you an influencer, travel blogger, YouTuber, or an online personality passionate about travel? Ayada Maldives invites you to become a part of our exciting Brand Ambassador Program. This is your chance to partner with one of the most exquisite resorts in the Maldives and earn attractive rewards.
Affiliate program details
The affiliate is entitled to 10% commission on all bookings made via his/her personal link.
Only the villa rate is commissionable. Other services (transfers, meal packages ...) and taxes are non-commissionable
The affiliate may choose to get the commission paid to his/her bank account OR to use it to book a stay at Ayada Maldives in which case the amount will be tripled and offered as a credit.
The commission is credited to the affiliate account at the beginning of each month for the bookings stayed during the previous month.
Unpaid, canceled bookings are not entitled to a commission
All bookings must be made via the Ayada Maldives Official website
Bookings made via a 3rd party website or a travel agency are non-commissionable
Payout & Credit example
Total commissionable value: $10,000
Choose from:
10% commission: $1,000
Or:
Credit for Affiliate personal stay: $3,000 (commission x 3)

This scheme only applies to bookings made on the website, not on Booking.com, Expedia, Agoda, or any other online or offline travel agents.
Track Your Success: Your unique code and link will be the key to tracking the bookings you generate.
Support & Guidance: Our team is here to support you with all the necessary information and materials for your online promotions.
