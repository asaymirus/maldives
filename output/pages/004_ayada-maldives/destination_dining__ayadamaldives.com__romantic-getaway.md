Welcome to Ayada Maldives
Ayada Maldives is an exceptional 5-star beach resort in the Maldives, offering a picture perfect romantic getaway with once in a lifetime experiences. With it’s pristine nature, beautiful villas with private pool and wide range of experiences, Ayada Maldives is a top choice for honeymoons and romantic getaways.

beautiful villas with pool
Our villas and suites offer larger than average luxurious interiors, stylishly finished with Turkish and Maldivian design elements. Beach villas and suites feature a secluded private garden with pool and outdoor shower - just a few steps away from the beach. Over-water villas and suites offer amazing views over the open ocean and also have private pools and hammocks over the ocean.
