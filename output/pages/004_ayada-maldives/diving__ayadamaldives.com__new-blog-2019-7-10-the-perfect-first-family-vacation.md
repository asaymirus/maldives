Gracie visited

Why did you choose Ayada Maldives?
I wanted to take Romeo somewhere safe and inviting for our first family holiday and Ayada Maldives felt like a second home.
What did you do?
We spent the days lounging by the pool at
Zero Degree
and exploring the reef by snorkeling just off of our
Sunset Ocean Suite
.
We also enjoyed
romantic beach dinners
at sunset & took a
dolphin spotting and snorkeling excursion
which was such a magical experience.
What was your overall experience?
The staff could not have been more welcoming, the
food
was incredible and our villa was amazing! Overall it was fantastic and I was sad to leave, we all cannot wait to soon come back and visit again.
