Resort Clinic
You are in good hands
The health and safety of our guests is a top priority, this is why we offer complimentary medical assistance on site.
At Ayada Maldives we have a fully equipped clinic and skilled medical staff to attend any injuries or health concerns. Our clinic is suitable for first aid and to treat minor injuries.
In the unlikely case of more severe issues, the resort team will make all the necessary arrangements with the nearest hospital and coordinate with the local health authorities to ensure the smoothest possible procedures.
Please note the nearest regional hospital is Dr. Abdul Samad Memorial Hospital, located in Thinadhoo, 60 minutes by speed boat from the resort.
Clinic opening hours:
- 9:00 - 18:00 every day
- On-call assistance 24/7
