AySpa: The Multi-Award Winning of Spa at Ayada Maldives
At the heart of Ayada Maldives lies AySpa, a tranquil 3,500 sqm wellness sanctuary crafted to rejuvenate your body, mind, and spirit. Designed with natural textures and inspired by traditional healing philosophies, AySpa invites guests into a space that is both grounding and luxurious. With terrazzo floors, warm timber finishes, and cool marble hues, every detail enhances the sense of indigenous beauty and calm.
This is more than a spa—it’s a destination within a destination.
An experience rooted in tradition
AySpa offers some of the most unique wellness facilities in the Maldives, including:
The first Vichy shower room in the country
A private Turkish Hammam, complete with an intricately carved marble plinth
Eight spacious treatment villas, some with hydrotherapy baths and open courtyards
Each treatment room has been designed for complete privacy and indulgence. Whether you're treating yourself solo or sharing a couple’s experience, every room includes a steam shower, private bathroom, and a sunken outdoor bath under the sky.
Your spa journey continues after your treatment
At AySpa, relaxation doesn’t end when your massage or facial is over. You’re welcome to spend time in the beautiful relaxation area, complete with:
Heated vitality pools
Cold plunge pools
Segregated steam rooms and saunas for men and women
Teas, fresh juices, and wellness drinks available at your leisure
This is the perfect space to extend the benefits of your treatment in silence, comfort, and total stillness.
An expansive menu of wellness offerings
Whether you seek deep tissue massage, Balinese healing rituals, or Turkish Hammam ceremonies, AySpa’s expert therapists tailor treatments to your preferences and needs. The open spa menu includes:
Facials using premium botanical products
Ayurvedic therapies
Body scrubs and wraps
Signature couples’ treatments
Prenatal massage option
Beauty services including manicures, pedicures, and hairstyling
Every visit to AySpa is an invitation to reconnect—with yourself, your senses, and the healing power of touch.
Award-winning excellence
AySpa has received numerous international spa awards, consistently praised for its setting, professionalism, and the extraordinary quality of its treatments. It’s a must-do experience for wellness travelers and anyone seeking a meaningful, nourishing escape during their stay.
Discover a sanctuary that celebrates the art of well-being. Let AySpa be one of the most memorable parts of your Ayada Maldives journey.
