Ile de Joie
Wine and Cheese Island
Lovers of the finer things in life will adore this tiny over-water open-air cheese and wine bar over the lagoon. Truly an ‘island of joy’, this is home to the island’s fine wines cellar where oenophiles will enjoy perusing the eclectic wine list.
The collection of specialty imported, hand-selected cheeses make this the perfect venue for open-air pre- or post-dinner relaxation over a fine cigar from the well-stocked humidor.
