Enjoy lunch at a whole new level at our Zero Degree poolside restaurant
Lunch is a meal you don’t want to skip and you definitely won’t want to skip lunch while staying at
Ayada Maldives
. Imagine yourself sitting at a poolside table, with your feet are in the warm infinity pool while our waiter serves you the most beautiful lobster you have ever seen. This is how you have lunch at Ayada Maldives.
Local, organic and delicious
We take both food and our
environment
seriously. This is why we put extra efforts into using the best local ingredients whenever possible. While we grow some of
our vegetables and herbs
here on our island, other ingredients are purchased from local farming communities on the nearby islands. Our chefs regularly visit these farms to monitor production standards and the quality of the produce.
Other ingredients that are not available from nearby sources are ordered from the most trusted sellers in Dubai - same as other top restaurants are sourcing their ingredients from.
A real foodie experience
Lobster, pasta, salads, burgers and delicious seafood dishes - you will find it all in superb quality and served beautifully at our
Zero Degree
bar. Elevate the experience by choosing one of our signature cocktails or non-alcoholic mocktails like “Make me cool” or our personal favorite “Fresh mint ale”.
We also cater for most dietary needs - vegan choices, gluten free versions of our dishes are all available. Please feel free to inform your waiter about your dietary requirements and our chef will personally attend to your request.
