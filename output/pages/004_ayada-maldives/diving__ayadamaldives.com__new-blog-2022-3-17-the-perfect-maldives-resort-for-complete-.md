The perfect Maldives resort for complete relaxation
When was the last time you truly pampered yourself with a relaxing break? Away from the noise, your work, notifications, and news? When was the last time you had a day without your mobile or laptop checking social media updates or feeling the pressure to respond to emails and other messages?
Get away from it all
You need a break. Here at Ayada Maldives, we have created an environment and curated experiences that are relaxing and refreshing for both your body and mind.
Just a short flight and speedboat trip away from the busy Male atoll, our island offers complete peace and serenity to people who want to get away from the hustle and bustle of their daily lives. Surrounded by pristine white-sand beaches and lush tropical greenery, Ayada Maldives is a luxury private island resort where you can forget all the stress, and appreciate all the real pleasures of life.
Unspoiled Maldives natural wonders
Due to the minimal air and sea traffic, as well as for the lower amount of resorts, the southern atolls of the Maldives tend to be even more pretty compared to the ones near Male, or in the north.
Ayada Maldives is one of the southernmost resorts in the Maldives, located on an island that is surrounded by rich marine life and unspoiled natural features. Here, you can experience dining on your very own sandbank, visit uninhabited islands, or local islands if you wish so.
Diving, surfing, or even snorkeling around the island are just a lot better in the south of Maldives. Another interesting fact about Ayada is that the island is all-natural. This means that it boasts rich natural flora and fauna - you can see flying foxes, garden lizards, tree crabs, and a large variety of sea creatures living here.
A wide range of activities
We have everything on our island to relax your body and mind, to refresh and rejuvenate yourself. Free and private yoga classes, beautiful beaches, villas with private pools, watersports activities, surfing, excursions, and a lot more.
If you prefer not to be busy or overly active, you can choose to have a refreshing yoga session in the morning, relaxing on the beach till lunch, then visit our beautiful AySpa for a rejuvenating massage therapy, followed by cycling around the island. The choice is yours, we made everything available for you.
Unparalleled food
Food at Ayada is something you will definitely love. Magu, our main buffet restaurant is packed with varieties of delicious meals every morning for breakfast, as well as dinners. In fact, every evening there is a different theme, including traditional Maldivian dishes, Asian delicacies, Italian, Turkish and more.
Besides the buffet, you will find a superb Asian a-la-carte restaurant, an overwater fine-dining restaurant specializing in Mediterranean, and international cuisine, Sea Salt BBQ popping up on the beach two times a week where you can enjoy lobster and other seafood alongside beef, lamb, and chicken.
We have a range of other restaurants on the island, including the guests' favorite Zero Degree, where you can enjoy snacks, pizzas, burgers, salads with cocktails right by the large pool, overlooking the Indian Ocean. If you want to try Teppanyaki, you can book an evening at Mizu.
If you love wine then you must try Ile De Joie, our over-water wine cellar.
Dining options do not end here. We can arrange specialty dining for you and your partner on the beach, on a dhoni (local word for boat), on a sandbank, or even on a private island.
If you choose to enjoy some bites in the comfort of your own villa, you can simply use our app to order food from our 24/7 in-villa-dining service.
We are happy to accommodate your special requests
Everyone has their own ways to enjoy their well-deserved vacation, and we are most happy to accommodate any special requests. Before making a reservation, feel free to discuss any options and ideas you may have with our sales and reservations team - and while on the island, your personal butler will be delighted to assist you with anything you might need.
