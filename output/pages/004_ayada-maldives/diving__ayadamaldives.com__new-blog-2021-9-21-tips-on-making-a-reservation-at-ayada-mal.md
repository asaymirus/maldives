Tips on making a reservation at Ayada Maldives
Here, at Ayada Maldives, guest experience and satisfaction is our main priority. We believe that a great experience starts before you arrive at our resort - as early as your first interaction with us.
This is why we put significant efforts into providing all the necessary information and support to everyone interested in staying at Ayada Maldives. Be it our website chat, social media messaging, calling us, or simply sending an email with your questions to our reservations team, we will be happy to attend.
Let our reservations team help arranging a perfect stay
Planning a vacation in the Maldives might be challenging especially for first-timers. You might have tons of questions regarding the airport arrival, the transfers, and of course about the resort and its facilities. Whenever you need any sort of information about the resort, how to get there, our island experiences, or anything related to your Maldives vacation, our reservation team will be very happy to assist in answering all your questions and offering options that will fit your needs.

Getting accurate information is not always easy and cannot be guaranteed when you book via a 3rd party like travel agencies or 3rd party online hotel booking sites. It is always recommended to contact the resort and book directly.

How to make a reservation at Ayada Maldives directly?
An increasing number of guests choose direct booking over online hotel booking portals and travel agencies when realizing how easy and convenient it is to make a reservation.
Making a direct reservation will not only save you significant time, but will also avoid you being a victim of internet fraud, and will guarantee your payments are safe and have well reached the resort and illuminate in kind of potential bad surprises on arrival to the Maldives.
Booking on our website
The fastest way to check our rates and availability, as well as to make a reservation is to use our website booking engine.
Simply click on the "Book Now" button on the top navigation bar on our website to access our online booking system. The system will enable you to search any dates, and check prices and available special offers based on the number of guests and villa types you are interested in.
Using our website booking engine, you will be automatically eligible for our exclusive discounts and other direct booking benefits.
We do our best to keep website prices the most attractive among other online booking platforms. Should you find lower rates anywhere else, feel free to contact our reservations team - they will work out a better price for you.
Once you have found your ideal villa and price for your stay, all you need to do is fill out your details to register your booking.
Shortly after, our reservation team will contact you with the available payment methods and further steps to guarantee your reservation at Ayada Maldives.
Besides booking your stay, you can also pre-book various enhancements and experiences at a discounted price.

Request a quotation for your stay, on our website
If you are unsure about your stay dates and other details of your planned visit to Ayada Maldives, then the best option is to request a quotation.

If you prefer to discuss every detail with our reservations team, you can also do that by sending an email to info@ayadamaldives.com. Our team will be happy to respond to any questions or to assist in making special arrangements for your visit to Ayada Maldives.
