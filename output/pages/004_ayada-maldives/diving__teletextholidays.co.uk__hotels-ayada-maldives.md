About this hotel
Step off the speedboat at Ayada Maldives, and something feels different. The architecture, the welcome ritual, and the scent drifting from the spa. There's a Turkish warmth to this place that most Maldives resorts don't have. Set on Maguhdhuvaa Island, it's a 5-star private island resort that has built a distinct identity around its Ottoman influences. However, it never feels like a theme park. It consistently appears among the best hotels in the Maldives for service and overall experience.
Every villa comes with a private pool and a dedicated personal butler from arrival. Guests can choose all-inclusive Maldives packages or opt for half board if they prefer flexibility. It works well for both couples and families. The resort attracts those who want five-star comfort along with something more off-beat than the standard overwater villa package.

Location & getting there
Ayada Maldives sits in the southern Gaafu Dhaalu Atoll. The resort offers a more secluded escape than many resorts closer to Male. Reaching the island takes a 55-minute domestic flight from Male, followed by a 25-55-minute speedboat transfer. The slightly longer journey has its advantages. A quieter setting, fewer crowds, and a peaceful beachfront arrival.
Every Ayada Maldives villa comes with a private pool 🏊 and dedicated butler service 🤵 as standard. The differences between room categories come down to location 📍, size 📏 and outlook 🌅, rather than any compromise in quality.
Room Types
🌿 Garden Villas-
At 101 sqm, Garden Villas sit within lush tropical gardens 🌴 close to the beach. They’re a solid starting point for guests who prefer a more natural, grounded setting over an overwater position. A good choice for those booking all-inclusive Maldives holidays ✈️ on a slightly tighter budget while keeping the same luxury standards.
🏖️ Beach Villas & Suites-
Ranging from 103 to 156 sqm 📏, these villas offer direct beach access 🏝️, soft sand underfoot and outdoor showers 🚿. The Sunset Suite 🌅 faces west, making it a popular option for couples wanting uninterrupted evening views without stepping up to an overwater villa.
🌊 Ocean Villas & Suites-
These overwater villas 🛖 range from 108 to 172 sqm and feature glass floor panels 🔍 plus a hammock suspended above the lagoon 🩵. The Ayada Maldives Ocean Suite ✨ delivers the classic Maldives overwater experience and is ideal for guests seeking that iconic stay.
👨‍👩‍👧‍👦 Family Suites-
Available on land 🌴 and over water 🌊, these spacious multi-bedroom suites give families and groups more room to spread out without giving up the private pool 🏊 and butler service 🤵 included with every villa. The Sunset Family Ocean Suite 🌅 is especially popular for larger groups wanting generous overwater space.
👑 Royal Ocean Suite-
The resort’s flagship accommodation ✨, a two-level suite 🏡 set at the end of the jetty 🌊, offers panoramic sea views 🌅 and complete privacy 🤍. As the highest room category, it sets the standard for an ultra-luxury Maldives escape.
Food & drink
🌍 Magu-
The resort’s main all-day restaurant, serving the Ayada Maldives buffet across breakfast, lunch and dinner. Expect themed international nights that rotate throughout the week, keeping things varied during longer stays.
🌊 Ocean Breeze-
An overwater à la carte restaurant specialising in modern European cuisine. Set directly above the lagoon, it’s one of the most atmospheric spots for a romantic dinner or relaxed evening meal.
🥢 Kai-
Serving Far East Asian cuisine in a quieter, more intimate setting. A good option for guests who prefer a calmer dining atmosphere in the evenings.
🔥 Mizu-
A Japanese teppanyaki grill set on the beach and one of the resort’s most popular à la carte restaurants. Booking ahead is strongly recommended due to high demand.
🏖️ Zero Degree-
A relaxed poolside à la carte restaurant, ideal for convenient daytime lunches between swims or beach time.
☕ Ottoman Lounge-
A distinctive venue serving authentic Turkish coffee, speciality teas and shisha, offering one of the clearest reflections of the resort’s Turkish heritage.
🧀🍷 Ile de Joie-
A specialist venue focused on cheese and wine pairings, with a smaller, slower-paced atmosphere that suits a leisurely evening.
The 🔮Crystal all-inclusive tier covers buffet meals, lunch at Zero Degree and Ocean Breeze, selected beverages, snorkelling equipment and kayaks; 💎Diamond extends to afternoon tea, shisha and wider dining access across the venues.
Kids club & family facilities
🎨 Zuzuu Kids Club keeps younger guests entertained with supervised activities, including arts and crafts, games, sports and themed fun. There’s also a dedicated children’s pool for splashing around. Age rules may vary, so it’s best to check with the resort when booking your Ayada Maldives family holiday.
🌊 The shallow, calm lagoon near the beach is ideal for younger swimmers and makes paddling a little easier for families. 🍽️ At the main restaurant, highchairs and a children’s menu are available to make mealtimes simpler. 👶 Babysitting services can be arranged for an extra charge, subject to availability. It’s a good idea to request these services when booking, especially during busy travel periods.
Spa, wellness & fitness
🌿 AySpa is one of the standout features at Ayada Maldives. Spanning around 3,500 sqm, it includes one of Maldives’ first Vichy showers, a private Turkish Hammam, treatment rooms and peaceful relaxation spaces. The hammam is a real highlight that reflects the resort’s Turkish heritage.
Most spa treatments come at an extra cost, so it’s worth setting aside a separate budget if spa time is a priority for you. 🏋️‍♀️ Alongside the spa, there’s a gym and yoga studio, with guided sessions available for guests. 🧘 The wellness facilities suit both guests who want to fully unwind and those who prefer to stay active during their Maldives package holiday.
📌 Some spa areas may have age restrictions, so it’s best to check this when booking.
Sports & activities
Ayada Maldives offers a great mix of activities, especially for guests who enjoy being on the water. The resort has access to excellent dive sites and seasonal surf spots in the southern atolls. 🏋️‍♀️ Free activities include tennis, football, yoga, gym access, snorkelling, kayaking, water chess and fitness classes. Snorkelling equipment is included for all-inclusive guests.
🐠 Paid experiences include scuba diving, surfing, water skiing, boat hire, dolphin spotting trips, desert island picnics and visits to local islands. 🎤 In the evenings, guests can enjoy a nightclub, karaoke and live entertainment. 📌 Most water sports and excursions come at an extra cost. Surfing is seasonal and depends on conditions in the southern atolls. It’s worth booking popular activities in advance, especially during busy travel periods.
Things to do near Ayada Maldives
🐢 Ayada House Reef (50 metres)-
Step straight into clear waters and snorkel among turtles, reef sharks and colourful fish. No boat needed.
🤿 Snorkelling & Diving Trips-
Explore nearby reefs and marine spots around Gaafu Dhaalu Atoll, known for its rich sea life and clear waters.
🏄 Surfing Excursions (10-45 minutes)-
Visit popular surf breaks such as Blue Bowls and Tiger Stripes, best for intermediate and experienced surfers.
🏝️ Desert Island Picnic-
Enjoy a peaceful escape to a nearby sandbank with a private picnic or romantic meal.
🐬 Dolphin Spotting Cruise-
A sunset trip where spinner dolphins are often seen playing in the water.
🚤 Local Island Visit- Vaadhoo (10.8 kms)-
Discover local Maldivian culture, crafts and traditional snacks on a nearby inhabited island.
