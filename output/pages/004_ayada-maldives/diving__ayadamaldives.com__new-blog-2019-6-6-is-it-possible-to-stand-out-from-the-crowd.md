Is it possible to stand out from the crowd? Ayada does...
We love hearing what our guests have to stay about their experience at
Ayada Maldives
and we think you will too. One of our guests recently visited with his family and this is what they had to say….
“There are over 100 resort hotels in the Maldives… Is it possible to stand out from the crowd? Ayada does.”
And it does so not with grand over-the-top extravagances and massive
ocean villas
, but rather with a subtle touch of warm, friendly, and efficient service, extreme comfort,
beautiful food
, and an overall value-for-money offering that is as good as anything I’ve ever experienced.
“It’s a remarkable property in every way”
We had high expectations – based on previous guest reviews, photographs, and the general setting of Maldives – but all perceptions were surpassed.  The accommodation was fabulous – comfortable beds, luxurious linen with a personalized selection of pillows, spacious and trendy bathrooms, substantial and functional deck, rim flow pool, and incredible views.
I’m pretty certain that the
Watersports Activity Centre
provided a wide array of exciting activities, but we found it impossible to give up time from our incredible snorkeling experiences enjoyed right from the deck of our
Ocean Villa
.
Snorkeling is world class with phenomenally diverse
ocean life
and daily encounters with black tip reef sharks, eagle rays, and incredibly personal experiences with sea turtles.
“The food was phenomenal”
From
Magu’s
sumptuous buffet with continually changing themes – remarkable variation in styles, cultures, and preparation; to an over-water cape-cod style offering at
Ocean Breeze
with bright and spacious decor, extensive wine selection, and the freshest of fish; or an extensive lighter meal selection at the poolside restaurant
Zero Degree
.
With a feast of different chefs preparing such incredible food, it was amazing how interactive and willing they were to spend time with guests and prepare additional customized food for our youngest child.
“This seems to be the overriding theme at Ayada – superior personalized service.”
Across the board, from the
landscaping
team to top management, every member of staff made you feel so welcome, always greeting us with welcome smiles and friendly engagement.  It’s clear that the owner and management are very hands-on, focusing intently on staff training and selection.  It really is something that separates Ayada from one of the large chain hotels.
Every staff member we had the pleasure of interacting with was fantastic, friendly, efficient, well mannered, and engaging and went out of their way to ensure our holiday was above expectation.
Despite the amazing setting, wonderful accommodation, and superb food, it was the staff that really made our holiday. A true testament to a very well run, owner-managed hotel.
This was a remarkable holiday and I cannot recommend Ayada highly enough.
The Maldives has a massive selection of branded chain hotels and is an extremely pricey destination, but Ayada delivers a top-of-the-charts five-star value-for-money experience.
