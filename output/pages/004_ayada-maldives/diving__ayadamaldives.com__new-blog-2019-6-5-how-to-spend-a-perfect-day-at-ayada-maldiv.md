How to spend a perfect day at Ayada Maldives?
You might think that the Maldives is only about beaches, turquoise waters and
overwater villas
. While some resorts offer only these, at
Ayada Maldives
you will have the chance to experience a lot more.
Morning
Right after waking up and stepping outside onto the terrace of your villa or suite, you will be tempted to splash into your private pool or straight into the ocean before even thinking about breakfast.
Talking about breakfast, you can have a variety of options:
Eating at
Magu
, our main restaurant
Ordering breakfast to your villa
Ordering our super popular floating tray breakfast and eat in your pool
Going out to the beach for a tropical breakfast full of healthy fruits
After breakfast
Besides relaxing at the beach or in your private pool, you can choose from a wide range of things to do. We suggest you experience as much as possible to make the most out of your stay at
Ayada Maldives
.
If you are looking for something truly local then board our
dhoni
(traditional Maldivian boat) and head out for a
dolphin spotting
and snorkeling trip. If your priority is relaxation then book a massage or treatment at our multiple award winning
AySpa
.
If you want to enjoy snorkeling around the island then visit our
water-sports cente
r where our team will provide fins and masks free of charge. Glass-bottom kayaks are also available to hire at the water-sports center.
Diving
trips and introductory diving courses are also available at the same location.
Lunch
We serve a la carte lunch at
Zero Degree
, our pool bar with fantastic views and a huge white-sandy beach. A large variety of delicious meals are available here, including burgers, pizza, pasta, salads and vegetarian dishes. Depending on the seasonality, buffet lunch is available in
Ocean Breeze
our over-water restaurant.
Afternoon
Most of our guests take it easy in the afternoon, indulging in afternoon tea at
Ottoman Lounge
, relaxing on the beach, being pampered in the
spa
, enjoying their pools or getting prepared for the evening
excursions
.
Evening
Sunset is our absolute favorite time of the day. A wonderful opportunity to take epic sunset photos and to enjoy cocktails, non-alcoholic mocktails or healthy juices in
Zero Degree
.
If you want something more active, then join our
sunset cruise or sunset fishing trip
which will be an experience you will never forget. If you are not sure which evening excursion to take, you can always ask your butler who will provide more information to aid in your decision making.
Lovers of fine wine and cheese will definitely love
Ile de Joie
, our over-water wine and cheese island where a curated variety of wines are available for tasting.
Dinner
“Where to eat tonight?” This is a question you will be asking every evening during your stay at
Ayada Maldives
, as we have quite a large variety of restaurants to choose from.
Dining options:
If you want a rich and tasty buffet dinner then go to
Magu
, our main restaurant
For fine Mediterranean and seafood choices, visit
Ocean Breeze
For authentic Asian flavors,
Kai
is the place to go
For a spectacular teppanyaki dinner, book your seats at
Mizu
For a beach bbq with lobster, go to
Sea Salt
every Tuesday and Friday
For a romantic dining experience, book a
private dining experience
with your very own chef
So many options… in order to avoid confusion, we recommend trying a different one every day.
