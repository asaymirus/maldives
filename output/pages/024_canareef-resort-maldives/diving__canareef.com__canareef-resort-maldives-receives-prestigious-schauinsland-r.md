Canareef Resort Maldives is thrilled to announce its recent recognition as a top hotel partner and Partner of Excellence 2023 by
Schauinsland Reisen
, one of Germany’s leading tour operators. This esteemed accolade underscores our commitment to providing exceptional service and unforgettable guest experiences.
About the Awards:The Partner of Excellence award places
Canareef Resort Maldives
among the top 25 hotel partners of SLR worldwide. Additionally, the Top Hotel Partner award signifies our status as one of the most sought-after destinations in the SLR portfolio, recognized for excellence in hospitality on a global scale.
About Schauinsland Reisen:
As the 5th largest German flight tour operator,
Schauinsland Reisen
boasts a rich history of pioneering spirit, creativity, and fairness. Their dedication to quality and fairness extends to both customers and partners, making them a trusted name in the travel industry.
About Canareef Resort Maldives:
Nestled in the breathtaking southern-most atoll of Addu, Canareef offers an unparalleled escape to paradise. Accessible by domestic flight or direct flight to Gan International Airport, our resort boasts turquoise waters, freshwater lakes, and unspoiled nature.
With its own private picnic island and one of the longest beaches in the Maldives, Canareef is a haven for relaxation and adventure. Guests can choose from 271 spacious beach villas, each featuring open-air bathrooms and stunning views of the surrounding landscape.
Indulge in delectable cuisine at our two restaurants, enjoy personalized in-villa dining experiences, and explore 25 unique dive spots teeming with marine life. From historical sites to protected areas, Canareef offers a diverse range of activities to cater to every traveler’s preferences.
We are honored to receive these prestigious awards from
Schauinsland Reisen
and remain committed to delivering exceptional experiences to our guests. Come and experience the magic of Canareef Resort Maldives for yourself, where every moment is a celebration of relaxation, and adventure.
