# Canareef Resort Maldives Appoints Brenda Prosper as New Human Resources Manager - Canareef

Scraped: 2026-10-04
Resort: Canareef Resort Maldives
Page type: diving

---

Canareef Resort Maldives
is delighted to announce the appointment of Brenda Prosper as the new Human Resources Manager. With a rich background in hospitality and HR management, Brenda brings a wealth of experience and a passion for fostering positive work environments to her new role.
Brenda began her illustrious career in 2013 as an HR Coordinator during the pre-opening of the 5-star Savoy Resort & Spa in Seychelles. Her journey continued at Creole Travel Services, a leading Destination Management Company in Seychelles, where she served as an HR Officer. This role marked the start of a dynamic career path that led her to the UAE, where she contributed her expertise to the 5-star Anantara Desert Island Resorts in Abu Dhabi and Bin Majid Resorts in Ras Al Khaimah.
Her dedication and skills were further recognized during her tenure as Assistant HR Manager at Komandoo Island Resort in the Maldives. Brenda’s exceptional performance led to her promotion to HR Manager after just one year. Following this success, she returned to Seychelles to join the 5-star Anantara Maia Seychelles as Assistant People & Culture Manager, continuing her commitment to enhancing employee experiences and organizational culture.
Brenda’s career is characterized by her unwavering commitment to creating a positive work environment and working with diverse cultures. Her leadership style, rooted in integrity, has earned her the respect and trust of her colleagues. As HR Manager at Canareef Resort Maldives, Brenda is dedicated to fostering a culture of open communication, continuous learning, and employee well-being and development.
“I am honored to take on this position and look forward to continuing to work closely with the team to further strengthen our commitment to both our employees and guests,” Brenda said. “My goal is to ensure that every team member feels valued and empowered, and that our resort continues to be a place where excellence thrives.”
Canareef Resort Maldives
is confident that Brenda’s expertise and vision will play a crucial role in enhancing the resort’s operational excellence and enriching the overall guest experience. Her appointment underscores the resort’s commitment to investing in its people and creating an exceptional work environment.
