# Canareef Resort Maldives Celebrates Environmental Awareness Week - Canareef

---

In a world where environmental issues are at the forefront of global concern, Canareef Resort Maldives stands as a beacon of environmental responsibility and sustainability. As part of our ongoing commitment to protecting our planet, we are delighted to announce an Environmental Awareness Week with a lineup of activities, commemorating significant environmental days throughout the upcoming week.
In partnership with Maldives National Airline, Maldivian Air, and the Maldives’ renowned Environment warrior Ms. Saazu Saeed, Canareef has planned a series of exciting and impactful events from June 1st to June 8th. Join us in commemorating these significant environmental days and contribute to the preservation of our beautiful globe.
World Reef Day – June 1: Coral Transplanting
To kick off the week, we are hosting a Coral Transplanting event at Bar-Kuda Beach on the 1
st
of June. This initiative aims to preserve and restore our precious coral reefs, essential ecosystems that are under threat from climate change and human activities.
World Environment Day –June 5:
A 100 for the Environment! Tree planting.
On this day, guests will have the opportunity to contribute to our reforestation efforts by participating in planting 100 Fruit and Vegetable Trees throughout Canareef. By planting trees, we not only mitigate climate change but also create habitats for local wildlife and contribute to sustainability.
World Ocean Day – 7 June:
Awareness Program and Nurdle Hunt
Canareef will host an enlightening awareness program for its Guests, staff and children from a local School. The event will be led by renowned Environment and Marine Life Expert, Ms. Shaziya Saeed (Saazu) at Meera Conference Hall and Meera Beach. Saazu’s extensive experience and passion for marine conservation will inspire guests, staff and students alike to take action in protecting our oceans. The program will include a Nurdle Hunt at Meera Beach, highlighting the urgent need to combat plastic pollution in our seas.
Global Wellness Day – 8 June:
Global Wellness Day 2K Run & Walk
We believe that wellness extends beyond personal health to encompass the well-being of our planet. Join us for a 2-kilometer run/walk from Meera Bar to Bar-Kuda Beach, promoting a holistic approach to wellness and sustainability.
About Saazu:
Saazu is a multifaceted professional, encompassing roles as a Dive Instructor, Surfing Coach, Athlete, and TEDx Speaker. Her dedication to environmental conservation and marine protection has earned her numerous accolades, including awards for her contributions to the diving and tourism industries.
About Canareef Resort Maldives:
Nestled in the pristine southern-most atoll of Addu, Canareef Resort Maldives offers an unparalleled retreat to paradise. With 271 spacious beach villas, two restaurants serving delectable cuisine, and 25 unique dive spots teeming with marine life, Canareef promises an unforgettable experience for every traveler.
About Maldivian Air:
As the national airline of the Maldives, Maldivian Air plays a pivotal role in connecting travelers to our beautiful islands. With daily flights to 16 airports within the Maldives and seamless air travel connections to India and Bangladesh, Maldivian Air ensures convenient access to Canareef Resort Maldives and other destinations across the archipelago.
At Canareef Resort Maldives, we are committed to fostering a culture of environmental stewardship and sustainability. Through initiatives like Environmental Awareness Week, we strive to inspire positive change and make a meaningful impact on the world around us.
Stay tuned for more updates and get ready to participate in these fantastic events. Your involvement can make a world of difference.
