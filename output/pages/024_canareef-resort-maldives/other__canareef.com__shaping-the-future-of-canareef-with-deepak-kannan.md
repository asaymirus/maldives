Canareef Resort Maldives is proud to spotlight the remarkable journey of
Deepak Kannan
, our
Assistant General Manager
, whose hospitality career spans over two decades across some of the world’s most renowned hotel brands and destinations.
Deepak’s story begins in the year 2000, when he stepped into the world of hospitality as a
Front Office Agent
with
Radisson Hotels
. Driven by curiosity and a genuine passion for guest service, he soon found himself in the heart of luxury, working with the iconic
Burj Al Arab by Jumeirah
in Dubai as a
Guest Service Executive
– a landmark property known for setting the gold standard in global luxury.
His next chapter unfolded with the
IHG Crowne Plaza brand
, where Deepak dedicated an impressive
14 years
, rising through the ranks from
Front Office Shift Leader to Front Office Manager
across various Emirates in the UAE and later spending
two years in Oman
. These years served as the cornerstone of his leadership style – blending operational excellence with an intuitive understanding of guest expectations.
In
2018
, Deepak took on his first
Hotel Manager role
with
Bin Majid Hotels and Resorts
in Ras Al Khaimah, UAE, where he was instrumental in driving team performance and enhancing guest experiences. He further honed his strategic and people-focused leadership during
General Manager tenures
at
Excelsior Downtown Dubai
and
Platinum Coast Hotel
in Fujairah.
With his deep expertise, he was also called upon in
2021
to serve as a
Hospitality Consultant
for a major convention center in
Chennai, India
, where he helped set up their rooms division and operational framework – showcasing his versatile skill set beyond hotel environments.
Today, at
Canareef Resort Maldives
, Deepak brings with him a global mindset, operational know-how, and a deep-rooted commitment to team empowerment. He envisions a future where the resort thrives not only through its natural beauty but also through a unified, people-first culture.
In his own words:
“To achieve a one vision, one team spirit and a strong people-centric culture that will empower the talent we have into shaping Canareef to be the most desired resort destination in the region.”
With leaders like Deepak Kannan shaping its future, Canareef stands poised to become a benchmark in Maldivian hospitality – where every guest experience is a reflection of dedication, teamwork, and heartfelt service.
