Discover the Spirit of Adventure in the Heart of Paradise
Welcome to a world of endless adventure at Canareef Resort. Enjoy many experiences designed to immerse you in the vibrancy of island living, the tranquility of our spa, or the thrill of marine expeditions around Gan Addu Atoll, Maldives.
Dolphin Cruise
An enchanting dance of nature with graceful dolphins amongst the waves
Experience the magic of the Maldives with our unforgettable Dolphin Cruise. The waters surrounding our island are home to an abundant variety of marine life, including playful Spinners and Bottle-nosed dolphins along with majestic pilot whales.
As the sun begins to dip below the horizon, set sail on an adventure to witness these incredible creatures in their natural habitat. Marvel at their acrobatics as they leap from the water, their graceful silhouettes illuminated by the warm hues of the setting sun. Their friendly nature and energetic displays are sure to leave you spellbound, creating a spectacle that is as captivating as it is unique.
This is more than just an excursion – it’s an opportunity to connect with nature in its purest form. As the dolphins dance in the ocean around you, you’re sure to create memories of the Maldives that will last a lifetime.
Don’t miss out on this must-book tour, guaranteed to add a dash of magic to your tropical getaway. Embrace this enriching experience, filling your holiday with joy, wonder, and unforgettable moments.
Join us on our Dolphin Cruise – where the ocean becomes a stage, and the dolphins, the stars.
Immerse in the local culture with a tour of Addu City. Admire the modern boatyards of Maradhoo and the laid-back lifestyle of Feydhoo. You can get a glimpse into the history of Gan, the former RAF base. Hithadhoo, the city’s capital, hosts one of the country’s largest natural lakes, attracting both inhabitants and migrating birds alike.
Set off on a fishing adventure on the open sea. Whether you’re a seasoned angler or a novice, our experienced crew will guide you to the best fishing spots and assist you throughout. After returning to the resort, all catches can be prepared and served fresh by our chefs.
Experience the rich history and way of life on our next-door neighbor’s island, Hulhumeedhoo. Visit the Taro farms, explore World War II relics, and tour the country’s oldest cemetery, Koagannu. Discover how Meedhoo Island’s inhabitants were the first Maldivians to embrace Islam.
Sail into the sunset on our romantic cruise. Witness the breathtaking Maldivian sunset as you look out for playful dolphins frolicking in the ocean. This unforgettable journey is a must-do for all our guests.
Delve into an underwater world of vibrant marine life. Our island’s lagoon is brimming with black-tip reef sharks, eels, and different species of fish, offering an unforgettable snorkeling experience.
Join the daily feeding frenzy at our jetty. Watch in awe as our resident marine life, like the Giant Trevally, feeds voraciously on food prepared by our staff.
Sip on signature cocktails as you cruise along the mesmerizing Maldivian coastline. Watch the setting sun paint the sky in stunning hues, making your evening truly memorable.
Our SSI Dive Centre offers a range of diving courses for both beginners and experienced divers. Explore 25 different dive sites that Addu Atoll has to offer, including the famous WWII shipwreck, the ‘British Loyalty’.
Escape to a sanctuary of relaxation at the Canareef Spa. Set amidst lush gardens, our spa offers a range of detoxifying massages and scrubs. Our private suites cater to both singles and couples.
Our Kids Club offers a range of exciting games, educational activities, and beach fun for our younger guests. Supervised by our Kids Club attendant, children between the ages of 5 to 12 are welcome.
Discover the freshness of our dishes at our Herb Garden. Using hydroponics technology and natural farming practices, we grow our own herbs and spices, delivering the freshest ingredients to your plate.
