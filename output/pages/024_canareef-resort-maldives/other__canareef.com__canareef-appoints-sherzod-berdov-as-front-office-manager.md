# Canareef Appoints Sherzod Berdov as Front Office Manager - Canareef

Source: https://canareef.com/canareef-appoints-sherzod-berdov-as-front-office-manager/
Scraped: 2026-10-04
Resort: Canareef Resort Maldives
Page type: other

---

We are excited to announce the appointment of Sherzod Berdov as the new Front Office Manager. With 14 years of extensive experience in the hospitality industry, Sherzod brings a wealth of knowledge and a passion for exceptional guest service to this key role.
Sherzod began his career in 2011 as a Guest Service Team Lead at a pre-opening hotel in his home city of Dushanbe. His outstanding performance led to a promotion to Duty Manager within the same property. Seeking to broaden his horizons, Sherzod joined the 5-star Redang Island Resort in Malaysia in 2014, where he spent nearly two years honing his skills in a new setting.
In 2016, Sherzod returned to his previous hotel in Dushanbe, this time as Assistant Front Office Manager (AFOM). His dedication and expertise earned him a promotion to Front Office Manager (FOM) in 2018, a position he held for nearly six years. During this time, Sherzod also managed eCommerce and Revenue responsibilities, showcasing his versatility and strategic thinking.
In 2023, Sherzod took on a new challenge at a 4-star resort in Seychelles, where he spent a year further enhancing his skills and broadening his international experience.
Sherzod’s journey is marked not only by his impressive career trajectory but also by his educational achievements. Initially holding a degree in Economics from Russian Slavonic University, Sherzod discovered his true passion for hospitality. To further his expertise, he pursued a degree in Hospitality Management from Cornell University, which he completed in 2020.
Believing that “service comes from the heart and only people make the difference,” Sherzod is committed to delivering exceptional guest experiences and fostering a positive and efficient front office environment.
“I am excited to join Canareef Resort Maldives and contribute to its renowned guest service excellence,” Sherzod said. “I look forward to working with the team to ensure our guests receive the highest level of service and enjoy unforgettable experiences at our resort.” Canareef Resort Maldives is confident that Sherzod Berdov’s extensive experience and dedication to exceptional service will greatly enhance the guest experience and contribute to the continued success of the resort.
