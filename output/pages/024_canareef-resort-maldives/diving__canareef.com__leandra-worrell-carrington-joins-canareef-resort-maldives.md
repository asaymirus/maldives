Leandra Worrell-Carrington Brings Exceptional Hospitality Expertise to Tropical Paradise
In a strategic move to enhance its organizational capacity and guest experience,
Canareef Resort Maldives
has announced the appointment of Leandra Worrell-Carrington as its new Assistant Training Manager, effective September 2024. This pivotal role promises to inject fresh perspectives and transformative training methodologies into the resort’s operational ecosystem.
Leandra arrives with an impressive academic and professional background that spans multiple facets of the hospitality industry. Her academic credentials include a Bachelor’s Degree in Hotel Management from the University of the West Indies and a Master’s in International Hospitality Management from the University of Derby, positioning her as a highly qualified professional in her field.
Her career trajectory is a testament to her versatility, having navigated roles across Accounts, Food and Beverage, Housekeeping, and passenger services in the airline industry before specializing in Training and Development in 2019. Her professional portfolio boasts an impressive roster of renowned hospitality brands, including The Palace Hotel Buxton, Sandals Royal Barbados, CROSSROADS Maldives, Soneva Jani, Royalton Grenada, and Six Senses La Sagesse.
“My primary goal is to create a warm and inclusive environment where each team member feels empowered to grow and contribute to our collective success,” Leandra shared. Her commitment to continuous learning is further evidenced by her upcoming enrollment in the CIPD Level 7 Advanced Diploma in Strategic Learning & Development.
Strategic Implications for Canareef
The resort’s leadership views this appointment as a strategic investment in human capital. By bringing Leandra ‘s expertise onboard, Canareef aims to elevate its operational excellence and create a more engaging, skill-driven workplace culture.
Her proven track record of enhancing guest satisfaction and operational efficiency makes her an ideal fit for a resort committed to delivering world-class hospitality experiences.
As
Canareef Resort Maldives
continues to position itself as a premier destination, Leandra represents the embodiment of their forward-thinking approach—investing in people, nurturing talent, and continuously raising the bar in tropical hospitality.
