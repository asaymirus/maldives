# Canareef Resort Welcomes SriLankan Airlines Partner Agents

Source: https://canareef.com/canareef-resort-srilankan-airlines-fam-tour/
Scraped: 2026-10-04
Resort: Canareef Resort Maldives
Page type: overview

---

Canareef Resort Maldives recently hosted a Familiarization (FAM) tour for SriLankan Airlines’ ten key partner agents from Abu Dhabi and Dubai, along with the airline’s DXB Key Account Manager and AUH Sales Manager. The purpose of the tour was to showcase the exceptional hospitality and unique experiences offered at Canareef Resort, while promoting the Gan sector through SriLankan Airlines’ strong connections.
As part of its mission to highlight the charm of Addu Atoll and increase the visibility of
Gan Airport
,
SriLankan Airlines
(UL119/120) has launched a campaign to raise awareness and promote the region as a top travel destination. The visiting agents, representing influential travel agencies, were given a fully immersive experience to grasp the distinct advantages of traveling to Addu Atoll via Gan Airport.
Nestled in the southernmost atoll of the Maldives, Canareef Resort is renowned for its breathtaking natural beauty and commitment to delivering unparalleled service. During the FAM tour, the agents were able to experience the resort’s charming amenities, including spacious beach villas, delectable dining options, and a variety of recreational activities.
A key highlight of the tour was showcasing Addu Atoll as a rising star in the diving world. Boasting rich marine biodiversity and pristine waters, Addu Atoll offers divers and snorkelers an unforgettable experience. Visitors can explore vibrant coral reefs, interact with playful dolphins, and marvel at majestic manta rays, making it an ideal spot for both novice and experienced divers.
SriLankan Airlines’ strategic push to promote
Gan Airport
is expected to unlock new opportunities for travelers looking to explore the hidden gems of the Maldives. By offering direct flights from Colombo to Gan, the airline is seamlessly connecting travelers to the serene beauty and unique offerings of Addu Atoll. This initiative is set to boost tourism and leave lasting impressions on those who visit this idyllic region.
Through this FAM tour,
Canareef Resort Maldives
and SriLankan Airlines aim to strengthen collaboration by enhancing the visibility of Addu Atoll and promoting its accessibility. By working closely with key travel agents, the goal is to foster a deeper appreciation for the tranquility, beauty, and vibrant culture awaiting visitors to Addu Atoll.
In conclusion, the partnership between
Canareef Resort Maldives
and
SriLankan Airlines
represents an exciting collaboration that not only elevates the overall travel experience but also positions Addu Atoll as a premier destination in the Maldives. With a focus on Gan Airport and its many unique offerings, the future looks bright for travelers seeking a perfect blend of adventure, relaxation, and exploration in one of the world’s most captivating tropical paradises.
