A Tropical Haven Awaits
Embark on an adventure to the tropical paradise that awaits you at Canareef Resort Maldives.
Set your compass for Canareef Resort, a gem nestled in the tranquil Addu Atoll, at the southernmost tip of the Maldives on Herathera Island.
Immerse yourself in our tropical paradise stepping into a picture-perfect island getaway. The natural beauty of the island unfolds in stunning landscapes, from azure waters that gently lap against endless white sandy beaches, to the lush greenery that drapes our resort.
As you explore, discover a vibrant undersea world teeming with marine life, painting a colorful spectacle beneath the crystal-clear waters. Canareef Resort beckons you to experience the magic and marvel at the breathtaking beauty firsthand, as you create your very own island memories.
Domestic Flight + Speedboat Ride
Your journey to Canareef begins with a flight to Male’ International Airport. From there, enjoy a scenic 75 – 95 minute domestic flight to Gan Airport, followed by a swift 10 minute speedboat ride to our island paradise.
Equator Crossing to The Resort
Cross the equator on your journey to Canareef and receive a personalized Equator Crossing Certificate from the airplane Captain. Our resort is located 0.6 degrees south of the equator, about 67 kilometers (41 miles) away, making this a truly unique experience.
International Flight + Speedboat Ride
Enjoy a seamless journey to Canareef Resort Maldives by flying directly to Gan International Airport via Colombo, followed by a quick and convenient 10 minute speed boat ride to our tropical paradise. Sri Lankan Airlines currently operates direct flights from Colombo to Gan International Airport.

ARRIVAL TO CANAREEF RESORT MALDIVES
Upon arrival to Male’ International Airport, guests will be met by our Airport Representative at the arrival terminal holding a placard with our Resort Name.
Our Airport Representative will escort guests to the Domestic Terminal and assist with check-in.
Once checked-in, guests will be invited to relax at the Moonimaa Lounge while waiting for the Domestic Flight. Guests can enjoy free Wi-Fi and light refreshments with the compliments of the Resort.
Upon arrival at Gan Airport, our Airport Representative will receive the guests and direct them to the Speed Boat as soon as it’s ready to depart.
LOUNGE SERVICE
Complimentary Lounge service from Moonimaa Lounge at the Domestic Terminal at Male’ Airport is available on arrival for ALL GUESTS of Canareef Resort Maldives.
Complimentary Lounge service from Moonimaa Lounge at the Domestic Terminal at Male’ Airport is also available on departure for those with over 4 hours of transit time between their domestic and international departure flights.
Ensuring a Smooth Journey
Embark on a scenic journey from Malé International Airport to our beautiful resort located 536 km away. A 90-minute domestic flight will take you to Gan International Airport (GAN), treating you to breathtaking views of the Maldivian archipelago.
Baggage Guidelines
For a comfortable flight, there is a per-person baggage restriction for the transfer. You are allowed a maximum of 20kg of check-in luggage and a handbag with a maximum weight of 5kg. Please note that any excess weight is chargeable.

Arrival Experience
Upon arrival at Male’ International Airport:
Meet & Greet:
Our airport representative will greet you at the arrival terminal with a placard displaying the resort’s name.
Assistance:
You will be escorted to the Domestic Terminal and assisted with check-in.
Lounge Access:
Lounge Access: Available on complimentary basis for Canareef guests who have booked their transfers through the resort. Depending on the waiting time, you will be invited to relax in either Moonimaa Lounge (located at the Domestic Terminal) or Sunnyside Lounge (located in Hulhumale’ Island, a 20-minute car ride from Velana International Airport). At both lounges, enjoy complimentary Wi-Fi, TV, Tea, Coffee, and light refreshments while waiting to board the flight. Washrooms, changing rooms, shower facilities, and real-time flight information screens are available for your convenience.
Waiting Time:
As transfers to Canareef Resort Maldives are dependent on scheduled domestic flight connections, waiting times may vary and can range between 1 to 4 hours. In the event that the waiting time exceeds 4 hours, complimentary day use will be provided.
Waiting Time Definition:
On Arrival
, waiting time is calculated from the time guests exit the international arrival terminal until the check-in for the domestic flight opens. This waiting time does not include the time taken for immigration clearance, customs procedures, or luggage collection after the international flight lands.

Late Arrivals at Male’ International Airport:
After 14:25 hrs:
Connection to the afternoon flight cannot be guaranteed. Guests may need to wait for the night flight (6+ hours) or book a day-use room or Male’ city tour at an additional cost.
After 22:00 hrs:
Same-day transfer to the resort cannot be guaranteed. Overnight accommodation in Male’ or Hulhumale can be arranged at the guest’s expense.
The Resort is not responsible for any compensation for long waiting, nor for arranging day-use accommodation or the Male’ city tour, though assistance can be provided to the agent or client. If clients do not wish to travel on the late-night flight, it is recommended to book overnight accommodation at an extra cost and arrange the first flight the following day. The agent must clearly communicate these details to the client, ensuring they are fully aware of the situation and recommending international flights with better domestic transfer connections.

Before 09:00 hrs:

TRANSFER TERMS AND CONDITIONS
Transfer Arrangement:
All transfers to and from Canareef Resort Maldives must be arranged exclusively through the Resort.
Transfer Requirements:
Domestic flights and Speedboat transfers are required for guests arriving at Male’ airport on international flights or from other atolls.
Third-Party Service Provider:
Domestic flights are operated by a third-party, Island Aviation Service (IAS).
Flight Rate Adjustments:
Domestic flight rates may increase due to fuel surcharges or new government taxes.
Automatic Booking of Transfers:
Domestic transfers (arrival and departure) will be automatically booked at the time of accommodation booking unless otherwise stated.
Transfer Delays:
Transfers may not always be available at the scheduled time, and the Resort is not liable for compensation for delays. Waiting times at Male’ airport cannot be confirmed in advance.
Transfer Scheduling:
All arrivals and departures are subject to the domestic flight schedule and weather conditions. Neither the Resort nor the Airline is obligated to change schedules or offer compensation for delays.
Liability for Missed Flights:
Neither the Resort nor the transfer provider is liable for missed or delayed international/domestic flights caused by adverse weather conditions, force majeure, or acts of God.
Possible Flight Stops:
Domestic flights may have en-route stops. The carrier will aim to maximize direct flights whenever possible.
