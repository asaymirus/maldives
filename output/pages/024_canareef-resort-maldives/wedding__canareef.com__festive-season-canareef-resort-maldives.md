# Celebrate a Magical Festive Season at Canareef Resort Maldives

Source: https://canareef.com/festive-season-canareef-resort-maldives/
Scraped: 2026-10-04
Resort: Canareef Resort Maldives
Page type: wedding

---

Canareef Resort Maldives is ready to welcome guests into a magical festive season with a vibrant lineup of activities designed to create unforgettable holiday memories. From December 22, 2024, to January 1, 2025, the resort will be transformed into a holiday wonderland, offering a range of unique experiences that cater to guests of all ages.
The celebrations kick off with the enchanting Christmas Tree Lighting ceremony. This heartwarming event sets the tone for the festivities, inviting guests to join in the spirit of the season as they gather around the beautifully adorned tree, illuminated by twinkling lights.
Throughout the holiday period, families can engage in a variety of exciting kids’ activities. From face painting to cookie and cupcake making, there’s something for every little one to enjoy. The joy of the season will resonate in the air, amplified by delightful Christmas carols echoing throughout the resort.
Guests are in for a special treat as Santa makes his grand arrival at Dhoni Beach. Accompanied by joyous Christmas carols sung at the
Dhoni Bar Deck
, both children and adults will delight in this festive spectacle, capturing the magic of Christmas.
The culinary experience at Canareef will be equally enchanting. The Christmas Gala Dinner promises to be a memorable evening, with a live musician serenading diners in a festive atmosphere. Guests can indulge in a delightful Christmas Brunch at
Kilhi
, featuring a sumptuous buffet brimming with seasonal delights.
As the year draws to a close, the excitement continues with a lavish New Year’s Eve Gala Dinner on December 31 followed by a vibrant New Year party with a lineup of exciting entertainment programs. Guests will gather to bid farewell to the year gone by in style, enjoying a night filled with fun, laughter and joyful reflections.
The celebrations continue into the New Year with a delicious brunch on January 1 at Kilhi, offering a perfect start to 2025. Later in the day, guests can join to welcome the first sunset of the new year with a spectacular sunset cocktail party at Meera Beach. Enjoy the live band as you sip on cocktails and savor light canapés, creating a perfect ambiance as you watch the magical first sunset over the Maldivian horizon.
Join us at
Canareef Resort Maldives
for a festive season filled with happiness, laughter, and unforgettable moments. Either you want to relax by the beach, enjoy delicious cuisine, or participate in exciting activities, our holiday program promises to make this holiday season absolutely memorable. Let the celebrations begin.
