# Canareef Resort Maldives Gets Ready to Oktoberfest - Canareef

Source: https://canareef.com/maldives-oktoberfest/
Scraped: 2026-10-04
Resort: Canareef Resort Maldives
Page type: diving

---

Canareef Resort Maldives is gearing up to host a three-day Oktoberfest celebration, bringing the spirit of Germany to the sunny shores of the Indian Ocean.
From September 29th to October 6th, guests can immerse themselves in the festive atmosphere with a variety of activities and entertainment. The celebration will be held at three picturesque beach locations: Bar-Kuda Beach on September 29th, Meera Bar Beach on October 2nd, and Dhoni Bar Beach on October 6th.
Oktoberfest wouldn’t be complete without beer, and Canareef will have plenty on tap. Guests can participate in exciting beer-drinking competitions and enjoy special promotions on a wide selection of beverages.
For those seeking a more active experience, beach games like tug of war and beach volleyball will be organized. Those interested in learning a new skill can participate in cocktail-making classes and witness the flair of professional bartenders.
To add to the festive atmosphere, live DJ performances will keep guests entertained throughout the celebration.
Canareef Resort Maldives
invites guests to join them for this unforgettable Oktoberfest experience. Whether you’re a beer enthusiast, a sports fan, or simply looking for a fun-filled day at the beach, there’s something for everyone.
