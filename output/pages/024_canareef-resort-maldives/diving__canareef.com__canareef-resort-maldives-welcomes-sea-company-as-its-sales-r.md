# Canareef Resort Maldives welcomes SEA Company as its Sales Representative in Russia and the CIS countries - Canareef

Source: https://canareef.com/canareef-resort-maldives-welcomes-sea-company-as-its-sales-representative-in-russia-and-the-cis-countries/
Scraped: 2026-10-04
Resort: Canareef Resort Maldives
Page type: diving

On December 1, 2023, Canareef Resort Maldives appoints SEA Company as its Sales Representative for the Russia and CIS markets as part of its strategic development in these countries.
With its headquarters based in Moscow,
SEA Company
has been successfully operating since 2013 and is a recognized leader in the hotel business in Russia. In 2019, the company was named “Best PR and consulting agency in the field of tourism in Russia” according to the Global Hospitality Awards (London). A distinctive feature of
SEA Company
is its team, comprising of professional hoteliers with years of experience working in global hotel brands.
“The Maldives is among the TOP 5 countries where tourists from Russia and the CIS go on vacation in winter. One of the main requirements of tourists with children is developed infrastructure, the presence of swimming pools, a kids club and an all-inclusive meal plans. Canareef Maldives has all that and we are confident that the resort will be successful and popular among our compatriots. We thank the management for their trust and choice of SEA Company as their permanent representative office in Russia and the CIS.”
– said Managing Partner of SEA Company Andrey Shemyakin.
Russia remains at the top of the list of most tourist arrivals to the Maldives Destination. Canareef Resort Maldives, together with SEA Company, will activate and globally develop all sales channels in the markets of Russia and the CIS countries in order to popularize and multiply the flow of tourists of all segments not only to the resort, but also to Addu Atoll as a whole.
“Today the Maldives is not only a luxury available to few selected segments. Our resort is open to everyone who wants to be in an atmosphere of magic, go on an unforgettable trip to a tropical paradise with family, a friend or a group of friends. By choosing SEA Company as our exclusive sales representative in Russia and the CIS, we want to give a large number of people the opportunity to experience our island paradise, where lifestyle takes on a whole new meaning.”
– said Mohamed Jaish Ibrahim, Resort Director, Canareef Maldives.
