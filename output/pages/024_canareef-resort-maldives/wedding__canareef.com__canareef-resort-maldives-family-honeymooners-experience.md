Welcome to Canareef Resort Maldives, where every moment is tailored for families and honeymooners seeking a blend of adventure and tranquility amidst the stunning natural beauty of the Maldives.
Our resort offers 271 spacious beach villas, scattered along a 4.5 km stretch of coastline, ensuring indulgent comfort for families and honeymooners alike. From the Sunrise Villa
overlooking the resort’s fringing reef to the Sunset Beach Villa with direct access to pristine sandy beaches, each accommodation promises breathtaking views and unmatched tranquility. Indulge in premium amenities, including private courtyards with whirlpools, open-air bathrooms, and inviting patios, designed for your utmost relaxation.
At Canareef, culinary delights await the entire family. Choose from
two restaurants and three bars
offering a diverse array of international cuisines and refreshing beverages. Enjoy sumptuous buffets at
Kilhi Restaurant
or savor à la carte dining at
Meera Bar
, where every palate is catered to with attention to detail and innovation.
Embark on exciting family adventures, from exploring WWII-era shipwrecks to encountering dolphins, manta rays, and tiger sharks in the pristine waters of Addu Atoll. Our dedication to sustainability and eco-conscious practices ensures a responsible and enriching experience for all. Engage with local farmers, participate in coral conservation efforts, and explore our eco-friendly transportation options, including push bicycles and electric vehicles.
Whether unwinding in the Dhunfini Spa, exploring the Addu Nature Park, or stargazing under the Southern Cross, Canareef Resort offers a myriad of experiences to captivate the senses and rejuvenate the soul. From intimate honeymoon escapes to memorable family vacations, every moment at Canareef is imbued with warmth and the unparalleled beauty of the Maldives.
As one of the most secluded islands in the Maldives, Canareef invites families and honeymooners to embark on a journey of discovery, adventure, and unmatched luxury. With pristine beaches, diverse ecosystems, and a commitment to sustainability, Canareef promises an unforgettable escape into paradise—a holiday of a lifetime for the entire family to treasure.
For our honeymoon and anniversary guests, special treats await you! Enjoy a cake in your villa during your stay, indulge in a romantic dinner under the stars with a buffet from the main restaurant (with a minimum 4 nights stay), receive a complimentary 750ml bottle of white or red wine upon arrival, and delight in a flower bed decoration and a fruit basket awaiting you in your villa upon arrival.
And for our younger guests, our Kids Club offers a range of exciting games, educational activities, and beach fun supervised by our Kids Club attendant. Children between the ages of 5 to 12 are welcome to join in the fun!
Come and experience the magic of Canareef Resort Maldives, where every detail is designed to create unforgettable memories for you and your loved ones.
