The full moon is a symbol of renewal, balance, and reflection. It’s the perfect time to release what no longer serves you and set intentions for new beginnings. At The Nautilus, the Full Moon Spa Rituals are designed to align with the moon’s gentle, restorative energy, helping you rejuvenate and connect with your truest self.
Moonlit Renewal
Join a serene evening under the full moon, where you can experience the calming effects of Tibetan Singing Bowls, ocean waves, and aromatherapy. Conclude the session with a warm herbal tea as you set intentions for personal renewal. This experience is complimentary, creating a peaceful space to reflect and relax.
Private Full Moon Experiences
For a more intimate experience, consider these special rituals:
Celestial Sandbank Escape
: A private yacht takes you to a moonlit sandbank for an aromatherapy session, crystal singing bowl therapy, and a spa meal under the stars.
Lunar Serenity Spa Ritual
: A 120-minute journey featuring a warm oil massage, soothing Shirodhara treatment, and moonlit bathing, paired with a spa meal.
Moonlit Beach Reverie
: A private beachside session with an aromatherapy massage, scalp massage, and crystal singing bowl therapy under the full moon.
