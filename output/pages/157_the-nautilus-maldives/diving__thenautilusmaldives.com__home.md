The Nautilus Travel Guide
What is the latest update on requirements for travelling to The Nautilus Maldives?
Tourists visiting the Maldives are no longer required to present a negative PCR test result upon arrival – this applies to unvaccinated travellers as well. Traveller Health Declaration must be filled in and submitted by all travellers travelling to and from the Maldives, within 96 hours prior to their travel. You may submit the form electronically via
https://imuga.immigration.gov.mv/traveller
When is the best time to visit?
The Maldives is an all-year-round destination, with a tropical climate, sunshine-filled days and temperatures averaging 30°C throughout the year. While snorkelling and diving are enjoyable throughout the year, the Southwest monsoon season (May to October) is the ideal season to sight manta rays in Hanifaru Bay and other marine life due to the higher quantities of plankton.
How to travel to and from The Nautilus?
You can choose between travelling to The Nautilus by our own luxury seaplane with an 8-business-class-seat configuration or the scheduled Manta Air seaplane with a 14-seat configuration. The travel time is approximately 30 minutes from Velana International Airport.
What childcare facilities and entertainment options are available at The Nautilus?
If you’re travelling with children, The Nautilus has some of the Maldives’ best childcare facilities. Young Wonderers, the playful hub located at the centre of our private island, is the base for an extraordinary interactive programme we offer.
Is it possible to book out the entire island?
Yes, for those looking to reserve the entire private island for themselves and their loved ones, there are exclusive island buyout options available.
