Bageecha offers all-day dining in its crisp and breezy blue-and-white interiors. Our food is a celebration of our location, with ingredients sustainably sourced from our organic gardens and surrounding oceans.
Healthy buffet-style breakfasts with tropical fruits and pressed juices are the perfect start to the day. For lunch and dinner, dine on authentic Maldivian and Asian dishes alongside a wide range of continental options. Menus change daily, with a good choice of vegetarian, pescatarian and gluten-free dishes.
