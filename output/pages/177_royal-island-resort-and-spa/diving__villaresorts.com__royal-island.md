A good spa should make you forget what time it is.

At Araamu Spa, Royal Island, that happens pretty quickly. It sits deep in the island greenery, with dark timber, running water, flowers and treatment rooms that feel completely cut off from everything else.

A few from the menu worth knowing:

🌿 Back to Roots is the signature ritual, with island-leaf smudging, sand and coconut milk poultice therapy, bamboo massage and a herbal bath to finish.

🥥 Exotic Cocoon is the tropical one, with coffee and coconut scrub, coconut yoghurt wrap and a scalp massage.

🎋 Voyager is for tired shoulders and serious tension, with deep tissue work, stretching and bamboo techniques.

💧 Shirodhara is the one for switching your mind off, ending with warm herbal oil poured slowly over the forehead.

Our recommendation? Book it for the afternoon and leave the rest of the day empty.

September in Baa Atoll can turn the sea into a feeding ground.

As monsoon currents push plankton into Hanifaru Bay, manta rays begin to gather in extraordinary numbers, and September is one of the strongest months to see it happen.

From Royal Island, our @diveoceanus team leads guided excursions to Hanifaru Bay, helping guests understand the conditions, the animals and how to experience the bay responsibly.

What makes it special is not just seeing mantas. It is watching an entire natural system come together in front of you.

If Hanifaru Bay is on your list, September is a very good time to be here.

Royal Island has officially been named Best Dive Resort at the TTM Awards 2026.

🐢 Turtles gliding over the reef
🦈 Reef sharks cruising through the blue
✨ Manta rays sweeping past in season
🐠 Huge schools of tropical fish
