Experiences at Villa Nautica
Crow's Nest
Description
Inspired by lifeguard towers, this colourful wooden structure offers guests a thrilling climb up a spiral staircase to two lofty platforms with panoramic views of the island.
At the first level – half way to the top – there are rope ladders and rope swings for families to play around or just enjoy the shaded spot. Head further up to the top and discover a cosy den with cushions and throws creating the ultimate chill-out nest – one of our most-photographed vantage-points on the island!
At a look-out window a beautifully crafted map, etched in copper, shows the geographical points of interest for each location. You’ll also find a pirate’s treasure chest filled with objects to entertain – a telescope, playing cards and binoculars – and even an honesty box for readymade cocktail drinks: help yourself to miniature bottles all concocted with a rum base.
