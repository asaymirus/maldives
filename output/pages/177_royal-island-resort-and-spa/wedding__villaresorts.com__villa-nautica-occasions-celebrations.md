Celebrate milestone birthdays or wedding anniversaries with multigenerational families or close groups of friends.
Every birthday and wedding anniversary deserves a joyful celebration so we’re here to make sure that you have an unforgettable island holiday together with a memorable event planned for your special day. We can create magical experiences for everything from candlelit beach dinners under the stars to interactive chef’s dinners at one of our restaurants – the choice is entirely yours!

Birthdays
Birthdays at Villa Nautica are celebrated in a myriad ways: you might want to head out on a dive or experience swimming with dolphins for the first time in your life, spend the night out at sea on our traditional Maldivian houseboat or experience a teppanyaki-style dinner at our Japanese restaurant. Every guest is honoured with a birthday cake baked especially by our chefs and is gifted a bottle of wine.
Anniversaries
If it’s your wedding anniversary while you’re staying with us at Villa Nautica, we’d like to congratulate you both by gifting a complimentary bottle of chilled sparkling wine and a celebratory cake. All you need to do is let us know by presenting a copy of your marriage certificate on arrival and we’d be delighted to take care of the rest!
