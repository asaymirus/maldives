Experiences at Villa Nautica
Captain's Class
Description
Appealing to the escapist spirit deep in every guest, nautical skills can easily be learnt. Why not join the Captain’s classes? Sign up to evening classes to listen to the Captain give a series of fascinating and informative talks. Subjects cover everything of nautical interest about the islands, the people and their traditions, the exotic marine life and, of course, folklore.
Listen to the experts and gain insider knowledge on everything from knots and navigation, marine life and mariners and the language and lore of the Maldives. Partner up, laugh and learn about half hitch and bowline knots, all in a series of competitive games.
