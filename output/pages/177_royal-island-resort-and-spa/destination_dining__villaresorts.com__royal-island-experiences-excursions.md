Our island tours, excursions and activities have been designed to help you make the most of your vacation in the Maldives.
Available from sunrise to sundown, our Maldivian excursions will help you experience the very best of the Baa Atoll. Venture to secluded sandbanks, colorful coral gardens, or inhabited islands rich in local culture. Embark on a big game fishing adventure and try your hand at catching supper the traditional way. And, above all else, make some unforgettable memories.
Ocean Quest
3 hours
Create your own perfect day at sea with Ocean Quest — a customisable speedboat excursion designed for explorers. Choose any three experiences from turtle snorkelling, reef snorkelling, dolphin watching, or a sandbank visit.
Wanderlust
3.5 hours
Set off on a half-day marine adventure that blends exploration and relaxation. Swim alongside sea turtles in their natural habitat, drift through vibrant coral gardens, and unwind on a secluded sandbank surrounded by turquoise waters. This curated itinerary captures the magic of the Baa Atoll in one unforgettable trip.
Sunset Cocktail
1 hour
Set sail aboard a traditional Maldivian dhoni and watch the sun dip below the horizon. Enjoy panoramic views of pastel-colored skies while sipping a handcrafted cocktail or mocktail of your choice.
Reef Snorkeling
2 hours
Glide through the warm, crystal-clear waters of the Baa Atoll and uncover a kaleidoscope of marine life. Our guided reef snorkelling experience takes you to vibrant coral formations teeming with reef fish, rays, and the occasional turtle.
Stargazing
1 hour
When the evening comes, watch as a candy-colored sunset is transformed into a starry night sky.
Moonlight Cruise
1-2 hours
When night falls, board a traditional dhoni and experience the beauty and tranquility of an evening on the water.
Big Game Fishing
3-6 hours
Try your hand at catching your own supper with a big game fishing adventure in the heart of the Indian Ocean.
Dolphin Safari
1 hour
Come sundown, board a high-powered speedboat or a traditional dhoni and watch dolphins play in the surf.
Local Island Experience
2 hours
Pay a visit to a neighboring island and learn more about traditional Maldivian culture. Speak with the island's locals, sample South Asian food made with home-grown ingredients, and discover more about what life is like on these remote islands in the Baa Atoll.
Sunset Fishing
2 hours 30 minutes
Board a Maldivian dhoni and embark on a nighttime fishing trip in the waters surrounding Royal Island.
Sandbank Picnics
4.5-6.5 hours
Swim, snorkel, and sample delicious Maldivian food with our sandbank picnic experience. Choose between morning, afternoon, and full-day experiences, or, enjoy a private tour with just the two of you and a bottle of champagne. Our packages include a packed lunch, refreshments, and a selection of fresh fruits.
