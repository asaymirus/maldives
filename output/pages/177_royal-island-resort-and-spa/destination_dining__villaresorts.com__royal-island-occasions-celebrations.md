Celebrations at Royal Island in the Maldives
From vow renewals to long-awaited reunions, celebrate with the Maldives' iconic Baa Atoll as your backdrop.
Whether you're in search of romance or revelry, our mile-wide sandy shorelines and secluded sandbanks have played host to all manner of celebrations. And when it comes to dining, our team can create everything from a candlelit dinner to a traditional banquet on the beach.
Birthdays
Birthdays at Royal Island are celebrated in a myriad ways: you might want to head out on a dive or experience swimming with dolphins for the first time in your life, watch the sunset on a cruise or experience a candle-lit beach dinner with your loved ones. Every guest is honoured with a birthday cake baked especially by our chefs and is gifted a bottle of wine.
Anniversaries
If it’s your wedding anniversary while you’re staying with us at Royal Island, we’d like to congratulate you both by gifting complimentary bottle of chilled sparkling wine and a celebratory cake. All you need to do is let us know by presenting a copy of your marriage certificate on arrival and we’d be delighted to take care of the rest!
