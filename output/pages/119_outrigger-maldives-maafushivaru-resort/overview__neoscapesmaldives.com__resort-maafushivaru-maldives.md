DESCRIPTION
With its intimate small island charm, lush vegetation surrounded by powder-white soft sand, and a house reef that a myriad of tropical fish call home, Outrigger Maldives Maafushivaru Resort is just over 350-metres in length and can be walked in just 10-minutes and a bit!
Outrigger Maldives Maafushivaru Resort located in the pristine South Ari Atoll, 93km from the airport, offers five different types of villas, each combining elements of Maldivian architecture with stylish and chic finishing touches, caters to couples seeking romantic pursuits. At Lti Maafushivaru, you are sure to make memories that will be forever cherished.
The resort offers 81 villas in different distinct styles, all providing direct access to the beach or with clear views to the ocean. The villas are well-appointed yet simple in design and architecture, blending in beautifully with the laid-back environment of the Maldives. Highlights From The Resort
Feel the natural well-being
An oasis individually designed to help you realise your ultimate state of wellbeing. Our signature product line, Voya and Yonka, uses hand-harvested seaweed to provide organic treatments to our guests that blend well with our environment.
Gym & Yoga Pavilion
The wellness centre of our celebrated beach resort in the Maldives also has a fully equipped gymnasium to help you keep fit even when on holiday.
Preceding the Spa and Gym, Lti Maafushivaru introduces the “Yoga Pavilion”. Conspicuous by its location, embraced by that invisible sea breeze. Guests can enjoy not only its spectacular ocean view but on schedule to greed that breathtaking sunset. Inhaling and exhaling with enchantment in various techniques rewarded by the goodness of self-care.
Diving
Our fully equipped PADI dive centre with enthusiastic multi-lingual instructors offers a wide range of underwater adventures catering for all levels from beginners to experienced divers. Beginners will become ‘hooked’ when they discover the amazing underwater world whereas for the experienced divers are sure to see something new amongst the many types of sharks, mantas, turtles, napoleons, tuna and other smaller, but maybe more colourful, dwellers on the reef.
Snorkelling
Snorkelling is a must in the Maldives. If diving isn’t your cue, you can still experience the beauty of marine flora and fauna while staying afloat with the aid of a snorkel, mask and flippers. Our team is available to help beginners gain their fi rst taste of the wonderful underwater world in the lagoon before progressing onto the house reef or even a boat trip to snorkel at the Coral Garden. For experienced snorkelers there is a range of full morning snorkel safaris by boat to other reefs, all guided by the resident marine biologist who helps you identify what you see!
Excursions
Discover the utterly sublime and flourishing underwater world around South Ari Atoll. Slient cruises with colourful sunsets view, exploration trips to nearby islands, or a fun fishing adventure, we got you covered.
Water Sports
We have a fully equipped water sports center for adrenaline seekers. Whether it’s paddling around the lagoon on a kayak or wakeboarding, we’ve got it all. Our team is always available to “show you the ropes” if you need any guidance.
Entertainment
A wide range of entertainment programs including live music, DJ afternoons, movie nights, yoga, beach and water activities are available throughout the week.
Renewal of Vows
The soft white beaches, the azure skies, and the endless turquoise Indian Ocean reflects that twinkling in the bright sun all beckoning you, to come and celebrate your new-found romance or renew your love for each other. You cannot ask for a more romantic setting.
