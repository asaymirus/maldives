Located in the southern tip of the picturesque atoll of Ari, Maafushivaru offers you villas in three distinct styles, each combining elements of Maldivian architecture with stylish and chic finishing touches. The resort was refurbished and reopened at the end of year 2010.
As is typical in the Maldives, the Reception, Library Lounge walkway and Water Bar are all sand floored. Going barefoot is the only way on this island. Located in the neighboring lagoon, just minutes away, is Lonubo, a stunning deserted islet where you can choose to while away your days or book a romantic lobster dinner for the evening, just the two of you, on your own private island for the night.
Transfer to the island is by seaplane and takes 25 minutes from Malé International Airport. Upon arrival, our airport hosts greet you, check you in for your seaplane and escort you to the terminal which features a lounge for you to relax in whilst you wait for your fl ight. Refreshments and complimentary internet access are offered here.
