About Us
Adaaran Club Rannalhi sits exclusively at the tip of the South Male atoll within the exotic collection of islands known as the Maldives.
Its unique location offers access to pristine beaches, excellent scuba diving opportunities and a relaxed environment with easy access to the capital city of Male. The resort’s vicinity to Male allows it prime access to the country’s most treasured attractions such as the stunning Grand Friday Mosque and the revered National Museum.
Bask in the golden sun and frolic on the soft sands as you experience friendly Maldivian hospitality and explore the rich tropical wonders of a vibrant island nation during your stay at our inviting Maldives hotel.
Reside in one of our elegant water bungalows situated on clear, turquoise water and admire the colourful sea fauna as they swim past you. Or simply laze in one of our many standard rooms offering both terrace and balcony views.
All retreats at Adaaran Club Rannalhi seamlessly blend modern comforts with the wonders of their natural surroundings.Savour a plethora of sumptuous cuisine featuring both local and international fare whipped up by our culinary maestros deeply influenced by the vast array of delicious seafood available daily at Adaaran Club Rannalhi.
Join one of the many excursions available from our Maldives hotel and partake in exciting activities such as windsurfing, canoeing and fishing.
