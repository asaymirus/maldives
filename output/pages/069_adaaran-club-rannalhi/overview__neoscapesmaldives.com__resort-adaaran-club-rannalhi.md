Adaaran Club Rannalhi – Fun and Freedom, Barefoot Island Exclusivity
Sparkling emerald waters and a salty breeze welcome you to Adaaran Club Rannalhi, a vibrant island retreat where carefree days meet effortless luxury. Quick speedboat access from Malé, All-Inclusive dining, daily excursions, and friendly Maldivian hospitality make every moment unforgettable. With palm-fringed beaches, thriving reefs, and social spaces alive with energy, this barefoot paradise blends relaxation, adventure, and memory-making into a unique Maldivian escape.

Highlights From The Resort
Diving & Snorkeling Adventures
Explore vibrant coral reefs at House Reef Rannalhi and swim with tropical fish, manta rays, and gentle sea turtles. Ideal for both beginners and experienced divers.
Island Excursions & Island Hopping
Discover nearby islands, untouched beaches, and tropical landscapes. Experience the charm of secluded Maldivian paradise and enjoy sun-soaked adventures.
Fishing Experiences
Try traditional long-line fishing or join a sunset fishing trip on a Dhoni. Perfect for beginners or avid anglers looking to catch local fish while enjoying breathtaking ocean views.
Sunset Cruises & Photo Experiences
Glide across turquoise waters as the sun sets over the horizon. Capture unforgettable moments of the tropical sky and sparkling sea.
Chavana Spa & Wellness
Relax and rejuvenate in a serene seafront spa. Choose from signature therapies like Journey of the Senses, Frangipani Body Glow, and Heaven & Earth Massage. Includes a complimentary $50 spa credit for guests.
Water Sports
Thrilling adventures like kayaking, snorkeling, and diving to experience the Maldives’ crystal-clear waters and marine life up close.
Local Culture & Male City Tours
Explore Maldivian life with visits to local villages, mosques, schools, and markets. Optional trips to Male, the capital city, showcase Maldivian history, culture, and everyday life.
