Dining at Adaaran Club Rannalhi Maldives
From the freshest picks of the ocean to colourful tropical cocktails we will let you relish fine dining in Maldives as a truly satisfying experience. Featuring a diverse buffet with dishes from around the world accentuated by authentic Maldivian cuisine, our bars and restaurants are located to capture the stunning views of the Indian Ocean.
A diverse international menu is available daily for breakfast, lunch and dinner. You can also take your pick at our theme nights that feature fare ranging from Asian to Italian.
In room dining or a private candlelit dinner on the beach are available on request. End the day at the Nika Bar with a chilled cocktail amidst the panoramic views of the endless sea.
