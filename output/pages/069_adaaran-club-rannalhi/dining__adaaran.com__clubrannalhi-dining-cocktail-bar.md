Dining
Cocktail Bar
After a fun-filled day of swimming in the sea or touring the nearby atolls of the Maldives, an ideal place to relax and unwind is at the relaxing cocktail bar.
Based in contemporary surroundings with soft background music to exude a chilled atmosphere, the Cocktail Bar is located facing the western beach, offering mesmerizing panoramic views of the ocean at sunset.
Sit back on a comfy sofa or snug lounge chair, enjoying a chilled drink at one of the finest bars in Maldives, and just let your hair down while simply admiring the beautiful surroundings amidst the sounds of the crashing waves.
Choose from a wide variety of beverages on offer, from a selection of whisky, rum, and fine spirits to tantalizing tropical cocktails, chilled beer, and fine wines. Let our experienced bartenders whip up a drink to suit your mood.
