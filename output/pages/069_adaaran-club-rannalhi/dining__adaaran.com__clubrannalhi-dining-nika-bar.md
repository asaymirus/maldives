Here, you can indulge in expertly crafted cocktails and a fine selection of whisky, rum, and other fine spirits, all thoughtfully curated to reflect both local and international tastes. At this Maldives resort bar, drinks are infused with a unique island-inspired twist, enhancing the flavors with a touch of tropical creativity. As you sip your beverage, let the breathtaking panoramic views captivate your senses, offering vistas that stretch endlessly into the horizon. Welcoming guests from 10 a.m. to midnight, the Nika Bar is your perfect retreat for an exquisite drinking experience.
