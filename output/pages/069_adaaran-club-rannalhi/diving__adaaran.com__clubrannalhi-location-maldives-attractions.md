Maldives Attractions
Explore Maldives’ capital, Male one of the best tourist places in Maldives, with its mosques, museums, and scenic sites. The National Museum showcases Sultanate relics, while the Grand Friday Mosque impresses with its design. The Esjehi Art Gallery houses artistic treasures, and the Maldives Fish Market offers a cultural experience with fresh produce. Observe the islands’ fishing activities, a mainstay before tourism.
