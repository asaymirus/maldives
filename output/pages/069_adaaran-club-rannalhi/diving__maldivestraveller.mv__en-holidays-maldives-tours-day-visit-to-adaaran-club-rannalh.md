Tour full description
Adaaran Club Rannalhi is an all-inclusive resort located in South Male Atoll. This full-day tour is inclusive of alcoholic and no alcoholic drinks, lunch, entrance fee and transport.
Swim and Snorkel around the turquoise lagoon and explore the amazing house reef. The island is located at the tip of the South Male atoll within the exotic collection of islands of the Maldives.
Its unique location offers access to pristine beaches, excellent scuba diving and snorkelling opportunities and a relaxed environment.
Bask in the golden sun and frolic on the soft sands as you experience friendly Maldivian hospitality and explore the rich tropical wonders of a vibrant island during your visit to the inviting Adaaran Club Rannalhi.
