Adaaran Club Rannalhi
Adaaran Club Rannalhi Resort Location
Adaaran Club Rannalhi is located 34 km from the Male International Airport; a 45-minute speedboat transfer to the resort.
The resort has its own house reef which is ideal for snorkeling, and neighboring dive sites with multi-colored corals, schools of small coral fish, stingrays, and sleek sharks that prowl the seabed. In the Maldives, diving resorts such as ours let you truly get a chance to witness the vibrant wonders that lie beneath the surface of the Indian ocean.
Weather
Holiday Weather in Maldives
Maldives is blessed with a year-round tropical climate featuring moderately high temperatures and considerable rainfall. The islands to the south of and in close proximity to the Equator are considered to have more regular rainfall.
In the drier season daily sunshine hours are approximately eight or nine. Turbulent weather conditions are rare and short lived making it the perfect Maldivian holiday weather for your next vacation planned on the islands. Overall climatic conditions in the Maldives are very pleasant and salubrious, providing an ideal environment for a delightful tropical holiday.
