Know Before You Go
Our
All-Inclusive
packaging is designed to make your stay completely carefree. You’ll enjoy buffet-style meals at our main restaurant, along with a wide selection of alcoholic and non-alcoholic beverages served at our bars. Light snacks are available daily, and for those staying three nights or more, we’ve included a special sunset photo cruise. To make your experience even more memorable, the package also covers complimentary water sports such as snorkeling, windsurfing, and paddleboarding. Evening entertainment includes live music, DJ nights, and beach parties. Plus, you’ll receive USD 50 spa credit per person, and we add extra touches for birthdays, honeymoons, and other celebrations.
Transfers are arranged by speedboat from Velana International Airport, with a journey time of about 45 minutes. Our team will be happy to assist you in confirming your
transfer details
to ensure a smooth and comfortable arrival and departure.
Our cancellation policy varies depending on the season. You’ll find the general guidelines in our
Terms & Conditions
. If you’ve booked a non-refundable offer, please note that a 100% cancellation fee will apply to the total room charges.
Our guests can enjoy a variety of
dining
experiences during their stay. The main restaurant offers buffet-style meals with international flavors and stunning ocean views, while the cocktail bar provides the perfect setting for refreshing drinks throughout the day. For lighter bites, our café serves snacks in a relaxed tropical atmosphere.
