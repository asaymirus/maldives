BEST ALL INCLUSIVE PACKAGE IN THE MALDIVES
Published on: 20 May 2024
If you're searching for a Maldives all inclusive resorts’ escape, look no further than the InterContinental Maldives. Renowned as one of the top all-inclusive resorts in the Maldives, this luxurious haven offers an unforgettable experience with its exclusive package.
All-Club InterContinental Benefits
We are unique as the only all-club InterContinental resort in the world. All guests can enjoy exclusive Club InterContinental benefits throughout their stay, including complimentary breakfast at Café Umi, complimentary afternoon tea, complimentary aperitif hour, and all-day refreshments from our menu.
Dining at Four Diverse Restaurants
Food enthusiasts will be thrilled by the dining choices at this all inclusive Maldives resort, where the package includes meals at four distinct restaurants. Start your day with a nutritious breakfast at Café Umi, have lunch at Café Umi, Collective, or The Retreat, and savor dinner at Café Umi, Collective, The Retreat, or Fish Market each with its unique flavour profiles.
Relax and Unwind at Three Unique Bars
Enjoy the special all inclusive beverage menu and unwind at any of the resort's three bars, each offering a unique ambiance and experience. Whether it's spending afternoons by the beach at the Pool Bar, enjoying the adults-only enclave at Retreat Bar, or taking in the Maldivian sunsets at Sunset Bar, there's something for everyone.
Complimentary Non-Motorized Water Sports
For the thrill-seekers, the resort offers the exhilarating freedom of complimentary non-motorized water sports, including kayaking, catamaran sailing, windsurfing, and stand-up paddle boarding. These activities are perfect for exploring the crystal-clear waters surrounding the island.
Wellness
Wellness enthusiasts will appreciate the 24-hour open fitness centre, with a resident personal trainer. Guests can also participate in complimentary morning yoga sessions with the resident yogi, ensuring a balanced and rejuvenating experience.
