CLUB INTERCONTINENTAL
IT'S IN THE DETAILS
Privileged to be a part of your story, the Island Curators at InterContinental® Maldives Maamunagau Resort are proud to go above and beyond to ensure you immerse yourself in the blissful calm and otherworldly beauty of the island. From thoughtful recommendations to a welcoming smile at breakfast, our dedicated team are highly trained in the art of hospitality and understand the heart-fluttering difference the small details can make. After all, when it comes to making everlasting memories, it’s the little delights that matter.
As the first and only all-Club InterContinental® resort, enjoy complimentary benefits ranging from à la carte breakfast to a multi-tiered afternoon tea and tantalising evening aperitif.

CLUB INTERCONTINENTAL EXPERIENCES INCLUDE:
Breakfast
Wake up in paradise to a gourmet breakfast at Cafe Umi, right on the beach overlooking the Indian Ocean. Treat yourself to delicious pastries or order a healthy and filling breakfast from our menu.
Afternoon Tea
Choose a variety of tempting delicacies from the extensive afternoon tea menu, which is served daily from 3:00 p.m. to 5:00 p.m.
Evening Aperitif
End each day with evening aperitif offering a deluxe selection of premium drinks, wine and cheese. For families, a non-alcoholic beverage selection is also available.
Club InterContinental Benefits
Club Intercontinental at InterContinental Maldives offers an unparalleled experience of luxury and exclusivity in one of the world's most stunning destinations.
Non-motorised Water Sports
Whether you're a seasoned water sports enthusiast or a beginner looking to try something new, the non-motorized water sports offerings at InterContinental Maldives provide an eco-conscious way to experience the Maldives' aquatic wonders.
Snorkelling Equipment
At InterContinental Maldives, your desire to explore the mesmerizing underwater world of the Maldives is made effortless and enjoyable with their top-notch snorkeling equipment.
Island Bicycles
At InterContinental Maldives, the allure of exploring this tropical paradise on two wheels is made both convenient and enjoyable with their Island Bicycles.
Private Seaplane Lounge Access
At InterContinental Maldives, your journey to paradise begins with a touch of exclusivity and convenience through Private Seaplane Lounge Access.
Island Curator
The Island Curator at InterContinental Maldives is your dedicated guide to unlocking the secrets of this tropical paradise.
