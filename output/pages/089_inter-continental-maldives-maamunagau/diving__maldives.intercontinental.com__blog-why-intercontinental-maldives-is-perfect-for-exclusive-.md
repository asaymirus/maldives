YOUR PRIVATE PARADISE: WHY INTERCONTINENTAL MALDIVES IS PERFECT FOR EXCLUSIVE RESORT BUYOUTS
Published on: 28 Aug 2025
There is a rare kind of luxury that goes beyond beautiful villas, bespoke service, and crystal-clear waters. It’s the luxury of absolute privacy, where every inch of an island is yours, and yours only. At InterContinental Maldives Maamunagau Resort, that dream is now a reality, with the launch of an exclusive resort buyout on one of the Maldives’ most breathtaking private paradises.
Nestled in the untouched beauty of the Raa Atoll, on the edge of a UNESCO Biosphere Reserve, this private island resort in the Maldives is no longer just a place to stay, it’s a place to completely take over. Whether you’re planning a milestone celebration, a multi-generational family escape, a destination wedding, or a luxury retreat with friends, every detail is tailored entirely around you and your group.
With 81 expansive villas and residences across the beach, lagoon and overwater locations, there is space for everyone and every preference. From sunset-facing Overwater Pool Villas to Three-Bedroom Royal Beachfront Pool Residence, each guest enjoys their own slice of paradise. Each villa comes with a private pool, a personal butler, BYREDO amenities and thoughtful touches that make every moment feel special. Booking a full resort buyout means you are the only guests on the island, making the island feel like a world all your own.
The culinary journey here is divine. With seven unique venues to explore, from the elevated elegance of The Lighthouse to sea-to-table at Fish Market and relaxed ambience dining at Café Umi, every culinary moment can be tailored to suit your desire. Host a champagne brunch, a progressive dinner around the island, or a floating breakfast in your villa’s private pool, the choice is yours. And with a dedicated team that includes sommeliers, mixologists, and chefs, every bite and every sip is part of the celebration.
But it’s not just the location that sets this experience apart. It's the freedom to shape every moment. The activities for Maldives private group travel can be as high-energy or low-key as you like. Paddle boarding, jet skiing, sailing and diving are all available, alongside more grounded options like sound healing, aerial yoga and cooking classes. For younger travellers, dedicated kids’ and teens’ programmes ensure everyone is entertained. Meanwhile, those seeking peace will find sanctuary at AVI Spa, where overwater treatment villas invite moments of deep relaxation, complete with therapies from Biologique Recherche, Eminence Organics, and more. The entire resort becomes your playground, with a team on hand to design a schedule that matches your group’s pace and personality.
From the moment you arrive in Malé, the experience is seamless. A private lounge, scenic seaplane transfer, and personalised welcome await. The island becomes not just a destination, but a shared memory in the making, thoughtfully crafted, effortlessly delivered, and completely unforgettable.
This is not just a luxury holiday. It’s a chance to take ownership of time, space and experience in one of the world’s most beautiful locations. A full luxury resort buyout at InterContinental Maldives Maamunagau Resort offers something truly rare — a private island, completely yours, with every moment designed around the people who matter most.
Ready to make the island yours?
