AN INTERVIEW WITH PHIL DAVIES
Published on: 29 Jan 2024
As we stride into the New Year, InterContinental Maldives is introduced a new addition to the AVI Spa portfolio with the arrival of visiting practitioner Phil Davies. Phil brings a wealth of experience in vibrational attunement, emotional freedom, and hypnotherapy, setting the tone for transformative well-being experiences on our island.
Can you share your background and how you ventured into vibrational attunement, emotional freedom, and hypnotherapy?
I started my journey into energy work through meditation during my teenage years, where I discovered the profound impact of directing energy. Recognizing its synergy with energy healing, I delved into this field. Hypnotherapy became a natural extension when I noticed parallels between my intuitive meditations and relaxation inductions in hypnotherapy.
For those unfamiliar, can you explain what vibrational attunement is and how it contributes to overall well-being?
Vibrational Attunement involves guiding my clients on an inward journey of self-connection and deep relaxation. This energy-healing technique fosters well-being by addressing challenges such as stress, anxiety, and chronic pain.
How does Emotional Freedom Techniques work, and in what ways can they help individuals achieve emotional balance and freedom?
Emotional Freedom Techniques, a needle-free form of acupuncture, diminishes the emotional intensity associated with memories. Its simplicity makes it highly effective for various emotional challenges, including trauma and phobias.
In your experience, how does hypnotherapy complement other holistic healing practices?
Hypnotherapy seamlessly complements diverse healing practices by tapping into the effectiveness of the unconscious mind. Hypnotherapy proves invaluable, whether breaking old patterns, relieving pain, or enhancing overall well-being.
Could you share a memorable experience where a client had a profound transformation or breakthrough through your sessions?
Over two decades, I have witnessed remarkable transformations, from liberating individuals from years of fear due to abuse to helping those housebound due to phobias to lead fulfilling lives post-session.
How do clients typically feel after a session, and what kind of ongoing support do you provide for their well-being?
EFT and Hypnotherapy sessions are tested, addressing root causes and fine-tuning until fear dissipates. Resources for continued self-work, a library of instructional movies, and online consultations ensure ongoing support.
How can individuals maximize their holistic healing journey during their stay at InterContinental Maldives?
I would like to emphasize the importance of a positive relationship with oneself, urging guests to be mindful of their inner dialogue. Addressing inner criticism, often rooted in childhood, significantly impacts overall health.
