MALDIVES PREMIUM ALL INCLUSIVE
AN ELEVATED ALL-INCLUSIVE EXPERIENCE, CRAFTED FOR THOSE WHO EXPECT MORE
Step into a more indulgent way to experience InterContinental Maldives Maamunagau Resort. Our Premium All-Inclusive offer builds on our classic All-Inclusive dining, with the same great selection of restaurants and menus, and adds an elevated beverage collection along with a daily replenished in-villa minibar, so that a little extra indulgence is always close at hand.
From champagne at sunset to a curated wine list poured with dinner, this is all-inclusive with more to pour, wherever your day takes you. The in-villa minibar includes a selection of beer, juices, soft drinks, water, and snacks as listed on the Premium All-Inclusive minibar menu.
Package Inclusions
Daily breakfast, lunch, and dinner across a selection of the resort's finest dining venues.
Stay in a beautifully appointed luxury villa or residence.
Unlimited beverages from our Premium All-Inclusive menu, including champagne, cognac, premium spirits, curated wines, and daily signature cocktails.
A daily replenished in-villa minibar, stocked with a selection of beer, juices, soft drinks, water, snacks, and wine.
Club InterContinental benefits, including daily afternoon tea from 3.00 pm to 5.00 pm and evening aperitif from 5.00 pm to 7.00 pm.
A range of complimentary non-motorised water sports activities, including catamaran sailing, windsurfing, and stand-up paddling.
Other Inclusions
Private lounge access at Seaplane terminal
Complimentary Planet Trekkers kids club access (Age 4-12)
Island Curator and 24 hours e-Curator service
Byredo luxury bathroom amenities
30 minutes resort photoshoot session
Island bicycles for use during stay
Book Direct and enjoy Resort credits
Stay 7 nights or more and receive USD 50 per day resort credit
Redeem your resort credit in all restaurants and bars, the AVI Spa and Planet Trekkers Kids Club
For direct bookings only (Terms and Conditions apply)
Terms & Conditions
Minimum length of stay 4 nights
Package inclusion based on 2 adults
Children aged 12 and above will be considered as adults, and an additional supplement will be applied during the payment process for accommodation and transfers.
One night's non-refundable deposit will be charged at the time of booking.
The 20% savings on the seaplane transfers will be applied upon confirming the reservation.
20% transfer discount does not apply when using IHG points, Ambassador Certificate, F&F rate or employee discount.
How to Book
To book this package, please select "Premium All Inclusive" after choosing your room type.
