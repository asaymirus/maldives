STAY AND BOOK DIRECT BENEFITS
EXCLUSIVE PRIVILEGES, EXCEPTIONAL VALUE, SEAMLESS EXPERIENCES
Don’t look no further for the best deal, learn all about our complimentary Stay and Book Direct Benefits here.
Exclusive privileges are extended to guests who reserve their stay directly with InterContinental Maldives, offering enhanced value and a more elevated experience from arrival to departure. These benefits include access to Club InterContinental, with daily breakfast, afternoon tea, and evening aperitif, alongside a selection of complimentary non-motorized water sports.
Additional inclusions such as VIP airport lounge access, dedicated Curator service for personalized experiences, and added-value incentives for longer stays, including daily resort credits, further enrich the stay. Booking direct ensures a seamless journey complemented by thoughtful privileges and exceptional value throughout.
RESORT CREDIT* - LIMITED TIME ONLY
Stay 7 nights or more and receive USD 50 per day resort credit
Redeem your resort credit in all restaurants and bars, the AVI Spa and Planet Trekkers Kids Club
For direct bookings only
