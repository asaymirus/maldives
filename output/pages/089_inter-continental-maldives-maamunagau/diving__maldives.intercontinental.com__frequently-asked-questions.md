Can my children stay in the room for free?
Children under 2 years stay for free although green tax (USD 12 per guest, per day) is chargeable. Children between the ages of 2 – 12 years stay and eat for free (on their parents meal plan), green tax and seaplane fare charges apply. Any guest age 12 and above is considered adult and charges apply accordingly.
Do you charge for cancellations?
Yes, the amount will be depending on the terms and conditions of each individual booking
Is there a minimum night stay policy at InterContinental Maldives?
Yes, during Christmas and New Year festive period.
How do I pay? And which method of payments are acceptable?
We accept cash, credit cards [Visa, Master, Amex, and Union Pay], pre-payments via online payment links, and bank wire transfers.
What are the check-in and check-out times?
Our standard check-in time is 02.00pm and check-out time is 12.00pm.
What kind of electrical sockets are provided in the room?
We have in-built British three pin sockets in all of our villas. For additional support to our guests, we offer adaptors on request.
Is there a library?
Yes, there is a library in The Retreat, an adult-only outlet and a selection of books is provided in the Collective as well.
Is there a safe in the room?
Yes, all guest villas and residences have an in-built safe for guest use.
Are babysitting services available?
Yes, we offer babysitting services with an additional charge.
Does the resort provide beach towels?
Yes, we offer beach towels in every villa, at Café Umi beach and at The Retreat.
Does InterContinental Maldives cater for special dietary requests?
Yes, we encourage guests to share their dietary requirements ahead of arrival, but are also able to accommodate the common dietary requirements once on site.
Are Drones allowed at InterContinental Maldives?
To protect the privacy of all our guests, the usage of drones for videography or photography purposes is prohibited at InterContinental Maldives Maamunagau Resort. Upon prior notice to your Island Curator, the flying of drones may be permitted during certain off-island excursions supervised by the resort team.
Can I book third-party excursions, vessels, or watercraft while staying at InterContinental Maldives?
No, guests are not allowed to hire third-party excursions, vessels, or watercraft. Our concierge team is happy to assist you in booking a variety of excursions, including private yacht charters, diving trips, and water sports, all offered through the resort's trusted partners on the island. This ensures the safety, and exclusivity of the island's unique experience.
ABOUT MALDIVES
Where is the Maldives?
Maldives is the smallest country in Asia nestled at South-West of Sri Lanka and India. Maldives is an archipelagic nation located in the Indian Ocean.
What is the weather like in the Maldives?
Maldives experiences a tropical monsoon climate with year-round warm weather.
What is the currency used in the Maldives?
We use US Dollars for payments at our hotel.
How do I get to the Maldives?
Male International Airport (MLE) is the main airport, which connects Maldives to all major countries in South-East Asia including India, China and Sri Lanka. There are also direct flights from Dubai, and chartered flights coming in from Europe, China and Singapore.
What is the best time to visit Maldives?
The best time to visit Maldives is between January to May and October to December.
What do I require to visit Maldives?
The basic entry requirements are as follows; A passport or a travel document with Machine Readable Zone (MRZ) with at least 1 month's validity.
Do I need to present the PCR test result?
No, This is no longer a requirement to enter the Maldives
Do I need pre-approval for visa to visit as a tourist?
No
What is the process for completing the traveler declaration form?
All travellers are required to submit the Traveller Declaration within 96 hours to the flight time, during arrival via http://imuga.immigration.gov.mv.
