TWO BEDROOM FAMILY LAGOON POOL VILLA WITH CLUB BENEFITS
The first of its kind in Maldives, the two-storey Family Lagoon Villas is ideal for couples travelling together or families with older children.
These family villas in the Maldives feature two separate bedrooms with one king bed and one sofa bed in a separate room, two bathrooms, outdoor shower, spacious living area with powder room, sundeck and infinity plunge pool located over water and a terrace and balcony overlooking the lagoon.
Indulge in a world of exclusive privileges with our complimentary Club InterContinental benefits, including private lounge access, daily continental breakfast,  evening cocktails and canapés. Upgrade your stay to Club InterContinental and experience the ultimate in luxury and personalized service.
