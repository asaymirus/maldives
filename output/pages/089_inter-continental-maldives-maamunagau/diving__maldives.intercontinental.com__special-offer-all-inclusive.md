MALDIVES ALL INCLUSIVE
DINING AND NON MOTORISED WATER SPORTS ACTIVITIES INCLUDED
Indulge in the ultimate island getaway at InterContinental Maldives with our All-Inclusive offer. Discover the true meaning of relaxation in this tropical paradise, where every aspect of your stay is carefully curated for your comfort and convenience.
Our All-Inclusive package includes not only luxurious accommodations but also a delectable dining experience, where you can savor an array of culinary delights at our world-class restaurants.
Additionally, Enjoy 20% savings on seaplane transfers and all meal plans when you book a stay of 4 nights or more.
Package Inclusions
Daily breakfast, lunch and dinner at a selection of dining venues.
Stay in a beautifully appointed luxury villa or residence
Unlimited alcoholic and non-alcoholic beverages from the all-inclusive menu
Club InterContinental benefits of daily afternoon tea from 3.00 pm – 5.00 pm and evening aperitif from 5.00 pm – 7.00 pm.
A range of complimentary non-motorized water sports activities including catamaran sailing, windsurfing and stand-up paddling
Other Inclusions
Private lounge access at Seaplane terminal
Complimentary Planet Trekkers kids club access (Age 4-12)
Island Curator and 24 hours e-Curator service
Byredo luxury bathroom amenities
30 minutes resort photoshoot session
Island bicycles for use during stay
Book Direct and enjoy Resort credits
Stay 7 nights or more and receive USD 50 per day resort credit
Redeem your resort credit in all restaurants and bars, the AVI Spa and Planet Trekkers Kids Club
For direct bookings only (Terms and Conditions apply)
Terms & Conditions
One night non-refundable deposit required 07 days prior to arrival.
For stays from 23rd December 2025 to 09th January 2026 a non-refundable deposit required 45 days prior to arrival. For stays from 10th January to 28th February 2026 a one night non-refundable deposit required 30 days prior to arrival.
The Festive supplement for Christmas Eve of USD 515 ++ and New Year’s Eve of USD 760++ applies as mandatory extra charge.
Children aged 12 and above will be considered as adults, and an additional supplement will be applied during the payment process for accommodation and transfers.
The 20% savings on the seaplane transfers will be applied upon confirming the reservation.
20% transfer discount does not apply when using IHG points, Ambassador Certificate, F&F rate or employee discount.
How to Book
To book this package, please select "All Inclusive" after choosing your room type.
