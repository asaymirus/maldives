CELEBRATING ALL THE GOOD THINGS IN LIFE
Published on: 20 Nov 2023
As we bid farewell to another year, we cordially invite you to the mesmerizing shores of Maamunagau, where life's greatest treasures await, promising an unforgettable festive season like no other.
We believe in celebrating the true joys of life – love, family, culture, mindfulness, and all the positive energies that make every moment extraordinary.
Magical Moments Unveiled
Tom Weil will leave you spellbound with his enchanting magic and mind-boggling mentalism. Get ready for a thrilling experience, complete with a Magic & Mystery Box Set – perfect for kids and adults alike!
Sand Art on Island Shores
Artist Hussain Ihfal Ahmed, aka 'Iphpha,' will bring mesmerizing sand art to life. His creations, inspired by the Maldivian culture and its tropical wonders, will transport you into the heart of Maldivian tales.
A Dance of Joy
Roboman, the cheerful character dedicated to spreading happiness and inspiring lives, will dance his way into our hearts, adding an extra layer of merriment to our celebrations.
Holistic Healing and Sound Therapy
Shree, rooted in Kerala, India, brings the ancient art of holistic healing to Maamunagau. A holistic approach focused on restoring balance throughout body, mind, and soul through sound healing, astrology, and past-life regression therapy.
This festive season, at our island we start a celebration of life's finest aspects, inviting you to create cherished memories against the backdrop of paradise. Let the festivities begin, and let's revel in the joy of the season!
