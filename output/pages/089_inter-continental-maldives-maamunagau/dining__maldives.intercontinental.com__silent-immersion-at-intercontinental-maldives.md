PERSONALISED WELLNESS JOURNEYS
SEEK SOLACE IN SILENCE AT INTERCONTINENTAL MALDIVES MAAMUNAGAU RESORT
Intercontinental Maldives invites you to try our new Silent Immersion Packages, including 'Hermit' and 'Digital Detox.' These packages offer tranquility in the Raa Atoll, Maldives, helping you to disconnect and reconnect with yourself.
Each package includes guidance from a wellness mentor, who can curate activities like yoga and meditation to promote inner peace and wellbeing. The Wellness Mentor, part of the spa team, is different from the curator.

HERMIT SILENT IMMERSION PACKAGE
The Hermit Silent Immersion begins from the moment of booking, where you are invited to share your preferences and needs via a pre-arrival questionnaire. On your arrival, you will enjoy privacy with customized amenities and limited interaction.
Communication can be done through the e-Curator which will signal your participation. When you are ready for the program to end, information on peaceful resort areas will be provided.
Your communication options include meeting points or a messaging box for arranging meetings with your Island Curator.
DIGITAL DETOX SILENT IMMERSION
For those who are seeking a complete break from technology, guests can opt for the Digital detox silent immersion. Upon arrival, guests will surrender their digital devices to the Island Curator and commit to time without digital interruption, giving you that well-earned break for the outside world.
Your communication will be facilitated through meeting points and messaging boxes. All resort facilities, including In-Villa Dining and spa services, will still be accessible to you.
All packages will include the guidance of a wellness mentor, who will provide guidance and support throughout the stay and can carefully curate a stay involving yoga and meditation to promote inner peace and wellbeing.
SLEEP WELL PACKAGE
Embrace tranquility and restore balance with our Sleep Well Package, thoughtfully curated to promote deep relaxation and restful sleep.
Through a blend of calming therapies, holistic rituals, and gentle aromatherapy, this program helps quiet the mind, release tension, and prepare the body for restorative rest. Available as 3-, 5-, or 7-day journeys, each experience combines personalized wellness consultations, soothing massages, mindful practices, and nurturing rituals to guide you toward peaceful, uninterrupted sleep and renewed wellbeing.
DETOX PACKAGE
Renew your energy and restore inner balance with our Detox Package, designed to purify the body and refresh the mind.
This holistic program combines cleansing therapies, invigorating massages, and mindful movement to help release toxins, stimulate circulation, and promote overall vitality.
Available in 3-, 5-, and 7-day programs, each journey includes a blend of targeted treatments and wellness practices that leave you feeling lighter, clearer, and deeply rejuvenated from the inside out.
FITNESS & METABOLISM BOOSTER PACKAGE
Energize your body and elevate your fitness journey with our Fitness & Metabolism Booster Package, designed to restore vitality and optimize performance.
This results-driven program combines dynamic workouts with targeted recovery therapies to stimulate metabolism, build strength, and improve endurance. Available in 3-, 5-, and 7-day programs, it includes personalized fitness consultations, high-energy training sessions, mindful movement, and restorative massages—helping you feel stronger, more balanced, and recharged from within.
