Our culinary offerings promise an unforgettable experience that tantalizes your taste buds and elevates your senses. Join us on a gastronomic journey through the creativity of our passionate chefs, showcased in seven distinctive restaurants and bars. Each venue offers a unique culinary adventure, from the ocean-inspired delights of the Fish Market to the Mediterranean-influenced masterpieces at The Lighthouse. Let's delve into the world of extraordinary flavors, all set within thoughtfully crafted and inviting atmospheres.

DINING BY DESIGN
AN EXQUISITE CULINARY EXPERIENCE
Bask in the company of your loved ones in a range of intimate island settings from a secluded beach, a remote sandbank or the privacy of your villa with a curated menu and the service of a personal chef to perfect your experience.

Crafted menus fusing the freshest local seafood and premium cuts paired with fine wines selected by our Sommelier are complemented by the dramatic views of the ocean, with the sound of the waves adding to the ambience.

THE LIGHTHOUSE
A MEDITERRANEAN CULINARY ODYSSEY
Step into a world of Mediterranean delights at The Lighthouse. Begin the evening with a 360-degree view of the resort and the Indian Ocean on top of the lighthouse before you sit down to vibrant flavours crafted from the finest seasonal ingredients and seafood; where each dish is brought to life by the passion and expertise of our culinary team. For a more intimate setting, opt for a private table on the terrace or a candle-lit table on the beach at our signature restaurant.The warm and inviting atmosphere, complete with breathtaking ocean views, makes this venue an ideal choice for a romantic dinner or a special occasion.
Dress Code
Island Chic (no shorts, bikinis or beach wear)
Opening Hours
Dinner: 07.00 pm - 10.00 pm

FISH MARKET
A TASTE OF THE OCEAN WITH SCENIC AMBIANCE
For those craving the exotic flavors of Asia, Fish Market is a culinary haven. Blending the freshest Maldivian seafood with aromatic flavours from South East Asia, Fish Market is a unique sea-to-table, interactive kitchen concept where guests select their own seafood under the knowledgeable guidance of our chefs

CAFE UMI
ALL-DAY DINING EXCELLENCE
Start the day with a hearty breakfast indulging in traditional favourites alongside authentic Asian selections, cooked-to-order dishes or mouthwatering pastries as you take in the majestic morning views from Cafe Umi. The relaxed ambience of Cafe Umi is the perfect place to tuck into a light lunch or to enjoy the extensive menu on offer for dinner.

For a more interactive dining experience, book a table at the teppanyaki counter where our chefs will serve up sizzling dishes whilst entertaining you with their theatrical culinary flair.

THE COLLECTIVE
SCENIC VIEWS MEET DELICIOUS DELIGHTS
Located adjacent to the main pool,
The Collective
offers stunning views of the beach and ocean, creating the perfect setting for relaxed afternoons. It features a curated retail space alongside a lounge where guests can enjoy afternoon tea and a refined aperitif hour.

The on-site "Pizzeria" serves up delectable pizzas, while the Island Boutique showcases a charming array of unique items, perfect for gifts or personal indulgences.

THE RETREAT
A TRANQUIL SANCTUARY EXCLUSIVELY FOR ADULTS
An adults-only enclave perched overwater with sweeping lagoon views, The Retreat is an idyllic setting to while away the day, beginning with a refreshing cocktail by the pool, followed by a selection of sweet and savoury finger foods paired with a curated tea offering, and ending with sundowners or evening digestifs accompanied by premium wines, canapés and assorted cheeses.
Minimum age: 18+ | Maximum occupancy: 18 guests
