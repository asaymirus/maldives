LUXURY SPRING PARADISE ESCAPE
ISLAND ROMANCE
Indulge in a romantic Maldives escape at InterContinental Maldives, designed for couples seeking privacy, luxury, and unforgettable island moments.
Enjoy a complimentary upgrade to Half Board Dine-Around with daily breakfast at Café Umi and dinner across a selection of romantic dining venues. Retreat to a beautifully appointed villa or residence, your private sanctuary in the Maldives
Enhance your romantic stay with spa credit at AVI Spa, a romantic bath ritual and intimate bed decoration, and a complimentary floating breakfast once per stay for stays of five nights or more. Enjoy Club InterContinental privileges including daily afternoon tea and evening aperitifs, perfect for sunset moments together.
For couples seeking both romance and adventure, a range of complimentary non-motorized water sports is included, featuring catamaran sailing, windsurfing, and stand-up paddleboarding—ideal for shared island experiences in the Maldives.
Package Inclusions
Complimentary meal plan upgrade to Half board dine-around (Breakfast and Dinner).
Daily breakfast at Cafe Umi and dinner at a selection of dining venues
Stay in a beautifully appointed villa or residence.
USD 100 Spa credit at Avi Spa
Romantic bath ritual and bed decoration once during the stay.
Complimentary floating breakfast once per stay, applicable for stays of five nights or more
Club InterContinental benefits of daily afternoon tea from 3.00 pm – 5.00 pm and evening aperitif from 5.00 pm – 7.00 pm.
A range of complimentary non-motorized water sports activities including catamaran sailing, windsurfing and stand-up paddling
Other Inclusions
Private lounge access at Seaplane terminal
Complimentary Planet Trekkers kids club access (Age 4-12)
Island Curator and 24 hours e-Curator service
Byredo luxury bathroom amenities
30 minutes resort photoshoot session
Island bicycles for use during stay
Book Direct and enjoy Resort credits
Stay 7 nights or more and receive USD 50 per day resort credit
Redeem your resort credit in all restaurants and bars, the AVI Spa and Planet Trekkers Kids Club
For direct bookings only (Terms and Conditions apply)
Terms & Conditions:
Minimum length of stay: 4 nights.
One night non-refundable deposit required 21 days prior to arrival.
Children aged 12 and above will be considered as adults, and an additional supplement will be applied during the payment process for accommodation and transfers.
The 20% savings on the seaplane transfers will be applied upon confirming the reservation.
20% transfer discount does not apply when using IHG points, Ambassador Certificate, F&F rate or employee discount.
How to Book
To book this package, please ensure you select "Luxury Spring Paradise Escape" after choosing your room type.
