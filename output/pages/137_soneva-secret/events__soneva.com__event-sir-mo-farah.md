Run club with Mo Farah
Sir Mo Farah became one of the most celebrated long-distance runners of his generation through an extraordinary career defined by endurance, discipline and consistency. Across the London and Rio Olympic Games, he achieved the historic “double-double”, winning both the 5,000 and 10,000 metre titles, alongside multiple World Championship victories.
Over the course of his career, Mo has become recognised not only for his achievements on the track, but also for inspiring a broader culture around movement, resilience and wellbeing. At Soneva, he will host an exclusive Run Club, inviting guests to train alongside him through guided runs, movement sessions and conversations centred around performance, recovery and the joy of running in nature.
