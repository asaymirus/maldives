What taxes and service charges do I pay?
Tax on villas at Soneva Secret stands at 29.92%, based on your room rate. This includes a 10% Service Charge, 2% Carbon Offset Tax, and 17% Goods and Services Tax. Additionally, a Green Tax of USD 12 per person per night applies to the villa rate.
Where is Soneva Secret located?
It is found on the remote island of Dhipparufushi at the westernmost tip of the Maldives.
What is Soneva Secret time?
At Soneva Secret, we set our clocks forward by one hour ahead of Malé time so that our guests can enjoy more daylight hours. Malé time is UTC +5.
What is the weather like?
The Maldives’ tropical climate is influenced by monsoons – the average temperature is a balmy 30°C (86°F), and the coolest days are a pleasant 25°C (77°F). Sea temperatures are around 22 to 28°C (72 to 82°F). In the dry season (November-April), expect clear blue skies, light sea breezes and tropical sunshine. In the monsoon season (May-October), the weather can be more mixed, with sunshine, showers and occasional storms.
When is the best time to visit?
The Maldives is an all-season destination, with a tropical climate, sunshine-filled days and temperatures averaging 30°C all year round.
Diving is good at any time of the year. However, the monsoon season (May-October) is the best season for manta rays and other marine life due to the higher quantities of plankton.
Will I need any vaccinations?
Vaccinations are only required for visitors travelling to the Maldives from a region where yellow fever and cholera are endemic. For all other guests, no vaccinations are needed.
Is there a doctor on site?
We have a resort doctor available 24/7 at Soneva Secret. The nearest medical clinic is on the neighbouring island of Haa Dhaalu, which is less than 18 minutes away by speedboat. For conditions requiring further medical attention, there is a hospital in Malé.
