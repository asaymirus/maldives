One-Bedroom Overwater Sea Loft
Watch the stars from the comfort of the master bedroom with the villa’s retractable roof or spot an incredible array of marine life from the comfort of the king-sized bed. An adjoining children’s room with ensuite shower room and toilet are also situated at the back of the villa.
In addition, there is a study area, fully equipped gym, pantry and minibar, bathroom with an enclosed toilet, dressing room, shower and sunken bathtub with private access to the lagoon, and expansive dining area with sunken seats. Upstairs is a spa treatment room, dining sala with daybeds and a curved waterslide delivering you to the turquoise waters below.
