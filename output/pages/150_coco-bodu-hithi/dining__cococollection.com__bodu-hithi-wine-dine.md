At Coco Bodu Hithi, guests find a gourmet paradise as well as a natural paradise.
The pleasures of food are openly celebrated at Coco Bodu Hithi – satisfaction of body and mind is our guiding goal. To realise this vision, we have created four restaurants and two bars that share the secrets of local cuisine as well as offering global favorites. Michelin-starred chefs visit our Maldives Restaurants every year to create one-off experiences – a testament to our reputation for serving some of the best food and drink in the Maldives. Add to this our inimitable service style and you can be sure that you will live well while you stay with us.
From Maldives fine dining to beach barbecues, there’s something different for you to discover every night, if you so please.
