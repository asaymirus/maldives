This holiday season, Coco Bodu Hithi invites guests to a lineup of enchanting celebrations that capture the festive spirit in the stunning setting of the Maldives. From December 22, 2024, through January 2, 2025, the resort will host an array of events and activities designed for creating unforgettable moments with family and friends.
Event Highlights:
Tree Lighting Ceremony
: Begin the season on December 22 with the joy of decorating the Christmas tree, accompanied by carols, canapés, and live music on Latitude Beach.
Beach & Water Activities
: Embrace the island life with beach volleyball, festive coral planting, and snorkeling excursions that explore nearby sandbanks and vibrant coral reefs, led by knowledgeable guides.
Wellness Experiences
: Daily sunrise and sunset yoga sessions are available, offering a perfect way to connect with nature. For fitness enthusiasts, there are also Zumba classes and a New Year’s tennis tournament.
Evening Entertainment
: Guests can enjoy happy hour cocktails, live music, and themed BBQ nights. Each evening, talented saxophonists and DJs perform at Latitude Bar, filling the night with festive tunes.
Christmas & New Year’s Celebrations
: Mark Christmas Eve with a grand gala dinner, and keep the excitement going with Christmas brunches, Santa’s arrival, and traditional Maldivian Bodu Beru performances. For New Year’s Eve, guests are invited to a unique street food gala dinner that continues into the early hours of January 1 with live entertainment.
Culinary Delights
: Special dinners, mixology classes, and wine tastings offer guests the chance to savor festive flavors, with expertly crafted menus featuring local seafood, grilled specialties, and creative cocktails.
Exclusive Excursions
: Join sunset cruises, dolphin-spotting trips, and unique underwater experiences like the Underwater Christmas Tree Decoration, exclusively for certified divers.
Family-Friendly Fun
: The resort also includes children’s activities, such as gingerbread man decorating and festive games, ensuring that all ages can join in on the holiday excitement.
Coco Bodu Hithi’s festive program offers a blend of relaxation, adventure, and celebration against a backdrop of clear waters, lush greenery, and stunning sunsets—ideal for creating cherished memories this holiday season.
