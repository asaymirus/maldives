FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS
1. What is the standard check-in/check-out time?
The standard check-in time is 14:00 and check-out time is 12:00 noon. Early check-in and late check-out are subject to availability.

3. Does the island have a Kids Corner/Activities?
Yes. To keep the younger ones occupied, Coco Bodu Hithi has organised entertaining and instructive kid-friendly activities in addition to a Kids Corner.
4. Do you have Internet Access/Wi-Fi?
Yes, free Wi-Fi is available in all villas and public areas.
5. What are the amenities included in the villas?
A vast array of features is included in each villa.
Villa Facilities
• King size bed • Sunken living area • Indoor and outdoor shower • Private pool • Courtyard with daybed • Private pathway with the beach at your doorstep • Sundeck with loungers and daybed • Mini bar • In-villa Smart TV • Espresso machine • Complimentary Wi-Fi
6. Do you offer group experiences?
Our group activities include Coral Garden Snorkelling, Afternoon Fishing, Sunset Cruise, Sunset Fishing Adventure, Snorkelling with Manta Rays and more.
7. What are the different types of villas you have?
Coco Bodu Hithi has 98 stunning villas. You can choose from:
1. Island Villas with Pool, equipped with a private pool, daybed and sunbeds with ocean views
2. Water Villas with Pool, built over-water with private sundecks, plunge pools and daybeds
3. Escape Water Villas with Pool, offering an additional and separate living room for more spacious accommodation
4. Coco Residence perched on stilts over the lagoon, offering the ultimate premium experience
5. Grand Premier Beach Villa with Pool, ideal for couples or families seeking extra space, features a private outdoor gazebo ideal for relaxing and soaking in the beauty of nature, along with a cosy seating area for peaceful open-air lounging.
8. Can we bring pets?
No, pets are not allowed on the island.
9. How do I get to Coco Bodu Hithi?
Coco Bodu Hithi is located in Male’ Atoll, approximately 35 minutes from Male’ Airport by speedboat.

We do not allow drones for safety reasons and to respect our guests’ privacy.

13. Do you have any medical facilities on the resort?
Yes, we do have first aid medical facilities on the island. Our in-house Nurse is available 24/7 to assist you. The nearest hospitals are located in Male' City, 30 minutes by speedboat.
14. What are the meal plans offered at Coco Bodu Hithi?
Coco Bodu Hithi offers a variety of meal plans.
Meal Plans
Inclusions
Bed & Breakfast
• Daily buffet breakfast at Air Restaurant, which includes water, coffee, and tea. • Guests staying in Coco Residences have the option of an à la carte breakfast at Stars Restaurant.
Half Board (Breakfast & Dinner)
• Daily breakfast and dinner featuring a generous buffet at Air Restaurant.
• Guests staying in the Coco Residences may opt for an à la carte breakfast and dinner at Stars Restaurant.
• The meal plan does not include the minibar, Beach BBQ, wine dinners, In-Villa Dining, and other private events.
• Beverages are not included, except for water (house pours), and coffee/tea at breakfast.
Full Board (Breakfast, Lunch, & Dinner)
• Daily breakfast, lunch, and dinner featuring a generous buffet at Air Restaurant.
• Guests staying in the Coco Residences may opt for an à la carte breakfast, lunch, and dinner at Stars Restaurant.
• The meal plan does not include the minibar, Beach BBQ, wine dinners, In-Villa Dining, and other private events.
• Beverages are not included, except for water (house pours), and coffee/tea at breakfast.
All-Inclusive (Breakfast, Lunch, & Dinner)
• All meals (daily breakfast, lunch, and dinner) served buffet-style at Air Restaurant.
• Guests staying in the Coco Residences can choose all meals from an á la carte menu at Stars Restaurant.
• Lunch available at Latitude Bar with a selected all-inclusive three-course menu.
• A minimum stay of 4 nights includes: - One dinner at Stars Restaurant - One dinner at Aqua Restaurant
Aqua is closed for urgent unscheduled maintenance as of 1 August 2026. Aqua menu will be served at Stars in addition to the menu at Stars.
• In-villa minibar soft drinks and water replenished daily.
15. What is the size of the island?
Coco Bodu Hithi is nestled in a total land area of 410 metre in length and 140 metre in width.
16. What are the accepted payment methods?
Payments can be made in cash, credit or debit card (Visa, MasterCard, Amex, Union Pay, JCB, Alipay, WeChat Pay) at Coco Bodu Hithi.
