Manta, Turtle, Dolphin & Sandbank Trip
Niva Dhigali Maldives
Snorkelling Adventures
Experience an unforgettable day of marine encounters, ocean adventures and a secluded sandbank escape.
Begin your day with the ocean’s most unforgettable encounters, searching for manta rays, sea turtles and dolphins as they move freely through their natural world. After moments of discovery beneath the surface, drift towards a secluded sandbank where the pace slows and island serenity takes over. Every marine encounter is a gift from nature, with all sightings taking place in the wild and never guaranteed, making each journey beautifully unique. Available as a private or combined trip, this 5-hour experience blends adventure, discovery and the simple joy of time spent by the sea.
