Created for guests who make our destinations part of their story, Versa Keys brings thoughtful benefits to the moments guests love most.”
Membership begins automatically from your first eligible stay, with every qualifying night bringing you closer to your next key. As you progress, you’ll unlock exclusive savings, curated experiences and enhanced privileges designed to make every return even more rewarding.
Frequently asked questions
How does Versa Keys work?
Versa Keys is based on eligible nights stayed across Versa Collection and Niva destinations. As your nights accumulate, you move through four key levels: Member, Silver, Gold and Platinum.
Do I need to sign up?
No. Membership begins automatically from your first stay at a Versa Collection or Niva destination.
Where can I earn nights?
Eligible nights accumulate across Versa Collection and Niva destinations.
When do I move to the next key level?
Silver begins from 7 nights, Gold from 48 nights and Platinum from 84 nights.
What benefits can I unlock?
Benefits vary by key level and may include enhanced arrival privileges, early check-in, selected savings, curated experiences and guaranteed late check-out.
Do I need to book directly to receive benefits?
No. Your benefits are available however you choose to book, subject to eligibility and verification by the destination team.
Is late check-out guaranteed?
Late check-out is subject to availability. A room move may be required depending on occupancy and room category.
Who can I contact about Versa Keys?
For questions about your membership or benefits, please contact

Terms & Conditions
Early check-in
Early check-in is available from Silver level and above, subject to availability on arrival.
Late check-out
Gold members receive guaranteed late check-out until 4:00 pm. Platinum members receive guaranteed late check-out until 6:00 pm. A room move may be required depending on occupancy and room category.
Food and beverage savings
Food and beverage savings exclude destination dining experiences.
Spa savings
Spa savings exclude retail items.
Laundry savings
Laundry savings exclude dry cleaning.
In-villa dining and minibar savings
In-villa dining and minibar savings are available to Platinum members only.
