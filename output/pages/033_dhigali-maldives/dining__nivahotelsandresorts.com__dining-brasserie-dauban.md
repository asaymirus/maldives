Cuisine without borders
Our bountiful buffets always include…
A fresh salad and soup bar, a kids’ corner and a charcuterie station.
Themed live cooking stations, from street food stalls to noodle bars.
Grilled specialities, including fresh fish, meats and seafood.
A decadent dessert corner, featuring sweet treats from chocolate fountains to tropical fruit medleys.
Scenic dining
Situated poolside with sweeping terrace seating, Brasserie Dauban, one of the best restaurants in Seychelles, offers a relaxed island ambience. From morning light to the end of a fun-packed day, enjoy casual dining in a bright, open space that overlooks the resort’s lush greenery and the sea beyond.
Soul food
Whether you’re fuelling a day of discovery with a tropical breakfast or sharing a feast with friends beneath a sky ablaze with stars, Brasserie Dauban is the place to enjoy the warm hospitality of Silhouette Island.
