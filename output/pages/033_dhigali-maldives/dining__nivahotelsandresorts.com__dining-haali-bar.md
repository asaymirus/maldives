Haali Bar
NIVA Dhigali Maldives
Sunsets, cocktails, music. Pure magic.
Haali Bar, perched at the western tip of Niva Dhigali, is the ultimate sunset spot. Designed like a bird’s nest cradled by tropical greenery, this rustic bar offers and upstairs platform, swing seats, beanbags, and a relaxed beachside atmosphere.
As the sun dips below the horizon, sip on crafted mixes, crisp wines, or chilled beers while fiery hues paint the sky.
Haali is one of Dhigali’s most talked about experiences, with live saxophone sessions, DJ sets, or performances by live bands all adding to the magic of golden hour.
Unwinding after a long day of adventure or celebrating a special moment – Haali and a Dhigali sunset – there is no better place to be.
