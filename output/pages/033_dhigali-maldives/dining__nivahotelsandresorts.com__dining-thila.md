Thila
NIVA Kurumba Maldives
Beautifully plated and delicious food perched over the water
Perched above the Indian Ocean, this spot serves up modern vibes, fresh flavours, and a view of the underwater world right beneath your feet.
Evening suppers
Begin dinner with cured sous vide Salmon Fillet with Oscietra Giaveri caviar, or the refreshing Gabulhi Ceviche – a blend of virgin coconut, pineapple, and lime with a hint of chilli. Delight in lobster fresh from Thila’s aquarium and fresh, flavourful dishes from our raw bar. There are a variety of mains to suit every palate: vegetarian options like Mascarpone Porcini Ravioli and Miso Quinoa, hearty meat dishes such as Duck Breast, Veal Cheek, and Slow Braised Beef Ribs, and seafood specialties including Maldivian Lobster Curry and Mahi Mahi.
Ambiance
Settle in beneath a cream-coloured canopy for a serene breakfast, or reserve a table for two on the overwater deck for a special dinner, flavoured by the open air and twinkling lights over the sea.
