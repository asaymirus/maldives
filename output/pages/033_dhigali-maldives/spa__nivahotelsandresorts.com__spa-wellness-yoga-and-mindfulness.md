Discover the restorative power of yoga and mindfulness at Niva Labriz, an idyllic yoga resort in Seychelles. Each session is thoughtfully designed to balance the body’s energy, calm the mind and reconnect the spirit. Beginners can enjoy gentle postures and meditation, while sunrise and sunset yoga invite you to move in harmony with the island’s rhythms. Prenatal yoga supports both mother and baby with safe, mindful practices. Yoga nidra offers deep relaxation, allowing the nervous system to reset and recharge.
Breathwork enhances vitality and oxygenates the body, while guided meditation fosters inner peace and clarity. With expert instructors and breathtaking surroundings, every session encourages mindfulness, presence and a sense of renewed wellbeing, leaving you feeling energised, centred and at ease.
