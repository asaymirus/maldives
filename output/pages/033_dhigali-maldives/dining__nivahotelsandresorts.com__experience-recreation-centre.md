Recreation Centre
One island hub – where movement finds you and precision sports sharpen your edge.
The Recreation Centre is the heart of it – the place you return to, day after day. Meet your host, share what exercises and activities interest you, and book a court or private session tailored around you.
The pool shimmers, cool and perfect for laps. The gym is fully equipped with weights and machines. The tennis and multipurpose courts are ready when you are – reserve your preferred time and play at your own pace. Archery (adults only, basketball and badminton invite a little friendly competition. And in the games room, the pace slows – darts, billiards, and table tennis offer a more relaxed kind of play, where precision matters more than exertion.
