Faru
NIVA Dhigali Maldives
Beachfront dining, Mediterranean flavours
Faru is a lively beachfront grillhouse with stunning ocean views as far as the eye can see. Enjoy the warm sand underfoot while indulging in fresh, vibrant Mediterranean flavours – perfect for laid-back lunches in the sun or dinners lit by candles and the stars.
The lunch menu is a sun-soaked taste of the good life, from light salads to grilled delights. Explore traditional Andalusian gazpacho, octopus carpaccio, grilled cuttlefish, or the chef’s signature seafood platter – a lavish feast of lobster, langoustine, and scallops. For a sweet finish – the rich chocolate jaffa and raspberry dome.
As evening falls, the menu transforms into a journey through the sun-ripened flavours of the Mediterranean. Drawing inspiration from Cyprus, Greece, Italy, and beyond, each dish tells a story of coastal villages and timeless traditions. Imagine pork ribs with Neapolitan ragù, yellowfin tuna tartare, and prawn tagliatelle in a zesty mint and orange sauce – simple ingredients, made well. End the night with a classic tiramisu, a sweet memory of a perfect day.
