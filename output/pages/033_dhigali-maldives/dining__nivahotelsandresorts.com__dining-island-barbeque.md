Island Barbeque
NIVA Kuramathi Maldives
Where fire dances with the sea breeze
The gentle flicker of flame, the soft glow of the infinity pool, and a canopy of stars overhead. Island Barbecue is a celebration of land and sea, where bold flavours meet the natural beauty of the island.
Elemental Experience
A signature of the restaurant, dramatic tableside flambé creations offer a mesmerising spectacle. Watch as our talented chefs present exquisite cuts of Tomahawk, Porterhouse, or Black Angus tenderloin, masterfully flambéed before your eyes. The drama continues with seafood and desserts, capturing the very essence of Island Barbeque.
Ambiance & Rhythm
Under the soft glow of the moonlit sky, the infinity pool reflects the stars and the ocean’s edge is just steps away. This isn’t just a meal – it’s an immersive experience, where flavours and barbecue come together in harmony with the elements of nature: fire, water, earth, and air.
