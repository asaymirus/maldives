ENQUIRE ABOUT THIS PROPERTY
A contemporary all-inclusive resort in Raa Atoll, set across a compact island with a strong sense of space and natural greenery. Accommodation ranges from beach bungalows to overwater villas, with sunset-facing options offering the strongest sell for couples. The resort has a clean, modern design throughout, with multiple dining concepts that remove the need for reservations. A good range of included activities and a relaxed house reef make it appealing for both couples and families. Service is efficient and unobtrusive. Best for clients wanting a straightforward, well-run Maldives stay with strong all-inclusive value and minimal complexity.
Review
Excellent all-round property at the lower mid-level of the 5* bracket. Everything runs smoothly, and is well thought out. The rooms from Beach Villa upwards are incredibly spacious and are great for families with 2 younger children. The reef is excellent, the all-inclusive concept is plentiful. Make sure not to miss sunsets at Haali Bar, a truly amazing Maldivian Sunset experience.

Food &  Beverage
Capers
Capers restaurant pampers the palate with international cuisine and local delicacies. Breakfast is served with brilliant sunrise hues, whilst dining at Capers is a great way to begin the day. Evenings welcome the breeze, perfect for relaxed dinners.
Faru
Discover the true essence of Maldives dining at Faru restaurant offering
Mediterranean cuisine. The dining space at Faru is an open area overlooking
beach sand whilst providing jaw dropping sunset views in the evening.
Jade
Enjoy dining amidst cool breeze, glimmering waters and ocean views.  As the sunsets, the restaurant transforms into a unique setting complementing a fusion of flavours from Asia.  Enjoy a dining experience that is contemporary, simple and  showcases the creativity of Dhigali Maldives.
Pizzeria
Nestled in the jungle between Haali Bar and Faru Restaurant is this “Pop Up Pizzeria” offering both traditional and unique flavours that will satisfy any appetite. Guests can simply order, pickup and takeaway to whatever quiet location they choose to dine.
East Bar
East bar is admirably located to suite the island vibes, opening up to the views of horizon, whilst providing exotic cocktails, classic premium wines and beer.
Cafe
Café is a place to stop by for a quick refuelling, standing out among restaurants in Maldives for amazing combinations of gourmet cuisine and snacks offered to suit different dining moods. Savour different types of coffees from all around the world with a selection of pastries and cakes that have been freshly made.
Battuta
Signature Restaurant Battuta - a journey through Middle Eastern + Asian flavours, named after Ibn Battuta, one of the greatest travellers of the 14th century. Follow in the culinary footsteps of his travel experiences from North Africa, to the Middle East and all the way to South Asia whilst dining in the Maldives at this unique restaurant.
Haali Bar
Haali, Dhigali's sunset bar, shapes itself into the tropical green like a bird's nest at the west end of the island. It is the perfect place to order a cocktail + watch the evening sun colouring the tip of the island whilst creating breath-taking views of the sky + the sea.
