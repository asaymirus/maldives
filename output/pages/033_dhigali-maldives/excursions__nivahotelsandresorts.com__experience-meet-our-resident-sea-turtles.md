Meet Our Resident Sea Turtles
Niva Kurumba Maldives
Water Sports
While snorkelling in the house reef, you share the water with seven resident sea turtles, each known by name, moving through the lagoon with a rhythm all their own.
In our house reef, where coral ledges cast soft shadows, familiar faces move through the lagoon. Hawkeye, Kurumba, Ruby, Jedi, Aventurine, Evergreen, and Jade. Seven resident sea turtles, each with their own patterns, personality, and presence.
As you move through the water, you may find them grazing peacefully along the reef or rising to the surface for air. Sun on the surface, reef sounds below, weightless stillness around you.
What seems a fleeting encounter soon deepens. Our Marine Biologist identifies each turtle by the unique scales on its face – a non-invasive way to recognise individuals. You’ll learn about nesting habits, long-distance navigation, and the ways they survive.
Ongoing monitoring by our Marine Biologist supports wider conservation. What you witness here is not only beautiful – it is part of something carefully observed and protected.
