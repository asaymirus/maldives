Turtle Snorkelling by Dhoni
Niva Dhigali Maldives
Snorkelling Adventures
Cruise by traditional dhoni to snorkel alongside graceful sea turtles in their natural island habitat.
There is something timeless about meeting a sea turtle in the wild. Cruise across the ocean by traditional dhoni and spend 2 hours discovering the calm beauty of these gentle creatures as they move effortlessly through their underwater world. A quiet, meaningful encounter that stays with you long after you leave the water.
