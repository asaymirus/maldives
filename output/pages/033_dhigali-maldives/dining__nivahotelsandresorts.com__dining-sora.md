Sora
NIVA Velassaru Maldives
Japanese plates. Crafted pours. Perched on the water.
SORA rises above the lagoon, floating somewhere between sky and water. The view opens wide and the experience unfolds with lively theatre.
Pours & mixes
Sake served cool and precise. House-crafted cocktails with Japanese spirit. Fine wines and sparkling choices that feel celebratory without trying too hard.
Creative fusion
Delicate sashimi, artfully crafted sushi, charcoal-kissed robata, flavours balanced with intention and influencers of Puru.
Ambience & entertainment
Lantern-soft lighting, gentle music, sky shifting to deep indigo. The ocean life moves below as if part of the experience. Nothing loud. Nothing forced.
