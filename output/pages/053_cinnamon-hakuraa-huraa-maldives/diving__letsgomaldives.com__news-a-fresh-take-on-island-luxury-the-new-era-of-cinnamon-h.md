There is a special kind of magic in the Maldives-where the ocean speaks softly, time seems to slow, and every sunrise looks like it was painted just for you. Now, one of the archipelago’s beloved island retreats has stepped into a new chapter, transforming itself into a refined, adults-only haven designed for travelers who crave something beyond the ordinary.
Cinnamon Hakuraa Huraa Maldives has been reimagined into a five-star sanctuary, tailored for grown-up travelers seeking serenity, style, and elevated comfort. The redesign focuses on sophisticated spaces, immersive dining, and meaningful experiences-creating an island escape that feels both luxurious and refreshingly effortless.
A New Culinary Story
The resort’s dining scene has undergone a complete transformation with the addition of two signature restaurants-each with its own character and flavor journey.
Summer
Opening in late November, this beachfront, open-air venue captures the fun and vibrant spirit of coastal living. The grill-led menu highlights flame-grilled favorites, gourmet burgers, and southern-style comfort food with a relaxed tropical feel. Days flow easily from sunny lunches to laid-back dinners by the sea.
Corals
Launching in mid-December, Corals brings an indoor, semi-fine dining experience influenced by the underwater beauty of the Maldives. Expect refined global and French-inspired cuisine, thoughtful wine pairings, and live jazz to set an elegant evening mood. It’s intimate, stylish, and made for slow, lingering dinners.
Made for Wellness, Movement, and Leisure
To balance peace and play, the resort has introduced a refreshed Sports and Recreation Centre. Opening mid-November, the space includes:
A badminton court
Tennis and pickleball courts
A dedicated table tennis hub
Whether guests want friendly competition or simply a fun way to stay active, the new facilities make wellness feel social, energetic, and enjoyable.
Thoughtful Touches for Every Journey
Not every arrival or departure aligns perfectly with flight schedules-and the resort understands that small comforts make a big difference. A brand-new Guest Waiting Lounge now welcomes travelers who come early or leave late. With cozy seating, warm lighting, high-speed Wi-Fi, signature teas and coffees, books, and shower facilities, it offers a calm, relaxing transition before or after island time.
A Sanctuary for Those Who’ve Outgrown Ordinary
The resort’s vision is simple: create a refined island escape for adults who want more than just a holiday. They want connection. They want depth. They want experiences that feel personal.
Everything-from the restaurants to the recreational spaces—has been shaped with that in mind. Cinnamon Hakuraa Huraa Maldives blends contemporary elegance with Maldivian soul, offering a retreat where couples, friends, and solo travelers can truly unwind.
