Overview
Based to the South of Male Island in the Meemu Atoll, accessible via seaplane, Cinnamon Hakuraa Huraa Maldives offers classic Maldivian beauty and high levels of service at fantastic value for money. There’s an exclusive feel to the place that can make it ideal for couples looking to get away together.
The adults only island is set across two stunning islands connected by a wooden walkway over a crystal-clear lagoon, it offers the ultimate escape for couples and honeymooners.
The main island is the vibrant heart of the resort, home to elegant overwater and beach bungalows, gourmet dining venues, a stylish bar, and a soothing spa. Here, every detail is designed to inspire connection and relaxation, whether you’re lounging by the pool or sipping cocktails at sunset.
Across the lagoon lies the Platinum Island – an intimate sanctuary within the resort, offering an elevated level of privacy and exclusivity. With its own restaurant, bar, and beachside hideaways, it’s the perfect setting for those seeking pure indulgence and peace.
The resort is a great choice if you’re a fan of exploring the sea. Complimentary boat trips are offered to all guests to a range of great snorkelling sites. And for an additional charge you can make use of the PADI certified dive centre.
Top Tip: The resort offers a number of off-site excursions. They come at an additional charge, but they are really worth it if you’d like to get out and explore.
