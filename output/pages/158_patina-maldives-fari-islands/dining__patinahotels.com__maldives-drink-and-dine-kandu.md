A New Current of Maldivian Dining
Kandu celebrates the Maldives through stories, ingredients, and traditions shaped by its atolls. Sourcing directly from local fishermen and island producers, it honours indigenous ingredients while supporting local communities. Rooted in heritage and reimagined through contemporary craftsmanship, Kandu offers a fresh perspective on Maldivian cuisine.
