DESCRIPTION
Patina Maldives is not just a place to stay, but a rhythm to live by.
Far from all you know lies freedom inspired by all you are. Patina Maldives, Fari Islands, is an island of natural beauty and soulful depth where serenity, creativity, and connection come together. Every moment invites you to follow your instincts and ﬁnd what you seek, whether it is stillness, discovery, or renewal.
Part of the Fari Islands collective, Patina shares its home with two neighbouring islands that blend culture, community, and modern design. Created by architect Marcio Kogan of Studio MK27, its 90 villas and 20 studios reﬂect minimalist elegance and warmth. Studios overlook the vibrant Fari Marina Village while villas open to endless horizons of sunrise or sunset, each celebrating harmony between land and sea.
Life moves with the rhythm of the ocean. Drift through turquoise waters, unwind at the Flow Spa, or recharge with yoga and meditation. Sustainability shapes every experience, from zero-waste dining and homegrown produce to eco-education at the Blue School and Footprints Kids Club. Art enriches the island through James Turrell’s Skyspace “Amarta,” the Patina Art Residency, and the lively Fari Marina Village ﬁlled with boutiques, restaurants, and music.
Patina Maldives is a living canvas of art, nature, and soul that inspires a deeper way to live and connect.

Dining
Roots
Roots celebrates slow food with seasonal, plant-based dishes that nourish body and soul. Guests dine amid a lush permaculture garden, enjoying fresh, homegrown ingredients harvested daily. Each plate reflects conscious, zero-waste practices and modern culinary artistry. Open from 7pm to 10pm, Roots offers a mindful dining experience where nature’s bounty and sustainable gastronomy take centre stage.
Portico
Portico is the social heart of the island, offering breakfast, lunch, and dinner in a lively yet relaxing setting. Guests can enjoy an international buffet in the morning and Indian Ocean-inspired dishes later in the day. With generous outdoor seating and a curated library of music and literature, Portico blends vibrant flavours, community, and comfort for a dining experience that feels both dynamic and welcoming.
Wok Society
Wok Society offers time-honoured Chinese cuisine with a modern twist. Guests can savour delicate dim sum, wok-fired mains, and fragrant broths crafted with signature techniques and authentic spices. The restaurant radiates a chic, convivial atmosphere inspired by Hong Kong, where visual flair meets bold flavours. Perfect for an evening of shared plates, fresh brews, and the lively energy of contemporary Chinese dining.
Fari Beach Club
From noon to night, Fari Beach Club sets the scene for relaxed beachfront luxury. Guests can enjoy fresh seafood at the crudo bar, international dishes, and tropical cocktails while soaking in the rhythm of the waves. With stylish indoor and outdoor seating, the club blends social energy with refined comfort, making it the perfect spot for unwinding, dining, and connecting in a sun-soaked, vibrant setting.
Kōen
Kōen offers an intimate dining journey where Japanese and Nordic techniques merge in elegant simplicity. Guests experience a nine-course tasting menu crafted in real time, highlighting premium seafood, seasonal ingredients, and refined culinary artistry. Inspired by Zen and Scandinavian minimalism, each dish balances aesthetics and flavour, creating a sophisticated, sensory experience. Kōen is perfect for those seeking thoughtful, elevated fine dining in the Maldives.
Brasa
Brasa brings the fiery spirit of Latin America to life with open-flame cooking. Guests enjoy prime Patagonian meats, fresh seafood, and seasonal vegetables, all grilled to perfection over smouldering embers. Each dish celebrates bold flavours and elemental techniques, from tender chicken skewers to hearty arrocito. Set in a vibrant hacienda style space, Brasa delivers an immersive dining experience where fire, smoke, and spice take centre stage.
Veli Bar
Veli Bar is a vibrant poolside retreat offering casual bites and inventive cocktails. Guests can enjoy crisp salads, artisanal burgers, and savoury skewers alongside refreshing drinks, all served in a chic, open-air setting. Perfect for cooling off after a swim or unwinding in the sun, Veli Bar delivers a lively, easy-going atmosphere where creativity and relaxation flow in equal measure.
Helios
Helios brings the vibrant flavours of Greece and Turkey to the Maldives, set against the backdrop of a stunning sunset. Guests can savour mezze platters, grilled seafood, and Mediterranean classics crafted from the freshest ingredients. With beachside seating and panoramic ocean views, Helios is a gathering place where the golden light of evening enhances every meal, making each dining experience warm, memorable, and full of coastal charm.
Farine
Farine offers a contemporary Italian dining experience inspired by the spirit of the tavola. Guests can enjoy artisanal breads, seasonal antipasti, and open-kitchen mains, including hand-crafted pasta. House-made dolci complete the journey, paired with curated selections from the wine cellar. With its warm, inviting atmosphere, Farine celebrates abundance, conviviality, and the pleasures of sharing, making every meal a relaxed yet refined culinary experience.
Arabesque
Arabesque blends the vibrant flavours of Lebanon and North India in a modern take on a Bedouin tent. Guests dine amid colourful fabrics and ornate lanterns, enjoying shareable mezze, tandoor-grilled meats, and locally sourced seafood. Signature dishes, aromatic spices, and artful presentation create a lively culinary journey, complemented by Turkish coffee, Moroccan tea, creative cocktails, and a warm, immersive atmosphere that celebrates Middle Eastern tradition with a contemporary twist.
Go Go Burgers
Juicy, flavour-packed burgers crafted with fresh ingredients make this a perfect beachside pitstop. Each bite delivers hearty satisfaction, ideal for a casual lunch or a quick indulgence between island adventures.
Tum Tum
Vibrant streets of Asia come alive with bao buns, steamed dumplings, aromatic soups, and flavourful noodle dishes. Guests can enjoy authentic, street-friendly fare in a relaxed, casual setting at Fari Marina.
Tuk Tuk Gelato
Creamy, naturally sweet gelatos and refreshing sorbets tempt with ever-changing playful flavours. A delightful treat for all ages, perfect for a sunny afternoon or poolside indulgence.

Highlights From The Resort
Flow
FLOW begins from the moment guests arrive, guiding them through immersive spa experiences, mindful movement, meditation, and balanced nutrition. This holistic approach nurtures both body and mind while deepening the connection to Fari Island’s natural rhythms. Every detail is designed to restore balance, encourage vitality, and foster a sense of calm.
Spa
The Flow Spa is a soulful sanctuary featuring therapeutic treatments, a Watsu aquatic therapy pool, contrast bathing, and luxurious natural products. Guests can unwind in serene spaces that rejuvenate both physical energy and mental clarity.
Rhythm & Vibes
Wellness, creativity, and exploration converge to harmonize guests’ energy with the island’s natural and cultural rhythm. Whether through music, movement, or immersive activities, visitors are invited to align their senses and embrace a state of flow throughout their stay.
Ocean Footprints
Marine life and conservation are celebrated through coral restoration, sea turtle adoption, night snorkelling, dolphin observation, and diving adventures. Guests leave a positive footprint while enjoying unique encounters with the Maldives’ vibrant underwater world.
Fari Marina Village
This lively hub offers yacht berths, boutique shopping, art installations, galleries, restaurants, and communal lifestyle spaces. It serves as the cultural and social heart of the islands, where guests can explore, relax, and connect.
Family & Children
Young guests enjoy Footprints Kids Club, F1 in Schools, 3D modelling, culinary workshops, private cinema, Blue School diving lessons, and private photo sessions. These engaging programmes combine education, creativity, and fun for all ages.
Culture & Craft
Art tours, artist residencies, wine tastings, ceramics and coral workshops, and library access provide opportunities to connect with local and global artistry while learning new skills.
Destination Dining
Private beach dinners, sandbank picnics, floating breakfasts, and cooking classes—including local Maldivian cuisine and plant-based dishes at Roots—offer unforgettable culinary experiences.
Adventure
From diving, snorkelling, and surfing to water sports, ocean cruises, fishing, and the Maldivian Walk of Life, every adventure is designed to immerse guests in the island’s natural beauty.
Wellbeing
Yoga, padel and tennis coaching, meditation, and curated holistic therapies support relaxation, fitness, and mental clarity, ensuring every guest leaves revitalized and inspired.
