BARIN
About Hirsch Vineyards
Located on California’s Sonoma Coast, Hirsch Vineyards is shaped by a cool, maritime climate, where Pacific fog and coastal winds define the character of the land. Working exclusively with estate-grown fruit, the winery is known for its Pinot Noir and Chardonnay. Guided by sustainable farming and minimal-intervention winemaking, each vintage expresses the individuality of its terroir with clarity and precision.
About Guest Speaker, Ms. Jasmine Hirsch
Born into Hirsch Vineyards on California’s Sonoma Coast, Jasmine Hirsch is the daughter of founder David Hirsch. Raised among the vines, she developed an early understanding of terroir shaped by the surrounding landscape. With a background in environmental science, her approach to winemaking is grounded in a close relationship with nature. After gaining experience at wineries in the United States and abroad, she now leads as winemaker, guiding the quality and style of Hirsch wines with clarity and restraint.

About Torres Winery
One of Spain’s most distinguished wineries, Torres has remained family-owned since its founding in 1870. Based in Catalonia, it balances tradition with innovation, producing wines that reflect both heritage and evolving craft. With a strong commitment to sustainability, Torres continues to reduce environmental impact through renewable energy and responsible practices.
About Guest Speaker, Mr. Gil Serra
Originally from Barcelona, Barcelona, Gil Serra brings over 15 years of experience in the wine industry. With advanced professional certifications and an international perspective, he shares a deep understanding of wine, conveying its diversity and character to a wide range of audiences.
