Location
What is the nearest International Airport to Patina Maldives, Fari Islands?
Velana International Airport is the nearest international airport to Patina Maldives, Fari Islands.
How far is Patina Maldives, Fari Islands from the Airport?
Patina Maldives, Fari Islands is reachable within a 45-minutes luxury speedboat transfer.
Are we able to travel within the Fari Islands?
Guests staying in Patina Maldives, Fari Islands will have access to the Ritz Carlton Maldives, Fari Islands which located within the same lagoon. Transfer between the Patina Maldives, Fari Islands and Ritz Carlton Maldives, Fari Islands is complimentary. You are required to have a restaurant reservation in Ritz Carlton Maldives should you wish to visit.
How is the weather at Patina Maldives, Fari Islands?
Please be advised that the weather in the Maldives is unpredictable, erratic and localized making it difficult to correctly forecast the weather. Maldives is generally hot and sunny all year around with an average temperature of 25 and 31 degrees Celsius during the day. Being a tropical country, there is no season without rain but we rather have a less rainy season and a rainier season. You can expect less rain throughout from November until beginning of May. The monsoon season runs from May to October. Even when it rains, it is very unlikely to rain for days to an end but might rain for a couple of hours during the day or night leaving plenty of time to engage in indoor and outdoor activities
Do you have a surfing point at the resort?
While we do not have a surfing point within paddling distance from the beach, we do have surfing points nearby. For instance, one of the longest and lively lefts with Surfing Point in the Maldives “Chicken” is about a 20 minutes speedboat trip.
What time zone is followed in Patina Maldives, Fari Islands?
Fari Islands Time is +6 GMT which is one hour ahead of Male’ time.
Do I need to arrange a visa before my trip to the Maldives?
Traveling to the Maldives for vacation purposes does not necessitate a pre-arranged visa. Delightfully, upon arrival, guests from all over the globe are welcomed with a complimentary 30-day visa, allowing you to commence your exquisite island retreat with ease. For further information, please refer to the official website of Maldives Immigration.
What are the check in and check out times?
Our official check in time is from 2pm, and check out time is before 12pm.
Booking & Payment
Will I get the same benefits as mentioned on the website offers, if I book directly with the resort?
You will get the same offers, benefits as well as inclusions as what is mentioned on the website if you prefer to book directly with resort. To contact the resort directly, please send an email to: reservations.maldives@patinahotels.com or call: +960 4000555.
What is the payment and cancellation policy of the resort?
Pre-payment is required in order to guarantee reservations. How far ahead the payment is required and how far ahead you can cancel the reservation free of charge will depend on your stay dates and the offer under which you have booked.
How do I pay for my reservation?
Once your reservation is complete, the reservations team will send you a secure online payment link though which you can settle the payment via your credit card. Alternatively, we do accept bank transfers as well.
What are the payment methods while on the island?
Credit card/Debit Card payment as well as Cash payments are accepted on the island. As for credit cards VISA, American Express, MasterCard, Union Pay, Discover, JCB and Diners club are accepted. For Cash payments, US Dollar, Sterling Pounds, Euros, Australian Dollars, Swiss Francs, Singaporean Dollars as well as Maldivian Rufiyas are accepted. (For USD payments, notes that are of 2006 series or older are not accepted in the Maldives)
What is the cost for accommodating an additional guest in the villa?
For the comfort and convenience of your stay, an additional guest in the villa is welcomed at a rate of USD $240 per night, exclusive of taxes. Please be aware that rates are subject to change.
What are the guest capacity guidelines for villas and suites at Patina Maldives?
In the serene embrace of our villas and suites, a Studio is ideal for two adults and a child. A One Bedroom offers sanctuary for a family of four or three adults. The Two Bedroom accommodates up to four adults and two children, or six adults, while the Three Bedroom provides a generous haven for six adults and two children, or can be a luxurious retreat for up to nine adults.
What tax rates are applicable to resort purchases?
At our resort, purchases are subject to a 10% service charge and a 17% Tourism Goods and Services Tax (TGST).
Are digital payment tokens or digital wallet services like Apple Pay, Alipay, or WeChat Pay accepted at the resort?
In a move toward digital innovation, we accept digital payment tokens Tether (USDT), USD Coin (USDC), and Worldwide USD (WUSD), providing you with a modern and secure payment option. We invite you to explore these digital options and other payment methods we offer to accommodate your needs. For more information on how digital token payments work, contact us.
Please note, Bitcoin (BTC) and Ethereum (ETH) are not accepted at this time. Additionally, we do not support payments via digital wallet services such as Apple Pay, Alipay, or WeChat Pay.
Transportation
What is the Transportation Policy at Patina Maldives, Fari Islands?
To ensure a seamless and reassuring journey, all arrival and departure transfers to Patina Maldives, Fari Islands are thoughtfully arranged exclusively through our resort. This allows us to uphold the highest standards of comfort, safety, and personalised coordination from the moment your journey begins.
Kindly note that transfers arranged through third parties cannot be accommodated, as every detail of your arrival and departure is carefully curated by our team to ensure a smooth and effortless experience.
Does the resort have an airport counter?
Counter number D9 is the airport counter of Patina Maldives, Fari Islands. However, our Airport Executive will be on standby at the Arrivals Hall Gate holding an iPad with the logo of “Patina Maldives, Fari Islands” displayed on the Screen, who will guide guests through the Airport and to the Speedboat.
What is the standard mode of Airport transfer to Patina Maldives, Fari Islands?
Patina Maldives, Fari Islands organizes Luxury Speed Boat Transfers for Arrival & Departure from and to Velana International Airport for all guests.
The Scenic Luxury Speedboat journey will be approximately 45-minutes to access the resort from the Airport. During the journey, Cold Towel, Refreshments and Mineral Water will be available.
What types of Boats are used for the airport transfers?
Our Luxury Speedboats are fully closed and airconditioned speedboats that come with comfortable cabin-style seating arrangement. We can accommodate a maximum of 13 person on the Luxury Speedboat.
Is there a luggage limit for the luxury speed boat transfers?
A Baggage allowance of 20kg checked luggage and 5kg carry-on luggage per person will be provided for the shared transfer journey. However it is not strictly enforced on our guests.
Should the luggage exceeds the mentioned limit, no additional charges are levied on extra luggage, unless and additional private speedboat is required just for luggage.
Is there a waiting time for the transfers to the resort?
Shared luxury boat transfers are scheduled in line with international flight arrival times to Velana International Airport. Please expect a waiting time of 30 to 60 minutes, as this is shared with other guests. Alternatively, private transfers are available upon request for an additional fee.
Is it possible to arrange seaplane transfer?
It is Indeed possible to arrange either shared seaplane transfer or private seaplane transfer to Patina Maldives, Fari Islands. The trip will take roughly 10 – 15 minutes.
How can I make use of the CIP terminal upon my arrival?
For a seamless and exclusive arrival experience, our CIP service must be arranged before your arrival. Guests who opt for this service will be greeted with a personalized name board at the aircraft's staircase immediately upon disembarking. You'll then be whisked away by a private vehicle to the comfort of the CIP lounge, where you can relax while immigration formalities are taken care of and your luggage is conveniently handled.
What happens if my international departure is scheduled for late evening?
We facilitate speedboat or yacht transfers at all times, day or night. Should your flight be in the late evening, indulge in Patina Maldives' full range of amenities up until your departure, and trust that we will coordinate your transfer to ensure you arrive at the airport with ample time, approximately 2.5 hours before your flight, for a serene farewell. Please note that transfers outside standard operating hours may incur additional charges. We recommend consulting with our concierge for detailed information on these charges to ensure clarity and peace of mind during your travel arrangements.
If I've booked multiple stays, will I be charged for transfers more than once?
For guests booking multiple stays, transfer charges are applied only once per person for each visit. In the event that any additional charges mistakenly appear on your account related to transfers, please bring them to our attention. Our team is committed to reviewing such instances carefully and will make adjustments as appropriate.
How early can I secure a reservation at the resort?
We offer the ability to book your stay up to 365 days in advance through our website. For those planning beyond a year, our reservation specialists are at your service to assist with your travel arrangements. Contact us at reservations.maldives@patinahotels.com for further assistance.
What is the process to arrange the fast track CIP service?
To elevate your arrival, the fast track CIP service can be arranged with ease. Simply reach out to our Reservations team and provide us with passport details for all traveling guests, along with your flight information, to secure this expedited service.
What details are required if I'm arriving via private jet?
For guests arriving with the exclusivity of a private jet, please provide us with the contact information for your jet handler. Our team will coordinate directly to gather all necessary flight details, ensuring your arrival is nothing short of seamless.

Accommodation
Is it possible to accommodate 2 children in a One Bedroom Villa?
We can accommodate 2 children or 01 child and 01 infant in additional to 02 adults in a One Bedroom Villa. We can provide 1 x roll-away bed and 1 x sofa bed or 1 x roll-away bed and 1 x baby cot in the villa.
How much do you charge for an extra child?

Can the sofa in the villas be converted to a bed?
We can convert the sofa in the villas to a sofa bed. The sofa beds are 34 inches (86 cm) in length and 85 inches (215 cm) in width and can accommodate children who are 11 years old and below.
Is it possible to arrange 2x villas next to each other?
If the booking is made in advance we can certainly arrange two or more villas next to each to other.

Kids Club
Do you have a kids club?
We have a very well equipped children’s creative and educational facility called Footprints.
What age is allowed in the kids club?
Children between ages of 4 – 11 years old will have complimentary access to the Footprints kids club. Children who are 3-years old and below will also have access to the Footprints kids club with the presence of a parent or a guardian. Alternatively, babysitting service can be booked for children who are 3-years old and below.
Is it possible to book babysitting service?
Babysitting service can be booked with the below prices and terms and conditions;
Prices;
Normal hours (8am – Midnight): $64 per hour
Extended hours (Midnight – 2:00am): $96 per hour
Pricing is subject to 10% service charge and 16% government tax.
Guests are required to book baby-sitting services 24-hours in advance and they may do so via their butler (Essentialist) or the Patina Lifestyle app.
