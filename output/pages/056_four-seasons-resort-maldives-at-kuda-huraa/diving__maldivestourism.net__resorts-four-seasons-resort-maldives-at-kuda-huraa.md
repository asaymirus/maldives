A short speedboat ride from Malé, this Maldives resort, infused with Maldivian village charm, has a vibrant garden island all to itself. Sail to the Resort’s Island Spa for an afternoon of natural bliss. Embark on a snorkelling shark safari, find your perfect wave at our Surf School, or cast away for a day on a Robinson Crusoe adventure. Celebrate your Maldives vacation with infinite views of sea and sky, sunshine and stars, and discover the unforgettable, on land and at sea, right at your doorstep.
Guest Rooms and Suites
Thatched beach pavilions and bungalows line the white-sand beach at Four Seasons Resort Maldives at Kuda Huraa, while water bungalows are built on stilts over the lagoon. The Royal Beach Villa offers lavish one-of-a-kind accommodations for guests. From their thatched roofs and timber latticework to the beautiful white stone walls, all the architectural details are constructed by local craftsmen using indigenous materials and techniques that date back centuries, to reflect a true sense of the Maldives.
Enhance your stay with a cruise on the Four Seasons Explorer, a private three-deck, 11-cabin catamaran that takes guests island-hopping, stargazing and diving in Four Seasons style. The Four Season resort at Landaa Giraavaru also makes an exciting complement to an experience at Kuda Huraa and a Four Seasons Explorer cruise.
Spa
Discover a haven of tranquillity at the Island Spa at Four Seasons Resort Maldives at Kuda Huraa, situated on its own tropical island just a minute’s sail by dhoni (traditional Maldivian boat) across Kuda Huraa’s lagoon.
Luxuriate in the Island Spa’s wholly natural treatments, complemented by the restorative power of marine-based nutrients. Feel at one with the ocean in seven over-water treatment pavilions – each designed to accommodate couples – featuring separate bathing areas, outdoor shower courtyards and massage tables with sea-viewing portals.
Spa programmes lasting two to seven days are complemented by the Island Spa Rejuvenation Menu, available in Reef Club restaurant, and healthy-eating recommendations are printed on all other restaurant menus.
Yoga pavilion
An open-air yoga and meditation platform faces the ocean. In addition to complimentary early morning Sun Salutation and evening Sunset Meditation sessions, the following private sessions can be booked with the Resort’s resident yogi:
Asanas – body positions
Pranayama – breathing techniques
Dhayan – concentration techniques
Yoga nidra – relaxation techniques
Dining
Dining at Four Seasons Resort Maldives at Kuda Huraa is an eclectic experience. Guests can savour fine Indian cuisine, celebrate the tastes of contemporary Italy or feast on Asian delicacies and choose from a cellar of handpicked fine wines.
Natural settings inspire the palate and invite toasts to the magnificent Maldivian landscape – claim a seaside table, settle into a cafe pavilion, swim up to the poolside bar or sit perched above the lagoon on a torchlit terrace for an unforgettable meal.
Special arrangements can be made for beach picnics and sandbank dinners, Robinson Crusoe picnics, dhoni breakfasts and private parties of any size. Delicious In-Bungalow Dining is available 24 hours a day.
