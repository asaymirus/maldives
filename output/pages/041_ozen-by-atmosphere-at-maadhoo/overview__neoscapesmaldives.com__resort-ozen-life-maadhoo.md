DESCRIPTION
Step into a dreamscape of blue at Ozen Life Maadhoo, where every moment is pure luxury and every detail is thoughtfully curated.
Set in the sparkling waters of the South Malé Atoll, this intimate all-inclusive island retreat blends modern elegance with authentic Maldivian charm. Luxurious beach and overwater villas, shaded by swaying palms and traditional thatched roofs, open out to vibrant coral reefs and endless ocean views. Indulge in champagne breakfasts, à la carte dining, premium beverages, and daily treats by the pool. With spa treatments, diving experiences, and water sports included for stays of four nights or more, Ozen Life Maadhoo lets you fully relax without worrying about the details. This is where your Maldives resort fantasy comes to life.

Dining
The Palms (All-day Dining)
A worldly gourmet escape with stunning lagoon views. Enjoy breakfast, lunch, and dinner featuring a diverse menu of international flavors, live cooking stations, and fresh ingredients from our Hydroponics Garden. Set in a bright, breezy pavilion with vibrant interiors and soaring ceilings, THE PALMS is a feast for all senses.
Traditional - IndoCeylon
A flavourful journey through South Asia. Enjoy authentic Sri Lankan and Indian dishes, beautifully presented on ethnic-style platters. Each dinner is a celebration of rich spices, regional recipes, and timeless culinary traditions by the sea.
Traditional - Peking
A journey to the heart of the Far East. Discover authentic Chinese cuisine crafted with time-honoured techniques and premium ingredients from the Silk Route. Each dish is artfully presented, offering bold flavours and a true taste of tradition in every bite.
M6m (Open for Lunch & Dinner)
Dive into an unforgettable dining experience at our signature underwater restaurant. Surrounded by vibrant marine life, savour exquisite seafood dishes crafted with finesse and paired with fine wines. Open for lunch and dinner, M6m blends culinary artistry with breathtaking ocean views.
Joie De Vivre
The heart of Ozen, this lively poolside bar and pizzeria lives up to its name, the joy of life. Sip handcrafted cocktails, enjoy tasty bites, and soak in spectacular sunset views over the turquoise lagoon. Open from 9 am to 1 am, it’s where island vibes meet Miami chic.
Gin Is In
Discover over 25 premium gins at this chic speciality bar, where expert bartenders craft perfect pours and creative cocktails. Included in the Indulgence Plan, enjoy tastings or cocktail classes while sipping your way through exotic blends and unforgettable sunsets.
Lonu
A modern tribute to Maldivian heritage. Lonu reimagines traditional island recipes into elegant small plates, infused with local spices and paired with handcrafted drinks. With live cooking and chef interactions, this is a multi-sensory dining experience that brings the spirit of the Maldives to life.
Hudhu bay
A chic beach club on Maadhoo’s northern shore, Hudhu Bay serves up laid-back island vibes, tropical cocktails, and stunning sunset views. As night falls, enjoy DJ beats and the signature Crab Dinner Experience featuring Sri Lanka’s famed mud crab in bold global flavours.
