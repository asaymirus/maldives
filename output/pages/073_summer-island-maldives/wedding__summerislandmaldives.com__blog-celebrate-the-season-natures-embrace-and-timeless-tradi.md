Celebrate the Season: Nature's Embrace and Timeless Traditions!
This festive season, join us in a vibrant tapestry of celebrations at Summer Island Maldives, where the tranquil beauty of our tropical paradise intertwines harmoniously with the spirit of Christmas. Embrace the magic of the holidays as we infuse our island sanctuary with the essence of nature’s greenery and the warmth of timeless traditions.
December 21: Christmas Tree Lighting Extravaganza with Live Music
Step into the enchantment as our island glowing with twinkling lights welcomes you to a magical evening. Join us for the illumination of our Christmas tree accompanied by live melodies that set the tone for the festive merriment ahead.
December 22: XMAS Beach BBQ
Sink your toes into the soft sands and indulge in a beachside feast under the stars. Delight in a tantalizing BBQ experience that captures the essence of the season while embracing thelaid-back charm of island living.
December 23: Live Music
Relax with the beautiful tunes, indulging in both local and international music that will delight the senses.
December 24: Festive Eve Extravaganza
Prepare for a night of unparalleled celebration! Sip on delectable cocktails, revel in a Christmas gala dinner that awes the taste buds, and dance the night away at our Christmas party. Brace yourself for a special appearance by none other than Santa himself!
December 25: Christmas Brunch and Acoustic Serenade
Join us for a delectable Christmas brunch that celebrates the flavors of the season. Be
enchanted by live acoustic melodies as Santa makes a memorable appearance, spreading joy and laughter.
December 26: Bonfire with live music
Enjoy a beautiful bonfire accompanied with fireside rhythms, for an unforgettable evening under the stars.
December 27: Wine and Cheese Tasting at the Beach
Savor the finer things in life against the backdrop of our breathtaking island. Indulge in a
sophisticated evening of wine and cheese tasting, serenaded by soothing acoustic tunes as the sun sets over the horizon.
December 28: New Year BBQ
Relish in the ultimate outdoor dining experience, capturing the essence of the year’s end with every delicious bite. Let the charm of island living infuse your last BBQ, creating a memorable evening filled with laughter and good company.
