Know more
From Velana International Airport, a mere 45 minutes on the speedboat or 15 minutes on the seaplane to reach our Summer shores.
Our usual check-in time is 2 pm and check out is at noon.
We are in North Male’ atoll just 35km away from Velana International Airport where tourists usually arrive in the Maldives
Yes, once you exit into the arrival hall with other passengers, make your way to the resort booths visible upon your exit. Our airport representative will welcome you at the Kaimoo Resorts and Hotels Booth A10 and direct you to your transfer to the resort.
Everything is allowed - except public nudity so skinny dipping sadly must remain out of your itinerary. Also, tourists are encouraged to dress a bit conservatively while visiting local islands by covering their shoulders and thighs.
