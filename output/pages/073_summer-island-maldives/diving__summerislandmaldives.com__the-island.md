The Island
OCEAN VIEWS, ZESTY ROOMS, NATURAL HUES
The Summer Island ethos combines a barefoot island style of living with all the comforts of a resort living. Owned and managed locally, this resort island in Maldives is carefully crafted for a quintessentially Maldivian experience.
