Dive Point
Travelers seeking unforgettable diving holidays in Maldives will find Adaaran Select Meedhupparu the perfect choice. Ideally located near some of the Maldives’ most renowned dive sites, our resort offers access to vibrant coral reefs teeming with diverse marine life. Dive into the hotel reef or explore neighboring dive spots, where you’ll encounter multi-colored corals, schools of mesmerizing coral fish, graceful stingrays gliding elegantly through the water, and sleek sharks prowling the seabed. Our professional dive center provides personalized guidance, ensuring every dive is safe, enjoyable, and tailored to your experience level. This makes our resort a premier destination for world-class diving experiences.
