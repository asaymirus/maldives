Adaaran Select Meedhupparu
Location
Adaaran Select Meedhupparu, a Maldives resort island which is located in Raa Atoll, 136 km to the north of the capital of the Maldives, Male. A seaplane journey will cover this distance in 45 minutes. The resort’s pristine location, with beautiful visible coral reefs, makes it one of the most sought-after Maldives private islands. Exceptional dive sites are available in the vicinity of the resort, along with spa facilities and a gym.
Weather
Maldives Weather
The Maldive Islands enjoy a delightful climate with sunshine and pleasant sea breezes for the greater part of the year. For the extent of the drier season the visitor will have the opportunity to take advantage of eight to nine hours of sunshine each day during the best season for Maldives. For the majority of the year the Maldives is blessed with one of the most agreeable climates on the globe.
During some parts of the year rainfall is experienced, with more predictability in the islands that lie close to or to the south of the Equator. Turbulent weather phenomena are very infrequently experienced in the Maldives and are of very short duration.
