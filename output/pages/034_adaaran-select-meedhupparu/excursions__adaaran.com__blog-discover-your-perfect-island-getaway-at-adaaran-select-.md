Discover Your Perfect Island Getaway at Adaaran Select Hudhuran Fushi
24 March 2026
Adaaran Select Hudhuran Fushi
invites guests to step into a summer shaped by movement, colour, and connection. Set in the heart of North Malé Atoll, the island brings together sunlit days, ocean adventures, warm hospitality, and a generous Premium All-Inclusive offering designed to make every stay feel easy from the moment you arrive.
The resort is known as one of the Maldives’ most dynamic escapes, where grand ocean views, wide beaches and lush tropical greenery create a setting that feels both energising and effortless. Just a short speedboat ride from Malé, the resort offers quick access and immediate immersion, making it ideal for families, couples, groups, and surfers looking to spend more time enjoying the island and less time in transit.
Days at Hudhuran Fushi move at your pace. Paddle out, snorkel in clear lagoon waters, set off on a dolphin cruise, or simply slow down beneath the shade of the palms. Across the island, guests can choose from experiences that feel active, social, or completely laid-back, all within a setting designed to give more of what matters.
Dining
is central to the stay, with restaurants and bars that carry guests from relaxed breakfasts to beachside lunches, sunset drinks, and lively island evenings. From buffet spreads and fresh seafood to casual bites and themed dinners, each
experience
reflects the easy rhythm of island life, with the Premium All-Inclusive offering bringing added freedom to enjoy more throughout the day.
Accommodation
is equally varied, with villas set across gardens, beachfront, and overwater settings. Whether guests are travelling as a couple, with family, or for a solo getaway, the resort offers spaces that feel comfortable, relaxed, and naturally connected to the island around them.
As the sun begins to dip, the atmosphere shifts once again. Sunset gatherings, live entertainment, social dining, and beachside moments give the island its signature energy, creating the kind of evenings that stay with you long after the journey home. It is this balance of movement and ease that gives Adaaran Select Hudhuran Fushi its distinct character.
At
Adaaran Select Hudhuran Fushi
