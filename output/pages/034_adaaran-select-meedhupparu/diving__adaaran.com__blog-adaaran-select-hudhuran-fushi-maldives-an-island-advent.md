Adaaran Select Hudhuran Fushi, Maldives: An Island Adventure Where Tropical Bliss Meets Endless Discovery
11 February 2026
Adaaran Select Hudhuran Fushi
sits a short speedboat transfer from Malé and combines palm-fringed beaches, a broad lagoon and a lively island culture with a
24-hour Premium All-Inclusive experience
.
Accommodation
ranges from beachfront
Beach Villas
and
Lohis Villas
close to the surf to private
Overwater
and
Ocean Villas
, all designed for privacy, comfort and easy access to sand and sea. This expansive mature island, complete with large banyan trees, features multiple family-friendly dining venues and bars with dedicated menus for children alongside its shaded terraces.
Families are central to the island’s offering. The resort’s dedicated kids’ programme at the
Koamas Kids Club
provides structured activities and supervised play, creative workshops, movie sessions, treasure hunts, nature walks and themed events that keep children engaged while parents relax. Calm, shallow waters near the shore make first swims and paddle experiences straightforward for small children; equipment for non-motorised water sports such as kayaks, stand-up paddleboards and beginner-friendly snorkelling gear are available, so families can explore reef gardens together. Island
excursions
such as dolphin cruises, sunset trips and guided reef excursions offer shared moments designed to build memories across generations.
Dining
and bars form a major feature of the guest experience with
Adaaran Select Hudhuran Fushi
recognized as a culinary haven for food and drink lovers alike. The resort operates four restaurants and four bars that cover buffet and live-kitchen formats, à la carte fine dining and casual beachfront options.
Banyan Restaurant
presents an international buffet showcasing traditional Maldivian fare to Western favourites together with live cooking stations where chefs prepare dishes to order and host demonstrations that connect guests to regional and island flavours.
Sunset Restaurant
is the resort’s fine-dining venue overlooking the harbour, offering an à la carte menu for special evenings featuring international, continental, Indian, Chinese and Arabic influences, which Ocean Villa guests enjoy exclusive and priority access to, and other guests may book and experience during their stay.
Indian Pavilion
showcases North and South Indian cuisine prepared with authentic methods and spices. Casual dining venues such as
Lohis Wave
, noted for its beach-bar refreshments, pizzas and proximity to the surf, and
Beach Grill Bar
, popular for serving up beachfront bites such as wraps, burgers, salads and snacks by day and then shifting into a casual cocktails & bar venue by night, offer a dining experience for those who prefer a laid-back island vibe.
The island’s bars support both social evenings and quiet moments.
Lohis Bar
provides a relaxed vantage for watching waves,
Hiyala Bar
serves poolside cocktails 24 hours a day,
Beach Grill Bar
blends daytime snacks with evening drinks, and
Tiki Bar
stages nightly entertainment and DJ sets.
Wellness and culture are integrated into the island narrative as well. Combining traditional therapies with island ingredients,
Chavana Spa
offers Balinese-style treatments, yoga and guided meditation. Cultural elements such as bodu beru drumming performances, Maldivian cooking demonstrations and curated island tours connect guests with the local heritage, giving context to shore excursions and dining programmes. Sustainability actions are visible in guest education activities, marine-awareness programming and reusable-resource initiatives across the island.
Hudhuran Fushi is internationally renowned for its
surf
culture
, anchored by the famous
Lohis Wave
– a long, peeling left-hand reef break directly in front of the resort. The resort provides guided access to multiple nearby breaks including Jails, Cokes, Ninjas, Sultans, Honkeys and Chickens via boat transfers as well and maintains a surf school and experienced guides to support sessions. All surfers are required to have at least an intermediate level of surfing ability, be capable swimmers, and meet suitable fitness criteria. To protect wave quality and guest safety the resort limits the maximum number of surfers per day to 45. The all-inclusive
surf package
typically covers meals, selected beverages, boat transfers to surrounding breaks, access to Lohis Wave facilities and dedicated surf catering, plus benefits such as a complimentary 30-minute massage for surfers and discounts on additional spa treatments.
Beyond surf, the island schedule includes snorkelling and diving excursions to vibrant reefs, dolphin-watching, sunset cruises, fishing trips, and non-motorised water sports. Special experiences such as guided reef walks, island nature trails and photography tours are available by arrangement to deepen your island adventure as well.
The
24-hour Premium All-Inclusive
plan includes meals, selected beverages, many daily activities and scheduled excursions for a frictionless experience with particular offerings such as private dinners, specialty excursions, certain premium transfers and guest-specific services offered as add-ons. For guests seeking a surf-focused itinerary, the dedicated all-inclusive surf package integrates daily boat transfers twice a day, dedicated dining options and tailored in-water support.
Adaaran Select Hudhuran Fushi
offers something for everyone. Families wanting supervised programmes for their children, adults looking for a broad choice of activities for an adventure, food lovers looking for wide culinary variety and seasoned surfers looking for a recognized destination to access world-class breaks, the resort supports both action and calm with stays built around waves, reef exploration, family play and memories of food, music and quiet horizon watching.
