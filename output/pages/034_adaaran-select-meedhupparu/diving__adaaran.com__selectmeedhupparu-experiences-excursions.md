Try trolling fishing under the guidance of our crew with hand lines while ‘Dhoni’ moves and enjoying the beautiful sunrise. Trolling is a method of fishing that allows to use more than one line with a lure, often behind the boat, to attract your catch while the captain keeps the craft in a steady motion. Trolling is the most used method in which you have the best chance of catching a large fish.

Visit Meedhoo Island (Local Island Tour)
A local island tour in the Maldives offers a unique experience, providing insights into the daily life, culture, and traditions of the Maldivian people. Embark on our Visit Meedhoo Excursion, a short Dhoni boat ride lasting 10-15 minutes from the resort, bringing you to Raa Meedhoo. This inhabited island in Raa Atoll features the presence of the Raa Atoll Education Center, offering insights into the local community and culture. Raa Meedhoo, settled 300 years ago from Bodufushi, has a rich history. The Old Friday Mosque, standing for over 250 years, reflects the island’s heritage. Located 154.56 km northwest of Malé, Meedhoo indicates with its historical significance and cultural attraction.
