Valentine’s Day at Adaaran Resorts: Romance Redefined
30 January 2025
Escape to the Maldives this Valentine’s Day with Adaaran Resorts, where love is celebrated in extraordinary ways across stunning island sanctuaries. Whether it’s a private beachfront dinner, a moonlit dolphin safari, or an electrifying beach party under the stars, Adaaran Resorts offers the ultimate romantic getaway with its Premium All-Inclusive experiences. Across each island, couples are invited to indulge in intimate moments, savor world-class cuisine and create unforgettable memories in paradise. Within the serene Maldivian waters, every moment here is designed to bring couples closer, making this Valentine’s Day one to remember.
At Adaaran Prestige Vadoo, love stories unfold in overwater sanctuaries designed for timeless moments of togetherness. This Valentine’s Day, couples can celebrate in true Maldivian style with an exclusive Romantic Dinner at the Honeymoon Villa Jetty, offering a five-course meal, a bottle of champagne and breathtaking ocean views, all set to the soft melodies of a solo guitarist. As the night goes on, the Valentine’s Traffic Light Party at the Main Beach brings energy and excitement, with three DJs, unlimited food and drinks, and a vibrant atmosphere where romance blossoms. Here, love is not just celebrated—it’s an experience to be treasured.
At Adaaran Select Hudhuran Fushi, Valentine’s Day is an invitation to adventure and indulgence. Dance beneath the stars at the Valentine’s Extravaganza Party, where live music, premium cocktails, and a lively beachside setting set the tone for an unforgettable evening. For couples seeking seclusion, the Romantic Jetty Dinner offers an intimate dining experience over the shimmering waters, complete with a multi-course gourmet meal, a bottle of sparkling wine, and the ambiance of the endless horizon. Whether it’s thrilling ambiance, vibrant marine life or world-class dining, Adaaran Select Hudhuran Fushi ensures couples can live more this summer with adventure-filled romance.
At Adaaran Select Meedhupparu, romance takes center stage with an exclusive Valentine’s Dinner at North Beach, where a six-course gourmet menu awaits couples under the starlit sky. A romantic turndown service, a box of chocolates, and a red rose for the lady elevate the experience, ensuring every detail is carefully designed for special moments. With its endless stretches of pristine beaches, indulgent spa treatments, and Premium All-Inclusive dining, Meedhupparu is a haven where couples can celebrate their connection in a serene yet exclusive setting.
For couples at the All-Inclusive Resort, Adaaran Club Rannalhi, the day begins with a Romantic Champagne Breakfast on their private deck, featuring Moët & Chandon paired with a specially curated breakfast menu. Love takes to the seas with a Lover’s Dolphin Safari, offering the magic of Maldivian waters, selected mocktails, and gourmet canapés. As the sun sets, enjoy a Six-Course Beachfront Dinner creates a picture-perfect evening, complete with live music and a choice of sparkling, red, or white wine. From excitement to breathtaking island escapes, Rannalhi is where romance meets relaxation.
