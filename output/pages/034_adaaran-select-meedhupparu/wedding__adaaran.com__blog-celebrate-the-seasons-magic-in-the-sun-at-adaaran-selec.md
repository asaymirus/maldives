Celebrate the Season's Magic in the Sun at Adaaran Select Meedhupparu
22 October 2024
Forget the frosty air and snowflakes; this holiday season, Adaaran Select Meedhupparu, a premium all-inclusive resort in the Maldives invites families and couples to trade the winter chill for sun-kissed shores and gentle ocean breezes. The mature island is popular for its lush landscapes and pristine beaches, making it a perfect island destination to unwind and relax.
The resort’s house reef is among the most stunning in the Raa Atoll and provides a mesmerizing backdrop for adventure while on holiday to snorkel, swim, and discover the vibrant marine life.
Seasonal celebrations begin on December 21, kicking off with the magical Christmas Tree Lighting at the Pool Deck, where the enchanting glow of festive lights sets the perfect holiday mood. Guests can delight in a captivating magic show at the Halaboli Bar, where illusions come to life and wonder fills the air. For a unique twist on breakfast, the resort offers a ‘Float Your Breakfast & Cocktails’ experience, allowing guests to savor morning delights from a cocktail or breakfast tray while relaxing by the Main Pool and Water Villas.
On December 24, the festivities ramp up with a Christmas Eve Pre-Dinner Cocktail at the Beach, toasting to the season’s spirit with expertly crafted drinks. Santa will make a joyful appearance, bringing holiday cheer alongside Christmas carols, creating a heartwarming atmosphere for families. The evening continues with a memorable Christmas Eve Dinner at Jaafaiy and Sufura Restaurants, offering a feast of festive flavors.
The holiday magic continues with ‘Christmas in Wonderland’ at the Halaboli Bar, where festive delights and holiday wonder fill the air. On Christmas Day, indulge in the ‘Jingle & Mingle Christmas Brunch’ at the Jaafaiy Restaurant, featuring a delightful spread of holiday favorites. As the sun sets, the night comes alive with a joyful singalong at the Halaboli Bar, bringing families together in celebration.
Boxing Day will feature a delightful festive lunch at Jaafaiy Restaurant, alongside the Fanviun Art Workshop, where guests can craft tropical memories through coconut leaf artistry. The evening culminates with a mesmerizing fire-breathing dance performance that showcases the vibrant culture of Sri Lanka.
As the year draws to a close, guests can look forward to a thrilling New Year’s Eve at Adaaran Select Meedhupparu. The evening begins with a Pre-Dinner Cocktail at the Beach, setting the tone for a night of celebration. The New Year’s Eve Gala Dinner Buffet at the Halaboli Bar features an exquisite spread of international flavors, ensuring that every palate is satisfied.
As midnight approaches, guests will marvel at a breathtaking fireworks display lighting up the night sky, welcoming the arrival of 2025 with a sky full of sparks. On January 1, the festivities continue with a Special New Year’s Brunch at the Halaboli Bar, offering indulgent dishes to start the year off right
At Adaaran Select Meedhupparu, the tropical holiday spirit shines bright, making it the ultimate destination for families looking to create unforgettable memories in paradise.
