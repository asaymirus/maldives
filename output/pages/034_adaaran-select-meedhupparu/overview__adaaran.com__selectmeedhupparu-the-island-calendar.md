Raa Atoll · Best Time to Visit
The Island Calendar
Weather, diving conditions, dolphin sightings and resort events all shift gently through the seasons. Open any month to see what to expect.
Weather & Sea
Iruvai / NE monsoon (dry season). Warm, sunny and relatively dry; around 30°C daytime. Raa Atoll averages about 5 rain days. Generally calm seas and strong underwater visibility.
Diving & Marine Life
Excellent diving period. Clearer water and calmer seas; western Raa sites are favoured. Mantas are regularly encountered at western cleaning stations such as Sola Corner / Maamunagau area
Dolphin & Whale Watching
Very good for dolphin watching because of calmer seas. Spinner and bottlenose dolphins occur year-round; whale/cetacean sightings are possible but never guaranteed.
Weather & Sea
Dry NE monsoon continues; one of the sunniest and driest periods. Around 31°C daytime; about 5 rain days. Calm seas are common.
Diving & Marine Life
Prime diving month with excellent visibility. Western channels, thilas and manta cleaning stations remain productive; reef sharks, turtles, rays and schooling fish common.
Dolphin & Whale Watching
Excellent excursion conditions. Feb–early May is among the calmest periods for cetacean watching; spinner dolphins are the most commonly seen.
