Family Beach Villa

Set along the island’s prime shoreline, it offers direct beach access and uninterrupted ocean views, bringing together indoor comfort and outdoor living throughout the day. With its thoughtful layout and generous space, the villa creates a more convenient and connected stay, without compromising the openness Meedhupparu is known for.
