Shared Domestic Flight + Speedboat Transfers
Adaaran Select Meedhupparu can be reached with convenient domestic flights to the nearest local airport. From there, a seamless transition awaits as you embark on a pleasant speedboat journey to the island.
Upon arrival at the local airport, our attentive airport representative will guide you to the awaiting speedboat ready to whisk you away to paradise. Your luggage will be loaded onto the boat ensuring hassle-free transfer. Now relax and enjoy a delightful speedboat ride as you glide through the blue waters to Adaaran Select Meedhupparu.
