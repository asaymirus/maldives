Celebrate ‘100 Days To Christmas’ with Adaaran Resorts
17 September 2025
As the countdown to Christmas begins,
Adaaran Resorts
Maldives, part of Aitken Spence Hotels, invites UK travellers to mark the season with festive escapes across its collection of island sanctuaries. With exclusive
Family G
etaway
and
Romantic Getaway
packages, each property offers its own rhythm of celebration – whether for multi-generational gatherings or intimate moments by the sea.
At
Adaaran Select Meedhupparu
,
Adaaran Select Hudhuran Fushi
, and
Adaaran Club Rannalhi
, families can rejoice with the
Family Getaway Package
, where one child under 12 stays and dines for free. From Christmas Tree Lightings and festive gala dinners to Santa’s arrival, carol singing, and joyful brunches, the islands set the stage for a truly magical celebration. Couples too can embrace the
Romantic Getaway Package
, with an in-villa or floating breakfast for stays of three nights or more, and an enchanting candlelit dinner on the beach for those staying seven nights or longer. Together, these resorts create an atmosphere where generations gather, and romance flourishes amidst the festivities.
For those seeking pure intimacy,
Adaaran Prestige Vadoo
and
Adaaran Prestige Water Villas
embody the essence of romance during the festive season. Couples staying three nights or more are treated to an in-villa or floating breakfast, while stays of seven nights or more are crowned with a candlelit dinner on the beach. Adding to the allure, the island comes alive with lagoon-front Tree Lighting ceremonies with fire dancers and mulled wine, a lantern-lit Christmas Eve Gala with Tanoura dancers and a DJ party, beachfront carolling, and captivating Balinese dance performances. Here, love takes centre stage, framed by the rhythms of the ocean and the sparkle of Christmas under the stars.
Across every Adaaran property, the season unfolds as a warm Maldivian celebration – families discovering joy together, couples immersed in treasured moments, and each island weaving its own story of wonder, togetherness, and festive enchantment.
