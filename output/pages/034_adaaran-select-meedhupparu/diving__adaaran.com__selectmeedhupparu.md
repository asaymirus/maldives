Adaaran Select Meedhupparu
24 Hour Premium All-Inclusive 5 Star Resort
Live Easy, Laugh Often
Sprawling, green and quietly powerful, Adaaran Select Meedhupparu is the kind of island that doesn’t just impress, it grounds you. Beneath the shade of banyan trees time feels unhurried. Home to one of the best house reefs in the Maldives, the island invites you to drift through coral gardens alive with colour and life. Rooted in some of the world’s best global sustainability practices, this Maldives resort remains a natural sanctuary, cared for, preserved and connected to its surroundings. Whether you’re here to explore or simply exhale, it’s the feeling you’ll carry home.

Know Before You Go
Our
Premium All Inclusive
package is designed to make your stay as carefree as possible. It includes dine-around options at our restaurants, a selection of premium beverages, a fully stocked mini bar, and even spa credits to help you unwind. Guests also enjoy special excursions and access to exclusive lounges, everything you need for a truly indulgent island escape
We offer our guests two comfortable and scenic ways to reach Adaaran Select Meedhupparu from Velana International Airport. You may enjoy a picturesque 45-minute seaplane journey or choose a domestic flight of about 20 minutes followed by a 45-minute speedboat ride.
Both options
are designed to make the start of your holiday as seamless and memorable as possible.
Enjoy complimentary domestic flight + speedboat transfers for stays with 7 nights or more from April to October, and for stays with 14 nights or more from November to March.
Our cancellation policy varies depending on the season. You’ll find the general guidelines in our
Terms & Conditions.
If you’ve booked a non-refundable offer, please note that a 100% cancellation fee will apply to the total room charges.
