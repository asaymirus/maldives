Drink
Indulge in a selection of five unique bars as part of our Premium All-Inclusive (PAI) package, an exclusive offering that sets us apart from other resorts. These bars include the Halaboli Bar, Pool Bar, Sunset Bar, Café Mass Bar, and Albino Bar, along with the dedicated Water Villa Restaurant and Bar that is dedicated for Water Villa’s guests.
Halaboli Main Bar
9:00 hrs – Midnight
The entertainment at our Halaboli Bar is unparalleled, with a unique show every evening that includes fire flaring, karaoke nights, a fire-breather show, movie nights, a beach parties, and Balinese dance.
Pool Bar
9:00 hrs – 19:00 hrs
Nestled alongside the glistening turquoise waters of the infinity pool, our swim-up bar is set upon the endless horizon. Quench your thirst with a refreshing beverage as you spend a leisurely day by the pool.
Sunset Bar
9:00 hrs – Midnight
Sunsets are stunning at this venue as vibrant hues of orange, pink, and gold paint the sky. Dive into the evening’s liveliness with live entertainment, while making new friends.
Albino Bar with 101 Cocktails
9:00 hrs – Midnight
Albino Bar is a standout feature of our resort, celebrated for its impressive selection of 101 expertly crafted cocktails. This extensive menu showcases the creativity and skill of our mixologists, who combine classic techniques with innovative flavors to create visually stunning and delicious drinks. Available exclusively under our Indulgence package, this diverse offering sets our resort apart in the Maldives, providing guests with a unique beverage experience that caters to both traditional preferences and adventurous tastes.
Café Mass Bar
Bar 24 hours
Café Mass Bar provides the unique convenience of 24-hour service, setting us apart from many other Maldivian resorts. Whether guests want an early morning coffee or a late-night drink, Café Mass Bar is always available, enhancing the overall experience at Adaaran Select Meedhupparu.
