SWEAT IT OUT
Join W Maldives for a week-long fitness sojourn with SWEAT IT OUT. Take part in the daily exclusive fitness classes led by world-class trainer,
Bradley Simmods, and the innovative and energizing workout sessions by Jumping Fitness. Book your spot, as limited space is available!
ESCAPE VIBE @ WET
Chill out with captivating tunes at WET with live DJ and groove with the ESCAPE vibes. Dancing or chilling, everything or nothing, soak in the vibe.
KADA NIGHT MARKET
KADA means
café
in the local Maldivian language, a place to gather and connect with friends with food & drinks. An evening of sampling food from
live stations, small or large bites of Asian Night market style with live music.
OFF THE HOOK
Local fish oriented menu curated with ocean to table concept. Taste the imagination of Chef BAKA, an elevated Maldivian cuisine with a W twist.
GET WET
As the energy heats up, GET WET and cool down at the pool party with frozen cocktails at WET with latest vibe & tracks by live DJ. Live tacos made
to your unique tastes, fuel up with local ingredients.
WINE ME UP
Unleash your imagination, and step into an evening of culinary journey with an interactive blind wine pairing. Chef Baka’s elevated Maldivian cuisine
and your own interpretation on the wine tasting profile. Taste palate champion will receive a bottle of wine as the prize.
PARASAILING
Ride in Dream, W Maldives’ parasailing boat. Take off and land in the boat without getting wet!
WOW SNORKELING
