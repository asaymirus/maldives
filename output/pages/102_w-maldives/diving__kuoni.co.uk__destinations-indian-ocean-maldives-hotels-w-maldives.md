In 2025, W Maldives emerged from a glamorous top-to-toe renovation, re-establishing it as one of the most sought-after resorts in the Maldives.
Harnessing a sense of rebellion the ‘W’ brand has always been known for, there’s a contemporary swagger to W Maldives that’s a departure from many traditionally styled Maldivian resorts. That’s not to say it’s a party island, but it invites a little camaraderie if that’s what you’re looking for. The pursuit of injecting some zing in the styling doesn’t mean you miss out on tropical island touches – W Maldives still has all the hallmarks of a quintessential Maldivian retreat, just with a touch more verve than most.
Why we love W Maldives
There are 77 villas here, cleverly designed to feel completely private and ideal if you want an escape that feels utterly exclusive. Still, there are enough villas here to create a sociable atmosphere when you’re ready to mingle
The mature house reef is a snorkeller’s dream – go prepared to be mesmerised by the kaleidoscopic sea life that calls this lagoon home. There’s incredible night snorkelling available, too
All the villas have private swimming pools with comfy daybeds and sun loungers, so you might find you seldom emerge from your paradisiacal bubble. Overwater villas have direct access to the house reef and lagoon, too
There are five distinctive restaurants and bars, giving you plenty of dining options throughout your holiday, whether you want something tropically casual, or want to enlist the help of the concierge team to plan a dining experience to remember

Rooms
There’s a seamless indoor-outdoor design to the villas, so you can effortlessly float from a spacious bedroom to your deck over the water or your personal patch of beach, depending on which villa you decide on. The bio-boho design harmonises nature and style, drawing inspiration from the marine life and lush flora. Overwater villas echo the patterns of whale sharks, while beach villas embrace the textures of mangroves, creating serene spaces that invite relaxation and adventure.

There are five places to eat and drink across the island, and the restaurants feel special without being pretentious – you can glam up or dress down, anything goes. If you’re celebrating (or just fancy pushing the boat out) ask the ever-helpful concierge team to arrange dinner on W Maldives’ private castaway island, Gaathafushi.
Kitchen
Head here for all-day dining with live cooking stations whipping up treats in front of your very eyes.
Fish
A seaview signature seafood restaurant that makes the most of the local catch for its delicious dishes, with its ocean-to-table concept.
Fire
With different themed nights, order slow-cooked sharing platters to feast on with your toes in the sand at W Maldives’ beachside smokehouse and lively chef table with grill for a more interactive dining experience.
Sip
Opulent Peruvian accents and Nikkei cuisine combine at this beachside bar. Theis bar transforms as the evening goes – from sunset session to romantic casual dining, and ends as a late evening hang-out with cocktails.
Wet
The social hub of the resort with Noon to Moon concept – a pool with sunken seats and iconic stainless slide with own cooling system.

The tranquil overwater spa, Away, has four treatment rooms where skilled spa therapists work their magic to soothe and pamper. For a cardio burst, there’s an ocean view gym with a wide selection of equipment including bikes and treadmills, strength equipment, and free weights.
Just a quick five-minute zoom by speedboat from W Maldives lies Gaathafushi, an off‑the‑radar island that feels like your own secret slice of paradise. Its somewhere you can just vibe on the sand doing absolutely nothing (a highly underrated sport).
Claim the island for a half‑day escape, a full‑day desert‑island adventure, or go all‑in and stay overnight under the stars on a signature W bed with the ocean as your lullaby. Whether you’re dreaming of a barefoot beach picnic, a candlelit BBQ crafted by your private chef, or a sunrise champagne breakfast after a night under the sky, Gaathafushi turns every castaway moment into a luxury experience. Sunsets hit different here. Freedom feels bigger. And everything beyond the horizon? It suddenly seems beautifully distant.
Please note this private island experience is an extra charge and booking in advance is recommended.
Complimentary snorkelling gear is available from the Down Under Dive Centre if you want to self-guide around the lagoon. Day and night snorkelling excursions are available with the in-house marine biologist, either around the house reef or exploring nearby locations by speedboat or even a luxury private yacht. Scuba diving is available, too, and there’s even the chance to swim with whale sharks.
Other water sports include windsurfing, jet skiing, and parasailing. Big game fishing excursions are bookable, too.
