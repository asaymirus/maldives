# W Maldives named “Leading Hotel Suite” at the 20th World Travel Awards - My Maldives

Source: https://www.mymaldives.com/blog/w-maldives-named-leading-hotel-suite-at-the-20th-world-travel-awards/
Scraped: 2026-10-04
Resort: W Maldives
Page type: diving

---

W Retreat & Spa Maldives’ Ocean Haven suite has been named “Indian Ocean’s Leading Hotel Suite” at the 20th edition of the World Travel Awards 2013.
W Maldives – a private island and luxury playground in a wonderland of white sand beaches, turquoise lagoons and breathtaking reefs. W Retreat & Spa Maldives offers jetsetters an exclusive escape, and indulgence in a pristine seaside sanctuary.
This year, what is known to be as” The Oscars of the Travel Industry” celebrated its 20th Anniversary. World Travel Awards is acknowledged across the globe as the ultimate travel accolade, recognizing excellence in product and service.
Jennifer Buhr, General Manager at W Maldives said, “When our guests visit the retreat, they expect an experience that is a universe away from the norm. This Leading Hotel Suite award is a testimony of the ultimate Jetsetter experience that we provide to our guests daily. The Ocean Haven suite is a tropical dream with its oversized infinity edge plunge pool overlooking the Indian Ocean”.
Congratulating winners, President and founder of World Travel Award, Graham E Cooke said “The Indian Ocean continues to serve as an example of a responsible eco-tourism region whilst offering the most unique hospitality products and services. It strives to set the highest possible bar to raise the standards of excellence in travel and tourism and it has been an honor to hold the first ever Indian Ocean World Travel Awards here.”
