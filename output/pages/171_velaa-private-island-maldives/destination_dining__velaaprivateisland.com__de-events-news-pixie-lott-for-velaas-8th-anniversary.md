Pixie Lott For Velaa's 8th Anniversary
Live at Velaa this December, award-winning British singer and songwriter Pixie Lott put on a spectacular show for our anniversary.
This year, Velaa celebrated its 8th anniversary with the vocal talents of British singer and songwriter Pixie Lott. Guests joined us this December for an evening of culinary delights as we marked another marvellous year.
