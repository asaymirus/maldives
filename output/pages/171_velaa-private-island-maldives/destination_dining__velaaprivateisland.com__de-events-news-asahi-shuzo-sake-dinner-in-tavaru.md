Asahi Shuzo Sake Dinner in Tavaru
Bringing his expertise in sake pairing, Kazuhiro Sakurai led a sake dinner in Tavaru on 20 February.
For one night only, CEO of Asahi Shuzo (4th Generation) Kazuhiro Sakurai visited Velaa to introduce an extraordinary Sake tasting menu inclusive of 7 award-winning sakes from the legendary Asahi Shuzo Sake Brewery from Shuto-machi Iwakuni-city, Japan.
