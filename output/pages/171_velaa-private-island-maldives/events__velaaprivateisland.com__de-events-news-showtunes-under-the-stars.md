Showtunes Under the Stars
This March, our guests embraced an evening of magical musical theatre with a showcase of iconic compositions on Athiri Beach!
This March, we journeyed through the iconic musical theatre canon, tipping our hats to the great works of Lerner & Loewe, Andrew Lloyd Webber, Rodgers & Hammerstein, Leonard Bernstein, Cole Porter, and Stephen Sondheim. Soprano Kaileigh Riess and Tenor Nathan Munson brought their star vocalism to the stage alongside Pianist Andrew Sun tickling the ivories for this one-night-only musical event curated exclusively for Velaa Private Island.
