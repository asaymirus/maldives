Candlelight Concerts with Vanity Strings
Bringing their elegance and refined talents to our shores, Vanity Strings will joined us for two exclusive candlelight concerts on 21 & 23 November.
A spectacular series of performances took place in Aragu Signature Restaurant on 21 November followed by Athiri Beach on the 23 November. The talented quartet brought a refreshing twist to the genre with a setlist of modern pop and rock hits performed with precision and elegance.
