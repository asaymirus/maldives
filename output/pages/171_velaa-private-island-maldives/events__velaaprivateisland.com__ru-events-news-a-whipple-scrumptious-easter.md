A Whipple-Scrumptious Easter
We celebrated the season of chocolate and Easter bunnies at Velaa as the island transformed into a tropical Chocolate factory!
This Easter, guests indulged in cocktail pairings, special tasting menus in Aragu and a grand Easter Gala dinner on 9 April with entertainment from award-winning British singers J-Sol and Hari. Along with this, small VIPs enjoyed an exciting selection of activities with Lha Velaa kids club, all styled around the vibrant wonders of Charlie and the Chocolate factory.
