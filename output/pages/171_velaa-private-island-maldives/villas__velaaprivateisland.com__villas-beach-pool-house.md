This two-bedroom beach house is ideal for families. Two detached villas, each with their own bedroom, ensuite bathroom and garden atrium, are connected by an expansive living room. This communal retreat and social space is fitted with kitchenette and wine bar. Outside, the vast terrace features a standalone dining platform/lounge and a large private pool with a greater swimming depth for adults and a shallow wading area for children.
