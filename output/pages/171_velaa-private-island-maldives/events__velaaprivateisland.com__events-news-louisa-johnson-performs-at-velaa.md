Louisa Johnson performs at Velaa
Velaa recently hosted a spectacular evening at Athiri as the incredible Louisa Johnson took the stage for an unforgettable live performance.
Rising to fame as the youngest ever winner of The X Factor UK, Louisa Johnson has continued to make her mark worldwide with powerhouse vocals, chart-topping hits, and magnetic stage presence. At Velaa, she delivered an electrifying performance filled with energy, emotion, and signature flair, creating a night defined by music, rhythm, and atmosphere beneath the stars. Her appearance marked a standout moment in our entertainment calendar, blending style, artistry, and unforgettable sound on the shores of Athiri.
