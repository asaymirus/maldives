Dec 30, 2024
Performances
Jazz Night & Seafood Extravaganza
Under the golden hues of a Maldivian sunset, guests indulged in an extraordinary evening of fine dining and live jazz, featuring the mesmerising Italian Riviera Swing Band.
Gourmet pop-up dining stations offered an exquisite selection of flavours, each dish a culinary masterpiece. As the night unfolded, the Italian Riviera Swing Band filled the air with their signature blend of vintage glamour and contemporary jazz, weaving a tapestry of melodies that perfectly complemented the idyllic island setting. A night of timeless elegance and sensory delight.
