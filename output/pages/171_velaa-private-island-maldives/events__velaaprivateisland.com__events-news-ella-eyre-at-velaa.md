Ella Eyre at Velaa
What a night! Velaa Private Island was rocking this October as the incredible Ella Eyre took the stage!
Ella Eyre brought her signature powerhouse vocals and vibrant energy to Velaa Private Island, creating an unforgettable experience. Known for her chart-topping hits like "Waiting All Night" and "Gravity," Ella captivated the audience with a thrilling mix of her iconic tracks and fresh renditions, leaving everyone in awe. The night was pure musical magic, and we can't wait to bring you more extraordinary moments like this at Velaa!
