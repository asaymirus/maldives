New Hammam in Nirolhu Private Residence
Hot and cold meets foam in this new exciting addition to Nirolhu Private Residence.
For thousands of years Hammam's have been used for health benefits and even the ancient Greeks used steam baths for their daily health rituals, socialising and cleaning themselves.
The spa room of Nirolhu Private Residence has undergone a major redesign to now hold a full Hammam inclusive of steam room, cold pool and marble bed for treatments. Guests staying in the residence can utilise both the steam room and cold pool at their own leisure. Our spa team team are able to also offer a full Turkish Bath Ritual inclusive of pure cold pressed Black Soap from exclusive Dr Burgener and a foam body massage performed on the heated Hammam marble bed.
