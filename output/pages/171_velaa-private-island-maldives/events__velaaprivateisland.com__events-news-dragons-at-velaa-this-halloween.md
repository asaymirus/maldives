Dragons at Velaa this Halloween
This Halloween, a swarm of mythical fire-breathing Dragons made their nest on Velaa!
From faraway lands, dragons found their way to Velaa this Halloween. Making their nest on our shores and frightening all who dare venture onto the island! Guests joined us for a programme of fun-packed activities and events for both children and adults and helped save the island from their terrifying reign.
