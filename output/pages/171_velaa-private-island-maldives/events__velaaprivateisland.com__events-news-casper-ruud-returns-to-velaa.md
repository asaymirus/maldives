Casper Ruud Returns to Velaa
Making his triumphant return to our shores, professional Tennis Player Casper Ruud, now ranked 3rd best singles player in the world is back to offer private lessons with guests.
Becoming the first Norwegian tennis player to win an ATP title and to reach the semifinals of ATP Tour Masters 1000 events. Casper Ruud is the highest ranked Norwegian player in ATP history and will be bringing his world class tennis skills to our pristine shores this December. Available for guests who are looking to hone their tennis ability in private tennis lessons with a true master.
