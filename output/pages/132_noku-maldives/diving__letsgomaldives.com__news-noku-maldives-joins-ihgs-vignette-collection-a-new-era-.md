Noku Maldives has proudly become the first resort in the Maldives to operate under IHG Hotels & Resorts’ Vignette Collection, marking an exciting chapter in luxury hospitality. This addition highlights the brand’s focus on offering personalized experiences, thoughtful service, and unforgettable moments.
A Serene Escape in Noonu Atoll
Situated in the pristine waters of Noonu Atoll, Noku Maldives is a sanctuary for those seeking tranquility and luxury. The resort features 50 modern private villas, each equipped with a private pool, offering an intimate retreat. Whether strolling along the resort’s private beach or enjoying the turquoise waters of the Indian Ocean, guests are immersed in a setting of unparalleled natural beauty.
Culinary Delights and Wellness Experiences
Guests can indulge in a range of dining experiences at the resort’s three restaurants and bar. Thari Restaurant specializes in Asian cuisine, while The Palms Bar offers expertly crafted cocktails blending Eastern and Western influences. For all-day dining, Palms Restaurant serves a diverse menu to satisfy every palate.
The Noku Spa provides a haven for relaxation, focusing on wellness programs that use natural ingredients to promote overall wellbeing.
Unique Offerings of the Vignette Collection
The Vignette Collection is renowned for uniting unique properties with a shared vision of authentic travel experiences. At Noku Maldives, this vision is brought to life through its
Memorable Rituals
and
A Means For Good
initiatives.
Memorable Rituals
: Guests can experience the Celestial Turtle Ritual, a stargazing activity inspired by the ancient Maldivian art of astronavigation. This enchanting experience connects visitors to the local culture and natural surroundings.
A Means For Good
: As part of the resort’s commitment to responsibility and community engagement, initiatives focused on sustainability and cultural preservation will be introduced in 2025.
Vignette Collection’s Rapid Growth
Since its inception, the Vignette Collection has grown rapidly, with 19 hotels currently open and 34 more in the pipeline. This global expansion highlights the brand’s appeal to travelers seeking distinct and meaningful stays.
For travelers looking to immerse themselves in the serene beauty of the Maldives while enjoying tailored luxury, Noku Maldives offers an exceptional choice. Visit the official website for more details and reservations.
