DESCRIPTION
With 50 modern private villas on Kuda Funafaru in Noonu Atoll. Noku Maldives offers comfort, space and tranquility that is second to none. The crystal blue lagoon reveals diverse fish life where one might even spot our resident sea turtle. Set sail on our Dhoni to discover spinner dolphins, savour the world on a plate with our tasty international cuisine. Indulge the senses with our wellness treatments at Noku Spa.
Nestled amidst the soft powdery white shores and crystal blue waters, Noku Maldives offers comfort, space and tranquility. Beach Villas and Water Villas enjoy an intimate and luxurious comfort with lush tropical nature and glorious sunset. Set sail on our Dhoni into the crystal blue lagoon to discover the rich and diverse marine life while savoring the world on a plate with our international dining experience.
A 45-minute flight from Velana International Airport by seaplane takes you to the balmy shores of Noku Maldives. Located on Kuda-Funafaru in Noonu Atoll, its pristine location houses a thriving marine ecosystem that includes dolphins, rays and populous fish diversity.

Highlights From The Resort
Diving and Snorkeling
The underwater world is right at the doorstep. Encounter jubilant clownfish, sea anemones and triggerish while snorkeling within our private lagoon. Our professional dive instructors will show guests the rich diversity of marine life suitable for all levels of experience.
Water Activities
The sun kissed Noonu Atoll is a prime location for dolphin watching. Catch the light ocean breeze on a chartered Catamaran, or feel the adrenaline rush while riding the waves on Jet Ski or Windsurfing activities. Be captivated by beautiful shades of the horizon while waiting for a prize catch during sunset fishing.
Wellness
Invigorate the mind, body and spirit with our unique holistic treatments that pair traditional Asian therapies with strong elements of nature.
Noku Spa
Embracing nature is an integral aspect of our wellness program. Rejuvenation begins with the beautiful garden walkway leading up to 6 double treatment suites with a relaxing view of the ocean. Our wellness treatments use the natural ingredients to enhance total wellbeing. Each treatment room is thoughtfully designed as a sanctuary where stone and wooden accents achieve a deep sense of calm and relaxation.
