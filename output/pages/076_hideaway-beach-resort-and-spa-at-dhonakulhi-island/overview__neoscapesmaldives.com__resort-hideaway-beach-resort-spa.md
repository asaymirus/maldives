DESCRIPTION
‘The sumptuous Hideaway Beach Resort is a glamorous retreat, with dense tropical foliage giving each villa an unparalleled sense of privacy.’
Welcome to an exclusive world for those who seek the ultimate in luxury and privacy. The Hideaway Beach Resort & Spa is a tropical sanctuary of endless blue skies and fine white sand. It is one of the few resorts in Haa Alifu Atoll, and the Marina is the first of its kind in the Maldives. The island is large and crescent-shaped with a white, sandy beach which stretches more than 1km on each side of the island. The turquoise waters of the shallow lagoon are abundant in colourful sea life. The island is unique, and its reef structure is split into two distinct parts by a natural channel, forming a natural harbour for the Marina. Set amidst the coconut groves and lush vegetation and spanning out towards the beautiful lagoon, the villas offer world-class facilities and uncompromised privacy. Capitalizing on the dramatic location, the Hideaway Spa & Beauty Palace is an invitation to experience total wellbeing, while there is also an array of water and land-based activities and excursions, not to mention world-class restaurants and a nightclub.

Dining
Meeru Bar and Grill
With relaxed dining by the pool, Meeru Bar and Grill is the perfect place to unwind after a day in paradise. Authentic pizzas, hand crafted and cooked in a wood-fired oven, succulent cuts of meat cooked to perfection on the Lava Stone Grill and lobsters straight from the water to the grill are just a few of the delectable treats on the international menu. Enjoy dinner on the deck outside whilst spotting stingrays, baby sharks and fish swimming in the ocean below.
Matheefaru - Haute Cuisine
This sand-floored oceanfront restaurant offers a warm welcome to food lovers with its expansive and daily changing buffet. A dining highlight at Matheefaru – which means coral reef in the local language – is the team of talented chefs who offer live cooking for the perfectly seasoned dish made the way you like best.
Dinner is a magical affair where diners sit on the open air deck under a sea of stars. For an utterly romantic experience we recommend booking one of the two over-water private dining pavilions.
Samsara Asian Fusion
Samsara Asian Fusion offers serene surroundings coupled with an exquisite fusion of Thai, Indian and Japanese influences. It boasts a romantic location of an open-air terrace perched on stilts above the impossibly blue lagoon; outstanding fine dining and a teppanyaki table that will excite the taste buds. Samsara will be a pleasure to be experienced more than once.
Sunset Pool Cafe
Take in the sublime panoramas from the Sunset Pool Café whilst sampling flavor infused dishes with Asian and European influences. Open for breakfast lunch and dinner, poolside dining can be enjoyed at anytime of day, and always with stunning views of the island.
Private Dining
A romantic candlelit dinner under a blanket of stars, a charming picnic on a deserted Maldivian island, a private barbecue on a secluded beach or an intimate seafood feast in a dining pavilion over the water – the only limit is the imagination, our chefs are waiting to make gastronomic dreams a reality.
In-villa Dining
The villas at Hideaway Beach Resort and Spa are spacious, secluded, and enchanting so much so you may find you don’t want to leave. And you don’t have to. The In-Villa Dining service enables complete enjoyment of the unprecedented privacy and utterly decadent location whilst sampling a world of exotic flavors from the 24-hour room service menu. Depending on whether your villa is located on the beach or over the ocean, choose whether to dine inside your villa, on your over-water terrace, or in your private beach-dining pavilion. Champagne breakfast by the pool, lunch on the terrace and dinner under the mesmerizing stars on the beach – the choice is all yours.

Snorkelling –
PREPARE TO BE AMAZED
The Maldives is world renowned for its stunning and abundant marine life and with a spectacular coral reef on your doorstep there is no better place to give snorkelling a try than at Hideaway Beach Resort and Spa. The residents of our House Reef include countless tropical fish of every imaginable color, moray eels, corals and turtles.
Scuba Diving –
A MAGICAL UNDERWATER DISCOVERY
With the picturesque setting above surface so perfect you will think you are dreaming, it’s hard to believe it can get any better. Well, it can – the aquatic realms of the Maldives will amaze you, there are colors beyond belief, prolific fish-life and a coral garden, which is simply stunning.
Water Sports –
THERE’S A LOT OF WATER TO ENJOY
Whether it’s sailing adventures to nearby uninhabited islands or feeling the rush of adrenalin as you Jet Ski around the island, Deep Blue Watersports will accommodate your needs. Just want to play? Visit the Maldives’ first floating Aqua Park, tucked away in the shallow waters of the lagoon.
Fishing –
CATCH OF THE DAY

Yoga –
FOR THE MIND & BODY
Yoga is a process of self-awareness using techniques to improve physical and emotional health and manage the mind. It is an ancient philosophy originating in India over 5,000 years ago. At Hideaway Beach Resort and Spa our yoga instructor, Ashok, offers private and group classes in a variety of yoga styles, including Hatha Yoga.
Sports Complex –
KEEPING FIT IN THE TROPICS
Fitness enthusiasts are truly spoiled here at Hideaway Beach Resort and Spa. Enjoy a game of tennis on one of our two outdoor, hard surface courts, shoot a few hoops on the half- basketball court or join the team members for a kick about on the astro-turf soccer pitch and the traditional beach holiday game of volleyball.
Tennis Lessons –
GAME, SET & MATCH
Tennis fans can indulge in their favourite game here at Hideaway Beach Resort and Spa. Two outdoor, hard surface courts await your match. If playing in the heat of the day is too much to bear, book the court for an evening game – floodlighting will ensure you don’t miss a point.
