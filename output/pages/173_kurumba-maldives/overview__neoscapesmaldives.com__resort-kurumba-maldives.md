DESCRIPTION
‘Kurumba draws on its proud history, charismatic character and heartfelt service to offer diverse experiences amidst a stunning jewel of an island’
Rich experiences. Warmth that enchants. Kurumba was the very first Maldives resort and is continually innovating and evolving in order to bring guests new experiences and facilities. From the grand Royal Kurumba Residence to the charming beach villas nestled on the powdery white sand, there is a villa to suit every desire and budget at this five-star resort. The many dining venues and entertainment areas are linked by meandering sandy pathways adorned with well-kept tropical gardens. Relax on the white sandy beach.  Swim in the sheltered lagoon. Treat yourself to an exquisite Maldivian spa treatment. Explore the underwater world on the vibrant house reef. Be energized with the daily scheduled activities, water-sports or scuba diving.  The resort also boasts the Maldives’ most vibrant nightly entertainment, including live bands and DJs. With so much choice, Kurumba is the favourite Maldives resort of many repeat visitors.

Highlights From The Resort
Majaa Recreation
Majaa (in the Local Language Means Fun) Recreation Team is Located in the middle of the Island. The Majaa Team is on Hand to Help Guests Choose Activities in Few Different Categories Such as Wellness, Fitness, Sports & Various Levels of Snorkeling Tours. in Addition to Individual Activities, a Seven-day Activity Timetable is Also Available to Ensure Guests Are Always Entertained.
Snorkelling in Maldives
The Kurumba Maldives Lagoon is Surrounded by Wave Breakers, Which Reduce Waves and Currents for Ideal Snorkelling Conditions. An Average Depth of 1 to 1.5 Metres. Small Coral Outcrops Abound, Filled With Vibrant Marine Life. Just Beyond the Breakers, a Dramatic Drop, Even Greater Variety of Coral, Home to Colourful Fish, Stingrays, and Turtles.
Majaa Kids’ Club
Leave the Kids in Good Hands, Enjoy Your Stay Stress-free, Thanks to the Majaa Kids Club Facilities. A Spacious Recreation Centre, Built for Fun. The Team’s Winning Smiles, Naturally Gifted With the Little Ones. Pick Them Up in the Evening, Bursting With Stories of Today’s Lessons. Traditional Craft-making, Beachfront Games, New Discoveries. They Share Their Treasures, Crafted With Local Materials. Beg You to Take Them Again Tomorrow. At Majaa Kids Club, We Offer a Fun-filled Schedule, Seven Days a Week for Children Aged 04-13 Years on Complimentary Basis.
Majaa Teens
Fill Your Teen’s Holidays With Fun Activities That Entertain. In Kurumba Maldives They Can Enjoy a Resort Vacation of Their Own Year Round. With Something New and Different on the Schedule Every Day, Teens Are Constantly Engaged to Have Fun Within the Resort.
Scuba Diving in Maldives
One of the Premier Snorkelling and Diving Resorts in the Maldives. PADI Certified Instructors Initiate You Into This Enchanting World. Abundant Marine Life, Millions of Years of Coral Growth. Schools of Playful Fish Darting as You Descend. Shafts of Sunlight From Above. Then Something Larger. Goosebumps on Your Arms, a Reef Shark Glides by. An Island That Teems With Life Above and Below the Water.
Water Sports
The Speedboat Driver Glances Back With a Broad Grin. Then the Roar of Engines. Hang on for Dear Life. Laughter and Shrieks of Excitement Fill the Air. A Rainbow Spray of Water. Cutting Through the Waves, Free as a Bird. Paddle Across the Atoll in a Kayak. Ride a Strong Gust on a Clear Morning. Balance on a Catamaran. Parasailing, Wakeboarding, Jet Skiing. An Array of Watersports Activities and Facilities to Indulge in. So Many Ways to Soar.
Nightly Entertainment
From a Fun Dj Night, Live Bands, Jazz, Social Guest Gatherings to the Local Maldivian Dance Night of the Bodu Beru. Lose Yourself in the Celebration. Dancing Under the Stars. Meeting New Friends. Daily Excitement at Maldives Most Vibrant Night Scene.
VELI SPA
The soul of Veli Spa is inspired by the solace of the islands, the balance of the oceans, the energy of the indigenous people of the Maldives and the healing effects of human touch while incorporating traditional Maldivian therapies. Let the healing powers of the Maldives embrace you.
