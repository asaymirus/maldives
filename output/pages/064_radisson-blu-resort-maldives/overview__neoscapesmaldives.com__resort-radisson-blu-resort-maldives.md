DESCRIPTION
Where turquoise waters meet timeless luxury.
Radisson Blu Resort Maldives offers 128 spacious beachfront and overwater villas, each with a private pool and sweeping ocean views. Discover vibrant reefs while snorkelling, dive with whale sharks, or enjoy thrilling water sports. Slow down with yoga at the overwater pavilion, a treatment at Blu Spa, or champagne by your private pool. Savor global flavours at fine-dining restaurants, sip cocktails by the pool or beach bar, and let little ones explore at the Kids Club while you embrace the island’s serene rhythm.
In Alifu Dhaalu Atoll:
30-minute seaplane journey or by a 20-minute domestic flight to the Maamigili Airport, followed by a 15-minute speedboat ride to the resort.

Dining
Raha
Raha, the resort’s casual all-day dining restaurant, serves traditional favourites alongside contemporary fusion cuisine. Start your morning with a hearty breakfast before heading out to the waves, or return for a light, flavour-packed lunch and a relaxed dinner. Inspired by Asian spices and global influences, each dish is a feast for the senses. Do not miss the homemade ice cream, crafted in delightful flavours, while you enjoy sweeping views in a laid-back, welcoming atmosphere.
Kabuki
Discover a fusion of Peruvian flavours and Japanese culinary traditions at Kabuki, the resort’s elegant overwater fine-dining restaurant. Enjoy exquisitely crafted omakase or à la carte dishes paired with creative cocktails. Choose a table indoors or on the open-air deck and savour vibrant flavours, artful presentations, and breathtaking ocean views in a truly theatrical dining experience.
Eats & Beats
Eats & Beats, the resort’s beachfront pool bar, is the perfect spot to unwind. Lounge in a cabana, sip refreshing cocktails, or cool off in the infinity pool with ocean views. Enjoy wood-fired pizzas, light snacks, or casual meals by day, then soak in Maldivian sunsets and lively beach club vibes by night.
Crusoe’s
Inspired by Robinson Crusoe, this adults-only beach bar offers a castaway-chic escape on its own little island. Spend sun-filled days lounging by the pool or dipping into the lagoon, with tapas-style bites, tropical cocktails, and fine wines. Do not miss the weekly seafood extravaganza, where freshly grilled lobster is served under the stars by the beach.
Mahurab Bar
Mahurab Bar invites you to unwind in a vibrant overwater setting with hand-crafted cocktails, fine wines, and premium cigars. As live entertainment fills the evening air, enjoy comfort bites or explore the extensive shisha menu. Perfect for romantic dates or lively late-night gatherings, this stylish lounge captures the island’s spirit of celebration.
Alifaan
Alifaan celebrates the art of Mediterranean dining with premium cuts and the freshest locally caught seafood, grilled over open flames. Watch chefs showcase their craft at the central show kitchen while you savour bold flavours with subtle international influences. The open-air setting, natural tones, and relaxed ambiance make every meal a truly memorable occasion.
The Lab
Discover an extraordinary wine and cocktail cellar where our sommelier curates over 3,500 bottles from world-renowned vineyards and hidden gems alike. This intimate venue invites you to indulge in exclusive pairing dinners, private tastings, or curated events, blending fine wines, premium champagnes, and crafted cocktails for an immersive, sensory dining experience unlike any other.
