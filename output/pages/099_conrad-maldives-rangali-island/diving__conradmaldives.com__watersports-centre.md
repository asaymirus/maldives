THRILLS ON THE OPEN OCEAN
The Indian Ocean is your playground where the thrill of speed meets the serenity of the open water. From high octane motorized sports to the quiet rhythm of paddling we offer endless ways for you to connect with the azure surroundings and craft your own island adventure.
Send an Enquiry
Catamaran sailing and glass-floored boat trips
Harness the tropical breeze on a catamaran sailing adventure where the only sound is the hull cutting through the water. For a glimpse of the reef without getting wet our luxury glass bottom boat offers a window into the vibrant marine world beneath you through the largest glass panel of its kind.
Private and guided catamaran excursions
Luxury boat with 2×3 meter viewing window
Perfect for families and non swimmers
Inflatable, kayak and sea-bob rides
Glide effortlessly through the water like a dolphin on a high performance Seabob or gather the family for a fun filled ride on our towable inflatables. For a slower pace explore the calm lagoon waters by kayak at your own rhythm allowing for a peaceful connection with the ocean.
Electric Seabob for underwater agility
Fun towable rides for groups and families
Glass bottom kayaks available
Jet skiing and water skiing
Feel the exhilaration of speed as you skip across the sapphire lagoon on a jet ski looking out for dolphins in the distance. Alternatively test your balance and skill with a water skiing session behind our speedboat cutting through the wake for a heart pounding experience.
Guided jet ski tours to spot marine life
Water skiing for beginners and experts
Mono ski and wakeboard options available
Paddle boarding and wakeboarding
Find your balance atop a paddle board enjoying a peaceful workout with uninterrupted views of the endless horizon. If you seek an adrenaline rush strap into a wakeboard and launch across the boat wake for an energetic challenge on the water.
Stand up paddle boarding for core strength
Wakeboarding sessions for all levels
Expert instruction available
Wind surfing and scooter-snorkeling
Master the elements as you learn to maneuver a windsurf board catching the gentle Maldivian breeze. To explore the reef with ease our underwater scooters propel you through the coral gardens allowing you to cover more distance with less effort and see more marine life.
Windsurfing lessons and rentals
Seadoo underwater scooter excursions
X-Jetblade hydro flights available
