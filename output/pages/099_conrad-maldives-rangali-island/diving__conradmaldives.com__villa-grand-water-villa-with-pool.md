Overview
Claim your own sunset stage with a private infinity pool and a wide sundeck for lounging, swimming, and soaking up Maldivian light. The villa features a separate living room and a refined bathroom with an ocean-view tub, rain shower, and double vanity, bringing modern comfort to a dramatic overwater setting.
Parents with children under 12 will be asked to sign a disclaimer upon arrival.
