DESCRIPTION
‘Barefoot luxury on two iconic islands, where extraordinary experiences unfold above and beneath the ocean.’
Conrad Maldives Rangali Island is a rare twin-island sanctuary in the South Ari Atoll, offering barefoot luxury across two distinct private islands connected by a footbridge above a turquoise lagoon. Renowned for year-round whale shark sightings, one of the Maldives’ finest house reefs, and world-first experiences such as Ithaa, the undersea restaurant, and The Muraka, the world’s first underwater residence, the resort blends iconic innovation with natural beauty. With 12 exceptional dining venues, two award-winning spas, and intuitive Conrad service, this is luxury shaped by imagination, nature, and effortless island living.

The Muraka
The Muraka is the world’s first undersea residence, offering an extraordinary two-level living experience above and beneath the ocean. Featuring an underwater master bedroom with a 180-degree acrylic dome, overwater living and dining spaces, a private infinity pool, and dedicated butler service, it delivers unparalleled privacy and prestige. Dining
Ithaa Undersea Restaurant
Five meters beneath the ocean, Ithaa offers an unforgettable dining experience surrounded by coral gardens and marine life. As the world’s first undersea restaurant, it presents set lunch and dinner menus blending local and Western flavors, paired with fine wines. Available for private dining and special occasions, Ithaa transforms a meal into a once-in-a-lifetime sensory journey.
Ufaa by Jereme Leung
Ufaa, meaning “happiness” in Dhivehi, presents innovative Chinese cuisine inspired by regional traditions. Featuring handmade noodles, dim sum, signature hot pots, and a relaxed teahouse atmosphere, the restaurant blends bold flavors with island ease. Communal spaces and private dining rooms make it ideal for both social gatherings and intimate meals.
Sunset Grill
Perched on stilts over the Indian Ocean, Sunset Grill offers premium meats and fresh seafood grilled to perfection. As the sun sets, diners enjoy sweeping lagoon views while spotting reef life below. Paired with an extensive wine list and signature mustards, the experience combines refined dining with one of the Maldives’ most beautiful settings.
Mandhoo Restaurant
Mandhoo is a wellness-focused dining destination celebrating plant-forward cuisine inspired by the five elements. Each dish is thoughtfully designed to nourish the body and mind, using organic ingredients and balanced flavors. Suspended over the lagoon, Mandhoo offers a calming atmosphere where healthy dining meets tranquil ocean views.
Koko Grill
Dine on Japanese cuisine with a side of stars. Located on the beach, with pristine sunset views, the award-winning Koko Grill is one of the most romantic dining experiences in the Maldives.
Have a Japanese menu prepared at your table, with a lively culinary performance by our expert chef. Sample the best of Japenese cuisine from Ebi Ten, Teppanyaki and other delicacies as you dine under the Maldivian sky.
Vilu Restaurant & Bar
Vilu delivers Italian-inspired cuisine in a relaxed beachfront setting overlooking the lagoon. From leisurely breakfasts to starlit dinners, the menu focuses on classic Italian flavors paired with an extensive wine selection. With both indoor and outdoor seating, Vilu is ideal for long, unhurried meals shaped by ocean views and island breezes.
Quiet Zone
An adults-only beach bar designed for stillness and simplicity, the Quiet Zone offers daybeds, chilled music, and a serene ocean backdrop. Guests can sip cocktails, dine casually, or simply relax in peace. It is a sanctuary for those who appreciate the luxury of doing nothing, beautifully.
Rangali Bar
Set beside the main pool overlooking the lagoon, Rangali Bar is the resort’s social heart. Guests gather here for casual lunches, sunset cocktails, and relaxed conversation in a beach-inspired setting. With sand-textured floors and creative mixology, it is the perfect place to unwind as day transitions into night.
Cheese & Wine Bar
The Maldives’ only purpose-designed cheese and wine bar offers a curated selection of artisanal cheeses paired with fine wines. With black volcanic sand beneath your feet and a relaxed, intimate atmosphere, it is a haven for true connoisseurs seeking indulgent, unhurried moments centered on classic pairings.
Wine Cellar
The Maldives’ first underground wine cellar invites guests to descend two meters below ground for an intimate degustation experience. Home to thousands of bottles and rare labels, the Wine Cellar offers a six-course menu expertly paired by the Chef de Cuisine and Head Sommelier, creating a refined and immersive culinary journey.
Atoll Market
Atoll Market is the resort’s lively beachfront dining hub, featuring live cooking stations and themed culinary nights. Offering a global selection of cuisines, it brings energy and variety to every meal. With soft sand underfoot and open-air seating, it delivers a relaxed, social dining experience from morning to evening.
Destination Dining
Destination Dining elevates private dining with bespoke beach setups and curated culinary experiences. Whether an intimate candlelit dinner or a celebratory gathering under the stars, each experience is crafted to highlight the island’s natural beauty, exceptional cuisine, and personalized service.
In-Villa Dining
In-Villa Dining offers the freedom to enjoy Conrad’s exceptional cuisine in complete privacy. From leisurely breakfasts on your sundeck to romantic dinners by your private pool, dishes are delivered directly to your villa, allowing guests to dine on their own schedule, in their own space, without compromise.

Highlights From The Resort
MAJAA KIDS’ CLUB
A vibrant and educational space for children aged 3–12, Majaa Kids’ Club blends fun with cultural discovery. Through themed games, island adventures, and creative play, young guests experience the Maldives in an engaging and meaningful way. Located on Rangali-Finolhu Island, the club offers complimentary access, with select activities available at an additional cost.
FOR THE BODY & SOUL
Wellness at Conrad Maldives Rangali Island goes beyond the ordinary. One of the few resorts in the Maldives with a dedicated naturopath, guests receive personalized guidance to complement fitness, nutrition, and lifestyle goals. Daily yoga sessions are available, including Hatha Yoga, Yoga for Body & Mind, and private one-to-one or couples’ instruction, all set within the calming rhythm of island life.
OVERWATER SPA EXPERIENCE
Suspended above the Indian Ocean, the award-winning Overwater Spa offers a deeply immersive wellness journey inspired by the body’s chakra systems. Recognized by the Haute Grandeur Awards, treatments focus on restoring balance, energy, and inner calm, enhanced by panoramic ocean views and the gentle movement of water beneath your treatment room.
INSPIRED ITINERARIES
Designed to connect guests with the Maldives on a deeper level, Conrad’s curated itineraries combine adventure, culture, and nature. From snorkeling vibrant coral reefs and swimming alongside whale sharks to sunset fishing aboard a traditional dhoni, each experience is led by expert guides who reveal both iconic sights and hidden island gems.
MARINE & OCEAN ADVENTURES
Located in the South Ari Atoll, one of the world’s only regions with year-round whale shark sightings, the resort offers exceptional marine encounters. Guests can explore the house reef just meters from shore, join snorkeling safaris, turtle expeditions, and manta ray adventures, or embark on scuba diving experiences suitable for both beginners and certified divers.
SIGNATURE CRUISES & EXCURSIONS
From dolphin-spotting sunset cruises to private luxury yacht journeys aboard Rangali Raani, guests can experience the ocean from every perspective. Exclusive sunrise breakfasts, champagne sunsets, and full-day island-hopping adventures combine refined service with breathtaking seascapes for unforgettable moments on the water.
OCEAN WATERSPORTS
Thrill-seekers and leisure lovers alike can enjoy a wide range of motorized and non-motorized watersports. Paddleboarding, kayaking, jet-blading, X-Jetpacks, and sailing experiences are all guided by professional instructors, allowing guests to explore the lagoon at their own pace or chase adrenaline across open water.
OCEAN DIVE CENTRE
The Ocean Dive Centre offers PADI-certified courses and guided dives for all experience levels. With access to 42 nearby dive sites and vibrant marine ecosystems, divers can encounter coral gardens, reef sharks, turtles, rays, and schools of tropical fish, making Conrad Maldives a true underwater playground.
LOCAL CULTURE & ISLAND LIFE
Guests are invited to explore beyond the resort with local island tours and cultural excursions that reveal the Maldivian way of life. From village visits and island picnics to culinary workshops and traditional fishing, these experiences offer authentic insight into the culture, traditions, and rhythms of the islands.
RETAIL & LIFESTYLE
Located on the main island, the luxury boutique offers curated international designer collections for women, men, children, and home. Featuring resort wear and lifestyle brands, it allows guests to shop effortlessly without leaving the island.
