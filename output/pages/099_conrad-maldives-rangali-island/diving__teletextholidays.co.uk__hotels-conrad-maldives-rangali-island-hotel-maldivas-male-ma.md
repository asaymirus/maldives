About this hotel
Indulge in the ultimate Maldives experience at one of the country’s most iconic luxury resorts.
Designed to please every kind of holidaymaker, this comprehensive resort caters to everyone across its two connected islands. Expect an expansive area, with the most stunning scenery that combines verdant tropics with crystal waters and white sand.
Each island has its own personality. Rengali Island exudes serenity, with adults-only areas, a luxurious spa, heavenly beaches and peaceful overwater villas. Hop across to Rangali Finolhu for a livelier vibe. This island sports a range of restaurants, larger pool areas and bespoke family-style villas.
With around a dozen restaurants, the dining options are unparalleled. There’s underwater dining, a wine cellar, sunset grill and even a food market.
The islands are surrounded by a turquoise house reef, accessible directly from the resort. Wake up to fresh coffee and a breathtaking sunrise, before spending your days snorkelling this underwater world of wonder or cycling around the island and wandering jungle pathways.

Room Types
Beach Villa-
Sleeps 4 people with a separate bedroom and seating area. Decorated in earthy and turquoise tones that bring the outdoors indoors, this stylish villa has its own intimate terrace with ocean view. There is also a rainshower, Nespresso machine, sofa and soft lighting.
Deluxe Beach Villa with Pool-
Upgrade for your own private pool.
Grand Water Villa-
Wake up to endless ocean views, the kind that epitomise The Maldives experience. Decorated in the same style as a beach villa, the bed also faces the ocean and the room features a rainshower, Nespresso machine, sofa and soft lighting. This villa offers direct access to the lagoon and infinity pool.
The Muraka-
A luxury residence spanning two floors, with the main bedroom lying 16 feet below sea level.
How far is the nearest beach?
Choose a villa that sits right on the shore for direct access to the beach. Otherwise, reside in an overwater villa, just steps from the nearest stretch of sand
