Where Wonder Begins
Barefoot Luxury on the Maldives’ Most Iconic Natural Islands
Set across two naturally formed islands in the South Ari Atoll, Conrad Maldives Rangali Island offers a secluded sanctuary suspended between sky and sea. Framed by endless ocean views, the horizon unfolds in layered shades known as Conrad Blue, visible from villas set above the water and along pristine white-sand beaches. Barefoot luxury is paired with warm, intuitive hospitality and a collection of exceptional dining experiences, including Ithaa, the world’s first undersea restaurant. Beneath the surface, The Muraka, the world’s first underwater luxury residence, redefines private luxury, while whale sharks and manta rays can be encountered year-round in the surrounding waters.
