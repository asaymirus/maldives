Uniting the Ocean and the Sky
Discover The Muraka, a first of its kind luxury two level residence where your master bedroom rests submerged over 16 feet below sea level in the Indian Ocean, cocooned by living coral and the mesmerizing rhythm of marine life drifting past your windows into sleep.
Overview
The world’s first undersea residence, The Muraka is an extraordinary two-level sanctum where an underwater suite with a 180-degree acrylic dome immerses you in life beneath the ocean’s surface. With an underwater master bedroom, an infinity pool, ocean-facing bathrooms, spacious living areas, a private arrival jetty, and full butler service, The Muraka is designed for guests who want the absolute pinnacle of privacy and status. Above and below sea level, every feature is bold, dramatic, and unforgettable, created for the kind of travel story most people only dream about.

OUR INSPIRATION
The Muraka, meaning “coral” in the local Dhivehi language, emerged from a singular vision: a residence truly belonging to the ocean while honoring every expectation of discerning travelers. Four years of engineering innovation and environmental commitment brought this underwater paradise to life.
The name honors the ancient coral formations building these islands grain by grain across millennia. Those same living structures now surround your bedroom, sheltering the parrotfish, rays, and blacktip reef sharks gliding past your windows. This connection to the authentic Maldives, the thriving wilderness below the surface, informs every detail.
We built The Muraka not simply to pioneer, but to prove that wonder and responsibility coexist beautifully, that luxury can protect the ecosystems it celebrates, that sleeping beneath the sea deepens reverence for fragile beauty.
The Muraka Experience
Your time at The Muraka extends beyond the residence itself. Marine biologists offer private consultations, identifying species drifting past your dome. Snorkeling and diving launch from your deck, guided by experts knowing every coral formation. Bespoke dining unfolds on your upper terrace as stars emerge, or within the viewing tunnel surrounded by water and wonder. Every detail adapts to your rhythm, orchestrated seamlessly.
