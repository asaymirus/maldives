PROTECTING OUR OCEANS
We act as guardians of the South Ari Atoll, a thriving marine ecosystem home to whale sharks, manta rays, and sea turtles. Our partnership with Parley for the Oceans drives our commitment to eliminate single-use plastics, ensuring our turquoise waters remain pristine for the creatures that call them home.
By replacing plastic with reusable alternatives and championing eco-friendly practices, we invite you to join a movement that safeguards the fragile beauty of the Maldives for future generations.
CORAL REGENERATION
Immerse yourself in the restoration of our reefs through our dedicated coral conservation program. Guided by our resident marine biologists, you can play an active role in rehabilitating the reef by planting your own coral frame in our nursery.
This hands-on experience allows you to leave a living legacy beneath the waves. You will receive updates on your coral’s growth, forging a lasting connection to the underwater world you helped to protect and restore.
CERTIFIED PADI ECO CENTER
Discover the underwater wonders of the Maldives with a conscience. Our dive center is more than a gateway to adventure; it is a hub of marine education and conservation. From guided snorkeling excursions to educational presentations on marine biodiversity, every interaction is designed to deepen your understanding of the ocean.
Whether you are swimming with whale sharks or exploring the house reef, our team ensures that every encounter is respectful of the marine environment, promoting a sustainable coexistence with the ocean’s majestic inhabitants.
The Hydroponic Garden Project
Growing A Greener Future, One Island At A Time
What began as a wish to grow fresh, local produce has become a wider movement. Since 2022, Conrad Maldives has installed hydroponic gardens on nearby islands, boosting food security, reducing imports, and inspiring young islanders to adopt sustainable farming. Each garden features 1 – 2 systems, producing up to 180 leafy greens, along with fertigation units and greenhouse structures. Supported by Hilton Action Grants, resort funding, and guests’ contributions, the initiative includes hands-on training for long-term, local management. Four islands now harvest their own produce, with three more installations on the way – in our effort to cultivate a growing sustainability circle across the atoll.
Our own resort garden has supplied kitchens, bars, and the spa since 2014, yielding over 1,260 kg of lettuce and 540 kg of mint annually, alongside basil, banana and aloe vera, lowering our carbon footprint and ensuring exceptional freshness.
