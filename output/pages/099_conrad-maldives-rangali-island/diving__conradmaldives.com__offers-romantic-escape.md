Overview
Set within the South Ari Atoll, where the Maldives reveals its most captivating expressions of beauty, experience an escape designed just for two. Shaped by stillness, connection and shared discovery, moments on these two islands unfold between sea and sky. Luxuriate in mornings overlooking the Indian Ocean, and days that drift effortlessly between reef explorations, quiet stretches of beach, and time suspended in the privacy of your villa.
As light softens into evening, fall deeper into romance with a three-course dinner served in a setting defined by the rhythm of the ocean, followed by champagne and chocolates awaiting your return. Each detail is considered, allowing space for moments that feel both effortless and deeply personal.
At Conrad, the islands offer a rare sense of seclusion, where nature and design exist in quiet harmony. Here, encounters with whale sharks and manta rays unfold year-round, and signature experiences such as Ithaa, the world’s first undersea restaurant, bring a sense of wonder to every day.
This tropical romantic getaway is an invitation for two souls to celebrate their connection.
