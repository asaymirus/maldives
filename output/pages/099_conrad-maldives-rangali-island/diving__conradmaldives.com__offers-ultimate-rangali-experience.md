Overview
Discover the pinnacle of luxury with the Ultimate Rangali Experience at Conrad Maldives. Arrive in style with 25% savings on seaplane transfers all-year-round, and settle into your private villa for at least three unforgettable nights. Relish full-board dining paired with premium beverage packages, and feel tension melt away with 25% savings on à la carte treatments at the award-winning Spa Retreat.
Spend your days exploring the Maldives’ vibrant marine life with complimentary snorkeling on the house reef, and glide across crystal-clear waters with unlimited non-motorized watersports like paddle-boarding and kayaking. From sunrise to sunset, every moment is crafted to inspire, rejuvenate and delight in this iconic paradise where barefoot elegance meets boundless natural beauty.

A selection of alcoholic and non-alcoholic beverages will be available in:

Beverages must be consumed exclusively at the designated outlets. They may not be ordered and transferred to any other place in the resort (including villas or other dining venues).
