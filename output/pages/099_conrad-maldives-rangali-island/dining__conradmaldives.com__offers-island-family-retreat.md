Overview
Reconnect and build treasured memories with a retreat designed for the whole family. Enjoy 25% savings on return seaplane transfers all-year-round as you arrive over glistening lagoons, and savor effortless dining with daily breakfast and dinner included. Explore our twin island paradise with an exciting lagoon tour and a magical visit to Ithaa Undersea Restaurant over delicate canapés.
Little explorers stay and dine free (for up to two children under 12), with complimentary access to the Kid’s Club and engaging family cooking classes that spark curiosity. Complimentary snorkeling and non-motorised watersports ensure days of adventure. Here, every moment is crafted to delight all ages in paradise.
