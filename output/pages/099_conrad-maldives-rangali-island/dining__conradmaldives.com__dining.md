A Feast For The Senses
WHERE OCEAN AND FLAVOR CONVERGE
Pioneers in the world of gastronomy, Conrad Maldives Rangali Island is home to award-winning restaurants and Ithaa, the world’s first undersea dining experience. Enhance your stay with private dining, intuitive service, and our unwavering dedication to crafting moments that linger long after the final course.
ITHAA UNDERSEA RESTAURANT
Five meters beneath the Indian Ocean, Ithaa dissolves the boundary between dining and dreaming. Schools of reef fish drift past curved acrylic walls as flavors unfold on your palate, each course a conversation between the kitchen’s artistry and the sea’s living theater. A meal not just eaten, but felt.

Flames dance, steel sings, each ingredient transformed before your eyes as chefs craft their art with precision and flair. The water's edge meets your sightline perfectly, a quiet testament to our devotion to detail.

Aaraha Champagne Bar
Perched above the Indian Ocean, Aaraha captures the island’s most breathtaking sunset. Champagne is poured by the glass as delicate canapés of caviar, lobster, and foie gras are served in quiet rhythm. As the sky softens into evening, Aaraha becomes a moment of pause where the night begins with elegance.

Ufaa
On Rangali Finolhu's tranquil shore, Ufaa, meaning "happiness" in Dhivehi, blends Chinese culinary artistry with Maldivian serenity. Sizzling woks harmonize with ocean breezes while fragrant teas steep as the sun melts into watercolor horizons. Bold ancestral flavors find new expression against a canvas of salt air and fading light.

Premium cuts and fresh seafood sizzle over open flames while the sky transforms into a canvas of amber and rose. An evening ritual where exceptional grilling meets nature's nightly masterpiece.

Where the lagoon meets the softening light of dusk, Vilu presents Italian inspired cuisine reimagined for island life. Linger over refined plates designed to share, as Mediterranean influences mingle with lapping waves. From morning pastries to evening aperitivos, the experience flows as naturally as the Maldivian tide.

Sustainably grown produce transforms into vibrant compositions, each plate a meditation on color, texture, and vitality. The gentle lap of waves beneath provides the soundtrack as nourishing flavors awaken body and mind.

Live cooking stations bring diverse cuisines to life, from Asian delicacies to Western classics, while the open air setting frames each meal with lagoon breezes and the easy rhythm of Maldivian mornings and evenings.

Rangali Bar
The relaxed social heart of the resort, Rangali Bar gathers guests where the main pool dissolves into lagoon views.
