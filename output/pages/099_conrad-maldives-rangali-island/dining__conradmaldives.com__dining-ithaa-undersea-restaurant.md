The Muraka
Our chefs translate the island’s rare natural beauty into gastronomy delights, ensuring every ingredient reflects the ecosystem’s protection. This authentic Maldivian spirit is tasted in signature dishes like Maldivian lobster and Wagyu beef. We curate this extraordinary experience, delivered with thoughtful service, in the unparalleled setting of the world’s first undersea restaurant.
A New Undersea Chapter with Dom Pérignon
In our pursuit of excellence, we have elevated the art of champagne aging beyond the traditional cellar. Beneath the surface, the ocean offers stable temperatures, gentle movement and natural pressure that together create an ideal environment for refinement. We have submerged cases of Dom Pérignon bottles in front of Ithaa and after six months of maturation in this remarkable setting, the bottles will be brought to the surface in December 2026. They will be offered exclusively to guests dining at Ithaa, providing a truly unforgettable opportunity to enjoy Champagne that has been shaped by the sea.

An Immersive Ballet of Sea and Sensation
Our tasting menus offer more than a meal; they are an immersive ballet of sea and sensation. Crafted with precision, each course is a symphony of flavors that honors the Maldives’ rich bounty and global elegance. Your dining experience unfolds as stingrays glide and schools of angelfish drift by like brushstrokes on a living canvas. This exquisite culinary journey five meters beneath the surface is quiet, unhurried, and truly unforgettable.
Luxury in Its Most Intimate Form
Ithaa seats only 14 guests per service, a true commitment to an intentional experience. This is far more refined than opulence—it is time slowed down, beauty amplified. Outside your glass cocoon, reef sharks circle and turtles glide past. Your personalized tasting menu honors the Maldives’ rich bounty and global elegance. The experience is quiet, unhurried, and truly unforgettable.
A Destination Worth Crossing Oceans For
Since its unveiling in 2005, Ithaa has graced the lists of the world’s most iconic dining destinations—and rightly so. But more than accolades, it has earned devotion from those who’ve experienced its quiet magic. It’s not just a dinner. It’s the kind of moment that becomes a story you’ll tell for the rest of your life. Whether you want to visit Ithaa for lunch or dinner, no overnight stay is required, simply arrive for the day and take your seat beneath the sea.
