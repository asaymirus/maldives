HERITAGE & VISION
THE PIONEERING SPIRIT OF RANGALI
Welcome to a realm where innovation meets unspoilt nature. As the original Maldivian icon we have spent decades curating a paradise that honors our heritage while constantly reimagining the possibilities of luxury travel. Our story is one of bold firsts and deep reverence for the environment.
INTIMATE RETREAT FOR THE SOUL
Heritage is the heartbeat of our islands. We balance the thrill of the new with a deep respect for tradition. Our spaces are designed to be intimate cocoons where you can breathe freely and reconnect with what matters most.
Here time slows down. The rustle of palm fronds and the soft lap of the tide create a natural symphony that guides you toward profound relaxation and holistic wellbeing.
A LEGACY OF INNOVATION
Innovation is our tradition. From the first steps on our jetty to the depths of the ocean, we continue to define the art of Maldivian hospitality.
1992
The Dawn of An Iconic Legacy
We unveiled our pristine shores to the world planting the seeds of a timeless sensory voyage. This inception invited travelers to embrace the raw beauty of the Maldives establishing a heritage of authentic warmth. It marked the beginning of a story written in the sands and turquoise waters.
1997
The First Bridge Connecting Two Islands
We bridged the gap between adventure and relaxation with the first structure connecting two separate islands in the Maldives. This architectural marvel spans 500 meters across the vibrant lagoon, allowing you to wander freely between the social heart of Rangali-Finolhu and the secluded quiet of Rangali Island. It is a path walked by dreamers seeking the soul of the destination.
1997
Hilton Debut
The resort officially opens as the Hilton Maldives Resort and Spa, managed by Hilton Worldwide. From its inception, it sets a new standard for luxury in the region, being the first internationally branded resort to launch in the Maldives. It also pioneers the concept of connecting two private islands to create a larger, more diverse resort experience.
Explore
2004
World’s First Glass Floored Overwater Spa
The resort unveils its iconic over-water spa. Set on stilts above the turquoise lagoon, it features glass-floored treatment rooms, offering guests a unique wellness experience with views of the marine life below. This further cements the resort’s reputation for innovative guest experiences.
2005
Ithaa
On April 15, 2005, the resort makes global headlines with the opening of
Ithaa Undersea Restaurant
, the world’s very first of its kind. Situated five meters below sea level, the all-glass restaurant offers 180-degree panoramic views of the vibrant coral gardens, revolutionizing the concept of destination dining.
Learn More
2007
Conrad Arrival
In December, the resort is rebranded to
Conrad Maldives Rangali Island
, becoming part of Hilton Worldwide’s most luxurious and contemporary brand portfolio, Conrad Hotels & Resorts. The name change reflects its status as a flagship property known for its sophisticated style and world-class service.
2013
The Maldives' First Underground Wine Cellar
Descend two meters below the soft white sands into a hidden cavern of epicurean delight. We introduced the country’s first subterranean wine cellar to curate unforgettable tasting menus paired with thousands of vintages. This intimate space offers a cool and quiet escape where gourmands celebrate life’s milestones surrounded by the rich heritage of the world’s finest vineyards.
2014
The Maldives' First Authentic Chinese Restaurant
Culinary boundaries expanded as we partnered with celebrity chef Jereme Leung to bring authentic Chinese heritage to our shores. Ufaa creates a gastronomic journey using fresh ingredients and masterful techniques. Dine on hand pulled noodles and dim sum while the ocean breeze flows through the open air pavilion, perfectly blending bold oriental flavors with our laid back island atmosphere.
2014
A Dedicated Home For Our Team
We believe service from the heart starts with a happy team. We developed a third island solely for our dedicated staff to ensure they have their own space to thrive. This commitment enables our team to deliver the warm Maldivian hug you receive upon arrival, fostering a genuine spirit of hospitality that turns fleeting moments into generational memories.
2018
The Muraka
In November, the resort once again captures the world’s attention with the launch of
THE MURAKA
. This $15 million, two-level residence is the world’s first underwater hotel suite, featuring a master bedroom submerged over 16 feet below sea level. The Muraka redefines the concept of luxury travel, offering an extraordinary and immersive marine experience.
Learn More
2022
Grand Relaunch
Following another extensive multi-million dollar refurbishment, the resort unveils a grand relaunch. The renovation includes reimagined water villas, refreshed dining venues, and enhanced guest facilities, solidifying its position as a leading luxury destination for the modern traveler.
