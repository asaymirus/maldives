Overview
Dive into the depths of opulence and surrender to the allure of the undersea residence at Conrad Maldives Rangali Island. Immerse yourself in the extraordinary with a master bedroom submerged beneath the turquoise waters, offering an unparalleled private paradise. Experience bespoke amenities, delectable gastronomy, and awe-inspiring activities as you answer the call of oceanic luxury in this secluded haven.

Inclusions
Welcome Ceremony
Feel the warmth of our Maruhabaa welcome marking the start of your extraordinary island odyssey.
Dedicated Butler Service
Allow your private butler to curate every moment ensuring a seamless and intuitive residential experience.
Personalized Stay
Craft your dream escape with exclusive daily experiences ranging from private dining to underwater adventures.
Choose an exclusive set of experiences to enhance your stay:
Private Dinner
Indulge in a private dinner under the clear night sky, with the sound of the lapping waves as your accompanying orchestra in the comfort of your own residence.
Spa Journey
Experience a complimentary personalized treatment at our award winning spa.
Excursion
Explore the natural beauty of the Indian Ocean with an unforgettable trip to catch the sunset, swim with whale sharks or spot sea turtles.
Photoshoot
Immortalize your stay with a Muraka photoshoot and take home your framed picture for everlasting memories.
Ithaa Couple’s Experience
The world’s first underwater restaurant welcomes you for lunch or cocktails under the iconic glass dome.
Sunset Cocktail
Enjoy a carefully crafted signature Muraka cocktail on your sunset deck with your loved one(s). A simple moment to cherish.
Wine Tasting
A private session with our sommelier and have a taste of his favorites in the Maldives’ first underground wine cellar.
Movie Night
Enjoy a quiet evening with popcorn and your favorite movie.
Maldivian Cooking
Join our chef to create our signature fish curry and enjoy our local delicacy.
Coral Conservation
Leave a piece of you at the Muraka by planting your own coral frame on the adjoining reef and join in the conservation efforts of our pristine and delicate ecosystem.
One experience per day and same experience cannot be repeated during the stay.
