AN EXTRAORDINARY VOYAGE
YOUR ODYSSEY BEGINS
Nestled in the South Ari Atoll, our secluded haven is accessible via a scenic 30 minute seaplane flight or a short domestic flight followed by a speedboat transfer. Your arrival is curated to be as seamless as the panoramic horizon itself, subject to availability and weather conditions.
AN UNFORGETTABLE ARRIVAL
Prepare to leave the ordinary behind as you traverse the Indian Ocean to our secluded paradise. Whether you choose to soar above the atolls for a bird’s eye view or glide across the azure waters, your journey to Conrad Maldives Rangali Island is curated to be as breathtaking as the destination itself.
Please note that transfer fees are not included in our room rates. For more information or to arrange your preferred method of transfer, contact our Reservations Team.

Seaplane Transfers
Witness boundless blues on a scenic 30 minute flight by Trans Maldivian Airways, offering a breathtaking aerial perspective.
Seaplanes operate daily from 6:00AM to 3:30PM.
Return transfer per adult: US$700 net
Children below 12 years of age receive 50% discount
Infants below two years of age are complimentary
Speedboat Transfers
Glide across the Indian Ocean on a curated cruise to our paradise, offering an immersive view of the azure oasis.
Duration: Approximately 4–5 hours
US$3,000 per trip per way, subject to 10% service charge and 17% GST
Transfers are subject to availability weather conditions
The Conrad Lounge
