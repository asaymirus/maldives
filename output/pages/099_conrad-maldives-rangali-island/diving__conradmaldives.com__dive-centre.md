Manta talk & Tour
Witness the graceful underwater ballet of manta rays just an hour boat ride from the resort. After learning about their fascinating behavior you will enter the water to watch them glide and feed. The abundance of marine life here also offers chances to spot turtles and sharks swimming nearby.

Turtle safari
Immerse yourself in the tranquility of Turtle Reef where endangered Hawksbill and Green turtles drift effortlessly among the corals. This guided safari allows you to observe these ancient mariners feeding and resting offering a serene and intimate connection with one of the oceans most beloved creatures.
