Overview
Start each day with sunrise views from your private deck, complete with an outdoor Jacuzzi and direct lagoon steps for snorkeling or swimming. Indoors, a spacious living room and a refined bathroom with an ocean-view tub, rain shower, and double vanity deliver modern comfort. Stylish, bright, and perfectly placed over the water, this villa is made for relaxed island living.
Parents with children under 12 will be asked to sign a disclaimer upon arrival.
