Overview
A tranquil overwater villa offering a private sundeck, outdoor whirlpool, and direct access to the lagoon for calm, effortless days on the Indian Ocean. The bathroom includes an ocean-view tub, rain shower, and double vanity, blending comfort with quiet elegance. This intimate retreat is shaped for rest, reflection, and uninterrupted time beside the sea.
