FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS
1
Where is COMO Maalifushi located in the Maldives and how do you reach the resort?
COMO Maalifushi is a secluded private island resort in the Maldives, located in the remote Thaa Atoll in the southern Maldives, a region known for its pristine coral reefs, turquoise lagoons and white-sand beaches. Guests reach the resort via a 60-minute scenic seaplane transfer from Malé International Airport, offering panoramic views of the Maldives’ islands and atolls before landing at the resort’s private jetty. This peaceful location makes COMO Maalifushi one of the most exclusive luxury island resorts in the Maldives for travellers seeking privacy, nature and world-class ocean experiences.
2
Is COMO Maalifushi a family-friendly Maldives resort with a kids club and beach villas?
Yes — COMO Maalifushi is widely recognised as a luxury family resort in the Maldives, offering spacious beach villas and overwater villas with direct lagoon or beach access. Families can enjoy creative activities and marine experiences at the Play by COMO Kids Club, while parents relax with wellness treatments at COMO Shambhala Retreat. With its calm lagoons, spacious villas and curated activities, COMO Maalifushi is frequently recommended among the best luxury resorts in the Maldives for families seeking a private island holiday.
3
Is COMO Maalifushi a good Maldives resort for a romantic getaway with overwater villas?
Yes — COMO Maalifushi is a renowned Maldives romantic resort, ideal for couples seeking overwater villas in the Maldives and secluded beachfront escapes. Guests can enjoy private lagoon terraces, sunset dining, couples’ spa treatments at COMO Shambhala Retreat and tranquil island surroundings, making the resort a popular choice for honeymoons, anniversaries and romantic island getaways. COMO Maalifushi is often highlighted among the best luxury resorts in the Maldives for couples seeking privacy and refined island experiences.
4
What is included when staying at COMO Maalifushi and what makes it one of the best luxury island resorts in the Maldives?
COMO Maalifushi Maldives, a private island resort in the pristine Thaa Atoll in the southern Maldives, offers curated experiences combining wellness, gourmet dining and ocean adventures such as snorkelling, surfing and diving. Guests enjoy daily yoga, access to COMO Shambhala Retreat with sauna, steam room, Jacuzzi and fitness centre, and use of snorkelling equipment and non-motorised water sports including kayaking and paddleboarding. Villas feature personalised butler service, and guests benefit from access to the COMO seaplane lounge in Malé before the scenic seaplane transfer to the island.
5
Are there private beach estates or villas with direct white-sand beach access?
Yes — COMO Maalifushi offers expansive private beach villas and luxury beachfront residences with direct access to the Maldives’ white-sand beaches and clear lagoons. These secluded villas feature spacious indoor and outdoor living areas, private swimming pools and personalised guest services, creating an exclusive Maldives private island villa experience. The combination of space, privacy and natural beauty makes COMO Maalifushi a leading destination for travellers seeking luxury beachfront villas in the Maldives.
6
What makes COMO Maalifushi one of the best luxury Maldives resorts for gourmet dining and oceanfront restaurant experiences?
COMO Maalifushi is widely regarded as one of the best luxury Maldives resorts for gourmet dining and oceanfront restaurant experiences. The resort offers refined culinary options including Madi, its all-day dining restaurant overlooking the lagoon, and Tai, a celebrated Japanese restaurant serving sushi and seafood over the water. Guests can also enjoy private beach dinners, sunset dining experiences and wellness-focused cuisine inspired by COMO Shambhala, making COMO Maalifushi a leading destination for fine dining in the Maldives with romantic island settings.
7
Is COMO Maalifushi committed to sustainability and eco-friendly practices?
Yes — COMO Maalifushi is dedicated to sustainable, eco-conscious operations in the Maldives. The resort holds EarthCheck Silver certification, supports coral restoration and marine conservation, reduces waste and single-use plastics, generates solar power, and grows organic produce on-site — all part of COMO’s mission to protect the Thaa Atoll’s natural ecosystem.
