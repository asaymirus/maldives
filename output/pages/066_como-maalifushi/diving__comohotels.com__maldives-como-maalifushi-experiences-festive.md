December 24th
CHRISTMAS ROAST AND TOAST LUNCH
A festive selection of gourmet sandwiches, signature sliders, and seasonal bites featuring premium grilled meats, melted cheeses, fresh island flavours, and smoky island-inspired flavours.
CHAMPAGNE BREAKFAST
Savour a festive Champagne breakfast with a bottle of Duval-Leroy Brut, accompanied by the soothing sounds of the ocean.
In-Villa: December 24th
On Palm Island: December 27th
In-Villa floating breakfast: December 29th
December 25th
CHRISTMAS AROUND THE WORLD DINNER
A five-course set menu served across three settings — Madi, Thila restaurant and poolside — with a live band, fire dance, belly dance and tableside magic to mark the occasion.
December 26th, 28th, 30th
FISHERMAN'S TABLE with market visit
Head to the market in the morning with a local fisherman and select your catch fresh from the Indian Ocean. Back at the resort, our chef prepares it to your liking, for lunch, dinner  in the privacy of your own villa if you wish.
December 27th
Sushi, Nigiri, Temaki and Sake Pairing
Learn the traditional techniques behind sushi, nigiri and temaki, then sit down to enjoy what you have made, paired with sake.
December 28th
Yakiniku Dinner with sake
Gather around the table for a Yakiniku dinner — an interactive Japanese grilling experience where guests cook their own selection of meats and vegetables over a live flame, at their own pace. Savour the smoky flavours and engage in an interactive dining experience alongside sake pairing options.
December 29th
The Art of the Steak
Choose your cut and leave the rest to our chef and enjoy a steak dinner centred on quality, preparation and the pleasure of a great meal shared.
December 27th
Noble Vines Wine Dinner
Begin with a Champagne and oyster reception before journeying through France's most celebrated wine regions — Burgundy, Bordeaux, Alsace, the Loire Valley and the Rhône — paired with a four-course dinner.
January 1st
New Year's Island Lunch
Welcome the New Year over a beachfront lunch of fresh seafood and grilled specialities, unhurried, open-air and right by the water.
December 25th and January 1st
Oyster Indulgences
Freshly shucked trio of oysters paired with chilled Champagne or Bellini, creating a refined seaside experience perfect for sunset celebrations.
Champagne and Trio of Oysters: December 25th, 5.00pm to 7.00pm
Bellini and Trio of Oysters: January 1st, 5.00pm to 7.00pm
FESTIVE COCKTAIL SPECIALS
Toast to the season with festive cocktails featuring daily-changing collection of cocktails — each reimagined with a joyful twist. And even a visit by Santa Claus! Highlights include:
Santa's Arrival: December 24th, 5.45pm to 6.45 pm
Santa's Signature Sips: December 25th,11:00am onwards
Joy on the Rocks: December 26th, 11:00am onwards
Daiquiri Day: December 26th, 11:00am onwards
New Year Champagne Cocktails: December 31st, 11:00am onwards
Sunset Cocktail Tropical Gatsby Evening: December 31st, 6.00pm to 7.00pm
