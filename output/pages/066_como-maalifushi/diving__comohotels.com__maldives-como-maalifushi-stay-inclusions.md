Restorative Wellness
Restore balance with inclusive COMO Shambhala treatment, daily movement classes and full access to our holistic wellness facilities.
Each stay includes:
One 60-minute COMO Shambhala massage per guest (for guest aged 18 years and above)
Access to Finnish sauna, steam room, plunge pool, relaxation lounge, gym, and padel court
Access to daily group fitness, yoga and meditation sessions.
1
Dolphin sunset boat cruise
As the sun sets over the Thaa Atoll, take a cruise to watch pods of wild dolphins. If you’re lucky you will catch dazzling acrobatic displays as the dolphins leap out of the water, or surf in the boat’s wake. This activity will be available as per resort's schedule.
2
Guided turtle and reef snorkelling safaris
Swim alongside these gentle creatures and  other reef fish in their natural habitat.
3
Offshore sunset drop line-fishing
Try traditional Maldivian reef bottom fishing, using weighted lines to reel in potential catches such as red snapper, grouper and barracuda. Your catch can be cooked by our chefs at Madi or Tai for lunch or dinner the following day. This activity will be available as per resort's schedule (one session per guest, per stay)
4
Splash net boat cruise
Take a cruise with a twist, setting off in search of dolphin pods migrating through the Thaa Atoll. Once in the open water you can climb onto our dhoni’s splash net and experience the thrill of being towed safely through the ocean. This activity will be available as per resort's schedule.
5
Selected Watersports
Including catamaran sailing, windsurfing, sea kayaking, stand up paddle boarding and snorkelling
