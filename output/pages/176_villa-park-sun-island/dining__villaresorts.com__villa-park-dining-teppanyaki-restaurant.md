An authentic and interactive Japanese dining experience or ‘food theatre’ in an idyllic beach setting. Watch as fish and meats are cooked in front of diners on the Teppanyaki grill while intricate preparations of sushi and tempura are always on the go.
Platters of freshly made sushi and sashimi flow here, with everything from prawns, octopus and tuna. Equally good are udon-noodle broths with some of the best Japanese beers. Our skilled chefs make Teppanyaki dining a fun, engaging experience. You’ll find the restaurant in front of the Araamu Spa and close to Southern Star.
