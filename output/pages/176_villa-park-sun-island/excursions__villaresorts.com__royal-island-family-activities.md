Family accommodation
Spacious, serene, and made for time together, the family villas at Royal Island offer generous room to unwind in Baa Atoll.
Choose from Beach Villas and Sunset Beach Villas steps from the shore, Beach Pool Villas with a private pool, Two-bedroom Family Beach Villas for up to six, or the Two-bedroom Beach Pool Residence, the island’s largest beachfront home with direct beach access.
Each villa blends indoor and outdoor living with warm Maldivian design details, creating a relaxed home base for your family’s slow-paced island adventure.

Starfish Kids' Club
The heart of family fun at Royal Island, Starfish Kids Club is a cosy, caring hub designed for younger children. This intimate space offers daily activities that spark creativity, curiosity, and gentle connection.
From shell crafts and treasure hunts to story time, simple baking, and short nature strolls, young guests enjoy meaningful play while parents relax and recharge nearby.

Flavours for Every Generation
Dining at Royal Island is a celebration of flavour, family, and island life. With inviting restaurants and bars to explore, there is something for every taste:
Beachfront seafood at Raabondhi with wide ocean views
All-day international buffets at Maakana with live stations
Relaxed drinks at Boli Bar or the Pool Bar
Late-night music and fun at the Fun Pub
Share a private beach barbecue or enjoy a sunset family dinner by the lagoon. Every meal feels like a memory in the making.
