When is the best time to visit Villa Haven?
The ideal time to visit Villa Haven is from November to April, when the weather is typically dry and sunny, perfect for exploring our island and enjoying outdoor activities.
When is Villa Haven opening?
Villa Haven is scheduled to open on February 2025.
Is Villa Haven open for booking?
Yes, bookings are now open for stays starting from February 2025, via our website.
Do you have a minimum night stay policy?
Our booking engine will inform you of any minimum stay requirements based on your selected travel dates.
What is your cancellation policy?
Cancellation policies depend on the accommodation type and booking period. Stricter terms may apply during peak seasons, with detailed information provided during booking.
Do I need a visa to travel to the Maldives?
Visitors receive a 30-day visa upon arrival in the Maldives; no prior visa is required.
Can I amend my reservation if I didn't book through the Villa Haven website?
Please contact your tour operator, travel agent, or online platform directly for reservation changes if booked through them.
Can I upgrade to all-inclusive or full board once I arrive at Villa Haven?
Upgrades to all-inclusive or full board are available upon arrival. For the best rates, consider upgrading as part of your prepaid package when booking.
Are children able to stay at Villa Haven free of charge?
One child under 12 stays free in your room, with additional children accommodated in some villas. Children's breakfast is charged at 50% of the adult rate. Please inform us of any children traveling with you when booking.
Are babysitting services available?
Yes, babysitting services are available upon request. Please inquire at reception for more details.
Can I reserve a specific villa?
We strive to accommodate specific villa requests but cannot guarantee availability.
Is there a safe in my villa?
Each villa includes a personal safe at no extra charge for securing your valuables.
Are beach towels provided?
Yes, beach towels are provided in your villa.
What kind of electric sockets are provided in my villa?
Villas feature standard UK-style 3-pin plug sockets and one international power socket. Adapters are available upon request.
Do you have mosquito nets in the villas?
Mosquitoes are rare, but if you are sensitive to bites, we recommend bringing repellent, which is also available for purchase on-site.
Are there medical facilities at Villa Haven?
A 24-hour clinic with a resident doctor is available on-site.
Is there Wi-Fi at Villa Haven?
Complimentary Wi-Fi is available in guest rooms and public areas
Will I get mobile phone coverage at Villa Haven?
Yes, the island offers good GSM International Roaming service through local service providers Dhiraagu and Ooredoo.
Will I be met at the airport when I arrive in the Maldives?
Our airport representative will meet you at Velana International Airport after customs and assist with your transfer to Villa Haven.
How do I get to Villa Haven?
Villa Haven is located on Dhiffushi Island in the South Ari Atoll, just a 35-minute seaplane ride from Velana International Airport.
What are the check-in and check-out times at Villa Haven?
Check-in is at 14:00, and check-out is at 12:00. Early arrivals should consider booking the villa from the night before. Late check-out options are available at special rates.
What currencies do you accept at Villa Haven?
We accept cash (Euro, GBP, USD) and credit cards (Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Maestro, UnionPay). Travelers' and personal cheques are not accepted. All charges will be billed to your villa account and settled at check-out.
Which languages are spoken at the resort?
English is widely spoken, along with German, Italian, French, Portuguese, Russian, Spanish, Arabic, and Chinese.
Is there a dress code at Villa Haven?
Our dress code is casual, reflecting our relaxed island atmosphere.
Does Villa Haven cater to specific dietary requirements?
Yes, our chef can accommodate special dietary requests. Please email your dietary needs to hello@villaresorts.com before your arrival.
What types of accommodations are available at Villa Haven?
Villa Haven offers a variety of accommodations, including Beach Pool Villas, Deluxe Beach Pool Villas, One Bedroom Residences with Beach and Garden Pools, Two Bedroom Residences with multiple pools, and a Three Bedroom Haven Reserve.
What dining options are available at Villa Haven?
Dining options include The Olive, Husk, Yuzu, Piazza, Solara and Nook.
What payment methods are accepted at Villa Haven?
We accept Mastercard, Visa, American Express, and Maestro.
How can I contact Villa Haven for more information or to make a reservation?
You can contact Villa Haven at +960 334 4777 or email us at hello@villaresorts.com.
