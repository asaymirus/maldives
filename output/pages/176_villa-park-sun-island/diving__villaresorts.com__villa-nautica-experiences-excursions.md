Embark on a big game fishing adventure, experience the magic of snorkelling around nearby reefs at sunrise, or spend the golden hour watching wild dolphins play in the waves on a traditional dhoni.
Speedboat tours
Explore the nearby atolls, and enjoy a spot of snorkelling, with one of our exhilarating speedboat tours. Our team will take you to deserted private islands, hidden sandy coves, and beautiful blue lagoons filled with colourful sea creatures.
Male' City Tour
This three-hour adventure winds through the city’s lively streets, where mopeds zip by and the scent of fresh tuna fills the air at the local fish market. Step inside the historic Friday Mosque, a masterpiece carved from coral stone, and take in the rhythm of daily life as you pass teahouses, markets, and timeworn landmarks. Colourful, chaotic, and full of character.
Whale submarine
Board our whale submarine and embark on an immersive, underwater tour of the Indian Ocean. This state-of-the-art passenger submarine will take you on an unforgettable journey around Villa Nautica's nearby reefs where you'll see tropical marine life and majestic coral.
Big game fishing
Discover more about the traditional methods used by Maldivian fishermen with a big game fishing adventure. All our equipment is specially designed to catch large fish, and anything you do catch can be barbecued or grilled by our chefs when you arrive back onshore.
Blue Escape
Experience the magic of a tropical Maldivian sunset with our dolphin safari. Choose between a high-powered speedboat or a traditional dhoni and keep your eye out for groups of dolphins playing in the surf.
Sunrise fishing
Board a dhoni or one of our high-powered speedboats at sunrise and try your hand at sea fishing. Our team will take you to some of the best local reefs and will teach you how to fish like the locals with a traditional line and weights. Anything you catch can be taken back to shore and barbecued or grilled by our chefs.
Sunset fishing
Try your hand at catching your own supper with our sunset fishing trip. Choose between a dhoni or one of our speedboats and let our team transport you to one of the nearby reefs for an evening of sea-fishing, the traditional way. Anything you catch can be taken back to shore and barbecued or grilled by our chefs.
Snorkelling
Head to nearby coral reefs, tranquil lagoons, and snorkelling hotspots and encounter endless tropical marine life. Our team knows all the best places to see dolphins, turtles, and stingrays, and all snorkelling equipment is provided.
Exotic Insula
Escape to a nearby sandbank and embark on the ultimate desert island castaway experience. Arrive on white sandy shores, spend time snorkelling in the shallows, and enjoy a barbecue lunch before heading back to Villa Nautica.
Semi-submarine
With our semi-submarines, you can enjoy the best of both worlds. Above deck, soak up the beautiful views of the surrounding beaches and palm trees. Head below and you’ll discover tropical fish, colourful corals, and graceful sea turtles.
All of our excursion experiences are provided by QAdventure.
