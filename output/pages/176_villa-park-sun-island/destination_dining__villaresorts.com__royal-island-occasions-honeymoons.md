At Royal Island, beauty, romance, and adventure combine to create the perfect honeymoon destination in the Maldives.
An idyllic location, once-in-a-lifetime experiences and excursions, and a destination dedicated to romance. Whether you want to fill your days with endless adventures or simply want to relax on our sugar-white sandy shorelines, our all-inclusive resort is the perfect place to begin your new life together.
A blissfully romantic escape
Book your slice of paradise with us: with impossibly clear blue waters and magical marine life to discover together, candlelit dinners on exquisite sandy shores and tropical jungles to explore, romantic honeymooners are spoilt for choice.
