Luxury island honeymoons in the Maldives
If there’s one honeymoon destination that gets it right, it’s the warm and welcoming Maldives. Quite simply the most dreamy and pampered treat after a wedding.
Undisputed as one of the most seductive honeymoon destinations in the world, these shimmering atolls will have you dreaming of lazing in a hammock over sparkling waters, romantic beach dinners, swimming with dolphins, snorkelling over colourful coral reefs and bobbing gently on a traditional wooden dhoni at sunset, cocktails in hand… Your honeymoon can be as laid-back or glamorous as you like: the choice is yours!
A blissfully romantic escape
Book your slice of paradise with us: with impossibly clear blue waters and magical marine life to discover together, candlelit dinners on exquisite sandy shores and stunning landscapes to traverse, romantic honeymooners are spoilt for choice.
Honeymooners who were married within the past year (present your certificate when you arrive) will receive a complimentary bottle of sparkling wine and a fruit basket.
And there’s more. If you book a secluded Beach Pool Villa or an Overwater Villa directly via our website, you will be treated to a complimentary private dining experience for two right on the beach!
