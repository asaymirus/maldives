Easter Celebrations at Villa Nautica in the Maldives
Celebrate Easter in the Maldives with beachside egg hunts, ocean-view brunches, and family fun under the sun at Villa Nautica.
View Brochure
This Easter, step into a world of sunshine, sea breeze, and island fun at Villa Nautica. Swap city scenes for sandy toes as your family enjoys a joyful celebration surrounded by turquoise waters.
Kids can get creative with bunny headband making, decorate cookies, splash around the pool, and join in a colourful sand art session. The Easter egg hunt by the beach is a favourite, filled with laughter and little surprises.
As the day winds down, families come together for live music, tropical drinks, and a delicious Easter Lobster BBQ by the sea—while the little ones dance under the stars at the kids’ Easter Disco Party.
Create lasting memories this Easter, where every moment brings smiles, connection, and the magic of the Maldives.
