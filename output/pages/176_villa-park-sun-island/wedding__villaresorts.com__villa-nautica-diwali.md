Diwali Celebrations at Villa Nautica
As Diwali lights up the horizon, Villa Nautica invites you to a series of exciting events that promise to make your festival truly memorable. Located by the peaceful shore, our resort offers a relaxing getaway and a chance to immerse yourself in the vibrant Diwali celebrations.
Diwali at Villa Nautica
Diwali Highlights
Monday, 20th October
Rangoli Competition
(Poolside)
10:30 to 12:00
Create bright rangoli by the pool using traditional patterns and bold color. A relaxed start to Diwali at Villa Nautica where your creativity does the talking.
Festival of Lights Culinary Journey
(Bageecha)
11:00 to 12:00
Cook with our chefs and taste classic Indian flavors. Learn simple spice pairings, plate your dishes, and enjoy a Diwali style lunch in Bageecha’s garden setting.
Diwali Card Making
(Wavy Navy Kids Club)
15:00 to 16:00
Young guests design keepsake cards with bright paper, stamps, and stickers. A quick, hands on craft they can gift or take home from their Maldives holiday.
Diwali Lantern Making
(Wavy Navy Kids Club)
16:00 to 17:00
Kids make colorful paper lanterns and hear the story of the Festival of Lights. Easy materials, helpful guidance, and a cheerful souvenir for sunset.
Henna Tattoo Counter
(Poolside)
18:30
Pick a delicate mehndi design and watch our artists bring it to life. Leave with a beautiful temporary pattern that captures Diwali by the water.
Festival of Lights Feast
(Sunset Beach)
19:00 to 21:00
Dine under the stars at an oceanfront buffet inspired by India’s regional kitchens. Think fragrant biryani, tandoori classics, fresh breads, and sweet desserts, served with warm Maldivian hospitality.
Fire Show
(Captain’s Bar, Beach)
21:30
Performers light up the night with rhythm and flame. A striking beach highlight for Diwali in the Maldives.
Bollywood Night (DJ)
(Captain’s Bar)
22:15
Finish on the dance floor with Bollywood hits, Dandiya favorites, and crowd pleasing remixes. A lively finale to the Festival of Lights at Villa Nautica.
