Celebrations and parties at Villa Park in the Maldives
Celebrate milestone birthdays or wedding anniversaries together with best friends or multigenerational families.
Every birthday and wedding anniversary deserves a joyful celebration so we’re here to make sure that you have an unforgettable island holiday together with a memorable event planned for your special day. We can create magical experiences for everything from candlelit beach dinners under the stars to interactive chef’s dinners at one of our restaurants – the choice is entirely yours!
Birthdays
Birthdays at Villa Park are celebrated in a myriad ways: you might want to head out on a dive or experience swimming with whale sharks for the first time in your life, book a romantic sunset dinner on a private sandbank, or experience sophisticated farm-to-fork dining in the treetops at ZERO restaurant. Every guest is honoured with a birthday cake baked especially by our chefs and is gifted a bottle of wine.
Anniversaries
If it’s your wedding anniversary while you’re staying with us at Villa Park, we’d like to congratulate you both by gifting complimentary bottle of chilled sparkling wine and a celebratory cake. All you need to do is let us know by presenting a copy of your marriage certificate on arrival and we’d be delighted to take care of the rest!
