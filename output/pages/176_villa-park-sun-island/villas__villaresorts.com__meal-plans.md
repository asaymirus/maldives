Villa Resorts / Meal Plans
Villa Resorts offers a range of meal plans designed to suit different travel styles, from simple buffet dining to more inclusive stays with added drinks, dining flexibility and extra experiences. Whether you prefer to keep things straightforward or enjoy a more indulgent island escape, each plan is designed to offer a different level of ease, variety and value.
Infinity™ Plan
The Infinity Plan is our most comprehensive meal plan, created for guests who want the highest level of ease and indulgence throughout their holiday. With premium dining, enhanced experiences and added benefits woven into the stay, it offers a seamless escape designed around comfort, variety and convenience from arrival to departure.
Premium All Inclusive Plan
For stays until 31 October 2026
The Premium All Inclusive Plan offers a more elevated take on the all-inclusive experience. With greater flexibility, a broader range of inclusions and a more premium overall feel, it is well suited to guests who want more variety and a more indulgent stay.
Dine-Around Premium All Inclusive Plan
For stays from 1 November 2026 onwards
The Dine-Around Premium All Inclusive Plan is designed for guests who want more freedom to explore different dining experiences across the resort, alongside a more premium level of inclusions. With a stronger focus on variety, flexibility and choice, it creates a more immersive and elevated all-inclusive stay.
All Inclusive Plan
The All Inclusive Plan is designed for guests who want a more relaxed, easy-going stay with more included throughout the day. In addition to daily dining, it brings in drinks, light bites and added leisure benefits, making it a convenient choice for those who want to enjoy more of the resort without constantly thinking about extras.
Fullboard Plan
Our most straightforward option, the Fullboard Plan includes daily breakfast, lunch and dinner in the main buffet restaurant. It is ideal for guests who want the ease of having their main meals covered while keeping the rest of their stay simple and flexible.
Complimentary Offerings
In addition to the meal plans above, all guests at Villa Resorts can enjoy a selection of complimentary services that enhance the overall stay. On arrival, guests are welcomed with meet and greet assistance at the airport, followed by welcome refreshments at the resort. Each room also includes a daily bottle of water, tea and coffee-making facilities, and complimentary Wi-Fi throughout the stay.
For leisure, guests have access to the gym and a variety of land sports, including badminton, snooker, table tennis, darts, beach volleyball, squash, tennis and basketball.
We also offer a few thoughtful touches for special occasions. Honeymooners receive a bottle of sparkling wine and a fruit basket, for weddings within 12 months of arrival. Wedding anniversaries are marked with a bottle of sparkling wine and a celebration cake, while birthdays include a celebration cake and a bottle of sparkling wine.
To arrange honeymoon or anniversary amenities, please share the details at the time of booking and present a marriage certificate at check-in.
All offerings are subject to terms and conditions. Please refer to the relevant meal plan section below based on your stay dates.

How It Works
At Villa Resorts, our all-inclusive packages are designed for your complete enjoyment and relaxation. Beverages are served for personal consumption at selected restaurants and bars, and all guests in the same room are required to be on the same board basis.
For safety, alcoholic beverages are only available to those 18 years and older. Our all-inclusive benefits are available throughout your entire stay, ensuring you get the most out of your experience, though services may occasionally be affected by weather or other factors beyond our control.
Guests will be required to wear a wristband for identification, which allows access to all-inclusive services. All meals and services are billed for tracking purposes, with package benefits lasting until checkout.
Please note that all services are subject to the terms and conditions of each respective resort. If you have any specific questions, please feel free to contact us at hello@villaresorts.com.

What is included in the Fullboard Plan?
Fullboard includes breakfast, lunch and dinner from the main buffet restaurant. It also includes water, coffee and tea during mealtimes, but does not include snacks, minibar, excursions, spa benefits, watersports, diving benefits or transfers.
Does Fullboard include drinks?
Yes, but only in a limited way. Fullboard includes water, coffee and tea during mealtimes. It does not include the wider beverage selection available under All Inclusive, Premium All Inclusive, Dine-Around Premium All Inclusive or Infinity.
What is included in the All Inclusive Plan?
The All Inclusive Plan includes breakfast, lunch and dinner from the main buffet restaurant, a broader selection of beverages than Fullboard, snacks, high tea, snorkelling gear, selected watersports, a daily minibar refill, and discounts on selected dive rates and spa treatments. For stays until
31 October 2026
, it also includes one à la carte meal per person per stay at selected restaurants on qualifying stays, along with a sunset fishing excursion on qualifying stays. For stays from
1 November 2026 onwards
, it includes one à la carte meal per person per stay at selected restaurants on qualifying stays, plus a discount on additional à la carte dining.
What is the difference between All Inclusive and Premium All Inclusive?
For stays until
31 October 2026
,
Premium All Inclusive
sits above All Inclusive. It offers one à la carte meal per person per day rather than one per stay, a more premium beverage selection, and a sunset fishing excursion with a shorter minimum stay requirement than standard All Inclusive.
What is the difference between All Inclusive and Dine-Around Premium All Inclusive?
For stays from
1 November 2026 onwards
,
Dine-Around Premium All Inclusive
includes one à la carte meal per person per day at selected restaurants, a broader beverage range including premium liquors, seasonal fresh juices, ice creams, milkshakes and smoothies, and one sunset fishing excursion per stay on qualifying bookings. All Inclusive is more limited in both dining flexibility and premium beverage inclusions.
What does “Dine-Around” mean in practice?
It means the plan is designed around greater dining flexibility at selected restaurants across the resort, rather than keeping guests primarily within the main buffet restaurant structure. In practice, it gives guests more freedom to enjoy included à la carte dining as part of the stay.
Are à la carte meals included in every plan?
No. Fullboard does not include à la carte dining. All Inclusive includes a more limited à la carte benefit, while Premium All Inclusive or Dine-Around Premium All Inclusive offer more frequent access. Infinity includes unlimited à la carte dining at selected restaurants.

Is minibar included in every meal plan?

Which meal plans include diving benefits?
Diving benefits are not included in Fullboard. All Inclusive and the premium mid-tier plans include a discount on selected dive rates. Infinity goes further, including one scheduled dive per person per day for certified divers and one introductory dive per stay for non-certified guests.
Which meal plans include spa benefits?
Spa benefits vary by plan. Fullboard does not include spa benefits. All Inclusive and the premium mid-tier plans include discounts on selected treatments, while Infinity includes one 60-minute spa treatment per person per day. In the future structure, sauna and steam are also included on qualifying plans, subject to availability.
Which plans include excursions?
Excursions are not included in Fullboard. For the older structure, All Inclusive includes one sunset fishing excursion on qualifying stays, Premium All Inclusive includes sunset fishing with a shorter stay threshold, and Infinity includes daily scheduled excursions. For the newer structure from
1 November 2026 onwards
, All Inclusive no longer shows an excursion inclusion, Dine-Around Premium All Inclusive includes one sunset fishing excursion per stay on qualifying bookings, and Infinity includes daily scheduled excursions.

Do all guests in the same room need to be on the same meal plan?
Yes. All guests staying in the same room are required to be on the same board basis.
Can meal plan services be affected by weather or operations?
Services may be affected by weather or other factors beyond the resort’s control. This is especially relevant for excursions, watersports and certain outdoor inclusions.
Do meal plan inclusions vary by resort?
Yes. While the meal plans follow the same overall structure across Villa Resorts, inclusions, timings, outlets and experiences may vary by resort.
What is the main difference between Infinity and the other plans?
Infinity is the most comprehensive option. It adds unlimited à la carte dining, the broadest experience-led inclusions, daily scheduled excursions, more extensive watersports, daily spa treatment, diving benefits and complimentary shared return transfers.
