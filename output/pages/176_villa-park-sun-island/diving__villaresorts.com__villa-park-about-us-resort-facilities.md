As one of the largest resorts in the Maldives, Villa Park is where you can find a wealth of bars and restaurants, a luxury spa, and some of the region's best excursions and diving experiences.
Villa Park's overwater and beachfront accommodation is positioned in all corners of the island. Shaded by jungle, but with the island's diving centre, leisure facilities, and restaurants never far away, we have created an environment that is both a hub of activity and a haven for relaxation and privacy.
