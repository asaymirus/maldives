Meetings and conferences at Villa Nautica
Villa Nautica is one of the largest meeting and conference venues in the Maldives and only a short speedboat ride from Malé. We have eight different indoor event spaces, each with excellent on-site amenities. In-between sessions you will discover the best of the Maldives: our world-renowned dive sites, easily accessible coral reefs, pristine sandy beaches, gourmet dining and more.

For those searching for a creative and unconventional venue, the Maldives is hands-down the most beautiful option for yoga and wellness retreats or more informal motivational workshops for teams, many of which can take place outdoors – after all, there’s no reason why brainstorming sessions can’t take place on the beach! Our water sports and excursions or spa treatments can be included as part of this exceptional and experience.
