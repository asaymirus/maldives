Stargazing
1 hour
Description
As evening falls over Baa Atoll, watch the last colours of sunset fade into a blanket of stars. Our stargazing experience in the Maldives invites you to slow down, look up and take in the night sky far from city lights. With minimal light pollution around Royal Island, countless stars, constellations and planets become visible to the naked eye.
This peaceful night-time activity in the Maldives is perfect for couples, families and anyone who loves the beauty of the sky. Listen to the soft hush of the ocean, feel the gentle night breeze and enjoy a quiet hour of pure island calm under the stars.
