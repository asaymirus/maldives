Top five reasons to visit our award-winning spas
Is there anything more serene than indulging in spa time? Imagine that spa nestled in a tranquil Maldivian island sanctuary. Intrigued? You should be.
Our Araamu Spas are secluded havens dedicated to your wellbeing and rejuvenation. Named after the Dhivehi word for the blissful state of complete relaxation, they offer the perfect escape for some much-needed pampering. Here are five compelling reasons to visit one of our spas during your stay.
1. You can reconnect with the world around you
Immerse yourself in the beauty of our outdoor treatments, set in serene pavilions hidden within private gardens. These tranquil settings allow you to unwind and enjoy peaceful moments of solitude, reconnecting with the world around you.
2. They’re the perfect places to relax with a loved one
Our garden pavilions, equipped with two beds, provide the ideal space for couples to share an intimate retreat. Enjoy a soothing massage together, followed by a visit to our private baths or Jacuzzi, making it a perfect romantic escape.
3. We have treatments for everyone
From traditional Maldivian therapies to massages inspired by Europe, Thailand, and Indonesia, we offer a variety of treatments to suit everyone. Whether it’s a quick facial or a comprehensive seven-day wellness program, you'll leave feeling refreshed and rejuvenated.
4. You’ll leave feeling gorgeous from head to toe
Our salons offer a plethora of beauty treatments and products, ensuring you look and feel your best. From nail therapy to hair and makeup services, and even foot massages, our specialists are here to help you ease everyday stress and enhance your natural beauty.
5. Our talented therapists are known for their expertise
The heart of our spas is our talented therapists. Highly qualified and knowledgeable, they are committed to creating a blissful experience for every guest. Many are multilingual and can recommend the best treatments to address your specific needs and concerns.
For a truly relaxing break, speak with one of our specialists and discover the perfect treatment for you.
