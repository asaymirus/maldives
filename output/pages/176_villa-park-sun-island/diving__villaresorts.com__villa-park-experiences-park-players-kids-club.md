Park Players Kids' Club at Villa Park
In what is the newest and one of the largest Kids’ Club in the Maldives, Villa Park’s light and airy kids’ club is all about having fun. At any time of the day, this happy and dynamic space is a hive of creative activity, absorbing play with interesting daily programmes on offer. It’s a safe and nurturing space for children of all ages. Knowing that kids are cared for by our enthusiastic and dedicated team, parents are able to take time out to relax together.

For energetic explorers who need to be kept busy so that parents can have fun doing water sports or dives, every day is a new and exciting adventure at the Park Players Kids’ Club.
Every child is treated as a very special guest in this fun, dynamic space with a wide range of creative activities and educational games to choose from. Energetic children can play games in the pool, build sandcastles or join on an island tour. And when they need to rest out of the sun, little ones can use the ‘nap lounge’, a peaceful, cool and cocooning space, while older siblings can try their hand at painting, drawing or origami.
Programme
Our Park Players Kids’ Club has a number of art and creative projects to keep everyone happily occupied. A host of activities are organised, from coconut-leaf weaving sessions to shell craft and paper origami. Budding artists love colourful hand printing and creating paper collage artworks to take home with them, whereas younger children find face painting immensely therapeutic and calming.
Afterwards, our talented team are on hand to inspire imaginative play while encouraging interaction with new friends. Older kids love going exploring on our island tours or building sandcastles – a favourite with happy-go-lucky adventurers who would prefer being outdoors. An enormous play area with wonderful facilities, one of the largest in the Maldives, is an absolute joy for energetic kids.
