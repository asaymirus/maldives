Villa Nautica/About
Villa Nautica is one of a string of islands in North Malé Atoll. Positioned northeast of the vibrant Maldivian capital, Malé, our tropical isle is renowned for its unspoiled jungle, luxury overwater villas, and sparkling lagoons filled with exotic marine life.
