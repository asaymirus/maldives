At the island's edge, Husk presents exquisite flavors and regionally inspired dishes beneath a grand canopy, enhanced by rustic screens, handwoven rugs, and inviting low seating that capture a sense of nature’s beauty.
Savor smoky, succulent flavors of freshly caught fish slow-cooked over glowing coals, and relish marinated meats expertly seared on giant grills. The alchemy of fire-cooked creations culminates in a selection of desserts, all crafted with a hint of sweetness from the flames.
