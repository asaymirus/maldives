Pot Tub
Description
Lit by a gently warming fire underneath the Kawa bath or ‘pot tub’, this ancient and deeply restorative bathing ritual is not only relieves aching muscles but calms and restores the soul.
Available in the early morning or from the early evening when the fire gently flickers until nightfall. Enjoy an indulgent and private soak surrounded by a sea of fragrant petals and aromatic herbs in this romantic glow with twinkling candles suspended in glass jars from the trees. This quiet sanctuary allows you to retreat, reflect and breathe deeply while listening to the meditative soundtrack of the jungle and the sea …
