A spectrum of water sports adventures await. Whether you are a thrill-seeker, or craving the tranquillity of crystal-clear waters, let the ocean be your canvas for unforgettable experiences.
Plunge into a world of adventures at Villa Haven, where the ocean becomes your playground. The glistening waters are the perfect place to tick off your bucket list of adventures in Maldives. Ride the waves on a jetski adventure or soar high above the crystal-clear waters during a mesmerising parasailing experience. And for those craving a more leisurely pace, the catamaran awaits. Whether you seek speed or serenity, Villa Haven's water sports adventures offer a diverse range of experiences.
Jetski
Let the adrenaline take over as you slice through the azure waves on the jetski. Leave a wake of excitement in your past as the pristine waters turn into your playground.
Parasailing
Soar above the blues you ascend into the clear cerulean skies. The vast expanse of the ocean stretches out below, revealing a breathtaking panorama – an awe inspiring sight to behold.
Seabob
Glide through the waves at high speed, and take in the boundless open space and unparalleled freedom. Dive into the deep or skim across the water's surface at speeds of up to 20 km/h on your Seabob.
Catamaran
Step aboard and embark on a leisurely voyage atop the waters. Set sail on our catamaran, and let the rhythm of the waves and the gentle caress of the sea breeze take you on an adventure.
Windsurfing
Salt-kissed air in your hair and the wind at your back, as you ride the rolling waves. Villa Haven invites you to seize the waves as you carve your way through the gentle lagoon blues.
