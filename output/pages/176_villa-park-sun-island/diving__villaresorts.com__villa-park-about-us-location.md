How to get to Villa Park
View Google Maps
Arriving at Villa Park is easy. Once you land at Velana International Airport in Malé, head to counter A07 in the arrival hall where our team will assist you with your transfer arrangements. Transfers can be booked in advance when you make your villa reservation or through our reservations team, so everything is confirmed before you arrive. You can choose between two routes: Velana International Airport, Malé to Villa Park via a 17 minute domestic flight followed by a 10 minute speedboat ride, or Velana International Airport, Malé to Villa Park via a 35 minute seaplane transfer.
The seaplane option is a direct journey that feels like a welcome to the Maldives from the sky, with no extra hassle. Villa Air seaplanes feature some of the widest panoramic windows in the Maldives, so almost every seat feels like a window seat. Guests glide over reefs, sandbanks and islands, enjoying picture perfect views before gently landing on the turquoise lagoon at Villa Park.

Experiences
Boundless thrills await our fun-loving, free-spirited travellers. A hive of energy and activity, Villa Park offers adrenaline-inducing water sports and incredible diversity of marine life. Swim with mantas, stingrays and whale sharks in one of the world’s top dive locations or simply float in our innovative saltwater therapy pool. Eat at Zero, an exceptional organic dining experience in the heart of the jungle or rejuvenate at Araamu Spa.
