Why Choose CCR
A Resort For Every Purpose
There are many reasons to visit the Maldives, and Crown & Champa Resorts (CCR) offers a resort for all. Catering to a variety of travellers, our resorts provide the perfect backdrop to every holiday be it for the rejuvenating solo traveller, a romantic getaway on an adults-only island or a family holiday for all the generations. Our resort offers something for each of our travellers, from a peaceful beach escapes to underwater adventures creating lasting memories of a lifetime.
Diversified Portfolio
From the effortless luxury to the affordable Maldives, our resort portfolio is diverse, accommodating every traveller on every occasion on all budgets. At our resorts, your dream Maldives holiday awaits, from a relaxing break to adventures underwater and indulgent experiences there truly is an experience for everyone.
Exceptional Value
The award-winning Crown & Champa Resorts experience comes at an unbeatable value with several accommodation options on offer. At our resorts, you can opt for the All-Inclusive packages that have been designed to ensure hassle-free holidays in paradise.
Paradise On Earth
Scattered across the Maldives, each Crown & Champa Resort occupies an enviable location, set on its own island, allowing you to experience the unspoiled natural beauty of the Maldives with awe-inspiring sunrises and sunsets. Walk on pristine powder-soft white sandy beaches as the towering palm trees sway, enveloping you in an island melody. Swim in the inviting, crystal-clear waters of the turquoise lagoons teeming with diverse marine life.
Rooted in Local Heritage
Crown & Champa Resorts is rooted in Maldivian heritage, led by inspiring Ownership, developing the islands, the countries young talent and shaping the experiences on offer. We are committed to supporting the local economy by providing employment opportunities and sourcing locally produced products. Discover more of the history of the Maldives during weekly-organised cultural nights at our resorts.
