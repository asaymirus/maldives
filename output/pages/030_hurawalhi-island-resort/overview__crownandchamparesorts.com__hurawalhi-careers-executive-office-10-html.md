MAIN RESPONSIBILITIES:
Greets customers immediately with a friendly and sincere welcome. Uses a positive and clear speaking voice.
Picks up and collects Guests departure luggage from the rooms and loads to the transfer boat accordingly.
Need to be present on arrival to deliver the luggage to the guest rooms.
Provide lifts to guests when needed.
Need to be present at the main reception area for arrivals, if not busy with other guests.
Keeps the buggies clean and dry always.
Maintains buggies so they operate 100% in liaison with the maintenance team.
Reports any damage, to the luggage either on departure or arrival, to the FO Manager
OUR REQUIREMENTS:
Any combination of education, training or experience. High school diploma preferred.
At least a year of experience is required.
Must maintain a neat, personal hygiene, clean and well-groomed (Specific standards available)
