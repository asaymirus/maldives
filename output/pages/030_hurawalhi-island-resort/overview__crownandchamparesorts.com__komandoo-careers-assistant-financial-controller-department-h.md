Assistant Financial Controller – Department Head
We are looking for a talented, self-efficient candidate with extensive experience to support and lead the Finance Department at Komandoo Island Resort & Spa, Maldives. This is a department head position, requiring the ability to independently perform Financial Controller functions and ensure strong financial governance of the resort.
Main Responsibilities:
Assists the Financial Controller in overseeing all accounting, financial reporting, budgeting, and forecasting functions of the resort.
Independently performs Financial Controller duties in the absence of the Financial Controller, ensuring continuity of financial operations.
Ensures accurate and timely preparation of monthly, quarterly, and annual financial statements in compliance with company policies and applicable accounting standards.
Monitors cash flow, working capital, and cost controls, ensuring effective financial management of resort operations.
Reviews and controls revenue, expenses, payroll, inventories, and fixed assets to ensure accuracy and compliance.
Oversees internal controls, audits, and financial procedures to safeguard company assets.
Coordinates with department heads to analyze financial performance, identify variances, and recommend corrective actions.
Manages statutory requirements, tax compliance, and liaison with external auditors and regulatory authorities.
Supports system integrity, financial software performance, and data accuracy within accounting and reporting systems.
Leads, trains, and develops the finance team to improve departmental efficiency and performance.
Requirements:
Minimum of a Bachelor’s degree in Accounting, Finance, or a related field (professional accounting qualification preferred).
Strong ability to analyze financial data and prepare accurate financial reports using accounting systems.
Minimum of 3–5 years’ experience in a similar role, preferably within the hospitality industry or resort operations.
Strong understanding of budgeting, forecasting, cost control, and internal audit processes.
Excellent verbal and written communication skills, with the ability to work closely with senior management and department heads.
Ability to work independently, demonstrate leadership, and handle department head responsibilities in a remote island environment.
