Island Host – German Speaking
Meeru Maldives Resort Island is seeking dynamic and highly motivated individuals with a passion for delivering exceptional customer service to join our team as an Island Host (German Speaking).
Key Responsibilities:
Welcome & greet guests and provide a smooth and seamless registration upon arrival.
Assist guests with restaurant reservations, excursion arrangements or any other requests that maybe needed.
Coordinate with all departments on all guest welfare matters for a smooth, courteous & efficient service.
Ensure all necessary reports like VIP list, arrival list is available.
Expectations:
Minimum of one year experience in the hotel industry preferably in Front Office / Guest Services.
Educational background in hospitality and tourism
With good command of the German language; Other languages (French, Spanish, Chinese) is an advantage.
Preferably with an island resort experience.
Excellent customer care skills and interpersonal relations.
Outgoing, friendly and has cheerful personality.
We offer a great opportunity to make your mark in a company that is focusing on development and career growth.
