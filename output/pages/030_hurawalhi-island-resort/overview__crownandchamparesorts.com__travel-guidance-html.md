Guest Guide
Maldives Traveller Declaration:
All travellers are required to submit the
Traveller Declaration
(electronic Embarkation/Disembarkation card of the Maldives) within 96 hours prior to their international travel departure time.
Maldives, Velana International Airport Arrival Information:
Upon arrival at Velana International Airport, proceed to the Arrivals Hall (Terminal 1), where our airport team will be available to assist with onward transfer arrangements to the resort. Guests will be greeted by our dedicated Crown & Champa Resorts ‘CCR’ Airport Representative immediately outside the Arrival Hall exit (Terminal 1), holding the resort signage. The CCR airport team will escort guests to the Trans Maldivian Airways (TMA) check-in counter (Gate 4) for seaplane check-in or to the Counter #B9 (Terminal 2) for boat transfers.
Guests who prefer may proceed directly to the TMA counter at Gate 4 (Terminal 1) or to Counter #B9 (Terminal 2) for boat transfers.
Should immediate assistance be required, representatives are also available at ‘CCR’ Counter #B9 (Terminal 2) to provide guidance and support.
