Reservations Agent
Hurawalhi Maldives is looking for a passionate and enthusiastic candidate for the position of
Reservations Agent.
The 90-villa, adults-only luxury resort.  A renowned culinary hotspot offering epic culinary journeys, which include the largest all-glass underwater restaurant, 5.8 Undersea Restaurant We promote a culture of happiness for the well-being of our team members through their employee journey. We also provide our team members with accommodation, food, laundry services, team shop and other indoor & out activities such as team pool, gym, paddles, tennis, badminton, billiards etc., that make your job perform the most.
Requirement and responsibilities:
Handles daily correspondence and responds to reservations inquiries and bookings
Processes reservations, cancellations and modifications in the property management system
Knows the types of rooms available as well as their location and layout, transportation options and has available accurate information regarding flight schedules etc.
Displays product knowledge with accuracy and confidence
Fluency in written and spoken English [additional language will be an added advantage]
Strong knowledge of Opera
Minimum of 1-year experience in the role.
Luxury resort experience required.
We offer a great opportunity to make your mark in a company that it is focusing on development and career growth.
