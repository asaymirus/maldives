We also provide our team members with accommodation, food, laundry services, team shop and other indoor & out activities such as team pool, gym, paddles, tennis, badminton, billiards etc., that make your job perform the most.
You’re Responsibilities:
To organize and maintain the day-to-day operation of the vessel, with the help of respected colleagues, and maintenance of the vessel, by keeping and maintaining the records of the vessel operations, and by organizing the repair, maintenance and cleanliness of the vessel, with the guidance of Guest Transport Management Team.
Look after all the stuff (luggage and supply items) and assist to all the passengers to load and unload their items.
To inform and instruct all passengers for the proper usage of safety equipment and to keep the equipment in good condition
Assist Captain to check the engine and other equipment and give boat condition reports to the Guest Transport Manager before mooring the Dhoni and keep clean and moor in a safe place
Qualifications:
Any combination of education, training or experience that provides the required knowledge, skills and abilities
At least one-year previous experience in the same role in a similar environment required
Fire Safety Training and First Aid Training preferred
All employees must maintain a neat, personal hygiene, clean and well-groomed (Specific standards available)
