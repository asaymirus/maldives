DESCRIPTION
‘Hurawalhi Island Resort offers elegance and innovation; redefining the Maldivian resort concept.’
The sophisticated new resort of Hurawalhi captures the exotic essence of the Maldives, re-imagines the luxury concept, and manifests it into reality. A tropical champagne pavilion, a breath-taking aquarium restaurant, seriously stylish villas, and an over 18s-only policy are just a handful of reasons why this resort transcends everything that came before. This is the Maldives redefined; a truly unique five-star resort experience, nestled amongst the spectacular coral reefs of Lhaviyani Atoll. The 90 contemporary villas, designed by Tokyo-born architect, Yuji Yamazaki, are the epitome of tropical chic. His clean, crisp lines, nuanced shades and choice of natural materials harmonise flawlessly with the pure tones of the lush, unspoiled island. Privacy, spaciousness and style define these sleek spaces. The lagoon offers a symphony of glittering azure hues for you to splash about in, float upon, or zip across on any number of gizmos. Lhaviyani Atoll is one of the most pristine atolls in the entire Maldives.

Highlights From The Resort
DUNIYE SPA
Are you longing for deep relaxation and peace? Awaken body and soul at Hurawalhi’s oasis of relaxation, beauty and well-being. Visit Duniye Spa once and you will set the course of your day with heart-centered treatments; seek pampering and inspiration regularly and enter the path of joyful, healthy living to last a lifetime.
SCUBA DIVING
Coral reefs of the Maldives, deemed the best year-round diving destination in the Indian Ocean, are proof that the country’s striking beauty extends to the underwater. Scuba diving around Hurawalhi is a sheer delight; marine life is abundant and varied, with manta rays, reef sharks and sea turtles regularly paying visits to dives sites literally on Hurawalhi’s doorstep.
Hurawalhi’s location smack bang in the middle of Lhaviyani Atoll’s top dive sites means that divers get to experience vivid coral reefs with dazzling schools of fish on display, and have close personal encounters with sea turtles. Friendly reef sharks can be seen cruising the waters around Hurawalhi and, if lucky, the conditions can throw in a dozen or so manta rays.
HOUSE REEF DIVING –
The reef on which Hurawalhi sits is home to a staggering plethora of fish, big and small. Schooling bluestripe snappers and bannerfish mingle with fusiliers, and oriental sweetlips and butterflyfish munch on the coral, completely indifferent to the divers. A variety of miniature creepy crawlers can be found by the eagle-eyed divers. The house reef is easily accessible, with tank service provided to the entry points for your utmost convenience and for you and your buddy to dive at your favourite time of the day.
DIVING EXCURSIONS –
Lhaviyani Atoll boasts over 50 dive sites, with new ones being discovered on a regular basis. For beginners and for those who have hundreds of dives under their belts, dive sites around Hurawalhi are a treat. Diving boats leave throughout the day, helping to ensure that you can make the most of your time at the resort. The dive center runs single dive trips (morning and afternoon), and regularly organises two tank and full day trips so as to showcase the very best the diving in the Maldives has in store.
SCOOTER DIVING –
Underwater scooters are available at Hurawalhi for you to be able to reach the places where the big fish action is without the hassle of swimming against the current. You will buzz effortlessly past reef sharks, see squadrons of eagle rays – and feel like a superhero seeing the best the Lhaviyani has to offer with little effort and lots of fun.
REBREATHER DIVING –
Get introducted to the world of rebreather diving at Hurawalhi and experience the sensation of bubble-free diving. In addition to almost infinite bottom times, rebreathers allow us to get much closer to creatures that are usually spooked by the bubbles, such as mantas and sharks. Tanks and Sofnolime can be arranged for divers bringing their own units.
SNORKELLING
Coral reefs around Hurawalhi are a part of one of the most spectacular underwater environments on the planet. Fish species come in thousands and coral in hundreds, and a lot of it can be admired by snorkelling. Several reefs around Hurawalhi, including the resort’s house reef, offer ample opportunity for seeing sea turtles and bustling schools of fish. To make your snorkelling adventure five star, join the seasonal manta mania – the joy of swimming alongside these gracious, gigantic fish is hard to beat!
HURAWALHI SNORKELLING SPOTS –
Not more than a few kicks of fins away from the resort a whopping amount and variety of reef fish, turtles and coral await. For your safety, be sure to familiarise yourself with Hurawalhi’s house reef before you set out to explore it – our team will gladly inform you about entry/exit point and other important considerations and specifics.
HALF DAY TRIPS –
Spend a morning or an afternoon seeing first hand what everyone is talking about: coral reefs around Hurawalhi make all other resorts jealous! Snorkel sites nearby the resort include sunken coral islands that are home to anemones with clown fish, hawksbill turtles, and to everything from small damsel fish on the reef to large tuna passing by in the blue water. Insider’s tip: visit Kuredu Caves for unique encounters with sea turtles, napoleons and possible eagle rays gliding past.
FULL DAY TRIPS –
Dedicate a full day to exploring the beauty of the Lhaviyani Atoll’s reefs. Venture out farther from Hurawalhi to see even more of the pristine landscape and seascape that make the area visit worthy, and take a look at what lies below the calm ocean. You will be wowed by all the marine life you come across; keep your fingers crossed for a rich plankton soup that manta rays might be drawn to!
HURAWALHI MARINE BIOLOGY CENTER
Offering understanding of the Maldives’ coral reefs, and insights into Hurawalhi’s research and conservation efforts. Led by a resident marine biologist and run in close cooperation with Prodivers Maldives, Hurawalhi Marine Biology Center is there to share our passion for the ocean with you, and to further study the unique marine environment of which Hurawalhi is a part.
Beneath the soothing swaying of palm trees and steps away from some of the world’s most biodiverse natural terrain, there could hardly be a better place to stimulate your curiosity and wonder of the ocean.
The bustling underwater metropolis that awaits below the water surface around Hurawalhi is certainly well worth a visit. Lhaviyani Atoll is made up of a number of narrow and shallow channels that funnel the nutrient-rich water directly into Hurawalhi’s proximity, resulting in an incredible amount and variety of marine life. The island sits between two such channels and as a result is home to an abundance of sea life.
Currents, abundant sun and clear water provide aquarium-like conditions for over 2,000 species of fish and more than 200 species of coral. Crustacea and invertebrates are aplenty in the Maldives too.
It becomes apparent that magic lies in wait around every corner; Hurawalhi offers unforgettable experiences with the ocean’s toughest survivors – sea turtles, dolphins are known to throw dazzling performances, and guests can come up close and personal with Hurawalhi’s majestic manta rays and reef sharks.

EXCURSIONS
Putting your feet up at your favourite relaxation spot at Hurawalhi is a fine way to spend your holiday, but for when your wanderlust pops in and you wish to venture out of the resort, the Maldives’ discovery is at your fingertips.
ROMANTIC ESCAPES –
Lovebirds will find oodles of romantic excursions at Hurawalhi, each guaranteeing a memorable day with your significant other. For the ultimate Maldives-style romantic retreat, visit Hurawalhi’s very own Dream Island to live out your tropical fantasy.
LUXURY SAILING –
Sail across the Lhaviyani Atoll and enjoy the serenity, peacefulness and romance that draws lovebirds to this heavenly piece of paradise.
FISHING TRIPS –
Catch a sight of the atoll’s picturesque landscape and try your luck at reeling in your triumphant fish too. Spice up your stay with a delicious self-caught dinner or simply enjoy the socialising and the magnificent ocean around Hurawalhi that is home to an incredible amount and variety of fish life.
WATERSPORTS
Listen to the pulse of the ocean and immerse yourself in all the pleasures Hurawalhi’s surroundings make available. The resort is ringed by boundless stretches of water that provide an excellent playground for guest looking to do more than sunbathe. For those after a tranquil enjoyment and for adrenaline junkies alike, Ocean Watersport has just the right leisure activity for you.
DOLPHIN SAFARI –
Hop aboard our speedboat as we go in quest of the acrobatic spinner dolphins that reside in the atoll. It is common to see them returning to the same locations day after day, so it is highly likely that you will see performances from a number of different pods throughout the trip.
PARASAILING –
A breathtaking adventure awaits as you get to admire the most scenic, tranquil views of Hurawalhi and the mesmerizing landscape surrounding it. Soar high above the sparkling waters and soak in the panoramic views of the magic that the Maldives is a synonym for.
MANTA RAY SEARCH –
Allow us to showcase you Hurawalhi’s number one snorkelling highlight – manta rays! The inviting Indian Ocean sets an amazing scene for you to be stunned by Maldives’ magnificent mantas, which impress with their ballet-like feeding behaviour, slurping on the plankton bowl that the ocean currents bring to the atoll.
PRIVATE SPEEDBOAT CHARTER –
To see the very best of Lhaviyani Atoll without sharing your space and time with other guests, private speedboat charters can be arranged. From the popular manta search to dolphin spotting or snorkelling on coral reefs, the choice is yours. Steer clear of the resort and have the ocean all to yourself!
JET SKI –
Enjoy a jet skiing tour of the waters surrounding Hurawalhi in a cool and refreshing way! A jet ski excursion is a great way to live summer life to the fullest and to soak in the beautiful seascapes of the island.
OTHER MUST-DOS –
Snorkelling trips by boat or jet ski – private speedboat excursions – water skiing, wakeboarding, kneeboarding – canoeing – pedalos – windurfing – catamaran sailing – SUP
FITNESS CENTER
Stay fit and energized during your holiday at Hurawalhi by visiting the resort’s state-of-the-art gym, which houses everything you need to get in shape and stay in shape in paradise. While enjoying an invigorating workout, the 2-storey fitness center affords spectacular views of the atoll channel and the nearby Dhanifaru lagoon – save some stamina for the excitement if you happen to spot some dolphins or manta rays in the distance!
SPORTS GROUND
Guests with a passion for sports and those who like to maintain a healthy lifestyle will appreciate Hurawalhi’s Sports Ground. Join a game of futsal, improve your strokes on the tennis court
THE GAMES ROOM
During the day’s hottest hours or for evening leisure, plenty of excitement is to be had at The Games Room inside Coco Bar. Air hockey, table tennis, pinball and a retro arcade game – Pacman are inviting you for thrilling fun for any age.
