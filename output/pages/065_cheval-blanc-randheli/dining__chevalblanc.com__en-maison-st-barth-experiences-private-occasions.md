Private Occasions
Redefine the destination occasion. The pearl in the middle of the Caribbean Sea: a dreamy destination of laid-back elegance perfect for private events and intimate occasions.
Ivory sands, a bougainvillea breeze and the fiery glow of the sunset. The extraordinary and intimate character of Cheval Blanc St-Barth makes it an ideal setting for private events and festive gatherings. The genuine kindness and imagination of our Cheval Blanc Artisans will surprise and delight you and your guests. Let them imagine, plan, and take care of the smallest details of your celebration - you and your loved ones can simply enjoy the laughter, the joy and the precious memories.
On one of the finest beaches in the Caribbean, in the heart of luxuriant tropical gardens or by the main pool, the horizon for backdrop - the Maison boasts a myriad of idyllic ceremonial venues. Floral arrangements, gourmet delicacies and entertainment: the Artisans orchestrate every facet of the event, becoming the accomplices of an exceptional, unforgettable and emotional day.
A haven in the middle of the Caribbean Sea. Cheval Blanc St-Barth invites long-time friends, loyal guests, and new Cheval Blanc devotees to be surprised, enjoy memorable and intimate moments and discover a new Art of Emotion. All the Maison may be privatized for truly one-of-a-kind event. Emotion is at the heart, its orchestration remains an art.
