Cheval Blanc Experiences
Share unforgettable moments with friends and family, or create timeless keepsakes with your loved one. Our Maison’s Alchemists meticulously orchestrate bespoke experiences to fulfil every desire.
Take to the skies for a mesmerising scenic flight, offering breathtaking views of the Seychelles' pristine landscapes from a unique perspective. Alternatively, explore multiple stunning locations in one seamless journey. This helicopter experience promises unforgettable moments, making your Seychelles exploration truly extraordinary.
Discover the magic of our dining experiences. Whether you choose an intimate starlit beach barbecue or an al-fresco meal on the iconic granite boulders overlooking the Indian Ocean, our Ambassadeurs will tailor every detail to your preferences.
Indulge in a romantic interlude that transcends the ordinary, where every moment is infused with magic. Onboard a private boat, gently glide through the crystalline waters of the Seychelles. As the sun begins its descent, the sky transforms into a stunning canvas of warm oranges, soft pinks, and deep purples. A delicious picnic awaits, enhancing the flavours of this enchanting evening.
