Le Carrousel
A magical haven for our young explorers, Le Carrousel unfolds a realm of enchanting adventures where tales of mermaids and pirates spring to life.
Legend has it that a mysterious treasure is hidden within Le Carrousel… A cove of wonders where young adventurers embark on unforgettable journeys, guided by our dedicated Ambassadeurs.

Le Carrousel is open daily from 10 am to 6 pm for children from 3 to 12 years old.
Anchored in adventure and discovery, this 250 sqm (2,370 sqft) indoor oasis unfurls various delightful spaces designed to captivate children of every age: a snug cocoon for the littlest ones, a serene alcove for quiet moments, a creative studio for artistic pursuits, and a dynamic playground for the more adventurous.
