Weave through the magical heart of Le Carrousel! Entirely designed with children in mind, this kingdom of 500 sqm invite little explorers to expand their imaginations.
Walls painted with imaginary fishes enliven a playroom opening onto an outdoor trampoline and revealing a huge net tree, a slide and a climbing wall. On another wall, an interactive screen allows children to create their own virtual fish, which evolves among the corals and lagoons of the atoll...
A shallow pool, shaded pergola, water slide, awe-inspiring collection of toys and games, and a never-ending list of creative activities await!
