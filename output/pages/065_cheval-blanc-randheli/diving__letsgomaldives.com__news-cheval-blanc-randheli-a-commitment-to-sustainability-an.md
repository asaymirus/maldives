At

Cheval Blanc Randheli

Sustainability Partnership with EarthCheck
Cheval Blanc Randheli has proudly continued their partnership with
EarthCheck
, the global leader in sustainability benchmarking, certification, and advisory services for the travel and tourism industry. This collaboration highlights their commitment to preserving the natural beauty that surrounds their Maison and ensuring that the pristine destination is protected for generations to come.
About EarthCheck
Established over three decades ago, EarthCheck has grown into a globally recognized organization dedicated to promoting sustainable practices across more than 70 countries.
Sustainable Practices at Cheval Blanc Randheli
Cheval Blanc Randheli’s adherence to EarthCheck’s comprehensive sustainable development framework empowers them to:
Efficiently manage energy consumption
Reduce their environmental footprint
Foster positive social practices
All of this is achieved while maintaining their signature luxury and service excellence.
Luxury and Sustainability in Harmony
At the heart of Cheval Blanc Randheli’s vision is the belief that luxury and sustainability can coexist seamlessly. Their efforts are guided by the conviction that it is not only a privilege but a responsibility to protect the unique environment of the Maldives. By integrating EarthCheck’s sustainability benchmarks, Cheval Blanc Randheli ensures that their guests can experience an oasis of elegance without compromising on environmental stewardship.

We at Lets Go Maldives are inspired by Cheval Blanc Randheli’s unwavering dedication to sustainability. Together, we can continue to elevate the art of exceptional hospitality while upholding the principles of environmental care and social responsibility.
