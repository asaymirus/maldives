La Table Ruinart x Cheval Blanc Paris
From the 15th to the 25th of October, Ruinart is returning to Cheval Blanc Paris for the second consecutive year with a pop-up dining experience dedicated to the Champagne vineyards. Set within an immersive design inspired by the historic vineyards of Taissy and the gardens at 4 rue des Crayères in Reims, chef Arnaud Donckele has created an exclusive menu paired with the House's iconic cuvées, complemented by masterclasses.
Around a large shared table for 16 to 18 guests, Chef Arnaud Donckele orchestrates two lunches and six dinners. For this second edition, he presents a seven-course menu designed in harmony with the cuvées of the oldest champagne House.
Showcasing his mastery of sauces and his knowledge of the terroirs, the Chef composes precise pairings that elevate the expression of the champagnes.
Lunches: October 20 & 23, 2026, at 12:00 PM - €890 per person
Dinners: October 15, 16, 17, 22, 23, and 24, 2026, at 7:30 PM - €890 per person
7 cuvées: Ruinart Blanc Singulier, édition 20 - Ruinart Rosé, magnum - Dégorgement Tardif 2007, bouteille - Dom Ruinart Blanc de Blancs 2013, bouteille - Dom Ruinart Blanc de Blancs 2009 magnum - Dom Ruinart Rosé 1996, bouteille - L’Exclusive
Book your lunch or dinner
Hosted by a Ruinart ambassador, the "Signature Chardonnay" and "Cave des Millésimes" masterclasses offer an exploration of the champagne House's emblematic cuvées.
Each tasting is accompanied by three signature bites created by Chef Arnaud Donckele, specially designed to elevate the pairings between gastronomy and champagne.
“Signature Chardonnay” tastings: Sundays, October 18 and 25, 2026, at 11:00 AM, 3:00 PM, and 5:30 PM – €95 per person (Ruinart Blanc de blancs - Ruinart Blanc Singulier Edition 2020 - Dom Ruinart Blanc de Blancs 2013)
“Secret Cellar” tasting, Saturdays: October 17 and 24, at 11:00 AM – €190 per person (Ruinart Dégorgement Tardif 2007 - Ruinart Millésimé 2007 - Dom Ruinart Blanc de blancs 2007)
