Dive into well-being
A portal into balance and tranquillity, a window into the natural beat of the island. The Maison's Alchemists conjure immersive well-being experiences.
The morning ritual created by the Alchemists helps you rebalance yourself. The reconnection of mind and body starts by gently awakening the senses with a delicious breakfast on the terrace, exploring the amazing Colombier Beach on a walk with a personal trainer, a yoga session in the tropical pavilion, followed by a delicious detox juice and a treatment at the Cheval Blanc Spa. Add a sunrise meditation session to personalise your experience.
