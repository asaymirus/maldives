Moments for Two
Synchronise one beat of two hearts in the quiet air of the tropics. Electrify a bond with a taste of the French West Indies.
The Alchimists craft precious moments for you to share, cherishing them for years to come. A relaxing interlude in the tropical pavilion at the Cheval Blanc Spa, followed by a trip to the charming Gustavia Port, where you board a magnificent sailing ship. Head for Colombier Bay, with its flamboyant landscape, and stay until dusk, experiencing the Cheval Blanc art of emotion for yourself.
The spirit of St Barth and the intimate setting at Cheval Blanc St-Barth make it an idyllic place to hold a ceremony for a wedding or renewal of vows.
