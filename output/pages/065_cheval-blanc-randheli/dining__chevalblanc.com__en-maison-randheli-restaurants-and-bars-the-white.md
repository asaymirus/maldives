The White
Interiors are exteriors and the exteriors boast idyllic tropical views... The White serves island-inspired flavours from dawn to dusk in airy relaxation.
Straddled between vast ocean views and a lush tropical garden, the White restaurant serves around the clock laid-back cuisine with thoughtful preparation.
Chilled gaspacho, catch of the day, traditional Indian Ocean fare - no matter the craving, the chefs of the White present an inspiring collection of dishes.
