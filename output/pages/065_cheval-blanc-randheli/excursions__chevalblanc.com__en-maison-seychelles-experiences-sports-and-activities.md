Sports & Activities
On your marks, get set, go! At Cheval Blanc Seychelles, a myriad of activities awaits, crafted for both occasional enthusiasts and enduring sports lovers.
Our state-of-the-art Gym, equipped with the latest Technogym machines, sets the stage for invigorating workouts, whether solo or guided by our expert coach. To ensure a rejuvenating finale, complete each session with the soothing indulgences of a sauna and hammam.
Adjacent to the Gym, a 25-metre pool glimmers like a jewel under the noon sun, fringed by elegant loungers. Here, each lap aligns with the rhythm of the island, resonating with the raw energy of the surrounding environment.
