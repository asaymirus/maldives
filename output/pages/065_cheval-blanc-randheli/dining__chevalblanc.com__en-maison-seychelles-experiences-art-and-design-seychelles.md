Art & Design
Within Cheval Blanc Seychelles, contemporary art merges seamlessly with the wild elegance of Nature.
Envisioned by architect Jean-Michel Gathy, known for his work at Cheval Blanc Randheli, Cheval Blanc Seychelles epitomises the perfect blend of tradition and modernity. Its architecture, inspired by the geometric lines of Creole houses, flourishes in a harmonious dialogue between grand volumes and expansive views, crafting a haven where life's rhythms resonate with the poetry of the tropics.
WILD GREEN
The Maison’s signature colour celebrates the vitality, creativity, and lush biodiversity of the Seychelles. This vibrant and revitalising shade delicately harmonises with the Maison's natural hues of beige and taupe.
JUNGLE CHIC
Crafted by Thierry Wasser, Guerlain’s renowned perfumer, Jungle Chic is the olfactory signature of the Maison. With its woody and musky notes, this fragrance encapsulates the exotic essence of the Seychelles, offering an evocative invitation to embark on a sensory escape.
French artist Prune Nourry, renowned for her immersive sculptures, has crafted our Maison’s emblematic piece as a poetic reinterpretation of a horse. "La Centauresse", a heroic warrior figure, rises gracefully among the banyans, her sculptural force heralding guests at the Maison's entrance.
At the forefront of the global art scene, Joël Andrianomearisoa unveils "Un souffle du temps sur la mélodie de nos songes", an expansive artwork that flows throughout the Maison. His textile pieces—wild as untamed grass and presented in 52 variations, one for each villa—and canvases created in the studio of Le White restaurant, engage in a dialogue with the raw power of the surrounding Nature, enriched by the artist’s Malagasy poetic sensibilities.
True to his passion for ancient frescoes, Parisian artist Maximilien Pellet has designed a masterful work to enhance Vivamento restaurant. Drawing inspiration from Etruscan banquets, his fresco adorns the bar with minimalist, graphic imagery, enlivened by the shimmering dance of light across its glossy, rippling surface—a celebration of Italian conviviality and the art of living.
