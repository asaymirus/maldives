Le 1947
A ballet for the senses, a theatre for the divine. French savoir-faire is unveiled course by course in gastronomic refinement.
Named after Château Cheval Blanc’s sought-after vintage, Le 1947 presents elegant French gastronomy in an haute-design ambience.
Facing a lush, tropical garden, discerning palates are delighted with a playful experience of taste.

7:00 pm - 10:30 pm
