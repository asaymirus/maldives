Families
The young and young at heart are invited to play a barefoot holiday away in the lush corners of the tropical Maison.
At the heart of the Caribbean’s lush surroundings,
Le Carrousel
invites children to step into the adventures of Aru and Naïa, two young Arawak characters. Through play, discovery and stories inspired by the island’s culture, little explorers embark on an immersive journey where imagination sets sail and legends come to life.
The Maison's Alchemists are always on-hand to design family activities and experiences for pure island bliss. Every season, the Maison presents new adventures to experience the French West Indies to the maximum.
Little hands, little feet - and a big wide world of wonder. The Maison welcomes our youngest guests with a collection of specially designed amenities and gifts. Babies are swaddled in refined French equipment. Children are spoilt in kid-sized flip-flops, slippers and bathrobes. Made-to-measure baby purées and kid-friendly menus, well-being treatments for looking their best, and unforgettable family memories.
