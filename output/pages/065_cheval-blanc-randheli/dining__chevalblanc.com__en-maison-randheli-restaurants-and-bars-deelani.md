Deelani
Perched over the water and dressed in turquoise hues - the Deelani celebrates saveurs of the Italian Riviera with flair.
Named after a Maldivian breeze, the Deelani is a lively Mediterranean restaurant serving flavours of the sea, inspired by Italian signatures.
Perched on stilts and basking in the glow of the blue lagoon, the Deelani is a sprightly escape to lands afar.
