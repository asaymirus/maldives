A Shimmering Winter at Cheval Blanc Paris
Cheval Blanc Paris glows ever brighter as the festive season approaches, transforming into a sparkling haven where every moment radiates with magic.
Between star-lit dinners at Le Tout-Paris, gourmet escapes at Langosteria and soothing interludes at the Dior Spa, the Maison invites guests to embrace the enchantment of the season with grace and elegance.
For our youngest guests, an enchanted forest comes to life with laughter and discovery, while our Artisans ensure each stay shines with thoughtful gestures and precious emotions.
Its airy puff pastry , made with Normandy butter and flour from Auvergne, is adorned with a veil of caramelised sugar that lends a warm golden hue.
Each petal, handcrafted with care is meticulously arranged by the bakers to form a perfectly symmetrical corolla with a generous centre that catches the light. Fine lines etched into the dough echo the graceful structure of a flower, as if sketched by a careful botanist.
Beneath this delicate attire lies a melting almond frangipane, with a subtle hint of bitter almond, blooming on the palate like a gentle blossom.
With this daisy-inspired galette, Cheval Blanc Paris celebrates the art of sharing simple joys at the start of a new year – a treat to enjoy with family,

In December, Le Carrousel at Cheval Blanc Paris transforms into an enchanted forest, welcoming children aged 4 to 12 to experience the magic of the festive season.
Each afternoon, creative workshops, film screenings and moments of sharing awaken their imagination and joy.
From greeting cards to snow globes, clay Christmas trees to Santa Claus puppets, our young guests craft a thousand little wonders to take home.
