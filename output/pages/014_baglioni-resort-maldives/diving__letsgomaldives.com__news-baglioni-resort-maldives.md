Location & Setting
Baglioni Resort Maldives is located on Maagau Island in Dhaalu Atoll, reachable via a scenic seaplane transfer of around 40 minutes from Malé.  Situated in one of the Maldives’ more tranquil atoll environments, the resort allows guests to immerse themselves in unspoilt turquoise waters, a pristine house reef and white‑sand beaches-all within reach yet distinct from higher‑traffic atolls.
For travel partners, this means you can offer clients the “iconic Maldives” experience (villa on water/beach, reef access, luxury service) with a strong narrative of exclusivity and Italian‑style refinement.
Accommodation & Villa Product
The resort features a variety of villa categories designed to appeal across segments: couples, families, groups. According to the resort materials there are approximately 85–96 villas.  Sample villa types include:
Beach Villas: Direct beach access, stylish interiors, family friendly layouts.
Sunset Beach Villas with Pool: Elevated beachfront experience with private pool, sunset outlook.
Water Villas with Pool: Over‑water sanctuaries with private pool and lagoon vistas.
Two‑Bedroom Family Beach Villas with Pool: Ideal for families or small groups seeking space and comfort.
What stands out from our LGM lens: the villa mix is flexible, the design is fresh, and the all‑inclusive model (discussed further below) enhances the guest proposition. For luxury travel partners, this means strong potential for packaging, upselling and segment differentiation.

Umami & other venues: Offering a mix of Japanese, Mediterranean and international cuisine.
