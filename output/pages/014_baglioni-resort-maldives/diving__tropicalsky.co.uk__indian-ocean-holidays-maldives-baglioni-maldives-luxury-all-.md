Overview
Baglioni Maldives is a refined island retreat set in the pristine Dhaalu Atoll, where Italian elegance blends seamlessly with the natural beauty of the Maldives. Surrounded by turquoise lagoons, powder-soft sands and swaying palms, it offers a peaceful and sophisticated escape in one of the Indian Ocean’s most idyllic settings.
The resort features a collection of beautifully crafted beach and overwater villas, each designed with contemporary Italian style and a strong connection to the surrounding landscape. Expect spacious interiors, private outdoor spaces and uninterrupted views of the ocean, along with thoughtful touches that create a sense of effortless luxury. The all inclusive plan adds a sense of ease to each day, with exceptional Italian and international dining, snacks, drinks and activities all taken care of.
Days at Baglioni Maldives can be as relaxing or active as you choose. Whether little ones are discovering colourful marine life just steps from the shore, joining fun experiences at the kids’ club, or splashing in the lagoon, there’s plenty to keep them entertained. Adults can indulge in spa treatments, enjoy peaceful moments by the infinity pool or explore the vibrant reefs. As the sun sets, the island takes on a magical glow, a perfect backdrop for families, couples or honeymooners seeking a luxurious, stress free Maldivian escape.

Location
Located on Maagau Island in the Dhaalu Atoll in the central Maldives, about 150 km southwest of Malé, the remote private-island setting is surrounded by clear lagoons and coral reefs.
A 45 minute seaplane transfer from Velana International Airport takes you directly to the island.

Restaurants & Bars
