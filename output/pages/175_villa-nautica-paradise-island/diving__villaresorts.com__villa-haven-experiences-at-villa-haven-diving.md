Dive with our award-winning team and explore the Maldives' top dive sites. Experience exhilarating underwater adventures together!
South Ari Atoll invites you to discover a world of wonder beneath the ocean waves. Here, a grand spectacle unfolds with whale sharks, manta rays, stingrays, and a myriad of colourful fishes. Learn about the marine life of Maldives, get certified in PADI courses, and explore the clear waters that beckon year-round. Whether you're just beginning your underwater adventure or are a seasoned PADI certified diver, Villa Haven promises an adventure to be remembered.
Whale Shark Point
Experience the thrill of swimming with whale sharks, the gentle giants of the sea. Villa Haven’s proximity to Whale Shark Point provides unparalleled access to these magnificent creatures. The 500-meter reef surrounding Maamigili Island is a hotspot for whale sharks, along with large fish like tuna, barracuda, snapper, and Napoleon wrasse.
Manta Point
The 800-meter reef known for its seasonal gatherings of majestic manta rays between December and April. Outside of the manta season, enjoy the diverse marine life including scorpion fish, moray eels, octopus, triggerfish, white-tip sharks, and sting rays. This site offers a rewarding experience year-round.
Kuda Rah Thila
Famed for its colorful soft corals and impressive underwater scenery. The reef features spectacular fan corals and a coral ring that offers a unique swimming experience. Look out for yellow-striped snappers and batfish that add to the vibrancy of this renowned dive site.
Five Rocks and Angaga Thila
Famous for their populations of white tip and grey reef sharks, this destination is a haven for shark enthusiasts. From patrols of barracuda to elusive octopus, stonefish, and scorpion fish, a captivating world awaits to be witnessed.
PADI-Certified Dive Courses
Begin your underwater journey or take the next steps at our PADI-certified dive centre.
Discover Scuba Diving
Take your first steps into the world of scuba diving. Discover Scuba Diving covers basic techniques and skills and allows you to put them into practice in the shallow lagoon. This introductory course can be credited towards a Scuba Diver certificate or an Open Water course.
Scuba Diver course
Covering three of the five parts of the Open Water course, Scuba Diver certifies you to dive to a maximum depth of 12m (40 ft) under the supervision of PADI Dive Master, assistant instructor or instructor. Once you have completed the course, you'll also be one step further to an Open Water Diver certification.
Open Water course
The Open Water course covers more complex dives. Upon completion, you'll be permitted to dive to a depth of 18m (60 ft) under supervision. As well as performing Confined Water Dives and Open Water Dives, you'll also be required to complete several online courses.
Adventure Diver course
To become certified as an Adventure Diver, you'll need to prove that you are more competent in the water. Once you have completed three dives, all of which meet the criteria of Deep Diver, Night Diver and Underwater Navigator, you'll gain the Adventure Diver qualification.
Advanced Open Water course
Once you have gained your Adventure Diver certification, you can complete a further two adventure dives that include lower depths and navigation, and you will become a certified Advanced Open Water Diver.
Rescue Diver course
In the Rescue Diver course, you'll learn how to prevent potential problems in the water and manage them effectively, from recognizing fellow divers in distress to rescuing someone unresponsive.
Dive Master certification
A Dive Master is certified to supervise diving excursions and assist student divers. To become a Dive Master, you will work closely with a PADI instructor to expand your knowledge and skills and develop your leadership qualities.
Bubble Maker
Specially designed to teach the basics of diving and build confidence in the water, our kids' diving courses are great for little ones looking to explore the underwater world surrounding Villa Haven.
Private Diving Experience
Enjoy an exclusive diving experience tailored to your preferences. Our private dives offer personalized attention and a unique opportunity to explore the underwater world at your own pace.
