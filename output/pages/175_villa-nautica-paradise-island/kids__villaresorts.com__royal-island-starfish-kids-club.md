For little discoverers, Starfish Kids Club offers creative play and gentle island adventures, so parents can unwind or head out for a snorkel, spa, or dive, knowing their children are happy, engaged, and well cared for.
Programme
At Starfish Kids Club, we offer a mix of art and creative projects to keep little ones happily occupied. Daily activities are arranged, from coconut-leaf weaving and shell craft to simple paper origami. Budding artists enjoy colourful hand printing and making paper collage artworks to take home, while younger children often find face painting soothing and calming.
Afterwards, our friendly team stays close to spark imaginative play and encourage interaction with new friends. Older kids can head out on short island strolls or build sandcastles on the beach, a favourite with happy-go-lucky adventurers who prefer being outdoors. Our cosy indoor playroom, along with shaded corners and soft mats, creates a safe, relaxed setting that makes energetic play welcome but never overwhelming.
