Easter Celebrations at Villa Park in the Maldives
Celebrate Easter in the Maldives with seaside egg hunts, tropical brunches, and joyful family moments at Villa Park.
View Brochure
This Easter, escape to the Maldives at Villa Park, nestled in the peaceful South Ari Atoll. Leave the everyday behind and enjoy a holiday filled with sunshine, island adventures, and unforgettable memories.
Kids can decorate Easter trees, meet the Easter Bunny, and take part in activities like Bunny Yoga and the Little Bunny Run. The Glow in the Dark Egg Hunt adds excitement, while Bunny Cookie Creation and crafts inspire their creativity.
As the day winds down, gather for an Easter Lobster Raffle, enjoy a beach bonfire, and join the festive beach bash. The evening ends under the stars, with the whole family celebrating together by the sea.
This Easter at Villa Park is about creating lasting memories in one of the world’s most beautiful places, where every moment brings smiles and connection.
