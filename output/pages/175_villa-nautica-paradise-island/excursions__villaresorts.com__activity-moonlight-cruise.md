Moonlight cruise
1-2 hours
Description
When night falls, step aboard a traditional Maldivian dhoni for a serene moonlight cruise in the Maldives. Glide over calm, inky-blue waters as the sky fills with stars and the gentle sound of the waves sets a peaceful rhythm. With the cool ocean breeze and soft moonlight reflecting on the lagoon, this is one of the most relaxing and romantic ways to end your day in Baa Atoll.
Perfect for couples and small groups, this night cruise from Royal Island offers a quiet escape from the bustle of daily life. Sit back on deck, enjoy the stillness of the ocean and take in uninterrupted views of the Maldivian night sky.
