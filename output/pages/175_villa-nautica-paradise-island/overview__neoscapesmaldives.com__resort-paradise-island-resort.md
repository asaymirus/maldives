DESCRIPTION
Tropical luxury with cosmopolitan flair
Villa Nautica Maldives is a vibrant island paradise, alive with tropical blooms and surrounded by turquoise waters and pristine beaches. Just a short trip from Malé, it blends island warmth with cosmopolitan sophistication.
The resort uniquely combines four-star villas with an exclusive five-star overwater enclave. Budget-conscious travellers enjoy comfort and amenities, while luxury seekers indulge in secluded overwater opulence, all within the same dynamic setting.
Ideal for water sports, diving, and surfing, the tranquil lagoon offers endless adventure. Its lively quayside atmosphere makes Villa Nautica a glamorous hub of activity and social life. Arrive by speedboat and immerse yourself in a chic, tropical seafaring lifestyle.

Dining
Ristorante Al Tramonto
Located at the end of the main jetty, Ristorante Al Tramonto offers romantic dining with panoramic ocean views. Savor fresh seafood, hand-made pizzas, classic pasta, or the Chef’s grilled Black Angus fillet, paired with signature Sandstone wines. End with authentic Italian gelato. The open-air terrace, lit by lanterns, makes every sunset unforgettable.
Fukuya Teppanyaki
Experience authentic Japanese cuisine with a touch of theatre at Fukuya Teppanyaki. Watch chefs prepare fresh seafood, meats, and vegetables on the teppanyaki grill, or enjoy sushi, sashimi, and udon broths. Pair your meal with a selection of sake or Japanese beers. Sophisticated yet interactive, this dining experience combines traditional flavours with captivating live cooking.
Lagoon Restaurant
Nestled among the water villas, Lagoon Restaurant offers breathtaking ocean views for breakfast, lunch, and dinner. Catering to Water Villa guests, it serves international dishes with pescatarian, vegetarian, and gluten-free options. Enjoy a relaxed lunch, drinks on the terrace, or a romantic candlelit dinner as the sun sets. Daily-changing menus ensure fresh, memorable dining experiences amid the sparkling lagoon.
Bageecha Restaurant
Bageecha Restaurant, the main dining venue at Paradise Island Resort & Spa, offers all-day buffet-style meals with stunning Indian Ocean views. Enjoy continental, Asian, and Maldivian cuisine, including fresh tropical fruits, pressed juices, and sustainably sourced ingredients from our gardens and surrounding seas. Menus change daily with vegetarian, pescatarian, and gluten-free options, celebrating the island’s vibrant flavours in breezy, elegant surroundings.
Hook Restaurant
Perched at the end of the overwater jetty, Hook Restaurant offers an intimate dining experience with serene ocean views and stunning Indian Ocean sunrises. Specializing in fresh seafood, including Maldivian lobster, guests can indulge in memorable meals while surrounded by the tranquil lagoon. Its overwater setting and romantic ambiance make it one of the island’s finest dining spots.
Captain’s Bar
Step off the jetty into the vibrant Captain’s Bar, where chic quayside vibes meet island relaxation. Enjoy poolside cocktails, coffee, or sunset drinks as live bands and resident DJs set the evening mood. With energetic nights on the dance floor and a welcoming atmosphere, the Captain’s Bar is the perfect spot to begin or end your glamorous Maldivian holiday.
Beach Bar
The Beach Bar is a vibrant seaside spot where friends and families gather to relax after water sports adventures. Enjoy ice-cold drinks with your feet in the sand, cooled by the gentle sea breeze. Embrace the island’s cosmopolitan, seafaring vibe and toast to unforgettable memories in a lively yet relaxing beachfront atmosphere.
Dhaavani Café
Dhaavani Café delights coffee lovers with expertly crafted, world-class brews, celebrating a vibrant Maldivian coffee culture alongside traditional teas. Open 24/7, it also serves delicious light meals, making it the perfect spot to relax any time of day. Enjoy a comforting beverage or snack while soaking in the casual, welcoming atmosphere of this all-day café.
Private Dining at Villa Nautica
Experience unforgettable dining at Villa Nautica with options like candlelit beach dinners, barefoot “dig-in” meals, private BBQs, Crow’s Nest overwater dinners, and Oriental-themed evenings. Savor fresh seafood, personalized menus, and desserts crafted by expert chefs. Whether celebrating a special occasion or enjoying family time, each setting combines romance, fun, and magical island views for truly memorable Maldivian experiences.

Highlights From The Resort
Adventure
Unleash your spirit of exploration with a world of watersports. From fun tubing, jet skiing, and parasailing to windsurfing and catamaran sailing, Paradise Island is a hub of ocean thrills. The professional diving school and snorkeling excursions open up a kaleidoscopic underwater world for both beginners and experts.
Dive Oceans
Step off the jetty into vibrant coral reefs and tropical marine sanctuaries. With crystal-clear waters, every snorkeling or diving trip reveals ancient coves, colorful reefs, and an abundance of sea life just waiting to be discovered.
Excursions
From sunrise fishing to sunset cruises, submarine dives, and island-hopping adventures, unforgettable journeys await beyond the lagoon. For a cultural touch, explore nearby fishing villages or spend a day sightseeing and shopping in bustling Malé.
Sports & Recreation
Keep active with basketball, volleyball, tennis, squash, or a fully equipped gym. Guests can also enjoy billiards, table tennis, and indoor badminton, or join aerobics sessions for a refreshing boost.
Beach & Pool
Fringed by white sands and tranquil waters, the resort’s beach and lagoon invite sun-soaked relaxation. Unwind poolside or enjoy long, refreshing strolls along the shoreline.
Araamu Spa
A tropical garden sanctuary where wellness takes center stage. Choose from soothing massages, Ayurvedic therapies, beauty care, and signature treatments designed to restore body, mind, and spirit in complete serenity.
Meetings & Events
With one of the Maldives’ largest conference and event spaces, Villa Nautica is perfect for business gatherings, weddings, or retreats. Eight versatile indoor venues and stunning oceanfront settings ensure every event is unforgettable.
