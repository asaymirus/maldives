Catering for guests staying in our Water Villas, this sophisticated but relaxed overwater restaurant also serves dishes from around the world. Expect mesmerising dawn-to-dusk ocean views from your table.
Enjoy a languid lunch in the shade, cooled by gentle sea breezes, or soak up the sun with drinks on the terrace. As the sun sets on yet another heavenly day, join us for a romantic, candlelit dinner as the twinkling lights of Villa Nautica begin to reflect on the shimmering sea. Menus change daily with a thoughtful selection of pescatarian, vegetarian and gluten-free dishes.
