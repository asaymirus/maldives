Honeymoons and romantic breaks in the Maldives
If there’s one destination that truly understands honeymooners it’s the warm and welcoming Maldives, where once-in-a-lifetime experiences combine with a pampered treat after a wedding.
Without doubt one of the most seductive honeymoon destinations in the world, these shimmering atolls will have you dreaming of sailing our sparkling waters, enjoying heavenly spa treatments together, sublime sunset dinners, swimming with whale sharks, snorkelling over colourful coral reefs and diving some of the best underwater sites in the world. Your honeymoon can be as laid-back or adventurous as you like: we’re here to create a seamless and idyllic island experience for you.
An idyllic island escape
We look forward to welcoming you to our adventure-filled island paradise! With crystal clear lagoons and spectacular reefs to explore, candlelit dinners on pristine beaches and tropical jungles to discover, romantic honeymooners are spoilt for choice.
Honeymooners who were married within the past year (present your certificate when you arrive) will receive a complimentary bottle of sparkling wine and a fruit basket.
And there’s more: if you book a secluded Beach Pool Villas or Overwater Villa rooms directly via our website, you will be treated to a complimentary private dining experience for two right on the beach!
