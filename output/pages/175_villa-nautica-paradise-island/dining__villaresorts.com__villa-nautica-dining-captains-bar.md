Chic quayside lifestyle at its best: step off our jetty to a warm welcome at the Captain’s Bar. Your glamorous island holiday starts here and we’re always open for poolside cocktails or even a good coffee.
The Captain’s Bar buzzes at sundown, when the evening’s entertainment begins. Live bands and resident DJs host regular sessions so come and join us for an energetic night on the dance floor.
