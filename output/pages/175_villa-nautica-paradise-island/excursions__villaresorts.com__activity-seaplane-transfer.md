Experiences at Villa Park
Seaplane Transfer
Description
Arriving at Villa Park by seaplane transfer in the Maldives is more than a journey, it is your first unforgettable island moment. With the widest panoramic windows in the Maldives, a Villa Air flight turns the view into part of the experience. Look down on bright lagoons, coral reefs, and thin sandbanks as you glide towards South Ari Atoll.
In just 35 minutes from Velana International Airport (Malé), you land gently on the water and taxi across turquoise to Villa Park. No stress, no extra steps, just a scenic welcome from the sky. If you want the most iconic start to your Maldives resort holiday, this is it.
