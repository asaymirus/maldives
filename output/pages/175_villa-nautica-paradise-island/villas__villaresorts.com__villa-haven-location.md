Villa Haven is an exclusive retreat located in the South Ari Atoll, renowned for its vibrant marine life and white-sand beaches. After landing in the Maldives, you’ll be greeted at the airport and seamlessly transferred to a seaplane for a scenic 35-minute flight to our tropical paradise. Once there, the resort’s blend of modern luxury and Maldivian charm awaits, with colorful reefs, secluded sandbanks, and opportunities to encounter whale sharks just moments away.
