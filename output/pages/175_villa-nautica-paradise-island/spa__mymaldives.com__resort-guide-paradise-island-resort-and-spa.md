Approximately 20 minutes by speedboat from Male’, on the eastern edge of North Male’ Atoll, in a reef-enclosed lagoon, Paradise Island is almost 1 km in length while the width of the island is just 230 meters. This resort has absolutely everything for the sun worshipers.
