DESTINATION DINING EXPERIENCES AT VILLA HAVEN
Savor unforgettable dining with stunning Indian Ocean views. The degustation menu features fresh, local ingredients, artfully showcasing the island's rich flavors in every dish.
Indulge in extraordinary dining where simplicity meets sophistication. Whether it's a private picnic on a secluded sandbank or an intimate meal overlooking the Indian Ocean, each moment is curated to create unforgettable memories. Savor unique flavors crafted from the freshest local ingredients, elegantly presented to reflect the essence of island living.
Seaside soiree
Enjoy a private beach picnic with an elegantly arranged table overlooking the Indian Ocean. Guests savor island delicacies in a tranquil setting, where laughter and the gentle sound of waves create a perfect backdrop. Ideal for sharing with friends or an intimate partner, this experience encapsulates the essence of Maldivian paradise, crafting unforgettable moments in a breathtaking location.
Castaway gourmet experience
Enjoy a refined picnic on a private sandbank, presenting a selection of gourmet dishes crafted from the freshest local ingredients. With breathtaking waters and the soothing sounds of nature, it’s an idyllic setting that promises unforgettable moments and genuine connection.
Floating island breakfast
Start your day with a unique floating breakfast in your villa’s private pool. Delight in an array of delicious treats, from tropical fruits to freshly baked pastries, all beautifully presented on a floating tray. Enjoy the tranquility of morning moments surrounded by stunning views and the gentle sounds of nature.
Night under the palms
Experience an elegant outdoor dining adventure beneath the swaying palms. This enchanting evening features a tasting menu that celebrates the vibrant flavors of the island, crafted from fresh, seasonal ingredients. Each exquisite bite is complemented by the gentle breeze and soothing sounds of the ocean, creating a truly captivating atmosphere.
