This five star resort called Paradise Island Resort has the local name Lankanfinolhu. Paradise Island Resort and Spa, opened in 1994 is located in North Male’ Atoll, which is 9.6km from the Airport. Paradise Island Maldives is 1km long and 230 meters wide and has beach all around with an excellent marine life around.
Accommodation
Super Deluxe Beach Rooms
Set just behind the tree line facing the beach, the beach bungalows provide comfortable accommodation, when in Paradise . Paved walkways connect the rooms from the back to all other facilities at the resort. While some rooms are detached, others are in blocks of two. The white walls and ceilings provide for an airy and light atmosphere. Flowery patterns on the curtains and the bed spreads complements the colorful painting to add touches of color to the interiors.
The rooms are furnished with double or twin beds. Cushioned rattan sofas with a glass-topped coffee table provide comfortable seating if you decide to spend some time in the room, enjoying a drink from the mini-bar or the choice of viewing offered on the television. A spacious closet, dressing table with mirror and chair completes the picture.
