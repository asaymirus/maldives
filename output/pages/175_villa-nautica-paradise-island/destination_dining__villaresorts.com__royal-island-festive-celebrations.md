September in Baa Atoll can turn the sea into a feeding ground.

As monsoon currents push plankton into Hanifaru Bay, manta rays begin to gather in extraordinary numbers, and September is one of the strongest months to see it happen.

From Royal Island, our @diveoceanus team leads guided excursions to Hanifaru Bay, helping guests understand the conditions, the animals and how to experience the bay responsibly.

What makes it special is not just seeing mantas. It is watching an entire natural system come together in front of you.

If Hanifaru Bay is on your list, September is a very good time to be here.
