Luxury weddings and packages in the Maldives
Weddings are a once-in-a-lifetime experience. Why not celebrate your special day at Villa Nautica with a private ceremony on the beach?
Our experienced team offer two packages to choose from for weddings at Villa Nautica. You can both relax knowing that every detail will be taken care of, so that you can enjoy your special day together and celebrate with family and close friends. Most of our bridal couples get married officially at home but come to the Maldives to say their vows together on our romantic shores and spend their honeymoon here. Bespoke arrangements are coordinated for larger weddings.
Menu
Package A
Both our wedding packages include a private ceremony on the beach, followed by a romantic barbeque with music and local Boduberu dancers and drummers, who will also walk you to your beautiful arch of flowers on the shoreline.
The event is photographed by our wedding photographer and every couple is presented with a leather-bound photo album and with 50 photographs (USB) of their special event. Both include a bouquet of fresh flowers for the bride, a wedding cake and sparkling wine for a toast.
Package B
Our alternative package includes the above plus canapés and drinks for up to 25 guests. The bridal couple also enjoy additional pampering, including a special breakfast on the morning of the wedding plus fruit and sparkling wine, a relaxing ‘flower bath’, as well as a blissful Loabi-Loabi massage for couples.
Bespoke packages are created for weddings larger than 25 guests. You simply need to fill in the form below and we will be in touch. We look forward to planning your special day with you!
