Hook Restaurant, located at the end of the overwater jetty, serves fresh seafood with serene ocean views and if one of the best spots on the island for an intimate dinner.
Boasting mesmerizing sunrises over the Indian Ocean, Hook restaurant presents a memorable dining experience. Situated over the lagoon, indulge in an array of delectable seafood, including the Maldivian lobster.
