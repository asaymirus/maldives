Catering for those staying in our Beach Villas, open-air Maaniya restaurant serves a selection of globally-inspired dishes, from dawn until dusk.
Our beachfront restaurant has something to suit every taste, from continental breakfasts with freshly baked pastries, fruit platters and juices to locally caught seafood and traditional Maldivian curries. Menus change daily, with vegetarian, pescatarian and gluten-free dishes always available.
