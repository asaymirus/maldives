Overview
Property Location
Villa Nautica is located in North Malé Atoll, just 20 minutes from Velana International Airport by speedboat.
Rooms
Suites and villas feature private terraces and plunge pools, stunning sunset views, and everything guests could need to make the most of their time in paradise. Villas and suites are inspired by the sun-soaked island, featuring its perfect pearl-white shores and abundant tropical vegetation. Enjoy the unspoiled natural charm of desert island living, with the shimmering lights of Malé sparkling nearby.
Amenities
Experience innovative health and wellness ideas featuring traditional body and facial treatments, time-honored Ayurvedic healing practices, along with beauty services. Rediscover the essence of nature in the tropical garden refuge while guaranteeing guests indulgent enjoyment with every spa experience. Recreational facilities at the resort also include the Wavy Navy Kids Club.
Dining
Nothing is more unforgettable on a beach vacation than dining al fresco by the shore or trying a new type of food. At Villa Nautica, guests have a multitude of options, whether its a hands-on teppanyaki-style dinner, a seafood lunch on our classic Maldivian fishing boat, or a private barbecue beneath the stars with the air infused with the delightful fragrance of the flower gardens.
Business, Other Amenities
Villa Nautica is one of the largest meeting and conference venues in the Maldives and only a short speedboat ride from Malé. Villa Nautica has eight different indoor event spaces, each with excellent on-site amenities.
