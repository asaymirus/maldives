Excursions
Local island experience
2 hours
Description
Step beyond the resort and experience everyday life in the Maldives with a local island tour from Royal Island. Travel by boat to a nearby inhabited island in Baa Atoll, where you can stroll quiet streets, visit small shops and see traditional Maldivian homes framed by coconut palms and sandy paths.
During this cultural island experience in the Maldives, meet friendly locals, learn about island customs and taste simple Maldivian dishes prepared with home-grown ingredients. It is a gentle, authentic way to understand how Maldivians live, work and stay connected to the sea.
