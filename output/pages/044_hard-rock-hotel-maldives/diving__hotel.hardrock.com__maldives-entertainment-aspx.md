Equatic Vibe is the successful result of five childhood friends getting together to jam and combine their passion for music. Guitars, drums, bass, percussion instruments, keys and saxophone all make up the beautiful sounds of Equatic Vibe. Their most recent accomplishment includes releasing their debut album titled ‘Kulathah’, at a house-full show held at Olympus theater in the capital island. Produced by German musician and producer Chris Kling, Kulathah consists of seven original numbers. The songs range from upbeat, progressive rock to a jazzy, romantic tunes – a perfect mixture of the five individual musicians’ creative personalities. Kulathah became a hit overnight and the album is available on iTunes, Spotify, and various other platforms.
