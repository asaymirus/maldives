BABYSITTING SERVICE
Babysitting service is available for kids aged 7 months and above.
Certified by Sitter Train (Australia), our babysitters are skilled professionals with diverse nationalities and backgrounds.
The rate for babysitting is USD 30++ per child, per hour.
Babysitting service is subject to availability and requires a reservation prior to guests’ arrival.
Babysitting hours cannot be split.
Each babysitter is assigned to care for one child at a time only.
If babysitting happens during meal hours (12pm to 2pm & 7pm to 9pm), kids can enjoy complimentary meals from the Kids Menu or avail 50% discount from the regular menu. However, the babysitter's meal with a value of no more than USD 30++ will be charged to the guest's room. All meals are accessible at Sessions restaurant or via Room Service.
To avoid 50% cancellation charges, cancellations must be made at least 4 hours in advance. In the event of a no-show, 100% cancellation fee will apply.
*All rates mentioned above are subject to 10% service charge and prevailing TGST.
