Surfing
Image TabSlide1 Link to Larger Image surfer-on-wave-at-ocean-beach
Feel the pull of the swell at Hard Rock Hotel Maldives, with some of the Maldives' best surf points just a short boat ride away. Here are a few of the breaks nearby:
Jailbreaks:
The Maldives' broadest wave field, with rides up to 100 yards long.
Honky's:
A wave that grows as it wraps toward the island, delivering a long, fast ride.
Sultans:
One of the Maldives' best waves, with long, user-friendly walls for surfers of all levels.
Ninja's:
Three distinct takeoff spots depending on swell and tide, each offering a fast righthander.
Chickens:
A challenging left tube with three to six bowl sections, depending on swell.

Aerial Hammock
Image TabSlide1 Link to Larger Image A woman playing with aerial hammock
Aerial hammock - also known as sling, the hammock is a fabric based apparatus with a loop at the bottom. We will learn a variety of upright and inverted moves such as backstar, gazelle, arch and splits away. The hammock also offers good opportunities to stretch out your back and shoulders.
Aerial Silks
Image TabSlide1 Link to Larger Image A woman playing with aerial silks
Aerial silks are another fabric based apparatus similar to hammock but slightly more challenging for beginners. When you are comfortable with the basic skills on other apparatus we will start to learn different techniques for climbing and footlock positions.
Aerial Hoop/Lyra
Image TabSlide1 Link to Larger Image A lady doing aerobics on a hoop
Aeiral hoop - also known as lyra - is a hanging metal ring which can be used for static and dynamic acrobatic poses. We will learn different mounts and poses such as man in the moon, mermaid, and splits.
Aerial Trapeze
Image TabSlide1 Link to Larger Image A woman playing with aerial trapeze
Static Trapeze is a bar based apparatus attached with ropes. We can perform positions both above and below the the bar. We will focus on some standing and sitting moves on the bar.
