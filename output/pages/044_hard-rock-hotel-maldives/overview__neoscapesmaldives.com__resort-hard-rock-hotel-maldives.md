DESCRIPTION
A melody you cannot shake, and a tropical experience you will not forget.
Just 20 minutes by speedboat from Velana International Airport, Hard Rock Hotel Maldives is set in the scenic Emboodhoo Lagoon and directly connected to The Marina at CROSSROADS Maldives, a lifestyle hub with boutique shops, restaurants, bars, a beach club, and a marine discovery centre. The resort offers 178 beach villas, family suites and overwater villas that blend tropical architecture with contemporary design and authentic music memorabilia. Guests can enjoy signature Hard Rock experiences such as in-room Fender guitars, Crosley turntables, the Rock Spa, and a vibrant, music-infused atmosphere throughout their stay.

Dining
Sessions
Dive into a world of flavour at Sessions, where fresh local ingredients meet global inspiration. Watch the magic unfold in the open kitchen, with à la carte menus and daily specials. For an unforgettable experience, gather in the private Chef’s Studio, perfect for up to 12 guests.
The Elephant and The Butterfly
Inspired by the passionate love story of Frida Kahlo and Diego Rivera, this oceanfront restaurant blends bold Latin American flavours with delicate presentation. Enjoy ceviche, tiradito, and signature tableside guacamole alongside a curated wine list by our in-house sommelier. With panoramic tropical views and a vibrant, artistic atmosphere, it is a dining experience that celebrates both rustic charm and refined elegance.
Hard Rock Cafe Maldives
Located oceanfront at The Marina @ CROSSROADS, Hard Rock Cafe Maldives serves up classic American fare, legendary cocktails, and live music every night. Whether you’re here for a casual hangout or a private event for up to 190 guests, this is the ultimate spot to eat, meet, and rock out in true Hard Rock style.
The Pool Bar
Lounge, swim, and sip at our iconic pool bar, where a sparkling infinity pool flows into the Emboodhoo Lagoon. Enjoy underwater tunes, a fun waterslide, and a swim-up bar with stools. From Pan-Latin bites to a special kids’ menu, it’s the perfect spot to chill by day and vibe by night with the Indian Ocean as your backdrop.
Private Dining – Rhythm & Dine
Make it unforgettable with a private beachside dining experience tailored just for you. From curated set menus to BBQ-style feasts or fully customized dishes, we will create the perfect mood and menu for your special moment at Hard Rock Hotel Maldives. Available on request.
