Overview
Property Location
Located in the stunning Emboodhoo Lagoon within CROSSROADS Maldives, Hard Rock Hotel Maldives blends tropical island living with the brand’s signature music-inspired lifestyle. Accessible by speedboat from Velana International Airport, the resort enjoys a prime setting surrounded by turquoise waters, white sandy beaches, and lush island scenery. Guests also benefit from direct access to The Marina @ CROSSROADS Maldives, home to an array of dining, shopping, entertainment, and cultural experiences.
Rooms
Hard Rock Hotel Maldives offers a collection of guestrooms, family suites, beach villas, and overwater villas designed for comfort and relaxation. Inspired by local culture and tropical surroundings, accommodations feature private outdoor spaces, stylish interiors, and views of the lagoon, beach, or ocean. Selected villas offer direct beach or lagoon access, while others include private pools, creating a memorable island retreat for couples, families, and groups alike. The resort’s signature music-themed touches enhance the stay with a unique and immersive atmosphere.
Amenities
Guests can enjoy a wide range of leisure and wellness facilities throughout the resort. Highlights include the oceanfront Rock Spa®, Body Rock® fitness center, a large swimming pool, music-inspired experiences through The Sound of Your Stay® program, and the Rock Shop® retail outlet. Families are catered for with the Roxity Kids Club® and Teen Spirit® Club, while active travelers can take part in water sports, diving experiences, excursions, yoga sessions, wellness activities, beach games, and various recreational programs. The resort also offers easy access to marine and cultural attractions within CROSSROADS Maldives.
Dining
Dining at Hard Rock Hotel Maldives showcases a diverse selection of international flavors and vibrant culinary experiences. Guests can enjoy all-day dining at Sessions, beachfront Latin American-inspired cuisine at The Elephant and The Butterfly, and the lively atmosphere of Hard Rock Cafe Maldives. The pool bar provides refreshing drinks and light bites throughout the day. Beyond the resort, guests have access to an extensive collection of restaurants at SAii Lagoon Maldives and The Marina @ CROSSROADS Maldives, featuring cuisines ranging from Mediterranean, Asian, Thai, Chinese, Japanese, Indian, seafood, steakhouse, and authentic Maldivian specialties.
Other, Business Amenities
Hard Rock Hotel Maldives provides facilities suitable for weddings, social gatherings, and corporate events. Through access to venues at CROSSROADS Maldives, guests can host meetings, conferences, celebrations, and special occasions in both indoor and outdoor settings overlooking the lagoon and the Indian Ocean. Event services include catering, event planning support, audiovisual capabilities, and flexible venue arrangements. The resort also offers unique private dining experiences and destination wedding opportunities, creating memorable occasions in an island setting.
