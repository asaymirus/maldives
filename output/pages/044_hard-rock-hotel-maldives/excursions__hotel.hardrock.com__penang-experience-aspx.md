Memorabilia
From Eric Clapton’s donation to simply mark his seat in Hard Rock Cafe London, Hard Rock International has been the preeminent collector of music and entertainment memorabilia for over 45 years. Today, Hard Rock International is the world’s largest original curator of music memorabilia with the most far-reaching collection. More than 88,000 (and counting) rock ‘n’ roll icons, from a lock of hair to a five-ton psychedelic bus, from some of the most important guitars in music history to some of the most incredible outfits ever to grace a rock star or stage.
At Hard Rock Hotel Penang, more than 500 pieces of unparalleled rock n’ roll memorabilia from both local and international artists adorn the walls showcasing amazing pieces of music history that set the stage for a one-of-a-kind experience.
