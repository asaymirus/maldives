Is there an entry free for hotel guests to visit The Marina @ CROSSROADS?
Hard Rock Hotel Maldives is located 450 meters from The Marina @ CROSSROADS. Hotel guests enjoy direct and free access to The Marina via a footbridge. Find out more at
CROSSROADS Website

What is the check-in & check-out time?
Check-in is from 2.00 PM onwards and check-out is at 12noon. Early check-in and late check-out is subject to availability and may be chargeable.
