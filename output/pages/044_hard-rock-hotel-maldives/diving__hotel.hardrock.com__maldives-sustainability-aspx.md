Sustainability
CROSSROADS Maldives
Would you like to stay in an eco-friendly resort in Maldives? We ensure that sustainability values are exercised at every level, from recreational activities to environmental conservation and resort operations. CROSSROADS endeavors to become a model of sustainability for future projects across the world.
We recognize that the true lasting value of the Maldives lies its delicate ecosystem and the strength of its local communities. CROSSROADS is dedicated to introducing global sustainable tourism schemes to preserve the bio-diversity of the Maldives’ prestigious marine ecosystem and enhance the well-being of those local communities. We are deeply committed to enhancing the natural beauty of the Maldives whilst working to maintain and preserve the precious ecosystems through several comprehensive sustainability initiatives.
Hard Rock Hotel Maldives is proud to be Green Globe certified. Behind the scenes, our crew works tirelessly to ensure your stage is set with thoughtful practices that protect our oceans, conserve energy and water, and reduce waste. Thank you for standing with us as we create a more sustainable encore. If you are interested in knowing more about how you are making a difference, we will be pleased to connect you with our dedicated sustainability champions.
