Honeymoon packages apply when booking a minimum of 3 nights, traveling until February 02, 2026.
To make a reservation for the With Love Honeymoon Package at no cost, the newlyweds must present a valid marriage certificate within the first year of marriage. To receive the aforementioned amenities, the newlyweds must mention that they are honeymooners to be identified as such at the time of booking. The With Love Honeymoon Package at no cost cannot be requested upon arrival.
After checking in at the reception, the newlyweds must go to the Concierge desk, where they will be welcomed to make their prior reservations for romantic dinner, breakfast in the room, hydro-massage tub experience, aromatherapy, and massage. The romantic dinner takes place at the specialty restaurant of your choice.
Tours must be booked through the Vacation Planners desk. Tours are subject to change without notice. Honeymoon packages must be settled upon check-in.
All services must be scheduled with 24 hours' notice.
