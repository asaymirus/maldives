Memorabilia
Discover one of the most unique experiences at Hard Rock Hotel Pattaya with our complimentary Memorabilia Tour. Explore iconic music memorabilia, including legendary guitars, stage outfits, portraits, and rare collectibles from world-famous artists throughout the hotel.
The Hard Rock memorabilia story began in 1979 when Eric Clapton donated his guitar to reserve his favorite seat at the first Hard Rock Cafe in London — inspiring a global collection of music history that continues today.
Contact the Front Desk for complimentary Memorabilia Tour information.
Rock Wall
Take on the challenge at our 5-meter Rock Wall Climbing experience located by the pool area. Perfect for adventurous kids and teens, the climbing wall features one level of difficulty with trained instructors on hand for safety and guidance. Available for guests over 110 cm tall and children up to 15 years old.
