DESCRIPTION
Romance in Maldivian hues at our luxury resort.
Set within a pristine lagoon in South Malé Atoll, Anantara Veli Maldives Resort is an adults-only retreat created for couples seeking romance, relaxation, and unforgettable island experiences. Surrounded by crystal-clear waters and endless ocean views, the resort combines elegant overwater and pool villa accommodation with exceptional dining, holistic wellness, and exciting marine adventures.
Guests can spend their days snorkeling alongside manta rays and whale sharks, enjoying overwater spa rituals, surfing world-class waves, or simply relaxing in the privacy of their villa. With sophisticated dining venues, personalised experiences, and a tranquil atmosphere, Anantara Veli offers the perfect Maldivian escape for honeymoons, romantic getaways, and luxurious island living.

Dining
Cumin
Cumin offers a vibrant South Asian dining experience featuring Indian spices, Sri Lankan flavours, and Maldivian-inspired dishes prepared with fresh herbs and local ingredients.
Origami
Origami presents a contemporary Japanese culinary journey with handcrafted sushi, sashimi, robatayaki skewers, and live Teppanyaki experiences in a stylish setting.
Baan Huraa
Set in a traditional teak house over the lagoon, Baan Huraa serves authentic Thai cuisine inspired by flavours from across Thailand, creating a romantic overwater dining experience.
Dhoni Bar
Dhoni Bar is a relaxed poolside venue serving Mediterranean cuisine, Spanish tapas, smoothies, cocktails, and refreshing drinks throughout the day and evening.
Fushi Café
Fushi Café offers international buffet dining with live cooking stations, tropical fruits, freshly baked pastries, and themed dinners featuring flavours from around the world.
Sea. Fire. Salt.
Sea.Fire.Salt specialises in premium grilled seafood and steaks, serving freshly caught Maldivian fish, lobster, tuna, and imported cuts in an elegant oceanfront setting.
The Yellow Fin Club
An exclusive adults-only cocktail bar offering signature drinks, sunset views, and a sophisticated atmosphere overlooking the lagoon.
Aqua Bar
Aqua serves authentic Italian cuisine, snacks, and refreshing beverages beside the pool, complemented by endless ocean views and a laid-back tropical atmosphere.
Gulhifushi Picnic Island
This pnrivate recreation island offers kayaking, paddleboarding, tropical drinks, snacks, and laid-back island experiences surrounded by crystal-clear waters and natural ocean pools.
