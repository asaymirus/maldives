Resort Overview
This resort, located in the South Male Atoll, is ideal for romantic retreats and honeymooners. Guests enjoy both space and solitude as the resort is entirely made up of over-water bungalows. Anantara Veli offers a variety of water sports, from waterskiing and parasailing to personalized yacht trips. Cooking workshops, yoga and meditation sessions, and a one-of-a-kind surf school are all available. Guests at this Anantara resort enjoy the benefits of two resorts in one, since a sister hotel is located just across the lake.
Dine at the restaurants, use the 24 hour room service, or visit the neighboring resort Anantara Dhigu for a more exclusive dining experience. After a day of exploring the aquatic world and watching the sunset, visit the spa for some calming treatments. Anantara Veli is a quiet, romantic resort suited for couples looking for a romantic getaway.

GOLF STUDIO
Indulge in a selection of inviting beverage packages as you strike the winning shot and enjoy the ultimate indoor golf experience.
BEACH GAMES
Let the rhythm of island life guide your play. Spend sun-drenched afternoons on the soft white sands with a friendly game of Petanque, volleyball, or beach badminton. Simply share your preference, and the nets and equipment will be prepared, transforming a stretch of shoreline into your private court. Gentle competition, sea breezes, and laughter combine to create moments that linger long after the tide recedes.
ISLAND PICNIC
Live out your castaway dreams with a private island escape designed for two. Journey across teh lagoon to a secluded, uninhabited isle where white sands, turquoise water, and vibrant reefs await. Swim, snorkel, and wander at your leisure before indulging in a beautifully prepared picnic set beneath the palms. A day of barefoot freedom and blissful seclusion, the essence of Maldivian paradise.
SURFING
Ride the waves with confidence under the guidance of our Tropicsurf experts. Whether you are catching your first break or perfecting your barrel, the surrounding lagoon offers waves suited to every level.
Young adventures can also join the fun with specially designed surf sessions that build confidence and skill through playful water drills. Led by our Surf Insiders from Tropicsurf, these sessions turn the lagoon into a safe and exciting playground for all ages.
SAILING
Set off on a catamaran sailing adventure on a K1 Top Cat, with or without your own skipper.
PADDLEBOARDING
Glide gently across the lagoon's glassy surface and discover the serenity of stand-up paddleboarding, the perfect blend of balance, movement, and mindfulness. NO experience is needed; simply stand, steady yourself, and let the rhythm of the ocean guide your way. As you float over coral gardens and schools of fish, soak in panoramic views of the island and the golden hues of sunset, a tranquil workout and a moment of pure Maldivian magic.
WATERSKIING WAKE & KNEEBOARDING
Feel the rush of adrenaline as you skim across the lagoon behind our twin Gulhicraft Swan 20 speedboats, aptly named WET and WILD. Each powered by 150-horsepower engines, they promise smooth, exhilarating rides whether you are water skiing, wakeboarding or kneeboarding  through the turquoise expanse.
PARASAILING
Soar above the lagoon and experience the Maldives from an entirely new perspective. Drift through the open skies as the ocean sparkles beneath you, revealing a kaleidoscope of coral and marine life. Take off and land smoothly from the boat - no splash required - and savour the quiet thrill of weightless freedom. A breathtaking journey where serenity meets adventure, high above the turquoise horizon.
ELECTRIC SURFBOARD
Skim across the turquoise waters in a thrilling dance of balance and speed. Our electric surfboards let you glide smoothly over the lagoon's surface with no waves required. Feel the rush of freedom as you carve through crystal-clear waters, surrounded by endless blue and the serene beauty of the Maldives.
SNORKELING
Discover a vibrant underwater world beyond the shore. Snorkel through the turquoise lagoon that surrounds the resort or join a guided trip to reefs alive with colour and movement. Encounter graceful nurse sharks, watch sea turtles glide effortlessly by, and swim among shimmering schools of tropical fish in the crystal-clear Maldivian waters.
NURSE SHARK SNORKELING
Opt for a truly remarkable experience and join our experienced professionals on an adventure with nurse sharks.
CORAL ADOPTION
Beneath the surface lies a world as delicate as it is breathtaking. Though resilient, coral reefs are sensitive to even the slightest change in their environment.
Join the Coral Adoption Programme and take part in restoring this vibrant ecosystem alongside our resident Marine Biologist. Transplant living corals into the lagoon's shallow waters and return year after year to witness their growth. A small act of care that helps our ocean bloom, preserving its beauty for generations.
DISCOVER SCUBA DIVING
Explore the wonders of the deep with diving experiences tailored to every level. Discover vibrant coral reefs close to the resort or embark on half or full-day safaris by speedboat to more distant sites. Beginners can join our Discover Scuba Diving (DSD) programme, which includes a gentle introduction to theory, shallow-water practice in the lagoon and a guided dive of up to twelve metres around the house reef.
PADI CERTIFICATION
Begin a lifelong journey beneath the surface with PADI Open Water certification and gain the confidence to dive up to eighteen metres into a world of vibrant marine life.
Certified divers can continue their adventure with Advanced, Rescue or Dive Master courses, available privately or in small groups. Speciality options include underwater photography, buoyancy control, drift, enriched air and wreck diving. Courses are also available for junior divers aged ten and above.
DIVING SAFARIS
Journey beneath the waves and discover a world of vibrant wonder. Glide past flourishing coral gardens, graceful sea turtles, and swirling schools of fusiliers, with the chance to encounter reef sharks or spotted eagle rays in their natural habitat. Embark on half- or full-day safaris by speedboat, complete with seamless valet service and expert guidance from Aquafanatics' PADI 5-Star Dive Centre. With every dive, experience the Maldives from a new perspective, alive with colour, movement, and quiet awe.
YACHT RIVERIE
Revel in the splendor of the sea. Discover the Indian Ocean's most captivating wonders aboard Raaya, your very own private yacht. With two elegant ensuites and two additional cabins. Raaya hosts up to six guests and is ideal for both leisurely day trips and overnight charters.
Dive into vibrant coral gardens, reel in the day's prized catch, or simply unwind beneath blue skies. Whether you are on the way to your tropical home or roving the Indian Ocean in pursuit of adventure, comfort and luxury are always part of the journey.
