Centara Mirage Lagoon Maldives
Located in the heart of the breathtaking North Male Atoll, Centara Mirage Lagoon Maldives is an enchanting underwater world-themed paradise offering families boundless fun, activities, and unique bonding experiences, all delivered with the warm embrace of modern Thai hospitality.
Our fun-filled island retreat features modern facilities designed with family-centric activities in mind. These include a stunning outdoor water complex with multiple attractions, such as a swimming pool, kids’ pool, meandering lazy river, and water playground, making it an unforgettable destination for all ages. There's endless entertainment for the family with 145 beach and overwater villas, diverse dining options, an exciting kids’ club, and the acclaimed SPA Cenvaree.
Centara Mirage Lagoon Maldives is part of The Atollia by Centara Hotels & Resorts, a unique multi-island destination. Guests staying on The Atollia Meal Plans enjoy access to both Centara Mirage Lagoon and Centara Grand Lagoon, unlocking an expanded collection of dining experiences, immersive adventures, and exceptional leisure facilities across the two islands.
Stunning Accommodation Choices
At our family-friendly resort in the Maldives, we take pride in offering a diverse choice of stunning lagoon villas and other accommodation options that cater to our guests’ unique needs and desires. Choose from the serene Mirage Overwater Villa with Jacuzzi or the spacious Mirage Beachfront Room with Jacuzzi - each villa and room is designed with comfort and luxury in mind. We also offer interconnecting rooms, perfect for families who desire both togetherness and individual space. For those who prefer a closer connection with nature, our Mirage Beachfront Room with an open-air bath provides an exquisite blend of indoor and outdoor living for unparalleled relaxation and rejuvenation. No matter your choice, each of our 145 accommodation options offers breathtaking views of the lagoon or the pristine beachfront, allowing you to fully immerse yourself in the natural beauty of the Maldives.
Exciting Facilities for All-ages
The amenities at our family-friendly resort in the Maldives are carefully designed to ensure that every member of the family, from the youngest to the oldest, finds joy and entertainment. Our expansive outdoor water complex is a highlight, featuring a variety of attractions that promise endless fun. Let your little adventurers conquer the kids’ pool and water playground, or gather the whole family and enjoy the leisurely flow of the meandering lazy river. With its multiple thrilling slides, the water park adds an extra layer of excitement to your stay. Meanwhile, the Kids’ Club and Games Room is a haven of activities so that your little ones are always engaged and entertained.
Rejuvenation at SPA Cenvaree
For those seeking a more serene experience, our award-winning SPA Cenvaree is a sanctuary of peace and wellness. Indulge in a range of traditional and modern treatments designed to soothe both mind and body. For our younger guests, our Candy Spa is a welcome retreat after a long day in the water park. Children aged 4 – 11 years old can enjoy manicures, pedicures.
Exceptional Concierge Services
Our exceptional concierge service is always on hand to assist you with every detail of your stay. They will be at your beck and call - whether you're looking to explore the stunning underwater world of the Maldives through guided excursions or simply need help crafting the perfect itinerary for your family. Our team is dedicated to making your experience at Centara Mirage Lagoon Maldives as seamless and memorable as possible.
