Wave of Celebrations: 40 Years of Excellence and Beyond
As a single drop of water transforms into a breathtaking wave, so has Centara Hotels & Resorts continued to evolve throughout the past 40 years. From a single Bangkok hotel to a portfolio of 93 properties spanning 13 countries, Centara’s history is a wave of celebrations and remarkable achievements.
As a thank you for your enduring support, we're rolling out a Wave of Celebrations throughout the remainder of 2023. Stay tuned for tantalising offers, huge discounts, lucky draws, complimentary nights, special vouchers and more!
Our Heritage
From a single Bangkok hotel to a portfolio of 93 properties in 13 countries, Centara’s history is a wave of celebrations and achievements. Inspired by our heritage, driven by innovation, and fueled by the desire to create memories, we endeavour to be the leading global hospitality group of Thai origin. Across six distinctive brands, we deliver Thai-inspired experiences guided by authentic family values.
Where Families Are Connected
A family-run business deeply rooted in the Thai values of caring, sharing and a sense of belonging, Centara specialises in unforgettable family experiences. With vibrant hotels and themed resorts offering family-friendly accommodations and immersive entertainment for all ages, including kids' clubs, family lounges, adventure parks and world-class dining options, Centara is the Place to Be for family travel.
Committed to a Greener Tomorrow
We are dedicated to sustainable practices and supporting our local communities. As part of our commitment to a greener tomorrow, we aim to eliminate single-use plastic and earn Global Sustainable Tourism Council (GSTC) certification for our properties by 2025; reduce energy, waste, water and greenhouse gas emissions by 20% by 2030; and achieve overall net zero emissions by 2050.
The Future is Bright
With the Centara wave gaining momentum, our goal is to become the world’s top 100 hotel operators by 2027. With a myriad of new projects in the pipeline across Asia, the Middle East, and beyond, we remain at the forefront of hospitality, enhancing our ever-growing range of guest benefits through exclusive partnerships with airlines, booking companies, and more – ensuring Centara is truly the place to be.

The Journey Begins
In 1983, the Central Group of companies formed Central Hotels &amp; Resorts and opened its first 5-star property. Since renamed as “Centara Grand at Central Plaza Ladprao Bangkok”, it is now seen as one of Bangkok’s premier hotels for MICE facilities.
