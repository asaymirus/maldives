As the festive season approaches,
Centara Hotels & Resorts
, Thailand's leading hotel operator, invites guests to revel in the spirit of Christmas and welcome in the New Year with a series of exquisite dining experiences and unforgettable celebrations across its world-renowned Centara Grand properties. From rooftop fireworks displays to decadent beachside buffets, Thailand’s strongest brand as named by Brand Finance’s Thailand 50 2023, is offering a spectacular finale to its incredible wave of 40th year anniversary festivities.
Centara Grand at CentralWorld
Experience the ultimate festive indulgence at Ventisi Restaurant with Christmas Eve dinner and Christmas Day lunch buffets each only THB 1999++ for adults or THB 999++ for children aged 6-11. New Year celebrations abound as six breathtaking rooftop venues, including famed CRU Champagne Bar and Red Sky Bar, are poised to offer gourmet dinners, exclusive beverage packages, live performances, and a dazzling fireworks display at midnight.
For bookings and more information, call 02-100-6255 or email
[email protected]
.
Centara Grand at Central Plaza Ladprao Bangkok
Revel in stunning rooftop views at Blue Sky restaurant with an indulgent Christmas Eve Great Celebration Dinner for only THB 1,699++ per person, or opt for the Drinks package for THB 999++ per person, including an alcoholic beverage of choice and delectable snack. Then, join the Countdown Party on 31st December for THB 1,699++ per person, inclusive of a glass of sparkling wine, while those looking to elevate their evening can partake in a luxurious 6-course dinner with 2 glasses of bubbles for just THB 5,999++ per person. Bookings made before 15th December 2023 also enjoy a 10% discount on food.

Centara Grand Beach Resort & Villas Hua Hin
Welcome the New Year in style at the 'Best of British' Gala Dinner on 31st December 2023, featuring an outdoor BBQ buffet, live band and entertainment, plus beachfront fireworks for THB 7,500++ per person. Reservations can be made via email:
[email protected]
or Tel: 032 512 021
Centara Grand Mirage Beach Resort Pattaya
Indulge in a glittering Christmas Eve Dinner Buffet at Coast Beach Club & Bistro, featuring an array of traditional delights and live cooking stations. The evening includes family fun with a live band trio, activities for kids, and a visit from Santa. On Christmas Day, indulge in a lavish Brunch Buffet with festive dishes, fresh seafood, and live music. For an unforgettable New Year's Eve, join the 'Casino Royale' extravaganza featuring an opulent International buffet, live entertainment, games, lucky draws, and more. Book by 20th December 2023 and enjoy a 15% discount.

Centara Grand Beach Resort Phuket
Experience a magical Christmas Eve at Cove Restaurant with a festive buffet dinner on the cosy lawn featuring holiday favourites and fresh seafood. Enjoy live music, games, carols, and a visit from Santa with prices starting at THB 2,990 net per adult and THB 1,490 net per child (6-12 years); free for children under 5. For guests with Half board, full board, or all-inclusive packages, dinner is only THB 2,000 net per person.
For an unforgettable New Year's Eve, the resort's Spectacular Millennium Circus Night promises awe-inspiring entertainment, a gourmet buffet, beachside fireworks, and an after-party with DJ. Adults are THB 11,000 net with kids just THB 5,500 net. A selection of sparkling wine and champagne is also available.
