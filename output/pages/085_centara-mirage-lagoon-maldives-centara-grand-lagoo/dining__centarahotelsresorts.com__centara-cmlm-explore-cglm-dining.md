# Two Islands. One Destination. A World of Elevated Dining. | Centara

Scraped: 2026-10-04
Resort: Centara Mirage Lagoon Maldives & Centara Grand Lagoon Maldives
Page type: dining

---

As part of the Atollia meal plans, guests at Centara Mirage Lagoon Maldives are invited to expand their culinary journey with exclusive access to the sophisticated dining experiences at Centara Grand Lagoon Maldives. From vibrant interactive dining to refined beachfront indulgence, guests can savour a more elevated and diverse collection of flavours across both island resorts.
Discover The Gallery, where flame tandoors, teppan grills, and an energetic selection of Asian live cooking stations create a dynamic all-day dining experience rich in flavour and theatre. At Bluefin, Mediterranean coastal dining meets relaxed beachfront elegance, with fresh seafood, refined dishes, and laid-back sophistication inspired by the sea. For sunset moments and elevated island evenings, Sunset Social offers an overwater setting perfect for Champagne, crafted cocktails, and panoramic Indian Ocean views. Meanwhile, Coco Drift brings a relaxed poolside atmosphere with refreshing cocktails, light bites, and effortless tropical indulgence beneath the Maldivian sun.
Together, Centara Mirage Lagoon Maldives and Centara Grand Lagoon Maldives offer a seamless culinary journey where vibrant family dining and elevated gastronomic experiences come together across two extraordinary island escapes.
