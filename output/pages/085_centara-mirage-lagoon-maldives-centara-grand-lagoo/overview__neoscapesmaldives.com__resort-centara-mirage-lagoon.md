DESCRIPTION
Every splash begins a new adventure.
Located in the heart of North Malé Atoll, Centara Mirage Lagoon Maldives is a vibrant underwater world-themed paradise made for families seeking fun, connection, and discovery. This lively island blends adventure and relaxation through the warmth of modern Thai hospitality. Guests can dive into endless excitement at the outdoor water complex featuring pools, slides, a lazy river, and a water playground, while little ones delight in the kids’ club and ice cream parlour. The resort offers 145 beach and overwater villas with Jacuzzis, plunge pools, or outdoor baths, along with interconnecting and accessible options for ultimate family comfort. Rejuvenate at the award-winning SPA Cenvaree or the playful Candy Spa for kids, then savour global flavours across five unique dining venues. As part of The Atollia by Centara, guests enjoy access to two connected islands filled with wonder, dining, and relaxation.

Dining
The Sailhouse
A breezy beachfront restaurant offering both indoor and alfresco dining, The Sailhouse serves a curated selection of international favourites complemented by interactive live cooking stations. Perfect for relaxed family moments, it is the ideal spot for leisurely breakfasts, laid-back lunches, and memorable dinners by the sea.
Acqua
Step into the heart of Italy at Acqua, a contemporary dining haven inspired by authentic osteria charm. Featuring an open kitchen with a pasta bar and pizza oven, it offers a delectable selection of à la carte dishes, fresh seafood, and generous family-style sharing platters, perfect for creating warm, memorable meals together.
Suan Bua
Experience Centara’s signature Thai restaurant, Suan Bua, where contemporary flair meets authentic flavours. Led by skilled Thai chefs, it offers a curated à la carte menu and a lively open kitchen with a cooking studio, complemented by a vibrant rum bar for a truly unforgettable dining experience.
Rum Bar
Set on the beach, Rum Bar is a relaxed tropical escape offering Thai beers, wines, and a vibrant selection of tropical-inspired cocktails. Perfect for sunset views or a leisurely break from the sun, it is the ideal spot to unwind and soak in the island vibe.
Dolphin Bar
Dolphin Bar is a playful poolside retreat where guests can enjoy refreshing cocktails and tasty light bites. The perfect spot to relax, stay cool, and soak up the sun while enjoying the vibrant island atmosphere.
Scoops
Scoops is a whimsical ice cream bar serving a delightful variety of flavours. This playful parlour offers the perfect sweet escape, making every visit a fun and indulgent treat for the whole family.
In-Room Dining
Enjoy a curated selection of international dishes from the comfort and privacy of your villa. In-Room Dining is available 24 hours, allowing you to savour delicious meals at any time, at your own pace.

Highlights From The Resort
Family Fun & Adventure
Centara Mirage Lagoon Maldives is a vibrant, underwater world-themed paradise perfect for families. Its outdoor water complex, lazy river, water playground, and slides provide endless entertainment for guests of all ages.
Relaxation & Wellness
Unwind at the award-winning SPA Cenvaree, offering traditional Thai and wellness treatments. Young guests can enjoy the playful Candy Spa for a family-friendly pampering experience.
Dining Experiences
Savour international cuisine at The Sailhouse, Italian favourites at Acqua, and authentic Thai dishes at Suan Bua. Enjoy cocktails and light bites at Rum Bar and Dolphin Bar or indulge in whimsical ice cream flavours at Scoops. In-Room Dining is available 24 hours.
The Atollia by Centara
Guests enjoy access to both Centara Mirage Lagoon and Centara Grand Lagoon, blending lively family activities with tranquil luxury. Choose from 145 beach and overwater villas, thrilling experiences, and curated culinary delights across both islands.
Special Experiences
Float away with a private pool breakfast or celebrate the festive season with vibrant decorations, family activities, and curated holiday experiences.
