Phuket, known for its stunning beaches and vibrant nightlife, is also home to some of the most breathtaking sunset views in Thailand. The spectacular sunset over the Andaman Sea, a breathtaking display of pink, orange, and purple, is unmatched in beauty. Watching the sunset in Phuket is a rite of passage, a moment of pure wonder that never gets old, no matter how many times you have witnessed it. Phuket's western coastline reveals some of the most spectacular sunset viewing spots in all of Thailand. Here are the top sunset spots in Phuket that should be on your bucket list:
Top Sunset Spots In Phuket
Promthep Cape
Perched dramatically at the island's southernmost tip, Promthep Cape is undoubtedly one of the most famous sunset spots in Phuket. This iconic clifftop viewpoint offers panoramic views that draw sunset chasers from around the globe to witness the sun's daily farewell performance across the Andaman Sea. For the best experience, arrive early to grab a good spot and enjoy the vibrant colors that transform the sky.
Nai Harn Beach
Nai Harn Beach is a hidden gem for a sunset spot in Phuket. For those seeking a more secluded experience, with less foot traffic and a more relaxed vibe, these pristine sands offer an intimate setting where the golden orb appears to melt directly into the turquoise waters below. This pristine beach, tucked away on Phuket’s southern coast, provides the perfect backdrop for a romantic sunset.
Windmill Hill Viewpoint
Windmill Hill Viewpoint, also known as the "Phuket Windmill Viewpoint” is a popular destination for watching sunset in Phuket among tourists and photographers. This elevated vantage point offers a panoramic canvas of the entire western coast, offering sweeping views that stretch as far as the eye can see, where you can take in stunning views of Nai Harn Beach, Ya Nui Beach, and the Andaman Sea.
Laem Singh Beach
Tucked away between Kamala and Surin Beach, Laem Singh Beach is a hidden sanctuary where time seems to pause as day transforms into night. A short walk through a narrow path will lead you to this untouched stretch of coastline, offering an intimate sunset experience. With its soft, golden sand and crystal-clear waters, Laem Singh Beach is the perfect spot to unwind and witness one of Phuket's most serene and picturesque sunsets.
Karon Beach
Karon Beach, a long and beloved west coast beach in Phuket, offers expansive soft sands and pristine waters—an idyllic spot for stunning sunsets. The vibrant beachfront boasts numerous cafes, restaurants, and bars, perfect for unwinding as the sun sets. Its lively atmosphere and open spaces make it ideal for both energetic sunset seekers and families.

Pink Social
If you like your sunsets with a side of style, Pink Social is the place to be. Koh Kool
Speaking of swim-up bars, Koh Kool might just be the highlight for a glorious sunset session on Karon Beach. There’s something undeniably indulgent about lounging in the water with a tropical cocktail in hand while the sky puts on its evening show. Koh Kool’s selection of light bites and chilled drinks makes it easy to linger until the stars start to appear. The relaxed ambiance is ideal for those who believe sunsets are best enjoyed with toes in the pool and worries left far behind.
Splash Bar
If you think sunsets are better with a side of mischief, Splash brings the energy. Located at the base of The Terraces tower, this in-pool hangout keeps the good times rolling with a menu of frosty drinks and snacks that hit the spot. In between laughter with friends, making new ones, and watching the colours dance across the sky, Splash turns the evening into a celebration. A swanky poolside playground serving modern Italian delights and flame-kissed goodies, Escape Pool Club is a dream come true for anyone hankering for bubbling pizzas or sizzling meats straight off the grill.
