Meal Plans
Immerse in Tranquillity
Design your own travel experience with a choice of meal plans and activities to fully unwind and recharge.
Half Board+
Indulge in the freedom of choice with our Half Board Meal Plan at Centara Mirage Lagoon Maldives. Start your day with a delectable international breakfast at The Sailhouse, then savour the evening with dinner at your preferred venue. Options include the diverse buffet at The Sailhouse, the authentic flavors of Suan Bua Thai restaurant, or homestyle Italian at Acqua each offering a tantalising 3-course à la carte menu. Plus, enjoy two selected drinks per person, catering to both alcohol and non-alcohol preferences.

Full Board+
Live Life To The Fullest
Embrace a carefree escape with a lavish breakfast spread, followed by leisurely lunch and dinner buffets at The Sailhouse. Alternatively, explore set menu options in the afternoon and evening at Suan Bua, Acqua. This meal plan includes two selected drinks per person, ranging from non-alcoholic to alcoholic beverages.

The Atollia Destination Half Board +
Double the Delight, Double the Choice
Experience the best of both worlds with international breakfast at either Centara Mirage Lagoon Maldives or neighbouring Centara Grand Lagoon Maldives. As evening arrives, embark on a culinary adventure across both resorts, where international buffets and indulgent 3-course à la carte meals await. From fragrant Thai curries at Suan Bua to fresh Mediterranean fare at Bluefin, the choice is yours. Enjoy two selected drinks per person and revel in Maldivian paradise.

The Atollia Destination Full Board +
A Taste of Two Islands
The ultimate dining experience awaits with this Full Board+ Meal Plan. Start your day with an international breakfast before exploring the beauty and activities available at both resorts. For lunch and dinner, try The Sailhouse's buffet, Thai cuisine at Suan Bua, or classic Italian dishes at Acqua in Centara Mirage, or choose the buffet at Mediterranean at Bluefin in Centara Grand. Each venue offers a sumptuous 3-course menu, accompanied by two selected drinks per person.

Unlimited Bliss
Immerse yourself in Atollia's culinary wonders with daily breakfast spreads, tempting lunch and dinner options, including both buffets and exquisite à la carte menus.
