An unforgettable honeymoon in the Maldives
The magical island chain of the Maldives is one of the most beloved destinations for newlyweds looking for an exotic and romantic honeymoon. The Maldives is a serene tropical paradise with over a thousand coral islands surrounded by sparkling blue waters and breath-taking scenery. It’s the perfect place to bring your sweetheart on a honeymoon. You can relax in your own private villa, watch the sunset over an endless blue horizon, enjoy delicious fine dining, see the wondrous sights, and create meaningful memories together.
Located on a private island in the Maldives South Ari Atoll,
Machchafushi Island Resort & Spa Maldives, The Centara Collection
is there to grant you and your new spouse with a dream honeymoon. You will be warmly welcomed by the staff who will be at your service whenever you need them.
While you are both being captivated by the beauty of the island, don’t forget to also have a look at the different activities provided at the resort. Aromatic spa treatments and massages, sunset cruises, and fine cuisine are all readily available. To make sure you don’t miss out on any romance or fun, here are some of the most popular activities to enjoy during your honeymoon.
Spa
Most of the packages provided by the resort include a 30-minute daily spa treatment with a variety of choices, including a facial treatment, Thai massage, and foot massage. The highly-skilled therapists will tailor their treatments to your specific needs. In addition to the daily spa treatment, there are many other treatments and massages on offer to relax and revitalise you in mind, body, and spirit.
Scuba Diving
The resort is home to its own PADI dive centre, where a team of experts is waiting to coach beginners and help experienced divers further improve their skills. Extraordinarily clear water gives you the opportunity to get a glimpse of the fascinating coral reefs and exotic marine life unique to the Maldives. You can also visit the sunken ship in front of the island.
Water Sports
The resort offers a variety of water sports that can be enjoyed by people of all ages and preferences. These include windsurfing, sailing, snorkelling, canoeing, and pedal boating. These non-motorised water sports are included in the Ultimate All-Inclusive programme, while other sports like wakeboarding are available for a fee.
Daily Activities and Cruises
In addition to the water sports, there are many other activities that can be enjoyed on the island, such as tennis and cooking classes. In the evenings, you will get to see a traditional Maldivian dance performance and play traditional bar games.
Daily tours include a snorkelling tour, a traditional dhoni cruise, a sunset cruise, a fishing trip, and more.
Snorkelling is a great way to become familiar with a range of exotic sea animals. You’ll be astonished by all the colours of the different species of fish. Dolphins and whale sharks can also be seen on the whale shark trip. If you’re fortunate enough, you’ll even get to swim amongst these majestic and gentle sea giants.
With your sweetheart right next to you, the traditional dhoni cruise through the serene waters that surround the island while the sun sets in the evening is a must see. Watch the sunset while you indulge in a glass of champagne and the delectable canapés. The dhoni then travels to a nearby island, where local villagers live traditionally. There you can shop for handicrafts, clothes, and other souvenirs.
You can also go on a fishing tour. If you’re lucky enough to catch a fish, the chefs will happily prepare it for your dinner.
With all of this and more to discover during your dream honeymoon in the Maldives, you and your new spouse will be sure to create fond memories that will last throughout your lifetime together.
