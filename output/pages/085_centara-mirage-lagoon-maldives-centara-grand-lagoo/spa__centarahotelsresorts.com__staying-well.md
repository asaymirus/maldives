Learn How Health And Wellness Can be Part of Your Stay With Us
A holiday or business trip doesn’t have to be a break from health. Centara now gives you more ways to take care of yourself during your stay with us.
Holistic wellness is essential to the Centara Hotels & Resorts guest experience. Our Stay Well programme includes tools to help you exercise more conveniently, sleep more comfortably, and eat more healthily. It’s more than just a fitness centre. It includes exercise aids in your room, comfortable pillows and sleepwear, special menus to choose from, and more.
Live
Well
We make it easy to LIVE a better life
Centara Hotels & Resorts makes it easier to stay in shape, both mentally and physically, with our holistic wellness experiences. In addition to the fitness centre, you can select from a range of activities during your vacation in Thailand, such as joining a jog or a beach yoga retreat. Prefer to exercise in your room? We’ll lend you equipment like mats, foam rollers, mini-weights or power bands. Forgot your workout clothes? We’ll provide you with a fresh set. Tune in to the wellness channel on your TV or check out a DVD to learn about exercise, language, cooking and other interests.

Information & menu selection to help you eat right
At Centara’s wellness retreats in Thailand,  you will find nutritional information about the dishes we offer in our restaurant and room service menus. For breakfast, you can also order diabetic, healthy-heart, gluten-free, low sodium, reduced calorie, or vegetarian options.
