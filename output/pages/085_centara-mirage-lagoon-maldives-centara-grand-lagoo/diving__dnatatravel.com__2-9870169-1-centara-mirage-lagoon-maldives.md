Overview
Property Location
Situated in the heart of the breathtaking North Male Atoll, Centara Mirage Lagoon Maldives is an enchanting underwater world-themed paradise offering families endless fun, activities, and unique bonding experiences, all delivered through the warm embrace of modern Thai hospitality. This lively island retreat features contemporary facilities designed with families in mind, including a stunning outdoor water complex with multiple attractions such as a swimming pool, children’s pool, lazy river, and water playground—making it an unforgettable destination for guests of all ages.
As a multi-island destination wonderland, it provides wholly distinct yet seamlessly connected travel experiences centred around its mesmerising Indian Ocean lagoon. Visitors enjoy unparalleled access to two captivating Centara properties: the elegant Centara Grand Lagoon Resort Maldives, a sophisticated and tranquil sanctuary offering exclusive and immersive experiences; and the vibrant Centara Mirage Lagoon Resort Maldives, a family-focused underwater world-themed haven. Within this united island sanctuary, guests can explore a rich tapestry of culinary delights, exciting activities, and unparalleled relaxation, effortlessly intertwined with the stunning beauty of the Maldives and Centara’s signature modern Thai hospitality.
Rooms
Guests can choose from 145 thoughtfully designed rooms ranging from 45 to 79 sqm, all crafted with family comfort in mind. The accommodation options include the Beachfront Room, Overwater Villa, Overwater Pool Villa, and Beach House with Patio.
Amenities
This lively island retreat boasts modern facilities tailored to family-centric activities. Highlights include a magnificent outdoor water complex featuring multiple attractions such as a swimming pool, children’s pool, lazy river, and water playground—ensuring an unforgettable experience for visitors of all ages. For those seeking a more tranquil escape, the award-winning SPA Cenvaree offers a sanctuary of peace and wellness, where guests can indulge in a range of traditional and contemporary treatments designed to soothe mind and body.
Dining
A journey of flavours and culinary delights awaits at Centara Mirage Lagoon Maldives. Each day begins at The Sailhouse, a vibrant all-day dining venue offering an array of international favourites, indulgent buffets, and bespoke creations to tantalise the palate.
For authentic osteria charm, guests can visit Acqua, where sumptuous seafood and Italian family-style sharing platters beckon, or immerse themselves in Thai cuisine at Suan Bua, a haven for innovative flavours.
Guests can splash the day away at Dolphin Bar, a poolside retreat serving refreshing drinks and tempting bites, before enjoying a sweet treat at the whimsical Scoops ice cream parlour. For a genuine tropical experience, Rum Bar offers a laid-back escape with Thai beers, wines, and a vibrant selection of tropical-themed cocktails.
Business, Other Amenities
Kids Club and Games Room are designed to spark imagination and create cherished memories through a variety of engaging activities. From arts and crafts, board games, movies, and playful learning, to many more delights, there is something to captivate younger guests of all ages.
