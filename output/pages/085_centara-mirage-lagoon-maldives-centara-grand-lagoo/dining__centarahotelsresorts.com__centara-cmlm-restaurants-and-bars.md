Your Culinary Journey
A journey of flavour and culinary delights awaits at Centara Mirage Lagoon Maldives. Begin each day at The Sailhouse, a vibrant all-day dining destination offering an array of international favourites, indulgent buffets, and bespoke creations that will tantalise your tastebuds.
For authentic osteria charm, head to Acqua, where sumptuous seafood and Italian family-style sharing platters beckon, or embrace the essence of Thai cuisine at Suan Bua, a haven for innovative flavours.
