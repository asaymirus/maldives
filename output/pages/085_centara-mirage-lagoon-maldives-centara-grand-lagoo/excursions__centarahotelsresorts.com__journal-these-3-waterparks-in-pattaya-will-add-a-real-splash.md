Tropical Thailand can certainly deliver some sizzling temperatures, making a nice swim feel even more refreshing. Waterparks in Pattaya are always a fantastic way for people to cool down, but the ones here have taken it to the next level.
Join us for a wet and wild adventure and bring your friends and family along! These waterparks in Pattaya are full of fun activities and unique entertainment zones, giving you plenty to explore and enough variety for anyone to enjoy the best water activities in Pattaya.
This list will cover three very special waterparks in Pattaya: Columbia Pictures Aquaverse, Ramayana Waterpark, and The Lost World.
Let’s begin!
Columbia Pictures Aquaverse
Price: 1,380-1,595 THB
Opening hours: 10:00 - 18:00 (Closed Wednesdays)
The world's first Columbia Pictures fully branded water and theme park is a hugely popular attraction and considered one of the best waterparks in Pattaya, Thailand by many visitors. This exciting Pattaya aqua park has replaced the former Cartoon Network water park in Pattaya, bringing beloved movie characters to life in an immersive aquatic experience.
Columbia Pictures Aquaverse features state-of-the-art rides and newly themed attractions that bring to life treasured characters from some of the most successful and beloved movies from Hollywood, including Bad Boys, Hotel Transylvania, Jumanji, Ghostbusters, Cloudy with a Chance of Meatballs, and Zombieland. This famous waterpark offers over 100 exciting attractions and up to nine thrilling water slides, making it the largest water slide collection in Southeast Asia.
The park provides top-quality service on all of its rides and other practical matters. From locker and towel rental, to free WiFi, to food and drink booths, to lifeguards and medical aid available throughout the park – everything is taken care of, so you can focus entirely on having fun with the whole family at this premier Pattaya aqua park.
Unique features include an outdoor water stage equipped with giant LEDs and a Dolby sound system, a wave pool surrounded by an Omnitheatre where you can watch Hollywood movies, and exciting go-kart racing through Miami-themed streets. The park also features Surfers' Paradise, where you can learn to surf and perform tricks on huge waves.
Since opening in October 2022 after its rebranding from the Cartoon Network waterpark in Pattaya, Columbia Pictures Aquaverse has quickly established itself as a new entertainment destination for Thailand and visitors from around the world, earning recognition as one of the top water activities in Pattaya.
Ramayana Waterpark
Price: 1,190 THB
Opening hours: 10:00 - 18:00
The biggest waterpark in Pattaya – and all of Thailand – is Ramayana Water Park. This massive Pattaya waterpark covers over 100 rai of land (nearly 40 acres), contains over 50 premium water rides and attractions, and employs over 350 highly trained personnel, including over 100 lifeguards.
The ideal location for a stress-free holiday adventure, Ramayana adds a local flourish to the setting by incorporating Thai cultural and mythical themes into its design. Yet the main attractions are impressive all by themselves, as they include a wave pool with a 160-metre beach, a lazy river that goes over 500 meters long, children's playgrounds, and a separate swimming pool for sports, as well as a creative obstacle course. This Pattaya water park in Thailand imports its waterslides from Canada, meeting international standards for product safety.
The waterpark has food and drink booths, as well as entertainment areas available for people of all ages, including a special sunbed area for relaxation. Those seeking diverse water activities in Pattaya will find plenty to keep them entertained here.
Ramayana is committed to supporting the community and preserving the natural environment. The waterpark makes a real effort to support blind children, both financially and emotionally. Ramayana also maintains a commitment to avoid waste and source 50% of its power through renewable energy sources.
Ramayana Waterpark was ranked 1st in Thailand, 2nd in Asia, and 12th in the world among waterparks in TripAdvisor’s 2019 Travellers’ Choice category, solidifying its reputation as one of the best waterparks in Pattaya for many visitors.
The Lost World
Price: 1,000 THB
Opening hours: 09:00 - 18:00
Perhaps the most convenient option for thrill-seekers of all ages is The Lost World, located right along the beach at Centara Grand Mirage Beach Resort Pattaya. With a dozen waterslides for excitement, a ‘lazy river’ for peaceful flotation, as well as several waterfalls, pools, and Jacuzzis, this onsite Pattaya waterpark offers plenty of fun just steps away from the main Centara rooms and other guest services.
The kids can enjoy ‘Monsoon Island’ and play with the water guns and miniature geysers to their hearts’ content. Meanwhile, parents can take a well-deserved breather by relaxing at the adults-only pool. Visitors of all ages will enjoy the 230-metre beach and a choice of water sports that include kayaking and several other options, making it perfect for families looking for convenient water activities in Pattaya.
Fully monitored and entirely safe, The Lost World delivers plenty of fun, without ever having to leave the Centara resort property. This Pattaya aqua park is ideal for resort guests who want easy access to aquatic entertainment.
From heart-pumping slides to lazy river relaxation, these waterparks have everything you need to beat the heat and create amazing memories. Don't forget your SPF and water shoes – adventure awaits, but comfort comes first!
