DESCRIPTION
An art-immersive island where sustainable glamour meets creative living.
Located on Muravandhoo Island in the pristine Raa Atoll, JOALI Maldives is a secluded luxury resort surrounded by white-sand beaches, turquoise lagoons and abundant marine life. What makes JOALI unique is its seamless fusion of art, design and sustainability, where curated installations, bespoke villas and personalised Jadugar service turn every stay into a sensory journey. Recognised by the MICHELIN Guide and Forbes Travel Guide, JOALI redefines Maldivian luxury through creativity, joy and meaningful experiences.

Dining
Vandhoo
Vandhoo is the resort’s all-day dining venue, offering a refined selection of Southeast Asian, Chinese, Indian and Mediterranean cuisines. Guests can enjoy vibrant breakfasts, themed evenings and art-immersive dining experiences. Fresh local seafood and a worldclass wine cellar elevate every meal, whether dining indoors or amidst lush outdoor surroundings.
Her Kitchen
Her Kitchen offers a personalised culinary experience where guests cook alongside expert chefs. Learn traditional Maldivian and international recipes in a professional kitchen environment, combining creativity with the joy of shared meals. Ideal for food lovers seeking engaging and memorable experiences.
Saoke
Designed by renowned architect Noriyoshi Muramatsu, Saoke is JOALI’s signature Japanese restaurant set above the lagoon. The menu features sushi, sashimi, robata and interactive teppanyaki, complemented by curated sake pairings. Sunset views and refined design create an exceptional dining experience.
Bellinis
Bellinis brings the spirit of Italy to the Maldives with an elegant fine-dining experience. From handcrafted pastas and traditional recipes to signature Bellinis and homemade limoncello, the restaurant celebrates Italian flavour and indulgence. Stylish interiors and alfresco seating enhance the Mediterranean ambience.
Mura Bar
Overlooking the pool and beach, Mura Bar is the island’s social hub. Inspired by global flavours, it serves street food favourites, light bites and crafted cocktails. As the sun sets, guests gather for live performances, sunset rituals and Champagne O’Clock celebrations.
Lajoie
This artisanal chocolate and ice cream boutique delights guests of all ages. Featuring handcrafted flavours from rich dark chocolate to refreshing fruit sorbets, Lajoie is a charming stop for sweet indulgence throughout the day.
Destination Dining
Destination Dining allows guests to enjoy gourmet restaurant experiences within the privacy of their villa. From beachfront barbecues to intimate candlelit dinners, each experience is tailored for comfort, romance or celebration.
TUH’H
Set alfresco on the sand beneath softly lit palms, TUH’U showcases authentic Levant cuisine inspired by ancient traditions. Rich flavours, warm hospitality and a beachfront setting create a deeply immersive dining experience rooted in culinary history.
Whiskey & Cigar Lounge
Located within Mura Bar, this exclusive lounge offers premium whiskies and fine cigars paired with sweeping ocean views. It is an intimate retreat for guests seeking quiet indulgence.

Highlights From The Resort
Curated Excursions
From private yacht josurneys to manta ray encounters and dhoni trips, JOALI’s excursions reveal the natural beauty and local culture of the Maldives. Guests may also visit nearby islands to experience authentic Maldivian life.
Signature Water Sports Experiences
Guests can enjoy wakeboarding, water skiing, parasailing or speedboat adventures, combining adrenaline with sweeping views of turquoise waters.
Island Sports and Outdoor Play
Beyond tennis, JOALI offers a variety of land-based activities that encourage movement, wellness and playful competition in a scenic island setting.
Marine Discovery with Experts
Snorkel and dive alongside marine biologists to explore coral gardens and learn about the island’s rich underwater ecosystem.
JOALI Ceremony
A symbolic island celebration welcoming spring, beginning with a cleansing bonfire and ending with wish-making at the iconic Wish Tree. This tradition reflects JOALI’s philosophy of renewal, joy and creative living.
Manta Ray Treehouse
An elevated hideaway offering panoramic views of ocean and jungle. Designed for both children and adults, it is an enchanting space for relaxation, music and bespoke dining experiences.
One Table to Share
A JOALI tradition that brings guests together over a communal meal, encouraging connection, storytelling and shared joy through carefully curated cuisine.
The Book Exchange
A thoughtful reading concept where guests can exchange books and share perspectives, fostering connection through stories from around the world.
Weddings and Memorable Moments
Tailored wedding celebrations and milestone events designed by expert planners, ranging from intimate ceremonies to grand beachfront celebrations.
Bachelorette and Bachelor Celebrations
Curated island experiences designed for unforgettable group celebrations, blending relaxation, adventure and personalised events.
Dhoni at Sunrise and Sunset
Romantic sailings aboard traditional Maldivian dhonis, featuring sunrise breakfasts or sunset tapas against breathtaking seascapes.
The First Dinner
A dedicated service for newlyweds, ensuring their first dinner together is flawlessly planned, intimate and memorable.
Kids of JOALI
A world of imagination for young guests, featuring treasure hunts, creative self-care activities and child-friendly cooking sessions designed to inspire confidence and curiosity.
Wellness and Fitness
JOALI Spa by ESPA
An indulgent wellness sanctuary inspired by nature, offering personalised treatments that reconnect mind and body. Facilities include sauna, steam room, hammam, vitality pool and relaxation areas.
Fitness Experience
A fully equipped fitness centre with panoramic ocean and jungle views, complemented by expert trainers and a holistic approach to wellbeing.
