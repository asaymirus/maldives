Resort Overview
Anantara Dhigu Maldives Resort is where you can receive 5-star treatment from some of the world's best staff.
Best Family Resorts in Maldives ,Anantara Dhigu Resort & Spa is spread on two gorgeous islands in the South Male Atoll and delivers the perfect escape. Guests will be transferred to the resort via a 35-minute private speedboat journey from the airport. When you arrive at the resort, you will find 68 beachfront villas, over-water suites, and beautiful beach bungalows. Each villa blends tropical charm with modern conveniences and facilities.
Guests can request a variety of unusual activities, such as cooking courses, yoga on the beach, and snorkeling, as well as sailing, kayaking to an island for a private picnic on the beach, and windsurfing. Fitness centers and tennis courts are available on each island, as well as a library and a spectacular freshwater beachfront infinity-edge pool. For dinner, the resort's gourmet restaurant, Anantara Veli, has open kitchens and table-side cooking.
Since April 9, 2010, Anantara Dhigu Maldives Resort has been welcoming maldivesholidaoffers.com guests.

WATER SKIING, WAKE & KNEE BOARDING
Feel the rush of adrenaline as you skim across the lagoon behind our twin Gulhicraft Swan 20 speedboats, aptly named WET and WILD. Each powered by 150-horsepower engines, they promise smooth, exhilarating rides whether you're water skiing, wakeboarding or kneeboarding through the turquoise expanse.
FUN RIDES
Hold on tight and feel the thrill as you glide, spin and soar across the waves on an exciting tow rise. Whether you're bouncing over the water on a doughnut or zigzagging through the lagoon, laughter and splashes are guaranteed with every twist and turn.
JET SKIING
Take to the open ocean on a Seadoo jet ski and feel the exhilaration of gliding across turquoise waters. Ride tandem with your partner as your guide leads the way through the lagoon's vast playground, balancing adventure and discovery with every splash and turn.
FLY BOARDING
Experience an unforgettable rush as you soar above the lagoon with the exhilarating sport of flyboarding. Guided by our professional instructors, feel the lift of the water jets beneath your feet and take your Maldivian adventure to new heights.
SAILING
Embrace the freedom of the open sea as you glide across the Indian Ocean's turquoise expanse. Set sail on a K1 Top Cat for a serene catamaran adventure, with or without the guidance of an experienced skipper. For a more playful journey, take to the water on the Bravo Mini Cat, Hobie's latest innovation that combines comfort, stability, and ease of handling. Whether you're a first-time sailor or a seasoned deckhand, each voyage promises gentle breezes, endless horizons, and the pure joy of ocean discovery.
PARASAILING
Soar above the lagoon and take in breathtaking views of the reef below as you drift through the sky. Enjoy the thrill of flight and the beauty of the ocean from a new perspective, with smooth take-off and landing from the boat ensuring you stay perfectly dry.
WINDSURFING & KITESURFING
Harness the power of the wind and the rhythm of the sea with windsurfing, a graceful blend of sailing and surfing. For those seeking an extra thrill, kitesurfing offers soaring jumps and exhilarating turns across the turquoise lagoon, where every moment is charged with freedom and energy.
PADDLEBOARDING & KAYAKING
Glide gently across the calm lagoon on a stand-up paddleboard or in a glass-bottom kayak and watch the marine world unfold beneath you. No experience is needed, making these peaceful adventures ideal for all ages. Enjoy a gentle workout that strengthens the body while soothing the mind.

CORAL ADOPTION
Beneath the surface lies a world as delicate as it is breathtaking. Though resilient, coral reefs are sensitive to even the slightest change in their environment. Join the Coral Adoption Programme and take part in restoring this vibrant ecosystem alongside the resident Marine Biologist. Transplant living corals into the lagoon's shallow waters and return year after year to witness their growth. A small act of care that helps our ocean bloom, preserving its beauty for generations to come.
SCUBA DIVING
Explore the wonders of the deep with diving experiences tailored to every level. Discover vibrant coral reefs close to Anantara Dhigu or embark on half or full-day safaris by speedboat to more distant sites. Beginners can join our Discover Scuba Diving (DSD) programme, which includes a gentle introduction to theory, shallow-water practice in the lagoon and a guided dive of up to twelve meters around the house reef.
PADI CERTIFICATION
Begin a lifelong journey beneath the surface with PADI Open Water certification and gain the confidence to dive up to eighteen metres into a world of vibrant marine life.
Certified divers can continue their adventure with Advanced, Rescue or Dive Master courses, available privately or in small groups. Speciality options include underwater photography, buoyancy control, drift, enriched air and wreck diving. Courses are also available for junior divers aged ten and above.
DIVING SAFARIS
Journey beneath the waves and discover a world of vibrant wonder. Glide past flourishing coral gardens, graceful sea turtles, and swirling schools of fusiliers, with the chance to encounter reef sharks or spotted eagle rays in their natural habitat. Embark on half- or full-day safaris by speedboat, complete with seamless valet service and expert guidance from Aquafanatics' PADI 5-star Dive Centre. With every dive, experience the Maldives from a new perspective, alive with colour, movement, and quiet awe.
PHOTOGRAPHY
Celebrate your time in the Maldives with moments beautifully captured to last a lifetime. Our professional in-house photography team preserves the beauty of your stay, from quiet reflections to shared laughter, through images crafted with care and artistry.
KIDS COOKING CLASSES
Just like mum and dad, little chefs can join in the fun at Spice Spoons. Roll up your sleeves for pizza spinning and sushi rolling, learn new skills and get creative in the kitchen. There's plenty of laughter, a little bit of mess and a delicious reward at the end.
END OF DAY RITUAL
As the sun sinks towards the horizon, the rhythm of Boduberu drums drifts across the island and torches are lit one by one. Join the gentle procession or pause where you are and take in the glow of sunset. It is a daily moment to reflect, to breathe and to savour the magic of the Maldives.
SURFING
Find your wave at one of the best Maldives surf resorts.
The area around Anantara Dhigu Maldives surf resort offers a variety of world class waves, from peeling, playful rights to down-the-line left barrels. The closest break is accessible from neighbouring Anantara Veli, while the furthest break is a thirty-minute boat ride away from the resort. Surrender to the Anantara experience with a collection of carefully thought out journeys, rituals and individual treatments.
