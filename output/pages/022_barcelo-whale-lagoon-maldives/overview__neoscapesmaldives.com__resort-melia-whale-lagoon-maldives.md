DESCRIPTION
Where marine wonders meet effortless relaxation.
Meliá Whale Lagoon Maldives is a stylish island retreat located in the breathtaking South Ari Atoll, surrounded by turquoise lagoons, pristine beaches, and vibrant marine life. Combining warm Spanish hospitality with authentic Maldivian charm, the resort offers an elegant escape for couples, families, and adventure seekers alike. Guests can choose from beautifully designed beach and overwater villas, enjoy world-class dining, relax at the Mandara Spa, or explore the ocean through snorkeling, diving, kayaking, and paddleboarding experiences.
Positioned inside the South Ari Marine Protected Area (SAMPA), the resort is especially renowned for year-round whale shark and manta ray sightings. With two infinity pools, wellness experiences, exceptional dining venues, and unforgettable ocean adventures, Meliá Whale Lagoon Maldives delivers the perfect balance of relaxation, luxury, and discovery in the Maldives.
