Dear Valued Guests,
Welcome to the eternal summer at Vilamendhoo Island. Forget your winter blues and hectic daily lives; come, and embrace the tropical experiences in the Maldives, as guests have done for the past 50 years. The Maldives celebrated the Golden Jubilee, the 50th anniversary of Tourism in the Maldives. We’re incredibly proud to be a part of the Maldivian tourism industry and to be able to share our rich Maldivian culture and history with all our guests.
Vilamendhoo’s sustainability efforts are continuous and have been recognized by Travelife for consecutive years, and we are happy to be
officially Travelife Gold Certified
again. At Vilamendhoo, we are continuously upgrading and improving our resort keep to provide exceptional accommodations, facilities, and services to our valued guests while being sustainable and greener.
This 20th October 2022, the culinary team will celebrate ”International Chefs Day”. This year’s theme is “growing a healthy future”, where our chefs will emphasize sustainable food and create awareness about eating healthy.
If you’re travelling as an extended family with young children, we have endless water and land activities to choose from. Guest can also go for a full-day Family Adventure excursion for wholesome family time or go for group snorkelling as part of an excursion.
Our Food & Beverage team is busy preparing for the Halloween party. Remember to bring your favourite costume if your stay falls on 31st October, and join us to dance the night away with a live band in a “spooky atmosphere”.
There is still time to plan a visit to Vilamendhoo Island.
We have extended our special offers
, and more details can be found at www.vilamendhoo.com or email us at reservations@vilamendhoo.com. Our team will work tirelessly to ensure your island experience is perfect.
We wish you the happiest holidays and look forward to seeing you soon!
Sincerely,
Mohamed Zahir
General Manager
