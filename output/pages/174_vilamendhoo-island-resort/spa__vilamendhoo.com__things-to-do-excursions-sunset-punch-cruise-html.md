Sunset ‘Punch’ Cruise
A scenic late afternoon cruise around the South Ari Atoll is one of the top things for couples to do on their honeymoon in the Maldives.
The Sunset ‘Punch’ Cruise provides guests at Vilamendhoo with a memorable experience that combines views of the glorious sunset, the thrill of being out at sea and the chance to spot a pod of dolphins at play!
Enjoy a special punch on board, chat with other resort guests and remember to bring your camera. Each moment spent on the cruise is worth capturing.
The Sunset ‘Punch’ Cruise is scheduled every Tuesday, Wednesday, Friday, Saturday and Sunday 1700 – 1830.
A minimum of 6 persons, a maximum of 20 guests, and a parent must accompany all children under the age of 16.
