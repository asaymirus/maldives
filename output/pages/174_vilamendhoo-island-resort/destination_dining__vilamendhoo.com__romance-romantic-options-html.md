Romantic Options
Vilamendhoo offers a variety of ‘optional’ amenities for couples to add to their romantic getaway package in the Maldives.
Private BBQ Dinner on the Beach –
Surprise your partner with a romantic private dinner on the beach, beside the melodic ocean and beneath a sky filled with stars.
Champagne Breakfast Room Service –
Enjoy a full American breakfast served in the privacy of your room, on your porch or sundeck anytime before noon. This indulgent breakfast includes a half bottle of Champagne.
Him & Her –
A revitalising Balinese Massage for two awaits, experience a candlelight room steeped in the relaxing aromas of pure essential oils and savour the moment with a bottle of sparkling wine after this rejuvenating couples’ treatment.
Symbolic Wedding & Vow Renewal Ceremony
Recreate the wedding of your dreams on the pearly white shores of Vilamendhoo. Recommit to each other in a ceremony of your choosing, or let us plan a traditional, non-binding, symbolic Maldivian ceremony officiated by the island’s ‘headman’. Opt in for personal photo-shooting with Vila Memories photo studio and capture every unforgettable moment.
VILA VoW Basic – Enjoy a small Maldivian-style ceremony in our tropical beach setup
VILA VoW Classic – Complete your day with our culinary treats. Wedding cake, sparkling wine, local elements and a Romantic Candle Light Dinner at the Beach will perfect your special day in paradise.
VILA VoW Premium – Treat yourself to our premium experience. Let us spoil you with champagne elements, professional bridal hair & makeup, a Private Beach BBQ with your personal chef and a delicious breakfast to wake up the next morning.
