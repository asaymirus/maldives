Continuing Education
If you are already a certified diver, we can offer you several possibilities from PADI Advanced Open Water Diver up to Dive Master as well as specialties courses.
PADI Advanced Open Water Course
Develop your diving skills during this practical and experience based course. Deep and Navigation dives are compulsory and three adventure dives as for example underwater photography or scooter diving can be chosen from a variety of options.
PADI Enriched Air Nitrox Course
Increase the safety of your dives by absorbing less nitrogen. Extend your bottom time, stay down longer if the show is just going on. The course can be easily completed in one evening and with Euro-Divers you can dive with NITROX at no extra charge.
PADI Specialty Courses
If you want to challenge yourself and develop your diving skills, get enrolled in our specialty courses. We offer a wide range of courses such as Drift Diver, Night Diver, Peak Performance Buoyancy and many more. Save your time and effort while on holiday by getting all the theory completed at home with the
PADI eLearning
initiative.
