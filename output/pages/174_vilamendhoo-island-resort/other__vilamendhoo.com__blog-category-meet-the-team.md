# Meet The Vilamendhoo Team: Farheen, Assistant Guest Service Manager

Soopun Farheen is the Assistant Guest Service Manager at Vilamendhoo Island Resort & Spa. We find out what a typical day for her is like and what she likes most about her job.
What do you like most about your job?
What I like most about my job is the sense of family around our workplace, everyone truly gets along and put up efforts to get the job done and that every team member has a smile on their face.
What are your hobbies?
One of my hobbies is diving. The ocean always casts its spell on me, holding me in its net of wonder forever.
What do you like most about the resort?
My favourite things about the resort are the amazing house reef, talking to the guests, learning something new every day and the team.
What is a typical day for you like?
A typical day for me starts with putting a smile on my face to create a positive start, then I attend all meetings prepared and on time. During the day, I am sure to respect my teammates, to listen to others with an open mind, to motivate each other and to trust the team in the attainment of success.
What has your job taught you?
Through my job, I have learned how to deal with challenging situations, how to be patient, the ability to multitask, how to keep a positive attitude and how to go the extra mile for guests.
