Best Honeymoon Experiences with Vilamendhoo Maldives
The Maldives is truly a lover’s paradise. Almost every round-up of the best destinations for romance includes the island nation, with a special mention of the over-the-top
overwater bungalows
, endless white-sandy beaches, and
unique experiences for couples.
Our island of adventure welcomes honeymooners and romantic travellers alike, with a vibrant house reef teeming with sea life, breathtaking tropical views, and
a multitude of things to do for two.
Whether you are seeking a romantic getaway, or something a little more daring, you will have
the honeymoon of your dreams
at Vilamendhoo Island Resort & Spa.
Here Are the Top Things to Do on Honeymoon at Vilamendhoo:
• Relax on
some of the most picturesque beaches
you could ever see
•
Snorkel with marine creatures
such as sea turtles, reef sharks, and a variety of colourful fishes in the house reef
• Pamper yourself after a huge day of adventure at
the over-the-water Duniye Spa
(listed as one of the best spa destinations in the Maldives by Travel + Leisure Southeast Asia)
• Opt for
a romantic beach dinner
under a blanket of stars
• Cruise around South Ari Atoll with
our Ocean Discovery Excursion
• Learn to
scuba dive with Euro-Divers Vilamendhoo
• Sip on
a refreshing cocktail or two at Bonthi Bar
while watching an incredible sunset
•
Explore the crystal-clear lagoon
by paddleboard or kayak
•
Renew your vows
amidst the inspiring beauty of the Indian Ocean
•
Capture those lovey-dovey moments
to treasure for a lifetime with Vila Memories
Over the Water or on the Beach?

a secluded Beach Villa
