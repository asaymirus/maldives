Funama Buffet Restaurant
Funama is the family-friendly buffet restaurant assigned to guests staying in the Garden Bungalows and Beach Villas. The open-aired restaurant’s architecture features Maldivian influences, down to the sandy floor. With two separate dining areas, including outdoor seating on the wooden overwater deck, Funama has ample space to accommodate guests without feeling crowded. The restaurant is conveniently located near Bonthi Bar, where the main entertainment takes place, as well as near the main reception area.
