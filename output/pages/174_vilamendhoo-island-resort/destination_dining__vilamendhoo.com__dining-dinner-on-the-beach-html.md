Dinner On The Beach
Treat your loved one to an unforgettable dining experience filled with memorable moments on the soft sandy beach at Vilamendhoo. The beach near Ahima Restaurant makes for the ideal location to host your candlelight dinner on the beach. Alternatively, you can dine on the terrace or the beach in front of your room.
Reservations are recommended for this wonderful experience. Kindly contact the Food & Beverages team, Reception or Guest Services before 12:00 (noon) on the day of your dinner or email
reservations@vilamendhoo.com
to pre-book your dining experience.
Kindly note that beach dinners are weather contingent and venues are therefore subject to change in case of unfavourable weather.
