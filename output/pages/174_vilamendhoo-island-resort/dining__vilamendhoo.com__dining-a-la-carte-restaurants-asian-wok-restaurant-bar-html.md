Asian Wok Restaurant & Bar
Vilamendhoo’s Asian Wok, located on the eastern end of the island, is one of the best places to visit in the Maldives. Perched atop wooden stilts above the glittering lagoon, Asian Wok offers boundless views of the Indian Ocean. The views are lovely, but the cuisine is even more spectacular. Asian Wok takes you on an Asian culinary journey, serving up exquisitely prepared Sushi, Thai Dishes, Vietnamese favourites and more. The rich and vibrant flavours of the region come alive in Asian Wok. Also available is a dedicated live station for traditional Japanese teppanyaki. The outdoor wooden deck provides the perfect location to enjoy a cooling drink as you admire the lagoon and look out for the marine life that is known to swim by.
Reservations are recommended.
