Kayaking
One of the most peaceful ways to explore the boundless Indian Ocean is through a kayaking adventure in the Maldives.
Glide across the turquoise waters and watch the magical marine life beneath you. Vilamendhoo’s
Water Sports Centre
offers equipment rentals, from single to double and even a fascinating glass bottom kayak.
Beginners can choose between a guided trip around Vilamendhoo island or a guided trip to watch the dazzling sunset.
Guests on our
All Inclusive Plus
package enjoy 1 hour free use of the kayaks daily during their stay at the resort.
