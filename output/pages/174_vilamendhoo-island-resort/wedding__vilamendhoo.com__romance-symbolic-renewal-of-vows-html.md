Symbolic Renewal of Vows
Recreate the tropical island wedding of your dreams in the Maldives. Vilamendhoo hosts symbolic vow renewal ceremonies.
Choose your own ceremony or let us plan a traditional, symbolic Maldivian ceremony carried out by our island ‘headman’. Walk down the sandy aisle with the ocean as your backdrop, under tropical skies you and your partner will be united with a special blessing by the headman before reciting your wedding vows and exchanging rings, before sealing the union with the quintessential wedding kiss and signing of the wedding certificate.
