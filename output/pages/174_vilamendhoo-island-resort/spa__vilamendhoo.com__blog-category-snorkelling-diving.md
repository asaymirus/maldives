Here are our suggestions for the top family-friendly excursions in the Maldives
Whale Shark Excursion
Just about an hour Dhoni boat ride away from our adventurer’s paradise, close to Maamigili Island, is
the Whale Shark Point of the South Ari Atoll
— known as the premier location in the world where divers and marine enthusiasts can spot these gentle giants throughout the year.
Families joining this adventure can embark on
a once-in-a-lifetime experience
where they can swim alongside these magnificent creatures of the ocean. Our team will always be on hand and take the utmost care to be ecologically considerate during the excursion, so that you may enjoy this experience in a mindful and responsible way.
Recommended for families. Children aged 4 and above can take part in the trip accompanied by a parent.

Sea Turtle & Manta Ray Excursion
Join our Sea Turtle & Manta Ray Excursion for a unique experience of the South Ari Atoll’s stunning marine ecosystem. This experience provides adventurous families with the chance to catch a glimpse of the Loggerhead, Leatherback, Hawksbill, Olive Ridley and Green Sea Turtle species. They can also discover several genera of Manta Rays which include
Black Spotted Eagle, Torpedo, Feather Tails and Guitar.
Recommended for a families trip.  Children aged 4 and above can participate in the excursion.
