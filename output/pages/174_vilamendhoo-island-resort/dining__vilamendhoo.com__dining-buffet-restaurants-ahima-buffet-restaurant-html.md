Ahima Buffet Restaurant
Located on the western end of the island, Ahima is the adults-only buffet restaurant assigned to guests staying in the Jacuzzi Beach Villas and Jacuzzi Water Villas. The open-air design reveals the stunning scenery all around, creating an inviting atmosphere to dine in. Open for breakfast, lunch and dinner, Ahima is also conveniently located near adults-only Sunset Bar. Enjoy wonderful meals in paradise as you take in the picturesque views on display.
