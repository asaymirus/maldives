Family Experience
Experience the joys of endless exploration at Vilamendhoo with an unforgettable family holiday in the Maldives.
Enjoy more with Vilamendhoo’s Family Plan,
children’s meal plans are reduced to 50% of the adult rate, for children younger than 12 years of age. Young guests may also be eligible for seasonal discounted offers on room rates.
Seaplane transfer rates are reduced to 50% of the adult rate for children younger than 12 years of age.
Infants aged 2 years and younger are not charged for their room, meal plan or transfers.
Choose from spacious Tropical Villa and Beach Villas (Adjoining or Standalone), which accommodate children and dine in the family-friendly Funama Restaurant. The ‘all you can eat’ buffet style restaurant ensures that there is plenty for kids to eat and our chefs are happy to cater for special dietary requests.
See Child Stays Free offer and room deals »
Fun adventures await the entire family on Vilamendhoo’s shores with activities, facilities and entertainment to suit guests of all ages. The soft sandy beach makes for the ideal play area for kids. Parents can enjoy a day at Boashi Bar Pool while younger guests swim around in the Children’s Wading Pool or explore the nearby the
Heron Kids Club
and playground with a multipurpose jungle gym. Naturally, parents must always keep an eye on their children when near the lagoon or pools.
Other activities include snorkelling lessons, organised upon request by our Sports, Activities & Entertainment Department, and introductory Scuba courses by Euro-Divers Dive Centre.
Babysitting services are available upon prior request only and can be arranged by contacting the reception. It is recommended that you provide a 24 hours’ notice.
For your convenience there is a medical clinic and resident doctor at Vilamendhoo.
Contact us with any suggestions or needs for your perfect family holiday.
Note: With safety in mind for operating during the new normal health & safety guidelines, babysitting as an add-on service is not available.
