Approximately 900 meters in length and 300 meters in width at its widest point, located in Southern part of Ari Atoll. The distance from the airport is 135 kilometers and it takes only 25 minutes to reach the resort by seaplane, which is surrounded by white sandy beaches with a beautiful house reef which is well suited for snorkeling. Vilamendhoo is located in one of the best diving spots in the area. The island has a beautiful sandbank at the west end.
