GDS codes and commission
Vilamendhoo Island Resort & Spa partners with GDS companies so Travel Agents can book our Resorts online.
Vilamendhoo Island Resort & Spa offers a 10 % commission on all rack rates excluding taxes.
Travel Agents can book directly on the Resort website using their IATA number or on the GDS.Vilamendhoo Island Resort & Spa participates in the following GDS (Crown & Champa Resorts code: YX):

GDS commission structure
Vilamendhoo Island Resort & Spa pays a commission to any licensed Travel Agent with an IATA code. Commission payment is made no later than 30 days after the client has checked out of the Resort.
