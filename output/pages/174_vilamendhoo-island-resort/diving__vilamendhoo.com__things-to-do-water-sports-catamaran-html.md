Catamaran
Set course for the Indian Ocean and embark on an unforgettable catamaran sailing experience in the Maldives. This ocean adventure offers you the chance to spot dolphins and other marine life swimming beside the catamaran’s twin hulls.
Take a leap into the warm ocean and snorkel in one of the most beautiful places on earth, experience the vibrant underwater wildlife of the South Ari Atoll. The
Water Sports Centre
offers guided sailing trips and romantic sunset sails.
Need to learn more? Opt for private lessons, resort course or the VDWS basic course with an international license.
