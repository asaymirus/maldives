Dhangethi Island Excursion
Immerse yourself in the local culture and enjoy the wonders of island hopping in the Maldives, the Vilamendhoo way. Explore the rich culture, heritage and history of the nearby local island, Dhangethi.
The local island, with an estimated population of 1,000 people, is known as the primary local island of the South Ari Atoll. Our neighbours here offer a variety of traditional, local crafts and souvenirs, as well as delicious local cuisine.
In respect of how our neighbouring local Maldivians work and live together on the island, the required dress code for the excursion is, at minimum, a short-sleeved t-shirt, knee-length shorts for women, and shorts for men.
The Dhangethi Island Excursion is scheduled every Monday and Thursday from 15:00 to 17:00.
