Taj Coral Reef – This picturesque island hideaway combines modern comforts with unspoilt natural beauty. An environmentally friendly hotel, the Vivanta by Taj Coral Reef boasts a variety of excellent amenities with dining options. Alfresco dining facility available – Hembadhu Palm grill serving seafood specialties. Numerous water sports include snorkelling, windsurfing, sailing and canoeing. Diving enthusiasts will enjoy numerous dive sites, including a house reef complete with a shipwreck! Truly a world-class resort, there is something for everyone, from the most active amongst you to those who wish to do nothing more than soak up the sun.
