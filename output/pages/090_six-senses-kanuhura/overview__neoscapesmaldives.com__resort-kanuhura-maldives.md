DESCRIPTION
‘Amidst the beautiful turquoise waters of the Indian Ocean, discover the rare natural beauty of the orchid-filled paradise of Six Senses Kanuhura’
This gorgeous luxury resort offers elegant villas set on stilts over the sparkling blue lagoon and on the island’s sandy beaches. Whether you’re looking for relaxation or a plethora of entertainment, there is something for everyone. Wander down the orchid-clad pathways, relax at the exceptional spa, take part in some fun water activities or dance the night away in the night club. The hotel also offers a gastronomic experience thanks to the expertise of its dynamic Head Chefs. One of the most exclusive dining venues is a private islet adjacent to the main island, Jehunuhura. Each villa has its own private outdoor area with mesmerizing views of the ocean. Extensive use of hand-crafted wood, rattan, and other natural materials creates an elegant rustic interior befitting the naturally-beautiful island surroundings. The island even has its own species of orchid, the Six Senses Kanuhura orchid.

Dining
Bottega Restaurant
Delectable, satisfying and undeniably moreish, each of the masterfully created Italian dishes at Bottega are made with beautifully fresh ingredients of the highest quality. Immerse yourself in the tantalising flavours of Italy with unparalleled Mediterranean dishes, wonderfully simple, yet sublime, salads, mouthwatering pastas and gourmet pizzas that are sure to have you coming back for more. Pair any of the meals with one of the exceptional imported wines from the wine cellar for a truly phenomenal culinary experience.
Open for lazy lunches in the sun between 12:00 and 15:00 and memorable candlelit dinners between 19:00 and 22:30.
Veli Restaurant
The epitome of contemporary beachside dining, this remarkable restaurant is the ultimate place to indulge in the exotic flavours of the East. In the open kitchen, highly-skilled chefs create a plethora of exquisite dishes which include everything from expertly prepared sushi and Chinese specialities to Korean favourites, Singaporean dishes and tandoori cuisine which is prepared in a traditional Indian oven. A phenomenal venue for a special dinner, this exceptional restaurant offers private Yakiniku-style dining or the simple pleasure of dining under the stars while enjoying sublime, Asian-inspired meals that promise to be unforgettable.
Veli is open for dinner between 19.00 and 22.30.
Chef’s Herb Garden
The Chef’s Herb Garden offers gourmet typesetters an extraordinary private culinary experience surrounded by gentle candlelight in Kanuhura’s magnificent herb garden. This exclusive venue allows guests to celebrate a special occasion—or simply enjoy a unique experience—in style under the exceptional Maldivian night sky in this private oasis. At the heart of this unconventional dining experience is a specifically tailored, seasonal set menu that centres on beautifully prepared dishes that are sure to be no less than extraordinary.
This venue is exclusively reserved for private dinner bookings so be sure to book ahead for an unforgettable gastronomic experience in paradise.
A Mano Restaurant
Ideally situated on Kanuhura’s powder-white beach and surrounded by idyllic tropical scenery with picture-perfect views of the ocean, A Mano provides diners with an unparalleled casual dining experience either inside the beautiful restaurant or outside under the palm trees. Here, diners are taken on a sensory journey with extraordinary live cooking stations and themed buffets which are sure to dazzle even the most discerning epicures with a host of magnificent dishes that range from the exotic to the familiar.
This stunning restaurant is open for breakfast between 06:30 and 10:30 and dinner between 19:00 and 22:30.
Drift Restaurant
Savour a day of barefoot frivolity on Jehunuhura as you make the most of the signature cocktails and delicious grilled fare at Drift. Make the most of a day spent in paradise; enjoy a lazy lunch at Drift, unwind on the sunbeds under the palm trees and immerse yourself in the topaz-blue water as you enjoy the serenity of this magical place where the cocktails are sublime and the food is spectacular.
Drift is open for lunch between 12:00 and 15:00 and can be accessed by boat shuttle which runs from 07:00 to 17:00 and leaves from Kanuhura every 20 minutes.
Deli Restaurant
Sometimes the quintessential, carefree island lifestyle calls for a simple, light lunch or a sneaky sweet treat instead of a full, lavish sit-down meal. That’s why Kanuhura has an exceptional Deli that serves up everything from healthy drinks, delicious light lunches, freshly-baked treats and ice cream to aromatic teas, coffees and artisanal picnic baskets for the ultimate languid lunch enjoyed in a special spot on the magical tropical island.
This wonderful and unique resort offering has an array of fresh, daily delights and is open daily between 10:00 and 18:00.
Cowry Club Bar
Discover unique signature cocktails and classic favourites that are whipped up in style by the expert mixologists at the Cowry Club. With one of the most extensive cocktail lists in the Maldives, you can enjoy anything from exotic Kanuhura specialities to your standard classics—created to perfection and gorgeously presented. Savour the stunning cocktails at the Cowry Club while you take in the mesmerising views or relax in the lounge area on the edge of the infinity pool—this is luxury island living at its best.
This exceptional poolside bar is open between 11:30 and 16:00 for light lunches and from 10:00 until late for drinks.
Iru Bar
Characterised by a distinct gypset style, Iru is a chic beachside bar and the perfect place to spend a glorious afternoon in the Maldivian sun while sipping on any of the extraordinary cocktails offered at the bar. A tropical haven unlike any other, at Iru, free-spirited, curious explorers have their pick of interesting places to relax; from bohemian beaded swing chairs at the bar, to low picnic-like tables and beach teepees, Iru provides the ultimate place to sit back and relax, cocktail in hand while soaking up the gorgeous sunset hues.
Open from 11:00 to midnight, guests have ample opportunity to embrace the wonders of this whimsical bar.

Highlights From The Resort
WATERSPORTS AND DIVING
Water Lovers and Sun Worshippers Have No Limit to the Ways They Can Enjoy Our Tropical Playground. Hop on Board a Private Boat and Head Towards the Endless Horizon or Spend the Day Sailing the Seas on a Catamaran. Skim the Water’s Surface on a Canoe or a Stand-Up Paddle Board, and Set Your Inner Thrillseeker Free While Jet Skiing, Wakeboarding, Windsurfing, Water Skiing or Mono Skiing. “fly” With Our Jetlev-flyer and Enjoy the Adrenaline Rush on Our Banana Boat and Fun Tubes. Explore the Ocean’s Hidden Wonders With Our Diving Excursions Through Our Padi Centre Which Also Offers Various Diving Courses.
CULINARY
Discover our gastronomic offerings and carefully crafted culinary activities at Kanuhura hotel. Welcome the dusk as you enjoy sundowners on the pristine beach in the shade of a teepee. Learn how to make delectable Maldivian dishes in our cooking class, or savour  a tailor-made wine tasting with our sommelier. For those who prefer whisky, we have a glorious poolside tasting and coffee addicts can relish the experience of a coffee and chocolate pairing. We also offer a selection of various dining experiences from romantic full moon dinners to picnics on the beach.
ESCAPES AND EXCURSIONS
Every day brings a new adventure in the Maldives. You have the incredible freedom to indulge your curiosity and deepen your connection to nature with our Kanuhura hotel excursions. Enjoy a private castaway picnic on one of our secluded islands or spend blissful sun-drenched hours frolicking between the two deserted islands as you snorkel in the waters and explore the lush interiors. Seek out gorgeously vibrant coral reefs adorned with colourful fish on a snorkelling excursion. Savour the sunset as you journey towards the horizon on a magical sunset cruise.
FITNESS AND SPORTS
Balance work and play with our phenomenal range of fitness activities at Kanuhura, centred on total wellbeing. Try something unconventional with a Maldivian warrior workout, enjoy the fusion of Pilates and boxing with a Piloxing class or push your limits with functional fitness or beach boot camp classes. Join us for a session of morning jogging or Pilates. Move your body with aqua fitness or beach Zumba, beat your best with circuit training, kickboxing or beach boxing and make the most of our squash and tennis courts. Tennis coaching and personal fitness training sessions are available for a nominal fee.
WILDLIFE
Many of the most outstanding experiences in Maldives involve the tropical sea. Our wildlife activities at Kanuhura hotel provide you with a rare opportunity to see a massive variety of incredible marine creatures in their natural habitat. Join us on this voyage in search of a sight that is totally breathtaking, one sure to resonate with your soul and become etched into your mind for the rest of time. Delight in our speedboat safari as we journey in search of gorgeous pods of dolphins riding the waves and phenomenal manta rays that appear to be flying as they glide through the water.
WELLBEING
Our wellbeing activities at Kanuhura are designed to increase relaxation and holistic wellness and to ensure that our global travellers feel their best. Whatever activity you may desire to nurture mind, body and soul, we have the right thing for you. Relish a full mind-body workout with a rejuvenating session of yoga (choose Sunrise /SunsetYoga, Ashtanga Yoga, Yoga Pranayama, Kids’ Yoga, Rhythmic Yoga, Aqua yoga or Acroyoga) or learn to be still with meditation. Complement these wonderful activities with a treatment at our highly acclaimed Kokaa spa, where we offer a huge range of luxuriating massages, facials, body wraps and more or indulge in some well-deserved pampering at our hair and beauty salon.
CULTURAL
The incredible cultural activities at Kanuhura hotel reflect the idiosyncrasies that set the Maldives apart from other destinations; appreciate a coconut tree climbing display, explore a local fishing village and be humbled by the work undertaken at the turtle sanctuary. Visit Kanuhura’s gorgeous orchid gardens, witness the hatching of turtles on Kanuhura’s beaches and be entertained by hermit crab racing. Relish a movie watched under the stars or enjoy the plethora of indoor games. Children are able to have equally wonderful cultural experiences at our exceptional kids’ club.
FISHING
Be surrounded by nothing but mesmerising blue waters as far as the eye can see on one of our incredible fishing trips. Whether you are an amateur or an avid fan, you are sure to have an unforgettable experience with our fishing activities at Kanuhura. Embrace the splendour of a Maldivian sunset while learning how to fish traditionally with a hand line, enjoy a lesson in fly fishing from the shores of a deserted island or brave the waves on a big game fishing trip to remember. If the odds have been in your favour, your prized catch can be specially prepared for lunch or dinner the next day for a nominal fee.
TENNIS AND SQUASH
Whether you are a seasoned professional or an absolute beginner, you are sure to have a memorable time on our hardcourt tennis courts in the heart of paradise. With competition standard floodlights, these outdoor courts are perfect for a casual day or night match, where the awe-inspiring natural surrounds are sure to make any game one remember.
Equal measures fun and skilful, a game of squash on our indoor courts may just be what you need. Whether it’s for points or fun, with family or friends or just for a solid workout, you can’t go wrong with a game of squash.
SUN KIDS
Our little gypsetters are in for an adventure of a lifetime at our kid-friendly hotel with activities and entertainment designed to thrill and enchant. The exclusive kid’s corner at the Boduberu Lounge provides a warm welcome for them, while our floating water park offers hours of fun for kids and adults alike. Allow them to fill their activity passport with amazing experiences: from the excursion to the deserted island Jehunuhura to an enthralling pirate adventure and mini Olympics. Watch them embrace their creativity with pottery and get closer to the wildlife and culture of the Maldives.
SPA
Surrender to the serenity of the elegant Kokaa Wellness Retreat and Spa, aligned with the gypset spirit and embodying the grace of a butterfly—Kokaa meaning butterfly in Dhivehi. Be transported to a world of tranquillity as you indulge in a treatment from our extensive list of luxurious facials, massages and body wraps, each tailored to your individual needs. These blissful experiences are enhanced by some of the finest products in the world—VOYA, Subtle Energies and Terres D’Afrique. Yoga and meditation are incorporated into our packages to ensure renewed balance of mind, body and soul.
