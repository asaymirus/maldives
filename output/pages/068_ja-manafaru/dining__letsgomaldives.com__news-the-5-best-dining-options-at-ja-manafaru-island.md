Immerse yourself in culinary delights at JA Manafaru in the Maldives. Choose from Bed & Breakfast, Half Board, or Full Board, or go all-out with their Dine Around All Inclusive option. This includes a welcome toast with champagne and macarons, daily breakfasts, and your choice of lunch and dinner venues, each boasting à la carte menus with over 120 dishes. Thematic nights like Asian street food and Maldivian feasts tantalize your taste buds, while over 150 wines, cocktails, and drinks keep you refreshed. In-villa dining provides ultimate comfort, and Andiamo’s complimentary ice cream adds a sweet touch to your stay. With seven restaurants offering separate menus for various dietary needs, JA Manafaru caters to every palate.
1. Ocean Grill:
Picture yourself dining barefoot on the beach, the gentle sea breeze caressing your skin, and the starlit sky as your canopy. Ocean Grill at
Ja Manafaru Island
offers precisely that – a serene ambiance coupled with
the freshest seafood
caught by local fishermen. Committed to sustainability, Ocean Grill emphasizes ethical practices, ensuring every dish not only tantalizes the taste buds but also aligns with environmental values. Don’t miss their
Zero Food Waste menus
, a testament to their dedication to culinary innovation and sustainability.
2. White Orchid:
Floating above the azure waters of the Indian Ocean,
White Orchid beckons
with its captivating views and delectable East Asian flavors. Whether you choose to dine indoors or bask in the ocean breeze on the terrace, this overwater restaurant promises a culinary journey through the Orient. Sample an array of favorites while marveling at the panoramic vistas, making every bite a moment to cherish.
3. Kakuni:
Start your day right with a sumptuous breakfast spread at Kakuni, designed to fuel your adventures on the island. From
fresh fruits to homemade yogurts
, there’s something to appease every palate. As the day progresses, indulge in a diverse lunch menu featuring international dishes that promise to keep your energy levels high. Make sure to partake in their themed nights, offering a unique culinary experience ranging from Maldivian feasts to Asian street food extravaganzas.
4. Andiamo Bistro and Pool:
Nestled amidst lush greenery, Andiamo Bistro invites you to savor
Italian specialties and perfect pizzas
next to the jungle pool. Indulge in the flavors of Italy while enjoying complimentary ice cream, served all day, every day. For health-conscious diners, Wellness Your Way offers vegan, dairy-free, gluten-free, and low-carb options, ensuring everyone can delight in their culinary offerings.
5. The Cellar:
For those seeking an extraordinary dining experience, The Cellar awaits as the Indian
Ocean’s hidden gem
. Tucked deep beneath the island’s surface, this subterranean chamber exudes an aura of exclusivity and refinement. With its volcanic rock-lined walls and meticulously curated wine collection, The Cellar sets the stage for an unforgettable journey of gastronomy and oenology, making it a must-visit for connoisseurs of fine dining.
Whether you’re craving a beachfront barbecue, an elegant overwater dining experience, or an underground culinary adventure, Ja Manafaru Island offers an array of dining options to satiate your appetite and elevate your vacation to new heights. So, indulge your senses and embark on a culinary odyssey amidst the beauty of the Maldives.
