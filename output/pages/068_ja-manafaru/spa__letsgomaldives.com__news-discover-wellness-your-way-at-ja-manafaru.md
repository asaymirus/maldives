JA Manafaru Introduces Tailored Wellness Retreats
JA Manafaru, located in the tranquil Haa Alifu Atoll of the Maldives, has launched exclusive wellness retreat packages, marking a new era of personalized wellness experiences on the island.
Personalized Wellness Journeys
Since the debut of its Wellness Your Way menus on World Food Day, 16th October 2023, JA Manafaru has been committed to offering guests tailored wellness options. The new retreat packages allow guests to choose from a variety of treatments and activities, ensuring a bespoke wellness journey that suits individual preferences and goals.
Explore Your Options
For those easing into wellness, the “Taste of Wellness” package offers a relaxed two- or three-day program. Guests seeking deeper immersion can opt for comprehensive programs like “Detox and Reset,” “Mindful Journey,” or “Active De-Stress,” spanning from five to ten days.
JA Signature Culinary Journey
A highlight is the JA Signature Culinary Journey, designed to transform dietary habits with expert guidance. This retreat includes culinary workshops and optional Epi-Genetic testing, providing personalized insights into optimal dietary choices.
Expert Insight
Victoria Kruse, JA Resorts & Hotels’ Brand Consultant for Wellness and Spa, emphasizes the transformative potential of the culinary retreat. “Our program supports guests in adopting healthier eating habits alongside mindfulness and stress relief,” she notes.
Wellness Your Way Menus
Central to these retreats are the Wellness Your Way menus, available across all dining outlets and in-villa dining. These menus cater to various dietary preferences, including vegan, vegetarian, dairy-free, gluten-free, and low-carb options, featuring locally sourced ingredients.
Plan Your Wellness Escape
Guests can embark on these rejuvenating wellness journeys from 1st May 2024, with special introductory pricing until 20th December 2024. For more details on the Wellness Your Way retreat packages, visit JA Manafaru’s official website.
