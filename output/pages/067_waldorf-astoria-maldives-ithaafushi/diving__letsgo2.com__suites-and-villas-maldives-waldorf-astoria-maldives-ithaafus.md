About the resort
Your holiday to Waldorf Astoria Maldives Ithaafushi starts in style with a very pleasant transfer aboard the resort's own luxury yacht - complete with canapes served on the sundeck. It only takes about 30 minutes to reach the resort. Being close to the busy main island of Male can sometimes be seen as a negative - yet Waldorf Astoria gives you the best of both worlds; it's easy to get to, yet feels entirely far-flung since there are no other signs of civilisation nearby.
There are only 122 villas in total, dotted across three separate islands - giving ample privacy.
Around the resort, there are two huge infinity pools (one reserved just for adults), a glass-walled fitness centre and enormous watersports centre. Spend days exploring nearby reefs, learning to windsurf or paddle-boarding around the lagoon - or simply drift between your swinging daybed to your hammock on the beach, stopping for a dip in the pool or a refreshing tipple at the beach club bar.
Every one of the resort's sleek villas come with generously-sized private pools (long enough for laps as well as lounging), together with ample indoor and outdoor living spaces.  They have massive floor to ceiling windows that ensure you're treated to superb scenes of sand and sea from every corner of your villa (the blinds open and close with a swipe of your in-room iPad). You'll be well looked after by your personal concierge - who can arrange restaurant reservations, help you with unpacking, or bring you a freshly squeezed juice whenever you feel like one.

OUR
restaurant and bars
The 11 different restaurants at this resort invite you on a culinary journey. For total intimacy dine on your villa's private terrace or for a romantic treetop setting try Terra for its private dining pods in the canopy. Amber, the champagne bar and Nava, the pool and beach club, round off this selection of truly special locations.
Amber
Enjoy the stunning sunset from Amber, the relaxed champagne bar. Savour a unique range of the finest champagnes along with hand-crafted champagne cocktails.
Glow
Glow offers a garden to table concept. Healthy cuisine made with fresh ingredients from the island's edible garden complemented by natural organic wines and healthy detoxifying drinks.
Li Long
Sample Li Long's signature Peking duck roasted in the Maldives first classic wood-fired oven. Contemporary Chinese cuisine is perfectly paired with specialty Chinese tea from the finest tea collections.
Nava
Indulge in the senses at the resort's playground with music, food and drinks. Relax in the vibrant ambiance of Nava, the pool and beach club featuring live DJ's, cocktails, small bites and wood fired pizzas.
Peacock Alley
Unique to Waldorf Astoria, Peacock Alley boasts ocean views and live music, enjoy hand crafted, signature barrel aged cocktails, wines and tapas.
Tangled
The Maldives only hand-pulled noodles and fresh Dim Sum are served at Tangled. Add to the experience with craft beer, baiju and sake.
Tasting Table
Indulge in alluring cuisines at the Tasting Table, start the day with a scrumptious breakfast, while in the evening enjoy a culinary journey from Asia to Europe.
Terra
Dine among the treetops in one of seven bamboo nests. A unique dining experience featuring the world's finest ingredients such as Wagyu Beef and Alba White Truffle. Each dish is complemented by an extensive collection of wines.
The Ledge by Dave Pynt
The Ledge brings the much loved modern Australian barbecue style to Waldorf Astoria. With the perfect spot by the pool for a leisurely lunch, while in the evening The Ledge turns into an intimate dining venue where the bar and grill come to life.
The Rock
Home to the an extensive wine cellar of the finest new world favourites and old world classics. The Rock offers exclusive wine dinners for 12 guests featuring food and wine pairing.
Yasmeen
Take a gastronomic journey of Levant cuisine in a Arabian village setting with views of the Indian Ocean.
