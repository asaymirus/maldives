A sense of
place
The airport and bustling capital are under an hour’s sail away, but you’d never know it.  Every inch of the resort begs relaxation and quality time, and although expansive by Maldivian standards, Ithaafushi has an air of intimacy about it.
Frequented by all ages of the discerning crowd, grown-ups often tuck themselves away in the cocktail bars or their private pools, looked after by butlers seeming gifted with foresight. Those with younger ones in tow, however, are usually found out and about, gearing up to take to the water whether on a paddleboard, in a kayak, or to discover the marine life during a spot of snorkelling.

Home to 11 exclusive dining settings, Waldorf Astoria not only caters for its own guests, but often sees those from surrounding resorts venturing to Ithaafushi to sample its cuisine.  Pinch-me culinary moments come at Terra with its romantic hand-crafted bamboo dining pods set in the treetops. And at poolside spot The Ledge, Michelin-starred chef Dave Pynt serves up Australian inspired wood-fire barbecued meats. Like all of Waldorf Astoria’s properties, this Indian Ocean outpost also has a Peacock Alley tapas and saxophone lounge — a charming nod to their debut hotel in NYC.

Each of Ithaafushi’s villas are vast when compared to the region's norm; the largest of which are tailor-made for multi-generational families. Think soaring ceilings, light, fresh, and warm tones, oversized bathtubs, alfresco showers and huge pools.
On location
Surrender yourself to the Ithaafushi’s pioneering Wellness Concierge — the first in the Maldives — offering an innovative consultation process before you choose from a menu of serene spa treatments (inspired by the natural elements of earth, water, fire and metal), or from an array of invigorating yoga and fitness classes.

Beyond
family-friendly
At Waldorf Astoria Ithaafushi, the adventures aren’t just for grown-ups. A jam-packed timetable at their Stars Club for children embraces everything from snorkelling to cookery classes.
The outdoor area is wonderfully shaded so kids can make the most of the trampoline, monkey bars and other playthings all day. And the excellent staff who entertain your little ones during the day could even be looking after them in the evenings if you avail yourselves of the resort’s excellent babysitting service.
Rounding up the relatives is also made blissfully easy here. A hop, skip and a jump away from the main resort lies Ithaafushi The Private Island , the largest exclusive-use island in the region. And it just so happens to be in the Waldorf family.

One for the foodies this island may be, but culinary daydreams aren’t confined to its pearly sands. Dubai is often overlooked as simply a stop en route, but book-ending a Maldivian escape with this pioneering city showcases delicacies hard to find anywhere else. Alternatively, a spot of island-hopping tickles the tastebuds on neighbouring atolls. If you’re looking for a place to end on a high though, we’d recommend that Waldorf Astoria Ithaafushi be the cherry on top.
