Romantic Retreats
Fall In Love All Over Again
Couples seeking an intimate escape should look no further. Romance is all around the island — reflected in our villas with private infinity pools, starlit dinners, and signature experiences like sandbank dining, cinema under the stars, and sunset cruises.
At Waldorf Astoria Maldives Ithaafushi, romance lies in knowing that every detail is taken care of, and in the beauty of doing nothing at all.
There is something about the island — perhaps its powdery soft sands, the hush of the tides, or shimmering moonlight on the water — that draws two people closer together.
The seclusion and space of our overwater and beach villas, each with private pools and breathtaking ocean views, invite you to slow down, relax, and simply be together.
Share a floating breakfast for two in the privacy of your pool, experience transformative couples spa treatments, and savor the romantic atmosphere at Amber — our sunset champagne lounge. Dine together in the treetops at Terra for the ultimate date night experience.
Dining on a sandbank, our cinema under the stars, and sunset cruises are essential signature experiences for true romance seekers. For couples with a taste for adventure, watersports and excursions at sea will create memories you will share forever.
Take the Maldives back home with a couple’s photoshoot. Our expansive island resort is teeming with dreamy backdrops for the perfect shot.
For proposals, engagements, anniversaries, honeymoons, and vowel renew options — our team is waiting to curate your romantic retreat beyond your expectations. We can’t wait to bring your vision to life.
