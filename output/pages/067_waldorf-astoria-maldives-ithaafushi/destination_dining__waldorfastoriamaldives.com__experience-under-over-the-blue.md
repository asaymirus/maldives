Under & Over The Blue
Enter a Realm of Discovery
Discover the ultimate sense of freedom on the water. The ocean is yours to explore — and impossible to forget.
Take it slow in a glass kayak, or lift off by Jetblade. Propel through coral gardens at full throttle via Seabob, snorkel, or dive deep to uncover lost shipwrecks. However you move through the water — solo, side by side, or full speed ahead — each moment offers its own kind of thrill.
Watersports
From soaring heights to sweeping curves, experience the Maldives from angles few ever do. Glide across the sea’s surface on a paddleboard, or parasail above it to watch the lagoon reveal itself from a new perspective — a view that will stay with you. Our curated selection of watersports invites all generations to discover something unforgettable.
Our Watersports Center is your gateway to the sea — thoughtfully equipped, well-staffed, and ready when you are. First-timers will find friendly guidance and trusted top-tier gear, while seasoned explorers will find the freedom to push further. Whether stepping onto a paddleboard for the first time or chasing your next adrenaline rush, our extensive range of watersports promises every guest a new way to connect with the ocean.
Ocean excursions range from sandbank adventures to deep-sea game fishing. Our expert team helps tailor each outing to your style, pace, and comfort. Whatever your vision, our team is waiting to bring it to life.

Non-motorized Watersports
Harness the Maldivian breeze with windsurfing, kitesurfing, or set sail with a catamaran lesson — graceful, wind-led ways to explore.
Motorized Watersports
Jet ski, parasail, or reach new heights with the X-Jetpack — elevated, high-thrill ways to slice through, soar above, or skim over the water.
Snorkeling
Follow your curiosity beneath the surface, where coral gardens bloom and vibrant marine life dances through the reef’s sunlit shallows.
Scuba Diving
Descend into serenity with expert guidance — discover thriving reefs, gentle giants, and the quiet magic of the Maldives’ untouched depths.
