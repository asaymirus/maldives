DESCRIPTION
Waldorf Astoria Maldives Ithaafushi is the art of island luxury, perfected.
Escape to Waldorf Astoria Maldives Ithaafushi, a sanctuary spread across three private islands with 119 all-pool Beach, Reef, and Overwater villas. Every villa is designed to blend timeless elegance with the breathtaking beauty of the Maldives, creating the ultimate private retreat.
Culinary journeys await at 11 distinctive dining venues. Dine among the treetops at Terra with panoramic ocean views, savor Middle Eastern ﬂavors at Yasmeen, or enjoy ﬂame-grilled specialties at The Ledge by Dave Pynt. End your day with sunset cocktails at Amber, or indulge in Destination Dining experiences such as a sandbank dinner under the stars or a ﬂoating breakfast in your villa pool.
Unwind at the Waldorf Astoria Spa, offering bespoke treatments across 10 villas, or discover vibrant marine life with the PADI-certified dive center and exciting water sports. Families are welcomed at the Stars Club, designed with creative and educational activities for kids and teens.
Every stay is enriched by True Waldorf Service, where personal concierges ensure every moment is seamless. Whether you seek relaxation, celebration, or adventure, the resort transforms your journey into unforgettable memories.

Dining
Terra
A Table in the Treetops. Perched at the highest point of the island, dine among the treetops in one of seven handcrafted bamboo nests. This elevated experience features the world’s finest ingredients, such as Miyazake Wagyu Beef and Alba White Truffle, paired with an extensive collection of premium wines.
Yasmeen
A Night in the Levant. Adorned with antique décor, Yasmeen presents a gastronomic journey of Levantine cuisine in a heritage Arabian village setting. Savor piping hot Saj bread, mezzes, and perfectly succulent charcoal-grilled meat and seafood overlooking the Indian Ocean.
The Ledge by Dave Pynt
From Flame to Finish.4 Created by Michelin-starred chef Dave Pynt (of Burnt Ends), this open-air, beachfront restaurant brings modern Australian barbecue to the Maldives. Enjoy wood-fired seafood, signature steak frites with caviar, and Wagyu Beef, making it the perfect pool-side spot for laid-back indulgence.
Zuma
A Global Japanese Icon. Zuma brings its globally celebrated Izakaya-style dining to the Indian Ocean with its first floating outpost. Enjoy a vibrant and relaxed setting featuring an island bar, robatayaki grill, and sushi counter, serving specialty dishes like sliced seabass with yuzu and roasted lobster.
Li Long
A House of Shanghai Heritage. Experience an authentic Chinese culinary journey inspired by old Shanghai. Relish the restaurant’s signature dish: wood-fired Beijing duck crisped to perfection and carved tableside, alongside delicate tasting menus and handmade dim sum.
Glow
Mediterranean at Soul. Embrace a garden-to-table concept in this greenhouse ambiance. Glow features well-being cuisine made with fresh ingredients from the island’s edible garden, serving a sunlit celebration of Mediterranean-inspired flavors complemented by organic and natural wines.
Nava
On Island Time. Our very own beach and pool club is a vibrant playground with art and music. Relax to live DJs while enjoying a refreshing selection of cocktails, wood-fired pizzas, and signature seafood platters like our luxury caviar set.
The Rock
A Date Set in Stone. Tucked within a sculpted chamber of limestone, The Rock offers an extraordinary, private, immersive wine dining experience. Reserved for just one party each evening, dine around a 200-year-old Jarrah wood table with curated wine pairings from an extensive cellar.
Tasting Table
Iconic Island Cuisine. At once relaxed and refined, Tasting Table brings the world’s cuisines to the heart of the island. Start your day with a sumptuous multi-cuisine breakfast spread, and return in the evening for a culinary journey spanning Asia to Europe, served on the al fresco beachside terrace.
Peacock Alley
A Lounge with Legacy. Unwind with ocean views and live music in this serene, airy lounge. Enjoy hand-crafted, signature barrel-aged cocktails, fine wines, and tapas, continuing a timeless Waldorf Astoria tradition day and night.
Amber
Our Specialty is the Sunset. Amber is the place where your evening begins. Savor the finest bubbles before the Indian Ocean from a cushioned sofa, enjoying front-row views of the changing orange sky. The bar serves an elegant champagne menu paired with hand-crafted cocktails and refined small plates.
Destination Dining
Dining Designed By You. Discover curated dining moments designed to inspire and elevate every occasion. Choose one of our spectacular venues, from a serene beachfront dinner or a celebratory sunset cruise, or have our private dining team bring your vision to life in a secluded island escape.

Highlights From The Resort
Water and Marine Discovery
Enter a realm of discovery and freedom on the water. The ocean is yours to explore, whether taking it slow in a glass kayak, propelling through coral gardens via Seabob, or uncovering lost shipwrecks. Guests can also try beach games, join marine conservation talks, or explore a wealth of marine activities. Experience spectacular views by parasailing 200m above sea level or ride an underwater scooter.
Wellness and Fitness
The Waldorf Astoria Spa is a sanctuary nestled in botanical gardens, offering holistic treatments focused on relaxing, rebalancing, and achieving results through customized wellness solutions. Revitalize your body at the Fitness Centre, which features an outdoor yoga pavilion. You can also improve mobility and flexibility with the guidance of dedicated personal trainers and yoga instructors. Guests are invited to sharpen their serve on the professional-grade tennis and padel courts.
Family and Play
The Stars Club and Club Nova offer a magical world for younger guests to create, connect, and play with purpose. The Young Discovery Park features an exciting water recreation area and enriching activities catered to kids and teens for their very own Maldivian experience. Daily activities are designed to spark curiosity and creativity, from shoreline games to hands-on art.
Lifestyle and Souvenirs
Capture your memories with a professional Maldives photoshoot, including a complimentary one-hour session across Nava Beach and the pools. Embark on an island escape, beautifully dressed, by visiting the Tropical Retail boutique spaces, which offer a refined selection of resort wear, jewelry, and accessories sourced from celebrated designers.
