The Stars Club
A Place for our Little Stars to Sparkle
Welcome to The Stars Club — a magical world made just for kids. Splash through our colorful water park, get creative with arts and crafts, or join a cooking class made for little chefs.
Each day brings new adventures, with games, storytelling, and nature-inspired fun designed to spark imagination and joy.
With caring hosts and plenty of play, every child is free to laugh, learn, and shine bright at our Maldives kids club.
