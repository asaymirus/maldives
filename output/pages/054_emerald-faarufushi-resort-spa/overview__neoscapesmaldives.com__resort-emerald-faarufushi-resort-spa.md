DESCRIPTION
Welcome to Emerald Faarufushi Resort & Spa, a 5-star Deluxe All-Inclusive escape and proud member of The Leading Hotels of the World. Here, tropical landscapes, swaying palms, and crystalline waters create a seamless fusion of nature and indulgence, defining an atmosphere of pure barefoot elegance.
Nestled in the unspoiled Raa Atoll, this 7-hectare private island stretches across 1.2 km of powder-white beaches, encircled by a 100-hectare turquoise lagoon with one of the most vibrant coral reefs in the Maldives.
Thoughtfully crafted for the modern, mindful traveler, Emerald Faarufushi blends minimalist design with authentic cultural soul, offering refined simplicity that speaks volumes.

Dining
Aqua Restaurant
Located on the beach with views of the turquoise lagoon, Aqua is the resort’s main dining destination. It offers a generous buffet for breakfast, lunch, and dinner, featuring live cooking stations with global cuisines including Mediterranean, Japanese, Italian, Chinese, and vegetarian options to satisfy every palate.
Beach Club Restaurant
Set directly on the beach with stunning views of the Maldivian lagoon, this restaurant specializes in freshly grilled dishes. Guests can enjoy a pizza and pasta station, along with a selection of fresh fish, seafood, meat, and vegetables cooked on a stone grill to enhance their natural flavours.
Carnivourous Restaurant
Located on the beach with stunning views of the Indian Ocean, Carnivorous Restaurant is inspired by South American cuisine. It brings the Churrascaria experience to the Maldives, offering a Brazilian-style steakhouse with premium cuts of meat.
Mediterraneo Restaurant
Perched over Faarufushi Lagoon, Mediterraneo offers an exceptional overwater Mediterranean dining experience. Ideal for romantic sunset dinners, the restaurant combines exquisite flavours with stunning views to create a multisensory culinary journey.
Le Asiatique and Teppanyaki Grill
Located on the second floor of the Beach Club Grill, Le Asiatique offers a refined, airconditioned dining experience inspired by authentic Asian flavours and culinary traditions. Guests can also enjoy live cooking shows at the Teppanyaki Grill, adding a touch of excitement to each meal.
Sunset Pool & Bar
Located on the main beach, the Sunset Pool & Bar is the perfect spot to unwind with a cocktail while soaking in panoramic ocean views. With chill-out music, a relaxed atmosphere, and breathtaking sunsets, it is the ideal place to enjoy good company and island serenity.
Eclipse Bar
Located at the far end of the Mediterraneo Restaurant jetty, Eclipse Bar invites guests to enjoy handcrafted cocktails and hand-picked wines that capture the essence of summer. It’s the perfect setting to experience the magic of Maldivian sunsets over the crystal-clear turquoise lagoon.

Highlights From The Resort
The Emerald Spa
Nestled within the island’s tropical greenery, The Emerald Spa is an oasis of tranquility offering world-class Thai and Balinese-inspired treatments. The spa features eight airconditioned treatment huts, a steam room, plunge pools, a yoga pavilion, and manicure and pedicure stations. Its serene design blends beautifully with the natural surroundings, making it the perfect retreat for mind and body rejuvenation.
Dolphin Kids Club
Designed for children aged 3 to 12, the Dolphin Kids Club offers creative, educational, and outdoor activities in a fun and safe environment. Managed by trained multilingual staff, it ensures each child enjoys a memorable Maldivian adventure while parents unwind worry-free.
Gym & Sports Center
Guests can maintain their fitness routines at the air-conditioned gym equipped with Technogym machines and professional trainers offering personalized workout programs. The Sports Center also includes a tennis court and a padel court located on the Marina Jetty.
Diving Center
Situated near the arrival jetty, the 5-Star Diving Center provides easy sea access for PADI-certified courses and guided excursions. Explore the underwater world and encounter turtles, sharks, eagle rays, and colorful reef fish with the help of certified PADI professionals.
Water Sports Center
The fully equipped Water Sports Center offers both motorized and non-motorized adventures, including Hobie-cat sailing, kayaking, kitesurfing, and windsurfing. Located on the island’s pristine beach, it’s perfect for guests looking to add excitement to their Maldivian getaway.
Private Dining Experiences
Guests can indulge in unique private dining experiences set in the most intimate and romantic spots around the island, enjoying exquisite culinary creations against the stunning natural backdrop of the Maldives.
