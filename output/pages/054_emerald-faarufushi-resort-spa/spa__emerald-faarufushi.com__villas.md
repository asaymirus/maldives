Villas
Championing a minimalistic, sophisticated design which incorporates natural elements of stone and wood, all villas were built with the intention of integrating with the surrounding environment. The villas are indulgently understated: Sleek, modern, deliciously relaxing. Perched either on the white sandy beach or directly on the water, all 80 villas are carefully curated havens with a breathtaking Indian Ocean view. Thoughtful luxuries abound; from flat satellite TVs, king size beds, walk-in and “under the stars” showers, bathtubs, generous wardrobes, room-controlled air conditioning and high-speed Wi-Fi available throughout the Resort, seventy of the villas have their own private pool overlooking the ocean.
