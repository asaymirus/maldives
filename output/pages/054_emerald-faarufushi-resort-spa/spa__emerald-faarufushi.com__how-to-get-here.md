How to Get Here
The Resort is reachable by a scenic 45 minutes seaplane journey from the Malé airport or, alternatively, 40 minutes domestic flight to the Ifuru Domestic Airport and 5 minutes speedboat to Emerald Faarufushi Resort & Spa (particularly convenient to easily connect with the Malé airport in case of evening’s international flights).
For arrival, same-day seaplane transfers can be arranged for international flights landing at the airport on or before 03:30 PM (Malé Time). For departure, same-day seaplane transfers can be arranged for international flights departing from the airport on or after 09:00 AM (Malé Time).
