EMERALD EXPERIENCES
Diving Center
Located near the arrival jetty with very easy access to the sea for PADI courses or excursions, the Resorts’ 5 Star Diving Center offers a wide range of activities that will allow guests to immerse themselves in the underwater world. Discover turtles, spotted eagle rays, napoleon, sharks and schools of colorful tropical fish with our trained divers. It also offers the support of certified PADI professionals for guests willing to obtain the PADI license.
