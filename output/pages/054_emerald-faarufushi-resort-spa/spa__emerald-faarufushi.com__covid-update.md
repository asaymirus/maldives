Covid-19 Update Details
Dear Guests, Emerald Faarufushi Resort & Spa ensures a Covid free, paradisal island safe for families and couples alike to return to and experience the luxury of travel again.
The Resort is set on 7-hectares of serene private island land and enveloped by 100-hectares lagoon, and offers to all guests
space comfort and privacy
. Boasting 80 villas, of which 38 on the Beach and 42 Overwater villas, each villa offers a private outdoor deck with direct access to the Indian Ocean, a lounge area and private pools. The endless azure blue sea, golden sand and green outdoor space ensures guests will truly feel like they’re on their own paradise island.
Wild at heart, Emerald Faarufushi Resort & Spa encapsulates
barefoot elegance
at its finest while paying homage to the thriving Maldivian environment that surrounds the resort.
Following both the health and hygiene standards set by the World Health Organization (WHO) and the Maldivian Government, all guests will have their temperature checked on arrival and departure, luggage surfaces will be disinfected, and
all staff members of Emerald Faarufushi have been vaccinated
and are serologically tested for Covid-19 every 10 days and are wearing face masks at all times. Guests will be provided with a Hygiene Kit in every villa including their own face masks, hand sanitizer, disinfecting wipes and disposable gloves – these will also be readily available in public areas. There will be a mobile sterilization UV light disinfectant and electrostatic sprayer with grade disinfectants used to clean villas between check-ins as well as all public areas and supplies. A medical doctor and new dedicated Hygiene Manager will be on property and on hand to ensure the new procedures are monitored on a daily basis and that guests and staff are safe and comfortable. Furthermore, a
PCR testing facility will be available in-house at an additional charge for guests who require it.
In addition, Emerald Faarufushi is paying extra attention to all surfaces and resort's public areas including the Emerald SPA, Gym & Sports Centre, the dedicated Kids Club, the Beach Club, Diving Centre and Water Sports Centre.
Operating on a Deluxe all-inclusive basis, Emerald Faarufushi is home to five atmospheric concept restaurants covering international cuisine from every continent and seating has been rearranged to ensure social distancing is effortless. For those that would rather relax in the privacy of their villa, in-room dining is available from resort butlers who will ensure guests are receiving the highest quality and safety service.
From stunning Island wide spaces to breathtaking views and excellent services,
we are delighted to meet and exceed expectations at all times and to welcome our beloved guests back with open arms!
Feel the “Natural Freedom” again with spacious facilities, close proximity to nature and safety for all guests!
Please don’t hesitate to contact us, should you have any questions –
reservations@emerald-faarufushi.com
Thank you for your continued support.
Emerald Faarufushi Resort & Spa
