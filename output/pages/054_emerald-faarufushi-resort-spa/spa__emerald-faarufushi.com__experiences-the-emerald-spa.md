EMERALD EXPERIENCES
The Emerald Spa
Considered an oasis of the soul, The Emerald SPA is located in the tropical nest of the island and offers world-class treatments from Thai and Balinese cultures. The contemporary architecture stands out for the way it blends into the natural surroundings. The Spa boasts 8 air-conditioned huts, a steam room, a relaxation area with plunge pools, a yoga pavilion and manicure and pedicure stations. Come here to escape your mind and give your body the break it deserves.
