EMERALD EXPERIENCES
Water Sports Center
Emerald Faarufushi’s fully-equipped water sports centre is the place to go when you need a break from relaxation. Located right on the stunning white sandy beach, it is accessible to any guest eager to try out Hobie-cat, kayak and windsurf gear under the supervision of our Waters Sport Team. Motorized water sports – like jet skis – are also available at extra charge.
