Activities for the Whole Family
For Parents: Serenity at The Emerald Spa
Slip into a world of calm while your children are happily entertained.
Nestled in the island’s lush greenery, The Emerald Spa offers Thai and Balinese treatments in peaceful garden huts, complete with a yoga pavilion, steam room, and serene plunge pools—perfect for recharging body and mind.
‍
For Kids: Play and Learn at the Dolphin Kids Club
While you unwind, your little ones dive into days filled with magic.
From age 3 to 12, children are guided by trained multilingual staff through fun, educational activities—arts and crafts, games, and outdoor adventures—in a vibrant and secure space made just for them.
‍
‍
For the Adventurous: Dive and Play in the Indian Ocean
Share the thrill or take turns exploring the sea! Our 5-Star Diving Center offers family-friendly snorkeling, beginner dive courses, and guided underwater excursions. Prefer to stay above the
surface? Enjoy kayaking, paddleboarding, or even a Hobie Cat ride as a family—or let the teens ride the waves with jet skis.

Gourmet Dining for Every Taste
Authentic flavors, global variety, and something for everyone—from curious little eaters to seasoned food lovers.
At Emerald Faarufushi, dining is more than a meal—it’s a journey. Thanks to our innovative Deluxe All-Inclusive concept, your family
can explore a world of cuisine crafted with the purest ingredients and faithful to the traditions they come from.
From sunny breakfast spreads to starlit dinners, every outlet offers a unique taste experience:
Aqua Restaurant – Mediterranean flavors served all day in an elegant island setting
Beach Club Grill – Laid-back beachside lunches with grilled specialties
Carnivorous Restaurant – A bold dinner spot for meat lovers
Le Asiatique – Teppanyaki Restaurant – A dinner show of sizzling Asian flavors
Mediterraneo Restaurant – An elevated overwater dining experience for dinner with a view
Sunset Pool & Bar and Eclipse Bar – For light bites, refreshing drinks, and golden hour moments
Whether it’s a romantic dinner for two while the kids are at the club, or a shared family meal with toes in the sand, dining at Emerald
Faarufushi is always an experience to savor.
Your Island Home, Made for Family
Spacious, stylish, and steps from the sea—perfect for making memories together.

FamilyBeach Villas with Pool
Our
Family Beach Villas with Pool
are designed for togetherness and comfort. Each villa features a serene master bedroom, a cozy kids’ bedroom, and two bathrooms for ease and privacy. Step outside to a covered patio, an open lounge area, and your own private infinity pool, all framed by lush greenery with direct access to the soft white sands of Emerald Faarufushi’s beach.
With room to relax and space to connect, it’s the ideal island escape for families who want both adventure and downtime—together, but never crowded.
‍
Presidential Beach Villa
Set between lush greenery and soft white sands, the Presidential Beach Villa offers 351 sqm of elegant space with vaulted ceilings, open-plan living and dining areas, and expansive windows that invite the outside in.
Enjoy a private 12x5m infinity pool, jacuzzi, outdoor dining space, and direct beach access—perfect for unforgettable family moments in total privacy.
‍
‍
Water Villas
While our Water Villas offer unparalleled privacy and stunning views—perfect for couples or honeymooners—they’re generally not designed forfamilies, with limited space for extra beds. Families seeking spacious, kid-friendly accommodations will find their perfect home in our Family Beach Villas and Presidential Villa.

Caring for the Island We Call Home
At Emerald Faarufushi, we’re committed to protecting the natural beauty that makes this paradise so special. From energy-saving technologies to marine conservation programs, our resort embraces sustainable practices that preserve the environment for generations to come.
Families can explore, learn, and grow with us—whether it’s through our eco-friendly initiatives or guided activities that celebrate the Maldives’ unique ecosystem. Because the best memories are made in a place that cares as much as you do.
