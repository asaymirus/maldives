DESCRIPTION
Sun Siyam Vilu Reef – where luxury and decadence meet in the heart of the Maldives. Get ready for pure indulgence: cool, contemporary spaces, stylish interiors, and beautiful beaches. Walk barefoot along the sand and watch the palm trees as they gently lean into the breeze. Here at Sun Siyam Vilu Reef, the days are long and sunny and the nights are a time of laughter – the perfect recipe for a luxury holiday in the Maldives. Relax on the beach, indulge in decadent spa treatments, and travel the world with your tastebuds.
Stay relaxed as you indulge in the ultimate luxury at Sun Siyam Vilu Reef. This 5-star beach resort will bring you so much closer to the unparalleled beauty of the Maldives. Immerse yourself in this paradise island whilst you stay in one of the luxury villas – whether you’re overlooking your own private pool, the lagoons, the beach, or the ocean – you are guaranteed to wake up to beautiful views every day.
Whatever you choose to do on your luxury holiday in the Maldives, you will make the most of it at Sun Siyam Vilu Reef.

Dining
The Aqua
A lively gastro-hub where you can rest and refuel from morning until night. Start with a glorious breakfast buffet, including piping hot pastries fresh from the bakery, then head back for lunch and dinner for Western classics and Maldivian house specials.
The Well Done
Fresh fish and seafood is best enjoyed right on the beach, and it doesn’t get any fresher than this. All our herbs, spices and vegetables are grown at our very own organic farm, bringing flavours bursting to life in all of our dishes.
The Nautilus
At Nautilus, sit back and enjoy perfect Indian Ocean views while we mix your perfect cocktail. Take a dip in the lagoon between drinks, before resurfacing for another delicious sip. For evening sundowners, there’s no better seat on the island.
Positive Energy
Choose from sweet tropical shakes, ice cold cocktails and vitamin-packed smoothies, all served poolside and brought right to your lounger. With a menu designed to replenish your body and calm your mind, this is the place to beat the heat and keep cool in the shade.
The Wine Cellar
If you’ve always been curious about the art of the grape, join an expert sommelier in our cellar of fine wines. Swirl and sip on the best vintage treasures by candlelight, all perfectly paired with accompanying nibbles and bites.
The Spice
During the day, dine on rustic Italian fare, from crisp homemade pizzas to the perfect tiramisu. Come sundown, the space transforms into an Indian restaurant with authentic curries and freshly baked breads, with a weekly buffet dinner taking guests on a culinary journey through the flavours of India.
Destination Dining
From a light lunch on the lagoon, a beach barbecue at sunset, or a romantic dinner date on your own private terrace, no request is too big for our team. This is your time together, so share with us your vision of what you want to eat and where, and we’ll make it happen.

Highlights From The Resort
Wellness & Spa
With a considered approach to wellness, our team embrace Eastern traditions whilst offering the best in contemporary Western practices. Book a treatment with one of our specialist practitioners and choose from therapeutic aromatherapy massages and traditional Ayurvedic rituals, or indulgent flower baths and scrubs to leave you glowing. Then, seek solitude in our hydrotherapy area, and enjoy a steam and soak as you relax further into holiday bliss.
Recreation
If you’re looking to get up and off of the sun lounger, sign up for one of the many daily activities on offer. Hit the courts for a game of tennis or badminton, or stretch out in a private yoga class. After sundown, the resort comes to life with DJs, live music and the beat of BoduBeru drums, or head to the beach to catch a film under the stars.
Diving
Whether it’s your first dive or your hundredth, our Sun Diving School will have a class to suit you. Explore our house reef, just 30 metres from the shoreline, or venture further to discover underwater caves and coral-covered shipwrecks. And with courses created especially for kids, the whole family can dive right in together.
Excursions
Explore beyond our shores with an island expedition, and take your pick from sailing trips and sunset cruises aboard our catamaran or traditional dhoni. For an extra dose of adventure, pack a picnic to enjoy from a secret island spot, and see how many dolphins you can spot over lunch.
Water Sports
The irresistible waters of the Maldives offer something for everyone, whether you’re an adrenaline junkie or a beginner looking to take a skill home. Explore the lagoon by canoe, learn to windsurf like a pro, or keep it simple with donut rings and banana-boat rides.
Kids’ Club
Sun Aqua Vilu Reef is a Luxury Family-Friendly Resort Island in the Heart of the Maldives. We Have Activities Available for Guests of All Ages and Even a Babysitting Service for Any Parents Who Want a Bit of Time Alone. Our Kids’ Club Encourages Your Children to Make Friends, Join Fun Activities and Get to Know Fellow Junior Travelers From Around the World.
