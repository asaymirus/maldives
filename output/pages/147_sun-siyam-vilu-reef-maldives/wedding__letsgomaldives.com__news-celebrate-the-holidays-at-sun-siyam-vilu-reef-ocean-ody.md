This festive season, Sun Siyam Vilu Reef invites you to experience “Ocean Odyssey – Festive 2025”, from December 22, 2025, to January 5, 2026. The island comes alive with vibrant shows, fun activities, and surprises around every corner, creating a holiday to remember.
A Season of Celebration
Evenings sparkle with fire and LED dance performances and mesmerizing belly dance shows, while special festive events and raffles, including the Grand New Year’s Eve Raffle and Spa Giveaway, add extra excitement to the season.
Relax, Reconnect, and Unwind
Couples can escape to serenity with exclusive spa treatments and discounts, creating the perfect setting for quiet, intimate moments together. It’s a season to relax, recharge, and enjoy the stunning Maldivian surroundings.
Adventure and Discovery
For thrill-seekers, there’s plenty on offer. Try the Double Action Safari, a rare chance to see both whale sharks and manta rays, or take a spin on the Jet Car across the lagoon. Those curious about the underwater world can enjoy discounted “Try Scuba” sessions, perfect for first-time divers.
Fun for the Whole Family
Little ones are in for a treat with activities like face painting, coconut leaf art, and cooking classes, ensuring they have their own festive adventures. Families can enjoy both shared experiences and special moments tailored for children.
An Unforgettable Holiday in the Maldives
With its natural beauty, lively entertainment, and curated festive experiences, Sun Siyam Vilu Reef turns the holiday season into a celebration of joy, relaxation, and adventure. Whether it’s dazzling shows, quiet spa moments, or underwater discoveries, every day offers something special.
