Diving at Sun Siyam Vilu Reef
Whether it’s your first dive or your hundredth, our SSI-certified dive centre will have a class to suit you. Explore our house reef, just 30 metres from the shoreline, or venture further to discover underwater caves and coral-covered shipwrecks. And with courses created especially for kids, the whole family can dive right in together.
