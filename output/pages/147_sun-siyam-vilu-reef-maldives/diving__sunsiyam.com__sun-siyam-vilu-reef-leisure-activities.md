Facilities at Sun Siyam Vilu Reef
Scuba diving or watching dolphins from a secret island spot in Maldives
Sun Siyam Vilu Reef invites you to a wealth of daily activities, with live music after dark
Watersports
The lagoon here has a rhythm to it: the hum of a jet ski, the snap of a kite catching wind, the dip of a paddle finding its stroke. However fast or slow you like to move through water, Sun Siyam Vilu Reef gives you the room (and the gear) to do exactly that.
Excursions
Where do you want to be today? A dhoni gliding past at sunset, a nearby sandbank with your name on it, a local island market buzzing with colour? Vilu Reef's excursions aren't so much a schedule as a set of very good options. Pick one, or several, and let the day take shape from there.
Diving
It's quiet down there, in a way that's hard to describe until you've felt it, just the sound of your own breath and a reef doing exactly what it's always done. At Sun Siyam Vilu Reef, our dive centre opens that door for you, first-timers and veterans alike, with trips out to some of the most untouched coral in the atoll.
