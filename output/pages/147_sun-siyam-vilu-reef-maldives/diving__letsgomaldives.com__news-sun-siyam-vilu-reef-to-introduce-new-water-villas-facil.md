We would like to inform you that
Sun Siyam Vilu Reef
will begin facility enhancement works to develop a
new collection of Water Villas
from
1 September 2025 to 31 March 2026
. This expansion is part of the resort’s ongoing efforts to enhance guest offerings while preserving the island’s natural charm and tranquility.
Scope of Work:
Development of new Water Villas along a designated jetty
Infrastructure works to support the new villa category
Landscaping and finishing touches to harmonize with the environment
Impact on Guests:
Noise and visual disturbance will be
minimal
, confined to the project area
All existing villas, restaurants, spa, diving center, and recreational facilities
remain fully operational
Guests continue to enjoy full access to the
lagoon, beaches, and water sports
Booking Relocation & Alternatives:
Guests will not be booked into villas directly adjacent to the project site
Alternative arrangements available within the resort or at sister properties, subject to availability
Additional comfort measures include
resort credit
,
20% spa discount
, and
20% discount on selected excursions
For your clients seeking stays during this period, LETS GO MALDIVES can
assist with bookings, villa selection, and alternative arrangements
to ensure a seamless experience.
