A Serene Sanctuary
Massages & Eastern Therapies
Our massages offer the perfect balance of relaxation and rejuvenation. Surrender to the skill of a 4-hand massage, where two therapists work in seamless harmony. Explore the deep tension relief of Swedish techniques, or let the fluid movements of traditional Thai massage stretch and restore. Every treatment is designed to transport you to a place of understated luxury and calm.
Spa Packages
Our exclusive spa packages have been thoughtfully crafted to offer the perfect escape for both couples and solo travellers. We even have a 7-day journey, where each day unveils a new, curated experience - allowing you to sample the spa’s signature treatments.
Body Works
Fun fact: our skin is our largest organ. That's why we have a whole menu devoted to body scrubs, wraps, and masks - all using nutrient-rich ingredients like seaweed, clay, coffee, and coconut. If you have been basking in the sun, be sure to try our soothing Aloe Vera wrap.
Ayurvedic Traditions
Explore our Ayurveda menu, inspired by ancient Indian healing traditions dating back over 5,000 years. Each treatment harnesses the power of natural herbs and oils to restore balance, detoxify, and promote holistic well-being. Experience timeless therapies designed to rejuvenate the body, mind, and spirit.
How to Spa
Our spa is open daily from 9:00 AM to 7:00 PM. It is tucked away inland but near the shores of the beating heart of the resort.
Please consult our
Spa Menu
for a full list of treatments.
We try to accommodate walk-ins but booking in advance is always better. Our team will gladly show you around and help you choose the treatment that’s right for you. You can also book by phone: Contact the Spa at extension 344.
To avoid any cancellation charges, kindly notify us at least 6 hours prior to your scheduled appointment. Cancellations made within 6 hours of the appointment will incur a 50% charge, while cancellations without prior notice or no-shows will incur a 100% charge.
Please arrive 15 minutes before your scheduled treatment time. This allows you time to fill out our health assessment form and unwind. Late arrivals may result in a shortened treatment time if we are unable to reschedule the next guest.
For your comfort and safety:
We recommend not bringing jewelry or valuables to the spa. The spa is not responsible for lost or damaged personal items.
To maintain a peaceful environment, please leave electronic devices in your villa or set them to airplane mode.
Kindly speak quietly while in the spa to preserve its serene atmosphere.
Guests must be 12 years or older for our regular treatments and junior spa therapies are available upon request, provided a parent or legal guardian is present in the treatment room or joins in a shared session.
Our spa grounds are smoke-free.
We currently do not offer any complimentary spa facilities.
Please inform us of any health issues, allergies, or conditions that may affect your treatment. If you are pregnant or have given birth within the last 3 months, please notify our Spa reception. We offer pregnancy treatments for the second and third trimesters, as well as for women up to three months postpartum.
Avoid sunbathing immediately after your treatment.
Gentlemen: For optimal results, we recommend shaving before your treatment, but not immediately prior.
Please remove contact lenses before your facial treatment.
All treatments will be charged to your suite and appear on your account at check-out. Prices are listed in USD and include a 10% service charge, along with applicable government taxes.
