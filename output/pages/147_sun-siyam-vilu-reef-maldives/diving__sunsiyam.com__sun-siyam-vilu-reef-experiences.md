Sun Siyam Vilu Reef Must-Do
Pleasure, leisure, and memories to treasure — you can have it all.
From relaxing to heart-pounding, here is a rundown of our favourite activities Sun Siyam has to offer for every guest mindset.
Athireege Immersion
Step into the rich heritage of the Maldives at Sun Siyam Vilu Reef’s Athireege, meaning “the beach house” in the local language. This cultural haven offers a glimpse into traditional island life, where you can enjoy an authentic Maldivian breakfast or dinner right by the ocean and delve deep into the local culture.
Local Island Visit
Discover the heart of Maldivian island life with a visit to a local fishing village. Wander its sandy pathways, meet the friendly residents, and glimpse daily life through stops at the mosque, the school, and small shops filled with handcrafted treasures. It’s a culture-rich journey that reveals the soul of the Maldives beyond the shoreline.
Traditional Fishing
Set out at dawn or dusk aboard a traditional Dhoni and try hand-reel fishing, just as it’s been done in the Maldives for generations. With a bit of luck, you might reel in a red snapper or catch sight of dolphins and other marine life as the reef comes to life around you.
Jet Ski Safari
Hop onto a jet ski and power across the lagoon for a fast, refreshing way to explore the open water. It’s a great activity for friends, offering the chance to ride side-by-side, pick up speed, and even jet out to a nearby sandbank for a quick escape.
Jet Car Cruise
Take control of our stylish jet car and skim across crystal-clear waters for an experience that feels straight out of the future. As the engine powers up and the spray lifts around you, the ocean opens wide, inviting you to chase the thrill across its shimmering surface.
Maldivian Cooking Class
Master the artistry of Maldivian cuisine as you transition from the prep table to the sizzle of the pan. Enjoy your creations during a shared lunch in the enchanting heart of Sun Siyam Vilu Reef, ensuring you take a flavorful piece of the Maldives home with you.
Tranquility Wellness Journey
As the island sun begins its descent, retreat to a private outdoor sensory journey designed to deepen your connection. From the warm embrace of a Foot Bath Ritual to the expert touch of an Asian Blend massage, every moment is crafted to melt away the world outside.
Mixology Class
Step behind the bar and transform into a creator with our 'Shake, Stir, Sip' Mixology Class. Designed as a 'Bartending for Non-Bartenders' experience, this masterclass invites you to master the delicate balance of selected cocktails under the guidance of our expert mixologists.
Waves of Adventure and Calm
A journey of adventure and stillness. Pair a high-speed Jet Ski Safari and private snorkelling with a 60-minute Full Body Massage on the shore. From the vibrant reef to the rhythmic touch of our therapists, find your perfect equilibrium.
Adventure Trip
An underwater voyage to the Atoll’s most legendary sites. Explore the Coral Garden, Turtle Point, and Nurse Shark Point, encountering stingrays and vibrant reef life along the way. Your journey culminates with a private sanctuary lunch on a pristine, sun-drenched sandbank.
East West Flash Wave Vibes
A day of limitless discovery awaits. Begin with a private breakfast, embrace the thrill of the ocean on a Jet Ski, and master the art of cooking with our chefs. Following a restful couple's massage, your evening culminates with a private movie under the stars.
Local Island Discovery
Experience the best of underwater life in one breathtaking journey. Dive into the world of Whale Sharks, Mantas, and Turtles before enjoying a gourmet lunch and a local island escape.

LEISURE ACTIVITIES
From night dives to freediving, from jetskiing to sunset cruises, we have it all. Truly.
Watersports
The lagoon here has a rhythm to it: the hum of a jet ski, the snap of a kite catching wind, the dip of a paddle finding its stroke. However fast or slow you like to move through water, Sun Siyam Vilu Reef gives you the room (and the gear) to do exactly that.
Excursions
Where do you want to be today? A dhoni gliding past at sunset, a nearby sandbank with your name on it, a local island market buzzing with colour? Vilu Reef's excursions aren't so much a schedule as a set of very good options. Pick one, or several, and let the day take shape from there.
Diving
It's quiet down there, in a way that's hard to describe until you've felt it, just the sound of your own breath and a reef doing exactly what it's always done. At Sun Siyam Vilu Reef, our dive centre opens that door for you, first-timers and veterans alike, with trips out to some of the most untouched coral in the atoll.
