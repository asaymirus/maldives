Four Seasons Resort Maldives at Landaa Giraavaru is an unspoilt natural wilderness in the remote Baa Atoll – a proposed UNESCO World Biosphere Reserve. With vast accommodation compounds and four isolated restaurants hidden down dense jungle paths, the island invites a real sense of discovery. The Resort’s 102 thatched bungalows and villas are located on the beach, over the lagoon or within the lush interior; features include spacious gardens, private pools, outdoor living rooms and sea-viewing lofts.
The Spa and Ayurvedic Retreat offers holistic therapies from Ayurvedic Immersions to Chakra Blessings. Two Ayurvedic Physicians guide guests through complimentary consultations and will customise programmes during their stay. Swim with turtles and dolphins in the pristine two-kilometre lagoon and from June to October, snorkel with mass gatherings of manta rays and whale sharks at nearby Hanifaru Bay (restrictions apply). The Marine Discovery Centre showcases pioneering research and conservation projects – with plenty of opportunities for guests to get involved. Four diverse restaurants – all with ayurvedic options – are complemented by numerous speciality options, from ‘floating’ platforms to candle-lit feasts on remote sandbanks.
