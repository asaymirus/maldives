NEW EXPERIENCES: BEACH BUMS CORNER
Looking for the Best Beach Spot in the Maldives?
Imagine stepping onto soft white sands, the turquoise waters of the Maldives sparkling endlessly before you, and knowing that the best spot on the beach has been reserved just for you. At Beach Bums Corner, this isn’t a dream… It’s your next unforgettable Maldives experience.
You and your better-half arrive to find a beautifully decorated setup waiting for you. Two chilled young coconut drink, Kurumba glisten in the sun, the perfect refreshment for a tropical island afternoon… A professional photographer ready to capture the quiet moment of connection… The gentle ocean breeze, the warmth of the sun, and the sound of waves create the perfect backdrop for a private, romantic escape designed just for you.
Our guests, Mr. Christian and Mrs. Marylin, were the first to try this exclusive experience. It was their first-time tasting Kurumba, and they instantly fell in love with its refreshing tropical flavour. They enjoyed a romantic sundowner with overlooking waves shimmering like liquid gold, and every detail of the moment felt as if it had been crafted just for them.
THE BEACH BUM CORNER
Beach Bums Corner is designed for couples seeking a private, intimate experience. Whether it’s a leisurely afternoon picnic, a relaxing ocean-side chill, or a romantic sundowner. You can choose from three exclusive time slots:
• 12:30 – 14:30
• 14:30 – 16:30
• 17:30 – 19:30
Every detail is curated to make your beach moment perfect. From the setup and refreshments to the natural beauty surrounding you. A professional photographer capturing the moments you, yourselves didn’t realize was worth freezing, you can take home more than just a story. You will have a visual keepsake of your paradisiacal beach romance.
Limited slots available on each day.
Whether it’s your first trip to the Maldives or your fiftieth or more, Beach Bums Corner offers a unique couple’s retreat. Step into your very own beach spot – sip, savour, and slow down.
