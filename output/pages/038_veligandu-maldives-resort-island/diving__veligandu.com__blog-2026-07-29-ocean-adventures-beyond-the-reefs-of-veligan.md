Excursions & Watersports
BEYOND THE REEF OF VELIGANDU
Discover Ocean Adventures
From racing across turquoise lagoons to swimming alongside gentle nurse sharks, discover the ocean adventures that reveal another side of the Maldives.
For many travellers, the Maldives begins with the view. Crystal-clear lagoons. Endless shades of blue. Tiny sandbanks scattered across the Indian Ocean. But the real adventure begins when you leave the shore.
Beyond the reef surrounding Veligandu Maldives lies a world waiting to be explored, where every journey across the water brings the possibility of discovering something extraordinary. Whether you are chasing dolphins across the horizon or snorkelling alongside gentle nurse sharks, these experiences offer a different perspective of the Maldives, one that is shaped by freedom, discovery, and unforgettable wildlife encounters.
“Some adventures begin the moment you leave the island behind.”
Ride Into The Blue
There is something incredibly liberating about riding a Jet Ski across the open ocean.
The lagoon gradually gives way to deeper shades of blue as the island becomes a distant silhouette behind you. The only sounds are the gentle rush of the water, the sea breeze, and the excitement of not knowing what you might discover next.
It is easy to see why Jet Skiing has become the most popular water sport at Veligandu Maldives. Far more than an adrenaline-filled ride, it is an opportunity to explore beyond the island. Guided by our experienced Water sports Team, guests venture across the surrounding waters in search of dolphins, manta rays, eagle rays, and other remarkable marine life that occasionally appears along the way.
Many journeys also include a visit to one of the nearby sandbanks, where endless white sand emerges from the turquoise sea, creating a secluded setting that feels almost untouched. Standing alone on a sandbank, surrounded by nothing but crystal-clear water in every direction, is one of those uniquely Maldivian moments that stays with you forever.
“The best views of the Maldives are often found beyond the shoreline.”
Exploring Responsibly
One question we are often asked is whether Jet Skis damage the coral reef… The answer is no.
Just like a speedboat, Jet Skis operate in deeper navigational waters and do not travel across shallow coral reefs. Every excursion follows carefully planned routes designed to respect the surrounding marine environment while allowing guests to experience the beauty of the Indian Ocean responsibly.
Adventure and environmental responsibility go hand in hand, ensuring these spectacular waters can continue to be enjoyed for generations to come.
DISCOVER MOTORIZED WATER SPORTS
An Encounter You Will Never Forget
If there is one excursion that continues to capture the imagination of travellers visiting the Maldives, it is Nurse Shark Snorkelling.
Although sharks are often misunderstood, nurse sharks are among the ocean’s gentlest inhabitants. Calm by nature and accustomed to sharing their environment with snorkelers, they offer guests the rare opportunity to observe these magnificent animals up close in their natural habitat.
As you slip into the warm, crystal-clear water, the excitement is immediate. A graceful silhouette appears beneath the surface. Then another. Within moments, several nurse sharks glide effortlessly below, moving with remarkable elegance and complete calm. It is an encounter that quickly replaces apprehension with admiration.
Suitable for couples, families, and first-time snorkelers alike, this experience has become one of the most sought-after excursions at Veligandu Maldives and one of the most popular snorkelling adventures in the Maldives.
For underwater photographers, it offers an incredible opportunity to capture unforgettable images. For everyone else, it is simply one of those moments that reminds us how extraordinary the natural world can be.
“The greatest adventures often begin with curiosity.”

During a Nurse Shark Snorkelling excursion, every encounter is unique, with no two experiences ever exactly the same. That is the beauty of exploring the Indian Ocean. The ocean follows no script. Every excursion is shaped by nature, making each adventure feel personal, authentic, and wonderfully unpredictable.
