Lifestyle at Veligandu
MOMENTS WE LOVE TO CREATE FOR YOU
Recommendations from Your Lifestyle Host Team
Here at Veligandu, we don’t call ourselves “butlers.” We are Lifestyle Hosts. Your dedicated point of contact throughout your stay. From the moment you arrive until the moment you leave, we are here to help you with anything you may need. We make your bookings, personalise your experiences, and stay in touch so your holiday flows smoothly with infinite moments to treasure for a lifetime.
Every villa at Veligandu has its own Lifestyle Host, and one of us will be honoured to be yours during your whole stay.
We have helped many guests from honeymooners to adventure seekers, peace-lovers, and families celebrating special moments. Today, we want to share our personal recommendations so you can make the most of your time in paradise.
FOR HONEYMOONERS & GUESTS CELEBRATING LOVE
If you are here for your honeymoon, an anniversary, a birthday, or simply a romantic escape, this natural island is a dream come true.
Our Top Recommendations
Villas & Bed Decorations
Let our amazing Housekeeping Team surprise you with beautiful decorations that make your celebration extra special.
Luxury Sunset Yacht Cruise
Nothing feels more magical than watching dolphins dance beside your
private yacht
as the sun paints the sky. You may even choose to enjoy a romantic dinner on the yacht, followed by stargazing on the open ocean.
Signature Private Dinners
If you prefer an
intimate dinner
on land, we recommend:
▪ Crystal Dinner at the Champagne Pavilion
▪ Veli Dinner (a beautiful sand-dining experience)
▪ Seaside Serenity Dinner by the ocean
Romantic Spa Packages
Try our Full Moon Magic or
Romantic Retreat
packages at the spa. Those are perfect for couples seeking connection and relaxation.
Sandbank Experience with Instagrameable or personal keepsake shots
Join our VMoment team for a drone and underwater shoot at the nearby sandbank. You will also get to explore the famous Wonder Rock, home to yellow reef fish, turtles, and incredible marine life. These moments become memories you will keep forever.
FOR ADVENTURE SEEKERS
If you love excitement, Veligandu is one of the best places in the Maldives to feed your adventurous spirit.
Our Top Recommendations
Whale Shark, Manta & Nurse Shark Snorkeling
Go on
snorkelling adventures
. Meet the gentle giants of the ocean. Swim with mantas and nurse sharks in their natural habitat.
Adventure Jet Ski Ride Or Jet Blade Experience
Chase dolphins, turtles, eagle rays and feel the thrill of speed on crystal-clear water. If you have never tried it before, don’t worry, our Ocean
Water Sports
team will guide you.
Two-Tank Morning Diving Trip
Experience Dives
to the manta cleaning station. Sitting at the bottom while mantas glide above your head feels like being in an underwater theatre. They may gently brush pass you. Just remember, look, admire, but never touch.
House Reef Snorkelling
Our house reef is one of the most vibrant in the Maldives. From turtles and eagle rays to moray eels and colourful fish, you can meet them all. The best spot starts from the famous Veligandu Sandbank, on the south end of the island.
Teppanyaki Experience
For a culinary adventure, join
Teppanyaki experience
with Chef Francis for lunch or dinner. He is funny, entertaining, and extremely skilled. You will laugh until your stomach hurts.
Hearts & Harvest (Wine Dinner) with Faris
Our Sommelier Faris not only knows wine; he also knows how to make dinner fun with
exciting smelling games
. To know what this smelling game is, join him at his show. We guarantee you will not be disappointed.
FOR GUESTS SEEKING PEACE & PURE RELAXATION
If your dream is to slow down and breathe, Veligandu is your sanctuary.
We Recommend:
▪ A slow walk to the sandbank at sunrise
▪ A quiet afternoon at the spa
▪ A gentle snorkelling session at our vibrant house reef, one of the best house reefs in the Maldives.
▪ A toast to the sunset at the 360-degree Ocean Platform- Sundowner SeaScape in the middle of the ocean
▪ A peaceful, heartful dinner, like an intimate dinner on the beach or a Seaside Serenity Dinner by the ocean.
Sometimes, the best activity is simply doing nothing… Just listening to the waves and letting the island bring calm back into your life.

LET US CREATE YOUR PERFECT VELIGANDU STORY
As your Lifestyle Hosts, our joy comes from seeing you happy. Whether you are here to celebrate love, chase adventure, or simply rest, we are here to guide you every step of the way.
Veligandu is not just an island. It is a feeling. And we can’t wait to help you experience it in your own special way.
