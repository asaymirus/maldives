TASTING MAGIC
Introducing New Experiences at Veligandu Maldives
By Guruh Rickyan Soekriono, F&B Manager
Since I joined Veligandu in November 2024, I have been inspired daily by the joy our guests feel when discovering the island’s wonders. On this day, September 01, 2025, I am thrilled to introduce two extraordinary dining experiences designed to delight the senses, spark connection, and create memories that linger long after the waves fade.
Hot POT & Grill by Shore:
Where Fire Meets Flavor, and Memories Simmer
Inspired by Asia’s rich culinary heritage​
Stepping onto Dhonveli Beach for the first Hot Pot & Grill by Shore trial evening, I was greeted by the scent of firewood and the subtle spices in the air. The open-air setting, with the sun dipping below the horizon, set the stage for something unforgettable.
Some repeat guests on the island were invited to take part in the culinary journey: premium seafood, tender meats, and vibrant vegetables are brought to your table, ready to be seasoned and grilled your way.
“Between the gentle crackle of the fire and the laughter of the invitees, the
moment felt intimate, celebratory, and utterly unforgettable.”
This is more than a meal…it is connection, indulgence, and a shared celebration of flavors beneath the Maldivian stars.

SereniTea Royal Float:
Afternoon High Tea Reimagined
Naturally luxurious, perfectly private
A few days later, I experienced the SereniTea Royal Float in the private luxury of Ocean Pool Villa. Floating gently on the water, I sipped fine loose-leaf teas, fresh tropical juice, Kurumba, and a 375ml bottle of Ernest Rapeneau Champagne while enjoying a curated selection of sweet and savory bites.
The surprise cake from our pastry chef Nandana made the moment feel truly magical. The serenity of the experience made me wish my wife was there with me…to share this intimate afternoon and imagined her smiling beside me.
“Floating in quiet luxury, with only the soft ripple of water around me, I felt a moment of pure peace.”
This is the essence of the SereniTea Royal Float: private indulgence, attention to every detail, and the chance to savor life’s simple pleasures.

A New Chapter of Veligandu Dining Experiences
Both Hot & Grill by Shore and the SereniTea Royal Float are designed to bring a fresh, immersive dimension to your stay. Watching guest’s eyes widen with surprise, their laughter mingles with the waves, and their hearts sink into pure relaxation reminds me why we create these moments. Whether it is the thrill of grilling your favorite seafood on the beach or the calm luxury of floating high tea, these experiences are made to be remembered.
