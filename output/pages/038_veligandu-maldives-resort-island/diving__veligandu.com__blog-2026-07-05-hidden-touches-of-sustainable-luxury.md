Sustainability
THE THOUGHTFUL SIDE OF SUSTAINABLE LUXURY
Sustainability Hidden in Plain Sight at Veligandu Maldives
When guests arrive at Veligandu Maldives, they are welcomed into villas designed for comfort, relaxation, and effortless luxury. Crisp linens, beautifully presented amenities, refreshing air-conditioned spaces, and attentive housekeeping all come together to create a seamless island experience.
What many guests may not realise is that some of our most meaningful sustainability initiatives are quietly woven into these everyday comforts.
In fact, one of the items guests interact with most often is also one of the most surprising.
“Sometimes, sustainability is hidden in plain sight.”
Looks Like Plastic, But Isn't
Take a closer look at the bottles found in
your villa
bathroom.
At first glance, many guests assume they are traditional plastic amenity bottles. The sleek appearance certainly suggests it. However, these bottles are actually made from cornstarch-based materials, offering a more environmentally responsible alternative while maintaining the premium look and feel expected from a luxury resort.
Inside each bottle, guests discover more than just essentials. Veligandu Maldives’ signature shower gel, shampoo, conditioner, handwash, bubble bath, bath salt and body lotions are infused with V-Lustre, a bespoke fragrance created exclusively for Veligandu Maldives and found nowhere else in the world.
The ingredients used are free from harmful chemicals, fully biodegradable, and carefully crafted to reflect our commitment to responsible hospitality. Guests who fall in love with the fragrance of V-Lustre can even take a piece of the experience home. The products are available for purchase at our Boutique Shop, allowing guests to continue enjoying the signature scent of Veligandu Maldives long after their stay.
Small Details, Big Difference
Sustainability extends beyond amenities.
Our dry amenities like dental kits, shaving kits, vanity kits, shower caps, hair combs, loofahs, and sewing kits are made from corn-starch-based materials and packaged in Kraft paper, offering a biodegradable and more sustainable alternative to conventional packaging.
While these items may appear simple, they reflect a thoughtful commitment to reducing unnecessary waste without compromising guest comfort or convenience.
” Sustainability is not always visible to the eye, but it is felt in every thoughtful choice we make.”
Partnering With Our Guests
Some of the most effective sustainability initiatives involve collaboration between our team and our guests.
As part of our linen and towel programme, bed linens are changed every second day as standard practice. Guests wishing to have their towels replaced are invited to place them on the floor, while towels left hanging are considered for reuse.
This simple approach helps reduce water and energy consumption while maintaining comfort and convenience throughout the stay.
“Sustainability works best when we take part in it together.”
Smart Technology For A Greener Stay
Behind the scenes, technology also plays an important role. 

Our villas are equipped with automatic sensor-operated systems connected to the air conditioning units. When sliding doors are opened, the air conditioning automatically switches off, preventing unnecessary energy consumption while maintaining operational efficiency. Working quietly in the background, this system helps reduce energy consumption across the island every day while maintaining uninterrupted guest comfort.
Caring For Linens, Responsibly​
Luxury hospitality requires exceptional care for every linen, towel, and fabric used throughout the resort. To support this while reducing resource consumption, our laundry facilities utilise steam boiler technology. This system improves washing performance, shortens washing cycles, reduces energy usage, and helps extend the lifespan of our linens. The result is an efficient process that supports both operational excellence and environmental responsibility.

Sustainable Luxury Through Everyday Actions
At Veligandu Maldives, sustainability is not defined by a single initiative. Rather, it is reflected in hundreds of thoughtful decisions made every day by dedicated team members across the island.
From biodegradable amenities and signature V-Lustre products to water-saving practices, energy-efficient technology, and responsible laundry operations, every initiative contributes to a more sustainable future.
Many of these efforts happen quietly behind the scenes. Yet together, they help preserve the natural beauty of the Maldives while ensuring that every guest enjoys the comfort, care, and hospitality that define the Veligandu Maldives experience.
Experience Veligandu Maldives
Whether it is the signature fragrance of V-Lustre the comfort of your villa, or the thoughtful details that support a more sustainable stay, every experience at Veligandu Maldives is shaped by purposeful choices. 

During your stay, take a moment to look a little closer. You may discover that some of the most meaningful sustainability initiatives are the ones hiding in plain sight.
