ACTIVE HOLIDAY IN THE MALDIVES: MOVE, PLAY & RELAX
How to stay active while embracing slow island life in the Maldives
What if staying active on holiday did not have to mean rushing from one workout to the next? At Veligandu Maldives, mornings have their own rhythm. The sun rises slowly over the Indian Ocean. The island wakes gently. There is nowhere you need to rush to and no reason to watch the clock.
And yet, you can still move.
Perhaps your day begins with a few yoga poses as the sea breeze passes by. Later, you pick up a racket for a game of Padel Tennis or Pickleball. By Saturday, you might find yourself joining a HIIT workout on the beach, with nothing but sand, sea and sky around you.
This is fitness in the Maldives, the Veligandu Maldives way.
Move At Your Own Pace
An active holiday does not always have to mean intense workouts or structured routines. Sometimes, it is simply about moving in ways that feel good.
Imagine starting your day by taking a deep breath of fresh sea air while moving through a series of grounding morning stretches.
Fitness on our island is never a chore; it is an uplifting part of the daily journey. Walking into our
VFitness Center
, you are immediately greeted by bright natural light and panoramic views of turquoise water, instantly inspiring your daily workout. Whether you prefer moving at your own pace or working alongside our dedicated private gym instructors, every routine feels completely tailored to your personal journey.
Throughout the week, our guest community comes together to share these moments of movement. On Sundays, guests gather under the guidance of our in-house expert to master 9 Yoga Poses to Practice Daily, carrying those grounding techniques back home.
Mondays spark friendly motivation during the VFitness Center Challenge, while Saturdays bring high energy as group HIIT Workouts on the beach leave everyone feeling refreshed and renewed. On Wednesdays, sports lovers assemble for our weekly Padel Tennis and Pickleball Challenge, turning regular workouts into memorable social highlights.
There is always something to look forward to, but nothing you have to rush towards.
When Sports Becomes Play
Some of the best workouts do not feel like workouts at all. Picture the gentle sound of ocean waves echoing softly in the background as you step onto a crisp court wrapped in lush greenery.
There is a unique kind of magic that happens when you mix competitive sports with the relaxed pace of an island hideaway. As the morning tropical heat softens, players gather near the courts to share a love for fast moving games. Padel Tennis and Pickleball have quickly become guest favorites here, bringing travelers together for spontaneous matches and lively rallies. For those seeking something fresh, TYPTI offers a fun and novel twist on classic racket play, inviting players of every skill level to enjoy long, rewarding rallies.
As afternoon turns to evening, the energy naturally shifts toward the oceanfront. Guests meet on soft white sands for casual games of Beach Volleyball as the sky painted in pink and orange hues. When you prefer a cooler setting, our air-conditioned Indoor Game Zone welcomes you inside. Here, hours glide by easily over friendly matches of Ping Pong, Foosball, Billiards, Pool, Darts, and Table Shuffleboard, making every afternoon feel like a playful retreat.
