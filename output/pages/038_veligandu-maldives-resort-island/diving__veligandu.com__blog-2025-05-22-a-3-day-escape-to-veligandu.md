Travel Guide
A 3-Day Escape to Veligandu
There are escapes, and then there is Veligandu. In just three days, the natural island (not artificial) paradise in the North Ari Atoll offers a soulful reset – one that stays with you long after you return to your busy lives back at home.
Day One: Arrival and Rejuvenation
A scenic 20-minute seaplane ride or a serene journey aboard
Veligandu’s
luxury yacht brings you to the island.
You’re
greeted by swaying palms, the rhythm of
Bodu Beru
drums, a chilled
Veli Refresher welcome drink
, and a cool towel – the perfect feel to start your tropical island holiday.
After a brief island tour, settle into your beach or overwater
villa – your private sanctuary.
Enjoy lunch barefoot at Dhonveli Buffet Restaurant before surrendering to the soothing hands of our spa therapists at the over-water
Himeyn Spa
.
As the sun dips,
take in golden hues from your deck or the waterfront bars.
Later, delight in an in-villa dinner ordered easily via the Veligandu Mobile App to slip into that truly vacation feeling.
Tip: To make the most of your day, we recommend an early check-in between 11 am and 12 noon.
Day Two: Discover and Dine in Style
Early rises can begin the day with private sunrise yoga on the 360° deck as the island stirs awake. Then enjoy a floating
in-villa breakfast
in your private pool before heading out to snorkel Veligandu’s vibrant house reef—perhaps even meet a sea turtle or two.
Tip: If you decide to sleep in, you can still enjoy a
floating breakfast
in your villa or a delightful breakfast in bed or on your deck. Then snorkle or catch some sun to relax.
Have a long lunch at
Madivaru à la carte restaurant
and discover a celebration of flavour inspired by the Silk Road. Have sunset dolphin cruise. End the day with a fine dining experience at Raalhu, where the cuisine is as elevated as the view.
Day 3: Reflect, Indulge & Celebrate
Start with breakfast at Dhonveli, then try stand-up paddleboarding or
simply
soak in the stillness from your villa.
After a light lunch at Thundi Bar, capture your holiday with a professional photoshoot by
VMoments
.
End your 3- day escape with a signature V
eli
Dinner
where the furniture is dug
into
the sand to celebrate the best of Veligandu. Multiple menues are available including plant based and wine pairing.
Return Home Perfectly Relaxed
Wake up relaxed, have a leisurely breakfast. Go for another swim or snorkelling. Visit the gift shop for souvenirs and get that last-minute manta ray necklace or matching him & her jewellery.
Your scenic seaplane will take you back to the airport with your island hosts waving goodbye.  The flight back home is a perfect time to plan your next vacation to Veligandu – your home in the Maldives.
