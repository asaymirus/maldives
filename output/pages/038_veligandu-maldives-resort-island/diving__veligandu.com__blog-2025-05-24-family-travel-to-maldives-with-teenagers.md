Travel Guide
Fun  Maldives Vacations for Families with Teens
The Veligandu Guide for Guests Aged 13–17 Years
Planning a family escape to the Maldives? At Veligandu Maldives, teens aged 14–17 can look forward to their island adventure filled with sun, sea, and unforgettable memories.
Stay in Style
The ultimate family and teen retreat is the two-bedroom, overwater
Sunset or Sunrise Ocean Pool Villas
. With direct access to the lagoon, each bedroom with its own private infinity pool and epic sunset and sunrise views, it’s the perfect mix of privacy and luxury. It’s great to share it with your family or besties – plenty of space for late-night chats, pool dips, Instagram and tiktok worthy snaps.
Veligandu offers beach accommodations and over-water villas in contemporary and classic Maldivian charm to experience different oceanfront living ambiences.
Adventures Beneath Ocean
Get ready to explore life beneath the waves. Our Discover Scuba Diving program is a safe and exciting way to try diving for the first time with Veligandu’s professional Ocean Dive Center dive team.
Prefer to stay closer to the surface? Join our
Discover Snorkelling
experience. You’ll learn the basics and dive into our
Manta Talk and Snorkeling session
— where a Marine Biologist shares fascinating insights before meeting these gentle giants in their natural Indian Ocean habitat. A parent must accompany younger teens up to age 16 – making it great for quality time with mum or dad.
Stay Active & Have Fun
Love a good game?
Try Padel Tennis
,  the fastest-growing sport in the world! It’s easy to learn and a blast to play with family or new island friends. Non-moterrized water sports, Jetsking with parents and tube rides adds to great water fun.
Relax, Wellness & Recharge
Need some downtime? Teens can melt away stress with a calming Indian Head & Scalp Massage at the Himeyn Spa or experience something new with a Sound Immersion session with the pranic and sound healing power of
Himalayan singing bowls
that will help release unwanted thoughts and emotions and amplify positive energy.
Teens who love yoga are welcome to join the Yoga sessions, including the sunrise yoga at the 360-platform at the east-facing pool beach lagoon.
At the bar, a young coconut ‘Kurumba’ is a must-taste to hydrate island style. There’s a range of mocktails and smoothies for guests of all ages.
Culinary Journeys for your Teen
The theme nights, authentic cuisine and the different types of fresh and ethically sourced food in the Dhonveli buffet will delight any teen. Here guests can make their own raw pressed juice for breakfast.
Teens can travel the Silk Road tasting cuisine from the Middle East and the Mediterranean to the Orient with a visit to Madivaru Silk Restaurant &
Madivaru Teppankai Grill
.
Celebrate Island Style
From birthdays to graduations, we make special moments shine. Join Sunday’s Starlit Sizzles – a beach BBQ under the stars – or chill out with Afternoon High Tea under the palms. For the ultimate escape, book a Sandbank Picnic for a barefoot lunch in the middle of the ocean and snorkel or cruise for half a day with family and friends aboard the fabulous Gulf Craft motor yacht Gadha 1 with a Luxury Yacht Escapade.
Teens celebrating sweet 16s’ and quinceaneras can opt for family photo and drone shoots with Veligandu’s awe-inspiring photo locations and get a beautiful printed album done by Veligandus
professional photo studio VMoments
.
At Veligandu, every teenager can find their kind of magic, great stories for their social media, and nature-immersive experiences and breathe the crisp, salty Maldivian island air. 

Come for the views, stay for the memories.
