VELIGANDUS' SENSORY WINE JOURNEY
Whispers of the Vine by Sommelier, Hassan Faris
On the powdery white sands of Veligandu Maldives Resort Island, where turquoise waters lap gently against overwater villas and the sun casts molten gold across the lagoon, a new journey awaits. One that transcends the ordinary and elevates wine tasting to an art of the senses. Here, amidst swaying palm trees and the soft whispers of the ocean, wine becomes more than a drink. It becomes a story, a voyage across vineyards, terroirs, and centuries of craftsmanship.
I am Hassan Faris, the in-house Sommelier, and it is my pleasure to unveil an experience thoughtfully designed to immerse both the curious newcomer and the seasoned enthusiast in the very soul of wine such as The Blindfolded Wine Tasting Masterclass on Friday, April 17, 2026 and
regularly scheduled
wine tasting experiences at Veligandu Island.
A WINE JOURNEY BEYOND SIGHT
Picture yourself stepping into a softly illuminated
Raalhu Bar
, the scent of oak, ripe berries, and sun-warmed grapes mingling with the faint salty breeze drifting in from the lagoon. A silk blindfold rests lightly over your eyes, and suddenly the world of visual distractions dissolves. What remains is an intimate communion of smell, taste, and touch.
Guided by my voice and a carefully curated selection of the world’s most expressive varietals, each sip becomes a revelation. The bold, velvety Malbec, the crisp elegance of Pinot Grigio, the aromatic vivacity of Sauvignon Blanc. Every drop tells the story of its vineyard, the soil that nurtured it, and the climate that shaped its character.
Deprived of sight, your senses sharpen: the subtle minerality on the tongue, the faintest floral note in the aroma, the gentle weight of tannins… all become vivid and unforgettable.
At the culmination of your exploration, you receive a personalized certificate signed by me, a refined keepsake to commemorate this extraordinary sensory voyage.
MONTHLY WINE EDITIONS: HONOURING ICONIC GRAPES
Each month, we celebrate a grape with a dedicated session:
World Malbec Day Edition:
Savour the full-bodied intensity and layered complexity of Malbec, and learn how altitude, soil, and climate intertwine to create its signature depth.
International Pinot Grigio Day Edition:
Explore the crisp elegance and delicate aromatics of Pinot Grigio, contrasting its subtle notes with other whites for a heightened sensory experience.
International Sauvignon Blanc Day Edition:
Delve into the vibrant, aromatic world of Sauvignon Blanc, discovering how environment and cultivation craft its distinctive character.
Every tasting invites you to not only compare varietals but to deepen your understanding of the meticulous artistry behind each bottle. “A journey from vineyard to glass.”

WHY THIS EXPERIENCE STANDS APART?
This is not a traditional wine tasting. Here, each session is a deliberate, sensory exploration, an intimate invitation to travel through vineyards without leaving the island. You are guided not only by expertise but by passion. The passion of a sommelier who delights in revealing the subtle interplay of earth, climate, and tradition that defines every varietal.
It is an experience that lingers, much like the final note of a finely aged wine, leaving your senses awakened and your memory enriched.
