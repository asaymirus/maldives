Overview
A gorgeous  Maldivian paradise, Veligandu Island Resort & Spa is a beautiful and elegant place. With a total of just 100 villas there is an exclusive feel to the resort, making it the perfect choice if you’re coming for a romantic getaway with your partner.  There’s a good range of facilities offering watersports and land sports as well as excursions and a games room – there’s even a scuba centre on site if you’d like to go diving in the stunning crystal clear water.

Location
Veligandu Island sits in the North Ari Atoll, surrounded by a stunning lagoon, world-class house reef and silky soft beach.
Transfer options:
- 20 minute seaplane transfer from Velana International Airport directly to the island
- 60 minute speedboat transfer from Velana International Airport to the island
Veligandu operates +1 hour ahead of Male for daylight saving time, your onwards seaplane to Male Airport will reflect Male time.

The following room types are available:
Beach Villas –
71sqm situated on east and north ends of the island and surrounded by vegetation and a few steps away from the beach. Each unit has direct beach access, a private wooden porch with comfortable furnishings and a partially open-air bathroom. Sleeps 3 adults or 2 adults and 2 children
Jacuzzi Beach Villas –
86sqm spacious sunrise villas located on the beach and surrounded by vegetation. Each unit has a private wooden porch with comfortable furnishings and an open-air Jacuzzi area with sun lounges. Sleeps 3 adults (12 years plus)
Ocean Jacuzzi Villas –
86sqm units suspended over the water, located on the eastern side of the island with breathtaking sunrise views. Each unit has, a private deck with comfortable furnishings, an open-air Jacuzzi area with sun lounges and a Jacuzzi facing the ocean, a partially open-air bathroom and immediate access to the house reef. Sleeps 3 adults or 2 adults and 2 children
Sunset Jacuzzi Beach Villas –
86sqm spacious sunset sea view villas, located on the beachfront surrounded by vegetation. Each unit has a private wooden porch with comfortable furnishings and an open-air Jacuzzi area with sun lounges. Sleeps 3 adults or 2 adults and 2 children
Sunset Ocean Villas –
102sqm villas located over the water with uninterrupted sea views. Each unit has a private eck with comfortable furnishings and an overwater hammock net, an open-air bathroom with a bathtub facing the ocean and wooden chairs and table, a partially open-air bathroom and direct access to the reef. Sleeps 3 adults (12 years plus)
Ocean Pool Villas
- 124sqm villas situated on the northern part of the island, perched over the water with sunset views. Each unit has a private wooden deck with furnishings and a swimming pool, an indoor bathroom with an ocean view bathtub and direct access to the reef. Sleeps 3 adults or 2 adults and 2 children
Sunset Ocean Pool Villas
- 152sqm villas perched over the water with sunset views. Each unit has a private wooden deck with furnishings and a swimming pool, an indoor bathroom with an ocean view bathtub and direct access to the house reef. Sleeps 3 adults (12 years plus)
Ocean Pool Villas - Two-Bedroom
- 276sqm villas perched over the water, situated on the northeast corner of the island with sunrise views. Each unit has two bedrooms, two indoor bathrooms, a large living area connecting the two bedrooms, two wooden decks with comfortable furnishings and two swimming pools. Sleeps 3 adults or 2 adults and 2 children
Sunset Ocean Pool Villa - Two-Bedroom
- 319sqm villas perched over the water, situated in the north tip of the island with sunset views. Each unit has two bedrooms, two indoor bathrooms with ocean view bathtubs a large living area connecting the two bedrooms, two wooden decks with comfortable furnishings and two swimming pools and direct access to the house reef. Sleeps 6 adults. (12 years plus)
