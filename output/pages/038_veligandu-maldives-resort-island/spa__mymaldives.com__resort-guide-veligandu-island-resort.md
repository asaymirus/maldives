Located in North Ari Atoll, a 20 minute Seaplane ride from the airport, Veligandu is a four star and offers a perfect small island getaway holiday, including an All Inclusive Plan option.
The resort has garnered impressive reviews for its exceptional service and amenities. Guest experiences often highlight the resort’s tranquil environment and personalized attention. On Tripadvisor, Veligandu Island Resort & Spa enjoys a top rating, reflecting its status as a cherished destination among travelers.
You’ll enjoy the convenience of modern amenities blended seamlessly with natural scenery. Free WiFi, multiple dining options, and leisure facilities await your stay.
Accommodation
At Veligandu Island Resort & Spa, you’ll find a variety of villas designed to suit your preferences for comfort and luxury. Each villa type offers a unique experience, tailored to maximize your enjoyment of the island paradise.
Beach Villa
: Your Beach Villa invites you to step onto the soft, powdery sand from your private beach access. You can enjoy modern amenities including air conditioning, a flatscreen TV, and a coffee/tea maker, all complemented by a serene seating area.
Jacuzzi Beach Villa
: For an added touch of luxury, the Jacuzzi Beach Villa features your own Jacuzzi, perfect for relaxation. These villas include blackout curtains and bathrobes to enhance your private, cozy island getaway.
Water Villa
: The Water Villa offers an over-the-water experience with a private balcony that gifts breathtaking views of the crystal-clear ocean. High-speed internet and room service are readily available, ensuring comfort and connection throughout your stay.
Jacuzzi Water Villa
: Elevate your stay with a Jacuzzi Water Villa, where indulgence meets the horizon. You’ll have a personal Jacuzzi, air conditioning, and the memorable experience of a walk-in shower overlooking the sea.
Throughout your stay, housekeeping ensures your villa is pristine, while essentials like bottled water, a clothes rack, an iron, and sofa provide the conveniences of home in a tropical setting. Your villa is equipped with a telephone and sofa for added comfort, allowing you to create your perfect island moment.
Dining
At Veligandu Island Resort & Spa, your taste buds are in for a treat with an array of dining experiences. Start your day with a complimentary breakfast, featuring a range of delicious options to fuel a day of island activities. For a refreshing interlude, sip on complimentary tea and instant coffee available throughout the resort.
The main restaurant presents a sumptuous spread, offering a kid-friendly buffet that caters to all ages, while special diet menus are provided to meet diverse dietary needs.
If you prefer to dine with the sound of waves in the background, the beach offers unique Destination Dining experiences. Enjoy the Bubbly Sunset Love Bed event, a private dinner setting on the beach paired with a glass of champagne, or choose the Veli Dinner for an intimate meal under the stars.
At the poolside bar, relax with a craft cocktail or select from an extensive list of wine / champagne. The snack bar nearby is perfect for light bites whenever you crave something quick and tasty. And to celebrate your arrival, delight in a complimentary welcome drink, a perfect start to your velvety smooth vacation.
Spa and Wellness
At Veligandu Island Resort & Spa, your relaxation and rejuvenation are taken to heart. The resort’s luxurious Duniye Spa offers an oasis of calm, where you can indulge in a variety of treatments designed to soothe your mind, body, and soul. From the calming ambiance to the skillful touch of the therapists, every detail is crafted to provide you with an unparalleled spa experience.
You can choose from an array of massages, including full body massage, couples massage, and focused treatments like foot massage, head massage, and even a rejuvenating hand massage. The spa’s tailored services also extend to facial treatments, manicure, and pedicure sessions, allowing for full-body care. The holistic approach ensures that your spa journey will leave you feeling refreshed and revitalized.
For a truly immersive wellness experience, take part in yoga classes set against the stunning backdrop of the Maldives. The resort boasts an infinity pool that merges seamlessly with the ocean, providing a tranquil setting for relaxation or a leisurely swim. For a more private dip, you may enjoy the adult pool or the plunge pool. If you’re looking to unwind with a view, the pool with a view offers a breathtaking perspective of the island’s natural beauty.
You can round off your day of pampering with a visit to the bar / lounge, which allows you to enjoy refreshing beverages in a serene setting.
Excursions and Activities
At Veligandu Island Resort & Spa, you’ll discover an array of exciting excursions and activities to enhance your stay.
Choose from water-based adventures such as snorkeling, where you’re invited to explore the vibrant house reef teeming with marine life, or indulge in windsurfing and canoeing for a touch of exhilaration on the waves.
For a relaxed evening, embark on a sunset cruise, a perfect romantic setting especially if you’re on your honeymoon.
Snorkeling:
Dive into clear waters and observe the underwater beauty.
Windsurfing/Canoeing:
Catch the breeze or paddle serenely in the lagoon.

Enjoy breathtaking views as the sun dips below the horizon.
The resort also provides a host of on-land activities to keep you engaged. Challenge yourself with an array of games ranging from table tennis to darts in the game room, or participate in the evening entertainment organized by the talented staff. For the little ones, a safe kids pool and children’s television networks ensure they stay entertained.

With 24-hour security, airport transportation, and concierge services, your safety and comfort are well taken care of, ensuring a carefree adventure during your stay at Veligandu Island Resort & Spa.
