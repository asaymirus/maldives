DESCRIPTION
A story woven with magic, adventure, and the enduring bonds of love and community
Envisioned as a wellbeing island in the Maldives, JOALI BEING is centred around “weightlessness”, a feeling of freedom, lightness and joy. This experiential destination is based on biophilic principles, facilitating the natural flow of energy from coast to coast. Discover a collection of inspiring spaces designed for reflection, exploration and growth, created in collaboration with knowledgeable wellbeing experts.
JOALI BEING aims to be the beginning of a transformative, educational and holistic wellbeing journey, combining evidence-based science and time-honoured traditions. In-house wellbeing consultants guide guests on a journey of self-discovery and renewal, while they are surrounded by the calming effects of nature and local wildlife.
