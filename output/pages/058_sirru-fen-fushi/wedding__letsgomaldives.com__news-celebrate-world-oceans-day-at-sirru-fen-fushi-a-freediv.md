One Ocean, One Breath
1–10 June 2025

Learn the art of freediving
with professional instructors
✅
Explore one of the Maldives’ largest house reefs
– a breathtaking 9km stretch of vibrant marine life
✅
Earn a certified freediver title
✅
Join advanced courses
upon assessment
✅
Immerse in 3–4 days of mindful training
(2 hours per day) that fosters connection with nature and inner calm

A perfect add-on for your clients’ Maldives holiday
