DESCRIPTION
Fairmont Maldives set on the idyllic island of Sirru Fen Fushi, within the Shaviyani Atoll and taking its name from the local expression for “secret water island”. Sirru Fen Fushi glistens like a pearl, its powder white sand beach fringed with swaying palms is kissed by the turquoise waters of the 600-hectare lagoon that gives way to the tranquil blues of the Indian Ocean and a never-ending horizon.
The 120 luxury villas are a seamless blend of art and nature with natural materials, indigenous decorations and native patterns creating a harmonious yet stimulating eclectic charm. Choose from hideaway Beach Villas set in the mangroves, stunning Water Villas perched over the Indian Ocean, or castaway Tented Jungle Villas. Ranging in size from one to three bedrooms, each villa is designed for exceptional comfort and relaxation with private pools, modern amenities, and more.
Guests can enjoy haute local and international cuisine at the resort’s contemporary dining outlets such as the international all-day dining Raha Market; Kata, the over-water Japanese restaurant, sushi bar and sea lounge, where chefs brings creative flair and Maldivian twists to Japanese dishes; and the over-water fine dining destination, Azure, where fresh seafood and fish-market displays of daily local catches are complemented by a fantastic selection of Old and New World wines. The striking all-bamboo poolside bar, Onu Onu, offers a selection of classic cocktails and light bites paired to gorgeous views of the horizon.
A range of unrivalled experiences is on offer at the Fairmont Maldives Sirru Fen Fushi. Sail away to a nearby deserted island for a picnic “just for two”, join a sunset dolphin cruise aboard the resort’s own private yacht, or go on a cultural visit to a near-by local community, and much more. Thai boxing, tennis, football and the 200-metre long swimming pool that bisects the island will keep fitness aficionados active. Inspired by its setting the renowned Willow Stream Spa draws on the powers of natural elements to tailor treatments that soothe and heal body, mind and soul. Families are well catered for too with a Kid’s Club, a play garden, and the Maldives’ only dedicated Teen Town.

Dining
RAHA MARKET
A rich bazaar of flavours, this marketplace offers something for every guest, from traditional Maldivian dishes and cuisine from across Asia to healthy gourmet meals served all day long.
AZURE
Azure draws on the culinary riches of the seas for a fine-dining experience at this tastefully appointed restaurant. The creative seafood menu is paired to fine wines, fresh ocean breezes, and stunning views of the Indian Ocean.
ONU ONU
Created by Balinese artisans, our striking poolside bar gets its name from the Maldivian word for bamboo. A laid-back space, perfect for lounging over exotic drinks while basking in the sun and watching gorgeous views of our prime beachfront and the infinite cerulean lagoon.
In-Villa Dining
Enjoy a barbecue in the private sala of your villa with a personal chef to grill perfectly marinated meats and vegetables. Leave the finer details for your butler to handle as you sit back and enjoy a full white-linen service. Or turn chef for the evening and grill a meal for you and the family.
Kata
Kata, the resort’s signature Japanese restaurant, celebrates Asian flavors with a modern menu offering an array of sushi and sashimi appetizers freshly made from the chef’s counter, robata yaki and a variety of seafood options cooked in agemono style.
