SUB AQUA Divecenter
Angaga Island Resort & Spa is the home of SUB AQUA DiveCenter in Maldives. You will find a full range of daily diving trips and PADI courses for all divers, whether at beginner level or more experienced.
Following an orientation dive, if you are an experienced diver, you can make unlimited dives on our house reef with your buddy (without guide). A guide can be provided for less experienced divers or for those without a dive buddy.
With famous dive sites such as Angaga Thila and Kuda Ra Thila all within easy reach by half day trip. Angaga Thila is just 7 minutes away by boat! There is also the famous whale shark trip where you can try your chances to dive with the biggest fish in the sea.
• Nitrox free of charge (to certified Nitrox divers)
• Scuba diving programs for non-certified divers
• PADI scuba diving courses from beginner to more experienced
• Get up to 10% discount by pre-booking online – more information about available diving packages and courses at
www.subaqua-divecenter.com
• Unlimited house reef diving
• Experience FLUO-diving and night diving
• Experience ‘manta madness’ during manta season
• Equipment rental available at daily and weekly rates
• Small diving groups
• Best variety of dive sites – house reef, outer reefs, underwater pinnacles, swim-throughs, caverns, channel dives and wreck dives
• Most popular dive sites in Maldives!
Enjoy some of the most varied diving in the world from SUB AQUA DiveCenter Angaga, located beside the Superior Water Bungalow jetty.
