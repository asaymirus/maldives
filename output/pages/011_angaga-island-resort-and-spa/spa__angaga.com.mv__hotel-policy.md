Hotel Policy
Located in south Ari Atoll, Angaga is reachable by a 25-30 minutes scenic seaplane flight over the Indian Ocean. International flight details must be provided 7 days prior to arrival for arranging the transfers to the resort.
Seaplane Transfers to Angaga are available during daylight hours only. Guests arriving at Male’ international airport on an international flight after 15:30 will be brought to the resort by domestic transfer.
Every effort will be made to arrange your resort transfer within 2 hours of arriving. Departure guests will be transferred to the airport at least 3 hours in advance of the scheduled international flight departure time.
– Check-in time is at 02:00 PM and Check-out time is 12:00 Noon
– Rates are per room per night for double occupancy
– Rates are inclusive to 10% service charge, 17% GST and Government
– Green Tax of USD12.00 per person per night will be charged additional
– Rates are dynamic and availability is subject to change.
– Room and rate is valid for 24 hours. Past this time the room will be released, and new rates may apply
– Additional beds: Maximum 01 (90*190cm)
Child Policy
0.0 to 1.99 years – Full complimentary transfer, baby cot and meals.
2.00 to 11.99 Years – 50% off adult seaplane transfers.
Transfers
Angaga Island is located a 25-minute seaplane journey from Male International Airport. Return transfers are USD 400 net per adult and per Child USD 200.
Every effort will be made to arrange your resort transfer within 2 hours of checkin in to Maldivian Seaplane . Departing guests will be transferred to the airport at least 3 hours in advance of the scheduled international flight departure time.
Seaplane Transfers to Angaga are available during daylight hours only. Guests arriving at Male’ international airport on an international flight after 15:30 will be brought to the resort by domestic transfer (latest available flight at 23:00 hrs departure from Male’ international airport).
