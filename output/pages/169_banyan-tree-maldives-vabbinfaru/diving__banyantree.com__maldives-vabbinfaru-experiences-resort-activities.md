Navigating Aquatic Adventures
Plan a trip to the Maldives with Banyan Tree Vabbinfaru's cruise packages. Activity Guide
Kahan'bu Cruise
Embark on a journey of tropical discovery with the Kahan'bu Catamaran. Aboard this luxurious cruise, you'll navigate the azure waters of the Maldives – be it a private morning of fishing, a sunrise breakfast cruise, or a full-day private cruise that includes lunch and sunset cocktails prepared by a personal butler. The Kahan'bu Catamaran will moor at different snorkelling spots throughout the day.

Diving and PADI courses
Equipped with a top class facility, get yourself dive-certified in one of the world’s most spectacular underwater forests. All our courses can be completed during your stay on the island.
We even have Bubblemaker courses to make diving a family activity. With access to Marine Protected Areas, where the native marine life roam free, you will be sure that your first dive experience will be one to remember.

Night Snorkelling
Night snorkelling offers the once in a lifetime opportunity to explore the marine environment under the moonlight.
The experience is like no other as you will witness the magical glow of bioluminescent plankton as you move through the water, surrounded by unique nocturnal marine creatures in their natural habitat.
Save the Reef
Join our marine biologists to protect vibrant coral reefs in the Maldives. Snorkel above coral gardens, identify and remove harmful species, and connect deeply with the ocean’s beauty—a journey of discovery, preservation, and purpose in an underwater sanctuary.
The primary goal of this activity is to remove two harmful starfish species, the Crown of Thorns and Pin-cushions, which prey on corals. In addition to targeting these starfish, we also collect  \n plastics and other hazardous materials we encounter.
Dolphin Cruise
Watching a pod of dolphins playfully swim up close is probably one of the most magical moments of your holiday. Relax aboard the resort's dolphin cruise with drinks and canapés as you wait to capture the picture-perfect moment.
