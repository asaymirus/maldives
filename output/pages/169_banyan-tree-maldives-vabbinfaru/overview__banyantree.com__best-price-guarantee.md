Best Price Guarantee
20% OFF ANY LOWER PRICE
When you book your stay directly on www.banyantree.com, you'll be guaranteed the best room rate. If you find a lower price elsewhere, we will match that rate and give you an additional 20% off.
CLAIMS PROCESS
Step 1
Found a lower price within 24 hours of booking on banyantree.com? Fill in and submit our online claims form to request for 20% off.
Step 2
We will review and process your refund claim and get back to you within 2 working days.
You can also enjoy:
• Flexible cancellation terms
• Access to a dedicated reservation specialist to assist with your booking.
Frequently Asked Questions
What rates are eligible for a claim?
The Best Price Guarantee applies only to prices advertised and available to the general public on websites other than
www.banyantree.com
at the time of verification.
Best Price Guarantee is available only for the exact same room type and view.
Rates must match the terms of the comparison rate, such as pre-payment, deposit and number of guests.
What if the rates are in a different currency?
If the compared rate is in a different currency from the currency reflected on
www.banyantree.com
, we will convert the competing rate into the same currency rate offered on
www.banyantree.com
. Best Price Guarantee will not apply if the variation in the applicable rate is less than or equal to 3% after conversion to the equivalent currency.
How do I submit a claim?
A claim must be submitted
through this form
within 24 hours after making the reservation through
www.banyantree.com
and at least 72 hours before the scheduled arrival.
During the submission of the online claim form, you are required to provide the Website URL and upload a screenshot that capture the lower rate was found with the same price conditions.
We will contact you within 2 business days after receiving your claim. Terms & Conditions apply.
Do I need to make a reservation in order to submit a claim?
Yes. A reservation is required through
www.banyantree.com
in order to submit a claim.
Is there a cancellation fee?
Best Price Guarantee does not apply to reservations booked through other websites. We are not responsible for any fees associated with the cancellation of reservation booked on websites other than
www.banyantree.com
.
Terms & Conditions
For a valid claim under the Best Price Guarantee, it must fulfil the terms & conditions listed below.
The Best Price Guarantee does not apply to:
Taxes, gratuities or fees imposed by local authorities, or any charges or fees that may apply during your stay.
Existing reservations not booked via the Banyan Tree brand website (
www.banyantree.com
)
Hotel package rates (which include combinations of accommodation, dining, spa packages, limousine transfers etc.)
Unpublished or negotiated rates (membership, partnership and rewards programs, corporate discounts, negotiated rates, group, convention, meeting and convention or other rates that are not available to the general public)
Rates offered to direct mail or email solicitations
The Best Price Guarantee will be suspended during the times when
www.banyantree.com
is not available due to an outrage, a technical issue or circumstance beyond such other conditions as we may implement or stipulate from time to time in accordance with our Banyan Tree Group prevailing practices.
Prepaid Reservations
In the event a fully prepaid rate is booked through
www.banyantree.com
, we will credit the difference in the form of hotel credits to be consumed during your stay at the resorts.
