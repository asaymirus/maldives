Naiboli Bar
Cocktails with stunning views
All-day dining selections are available at the open-air Naiboli Bar, including an extensive range of delicious appetizers, light meals, and cocktails. Set on the sandy beachfront, you can enjoy the ocean breeze in a setting reflecting the best of Maldivian charm. Come Friday, watch the mesmerizing Bodu Beru, a drumming music performance by lively musicians in traditional costumes.
