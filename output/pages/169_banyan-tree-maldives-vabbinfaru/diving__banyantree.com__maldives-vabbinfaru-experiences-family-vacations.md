Family Vacations
Family vacations with memories to last a lifetime
Spend time with those closest to you and delight in your choice of various resort activities on offer to make your stay most memorable. Liaise with your personal Resort Host to create a bespoke experience catering to your family’s preferences.

Diving for Kids
Introduce your eight or nine-year-old to diving through a fun bubble-making lesson in a shallow lagoon. Safe child-friendly scuba gear assures you of your child’s safety. Our certified instructors are on hand to make your child’s first diving experience absolutely fabulous.
Night Fishing
Learn age-old Maldivian fishing methods assisted by our experienced boat captain and his deck hand. Master the local hand line method and be amazed by what may be caught: snappers, jacks, barracudas, to mention but a few. Catch a sizeable fish and our chef will be more than willing to prepare and serve it for the lunch following day.
Join the Marine Conservation Programme
Learn about the amazing underwater world from the Marine Conservation Lab experts. See how the resort cares for the local wildlife and corals. Help protect a dazzling array of turtles, seabirds and coral communities.
Maldivian Cooking Class
Explore the local culture through its flavours and cuisine and let our Sous Chef take you and your loved ones on a delightful Maldivian culinary journey.
