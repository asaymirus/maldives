Hue Imperial City - UNESCO World Heritage Site
Just one-hour-drive from the resort, Hue Imperial City is a majestic testament to the Vietnam’s rich history and culture. Designated as a UNESCO World Heritage Site, this historical gem offers visitors a glimpse into the splendours of Vietnamese tradition and craftsmanship.
READ MORE
Hoi An Ancient Town - UNESCO World Heritage Site
Hoi An is a symbol of tradition and rich cultural heritage even as the sun sets. Nestled along the Thu Bon River, this UNESCO World Heritage Site is a popular destination in Vietnam. It is known for its well-preserved architecture, vibrant markets and enchanting atmosphere.
READ MORE
My Son Sanctuary - UNESCO World Heritage Site
My Son Sanctuary is a UNESCO World Heritage Site that invites discerning travellers to discover the rich history of the Cham civilisation. Set in a lush valley, this archaeological site near Banyan Tree Lang Co features ancient Hindu temples that offer a unique glimpse into Vietnam’s cultural heritage.
Bach Ma National Park
Established in 1991, Bach Ma is recognised as one of Indochina's biodiversity centres for its ecological diversification and is a must-visit spot for wildlife enthusiasts. Bach Ma's peak stands at 1,450 metres in height and is covered by clouds all year round.
READ MORE
Lap An Lagoon - Lang Co Bay
Lap An Lagoon, framed by the towering mountains of Bach Ma National Park and the magnificent Lang Co Bay, is widely regarded as one of the most breathtaking landscapes in Hue, Vietnam. This hidden gem boasts pristine waters, verdant surroundings and a tranquil atmosphere that feels worlds away from the pace of city life.
