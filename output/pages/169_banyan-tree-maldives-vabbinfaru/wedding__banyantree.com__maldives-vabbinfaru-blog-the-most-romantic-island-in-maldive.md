Frequently Asked Questions
What is the most romantic resort in the Maldives?
Banyan Tree Vabbinfaru is consistently ranked among the most romantic resorts in the Maldives — a private island of beachfront pool villas with no shared beach, no resort crowds, and a Wellbeing Sanctuary programme centred on Rest, Connection, and Mindfulness for couples.
Is Banyan Tree Vabbinfaru good for a honeymoon?
Yes. Banyan Tree Vabbinfaru is one of the Maldives' most established honeymoon destinations, operating for 30 years as a private island resort with beachfront pool villas, a Banyan Tree Wellbeing Sanctuary, and unique
romantic experiences in Maldives
including couples Sense of Place spa rituals, and private dining at Madi Hiyaa. Speedboat transfer from Malé airport takes 25 minutes — no seaplane required.
What unique romantic experiences does Banyan Tree Vabbinfaru offer?
Unique romantic experiences at Vabbinfaru include: bioluminescent night snorkelling in naturally phosphorescent Indian Ocean waters; couples coral planting under a resident marine biologist, with the coral documented and traceable on return visits; sunset dining at Madi Hiyaa, the Maldives' only overwater yakitori restaurant and 2024 Prix Versailles award winner; beachfront sound healing rituals at dusk; and Sense of Place couples spa treatments using locally sourced Maldivian ingredients. None of these experiences are available at any other resort in the Maldives.
How far is Banyan Tree Vabbinfaru from Male airport?
Banyan Tree Vabbinfaru is approximately 25 minutes by speedboat from Velana International Airport, Male. No domestic flight or seaplane transfer is required — most couples arrive on the island within the same afternoon as their international flight lands.
Can we return to Banyan Tree Vabbinfaru for our wedding anniversary?
Yes. Vabbinfaru is specifically designed to reward return visits. The Banyan Tree Wellbeing Passport allows couples to continue their wellness programme across all Banyan Tree Wellbeing Sanctuary properties globally, with each stay building on the last. The Marine Lab's coral planting programme creates a living, trackable record of your first visit that continues to grow between anniversaries.
