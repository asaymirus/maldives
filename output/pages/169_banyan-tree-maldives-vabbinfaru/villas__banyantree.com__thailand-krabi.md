The White Pearl
Create your own perfect day in paradise with a private excursion onboard The White Pearl – our traditional longtail boat, a storied element of local life in the coastal communities throughout southern Thailand in generations past through to modern times.
Unique in its design, our longtail boat – named after the exquisite gem historically found throughout the waters of the Andaman Sea – provides the rustic feel of vessels that are prominently found around the region with bespoke touches to enhance your comfort during your day on the water.
Dive in and explore the diverse marine ecosystem or relax and take in the scenic vistas as you delight in an unforgettable experience amidst the coastal beauty of the Andaman Sea.

What natural landscape surrounds Tubkaek Beach where the resort is located?
Banyan Tree Krabi is set on a 10-acre beachfront plot on the northernmost point of secluded Tubkaek Beach, offering views over the Andaman Sea. Located adjacent to Dragon Crest Mountain within Hat Noppharat Thara-Mu Ko Phi Phi National Park, the resort is backed by the rolling green mountains of a conserved forest.
When is the best season clear views in Phang Nga Bay?
The best time to visit Banyan Tree Krabi is from November to April. Clear views are also possible during the rainy season (May to October), though they are intermittent and subject to changing weather conditions.

What complimentary water sports equipment is available at the beach?
Guests at Banyan Tree Krabi can enjoy water sports including Kayaking, Stand-up Paddleboarding, and Sailing. Beginner sailing classes are complimentary, while private sailing equipment use outside of scheduled classes is chargeable.
Does Saffron restaurant offer southern Thai cooking classes?

How close is the entrance to the Dragon Crest Mountain (Khao Ngon Nak) hiking trail?
The entrance to the Dragon Crest Mountain (Khao Ngon Nak) nature trail is located just 200 meters from the resort's entrance.
How long does it take to reach Hong Island by longtail boat from the resort?
Hong Island is located approximately 30 minutes away from Tubkaek Beach by traditional longtail boat.
