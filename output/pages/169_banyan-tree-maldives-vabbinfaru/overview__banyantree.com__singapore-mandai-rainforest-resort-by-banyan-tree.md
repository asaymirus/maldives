Where is Mandai Rainforest Resort by Banyan Tree located?
Mandai Rainforest Resort by Banyan Tree is located within Singapore’s Mandai Wildlife Reserve - a world-leading nature and wildlife destination with direct access to Singapore Zoo, Night Safari, River Wonders, Bird Paradise, and the brand-new Rainforest Wild Adventure. Convenient and easy access to these popular attractions offers guests immersive wildlife encounters and experiential learning journeys during their stay at the resort.
Does Mandai Rainforest Resort by Banyan Tree offer special packages that include attractions or experiences?
Mandai Rainforest Resort by Banyan Tree offers room packages with admission tickets to the Mandai Parks for an immersive native wildlife exploration.
There are also exclusive experiences available to young resort guests at the parks. For instance, the Wild Zoo Day Stay package includes a day of adventure and discovery where young ones can explore Singapore Zoo through guided tours and hands-on animal interaction at KidzWorld. Visit our

Does Mandai Rainforest Resort by Banyan Tree offer on-site parking for guests?
Each guest will be presented with 1 carpark coupon per room per day. Ticket is applicable to all room types. Please approach our Concierge for the carpark tickets and share your room number after checking-in.

We regret to inform you that the resort is not pet-friendly. We understand that pets are an important part of many families, and we sincerely apologize for any inconvenience caused.

Are special packages available during Singapore school holidays?
Yes, Mandai Rainforest Resort offers curated family stay packages aligned with Singapore’s school holiday periods. Designed for family nature getaways within the Mandai Wildlife Reserve, seasonal inclusions may feature Singapore wildlife park admission, in-resort family activities, or dining credits. Guests can check current availability on the resort’s official offers page.
What amenities are included with a stay in the Treehouse?
A stay in the Mandai Sanctuary or Rainforest Treehouse is thoughtfully appointed with daily breakfast and afternoon tea at Forage, a bottle of champagne upon arrival, and refreshed minibar offerings, all extended once per stay. Guests are also welcome to complimentary laundry or pressing services for up to five pieces during their time with us. Beyond these touches, each treehouse offers a private patio with sweeping reservoir or rainforest views and exclusive access to the East and West Pavilion pools.
Does the resort offer family room options with bunk beds?
Yes, Mandai Rainforest Resort offers nature-inspired family rooms configured with one king-size bed and a set of bunk beds to accommodate families comfortably. In addition, children under 12 dine with compliments at Planter's Shed, the resort's all-day dining restaurant.
What signature treatments are available at Banyan Tree Spa?
Banyan Tree Spa at Mandai provides signature Asian wellness treatments delivered within pangolin-inspired treatment pavilions that honor the native wildlife of the surrounding reserve. Guests are encouraged to confirm current seasonal spa menus and signature treatments when booking.
How do guests get from downtown Singapore to the resort?
Mandai Rainforest Resort is located directly within the Mandai Wildlife Reserve, approximately a 30-minute drive from downtown Singapore, Orchard Road, and Marina Bay Sands. Guests can arrive via private car, taxi, or ride-hailing service, and the resort concierge can assist with organizing private transportation.
Is Mandai Rainforest Resort an eco-certified or sustainable hotel?
Yes, Mandai Rainforest Resort is the first hotel in Singapore to achieve BCA Green Mark Platinum Super Low Energy status. Its commitment to sustainability is reflected in its biophilic architecture and interactive in-room features that enable guests to track their real-time power consumption during their stay.
What weather-protected or rainy-day activities are available at the resort?
Mandai Rainforest Resort offers a curated calendar of in resort activities each month, inviting guests to engage with the surrounding rainforest at their own pace. Past programming has included Nocturnal Walks exploring after dark wildlife, Wing Watchers sessions for birdlife observation, guided Nature Walks along the resort's trails, and Batik Art workshops inspired by local craft. Activities rotate monthly and are subject to availability, so guests are encouraged to check the current schedule closer to their stay or upon arrival at the lobby.
