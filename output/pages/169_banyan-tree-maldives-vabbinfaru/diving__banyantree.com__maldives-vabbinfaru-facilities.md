Catamaran Kahan'bu
Banyan “Kahan’bu” offers specially tailored itineraries that take you to secluded and picturesque snorkeling sites around the North Male Atoll. With its shallow draft, Banyan Kahan’bu can moor directly adjacent to the fantastic reefs below, making snorkeling a real pleasurable experience.

Attentive Associates are on board to make sure you have an enjoyable and memorable day or overnight cruise.
