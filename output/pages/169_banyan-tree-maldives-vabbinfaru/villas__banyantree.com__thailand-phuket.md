Local Culture and Sights
With its extensive beachfront, lush greenery, colourful settings and diverse flavours, Phuket has something to offer every traveller. During your stay at our 5-star Phuket resort, explore nearby islands, visit historic temples and discover island crafts, colourful architecture, lively street art and the delicious Phuketian "Kanom" in Phuket Town.

Our Local Community: Thalang
Let our team take you on a guided tour of Thalang and meet the locals who bring our community to life. Just a short tuk-tuk ride from the resort, you can visit an organic goat farm, explore a bustling local market and share your blessings at a nearby local temple.
Explore
Phuket Elephant Sanctuary
No visit to Phuket is complete without meeting the gentle giants who call the island home. The Phuket Elephant Sanctuary is a permanent refuge for retired working elephants, set on 30 acres of lush tropical jungle around 30 minutes from the resort. Observe how they rehabilitate into forest life after decades of hardship, and witness the remarkable presence of the largest land mammal on earth.

What indoor activities are available during heavy monsoon rains?
Banyan Tree Phuket offers a variety of indoor activities during Phuket’s rainy season, including treatments at Banyan Tree Spa, yoga and meditation sessions, Thai cooking classes and creative wellness workshops. Children can enjoy indoor arts and crafts at Terra Kids Club, while guests seeking a quieter day can relax with in-villa dining and private wellbeing sessions in the comfort of their pool villa. Activities are subject to the daily programme and availability.
What extra privileges are included with DoublePool Villa bookings?
DoublePool Villa privileges at Banyan Tree Phuket include daily breakfast at TRE and personalized service from a dedicated Villa Host (depending on the selected rate or package). Guests also enjoy curated in-villa rituals inspired by local Thai culture and holistic wellbeing.
Can guests book tee times at Laguna Golf Phuket through the resort?
Guests wishing to arrange tee times at Laguna Golf Phuket should contact Banyan Tree Phuket’s team with their preferred playing date and time. Located within Laguna Phuket, the 18-hole, par-71 golf course offers convenient access from the resort for a Phuket golf holiday. Tee times can also be reserved directly through Laguna Golf Phuket’s online booking system, subject to availability.
What wellness workshops are offered at the Banyan Tree Spa Academy?
The Banyan Spa & Wellbeing Academy at Banyan Tree Phuket offers hands-on wellness workshops including face yoga, hand reflexology, herbal massage oil making, tea blending and botanical aroma roll-on creation. These guided sessions introduce practical self-care techniques and Thailand’s herbs and botanicals, giving guests the opportunity to explore Thai wellness traditions and create personalised blends to complement their wellbeing routines.
How long is the drive from Phuket International Airport?
Banyan Tree Phuket is approximately 16 kilometers (10 miles) from Phuket International Airport (HKT). The drive typically takes 25 to 30 minutes depending on traffic, and private airport transfers can be arranged directly through the resort.
Does Saffron restaurant host Thai cooking masterclasses?
Yes. Banyan Tree Phuket offers hands-on Thai cooking classes where guests learn authentic recipes and traditional cooking techniques with the resort’s culinary team. The class begins with a visit to the on-site Pharm to select fresh herbs and aromatics, followed by the preparation of a three-course Thai meal. Guests then enjoy their creations for lunch, taking home new skills and an appreciation of Thailand’s culinary heritage.
How do guests access Bang Tao Beach from the resort?
Guests can reach Bang Tao Beach through Banyan Tree Phuket’s dedicated beach exit. For beachfront dining and relaxation, guests can visit RAVA Beach Club, the resort’s beach club on Bang Tao Beach. With daybeds, refreshing cocktails, charcoal-grilled dishes and views of the Andaman Sea, RAVA brings together beachside leisure, dining and music in a vibrant coastal setting.
