Banyan Tree Higashiyama Kyoto
Kyoto, Japan
SERENE HILLTOP SANCTUARY IN KYOTO’S GION AND HIGASHIYAMA DISTRICT
Nestled on a hill beside the renowned Kiyomizu-dera Temple and embraced by a tranquil bamboo forest, Banyan Tree Higashiyama Kyoto hotel offers an intimate retreat. Stay in one of 52 luxurious rooms, each featuring contemporary Japanese accents, modern amenities and stunning views of Kyoto's historic cityscape.
This hilltop Kyoto luxury hotel is the only one in the city with a Bamboo Pavilion, natural hot springs, where guests can enjoy classical Japanese performances. Find peace and rejuvenation away from the city's hustle and bustle at our Kyoto hotel with private onsen.

Is parking available for guests at Banyan Tree Higashiyama Kyoto?
Yes, there are designated parking spaces for guests with disabilities on the hotel grounds, as well as free parking spaces nearby.
Upon your arrival at the hotel entrance, we will direct you to the parking lot. (Valet service is not available.)
*Parking reservations are not accepted.
*The parking lot entrance is 2.5 meters wide.
*Designated parking spaces for guests with disabilities are located on the hotel grounds.
Please inform hotel staff if you wish to use these spaces.
*If the parking lot is full, you will need to use the coin-operated parking lot in the public area at your own expense.
We appreciate your understanding in advance.
What are the driving or public transportation directions to Banyan Tree Higashiyama Kyoto
from the nearest airport  or train station, and how long does it take toreach the hotel?
Kansai International Airport (KIX) is the nearest airport to Banyan Tree Higashiyama Kyoto. From KIX, it takes approximately:
90 minutes by private taxi directly to the hotel
90 minutes by Airport Limousine Bus to Kyoto Station
75 minutes by JR Limited Express Haruka train to Kyoto Station
From Kyoto Station, it takes about 20 minutes by taxi to reach the hotel.
What is the check-in and check -out times of Banyan Tree Higashiyama Kyoto?
The check-in time at Banyan Tree Higashiyama Kyoto is 3:00 p.m. and the check-out time is 12:00 p.m.
