DESCRIPTION
‘Experience the Original Maldives at Banyan Tree Vabbinfaru’
Encircled by a vibrant house reef where turtles, reef sharks and rays thrive, Banyan Tree Vabbinfaru is a sanctuary of natural beauty in North Malé Atoll. Just 25 minutes by speedboat from Velana International Airport, this intimate retreat embodies the essence of the Maldives.
With only 48 all-pool villas, privacy is a promise. Designed in the shape of a seashell spiral and crowned with coconut-thatch roofs, each villa blends harmoniously with its surroundings. Light-filled interiors flow into private gardens, terraces, pools and jet pools, with the ocean only steps away.
At the award-winning Banyan Tree Spa, time-honoured Oriental rituals revive body and spirit, while the pioneering Marine Lab invites guests to engage in reef conservation. Locally inspired dining and cultural touches enrich the experience, weaving heritage into every detail.
More than a destination, Banyan Tree Vabbinfaru is a true barefoot sanctuary for the senses.

Dining
Madi Hiyaa
Experience an unparalleled overwater dining escape at Madi Hiyaa, perched above the Maldives’ turquoise waters. This exclusive restaurant offers breathtaking sunset views, a serene sea pool, and a horizon that captivates the senses. Savor authentic Japanese cuisine with a unique Maldivian twist, crafted with locally sourced, sustainable ingredients for a dining experience that celebrates both flavour and the natural beauty of the islands.
Saffron Restaurant
Saffron brings the vibrant flavours of Thailand to the Maldives, blending authentic culinary traditions with modern innovation. Thoughtfully sourced, seasonal ingredients reflect a commitment to sustainability and local communities. Recognized in the MICHELIN Guide Thailand 2024, the restaurant offers an exceptional dining experience where refined elegance, creativity, and Thai heritage come together in a breathtaking island setting.
Naiboli Bar
This charming open-air bar on the seafront tempts guests will all day drinks and dining. The menu includes an extensive range of delicious appetisers and light meals, as well as wine, spirits and cocktails. On Fridays you can watch a mesmerising Bodu Beru performance, the traditional drumming and dancing of the Maldives.
Sangu Garden
Sangu Garden celebrates the essence of the Maldives with a design inspired by the ocean and crafted from sustainable materials like bamboo and palm leaves. The restaurant offers a menu of fresh, locally sourced ingredients prepared with care at grills, buffets, and live cooking stations. Every dish reflects respect for nature, blending Maldivian heritage with sustainability for a truly memorable dining experience.
Destination Dining
Discover an alfresco dining experience like no other. These private dining experiences take place in the location of your choosing – a romantic dinner on the beach under the stars; on the beachfront at Sharkpoint with a specially prepared menu of Maldivian lobster, scallops and decadent desserts; or the popular Tuesday BBQ Beach Dinner, featuring a four course meal prepared right before your eyes.
