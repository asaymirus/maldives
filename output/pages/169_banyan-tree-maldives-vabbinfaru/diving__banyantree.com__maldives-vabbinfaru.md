Banyan Tree Vabbinfaru
North Malé Atoll, Maldives
Experience THE ORIGINAL MALDIVES
Experience the Original Maldives at Banyan Tree Vabbinfaru, an award-winning luxury resort in North Malé Atoll, just 25 minutes by speedboat from Velana International Airport.
Encircled by a vibrant house reef just steps from the shore, the resort offers 48 private all-pool beach villas designed for intimacy, wellbeing, and connection with nature. Discover authentic Maldivian culture through signature rituals, locally inspired dining, and immersive island experiences.
Home to the Maldives' first resort-based Marine Lab, Banyan Tree Vabbinfaru has been pioneering coral restoration, marine conservation, and environmental stewardship since 2004, offering a true Sanctuary for the Senses rooted in nature and local heritage.

Where is Banyan Tree Vabbinfaru located?
We are located in North Malé Atoll, Maldives, just a 25-minute speedboat ride from Velana International Airport – making it one of the most accessible luxury island resorts in the Maldives.
Why is Banyan Tree Vabbinfaru known as “The Original Maldives”?
Founded in 1995, Banyan Tree Vabbinfaru is one of the pioneers of luxury hospitality in the Maldives, offering an authentic intimate island experience shaped by nature, culture, and a long-standing commitment to sustainability and wellbeing.
Is Banyan Tree Vabbinfaru good for snorkelling?
Yes. Known as a Maldives house reef snorkelling resort, guests can step from the beach into crystal-clear waters, where coral gardens and marine life begin just moments from shore.
Can I go diving at Banyan Tree Vabbinfaru?
Yes. With a PADI dive centre, you can explore the vibrant house reef, discover the nearby Rannamaari shipwreck – resting since 1999 – or explore over 40 surrounding dive sites. PADI courses are available, allowing you to begin or advance your diving journey during your stay.
What is the Marine Lab at Banyan Tree Vabbinfaru?
Established in 2004, Banyan Tree Vabbinfaru’s Marine Lab is one of the Maldives’ first resort-based marine conservation centres, pioneering coral restoration, reef monitoring, and marine education for guests and the local community.
Is Banyan Tree Vabbinfaru a sustainable resort in the Maldives?
Yes. Recognised among the Maldives’ leading sustainable resorts, Banyan Tree Vabbinfaru integrates eco-conscious design, marine conservation, coral restoration, and community engagement – recently honoured as a Leading Eco-Friendly Resort (South Asia) at the 2025 SATA Awards.
Is Banyan Tree Vabbinfaru suitable for couples and honeymooners?
With just 48 villas and a naturally intimate setting, this romantic Maldives resort is ideal for couples and honeymooners seeking privacy, quiet moments, and meaningful connection.
What wellness experiences are available at the resort?
Guided by our eight pillars of wellbeing, guests can experience yoga, meditation, Lagoon Floating Therapy, and personalised wellbeing journeys – or join Banyan Tree Connections, a private experience for two designed to deepen connection and restore balance.
What makes Banyan Tree Spa special?
Banyan Tree Spa, the first international spa in the Maldives, offers intuitive, holistic treatments rooted in centuries of Asian healing wisdom, guided by therapists trained for over 600 hours at the Banyan Tree Spa & Wellbeing Academy.

How private is Banyan Tree Vabbinfaru?
As an intimate Maldives island resort with only 48 villas, guests enjoy a secluded, tranquil setting – ideal for those seeking privacy, space, and a slower rhythm of island life. The island may also be exclusively reserved for private celebrations and special occasions.
What is Banyan Tree Connections in the Maldives, and who is it for?
Banyan Tree Connections is a private wellbeing experience for two in the Maldives, designed to deepen connection through guided rituals, mindful movement, and shared moments in a tranquil island setting – ideal for couples, close friends, or family members.
Why is the Maldives one of the world's most popular island destinations?
The Maldives is a world-renowned luxury destination, celebrated for its private island resorts, crystal-clear lagoons, white-sand beaches, and rich marine biodiversity.
What makes North Malé Atoll a great place to stay in the Maldives?
North Malé Atoll is among the premier locations to stay in the Maldives, featuring vibrant coral reefs, world-class diving and snorkeling, luxury island resorts, and quick, convenient speedboat transfers directly from Velana International Airport.
When is the best time to visit the Maldives?
The Maldives is a year-round destination, with November to April providing dry, sunny weather and calm seas ideal for leisure. The May to October period offers prime conditions for scuba diving alongside attractive seasonal offers.
What is the weather like in the Maldives throughout the year?
The Maldives enjoys a warm tropical climate year-round, with temperatures averaging 26–31°C (79–88°F). The island destination experiences abundant sunshine throughout the year, featuring a dry season from November to April and brief tropical showers from May to October.
Is the Maldives a good destination for couples, families or solo travellers?
Yes, the Maldives is an ideal destination for couples, families, and solo travelers alike. It offers romantic escapes, family-focused resorts with activities for all ages, and peaceful island retreats designed for relaxation, wellness, and adventure.
What water sports can you enjoy at Banyan Tree Vabbinfaru?
Banyan Tree Vabbinfaru provides an extensive selection of ocean activities, including house reef snorkeling, scuba diving, kayaking, stand-up paddleboarding, catamaran sailing, windsurfing, jet skiing, dolphin cruises, and private charter excursions.
Why is Banyan Tree Vabbinfaru a popular honeymoon destination?
Banyan Tree Vabbinfaru is a favored honeymoon retreat for its private pool villas, pristine white-sand beaches, romantic beachfront dining, award-winning spa, and personalized service set on an intimate island escape.
