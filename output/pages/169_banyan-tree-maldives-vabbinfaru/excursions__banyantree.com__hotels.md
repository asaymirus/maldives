Tread through cities rich in ancient history and traditions, explore hidden lagoons at an unhurried pace, or pause time while lounging on balmy shores. From vibrant concrete jungles to vast majestic landscapes, a world of wonder awaits across our inspiring hotel and resort locations.
Banyan Tree Higashiyama Kyoto
Perched on a hillside next to the iconic Kiyomizu-dera Temple, Banyan Tree Higashiyama Kyoto offers a sophisticated retreat in the heart of the city. Featuring thoughtfully designed rooms with private onsen, this Kyoto luxury hotel invites you to relax and reconnect in the historic city.
Its central position also allows guests to explore nearby UNESCO World Heritage sites, bustling local markets, and serene Zen gardens, making it one of our most culturally rich hotel locations.

What are the newest Banyan Tree resort destinations?
Banyan Tree’s newest destinations span Montenegro, Singapore, and China. The resorts are based in Mamula Island, Mandai Rainforest Resort, and Zhuhai Phoenix Bay.
Which Banyan Tree locations are best for wellness retreats?
Banyan Tree Veya Phuket, Veya Valle de Guadalupe and Banyan Tree Samui are well suited to wellness retreats. The Veya resorts centre on personalised wellbeing journeys, while Samui pairs holistic practices with a secluded tropical setting.
Which Banyan Tree locations are best for honeymoons?
Banyan Tree Vabbinfaru, Samui, and Lang Co are ideal honeymoon destinations. Secluded pool villas, couple’s spa treatments, and intimate dining experiences foster meaningful time and memories for two.
What are the top tropical destinations for Banyan Tree?
Standout tropical destinations include Vabbinfaru in the Maldives, Phuket and Samui in Thailand, Bintan in Indonesia, and Mayakoba in Mexico. Each resort blends lush nature with a distinct sense of place.
What exclusive benefits come with the withBanyan member recognition program?
The withBanyan members receive up to 10% off online rates, a complimentary drink, and 15% savings at participating spas and Gallery stores. Higher tiers add benefits such as room upgrades and flexible check-in and check-out.
Does Banyan Tree offer all-inclusive packages?
Yes. Selected resorts feature all-inclusive packages, including Wellbeing Sanctuary stays at Banyan Tree Phuket, Samui, Krabi, Lang Co, Anji, and AlUla. Meals, spa benefits, and activities vary by package.
What is the "Banyan Tree Connections" program?
“Banyan Tree Connections” is a private wellbeing programme for two at selected resorts. Rooted in the brand’s eight pillars, shared rituals in movement, mindfulness, and nature create space to reconnect with each other.
