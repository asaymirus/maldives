“Phuket Rediscovered” campaign starts from THB 55,440 for a 7-night stay
Centara Hotels & Resorts
, Thailand’s leading hotel operator, invites international travellers to “
Rediscover Phuket
” and enjoy the ultimate island luxurious experience with a 7-night stay at
Centara Grand Beach Resort Phuket
and
Maikhao Dream Villa Resort & Spa.
Designed to allow guests to immerse in the destination, the offer aligns with the “
Phuket is now open
” marketing campaign under the theme of ‘Amazing Thailand, now even more Amazing’, which was recently launched by the Tourism Authority of Thailand, inviting fully vaccinated visitors to visit the island under the
Phuket Sandbox
scheme.
Centara’s Rediscover Phuket experiential package includes a full or half board meal plan, round-trip airport transfer, and a choice of complimentary cultural, cooking, fitness or watersports activities at upscale beachfront resorts in Phuket.
At
Centara Grand Beach Resort Phuket
, guests can relax in the privacy of a pool suite or pool villa, and enjoy the comforts of a full-board stay, inclusive of breakfast, lunch, and dinner, complete with a choice of beverages. Every week, guests are offered a choice of two complimentary activities, which include Muay Thai and Thai cooking classes, water sports, a spa indulgence, or a Thai high tea set for two. Located directly on the beachfront of Karon Bay less than an hour from Phuket International Airport,
Centara Grand Beach Resort Phuket
is a favourite holiday destination for couples and families who love the resort’s very own water park, leisure activities, and exhilarating water sports.
Discover a serene coastal escape at
Maikhao Dream Villa Resort & Spa
Further north along the sunset coast, Maikhao Dream Villa Resort & Spa invites guests to fully unwind with a choice of 1- to 3-bedroom pool villas, each set in their own lush tropical gardens. A half-board offer, inclusive of breakfast and a choice of set lunch or dinner, ensures a seamless stay in the tranquil beachfront sanctuary, enhanced with complimentary Thai dessert cooking, fitness, and yoga classes, plus daily paddleboard, kayak, and bike rentals to reconnect with nature. The resort’s 22 pool villas each feature soothing interiors, a fully equipped kitchen, and a private outdoor sala ideal for poolside barbeque dinners or to unwind with the sound of waves breaking gently on the shore.
Centara Grand Beach Resort Phuket
or
Maikhao Dream Villa Resort & Spa
are both certified “
SHA+” (SHA Plus) by the Amazing Thailand Safety & Health Administration (SHA).
Guests also enjoy peace of mind when staying with Centara as the group’s hotels and resorts continue to follow strict safety protocols and adhere to certified health and hygiene procedures with the comprehensive
Centara Complete Care
programme.
The “
Phuket Rediscovered
” offer can be booked from now until 31st August 2021 for stays until 31st December 2021. Rates start at THB 55,440 for a 7-night stay, and up to two children under 12 years old stay for free when an extra bed is not required.
For more information, please visit
