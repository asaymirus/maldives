Deluxe Island Getaway
Enjoy a Fully Flexible, Curated Experience
Designed as an adults-only sanctuary, the upscale Centara Ras Fushi Resort & Spa Maldives pairs stunning overwater or romantic beachside villas with a crystalline lagoon in the North Male Atoll. On arrival at Velana International Airport, our resort staff greet guests and offer assistance with the 15-minute speed boat transfer to the resort, where they are welcomed with warm smiles and refreshing signature mocktails.
A choice of dining programmes, along with daily resort activities and facilities including watersports, beach volleyball, snorkelling equipment, fitness centre, E-Zone, and chill lounge, makes this intimately scaled resort the perfect escape for couples and honeymooners seeking a serene tropical escape.

Half Board +
Simplify your Maldives holiday with a meal plan which includes daily breakfast at the Oceans Restaurant. For dinner, guests enjoy an international buffet at Oceans Restaurant, a 3-course set menu at Suan Bua, Al Khaimah or La Brezza. Half Board Plus includes two house beverages per person for dinner, 1 alcoholic drink and 1 non-alcoholic drink.
Full Board +
Unwind and revel in the tranquil beauty of the island with our Full Board Plus offering, complemented by the use of non-motorised water sports and snorkelling equipment to explore the vibrant house reef. Daily breakfast is served at Oceans Restaurant. For lunch, enjoy dine-around options with an international buffet at Oceans Restaurant or a selection of light bites at the Beach Club in a relaxed, poolside setting. Dinner includes themed international buffets at Oceans Restaurant or dine-around options with a 3-course set menu at Suan Bua, La Brezza, or Al Khaimah, offering Thai, Italian, and Middle Eastern specialities. Includes 2 beverages per person during lunch and dinner (1 alcoholic and 1 non-alcoholic). Complimentary water is provided during all meals.
Premium All-Inclusive
The Premium All-Inclusive plan offers an elevated island experience with exceptional culinary variety and indulgent privileges throughout the day. Guests may enjoy international and Asian-inspired buffets at Oceans Restaurant or a curated 3-course set menu at Suan Bua, La Brezza, or Al Khaimah, complemented by a selection of premium beverages.
Daily breakfast is served at Oceans Restaurant. Lunch and dinner are available on a dine-around basis across Oceans Restaurant and the speciality restaurants, offering Thai, Italian, and Middle Eastern cuisine. Light bites are also available at the Beach Club for lunch. Guests enjoy a daily selection of premium beverages, including wines, beers, cocktails, soft drinks, speciality coffees, tea, and juices, served across selected outlets.
Afternoon tea is served daily with pastries, sandwiches, and a choice of tea or coffee. Sundowner canapés are presented each evening at Viu Bar, offering a relaxed sunset experience. The in-villa minibar is replenished daily with a curated selection of premium beverages. After-hours, in-villa dining with selected food and beverages is also available. The Premium All-Inclusive package also includes a spa credit of USD 50 per adult per stay at the award-winning SPA Cenvaree (non-transferable and not applicable on promotions, salon, or retail items; advance booking required), along with a 10% discount on water sports and diving activities.
