DESCRIPTION
‘The youthful, fun and affordable resort of
Centara Ras Fushi is excellent value for money and one of the most sociable in the Maldives’
Centara Ras Fushi is cheerful, friendly and fun, with well-designed, compact villas and an outstanding Thai restaurant. It attracts a mostly young crowd which enjoys socialising by the swimming pool, which has a swim-up bar, as well as the vast array of land and water-based activities to keep everyone entertained. The contemporary villas are simple but well-appointed and the space is used well. There is a stunning over-water bar located on the jetty leading to the water villas, complete with hammocks strung over the turquoise water. For those who aren’t very confident swimmers, ropes attached to buoys are strung across the water, close to the beach. There is also an authentic Thai spa.
In North Male’ Atoll:
20 minutes by speedboat

Highlights From The Resort
Discover an enchanting and pristine island with endless opportunities to relax and enjoy the beautiful Maldives setting. Laze the day away beachside or by the ocean-side pool with its own pool bar, or indulge in absolute pampering at SPA Cenvaree. Sensational treatments inspired by Thailand’s well-being heritage and modern rejuvenation practices are performed in single and double treatment rooms, with romantic couple spa journeys and multi-day authentic Ayurvedic programmes a speciality. You will love our beachside yoga sessions.
For fun and active holidays, our PADI dive and water sports centre offers an abundance of exciting activities including night snorkelling and snorkel safari, sunset fishing and big game fishing, fun tubes and banana rides, excursions such as visit to a local island and a Malé sightseeing tour, and water sports with kite surfing, windsurfing, water skiing and catamaran sailing.
Explore some of the Maldives’ most sought-after snorkelling and dive sites with our PADI dive centre offering programmes for avid divers and beginners alike. The warm waters of the North Malé Atoll offer great visibility allowing you to immerse in a mesmerising world. Exhilarating dive sites to be found between a five to 20 minutes’ dhoni ride from the resort include Giraavaru Cave, Kuda Haa, Shark Point, Lion’s Head, Kuda Faru and Kikki Reef. Farther out at sea, Black Coral Reef, Bandos Rock, Banana Reef, and Maldives Victory Wreck are less than one hour away.
