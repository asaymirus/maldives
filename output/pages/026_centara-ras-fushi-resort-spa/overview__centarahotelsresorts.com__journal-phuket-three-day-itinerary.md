When planning a short holiday, you want it all: sun-kissed beaches, iconic landmarks, colourful markets, and an epic night out or two. But with so many things to do in a place as thrilling as Phuket, the dilemma becomes real—how do you fit in the highlights without feeling rushed or missing out?
That’s where this 3-day itinerary comes in, designed to give you a taste of everything Phuket has to offer and where to go when you visit the island. And for your adventure base, Centara Karon Resort Phuket is a fantastic choice. Located on the island’s laid-back western coast, Centara Karon is a top-notch place to stay for exploring, relaxing, and experiencing everything this lively island has in store.
In our blog, we will explore the best places to visit and things to do in 3 days during your Phuket trip.
Day 1: Relax and Discover Karon
After arriving at Centara Karon Resort, it’s time to settle into your stylish room. Your first day should be all about relaxing. Choose from stunning city, pool, or ocean-view rooms. If you are feeling particularly lavish and want to focus on wellness, we recommend the Deluxe Spa Suite Ocean View room for its private outdoor jacuzzi. Start your morning with a hearty breakfast overlooking the Andaman Sea.
Morning
One of the relaxing things to do in the morning is to take a short walk down to Karon Beach, one of Phuket’s most beautiful stretches of sand, only minutes from Centara. The clear waters and gentle waves invite you to unwind and enjoy the sun, perhaps with a good book in hand. Karon Beach has a unique vibe: it’s lively but never too crowded. Indulge in some fun beach sports, dip in the water or simply take a relaxing nap—whatever your mood demands.
Afternoon
Once you’ve had your fill of the beach, let’s explore some of the other things to do in Phuket. Venture into Karon town for a leisurely stroll; the local market here is a must-stop for authentic Thai snacks, souvenirs, and handcrafted trinkets. Devour a traditional Thai lunch at one of the local restaurants, where you can try some popular dishes such as a tangy Som Tam (papaya salad) or a rich Massaman curry.
Evening
Head back to Centara for a rejuvenating spa session. SPA Cenvaree is famed for its curated menu, where they offer treatments designed to melt away any lingering travel tension. Afterwards, enjoy a delicious dinner and follow up with drinks at one of the resort’s many dining venues, which often feature live entertainment.
If you want to enjoy a range of international and Thai favourites, head to Bistro Grill & Bar. Craving Italian? Escape Pool Club serves up modern Italian specialities and grilled delights. After a hearty meal, unwind with a hand-poured cocktail at Pink Social, Centara’s chic lounge bar or at any of the three poolside bars—Tropix, Koh Kool, or Splash Deck.
With soothing music and fantastic food, it’s the ideal end to the first day of your 3-day itinerary in Phuket.
Day 2: Explore Phuket’s Landmarks and Nightlife
To kick off your second day, we will be focusing on some of the most iconic places to visit in Phuket. With Centara Karon Resort as your base, you’re within easy reach of the island’s celebrated attractions and nightlife.
Morning
Start your day by visiting the Big Buddha, a towering, serene statue that watches over Phuket from its hilltop perch. The views from up here are unbeatable, with a panoramic look over the island’s lush forests and beaches. Next, stop by Wat Chalong, Phuket’s largest and most significant temple. Wat Chalong is a stunning example of traditional Thai architecture and spirituality, and wandering through its peaceful grounds is a truly enriching experience.
Afternoon
After your spiritual journey, head to Old Phuket Town to explore a different side of the island. Known for its unique blend of Sino-Portuguese architecture, this famous area in Phuket is filled with places to visit, from historical buildings to quirky cafés, and boutique shops. Enjoy lunch in one of Old Town’s popular spots, where you can savour fusion dishes that mix Thai flavours with Western influences. Strolling through Old Town is like stepping back in time, making it a fascinating contrast to the beach life in Karon.
Evening
As the sun sets, get ready to experience the legendary nightlife of Patong Beach. If you’re wondering where to go in Phuket for a fun night out, Patong is just a short drive from Centara and brims with energy, neon lights, and an endless variety of entertainment. From open-air bars and thumping nightclubs to lively street performances, Patong has something for every night owl. Grab dinner here to fuel up for an evening of excitement, and when you’re ready to wind down, return to the tranquil comforts of Centara Karon Resort.
Day 3: Island Hopping Adventure
For the last full day of your 3-day itinerary in Phuket, it’s time to venture off Phuket’s shores to explore the tropical islands that make this region famous. Phuket is a gateway to some of Thailand’s most stunning islands, so prepare for a day filled with snorkelling, sandy beaches, and crystal-clear waters.
Day Trip
The surrounding islands are some of the top places to visit while in Phuket. Choose between the Phi Phi Islands or Coral Island for a day of island hopping. Both options offer unforgettable experiences. Phi Phi Islands, made famous by films and known for their dramatic limestone cliffs, are a snorkeller’s dream, teeming with vibrant marine life and hidden lagoons. Alternatively, Coral Island offers a more laid-back vibe, with soft sandy beaches and shallow waters that are perfect for a relaxed beach day. Either choice promises postcard-perfect scenery and the thrill of exploring Thailand’s tropical seas.
Evening
Return to Centara Karon Resort and unwind by the pool, reflecting on the highlights of your trip. Wrap up your Phuket adventure with a farewell dinner at the resort, indulging in Thai favourites or international dishes while soaking in the last of Phuket’s balmy evenings. Centara’s warm hospitality and inviting atmosphere make this the perfect way to end your 3-day trip in Phuket—with a sense of rejuvenation and adventure.
