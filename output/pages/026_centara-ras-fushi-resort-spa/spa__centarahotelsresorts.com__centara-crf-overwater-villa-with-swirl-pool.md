Overwater Villa with Swirl Pool
A stroll on the wooden jetties leads to the intimate enclave of Overwater Villa with Swirl Pool with direct access to the lagoon reef. Each 42-square-metre retreat accommodates two adults and features a king-size bed and a bathroom with shower. An outdoor spa bathtub invites guests to unwind while taking in unforgettable ocean views.

Good to Know
Check-In & Check-Out Time
Check-in is from 03:00 PM, and check-out is until 12:00 PM.
Early Arrivals & Late Departures
You may request early check-in or late check-out during booking, subject to availability. Guests checking in or out before or after the designated periods may be charged an additional fee. We offer luggage storage to guests for before check-in and after check-out.
Speedboat Transfer
The mandatory roundtrip speedboat transfer from the domestic terminal at Velana International Airport in Malé to the hotel is priced at USD 155 net per adult. This is not included in the price and will be collected at the hotel.
Payment, Cancellation, and Non-Arrival Policies
- All reservations must be guaranteed with a credit card number that is valid throughout the stay.
- Full charge is applied after booking confirmed.
- Full charge is applied if reservation is being cancelled less than 14 days prior to arrival.
- Full charge is applied in the event of non-arrival, shortened stay or early departure.
- Booking amendment is permitted subject to space availability at time of reservation.
- You may be required to present the card used for the online payment or other proof of purchase when checking-in.
