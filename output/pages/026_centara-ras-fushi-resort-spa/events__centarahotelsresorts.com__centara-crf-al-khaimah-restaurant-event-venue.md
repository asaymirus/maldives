Time to Start Planning an Event
Depending on the event, most will need planning at least 3-12 months in advance. For large-scale events that involve hundreds of individuals, those can take 12+ months to plan. Please get in touch with our coordinators as soon as you plan to hold an event or meeting with us.
About the Location
Located a short speedboat ride away from Malé's Velana International Airport, Centara Ras Fushi Resort & Spa Maldives welcomes small incentive groups, with a large selection of dining and cocktail reception options, a wide choice of team-building activities and a series of water sports, snorkelling and diving opportunities.
Available Activities
Group activities include cooking classes, tai chi and hatha yoga, as well as island hopping and foot massage lessons.
Outdoor Event Space
