Centara Ras Fushi Resort & Spa Maldives
Escape to paradise at Centara Ras Fushi, a vibrant adults-only resort designed for indulgence, connection, and rejuvenation. The calm turquoise waters and powder-soft sand set the tone for your romantic getaway – a private world for two, made up of quiet conversations and intimate moments.
In this paradise, intimacy is created in all its forms: from enjoying watersports together or exploring marine life by day, to unwinding with a golden candlelight dinner by night.
Romance is painted across the sunset skies, reflected in the serene Maldives lagoon, and felt in the hushed moments from your villa.
Our
intimate resort in the Maldives
promises an unforgettable experience. With our world-class accommodation and thoughtfully designed facilities, you will find yourselves drawing closer in this idyllic setting.
A Peaceful Adults-Only Hideaway in the Maldives
This pristine island is perfect for a romantic retreat. Spend intentional time with one another with no one else in sight.
Whether you choose an overwater or beachfront villa, you will discover an exclusive hideaway with mesmerising views of the Indian Ocean. The pace here is gentle, set by the ripple of the lagoon and swaying palm trees.
Our adults-only resort creates dreamy experiences, with a private ambience that shows you how to unplug, slow down, and focus on each other.
Overwater and Beachfront Villas Designed for Comfort
We are home to an array of premium accommodations, from overwater to
beachfront villas
, that reveal the best of the Maldives.
If you love direct access to the sandy shores, unwind in our Lagoon-View Beach Villa with Swirl Pool or the classic Lagoon View Beach Villa. Open your doors to breathtaking views of the turquoise lagoon or take a refreshing swim in your private pool, depending on which option you choose.
Couples seeking the epitome of romance will find it in our Sunset Overwater Villa. With uninterrupted vistas, you can watch the sunset from your private sundeck and turn it into a moment of wonder together.
For a sweet indulgence, our Overwater Villas are the perfect choice. These expansive villas feature a beautifully appointed bathroom with an oversized bathtub, inviting you to disconnect from the rest.
Both of these prestigious overwater villas also offer an option with a sparkling swirl pool for a deep, restorative experience.
From the beach to the lagoon, each villa type at our adults-only resort in the Maldives is designed for a time of bliss and romance.
Premium All-Inclusive Dining and Memorable Island Experiences
Centara Ras Fushi & Spa Maldives offers a plethora of experiences to make your Maldivian retreat truly unforgettable.
Our all-inclusive plan is your key to indulgence. Savour exquisite cuisine in one of our speciality restaurants, or let us arrange a romantic candlelit dinner on the beach, all part of your seamless getaway.
Beyond dining, we are the most romantic place to celebrate your story. As a couples resort in the Maldives, we can help you renew your vows against the scarlet sunset, host an extravagant birthday party, or celebrate an anniversary. The Maldivian sky is the perfect backdrop for all these moments.
Celebrate Love, Friendship, and Special Moments
The Maldives is the ideal place to celebrate the people you love, be it for a major milestone, a small anniversary, or just to honour your friendship. The entire island offers a postcard feel that deepens those bonds.
