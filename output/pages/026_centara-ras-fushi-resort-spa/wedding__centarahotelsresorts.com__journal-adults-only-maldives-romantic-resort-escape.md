FAQs
What makes overwater villas in the Maldives popular for luxury travellers?
Overwater villas are popular because they offer total privacy and instant access to the ocean. Instead of walking through the resort to get to the beach, you can step straight from your private deck into the lagoon. Waking up to panoramic ocean views and falling asleep to the sound of the waves gives travellers a uniquely exclusive and relaxing experience.
Why is speedboat access important for luxury resorts in the Maldives?
Speedboat access saves you time and cuts out travel stress. Because our resort is close to Velana International Airport, you don't have to wait around for seaplane schedules or deal with strict seaplane luggage limits. A quick 15-to-20-minute boat ride means your vacation starts almost immediately after your flight lands.
What activities can guests enjoy at a luxury Maldives resort?
You can do as much or as little as you like. For adventure, we offer world-class snorkelling and diving on our house reefs, along with water sports such as windsurfing and catamaran sailing. If you prefer to relax, you can book a couple's treatment at SPA Cenvaree, enjoy a candlelit dinner on the beach, or go on a sunset cruise.
