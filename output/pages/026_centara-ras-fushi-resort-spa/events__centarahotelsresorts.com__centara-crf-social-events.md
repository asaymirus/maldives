The Place to Celebrate
What could be more memorable than spending time with family and friends amongst the Maldives’ incredible natural beauty? For special occasions, Centara Ras Fushi Resort & Spa Maldives welcomes small parties to enjoy its private villas, as well as dining and cocktail venues.
Along with excellent catering options and a stylish choice of venues, the adults-only resort’s event team can also organise an array of activities or excursions, from water sports, snorkelling and diving to island hopping, cooking classes and yoga. Whatever you choose to do, you’re sure to make memories to last a lifetime.
