A Place to Connect
Centara Ras Fushi Resort & Spa Maldives welcomes small incentive groups with an extensive selection of dining and cocktail reception options, a varied choice of unique team-building activities, and a wide selection of water sports, snorkelling and diving opportunities.
Located a short speedboat ride away from Malé's Velana International Airport, the resort provides an exotic but easily accessible escape for team connection and relaxation. Group activities include cooking classes, tai chi and hatha yoga, as well as island hopping and foot massage lessons.
