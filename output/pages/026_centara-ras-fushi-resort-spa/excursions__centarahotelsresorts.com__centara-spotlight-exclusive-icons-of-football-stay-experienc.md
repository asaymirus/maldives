Members-Only Package Delivers Special Rates and Tournament Access as Football Legends Prepare to Tee Off in Bangkok
Centara Hotels & Resorts,
Thailand’s leading hotel operator, is thrilled to announce the
Icons of Football Stay Experience,
an exclusive package for CentaraThe1 members attending the Reignwood Icons of Football 2026 taking place at Robinswood Golf Club from 31st January until 1st February 2026.
As the Official Hotel Partner for this highly anticipated event, Centara invites guests to enjoy special members-only rates with complimentary General Admission tickets at five stunning Bangkok properties. The tournament will see football icons, including Teddy Sheringham, Robbie Fowler, Ryan Giggs, and Luis Figo, compete in an exciting Team England versus Team World showdown.
The participating properties include:
Centara Grand at CentralWorld
Centara Grand at Central Plaza Ladprao Bangkok
Centara Watergate Pavilion Hotel Bangkok
Centara Life Government & Convention Chaeng Watthana
Centara Life Hotel Bangkok Phra Nakhon
Members receive up to two tickets to Reignwood Icons of Football 2026 per room per night, with single-night bookings (31st January or 1st February) including one-day access, and two-night stays receiving full two-day passes. Bookings are available until 31st January 2026 for stays from 26thJanuary until 7th February 2026.
CentaraThe1 membership is free at
https://centara1card.com/
, unlocking the Icons of Football Stay Experience, along with more exclusive privileges at Centara destinations worldwide. To book the Icons of Football Stay Experience, please visit
https://www.centarahotelsresorts.com/icons2026
Beyond the CentaraThe1 stay experience, a special 30% discount on Hospitality Tickets for Reignwood Icons of Football 2026 is also available to the general public, offering access to exclusive viewing areas, complimentary drinks and snacks, and more curated privileges throughout the event. Tickets can be purchased at
https://www.ticketmelon.com/iconsseries/reignwoodicons2026
For more information about Centara Hotels & Resorts, please visit
www.centarahotelsresorts.com.
For more information about Icons Series, please visit
www.icons-series.com.
