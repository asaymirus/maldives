Tour full description
Enjoy a relaxing adults-only Maldives resort day pass at Centara Ras Fushi Resort & Spa, a private island resort near Malé. This all-inclusive day tour is ideal for couples, honeymooners, cruise ship passengers, airport layover guests, and visitors staying in Malé or Hulhumalé who want to enjoy a Maldives resort without booking an overnight stay.
The tour includes round-trip speedboat transfers, resort entrance, buffet lunch, selected drinks, beach access, pool access, towel use, Wi-Fi, and free time to enjoy the island at your own pace.
After arriving at the resort, you can relax on the beach, swim in the lagoon, use the pool at Silent Beach, walk around the island, or take photos near the beach, lagoon, and outside the water villas.
Lunch is served at Ocean Buffet Restaurant, with selected drinks included during lunch. Guests can also enjoy unlimited beer, wine, spirits, cocktails, and non-alcoholic drinks at Silent Bar during the day-pass period, based on the resort package.
Centara Ras Fushi is known for its peaceful adults-only setting, clear lagoon, beautiful beach areas, and easy access from Malé. As this is an adults-only resort, guests below 18 years old cannot join this tour.
Centara Ras Fushi also supports sustainability through solar power, marine conservation, and waste-reduction efforts. Centara reports that solar panels at the resort generate enough clean energy to supply close to 40% of the property’s energy needs.

FAQ
Is Centara Ras Fushi day pass adults-only?
As this is an adults-only resort, guests below 18 years old cannot join this tour.
What is included in the Centara Ras Fushi day pass?
The day pass includes speedboat transfers, resort entrance fee, buffet lunch, selected alcoholic and non-alcoholic drinks during lunch, unlimited drinks at Silent Bar, pool access, beach access, towel use, Wi-Fi, and free time at the resort. For more details please check under What is Included section.
Can cruise ship passengers join this tour?
Yes. Cruise ship passengers can be picked up and dropped off at the Cruise Ship Pier in Malé, with schedules adjusted according to ship arrival and departure times.
Can I pay at the meeting point?
Yes. Payment can be made online or at the tour meeting point by cash or card. You can also book without a credit card and pay when you meet our team.
Is this tour suitable for families?
No. This tour is not suitable for families with children because Centara Ras Fushi only accepts guests aged 18 and above. Families with children should choose a family-friendly resort day pass instead.
How far is Centara Ras Fushi from Malé?
Centara Ras Fushi is located near Malé and is reached by a 15-minute speedboat ride. Round-trip speedboat transfers are included in this day pass.
What is the dress code for the day visit?
While you are free to wear swimwear, bikinis, and casual beach attire around the beaches and pools, a smart-casual dress code is required for lunch. You must wear a dry T-shirt, dress, or proper cover-up over your swimwear when dining at the Ocean Buffet restaurant.
What should I bring for the Centara Ras Fushi day pass?
Bring swimwear, sunscreen, sunglasses, a hat, suitable footwear and any personal medication you may need. Towels and shower facilities are included.
What happens if it rains on the day of my tour?
The tour operates in all weather conditions. Tropical showers in the Maldives are frequently brief, and the resort features plenty of covered lounge spaces, bars, and restaurants where you can relax. Because the speedboats are covered and safe, tours are not canceled due to rain unless the coast guard issues an official severe weather warning.
