1. Umeda Shopping Arcade
One good place to start your shopping experience in Osaka is the Umeda Shopping Arcade, located near Umeda Station. This long shopping street is filled with stores that are open from 10 am to 9 pm daily, selling clothes, electronics, books, and other essential items.
Umeda is known for its summer and winter sales, so it’s worth checking out what deals are available when you visit. The area’s design is another highlight, featuring indoor rivers, beautifully maintained gardens, eye-catching fountains, and bubbling waterfalls.
One thing to note is that you can easily travel between Centara Grand Hotel Osaka and Umeda Station via the subway. If you are looking for a faster route, a taxi is another option, but it is usually fairly expensive. Remember that Umeda is a lively area, especially around Osaka Station and popular shopping complexes. To have a more relaxed experience, consider visiting on weekdays rather than weekends to avoid larger crowds. Additionally, try to steer clear of public holidays when locals flock to shopping areas.
2. HEP FIVE
HEP FIVE, short for Hankyu Entertainment Park, is a huge leisure complex for shopping and dining located in Umeda Osaka. It has over 160 stores featuring both Japanese and Western fashion, as well as a variety of unique specialty shops. One of its highlights is the iconic rooftop ferris wheel, where you can enjoy stunning city views after a day of shopping. It also has movie theatres and several restaurants where you can relax, watch a movie, or just grab a bite.
Like Umeda Shopping Arcade, you can travel via the subway to get to the Centara Grand Hotel in Namba Osaka. The shops are open from 11 am to 9 pm, and tourists can take advantage of tax-free shopping, which is a great way to save money. Additionally, you can show your passport at the information counter to get coupons for shopping and dining in the mall.
3. Rinku Premium Outlets
For those who love discounted branded goods, Rinku Premium Outlets is the perfect place to shop. It’s located near Kansai Airport, which is a bit outside the city centre, but the deals make it worth the trip. You can shop for popular brands like GAP, Ralph Lauren, Kate Spade, Nike, Coach, and Adidas. The outlet also has several restaurants and cafes, so you can take a break, grab a bite, and enjoy some downtime.
If you plan to stay at Centara Grand Hotel Osaka, you can get to the Rinku Premium Outlet by train from the Rinku town station. It is best to visit on weekdays, when you will find the best deals and special promotions. Rinku Premium Outlet is usually open between 10 am and 8 pm.
4. Tennoji
The Tennoji area is sometimes overlooked by tourists, but it has a lot to offer. Located right above JR Tennoji Station, this shopping mall features different stores offering designer clothes, homewares, and vintage fashion. There are also various places to eat, including pastry and dessert shops and convenience stores for quick bites and drinks. If you’re into Japanese cosmetics, Tennoji is a great place to find them, and you can often find good deals during sales.
Shops here are open from 10 am to 8 pm, and if you want to avoid large crowds, visit during weekdays. The JR Tennoji station nearby can take you to various parts of the city, including Namba.
5. Crysta Nagahori
Crysta Nagahori is a large underground shopping arcade that stretches from Yotsubashi Station in the west to Nagahoribashi Station in the east, passing through Shinsaibashi. Thanks to glass ceilings and filtered sunlight, the design gives the feel of shopping at street level. Its natural touches, like gardens and waterfalls, help create a soothing shopping environment.
Crysta Nagahori is very large, with over 100 shops that offer a wide range of products, from cosmetics and fashion to books. Its location near five major train and subway stations makes it incredibly convenient. Most shops are open from 10 am to 9 pm, so you have plenty of time to explore.
6. Daimaru Department Store
The Daimaru Department Store at Shinsaibashi is another must-visit shopping destination in Osaka, especially if you’re staying at Centara Grand Hotel in Namba. The store is in the Shinsaibashi area, just a short walk from the hotel. This makes it a great choice for those who want to enjoy shopping without travelling too far.
It’s a multi-level shopping complex, with each floor dedicated to different categories. At Daimaru Shinsaibashi, you will find different international and Japanese brands including Hermes, Chanel, Louis Vuitton and Shiseido as well as boutiques that offer unique and exclusive products.
One of the highlights of Daimaru Shinsaibashi is the food hall, where you can enjoy a variety of Japanese and international delicacies, such as sushi, sashimi, pastries, sandwiches, and gourmet salads.
The department store is known for its impeccable customer service, and tourists can enjoy tax-free shopping on certain purchases. The store is open daily from 10 a.m. to 8 p.m., allowing ample time to explore its many floors.
