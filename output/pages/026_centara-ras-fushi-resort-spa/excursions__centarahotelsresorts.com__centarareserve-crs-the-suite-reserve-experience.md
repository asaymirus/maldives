Offer Details
The Suite Reserve Experience offers an elevated 5-night stay starting from THB 57,500++ per package for two persons, available for stays from 01 February to 15 December 2026.
The offer is applicable for Reserve Ocean Suite and above room categories, including Reserve Ocean Suite, Luxury Garden Suite, Luxury Ocean Suite, The Garden Pool Suite, and The Reserve Suite.
Guests staying in Reserve Ocean Suite, Luxury Garden Suite, and Luxury Ocean Suite will enjoy daily breakfast for two persons, round-trip airport transfers (Samui Airport), a complimentary bottle of wine per stay, resort credit valued at THB 3,000 net per stay, complimentary laundry (4 pieces per day), a 60-minute spa treatment for two persons (one-time per stay), 15% savings on Food & Beverage (excluding In-Room Dining), and 20% savings on treatments at Reserve Spa Cenvaree.
For guests staying in The Garden Pool Suite and The Reserve Suite, the package includes the same privileges with an enhanced resort credit valued at THB 3,500 net per stay.
