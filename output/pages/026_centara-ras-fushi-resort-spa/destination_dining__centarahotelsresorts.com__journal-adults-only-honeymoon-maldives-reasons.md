For a honeymoon, it's important to choose a destination that offers not only beautiful scenery but also a relaxing environment where you can spend time together. An adults-only Maldivian honeymoon resort allows you to enjoy a private and romantic time on an island surrounded by the sea. Centara Ras Fushi Resort & Spa Maldives is an adults-only resort, exclusively for guests 18 years of age and older. Located in the North Malé Atoll of the Maldives, it's less than a 20-minute speedboat ride from Velana International Airport, allowing you to begin your tranquil resort stay immediately upon arrival.
Why the Maldives is a Popular Honeymoon Choice
The appeal of Maldivian honeymoon resorts lies not only in the scenery surrounded by the sea, sky, and white sandy beaches, but also in the opportunity to escape from everyday life and spend time at a leisurely pace while sharing the same views with your partner. Enjoy lagoon views in the morning, snorkeling and spa treatments in the afternoon, and a beachfront dinner at sunset. A stay that isn't too packed with activities and allows you to surrender to the rhythm of nature leaves a lasting impression befitting a honeymoon. A romantic resort in the Maldives, perfect for couples, is one such option.
Reason 1: A private and romantic environment made possible by being adults-only
The charm of a Maldivian honeymoon at an adults-only resort lies in the calm atmosphere that naturally allows you to focus on your time together. Whether it's chatting while gazing at the ocean, enjoying drinks at the bar, or simply spending an afternoon doing nothing, the slow pace of time itself becomes a cherished memory, without relying on extravagant arrangements. The tranquil atmosphere makes it suitable for anniversary trips or milestone getaways, and is ideal for couples seeking a private honeymoon.
Reason 2: Villas and facilities designed with couples in mind
The resort has a total of 140 villas, each accommodating up to two adults. There are various room types to choose from, including beachfront villas, overwater villas, sunset overwater villas, and villas with swirl pools, to suit your preferred style of stay. Each villa is designed with privacy in mind, allowing you to enjoy your time together without worrying about your surroundings. The time spent gazing at the ocean from the deck or feeling close to the lagoon will make your trip even more memorable. Details of the rooms can be found in the Maldives honeymoon resort with overwater villas section.
Reason 3: A quiet and relaxing resort stay for adults
For a honeymoon, it's important to choose a lifestyle with plenty of free time and not over-packed with activities. The resort has multiple restaurants and bars, allowing you to enjoy meals and drinks according to your mood. Romantic dining experiences such as candlelight dinners and sand sofa dinners add a subtle touch of elegance to your evening. At SPA Cenvaree, you can unwind in a space overlooking the ocean or in couples' treatment rooms.
To book your honeymoon at Centara Ras Fushi Resort & Spa Maldives
When planning an adults-only honeymoon in the Maldives, it's reassuring to check the dates of stay, villa type, meal plan, and benefits conditions in advance. The official website offers honeymoon benefits exclusively for website bookings, including sparkling wine, homemade chocolates, and bed decorations. Eligibility requirements and booking periods may change, so please check the details before booking. Check out the details of the adults-only Maldives honeymoon packages and choose the stay that best suits you and your partner.
