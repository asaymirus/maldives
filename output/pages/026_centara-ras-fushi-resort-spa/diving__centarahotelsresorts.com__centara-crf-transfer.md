Centara Ras Fushi Resort & Spa Maldives
Scenic Boat Transfers
Your Journey to Ras Fushi
From Malé International Airport, guests can enjoy a scenic speedboat transfer to reach Centara Ras Fushi Resort. The journey typically takes 15 to 20 minutes, offering stunning views of the surrounding islands. The resort provides mandatory shared speedboat transfers from the airport, priced at USD 180 net per person for a round trip.
Alternative Private Transfers to Centara Ras Fushi
Private Yacht:
USD 2,700 return (48-hour booking window required)
External Speedboat Docking Fee:
A fee of USD 150++ applies for all arrivals and departures using a private speedboat
Meet & Greet:
Upon arrival, airport representatives will be waiting with a paging signboard at Terminal 1 to guide guests to the speedboats at Terminal 2.
Our team is also available at Counter A14 in Terminal 2 to assist with any queries.
Note:
Speedboat transfers are operated from Terminal 2.
