The Place to Savour
Your experience at Centara Ras Fushi Resort & Spa Maldives extends to a world of culinary discovery. Here, seven dining venues feature a plethora of epicurean possibilities featuring Asian, European, Middle Eastern and South American flavours.
While Suan Bua serves up authentic Thai, La Brezza offers Italian fare. At Al Khaimah, Arabian cuisine takes centre stage and Oceans boasts international buffets. Catch the sunset and cocktails at Viu Bar, or find more refreshments at Waves Pool Bar and Giraavaru Lobby Bar.
