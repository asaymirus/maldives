DESCRIPTION
Noonu Atoll’s Haven of Luxury and Tranquility
A pristine escape awaits at Kuredhivaru Resort and Spa, set within the untouched beauty of the Maldives’ Noonu Atoll. Just 45 minutes from Malé by seaplane, the resort offers unmatched privacy with 72 Overwater Pool Villas, 30 Beach Pool Suites and three Beach Spa Pool Residences, each with a private plunge pool and panoramic views of turquoise waters or powder-white sands.
Guests can indulge in international and Maldivian flavours across three restaurants or enjoy an intimate dinner under the stars. Wellness is at the heart of the experience with Sun Spa by Esthederm, yoga sessions at sunrise and a fully equipped fitness centre, while younger guests discover fun and learning at the Little Birds Club.
The surrounding ocean unveils vibrant adventures with a colourful house reef, guided snorkelling, diving and water sports. From sunset sailing and dolphin spotting to simply unwinding poolside, every moment is created for both relaxation and discovery.
Kuredhivaru is more than a holiday destination. It is a sanctuary where couples, families and solo travellers can embrace the natural beauty and tranquillity of the Maldives.

Dining
Onu marché – A Culinary Journey
Gather under the bamboo roof of ONU Marché, Kuredhivaru’s breezy market-style restaurant. Enjoy à la carte and buffet breakfasts, relaxed lunches, and indulgent dinners, or join the lively weekly theme night with live cooking stations. Fresh, locally sourced produce, Maldivian specialties, seafood, and international favorites fill the menu, alongside healthy options like the Go Healthy menu and Power Bites for kids. A beachfront setting makes every meal a sensory feast.
Bodumas – Overwater Dining Redefined
Perched on an overwater pavilion at Kuredhivaru Resort & Spa, Bodumas sets the stage for unforgettable dining with sweeping ocean views and a gentle sea breeze. Renowned for its seafood-centric menu with international flair, highlights include lobster, tuna, and fresh ceviche, complemented by gourmet alternatives and expert wine pairings. Whether for a romantic dinner or a luxurious à la carte breakfast, Bodumas blends island charm with refined elegance for a truly memorable experience.
Latitude 5.5 – Poolside Dining with Ocean Views
Set beside the infinity pool, Latitude 5.5 combines laid-back island vibes with sweeping views of the ocean and unforgettable sunsets. Watch chefs at the open kitchen prepare sizzling Angus rib-eye, jumbo prawns, and the daily catch, all crafted with Asian and Western flair. Pair your meal with classic or exotic cocktails as tropical music sets the mood. Perfect for a relaxed lunch or a sunset dinner, this stylish spot celebrates the best of Maldivian dining.
Oak lounge – A Wine Lover’s Paradise
Unwind in sophistication at Oak Lounge, Kuredhivaru Resort & Spa’s exclusive retreat for wine enthusiasts. Explore a curated collection of over 260 premium labels from 15 renowned regions, perfectly paired with cuisine by our Chef and Sommelier. Complement your experience with rare cognacs, fine whiskeys, and hand-rolled cigars, all in a luxurious setting that celebrates the art of taste and refinement.
Destination dining – Moments to Cherish
Celebrate love, milestones, or simple togetherness with unforgettable dining at Kuredhivaru Resort & Spa. From Champagne breakfasts by the shore to lagoon lunches, sunset cocktails, private BBQs, or starlit dinners on the beach, every detail is tailored to your wishes. Surrounded by ocean breezes and magical skies, our destination dining transforms any occasion into an extraordinary Maldivian memory.
