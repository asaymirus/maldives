Who can play
Open to all ages. Younger players may need a parent or guardian nearby for motion-based stations, and each machine lists its own recommended minimum age.
Groups & parties
Birthday parties and special events can book the room for a private session, including party host support and refreshments for the group.
What to bring
Just yourselves — headsets, controllers and safety gear are provided and sanitized between guests. Comfortable, closed-toe shoes are recommended for the motion stations.
