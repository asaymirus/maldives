The Grand Anniversary Gala & Ceremonial Kick-Off
A week of joy begins with an elegant cocktail reception and a ceremonial cake cutting, setting the stage for a paradise of celebration. Indulge in an exclusive gala dinner featuring specialty menus crafted by our culinary team.
Spectacular Evening Extravaganzas
Be captivated by world-class performances, including fire performers and graceful aerial and hoop acts, as seen in past celebrations. The evenings culminate in a vibrant fireworks display and music from a renowned DJ, ensuring the celebration continues until midnight.
A Journey of Culinary Delights
Experience special, unique dining concepts such as a Gourmet Week, which previously featured acclaimed international chefs with unique offerings like Nippo-Brazilian cuisine. Guests can also enjoy freshly grilled barbeque at Palm Beach and intimate destination dining experiences.
Vibrant Island Events & Water Thrills
Keep the vibrant island energy high with exciting activities such as the International Kite Festival, featuring live demonstrations from international champions and workshops. Explore our pristine waters with snorkeling on the house reef, sunset dolphin cruises, and a complete range of water sports.
Cultural Immersion and Wellness Retreat
Experience the warmth of Maldivian culture with traditional music and dance performances, including Boduberu. For relaxation, guests can enjoy holistic spa treatments at the Oasis Spa or a guided nature walk through the island's Orchid Garden.
