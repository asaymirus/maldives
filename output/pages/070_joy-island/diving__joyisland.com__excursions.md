Excursions
Discover a wide range of exciting excursions and tailor your trip to match your preferences. To catch a
glimpse into local life, book a Local Island Experience or go for fishing in Maldives style. If you are an
underwater lover, book Turtle &amp; Coral Garden Expedition, Manta Trip, Hanifaru Bay Trip or one the
magnificent Private Turtle Trip. Cruise in the Maldives ocean with dolphins with Sunset Dolphin Cruise.
See Brochure
Sunset Dolphin Cruise
The Maldives ranks among the top five places on Earth for watching Dolphins. You will sail the the Ocean while enjoying the splendid view of the sunset as the dolphins jump and play.
Local Island Tour
Discover the authentic side of the Maldivian culture! Engage with local children and be welcomed into a Maldivian home for an authentic Maldives private island experience.
Sunset Fishing
The better way to enjoy the beautiful Maldivian sunset with a traditional sunset fishing trip. Experience the traditional bottom-line fishing with family and friends.
Big Game Fishing
Pursue some of the Maldives’ notorious big game fish on this adventure.  On your return, our Chefs will transform your catch into a delicious dish.
Hand Line Fishing
The Maldivian Hand Line Fishing experience will be a once in a  lifetime adventure whether you are a complete beginner or an expert.
Full Moon Cruise
Enjoy the beauty of the full moon shimmering on the surface as you cruise across the Ocean. Relax on a traditional Maldivian dhoni and feel the warm night breeze.
