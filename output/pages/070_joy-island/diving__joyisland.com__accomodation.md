Accommodation
Joy Island has 151 villas, 60 rooms located overwater with breathtaking views of the Indian Ocean, and 91 rooms on the beach surrounded by beautiful tropical vegetation. From spacious villas to elegantly luxurious suites, each accommodation is thoughtfully designed to ensure your utmost comfort. Savor the added luxury of private plunge pools for an intimate experience for a refreshing dip.
