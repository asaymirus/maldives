Joy Island Maldives, a name could never be more fitting for a joyful place
An island of happiness, full of activities and services for the most fun holiday in the Maldives. Featuring 151 villas on the beach and in the lagoon, Joy Island Maldives is located in North Malé Atoll close to Velana International Airport, only 40 minutes away by speedboat. The shape of this beautiful island resembles a butterfly with its wide white sand beach, surrounded by a stunning turquoise lagoon.
Joy Island Maldives is your joyful escape, where vibrant energy, natural beauty, and Italian elegance come together to create a haven of happiness and serenity.
Your villa is designed with your ultimate comfort in mind. Surrounded by the turquoise waters of the Indian Ocean or nestled along the pristine beach amidst lush tropical greenery, each space is a perfect blend of luxury and relaxed island living. Joy Island features 151 villas, including 60 overwater villas offering panoramic ocean views and 91 beach villas embraced by nature. From spacious beachfront retreats to elegantly luxurious overwater suites, every accommodation is thoughtfully crafted to ensure a sense of peace and connection with nature. Indulge in the added luxury of private plunge pools, perfect for an intimate and refreshing dip under the Maldivian sun. With contemporary interiors, and a playful spirit that celebrates the joy of island life, Joy Island offers a chic, stylish escape where comfort meets the carefree rhythm of paradise.

From ocean sunsets to island delights: your joyful dining experience in the Maldives
Indulge in a world of flavours at Joy Island, where every meal celebrates the art of dining in paradise. With a selection of four restaurants and two bar, guests can savour a diverse range of exquisite culinary experiences surrounded by the island’s vibrant atmosphere. At the heart of the island, The Market invites you to explore an array of international cuisines through its interactive live cooking stations, offering a delightful journey of tastes and aromas. Palm Beach Restaurant provides an elegant à la carte dining experience right on the beach, with a magnificent view of the Indian Ocean — the perfect setting for a romantic evening or a leisurely lunch by the waves. For authentic Italian wood-fired pizzas, Il Basilico is the place to be, combining the warmth of Italian tradition with the island’s tropical charm. Japanese Corner is the teppanyaki restaurant where the real magic comes alive and Rainbow Bar for the best cocktails during your stay.
