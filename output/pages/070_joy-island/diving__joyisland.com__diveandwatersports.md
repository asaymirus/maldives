Dive, Play and Explore
​
Discover endless adventure at Joy Island’s Dive & Water Sports Centre, where the ocean is your playground! Whether you're a thrill-seeker or a first-time explorer, dive into vibrant coral gardens, glide alongside marine life, or feel the rush with jet skiing, parasailing, and more.
From guided snorkeling excursions to certified scuba diving, windsurfing, kayaking, and paddleboarding, our expert team is here to ensure safe, exciting, and unforgettable aquatic experiences in the stunning Maldivian waters.
Glass Canoe
Discover the lagoon like never before with our Glass Canoe experience at Joy Island Maldives. Glide gently over crystal-clear waters while observing vibrant marine life beneath you, thanks to the canoe’s fully transparent design.
Sea Bob
Glide effortlessly above and below the surface with our high-tech Seabob. Explore the lagoon like a dolphin, diving into the turquoise waters for a fun and unique way to experience the ocean.
