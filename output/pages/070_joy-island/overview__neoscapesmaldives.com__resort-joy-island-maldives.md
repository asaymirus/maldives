DESCRIPTION
Joy Island, the joyful island, perfectly named for the happiness, adventure, and unforgettable moments that await every guest.
Joy Island truly lives up to its name, offering a vibrant escape where every moment feels light, carefree, and full of happiness. Shaped like a butterfly and surrounded by dazzling turquoise waters, this stunning retreat in North Malé Atoll is just a 40-minute speedboat ride from Velana International Airport.
The island features 151 villas, some nestled along soft white sands and others perched above the lagoon, each designed in refreshing tones that reflect the island’s joyful spirit, with options for private plunge pools.
Guests can indulge in international flavours at The Market, beachfront dining at Palm Beach, authentic Italian pizzas at Il Basilico, or cocktails and live entertainment at the lively Rainbow Bar.
Rejuvenation awaits at the overwater Oasis Spa, while adventure seekers can enjoy snorkelling, diving, and a variety of water sports. With daily excursions, family fun at Kiddie Cove, and a strong commitment to sustainability through The Cocoon Foundation, Joy Island is a radiant paradise where happiness takes flight.

Dining
The Market
Step into The Market, Joy Island’s vibrant main restaurant, and embark on a culinary journey around the world. Set in an open-air, market-style environment, it features a variety of lively food stations and daily themed menus that celebrate diverse flavours and cultures. Every meal is a feast for the senses, paired with stunning views of the Indian Ocean, making dining here a truly joyful experience.
Il Basilico
Experience the flavours of Italy at Il Basilico, Joy Island’s charming poolside pizzeria. Delight in authentic wood-fired pizzas, classic Italian dishes, and the indulgent finale of Tiramisu, all set against the vibrant colours of the tropical surroundings for a truly memorable dining experience.
Palm Beach
Set at the tip of the island, Palm Beach combines a restaurant and bar with stunning sunset views. Enjoy classic Spanish paella for lunch under shaded palms or a relaxed outdoor meal, then return in the evening for freshly grilled barbecue beneath the starry sky, creating a perfect blend of flavours and ambiance.
Rainbow Bar
Bursting with colour and energy, Rainbow Bar is Joy Island’s lively social hub. Sip on expertly crafted cocktails from the JOY drinks menu, enjoy an extensive selection of fine wines, and soak in live music and nightly entertainment. Just steps away, the inviting pool adds a refreshing touch to your vibrant island experience.
Japanese Corner
At Japanese Corner, the art of teppanyaki comes alive. Skilled chefs prepare fresh seafood, tender meats, and seasonal vegetables on a sizzling grill right before your eyes, blending flavour, skill, and entertainment. Every meal is a performance and every dish a masterpiece, offering an unforgettable journey through authentic Japanese cuisine in the heart of paradise.
Destination Dining
Experience unforgettable culinary adventures with Joy Island’s destination dining. Enjoy a romantic beach dinner to the sound of gentle waves, a serene sandbank picnic amid the turquoise Indian Ocean, or an enchanting lagoon dinner under the stars. Each setting blends nature and indulgence, creating magical moments and memories that last a lifetime. Bookings can be arranged through your island host.
