Celebrate Eid Al-Adha at Joy Island Maldives
Join us at Joy Island Maldives for a vibrant celebration of Eid Al-Adha, where authentic Maldivian traditions, island festivities, and joyful community moments come together in paradise.
​
From cultural performances and traditional rituals to interactive games and colourful celebrations, guests are invited to experience the warmth and spirit of one of the Maldives’ most meaningful festive occasions.
Eid Al-Adha Celebration
27th May 2026
Afternoon Cultural Celebration
Water Sports Beach | From 16:00hrs
​
Guests and team members come together for a lively cultural celebration filled with colour, music, and traditional performances.
​
The Eid Maali Parade will feature:
Bodumas performances
Traditional Maali characters
Ladies’ cultural dance performances
Live Boduberu music
​
Guests will be invited to participate in the festivities while learning about traditional Maldivian Eid celebrations and local island customs.
​
The afternoon continues with:
Traditional Eid dance performances
Bodumas Beynun
Interactive Maldivian games
Tug of War
Naashi Vehttu
Faigathalhaa
Eid Kula Jehun colour celebration
Designed to bring people together, the celebration creates unforgettable moments of joy, culture, and connection in a vibrant beachfront setting.
Special Maldivian Night
28th May 2026
The celebrations conclude with a special Maldivian Night, where guests can enjoy an evening inspired by the rich culture, traditions, and flavours of the Maldives, creating the perfect ending to a memorable Eid experience at Joy Island Maldives.
