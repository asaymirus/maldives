Restaurants & Bars
Dining at Joy Island is a culinary adventure that tantalizes the senses with a diverse selection of flavors. Guests can indulge in exquisite cuisine at three distinctive restaurants: The Market offers a vibrant buffet with international dishes, Palm Beach serves fresh seafood in a stunning beachfront setting, Japanese Corner for an exclusive teppanyaki experience and Il Basilico delights with authentic Italian specialties. Complementing these dining experiences is the Rainbow Bar, where guests can unwind with expertly crafted cocktails and refreshing beverages.
The Market
​
The Market, a culinary haven that invites you to embark on a remarkable expedition of flavors from around the globe. This lively main restaurant is a paradise for foodies, featuring vibrant food stations that showcase a diverse array of beloved cuisines, each paying homage to unique cultures. Every dish is crafted with care, creating a symphony of taste sensations that will delight your palate. From fresh seafood to aromatic spices, The Market promises a culinary adventure like no other. Join us and experience a journey where every bite transports you to a new and exciting destination in flavor.

Japanese Corner
Step into Japanese Corner, where the art of teppanyaki meets a vibrant culinary experience. Immerse yourself in the sizzling action as our skilled chefs prepare mouthwatering dishes right before your eyes, using the finest ingredients and bold flavors. From the first bite to the last, enjoy an unforgettable dining adventure that blends tradition with innovation. At Japanese Corner, every meal is a performance, and every dish is a masterpiece. Join us for a memorable journey through the best of Japanese cuisine, right here at Joy Island.

Il Basilico
Il Basilico, where a captivating voyage to the Italian Riviera awaits. Immerse yourself in a charming ambiance that perfectly complements the delightful flavors of Italy. Every dish is a harmonious blend of tradition and passion, featuring scrumptious wood-fired oven-baked pizzas that truly capture the essence of authentic Italian cuisine. Each bite transports you to sun-kissed coasts and bustling piazzas. To complete your culinary adventure, indulge in our divine Tiramisu—an unforgettable finale that perfectly balances rich flavors and creamy textures. Join us at Il Basilico for an enchanting dining experience that promises to leave you saying, "Buon Appetito!"
Il Basilico
Palm Beach
Nestled at the island's tip, Palm Beach offers a charming blend of restaurant and bar, making it the perfect spot to savor fascinating sunsets. Enjoy exquisite dishes under the shade of sails and swaying palms, or drop in for a relaxed outdoor lunch. As evening falls, this oasis transforms into a delightful escape, inviting you to indulge in freshly grilled barbecue under the velvety night sky. With its inviting ambiance and diverse menu, Palm Beach promises an unforgettable dining experience that beautifully captures the essence of island life. Join us for a taste of paradise!
Palm Beach
Rainbow
At Rainbow Bar, shared happiness is at the heart of every moment! Elevate your experience with our meticulously crafted JOY drinks menu, designed for leisurely sipping. Indulge in our impressive selection of wines, perfect for toasting special occasions or simply unwinding. Nestled beside the bar, our inviting swimming pool offers a refreshing escape, allowing guests to relax and soak up the sun every day. Whether you're enjoying a cocktail with friends or taking a dip in the cool water, Rainbow Bar promises a vibrant atmosphere that enhances your island getaway and creates unforgettable memories. Cheers to joy!
