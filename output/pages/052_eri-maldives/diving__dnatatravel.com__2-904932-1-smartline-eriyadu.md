Overview
Property Location
You can reach the lively paradise of Eri Maldives from Velana International Airport with a picturesque 45-minute speedboat journey, and spend more time enjoying island life rather than traveling.
Rooms
Find your sanctuary at Eri, where tastefully constructed villas blend contemporary luxury with unspoiled beauty. Choose from Overwater Villas with private pools and unobstructed ocean views or sophisticated Beach Villas only a short distance from fine white sands. Every room offers cozy luxury and stunning surroundings, all adorned with modern accents and Maldivian charm.
Amenities
Enjoy island-inspired cuisine, relax on pristine beaches, or revitalize at their serene Eskape Spa. Explore the exhilaration of water sports or dive into the rich marine life for those looking for adventure. Every moment at Eri is vibrant, restful, and full of treasured memories.
Dining
Every meal at Eri is a celebration of flavor and island charm as you embark on a lively culinary adventure. Their dining experiences are designed to excite your senses, featuring meals inspired by across the world and freshly caught seafood.
Business, other Amenities
With tastefully decorated villas, a magnificent house reef, and countless water activities, this island retreat combines contemporary elegance with the Maldives breathtaking natural beauty.
