Your first diving experience at Eri Maldives can begin without any previous scuba experience or certification. The PADI Discover Scuba Diving programme introduces you to the essential skills and equipment before you head onto Eri’s house reef with an instructor, reaching a maximum depth of 12 metres.
Once you have experienced the reef for the first time, you may find yourself curious to go further. Eri’s PADI 5-Star dive centre offers courses across different levels, giving you the opportunity to develop your skills and discover more of the underwater world during your stay.
Do I Need to Be a Certified Diver?
No. If you have never tried
scuba diving
and do not hold a certification, Eri offers the
PADI Discover Scuba Diving programme
as an introduction to the experience. You will learn basic scuba skills before diving with an instructor on Eri’s house reef, with a maximum depth of 12 metres for the programme. If that first experience leaves you wanting to learn more, the dive centre offers PADI courses across different levels, giving you the opportunity to continue building your skills during your time on the island.
Where Will My First Dive Take Place?
Your first scuba diving experience in the Maldives can begin just steps from the shore..Eri’s house reef and shallow lagoon provide an accessible setting for beginner divers to become comfortable beneath the surface before exploring further. As the reef comes into view, coral formations and tropical marine life reveal another side of North Malé Atoll. Eagle rays, schooling jacks, sea turtles, stingrays and several species of reef shark can be found in the waters around Eri, although marine encounters naturally vary from dive to dive.
Who Will Guide Me Underwater?
Your first dive is supported by the experienced team at Euro-Divers’ fully equipped PADI 5-Star dive centre. For beginners, having an instructor alongside you means there is someone there to explain the essentials, help you become familiar with the equipment, and guide you as you begin exploring beneath the surface. Eri also confirms that its boat dives are professionally guided, while house reef dives can be guided or undertaken with a buddy by appropriately qualified divers. It creates a natural path from taking those first breaths underwater to becoming increasingly comfortable in the blue.
What Should I Know Before My First Dive?
Before taking part in diving activities, you will need to complete and sign a medical questionnaire. A medical certificate is not mandatory for someone who is generally in good health, although additional requirements apply in certain circumstances, including if you take prescribed medication. Eri also requires all divers to have valid dive insurance. If you do not already have it, DAN Short-Term Diving Insurance can be purchased through the dive centre for your stay. Checking these requirements before travelling can leave more time on the island for the part you came for: discovering what waits beneath the water.
When Is the Best Time to Try Diving at Eri?
There is no need to plan your first dive around one narrow season, as
diving
at Eri is available year-round. The underwater encounters will naturally change with conditions and the movement of marine life. Manta rays, for example, can be seen around Eri between January and April, while other marine encounters may include
turtles
, rays, reef sharks, and schools of fish. Rather than arriving with a checklist, let the ocean reveal itself in its own time. Your first dive may be remembered for an unexpected turtle encounter, a flicker of reef fish, or simply the feeling of becoming comfortable beneath the surface.
What If I Am Not Ready to Scuba Dive Yet?
You can still discover Eri’s underwater world without putting on a scuba tank.
Snorkelling
at Eri brings you close to the house reef, with experienced snorkel guides available for beginners and more confident ocean explorers. Freediving offers another progression for those curious about exploring beneath the surface on a single breath, with first-time experiences and certification courses available through Eri’s instructors. Starting with a gentle snorkel can also give you time to become familiar with the lagoon before deciding whether scuba diving feels right for you.
What Comes After Your First Dive?
Eri sits within reach of
more than 30 dive sites
around North Malé Atoll, so your first experience can be the beginning of a much bigger underwater story. As your confidence and qualifications develop,
diving
at Eri can take you beyond the house reef to coral gardens, channels, and nearby dive sites, each shaped by different underwater conditions and marine life. The island becomes your place to return to between adventures, where palms move overhead, and the familiar sound of the sea follows you back onto shore.
Your First Breath Beneath the Blue
You do not need to arrive at Eri as a diver. You can arrive curious. With a beginner-friendly house reef, professional instruction, and a PADI Discover Scuba Diving programme available for those without certification, your first experience can begin gently and grow at a pace that feels natural.
Sometimes becoming an ocean explorer begins with one breath beneath the surface. Discover diving at
Eri
