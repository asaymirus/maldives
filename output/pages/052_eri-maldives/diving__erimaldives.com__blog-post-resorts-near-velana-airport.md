Set in North Malé Atoll, just a short speedboat ride from Velana International Airport,
Eri Maldives
is an eco-chic island resort shaped by connection, culture, and mindful living. Designed to feel vibrant yet unhurried, the island blends contemporary comfort with Maldivian heritage, inviting guests to slow down and live intentionally. From barefoot spaces and lush greenery to meaningful experiences rooted in nature, Eri is a place where everyday moments feel deeply personal and quietly memorable.
Thoughtfully Designed Stays
Stays
at Eri
are designed to feel relaxed, thoughtful, and closely connected to the island. Guests can choose from
Beach Studios
and
Sky Studios
,
offering cosy retreats near the sand or elevated views above the palms, as well as
Beach Villas
set just steps from the shore. For added privacy, the
Beach Pool Villa
and
Sunset Beach Villa
invite slow mornings and glowing evenings, each shaped around comfort, space, and effortless island living.
Flavours of the Island
Dining
at Eri is shaped by fresh flavours and an easy island pace.
Soul Kitchen
is the heart of all-day dining, offering a welcoming space where local and global dishes are prepared with care.
Sip & Dip
keeps things light with refreshing drinks by the pool, while the
Beach Shack
serves alcoholic and non-alcoholic sips by the shore, perfect for enjoying the island atmosphere.
Beneath the Blue
Underwater
experiences at Eri invite guests to explore the ocean at their own pace. Snorkelling reveals colourful reef life close to shore, while guided diving opens access to over
30 dive sites
nearby, each offering something different beneath the surface. For those who prefer to stay above the water, a range of water sports adds energy and adventure, making the sea an essential part of life at Eri.
