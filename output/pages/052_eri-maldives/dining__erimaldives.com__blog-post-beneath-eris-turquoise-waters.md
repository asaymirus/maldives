Set in the heart of North Malé Atoll,
Eri Maldives
is surrounded by crystal-clear lagoons, vibrant coral reefs, and some of the Indian Ocean’s most captivating marine habitats. Beneath the surface, a living underwater world unfolds with graceful manta rays, sea turtles, reef sharks, colourful tropical fish, and flourishing coral gardens.
With more than
30 renowned dive sites
within easy reach and a thriving house reef just moments from the shore, Eri is a natural gateway to unforgettable underwater adventures for both first-time divers and experienced explorers.
A Living Reef, Just Steps Away
Encircling Eri is a vibrant house reef that begins just steps from the shore, inviting guests into an underwater world filled with life. Colourful coral gardens provide a home for countless species of tropical fish, graceful rays, sea turtles, and other fascinating marine creatures that move through the clear turquoise waters each day. Calm, accessible, and teeming with biodiversity, the house reef offers an exceptional setting for both snorkelling and diving.
30 Dive Sites, Endless Ocean Stories
Beyond the house reef, Eri opens the door to more than 30 remarkable
dive
sites, each offering its own underwater character. Within just 20 minutes, Makunudu Garden welcomes divers with vibrant coral formations, thriving reef life, and gentle currents that make every dive feel relaxed and rewarding. Venture a little further to sites such as Bodu Hithi Thila, around 50 minutes away, where dramatic pinnacles, overhangs, and deeper waters attract larger pelagic species and create a completely different experience. No two dive sites are the same. Some are celebrated for colourful coral landscapes, others for encounters with turtles, reef sharks, rays, or schools of shimmering fish.
After the Ocean, Island Ease
After a day beneath the waves, Eri invites you to settle into the gentle rhythm of island life. Sip a refreshing cocktail or fresh tropical juice at the
Beach Shack
as the sea breeze drifts through the palms, then gather at
Soul Kitchen
, where fresh seafood, local ingredients, and globally inspired dishes bring everyone together around the table. As daylight softens into evening, return to the comfort of your
Beach Villa
, where the sound of the waves and the scent of the ocean create the perfect setting to rest, reflect, and wake ready for another day of discovery.
Dive Deeper into Eri’s Ocean Soul
From colourful house reef discoveries to world-class dive sites beyond the lagoon, every underwater experience at Eri reveals a new side of the Maldives. Whether you spend your days exploring beneath the surface or simply embracing the island’s easy rhythm, every moment invites you to feel more connected to the ocean and its remarkable beauty.
