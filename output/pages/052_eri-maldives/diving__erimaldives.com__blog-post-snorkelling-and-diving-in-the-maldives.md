Eri Maldives
is surrounded by the kind of ocean beauty that makes the Maldives truly special. Formed by natural atolls, clear lagoons, coral reefs, and ocean channels, the islands create a wonderful setting for snorkelling and diving. At Eri, this experience begins right by the shore, with a beautiful house reef waiting just a few steps from the beach. It is a simple and beautiful way to experience the rich marine life that makes the Maldives so loved by ocean explorers.
Snorkelling, Just Steps from Shore
Snorkelling
at Eri feels wonderfully effortless, with a beautiful house reef that curves around the island and brings the ocean world close to shore. From your
Villa
, you can step out, follow the sand to the sea, and be in the water within moments. It is this easy access that makes Eri such a special place for snorkelling, whether you are floating above coral gardens, spotting colourful reef fish, or simply enjoying the calm beauty of the lagoon.
A Living Reef Full of Colour and Character
Eri is home to a vibrant house reef filled with colour, movement, and life. As you snorkel or dive around the island, you can discover a beautiful mix of reef fish, striking coral formations, and gentle
turtles
gliding through the water. Many of these turtles are long-time residents of our house reef, each recognised by their distinct markings, personalities, and stories, from familiar Hawksbill turtles to the very first Green sea turtle spotted around Eri.
Diving Adventures for Every Level
Diving
here opens up a world of discovery, with more than
30 dive sites
found around the island. This makes every dive feel different, from colourful reef walls and coral gardens to channels filled with marine life. With diving available all year round, Eri is a wonderful choice for both beginners and experienced divers. Guests can also take part in a full range of PADI courses, making it easy to learn, build confidence, or continue their diving journey in the Maldives.
Island Ease
Beyond the reef, Eri invites you to slow down and enjoy the island in your own way. Ease into calm at
Eskape Spa
, refuel after snorkelling or diving at
Soul Kitchen,
then head for a refreshing drink at
Sip & Dip
or
Beach Shack
. As the day softens, return to your
Beach Pool Villa
, unwind by the water, and watch the sky glow with one of the Maldives’ unforgettable sunsets.
A Diver’s Island in the Maldives
At Eri, snorkelling and diving are only the beginning of the story. With a vibrant house reef, year-round dive adventures, rich marine life, and easy island comforts waiting after every ocean moment, Eri is a warm and wonderful place to experience the Maldives above and below the water.
