Choosing the perfect villa is the first step towards an unforgettable island escape, and at
Eri
, every stay is designed to help guests slow down, reconnect and embrace the island’s gentle rhythm. Surrounded by turquoise lagoons, lush tropical gardens and the vibrant beauty of North Malé Atoll, each villa blends contemporary comfort with vintage Maldivian charm. Whether waking to the sound of the waves, stepping directly onto powder-soft sand or unwinding beside a private pool, every space offers a peaceful sanctuary where meaningful moments unfold naturally.
Sky Studios
Perched above the swaying palms, the
Sky Studios
offer a peaceful retreat with sweeping views across the lagoon and the Indian Ocean. Designed for couples and solo travellers, these light-filled spaces feature a private balcony where mornings begin with fresh sea air and evenings end beneath colourful island sunsets. The calming interiors create an inviting atmosphere, making every stay feel effortlessly relaxing.
Beach Studios
Located just steps from the shoreline, the
Beach Studios
bring guests closer to the island’s natural beauty. Opening onto a private terrace surrounded by tropical greenery, these studios offer direct access to the beach, where soft sands and crystal-clear waters are only moments away. It is the perfect choice for those who love spending their days immersed in nature while enjoying the comfort of a thoughtfully designed island retreat.
Beach Villas
Surrounded by lush vegetation and just moments from the sea, the
Beach Villas
capture the essence of barefoot island living. Inspired by Maldivian artistry, each villa combines warm natural textures with contemporary design, creating a welcoming space to relax after a day of adventure. Guests can step directly onto the beach, unwind on their private terrace or simply enjoy the peaceful surroundings as island life unfolds at an unhurried pace.
Sunset Beach Villas
For those who appreciate golden evenings, the
Sunset Beach Villas
offer front-row views of the Indian Ocean as the sky transforms into shades of amber, pink and gold. Set among swaying palms with direct beach access, these villas provide a tranquil setting where every sunset becomes a memorable part of the stay. Thoughtfully designed interiors and private outdoor spaces encourage moments of reflection, relaxation and connection.
Beach Pool Villas
Blending privacy with easy island living, the
Beach Pool Villas
feature a sparkling private pool surrounded by tropical greenery. Spacious indoor and outdoor living areas invite guests to move seamlessly between cooling swims, peaceful afternoons on the sun deck and quiet moments by the beach. It is an ideal sanctuary for couples seeking an elevated island escape with added comfort and seclusion.
Beach Suite
Offering even more space to unwind, the
Beach Suite
is designed for guests who appreciate the comforts of home alongside the beauty of island living. A tranquil bedroom with beach views is complemented by a separate living area, creating distinct spaces to relax, gather or simply enjoy a slower pace of life. Outside, an expansive sun deck opens onto lush tropical greenery, with the beach just a few steps away.
Discover More Beyond Your Villa
While every villa provides a private sanctuary, Eri invites guests to experience the island beyond their doorstep. Savour fresh local and international flavours at
Soul Kitchen
, unwind with handcrafted drinks at the
Sip & Dip
or
Beach Shack
, and discover holistic wellbeing at
Eskape Spa
, where nature-inspired treatments encourage complete relaxation. Beneath the turquoise waters, vibrant coral reefs, colourful marine life and world-class
diving
experiences await.
Your Island Sanctuary Awaits
Whether choosing a peaceful studio, a spacious beach villa or a private pool retreat, every stay at Eri Maldives is thoughtfully designed to create a genuine sense of belonging. Surrounded by nature and shaped by meaningful experiences, each villa becomes more than a place to stay. It becomes the beginning of a journey where every moment encourages guests to slow down, reconnect and embrace the island’s gentle rhythm.
