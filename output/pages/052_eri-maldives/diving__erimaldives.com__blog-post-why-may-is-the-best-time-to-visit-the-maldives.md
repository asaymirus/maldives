May is considered one of the best times to visit the Maldives, offering warm weather, calm seas, and a more relaxed island atmosphere. At
Eri Maldives
, this time of year brings a softer, more intimate rhythm that makes every stay feel more personal.
Located just 42km from Velana International Airport in North Malé Atoll, Eri is an eco-chic island surrounded by lush greenery, clear lagoons, and a vibrant house reef just moments from shore.
Easy Access, Effortless Island Living
Tucked within the serene waters of North Malé Atoll, Eri sits among turquoise lagoons, swaying palms, and a vibrant house reef just beyond the shore. In May, calm seas and sun-filled days make
the journey
especially smooth, whether you choose a scenic 15-minute seaplane transfer or a 45-minute speedboat ride across gently glistening waters from Velana International Airport. Designed to welcome families, couples, and solo travellers alike, Eri offers a relaxed, easy-going setting where every guest feels at home.
Stays Designed Around You
After sun-filled moments out in the warm May weather, the
Sky Studio
offers elevated views over swaying palms and endless blues, while
Beach Villas
place you just steps from the shoreline, where the sea invites a refreshing pause. For a more private retreat,
Beach Pool Villas
invite slow, cooling dips in your own pool, surrounded by tropical greenery and the gentle rhythm of the sea.
Flavours of the Island
Dining brings people together through flavour, setting, and easy-going moments by the sea. At Eri,
Soul Kitchen
serves fresh, globally inspired dishes throughout the day in a relaxed, welcoming space.
Beach Shack
offers laid-back bites by the shore, perfect between swims, while
Sip & Dip
invites you to unwind with refreshing drinks and light fare as you soak in the island’s gentle rhythm.
Island Experiences at Your Pace
During the month of May, days here are filled with ways to explore and unwind at your own pace.
Dive
into nearby
world-class sites
where reefs, channels, and marine life reveal something new with every outing, or simply slip into the house reef for an easy snorkel just steps from shore. On the surface, water sports add a sense of play, while
Eskape Spa
offers a quieter pause with treatments designed to restore and rebalance.
Where Every Moment Feels Easy
With its easy access, welcoming spirit, and thoughtful experiences, Eri in May offers a relaxed island escape where every moment feels effortlessly rewarding.
