Eri
is a place where the thrill of discovery meets the calm of island serenity. Located in the North Malé Atoll, this eco-chic island resort offers the best of both worlds for travellers seeking variety in their getaway. From adrenaline-filled water sports and vibrant reef dives to quiet beach strolls and endless sunset views, every moment is yours to enjoy.
Whether you’re travelling solo, as a couple or with family, Eri strikes the perfect balance between exciting adventures and peaceful relaxation, all set within the natural beauty of the Maldives.
Your Gateway to Ocean Adventures
Ocean adventure
comes naturally at Eri. Snorkel the vibrant house reef just steps from the beach or dive into nearby dive sites known for their rich marine life, including turtles, reef sharks and colourful corals. Above the waves, enjoy jet skiing, paddleboarding or windsurfing. Excursions like dolphin cruises, island hopping and sunset sailing add even more ways to experience the beauty and energy of the Maldives.
Where Every Villa is a Retreat
After a day of ocean thrills, each
accommodation
option at Eri is designed for rest, ease and a front-row seat to the island’s natural beauty. The Seaview Sky Rooms offer cosy simplicity just steps from the shore and provide a private veranda for soaking in the views. For those craving a little more indulgence, the Premium Beach Villas combine stunning twilight skies with direct beach access. Here, every space is made for recharging in total island style.
Flavours to Fuel and Unwind
Meal times
at Eri are an adventure of their own, blending fresh island flavours with global favourites. Soul Kitchen, our all-day dining restaurant, offers themed buffets and live cooking stations that cater to every craving. For something more laidback, Sip & Dip and Beach Shack serve light bites and tropical cocktails with unbeatable sunset views. Whether you’re fuelling up after a dive or unwinding under the stars, every bite and sip at Eri brings the island spirit to your table.
The Best of Both Worlds Awaits
Eri brings together the best of both worlds with adventure and relaxation in perfect balance. Whether exploring the reef or unwinding by the shore, every moment feels naturally effortless and completely your own.
