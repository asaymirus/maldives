At
Eri Maldives
, the ocean is never far away. Surrounded by turquoise lagoons and a vibrant house reef that encircles the island, Eri offers endless opportunities to discover the incredible marine life of the Maldives. From colourful reef fish drifting through coral gardens to graceful turtles gliding beneath the surface, every swim, snorkel, or dive reveals a new moment of wonder in the water.
Turtles of Eri
Just steps from the shore, beneath the shimmer of turquoise water, a remarkable community lives among Eri’s coral gardens. Hawksbill
turtles
glide through the reef with quiet grace, while Green sea turtles drift across the lagoon in search of seagrass beds. Some are easy to recognise by their shell patterns or tiny markings, while others have become familiar faces with names like Shelby, Hank and Waffle, lovingly given by guests and hosts who encounter them beneath the surface.
Dive Into the Blue
Snorkelling and diving
at Eri open the door to a vivid underwater world. With a house reef close to shore and over
30 dive sites
within reach, every outing brings something new to discover, from schools of pelagic fish and tropical reef species to stingrays, mantas, and sharks. Whether floating above coral gardens or descending into deeper blue, Eri is made for ocean explorers.

Life Beneath the Island Blue
From curious turtles and colourful reef fish to unforgettable moments beneath the surface, Eri Maldives brings you closer to the marine wonders that make the Maldives so extraordinary.
