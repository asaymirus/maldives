One of the most common questions travellers ask before visiting the Maldives is whether it’s possible to swim in the ocean all year round. The answer is a resounding yes. With warm tropical waters, crystal-clear lagoons, and inviting beaches throughout the year, the Maldives offers the perfect setting to enjoy the sea whenever you visit. At
Eri Maldives
, you’ll find calm, turquoise waters that invite you to swim, snorkel, and explore from sunrise to sunset, making every day an opportunity to reconnect with the ocean.
Warm Waters in Every Season
The Maldives enjoys a tropical climate, with sea temperatures typically ranging between 27°C and 30°C throughout the year. Whether you’re taking an early morning dip,
snorkelling
in the afternoon, or enjoying a sunset swim, the water remains comfortably warm in every season. Combined with excellent visibility and calm lagoons, it’s easy to spend hours exploring the ocean.
Swim Alongside Resident Turtles
One of the most memorable encounters at Eri is meeting the island’s resident
sea turtles
. These graceful creatures are often spotted around the house reef, gliding effortlessly through the water or feeding among the coral. Seeing turtles in their natural habitat is a magical experience for guests of all ages and a reminder of the incredible biodiversity that surrounds the island.
Explore More Beneath the Surface
For those looking to venture further, Eri is perfectly positioned near more than
30 dive sites
, each offering its own unique underwater landscapes and marine encounters. Whether you’re taking your first dive or you’re an experienced diver, there are sites suited to every level of experience.
Is It Safe to Swim in the Maldives?
Yes. The Maldives is widely regarded as one of the world’s safest destinations for swimming and snorkelling. The lagoon surrounding Eri is calm and sheltered, making it ideal for both confident swimmers and beginners. While the ocean is home to a diverse range of marine life, the species commonly encountered around resort reefs, including reef sharks, are generally shy and pose little threat to people. In fact, many guests consider spotting a reef shark to be one of the highlights of their holiday. As with any ocean environment, it’s always best to follow best practices such as respecting marine life and avoiding touching coral or wildlife.
Make Every Swim an Adventure
Whether you’re floating in the lagoon, snorkelling over vibrant coral, swimming with resident turtles or setting off to explore nearby dive sites, every moment in the water offers something new to discover. At Eri, the ocean is never far away. From your first morning swim to your final sunset dip, every day is an invitation to reconnect with nature and experience the remarkable beauty of the Maldives, one swim at a time.
