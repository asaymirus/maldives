At
Eri
, every stay is shaped by a thoughtful blend of island living and eco-conscious design. Set amid turquoise lagoons and lush greenery, Eri invites you into spaces that feel grounded, warm, and deeply connected to nature. Natural textures, soft tones, and Maldivian-inspired details create a sense of ease that mirrors the rhythm of the island.
Each villa and studio
is designed as a personal retreat where sustainability and style exist in harmony. Whether waking to the sound of the sea or unwinding beneath swaying palms, your stay at Eri feels intentional, welcoming, and beautifully unhurried.
Barefoot Living by the Sea
Perched above the palms, the
Sky Studio
offers a peaceful escape where elevated lagoon views and soft natural light create a sense of quiet seclusion. Designed for calm mornings and slow evenings, this upper-floor retreat feels open, airy, and deeply connected to the island’s gentle rhythm. On the ground floor, the
Beach Studio
places you just steps from the sand, inviting the ocean breeze and the sound of the sea into your everyday moments. Together, these studios offer two distinct ways to experience Eri, each shaped by comfort, simplicity, and effortless barefoot living.
A Private Sanctuary in the Greenery
Set within lush tropical greenery, the
Beach Villa
offers a relaxed island sanctuary with direct access to soft white sands. Maldivian-inspired design details blend seamlessly with refined comforts, creating a space that feels both grounded and inviting. As the day fades, the
Sunset Beach Villa
takes centre stage, offering uninterrupted views of the horizon as it glows in warm evening light. Peaceful and soulful, it is a place to slow down, reflect, and watch nature’s most beautiful moments unfold.
Your Private Poolside Retreat by the Shore
The
Beach Pool Villa
invites you to slow down in complete privacy, where refined comfort meets the ease of island living. Step onto your sun deck, slip into your own private pool, and let the rhythm of the sea guide your day. Just steps from the shore, this villa offers a serene setting for sun-soaked afternoons and quiet evenings shaped by warmth, space, and calm.
A Stay That Feels Like Home
At Eri, every stay is an invitation to slow down and reconnect with island life. From elevated studios to beachfront villas and private pool retreats, each space is designed to feel personal and in harmony with nature. Days unfold at your own pace, offering comfort, calm, and a true sense of belonging in the Maldives.
