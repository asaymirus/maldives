A holiday is the perfect opportunity to recharge, and at
Eri Maldives
, staying healthy comes naturally. Surrounded by turquoise waters, lush tropical gardens and fresh sea air, every day offers new ways to care for your body and mind. Whether you enjoy keeping active, making mindful food choices or simply spending more time outdoors, the island provides the perfect setting to embrace a balanced lifestyle while enjoying everything the Maldives has to offer.
Start Your Day with a Workout
Begin the morning with an energising session at Eri’s gym,
Muscles
, where modern equipment makes it easy to maintain your fitness routine while away from home. If you prefer exercising outdoors, take a walk along the beach, stretch beneath the palms or enjoy the refreshing ocean breeze before breakfast. Staying active is effortless when every view is an invitation to step outside.
Nourish Your Body with Fresh Flavours
Eating well is easy when every meal celebrates fresh, wholesome ingredients. At
Soul Kitchen
, you’ll find a variety of nutritious dishes, including seasonal fruits, crisp salads, freshly prepared seafood and balanced international cuisine. Throughout the day,
Sip & Dip
offers refreshing smoothies, fresh juices and light beverages that help you stay hydrated under the Maldivian sun, making healthy choices just as enjoyable as indulgent ones.
Restore Body and Mind at Eskape Spa
A healthy holiday is also about making time to slow down and recharge. At
Eskape Spa
, a selection of soothing massages and wellness treatments helps ease tired muscles, relieve tension and restore a sense of balance after a day of island adventures. Whether you choose a traditional massage, a calming body treatment or a peaceful moment in the tranquil surroundings, every experience is designed to leave you feeling refreshed, revitalised and ready to embrace another day in paradise.
Stay Active in the Ocean and Beyond
The Indian Ocean is your natural playground. Explore the vibrant house reef while
snorkelling
, discover colourful coral gardens during a diving excursion or spend the day kayaking, paddleboarding or enjoying other watersports. Back on land, the
multi-purpose sports arena
offers another way to keep moving, whether you’re enjoying a friendly game or simply adding a little variety to your day. Every activity lets you experience the island while supporting an active lifestyle.
Feel Your Best Every Day
Wellness
is about more than exercise and nutrition. Taking time to unwind, breathe in the fresh ocean air and reconnect with nature is equally important. Whether you’re watching the sunrise, enjoying a peaceful afternoon by the beach or ending your day with a gentle stroll beneath the stars, Eri makes it easy to return home feeling refreshed, re-energised and inspired by the simple pleasures of island living.
