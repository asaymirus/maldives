Just beneath the surface of
Eri’s
crystal-clear waters lies a world waiting to be discovered. Surrounded by one of the Maldives’ most vibrant and untouched house reefs, our island is a natural playground for snorkellers, divers and ocean explorers. Here, every dip invites you into a kaleidoscope of coral gardens, colourful marine life and moments of pure underwater magic—no boat rides, no fuss, just you and the reef.
Whether you’re a first-timer or a seasoned snorkeller, Eri makes it easy to explore. Simply step off the beach and into a thriving marine ecosystem filled with graceful turtles and brilliant corals in every hue.
A Snorkeller’s Paradise, Right at Your Feet
Snorkelling at Eri is a must for ocean lovers. What makes it truly special is the house reef that completely surrounds the island, creating a stunning underwater world just steps away from the beach. No boat rides are required, as you can simply step into the water and explore vibrant coral gardens teeming with tropical fish, turtles and more. With calm lagoons and gentle currents, it’s the perfect spot for families and first-timers to enjoy safe, relaxed snorkelling right on their doorstep.
Dive Deep or Drift Free
For those craving deeper
adventure
, Eri is a diver’s dream. With over 30 dive sites
just a short boat ride away, there’s something for every level of diver to explore. And if you prefer to stay above the surface, our watersports centre offers everything from kayaking and paddleboarding to jet skiing, perfect for adding a splash of excitement to your island days.
Beyond Snorkelling, Discover Island Bliss
After a day spent exploring Eri’s vibrant reef, the island invites you to slow down and savour the simple pleasures. Retreat to your cosy
Premium Beach Villa or Seaview Sky Room
, where comfort meets calm and the sound of the waves does all the talking. Refuel with delicious bites at our laid-back
restaurant
and bars, where fresh flavours and island favourites fill every plate. Whether you’re lounging under the palms or enjoying a sunset cocktail by the shore, life beyond the reef is just as rewarding.
The True Essence of Island Living
Eri invites you to experience the Maldives in its most natural, unspoilt form, whether you’re drifting over coral gardens, diving into deeper wonders, or simply soaking up the serenity of island life. Here, every moment is easy, every adventure effortless, and every day ends with the soft glow of sunset and the promise of more to explore.
