The Maldives has long been one of the world’s most sought-after destinations for couples, honeymooners, and anyone looking to celebrate love in an extraordinary setting. Among the many resorts scattered across its islands,
Eri Maldives
stands out as one of the most romantic resorts in the Maldives, blending intimate experiences, beautiful natural surroundings, and heartfelt hospitality. Located in North Malé Atoll, just 45 minutes by speedboat or 15 minutes by seaplane from Velana International Airport, Eri makes it easy for couples to begin their island escape from the moment they arrive.

villas

Beach Studio

Romantic Dining Experiences in Paradise
Great food has a way of bringing people closer, and at Eri, every
dining

Soul Kitchen,
