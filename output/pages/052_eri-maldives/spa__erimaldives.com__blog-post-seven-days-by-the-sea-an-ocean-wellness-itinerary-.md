---

Seven days at Eri gives you time to build a wellbeing journey around the ocean, moving from gentle arrival and mindful movement to reef exploration, restorative spa rituals, and slower moments beside the sea. With a house reef just steps from shore, yoga and meditation in ocean-facing settings, and nature-inspired treatments at Eskape Spa, the island makes the sea a natural part of how you rest, move, and reconnect.
This seven-day
Eri
itinerary is designed for wellness travellers who want their time away to feel restorative without filling every hour. Think of it as a guide rather than a schedule. Follow the water, listen to your energy, and allow each day to find its own flow.
Day One: Arrive and Find Your Island Rhythm
Let your first day be about arriving fully. After
the journey to Eri
in North Malé Atoll, settle into your island sanctuary and give yourself time to become familiar with the sea air, warm sand, and sound of the lagoon. A quiet walk along the shoreline can gently bring movement back into the body after travelling, followed by an easy evening watching the changing light over the water. There is no need to begin your Maldives wellness holiday with a long list of plans. Start by slowing your pace and letting the natural rhythm of the island take over.
Day Two: Breathe and Move Beside the Ocean
Wake with the island and bring your attention to movement. Eri’s wellness
experiences
include yoga and meditation in settings where the sea becomes part of the practice, from soft sands and open pavilions to floating sessions at sunrise. Let breath follow the movement and the surrounding sounds become part of the morning. Afterwards, keep the daylight with time by the water and fresh, seasonal flavours at
Soul Kitchen
. The aim is not to achieve anything. It is simply to notice how different movement feels when the horizon is open and the morning begins without hurry.
Day Three: Let the House Reef Lead You
Today, take your wellbeing journey beneath the surface. Eri’s house reef lies just steps from shore, bringing coral gardens and colourful marine life within easy reach for a morning snorkel. Moving gently through the lagoon offers a different kind of mindfulness, shaped by breath, buoyancy and the shifting light beneath the water. Eri also offers guided
snorkelling
for those who would like support exploring the reef. Once you return to shore, leave the rest of the afternoon open for quiet time beneath the palms or a refreshing drink by the water.
Day Four: Restore at Eskape Spa
By the middle of the week, give your body a slower day at
Eskape Spa
. Eri’s
wellness rituals
draw on nature, with experiences including herbal compresses designed to ease tension and ocean-salt polishes that gently awaken the skin. Botanical facials and other beauty therapies offer further ways to pause and restore. The setting matters too. Tucked into the island’s greenery, Eskape Spa allows the day to soften around you, making this a moment to turn your attention inward before returning to the sea feeling rested and renewed.
Day Five: Move with the Water
Begin your fifth day on the lagoon with
water sports
at Eri, choosing an experience that suits how you want to move. Kayaking and paddle boarding offer gentler ways to explore the water, while other activities bring more energy into the day. Rather than thinking about exercise as a routine to maintain while travelling, let the island change the way you move. Paddle across clear water, feel the warmth of the sun and notice Eri from a different perspective before returning to shore for an afternoon at your own pace.
Day Six: Nourish, Pause and Reconnect
After several days of movement and discovery, allow day six to centre on nourishment. At
Soul Kitchen
, fresh ingredients and seasonal produce make it easy to choose dishes that feel good after mornings spent in and around the ocean, while juices and lighter refreshments can keep the day feeling easy. Follow lunch with a mindful island walk, a swim, or time resting near the shore. Wellness at Eri is not confined to a treatment room. It can be found in how you eat, how slowly you walk, and how much attention you give to the simple sensations of island life.
Day Seven: Return to the Sea
For your final morning, return to whichever ocean ritual has stayed with you most. It might be another sunrise yoga session, a quiet snorkel over the reef, a swim in the lagoon, or simply sitting beside the water as the island wakes. There is something meaningful about ending where your journey began, only now the surroundings feel familiar. Spend the rest of the day lightly, leaving enough space to notice the breeze, the movement of the palms and the sound of water reaching the shore. Your last day does not need a grand finale. Let it be a gentle reminder of the rhythm you found here.
Take the Ocean Rhythm Home
Seven days of ocean-inspired wellbeing at Eri are less about following a strict wellness programme and more about discovering what helps you feel present. The sea can energise you during a morning paddle, quieten the mind during a snorkel, and become the backdrop to movement, nourishment and rest. By allowing these experiences to unfold gradually, wellbeing begins to feel like part of island life rather than something added to it.
