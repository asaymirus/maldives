Looking for a Maldives resort near the airport?
Eri Maldives
offers the perfect blend of convenience and charm. Just a short speedboat transfer from Velana International Airport, this easy-to-reach island paradise lets you skip the long journey and dive straight into relaxation.
Set in a secluded corner of North Malé Atoll, Eri is surrounded by crystal-clear waters, a beautiful house reef with thriving marine life, and lush tropical greenery. Whether you’re here to snorkel or dive into vibrant reefs, unwind on soft sandy beaches, or simply soak in the laid-back island vibes, every moment feels effortlessly easy and entirely unforgettable.
Flavours of the Island
Dining
at Eri is as vibrant and relaxed as the island itself. Start your day with a hearty breakfast at Soul Kitchen and head to Sip & Dip for the best cocktails. For light bites and refreshing drinks throughout the day, Sip & Dip and Beach Shack are your go-to places, offering a relaxed setting by the beach till the sun sets. Whether casual or curated, dining at Eri always comes with a side of sea breeze.
Your Perfect Spot by the Sea
Eri offers a
range of inviting stays
to suit different preferences. The Seaview Sky Rooms offer endless wide ocean views and are mere steps from the beach. If more space is what you need, the Premium Beach Villas offer exactly that with direct beach access. No matter which room you choose, you’ll enjoy a peaceful setting, lush greenery and all the essentials for a relaxed island escape.
Make the Most of Your Stay
At Eri, you’ll find just the right mix of relaxation and memorable
experiences
. Head out on a sunset cruise, try your hand at fishing, or go dolphin watching just beyond the reef. Back on the island, the Eskape Spa is the go-to for calming massages and refreshing treatments in a peaceful setting. And for couples, a vow renewal ceremony on the beach offers a simple, meaningful way to celebrate love in the simplicity of the tropics.
Ocean Adventures All Around
The island is surrounded by a vibrant house reef that circles the entire island, making it the perfect spot for ocean lovers. For certified
divers
and beginners alike, the island is just a short drive away from some of the top
dive spots
in North Malé Atoll. If you’re after a bit more thrill, try out watersports like jet skiing, paddleboarding, or kayaking. Whether you’re under the surface or skimming across it, the water is where the adventure begins.
The Island of Becoming
Eri offers the perfect mix of easy access, authentic Maldivian island charm, and unforgettable experiences, just a short ride from Malé, but a world away.
