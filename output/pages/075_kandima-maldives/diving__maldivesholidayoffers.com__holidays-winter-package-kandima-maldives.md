Kandima Maldives
Kandima Maldives is a lush tropical island located in the Dhaalu Atoll. This affordable Maldives resort is accessible via a scenic
45-minute seaplane
journey or a
30-minute domestic
flight from Malé International Airport, followed by a
20-minute speedboat ride from Dhaalu Airport
. Perfect for a stylish and fun-filled Maldives vacation, Kandima offers an ideal blend of comfort and value with its all-inclusive resort experience.
Kandima Maldives is a chic and affordable holiday destination featuring stunning studios and villas, extraordinary restaurants, trendy bars, and a wide range of on-site activities. Guests can immerse themselves in the island’s vibrant atmosphere while enjoying the best of Maldivian hospitality.
The resort boasts
272 spacious studios and villas
, designed with flexible furniture concepts to suit every guest. Spend your days on private terraces with endless beach views, and your evenings gazing up at the magical Milky Way galaxy.
Whether you're looking for action or relaxation, Kandima has it all. Take a dip in the 100-meter outdoor pool at Breeze Pool Bar, enjoy tennis or volleyball, work out at the state-of-the-art Burn Fitness Centre, or dive into water adventures with Aquaholics Water Sports and Dive Center. Unleash your creativity at the Kula Art Studio, or drop the kids off at the fun-filled Kandiland Kids Club. For ultimate relaxation, the esKape Spa offers Asia-inspired massages, along with stylish beauty and hair treatments.
Kandima Maldives will not disappoint when it comes to dining. With ten distinct restaurants and bars, guests can savor a world of culinary delights. Relax at Breeze Pool Bar, hang out with family at Beach Club, or enjoy a quick bite at the chic Deli. For refined dining, visit Azure for elegant signature cuisine. Watch international flavors come to life at live cooking stations at Flavour and Zest. Sip specialty coffees at Aroma Café, indulge in smoky perfection at Smoked, and savor authentic Asian dishes at Sea Dragon and the Forbidden Bar. For a unique experience, book a private Destination Dining BBQ with your loved ones.
