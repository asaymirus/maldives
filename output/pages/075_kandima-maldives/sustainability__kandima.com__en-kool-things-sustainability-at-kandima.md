Farm Fresh, Island Style!
Launched in 2017, Fresh Labs is our culinary heart, pulsing with the freshest Maldivian produce. We've ditched imported goods for homegrown freshness wherever possible, supporting local agriculture while ensuring your meals are top-notch and eco-friendly. Bite into the greenest, freshest dishes and taste the difference in our farm-to-table philosophy!
Making Waves with Coral Restoration
Dive into a world of underwater wonder with our coral adoption programme! Since 2017, we've been on a mission to protect our dazzling coral reefs. Adopt a coral frame, track its growth online, and become a part of the marine conservation tribe.
Going Coconuts for the Planet!
Our garden waste recycling initiative, launched in 2017, transforms waste into wonders. Utilising everything from coconut shells to dry leaves, we create mulch that enriches the soil and conserves water. This simple yet effective approach underlines our dedication to sustainable practices and a healthier, happier planet.
Giving is in Our Blood
Kicked off in November 2023, our annual blood drive turns compassion into action! With enthusiastic participation, our diverse Krew collected an impressive number of blood bags, vital for those battling thalassaemia in the Maldives. At Kandima, we're deeply committed to community well-being, showcasing our unique approach to helping those in need and having a blast while doing it!
