Get ready for an unforgettable experience! Kandima is thrilled to announce a spectacular Eid Al Adha celebration from June 16 to 19, 2024. This year, we're bringing you a unique fusion of the rich traditions of Maldivian culture and the enchanting allure of Arabian Nights. It's going to be a vibrant four-day festival that promises to captivate guests of all ages.
A Feast to Remember
The celebration kicks off with an exquisite Arabic-themed buffet that will leave your taste buds tingling. Indulge in a delectable array of traditional sweets and juices, all while soaking in an immersive dining experience.
A Cultural Extravaganza
Next, head over to Breeze Bar for the unique Maldivian Maafaaiy event. Watch in awe as our Krew members demonstrate traditional thatching techniques, and be transported to the vibrant atmosphere of a
holhuashi
(communal resting place), recreated by local artists and storytellers.
And the night doesn't end there – groove to the beats at our energetic Arabic DJ Night, where the rhythms of Arabic and Maldivian music seamlessly blend for an unforgettable party under the stars.
Fabulous Artist Workshops
The festivities continue with artist workshops that are designed to engage and inspire you. Breeze Bar will transform into a lively art space, showcasing the incredible talents of local artists. From libaas and thatching artists to ballpoint pen artists, you'll have the unique opportunity to interact with these creative minds, observe their artistic process, and even purchase one-of-a-kind artworks as souvenirs. It's a chance to take home a piece of Maldivian culture and support local talent.
Join the Celebration
So, what are you waiting for? Pack your bags, bring your loved ones, and get ready to immerse yourself in this extraordinary celebration at Kandima!
