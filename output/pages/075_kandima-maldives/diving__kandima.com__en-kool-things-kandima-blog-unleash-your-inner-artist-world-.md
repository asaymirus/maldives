It's time to get artsy at Kandima because we're gearing up to celebrate World Art Day 2024 in style!
At Kandima, we're not just your average island (desti)nation; we're all about embracing life with zest, and that includes nurturing creativity in every form imaginable. Nestled on our lush 3-kilometre island, we're a haven for families, couples, friends, solo travellers, and honeymooners alike. With our chic overwater villas, stylish beach studios, and a plethora of on-island amenities, we promise you a stay that's anything but ordinary!
Now, let's talk about the beating heart of our artistic endeavours: the
KULA Art Studio
. A sanctuary for artists and art enthusiasts alike, tucked away by a serene natural lake, this studio is where magic happens. From regular exhibitions to art performances, there's always something inspiring happening here.
Through our
KULA Art Initiative
, we're empowering the local community by allocating $1 from every room night to support schools and provide art supplies, scholarships, and educational programs. What's more, Kandima hosts an annual KULA Art Festival, a vibrant celebration of local talent, exciting activities, and of course, the epic KULA Run!
But wait, there's even more excitement brewing! In honour of World Art Day, we're teaming up with international artists to bring captivating murals to our island (desti)nation throughout 2024. It's all part of our mission to support art on a global scale and foster a sense of community.
