Maldives is not only the ultimate destination for couples and honeymooners but also the perfect place for family vacations. The island country presents various activities to engage the family and kids of all ages. With snorkelling, varied dining options and numerous outdoor and indoor experiences, visiting the Maldives is the perfect family holiday.
With an
Olympic size football pitch
where kids and teens can play their heart out, beach volleyball, badminton and a tennis court, teenagers will be in for a fun time at Kandima Maldives. That's not all — the game room with the play areas at our lifestyle resort has a snooker, baby foot table, table tennis, board games, and there's a giant chess board on the beach too.
For youngsters with a creative streak, the
KULA Art Studio
presents the perfect place to be at. It is where they can unleash their creativity with acrylics on a canvas, or go wild with coconut painting, henna art, and even coffee painting art classes. They can indulge in endless artistic adventures while also learning some cool and new art techniques.
For those seeking some vitamin sea, Kandima's got daily complimentary house reef snorkelling excursions and special snorkelling trips to spot turtles and reef sharks as well. Teens can have some splashing fun with lots of exciting water sports, with sea bob, jet ski, wakeboard, fun tube or parasailing. And for the young ones who love for the underwater world, our PADI 5-star certified dive centre,
Aquaholics
, offers world-class Discover Scuba Diving initiation to advanced diving courses. We also have a coral adoption program for the ones looking to leave their mark on marine life conservation.
When in Kandima, parents won't have to worry about not having enough options for their teens, who are picky eaters. With
10 dining options
, ranging from modern Asian fusion, Mediterranean, and grilled delicacies to a huge selection of international cuisine, even the choosiest of eaters will find exactly what they crave at our destination. Kandima also offers a mouth-watering sustainable beverage selection and vegan food options.
At Kandima, teens can groove along to the daily live entertainment from DJs and live bands, or go wild at pool parties at the
Breeze Pool Bar
— with one of the longest swimming pools in the Maldives. From movie nights under the stars to local island hopping, there's something for everyone at our uber-fun island destination.
As always, Kandima goes above and beyond to make everyone's vacation anything but ordinary. Our game-changing destination is preparing to introduce an oh-so-fun adventure and theme park, where thrill seekers of all ages are invited to immerse themselves in unforgettable experiences.
The youth can be transported into a fun-filled nation with adrenaline-pumping go-kart rides and experience the sensation of flying and enjoy beautiful panoramic views. with the adrenaline rush guiding them on our zip-line and mini golf with unique obstacles, there are countless games that will challenge the entire family. At Kandima, the whole crown can enjoy the thrilling escapades from oh-so-exciting adventures such as archery and assault obstacles. Let your teens refine their climbing skills in the safe environment of our climbing wall and explore the wide variety of other exciting adventures at our game-changing destination.
