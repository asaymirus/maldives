Jet Ski
Jump on that jet ski and zoom around Kandima's shimmering blue wonderland. If you’re in luck, you might just find yourself racing alongside some playful dolphins.
Water Ski
Zip through the lagoon on water skis and have yourself an epic splash saga. Ride that thrill wave and let your adrenaline do the talking.
Parasailing
Ready for a sky-high escapade? Soar over Kandima's infinite blues and enjoy a bird’s eye view of those oh-so-dreamy shores and lush tropical surroundings.
Windsurfing / Top Cat Sailing
With a high hull design offering a lot of buoyancy, the top catamaran is a beginner’s dream! Skim the waves and soak in Kandima's seriously stylish vistas.
Standup Paddleboarding
Slide serenely on the lagoon's mirror, all while standing tall and catching those high-flying sights. Enjoy the scenery below as fishy friends dance in the water.
Seabob
This underwater scooter gives you a dolphin’s perspective, letting you explore, dive and take in the mesmerising sights. Uber-fun guaranteed!
Kite Surfing
Prop up a kite and surf the waves! Whether you’re a rookie or surf sage, you’re in for a splash-tastic time at Kandima.
Fun Ride
Hold on tight, it’s about to be a bumpy ride! Hop on our fun ride with your loved ones for an anything-but-ordinary experience.
Kayak
Grab a paddle and kayak your way into paradise! Lull yourself with the sea's serenades and the magical world peeking from underneath.
