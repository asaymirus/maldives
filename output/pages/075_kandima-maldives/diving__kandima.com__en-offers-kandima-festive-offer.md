What’s the difference between the main offers (Family, Honeymoon, Stay Longer, Advance Purchase)?
Kandima has an offer for every vibe!
Family Eskape – fun and savings for the whole crew, with kids staying & eating free.
Honeymoon & Anniversary – romantic perks to make your getaway unforgettable.
Stay Longer – the more nights you book, the more you save (plus extra perks!).
Advance Purchase – plan ahead, save, and enjoy added benefits.
Pick your perfect offer and let the island magic do the rest!
Are there any seasonal or limited-time offers?
Oh yes! Kandima always keeps things fresh with seasonal deals and limited-time offers. 
 From festive escapes and honeymoon perks to Stay Longer and Advance Purchase savings, there’s always something kool waiting for you. Check out our Deals page for the latest deals and island surprises!
Do kids stay or dine for free under certain packages?
Yes, in many of our Family Eskape and meal-plan offers, kids can stay and eat for free! Under the Family Eskape deal, for example, two children under 11.99 years can stay and dine on the same meal plan as their parents. Some of our meal plan options also include similar perks depending on the offer period. For the latest details and validity, just reach out to our Krew and we’ll be happy to help you find the best deal for your family!
What special perks do honeymooners receive?
At Kandima, we know that love deserves a little extra sparkle! Honeymooners can look forward to romantic perks like a fruit basket and bubbly on arrival, dreamy bed decorations, and a private three-course dinner under the stars. Stay seven nights or more and enjoy an indulgent couple’s massage too! Plus, a few sweet surprises await to make your island escape as oh-so-Kool as your love story.
Are there discounts for family-friendly or group booking offers?
Yes, we’ve got some pretty sweet perks when you travel with family or in a group.  With our Family Eskape offer, you can enjoy up to 60% off, plus perks like kids staying & eating free (for certain ages), free activities at Kandiland, and fun extras! Groups also benefit, depending on how many rooms/villas and what package you pick, you might unlock meal plan upgrades, room-category upgrades, or discounted transfers. Just reach out to our Krew and we’re happy to help you put together the best deal for your group!
Do you offer any exclusive perks for direct bookings?
Yes! Book directly with us and enjoy kool perks like the best rate guaranteed, extra discounts on spa and water adventures, and special treats for families and honeymooners. Booking directly is always oh-so-rewarding at Kandima!
Are the offers available for all room types?
Mostly yes! Many of our offers apply to a wide range of our villas and studios. Some deals do have selected room-type restrictions or limited availability (especially premium or special categories), so it’s best to check the terms when you book.
If you tell us which room you’re eyeing, we can check whether it qualifies for your chosen offer, we want you to get the best deal possible!
Are there packages that include excursions or water sports?
Yes, some of our offers do include water adventures and island excursions! For example, our Family Eskape package gives you free daily snorkelling, plus a discount on water sports & excursions. Also, many of our all-inclusive and meal-plan deals include complimentary non-motorised water sports (like kayaking, paddle-boarding) and access to scheduled house reef snorkel trips.
