1. Big Ball Volley
While traditional Beach Soccer and Beach Volley are fun to enjoy by the shore, you might want to try out the big ball volley and have an extraordinary time. The rules of the games are easy — to start off; you have to create two teams, with each team playing on either side of the volleyball court. The game is played quite similarly to a regular volleyball game, but the twist is that the volleyball is huge. So, it requires a collective effort from the team to hit the ball. With the ball's massive size, it only adds to the fun time as you bond with each other while scoring some wins.

4. Beach Ball Towel Game
Another fun game that matches the thrill of Big Ball Volley is the Beach Ball Towel Game. In this exciting activity, one person grabs one end of a towel, while the other clutches the other end. Then, they work together to bounce the beach ball — or a balloon filled with water — on the towel and pitch it to the other side of the court. The other team also catches the ball with the towel and tosses it back and forth, adding to the score with the throws before it falls off. Bringing the perfect mix of sun, sand and fun, this game allows you to be active while having a fun time.
5. Pass the water
This is a fun and a definite musty-try for those looking for a simple yet ecstatic time. The rules are simple — While the front of the line has a bucket full of water and an empty plastic cup, the first player fills the cup and passes it backwards over their head to the next player. The players continue passing the cup backwards overhead from player to player until the cup reaches the final player. The challenge of this thrilling game is not to get too wet. And for those looking to take the game to another notch, you can add a blindfold to the game and make it more interesting.
