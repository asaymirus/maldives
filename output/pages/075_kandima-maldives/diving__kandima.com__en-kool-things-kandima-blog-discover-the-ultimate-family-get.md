Room to Play, Space to Chill
Get ready to be wowed! Our uber-kool and extra roomy villas and studios are where style meets comfort on a whole new level of awesome. From the stylish decor in our Beach and Sky Studios to the breezy elegance of our Aqua Villas, we've got the perfect base for your squad. Non-Stop Island Thrills
At Kandima, we turn every moment into an opportunity for excitement and discovery. Our island is buzzing with energy and loaded with activities that will make every family member's heart race. Feasts of Fun
Food at Kandima is far from the usual family fare—it's a circus of flavours and fun! With ten vibrant dining options, we serve up everything from sizzling pizzas to ice cream that's as colourful as our sunsets. Our family-friendly buffets are a playground for your taste buds, and our chefs are the ringmasters of the culinary extravaganza!
Safety in Paradise
We're serious about fun, and even more serious about safety! You'll have peace of mind knowing our on-site medical team is always at the ready. And with our top-notch health and safety protocols, you can kick back, relax, and focus on what's important—like whether to build a sandcastle or jump into the next poolside dance-off!
Moments of Magic
Get set to capture the magic as your family crafts their story in our tropical wonderland. Snap goofy underwater selfies with vibrant fish, giggle through family yoga on the beach, or cosy up for movie night under the stars. And with our SNAP studio, you can freeze these moments into vibrant snapshots that'll outlast any sandcastle.
The Kandima vibe is electric, playful, and bursting with joy—it's the ultimate family hangout in the Maldives. So, if you're wondering which Maldives island is best for families, end your search with a splash at Kandima.
