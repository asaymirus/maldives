The Maldives is not just your typical slice of paradise with sun, sand, and sea. Dive deeper, and you'll uncover a vibrant culture that's as colourful as the coral reefs below!
Enter the vibrant 'Maldivian Malaafaiy' gathering at Kandima Maldives—an anything-but-ordinary celebration of local heritage. Ready for a fun-packed ride deep into tradition, colours, and flavours at your kind of place? Here we go!
Groove to Age-Old Drum Beats
: Let's kick things off with some groove! The moment you enter, the pulsating rhythms of bodu beru drums will get your hips swaying and feet tapping. And if you've never tried dancing barefoot on the beach, now's your chance! The Kandima Krew is more than eager to teach you their steps, so you'll be twirling and laughing under the sun in no time.
Taste the Zest of Maldivian Delights
: Ready to tantalise your taste buds? From savoury short eats to sweet coconut-based treats, the Maldivian Malaafaiy is a foodie's dream come true! Each traditional dish is rooted in history and will make your heart sing, so you're in for a delightful surprise!
Explore Oh-So-Stunning Attire
: Ever wondered what Maldivian attire looks like? Explore the seriously stylish colours and patterns of Maldivian libaas or feyli. Snap some Instagram-worthy photos, and maybe even join in an impromptu fashion parade along the shore!
Kandima invites you to not just visit but truly immerse yourself in the heart and soul of the Maldives. So, wear your brightest smile, bring an open heart, and let's dance, dine, and dive into Maldivian tradition together!
