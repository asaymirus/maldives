This festive season at Kandima Maldives promises to be a seriously stylish affair, as a dazzling
Fire & Ice
celebration immerses guests in a world of holiday magic and adventure. From 22nd December 2024 to 7th January 2025, Kandima transforms into a vibrant elemental wonderland, bringing the heat of festive fire and the cool allure of ice to life in a series of unforgettable events, making this festive season anything but ordinary.
The Battle of Fire & Ice: An Elemental Wonderland
It's time to turn up the fun factor with our Fire & Ice Kids Party! Our little VIPs can dive into an evening of awesome games, dancing, and treats that will kick off their holidays with pure joy. But the excitement doesn't stop there—once the sun sets, the energy shifts to our Frozen Glow Party, where you'll dance under the stars to some epic DJ beats.
Looking to spice up your festive nights? Don't miss the Fire & Ice Cocktail Mixology Class, where you can master the art of mixing up some seriously kool cocktails inspired by the elements. Or, if you're in the mood to chill, head over to
Eskape Spa
and experience our exclusive Fire & Ice Spa Treatments—a blissful fusion of warm and cool therapies that will leave you feeling oh-so-kool.
Family Fun, Kandima Style
Families can look forward to an array of
fun-filled activities
. For those seeking adventure, the Paddle Boat Race on the Lake combines friendly competition with the stunning beauty of Kandima's natural surroundings. After a thrilling race, little ones can cosy up at the Hot Chocolate Station, enjoying warm cocoa as they unwind from the day's excitement.
Feel the festive magic as your krew decorates gingerbread houses, writes letters to Santa, and waits for the big man himself to make a special appearance! Get your heart racing at the KULA Run, Kandima's seriously stylish island race that's anything but ordinary – perfect for families who love a bit of friendly competition. As the day winds down, families can gather under the stars for a movie night, where holiday classics, popcorn, and snacks bring a sense of cosy togetherness.
Sizzle & Sparkle into the New Year
Say goodbye to 2024 and hello to 2025 in true Kandima style! Our NYE Gala Dinner is the ultimate way to kick off the night, setting the stage for a countdown bash with fireworks, live entertainment, and endless vibes. Keep the party going with our New Year's Brunch and another round of the Fire & Ice Mixology session—because we're all about keeping things kool and stylish, long after the clock strikes midnight.
