Pack your bags for a burst of brilliance at Kandima, where we turn the ordinary into the extraordinary with our spectacular KULA Run! The word 'kula' means 'colour' in Dhivehi, the official language of the Maldives, and we take this to a whole new level. Imagine sprinting along the shores of paradise, splashed in vibrant hues, amidst an island (desti)nation buzzing with artistic energy!
Since 2017, Kandima has been the canvas for the
KULA Art Festival
, a riot of colours and creativity. But the highlight? The KULA Run – it's not just a race; it's a technicolour adventure! Picture this: a running trail where every step sprays a rainbow, turning you and the island into a moving masterpiece. This run is where fitness meets fun and every stride is a stroke of paint!
The KULA Run is just one part of the excitement. You'll be diving into beach games that'll have you laughing till your sides hurt, and exploring art in ways you've never imagined. From the elegance of henna to the buzz of digital art, each activity is a chance to unleash your inner artist.
Feeling peckish after all that fun? We've got you covered with some lip-smacking treats that are as delicious as they are artistic. And for those who love to get hands-on, our interactive sessions and art classes will let you create your own souvenirs of this unforgettable experience.
So, are you ready to dash through a dreamscape of dazzling hues? Lace up your shoes for the KULA Run, where every step is an explosion of colour!
