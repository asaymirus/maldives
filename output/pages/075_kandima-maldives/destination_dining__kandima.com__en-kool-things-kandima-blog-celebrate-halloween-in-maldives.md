Eerie Art and Spooky Crafts
If you're ready to get your creative juices flowing, we've got what you are looking for! Dive into Ghostbusters Rock Painting, where you can unleash your inner artist by crafting unique rock souvenirs that will remind you of your island adventure. And for those who want to take their Halloween costume game to the next level, join our DIY Ghostbusters Costume Workshop to transform yourself into the ultimate Ghostbuster. Get crafty with your own proton pack and ecto-goggles—perfect for all the creative spirits out there!
For our little VIPs, we've got even more spooky fun. They can decorate their very own Ghostbusters Treat Bags to gear up for a night of trick-or-treating and join in on the Ghostly Craft Workshop, where they'll create eerie decorations and figurines. It's the perfect way for kids to dive into the Halloween spirit!
Thrills, Chills, and Family Spills
At Kandima,
Halloween
is all about family fun, and we've got tons of activities to keep everyone entertained! Gather the whole family for an afternoon of Ghostbusters Family Games at Breeze Beach. With interactive challenges and oh-so-thrilling dance performances, it's the perfect mix of ghostly excitement and family bonding.
And no Halloween is complete without the classics! Follow the spooky trail across the island for an epic Treat-or-Treating Adventure, where sweet surprises await at every turn. Plus, don't forget to embrace your ghost-busting style at our Ghostbusters Face Painting session and show off your spooktacular look!
