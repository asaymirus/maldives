Aroma Lake: A Peaceful Paddle into Nature
Nestled in the heart of Kandima, just steps from Aroma Café and the vibrant KULA Art Studio, you'll find the serene Aroma Lake. Surrounded by lush mangroves that support the Maldives' ecosystem, this hidden gem invites you to hop on a paddleboat and glide across its tranquil waters. Whether you're paddling or relaxing on the shore, Aroma Lake is your kind of place for island (desti)nation magic and the perfect escape into nature.
Kandiland: A World of Fun for Our Little VIPs
For our little VIPs,
Kandiland
is the ultimate playground! Our kids' club is run by a friendly, multilingual team of fully certified carers, offering a daily programme packed with activities designed to entertain and educate. Whether it's beach treasure hunts, crafty creations, or outdoor games, the fun never stops at Kandiland. So, let your little ones dive into their own adventure while you enjoy some well-deserved me-time!
Find Your Zen at esKape Spa
The award-winning
esKape Spa
is your ultimate retreat for rest and relaxation. Nestled in lush tropical surroundings, it's designed to help you unwind and reset. From exfoliating treatments that leave your skin glowing to healing therapies that soothe your soul, every experience here is crafted to melt your stress away. Try our onsite steam room and plunge pool, or indulge in signature full-body treatments that promise to take your relaxation to a whole new level. It's time to press pause and feel refreshed!
Express Your Creativity at KULA Art Studio
Unleash your creativity at Kandima's
KULA Art Studio

With a palette of vibrant colours and endless inspiration, you can craft your own masterpiece in this seriously stylish space. Here, the only limit is your imagination!
Endless Ocean Thrills with Aquaholics
Aquaholics
is your gateway to the ocean's wonders! From fast-paced water sports like jet-skiing and windsurfing to more leisurely adventures like paddleboarding and snorkelling, there's an aquatic experience for every mood. Explore the stunning coral reefs, glide across the turquoise waters, or relax on a private sunset cruise. Whatever you choose, Aquaholics makes sure your time in the Maldives is nothing short of spectacular!
