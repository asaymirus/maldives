Dreaming of a Maldivian adventure but worried about the cost? Think again! At Kandima Maldives, we're all about redefining the experience with vibrant, playful, and affordable options. Here's your ultimate guide to enjoying the breathtaking beauty of the Maldives without breaking the bank!
Pick the Right Time to Visit
Start your budget-friendly journey with smart travel tips. Booking your flights and stay at Kandima during the off-peak months of May to October can save you a bundle, plus you'll enjoy the bliss of our island (desti)nation with fewer crowds. Keep an eye out for
package deals and promotional offers
– we often surprise our guests with deals that are too good to resist!
Flexible Studios & Villas
At Kandima, we believe that style and affordability go hand-in-hand. Opt for our
flexible accommodation options
ranging from cosy studios to spacious family-friendly suites that are not only for families, but also are a perfect place to stay for you and your friends. Our smart and seriously stylish rooms are designed to provide the ultimate comfort, minus the extravagant price tag.
Dine in Style, Save in Style
Dive into our
culinary delights
without diving deep into your pockets. Kandima hosts a range of dining venues that cater to every taste and budget. From beachside grills to chic cafés, you can savour the flavours of the island while enjoying breathtaking views. Pro tip: check out our
meal plans
for the best dining deals that will keep both your belly and wallet full!
Endless Entertainment
From exciting beach parties to DJ nights, we make sure there's never a dull moment, all while keeping an eye on affordability. Our aim? To provide an insanely stylish yet smartly priced holiday experience!
Adventure Awaits
Who says you can't have fun for less? At Kandima, we've got an array of activities that won't cost you the earth. Snorkelling in our house reef, complimentary yoga and fitness programs, cycling around the island, weekly beach games and pool parties are just a few of the budget-friendly activities you can indulge in.
