Your kool family bucket list summer vacay is awaiting.
Some sun-splashed fun, oh-so delicious food and a streak of K'adventure are all you need to create the koolest tropical vacation with your loved ones.
Here are 7 reasons why Kandima is your kind of place to be!
A fun family experience:
Are you dreaming of lazing on your pool deck with gorgeous ocean views while watching your kids have some sun-splashed fun? Then Kandima is your place to be! From stunning beach side villas and family sky studios to those incredible water villas, there are options in Kandima for every group size. A relaxing and fun-filled vacation with your loved ones is what we strive for at Kandima.
Entertaining kids made easy:
Looking for the ideal resort for your family vacation can be exhausting. In Kandima, our little VIP guests are in for a fun time at Kandiland, with its very own waterplay zone and every day bringing a new set of entertaining activities for kids. With our dedicated, multilingual team of fully certified child caregivers at Kandiland, your kids are in safe hands. There's surely no room for boredom for our little VIPs. Kandima also offers a bespoke nanny service to give parents some well-deserved alone time.
Go big on food:
From freshly-caught seafood to local ingredients, you don't have to let kids' picky young palates cancel out those gourmet meals. Kandima offers a slew of ten memorable dining options including three À la carte dining restaurants apart from all-day dining multi-cuisine buffet areas catering to even the pickiest of eaters – which means that your family will face no shortage of healthy, delicious and kid-friendly food options you'd expect from a tropikal paradise.
Safety under one roof:
Your safety is our priority! Apart from a full-fledged safety programme named 'Kandima OnGuard' to take care of today's critical safety & hygiene needs, Kandima also maintains an in-house team of international general practitioners, nurses, a dentist and a diving safety officer and is open for guests 24/7. We have a full consultation, covid testing, isolation and an ultra-sound unit along with sound machines, a dental clinic, first-attempt cardio support and first-level basic X-rays. You can truly rest your mind in our safe haven.
Unlimited fun for adventurous spirits:
It can be difficult to please everyone in your family on a vacation. However, Kandima leaves no shell unturned to satisfy the desires of diverse personalities. Besides enjoying some of the world's best beaches, there is no dearth of fun activities to do at Kandima. The islands's aquatic center Aquaholics offers a variety of pursuits from PADI certified dive courses to adrenaline-filled activities such as parasailing and taking on the waters with a sea bob. Families can also go on night fishing trips, big game fishing and sunset cruises on dhoni boats and speedboats.
Discover the underwater wonders:
Holidays can be a great learning experience for children and adults alike. Maldives boasts some of the world's most amazing marine life. The coral reefs surrounding the islands are home to more than 1000 species of tropical fish. Kandima's unique coral adoption programme gives you the chance to replant reefs in the ocean. It's a great way to educate your children about the importance of conserving the reefs and protecting endangered species.
Entertainment 7 days a week:
At Kandima, the fun never ends! Make a splash at the Beach Club or head to the Breeze Pool Bar with one of the longest infinity pools in Maldives and let the live bands or DJs make you groove into the night. What's more? Splash the Sun Away Party, Mix and Mingle Kocktail evening, White Parties at the adults-only Forbidden Bar are other special weekly fun parties which you could join. Keep entertained with movie nights under the stars, destination dining, sunset cruises, aqua aerobics or Cast Away Bento picnics, you name it!
