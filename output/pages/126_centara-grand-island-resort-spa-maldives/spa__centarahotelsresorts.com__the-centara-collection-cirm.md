Our Story
Machchafushi Island Resort & Spa Maldives, The Centara Collection
Indulge in the ultimate immersive journey at Machchafushi Island Resort & Spa Maldives, The Centara Collection, where authentic Maldivian-Thai experiences seamlessly intertwine with breathtaking nature and unparalleled underwater treasures waiting to be explored. Discover 112 spacious beachfront and overwater villas with unrivalled views of the twinkling Indian Ocean and award-winning house reef, enriched with vibrant island life.
There are abundant opportunities to discover the exotic life of the Maldives underwater. Snorkelers will find a kaleidoscope of colours in our house reef, and divers can explore an ancient shipwreck dive site just off the island. Machchafushi Island Resort & Spa Maldives, The Centara Collection, provides easy access to some of the world’s most famous dive sites.
At our all-inclusive resort in the beautiful Maldives, we cater to the desires of every traveller, from couples seeking a romantic getaway to families looking for an adventurous holiday.
