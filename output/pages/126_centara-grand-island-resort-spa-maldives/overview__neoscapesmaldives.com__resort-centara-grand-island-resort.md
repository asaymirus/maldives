DESCRIPTION
‘Machchafushi Island Resort offers an affordable five-star resort experience that will appeal to both couples and families alike’
This charming resort offers all guests an “ultimate all-inclusive” holiday experience besides the crystal-clear waters of the Indian Ocean. White colonial-style villas contrast with the intense blue of the water surrounding them. Voted as the Maldives’ most family-friendly resort two years in succession, the island also has all kinds of entertainment and facilities for kids including bunk beds and in-villa PlayStations. Snorkelling and diving enthusiasts will be thrilled by the range of opportunities for outstanding underwater experiences; from the excellent house reef which has its own submerged shipwreck to some of the Maldives’ best snorkelling and diving sites within a stone’s throw of the resort.

Highlights From The Resort
The Ultimate All-Inclusive programme provides activities for everyone, no matter what age and preference.
Voted as Maldives’ most family-friendly resort two years in succession, the island has an ideal family itinerary that includes visits to Camp Safari and E-Zone for the youngsters, and exciting activities like banana boat and tube rides, water sports, beach games and activities at the pool, which includes a pool for the young ones.
Surrounded by warm, azure waters with extraordinary underwater visibility, beautiful coral formations and an exotic marine life, Maldives is one of the world’s top diving destinations. Our PADI Best Dives experts will provide everything that is needed for beginners, those with medium skills, and expert divers.
Our house reef and lagoon provide fascination for snorkellers, and just off the reef there is a dedicated shipwreck that offers easy diving and a close-up view of turtles and whale sharks, manta rays, parrotfish, anemone fish, and surgeonfish.
For a true feel of discovery and adventure, our Ultimate All-Inclusive experience includes a choice of half-day and full-day excursions. Each guest can enjoy all excursions once per stay, choosing from a snorkelling tour, sunset cruise, local island discovery tour to visit a Maldivian village on a nearby island, and sunset fishing.
