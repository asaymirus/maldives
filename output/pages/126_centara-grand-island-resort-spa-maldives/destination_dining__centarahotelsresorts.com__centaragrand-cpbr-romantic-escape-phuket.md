Details
Slow down and reconnect by the shores of Phuket with a romantic escape thoughtfully designed for couples. Your stay begins with a complimentary one-category room upgrade (subject to availability), a sparkling wine welcome, and elegant romantic bed decoration — setting the tone for a truly memorable arrival.
As the sun sets over the Andaman Sea, enjoy an intimate beachfront dinner for two, complemented by a sweet chocolate and macaron amenity prepared in your room. Complete your experience with a soothing 30-minute couple’s spa treatment, created to bring relaxation and balance to your time together.
Perfect for honeymoons, anniversaries, proposals, or simply a meaningful getaway, Romantic Getaway by the Sea invites you to celebrate love through curated moments, personalised touches, and the warm, attentive service that defines the Centara experience.
