If you are planning a trip to Thailand during April, attending the iconic Songkran festival should be on your bucket list. Songkran is Thailand’s traditional New Year festival and is also considered the world’s biggest and most fun water fight! Beyond that, Songkran is also an important cultural ritual and a time for Thai families and the community to come together for festive gatherings and to seek merit from Budhha and blessings from the elderly.
Festivities take place all across the country, and Pattaya, one of Thailand’s famed coastal cities, is no exception. Celebrating the Songkran festival in Pattaya is a great choice for a family-friendly holiday with its beachside fun, and a wide range of activities that are suitable for all ages.
When Is Songkran Celebrated In Pattaya?
Songkran is typically celebrated throughout the country from April 13 to 15; however, Pattaya extends the fun with its local Wan Lai Festival, which runs from April 12 to April 19, 2025. Wan Lai, which translates to "Flowing Day," is a local tradition in Pattaya and nearby coastal towns like Naklua and Bang Saen. It’s simply an extended celebration of all that entails Songkran. This gives families an excellent opportunity to explore both the fun and traditional aspects of the Songkran festival in Pattaya at their own pace without feeling rushed.
Let’s explore why you should be celebrating Songkran in Pattaya:
Beachside Fun
Amp up your water fights by hitting the beach! One of the best things about celebrating Songkran in Pattaya is soaking up the sun, enjoying the salty sea breeze, and diving into some epic water battles right by the shore. It's the perfect chance to hang out with locals, meet fellow travellers, and have a blast! And if families need a little downtime from the action, chilling by the sea or building sandcastles with the kids is a super fun option, too.
A Fun and Family-Friendly Atmosphere
The Songkran festival in Pattaya is famous for its lively celebrations, and it's also a great spot for families! With designated family zones, cool cultural activities, and loads of entertainment, everyone can join in on the fun at their own pace - no rush!
Top Spots for Songkran Fun in Pattaya
To get you started, here’s our guide on the top spots to celebrate Songkran in Pattaya.
Pattaya Beach Road
If you want to be in the centre of the action, Pattaya Beach Road is the place to be. Get ready for epic water battles in the streets, where the full family can grab some water guns and dive straight into the action. You will also find live music and some amazing cultural performances to indulge in. After all the fun, families with younger children may want to unwind for a while. Don't worry - there are plenty of quieter nooks and areas where families can relax and enjoy the festivities without the chaos.
Naklua
If you are seeking a more traditional and cultural experience, visit Nakula. Here, you can observe colourful parades, cultural performances, and religious ceremonies. This part of Pattaya is more focused on embracing the spiritual side of Songkran and is perfect for families who want to introduce their children to the cultural aspects of the festival and the quintessential Thai way of life.
Central Festival Pattaya
Songkran is also a fantastic time for retail therapy, with many local and international brands offering discounts or promotions. If you want a good mix of both entertainment and shopping, there’s much to do at Central Festival Pattaya. This mall also hosts its own special Songkran festival events in Pattaya, which usually include live music, dance performances, and food stalls serving delicious, traditional Thai food. It’s also a good spot to take a break to cool down from the burning April sun!
