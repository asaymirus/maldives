The most wonderful time of the year is just around the corner, and
Nova
is set to turn up the island glow. As the festive season unfolds under island skies, the island comes alive with Maldivian Magic. Picture lanterns flickering by the beach, the scent of fresh seafood on the grill, and Boduberu drums calling everyone to gather and celebrate.
The countdown to island joy has begun, and it is looking vibrant, soulful and full of cheer. Every gathering, every song, every shared meal is crafted to bring people together in true Nova spirit, creating memories that feel as warm as the island sun.
Where Culture Meets Celebration
At Nova, this festive season is an invitation to slow down and reconnect. The festive theme,
Maldivian Magic – A Festive Fusion,
is about feeling the heartbeat of the islands through their people, traditions and timeless beauty. Every breeze carries a story from the past, every wave shares ancient wisdom, and every shared moment adds to a living tapestry that has shaped the Maldives for centuries. From music and craft to food and friendship, each experience reflects the warmth of authentic Maldivian culture, heritage and the joy of togetherness.
Island Sparkle and Festive Cheer
Christmas at Nova is a celebration of joy, light and togetherness. The festivities begin with a magical tree lighting ceremony that fills the island with warmth and wonder, followed by a gala dinner where island flavours and global favourites come together. Santa makes a special arrival, spreading smiles and festive cheer across the beach. As the year draws to a close, Nova welcomes the New Year with a dazzling gala dinner, a mesmerising fire show and fireworks that shimmer over the Indian Ocean. It is a time to celebrate new beginnings and the promise of more good soul days ahead.
A Season to Remember
From Christmas to New Year’s, this festive season is a celebration of Maldivian authenticity, connection, and joy – a time to share the warmth of the islands and the spirit of togetherness, as we welcome a new year filled with more good-soul days ahead.
