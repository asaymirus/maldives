Your time in the Maldives becomes even more meaningful when you begin to experience the culture that shapes everyday island life. You discover and feel these traditions in simple island moments, shared stories, and the comforting flavours of the freshly prepared island dishes. At
Nova Maldives
, your soul place, these cultural touches slip naturally into your good soul days.
Here, culture is something you experience gently through people, places, and the surrounding sea. Your Soulmates are always nearby to share stories, introduce traditions, and guide you towards moments that feel welcoming and real.
Where Island Stories Are Shared
At Nova,
Holhuashi
is where Maldivian
culture
comes alive through relaxed island moments shared together. Here, you settle into a welcoming space where conversations flow easily, and stories of island life are shared. Sit beneath the shade as Soulmates tell local tales, or watch traditional crafts take shape in the hands of skilled artisans. You learn a few Dhivehi words, hear the gentle rhythm of island drums, and enjoy the quiet warmth of conversation. Each moment at Holhuashi invites you to slow down, connect, and feel closer to the traditions of the Maldives.
Discovering Maldivian Flavours
At Nova, discovering Maldivian culture often begins at the table. At
Soul Kitchen
, island-inspired dishes and themed evenings celebrate local favourites and traditional flavours. You taste coconut-rich curries, fresh reef fish, and fragrant spices that reflect the islands’ deep connection to the ocean. You can even join a relaxed
cooking session
and learn how these traditional flavours come together. Every dish brings you closer to the comforting taste of Maldivian home cooking.
Where Island Life Meets the Ocean
Life in the Maldives has always been shaped by the ocean, and here at Nova, you feel that connection the moment you step into the
water
. Located in the heart of
South Ari Atoll
, the surrounding seas are home to vibrant reefs, gentle
whale sharks
, graceful manta rays, and countless tropical fish. Slip into the water with your
snorkel
or
dive
gear and discover a reef alive with colour and marine life. Bright coral gardens spread across the reef while schools of tropical fish glide past in every direction. Each encounter beneath the surface like these reminds you of how deeply life in the Maldives is connected to the ocean.
A Deeper Connection to the Maldives
From relaxed evenings at Holhuashi to shared meals and ocean adventures just beyond the shore, Maldivian culture becomes part of your everyday island moments. These are the moments that make your time at nova feel extra special. Whenever curiosity leads the way, your Soulmates are always nearby to share more stories and traditions.
