The Best Time to Visit
The Maldives is a year-round destination, but the best time to visit depends on your travel preferences. The dry season, from November to April, brings warm temperatures, calm seas, and endless sunshine—ideal for those who want to make the most of the island’s outdoor experiences. The monsoon season, from May to October, offers a quieter, more tranquil atmosphere, with the added benefit of lush, refreshed landscapes. For marine enthusiasts, South Ari Atoll is one of the few places in the world where
whale sharks
can be spotted year-round, making it a dream destination for
divers
and
snorkellers
alike.
Nova embraces the beauty of every season. Whether it’s basking under the sun or watching a tropical shower pass over the ocean, the island’s charm never fades. With consistent warm weather, incredible
