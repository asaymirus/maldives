The Floating Coral Nursery
Join our marine biologists in launching Nova’s latest innovation—a floating, depth-adjustable
coral nursery
crafted to help coral fragments flourish even amid rising ocean temperatures. In this secure, temperature-stable haven, resilient coral fragments, carefully gathered from surrounding waters, are given the perfect environment to grow strong. Once they reach maturity, these young corals will be transplanted to Nova’s house reef, enhancing the ecosystem and supporting future marine habitats. In the coming weeks, Nova’s marine biology team will lead planting sessions where you’ll have the unique opportunity to place “coral seeds” into the nursery, nurturing a new generation of coral and directly contributing to the reef’s future.
Adopt Your Coral Frame
For guests looking to make an even more personal impact, Nova offers the chance to
adopt and plant your own coral frame
. Our marine biologists carefully monitor each frame, providing bi-annual updates so you can watch your coral’s journey long after you’ve left the island. Imagine knowing that your stay contributed to a thriving underwater world—a memory that endures and grows with each passing year.
Note from Our Resident Marine Biologist
Coral reefs are the life source of the Maldivian ecosystem, yet they’re increasingly at risk. Rising ocean temperatures and the stress of coral bleaching threaten these delicate ecosystems. At Nova’s new coral nursery, you’re helping fight back by providing a safe environment for young coral to grow and ultimately strengthening the local reef’s ecosystem. Our oceans are experiencing their fourth significant bleaching event. We hope to take direct action to cultivate the next generation of corals and support the vibrant marine ecosystem that the Maldives is so well known for.
