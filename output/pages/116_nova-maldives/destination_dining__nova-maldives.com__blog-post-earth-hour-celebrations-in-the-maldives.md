This Earth Hour, we’re embracing the beauty of the night and the power of connection. As the island dims its lights in honour of our planet, our good soul community will come together for an evening of mindful moments, creative expression, and eco-conscious celebration.
Join us as we journey through a night of soulful experiences and eco-conscious celebrations at
Nova Maldives
.
Powering Change, One Pedal at a Time
Imagine a dining experience powered by you. In an exciting twist to Earth Hour, guests will have the chance to hop on a cycle that will generate energy for
Soul Kitchen
, keeping the spirit of sustainability alive in the most interactive way possible. As the night unfolds, this initiative will shine a light on the power of collective action, proving that even the smallest efforts can contribute to a more sustainable future.
A Beachfront Dinner Under the Stars
Earth Hour is a moment to pause, reflect, and appreciate the beauty of our planet. To mark the occasion, guests will be treated to a special beachfront
dining experience
, with a carefully curated set menu available. Under the soft glow of candlelight and the shimmering Maldivian sky, this will be an opportunity to savour soulful flavours in the most enchanting setting.
Sip, Glow, and Celebrate
No Nova celebration is complete without a little magic in a glass. Throughout the evening, guests can enjoy our signature Glow Cocktail, a special creation available exclusively during Earth Hour.
This vibrant concoction, inspired by the beauty of bioluminescent waters, is a testament that even in darkness, light always finds a way to shine.
The 60+ Challenge
As part of Nova’s Earth Hour celebrations, our teams will take on the 60+ Creative Challenge—an inter-club competition where each team will craft their own sustainable 60+ display on the beach in
representation of Earth Hour. Guests will have the chance to explore these stunning installations and vote for their favourite, adding an extra layer of excitement to the evening.
A Moment to Reflect, A Night to Remember
Here, we believe in the power of small actions to create big ripples of change. This Earth Hour is not just about switching off lights – it’s about embracing
sustainability
, fostering community, and celebrating the beauty of our planet together.
Join us for a night where good soul energy meets conscious living, and let’s make every moment count.
