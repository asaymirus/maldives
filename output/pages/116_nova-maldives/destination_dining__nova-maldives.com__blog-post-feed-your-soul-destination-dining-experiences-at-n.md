Dining in the Maldives
is an experience that goes beyond the plate, and at Nova, we turn every meal into a soulful journey. Set against the backdrop of our stunning natural island, our
destination dining options
offer more than just a taste of global cuisines — they create unforgettable moments that nourish both your palate and your spirit.
From romantic dinners under the stars to private feasts on secluded sandbanks, each dining experience at Nova is crafted to spark joy and leave you with memories that will last a lifetime.
Floating Breakfast
For a truly unforgettable start to your day, indulge in a sumptuous
floating breakfast
as you bask in your private pool, surrounded by the gentle warmth of the sun. Whether you’re indulging in smoked salmon and chive blinis, perfectly prepared eggs benedict, or a selection of buttery pastries and exotic fruits, this experience is a feast for the senses.
Dining at the Nava Pavilion
For those seeking a truly magical dining experience, the Nava Pavilion offers an intimate setting like no other. Nestled on a secluded wooden platform in the middle of Nova’s turquoise lagoon, this exclusive dining spot is perfect for a romantic evening or a special celebration. Savour the freshest maki rolls or a crisp spinach and panzanella salad as the stars shimmer overhead.
Rendezvous at Pearl
For a dining experience that’s as close to the ocean as you can get, Pearl is your destination. Set on a platform just metres from the gentle waves, this romantic spot invites you to embrace the soothing sea breeze and the sound of the ocean as your backdrop. As you dine under the stars, the sky painted in hues of twilight, Pearl offers a menu that’s as delightful as the surroundings.
Beachfront Dining
Why dine indoors when you can have the soft, powdery sands of Nova’s beaches beneath your feet? Our beachfront dining experience takes you to a secluded spot on the island’s pristine shores, where you can enjoy a sumptuous meal under the open sky. Surrounded by whispering palm trees and the gentle lapping of the waves, this setting is perfect for a romantic dinner or a quiet, soulful meal with loved ones.
Secluded Sandbank Soirée
Allow us to whisk you away to a tiny, untouched patch of sand surrounded by nothing but turquoise waters and endless horizons. Here, you’ll be treated to a gastronomic feast prepared by our expert chefs. As you marvel at the breathtaking views and soak in the seclusion, you’ll discover that this is more than just a meal—it’s a moment of pure bliss that will stay with you forever.
