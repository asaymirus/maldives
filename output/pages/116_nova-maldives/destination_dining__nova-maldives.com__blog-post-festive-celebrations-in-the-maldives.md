Where Culture Meets Celebration
At Nova, the
festive season
is where culture and celebration come together in the most natural way. Evenings at the
Holhuashi
invite you to sit beneath the banyan tree, share stories and feel like part of the Nova family. The rhythm of Boduberu, local crafts and simple island rituals give each gathering a warm Maldivian heartbeat.
Christmas
brings tree lighting, island-style carols and a joyful visit from Santa on the beach, filling the island with smiles and gentle festive cheer. As the year closes, gala dinners, live performances and fireworks over the Indian Ocean create a New Year’s Eve filled with light, connection and easy island energy.
Festive Flavours & Soulful Feasts
Festive dining at Nova brings everyone together around shared tables, with every meal feeling relaxed, generous and full of flavour. At
Soul Kitchen
and
Solis
, you can savour long, easy meals with ocean views, while
Flames
brings the glow of the grill to balmy island evenings.
Mizu
adds a different kind of magic with Ocean Umami dinners and fresh creations that showcase the best of the sea. On special nights, the Maldivian Fisherman’s Feast under the stars turns dinner into a celebration of local flavours, stories and connection. From crafted cocktails at
Wink
to shared plates passed between friends, every bite and sip during the festive season feels made for togetherness.
A Festive Season to Remember
At Nova, Maldivian magic turns the festive season into an easy flow of island days shaped by the ocean and the warmth of local culture. Whether you are gathering at Holhuashi or sharing a festive dinner by the water, every moment is about being together and feeling at ease. This is your soul place, where festive celebrations become good soul days you carry with you long after you leave the island.
