Soulful Dining for Two
A vegan honeymoon feels even more special with dining that brings colour, flavour and connection to every moment.
Soul Kitchen
, the island’s bright all day dining buffet restaurant, offers a generous variety of plant-based dishes that are nourishing and delicious.
Flames
, the signature grill house, adds its own charm with plant-based burgers, thoughtful appetisers and sweet vegan treats. Each meal becomes a relaxed moment to share and savour together.
Plant-Based Goodness in Every Corner
Mizu
brings a vibrant plant-based touch to overwater dining with vegan sushi rolls, flavourful vegetarian teppanyaki and creative dishes that feel good for the soul.
Wink
keeps the playful energy flowing with vegan wraps, burgers, pizzas and the option to build your own pizza with colourful toppings.
Solis
adds sunny, easygoing moments with refreshing drinks to quench your thirst whenever you need, making each stop on the island feel relaxed and effortlessly joyful.
Island Experiences to Share Together
Destination dining
adds a beautiful layer to your honeymoon, with moments that feel intimate, calm and made just for two. You can enjoy a private meal by the beach with your soulmate, with thoughtful vegan options that make every bite feel nourishing and special. Floating breakfasts also come with plant-based choices, letting you begin the day in the water with a sense of lightness and joy. These experiences take your honeymoon to the next level, inviting you to slow down, savour the moment and feel deeply connected in your soul place.
