Traditions Woven into Celebration
This festive season, we celebrate the soul of the island. We invite you to slow down and tune in to Boduberu rhythms and the hush of the coconut palms. Our festive moments are inspired by the culture that has shaped Maldivian life for over two millennia, reimagined with a fresh island spirit at Nova. You will savour flavours inspired by true Maldivian customs and discover timeless traditions that celebrate togetherness.
