Is Nova Maldives All Inclusive?
Yes, we offer two meal plans for you to choose from, one of which is an All Inclusive plan. You can check out the plans to select the perfect one for your soulful stay!
What is included in the Nova Maldives All Inclusive package?
Nova’s All Inclusive plan comes with many perks. It includes breakfast, lunch and dinner at our main restaurant, Soul Kitchen, for the entirety of your stay, and even a three-course dinner at our speciality grill house Flame, plus a 20% discount on dinner at our à la carte restaurant Mizu. Additionally, you can join the communal brunch and community gathering tea party at our main bar, Wink, and enjoy unlimited daily beverages. The All Inclusive perks also include complimentary minibar access and daily restocking of the in-Villa tea and coffee facilities. You can also enjoy the complimentary rental of snorkelling equipment, standard kayaks (excluding glass kayaks), and paddleboards and join the group classes at our state-of-the-art fitness centre Muscles.

By choosing the All Inclusive package, you will be able to relax and enjoy all of the facilities around our island without having to worry about additional bills or planning, and get an excellent value for money. Let us take care of everything for you, while you spend your days relaxing, rejuvenating and making soulful memories.
