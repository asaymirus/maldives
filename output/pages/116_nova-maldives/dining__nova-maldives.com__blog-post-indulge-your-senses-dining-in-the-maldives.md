At Nova Maldives, we believe that the essence of a truly memorable vacation not only lies in the beauty of its surroundings but also in the richness of its
dining experiences
. Set against the backdrop of the mesmerising Maldives, our mouthwatering dining options are designed not just to nourish the body but also to feed the soul.
Soul Kitchen – All-Day Dining
At
Soul Kitchen
, our commitment is to bring you the freshest tastes from around the globe. Whether you’re starting your day with a vibrant breakfast or winding down with a hearty dinner, our menu offers an array of flavours that promise to take you on an unforgettable culinary journey. Accompanied by incredible views, each meal here is a celebration of global cuisine made to inspire and delight.
Flames – The Art of Grilling
Experience the sizzle at
Flames
, our specialised grill house where the freshest local fish, seafood, meats and vegetables are transformed into exquisite dishes. Grilled to perfection using time-honoured techniques, the flavours of our ingredients speak for themselves. Sit back and enjoy a meal that tantalises your taste buds while the aromatic scents of grilled delights fill the air. And of course, all our restaurants offer plenty of vegetarian and vegan options to please any palate.
Mizu – Overwater Teppanyaki
Mizu
offers more than just a meal; it provides a spectacle. Located over the water, our Teppanyaki restaurant pairs the fresh bounty of the ocean with the flair of live cooking. Watch as our chefs whip up delights on the Teppanyaki grill, making every dish a theatrical performance that’s as entertaining as it is delicious.
Wink – The Heart of Nova
Step into
Wink
, our main bar, where the vibe is always right for an evening of relaxation and good company. Pair your favourite handcrafted cocktail with the rhythm of live music and the crunch of savoury snacks. It’s the perfect spot to unwind, laugh, and enjoy the night with new friends or old.
Solis – Poolside Bliss
At
Solis
, our pool bar, the vibe is laidback and utterly relaxing. Dive into a selection of delicious cocktails, tropical blends, and freshly squeezed juices. It’s the ideal place to lounge, sip, and enjoy the Maldivian sun. Let each drink refresh and rejuvenate your spirit as you soak in the panoramic views.
Destination Dining – An Epicurean Escape
Elevate your experience with our
destination dining
options. Imagine a private banquet on the sandy shores or a castaway dinner as the sunset paints the sky in hues of orange and pink. Here, exquisite flavours meet the world’s finest wines, creating a dining event that is as breathtaking as the scenery around you.
Floating Breakfast – Elevate Your Mornings
Begin your day floating over serene waters with our signature
floating breakfast
. Indulge in pastries, tropical fruits, and an assortment of exquisite breakfast items while you bask in the early morning tranquillity of the Indian Ocean.
At Nova, every dining venue is designed with the intention of creating soulful moments and real connections. Come, let us take you on a journey that’s as inspiring as it is indulgent.
