In the serene embrace of Nova, where the symphony of waves whispers tales of wonder, lies a realm that calls to the dreamers and the wanderers. This laidback oasis is a canvas where your deepest desires come to life.
Here, we unveil the top five experiences that will etch themselves into your soul at Nova.
Staying at a Water Villa – A Dream Over the Lagoon
Retreat to our
water villas
, where elegance floats on the serene sea. Each villa is a cocoon of laidback luxury, a whisper of romance and comfort suspended over the azure waters. Wake to the kiss of the morning sun, and sleep to the lullaby of the waves.
Whale Shark Snorkeling – A Dance with the Ocean’s Gentle Giants
Beneath the shimmering surface of
the endless sea
, an extraordinary encounter awaits. Glide through the azure depths with the majestic whale sharks, these gentle titans of the deep. Feel the pulse of the ocean, and let your heart synchronise with the graceful ballet of these magnificent creatures.
Eskape Spa – A Sanctuary for the Soul
At
Eskape Spa
, find a haven where serenity kisses the soul. Here, treatments are not mere rituals; they are conversations with your inner self, whispered through the language of touch and nature’s healing embrace. Let the gentle breeze and the rhythmic lullaby of the waves escort you to a world of inner peace and rejuvenation.
Overwater Teppanyaki Dining at Mizu – A Symphony of Flavours
Imagine a culinary experience where the ocean’s heartbeat sets the rhythm. At
Mizu
, overwater teppanyaki dining is not just a meal; it’s a celebration of the senses. Watch the horizon melt into a palette of fiery hues as our chefs craft a tapestry of flavours, each bite a testament to the artistry and finesse that define Nova’s culinary journeys.
Sunset Dolphin Cruise – A Serenade by the Sea
As the sun dips below the horizon, embark on a voyage where the ocean’s most playful denizens, the dolphins, perform a ballet in the golden twilight. Watch them leap and dance in the rosy glow of the setting sun, a spectacle that captures the heart and liberates the spirit. It’s more than a cruise; it’s a rendezvous with joy and wonder.
