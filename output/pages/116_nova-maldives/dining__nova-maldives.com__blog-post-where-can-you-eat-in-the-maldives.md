At
Nova Maldives,
dining is an experience shaped by feeling, where every moment unfolds with ease and intention. Here, flavours are guided by nature and inspired by the island’s rhythms, bringing people together over dishes that feel both nourishing and meaningful. From relaxed shared meals to thoughtfully crafted creations, each bite invites you to slow down, savour, and connect more deeply with the place, the people, and yourself.
Where Flavours Flow Naturally
At
Soul Kitchen
, a globally inspired menu brings together the freshest tastes from around the world, served in a bright, easy-going space where meals unfold naturally from morning to evening. Each dish is crafted to nourish and connect, inviting you to savour every moment. Just steps away,
Solis
, the poolside beach bar, offers refreshing drinks and light bites, where sunlit afternoons drift into relaxed, feel-good gatherings by the water.
A Journey of Taste and Atmosphere
At
Flames
, the art of grilling takes centre stage, where premium meats, fresh seafood, and seasonal produce are transformed over open fire into deeply flavourful dishes. At
Mizu
, an overwater teppanyaki experience draws from East Asian traditions, with skilled chefs crafting each dish against sweeping views of the Indian Ocean. As evening settles,
Wink
, the island’s main bar, becomes a place to gather, where expertly mixed cocktails and easy conversation carry the night forward.
Where Dining Becomes an Experience
Dining
at Nova extends
beyond the table
into moments shaped by setting and feeling. Begin the day with a floating breakfast in the privacy of your villa, or gather for a twilight seafood barbecue by the shore. As evening falls, intimate dinners unfold under the stars, from the quiet charm of Nava Pavilion to secluded sandbank escapes. Sundowners by the sea invite a gentle pause, where each experience feels personal, unhurried, and deeply memorable.
Where the Day Gently Unfolds
In between meals, the island invites you to explore at your own pace. Visit
Holhuashi
on Friday afternoons to discover the culture and traditions of the Maldives, or set out to swim alongside
whale sharks
with the team at Sampa. Ease into stillness at
Eskape Spa,
or simply retreat to your
sunset beach villa with pool,
where time slows and each moment unfolds gently, just as it should.
More Than Just a Meal
Dining at Nova Maldives is simply about enjoying good food in good company, in places that feel just right. Some moments are lively, others are quiet, but all of them invite you to slow down and be present. It’s not just about what’s on the plate, it’s how it makes you feel.
