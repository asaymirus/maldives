At
Nova
, dining is about more than what’s on your plate. Set within a warm, social island setting, Nova brings people together through flavours that spark conversation and moments that feel easy and unforced. From laidback beachfront dining in the Maldives to lively evenings filled with laughter, every experience is designed to feel welcoming, joyful, and easy.
Each restaurant and bar reflects Nova’s community spirit, blending fresh global flavours with laidback island energy. Whether you’re sharing a long meal with new friends or enjoying something simple by the sea, dining at Nova feels natural, inclusive, and meant to be enjoyed together.
Good Food for Good Soul Days
Dining
at
Soul Kitchen
feels relaxed, social, and effortlessly inviting. This all-day dining space brings people together over fresh, flavourful dishes inspired by cuisines from around the world, served in a bright setting with sweeping ocean views. Whether you’re starting your day slowly or gathering for an easygoing meal, Soul Kitchen sets the tone for shared moments, good conversation, and food that feels comforting and generous.
Where the Grill Sets the Mood
Flames
take on a warmer, more intimate rhythm. This speciality grill is where expertly prepared local seafood, premium meats, vegetarian delights and flame-kissed flavours come together in a setting that feels relaxed yet refined. As the evening unfolds, Flames invites you to slow down, savour each bite, and enjoy a meal shaped by bold tastes, gentle conversation, and the easy glow of island nights.
Where Skill Meets the Sea
At
Mizu
, dining becomes an experience shaped by movement, sound, and skill. Set over the lagoon, this teppanyaki restaurant invites you to gather around the grill as chefs prepare each dish with precision and flair, turning every meal into a shared moment. Fresh ingredients, ocean views, and the gentle rhythm of the water create an atmosphere that feels immersive, engaging, and quietly memorable.
From Poolside Sips to Social Nights
At
Wink
and
Solis
, drinks and moments unfold at their own pace. Wink is Nova’s lively social hub, where handcrafted cocktails, easy bites, and music bring people together from morning through to evening. By the pool, Solis offers a laidback setting for freshly squeezed juices, refreshing drinks, and relaxed sun-soaked moments. Together, they create easygoing spaces to gather, unwind, and enjoy the flow of island life at Nova.
Dining That Brings Us Together
From relaxed meals to immersive experiences and easy poolside moments, every space invites you to eat well, linger longer, and savour good soul days, one shared moment at a time.
