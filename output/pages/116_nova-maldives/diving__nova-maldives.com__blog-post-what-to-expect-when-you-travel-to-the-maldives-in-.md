Sunshine-filled days make May a beautiful time to visit the Maldives, and
Nova Maldives
feels especially inviting during this season. As the waters begin to welcome the first manta rays, a sense of quiet wonder builds beneath the surface, adding something special to each day. It is a month shaped by warmth, gentle energy, and moments that draw you closer to the rhythm of the island.
A Season of Mantas and Ocean Encounters
May marks the beginning of manta season in the Maldives, when these
gentle giants
begin to grace the waters once more. There is a quiet sense of luck in being there for those first encounters. Set within the protected South Ari Marine Protected Area, Nova offers exceptional access, with
Aquaholics
guides leading the way.
Whale sharks
can be seen here year-round, and with May’s bright, sunlit conditions, every moment beneath the surface feels even more vivid.
Soak Up the Island Sunshine
With long, sunlit days, May invites you to spend more time in and around the water. Slip into clear lagoons for easy
snorkelling
, where reef life moves just beneath the surface, or head out on a
dive
to explore deeper, more vibrant sites. Above the water, days can be filled with playful
water sports
or simply unwinding on the shore, letting the warmth of the Maldivian sun linger a little longer.
Evenings to Unwind and Indulge
As the day winds down, retreat to the comfort of your
water villa
, where your private pool becomes the perfect place to pause and take in the changing light. Evenings flow easily into flavourful moments at
Soul Kitchen
or
Mizu
, where fresh, vibrant dishes bring people together. Later, ease into the night with a refreshing drink at
Wink
or
Solis
, letting the rhythm of the island carry you gently into the evening.
A Month to Feel Good, Inside and Out
May brings together sunshine, ocean encounters, and a slower island rhythm, creating the kind of days that stay with you long after you leave. At Nova, it is a time to reconnect, explore, and simply enjoy each moment as it comes.
