Planning your honeymoon in the Maldives begins with finding a place that feels romantic, relaxed and made for meaningful time together. At
Nova Maldives
, couples can enjoy ocean-view villas, tropical island days and soulful experiences in the heart of South Ari Atoll. From private villa mornings to candlelit dinners by the sea, every day gives you space to slow down, reconnect and celebrate the beginning of forever in a way that feels naturally yours.
Romantic Villas Made for Two
Your honeymoon begins with a
stay
that feels close to the ocean. The
Sunset Beach Villa with Pool
is perfect for couples who love the feeling of sand beneath their feet, with a private pool just steps from the beach and beautiful sunset views that set the scene for slow, romantic evenings. For couples drawn to the lagoon, the
Water Villa with Private Pool
offers an overwater escape where you can wake up to endless blues, take peaceful dips in your pool and enjoy the feeling of being surrounded by the sea.
Soulful Dining and Private Moments
Dining
at Nova brings flavour, connection and a sense of ease to your honeymoon. From generous all-day dining at
Soul Kitchen
to flame-grilled favourites at
Flames
, the theatre of teppanyaki at
Mizu
, relaxed beachfront drinks at
Solis
, and easy evenings at
Wink
, every meal offers a new way to enjoy time together. For something more intimate, Nova’s
destination dining
experiences create beautiful private moments by the sea, whether it is a romantic dinner on the beach, a special celebration under the stars or a table set just for two in a dreamy island setting.
Experiences to Remember Together
A honeymoon at Nova is also about the memories you make side by side. Spend your days
snorkelling
together in clear waters, exploring the house reef and discovering the beauty of the Maldives beneath the surface. In South Ari Atoll, couples can also experience the wonder of
swimming with whale sharks
, creating once-in-a-lifetime memories in one of the Maldives’ most remarkable marine settings. For slower moments, unwind with a couples’ massage at
EsKape Spa
, or capture your island love story with a photoshoot at
Memories
, creating beautiful keepsakes from your honeymoon that you can cherish forever.
A Soulful Start to Forever
At Nova, your honeymoon becomes more than a romantic holiday; it becomes a collection of moments that feel personal, peaceful and full of meaning. From oceanfront villas to soulful dining and unforgettable experiences, Nova gives you the space to begin forever in your own beautiful way.
