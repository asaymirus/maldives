On the soulful island of Nova Maldives, nestled in the enchanting South Ari Atoll, lies a world inviting dreamers and adventurers alike. This laidback haven is where your deepest desires come alive. Here, we reveal the top five experiences that will leave an indelible mark on your soul at Nova.
Staying at a Water Villa – A Dream Above the Lagoon
Indulge in our
water villas
, where sophistication floats on the calm sea. Each villa is a sanctuary of relaxation and comfort suspended over the blue waters. Wake up to the touch of the morning sun and fall asleep to the soothing lull of the waves.
Overwater Teppanyaki Dining at Mizu – A Symphony of Tastes
Imagine a dining experience where the ocean’s heartbeat sets the tone. At
Mizu
, overwater teppanyaki dining is a celebration of the senses. Watch the horizon blend into a canvas of vibrant hues as our chefs create a tapestry of flavours, each bite a testament to Nova’s culinary artistry and refinement.
Whale Shark Snorkeling – A Dance with the Ocean’s Giants
Below the glistening ocean surface lies an extraordinary encounter. Glide through the
azure depths
with whale sharks, the gentle giants of the deep. Feel the ocean’s rhythm and let your heart dance with these magnificent creatures.
Sunset Dolphin Cruise – A Serenade by the Sea
As the sun sets below the horizon, embark on a journey where the ocean’s most playful inhabitants, the dolphins, perform a ballet in the golden twilight. Witness their playful leaps and spins in the warm glow of the setting sun, a spectacle that captures the heart and uplifts the spirit.
Eskape Spa – A Retreat for the Soul
Discover a sanctuary where tranquillity touches the soul. At
Eskape Spa
, treatments are intimate dialogues with your inner self, communicated through touch and nature’s healing embrace. Allow the gentle breeze and the rhythmic lullaby of the waves to guide you to a world of inner peace and revitalisation.
