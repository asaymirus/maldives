Let your bond deepen with every shared moment, surrounded by the island’s enchanting beauty. Valentine’s Day at
Nova Maldives
is a celebration of soulful connections, shared adventures, and the boundless beauty of the island paradise. With curated experiences to inspire love and joy, here’s how you can spend the day with your soulmate, grouped into themes of wellness, excursions, culinary delights, and creative moments.
Together in Bliss
Start your day with serenity and connection through a guided
Valentine’s yoga session
by the lagoon. This calming ritual is perfect for harmonising your mind, body, and soul, all while soaking in the peaceful Maldivian surroundings. Then, deepen your bond with a couple’s massage workshop, where expert therapists guide you in creating moments of care and tenderness—leaving you both refreshed and connected.
A Feast for the Heart
Start your day with a romantic
Champagne lagoon breakfast
, surrounded by tranquil waters and mouthwatering treats. As the sun sets, indulge in a bespoke beach dinner, where candlelight and the sound of gentle waves set the stage for an evening of connection.
For a more lively celebration, join other soulmates at Love’s Oasis—a vibrant sandbank party with crafted cocktails, laughter, and endless good vibes.
Love Beyond the Horizon
Leave your mark on Nova’s vibrant underwater world by
planting a coral together
—a lasting symbol of your love and commitment to sustainability. As the evening unfolds, set sail on a dreamy
sunset cruise
and toast to your love under a golden sky. Wrap up the day stargazing at
Nava Pavilion
, where twinkling stars and gentle waves create a magical finale for your Valentine’s celebration.
Create, Connect, Cherish
Unleash your creativity at our painting workshop, where you and your partner can craft a one-of-a-kind keepsake of your time together. Dive into unforgettable moments with an
underwater photoshoot
that captures the magic of your connection beneath the waves. Finish your day with a couple’s dance class, sharing laughter, new moves, and joyful memories that bring you closer than ever.
