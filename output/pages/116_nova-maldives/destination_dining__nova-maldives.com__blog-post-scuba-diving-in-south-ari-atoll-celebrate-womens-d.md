Nova
sits in the heart of South Ari Atoll, one of the best places for scuba diving in the Maldives. Known for year-round whale shark sightings, graceful manta rays, colourful reefs and unforgettable dive sites, this ocean-rich corner of the Maldives is made for good soul days beneath the blue.
For Women’s Dive Day, Nova brings together ocean lovers, local students, inspiring women in diving and the warm spirit of the Nova community for a celebration filled with connection, confidence and shared discovery.
How Does Nova Celebrate Women’s Dive Day?
From 17 to 19 July, Nova celebrates Women’s Dive Day with a soulful ocean gathering designed to inspire connection above and beneath the surface. This year, Nova welcomes Zoona Naseem, the Maldives’ first female PADI-certified Course Director, for a series of fun dives alongside guests and girls from nearby local island schools.
Set among the breathtaking dive sites of South Ari Atoll, the experience brings together ocean discovery, mentorship and community in a way that feels deeply meaningful to the spirit of Nova.
Meet the Women Leading the Conversation
As the sun dips below the horizon, the celebration continues with a special fireside chat featuring Zoona Naseem and Nova’s resident marine biologist, Jenna. Centred around diving, ocean exploration and the underwater world of the Maldives, the conversation promises inspiring stories, personal experiences and meaningful insights from two women deeply connected to life beneath the surface.
Diving in South Ari Atoll
South Ari Atoll is a dream for divers and snorkellers. Around Nova, the ocean opens into vibrant pinnacles, coral channels, nearby shipwrecks and marine-life encounters that linger long after you surface.

This Women’s Dive Day, Nova brings together ocean lovers, inspiring conversations and unforgettable underwater moments in the heart of South Ari Atoll. From shared dives to sunset stories, it is a soulful celebration of connection, community and the women making waves beneath the surface.
