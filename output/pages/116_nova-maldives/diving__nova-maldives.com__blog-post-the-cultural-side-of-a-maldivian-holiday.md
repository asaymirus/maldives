Local Island Excursions and Everyday Maldivian Life
Just a
short boat ride
from Nova, local islands invite you into the Maldives as it’s lived each day, slow, social, and full of small details. With a Soulmate leading the way, you’ll get a simple, friendly intro to island etiquette, so you feel comfortable from the moment you arrive. Wander sandy lanes lined with colourful homes, pop into little cafés, and browse local shops where island life hums along in its own easy rhythm. Look a little closer and you’ll spot the moments that make it real, neighbours catching up in the shade, fishermen unloading the day’s catch, children laughing by the shoreline. You’ll come back to your soul place with more than photos, carrying a warmer connection to the people and stories that shape these islands.
Holhuashi Cultural Gatherings at Nova
Some evenings at Nova are made for slowing down and leaning into the local rhythm, and
Holhuashi
is one of them. Set in a relaxed, social space, it’s a chance to experience Maldivian culture without leaving your soul place. You might meet local artisans, watch traditional handiwork come to life, or see simple crafts like palm weaving up close. As the music starts and the beat of Maldivian drumming fills the air, you can listen, ask questions, and learn the meaning behind the traditions being shared. With Soulmates welcoming you in, Holhuashi feels easy to join, with plenty of space to connect, learn, and share the moment.
Maldivian flavours and food stories
In the Maldives, culture is something you can taste, from coconut and lime to chilli heat and fragrant curries made for sharing. At
Soul Kitchen

Pull up a chair, take your time, and let the conversation flow as the food feeds your soul.
