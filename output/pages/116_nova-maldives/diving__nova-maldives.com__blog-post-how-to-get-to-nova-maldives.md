Getting to
Nova Maldives
is all part of the adventure. From the moment you land at Velana International Airport (MLE) in Malé, your journey continues with a scenic 25-minute seaplane transfer across the turquoise waters of the Indian Ocean, offering your first glimpse of the incredible beauty that awaits in South Ari Atoll.
Here’s everything you need to know before travelling to Nova.
Where is Nova Maldives?
Nova is located in the South Ari Atoll in the SAMPA area, one of the Maldives’ most celebrated marine destinations. The atoll is renowned for its vibrant coral reefs and abundant marine life, including the rare opportunity to encounter
whale sharks
throughout the year. Surrounded by crystal-clear lagoons and white sandy beaches, Nova offers a peaceful island setting where every day invites you to slow down, reconnect, and embrace the spirit of good soul days.
Arriving in the Maldives
Your journey begins at Velana International Airport (MLE), the Maldives’ main international gateway, located just outside the capital city of Malé. After clearing immigration and collecting your luggage, a Nova Soulmate will welcome you and assist with your seaplane transfer, ensuring your onward journey is smooth and stress-free.
A Scenic Seaplane Journey
Reaching Nova is an experience in itself. The 25-minute seaplane flight offers spectacular aerial views of the Maldives, revealing tiny islands, turquoise lagoons, coral reefs, and endless shades of blue that are impossible to appreciate from the ground. Flying low over the atolls, it’s the perfect introduction to island life and often one of the most memorable parts of the journey.
What to Know Before Flying by Seaplane
If it’s your first time travelling by seaplane, there are a few things worth knowing. Seaplane transfers operate during daylight hours, so your international flight schedule may influence your transfer time. Depending on your arrival, you may have a short wait in the seaplane terminal before boarding.  As weather conditions and flight schedules can occasionally affect departure times, a little flexibility is always recommended. The views along the way are more than worth the wait.
