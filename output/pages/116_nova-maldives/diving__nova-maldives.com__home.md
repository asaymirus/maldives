Where is Nova located?
Nova is located in South Ari Atoll, just a 25-minute scenic seaplane ride from Velana International Airport. South Ari Atoll is renowned for its underwater beauty and has some of the best locations for diving and snorkelling in the Maldives. With over 30 manta points, whale shark points, shipwrecks and
dive locations
within a close 40-minute range from the island, Nova is a unique spot for those looking for the ultimate
marine adventure
.
