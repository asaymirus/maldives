At
Nova Maldives
, we believe magic doesn’t end with the sunset, it simply shifts into a new kind of glow. As the sun dips below the horizon, our soulful island awakens with a different rhythm. One where glowing waters, silent stargazing, and night-lit adventures invite you to see the Maldives from a new perspective.
We welcome you to nighttime experiences that reconnect you to the island and to yourself, in fresh, luminous ways.
Watch the Shore Light Up with Bioluminescence
There’s nothing quite like watching the shoreline sparkle with every step you take. From June to November, the shores of Nova come alive with glowing blue waves, thanks to bioluminescent plankton thriving in warm, nutrient-rich waters stirred by tropical weather.
Whether you’re walking hand in hand or sitting barefoot by the shoreline, the experience is pure wonder, reminding us that even the smallest things can shine.
Discover a Hidden World with Fluro-Snorkelling
Adventure doesn’t sleep, and neither does Nova’s house reef. Our
fluro-snorkelling
experiences are a vivid journey into the Maldives’ nocturnal marine life. With special fluorescent lights, you’ll uncover neon flashes of coral, watch glowing plankton pulse with colour, and spot shy night creatures like crabs, shrimps, and nocturnal fish, all shimmering in the quiet of the dark.
This is underwater discovery in its most playful, awe-inspiring form, perfect for curious souls who love to explore life in full colour, even after dusk.
Dive Deeper into the Night
Ready to take it up a notch? Nova’s
night dives
are your deep-blue ticket to after-hours marine magic. Guided by expert dive instructors, you’ll encounter creatures that only emerge when the ocean sleeps. Watch octopuses showcase their dazzling camouflage, and spot lionfish and moray eels as they slip from their daytime hideouts.
It’s a calm yet thrilling experience that offers a new way to see the reef, through the lens of stillness, focus, and deep respect for nature’s night shift.
Stargaze from a Secluded Sandbank
When was the last time you looked up and truly saw the stars? At Nova, we invite you to unwind under a starlit sky on a sandbank, far from the noise of the world. It’s just you, the waves, and the celestial wonders overhead.
For an extra touch of magic, indulge in a
romantic candlelit dinner on the sandbank
, complete with your choice of curated menus and the soft symphony of the sea. Whether you’re wrapped in conversation, lost in thought, or simply listening to the quiet, those beautifully still moments stay with you.
The Best of Both Worlds
The summer months invite you to experience the island at its most peaceful, while also unlocking a world of soul-stirring noctourism adventures. From glowing seas to starlit skies, the Maldives truly shines after dark—and Nova is your gateway to discovering it all, your way.
Because at Nova, every moment matters. Especially the ones under the moon.
