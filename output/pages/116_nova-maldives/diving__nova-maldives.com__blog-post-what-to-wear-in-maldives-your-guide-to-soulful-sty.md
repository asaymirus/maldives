Embarking on a journey to the enchanting Nova Maldives? Dive into our sun-kissed natural island, where fresh outfits meet good soul days. Here are some style essentials and tips to ensure you effortlessly blend into the laidback magic of Nova.
Breezy Island Chic
Nova welcomes you to embrace the island living vibe with lightweight and breathable clothing. Think flowy sundresses, loose linen shirts, and comfortable shorts that effortlessly transition from beach strolls to sunset cocktails.
Sun Smart Accessories
As the Maldivian sun graces Nova’s shores, shield yourself with sun protection. A wide-brimmed hat not only adds a touch of glam but also keeps you cool. Don’t forget your favourite sunglasses to complement your beach-ready look.
Swimwear with Soul
Nova’s pristine beaches are calling for your chicest swimwear. Whether it’s a bikini or a trendy one-piece, make a splash with your beach attire. Remember, the Maldives is all about embracing the sea, so pack a couple of swimsuits to ensure you’re always ready for a refreshing dip.
Modest Attire for Local Visits
If you plan on exploring the local islands, it’s respectful to cover up a bit more. Throw in a cover-up or a lightweight scarf for these excursions. Modest yet fashionable attire will ensure you seamlessly connect with the local culture.
Footwear for Every Occasion
From sandy shores to charming island paths, versatile footwear is key. Comfortable sandals or flip-flops are perfect for beach days, while a pair of flats or loafers can accompany you on island explorations.
At Nova, we encourage you to express your unique style while embracing the easygoing spirit of the islands. Whether you’re lounging by the infinity pool, exploring the vibrant coral reefs, or savouring a delicious meal, let your wardrobe be an extension of the Maldivian magic.
