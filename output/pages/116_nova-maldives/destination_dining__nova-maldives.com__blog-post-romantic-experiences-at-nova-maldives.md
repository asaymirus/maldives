At
Nova
, love is more than just a feeling—it’s an adventure, a journey, and a celebration of connection. Here, your love story unfolds in inspiring surroundings, where every occasion is thoughtfully crafted to spark joy and bring you closer. With countless ways to celebrate your bond, Nova is where romance blossoms and where good soul days turn into cherished memories. We offer the perfect setting for lovers to escape, unwind, and fall in love all over again.
Couple’s Bliss
Embark on a journey of wellness at
Eskape Spa
, where holistic treatments are designed to rejuvenate the body, mind, and soul. Choose from a rejuvenating or relaxing massage, complemented by a luxurious warm body mask and invigorating foot reflexology. Conclude your experience with a soothing flower bath session, sipping on refreshing spa elixirs as you soak in the blissful surroundings. Set against the dreamlike backdrop of Nova’s azure lagoon,
Eskape Spa
offers a sanctuary for couples seeking deep relaxation and moments of pure connection.
Sail Into the Horizon
Set sail into a world of wonder with Nova’s unforgettable
romantic cruises
. Experience the magic of a sunset cruise across the Indian Ocean, where the sky transforms into a canvas of vibrant hues as the sun dips below the horizon. Toast to your love with a glass of champagne, feeling the quiet intimacy as the rhythmic sway of the waves brings you closer. For a touch of adventure, embark on a thrilling
dolphin cruise
, watching these playful creatures dance alongside your boat or dive into shared passions by
snorkelling
with
whale sharks
. For a more lasting memory, plant a coral frame together as a symbol of your love, contributing to the ocean’s vibrant life. Let every moment be filled with endless possibilities for connection and joy.
Dinner Under the Stars
Indulge in a
romantic dinner
at Nava Pavilion, where the shimmering lagoon becomes the perfect setting for an unforgettable evening. The secluded wooden platform offers an intimate escape, inviting you to savour flavours as captivating as the surroundings. Delight in a menu crafted to your desires, featuring Maldivian lobster, succulent Angus beef fillet or a vegetarian/vegan option, and freshly prepared maki rolls. As you dine beneath the starlit sky, let the enchanting atmosphere and exquisite cuisine wrap you in a world of pure, soulful connection.

exchanging vows on powder-soft sands

Whether it’s an intimate ceremony for two or a celebration surrounded by family and friends, the promise of tropical bliss, where your souls will sparkle forevermore.
