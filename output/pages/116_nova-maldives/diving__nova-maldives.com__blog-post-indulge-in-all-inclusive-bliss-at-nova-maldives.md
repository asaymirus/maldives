Dive into the warmth of Nova – where serene waves, pristine white sands, and endless good vibes welcome you to an all-inclusive escape like no other. Nestled in the heart of South Ari Atoll, Nova invites you to a laidback haven where every detail is crafted to stir the soul.
At Nova, our thoughtfully designed all-inclusive package lets you soak up the sun and savour the beauty of the Maldives without a single worry. From the moment you arrive, you’re welcomed into a world of pure relaxation and heartfelt joy, where everything you need is at your fingertips.
Culinary Bliss
Begin each day with a smile at Soul Kitchen, where a delicious breakfast greets you as the morning sun dances on the ocean. Lunchtime is a relaxed affair with a variety of international flavours, and dinner transforms into a soulful feast under a starlit sky. For an elevated dining experience, treat yourself to a three-course dinner at Flames, our grill house, where our skilled chefs transform succulent cuts of premium meat, seafood and vegetables into fire-kissed masterpieces – grilled to absolute perfection.
