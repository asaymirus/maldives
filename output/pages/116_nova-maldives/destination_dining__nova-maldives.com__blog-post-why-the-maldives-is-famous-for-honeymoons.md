The Maldives, renowned for its breathtaking archipelago, is a lovers’ paradise, offering an idyllic setting that seems almost surreal. Among these jewels of nature is Nova Maldives, a laidback natural island that epitomises romantic bliss and stands out as one of the finest destinations for couples.
Ignite Your Love Story at Nova
At Nova, we believe in crafting experiences that kindle the flames of love. From gourmet adventures to enchanting sunset cruises, every moment is an opportunity to create soulful memories with your loved one.
Romantic Retreats Crafted for Couples
Our laidback island tells a story of romance in every corner. The
intimate beach villas
offer direct access to soft, white sands, while our
water villas
present breathtaking panoramic views of the ocean. Each abode at Nova is designed with the desires of couples in mind, ensuring a private and romantic experience.
Explore an Underwater Wonderland
Nova isn’t just located in the heart of the Maldives; it’s at the heart of an
underwater spectacle
. The South Ari Atoll is known for its rich marine life, where couples have a chance to explore the ocean’s wonders together. Swim alongside
graceful whale sharks and serene manta rays
– a surreal addition to your love story.
Gastronomic Journeys for Two
A honeymoon is incomplete without indulging in
culinary delights
. At Nova, you can experience secluded beach dinners under the stars, on a luxury cruise, or even at the Nava Pavilion. Each meal is not just about the food; it’s about the experience, the setting, and the company.
Bonding Adventures at Nova
Whether it’s a
private cruise
to admire the stunning Maldivian sunset or a couple’s treatment at our
Eskape Spa
, at Nova you will find a range of activities designed to strengthen your bond. Experience relaxation and adventure in equal measure, tailored to deepen the connection between you and your loved one.
