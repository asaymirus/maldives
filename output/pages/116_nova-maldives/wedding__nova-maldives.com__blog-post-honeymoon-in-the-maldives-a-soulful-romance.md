The Maldives, with its stunning archipelago, is a haven for lovers, offering an almost otherworldly romantic setting. Among these natural gems is Nova Maldives, a laidback island that embodies romantic bliss.
Ignite the Spark of Love
At Nova, we specialise in creating
soulful experiences
that spark the flames of love. From gourmet dining adventures to magical sunset cruises, every moment is an opportunity to make memories with your special someone.
Retreats for Romance
Nova’s relaxed island vibe narrates a tale of romance at every turn. Intimate beach villas provide direct access to pristine, white sands, while our water villas offer stunning panoramic ocean views.
Every villa
at Nova is crafted with couples in mind, ensuring a private and romantic stay.
Dive Into an Ocean of Wonder
Situated in the heart of the Maldives, Nova offers access to an
underwater wonderland
. The South Ari Atoll is famous for its abundant marine life, allowing couples to explore the ocean’s beauty together. Swim alongside majestic whale sharks and graceful manta rays – an unforgettable chapter in your love story.
Dining Delights
No honeymoon is complete without indulging in
culinary delights
. At Nova, you can enjoy private beach dinners under the stars, a luxury cruise, or dining at the Nava Pavilion. Each meal is more than just food; it’s about the experience, the setting, and sharing it with the one you love.
Adventures to Bond Over
From private sunset cruises to couples’ treatments at our
Eskape Spa
