In the heart of the Indian Ocean, where the sun-kissed waves and azure expanses cradle the soul, Nova Maldives invites you to truly immerse in the spirit of the islands. Each
excursion
we offer is a journey into the essence of the Maldives, creating memories that resonate deeply.
Dolphin Cruise: Conversations with Nature
Sail into the embrace of the ocean, and let the rhythmic dance of dolphins serenade your spirit. Their playful symphony, echoing the heartbeat of the sea, offers a connection that transcends the everyday and touches the profound.
Whale Shark Excursion: Gentle Giants’ Embrace
The Maldives opens its secrets as you journey alongside the majestic
whale sharks
. These gentle behemoths, kings of the deep, offer an experience of awe and soulful connection to the rhythm of the ocean, grounding us in the beauty and depth of life itself.
Snorkelling with Turtles: Grace and Tranquillity
Descend into the clear embrace of the waters and let the ancient grace of turtles guide you. Floating alongside them, one experiences a quietude and a bond with nature that reminds us of the wonders that surround us.
Island Hopping: The Heartbeat of the Maldives
Experience the Maldives beyond our laidback island. Visit nearby local islands like Dhigurah or Dhangethi, and feel the pulsating heartbeat of Maldivian life. Each island tells its own tale, woven with culture, tradition, and the warmth of its people.
Sunset Fishing: Moments of Reflection
As day transitions to night, indulge in the tradition of sunset fishing. With the warm hues enveloping the sky, it’s a moment of introspection and connection. Each cast into the golden reflections is not just a search for a catch, but also a communion with one’s inner self.
