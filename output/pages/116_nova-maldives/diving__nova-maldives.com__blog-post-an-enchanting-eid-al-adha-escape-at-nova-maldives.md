This Eid Al-Adha, escape to the enchanting shores of Nova for an unforgettable retreat filled with good soul days. Our
specially curated programme
offers you and your loved ones a unique opportunity to immerse in tranquillity, celebrate joyous moments, and create memories that last a lifetime.
Holhuashi: A Cultural Celebration
Central to our Eid Al-Adha festivities is the
Holhuashi
event, a beautiful fusion of tradition and innovation. Holhuashi, meaning “communal resting place,” has been reimagined on Wink Beach to create a dynamic space for cultural exchange. Here, master storytellers will share traditional Maldivian stories, connecting guests with the rich historical roots of the Maldives.
Discover Maldivian Art and Craftsmanship
Eid Al-Adha at Nova also offers a unique opportunity to witness and participate in traditional Maldivian arts and crafts. Renowned Maldivian artists and craftspeople will demonstrate their skills, showcasing the blend of heritage and modern innovation. This hands-on experience is designed to foster a deeper connection with
Maldivian culture
, allowing you to take home more than just memories.
Immersive Cultural Activities
Throughout the Eid Al-Adha period, we have planned a series of activities that will immerse you in the Maldivian way of life. Join us for language lessons where you can learn to speak Dhivehi, the beautiful language of the Maldives. Dive into culinary experiences featuring local cuisine, where you can taste the flavours of the islands and even learn to cook traditional Maldivian dishes.

Whether you’re lounging on the beach, exploring the underwater wonders, or participating in our cultural activities, every moment at Nova is crafted to spark joy.
