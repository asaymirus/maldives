Float into Stillness with the Maldivian Ocean Dream Massage
This one’s for the dreamers. The drifters. The ones who need to let go of what they’re holding onto. Using a mix of deep pressure and gentle stretching, the Maldivian Ocean Dream Massage eases away built-up tension in all the right places. Think of it as a full-body sigh—where aches dissolve and calm takes over. You’ll walk away feeling as if you’ve been carried by the tide and kissed by the sun.
Glow from the Inside with the Seaweed Laminaria Remineralising & Detoxifying Wrap
Salt in the air, minerals on your skin. This ocean-inspired wrap is more than a treatment—it’s a transformation. Your journey begins with dry brushing, awakening your skin and jumpstarting your circulation. Then, we cocoon you in a potent blend of Laminaria seaweed and Spirulina to detox, tone, and deeply nourish. The Seaweed Laminaria Remineralising & Detoxifying Wrap is your go-to for that post-ocean-dip glow, minus the sand.
Feel Good All Over with Paradise-Me
Looking for a full-body reset? Paradise-Me is your new ritual. It starts with an aromatic coffee scrub to gently exfoliate and energise, followed by a massage so soothing it feels like your muscles are exhaling. The finale? A silky coconut milk soak that wraps your body—and your thoughts—in stillness. It’s a sensory journey from start to finish, designed to leave you floating, not just relaxed but completely restored.
Nourish Your Skin with the Sea Coconut Secret
When your skin needs a little extra love, let the island’s most iconic ingredient work its magic. The Sea Coconut Secret begins with a soothing foot ritual before moving into a gentle toasted coconut scrub that softens, hydrates, and revives. The scent? Pure tropical bliss. The results? Silky-smooth skin and a soul that feels deeply cared for. It’s a ritual rooted in nature, designed with heart.
Clear Your Mind with the Eskape Detox
Sometimes, your body and mind just need a proper refresh. That’s where the Eskape Detox comes in. This two-hour journey blends targeted techniques to boost circulation and draw out toxins, followed by a powerful seaweed wrap that gets deep into detox mode. It all ends with a flowing massage using seaweed oil and sweet orange essence—a final touch that uplifts, purifies, and rebalances. You’ll feel lighter, clearer, and more grounded in your own rhythm.
