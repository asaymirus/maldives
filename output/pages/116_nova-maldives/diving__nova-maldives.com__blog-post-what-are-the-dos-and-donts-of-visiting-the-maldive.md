Planning a trip to the Maldives? With its turquoise lagoons, white-sand beaches, and laid-back island lifestyle, this Indian Ocean paradise is on many travellers’ bucket lists. But before you set off, it’s helpful to know a few key dos and don’ts to ensure a smooth, respectful, and unforgettable stay.
Allow us to guide you through what to know before you go, especially if you’re heading to a soulful, feel-good island like
Nova Maldives
, where connection and community come naturally.
Into the Blue With Care
One of the most memorable things to do at Nova is to
dive
into the
world beneath the waves
.
Join a
snorkelling
trip or set out on an
excursion
to nearby
dive sites,
each offering a glimpse into a vibrant, living world. Swim slowly, observe with care, and allow
marine life
the space to move freely. Help protect its beauty and preserve it for generations to come by maintaining a safe distance. The ocean always responds best to those who explore it with gentle curiosity.
A Gentle Way to Connect With Culture
Take time to connect with local culture. At Nova,
Holhuashi
offers a window into this rich heritage. Nestled beneath the gentle shade of our great banyan tree, this Friday gathering reflects a timeless Maldivian tradition, where stories are shared, food is savoured, and the rhythm of island life flows freely. Sit with intention, listen deeply, and experience the culture not just as an observer, but as part of the community.
Taste Your Way Through the Island
Dining
at Nova is all about sharing flavour-filled moments in beautiful settings. Don’t miss out on exploring all our culinary offerings, from wholesome dishes at
Soul Kitchen
to interactive teppanyaki at
Mizu
, where every meal feels fresh and alive. For something more relaxed,
Wink
offers light bites and island-crafted drinks. Try a little of everything, enjoy the pace and let each meal become part of your good soul day.
Wellness That Moves With You
If there’s one thing you should absolutely do in the Maldives, it’s to give yourself the gift of well-being. At
Eskape Spa
, treatments and rituals help you feel grounded and renewed. For a more immersive path,
wellness scuba diving
in the South Ari Marine Park (SAMPA) brings together mindful movement and breath beneath the surface. From underwater yoga to floating meditation, these soul-soothing experiences gently remind you that feeling good is part of the journey too.
The Art of Visiting with Heart
The best way to experience the Maldives is to stay present, be kind and enjoy each moment as it comes. At Nova, it’s the small things that turn into good soul memories. Share a meal, explore gently or simply take time to breathe. Let your stay be easy, mindful and full of moments that feel true to you.
