Maldivian culture is a beautiful tapestry woven through generations, carrying stories, traditions, and a deep connection to the sea. Today, celebrating it feels even more special—a chance to honour the past while creating new memories.
At
Nova Maldives

Holhuashi

The Essence of Holhuashi
As the sun dips below the horizon, the air hums with gentle laughter, and the scent of the ocean mingles with the earthy aroma of aged wood. This is
Holhuashi
, an age-old Maldivian tradition where islanders come together under the shade of a banyan tree to share stories, exchange ideas, and simply be present in each other’s company. It’s a space of warmth, of connection, of unwinding after the day’s work. And now, it’s your turn to be part of it.
The Language of the Maldives
Language is more than just words; it’s the rhythm of a people, the melody of a culture. At Holhuashi, we’ll introduce you to the beauty of Dhivehi, the Maldivian language, spoken with the same fluidity as the ocean waves.
Learn a few phrases, immerse yourself in the lyrical nature of the language, and discover how every syllable carries centuries of island life. Whether you master a greeting or simply let the sounds wash over you, you’ll walk away with a deeper connection to this paradise.
A Taste of Tradition
No Maldivian gathering is complete without a
feast
, and at Nova, we take this to heart. At Holhuashi, you’ll savour the rich and diverse flavours of
Maldivian cuisine
—fragrant coconut curries, freshly caught reef fish, and handmade roshi, all prepared with the same love and care that islanders have poured into their meals for generations. Every bite tells a story of seafarers, spice routes, and the intimate relationship between the people and the ocean.
Stories That Echo Through Time
Under the twinkling night sky, let yourself be transported by the tales of old. Maldivian folklore is woven with myths of the sea, spirits of the islands, and legends passed down through generations. It’s storytelling as it was meant to be—raw, enchanting, and alive.
