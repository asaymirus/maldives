Located on South Ari Atoll, a 25 minute seaplane ride from the airport, Vakarufalhi is 4 stars, and offers the perfect small island getaway holiday including an All Inclusive Plan option. Relax on the magnificent beach, snorkel on the excellent house reef in the crystal clear lagoon, scuba Dive with ProDivers and much more! Do nothing at all or join the water sports, activities, excursions and entertainment programs.
