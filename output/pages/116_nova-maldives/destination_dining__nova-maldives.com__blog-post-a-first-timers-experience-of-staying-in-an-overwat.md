Waking up to the rhythm of the waves, with sunlight glistening over the endless ocean views and the sky stretching limitlessly ahead, this is what it feels like to stay in an overwater villa at
Nova Maldives.
Surrounded by sea, serenity, and space to breathe, it’s a moment that invites you to pause, soften, and settle into the now.
At Nova, every overwater villa is designed to help you reconnect with nature, with yourself, and with the joy of simply being. Unrushed, open, and filled with feel-good comforts, it’s your own little soul space above the sea.
Water Villas
Peaceful, grounding, and effortlessly yours. Our
Water Villas
are made for slow gentle mornings and salt-kissed moments. Perched right above the turquoise lagoon, they offer direct access to the water from your private deck. It’s perfect for an early-morning dip, an afternoon laze in the sun, or a quiet coffee with the rhythm of the waves below. It’s the kind of place where you can just be, with nothing on your to-do list but breathe and bask.
Water Villas with Jacuzzi
The dreamy
Water Villas with Jacuzzi
are all the charm of a water villa topped with a blissful, bubbly twist. These villas feature a private jacuzzi overlooking the endless blue, inviting you to soak under the stars or sink into stillness as the sun gently slips away. It’s your private soul space to pause, breathe, and let the outside world fade. Whether you’re indulging in solo serenity or sharing precious moments with someone special, this villa offers comfort with a splash of sparkle.
Water Villa with Private Pool
There’s something uniquely magical about staying in a
Water Villa with a Private Pool
, where both the ocean and your own pool stretch out before you. Whether you’re welcoming the sunrise with a swim, lounging under the afternoon sun, or enjoying a quiet evening soak as the stars begin to shimmer, this villa wraps you in privacy and ease. Step from bed to deck to sea, with nothing but soft skies and stillness all around. If your soul’s been craving calm, this is where you’ll find it.
Above the Sea, Beyond the Ordinary
Above the sea, something beautiful happens, the noise of everyday life fades, and you start to tune into the rhythm of the waves below. To the stillness within. Here, it’s not just about where you sleep, but how you feel. Present. Connected. Free.
