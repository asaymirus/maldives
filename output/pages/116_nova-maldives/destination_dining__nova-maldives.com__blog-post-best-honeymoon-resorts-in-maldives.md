Dive Into Love
Nova’s location in the South Ari Atoll places you at the heart of a vibrant underwater wonderland. Together, you can dive into crystal-clear waters or snorkel side by side, sharing magical moments as you swim alongside graceful
whale sharks and majestic manta rays
