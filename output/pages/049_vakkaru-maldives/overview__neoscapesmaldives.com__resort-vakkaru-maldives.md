DESCRIPTION
Lying in the heart of Baa Atoll, a UNESCO Biosphere Reserve, Vakkaru Maldives is a secluded reef island blessed with timeless ocean views, powder-soft, brilliant white sands, deep blue holes and a house reef with unique marine biodiversity.
A scenic 30-minute seaplane flight from Male International Airport transport you into this idyllic paradise intuitively designed for guests seeking timeless experiences.
Home to over 2,300 fully grown coconut trees, our tropical island also features 113 luxury villas and suites, five restaurants, two bars, a wine cellar and an over-the-water Spa. Engaging experiences are offered by Splash water sports and dive centre, Sports Fields, Parrotfish Club, 24/7 Coconut Club and gym.
Embark on a timeless journey as soon as you step onto our island. Leave the time behind, disconnect from reality and reconnect to each other while discovering what our timeless sanctuary has to offer.

To complement your memorable family vacation, let your little ones enjoy our extensive kids’ club facilities with tailored activities and certified team members for children aged between 3 and 12 years.

Splash
Famed for its verdant tropical splendor and crystal-clear waters, the Maldives brings you close to tranquil underwater nature and ultra-immersive experiences.
Away from the resort and amongst the many atolls in the Maldives, Baa Atoll is renowned as a place of outstanding pure beauty, reflected in its status as a UNESCO Biosphere Reserve because of its unique biodiversity. Guests can explore the underwater marvels on the magnificent house reef or venture further to see the whale sharks and manta rays at Hanifaru Bay.
Diving Center
For an invigorating day spent out at sea, enjoy scuba diving with accredited PADI dive instructors from beginner to advanced level.
Water sports and excursions
Exhilarating aqua escapades abound in the Maldives. From adventurous boat trips to catch magnificent fish which the chef will prepare for your evening meal. Try parasailing with a breathtaking view of the stunning islands below.When you are seeking more serene aqua activities you may choose to take a kayak and paddle around the protected lagoon or relax in the sun on the ocean with a private dhoni journey at sunset.Alternatively, choose from a host of water sports, including snorkeling on the house reef and the unique “blue hole” within the lagoon, sunset fishing, dolphin cruise, and expeditions with a marine biologist.
