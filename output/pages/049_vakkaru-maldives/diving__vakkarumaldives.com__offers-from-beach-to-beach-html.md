From Beach to Beach
Overview
A curated seven-night journey from the mountain-framed serenity of Naäma in Fujairah to the reef-fringed sanctuary of Vakkaru Maldives.
Begin with mindful rituals and archery beneath the Fujairah sky, followed by a private beach barbecue prepared by your own chef. Then ease into island time in the Maldives with spa moments, guided reef exploration and breakfast served just above the sea.
