Spa & Wellness
At Vakkaru Maldives, wellbeing is woven into the rhythm of island life. Days move between ocean swims, yoga and quiet moments of stillness, leading to Merana Spa where overwater pavilions host therapies inspired by global healing traditions, alongside Ayurveda and a tranquil wellness sanctuary.
Salon
Refined rituals for effortless grooming
Complete your wellness experience with a collection of beauty and grooming rituals designed to leave you feeling polished and refreshed. From nourishing facials and meticulous nail care to professional hair styling and finishing touches, each treatment combines expert technique with premium products to ensure you look and feel your best throughout your island stay.
Treatments
Hair Studio
Professional hair services designed to refresh your look throughout your stay. From styling and blow-dries to cuts and grooming, expert stylists ensure effortless island elegance for every occasion.
Nail Care
Meticulous manicure and pedicure rituals using premium OPI products. Treatments include nail shaping, cuticle care and soothing hand or foot massage, leaving hands and feet beautifully polished.
Beauty Rituals
A selection of grooming and finishing treatments designed to complement your spa experience, including skincare rituals and beauty touch-ups for a polished, radiant look.
Facilities
Hydrotherapy & Wellness Area
A tranquil wellness space featuring sauna, steam room, warm Jacuzzi and a refreshing cold plunge pool. Between treatments, unwind on the ocean-facing sundeck and enjoy moments of quiet restoration.
Yoga Pavilion
A serene setting for guided yoga and mindful movement. Whether new to the practice or deepening your flow, sessions support flexibility, balance and inner calm.
Fitness Centre
Located beside Merana Spa, the air-conditioned gym features panoramic ocean views, Technogym equipment, free weights and personal training sessions.
Relaxation Deck
An ocean-facing deck designed for quiet moments between treatments. Overlooking the lagoon, it offers a peaceful place to unwind, breathe deeply and reconnect with the calm rhythm of the sea.
