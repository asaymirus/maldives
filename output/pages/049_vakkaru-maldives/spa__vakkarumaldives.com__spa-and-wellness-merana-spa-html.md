Spa & Wellness
At Vakkaru Maldives, wellbeing is woven into the rhythm of island life. Days move between ocean swims, yoga and quiet moments of stillness, leading to Merana Spa where overwater pavilions host therapies inspired by global healing traditions, alongside Ayurveda and a tranquil wellness sanctuary.
Merana Spa
A tranquil sanctuary for restoration and renewal
Inspired by time-honoured healing rituals and the restorative energy of the ocean, Merana Spa offers a tranquil sanctuary for relaxation and renewal at one of the finest
Maldives beach resorts
. Set above the water in serene treatment pavilions, therapies blend natural ingredients with advanced skincare and therapeutic techniques to restore balance and wellbeing.
A curated menu of massages, facials, beauty rituals and restorative body treatments is complemented by yoga and mindful wellness experiences, allowing you to slow down, reconnect and rediscover a natural sense of calm.
Treatments
Maldives Indulgent Experiences
Our Merana Spa Maldivian Signature selections utilize ingredients, plants and herbs that are organic and indigenous to the region, using local traditions with a wellness focus. Explore a variety of indulgent experiences that have been created for your relaxation and wellbeing, which are deeply rooted in spa tradition, culturally connected and exclusive to Vakkaru.
Merana Royal Rituals Inspired by Natura Bisse
An indulgent full-body experience inspired by Natura Bissé's haute-skincare philosophy. This refined ritual blends delicate exfoliation, infused aromatic essences, and expertly choreographed massage techniques to deeply nourish the skin and relax the senses. The result is silky, radiant skin, and an elevated state of wellbeing.
Couples’ Journeys
Escape to a place where romance reigns, and every experience inspires tempting indulgence you won't be able to resist sharing. Our all-day luxury signature treatment journeys are designed to reignite your love and passion, inspire a sublime sense of wellbeing and decadently pamper you from head to toe.
Merana Signature Packages

Merana Massages
At Merana Spa, a massage is a holistic experience which discovers the power of touch through a personalized experience to harmonies and balance your body.
Facilities & Services
Hydrotherapy & Wellness Area
A tranquil wellness space featuring sauna, steam room, warm Jacuzzi and a refreshing cold plunge pool. Between treatments, unwind on the ocean-facing sundeck and enjoy moments of quiet restoration.
Yoga Pavilion
A serene setting for guided yoga and mindful movement. Whether new to the practice or deepening your flow, sessions support flexibility, balance and inner calm.
Fitness Centre
Located beside Merana Spa, the air-conditioned gym features panoramic ocean views, Technogym equipment, free weights and personal training sessions.
Relaxation Deck
An ocean-facing deck designed for quiet moments between treatments. Overlooking the lagoon, it offers a peaceful place to unwind, breathe deeply and reconnect with the calm rhythm of the sea.
In-Villa Indulgence
Experience the calm of Merana Spa without leaving your villa. Personalised treatments and wellness rituals unfold in complete privacy, allowing you to relax, restore and reconnect with the quiet rhythm of the ocean.
