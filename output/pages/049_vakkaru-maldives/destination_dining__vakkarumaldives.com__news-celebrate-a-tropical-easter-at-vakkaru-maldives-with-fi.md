Celebrate a tropical Easter at Vakkaru Maldives with ‘Five Days in Wonderland’ programme
27 Feb 2023
Vakkaru Maldives invites guests to embrace the tropical magic of the Maldives with “Five Days in Wonderland” Easter programme from 6-10 April 2023. Inspired by the renowned classic tales and in conjunction with the resort’s fifth anniversary, this five-day action-packed extravaganza promises to be a one-of-a-kind experience for guests of all ages as the island is transformed to a world of wonderland.
From themed gastronomic feasts to unique wellness and fitness rituals to egg hunts and creative crafts workshops for little ones, this timeless sanctuary is the place to be this Easter.
Guests can look forward to exclusive wine tastings, live taco and shawarma stations by the main pool and a Lobster & Champagne Dinner. Wellness seekers can use the opportunity to rejuvenate body, mind and soul with a range of new therapies from renowned wellness and fitness expert Domingos Folgado and resident yogi Ann Tsoy, plus workshops by Siddhalepa Ayurveda.
The young ones will be kept busy with creative art workshops, family Olympics, and more. Other sporty activities include tennis and ping pong tournaments, football matches and the family kayak race. For those looking for exciting ocean adventures, there are dolphin cruises, sunset fishing, turtle snorkelling, and thrilling watersports.
The main highlight of the celebrations is the Easter Gala Dinner on Saturday, 8 April, at Isoletta Beach. The sumptuous international buffet with live stations will take guests on an epic culinary journey while Aura band and DJ Mira provide a soundtrack for them to dance under the stars. On Easter Sunday, 9 April, the Easter Bunny will make his appearance, followed by an exciting Easter Egg Hunt at Isoletta Beach. Kids and their families can join this fun-filled event to meet and greet the Easter Bunny and hunt for eggs around the beach.
“We are delighted that the Five Days in Wonderland Easter celebrations at Vakkaru Maldives will be an exceptional and unforgettable experience for all our guests,” said Iain McCormack, General Manager of Vakkaru Maldives. “We extend a warm invitation to families and couples to join us for this five-day extravaganza and look forward to celebrating Easter with our guests in the stunning surroundings of our idyllic island resort.”
