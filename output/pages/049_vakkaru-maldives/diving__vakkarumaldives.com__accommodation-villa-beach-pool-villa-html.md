Beach Pool Villa
Ideal for couples or small families, this villa features a private garden, an upgraded pool and easy access to the beach. Indoors, the airy lounge converts into a sleeping space for children, while outside you can unwind in shaded comfort or enjoy the sun surrounded by tropical jungle.
Overview
Bordered by lush greenery and just steps from the shoreline, the Beach Pool Villa offers a relaxed setting for couples or smaller families. Inside, calming interiors combine natural textures with subtle island influences, creating a light and welcoming atmosphere.
A separate indoor lounge provides additional space to gather during the day and can easily transform into a sleeping area for children. Outdoors, a shaded terrace opens onto a private garden where the pool becomes the centre of villa life.
Days unfold between time on the sand, refreshing swims and quiet moments beneath the palms. With the beach just beyond the garden and generous outdoor living areas throughout, the villa offers an easy balance of comfort, space and natural beauty.

Family Time by the Beach
Life settles into a gentle rhythm from the moment you arrive. Step from the garden onto the sand as the lagoon shifts through soft shades of blue and the day begins slowly.
Mornings might start with coffee on the terrace while the island wakes around you. Children drift between the pool and the beach, while shaded corners of the garden offer quiet places to pause.
As the sun rises higher, the villa becomes a cool retreat between moments outdoors. Afternoons move easily between time on the sand, swims in the pool and relaxed hours spent together.
When evening arrives, the garden grows still and the sky softens above the sea. With the sound of the waves nearby and the pool reflecting the fading light, the day comes to a calm and easy close.
