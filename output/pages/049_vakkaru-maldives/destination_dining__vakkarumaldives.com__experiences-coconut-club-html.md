Coconut Club
Coconut Club is a vibrant social space for teens and adults, offering a relaxed setting to unwind, connect and enjoy a variety of games and activities. From friendly tournaments to quiet moments with a book, it is the perfect place to spend time together or simply relax at your own pace.

Games, Relaxation and Social Moments
Opening Hours:
Open 24/7, with staff available from 8.00am to 10.00pm.
Coconut Club is a welcoming space designed for teens, adults and families to come together for entertainment and relaxation. Open around the clock, the club offers a lively yet laid-back atmosphere where guests can enjoy a range of indoor games including billiards, table tennis, foosball, darts and a variety of board games.
For quieter moments, the library lounge provides a comfortable setting to browse an extensive collection of books covering topics from marine life and history to art and travel. Coffee is also available, making it an inviting place to unwind while reading or catching up with friends.
Guests can also join the daily activity programme hosted by our recreation team, featuring friendly competitions, creative sessions and engaging games designed to bring people together.
