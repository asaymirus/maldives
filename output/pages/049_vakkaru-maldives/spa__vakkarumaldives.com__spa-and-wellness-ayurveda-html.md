Spa & Wellness

Ayurveda
Ancient healing traditions for modern wellbeing
Rooted in one of the world’s oldest holistic healing traditions, Ayurveda focuses on restoring balance between body, mind and spirit. At Vakkaru Maldives, these time-honoured therapies are offered in partnership with the renowned Sri Lankan Ayurveda brand Siddhalepa, whose heritage spans generations of traditional practice.
Each Ayurvedic journey begins with a personal consultation with our resident Ayurvedic doctor, who will assess your individual constitution, or dosha, and recommend therapies tailored to your needs. Treatments are designed to detoxify, restore energy and support long-term wellbeing through personalised care, natural ingredients and time-tested techniques.
