Spa & Wellness
At Vakkaru Maldives, wellbeing is woven into the rhythm of island life. Days move between ocean swims, yoga and quiet moments of stillness, leading to Merana Spa where overwater pavilions host therapies inspired by global healing traditions, alongside Ayurveda and a tranquil wellness sanctuary.
Wellness
A holistic approach to mind, body and energy
Wellness at Vakkaru Maldives embraces movement, mindfulness and holistic healing in harmony with the island’s natural rhythm. From yoga, meditation and energy work to personalised fitness and restorative sound therapies, each experience is designed to nurture balance and vitality.
Throughout the year, visiting wellness specialists also bring new perspectives and practices to the island, offering guests the opportunity to explore different approaches to wellbeing through private sessions and guided experiences.
Treatments
Holistic Wellness
Reconnect mind and energy through practices such as Reiki, meditation and breathwork. These gentle yet powerful therapies encourage relaxation, restore balance and create a deeper sense of calm and clarity.
Fitness & Bodywork
Stay energised with personalised fitness and movement sessions including Pilates, functional training, HIIT and strength conditioning, designed to enhance flexibility, endurance and overall wellbeing.
Inner Wellbeing
Explore deeper states of relaxation through sound healing, yoga and guided meditation. These restorative experiences help calm the mind, release tension and reconnect with a profound sense of inner balance.
Facilities
Hydrotherapy & Wellness Area
A tranquil wellness space featuring sauna, steam room, warm Jacuzzi and a refreshing cold plunge pool. Between treatments, unwind on the ocean-facing sundeck and enjoy moments of quiet restoration.
Yoga Pavilion
A serene setting for guided yoga and mindful movement. Whether new to the practice or deepening your flow, sessions support flexibility, balance and inner calm.
Fitness Centre
Located beside Merana Spa, the air-conditioned gym features panoramic ocean views, Technogym equipment, free weights and personal training sessions.
Relaxation Deck
An ocean-facing deck designed for quiet moments between treatments. Overlooking the lagoon, it offers a peaceful place to unwind, breathe deeply and reconnect with the calm rhythm of the sea.
Global Expertise for Deeper Wellbeing
Throughout the year, visiting wellness specialists bring their expertise to Merana Spa. Through private sessions, guided practices and immersive workshops, these experiences offer new perspectives on healing, mindfulness and holistic wellbeing.
