An Exciting New Way to Play
Vakkaru Maldives is proud to be among the first resorts in Baa Atoll to introduce padel, one of the fastest-growing sports in the world. Played on a professional-grade court enclosed with mesh netting, the game combines the best elements of tennis and squash to create an exciting and highly social experience.
Enjoy casual matches with friends or family, take part in group sessions or book one-on-one coaching with our resident tennis professional to refine your skills. The sport is suitable for beginners and experienced players alike, making it a fun and accessible activity for all ages.
Advance reservations are required to secure court time, and equipment is available for guests to enjoy this energetic new addition to the island’s sporting experiences.
