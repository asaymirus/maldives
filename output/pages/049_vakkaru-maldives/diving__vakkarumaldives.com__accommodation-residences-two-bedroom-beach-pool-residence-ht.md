Two Bedroom Beach Pool Residence
This generously-sized residence features two bedrooms, a comfortable living room and a private pool surrounded by lush gardens. With an expansive terrace and the beach just beyond, it offers an ideal setting for families or friends seeking relaxed island living together.
Overview
Nestled among tropical greenery close to the shoreline, the Two Bedroom Beach Pool Residence offers an inviting setting for families or friends travelling together. The residence includes two well-appointed bedrooms, a newly added living room and generous indoor-outdoor areas designed for easy island living.
The terrace becomes a natural gathering space, where the pool, shaded seating and outdoor dining area encourage long afternoons outdoors. With the beach only steps away, time moves easily between the garden, the water and the comfort of the residence.
Inside, natural materials and soft tones reflect the relaxed ambiance of the island. Thoughtful design and generous proportions allow everyone to settle in comfortably, creating a welcoming place to spend time together.

Time Well Spent
The residence opens naturally towards the garden, where the terrace and pool become the centre of the experience. Doors slide open, conversations drift outside and the beach lies just beyond the palms.
Days settle into an easy rhythm. Gather by the pool or wander down to the sand, while shaded corners of the terrace offer quiet places to pause. The living room provides a comfortable retreat between moments outdoors.
With two bedrooms and generous shared spaces, the residence allows everyone to spend time together while still finding quiet corners of their own. It is a setting that encourages long meals, relaxed afternoons and the simple pleasure of being together on the island.
