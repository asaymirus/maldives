Organic Garden Dining
Begin with a guided walk through our organic garden, selecting fresh ingredients before enjoying a garden-to-table dining experience prepared by our chefs in a tranquil palm-shaded setting.
Inquire Now
Nestled within the island's kitchen garden, this is dining with a story behind every dish. A guided walk through the garden precedes the meal, with fresh ingredients selected at their peak before being transformed by our chefs into a menu that is as considered as the setting itself.
Shaded by palms and entirely at your own pace, it is one of the most quietly memorable experiences the island has to offer.
