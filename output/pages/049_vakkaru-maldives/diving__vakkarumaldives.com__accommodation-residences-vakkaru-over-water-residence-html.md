The Vakkaru Overwater Residence
An award-winning sanctuary set above the lagoon, this two-storey residence is among the Maldives’ largest. Four bedrooms, a sweeping infinity pool, private chef and dedicated butler service are complemented by a spa terrace and panoramic ocean views, creating an extraordinary retreat for families or friends.
Overview
An award-winning retreat and one of the Maldives’ largest overwater residences, this remarkable two-storey sanctuary combines expansive living with deeply personalised service. Two king-size bedrooms on the lower level and two twin rooms above each enjoy private terraces or balconies overlooking uninterrupted ocean views.
At the heart of the residence lies a 55 sqm infinity pool that appears to merge seamlessly with the lagoon beyond. Surrounding terraces, indoor and outdoor dining spaces and a fully equipped butler’s pantry create the feeling of a private overwater home.
A dedicated chef and personal butler shape each day around your preferences, from bespoke menus and sunset cocktails to effortless daily arrangements.
Thoughtful experiences further enrich the stay, including Champagne on arrival, afternoon tea, a private beach barbecue, sunset yoga, jungle cinema evenings and in-villa spa rituals. Spanning more than 950 sqm, the residence offers exceptional scale and privacy for families and friends seeking a truly extraordinary Maldivian escape.

Endless Horizons
Some places feel extraordinary from the moment you arrive.
Suspended above the lagoon, our flagship residence unfolds across two levels of light, space and uninterrupted horizon. The ocean surrounds you on every side, shifting in colour throughout the day as the sun moves across the water.
Mornings begin quietly with the sound of the sea beneath the terrace. Breakfast appears just as the day warms, prepared by your private chef while the lagoon glimmers below.
Afternoons drift easily between the infinity pool, the spa terrace and long lunches shared around the dining table. Some slip away for a moment of stillness, others gather again as the light softens across the water.
As evening arrives, the residence takes on a different rhythm. Lanterns glow across the deck, the horizon fades into dusk and the ocean becomes an endless field of stars.
For a time, the Maldives feels entirely your own.
