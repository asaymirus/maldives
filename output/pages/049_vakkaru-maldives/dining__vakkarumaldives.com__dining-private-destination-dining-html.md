Destination Dining
From castaway picnics and candlelit dinners to jungle cinema nights and wine pairings, destination dining at Vakkaru is crafted for moments to savour, share and remember.
Inquire Now
At Vakkaru, dining is never confined to four walls. The island itself becomes the setting: a private sandbank at sunset, a table on the beach as the tide comes in, a candlelit dinner beneath the trees at the Jungle Cinema as night falls. Each experience is shaped around the moment, with menus, details and surroundings considered as carefully as the company you keep.
Whether you are marking a special occasion or simply seeking something beyond the ordinary, our team will create a setting and an experience that is entirely your own.
