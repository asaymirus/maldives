Luxury shaped by nature
A Timeless Sanctuary
Set within the UNESCO Biosphere Reserve of Baa Atoll, Vakkaru Maldives is a secluded island surrounded by turquoise lagoons, powder-soft beaches and a vibrant house reef.
A scenic 30-minute seaplane journey from Malé brings you to this tranquil retreat, where space, privacy and the natural rhythm of island life shape every stay.

Ocean Experiences
Encounters with the ocean begin just beyond our shores
Scuba Diving
Discover the vibrant underwater world of Baa Atoll with guided scuba dives for all experience levels. Explore colourful coral reefs, encounter tropical marine life and enjoy personalised dives led by experienced instructors in the crystal-clear waters surrounding Vakkaru Maldives.
Explore More
Diving Courses
From first-time divers to advanced certifications, our PADI diving courses offer expert guidance in a relaxed island setting. Learn new skills, build confidence underwater and gain internationally recognised certifications while exploring the spectacular marine life of the Maldives.
Explore More
Snorkelling
Explore the rich marine life of Baa Atoll with unforgettable snorkelling adventures. Discover the vibrant blue hole house reefs, swim alongside sea turtles, visit colourful coral gardens or journey to Hanifaru Bay to witness majestic manta rays in their natural habitat.
Explore More
Watersports
Dive into exhilarating watersports at Vakkaru Maldives. From jet skiing and parasailing to wakeboarding, kite surfing and sailing, enjoy a wide range of motorised and non-motorised adventures designed for beginners and experienced thrill seekers alike.
Explore More
Excursions & Cruises
Set sail across the turquoise waters of Baa Atoll with unforgettable ocean excursions. Discover marine life on guided snorkelling trips, enjoy leisurely sunset cruises or experience the beauty of the Maldives from the comfort of a luxury yacht.
Explore More
Fishing Adventures
Experience the thrill of fishing in the Indian Ocean with traditional Maldivian sunset fishing or exciting big game expeditions. Cast your line for sailfish, tuna and mahi-mahi and enjoy the timeless connection between island culture and the sea.
Explore More
Private Charters
Create your own ocean adventure aboard a private yacht charter. Cruise through the UNESCO Biosphere Reserve of Baa Atoll, discover hidden reefs, search for dolphins or enjoy a personalised snorkelling or sunset experience tailored entirely to you.
