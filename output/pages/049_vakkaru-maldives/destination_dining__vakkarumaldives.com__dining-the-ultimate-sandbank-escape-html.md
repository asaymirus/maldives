Sandbank Escape
Escape to a secluded sandbank for a private dining experience, from candlelit dinners to beachside barbecues, stargazing and relaxed evenings beneath the open sky. A true castaway moment to savour.
Inquire Now
Reached by boat and reserved entirely for you, the sandbank sits at the edge of the lagoon with nothing but open water and sky in every direction. It is one of the most singular settings Vakkaru has to offer, utterly private, unhurried and unlike anywhere else on the island.
Our team will have everything in place on arrival, with the table set, the details considered and the evening ready to unfold entirely at your own pace.
