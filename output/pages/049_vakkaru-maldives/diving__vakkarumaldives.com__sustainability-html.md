Sustainability
At Vakkaru Maldives, sustainability is woven into the island's
kitchens and bars
. From protecting marine ecosystems to reducing waste and supporting responsible sourcing, our initiatives are designed to preserve the natural beauty of the Baa Atoll while nurturing the environment and community that surrounds us.
Fresh Drinking Water
Our on-island reverse osmosis plant produces purified still and sparkling drinking water using advanced filtration technology. Bottled in reusable glass containers and served across villas and restaurants, this initiative significantly reduces single-use plastics while ensuring the highest quality drinking water.
The Organic Farm
Our organic farm supplies fresh fruits, vegetables and herbs for the island’s kitchens and bars. Sustainable gardening practices include composting organic waste and irrigating crops with recycled water. Guests may also visit the farm to harvest ingredients and enjoy a personalised garden-to-table culinary experience.
Coral Planting
Protecting our surrounding reef is central to island conservation. Through coral propagation initiatives, fragmented corals are carefully attached to specially designed frames and nurtured within the lagoon. These growing colonies help restore reef ecosystems and provide vital habitats for marine life.
Ocean Stewardship
Regular reef clean-up initiatives help protect the vibrant marine ecosystem surrounding the island. Guests and team members work together to remove debris and support ocean conservation efforts, helping maintain a healthy reef for future generations.
