About Vakkaru Maldives
Set within the UNESCO Biosphere Reserve of Baa Atoll, Vakkaru Maldives is a secluded reef island where powder-soft white sands meet vibrant marine life and endless ocean horizons. Surrounded by over 2,300 coconut palms, the island offers a timeless sanctuary designed for privacy, connection and unforgettable experiences.
Seamless Transfers
Your journey to Vakkaru Maldives begins with a scenic seaplane flight over the turquoise atolls of the Indian Ocean. Guests are welcomed at our private lounge at the seaplane terminal, where refreshments and WiFi await before departure. From there, a 30-minute flight brings you to the island, revealing breathtaking views of coral reefs, lagoons and sandbanks along the way.
Domestic flight transfers via Dharavandhoo Airport are also available, followed by a short speedboat journey to the resort.
