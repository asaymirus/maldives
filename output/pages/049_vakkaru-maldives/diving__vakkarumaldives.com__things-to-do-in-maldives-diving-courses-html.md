Diving Courses
From first-time discovery dives and PADI courses to guided house reef dives and night diving in the Baa Atoll, Splash offers a full range of scuba experiences for every level. All courses include tank, weights, e-learning fees and certification.

PADI Open Water Diver Course
Get PADI Open Water certified with online theory, shallow water skills practice and four open water dives. The most complete entry-level certification available at Splash. Suitable for ages 10 and above.

PADI Advanced Open Water Course
Advance your skills with five dives including deep and navigation dives plus three specialty dives. Completed over two days. Suitable for ages 12 and above with open water certification.

On request

2 days
