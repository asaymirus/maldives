Where Energy Meets the Outdoors
Opening Hours:
7am - 10pm
The Sports Fields at Vakkaru Maldives provide a dynamic outdoor space for guests who enjoy staying active during their island escape. Set within the lush surroundings of the resort, these facilities offer opportunities for both friendly matches and energetic workouts.
Enjoy a variety of sports including football, beach volleyball and badminton, as well as tennis on our all-weather floodlit courts. Whether playing competitively or simply for fun with family and friends, the setting encourages movement, connection and enjoyment in the open air.
Advance reservations are recommended to ensure availability. Equipment is provided, making it easy to step onto the court or field and enjoy a game at any time of day.
