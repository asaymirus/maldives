Vakkaru Maldives Receives One MICHELIN Key
20 Dec 2025
A Prestigious Distinction in the MICHELIN Guide Hotel Selection 2025: Vakkaru Maldives is honoured to be recognised with One MICHELIN Key in the MICHELIN Guide Hotel Selection 2025. This global accolade is awarded to hotels that offer an
exceptional stay
, where every element comes together to create a memorable and meaningful experience.
The MICHELIN Key is awarded to properties that offer far more than a place to stay. It recognises hotels defined by character, consistency and a strong sense of place, where thoughtful design, refined service and emotional connection come together to create a truly memorable experience.
This honour reflects the philosophy that guides Vakkaru Maldives every day. A commitment to understated luxury, intuitive hospitality and moments that feel both effortless and deeply personal.
To receive a MICHELIN Key is a meaningful milestone. It stands as a reflection of the passion, care and dedication of the team who bring the spirit of Vakkaru to life, and of the guests who continue to choose the island as their place of return.
