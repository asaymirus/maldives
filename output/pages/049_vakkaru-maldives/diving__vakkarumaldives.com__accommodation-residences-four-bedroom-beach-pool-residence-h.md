Four Bedroom Beach Pool Residence
Positioned along a prime stretch of beach, this four-bedroom residence combines two plunge pool villas with a spacious two-bedroom core residence featuring a 42 sqm pool, shaded gazebo and outdoor dining terrace. With en suite bathrooms, outdoor showers and direct beach access throughout, it offers an exceptional setting for families or groups travelling together.
Overview
Located along one of the island’s most secluded beachfront settings, the Four Bedroom Beach Pool Residence offers an expansive island home designed for families or groups seeking both shared living and individual retreat.
At its centre lies a beautifully enhanced two-bedroom residence featuring a 42 sqm swimming pool, broad terrace and a shaded dining gazebo where guests naturally gather throughout the day. On either side, two standalone Beach Villas provide additional independence, each with its own plunge pool, whirlpool and direct access to the beach.
Every bedroom includes an en suite bathroom, outdoor rain shower and a private terrace that opens onto the sand. Natural wood textures, soft tones and open-plan living spaces reflect the relaxed elegance of the Maldives while allowing light and ocean breezes to flow easily through the residence.

Your Own Place on the Beach
There is a rare feeling that comes with arriving somewhere that is entirely yours.
A path through tropical gardens opens onto a private stretch of beach, where villas sit quietly among the palms and the sea moves gently beyond the sand. At the centre, a wide terrace and shimmering pool become the natural gathering place, where mornings begin slowly and evenings unfold beneath the glow of lantern light.
Around it, each villa offers its own retreat. Doors open to outdoor showers beneath the sky, plunge pools hidden in greenery, and shaded verandas where the day can pass unhurried.
Families drift between spaces, sometimes together, sometimes apart, always returning to the shared rhythm of the residence.
Here, time feels generous. The ocean sets the pace, and every moment belongs entirely to you.
