One Bedroom Beach Pool Residence
Set within its own garden and just moments from the beach, this one-bedroom residence offers comfort, calm and a sense of ease. Refined interiors, a shaded sundeck and a pool create a relaxed setting, ideal for couples or solo travellers seeking a more spacious island escape.
Overview
Nestled within tropical forest close to the shoreline, the One Bedroom Beach Pool Residence offers a calm and spacious retreat. Created for couples or solo travellers who appreciate extra room to unwind, the residence features a large bedroom with elegant island-inspired interiors, a walk-in wardrobe and a separate living area.
Outside, the garden terrace becomes the natural centre of daily life. A shaded sundeck and pool provide a quiet setting for afternoons in the sun or evenings beneath the stars.
Inside, soft tones and natural materials reflect the relaxed rhythm of the island. With its larger living spaces and seamless flow between indoors and out, the residence offers a refined balance of comfort, privacy and understated luxury.

A Place to Settle In
Some villas invite you to visit. This one encourages you to slow down and stay a while.
Set within its own leafy garden, the residence feels quietly removed from the rhythm of the island while remaining only steps from the shore. Doors open easily between indoors and out, where the terrace and pool become natural extensions of the living space.
A swim before breakfast, an afternoon spent reading in the shade or a walk along the beach just beyond the garden wall becomes part of the gentle rhythm of each day.
As evening settles over the island, the garden grows still and the sound of the ocean carries through the palms. With generous interiors and an easy flow between spaces, the residence offers the quiet comfort of a private island home.
