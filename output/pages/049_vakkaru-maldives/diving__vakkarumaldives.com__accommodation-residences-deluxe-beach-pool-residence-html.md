One Bedroom Deluxe Beach Pool Residence
Surrounded by tropical gardens and just steps from the shoreline, this deluxe residence features a spacious living room, an expansive beachfront terrace and its own pool. Natural materials and soft island tones frame open views across the lagoon, creating a setting for guests who value both comfort and a heightened sense of space.
Overview
Facing the turquoise lagoon and bordered by tropical gardens, the One Bedroom Deluxe Beach Pool Residence offers a refined island retreat with generous living areas throughout. The residence features a king bedroom with warm natural finishes, a walk-in wardrobe and an elegant en-suite bathroom.
A separate living room opens onto a broad beachfront terrace where loungers, outdoor dining space and a private pool create a natural setting for long, relaxed days beside the sea.
Throughout the residence, soft lighting and handcrafted details reflect the island’s natural textures and tones. With its expanded layout and seamless flow between indoor and outdoor spaces, this residence offers an elevated sense of comfort overlooking the ocean.

Room to Breathe
Some spaces immediately change the pace of a stay. The moment you step inside, the residence opens out towards the terrace and the lagoon beyond, creating a sense of quiet ease.
The separate living room becomes the heart of the space, somewhere to gather, linger over coffee, or retreat from the midday sun. Doors slide open to the terrace, where the pool and outdoor dining area extend the living space into the garden.
There is a sense of freedom in the way the residence unfolds. Move easily between indoors and out, spend the afternoon by the pool, or wander down to the beach when the ocean calls.
As the day draws to a close, the terrace settles into calm. The lagoon darkens, the garden falls quiet, and the residence becomes a private retreat where the evening can unfold slowly and without interruption.
