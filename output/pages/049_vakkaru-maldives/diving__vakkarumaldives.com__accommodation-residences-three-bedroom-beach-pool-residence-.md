Three Bedroom Beach Pool Residence
Set among tropical gardens just steps from the shoreline, this three-bedroom residence offers a relaxed setting for families or groups travelling together. A spacious living room, large pool, shaded gazebo and terraces create an easy balance between gathering and private moments.
Overview
Located along a quiet stretch of beach framed by tropical jungle, this Three Bedroom Beach Pool Residence offers a spacious island setting for families or groups. The residence combines a fully upgraded two-bedroom unit with a separate Beach Villa, creating a layout that balances shared living with individual privacy.
The main residence features a comfortable living room, expansive terrace, shaded gazebo and a large swimming pool that becomes the natural gathering point of the home. The adjoining Beach Villa adds a third bedroom with its own plunge pool, offering additional independence for guests.
Each bedroom includes an en suite bathroom, outdoor rain shower and a private terrace opening directly onto the sand. Natural wood finishes, soft tones and open-plan living create a relaxed atmosphere that connects easily with the surrounding landscape.

A Place to Reconnect
Life here unfolds around shared spaces. The terrace becomes the centre of the residence, where the pool, gazebo and garden create a natural setting for time spent together.
Mornings begin slowly with coffee on the veranda while others wander down to the beach. Later, the pool draws everyone back again, offering a cool retreat from the warmth of the day.
The layout of the residence allows each guest to move easily between the main house and the separate villa, offering both connection and independence. Some gather around the table while others find a quiet corner of the garden, with the beach just beyond the palms.
Even with its generous scale, the residence feels calm and unhurried. It is a place where families and friends settle naturally into the rhythm of island life.
