Parrotfish Club
Designed for younger guests aged 3 to 12, Parrotfish Club offers a vibrant space for creativity, discovery and play. From arts and crafts to outdoor adventures, children can enjoy engaging activities guided by our dedicated team in a safe and inspiring environment.

A Playground for Imagination
Opening Hours:
8.30am - 6pm
Parrotfish Club offers a colourful world of creativity, discovery and adventure for children aged 3 to 12. Thoughtfully designed indoor spaces encourage arts, crafts and imaginative play, while outdoor areas feature a children’s pool, activity zones and space for energetic exploration.
Daily programmes include games, themed activities and hands-on experiences such as cookie and pizza making, as well as the Junior Master Chef programme. Young adventurers can also enjoy exciting highlights like pirate-themed activities and obstacle challenges.
Seasonal programmes ensure a fresh calendar of activities throughout the year. Complimentary supervised group care is available for children aged 3 to 12 during opening hours, while children under 3 are welcome when accompanied by a parent or babysitter.
