Lagoon Bar
Perched above the lagoon, Lagoon Bar offers a relaxed overwater setting for lingering lunches, sunset cocktails and light Asian-inspired dining. Creative drinks are paired with sushi, sashimi and vibrant tapas-style plates, served alongside sweeping ocean views. Day beds, hammocks above the water and occasional live music add to the atmosphere, making it a favourite place to pause, gather and watch the sun dip beneath the horizon.
