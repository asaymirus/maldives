About The Maldives
Scattered across the Indian Ocean southwest of Sri Lanka, the Maldives is an archipelago of coral islands, turquoise lagoons and vibrant marine life. Formed by ancient reefs rising from the ocean floor, this remarkable nation is composed of 26 natural atolls and more than 1,200 islands, many of which remain untouched and uninhabited.
An Island Nation
The Maldives stretches across a vast underwater mountain range, forming a chain of more than 1,200 coral islands and sandbanks arranged across 26 natural atolls. Around 200 islands are home to local communities, while others host private resorts or remain uninhabited. Each island is encircled by a lagoon of clear turquoise water and protected by a coral reef, creating the distinctive “one island, one resort” concept that defines the Maldivian experience.
History and Culture
Maldivian culture reflects centuries of maritime trade and exchange across the Indian Ocean. Influences from India, Sri Lanka, the Middle East and Africa have shaped local traditions, cuisine and music. One of the most celebrated cultural expressions is Boduberu, a rhythmic performance of drumming, chanting and dance traditionally performed barefoot on the sand, bringing communities together in a vibrant display of island heritage.
Connectivity
Velana International Airport in Malé is the main gateway to the Maldives, well connected with major cities worldwide. From here, guests continue their journey by seaplane to Vakkaru Maldives, enjoying a scenic 30-minute flight over coral atolls and turquoise lagoons.
Weather
Located close to the equator, the Maldives enjoys a warm tropical climate throughout the year, with temperatures typically ranging between 23°C and 32°C. The drier season runs from November to April, while May to October brings occasional tropical showers and cooling ocean breezes.
Time Zone & Currency
The Maldives operates on GMT +5, while Vakkaru Maldives follows island time at GMT +6. The US dollar is widely accepted throughout the resort, and major credit cards including Visa, Mastercard and American Express are also accepted for most services.
