Anguru Teppanyaki
Anguru celebrates the art of Japan’s teppan grill. Inspired by the island’s heritage as a coconut plantation, the name refers to the fire created from coconut shells in traditional Maldivian kitchens. Seating just ten guests around the grill, the semi-open pavilion invites diners to watch skilled chefs prepare each dish with precision and flair, creating a memorable culinary performance from lunch through to evening.
