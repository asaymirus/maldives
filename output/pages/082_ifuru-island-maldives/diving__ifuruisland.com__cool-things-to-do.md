COOL Things To Do at Ifuru
Explore fun activities in Maldives
Ifuru Island Maldives resort offers an array of exciting activities and experiences to make your stay truly unforgettable. Dive into the mesmerizing underwater world with scuba diving adventures guided by experienced instructors, where you’ll explore vibrant coral reefs and encounter diverse marine life.
For a more leisurely exploration, snorkel through crystal-clear waters, discovering colorful coral gardens and an abundance of marine species. Fishing enthusiasts can embark on thrilling fishing charters, casting their lines and enjoying the thrill of reeling in impressive catches while taking in the stunning ocean views. The resort also offers a range of non-motorized watersports, allowing you to try kayaking, and stand-up paddleboarding. Set sail on a sailing or sunset charter cruise, where you can bask in the golden hues of the sunset and indulge in a serene and luxurious experience surrounded by the pristine beauty of the Maldives.
If you’re looking to stay active, the resort’s Star Fitness facility is open 24/7, equipped with state-of-the-art equipment for cardio and strength training, along with amenities for flexibility exercises and yoga. The Play All Day Recreation Centre and Indoor Cinema provide endless entertainment with thrilling games, exciting activities, and cozy family movie nights.
