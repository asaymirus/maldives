Must-Visit Sites: The Raa Atoll Advantage
The Raa Atoll is renowned for its healthy coral reefs and dramatic underwater topography. Guests at Ifuru Island have direct access to:
Ifuru Thila:
An iconic submerged pinnacle known for its rich marine biodiversity and strong currents that attract schools of fusiliers, sharks, and rays.
The House Reef:
A vibrant ecosystem just steps from the villas, perfect for effortless snorkeling and introductory dives.
Unique Biodiversity:
Regular sightings include
Napoleon wrasses, grey reef sharks, eagle rays
, and seasonal
manta rays
(particularly at nearby Manta Quest sites).
