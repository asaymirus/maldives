ONSITE MEDICAL CLINIC
Feel secure knowing that our Resort Doctor is available 24/7 at the Clinic near the Social House South Entrance. While we hope your stay remains worry-free, our priority is your health and safety, which is why we provide onsite medical assistance.
Our fully equipped clinic and skilled medical staff are ready to address any injuries or health concerns, including first aid and minor injuries.
In rare instances of more serious issues, our resort team will handle all necessary arrangements with the nearest hospital and coordinate with local health authorities for a seamless experience.
