Accessibility Statement
At Ifuru, we are committed to providing a welcoming and inclusive environment for all guests, including those with disabilities. We strive to ensure that individuals with accessibility needs have a comfortable and enjoyable stay. Please contact our team directly if you have any queries or requests regarding accessibility. We are committed to working with guests to ensure their needs are met to the best of our abilities.
