The Waterfront
Experience a unique culinary journey at our resort, blending Asian cuisine with Mediterranean and South American influences. Our talented chefs create personalized dishes before your eyes. For a bespoke experience, visit our top-floor Japanese Teppanyaki & Sushi Counter. Enjoy tailor-made creations and an unforgettable culinary adventure.
Hubba Hubba
At Hubba Hubba, our vibrant heart, the magic unfolds! With funky tunes, refreshing cocktails, and an upbeat pool party scene, the day starts strong. In-house and international DJs create an electrifying atmosphere, while the renowned Gong Experience welcomes new guests with complimentary shots. The enchanting Sunset Ceremony offers tranquility, followed by thrilling night session DJ sets. Hubba Hubba is the ultimate island hotspot for unforgettable entertainment!
Poolside Pizza
Indulge in a serene pool day on Maldives’ Ifuru Island with Poolside Pizza by the Meter. Enjoy thin-crust masterpieces with your favorite toppings, from classics like Margherita to gourmet options like prosciutto and truffle oil. With a meter-long display, create a personalized feast for any dietary preference. Whether it’s a quick snack or leisurely poolside dining, let Ifuru Island elevate your culinary adventure.
Beach Club
Located in the prime beachfront area, our Beach Club offers the perfect spot to witness stunning sunsets. Enjoy a relaxed atmosphere with lively tunes, beach buckets filled with cold drinks, and tailored cocktails. It’s the ultimate boho beach club experience. Indulge in healthy bowls, light bites, and a wide selection of cocktails, premium spirits, and handpicked wines from around the world. Explore signature cocktails and mocktails curated for your enjoyment.
Palm Grill
Indulge in a rustic grill steakhouse experience that celebrates the rich flavors of prime beef and locally caught fresh fish cooked over a charcoal grill. Experience a fusion of meat lovers’ delights, seafood delicacies, and exquisite vegan creations in a vibrant setting that blends dining and entertainment. Whether you seek a romantic evening under the sparkling ocean or a starry night sky, our steakhouse offers the perfect mix of ambiance and breathtaking vistas.
Social House
Social House is where diverse culinary traditions from around the globe unite. Experience a remarkable food culture that showcases the finest dishes from around the world, meticulously remastered to perfection by our talented culinary team. Indulge in a delightful buffet-style service, complete with live cooking stations and a tempting array of à la carte options for breakfast, lunch, and dinner. Prepare to be thrilled by cuisine inspired from dishes cooked in all parts of the world across the 5 oceans, paired with a daily special selection of wines from New and Old World wineries.
Ifuru Eats

When hunger strikes, request Ifuru Eats for a delightful late-night indulgence and anticipate the next surprise treat. Enhance your island getaway with culinary delights.
