Sustainability
Ifuru’s Commitment to Sustainability
Maldives’ eco=friendly resort
At Ifuru Island Maldives, sustainability is part of how we care for our island, our people and the experiences we create for our guests. Set within the natural beauty of Raa Atoll, we recognise the importance of operating with care, respect and responsibility. Our approach focuses on responsible resource use, protection of marine life and biodiversity, respect for local culture, fair treatment of our team, human rights, child safeguarding, community support and honest communication with our guests, partners and suppliers. We continue to review our practices and strengthen awareness across the resort so that sustainability remains part of our daily decision-making.

June 2026
Our Approach
Sustainability is a shared responsibility across Ifuru Island Maldives. Every department plays a role in reducing unnecessary impact, improving daily practices and supporting a more responsible island experience. Our teams work together to manage resources carefully, support responsible operations, communicate honestly and encourage guests to make mindful choices during their stay.
Responsible Use of Resources
We are committed to using energy, water and other resources responsibly across guest areas, operational spaces, staff facilities and resort services. Our teams monitor resource use, maintain equipment carefully and work to reduce unnecessary consumption wherever practical. This includes encouraging responsible use of lighting, air conditioning, water systems and other equipment that supports daily island operations.
We support this through:
Encouraging responsible use of lighting, air conditioning and electrical equipment.
Maintaining systems and equipment to support efficient operation.
Promoting careful water use across the resort.
Supporting towel and linen reuse practices.
Encouraging team members to report leaks, faults or unnecessary resource use.
Reviewing operational practices that may help reduce avoidable greenhouse gas emissions.
Waste Reduction
Waste management is an important part of responsible island operations. We work to reduce unnecessary waste, separate waste where possible and review opportunities to reuse or recycle materials. We encourage both guests and team members to dispose of waste correctly and to avoid unnecessary single-use items where suitable alternatives are available.
Our approach includes:
Reducing avoidable single-use items where practical.
Encouraging correct waste separation in guest and operational areas.
Reviewing food waste practices across kitchens and dining outlets.
Working with relevant service providers for responsible waste handling.
Sharing waste reduction reminders with guests and team members.
Protecting Marine Life and Biodiversity
The natural environment is one of the Maldives’ greatest treasures. At Ifuru Island Maldives, we are committed to protecting the island, lagoon, reef and marine life through responsible practices and guest awareness. We encourage all guests to enjoy the ocean respectfully and help us protect the delicate marine ecosystems around the island.
Guests are kindly asked to:
Avoid touching, standing on or damaging coral.
Keep a respectful distance from marine life.
Do not chase, feed, hold or disturb wildlife.
Avoid collecting shells, coral, sand or marine life.
Follow all snorkelling, diving and excursion briefings.
Use reef-safe sun protection where possible.
Dispose of waste properly and never leave litter on the beach or in the ocean.
Responsible Food and Beverage Practices
Food and beverage experiences are an important part of island hospitality. We continue to review how we purchase, prepare and serve food in ways that support responsible operations and reduce avoidable waste. Our culinary teams work to balance guest choice with responsible practices, including mindful sourcing, thoughtful menu planning and food waste awareness.
Our approach includes:
Offering vegetarian and plant-based options.
Including Maldivian and locally inspired cuisine in the guest experience.
Encouraging responsible buffet practices.
Reviewing food waste across kitchens and outlets.
Working with suppliers to better understand sourcing and product choices.
Responsible Procurement
We aim to make thoughtful purchasing decisions that support responsible operations, reduce unnecessary environmental impact and encourage positive value through our supply chain.
Where possible and suitable, we give preference to products and services that are:
Locally supplied or locally produced.
Reusable, recyclable or made with recycled materials.
Supplied with reduced or more responsible packaging.
Energy or water efficient.
Responsibly sourced or certified.
Lower in pollution or environmental impact.
Ethically produced and responsibly supplied.
Reflective of local culture, nature and community value.
We also work to avoid products and practices that may contribute to human exploitation, harmful wildlife trade, marine pollution, invasive species, harmful microplastics or unnecessary environmental harm.
Respect for People and Human Rights
We are committed to respecting human rights and treating people with dignity, fairness and care. We do not tolerate exploitation, forced labour, human trafficking, harassment, abuse or discrimination in any form. This commitment applies to our team members, guests, suppliers, partners and the communities connected to our operations.
Our approach includes:
Supporting fair and respectful working conditions.
Promoting equal opportunity and non-discrimination.
Encouraging safe and open communication.
Providing channels for feedback and concerns.
Working with suppliers and partners who are expected to act responsibly.

Our Team
Our team members are central to the Ifuru Island experience. We are committed to supporting a safe, respectful and inclusive workplace where team members are treated fairly and given opportunities to grow.
Our approach includes:
Clear employment terms and working conditions.
Respect for applicable employment laws.
Safe and respectful working environments.
Training and awareness for responsible operations.
Communication channels for staff feedback and concerns.
Support for learning, development and professional growth where possible.
Local Culture and Community
The Maldives has a rich culture, heritage and way of life. We aim to represent local culture respectfully through hospitality, cuisine, design, storytelling and guest experiences where appropriate. We encourage guests to respect local customs and traditions, especially when visiting local islands or community areas. Respectful dress, mindful behaviour and asking permission before taking photos or videos of people are important parts of responsible travel. We also aim to support the wider community through responsible partnerships, local engagement and opportunities that contribute positively where possible.
Responsible Guest Travel
Guests play an important role in helping us protect the island and the destination. Small actions during each stay can support a more responsible travel experience.
Guests can support us by:
Using energy and water mindfully.
Reusing towels and linens where possible.
Disposing of waste correctly.
Respecting coral reefs, marine life and natural habitats.
Following resort safety and environmental guidance.
Respecting local culture, customs and communities.
Asking permission before photographing or filming people, especially children.
Reporting any concerns relating to safety, exploitation, abuse or environmental harm.

Working With Suppliers and Partners
We expect our suppliers and partners to support responsible business practices. This includes respect for human rights, legal compliance, environmental responsibility, fair treatment of workers and avoidance of harmful or exploitative practices. Where applicable, we communicate our expectations to suppliers and request supporting information relating to responsible sourcing, licences, product specifications or compliance. We will not knowingly support or promote practices that involve exploitation, abuse, illegal wildlife trade, irresponsible animal interactions or serious harm to people, animals or the environment.
