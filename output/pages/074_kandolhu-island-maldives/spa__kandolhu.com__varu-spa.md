Good to Know
SMALL DETAILS, CONSIDERED IN ADVANCE
We want your time at Varu to feel effortless from start to finish. A few friendly reminders help keep things calm, considered, and easy for everyone.
MOBILE PHONES
To keep the spa serene, we ask that phones are switched off before entering the locker rooms or treatment areas.
QUIET, PLEASE
Help us keep the calm by keeping noise to a minimum, especially near the treatment rooms.
AGE GUIDELINES
Guests aged 12 and above are welcome; younger visitors will need to be accompanied by an adult.
NO SMOKING
The spa is a smoke-free space throughout.
VALUABLES
For peace of mind, we recommend leaving valuables in your villa.
PRICING
Treatment prices are subject to change; we’re always happy to answer questions when you book.
RESERVATIONS
To secure your preferred time, treatment, or therapist, we recommend booking ahead. Same-day bookings and walk-ins are welcomed where availability allows – simply call ext. 122 from your villa or visit guest services.
CANCELLATIONS
Plans change. Please let us know at least 6 hours ahead to avoid a rescheduling charge; for late arrivals, we’ll do our best to fit your treatment into the time remaining.
