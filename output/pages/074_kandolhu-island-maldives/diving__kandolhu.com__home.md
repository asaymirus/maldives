Most islands in the Maldives ask you to arrive and be impressed. Kandolhu does something quieter. With only thirty villas, the island reveals itself with ease. The reef is steps away. Restaurants feel unhurried. Spaces are beautiful without asking to be noticed. People here remember your name before you expect them to. It’s the Maldives as it was meant to be – natural, effortless, and deeply personal.
