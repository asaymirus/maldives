Water Sports
Step from your villa into water built for wandering. Sail, paddle, or drift across the reef, at your own pace.
Windsurfing, paddleboarding, catamaran sailing, and kayaking are yours whenever the mood takes you, complimentary and ready when you are. Wakeboarding, kneeboarding, and guided excursions are available for a fee.
ON THE WATER
Windsurf, paddle, or sail, no lessons required unless you want them. Beyond the reef, the water is yours to explore, far and wide.
Water sport Activities
Our team gives a quick introduction to each activity below, so you’re moving in minutes whether you’ve done it before or you’re starting from scratch.
Water Sports & Excursions Brochure
WINDSURFING
Harness the wind and glide across the reef, one exhilarating stretch at a time. If you are new to it, our team offers a quick check and complimentary rental to get you started, no experience required.
CATAMARAN
Sail out past the reef for cool breezes and open water, solo, in tandem, or led by one of our instructors. Private lessons and high-wind joyrides are available too, for those chasing something faster.
KAYAKING
Paddle along the house reef, or venture further out to a quiet stretch of sand nearby, however far you feel like going. A quick introduction and all equipment are included, whatever your experience level.
STAND-UP PADDLEBOARDING
Steady your balance and drift across calm water, best at sunrise or as the sky turns at day’s end. A brief introduction is included if you are new to it, along with all equipment.
WAKEBOARDING / KNEEBOARDING
For those chasing a thrill, our instructors are ready to get you up and gliding, whatever your experience level. Both activities come at an additional fee, with equipment and tuition provided on the boat.
