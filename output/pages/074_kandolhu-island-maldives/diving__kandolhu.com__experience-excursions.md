Excursions
Charter a boat and go further, chasing whale sharks, sunsets, and quiet stretches of sand only the sea can offer.
From dawn fishing trips to torchlit evenings and Champagne toast to the setting sun – each excursion is led by our crew and shaped around you.
THE MALDIVES IS YOURS TO EXPLORE
Our crew knows these waters well, whether that means chasing wahoo offshore, stalking sharks by torchlight, or ferrying you to a desert island hemmed in by reefs and dappled with palms.
Beyond Kandolhu
Uncover each excursion below, from manta ray encounters to quieter days adrift on a private sandbank. Prefer to charter the whole boat for the day? Our team can arrange that too. Download our full guide for timings and booking notes.
Water Sports & Excursions Brochure
MANTA RAY MISSION
Slip into the water as manta rays glide past, wingtip to wingtip, one of the ocean’s most graceful encounters.
BIG GAME FISHING
Head into open water for sail fish, tuna, and wahoo, guided by a crew who cater to first-timers and seasoned anglers alike.
BITE AT NIGHT
Cast a line as the sky turns, hunting reef fish by torchlight, with your catch ready to cook if you would like.
TAKE IT TO THE BANK
A private sandbank, ringed by reef and nothing else, is yours for an afternoon of sun and quiet water.
LOCAL ISLAND EXPERIENCE
Head further out to a local island of palms and reef, where the only real decision is snorkel or sunbathe.
CHAMPAGNE SUNSET CRUISE
Sit back with canapés and a glass of Champagne as the sun eases into the Indian Ocean.
