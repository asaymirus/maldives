Snorkelling
Slip into quiet water, from reefs just off the sand to others further out, each one waiting to be uncovered.
Beyond the house reef, a guide can lead you further, to hidden coral gardens by day or torchlit waters after dark, with equipment ready whenever curiosity strikes.
BENEATH THE SURFACE
Snorkelling here goes well past the doorstep. Guided trips reveal reefs, sandbanks, and after-dark corners you would not find alone.
SNORKELLING EXPERIENCES
Explore the reef guided or on your own, from reef hopping to night snorkelling, with full gear included whenever you’re ready to go in.
REEF HOPPING
Follow our expert guide across two of our favourite reefs nearby, an easy two-hour outing suited to first-timers and experienced snorkellers alike. Life jackets, gear, and a guide – all provided.
KANDOLHU REEF EXPLORATION
A private boat and guide take you to the far side of the reef, where you can jump in and out as you please. Ninety minutes, gear included.
NIGHT SNORKELLING
As the light fades, a different reef comes alive. Follow your guide with torch in hand to watch nocturnal creatures emerge and colours glow after dark. One hour, fully guided.
Complimentary Equipment
Full snorkelling gear and a quick introduction are included for every guest, ready whenever curiosity strikes. Marked channels lead straight from the shore to the reef’s outer edge.
