Follow the currents of the day
Experiences
From the house reef to open water
Some mornings begin with turtles just off the sand, snorkel mask on before breakfast. Others call for a catamaran past the reef, or a windsurfer catching an afternoon breeze. Head out and the Maldives widens further still, whale sharks at dawn, a private sandbank by lunch, Champagne as the sun goes down. Divers can drop into Protected Marine Areas minutes from the jetty. Wherever the day leads, it starts here, at Noovilu Sports, and ends wherever the water carries you.
The House Reef
Few resorts in the Maldives can reach a reef this alive without a boat. At Kandolhu, it circles the island itself, close enough that snorkelling becomes part of the day rather than something you plan for.
Discover the Reef
Snorkelling
Brimming with Life
Slip beneath the surface and explore further, with snorkelling adventures across North Ari Atoll bringing colourful reefs, turtles, manta rays and remarkable marine encounters within reach.

Water Sports
Windsurf, Sail, or Simply Drift
Windsurfing, paddleboarding, catamaran sailing, and kayaking are yours whenever the mood takes you, right off the beach. For something faster, wakeboarding, kneeboarding, and guided excursions are ready too, all departing from Noovilu Sports.
More detail
Excursions
Manta Rays, Sunsets, Sandbanks
Charter a boat and go further, whether that means chasing Manta rays at dawn, fishing for wahoo and tuna, or a private sandbank with nothing on it but you. Every trip is led by our crew and shaped around you.
More detail
Diving
Deeper Into the Atoll
Just steps from your villa, the house reef offers hawksbill turtles, reef sharks, and eagle rays. Beyond it, Ari Atoll’s Protected Marine Areas, including Maaya Thila and Fish Head, are minutes away by boat.
