Wellbeing
Wellbeing comes naturally here
On an island this small, it doesn’t take long to change pace. Swim before breakfast. Walk barefoot. And when you want a little help switching off, Varu Spa is tucked into the island’s green heart, ready to bring you gently back into balance.
VARU Spa
Varu means energy in Dhivehi. At the heart of Kandolhu, our spa is a quiet place to restore yours – with thoughtful treatments, skilled hands, and time that belongs entirely to you.

Treatments
Feeling well here takes the shape you need. A massage to ease travel from your shoulders, a facial to restore some glow, or a full day drifting from one treatment to the next.

Varu Journeys
Stay with the feeling a little longer. Multi-day journeys give you more time to reset, restore, and carry it home with you.
Moments for Two
Varu Haven is a couples’ treatment made to share – a sea salt soak, a tailored massage, and a facial, each shaped around the two of you. It closes with a quiet ritual of connection, marking the moment as something worth remembering.
THE GYM
For days that call for a little more movement, a fully equipped gym sits quietly among the trees. Treadmills, free weights, and everything else you need, ready whenever the mood to move finds you.
