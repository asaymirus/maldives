Kandolhu Maldives is setting a new standard for island luxury with its latest enhancements, inviting travelers to discover a season of elevated experiences. From the newly refurbished Deluxe Ocean Pool Villas to exclusive dining collaborations with acclaimed international chefs, Kandolhu now offers a perfect blend of thoughtful design, world-class cuisine, and holistic wellbeing.
Rediscover the Magic of Kandolhu
The island’s Deluxe Ocean Pool Villas have been redesigned with comfort, relaxation, and understated luxury in mind. Spanning 120 sqm, each villa features a spacious bedroom, a light-filled bathroom with a deep oval bathtub overlooking the Indian Ocean, and a private deck with sun loungers, an umbrella, and a 23 sqm pool. For moments of pure serenity, a hammock suspended over the water offers breathtaking views while you unwind.
Exclusive Culinary Journeys
Kandolhu Maldives continues to impress with its exclusive chef collaborations, transforming island dining into unforgettable experiences.
3 October 2025
– Chef Bernd Bachofer presents a fusion of Japanese, Southeast Asian, and French haute cuisine, paired with award-winning New Zealand vintages from Schubert Wines. Chef Bachofer brings Michelin-starred expertise from Germany and Switzerland for this intimate evening.
23 November 2025
– Chef Justin and the Kandolhu team craft a 5-course Mediterranean dinner, perfectly complemented by Stonefish wines, with expert host Peter Papanikitas guiding guests through each course.
3 December 2025
– Michelin-starred Chef Theodor Falser presents modern interpretations of Italian classics for an intimate 14-guest dinner, paired with exceptional Italian wines. Each dish is a blend of innovation and tradition, offering a truly memorable culinary experience.
Claudio Stupan and Luciana Codiferro, Island Hosts at Kandolhu, share:
“Our vision has always been to create a space where guests feel both completely relaxed and deeply inspired. With refreshed villas and new culinary journeys ahead, we are proud to offer an experience that truly reflects the spirit of Kandolhu.”
