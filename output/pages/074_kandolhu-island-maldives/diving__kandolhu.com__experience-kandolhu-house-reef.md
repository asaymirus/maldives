An entire world, one breath away
Kandolhu house reef
A short swim from your villa is all it takes. The reef begins right where the shallows end.
Hawksbill turtles feed within reach of the shore, reef sharks move past without concern, and eagle rays cross overhead. Few reefs in the Maldives offer this much life this close to your villa.
A RING OF LIFE
Life gathers in the shelter of our house reef, a calm, coral ring with so much to reveal. Swim out from your villa and it’s simply there, turtles grazing at dawn, a reef shark passing by evening. Nothing to book, nothing to plan.

NIGHT SNORKELLING
After dark, the reef turns over its cast entirely. Guided night snorkels follow torchlight past nocturnal creatures most guests never see by day: moray eels out hunting, reef fish tucked into coral, the beam picking out movement you’d otherwise swim straight past.
STRAIGHT FROM THE SHORE
Dedicated snorkel channels lead from the beach to the reef’s outer edge, marked clearly around the island so you always know the way. No boat, no booking, just a short swim before the coral begins.
ABOVE AND BELOW
You don’t need to be in the water to know it’s there. Glance up from your deck, or from breakfast at The Market, and you’ll catch small waves breaking along the reef – a constant reminder of what surrounds you.
