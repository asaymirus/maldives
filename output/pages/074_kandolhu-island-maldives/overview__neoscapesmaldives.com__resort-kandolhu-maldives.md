DESCRIPTION
‘An island so perfectly-formed as Kandolhu is rare; with a pristine coral reef encircling the beautifully-designed contemporary villas’
Measuring just 200 metres by 150 metres, this bijou island is lush with wide sandy beaches surrounding emerald green vegetation. It is also home to one of the most pristine, colourful house reefs in the Maldives. Easily accessible from all around the island, you can find large table corals as well as many clownfish living amongst the sea anemones. Offering just 30 stylish villas both over the water and on the beach, the island is perfect for couples wanting some alone-time in paradise. The distinctive natural elements are perfectly combined with contemporary architecture and modern amenities. There is also an array of world-class dining venues serving international cuisine, including the distinctive Sea Grill restaurant surrounded by coral stone arches; plus a host of superb diving and water-sports activities in the splendid waters surrounding the island.

Highlights From The Resort
RESORT ACTIVITIES:
DIVING
–
Looking for some of the best Maldives diving? Located just a few minutes from some of the best dive sites in North and South Ari Atoll, Kandolhu is a favourite among Maldives diving resorts, offers three dives each day, two in the morning and one in the afternoon, on board a Maldivian dhoni. Night dives, half day and full day trips are also organized regularly.
WATER SPORTS
–
A wide range of non-motorised water sports are offered on Kandolhu ranging from windsurf, stand-up paddle boat, catamaran sailing, sail boat, kayaks and glass-bottomed kayaks.
SNORKELING
–
Snorkeling in the Maldives is a must and Kandolhu house reef offers one of the best snorkeling experience in this part of the country.
EXCURSIONS
–
Step off Kandolhu and explore our beautiful Maldivian surroundings by taking part in our excursions. A variety of specially tailored trips are available throughout the week, ranging from Big Game Fishing and Parasailing for those seeking thrills, to sunset cruises and snorkelling desert islands for those preferring a more relaxed ambiance.
Varu Spa – Let your senses unravel…
Situated in the heart of our spa resort Maldives, you will discover its hidden secret; that is Varu Spa.
Our beautiful couple spa in Maldives boasts 3 couples’ treatment rooms with private shower rooms, all equipped with the latest Gheriani beds, a pedicure and manicure room, and separate changing rooms and wet areas for men and women, each featuring a sauna, steam room, cold pool, rain showers and lockers for personal belongings. Prior to, or following your treatment, our relaxation terrace provides the perfect space to unwind in quietude of our tropical paradise.
The spa facilities also include a well-equipped gym to help you keep fit whilst on holiday.
