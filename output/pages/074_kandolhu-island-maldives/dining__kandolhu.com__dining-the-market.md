The Market
The Market sits open to the ocean. Breakfast comes from a small, considered spread and a full à la carte menu. From midday, everything is made to order, blending island flavours with international ones.
Mornings start light: fresh fruit, pastries, overnight oats, a little cheese and charcuterie, with fresh juices and coffee to match. It’s a small buffet by design, built to support the à la carte, not replace it, so a Maldivian breakfast of tuna curry and mashuni sits alongside a Kandolhu Muffin just as easily. By lunch, the kitchen turns to seared tuna salads and lighter plates, carrying through to an easy international spread at dinner. Sundays bring Maldivian Night, a set menu built around the island’s own flavours.
