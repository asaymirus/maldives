Sea Grill
Set right on the beach, Sea Grill opens as the sun goes down, with the day’s catch coming straight off an open flame.
Whole grouper, red snapper or yellowfin tuna come off the grill depending on the day’s catch, alongside seared scallops, lamb cutlets and jumbo prawns straight off the fire. Themed nights run on select nights during the week, each worth reserving a seat for.
