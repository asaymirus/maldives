Vilu Bar
Vilu Bar sits right on the sand, a thatched roof over cocktails, fine wines and tropical juices, with light bites on hand throughout the day. No reservation, no rush.
Settle onto the deck as the sand meets the sea, glasses catching the light as the day unfolds around you. Proper cocktail craft runs through every pour, several drinks named for the resident marine life, and many are made from sustainable ingredients other bars overlook. Classics are just as welcome; tell the bartender what you’re craving, and they’ll mix something to match.
