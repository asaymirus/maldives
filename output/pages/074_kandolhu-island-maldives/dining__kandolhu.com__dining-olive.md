Olive
Set on a raised deck with the ocean in view, Olive serves Mediterranean plates shaped by the Italian coast. The light plays on the water as the evening deepens.
Olive brings the feel of the Italian coast to the table – Mediterranean plates built around the day’s catch, fresh ingredients and considered technique. Small plates lead into pasta, then something more substantial, the courses arriving at a relaxed pace as the ocean darkens outside. As the sunset fades, the evening unfolds at an easy pace, with conversation continuing long after the last course.
