Local Island Visits
A local island visit involves exploring the nearby islands and their culture. Passengers can enjoy a boat ride to the island and then explore the local culture, cuisine, and traditions. These visits are perfect for anyone who wants to experience the Maldivian way of life and learn about the history and culture of Maldives.
