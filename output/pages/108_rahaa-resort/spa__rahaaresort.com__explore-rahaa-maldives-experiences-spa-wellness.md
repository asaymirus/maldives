Spa & Wellness
We are proud to offer an unparalleled spa and wellness experience at Rahaa. Our Rahaa Spa, staffed by expert therapists, provides a serene oasis to unwind and indulge in various rejuvenating treatments.

Spa
Massages
Coffee Salt Scrub
Restore your skin and improve your blood circulation. The antioxidant properties of the coffee help to treat inflamed and uneven skin tone leaves your skin fresh and smooth. The treatment is completed with a warm vitamin E oil body massage for the perfect healthy glow.
Suitable for all skin types.
Honey & Sugar Scrub
Gently refine and nourish your skin. The honey has anti-bacterial properties that can help the skin retain moisture. The treatment is completed with a warm coconut oil body massage for the ultimate relaxation.
Suitable for normal to dry skin.
Exfoliating Body Treatments
With salt & sugar paste infused with soothing essential oils; This treatment detoxifies, eliminates impurities, and relaxes the body. Be sure to soak yourself in our hot bath in your private en suite bathroom for 20-minutes prior.
Back, Neck & Shoulder Massage
A pressure massage that focuses on the build-up of stress and muscle tension in all areas around the neck, shoulders and across the back. This massage encourages relaxation, relieves stress, improves circulation and relax the muscles.
Couples Massage
Share a moment of well being together with a relaxing Swedish or therapeutic massage in a welcoming environment.
Aromatherapy
Add on aromatherapy to your massage to promote your well being and stimulate your senses. Essential oils are used to relax the nervous system while releasing stress and tension. Whether you want to rejuvenate or simply smell delicious; choose from relaxing lavender or uplifting lemongrass.
Foot Reflexology
Giving your whole body this incredible sensation of perfect harmony and equilibrium, this foot massage technique will improve circulation, reduce tensions and bring a revival feeling of lightness on the legs and feet.
Deep Massage
This deep tissue massage incorporates a technique applied with a fair amount of pressure and with slower movements than those used for a Swedish massage. Perfect for relieving chronic tension, contractions and knots.
Swedish Massage
A traditional massage to suit your individual needs. Choose from therapeutic or relaxing to ease muscle tension and relax the nervous system.
4 Hands Massage
A unique experience given by two therapists at the same time. All you need to put stress behind you and enjoy twice the relaxation.
