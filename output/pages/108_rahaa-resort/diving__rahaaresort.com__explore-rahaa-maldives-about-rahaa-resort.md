About Rahaa Resort
Welcome to Rahaa Resort – your ultimate escape to luxury, comfort, and nature combined. Tucked
away in the pristine Laamu Atoll, our 4-star resort is a hidden paradise where you can immerse
yourself in the natural beauty of the Maldives.
Deep into the Archipelago,
A new atoll for Tourism, an untouched Island
With crystal-clear waters, white sandy beaches, and lush vegetation, our resort is the perfect
destination for anyone looking to unwind, relax, and rejuvenate. Whether you’re planning a
romantic getaway, an adventurous expedition, or a fun-filled family vacation, Rahaa Resort has
everything you need to make your stay truly unforgettable.
At our resort, you'll experience the warm hospitality and attentive service of our friendly staff. From
the moment you step foot on our island, we’ll take care of every detail, ensuring that your stay with
us is nothing short of extraordinary.
With a range of luxurious accommodations to choose from, including our Lagoon Villas and Private
Beach Villas, you’ll find the perfect retreat to suit your needs. Each room is elegantly designed,
offering modern amenities and breath taking views of the surrounding ocean or lush greenery.
Adventures Await. Discover Maldives' Natural Beauty
One of the Biggest Lagoon in the Maldives & Longest House Reef
For those seeking adventure,
Our resort offers a range of thrilling water sports and activities,
including snorkelling, diving, windsurfing, and kayaking. Alternatively, you can choose to unwind and
relax at our spa, where you can indulge in a range of rejuvenating treatments and therapies.
And when it comes to dining, our resort offers a variety of culinary experiences, serving delicious
local and international cuisine. From casual beachside dining to fine dining under the stars, we have
something to suit every taste and occasion.
