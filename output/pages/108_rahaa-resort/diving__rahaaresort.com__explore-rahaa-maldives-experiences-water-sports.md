Water Sports
Our water sports offer endless fun and adventure for all ages. Glide across the crystal-clear waters on a paddle board or kayak, ride the waves on a boogie board or tube, and discover the colorful marine life while snorkeling or diving. From the exhilarating water skiing and wakeboarding to the relaxing glass bottom boat rides, there's something for everyone to enjoy.
Fun Tube
Splash on hard as the boat pulls your inflatable tube through the turquoise lagoon with our fun tubes. This high-speed water sport is perfect for families and thrill-seekers.

Windsurfing
With its shallow waters and blessed with tropical breeze throughout the year, Rahaa Resort is a hot destination to try out your windsurfing skills. Our skilled surfers will provide you with the necessary guidance and introduce you to the tricks and basics of windsurfing in the Maldives.

Canoeing, Kayaking
Looking for a way to explore Rahaa  on your own terms? Our Canoes & kayaks are the perfect option for independent adventurers. With our sit-on-top design, you can easily hop in and out of the warm, tropical waters. Rahaa Resort with an infinite lagoon, you can do it everyday and still there is more of our stunning blue lagoon untouched. This is a low-impact activity suitable for all ages, and we offer both double and single kayaks & canoes for your convenience.

Stand-up Paddle Boarding
Experience the tranquillity of the calm lagoons around the island with stand-up paddleboarding. Whether you opt for a lesson or choose to use our equipment, you'll spend a few peaceful hours spotting tropical sea life while improving your core strength and fitness.

One of the great things about stand-up paddleboarding is that it is accessible to people of all ages and skill levels. You don't need any experience to get started, and with a little practice, you can become proficient in no time. A fun sport to test your balance skills.

Snorkeling
Snorkelling is a fun and exciting way to explore the underwater world and witness the beauty of marine life. The Maldives is renowned for its crystal-clear waters, which provide excellent visibility for snorkelling. With the longest house reef in the Maldives, you'll have access to an incredible array of marine life just steps away from your accommodation.
