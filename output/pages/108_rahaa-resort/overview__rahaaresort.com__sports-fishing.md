Sport fishing is a popular activity that involves catching fish for sport rather than for food. Passengers can enjoy a boat ride while fishing for a variety of fish species, such as marlin, tuna, and mahi-mahi. These fishing trips are perfect for anyone who loves fishing or wants to try their hand at a new hobby
