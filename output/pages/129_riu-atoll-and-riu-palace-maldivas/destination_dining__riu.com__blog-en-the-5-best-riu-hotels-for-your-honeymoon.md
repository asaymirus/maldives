Your honeymoon is one of the most special trips of your life. At RIU we know that, and so we want to propose the
most romantic hotels for your honeymoon
. All of them meet the most important requirements for ensuring that you enjoy your
honeymoon
: a spectacular destination and the quality of RIU service.
Riu Palace Cabo San Lucas, Cabo San Lucas
The newly renovated Riu Palace Cabo San Lucas has several types of rooms that are perfect for enjoying your honeymoon. The new
Swim Out Junior Suites
include a small private pool with a terrace, an integrated lounge area and bathrobes. The hotel also has
suites with outdoor jacuzzis
.
Riu Palace Las Americas, Canc
ú
n
You’ll find our first
Mexican adults-only hotel
in Cancún. The Riu Palace Las Americas gives you all the special services of our Palace range, plus all the benefits of the
Adults-Only
concept, which include concierge service, international brands of liquors and a Premium wine list, for an extra charge.
Riu Palace Tres Islas, Fuerteventura
The north side of the island of Fuerteventura, in the Canary Islands, is the site of the Riu Palace Tres Islas. This hotel offers you a unique location on the spectacular
Corralejo beach
for a romantic walk. Enjoy dinner at its fusion restaurant
Krystal
while you take in the fascinating view from its terrace.
Riu Le Morne, Mauricio
If there’s one honeymoon destination par excellence, it’s Mauritius. We recommend the Hotel Riu Le Morne. Its privileged location at the foot of
Morne Brabant
, a world heritage site, and right on the beach make it the perfect resort for your honeymoon. Let yourself be pampered by our exceptional service and
24-hour All-Inclusive
concept.
Riu Palace Tropical Bay, Jamaica
Located on one of the largest and loveliest beaches of Jamaica, Negril, the Hotel Riu Palace Tropical Bay awaits you with everything you need to enjoy a romantic trip.
Room service 24 hours a day
, a Jamaican barbecue on the beach, a
spa with beauty treatments
… All of these amenities and much more for you to enjoy an unforgettable honeymoon.
This is just a sampling of all the hotels we offer you for your most romantic trip. Go to the
RIU
website to book your
honeymoon
and guarantee yourself an unforgettable experience in one of the world’s best destinations.
