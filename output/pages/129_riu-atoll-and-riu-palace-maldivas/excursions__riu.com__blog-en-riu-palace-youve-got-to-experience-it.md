Today we want to tell you a little bit more about our Riu Palace line. This is an exclusive collection of the most sophisticated
RIU hotels
, where you’ll find excellent and much more personal service. Our guests really feel like royalty in them.
These hotels are right on the beach. Each and every one has a location that can only be considered… spectacular!
We want you to have a unique, unforgettable, and luxurious experience. So service is one of their strongest points; you deserve pleasant and friendly treatment, and we work hard to make sure you get it. The main thing is for you to feel comfortable and relaxed. Our spa and wellness centres will help you achieve this:
forget about the world in the sauna; disconnect from everything in the jacuzzi; sign up for any treatment you feel like and let us give you a great massage
. You won’t want to return to reality…
If you’re a foodie, you’re going to love the restaurants at these hotels.
We serve fresh, high quality ingredients that our chefs prepare just for you
. Plus, you’ll be able to enjoy thematic nights at the buffet and a very interesting gastronomic offer. At RIU, we like variety. You can choose a different type of restaurant every night: Japanese, Mexican, Italian, a steakhouse, Asian, and gourmet.
There’s one thing we all love when we’re on holiday… luxuriating in a pool!
And if it’s one of these pools, all the better. Sunbathe in a lounge chair, sip your favourite cocktail, enjoy incredible views, have fun with our activity leaders… all without ever leaving the water.
