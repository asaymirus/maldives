RIU Land
is the kids club at
RIU Hotels & Resorts
, available at the chain’s family-friendly hotels in beach destinations. The program is led by Calú, the sea turtle that serves as mascot, and runs activities and themed days for young guests, organized by a team of kids club staff.
How are activities organized?
RIU Land favors physical, outdoor, group activities over more sedentary options like arts and crafts or board games: pool and beach games, drawing, singing, face painting, and short choreographies or skits where the kids take center stage.
The club splits children into different age groups, with activities tailored to each age range.
Themed days: Pirate Day and other special events
The program runs themed days, including Pirate Day, with challenges and a treasure hunt, along with other days that combine pool games with group sports and individual challenges. Also includes Ecoland and Magic Circus Day, focused on environmental awareness and circus arts, respectively.
Calú, RIU Land ‘s mascot
Calú is the sea turtle that represents RIU Land and joins children in games, choreographies, and short skits organized by the club’s staff.
Themed pools and kids’ areas
Water games, slides, and outdoor play areas themed around pirate ships, fish, water-spraying mushrooms, and dragons are available at select hotels offering the program.

Which hotels offer RIU Land?
RIU Land is available at all of the chain’s family-friendly hotels.
If you’re planning a family trip,
you can check availability when choosing your hotel.
Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)
What age can children join RIU Land?
The club is designed for children ages 4 to 12, with activities suited to each age group.
Who is Calú?
Calú is the sea turtle mascot of RIU Land, who joins the club’s staff in games and activities.
Is RIU Land available at every hotel in the chain?
No, it’s available at the chain’s family-friendly hotels. We recommend checking the official website before booking.
What kind of activities are most common at RIU Land?
The program favors physical, outdoor, group activities — pool and beach games, sports, and themed days — over more sedentary options like arts and crafts or board games.
