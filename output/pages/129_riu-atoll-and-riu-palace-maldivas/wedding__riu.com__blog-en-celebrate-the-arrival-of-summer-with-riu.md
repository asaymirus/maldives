We all know that exciting feeling when summer arrives. Days at the beach that end with a sunset, sand on your feet, salt on your skin, the smell of sun cream and all those sensations that mean it’s the best time of the year. And of course the start of our long-awaited holiday.
Still haven’t made any plans for this summer? As you know,
RIU
has a wide range of beachfront hotels ideal for enjoying a summer vacation with friends, your partner or your family! But today we want to show you what a summer day with RIU is like and all the plans you can make with us to tackle the hottest days.
A summer day with RIU
Start your day off by recharging your batteries in the main restaurant. Here you will find a large
buffet
with a wide range of options for your breakfast, like juices of all flavours, fruits, toast, and there is always a corner containing products local to the destination where you are staying.  After this, you can go for a walk along the beach in front of your hotel, stroll along and enjoy the cool breeze and the water at your feet.  Next, you can go to the pool and get a tan while lying back on a sun lounger, or who knows, there might be an
infinity pool
from which you can bask in the wonderful views that the destination has to offer, as you immerse yourself in them.
Riu Palace Baja California, Mexico.
Or just take a quick dip in one of the hotel’s pools. Jump right in!
RiuFit
When you arrive, don’t forget to log into the hotel’s app, to check out the entertainment activities you can enjoy during your stay. Today we are going to talk to you about the most summery ones! In our hotel pools you can always do
aquagym
with one of our instructors and move your body to the rhythm of the music while you cool off.
Riu Palace Boavista, Cape Verde.
Also, depending on the destination, you can do activities like
paddle surfing
,
snorkelling, diving, kayaking, windsurfing
and all those sports that you’ve always wanted to try. All this is thanks to our partner Scuba Caribe. We guarantee you refreshing fun!
Riu Atoll, Maldives
Splash Water World
And there’s more! In some of our destinations you can find our famous Splash Water World, a park full of slides where you can have the time of your life. Haven’t you tried it yet? It’s the perfect place to make your hot afternoons more fun with the kids… or even without them!
Splash Water World, Riu Dunamar, Mexico .
Sunset with RIU
Now, it’s time to put the finishing touch to a summer day at your RIU hotel. We recommend that before dinner you enjoy a tasty cocktail in one of the hotel’s bars while you watch the sunset. The perfect way to round off a summer day with RIU.
Riu Party
If you’ve still got enough energy left, you can dance and enjoy the rhythms of the best DJs at the now famous
Riu Parties
, some of which include a pool!
What do you think of our proposal? If you are still not sure what to do this summer, come and cool off with RIU. We’ll be waiting for you!
