Come experience the exciting Splash Water World at the Hotel Riu Republica
19 December, 2017
Share

Hotels
»
In November, the new wing of the Hotel Riu Republica was unveiled, following completion of its enlargement. Besides adding new rooms, swimming pools and restaurants, the hotel finally has its own Splash Water World, a water park where youngsters (and the young at heart) can enjoy different types of slides, pools for cooling off and lounge chairs for sunbathing.
The hotels in our resort in Punta Cana (the Riu Palace Punta Cana, Riu Palace Macao, Riu Palace Bavaro, Riu Naiboa and the ClubHotel Riu Bambu) already had their own water park. Now people who love water parks have another reason to stay at the Riu Republica.
Splash Water World includes all types of slides. You’ll get an authentic adrenaline rush when you whoosh down the Kamikaze from heights of several metres, or the free-fall slide for a unique sensation. Another option is the Body Vortex, a tube slide that shoots you towards the pool. There’s nothing like it!
We hope you come and try these slides and see the rest of Splash Water World. The
enlarged
Riu Republica, with its 300 new rooms, three new restaurants and water park awaits you. Don’t hesitate,
book now
and enjoy Punta Cana with us. See you soon!
