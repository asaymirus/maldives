Getting Here
On the far North East border of Lhaviyani Atoll, Fushifaru Maldives is a 35-minute breath-taking seaplane flight away from Velana International Airport. Your journey to Fushifaru Maldives starts with views of azure blue waters of the Indian Ocean and over remote islands.
Upon your arrival, our Fushifaru Airport host will be there to assist you with your luggage and guide you to the appropriate terminal for your journey to the resort. If you are traveling by seaplane, you will be taken from the main airport terminal on a 5-minute bus ride to the TMA Seaplane Terminal for check-in and boarding.
Alternatively, if you are traveling via domestic flight, our airport representative will assist you with check-in at the Maldivian Airlines counter and escort you to the Moonimaa Lounge at the Domestic Terminal. Your flight to Madivaru Island will take approximately 35 minutes. Upon arrival, our resident host will welcome you at the terminal and accompany you to the speedboat waiting at the island harbour for a 15-minute ride to Fushifaru.
Arrival Process
Upon your arrival, our Fushifaru Airport host will be there to assist you with your luggage and guide you to the appropriate terminal for your journey to the resort. If you are traveling by seaplane, you will be taken from the main airport terminal on a 5-minute bus ride to the TMA Seaplane Terminal for check-in and boarding. Alternatively, if you are traveling via domestic flight, our airport representative will assist you with check-in at the Maldivian Airlines counter and escort you to the Moonimaa Lounge at the Domestic Terminal, where you can relax before your flight. The domestic flight to Madivaru Island, the nearest domestic airport to Fushifaru, takes approximately 35 minutes. Upon arrival at Madivaru, our resident host will greet you at the terminal and escort you to the speedboat waiting at the island harbour. From there, it’s a 15-minute boat ride across the turquoise waters to Fushifaru.
Departure Process
Your Resident Host will share your departure details on the evening prior to your departure day. To ensure a smooth and stress-free transfer, we kindly request all guests to visit the Reception the night before departure to review and settle any outstanding bills. This helps avoid last-minute delays and allows for a seamless journey from the island to the airport. On the day of departure, our Airport Host will meet and assist you at the Velana International Airport and guide you to your international departure gate.
