This Easter, trade your city backdrop for soft white sands and turquoise waters—Fushifaru Maldives is inviting families to celebrate the season in true island style. Nestled in a pristine corner of the Indian Ocean, this boutique resort is transforming into a joyful playground, perfect for guests of all ages to reconnect, relax, and revel in the Easter spirit.
From sunrise to sunset, every moment at Fushifaru is infused with wonder and warmth. The day kicks off with a vibrant Easter breakfast where guests can indulge in seasonal delights while welcoming a special visitor—the Easter Bunny himself. Expect laughter, wide-eyed wonder, and plenty of photo-worthy moments.
Children will love getting hands-on with egg painting sessions, crafting colorful memories to take home. Later, the whole family is invited to the island’s Easter Pool Party—a lively mix of music, games, and refreshing poolside treats, all set beside the resort’s sparkling infinity pool.
While the adults unwind, young guests can enjoy a little luxury of their own at the Egg-citing Kids’ Spa. Specially curated for little ones, this playful wellness experience blends pampering with fun—because even the smallest travelers deserve a taste of island indulgence.
The Easter magic continues with a classic island tradition: the Easter Egg Hunt. Little adventurers will explore hidden corners of the island in search of sweet surprises, creating memories that will last long after the holiday ends. As the sun dips below the horizon, guests can gather for an open-air Easter Movie Night, complete with popcorn and comfy bean bags under a starry sky.
To round out the celebrations, Fanihandi Bar sets the stage for an upbeat Easter Disco Party—a final, festive send-off filled with dancing, music, and island vibes.
Whether you’re traveling with kids, as a couple, or with friends, Fushifaru’s Easter celebration is the perfect blend of relaxation and play, all in one of the world’s most beautiful destinations.
