Coral Reef Restoration in the Maldives: A Hopeful Future at Fushifaru
Date: 2024-02-15
The
coral reefs of the Maldives
are at the heart of the country’s extraordinary natural beauty.
Beneath the turquoise waters surrounding our islands lies an ecosystem that supports an incredible diversity of marine life, while also helping protect islands and coastlines.
At
Fushifaru Maldives
, located in the pristine
Lhaviyani Atoll
, protecting this fragile underwater world is an important part of our commitment to sustainability. In 2020, we launched our
Coral Propagation Project
with the aim of supporting coral reef restoration and helping regenerate areas of reef surrounding the island.
Our Coral Propagation Project
With the support of our dive team and in-house marine biologist, broken coral fragments were carefully attached to specially designed coral frames before being placed on the ocean floor within Fushifaru’s lagoon.
Rather than allowing naturally broken coral fragments to disappear beneath the sand, coral propagation gives them an opportunity to establish themselves, grow and eventually contribute to a healthier reef ecosystem.
Over the years, our coral frames have continued to develop, becoming home to growing corals and attracting marine life around the lagoon.
Increasing Coral Biodiversity at Fushifaru
One of the most encouraging developments has been the increased diversity surrounding our coral restoration area.
According to our marine biology team, the project has helped introduce different coral species and phenotypes into the lagoon, encouraging a more varied ecosystem.
“We have increased the genetic variability up to 60% in our lagoon, and the biodiversity is also increasing,” explains Natalia Fernández Cabrera, our in-house marine biologist, Marine Zoologist, Exotic Vet Technician and Diving Instructor.
Species introduced through the project have included
Montipora capricornis
and
Acropora muricata
, adding greater variety to the reef environment surrounding Fushifaru.
Natalia explains that introducing different coral species alongside existing varieties can help create new colonies and contribute to a more diverse reef ecosystem.
Why Coral Reef Restoration Matters in the Maldives
Coral reefs are among the world’s most important marine ecosystems.
In the Maldives, they provide habitats for countless species of fish and marine life while also contributing to the natural protection of our islands.
However, coral reefs around the world face growing pressure from factors including
rising ocean temperatures, coral bleaching, pollution and other environmental changes
.
Coral propagation cannot solve these challenges alone, but carefully managed restoration projects can help damaged reef areas recover while supporting biodiversity and creating greater awareness about the importance of marine conservation.
At Fushifaru, our coral frames offer a living example of how small, continued actions can contribute to the wider protection of the marine environment.
Discovering the Coral Frames at Fushifaru
The project has also become an opportunity for our guests to learn more about
marine conservation in the Maldives
.
Guests can snorkel around the coral frames and observe their development while discovering the extraordinary underwater world of Lhaviyani Atoll.
Seeing coral restoration first-hand can create a much deeper understanding of how delicate these ecosystems are—and why protecting them matters.
For many guests, a snorkelling experience becomes more than simply spotting colourful fish. It becomes an opportunity to understand the reef, its role within the Maldivian environment and the importance of preserving it for future generations.
Creating a More Sustainable Future for Our Reefs
Several years after the launch of our Coral Propagation Project, watching the coral frames continue to grow gives us hope for the future.
The project reflects our wider commitment to protecting the environment surrounding Fushifaru and demonstrates what can be achieved through consistent conservation efforts, education and care for the ocean.
There is still much more work to be done to protect the world’s coral reefs. However, every healthy coral colony, every restored section of reef and every person inspired to care for the ocean represents progress.
At
Fushifaru Maldives
, we believe we all have a role to play in protecting the extraordinary environment that makes the Maldives so special.
Together, we can help create a more hopeful future for our coral reefs.
