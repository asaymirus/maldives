Scuba Diving
Plunge into the crystal-clear waters of the Indian Ocean and uncover an underwater haven teeming with fish, turtles, coral reefs, manta rays, dolphins, white-tip sharks, and much more! Craft your unique day at sea with personalised boat charters for fishing, snorkelling, and diving, all guided by our Resident Marine Biologist.
Watersports
Dive into adventure with our expert instructors offering insider tips on water-skiing, kayaking, catamaran sailing, jet skiing, parasailing, kite surfing, sea bobbing, windsurfing, or earn your PADI certification for scuba diving. An exhilarating experience awaits you!
Excursions
Embark on a fascinating snorkelling excursions and sunset cruises to find and swim with sea turtles and marvel at the dolphins. Our knowledgeable guides will take you to the best spots, ensuring a memorable experiences.
