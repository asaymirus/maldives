Weddings & Celebrations
Feeling fantastic at Fushifaru
Say “I do” at an intimate beachfront ceremony at sunset or on a crystal-clear turquoise sandbank. Allow our dedicated Wedding Planner to plan your dream wedding in an exotic locale and capture every treasured moment from the beginning to the end.
Traditional Maldivian Wedding
Make your beach wedding turn into reality and wed in traditional Maldivian style, for the ultimate dream come true! With Boduberu drummers escorting you and your loved one down the white sanded aisle, enjoy the turquoise Indian ocean in the background. The wedding is topped off by having a local magistrate (Gaazee) preside over the ceremony, all of which is done in the local Dhivehi dialect.
