The St. Regis Maldives to Host Celebrated Chef Jolly for Exclusive Culinary Experience
This mid-October to mid-December 2025
, the turquoise waters of the Dhaalu Atoll will set the stage for a remarkable gastronomic event as
The St. Regis Maldives Vommuli Resort
welcomes celebrated Chef
Surjan Singh
, affectionately known as
Chef Jolly
, for a
two-month pop-up residency
. Coinciding with the festive spirit of
Diwali
, this immersive experience will blend the richness of Indian culinary tradition with contemporary flair in an unforgettable island setting.
A beloved figure from
MasterChef India
and a veteran with over 30 years in the culinary world,
Chef Jolly
is renowned for infusing royal Indian heritage into approachable, soul-satisfying dishes. For this exclusive collaboration, he has curated a vibrant menu inspired by his personal journey, global travels, and the diverse regional flavors of the Indian subcontinent.
Signature highlights include:
Lamb Ghee Roast
served on
Malabar Bread Tacos
Cafreal Tiger Prawns
His iconic
Butter Chicken
These dishes will be accompanied by
fragrant biryanis
,
artisanal breads
,
toasted spices
, and
house-made chutneys
—each plated to evoke both nostalgia and culinary discovery. The experience is further elevated by a
bespoke cocktail menu
, featuring Indian-inspired creations such as
The Great Indian
and
Imli Margarita
, along with a thoughtfully curated selection of wines and low-ABV options.
“Food is memory. It’s emotion. It’s a journey,”
says Chef Jolly.
“Bringing the vibrancy and soul of Indian cuisine to The St. Regis Maldives is a true privilege. Each dish tells a story—of heritage, creativity, and joy.”
As part of this exclusive culinary celebration,
Marriott Bonvoy members
can bid on a once-in-a-lifetime
Marriott Bonvoy Moments
package. The offering includes:
A
five-night stay
for two in an
Overwater Villa with Pool
(October 19–24)
Daily breakfast
at
Alba
A
private six-course wine-paired dinner
hosted by Chef Jolly
A
curated wellness experience
, including a
signature massage
and the iconic
Blue Hole hydrotherapy journey
at the
Iridium Spa
Round-trip seaplane transfers
from
Malé International Airport
Located on a secluded private island,
The St. Regis Maldives Vommuli Resort
is the epitome of barefoot luxury and refined elegance. Its
77 expansive villas
, offering sweeping ocean or jungle views, are complemented by
personalized St. Regis Butler Service
. Guests can unwind at the award-winning
Iridium Spa
or embark on a yachting adventure aboard the resort’s private
Azimut Flybridge yacht
For more information, visit
maldivesexclusive.com
