This November, The St. Regis Maldives Vommuli Resort is introducing something truly special: The Tastemaker Series. Designed as a celebration of culture, flavor, and creativity, the program brings together globally recognized chefs, mixologists, and wellness leaders to create unforgettable moments in one of the most beautiful corners of the world.
Culinary Highlights
The series begins with some of the biggest names in international cuisine. Chef Jolly will bring a refined take on traditional Indian dishes, while Chef Alexander Herrmann, one of Germany’s most acclaimed two-Michelin-starred chefs, will showcase a distinctive approach to modern European gastronomy. December welcomes Chef Gregory Doyen, a French pastry master known for desserts that are as striking to look at as they are to taste.
Wine Journeys
Wine lovers will also have the chance to explore exceptional vintages in intimate dinners. Ilaria Felluga, sixth-generation winemaker of the famed Marco Felluga and Russiz Superiore estates, will guide guests through the rich flavors of Collio. Later, François Labet of Burgundy’s historic Château de la Tour will share insights from one of the world’s most iconic wine regions.
Beyond the Kitchen
The Tastemaker Series isn’t limited to dining. Guests can join celebrity trainer Jono Castano for personalized fitness sessions, explore Pilates programs with Tash & Andre, or discover holistic therapies with Tricia Tee, Asia’s only certified Seifu practitioner.
Evenings Reimagined
When the sun sets, The Whale Bar will come alive with mixology residencies from two of the world’s most celebrated bars: London’s Satan’s Whiskers and Mexico City’s Licorería Limantour. Each will bring a signature style of cocktail-making, adding even more vibrance to island nights.
The Setting
Set on its own private island, The St. Regis Maldives Vommuli Resort is known for its striking blend of tropical modernism and Maldivian heritage. Its 77 villas-surrounded by either ocean or jungle-offer complete seclusion, paired with the legendary St. Regis Butler Service. From the award-winning Iridium Spa to private yacht adventures, every detail has been designed to balance indulgence with discovery.
This series is more than a calendar of events-it’s a chance to experience the Maldives through the eyes of global tastemakers, while enjoying the understated luxury of one of the world’s most exclusive resorts.
