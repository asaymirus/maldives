DESCRIPTION
Tucked away, a distant private island reachable by seaplane invites guests to uncover the most luxurious resort in the Maldives. Combining contemporary design and local craftsmanship with a profound appreciation for the ecological environment, there is no other destination quite like this exquisite beach resort on Vommuli Island.
The resort boasts the largest 3-bedroom over-water villa in the Maldives, the John Jacob Astor Estate. Expansive private terraces leading to pristine beaches and infinity pools with the Indian Ocean as a tranquil backdrop inspire moments of relaxation.
Savor epicurean delights from all over the world in any of the six restaurants and bars—each inspired by authentic Italian, Middle Eastern and Asian flavors served in buffet, a la carte or tapas style, in addition to an extensive wine cellar. Restore your well-being with custom-made treatments at the over-water Iridium Spa or maintain your workout regime at Vommuli House. At this 5-star Luxury Resort, the St. Regis Maldives Vommuli, impeccable service meets effortless luxury.
Ecologically conscious in design and fulfilling our legacy of unparalleled service, the 77 visionary accommodations — including 33 on-land and 44 overwater villas — offer an array of impeccable amenities. Each offers unfettered views of the Indian Ocean or the island’s lush tropical gardens from exquisite private terraces with sparkling pools. State-of-the-art entertainment includes a Bang & Olufsen BeoVision television and entertainment system while complimentary wireless High Speed Internet Access ensures effortless communications.
Enjoy a peaceful night’s rest in the crisp linens of our pillow-top beds. Generous bathrooms with glass-enclosed rainforest showers and separate deep soaking tubs ensure a tranquil retreat. Each stay is elevated by pampering Remède bath amenities as well as plush bathrobes and slippers.
Families enjoy distinctive welcome amenities and turn-down rituals. Providing discreet, bespoke service, the St. Regis Butlers are at the ready day or night to fulfill any request.

Exclusive Private Dining Experiences
Whether you want an elevated level of intimacy to compliment your honeymoon experience, a delightful private setting to propose to your special someone or an extra touch of luxury added to your dining experience, our refined private dining arrangements creates timeless memories shared between you and your loved one
Crust
Revealing captivating views of our resort pool and the sparkling Indian Ocean, Crust, our family-friendly pizzeria, is defined by its breezy, inviting ambience.  Thin-crust pizzas with inspired toppings like tandoori chicken or Maldivian tuna are cooked to perfection in the outdoor pizza oven, and sandwiches and snacks are complemented by cold draft beers and fresh juices.
Featuring a patio-style setting with sand beneath guests’ feet, our beachfront restaurant boasts an outdoor pizza oven, where our dedicated staff are at the ready to prepare traditional thin-crust pizzas, sandwiches, and snacks. Guests may also build their own pies with inventive toppings, such as Maldivian tuna, tandoori chicken, and rocket leaves. Selections from our carefully curated menu of refreshing juices and draft beers complement each meal.
The Whale Bar
At the Whale Bar, our overwater lounge, we invite guests to partake of delectable tapas and locally sourced seafood as well as hand-selected wines and spirits while enjoying panoramic views of the Indian Ocean and our private coral reef.
A tour de force of marine-inspired architecture, The Whale Bar’s distinctive shape recalls the majestic whale shark. This whimsical but elegant retreat is elevated by light oak interiors, a refined bar, and a softly lit neutral palette. The venue’s cynosure, our traditional St. Regis mural, adorns the ceiling. A pyrographic masterpiece on wood by renowned artist Maya Burman, the installation depicts a father and son discovering a paradise of corals, fish, and plankton—a celebration of the Maldives’ vibrant sea life. Our distinctive lounge opens directly onto an open-air terrace, where plush sofas and armchairs invite guests to admire seductive views of the sea and Vommuli Island’s immaculate reef.
Our experienced team of chefs work tirelessly to offer flavorful à la carte dishes as well as three- and five-course set menus. Specialties include garlic-marinated king prawns with white wine sauce and smoked paprika as well as salted cod with capsicum confit and roasted aubergine. Designed to please connoisseurs and dilettantes alike, our worldly array of cocktails includes the Island Mary, our signature interpretation of The St. Regis Bloody Mary tradition. Allow our knowledgeable sommeliers to recommend selections from our extensive assemblage of traditional and exclusive vintages. Iconic Meduse hookahs are also available.

Boasting a tranquil setting of white oak and sleek gray décor, the beautifully appointed dining space is illuminated by contemporary black iron chandeliers and romantic recessed lighting. Against the colorful tile backdrops distinguishing each of our three distinctive show kitchens, our experienced chefs showcase their culinary talents. Meanwhile, guests can enjoy unfettered views of azure sky and sea framed by tropical greenery and white-sand beaches from our partially enclosed dining room and open-air terrace.
At our Indian and Pakistani grill, delectable curries and biryanis are accompanied by fresh naan and chapati from our traditional tandoor. Our Japanese bar offers tantalizing sushi and sashimi as well as charcoal-grilled yakitori elevated by an extensive collection of renowned sakes. Our seasonal Asian kitchen features a curated selection of Korean, Sichuan, and Cantonese dishes prepared from the freshest ingredients, including dim sum, prawn dumplings, and roasted duck paired with traditional sakes and sojus.

Alba
Delectable cuisine meets uncompromising service at our signature restaurant, Alba. Our all-day open kitchen promises indelible Italian cuisine accompanied by breathtaking views of the resort’s pristine beachfront and shimmering pool from our picturesque terrace and dining room.
White oak floors and a natural palette of ivory and sand enhance the dining room’s airy atmosphere while ample natural light streams in through floor-to-ceiling windows framed in elegant black iron. The space is further distinguished by a lively central show kitchen, a separate island kitchen, and a refined bar encased in natural oak.
Our executive chef, Ayyoub Salameh, evinces his culinary prowess by reinterpreting traditional Italian dishes. Breakfast specialties include poached egg with orange hollandaise and black truffles; inspired appetizers and entrées such as foie gras terrine, white asparagus with fried polenta, and a variety of risottos and handmade pastas are followed by tempting desserts, including gelato and spice cake.
Enticing vegetarian and gluten-free selections are also available, and a children’s menu features dishes favored by younger palates, such as French toast and deli sandwiches.
Alba’s knowledgeable sommelier is pleased to recommend top-shelf labels from our collection of renowned vintages.

Scuba Diving
The St. Regis Maldives Resort, situated on the exclusive island of Vommuli, is the ideal place for diving year-round. Vommuli boasts a crystal clear turquoise lagoon and a private house reef with bountiful marine life – an impeccable setting for a dive resort.Surround yourself with all the beauty the Indian Ocean has to offer through our Professional Association of Diving Instructors (PADI) recognized Vommuli Dive & Water Sports Centre. The centre offers an extensive array of scuba diving packages dedicated to tailoring to all your personal diving needs.
Private Boat Charters
Take your loved ones to indulge in a truly luxurious escape by private boat. Craft your ideal Maldivian boat trip and create timeless memories while island hopping or rejoice over a glass of Champagne as you bathe in the vibrant hues of sunset.
Parasailing Boat
Glide across the crisp Maldivian air as you are tethered to a parasailing boat and enjoy a picturesque aerial vista of Dhaalu Atoll.
Children’s Club
For guests travelling to the Maldives with kids, our Children’s Club offers a variety of enriching and engaging activities for your children to enjoy, with our compliments.
