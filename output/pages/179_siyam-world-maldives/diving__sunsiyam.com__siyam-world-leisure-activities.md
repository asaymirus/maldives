Maldives Adventures
A world-class spa, wellness & sports facilities along with some of the region’s best dive sites just meters away, makes Siyam World, Maldives truly an island of infinite choices, fun and memorable escapades
Watersports
Jet ski across the lagoon, carve up the waves on a wakeboard, or paddle out just to see how far the reef stretches. However you like to move on water, the lagoon's ready when you are.
Excursions
Consider this your permission slip to wander. From sunset sails aboard a traditional dhoni to island-hopping adventures, our world of possibilities was made for saying yes.
Recreation
It’s all about endless choices, fun activities and unforgettable memories at Siyam World Maldives.
Diving
Trade the sun loungers for scuba fins and go where the real show is. Beginner or seasoned bubble-blower, one thing's certain, you haven't really done the Maldives until you've seen it from below the surface.
