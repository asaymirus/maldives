Diving at Siyam World
Somewhere below the surface of Noonu Atoll, a whole other world is waiting. And it's more spectacular than anything on land. Our SSI-certified dive centre takes first-timers and seasoned pros alike into the blue, from sun-drenched reef dives to moonlit descents where the ocean turns into something otherworldly. Glide past coral gardens, drift with reef sharks, and let a marine biologist fill you in on who else calls these waters home. Go with the group, or keep it just for you. Either way, one thing's certain: you haven't really seen the Maldives until you've dived beneath it.
