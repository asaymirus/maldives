Siyam World Must-Do
Pleasure, leisure, and memories to treasure — you can have it all.
From relaxing to heart-pounding, here is a rundown of our favourite activities Sun Siyam has to offer for every guest mindset.
The Maldives’ only Horse Ranch
Did you know Siyam World is home to the Maldives’ only horse ranch? Riding a horse here is the last thing you’d expect on a tropical island, yet it’s one of the resort’s most unique experiences. Meet their stunning Marwari horses—an elegant and rare breed easily recognized by their curved, upright ears—and enjoy an unforgettable ride along the beach at sunrise or sunset.
Jet Car Ride
Slip into the driver’s seat of our sleek jet car and carve your way through turquoise waters in true Siyam World style. With every burst of speed and spray of sea breeze, the lagoon becomes your open playground.
Pool Parties
Siyam World knows how to throw a good party. Dive into the ultimate poolside vibe at Mint, our adults-only bar, where mai tais and piña coladas flow and the DJ keeps the energy alive. Celebrate Siyam World style and let the sun, music, and laughter carry you through a few unforgettable hours.
Rent-a-Moke
Another first in the Maldives—Siyam World guests can rent a Moke for the duration of their stay and explore the island in style. These visually striking electric cars are eco-friendly, carbon-free, and a sun-soaked twist on the classic beach cruiser. The iconic Moke design makes every island drive effortlessly cool.
Sunset Meditation
As the sun dips behind the water villas and the beach, painting the horizon in a kaleidoscope of colors, join us to pause, breathe, and watch the sky transform in all its island glory.
Kidz Carnival
Mark your calendars for the Kidz Carnival at Siyam World happening twice a month at Tempo Beach from 16:00 to 17:30. We are rolling out the red carpet for all the little rockstars out there. Get ready for a blast!
Asana Yoga
Harmonize mind and body through yoga, combining gentle postures and mindful breathing to nurture both mental and physical well-being. Join fellow participants and awaken your inner balance, connecting with the conscious seer within amidst the island’s serene surroundings.
Padel Tennis
Enjoy an exciting game at Siyam World's padel tennis court. Perfect for beginners and experience players alike, have fun with this social and energetic activity ideal for friendly matches with family and friends or to keep up with the routine even on holiday!
Gaadiya
Located at various places on the island with a twist of our local food delicacies and mouthwatering snacks of both sweet and savory, including coconuts, soft drinks and tropical fruits, that are sure to leave you wanting for more.
Speed Racer’s Circuit
It's time to buckle up, hold on tight, and experience the thrill of a lifetime at Siyam World Maldives' Electric Go-Kart Track. Book your race now and get ready to unleash your inner speed champion!
Ultimate Football Camps
Refine your skills and ignite your passion with our weekly 3-day football camps in paradise. Led by Maldivian football legend and former national star Anwar Abdul Gani, campers will sharpen their techniques, improve their game, and experience the thrill of football through fun, engaging sessions. Joining him is former Maldives national team captain and renowned athlete Assad Abdul Ghani.
Siyam Water World
The Maldives' biggest floating water park. It's here, and it comes with slippery slides, parkours, obstacles, trampolines, and climbing walls.

LEISURE ACTIVITIES
From night dives to freediving, from jetskiing to sunset cruises, we have it all. Truly.
Watersports
Jet ski across the lagoon, carve up the waves on a wakeboard, or paddle out just to see how far the reef stretches. However you like to move on water, the lagoon's ready when you are.
Excursions
Consider this your permission slip to wander. From sunset sails aboard a traditional dhoni to island-hopping adventures, our world of possibilities was made for saying yes.
Recreation
It’s all about endless choices, fun activities and unforgettable memories at Siyam World Maldives.
Diving
Trade the sun loungers for scuba fins and go where the real show is. Beginner or seasoned bubble-blower, one thing's certain, you haven't really done the Maldives until you've seen it from below the surface.
