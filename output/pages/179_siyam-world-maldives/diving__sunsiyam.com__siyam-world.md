Siyam World
Must-Do
Siyam World Horse Ranch
Did you know Siyam World is home to the Maldives’ only horse ranch? Riding a horse here is the last thing you’d expect on a tropical island, yet it’s one of the resort’s most unique experiences. Meet their stunning Marwari horses—an elegant and rare breed easily recognized by their curved, upright ears—and enjoy an unforgettable ride along the beach at sunrise or sunset.
Thrilling Jet Car
Slip into the driver’s seat of our sleek jet car and carve your way through turquoise waters in true Siyam World style. With every burst of speed and spray of sea breeze, the lagoon becomes your open playground.
Pool Parties
Siyam World knows how to throw a good party. Dive into the ultimate poolside vibe at Mint, our adults-only bar, where mai tais and piña coladas flow and the DJ keeps the energy alive. Celebrate Siyam World style and let the sun, music, and laughter carry you through a few unforgettable hours.
Rent-a-Moke
Another first in the Maldives—Siyam World guests can rent a Moke for the duration of their stay and explore the island in style. These visually striking electric cars are eco-friendly, carbon-free, and a sun-soaked twist on the classic beach cruiser. The iconic Moke design makes every island drive effortlessly cool.
Go-Kart
It's time to buckle up, hold on tight, and experience the thrill of a lifetime at Siyam World Maldives' Electric Go-Kart Track. Book your race now and get ready to unleash your inner speed champion!
Football Camps
Refine your skills and ignite your passion with our weekly 3-day football camps in paradise. Led by Maldivian football legend and former national star Anwar Abdul Gani, campers will sharpen their techniques, improve their game, and experience the thrill of football through fun, engaging sessions. Joining him is former Maldives national team captain and renowned athlete Assad Abdul Ghani.
Siyam Water World
The Maldives' biggest floating water park. It's here, and it comes with slippery slides, parkours, obstacles, trampolines, and climbing walls.
Kidz Carnival
Mark your calendars for the Kidz Carnival at Siyam World happening twice a month at Tempo Beach from 16:00 to 17:30. We are rolling out the red carpet for all the little rockstars out there. Get ready for a blast!
