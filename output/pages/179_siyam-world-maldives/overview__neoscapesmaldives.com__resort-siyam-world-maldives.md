The Maldives Reimagined
Siyam World is a bold new vision of island life, a carefree playground where imagination comes alive. Spanning 54 hectares in the pristine Noonu Atoll, this five-star premium all-inclusive resort is bursting with energy, surprises, and boundless possibilities.
Dive into the Maldives’ largest floating water park, gallop along the beach at the country’s only horse ranch, or stay in one of the widest collections of villas and residences in the islands. From beachfront hideaways and lush pool villas to overwater retreats with slides that drop straight into the sea, there’s something for every traveler.
Savor global flavors at twelve restaurants and bars, explore a vibrant house reef just steps away, or unwind at the spa before catching a Maldivian sunset. Accessible by a 40-minute scenic seaplane or short domestic transfer, Siyam World redefines paradise for those who want it all.

Dining
Tempo - All-Day Dining
Located on a beachfront terrace on the island’s east, Tempo offers casual all-day dining with live cooking stations and themed buffet evenings. Open for breakfast, lunch, and dinner, it presents an international spread that delights every palate. From hearty mornings to relaxed lunches and global culinary adventures at dinner, Tempo is the perfect place to savour flavours from around the world while enjoying stunning beach views.
Baraabaru
Baraabaru is a beachfront favourite with uninterrupted lagoon views. Start the day with a bountiful breakfast buffet and live cooking stations or join for a special Friday dinner. Warm and welcoming, Baraabaru captures the essence of Maldivian hospitality with every meal.
Andalucia – Spanish Cuisine
Nestled between coconut palms and the sandy shore, Andalucia serves authentic Spanish favourites in a relaxed island setting. Savor a rich Paella de Marisco for two, Patatas Bravas, tender Pulpo, and Gambas bursting with flavour. With its vibrant dishes and oceanfront views, Andalucia offers guests a warm taste of Spain, just steps from their villas and the pool.
Kaage – Maldivian Cuisine
With its stunning ocean backdrop, Kaage invites you to experience the warmth of Maldivian home cooking. Recipes passed down through generations are crafted with fresh, locally sourced ingredients, from fragrant curries to traditional island specialties. Every dish tells a story of heritage and love, offering an authentic taste of the Maldives in a setting as inviting as the cuisine itself.
Takrai – Thai Restaurant
Takrai brings the vibrant flavours of Thailand to the Maldives. Balanced with sweet, salty, sour, and spicy notes, the menu features aromatic curries, noodles, stir-fries, and fresh herb-laced salads. Set overwater with breathtaking views, it’s a true culinary adventure.
Kurry Leaf – Indian Restaurant
Floating above turquoise waters, Kurry Leaf celebrates India’s culinary heritage with a modern touch. Enjoy aromatic vegetarian curries, tandoor-grilled meats, fresh naan, and decadent desserts. With its inviting overwater setting, every meal feels like a feast for the senses.
The Wahoo Grill
Set along the beach, The Wahoo Grill delivers a Surf & Turf experience highlighting the island’s freshest seafood and premium cuts of meat. From Maldivian lobster and yellowfin tuna to the signature wahoo, every dish is grilled to perfection and best enjoyed with ocean views.
Arigato – Japanese Restaurant
Arigato presents refined Japanese dining in a tranquil setting. From delicate sashimi and sushi to ramen, shabu-shabu, and yakiniku, each dish showcases authentic flavours with artistry. Pair your meal with expert wine recommendations or book the Chef’s Table for an exclusive experience.
Barrique – Wine Cellar
Hidden within an intimate underground cellar, Barrique offers a one-of-a-kind fine dining experience. Indulge in gourmet tasting menus paired with world-class wines curated by our sommelier. This exclusive venue promises a sophisticated culinary journey for discerning food and wine lovers.
Mint – Adults-Only Pool Bar
Set beside a sleek infinity pool overlooking Noonu Atoll, Mint is a serene escape exclusively for adults. Enjoy cocktails, mocktails, tropical juices, and shisha on the sun-drenched deck. With laidback music and panoramic lagoon views, Mint invites you to relax in style., this is one of the most popular watering holes on the island.
Together – Poolside Bar
Together is the island’s vibrant social hub, perfect for cocktails, light snacks, and panoramic sunset views over Noonu Atoll. Lively yet relaxed, it’s the ideal spot to gather, connect, and create unforgettable moments by the pool, all part of the WOW! All-Inclusive experience.
Jungali – Jungle Pool Bar
Nestled within the island’s lush greenery, Jungali offers a family-friendly tropical retreat. Swim in the enchanting jungle pool, glide up to the semi-submerged bar, or unwind in shaded nooks among the palms. With fresh salads, pizzas, and light bites, it’s a laidback escape with rainforest charm.
Kulhivaru – Sports Bar
Kulhivaru is the ultimate spot for sports fans who don’t want to miss a moment. Catch live national and international games on screen while enjoying cold beers, bar bites, and karaoke. Whether it’s a midnight snack or cheering on your favourite team, Kulhivaru keeps the energy alive in the heart of the Maldives.
Shipwrecked Beach & Pool Bar
Located near Siyam Water World, Shipwrecked is a fun-filled beachfront and pool bar shaped like a traditional wooden pirogue. Open daily from 10:30 to midnight, enjoy ocean views while relaxing on a chaise-longue with a cocktail in hand. Pizzas are served from 10:30 to 15:00, making it the perfect spot for a quick bite or to soak up laughter, adventure, and island vibes.
The Orchid - Sunset Infinity Pool Bar
Set beside a stunning infinity pool, The Orchid is the ultimate spot to relax with your favourite drinks while watching the golden Maldivian sunset. Open from 10:00, it features shisha and a cigar menu, sunbeds, and panoramic ocean views. Whether for a mid-morning tipple or evening unwind, this bar offers the perfect blend of tranquility, style, and scenic beauty.
The Cube – Chef’s Table
The Cube is a striking glass-fronted dining space set directly on the beach, offering one of the island’s most exclusive culinary experiences. Each evening, just eight guests are treated to a multi-course tasting menu prepared live in front of them. With rotating chef residencies, every night brings a new story, artistry, and flavour journey, making The Cube an unforgettable dining theatre by the ocean.
Good Vibrations – Laidback Beach Bar
Named after the iconic Beach Boys’ song, Good Vibrations is all about easy-going island living. With swings at the bar, bean bags on the sand, and rustic log seating, it’s the ultimate barefoot hangout. Drop by after a water sports session or simply lounge with a drink in hand as you soak up the sun, sea breeze, and laidback vibes.
Teppanyaki – Japanese Grill
Savor the art of Japanese grill dining with a lively twist. At Teppanyaki, skilled chefs prepare your meal right before your eyes, blending precision with performance. From sizzling seafood to tender meats, each dish is cooked to perfection with flair and flavour. A dining experience that’s as entertaining as it is unforgettable, best enjoyed with family and friends.

Highlights From The Resort
Watersports
Snorkel at your own pace, then try windsurfing, kiteboarding, and jet skiing. Feel the adrenaline on a banana boat ride across the open water.
Excursions
Explore nearby islands with knowledgeable guides who introduce you to exotic wildlife, plants, and flowers. End the day with a lucky dolphin sunset cruise.
Recreation
From go-karting to horse rides, kids’ carnivals, and waterpark adventures, there’s fun for all ages.
Diving
Dive with SSI and PADI-certified instructors by day or night, discovering vibrant reefs and open water marine life.
Spa & Wellness
Veyo Spa offers treatments tailored to your needs including yoga, meditation, Ayurveda, naturopathy, Chinese acupuncture, and beauty therapies for total rejuvenation.
Entertainment
Evenings feature fire dances, acrobatics, glow parties, live DJs, and poolside festivities.
