General Questions
1. How do I get to Gili Lankanfushi?
Being one of the top Maldives resorts with speedboat transfers, Gili Lankanfushi is approximately 20 kilometers from Velana International Airport (Malé), and is conveniently accessible via a 30-minute speedboat transfer from the airport.  We’ll take care of all the arrangements to ensure our guests will be greeted and escorted to our speedboat for a seamless and scenic journey to the island.
2. When is the best time to visit the Maldives for optimal weather?
The best time to visit the Maldives largely depends on your preferences for weather and activities.
For those seeking sunny days with excellent underwater visibility, the recommended time is between November to April. During this time of the year, guests can fully enjoy all the things to do in Maldives, from swimming in the warm lagoons to relaxing under the clear night sky.
Underwater enthusiasts will enjoy visiting the Maldives from May to November, where the occasional torrential rain is expected. However, it is also the best time for manta ray and whale shark sightings.
