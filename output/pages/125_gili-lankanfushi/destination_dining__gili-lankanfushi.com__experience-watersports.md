Chase aquatic thrills
Name your adventure, whether it’s windsurfing, kayaking, canoeing, catamaran sailing, or paddle-boarding—we offer it! Our blue lagoon is the perfect place to hone your moves, gain speed, and witness a dazzling underwater display of colourful aquatic life and healthy reefs. Need a lesson? We’re happy to help you brush up your skills. If you crave more motion in the ocean, we can also arrange wakeboarding, water skiing, tubing, and private speed boat adventures.
Kayaking & Stand-Up Paddle Boarding
Float along aquamarine waters in our complimentary kayaks and stand-up paddleboards. You won’t believe your eyes as you glide over shallow reef filled with vibrant fish and other wildlife.
Windsurfing Lessons
Enjoy a short explanation of wind, board and sail techniques before heading to the shallow lagoon for some practical fun. One hour is usually enough for beginners to grasp the fundamentals of windsurfing up and down the beach.
Catamaran Sailing Lessons
Starting with a safety briefing and explanation of controlling direction of the boat, we head to the Wedding Champa for a controlled catamaran sailing session with one of our instructors. Minimum age 8 years old.
Water Ski & Wake Boarding
Following a safety briefing and instruction the guests move to the calmer waters behind the Wedding Champa for half an hour of adrenaline-charged fun times. Instruction can be offered for complete beginners. Minimum age 8 years old.
Tubing
Fly along the water’s surface in a banana tube, ring tube, or sofa tube.
