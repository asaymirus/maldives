Your most memorable meal yet
Discover the pleasure of the unexpected by opting for a private dinner hosted in different locations around the island. From a beachside barbeque with live music to a blindfolded “Dining for the Senses” experience, guests can customize the meal to their tastes and preferences. Or, let our team surprise you, whisking you away to a surprise location where you’ll enjoy our chef’s culinary creations in a dreamy setting.
BBQ dine with a private chef
Dine with your toes dipped in the sand as a private chef creates an unforgettable barbeque feast. Choose from a surf-and-turf BBQ, a Maldivian lobster BBQ, or a vegan BBQ.

Dining for the Senses
Put your taste buds to the test with a blindfolded sensory culinary experience comprising four courses and spotlighting locally sourced seafood and the daily harvest from our organic garden.
Dine under the stars
Indulge in a night of pure romance as you dine under the stars in the intimacy and privacy of your own villa’s rooftop terrace. Allow our culinary team to spoil you with a fine-dining experience as we make your dream of a romantic evening come true.
