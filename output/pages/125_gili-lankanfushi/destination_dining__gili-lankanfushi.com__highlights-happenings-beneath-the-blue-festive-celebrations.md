# Beneath the Blue: Festive Celebrations - Gili Lankanfushi Maldives

Immerse yourself in a season of ocean-inspired celebrations and unforgettable island moments at Gili Lankanfushi. From enchanting festive gatherings and exceptional culinary experiences to meaningful connections with nature, Beneath the Blue invites you to discover the magic of the season. Here’s a glimpse of what’s to come:
The First Spark: Christmas Tree Lighting
22 December 2026
The festive season begins as the island gathers for one of Gili’s most cherished traditions. Against a backdrop of turquoise waters and swaying palms, join us for an enchanting evening as our ocean-inspired Christmas tree is illuminated for the very first time.
Celebrating the beauty of the marine world and the magic of the season, the evening will feature festive entertainment, seasonal culinary delights and heartwarming moments designed to bring all of us together as we welcome the start of an extraordinary festive journey.
Sea of Stars: Christmas Eve Dinner
24 December 2026
Celebrate Christmas Eve beneath a canopy of stars with an unforgettable evening inspired by the wonders of the ocean. Thoughtfully curated by our culinary team, this festive feast showcases exceptional ingredients, creative flavours and immersive experiences designed to delight every sense.
As the waves gently lap the shore and the night sky sparkles above, gather with loved ones for a celebration where island elegance, festive traditions and the spirit of togetherness come beautifully together.
Christmas Day Celebration
25 December 2026
Awake to the magic of Christmas in paradise, where a day of festive discoveries, family-friendly experiences and island-inspired celebrations awaits. Whether gathering with loved ones, indulging in seasonal delights or embracing the relaxed rhythm of island life, every moment is designed to create lasting memories.
Celebrate the joy of togetherness and experience Christmas, the Gili way.
Beneath the Blue Cocktail Reception
26 December 2026
Raise a glass to the festive season during an elegant ocean-inspired soirée.
Enjoy handcrafted cocktails, thoughtfully curated culinary creations and an atmosphere infused with island sophistication, as the colours of the sunset give way to an enchanting evening Beneath the Blue.
New Year’s Eve Celebration
31 December 2026
As the final sunset of the year paints the horizon in shades of gold and blue, join us for the most anticipated celebration of the season. Savour exceptional dining, captivating entertainment and unforgettable moments as we count down to midnight beneath the stars.
Welcome the New Year surrounded by loved ones, cherished connections and the warmth of the Gili family, creating memories to carry into the year ahead.
Taittinger Champagne Dinner
07 January 2027
Conclude the festive season with an elegant evening celebrating exceptional cuisine and world-renowned Champagne. Thoughtfully crafted by our culinary team, this intimate dining experience brings together remarkable flavours, carefully paired vintages and meaningful moments shared in a truly spectacular island setting.
This is only a glimpse of what awaits Beneath the Blue. Our full festive programme, featuring signature celebrations, family experiences, wellness journeys, ocean adventures and exclusive dining events, will be unveiled soon.
Stay tuned as the magic Beneath the Blue continues to unfold.
