A private sanctuary amid crystal waters
These 18 one-bedroom hideaways are perfect for couples. Spread across two floors, each boasts an open-air living room, a spacious bathroom, and a private rooftop terrace from which you can take in the tropical scenery. With direct ocean access, spend your days swimming and snorkelling around the coral gardens at the base of your sundeck. At night, lounge on catamaran nets while spotting shooting stars.
