Quench your thirst for something new
Our menu of private beverage experiences will allow guests to hone in on the techniques, styles and flavour profiling that go into making a delicious drink. Partake in a cocktail competition, sign up for a mixology class, and learn about the fascinating history and language of wine in a private tasting.
Battle of the Bartenders
Every day at sunset, our resident bartenders face off in a lively and spirited cocktail competition, where mixologists craft two drinks apiece and guests vote on their favourite boozy creation. Do reserve your spot ahead of time for an engaging evening.
Cocktail Masterclass
Our private beverage experiences will teach you how to select ingredients, pair liquors and spirits, and experiment with unique flavours to create the perfect cocktail — all in an inspiring outdoor “classroom” with sea views and swaying palms.
Wine Tastings
Learn to swish, swirl, and sip like a pro with our private wine tastings, led by our team of sommeliers. You’ll develop an appreciation for the art of tasting as you sample a range of old and new world wines produced in a variety of styles.
Castaway Sunset Experience
Located in the middle of our sparkling lagoon, our castaway platform is a perfect spot to watch the sunset in peace and seclusion, while indulging in creative canapés and delicious cocktails.
