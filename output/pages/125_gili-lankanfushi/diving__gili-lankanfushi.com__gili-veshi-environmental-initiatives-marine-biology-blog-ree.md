# Reef Cleaning Report – another 15kg of rubbish hauled out - Gili Lankanfushi Maldives

Source: https://gili-lankanfushi.com/gili-veshi-environmental-initiatives/marine-biology-blog/reef-cleaning-report-another-15kg-of-rubbish-hauled-out/
Scraped: 2026-10-04
Resort: Gili Lankanfushi
Page type: diving

---

Just as every month, hosts and guests at Gili Lankanfushi undertook the Reef Cleaning activity last Sunday. After a 45 minute dive at the One Palm Island Reef, we surfaced with 15kg of rubbish, composed mainly of fishing line, plastic bottles and aluminum cans – all of these seem to be a big problem in the Maldives (judging from our findings during reef cleanups). Oddest item this month – a pair of fins, one of the fins had a small chunk bitten out of it – a curious triggerfish is likely to be responsible for that…
