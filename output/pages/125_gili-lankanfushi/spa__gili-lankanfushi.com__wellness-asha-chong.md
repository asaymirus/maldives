Asha is a visiting wellness practitioner specialising in Five Element and Traditional Chinese Medicine (TCM) acupuncture. She is based in central London, UK and offers treatments that aim to balance the body and mind from the inside out.
Asha’s treatments focus on regulating the body’s energy life force, known as qi, through the body’s energetic pathways. She helps release stagnated energy, toxins, and emotions by doing so. Her primary method is Five Element acupuncture, based on the theory that the mind, body, and spirit are made of five elements – Wood, Fire, Earth, Metal, and Water. The belief is that when all five elements are in balance, we are in good health physically, mentally, and emotionally.
