Start your day with sublime sunrise views
Facing the eastern side of the island, these exclusive two-bedroom villas boast the most spectacular sunrise vistas. At the center of the villa is an overwater sundeck with a catamaran net, daybeds, sun loungers, and an L-shaped swimming pool that appears to extend into crystal-clear waters below. Other amenities include a spacious en suite master bedroom, an en suite guest room, a living room with Indian Ocean views, and a private rooftop terrace— the perfect spot to gather as a family or group of friends to enjoy a meal or a sundowner.
