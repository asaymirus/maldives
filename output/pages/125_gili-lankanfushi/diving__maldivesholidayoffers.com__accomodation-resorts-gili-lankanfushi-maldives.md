Resort Overview
Set on the private island of Lankanfushi in the North Malé Atoll, Gili Lankanfushi™, Maldives is an intimate coral island in a sparkling lagoon, with jetties threading across the water out to spacious villa accommodations, where ultimate privacy can be found and where Gili stories with lasting memories are made. A place where you arrive as a guest, leave as a friend and return as family.
This luxury overwater resort is just a 20-minute speedboat ride from Velana International Airport at Malé, the capital of the Republic of Maldives. Here your idyllic personal hideaway is one of our 45 spacious, rustic overwater villas crafted from natural wood and glass. We invite you to experience our signature ‘Gili Wellness Journey’ led by the qualified practitioners of Meera Spa. No noise, no distraction and a private, exclusive home that has to be seen to be believed.
