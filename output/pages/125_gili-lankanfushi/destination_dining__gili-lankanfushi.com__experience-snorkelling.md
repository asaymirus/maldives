Glide through our underwater environment
Slip on a snorkelling mask and a pair of fins and head to the 15-metre drop-off zone at the edge of our house reef. There, discover vibrant coral walls inhabited by small sharks and colourful schools of fish, turtles, and rays. Our resident marine biologist can help you identify the array of aquatic life on guided trips, departing day and night, and explain the significance of our coral line projects and other underwater initiatives.
Snorkelling at Gili Lankanfushi Maldives
The Maldives is an underwater auditorium teeming with mesmerizing coral formations and kaleidoscopic wildlife. Embark on a self-guided adventure or our house reef, team up with a marine biologist to deepen your understanding of the natural world, or travel to the world-class snorkelling destinations within striking distance of our hotel.
Guided Trips with Marine Biologists
Turtle Snorkel
A speedboat whisks guests to the Makunudhoo, one of the healthiest reefs in the North Malé Atoll, where the shallow formations allow snorkellers to observe Hawksbill Turtles like never before.
Reef Shark Snorkel
Guests are practically guaranteed to see Nurse Sharks at Gamarugiri, a sandy lagoon located 35 minutes away by speedboat. Alternatively, a Dhoni can take guests to the Bandos Resort House Reef, just 20 minutes away, to observe Blacktip Reef Sharks.
Private Snorkelling Trips
Design your dream snorkelling trip and let our experience team take care of the logistics. Whether you want to see turtles, sharks, or vibrant coral, our guides can handle the details, developing an itinerary based on your preferences and experience level.
