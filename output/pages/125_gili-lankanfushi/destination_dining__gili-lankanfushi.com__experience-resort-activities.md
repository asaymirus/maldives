Discovery at your doorstep
At Gili Lankanfushi, guests don’t have to wander far to discover their next great adventure. From tennis lessons and cooking classes to movie nights under the stars, our roster of on-property experiences give guests the option to be as busy as they want to be. Families, couples, adventurers, wellness-seekers—all are welcome at our island paradise.
Tennis with a Pro
Work on your serve and perfect your backhand in a glorious outdoor setting shaded by towering palms. Whether you’re new to the game or an expert player, our tennis coach can provide instruction and helpful pointers for all levels. In partnership with Tipsarevic Luxury Tennis, our lessons are a great way to improve your game.
Cooking Class
The ultimate souvenir? Learning a few recipes to bring home with you! Sign up for a cooking class and whip up traditional Maldivian fish curry, sushi rolls, and other favourites off our menus. With the basic preparations already done, guests will spend their time bringing unique flavours to life under the direction of a specialist chef (and, of course, enjoying the fruits of their labour).
Photo Moments with Gili Memories Studio
Capture precious moments at the Gili Memories Studio. A photographer will arrange a fun and hassle-free photo shoot to help you commemorate your favourite vacation memories.
Picnic Experience
Enjoy a picnic basket or a seaside barbeque on a secluded sandbank located a short boat ride from the resort. We’ll set up your meal on the sand, complete with parasols, sun loungers, and dining trays. All you have to do is bring your appetite.
Movie Under the Stars
Jungle Cinema
Take in a flick at our tropical cinema, surrounded by lush palms and the sounds of nature.
Beach Cinema
Savour a movie under the stars at our beach cinema, with lantern lighting, sumptuous sunbeds, and velvety sand underfoot.
