What’s your definition of island bliss? Our team is delighted to help you design your dream day, whether you dream of bucket-list romantic excursions, thrilling aquatic adventures, or on-property pursuits.
Honeymooners delight in our sumptuous spa and romantic experiences, like dinners on lantern-lit beaches and private wine tastings, while families and solo travellers come together over high-octane adventures ranging from surf lessons to marine biologist-led snorkelling trips.
Activities
Diving
Dive into the wonders of the Maldives with Gili Lankanfushi’s PADI certified dive center. Explore the vibrant coral reefs and diverse marine life while experiencing top-notch service and equipment.

Snorkelling
Embark on a self-guided adventure or our house reef, team up with a marine biologist to deepen your understanding of the natural world, or take a boat ride to nearby snorkelling spots for the chance to swim with turtles, sharks, and more.
Discover More
Surfing with Tropicsurf
Guests can ride barrels in the same spot where surfing in the Maldives was born. A Dhoni boat whisks surfers to some of the country’s finest surf breaks, three of which are just a five-minute ride away (with many more within 20 minutes).

Excursions
We offer an extensive range of guided excursions for guests looking to explore what lies beyond our resort—sunset dolphin cruises, fishing trips, visits to local islands, yacht cruises, and more.
