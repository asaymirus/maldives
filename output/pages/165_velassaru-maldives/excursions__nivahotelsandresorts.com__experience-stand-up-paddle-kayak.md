Stand Up Paddle & Kayak
Niva Dhigali Maldives
Water Sports
Discover the lagoon by paddleboard, kayak or glass kayak at a relaxed and unforgettable pace.
Leave the shore behind and embrace a slower way of exploring the Maldives. With the gentle rhythm of each paddle stroke, discover the beauty of Dhigali’s lagoon from a new perspective. Stand-up paddleboards offer a peaceful journey across the water, while single, double and triple kayaks create moments to share with family and friends. For a truly unique experience, the glass kayak reveals the underwater world beneath you as you glide across the lagoon. Surrounded by calm waters, tropical scenery and the natural beauty of the Indian Ocean, every adventure becomes a moment to reconnect, explore and unwind.
