Sunset Sports Fishing
Niva Kurumba Maldives
Excursions
Cast your line into the Indian Ocean and experience exhilarating ocean adventures, breathtaking sunsets, and unforgettable moments in North Malé Atoll.
The azure waters of North Malé Atoll are a paradise for fishing enthusiasts, offering deep-sea adventures in one of the world’s most pristine marine environments.
Experience the thrill of one of Maldives most famous activities, fish for groupers, snappers, jack fish & job fish.
Savour your catch, our talented culinary team will prepare it your way at Vihamanaa or Cafe for your next days lunch as part of your experience.
