House Lagoon & Reef Snorkel
Niva Velassaru Maldives
Snorkelling Adventures
Crystal-clear lagoon waters reveal coral rope nurseries, tropical fish, rays, and baby reef sharks, just steps from shore.
Just a few kicks from shore, Velassaru’s lagoon opens into an active house reef, supported by coral nurseries around the island. Complimentary snorkel gear lets you explore freely among clownfish, parrotfish, butterflyfish, and Moorish idols. You may glimpse hawksbill turtles gliding past, black tip reef sharks cruising the blue, or stingrays resting on the sand. Giant clams, branching corals, and Porites coral shape a living underwater landscape, protected, accessible, and quietly alive.
