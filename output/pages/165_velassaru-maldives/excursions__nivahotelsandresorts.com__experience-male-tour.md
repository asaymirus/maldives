Malé Tour
Niva Kurumba Maldives
Excursions
Though modest in scale, the island capital reveals a world rich in culture and steeped in history.
Discover the vibrant charm of Malé, the lively capital of the Maldives. Though small in size, this capital city is rich in culture and history. With your local guide, explore highlights such as the National Museum, home to fascinating artefacts, the iconic Islamic Centre, and the stunning Friday Mosque, crafted entirely from coral. Stroll past the Mulee Aage presidential palace and soak up the energy of the local fish and fruit markets. Afterwards, browse for souvenirs or relax in a cosy café. In just three hours, this tour offers a fresh, authentic glimpse into daily life in Malé.
