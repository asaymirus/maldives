Manta, Turtle & Dolphin Trip
Niva Dhigali Maldives
Snorkelling Adventures
Discover three of the ocean’s most iconic marine creatures on one unforgettable adventure across crystal-clear waters.
Set out on an ocean journey filled with unforgettable encounters, from graceful manta rays gliding through the water to playful dolphins dancing alongside the boat and gentle turtles beneath the surface. This 4-hour adventure brings together some of the ocean’s most captivating marine life in one remarkable experience. Available as both private and combined trips, choose the way you wish to explore — whether through an intimate journey or a shared adventure discovering the wonders of the sea.
