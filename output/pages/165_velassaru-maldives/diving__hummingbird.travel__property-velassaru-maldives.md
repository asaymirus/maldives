ENQUIRE ABOUT THIS PROPERTY
A contemporary private island resort in South Malé Atoll, offering a polished but understated take on Maldivian luxury. Accommodation includes beach and overwater villas, with water villas delivering the strongest sell for couples seeking direct lagoon access and privacy. The island has a clean, minimal aesthetic with a strong focus on space and calm rather than excess facilities. Dining is well-executed across several venues, with a focus on quality rather than volume. Service is discreet and efficient. Best for couples or honeymoon clients wanting a close-to-Malé luxury escape with strong design, easy access, and a quieter overall atmosphere.
Review
I loved the laidback vibe of the resort, set on a smaller island that offers a more intimate experience, perfect for couples seeking a peaceful escape. The stunning beach, with its white sand and crystal clear water, and the breathtaking colours of the sea will captivate you at every turn. It's a paradise for food lovers, with a wide range of tasty dishes from around the world, including open-air buffet lunches set right on the sand. The Chill Bar is a must visit for sundowners and tapas-style dishes, best enjoyed while taking in the most amazing sunset views. Add to that live music and cocktail parties several times a week, plus an incredible overwater spa, and you have the perfect blend of relaxation, indulgence, and island charm.
Julia Andrei
