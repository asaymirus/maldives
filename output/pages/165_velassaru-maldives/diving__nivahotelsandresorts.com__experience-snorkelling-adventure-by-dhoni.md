Snorkelling Adventure by Dhoni
Niva Dhigali Maldives
Snorkelling Adventures
Set sail by traditional dhoni to vibrant snorkelling spots and discover the Maldives’ extraordinary underwater world.
Leave the shoreline behind and journey into the open ocean aboard a traditional dhoni. This 2-hour snorkelling adventure reveals a different side of island life, where every stop brings new possibilities beneath the waves. Guided by our team, discover the beauty of the ocean while enjoying the freedom and tranquillity of the sea. Available every Wednesday and Saturday from 2:00 PM to 4:00 PM.
