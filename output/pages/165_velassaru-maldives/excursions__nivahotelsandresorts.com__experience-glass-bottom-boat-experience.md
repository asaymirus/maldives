Peek through the glass and let your curiosity lead the way through this spectacular aquatic wonderland.
Experience the vibrant marine magic of the reef without even getting wet! Perfect for explorers of all ages, our Glass-Bottom Boat offers a whimsical window into a hidden underwater kingdom. Watch as a kaleidoscope of colourful fish and intricate coral formations glide gracefully beneath your feet – an evolving, healthy eco-system.
It’s an enchanting, immersive journey that invites everyone to marvel at the ocean’s secrets from the comfort of the deck.
