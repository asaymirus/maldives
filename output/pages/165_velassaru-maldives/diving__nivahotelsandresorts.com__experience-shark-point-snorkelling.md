Shark Point Snorkelling
Niva Kurumba Maldives
Snorkelling Adventures
Experience an unforgettable adventure at Shark Point, where you can snorkel alongside Nurse Sharks in their natural habitat.
Embark on an extraordinary aquatic journey to the nearby Shark Point reef, where you will glide alongside magnificent nurse sharks in their natural sanctuary. Often accompanied by elegant stingrays and diligent cleaner fish, these gentle giants create a tableau that is both powerful and deeply serene. Our experienced team will guide you through this open-water immersion, anchoring a guide rope so you can pause from swimming while remaining immersed in the encounter. This is a truly soul-stirring experience and a memory that will linger with you forever.
