Capers
NIVA Dhigali Maldives
It’s a culinary adventure.
Sunlight on the water, a new day begins. Your seaside breakfast awaits – a vibrant spread of island fruits, cold-pressed juices, and live cooking stations bringing classic egg dishes from around the world to life. Return for long lunches and relaxed dinners, each plate packed with bold flavours. At Capers, our buffet is a beautiful blend of international classics and Maldivian specialities – the perfect place to gather, share, and savour.
A week of culinary adventure awaits each evening. Your journey begins Monday with a special invitation – to go ‘Behind the Apron’ and meet our chefs as they prepare Angus steaks, fresh seafood, and made-to-order pasta. Every Wednesday, discover South American and Californian fusion at its best — bold flavors, fresh ingredients, and a modern coastal twist. Thursdays sizzle with live grills at ‘Meet Me At Capers’, and Fridays delight with the fragrant ‘Flavours of the Indian Ocean’ – think spicy devilled prawns and rich pumpkin curry. ‘Journey to Italy’ on Saturday, and on Sunday, explore ‘A Thousand And One Flavours’ from the Middle East.
