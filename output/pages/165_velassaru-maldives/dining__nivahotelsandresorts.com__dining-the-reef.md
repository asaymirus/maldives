The Reef
NIVA Kuramathi Maldives
The day’s finest catch alongside perfectly grilled wagyu steaks
The Reef is where turquoise waters meet endless ocean views, creating a fine dining experience that feels as refreshing as it is memorable. Gentle breezes drift through the beautiful open space, while the soothing rhythm of the waves sets the tone for a meal inspired by the Maldives’ vibrant marine life.
Catch of the day and meat from the grill
The menu celebrates the sea’s freshest offerings, with the day’s finest catch sitting alongside perfectly grilled wagyu steaks. Every dish is prepared just the way you like it, allowing the flavours to shine.
Stirring ambiance
With open-air seating over the water, The Reef feels alive with light and energy, yet deeply calming. It’s a place where the ocean’s beauty and the joy of good food come together naturally, creating memories that linger long after the meal.
