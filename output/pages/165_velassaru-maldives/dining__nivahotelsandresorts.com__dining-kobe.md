Kobe
NIVA Kuramathi Maldives
Dinner with a show
Kobe offers a refined dining journey where every detail is thoughtfully curated. Expert chefs transform fresh seafood and premium cuts into exquisite dishes, blending precision with creativity. The elegant marble counter fosters connection, while the atmosphere balances sophistication with warmth.
Craftsmen of taste
The hibachi grill is the stage as master chefs perform with skill and precision. Their samurai-like knife skills a mesmerising blur. The ocean’s finest is on offer – scallops, reef fish, tiger prawns, and salmon – along with marbled Black Angus beef tenderloin and ribeye.
From the Sushi Bar
Our chefs craft the ocean’s finest catches into stunning nigiri, hosomaki, and sashimi – almost too beautiful to eat. Almost.
The Sake Experience
Sake pairs perfectly with sushi and teppanyaki. Explore the crisp elegance of Hakushika Josen and the bold depths of undiluted genshu – every sip tells a story.
