DESCRIPTION
Soft white sand, crystal-clear seas. A picture-perfect beautiful lagoon complete with stunning sunset views. Chic private hideaways dotted along the shoreline. Five restaurants and two bars offering gourmet flavours from around the globe. Explore our teeming coral reefs, cruise out beyond the horizon, or just relax on our perfect Maldivian beaches. Exploration, adventure and repose – Velassaru Maldives sets the scene for the perfect island escape.
Velassaru Maldives is situated in the South Malé Atoll, a 25-minute speedboat ride from Malé International Airport. Our guest services team will be at the airport to escort you to our waiting speedboats. Chilled drinks and a warm welcome await you at Velassaru.
