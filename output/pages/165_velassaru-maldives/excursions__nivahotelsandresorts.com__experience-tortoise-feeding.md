Tortoise feeding
Niva Labriz Seychelles
Family Activities
Get up close and personal with Labriz’s giant tortoises, hand-feed them and learn about their habits and conservation from our expert guides.
Step into the shaded gardens of Niva Labriz, among the best places to go in Seychelles, and meet the island’s giant tortoises, some of its oldest and most gentle inhabitants. Guided by our knowledgeable team, you’ll hand-feed these magnificent creatures, discover quirky facts about their habits and understand the conservation efforts that keep their populations thriving. Perfect for families and anyone with a curiosity for wildlife, this experience blends education, interaction and fun.
As you watch the tortoises munch and slowly wander through their lush habitat, you’ll gain a sense of wonder, connection and respect for these remarkable animals and the island they call home.
