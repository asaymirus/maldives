Hotel Policy
Located in the Baa Atoll, Dreamland is reachable by a 35-minute scenic seaplane flight over the Indian Ocean, or a 20-minute domestic flight to a nearby island Dharavandhoo airport followed by a 10-minute speedboat transfer to the resort. International flight details must be provided 7 days prior to arrival for arranging the transfers to the hotel.
Seaplane journeys to Dreamland are available during daylight hours only. Guests arriving at Male’ International Airport on an international flight after 15:30 will be brought to the resort by Domestic Transfer (latest available flight at 23:00 hrs dep from Male’ International Airport.
Every effort will be made to arrange your resort transfer within 2 hours of arriving. Departing guests will be transferred to the airport at least 3 hours in advance of the scheduled international flight departure time.
– Check-in time is at 02:00pm and Check-out time is 12:00 Noon
– Rates are per room per night for double occupancy
– Rates are inclusive to 10% service charge, 12% GST and Government Green Tax of USD6.00 per person per night.
– Rates are dynamic and availability is subject to change.
– Room and rate is valid for 48hours. Past this time the room will be released and new rates may apply
– Additional beds: Maximum 01 (90*190cm)
