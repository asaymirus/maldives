Water Sports
Experience boundless Dreamland adventures
Float over fish and black-tip sharks in your kayak. Hear the wind whistling past as you slice up the waters on a wakeboard. There are so many ways to experience Dreamland waters from serene adventures on the sea to exciting offshore escapades. Just let us know your tastes and your skill levels and we will find the perfect offshore adventure for you.
WINDSURFING
Courses and rental equipment For beginner and experienced rider.
STAND UP PADDLE
The fun and perfect workout training for the whole body.
POWER WATERSPORTS
Unlimited fun with wakeboard, waterski and sofa rides…
GUIDED JETSKI RIDES
Individual Jetski rides around the North of Baa Atoll.
GUIDED SNORKELING
Explore Dreamlands house reef with one of our professional guides and get some amazing information about the marine life.
Or do you like to explore the reef at night?
NIGHT SNORKELING
– your new experience!
Professional rental equipment is available.
MANTA SNORKELING
HANIFARU BAY
is the most extraordinary Manta Point in the whole Maldives. Do not miss your chance to experience the gentle giants first hand and – with a little luck – even whale sharks. (seasonal from May – October)
INDIVIDUAL PACKAGES
The perfect choice for couples and families. Choose your personal combination out of 3 to 4 activities and enjoy your package discount.
