Excursions
Breathtaking Dreamland journeys
Salt spray flicks across the hull. Your boat cuts through a sea of Dreamland blue. You set out on another day of adventure, exploring above and below the surface of the sea.
Dreamland is unique for a number of reasons. You’ve got a world of beauty right at your doorstep, with the house reef shimmering just offshore. You can paddle out and snorkel around our house reef, you can hop on a boat and explore the culture of locals Islands nearby or take in another stunning Dreamland sunset from the deck of your excursion dhoni (Wooden Motor Boat)
Our Guest Experiences Team is here to arrange the ideal island adventure for you, whether you join a group excursion or opt for a private experience.
Sunset Dhoni Cruise
Tuesday & Saturday @ 17:30 Hrs. USD 25 per person | Minimum 06 Adults
Enjoy a glorious sunset trip around Dreamland with a refreshing drink. Always take your camera.
Sunset Fishing Trip
Monday & Friday @ 17:30 Hrs. USD 25 per person | Minimum 06 Adults
Enjoy the beauty of glorious sunset with all the fun on fishing with traditional Maldivian way. You won’t find our until you try.
Adventure Snorkelling
Tuesday & Saturday @ 09:00 Hrs. USD 30 per person | Minimum 06 Adults
Join us as we venture into one of the finest snorkeling sites at Baa Atoll and experience the unique marine life.
Turtle Snorkelling
Monday & Friday @ 14:00 Hrs. USD 30 per person | Minimum 06 Adults
Enjoy snorkeling with these magnificent turtles, of course we cannot guarantee sightings, but we always experience the best snorkeling spots at Baa atoll.
Lucky Dolphin Adventure Trip
Sunday & Thursday @ 17:00 Hrs. USD 30 per person | Minimum 06 Adults
Escape to the sea, before sunset and try your luck. Some dolphin may join. (Of cause, sighting is not guaranteed).
Kamadhoo Island Trip
Sunday & Wednesday @ 10:00Hrs. USD 30 per person | Minimum 06 Adults
Kamadhoo is a typical fisherman’s Island with around 400 inhabitants, it is just 30 minutes away from Dreamland with boat. Enjoy the rich traditional Maldivian lifestyle.
Dharavandhoo Island Trip
Monday & Thursday @ 09:00 Hrs. USD 30 per person | Minimum 06 Adults
Dharavandhoo is the former capital island of Baa Atoll, it is just 20 minutes away from Dreamland by boat. On this trip, you can enjoy Maldivian life, and culture, enjoy some shopping at local souvenir shops, and snacks at local cafes.
Robinson Trip
All weekdays @ any time of the day for a duration of 04 Hrs. USD 70 per person | Minimum 02 Person
Castaway to your very own tropical island, you can explore and enjoy snorkeling. This is the ultimate escape. Please note complete exclusivity is not guaranteed and depends on the availability of the island.
