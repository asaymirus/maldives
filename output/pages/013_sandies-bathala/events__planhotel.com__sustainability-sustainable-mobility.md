SUSTAINABLE MOBILITY
AN ECO-FRIENDLY WAY TO MOVE AROUND
At THE VIEW Lugano, guests are invited to explore the beauty of Lugano and its surroundings with complimentary access to electric Smart cars and e-bikes.
As the first boutique hotel in the area to offer this eco-friendly amenity, THE VIEW Lugano provides a sustainable and stylish way to discover the city and the scenic Ticino region.
The electric Smart cars, available upon request, boast an autonomy of approximately 90 km, allowing guests to comfortably explore not only the city of Lugano but also other parts of Ticino.
For those who prefer a more active approach, the electric bicycles offer a comfortable and sustainable means to travel around Lugano and its surrounding areas.
Guests can request the use of an electric vehicle for the entire duration of their stay, ensuring they can travel around freely, enjoy, and discover the city at their own pace.
