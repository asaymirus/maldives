INVESTING IN OUR PEOPLE
More Than a Workplace: A Community
At the heart of everything we do are
our people
, whose passion, professionalism, and purpose drive both the exceptional guest experiences we offer and the sustainability goals we pursue. We believe that a
strong, supported team
is essential to delivering great service and fostering a positive workplace culture, that’s why we invest continuously in staff training, with regular sessions focused on social responsibility, sustainability, and health and safety—equipping our team not just with skills, but
with purpose
.
Beyond professional development, we prioritise wellbeing both at work and during free time. From football and volleyball matches to team-building games, fishing trips, and special events like Women’s Day celebrations, we create regular opportunities for staff to
connect, relax, and have fun
. These activities strengthen our sense of community and ensure that everyone feels valued, supported, and
part of something greater
.
