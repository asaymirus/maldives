GDS Codes And Commission
Crown & Champa Resorts (CCR) partners with GDS companies so Travel Agents can book our Resorts online.
GDS commission structure
: CCR offers a 10 % commission on all rack rates and package rates. For Kudadoo Maldives Private Island By Hurawalhi, agents, a 15% commission is paid on the rack rate. Commissions are paid to any licensed Travel Agent with an IATA code. Commission payment is made no later than 30 days after the client has checked out of the Resort.
