About us
Crown & Champa Resorts (CCR) is a collection of properties, each unique in their own way.
Established in 2000 by inspiring Maldivian and foreign partners with a shared vision to create authentic Maldivian experiences for the curious traveller with a passion for a life fully lived.
Underpinned by our shared belief in responsible living each resort offers guests the freedom to enjoy the cuisine on offer, experience mindful wellness and the chance of adventure no matter if that choice leads to underwater exploration or the enjoyment of our palm-lined beaches.

Current Resorts
