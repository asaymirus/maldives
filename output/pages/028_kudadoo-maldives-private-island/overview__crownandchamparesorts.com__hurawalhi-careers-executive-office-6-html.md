Hurawalhi Island Resort is looking for a dedicated and detail-oriented
Laundry Supervisor
to oversee the daily operations of the laundry department.

We also provide our team members with accommodation, food, laundry services, team shop and other indoor & out activities such as team pool, gym, paddles, tennis, badminton, billiards etc., that make your job perform the most.
You’re Responsibilities:
Supervise and coordinate the daily operations of the laundry department.
Ensure proper washing, drying, ironing, and folding of guest laundry, staff uniforms, and hotel linens.
Maintain high standards of cleanliness, hygiene, and presentation of all laundry items.
Monitor laundry equipment and report any maintenance issues promptly.
Train, guide, and supervise laundry attendants to ensure efficient workflow.
Ensure proper use of detergents, chemicals, and laundry equipment according to safety standards.
Maintain accurate records of laundry items, chemicals, and inventory.
Coordinate with Housekeeping and other departments to meet operational requirements.
Ensure compliance with health, safety, and environmental regulations.
Assist in scheduling staff shifts and managing daily workloads
Our Requirements:
Minimum
2–3 years of experience
in a laundry operation, preferably in a hotel or resort environment.
Previous
supervisory experience
in a similar role is preferred.
Good knowledge of laundry operations, equipment, and chemicals.
Strong organizational and leadership skills.
Ability to work in a fast-paced environment and manage a team effectively.
Good communication skills and attention to detail.
Basic computer knowledge will be an advantage.
Maldivian nationality preferred
