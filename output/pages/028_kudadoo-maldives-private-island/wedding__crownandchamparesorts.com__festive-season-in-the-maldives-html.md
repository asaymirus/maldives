MEMORABLE FESTIVE EVENTS
Embrace the magic of the festive season, with sparkling evenings, joyful festivities and cherished moments under the tropical sky.
Christmas Tree Lighting – Gather as the island shimmers to life, marking the beginning of a joyous season.
Christmas Gala Dinner – Indulge in a sumptuous celebration of the season, featuring timeless holiday classics and vibrant international cuisine.
New Year’s Eve Gala Dinner – Dine in splendour as you toast to the past year and welcome the promise of the next.
New Year’s Eve Countdown Parties – Dance into the New Year as the island sparkles with festive energy and celebration.
SHARED FAMILY MOMENTS
Share the joy of the festivities with heartwarming experiences that make every moment unforgettable.
Santa’s Arrival – Magical moment for the young and young at heart as Santa appears in true island style. Will he arrive by seaplane, by boat, or perhaps emerge from the sea?
Holiday Fun – Enjoy festive gingerbread house building, seasonal activities, friendly land and sea games and competitions.
Treasure Hunt – Uncover hidden clues and surprises together on an exciting island adventure.
Delightful Entertainment – From movie nights under the stars to a variety of evening entertainment and cultural shows, enjoy magical nights filled with joy and wonder.
A SEASON OF INDULGENCE
Indulge in a holiday season that harmonises relaxation and celebration. Begin your day with a sumptuous floating breakfast, sunrise snorkel, or morning yoga. Enjoy a tranquil morning on the golf course, revitalise at the spa and linger over a leisurely lunch with endless sea views. 

Spend the afternoon dozing beneath swaying palms, then let the evening unfold with a sunset cruise, wine tasting, or an exquisite culinary journey — from destination dining to a candlelit beach dinner beneath the stars.
DISCOVER THE INDIAN OCEAN
Continue the festive celebration playing in the warm Maldivian seas. Explore the breathtaking underwater world filled with rich marine life and immerse yourself in water sports adventures.
Exhilarating Scuba Dives  – The season brings fantastic water clarity, Manta rays, ship wrecks and special dives.
Snorkel with Turtles  – Discover the house reefs.
Water Sports Adventures – From thrilling jet ski rides and exhilarating tubing to catamaran sailing with expert skippers, or leisurely kayaking, family boating, and SUP across the lagoon, embrace every wave and moment of island excitement.
