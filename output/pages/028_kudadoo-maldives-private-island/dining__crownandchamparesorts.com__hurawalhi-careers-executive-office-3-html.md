Chef De Cuisine
We are seeking a passionate and efficient culinary professional to join our team as Chef De Cuisine. Our ideal candidate is dedicated to delivering exceptional service and thrives in an environment where we believe anything is possible. if you are drives, creative, and committed to culinary excellence, we invite you to apply and join our dynamic team.
The 90-villa, adults-only luxury resort.  A renowned culinary hotspot offering epic culinary journeys, which include the largest all-glass underwater restaurant, 5.8 Undersea Restaurant We promote a culture of happiness for the well-being of our team members through their employee journey. We also provide our team members with accommodation, food, laundry services, team shop and other indoor & out activities such as team pool, gym, paddles, tennis, badminton, billiards etc., that make your job perform the most.
Responsibilities Requirements:
Demonstrated expertise as a Chef de Cuisine or Senior Sous Chef, with a strong background in Michelin-starred establishments, fine dining, and Asian cuisine
Strong leadership and kitchen management skills, including team training and quality control.
Exceptional expertise in menu creation, fine dining presentation, and consistency.
Solid knowledge of food cost control, hygiene, and HACCP standards.
Ability to deliver creativity and operational excellence in a luxury Island resort environment.
Minimum 5 years experience in the same capacity in a similar environment.
Excellent communication skills &  computer literate.
Formal culinary education or equivalent professional training is required.
We offer a great opportunity to make your mark in a company that it is focusing on development and career growth.
