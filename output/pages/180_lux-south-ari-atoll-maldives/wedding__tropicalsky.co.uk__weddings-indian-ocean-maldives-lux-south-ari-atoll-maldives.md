Overview
Close your eyes and you imagine your dream Indian Ocean ceremony. Can you see the beautiful, deserted beach, with the waves gently lapping at your feet? The incomparable sunset, the coconut palms swaying gently in the breeze, the two of you hand in hand? Perhaps only your family and a few very special friends in attendance, an intimate and magical moment...  LUX* South Ari Atoll is the perfect location for utterly romantic vow renewals and symbolic ceremonies.
With crystal-clear lagoons, lush tropical flora, and soft sugary sands, LUX* South Ari Atoll boasts an idyllic location and tranquil ambience. With our
Classic Wedding
package, you’ll be treated to a beautiful setup on the beach and a coconut lover drink, a bridal flower bouquet and groom’s buttonhole, a traditional Bodu Beru live performance for the ceremony, sparkling wine, wedding cake and palm tree planting to celebrate as well as a romantic turndown in the evening. If you celebrate with our
Premier Wedding
package, you’ll both be further indulged in a couples’ massage, wedding photo shoot, sunset cruise with canapes and sparkling wine, an exclusive dinner in a location of your choice, a tropical decorated buggy and in-villa breakfast.
Featured in our guides to the top 10 luxury hotels in the Maldives and the top 10 spa hotels in the Indian Ocean, it doesn’t get more luxurious than this. Perfect for couples and families, you’ll enjoy the beautiful surroundings of soft white sand and clear, azure waters of the Indian Ocean. You could spend all day just lounging on the sand, enjoy the relaxation you deserve. But if you grow restless, LUX* has plenty of activities to offer you too. Go deep sea fishing or scuba diving, if you’re feeling active. Alternatively there’s a spa if you’d just like to be pampered.

Legal Information
On Arrival
On arrival at your hotel, you will be advised when your pre Renewal of Vows meeting will be with the hotels Renewal of Vows Coordinator.  During the meeting, they will cover all aspects of your Renewal of Vows day and show you the various Renewal of Vows venues.
The Renewal of Vows Ceremony
In most destinations, your Renewal of Vows ceremony will be conducted Monday to Friday (excluding local holidays).  In some destinations, local festivals and holidays will determine available Renewal of Vows dates.  Generally, your Renewal of Vows will take place in the grounds of the hotel unless otherwise specified.
Renewal of Vows Attire
What you wear for your Renewal of Vows is a highly individual choice, however bearing in mind the tropical climate, we recommend that a light weight suit is worn by the Groom, and the Bride considers a dress to give maximum comfort in the heat.  More casual clothes are quote acceptable in some destinations.
