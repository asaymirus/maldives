The only Maldives resort where island life and whale sharks thrive all year round.

Dining
Senses Restaurant
Set at the edge of the infinity pool with sweeping ocean views, Senses offers a menu inspired by Middle Eastern and Indian Ocean flavors. Guests can indulge in Moroccan tagines, tandoori prawns, fresh seafood, tropical sorbets, and seasonal salads. Lunch is light and vibrant while dinner transforms into an à la carte experience under the stars. The elegant poolside setting, combined with flavorful dishes and attentive service, creates a dining experience that awakens the senses and celebrates both local ingredients and international culinary influences.
Senses Lounge & Bar
Senses Lounge & Bar provides a relaxed, stylish retreat for drinks and light bites throughout the day. Sip tropical cocktails, rums, liqueurs, or cold beers, or enjoy Sri Lankan teas in the afternoon. Guests can linger poolside for a light lunch, unwind in a giant hammock with evening snacks, or toast the sunset with a refreshing beverage. The lounge blends casual elegance with oceanfront charm, making it an ideal spot for socializing, savoring flavors, and enjoying the tranquil vibes of the Maldives.
MIXE – Inventive International Cuisine
Set on a sandy beach under thatched roofs with slow-spinning ceiling fans, MIXE immerses guests in authentic island living. The main restaurant serves breakfast and dinner with fresh buffets, live cooking stations, and wood-fired pizzas, grills, and stir-fries infused with island flavors. Guests can feel the sand beneath their feet while enjoying inventive international cuisine. In the evenings, local performers bring the beach to life with vibrant entertainment. MIXE’s lively atmosphere, diverse menu, and beachfront setting make it the perfect spot to savor global flavors in a uniquely Maldivian environment.
Umami – Modern Japanese Cuisine
UMAMI offers a contemporary Japanese dining experience where flavor meets theatrical presentation. Guests can perch at the cobalt-blue sushi bar to watch chefs craft sashimi, nigiri, and maki from the freshest seafood while the robata grill serves perfectly barbecued Kobe beef and Australian lamb. Teppanyaki demonstrations add excitement to every meal, complemented by 30 sakés and a curated selection of fine Japanese whiskies. Bold red coral reef artwork and elegant décor enhance the visual appeal. UMAMI celebrates the artistry, variety, and precision of Japanese cuisine in a chic, immersive setting by the Maldives lagoon.
East Market – South-East Asian Cuisine
Inspired by Asia’s vibrant night markets, East Market offers a dynamic culinary journey across Thailand, China, Vietnam, Taiwan, the Philippines, and India. Guests can explore interactive overwater stalls where chefs prepare dim sum, stir-fries, noodles, and Peking duck live before their eyes. Rustic baskets of fresh produce and aromatic spices complement the experience while terraces provide sweeping Indian Ocean views. Breakfast also features classic European pastries fresh from the oven. Combining immersive visuals, tantalizing aromas, and bold flavors, East Market delivers an unforgettable South-East Asian dining adventure in a stunning Maldives overwater setting.
East Bar – Overwater Cocktails & Chill
Perched above the lagoon, East Bar is the ultimate sunset-to-midnight escape. Swing gently on double seats, sink into loungers set over catamaran nets, or gather with friends on the open-air deck as the ocean shimmers beneath you. The menu flows from creative cocktails to smooth nightcaps, served against panoramic sea views. For something unexpected, wander inside to discover a secret under-the-sea-inspired musical lounge where the mood shifts from laid-back to lively. Open from 5pm to midnight, East Bar blends Maldivian overwater charm with cosmopolitan nightlife vibes.
Allegria – Italian Signatures & Sunset Vibes
A little slice of the Mediterranean in the Maldives, Allegria is where rustic charm meets refined Italian flavors. Watch chefs at the open kitchen handcraft fresh pasta, gnocchi, and wood-fired pizzas or savor just-caught seafood elevated with southern European flair. Dinner is served on the deck or directly on the beach, where the sunsets are as indulgent as the cuisine. For a more intimate affair, private dining can be arranged with a curated selection of cheeses paired with fine Italian, French, and New World wines. Comforting, elegant, and authentic, Allegria is Italian dining at its most memorable.
Beach Rouge – Mediterranean by the Shore
Beach Rouge blends the spirit of a beach club with the flavors of the Mediterranean. Crisp wood-fired pizzas, fresh salads, and perfectly grilled seafood are served right on the soft white sand. With toes buried in the beach, eyes on the lagoon, and a gentle sea breeze drifting through, every meal feels like a celebration. By day, it is laid-back dining at its best; by night, the venue transforms into a lively spot with music, cocktails, and vibrant island energy.
Veli Pool Bar – Lagoon Views & Refreshing Sips
Set beside a crystal-clear swimming pool that stretches toward the sea, Veli Pool Bar is the ultimate poolside escape. With shimmering lagoon views on three sides, it is the perfect spot to relax between swims or after a sun-soaked afternoon. Guests can sip on freshly shaken cocktails, chilled wines, or tropical mocktails while light bites keep hunger at bay. Whether lounging in the shade or soaking in the Maldivian sun, every moment here is framed by panoramic ocean vistas, making it an idyllic place to unwind from day to night.
Café LUX*
At LUX* South Ari Atoll, great coffee is essential. Café LUX* serves expertly crafted barista coffee, roasted fresh on-site for the richest aroma and flavor. Guests can explore the roasting rooms on a behind-the-scenes tour. From Cold Press and Pour Overs to Nitro brews, the baristas master the latest coffee trends. For a taste beyond the café, bespoke pod machines and signature blends allow guests to enjoy Café LUX* in their villa or at home.
Lagoon Bar – Reggae Beats & Beachfront Vibes
Steps from the reception and watersports centre, Lagoon Bar is the go-to spot for sunrise views, reggae rhythms, and laid-back island energy. Swap stories of your latest dive or watersports adventure while sipping your favorite drink in one of the island’s most loved hangouts.
Divine Dinner – Romance Under the Stars
Imagine a real bed set on a secluded beach with candlelight flickering beside a bonfire and the lagoon shimmering under a starlit sky. Just the two of you, the beach all to yourselves. Indulge in a decadent chef-crafted menu paired with champagne as the ocean whispers nearby. Three hours of pure Maldivian magic.

Highlights From The Resort
Spa & Wellbeing
LUX* Me Spa offers a personalized approach to wellness with Balinese and Shirley Page massages, signature facials, Chinese medicine therapies, yoga and meditation. Every treatment is tailored to restore balance, refresh the body and calm the mind, using exclusive LUX* Me products for a truly holistic experience.
Fitness
Stay active at the LUX* Me Fitness Centre with tailored programmes and functional training designed to optimise your strengths. A complimentary body balance analysis guides a personalised plan that links seamlessly with spa and wellness therapies for a complete health journey.
Sports & Leisure
Adventure meets relaxation with jet skiing, kite surfing, flyboarding, paddleboarding and sailing on the turquoise lagoon. On land, tennis, volleyball, cycling and beach yoga ensure a variety of ways to stay active while soaking in the island beauty.
Diving & Snorkelling
South Ari Atoll offers world-class diving and snorkelling. The PADI-certified Dive Centre welcomes beginners and pros to colourful reefs teeming with fish, manta rays and stingrays. The sheltered lagoon provides easy encounters with baby reef sharks and abundant marine life.
Signature Experiences
Encounter whale sharks with a marine biologist on a traditional dhoni, enjoy the thrill of a jet ski safari, indulge in a romantic Divine Dinner on a private beach bed, or experience sunset yoga and a Tibetan sound bath aboard a dhoni. Each moment is crafted to create unforgettable memories in the Maldives.
