Overview
Property location
The property is situated on a private island within South Ari Atoll in the Maldives. It is surrounded by a lagoon and marine protected area known for whale shark sightings and diverse marine life.
Rooms
The property offers 193 villas and pavilions across 10 accommodation categories. Options include beach villas, water villas, and family-oriented accommodations.
Amenities
The facilities include a spa, fitness center, tennis courts, volleyball, badminton, table tennis, and a dive center with PADI certification. A water sports center provides activities such as snorkeling, kite surfing, parasailing, flyboarding, paddleboarding, and canoeing. Wi‑Fi is available.
Dining
The property has eight restaurants and five bars. Dining options include Italian, Japanese, Middle Eastern, Mediterranean, Indian, and Asian cuisines. Café LUX* and ICI ice cream are signature venues. Plant-based menus are available at all restaurants.
