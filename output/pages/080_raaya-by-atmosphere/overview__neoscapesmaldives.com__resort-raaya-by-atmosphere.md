DESCRIPTION
Rediscover the Spirit of Adventure
Where stories come alive and every moment is a journey.
Follow in the footsteps of Seb, a shipwrecked artist who found beauty, balance, and inspiration on RAAYA Island. Nestled among whispering mangroves and vibrant coral reefs, this enchanting retreat blends nature’s charm with architectural elegance across 167 luxurious beach and water villas.
The signature RAAYA Plan includes world cuisines, premium beverages, soothing spa rituals, ocean thrills, and artistic experiences.
Whether you’re traveling as a couple, with family, or friends, RAAYA by Atmosphere invites you to embrace a vibrant island lifestyle filled with exploration, indulgence, and unforgettable memories.

Dining
RAAYA Life
Enjoy a vibrant all-day dining experience at RAAYA Life, featuring a diverse buffet of Asian, European, and Maldivian cuisines with live cooking stations, salad bars, and tempting desserts. Set in a contemporary space overlooking stunning ocean views, the restaurant offers a relaxed ambience full of life and warmth. From sunlit mornings to starlit evenings, every meal is a celebration of flavours and heartfelt moments.
SOAQ Pool Bar
Nestled beneath the shade of screw pine trees, SOAQ Pool Bar offers stunning views of the sandy beach and turquoise ocean. Cool off with refreshing cocktails and take a dip in the pool or enjoy a variety of flavourful bar snacks in a stylish, laid-back setting.
AMPERS&ND, Bar & Restaurant
Experience the vibrant spirit of AMPERS&ND, where Pan-Asian flavours come alive with locally sourced seafood, premium meats, and vegetarian creations, all paired with handcrafted cocktails. Set against a backdrop of palm-fringed beaches and crystal-clear waters, you can unwind in beachfront cabanas, enjoy live music indoors, or soak in sunset views from the breezy rooftop bar.
The Village
Experience the charm of an Italian coastal village at The Village, set in the heart of RAAYA Island’s vibrant Village Square. Enjoy a buffet featuring a variety of regional favourites including pasta, gnocchi, and risotto, thoughtfully paired with fine wine.
Relax in a warm, rustic setting that brings the spirit of Italy to the tropics.
Visit The Village Café to enjoy freshly baked pastries, handcrafted coffee, creamy desserts, and a refreshing selection of ice creams. It’s the perfect spot for a sweet escape or a quick pick-me-up.
MIZĀJ
As evening sets in, the pool bar transforms into MIZĀJ, a fine-dining restaurant offering a curated à la carte menu inspired by the Middle East.
Dine under the stars with ocean views and a gentle breeze, as authentic flavours and elegant presentation come together in a serene, sophisticated setting.
Seb’s Shack
Kick back at Seb’s Shack, a rustic beach hangout with a vibrant twist. Sip on refreshing teas, smoothies, cold-pressed juices, ice-cold beers, and handcrafted rum cocktails, paired with light bites that nourish the soul. Infused with laid-back surfer vibes, it’s the perfect spot to mingle, share stories, or unwind after a snorkelling adventure. Live it up, Seb style.
Seb’s Farm
Savor a farm-to-table dining experience that celebrates the island’s freshest harvest, from organic vegetables and herbs to locally sourced seafood. Dine at rustic wooden tables in an eco-friendly setting where every dish is crafted with artistry and care.
Go beyond the plate with immersive experiences. Tour the farm, handpick seasonal ingredients, and enjoy a private gourmet dinner paired with fine wine.
Inspired by the island’s beauty, artist Seb made this his home, now yours to discover.
Please note: Seb’s Farm is not included in the RAAYA Plan.

Highlights From The Resort
Island Escapes
Let Raaya’s castaway spirit sweeps you into a world of adventure. Paddle through mangroves, dive vibrant coral gardens, or zip around on a jet ski before unwinding under the palms with a cocktail in hand. Every path leads to discovery.
Wellness in Nature
Balance body and soul amidst tropical stillness. At ELE|NA Spa, ancient rituals meet island ingredients, while the open-air yoga pavilion and jungle-fringed gym offer energising serenity under the sky.
Flavours of the World
From wood-fired pizzas and spice-laced mezze to ocean-fresh seafood and rustic farm-to-table delights, dining at Raaya is a sensory journey. Rooftop bars and barefoot beach dinners bring every meal to life with a view.
Family Wonderlands
Little adventurers explore shipwreck play zones, zip lines and treasure hunts at the Discovery Den, while families bond over crafts, beach games, or simply sharing hammocks in sun-dappled peace.
