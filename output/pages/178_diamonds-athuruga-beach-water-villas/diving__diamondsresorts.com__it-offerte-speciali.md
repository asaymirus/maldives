Offerta Safari & Spiaggia
Diamonds Malindi - Kenya
Prenota ora
Enjoy a minimum 4-night stay at Diamonds Malindi, where you can fully experience the resort’s facilities, restaurants, bars, and cultural workshops, before extending your journey into the wild and along the coast. This curated experience combines relaxation, discovery, and adventure, featuring a safari in Tsavo East with a 1 night stay at
KuduSafariCamp
and a beach escape to Watamu.
The Experience
Arrival & Resort Life
Settle into Diamonds Malindi and enjoy the full All-Inclusive experience, from diverse dining venues and vibrant bars to enriching cultural workshops and activities.
Beach Escape
Discover the beauty of our wild coastline with a day excursion to Exploreans Beach Club (just 20 minutes away), where you can unwind on white sandy beaches and enjoy a relaxed seaside lunch.
Safari Adventure
Embark on a 1 night / 2 days safari to Tsavo East National Park with a stay at Kudu Safari Luxury Tented Camp. Enjoy immersive wildlife encounters and two game drives in one of Kenya’s most iconic parks.
Return to the Coast
After your safari, return to Diamonds Malindi to continue your stay and unwind by the ocean.
What’s Included
– Minimum 4-night stay at Diamonds Malindi on All-Inclusive basis
– 1 night at Kudu Safari Tented Camp (Tsavo East) on Full Board
(including soft drinks, wine by the glass, and local beers)
– Round-trip transfers to/from the safari
– 2 game drives in Tsavo East National Park
– Full-day excursion to Exploreans Beach Club, Watamu
(including transport, sunbeds, umbrellas, and lunch as per Soft AI)
Not Included
Tsavo East National Park entrance fees:
USD 85 per adult / USD 45 per child (under 12)
(valid for 24 hours, payable at the Kudu Safari Desk upon arrival)
Terms & Conditions
Minimum stay: 4 nights, Minimum 2 pax
