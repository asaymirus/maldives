South Ari Ocean Season – 7 Night All Inclusive Dive Escape
Discover the quieter, wilder side of the Maldives.
Our Ocean Season Dive Package is crafted for certified divers who wish to explore the vibrant reefs of South Ari Atoll while enjoying the comfort of a relaxed all inclusive island stay.
Fewer boats, warm waters, dramatic skies and thriving marine life make this season especially rewarding for divers.
This seven night experience includes everything you need for a seamless and professionally organised dive holiday.
What Is Included
Accommodation
• 7 nights in your selected villa category
• Full All Inclusive meal plan
• Daily breakfast, lunch and dinner
• Selected beverages throughout your stay
Transfers
• Return seaplane transfers from Malé International Airport
Diving Experience
• Guided boat dives in South Ari Atoll
• Up to two dives per day
• Check dive included
• Professional iDive dive guides
• Tanks, weights and standard equipment
• Dive insurance coverage during your stay
The diving schedule is thoughtfully arranged around arrival and departure times to ensure safety and comfort. On arrival day, divers complete a check dive, followed by guided dives on subsequent days, with up to two dives daily depending on weather and sea conditions.

Our professional iDive instructors coordinate schedules so both guests can share the experience and maximise their time in the water.
