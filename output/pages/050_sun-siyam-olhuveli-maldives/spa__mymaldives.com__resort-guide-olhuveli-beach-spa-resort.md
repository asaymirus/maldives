This luxury resort situated in South Male’ Atoll offers beautifully appointed villas both on land and water.
Sun Siyam Olhuveli Resort presents you with the opportunity to experience the un-spoilt charm of the Maldives in surroundings that have been carefully developed to give you all the modern comforts while in no way detracting the island’s natural beauty.
