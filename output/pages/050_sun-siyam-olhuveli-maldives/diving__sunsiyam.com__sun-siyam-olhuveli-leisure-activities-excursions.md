Excursions at Sun Siyam Olhuveli
Explore the islands of the atoll while staying connected with complimentary WiFi available on the boat. Try your hand at catching a prized mahi-mahi on a fishing excursion. To catch a glimpse into local life, our Maldivian local island tour gives you the chance to meet local fisherman. Or for something totally different, head to our lively capital, Malé, for buzzing markets, mosques, and motorbikes galore.
