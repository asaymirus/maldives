Sun Siyam Olhuveli has kicked off the 2025–2026 festive season with a dynamic new take on its annual Festive Cake Mixing event-this year brought to life by none other than Charith N. Silva, one of South Asia’s most influential culinary creators. Shared with the Lets Go Maldives team, the event marks a bold and vibrant shift toward a more experiential, lifestyle-driven festive programme at the resort.
A Viral Culinary Star at Olhuveli
Held on 23 November, this year’s celebration was headlined by Sri Lankan chef, storyteller, and social media sensation Charith N. Silva, the creative force behind @wildcookbook. With millions of followers across YouTube, TikTok, and Instagram, Charith is globally recognised for his dramatic fire-cooking, immersive outdoor rituals, and energetic narrative style.
His recent honour-being named to the Forbes 30 Under 30 Asia 2025 list in the Arts (Art & Style, Food & Drink) category-cements his status as one of South Asia’s leading young culinary innovators. As the owner of Colombo’s trending restaurant Wildish, Charith blends local authenticity with modern inspiration, making him the ideal personality to elevate Olhuveli’s festive celebrations.
“Bringing my energy to Sun Siyam Olhuveli was surreal; the people, the Maldivian spirit, the flavour play-everything clicked into one unforgettable festive moment,” Charith shared.
A Festive Tradition Reimagined
Under Charith’s creative influence, the cake mixing took on the energy of an island festival. Guests and resort teams joined together to layer fruits, nuts, and spirits in an atmosphere buzzing with music, spices, and lively banter. Charith’s spontaneous style infused the experience with joy, fun, and a sense of shared celebration-transforming the beloved tradition into a social, sensory spectacle.
Exclusive Live Cooking Experience
The following day, on 24 November, guests were treated to an exclusive lagoon-front live cooking demonstration. Charith showcased his signature “wild” techniques-bringing fire, flavour, and storytelling together in a way that fully immersed guests in his creative world.
General Manager Hassan Adil noted,
“This celebration reflects the new creative pulse of Sun Siyam Olhuveli. Charith brought an energy that aligns perfectly with our evolving lifestyle identity, making this year’s cake mixing one of our most memorable yet.”
MYSTIVAL 2025–2026: A Festive Series to Remember
With the festive season now officially underway, Sun Siyam Olhuveli is preparing to welcome guests to MYSTIVAL 2025–2026, running from 21 December 2025 to 8 January 2026. This year’s edition promises:
Hypnotic beats and island rhythms
Immersive dining journeys
Playful festive rituals
Kaleidoscopic island experiences designed for connection, joy, and celebration
The resort’s refreshed festive identity continues to blend creativity, lifestyle energy, and the spirit of the Maldives-offering an elevated year-end escape for travellers from around the world.
