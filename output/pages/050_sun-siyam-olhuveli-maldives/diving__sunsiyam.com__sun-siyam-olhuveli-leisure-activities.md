Watersports
Not everyone comes to the Maldives to slow down. For every guest perfecting the art of doing nothing, there's another strapping into a kitesurf harness or gunning a jet ski across the lagoon. Olhuveli's built for both, and if you've never tried any of it before, that's exactly what our instructors are for.
Excursions
Ask ten guests what they did yesterday and you'll get ten different answers. One was chasing dolphins at sunset, another was wandering a local island market, someone else hasn't left their sunbed since breakfast. That's the whole point of an excursion here: the itinerary is yours to write.
Diving
There's the Maldives with its island-resorts, sandbanks and stretches of blue. And then there’s the Maldives below the surface of the water, with its sheltered reefs bursting with colour and aquatic life. Our dive centre gets you there, whether that means your very first breath through a regulator or your fiftieth night dive.
