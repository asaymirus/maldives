DESCRIPTION
The Paradise of Plenty
Set across three islands in the South Malé Atoll, Sun Siyam Olhuveli is just 45 minutes by speedboat from Malé. This four-star deluxe retreat combines affordable luxury with Maldivian charm, where turquoise lagoons, soft sands, and lush tropical beauty create the perfect island escape.
Stay in beachfront or overwater villas, swim in six inviting pools, and explore a vibrant house reef or vast lagoon ideal for water sports. Dive shipwrecks, surf world-class breaks, or spot dolphins at sunset before retreating to the spa for pure bliss.
With dining inspired by the sea and a choice of exceptional restaurants and bars, Olhuveli is a destination for couples, families, and friends seeking it all.

Dining
Sunset Restaurant
Set on the main island with an expansive overwater terrace, Sunset Restaurant offers all-day dining with a Maldivian touch. Breakfast, lunch, and dinner are served buffet-style, complemented by live cooking stations where chefs prepare dishes from around the world right before your eyes. From tropical mornings to themed evenings by the lagoon, it’s a vibrant dining experience blending global flavours with breathtaking views.
Island Pizza
Located next to the Sunrise Pool on the main island, Island Pizza offers a relaxed beachfront setting for lunch. Enjoy classic and seasonal pizzas made with fresh island ingredients, along with deli-style sandwiches, gourmet burgers, and light snacks. Perfect for a midday break from the sun, this casual pizzeria combines tropical charm with easygoing flavours for a laid-back dining experience by the water.
Dhoni Bar
Adjacent to the Infinity Pool on the main island, Dhoni Bar is a chic lounge perfect for sunset cocktails. Named after the traditional Maldivian dhoni boat, it offers some of the best views on the island. Relax on a chaise-longue on the expansive deck while sipping your favourite drink and enjoying the warm tropical evenings in this stylish and serene poolside setting.
Lagoon Bar
Set on the main island’s beach with a terrace extending over the lagoon, Lagoon Bar is the perfect spot for cocktails and evening entertainment. Enjoy a wide selection of drinks while watching lively Maldivian performances and soaking in the stunning lagoon views. It is an ideal place to relax, unwind, and enjoy vibrant tropical evenings in a chic overwater setting.
Sunrise Bar
Located next to the main pool on the main island, Sunrise Bar is the perfect spot for light bites and refreshing drinks throughout the day. Relax in comfortable chairs while enjoying first-class service and poolside views. As the sun sets, it becomes a vibrant yet relaxed space to unwind, making every moment from morning to evening an enjoyable part of your tropical escape.
Dream Bar
Located on Dream Island beside the infinity pool, Dream Bar serves light bites and refreshing drinks from morning until midnight. Whether you prefer lounging in the sun or cooling off in the water, this poolside spot ensures you never have to leave your perfect tropical perch while enjoying your favourite refreshments in a relaxed, vibrant setting.
Romance Bar
Exclusively for guests on Romance Island, this poolside bar offers a serene spot to enjoy mid-afternoon margaritas or evening sundowners. From morning until midnight, sip fruity cocktails, fresh juices, and refreshing drinks while savouring light bites. Set at the edge of the adults-only infinity pool, it is the perfect place to unwind, soak in the tropical surroundings, and enjoy a calm, relaxing escape in paradise.
Siyam Orchid
Siyam Orchid offers an elegant fine-dining experience with Japanese, Indian, and Thai dishes infused with Maldivian flavours. Set in a sleek, moody interior, it is perfect for dinner accompanied by a curated wine selection. Open in the evenings, it also serves breakfast for select VIP guests. With its refined atmosphere and expertly crafted cuisine, Siyam Orchid delivers a memorable culinary journey for guests seeking a sophisticated dining experience.

With something to suit every palate, this vibrant restaurant combines delicious flavours with a relaxed, welcoming tropical atmosphere.

This intimate beachfront venue delivers a unique and memorable culinary experience in paradise.

Highlights From The Resort
Wellness & Spa
Relax in eight Spa Pavilions blending traditional Asian rituals with modern therapies. Enjoy twin massage beds, rain showers, hydrotherapy pools, flower baths, and salon services for body, mind, and soul rejuvenation.
Diving & Snorkeling
Explore vibrant house reefs, underwater caves, shipwrecks, and manta ray sites. Snorkeling trips and diving courses cater to all ages and abilities.
Water Sports & Thrill Activities
Learn windsurfing, kayaking, sailing, kitesurfing, parasailing, or jet skiing with expert instructors. Packages include surfing at Guraidhoo Corner and adrenaline-filled experiences for adventure seekers.
Excursions & Nature
Island hopping, local Maldivian tours, dolphin cruises, night fishing, stargazing trips, and visits to marine hotspots for turtles, sharks, and manta rays.
Recreation & Entertainment
Six main pools, five children’s pools, infinity and sunrise pools, cultural evenings, live music, disco nights, and friendly sports competitions ensure fun for all.
Family & Kids
Kids eat and sleep free when sharing a villa with adults. Dedicated children’s pools, activities, and attentive services make the resort family-friendly and fuss-free.
