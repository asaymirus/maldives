Watersports at Sun Siyam Olhuveli
Our lagoon is the perfect playground for watersport enthusiasts. Whatever your experience, learn from the pros as they help you get to grips with windsurfing, kayaking or sailing – the choice is yours. For a fast paced afternoon jet skiing is a great option, or slow it down by simply hopping in a canoe.
