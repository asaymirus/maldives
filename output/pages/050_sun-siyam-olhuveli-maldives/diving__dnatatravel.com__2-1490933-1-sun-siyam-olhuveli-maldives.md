Overview
Property Location
Located in South Male Atoll, Sun Siyam Olhuveli spans three interconnected islands—Main, Dream, and Romance—offering a scenic retreat accessible by speedboat from Malé International Airport.
Rooms
The resort features a wide range of beach, water, and romance villas, each thoughtfully designed with private outdoor spaces and views of the lagoon, beach, or ocean. Options include multi-bedroom residences and villas with pools, catering to couples, families, and groups.
Amenities
Guests can enjoy multiple swimming pools, a wellness spa, fitness facilities, and a dive center. The resort also offers various water sports, excursions, and curated experiences that reflect Maldivian heritage and island living.
Dining
Dining venues include beachfront and overwater restaurants serving a variety of cuisines, from Asian and Indian to international favorites. Bars and lounges provide relaxed settings for drinks and light bites throughout the day and evening.
Other, Business Amenities
The resort offers event and meeting facilities suitable for business gatherings, weddings, and celebrations. A dedicated team assists with planning and entertainment, ensuring a seamless experience for all occasions.
