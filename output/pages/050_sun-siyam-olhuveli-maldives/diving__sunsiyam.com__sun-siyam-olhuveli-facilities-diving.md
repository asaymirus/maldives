Diving at Sun Siyam Olhuveli
Located at the welcome jetty on Main Island, our SSI-certified dive centre offers easy access to the beautiful house reef. Swim along and meet our underwater neighbours, or take the plunge to uncover underwater caves and sunken ships. If you’re lucky, you may even spot a sea turtle or get up close with the majestic manta rays living close by.
