Sun Siyam Olhuveli Must-Do
Pleasure, leisure, and memories to treasure — you can have it all.
From relaxing to heart-pounding, here is a rundown of our favourite activities Sun Siyam has to offer for every guest mindset.
Traditional Fishing
Set out at dawn or dusk aboard a traditional Dhoni and try your hand at hand-reel fishing, just as it has been practiced in the Maldives for generations. Cast your line into waters teeming with life. With a bit of luck, you might reel in a catch or simply enjoy the serene rhythm of the reef as the sun paints the sky in golden hues.
Local Island Exploration
Curious travellers take note: the Maldives is more than just white-sand beaches. Venture out to the neighbouring local islands and discover island life: bustling fishing villages, vibrant markets, and the warmth of Maldivian hospitality. Expert local guides can lead the way, sharing stories, traditions, and hidden gems, so you can experience the sights, sounds, and rhythms of everyday island living in an authentic way.
Snorkeling Excursion
A gentle and memorable way for the whole family to discover life beneath the waves, snorkeling lets you explore the vibrant reefs of the atoll. Suitable for all ages and abilities, equipment can be provided, and our guides will lead you to the best spots to encounter the region’s colorful marine life.
Jet Car
Discover a one-of-a-kind watersport with our sporty jet car. Glide across the lagoon and capture unforgettable, newsfeed-worthy moments as you experience the rush of this original island adventure. Perfect for thrill-seekers looking to combine fun, style, and an extraordinary ride.
Whale Shark Expedition
The Maldives is home to the ocean's gentle giants, the whale sharks. Set out with our team on an expedition to encounter whale sharks. It’s a perfect blend of adventure, discovery, and the serene beauty of the Maldives.

LEISURE ACTIVITIES
From night dives to freediving, from jetskiing to sunset cruises, we have it all. Truly.
Watersports
Not everyone comes to the Maldives to slow down. For every guest perfecting the art of doing nothing, there's another strapping into a kitesurf harness or gunning a jet ski across the lagoon. Olhuveli's built for both, and if you've never tried any of it before, that's exactly what our instructors are for.
Excursions
Ask ten guests what they did yesterday and you'll get ten different answers. One was chasing dolphins at sunset, another was wandering a local island market, someone else hasn't left their sunbed since breakfast. That's the whole point of an excursion here: the itinerary is yours to write.
Diving
There's the Maldives with its island-resorts, sandbanks and stretches of blue. And then there’s the Maldives below the surface of the water, with its sheltered reefs bursting with colour and aquatic life. Our dive centre gets you there, whether that means your very first breath through a regulator or your fiftieth night dive.
