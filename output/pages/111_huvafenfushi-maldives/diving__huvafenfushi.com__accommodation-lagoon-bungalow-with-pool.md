Maldives luxury villa living begins above the lagoon, where your private overwater deck opens onto uninterrupted sunrise views and a freshwater pool with direct access to the sea.
The Lagoon Bungalow is designed for life at the water’s edge, with a spacious interior, contemporary details and the Indian Ocean stretching endlessly beyond.
Your dedicated thakuru is on hand when you need them, leaving you free to settle into the rhythm of island life. Swim, snorkel, linger over breakfast or simply watch the world drift by.
There’s nowhere to rush to. And nothing you need to do.
Just turquoise water, open skies and your own corner of Huvafen.
