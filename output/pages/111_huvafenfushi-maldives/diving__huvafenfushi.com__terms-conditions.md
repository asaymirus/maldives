Special periods and length of stay during these dates
A minimum stay of 5 nights per bungalow is required for stays falling over the New Year’s period (30th December, 31st December & 1st January).
These dates — from 30th December to 1st January (inclusive) — are also considered no check-in and no check-out dates.
Speed boat transfers:
The resort is approximately 30 minutes away from Velana International Airport by speedboat.
The shared speedboat transfer rate is US$300 per adult return trip.
The private speedboat transfer rate is US$750 per way*
The private Luxury Speedboat transfer rate is US$3000 per way*
For shared transfers, there may be a waiting time at Huvafen Airport Lounge, a maximum of 30-40 minutes.
*Prices are for up to 6 pax per boat, per way.
* Transfer rates are subject to a 10% service charge & 17% government goods & service (GST) tax.
