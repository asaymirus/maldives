For a 2 Bedroom Villa Maldives retreat, expect space to share, space to escape and an Indian Ocean view that’s impossible to ignore.
The Two Bedroom Ocean Pavilion brings together two bedrooms, two bathrooms and generous living spaces around a private indoor-outdoor freshwater pool, with direct access to the lagoon.
As the sun drops towards the horizon, the pavilion comes into its own. Swim beneath the changing light, gather for a drink on the deck or retreat indoors when the mood takes you.
With thoughtful design, unexpected details and plenty of room to simply be together, this is Huvafen at its most expansive – made for families and friends who want the island to themselves.
