Hotel Highlights
Huvafen Fushi
Just 46 modern bungalows ensure an exclusively intimate experience at this super-chic resort. Uber-stylish bungalows, all featuring a private plunge pool are swathed in calming, sophisticated hues; every imaginable luxury sits side by side with jaw-dropping views au naturel! Beach Bungalows have a gorgeous plunge pool in the rear courtyard and Deluxe Beach Bungalows, boasting a whopping 195 square metres of space have two pools, one on the beach and one at the rear. They have direct access to the soft sands and face the gentle waters of the lagoon; a private courtyard reveals an oversized bath, fresh water plunge pool and a waterfall shower. At Turquoise we have a real soft spot for the Lagoon Bungalows, the sun kissed deck leads you down into the warm, tropical waters and an oversized day bed sits next to your plunge pool, while your free-standing bath is cantilevered over the Indian Ocean! Ocean Bungalows are aptly named and look out over the miles of sparkling ocean that surrounds you. A freshwater plunge pool sits on one of three decks, while your living space offers glass flooring for epic marine viewing. Water-bliss continues with the addition of an oversized Jacuzzi bath; perfect for sundowners. Naturally, a dedicated Thakuru (butler) is on call 24 hours. There is a choice of stunning Pool Pavilions - Two Bedroom Ocean Pavilion with Pool, Two Bedroom Beach Pavilion with Pool.
Dining takes shape in the form of Feeling Koi, a divine overwater Japanese extravaganza with a Latin twist and at Celsius, an overwater honey-hued pavilion offering breakfast and dinner. Raw is a healthy Eastern inspired spot by day and a chilled lounge bar by night. Forno brings the Mediterranean to the Indian Ocean with fun wood-fired pizzas and fresh antipasti – all served pool side for the perfect lunch. Choose from a selection of destination dining experiences with wine and cheeses tastings, a sandbank picnic or dine eight metres below the ocean for an unforgettable experience. After dinner, head to Vinum, the Maldives first underground cellar holding one of the largest collections consisting of over 600 labels and 5,000 bottles!
Huvafen Spa is like nothing else we have seen. From the surreal to the sublime with eight treatment rooms, two of which are underwater with glass walls to view the marine life. Everywhere the slow, rhythmic flow of the water surrounds you and it’s almost an unspoken rule between guests that you must try at least one treatment in the underwater space – we have to say we definitely agree.
If you are honeymooning straight after your wedding, the chances are you are going to be very tired from the weeks of planning and huge celebrations! Huvafen Fushi will take care of you and as every villa has its own private pool you don’t need to leave the comfort and privacy of your private villa to enjoy the delights of the Maldives. So relax, sunbathe on your loungers, take a dip in your own pool and enjoy each other’s company.
Romantic dinners – what better and more romantic way to enjoy dinner together than on board a traditional Maldivian Dhoni boat cruising in to the sunset with Dolphins playing by your boat or if that doesn’t take your fancy, dine in for two with a twist and enjoy dinner underground at Vinum, where you can have dinner for two, surrounded by over 6,000 bottles of wine and with a hot stone beneath your feet to keep you warm.
The spa is one of the best in the Indian Ocean, with a choice of underwater treatment rooms, indulge in a couples massage whilst watching the marine life. You cant get more relaxed than this!
If this doesn’t tempt you then I don’t know what will!
Honeymooners also receive luxury extras like Champagne in your villa and a Spa Sampler!
P.s Don’t forget to head to the beach at 5:30pm to feed the Stingray!
