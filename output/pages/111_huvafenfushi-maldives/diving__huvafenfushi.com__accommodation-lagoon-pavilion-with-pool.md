Wake to the first light over North Malé Atoll from a luxury water villa Maldives escape, with the lagoon at the centre of everything.
The Two Bedroom Lagoon Pavilion looks across Huvafen’s central seawater lagoon, with two bedrooms, two bathrooms and a private outdoor pool. Step from your deck into the water, settle in for breakfast as the island wakes or watch the colours change across the horizon.
Designed for time together, with plenty of space for everyone to find their own rhythm, this is an overwater escape made for family and friends.
Your thakuru is on hand when you need them. Otherwise, the lagoon has your attention.
