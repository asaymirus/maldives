LOCATION
It is a feeling of pure liberation. An enchanted hideaway on North Malé Atoll, just a 30-minute glide by luxury speedboat from the international airport.
Welcome to paradise. Settle into the comforts of our dedicated private lounge at the airport, and within an hour you’ll be relaxing on the shimmering sands.
Huvafen Fushi is where serene privacy meets effortless freedom. We are close enough to the airport to provide easy access, yet far enough to provide total seclusion – and direct transfers are available any time of day or night.
GETTING HERE
Our airport team will meet you at the arrivals hall when you land, and escort you to our private airport lounge to prepare for departure. Huvafen Fushi is only 26 km from Velana International Airport.
Transfers are on a shared basis, and combined with other guests arriving at similar times. Private transfer upgrades are also available by advanced request.
