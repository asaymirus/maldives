Experience serene, personalised self-discovery on our dream island. Indulge in calming solitude amidst pristine beaches and aqua-blue waters. Enjoy yoga sessions, restorative spa treatments, and enchanting feasts, all with a naturally enchanting backdrop.
Indulge
in exquisite dining at Celsius. Start your morning with freshly squeezed juices, energising acai bowls or indulgent Belgian waffles, setting the tone for the perfect day. As evening falls, choose from a diverse menu featuring premium ingredients from land and sea. Allow the tantalising aromas of Asian spices, expertly prepared meats and the freshest local fish to captivate your senses and elevate your dining experience.
Discover
the dazzling underwater world of our house reef with an orientation dive and two thrilling single-tank dives.
Retreat
to our overwater treatment pavilions, poised above the surf, for two personalised treatments designed to soothe and restore.
Whether you’re travelling with a loved one, a sibling or a friend, you can customise your escape to suit your unique interests. Perhaps you both share a passion for diving or maybe one of you prefers the serenity of the spa.
How you experience your personalised holiday is up to you.
For example, one guest might embark on an underwater adventure, enjoying the orientation dive and two single-tank dives, while their companion finds serenity in the spa with two rejuvenating treatments.
Alternatively, both of you can blend the best of both worlds, each enjoying one dive and one spa treatment, along with an additional mandatory orientation dive. You can also enhance your experience with a 10% discount on additional dives or spa sessions.
Experience the perfect balance of adventure, relaxation and gourmet indulgence, and create unforgettable memories with every moment of your tailored retreat.
