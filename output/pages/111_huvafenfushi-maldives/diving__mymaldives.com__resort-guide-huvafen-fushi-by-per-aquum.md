Find yourself in a waking dream at Huvafen Fushi by Per AQUUM. Show casing the world’s first underwater spa treatment rooms. Flowing. Continuous. Freedom. 43 bungalows, indulge in a private courtyard and freshwater pool along with the luxurious amenities expected of a boutique island resort. The privileged who have the pleasure of experiencing the pavilions will experience pure escapism and unbridled luxury. Seductive cuisine is superb and extremely intimate, with 3 restaurants, bar and underground wine cellar. Additional facilities are abundant, experience the yoga pavilion or enjoy complimentary non- motorized water sports to name a few.
