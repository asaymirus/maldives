RESTAURANTS
At Huvafen Fushi, every dish is curated to spark joy and create lasting memories. Discover contemporary classics, international favourites and authentic Maldivian specialities from dawn until dusk at our many idyllic venues dotted across the island. Here, exquisite flavours are served with a gentle sea breeze and uninterrupted views, while intimate fine dining experiences are creatively presented in the most enchanting private settings.
SALT
Considered one of the best restaurants in Maldives, SALT blends elegant Japanese dining with distinct Latin influences. Settle into a relaxed Izakaya-style environment, poised over the sea, then uncover the epicurean secrets of Salt as the sun dips below the horizon.
