RAW FISHING
Sail over crystal waters in a dhoni, beneath the setting sun and learn about one of the oldest Maldives traditions. Also known as Handline Fishing, this method has been practiced for countless generations. It is simple yet effective, and involves no reels. Instead, bait dances near the sea floor, completely controlled by hand.
