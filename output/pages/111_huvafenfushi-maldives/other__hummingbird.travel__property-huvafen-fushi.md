ENQUIRE ABOUT THIS PROPERTY
In the clear waters of North Malé Atoll, a contemporary, design-led resort known for its intimate scale and strong focus on understated luxury and marine experiences. Accommodation is sleek and private, with beach and overwater bungalows featuring plunge pools and direct lagoon access. The atmosphere is calm and highly refined, with a signature underwater spa and a curated dining offering shaping the experience. A natural fit for couples seeking a discreet, high-comfort Maldives stay with strong design identity and close proximity to Malé.
Review
I was really excited to return to Huvafen Fushi after their refurbishment and it didn't disappoint. The rooms are light, elegant and serene, with newly enclosed bathrooms and private pools at both the back and front of each villa. All the best features of this very pretty island remain reassuringly intact - their excellent house reef, lush greenery, intimate atmosphere and delicious food - but everything now feels fresh, elevated and back where Huvafen belongs.

Food &  Beverage
Celsius
At Celsius, guests awaken to the aroma of fresh-ground Arabica in the early morning, stimulating the senses. In the evening, the faint aroma of piquant curry spices drifts on the ocean breeze, enhancing the dining experience. This partial over-water venue offers international cuisine against a backdrop of sparkling seas, tantalising the palate with a selection of flavours for breakfast and dinner.
Fogliani's
At Fogliani's, guests enjoy contemporary Mediterranean cuisine in a casual beachside setting under palm shade. Artisanal pizzas, handmade pastas, and imaginative fusion dishes are highlights, elevated by weekly live cooking shows.
RAW
At RAW, guests savor 100% raw, organic, and wholesome food in a relaxed bistro-style setting overlooking uninterrupted ocean panoramas. Invigorating juices and freshly handpicked ingredients from the chef’s garden complement lean and clean selections for a healthy lunch, all paired with glittering views of the Maldives.
SALT
At SALT, guests indulge in incredible dishes shared over serene waters, where the Japanese gastropub concept meets Latin inspiration. Each dish is meticulously prepared using the finest quality ingredients, presented simply yet exquisitely in true Huvafen Fushi style.
UMBAR
At UMBar, guests lounge on oversized cushions by the poolside, enjoying uninterrupted views of the white-sand beach with waves lapping the shore. Sophisticated bites and savory snacks are plated to perfection, complemented by weekly gin tastings for the curious connoisseur. Stirring jazz notes fill the air as UMBar's handcrafted cocktails and refined bar offer an effortless indulgence.
Vinum
Discover a hidden treasure—the Maldives’ first underground wine cellar. This unique space offers exquisite wine tastings, six-course gourmet dinners, and degustation menus. Guests can awaken their palate amidst chic décor and suspended art installations, complemented by daily wine and cheese pairings in an atmosphere like no other.
