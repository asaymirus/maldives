EXPERIENCES
INSPIRATIONAL SPACES
Iconic and original, with singular experiences waiting to be discovered in your home away from home.
INFINITY POOL
Slip into the cool water of your infinity pool with the horizon at its edge. Marvel at the postcard surroundings and nature’s radiance. Sip a cocktail and revel in your refreshing dip. By night, watch stars twinkle overhead as you serenely float in the moonlight below or lounge on a deck chair.
LONU VEYO
Private hideaways. Slipping between trees, to hidden retreats. At Lonu Veyo, swim languidly in a circular saltwater pool. Buoyant and carefree. The waters of the Indian Ocean support your blissful reverie. Heavenly.
YOGA
Rediscover yourself with a little healthy me time. Revitalise. Rejuvenate. An indulgence for your body and even your mind with our internationally certified wellness and yoga instructors. Learn healing postures and breathing techniques for holistic wellness. Invigorating.
VINUM
Mine the island’s depths in the Maldives’ first underground wine cellar. Eight metres below ground discover a treasure trove of rare vintages and hidden gems. Uncork something magnificent and savour every drop.
MOVIE UNDER THE STARS
Sink into an oversized beanbag for two under the arc of the Milky Way. Take in epic Hollywood romances and international classics. Popcorn plus a selection of gourmet snacks and beverages are there to share, like the memory you are creating.
LAIR
The Lair awaits with tales of lands afar. Indulge in some time by yourself with a favourite novel or beloved yarn from long ago. Sip coffee or tea at computer terminals that connect you to the world beyond the horizon. A refuge of distraction.
GYM
Work out without working. Feel your heart race from a blend of exertion and excitement for what is to come. Our singular overwater workout facililty, available 24/7, will leave you with renewed vitality and a clear mind.
