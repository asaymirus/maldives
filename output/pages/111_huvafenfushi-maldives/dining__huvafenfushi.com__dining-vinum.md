VINUM
BACK
Sink into the depths eight metres below the surface. Gaze upon a treasure vault of rare vintages and hidden gems. With more than 600 labels and 5,000 bottles, Vinum holds one of the largest collections among any Maldives resort bar in the Indian Ocean.
The Maldives’ first underground wine cellar also offers degustation menus, six-course gourmet dinners and daily wine and cheese tastings. Awaken your palate amidst chic décor, beneath suspended art installations. Reservations required.
