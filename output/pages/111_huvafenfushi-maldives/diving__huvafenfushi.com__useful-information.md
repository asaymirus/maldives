PAYMENTS AT THE RESORT
For guests’ convenience, all purchases at the resort can be easily signed to their room account with a renewable credit limit of USD 5,000, extended to all rooms. Payments can be made in cash, credit or debit card (Visa, MasterCard, Amex, Union Pay, JCB).
TAXES
A mandatory government service tax (GST) of 17% and a 10% resort service charge will be applied to all bills.
WHAT TO PACK
The island’s laidback, luxury style invites light, loose-fitting clothing. Light cotton and linen fabrics. Shorts, Kaftans, sandals and swimwear are staples.
For all those carefree days lounging poolside and basking in the breeze, we recommend a good book and a curated playlist. The Maldives sun is strong, so a hat, sunglasses and high-SPF sunscreen are essential.
At our dining venues in the evenings, you are best served wearing smart casual attire. Due to the region’s environment, suitable footwear is also recommended.
FACILITIES
ELECTRICITY
Electricity is supplied at 220 to 240 volts. Adaptors are available on request.
INTERNET ACCESS
Complimentary Wi-Fi is accessible across the island at all times.
LAUNDRY
Laundry bags are provided in all guest rooms for those who wish to use our convenient pick-up and drop-off laundry service. Dry cleaning is also not available on the island — however, garments can be sent to Malé for dry cleaning upon request.
MEDICAL FACILITIES
A resident doctor is available at the resort clinic during scheduled opening hours and is on call 24/7. In case of emergency, the main hospitals at Malé can be reached via a 30-minute speedboat ride. For diving emergencies, decompression chambers can be reached in approximately 45 minutes by speedboat.
SNORKELING & DIVING
Plunge beneath the surface. A world teeming with colour awaits. Swim straight from the beach to Huvafen Fushi’s own house reef, which rings the island around 25 metres from shore. Book a guided house reef snorkelling trip with the friendly FLOAT team, or take a complimentary snorkel set and explore on your own. Dive deeper into the magical underwater realm with variety of PADI certified dives and courses.

LOCATION
ISLAND SIZE
Huvafen Fushi is located in a compact and utterly discreet location. It measures approximately 350 meters by 150 meters and can be circumvented on foot in 10 to 15 minutes. The overwater jetty extends 500 meters out over the ocean to the furthest pavilion.
GETTING HERE
Conveniently located in the North Male atoll and a mere 26km from Velana International Airport, Huvafen Fushi can be reached in 30 minutes by speedboat. Upon arrival to the Maldives from your international flight, our airport team will meet you at the arrivals hall and escort you to our private airport lounge to prepare you for departure. Transfers are on a shared basis combined with other guests arriving at similar times. Private transfer upgrades are available upon advanced request.
TIME ZONE
Huvafen Fushi is five hours ahead of Greenwich Mean Time (GMT +5).
WEATHER
The Maldives is a year-round destination, with two main seasons. November to April is typically hot and dry with temperatures reaching low to mid 30s (degrees Celsius). May to October can bring rain showers, followed by sunshine and slightly cooler temperatures around the mid to high 20s.

CURRENCY

SUSTAINABILITY
Huvafen Fushi strives to reduce its environmental footprint through ethically-oriented practices. This includes a reef-restoration project with the marine biology team at OCEANPEDIA. Methods like frames, ropes, lines and nubbins (a single coral fragment) are used to boost the health of the house reef and propagate further growth.
Water is desalinated, treated and bottled in reusable glass bottles. No plastic bottles are imported, and paper straws have long-since replaced plastic. Green refuse such as fallen palm fronds and dead leaves are composed and used to nourish the island’s lush vegetation. Gardens are irrigated with the island’s treated water, and solar power is harnessed to provide hot water in guest rooms. All waste is managed, segregated and recycled where possible.
Huvafen Fushi supports local communities by sourcing products from local island suppliers wherever possible – including seafood and fresh produce. Younger community members are invited to participate in the Dream Journey apprenticeship programme, providing hands-on work experience in various departments throughout the resort to build their knowledge of the hospitality industry and support their studies.
