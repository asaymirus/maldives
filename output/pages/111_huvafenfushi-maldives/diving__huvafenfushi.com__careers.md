CAREERS AT HUVAFEN FUSHI
Huvafen Fushi is Dhivehi for ‘Dream Island’, a dream created for only the most discerning of travellers. This dream would be impossible without the talent, creativity and unsurpassed levels of service by our staff, or as we like to call them – The Dream Team.
MISSION & VISION
Huvafen Fushi was a pioneer of Maldivian luxury tourism, crafting distinctly imagined journeys that inspire and enrich our guests. Our vision is to see travel serve a greater purpose – to empower people to reach their full potential.
OUR DREAM TEAM
Caring for paradise takes vision, effort, collaboration and passion. Each day we strive to make the world a little better, and every member of the team plays an integral role in bringing new dreams to life.
From marine biologists and renowned health experts, to world-class chefs, fishing guides and Maldivian Thakuru, together we protect the heaven we’ve inherited and help turn days in North Malé atoll into fantasies made real.
