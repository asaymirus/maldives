SUSTAINABILITY
A SUSTAINABLE JOURNEY
Nestled in the heart of the Maldives, Huvafen Fushi stands as an embodiment of paradise on Earth. Its pristine beaches, crystal-clear waters, and luxurious accommodations have long captured the hearts of travellers seeking the ultimate island escape. But beneath the lavish exterior lies our ongoing commitment to sustainability.
A DEEP CONNECTION TO NATURE
The resort’s sustainability journey begins with its profound connection to nature. Surrounded by the azure waters of the Indian Ocean, Huvafen Fushi understands the importance of preserving this natural wonder. The resort’s dedication to sustainable practices is rooted in its desire to protect the very environment that makes it so special.
ECO-FRIENDLY ACCOMMODATIONS
Huvafen Fushi offers a range of eco-friendly accommodations, including overwater and beachfront bungalows and pavilions designed with sustainability in mind. These luxurious yet eco-conscious dwellings blend seamlessly with the island’s natural beauty while minimizing their environmental footprint.
SOLAR POWER AND
ENERGY EFFICIENCY
In the heart of this pristine paradise we harness the power of the sun to illuminate both its luxurious accommodations and our unwavering commitment to responsible environmental stewardship.
WATER CONSERVATION
As an island paradise, water is a precious resource at Huvafen Fushi. The resort employs advanced water-saving technologies, such as desalination and rainwater harvesting systems, to ensure responsible water usage. Guests are also encouraged to participate in water conservation efforts during their stay.
GLASS BOTTLING CONCEPT
Through our ongoing campaign against plastic pollution, Huvafen Fushi has eliminated plastic bottles from the premises. Since February 1st, 2018, the resort has partnered with the renowned manufacturer Eco Pure Waters to introduce the glass bottling concept.
CULINARY EXCELLENCE WITH A GREEN TOUCH
Even the resort’s culinary experiences are infused with sustainability. Huvafen Fushi’s restaurants prioritise locally sourced ingredients, reducing food miles and supporting the local economy. Our innovative chefs incorporate organic produce and fresh seafood from nearby waters into delectable dishes that highlight the flavors of the Maldives.
MARINE CONSERVATION
Huvafen Fushi takes marine conservation to heart. The resort actively participates in coral reef restoration projects and hosts educational programs to raise awareness about the fragile marine ecosystems surrounding the Maldives. Guests can join snorkelling and diving excursions to witness the vibrant underwater world and gain a deeper appreciation for the need to protect it.
A GREEN LEGACY
Huvafen Fushi’s sustainability journey is not just a passing phase; it’s a commitment to leave a green legacy. The resort’s eco-conscious practices extend to waste reduction, recycling, and outreach programs that benefit the local island communities.
SEGREGATION OF WASTE (SMART BINS USAGE)
Recognizing the importance of responsible waste management, Huvafen Fushi adopted a strategic approach to waste segregation. The island employs a SMART BINS system, meticulously categorising waste to facilitate efficient recycling. This commitment to waste reduction showcases the resort’s dedication to preserving the pristine environment that surrounds us.
