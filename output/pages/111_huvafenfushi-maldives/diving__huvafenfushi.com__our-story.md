A SANCTUARY OF WONDER
Reject the ordinary. Rethink what matters. Return to yourself. It’s time to create a new reality at Huvafen Fushi, the place where your dreams become your days.
PARADISE REDEFINED
Huvafen is a living dream. A lush tropical hideaway of cinematic seascapes and unparalleled luxury. It is a paradise of pristine beauty set in the heart of the Maldives, where pleasure and purpose coexist. Its timeless allure and fresh laid-back sophistication have captivated curious dreamers for decades.
We are the benchmark for awe-inspiring tropical life, and with fewer than 50 rooms and
Thakuru
butler service, our five-star resort epitomises the intimacy of an idyllic private island getaway. Plunge into aquamarine waters within an hour of landing, explore the North Malé atoll house reef and revel in spectacular Maldivian horizons while expanding your own.
Grow, Rest and Indulge.
Expand your mind on a personalised dive. Learn about marine wildlife and habitats as you slip into the water … Sink into calm and bliss in the world’s only underwater spa. Explore wellbeing, nutrition, sleep, fitness and mindfulness … Savour captivating cuisine in island spaces and be transported to realms of wonder.
From underwater adventures and sophisticated scenes to wellness journeys and days cut from dreams, there is something at Huvafen for every imagination.
SMALL LUXURY HOTELS OF THE WORLD
Proudly part of the illustrious Small Luxury Hotels of the World (SLH) family, Huvafen Fushi epitomises legendary quality, style and service, earning the coveted SLH seal of excellence.
