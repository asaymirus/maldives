DESCRIPTION
‘The charming Italian-influenced Nika Island Resort & Spa
is full of quirky character, with friendly staff and a laid-back atmosphere’
The spacious beach villas were designed by an architect who was inspired by the swirling shape of a seashell. They are amongst the largest in the Maldives and feature romantic four poster beds and private beaches, complete with Jacuzzis. The water villas even have their own stretch of ‘beach’, as one of the multi-layer sun-decks is covered in soft sand from the beach. The top deck, on the roof, commands spectacular views across the ocean. The Italian cuisine at the restaurant is first class, and the menu of course features home-made ice-cream, as well as international dishes. Dine in the open pavilion or under the stars next to the beach. Swim in the sparkling water, explore the dive sites or sip a sunset cocktail at the lagoon-side bar.

Dining
Nika Restaurant
Nika Restaurant offers full-board, half-board or all-inclusive packages, with a buffet menu alternating with the à la carte menu.
Once a week, a Maldivian buffet and barbecue is served.
The restaurant is a welcoming, large open-air structure.
The cuisine is predominantly Italian and Mediterranean.
Our chefs are available to fulfill any request, to cater also to guests with food allergies. The island’s wine cellar is stocked with over 100 types of wine.
Based upon request, a special candlelit dinner can be arranged with a fish- and shellfish-based menu, either on your villa’s private beach or on the desert island just a few minutes boat ride from Nika.
Bepi Bar
Bepi Bar and the Coffee Shop are two welcoming meeting spots where to enjoy spectacular sunsets and sip amazing aperitif cocktails. The Coffee Shop also features a small library with a collection of books and publications in various languages.
Dining Experience
