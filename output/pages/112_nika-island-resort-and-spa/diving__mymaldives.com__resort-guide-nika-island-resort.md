Nika Island Resort is a hidden gem nestled in the Maldives, specifically within the North Ari Atoll. Your private island getaway awaits at this Italian-owned and Maldivian-managed haven, with villas generously scattered throughout lush gardens. Each villa provides
your
own stretch of sandy beach, ensuring seclusion and direct access to the turquoise waters.
The journey to Nika Island Resort begins with a scenic 25-minute seaplane flight from Male International Airport, offering breathtaking views of the atoll. You’ll be greeted upon arrival and guided through a seamless check-in process, setting the tone for a relaxing stay.
When you arrive, you’ll find a range of accommodations to choose from, each promising comfort and privacy.
At Nika Island Resort, expect to immerse yourself in aquatic adventures right from your doorstep. Outstanding snorkeling opportunities abound at the house reef, where you’ll encounter vibrant marine life. To make the most of
your
sunny days, the island operates on a unique two-hour time difference from Male, extending
your
daylight hours for exploration or simply unwinding on
your
own beach with a cocktail in hand.

Restaurants, Coffee Shops and Bars
At Nika Island Resort, your culinary experiences are diverse and cater to your palate. Nika Restaurant offers globally inspired dishes with a focus on Italian and Mediterranean flavors. It provides various meal plans, including full-board, half-board or an all-inclusive option for your convenience.
Breakfast starts your day with a satisfying buffet, letting you choose from an array of international and Maldivian staples. For lunch and dinner, delight in the alternating buffet and à la carte menus.
Don’t miss the Maldivian buffet and barbecue offered weekly, giving you a taste of local tradition.
Feel the ocean breeze at the casual PEPI BAR, where you can relax with pre- or post-dinner drinks. Enjoy a fine selection of wines or island-inspired cocktails. At the Coffee Shop/Library, grab a light refreshment and a book for a perfect beachside afternoon.
Your stay is enriched with the flavors and ambience of Maldives at Nika Island Resort’s dining venues.
