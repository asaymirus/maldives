OBLU NATURE
Helengeli
Explore, Dive In, Experience The Wonder
In the North Malé Atoll, there is a rare, untouched corner of the Maldives where the jungle canopy grows thick, and the ocean feels completely alive. OBLU NATURE Helengeli by Sentido is built into that wild beauty. Encircled by a legendary house reef and cloaked in tropical green, this island resort stays true to its roots, with a vibrant underwater world waiting just metres from your villa. Few places blur the line between island life and the natural world quite like this. The Island Plan invites you to experience the true spirit of Helengeli with all-inclusive dining, beverages, a replenished minibar, snorkelling equipment, exciting excursions, and immersive island experiences surrounded by nature’s beauty.

IMMERSE IN THE WILD SIDE OF PARADISE.
Some Maldivian islands feel carefully curated. Helengeli feels wonderfully alive. Sitting at the entrance to a primary marine channel, the island is home to silver-tipped sharks, sea turtles, giant lobsters, and a thriving underwater world. This is a place where nature still calls the shots.
