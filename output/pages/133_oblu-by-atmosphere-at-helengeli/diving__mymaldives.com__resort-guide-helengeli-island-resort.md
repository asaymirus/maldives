Helengeli Island Resort, situated at the very north of the Male Atoll, this island lies far away from other resorts, making this the ideal resort for divers and snorkelers. Divers will rarely see another dive boat and many dive sites are hardly visited enabling guests to see many fish otherwise rarely seen. The untouched housereef allows for unforgettable experiences while the fine sandy beach invites sunbathers. Several Excursions including dolphin tours and island hopping are offered. Visit www.helengeli.net for detailed information
