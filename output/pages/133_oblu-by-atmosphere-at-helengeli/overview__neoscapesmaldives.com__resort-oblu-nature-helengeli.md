DESCRIPTION
‘Immerse in the Wild Side of Paradise’
Refreshed and reimagined, OBLU NATURE Helengeli by SENTIDO is a vibrant four-star retreat where nature and adventure come alive. Discover the island’s legendary house reef, home to reef sharks, turtles, and colourful marine life. Dive or snorkel in crystal-clear waters or unwind in stylish new water villas with stunning lagoon views.
With a generous all-inclusive plan, guests enjoy premium dining, revitalizing spa treatments, and a variety of island activities. This is the ideal escape for those who seek quality, excitement, and connection with nature.

Dining
RAA BANDHI - Bar & Pool
Bohemian charm meets tropical indulgence at this lively ocean-view bar and family pool. Sip unlimited signature cocktails, mocktails, and refreshing drinks crafted by expert mixologists. Lounge by the pool, sway on overwater hammocks, or enjoy the indoor games and occasional dance floor vibes. Perfect for sunset sips, light bites, and relaxed island moments.
THE SPICE – All-Day Dining
Savor global flavours with a Maldivian twist at this vibrant lagoon-side restaurant, open daily for breakfast, lunch, and dinner. Enjoy a relaxed assisted buffet with international favourites, local specialties, and live cooking stations that bring fresh dishes to life.
Dine indoors on a sandy floor or out on the wooden deck with ocean views. Fridays come alive with Indian Ocean Night, featuring an elaborate spread, unlimited cocktails, and traditional Bodu Beru music and dance for an unforgettable cultural celebration.
JUST GRILL – Beach Grill
Dine barefoot on the sand with the ocean as your backdrop at this Mediterranean-inspired beach grill. Enjoy freshly grilled seafood and meats made with locally sourced ingredients, served in a warm, romantic setting. With candlelight, sea breeze, and the sizzle of the grill, Just Grill offers an intimate dining experience full of flavour and island charm. Open for lunch and dinner.
RAGA ROUTE
Embark on a sensory journey where traditional Indian flavours meet modern flair. This elegant restaurant blends bold spices with creative presentation, offering a fresh take on classic dishes. Set against stunning sunset views, the ambiance comes alive with the rhythms of tabla, sitar, and soulful vocals. Enjoy a thoughtfully curated menu alongside wines, beers, soft drinks, and warm brews. Open for dinner.
Dine by Design
Savor a private, candlelit dinner at one of the island’s most scenic spots. Enjoy a specially curated menu and personalised service for an unforgettable dining experience under the stars.
THUNDI Beach Bar
This tranquil, barefoot beachside retreat is nestled between two ocean fronts, offering guests a peaceful escape with privacy and uninterrupted panoramic views of the Indian Ocean.
